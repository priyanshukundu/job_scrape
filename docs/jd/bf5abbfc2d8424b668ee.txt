The Opportunity EY has curated a dynamic ecosystem of Alliance and Ecosystem relationships which creates new forms of customer value to help our clients rapidly and digitally transform. Our ecosystem consists of more than 100 relationships which allow us to provide a wide range of collaborative opportunities and solutions to our clients. We help them tap into the latest technologies to achieve transformational outcomes and be more agile, innovative, resilient and better equipped to respond to disruptive change. Are you passionate about shaping the future of technology through strategic partnerships and innovation ecosystems? EY is seeking a Emerging Technology Ecosystems Manager to manage the development and execution of high-impact collaborations across the tech landscape. In this role, you’ll work with a strategically lean team at the intersection of innovation, strategy, and relationship management, supporting alliance and ecosystem transformative initiatives with leading technology providers, managing the day-to-day aspects of AI partnerships while working across multiple service lines and sectors. Your Key Responsibilities You’ll be part of EY’s Emerging Relationships team that sits under the Alliance and Ecosystems group, closely connected with our AI practice and CTO network, helping to accelerate the adoption of emerging technologies by supporting engagements with venture capital and early-stage startup. This position involves relationship management among diverse stakeholders, including technology providers, research institutions, and industry leaders. By facilitating knowledge sharing and resource exchange, you will aim to drive the development of cutting-edge technology solutions that address complex challenges. Additionally, you will be support the identification of emerging trends, aligning ecosystem initiatives with EY goals, and promoting best practices to ensure sustainable growth and competitive advantage in the rapidly evolving tech market. Your primary responsibilities will include: Execute emerging tech ecosystem strategy and objectives Facilitate top-to-top emerging tech alliance relationships and program management to mobilize on sales plans, craft joint opportunities and market activity, and drive action through internal and external organizations. Drive results through disciplined, industrialized governance with clear accountability across Global level Service Line/practice, sectors, accounts/regions Work with Go-to-Market Alliance Leadership to develop emerging tech business cases, use cases, opportunities and sales plans. Drive compliance on emerging tech initiatives, ensuring compliance with relevant regulations, ethical standards, and best practices in data privacy and security, closely collaborating with Ecosystem Relationship Enablement function on formation efforts. Deliver alliance intelligence with complete and accurate Financial reporting, Emerging relationship impact/influence. Activate new countries and regions ensuring proper governance is in place, e.g. supporting business plans, executive relationships, operating cadence and enabling alliance contracts Encourage and support R&D efforts by alliance partners to explore new emerging tech methodologies and applications that can benefit internal and external clients . Contributes to the Global Alliance and Ecosystem Relationship network by contributing content, knowledge sharing, and market insights This role is based in the US, offering a mix of virtual work and regular coordination with team members globally. Some travel may be required. Skills and Attributes for Success To succeed in the role, you must have: Experience in alliance management or business development for technology or professional services company. Experience and evidence of success in building and nurturing win-win alliance relationships. Ability to work effectively within high-performing teams, contributing to a full feedback loop that fosters continuous improvement, transparency, and shared success. Strong executive presence and ability to build and sustain client / Alliance relationships at the highest levels of the firm and with C-suite clients. Expert versatility and adaptability skills. Strong collaboration skills. Appreciation of cross cultures and the importance of diversity. Strong people, change, and stakeholder management experience. Strong interpersonal communication and influencing skills with confidence and maturity to build relationships and work with various leadership teams. Effective business leadership, client leadership, team leadership and personal leadership skills, with a strong personal brand. Ability to coordinate multiple projects and initiatives simultaneously through effective prioritization, organization, flexibility and time management. Ability to work effectively under pressure. Excellent written, oral, presentation and facilitation skills. Highly collaborative approach to work in a connected way – removing barriers to enable high-impact results. A global mindset and ability to work effectively across cultures. Preferred Qualifications Relevant exposure to emerging technologies and ecosystems. Experience working with AI, cloud, or other emerging technologies. Familiarity with innovation frameworks or startup ecosystems.

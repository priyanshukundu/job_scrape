You will own the building blocks agent orchestration, tool/function calling, retrieval and grounding, voice interaction, document extraction, and agentic memory and drive measurable, iterative improvement ("hill-climbing") of capabilities such as NL2SQL and RAG. Job responsibilities Lead end-to-end delivery of the AI Agent Platform SDK and frameworks—from problem framing and technical design through production deployment, scaling, and monitoring—that engineering teams use to build, evaluate, and operate AI agents.

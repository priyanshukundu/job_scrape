ABOUT THE ROLE: The Software Development Engineer in Test (SDET) will be responsible for advancing software quality across the Post Production Automation platform through a combination of manual validation, test automation, performance testing, and quality engineering practices. The role will partner closely with Development, DevOps, Architecture, and operational teams to improve test coverage, shorten feedback loops, and establish repeatable quality practices throughout the software delivery lifecycle. RESPONSIBILITIES: Design, develop, and maintain automated UI, API, integration, performance, and regression tests for the Post Production Automation platform. Develop and maintain reusable test frameworks, fixtures, utilities, test data, and automation standards to support scalable testing. Create and maintain test cases for critical user workflows, including functional, negative, regression, integration, and edge-case scenarios. Partner with Development and DevOps to identify high-value manual validation scenarios and convert appropriate scenarios into repeatable automated tests. Partner with Development early in the delivery lifecycle to review requirements and acceptance criteria for testability, completeness, business risk, adjacent workflow impact, and potential defects. Perform exploratory and manual testing when automation is not appropriate or additional investigation is required. Troubleshoot test failures and application issues to distinguish between software defects, automation defects, test data issues, and environment-related failures. Support root cause analysis for significant defects and production issues and develop regression coverage to help prevent recurrence. Integrate automated testing into CI/CD pipelines and establish practical quality gates for software delivery. Develop and maintain test reporting, dashboards, and quality metrics that provide visibility into test results, coverage, defects, and application quality. Ability to provide off-hours operational support, including nightly deployments and incident response. Collaborate with DevOps on release readiness, deployment validation, and production issues requiring additional test coverage or defect reproduction. Continuously identify opportunities to improve test reliability, execution time, automation coverage, and overall quality engineering practices. REQUIREMENTS: 3+ years of experience in hybrid QA, SDET, Software Quality Engineering, or similar roles combining manual testing and test automation. Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field, or equivalent professional experience. Experience with test management tools such as QMetry, TestRail, or similar platforms. Strong experience designing and maintaining automated UI, API, integration, regression, and performance test suites. Experience with modern test automation frameworks such as Playwright, Cypress, Selenium, or similar technologies. Experience with REST API and performance testing, including analysis of application behavior under load. Experience creating and maintaining reusable test cases, test data, fixtures, and regression coverage for business-critical workflows. Strong systems-level troubleshooting and debugging skills, with the ability to trace failures across UI, API, application services, test automation, databases, and infrastructure. Demonstrated critical-thinking skills and the ability to analyze complex problems, identify risk, and recommend practical solutions. Experience performing root cause analysis and converting production defects into preventative regression coverage. Experience with source control management, preferably Git. Familiarity with CI/CD pipelines and software build and deployment processes. Ability to read and understand application code, logs, API responses, and system behavior to support defect investigation. Strong cross-team collaboration skills with experience working across Development, DevOps, Architecture, Product, and operational teams. Experience working in Agile, Scrum, or similar iterative software development environments. PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS: Experience with Production Asset Management and Media Asset Management systems. Experience mentoring developers on unit testing, testability, and quality-engineering practices. Experience testing applications deployed across hybrid cloud and on-premises environments. Experience with Docker, Kubernetes, Terraform, Ansible, or similar infrastructure and deployment technologies. Experience testing complex integrations, asynchronous workflows, file movement, databases, messaging, and distributed application components. Familiarity with observability tools, logging, monitoring, dashboards, and test reporting platforms. Ability to work effectively across geographically distributed teams. Ability to adapt to changing requirements, priorities, release schedules, and production needs.

The application window is expected to close on: 11/29/2026 Meet the Team The Observability Cloud AI team builds the intelligent AI SRE experience for Splunk Observability Cloud, including an AI Assistant and Troubleshooting Agent that investigate production problems across metrics, traces, logs, and infrastructure. We operate within one of the industry's largest observability data estates, enabling us to deliver autonomous detection, incident grouping, and code-level remediation. The team is composed of dedicated engineers focused on applied AI who utilize a "customer-zero" methodology, deploying to our own internal SRE teams before any public release. This collaborative and fast-paced environment prioritizes rigorous evaluation over prototypes, ensuring that every contribution directly improves the reliability of our customers' complex digital systems. Your Impact As the Principal Engineer, you will define the technical trajectory for agentic investigation, ensuring our AI solutions achieve production-grade trust and efficiency. You will architect the critical evaluation systems and retrieval layers that allow models to reason effectively over vast telemetry datasets, directly improving accuracy and time-to-answer for incident resolution. You will establish the production standards for our AI agent's latency, cost, and security, providing the necessary guardrails for safe, autonomous remediation. By mentoring team members and partnering with chief architects, you will raise the applied-AI capabilities across the observability portfolio and set the technical bar for the entire organization. You will successfully drive long-term technical direction for these systems, effectively bridging the gap between innovative AI research and mission-critical product features. Minimum Qualifications 12+ years of professional software engineering experience. 3+ years of experience designing, building, and deploying production-grade LLM-based systems, including at least one agentic system with active tool use. Experience developing evaluation frameworks for AI/ML systems, including dataset creation, offline/online scoring, and release validation. Experience engineering retrieval and context pipelines for structured and semi-structured data, such as telemetry, logs, or time-series databases. Knowledge of production-grade engineering principles including latency optimization, cost management, multi-tenancy, and security guardrails for AI. Preferred Qualifications Experience with Observability, SRE, or incident management domains, including distributed tracing and alerting workflows. Experience working with cloud-native infrastructure at scale, specifically Kubernetes. History of active open-source contributions in AI, agent frameworks, or observability ecosystems (e.g., OpenTelemetry, MCP). Experience leading customer-zero or field-deployed programs to gather actionable feedback for product improvements.

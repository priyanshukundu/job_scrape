SEM Associate Director, Paid Search Activation

The Citi Paid Search Team is seeking a highly strategic and results-driven Paid Search Senior Manager to lead one of the largest and most important customer acquisition channels across Citi's U.S. Credit Cards business. This role is responsible for defining the vision, strategy, and growth roadmap for the paid search program, overseeing a multimillion-dollar investment portfolio, and delivering against ambitious acquisition, efficiency, and franchise growth objectives. The successful candidate will serve as the strategic lead for the U.S. Credit Cards paid search program, partnering across Marketing, Product, Analytics, Finance, Risk, Technology, Legal, Compliance, and the in-house activation team to drive business performance and accelerate innovation. This individual will set the strategic vision for the program, owning investment strategy, optimization frameworks, testing and innovation roadmaps, and overall business outcomes. This role requires a strong blend of strategic thinking, analytical rigor, stakeholder management, and operational leadership, along with a proven track record of driving measurable growth at scale within a highly matrixed organization. Key Responsibilities: Define and lead the strategy, vision, and growth roadmap for the U.S. Credit Cards Paid Search program, ensuring alignment with acquisition, customer, and business objectives. Own channel performance, delivering volume, efficiency, and ROI targets across the credit card portfolio. Manage a multimillion-dollar marketing investment budget, optimizing spend allocation to maximize business impact and incremental growth. Identify and unlock new growth opportunities across audiences, keywords, products, platforms, and emerging search capabilities. Develop, maintain, and communicate weekly, monthly, and quarterly forecasts, proactively identifying performance risks and opportunities. Develop and present performance updates, business reviews, and strategic recommendations for Monthly Operating Reviews (MORs), Quarterly Business Reviews (QBRs), senior leadership meetings, and ad hoc stakeholder requests. Provide strategic oversight of campaign execution, partnering with internal activation teams and external partners to ensure alignment with business objectives. Lead the end-to-end legal, compliance, risk, and control approval process for all Paid Search initiatives while maintaining strong governance and operational rigor. Develop and lead a testing and innovation roadmap, leveraging A/B testing, experimentation and measurement to drive continuous performance improvement. Evaluate, launch, and scale emerging search capabilities, including AI-driven solutions such as Google AI Max, automation, bidding technologies, and advanced measurement solutions. Partner with Analytics and Measurement teams to enhance attribution, conversion tracking, incrementality measurement, and performance reporting. Serve as the primary point of contact and paid search subject matter expert for senior stakeholders across Marketing, Product, Analytics, Finance, Risk, Technology, Legal, and Compliance. Qualifications: 8+ years of experience in Paid Search, with deep expertise in paid search strategy and performance management within a large-scale organization. Proven experience managing multimillion-dollar media investments and delivering acquisition growth at scale. Experience leading large-scale testing programs, including A/B testing, incrementality measurement, and optimization initiatives. Experience evaluating and launching AI-driven search solutions, automation strategies, and emerging platform capabilities. Strong understanding of attribution methodologies, conversion tracking, measurement frameworks, and forecasting. Exceptional analytical and strategic thinking skills, with the ability to translate complex data into actionable business recommendations. Proven ability to influence senior leaders and drive results within a highly matrixed organization. Excellent communication, executive presentation, and relationship management skills. Education: Bachelor's degree required. What We Offer Joining Citi's U.S. Consumer Cards Paid Media team means working at the centre of one of the largest card businesses in the United States, where your decisions directly influence how millions of customers discover and engage with Citi's products. This is a senior role with real ownership, cross-functional reach, and the opportunity to shape how paid search evolves at enterprise scale. A hybrid working arrangement with 3 days in the office and 2 days working remotely, giving you flexibility alongside meaningful in-person collaboration. Ownership of a high-impact paid search program that operates at significant scale across multiple products and business lines. Direct involvement in the adoption of AI-driven search capabilities and automation, keeping you at the forefront of how the discipline is evolving. Access to learning and development opportunities that support your continued growth as a senior practitioner and people leader. Collaboration with cross-functional teams spanning Marketing, Technology, Product, Risk, Legal, and Finance, giving you broad exposure and influence across the organization. Competitive financial wellbeing and benefits package, including support for long-term financial planning. Wellbeing support and work-life balance resources designed to help you perform at your best.

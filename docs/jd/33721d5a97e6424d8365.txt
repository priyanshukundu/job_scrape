JPMorgan Chase

Join a team that makes CCB Data Product development clear and measurable, helping leaders understand what is being built, why it matters, and how CCB Data Products are being adopted across the business. As an Analytics Senior Associate, within the CCB Data and Analytics Products Organization, you will help define and implement CCB’s Data Strategy and own the CCB Data Product performance and visibility capability and provide teams and leaders across CCB with a trusted view of progress and outcomes and interpret strategic objectives, propose plans, and execute with a high degree of independence. Job responsibilities: Own the CCB Data Product performance and visibility capability, providing a trusted view of roadmap progress, adoption, and business value and support the definition and implementation of the CCB Data Strategy, translating strategic priorities into outcome-based objectives and key results (OKRs) for the capability you own. Track the CCB Data Product roadmap and explain what is being developed, why it matters, and how it supports CCB priorities and conduct ongoing discovery with stakeholders and CCB Data Product consumers to understand their needs, identify adoption barriers, and surface opportunities for improvement. Define and report on CCB Data Product adoption measures, such as consuming teams, use cases onboarded, and usage volume, and connect those measures to business outcomes where possible. Improve reporting on the CCB Data Product development process and automate recurring reporting to increase efficiency, accuracy, and timeliness. Query and analyze data using SQL to support CCB Data Product roadmap, adoption, and business-value reporting. Partner with multiple lines of business and cross-functional teams to align priorities, resolve day-to-day issues, and escalate tradeoffs when needed and communicate CCB Data Product progress, decisions, and outcomes to senior leaders and strategy partners through executive-level presentations and transparent decision logs. Required qualifications, capabilities, and skills: Bachelor’s degree in a quantitative, scientific, technical, or business field (for example, Mathematics, Statistics, Computer Science, Engineering, Economics, Finance, or Business Administration), or equivalent practical experience; 3–5 years of relevant experience in data management, data product reporting, analytics, or a related field. Solid SQL (Structured Query Language) skills, with the ability to query and analyze data independently using platforms such as Snowflake, Teradata, AWS Athena, or similar. Comfort leveraging AI-powered tools, such as generative AI and large language model assistants, to accelerate reporting, analysis, and presentation development. Strong data management skills, including a working knowledge of relevant data structures. Experience defining and tracking usage metrics for data assets or platforms and using those insights to inform decisions and working with governed data and partnering with engineering, product, and business teams in a regulated environment. Excellent written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to explain technical data product work to varied audiences and strong presentation skills. including experience developing and delivering executive-level presentations using tools such as Tableau or PowerPoint. Experience using Jira and Confluence to track roadmaps, document processes, and manage reporting workflows. Preferred qualifications, capabilities, and skills: Exposure to product management or roadmap ownership, including setting OKRs or conducting stakeholder discovery. Previous experience in the financial services industry. Knowledge of CCB or consumer banking data and data products. Experience automating reporting processes using tools such as Python, Alteryx, Tableau, or SQL-based workflows.

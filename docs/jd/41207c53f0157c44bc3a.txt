Senior Staff Embedded Software Engineer (ONSITE)
Stryker
Location: Portage, Michigan; Grand Rapids, Michigan
Link: https://stryker.wd1.myworkdayjobs.com/StrykerCareers/job/Portage-Michigan/Senior-Staff-Embedded-Software-Engineer--ONSITE-_R576090

Work Flexibility: Onsite Stryker Acute Care is seeking a Senior Staff Embedded Software Engineer to lead the architecture and development of embedded software for medical devices. In this role, you will be responsible for technical direction and delivering high-quality, reliable, and compliant software solutions while adhering to internal software development lifecycle processes and FDA software development guidance. This role requires strong expertise in embedded systems, excellent problem-solving skills, and the ability to provide technical direction, drive software engineering best practices, mentor engineers while delivering solutions that enhance product quality, reliability, and operational efficiency. What You Will Do Lead the architecture and development of embedded software for medical devices in accordance with internal software development lifecycle processes and FDA software development guidance. Collaborate with cross-functional teams, including R&D, Systems Engineering, Quality, Manufacturing, Regulatory Affairs, and Advanced Operations, to ensure effective communication and successful product development. Optimize system performance, memory footprint, boot time, and power consumption. Drive requirements traceability, risk management, verification, and validation activities. Lead root-cause investigations, debugging, and defect resolution for complex issues. Contribute to and execute customer facing activities like voice of customer and customer centric design in partnership with internal and external stakeholders. Independently make design tradeoff decisions while designing multiple components of the system. Drive design reviews, code reviews, and engineering best practices across teams. Provide technical guidance and mentorship to software engineers. Minimum Qualifications (Required) Bachelor's degree in Software Engineering, Computer Science or related discipline 6+ years of professional software development experience Experience in C/C++ software development for embedded systems. Experience with embedded software architecture, design patterns, and system-level debugging. Preferred Qualifications (Strongly Desired) Experience developing software for real-time embedded systems using C/C++ Experience driving requirements traceability, risk management, and software lifecycle management processes. Experience designing software for microcontrollers and embedded processors in resource-constrained environments. Deep knowledge of embedded communication protocols, including CAN, SPI, I2C, UART, USB, Ethernet, and similar. Ability to lead the adoption and effective use of static analysis, dynamic analysis, memory management, code coverage, and other software quality and reliability practices. Experience creating and reviewing effort estimates, including implementation timelines, resource requirements, and technical risks for projects and product initiatives. Familiarity with regulatory and compliance standards applied to the SDLC (Software Development Life Cycle). Experience using source control systems such as GitHub, Perforce or similar, including branching, merging, code reviews, and release management workflows. Experience with build systems and automation tools such as Jenkins, CMake, or equivalent CI/CD frameworks.

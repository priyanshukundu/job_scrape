Job Area: Engineering Group, Engineering Group > Software Engineering General Summary: The Qualcomm Computer Vision SW team is actively engaged in offering optimized visual analytics solutions in SW and HW for enabling use-cases spanning various business units like Mobile, XR, IOT, Compute and Auto. We are seeking ambitious, bright and innovative software engineers and to-be leaders to be part of this effort. Job activities span the whole product life cycle from early R&D prototyping to commercial deployment. The environment is fast-paced and requires cross-functional interactions on a daily basis, so good communication, planning and execution skills are a must. Machine learning, Image processing and computer vision knowledge or API/SDK development experience Operating system knowledge and computer architecture related project experience Solid hands-on software design and development experience on complex embedded computing platforms. Strong programming experience in C/C++/Python/STL, as well as hands-on experience debugging complex embedded software and with version-based tools. Ability to contribute to all phases of software development - bring-up, design, deployment, and customer support. Demonstrated ability to work with engineers, partners and customers, and across the different geographical sites and technology domains (Software/firmware/hardware). Excellent verbal and written communication skills. Experience in embedded system software development including kernels, device drivers and board-support packages is a plus. Previous projects’ github link if available Minimum Qualifications: • Bachelor's degree in Engineering, Information Systems, Computer Science, or related field and 2+ years of Software Engineering or related work experience. OR Master's degree in Engineering, Information Systems, Computer Science, or related field and 1+ year of Software Engineering or related work experience. OR PhD in Engineering, Information Systems, Computer Science, or related field. • 2+ years of academic or work experience with Programming Language such as C, C++, Java, Python, etc. Responsibilities: Design and implementation of vision and image processing API/pipelines on Qualcomm SoCs and platforms, closely collaborate with cross-functional teams (SW/FW/HW) and domain/technology experts to understand and define specifications, and serve as feature owner for software design/implementation/commercialization, and integrate/validate software in a larger system. Actively engage with internal clients (Camera/Video/XR technology areas), OEMs, Tier-1s and ISVs to implement solutions based on of Qualcomm Computer Vision offerings.

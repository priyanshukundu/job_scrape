Company Federal Reserve Bank of Minneapolis The Federal Reserve Bank of Minneapolis (Bank) is seeking a Business Analyst to become the Training Coordinator for the division of Supervision, Regulation, and Credit (SRC). In SRC, we accomplish our mission through supervising the financial health and stability of banking organizations, upholding statutes designed to protect consumers, providing credit to the banking system, and managing national programs which promote the stability of the U.S. banking system. To achieve this work, we place great importance on the quality of our staff’s training, certification, and continuous learning as they adapt to the ever-evolving financial industry landscape. The SRC Training Coordinator serves as the liaison between the Board of Governors Supervision Learning function, and our 9th District SRC department. This role is responsible for gathering training requirements passed down by the Board and leadership channels, and ensuring that our department policies, procedures, and outcomes uphold those rigorous standards. This role also coordinates with local teams, state regulatory counterparts, and Federal Reserve System peers to advance strategic priorities connected to training, professional development, and operational excellence. Application Deadline: Sunday January 3rd, 2027 at Midnight This position requires a regular on-site presence. Essential Functions: Communicate and track all mandatory learning issued during onboarding, the Examiner Commissioning Programs (ECP), or as Continuing Professional Development (CPD). Manage ECP enrollment, onboarding, progress tracking, and general administration for all virtual, classroom, and on-the-job training milestones in each of the 3 ECP commissioning programs (a journey which lasts 3-5 years per learner, on average). Coordinate across learners, trainers, and schedulers to ensure prerequisites are met, outcomes are documented, feedback is provided, and we advance our learners’ progress with speed and quality. Proctor ECP tests, processing commission requests, and maintain documentation for active vs. dormant status. Host and administer in-person courses held in Minneapolis (for applicants whose home-base is the Helena Federal Reserve Branch, travel would be required). Coordinate the annual Workforce Analysis for our district. State Banking Agency Support: Communicate learning resources to examiners within our district’s 6 states, and coordinate registrations. Maintain alignment between the SRC business lines and Board’s Supervision Learning function: 2-way communication, supporting Board initiatives, subcommittees, centralized tool adoption, program documentation, and responding to oversight activities (audits). May assist the division with general administrative support tasks or projects, to improve division operations or further key initiatives. Perform other duties or responsibilities as needed or assigned. Qualifications: Business Analyst II: Bachelor's degree and 3+ years of direct work-related experience or equivalent combination of related higher education and experience. Business Analyst III: Bachelor's degree and 5+ years of direct work-related experience or equivalent combination of related higher education and experience. Strong service mindset, with an emphasis on curiosity, proactive communication, professionalism, and creative solutioning. Detail oriented, organized, with the ability to manage multiple tasks / projects concurrently. Strong communication and interpersonal skills – ability to build trust via 1:1 conversation with learners, ability to manage projects in small group cohorts, and ability to engage broadly within the division to drive our culture of learning and professional development. Diligent in tracking, documentation, and analysis of business processes. Individual accountability and sense of ownership over the work assigned. Ability to travel up to 5% of the time Bachelor's degree in business or a related field, preferred. Experience in a business operations or financial environment preferred.

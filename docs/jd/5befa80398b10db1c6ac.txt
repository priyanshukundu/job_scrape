Job Description: NLP Data Science Analyst Do you want to help shape the future of the Fidelity Assistant, Fidelity's AI-powered customer experience platform? Are you passionate about defining intelligent, personalized experiences that transform how customers invest, learn, and engage with Fidelity? As the competitive landscape in financial services rapidly evolves, there is a growing need for strategic problem solvers who can turn customer insights into impactful digital experiences. Join a world-class brokerage team focused on advancing the Fidelity Assistant and related AI-enabled experiences - influencing product strategy, improving customer outcomes, and helping deliver the next generation of brokerage services. The Role As a NLP Data Science Analyst, you will leverage your expertise in brokerage products, customer experiences, and customer insights to help shape and improve the Fidelity Assistant. You will support the delivery of new capabilities and improved experiences by: Partnering with Product, User Experience, Technology, Operations, and Analytics teams. Translating business needs and insights into clear requirements, user stories, acceptance criteria, and prioritized backlogs that enable efficient delivery. Using data, customer feedback, and business trends to identify opportunities, prioritize enhancements, and support data-driven decision-making. Conducting testing and validation activities to ensure solutions meet requirements and deliver the intended customer and business outcomes. Supporting product launches through readiness activities, content reviews, communications, and educational materials that promote successful adoption. Monitoring product performance, measure outcomes, and provide ongoing production support, using insights and learnings to drive continuous improvement. Leverage and refine AI skills and tools to streamline and perform daily tasks. The Expertise and Skills You Bring Bachelor's degree or higher in Computer Science, Mathematics, Computational Statistics, Computational Linguistics, Machine Learning or related technical fields 1-3 plus years of commercial or academic experience executing/leading work for digital or AI powered products. Proficiency with Natural Language Processing (NLP) and Data Science Python Modules: Pandas, Spacy, nltk, etc. Experience developing, tuning, and evaluating various types of machine learning algorithms, e.g. supervised, unsupervised, deep learning, neural networks Proficiency in the entire Predictive Modeling life cycle – From exploratory data analysis to data cleaning to Feature Engineering to Model Building and Validation, Operationalization and Scalability of scientific techniques and establishing decision strategies Strong proficiency with data analytics and related toolsets – e.g. SQL, AWS-Athena, Snowflake Exposure to or experience with AWS Cloud ML or similar Exposure to or experience working with dialog platforms like DialogFlow, Amazon Lex, etc. Ability and willingness to work with third-party ASR and NLU platforms, with an eye toward innovation and augmentation.

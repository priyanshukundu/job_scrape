To learn more about CIBC, please visit CIBC.com What you'll be doing As a Private Banking Underwriting Manager, you'll provide leadership, coaching, and direction to a team of Private Banking Underwriters primarily centralized in Chicago. You'll manage the underwriting workflow by reviewing and triaging credit requests and partnering with Group Portfolio Managers to prioritize work in the underwriting queue. You'll also complete analysis and prepare loan presentations as needed, ensuring credit requests are handled with urgency and care. In this role, you'll collaborate closely with Underwriters, Private Bankers, Market Managers, Regional Managers, and Group Portfolio Managers to deliver a strong client experience. You'll help ensure the team balances quality, responsiveness, sound risk management, and cost awareness across all underwriting activities. How you'll succeed Workflow management - Review and triage incoming credit requests to ensure submissions include the information needed for timely processing and maintain a well-organized underwriting queue with timely, accurate task assignment. Team coordination - Assign work based on team expertise, capacity, and business priorities while coordinating competing demands to meet business line expectations. Priority setting - Partner with Group Portfolio Managers and the Private Banking Chief Operating Officer to establish and manage weekly priorities that support timely delivery and effective service. Stakeholder communication - Provide timely updates to Private Bankers, Market Leads, and Regional Managers on the status of credit requests to support a seamless client and partner experience. Quality execution - Produce and oversee the delivery of high-quality loan presentations, material amendments, and other credit documentation within established timelines. Risk, reporting, and continuous improvement - Maintain accuracy across underwriting, processing, and compliance logs, ensure required weekly, monthly, and quarterly reporting is completed on time, continue building market and policy knowledge, and support cost-effective underwriting practices. Who you are You have relevant experience. You bring 3-5 years of underwriting experience, ideally within a private banking environment. You understand complex client needs. You have experience supporting high-net-worth and ultra-high-net-worth clients and understand the products and solutions commonly aligned to their financial objectives. You're skilled in credit structuring and analysis. You can assess complex balance sheets, cash flows, and financial information to recommend customized credit solutions within the bank's risk parameters. You communicate and collaborate effectively. You build strong partnerships across teams and communicate clearly with internal stakeholders to move work forward and deliver a high standard of service. You're organized and adaptable. You can manage multiple priorities, respond to changing demands, and maintain attention to detail in a fast-paced environment. You have the technical and regulatory knowledge to succeed. You bring strong knowledge of personal and business financial analysis, familiarity with applicable regulatory requirements, and proficiency in Microsoft Word and Microsoft Excel. Values matter to you. You bring your real self to work, and you live our values - trust, teamwork, and accountability.

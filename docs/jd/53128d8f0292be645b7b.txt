We are looking for a Senior Software Engineer to join our mission to continue improving our HPC infrastructure and workflow systems. Our team builds and operates systems supporting millions of compute cores and thousands of engineers driving new chip development. We are guided by a developer-first ethos focused on reliability, simplicity, and maintainability. You will be working with a team of passionate and skilled engineers that are continuously working to provide better tools to build and manage this infrastructure. The ideal candidate is strong in software development, crafting and building reliable distributed systems, and has the ability to implement well thought out long term maintenance strategy. What you'll be doing: Apply distributed systems patterns to improve the scale, latency, and reliability of our services. Operate in a globally distributed, hybrid multi-cloud environment (AWS, GCP, on-prem), building systems that are cloud-native and location-agnostic. Own your services from building through production: architecture review, implementation, testing, rollout, and monitoring. Integrate tools and schedulers through versioned interfaces that explicitly define inputs, outputs, resource needs, and completion checks. Work directly with chip build, workflow, and runtime engineers to understand their problems and build the right solution. Use AI coding agents such as Claude Code or Codex in your daily work to build, write, test, and review code. Help the team keep evaluating which agent practices are worth adopting. Participate in the team’s on-call rotation and help lead incident response when needed. What we need to see: B.S. in Computer Science or a related field (or equivalent experience), with 5+ years of relevant experience. Strong coding skills in at least two of Go, Python, Java, C/C++, or Rust, with a focus on backend or infrastructure work. A solid grasp of distributed systems fundamentals: consistency, scalability, failure handling, and performance trade-offs. Experience owning a production service end to end, including on-call and follow-up after incidents. Hands-on experience with at least one major cloud provider (AWS, GCP, or Azure) and with Kubernetes or a similar orchestrator. Proven understanding of CI/CD, GitOps, and Infrastructure as Code for safe, repeatable changes. Experience using AI coding tools on real projects, and good judgment about when to trust the output and when to check it. Clear written and verbal communication, and comfort working through technical decisions with other teams. Ways to stand out from the crowd: Experience building or operating HPC or batch scheduling infrastructure (LSF, Slurm) or Kubernetes controllers and operators. Experience building your own agent-based developer tooling or internal AI workflows, beyond using off-the-shelf tools. Familiarity with EDA tools or chip build workflows. Maintainer or co-maintainer of a publicly available project used in production.

Join us and build for travelers everywhere. Introduction to the team Expedia Group Advertising builds the platforms that connect advertisers with millions of travelers around the world. Our mission is to help partners reach the right traveler with the right message at the right time. We’re looking for a Software Development Engineer III who enjoys designing and building reliable backend systems and wants to help shape how we select and deliver ads across Expedia Group. You’ll join our Advertising Technology team and work closely with Product and Machine Learning Science (MLS) to bring data and model outputs into production, measure what works, and make ads more relevant to travelers. In this role, you will Design, build, and operate backend APIs and services that support ad selection and delivery. Lead end-to-end design discussions for features that span multiple backend services. Design for reliability and maintainability while meeting latency requirements. Partner with Product and MLS to define success metrics, integrate signals or model outputs into backend services, and use A/B tests to measure their impact. Improve service reliability and performance through automated testing, monitoring, and lessons from production issues. Share feedback through design and code reviews and mentor teammates. Minimum Qualifications: 5+ years of software development experience, or equivalent practical experience building and operating backend services. Proficiency in at least one general-purpose programming language, such as Java or Kotlin. Strong system design skills: ability to design APIs and data flows, reason about how distributed systems scale and fail, and explain your design choices. Experience deploying and supporting software in production, including testing, monitoring, and troubleshooting. Experience working with machine learning (ML) systems, such as integrating model outputs into backend services or supporting model serving or monitoring. Experience working with both stream and batch data processing(Kafka, Spark). Preferred Qualifications: A background in statistics or core computer science, or experience using data, metrics, or experiments to make decisions.

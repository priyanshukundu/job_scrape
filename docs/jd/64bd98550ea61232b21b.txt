About this role: Wells Fargo is seeking a Lead Software Engineer (Python) within the Secured Network Services organization. This role will focus on designing and delivering automation solutions that enable a software-driven infrastructure environment. The ideal candidate will bring strong Python development, automation, API integration, and cloud-native engineering experience to build scalable, reliable, and secure solutions. In this role, you will: Lead complex technology initiatives including those that are companywide with broad impact Design and develop API-driven integrations between network platforms and internal systems Develop and maintain Python-based automation frameworks to improve reliability, scalability, and operational efficiency Transform manual operational processes into automated, repeatable, and maintainable workflows Design automation solutions aligned with performance, security, reliability, and maintainability standards Partner with network engineering, platform, and technology teams to align automation solutions with broader architecture and infrastructure strategies Lead automation efforts supporting product deployments, service delivery, customer journeys, and API development Use approved AI-assisted development tools, including GitHub Copilot and code-focused AI agents, to support software engineering activities Leverage AI capabilities to accelerate solution design, development, testing, analysis, and troubleshooting Apply sound technical judgment when reviewing, validating, and integrating AI-assisted outputs into production solutions. Lead projects, teams, or serve as a peer mentor Required Qualifications: 5+ years of Software Engineering experience, or equivalent demonstrated through one or a combination of the following: work experience, training, military experience, education 4+ years of Python development experience, including building automation tools, system integrations, APIs, or workflow orchestration solutions Experience designing, developing, testing, and supporting software solutions within an enterprise environment Experience developing and consuming REST APIs, including authentication, schema validation, error handling, and versioning Experience using source control and modern software development practices, preferably GitHub. Desired Qualifications: Experience with network engineering concepts and network infrastructure automation Experience with Nautobot for network source-of-truth, automation, or infrastructure management Experience developing API-driven integrations between network platforms and enterprise systems Experience with FastAPI, Django, GraphQL, PostgreSQL, NoSQL databases, React, and OAuth Experience with Model Context Protocol (MCP) and integrating applications with enterprise AI models or model APIs Experience working with OpenAI API specifications or similar API-based AI integrations Experience developing microservices, RESTful APIs, and event-driven architectures Experience with container and cloud-native platforms such as Red Hat OpenShift, Cloud Foundry, and Kubernetes Working knowledge of Unix/Linux, MongoDB, and Postman Experience working with legacy infrastructure as well as modern cloud and container-based infrastructure Experience working in large-scale enterprise environments with structured change management, security, risk, and release processes, which fits the broader Senior Software Engineer profile expectations Experience using GitHub Copilot or other approved AI-assisted development tools for software design, coding, testing, troubleshooting, and analysis Ability to evaluate AI-generated code or recommendations for accuracy, maintainability, security, and operational risk.

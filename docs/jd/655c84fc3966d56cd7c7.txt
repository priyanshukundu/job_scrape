Drive continuous improvement of investigative processes, workflows, technology, data, and controls. Maintain governance over TRIP procedures, quality assurance, issue management, and corrective actions. Support audits, regulatory examinations, special projects, and enterprise initiatives while escalating significant financial crime risks and trends.

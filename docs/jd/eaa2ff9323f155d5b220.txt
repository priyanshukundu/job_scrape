Associate Scientist
Regeneron
Location: TARRYTOWN
Link: https://regeneron.wd1.myworkdayjobs.com/Careers/job/TARRYTOWN/Associate-Scientist_R51622

Build our future together: At Regeneron, we use science and innovation to develop life-changing medicines for people with serious diseases. We are seeking an Associate Scientist to join our Protein Expression Sciences team, supporting our VelociMab antibody discovery efforts in an on-site capacity. In this role, you will operate automated liquid handlers to perform high-throughput cloning and isolation of potential therapeutic antibodies while collaborating with your Protein Expression Sciences colleagues. This position offers the opportunity to contribute to a fast-growing, science-driven organization making a meaningful difference to patients worldwide. When & where: Location: Tarrytown, NY Work model/flexibility: On-site Discover your role: Operating and troubleshooting automated liquid handling systems to support antibody discovery Performing high-throughput PCR, cloning and DNA isolation Isolating potential therapeutic antibodies for our VelociMab platform Seeding and transfecting CHO transient cell lines on an automated platform Recording experimental data accurately in our electronic systems Adapting to shifting priorities in a dynamic, fast-paced lab environment This role requires: B.S. with 2+ years of laboratory experience, or M.S., in Molecular Biology, Cellular Biology, Biology, or a related field Experience with general molecular biology skills such as PCR, DNA sequencing, mutagenesis, plasmid subcloning and mammalian cell transfection Strong communication and organizational skills, adept technical and problem-solving skills Comfortable when priorities change from day to day, meticulous and detail-oriented in your work Preferred: Familiarity with automated liquid handlers (preferably Hamilton STAR, Vantage and HighRes PRIME) Preferred: Experience in high-throughput assays Does this sound like you?

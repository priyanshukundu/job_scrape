Job Description Role Summary As Senior Director of FP&A - Client , you will lead financial planning and analysis supporting FIS’s Client Office organization. Partnering with the organization’s leadership and Finance teams, you will translate business priorities into financial plans, provide insight into performance, and guide investment and resource decisions. This role combines hands-on analytical depth with executive-level partnership to strengthen financial discipline, improve operating efficiency, and support profitable growth and better client outcomes. Key Responsibilities Lead planning and forecasting: Own annual budgets, rolling forecasts, and long-range financial plans for the Client organization, aligning spending and resources with business priorities. Deliver actionable performance insights: Analyze results against plans, identify key drivers and risks, and provide clear recommendations through monthly and quarterly business reviews. Support functional economics: Partner with business and Finance leaders to assess cost to serve, profitability, and the financial implications of service and relationship investments. Guide investment and resource decisions: Develop business cases and scenario analyses for headcount, technology, service capabilities, and strategic initiatives; track results against expected benefits. Improve financial processes and reporting: Strengthen planning, reporting, and KPI frameworks, using automation and analytics to improve accuracy, efficiency, and visibility into performance. Provide financial leadership: Serve as a trusted advisor to senior leaders, coordinate across Finance and business teams, and lead and develop FP&A talent. Provide Strategic Business / Functional support: Partner with business / functional leaders to drive strategic long-term priorities and financial outlook Support Future Forward priorities: Support long range plan Agentic AI and other key business priorities, which will enable efficiencies and enhance client service Required Qualifications Significant progressive experience in FP&A or strategic finance, including financial leadership supporting a large business or enterprise function. Demonstrated expertise in budgeting, forecasting, financial modeling, variance analysis, and management reporting. Ability to connect operating metrics with financial outcomes and evaluate investment returns, workforce costs, and productivity. Experience partnering with senior executives, constructively challenging assumptions, and translating analysis into clear recommendations. Proven ability to lead teams and manage competing priorities across a complex, matrixed organization. Bachelor’s degree in Finance, Accounting, Economics, or equivalent practical experience, with proficiency in enterprise planning systems and analytical tools. Preferred Qualifications Experience supporting client service, account management, client success, or other client-facing organizations. Background in fintech, payments, financial services technology, or another enterprise technology business. Experience analyzing client profitability, cost to serve, retention, or service delivery economics. Experience improving planning processes, developing operational dashboards, or applying automation and AI-enabled analytics. MBA, CPA, CFA, or comparable advanced qualification.

Your role in the team We are seeking an outstanding Senior Data Scientist to lead the development and application of machine learning and predictive modelling solutions that generate achievable marketing insights and drive business decisions. This includes developing and managing predictive models, integrating new data (both internal and external), and identifying innovative solutions to improve marketing spend, customer acquisition and overall effectiveness. As part of the Marketing Analytics & Data Science team, the role will influence the long-term evolution of our innovative marketing capabilities, manage projects of medium complexity, and provide mentoring and development support to emerging data scientists. Key Responsibilities: Support the analytical needs of the Allstate marketing team through deep dive analytics, predictive modeling, ML engineering to enhance customer acquisition sophistication and marketing effectiveness. Analyse complex datasets to identify patterns, generate actionable insight and inform data driven business decisions. Design, develop and implement complex statistical and machine learning models, applying appropriate techniques to address business needs and improve modelling accuracy and outcomes. Collaborate with marketing data engineers, stakeholders and the wider team to identify business problems, determined optimal modelling approaches and integrate solutions into business processes. Evaluate and communicate modelling techniques, results and recommendations to senior management and stakeholders, influencing decision-making and enduring alignment with business objectives Identify new data sources, tools, technologies and techniques that improve analytical capabilities, drive efficiencies and address emerging business challenges. Manage moderately complex machine learning and predictive modelling projects, applying effective planning and delivery techniques to meet agreed deadlines. Mentor and support the development of other team members. Essential Criteria: All applicants must demonstrate they have a legal right to work in the UK for employment at Allstate.

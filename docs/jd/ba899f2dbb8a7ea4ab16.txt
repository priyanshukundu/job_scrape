Please note that this role is only available in Chicago, IL Introduction to the Team Expedia Group Advertising helps Expedia Group and its partners connect with travelers and understand the impact of advertising across Expedia Group brands and partner channels. Within Emerging Business Technology, the Measurement, Attribution & Data Platform organization builds the capabilities that turn advertising interactions and outcomes into reliable measurements, attribution, and insights. As a Software Development Engineer II, you will contribute to the backend services and data platforms that support this work—from ingesting and processing advertising and conversion data to powering measurement and reporting experiences. The systems you build help advertisers and internal teams understand campaign performance and make informed decisions. You’ll work with engineers, product partners, and analytics stakeholders on distributed systems where data quality, timeliness, scale, and operational reliability matter. We’re looking for an engineer who takes ownership, delivers thoughtfully, and cares about building maintainable systems that work reliably in production. You’ll have opportunities to contribute across the development lifecycle, improve existing platforms, and help evolve the way Expedia Group measures advertising outcomes. In This Role, You Will Analyze large, complex datasets to solve business and product challenges. Develop and apply statistical, machine learning, and experimentation methodologies to generate actionable insights. Partner with stakeholders across Product, Engineering, and the business to define problems and drive data-informed decisions. Build scalable analytical solutions, dashboards, and measurement frameworks. Communicate findings and recommendations clearly to technical and non-technical audiences. Mentor junior team members and contribute to analytics best practices. Minimum Qualifications Bachelor's degree in Data Science, Statistics, Mathematics, Computer Science, Economics, Engineering, or a related quantitative field. 4+ years of experience in data science, analytics, or a related field applying statistical analysis, experimentation, machine learning, or predictive modeling to business problems. Advanced proficiency in SQL and Python, R, or similar programming languages. Experience designing and executing experiments, statistical analyses, and predictive models, and translating findings into business recommendations. Ability to independently manage analytical projects from problem definition through implementation and stakeholder adoption. Strong communication skills with experience presenting insights to both technical and non-technical audiences. Preferred Qualifications Master's degree or PhD in a quantitative field. Experience developing, deploying, or monitoring machine learning models in production environments. Expertise in experimentation, causal inference, A/B testing, and advanced statistical methods. Demonstrated ability to influence business strategy and decision-making through data-driven insights. Experience mentoring junior team members and partnering with senior stakeholders. Familiarity with AI/ML applications, generative AI, and large-scale data environments. Please note that this role is only available in Chicago, IL .

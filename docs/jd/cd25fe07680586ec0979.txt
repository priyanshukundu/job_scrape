Position Summary The Associate Principal, Go-To-Market (GTM) Engineering is the senior architect for the systems that turn product usage and marketing funnel data into business decisions. Illumina BioInsight depends on trustworthy product telemetry, data pipelines, agent frameworks, and related GTM engineering surfaces. You upgrade that capability through architecture judgment, solution design, and delivery leadership, so product and commercial leaders can see reality and act on it. This is a senior GTM engineering role for the entire Illumina SW business. You set the technical and product-adjacent bar for how BioInsight measures adoption, usage, signup-to-paid conversion, customer journey, and portfolio health. You make the systems and models durable to scale with BioInsight revenue. Responsibilities Scope Own the technical and solution direction for BioInsight GTM engineering and product Business Intelligence systems Set architecture and data-model standards so product telemetry, commercial metrics, and executive views share one coherent ground truth Lead solution design across product, platform, data engineering, and commercial systems (for example usage events, signup funnels, billing-adjacent metrics, portfolio dashboards) Operate as a senior IC force-multiplier: unblock hard integrations, raise modeling quality, and create reusable patterns rather than one-off reports Partner with Software Product Management, Marketing, Sales Ops and Deal Desk, Finance, and platform engineering so metrics match how the business actually sells and runs software Own metric definitions and data governance for the GTM domain, including a published metric catalog, named owners, and change control for definitions that executives rely on Define outcome metrics for the GTM engineering system itself: trust in numbers, time-to-insight, coverage of critical product funnels, and reduction of manual reconciliation Delivery Establish a multi-quarter roadmap for GTM engineering and product BI capabilities with clear problem statements and business outcomes Design and land durable data models, pipelines, and analytical products that product leaders use to prioritize and that leadership uses to steer Drive technical decisions on event instrumentation, mart design, semantic consistency across products, and interfaces to commercial systems of record Lead discovery with product managers and commercial stakeholders; translate ambiguous "we need a dashboard" asks into governed metrics and scalable solutions Raise the quality bar on SQL and dbt, warehouse modeling, dashboard semantics, and documentation so insights survive personnel and tool changes Partner with data platform and application engineering teams to instrument products correctly at the source, not only to clean data downstream Facilitate experimentation and causal methods where decisions depend on them, such as pricing, packaging, and onboarding changes, and set the standards other analysts follow Support critical GTM moments (launches, pricing changes, entitlement model changes, executive leadership team reviews) with decision-grade analytics on deadline Mentor analysts and contractors and set working agreements without needing to own a large formal org Create investment cases and sequencing plans so GTM engineering capacity funds the highest-importance problems first Listed responsibilities are an essential, but not exhaustive, list of the usual duties associated with the position. ability to defend metric definitions and tradeoffs with VP-level stakeholders Proven ability to set technical direction for an analytics or GTM engineering domain and land it through influence Comfort owning architecture and solution design end-to-end, from event design through mart to decision surface Ability to create clarity when product, finance, and sales definitions of "the number" disagree Analytical rigor: cohort analysis, funnel instrumentation, data quality SLAs, and prioritization frameworks Working knowledge of experimentation design and causal inference, and judgment about when each is warranted Experience with data governance: metric catalogs, lineage, ownership, and access controls Bias to durable systems and shared semantics over one-off slideware Experience mentoring analysts and engineers and raising the craft bar as a senior IC Preferred Experience bridging product telemetry and commercial systems (usage, entitlements, billing, CRM) Life sciences, genomics, or health tech domain experience;

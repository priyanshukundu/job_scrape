THE OPPORTUNITY This Principal AI/ML Engineer position can work out of Alameda, CA location. As Principal AI/ML Engineer, you will define and build the AI layer that turns glucose data and model outputs into personalized, conversational insights for users. You will set the architecture, evaluation standards, and guardrail policy for all agentic and LLM-based features, and act as the technical lead for the AI engineering team. This is a hands-on, principal-level individual contributor role and a founding position on the team, with direct influence on its architecture, standards, and future hires. You will work closely with data science, clinical, regulatory, product, and mobile engineering, so a high level of technical judgement and professionalism is required. What You'll Work On Define the technical vision and architecture for agentic and LLM-based features across cloud and device. Design and build agents that combine glucose history, model predictions, meals, activity, and user context to deliver actionable insights. Own agent orchestration, tool use, memory, and retrieval patterns, choosing between hosted and self-managed models based on safety, cost, and latency. Stay current on advances in LLMs and agentic systems and translate them into a practical roadmap. Establish guardrails so agent responses stay within approved product and regulatory claims, including no unapproved dosing or treatment advice. Build evaluation frameworks with clinically grounded test sets, co-owned with data science and clinical teams, covering accuracy, safety, and user impact. Ensure every agent response is traceable and auditable in line with design controls and regulatory requirements. Partner with regulatory and quality teams on risk analysis, documentation, and evidence for AI-enabled features. Use sound judgement and discretion when handling confidential and personal health data. Provide technical leadership and mentorship across the AI engineering team and lead architecture and design reviews. Productionize time-series and personalization models for glucose insights, from prototype to scalable, monitored services. Build batch and real-time inference services with defined latency, cost, and availability targets. Required Qualifications: Ph.D. or master’s degree in computer science, Machine Learning or a related field. Minimum of 10+ years of software or ML engineering experience, including 3+ years building LLM-based or agentic systems in production. Deep experience with agent frameworks, tool calling, retrieval-augmented generation, and prompt and model evaluation. Proven track record designing guardrails, safety controls, and evaluation pipelines for user-facing AI. Strong proficiency in Python and cloud platforms (Azure, AWS, or GCP). Demonstrated technical leadership, setting architecture and standards across teams without direct authority. Ability to operate with little instruction and minimal supervision. Excellent written and verbal communication skills, able to explain AI risk and trade-offs to clinical, regulatory, and executive audiences. Preferred Qualifications Experience in a regulated industry, ideally medical devices, digital health, or SaMD. Experience with physiological or wearable sensor data, such as continuous glucose monitoring. Experience with on-device or small language models. Publications, patents, or open-source contributions in applied AI. Experience with personalization, recommendation, or anomaly detection systems.

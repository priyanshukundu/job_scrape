At Cadence, we hire and develop leaders and innovators who want to make an impact on the world of technology. We are seeking a Simulation Engineer to develop simulation technology for physical AI and robotics applications. In this role, you will develop simulation algorithms spanning many-body dynamics and finite element analysis, with a particular emphasis on contact algorithms, and validate their performance on robotics hardware. Working at the intersection of simulation and real-world deployment, you will connect numerical methods with practical robotics applications. Key Responsibilities Develop simulation algorithms for physical AI and robotics applications, including many-body dynamics and finite element analysis. Develop and refine contact algorithms to model physical interactions in robotics scenarios. Validate simulation algorithms on robotics hardware and use experimental results to guide improvements. Evaluate simulation behavior against real-world deployment scenarios to identify and address differences between simulated and hardware results. Apply control theory and controller optimization and tuning methods to support simulation and hardware validation, as applicable. Required Qualifications Degree in mechanical engineering, computer science, or a related discipline. Preferred Qualifications PhD in mechanical engineering, computer science, or a related discipline. Expertise spanning both numerical methods or simulation and practical robotics deployment scenarios. Experience with many-body dynamics, finite element analysis, and contact algorithms.

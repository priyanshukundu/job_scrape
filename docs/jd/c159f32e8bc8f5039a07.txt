Excited to grow your career? BBVA is a global company with more than 160 years of history that operates in more than 25 countries where we serve more than 80 million customers. We are more than 121,000 professionals working in multidisciplinary teams with profiles as diverse as financiers, legal experts, data scientists, developers, engineers and designers. About the job: Department Description Global Markets provides risk management, financing and investment solutions to Corporate and Institutional clients globally across Foreign Exchange, Rates, Credit, Equities and Investment Solutions. Through the collaboration of Sales, Trading, Structuring and Coverage teams, Global Markets supports clients in managing financial risks, accessing liquidity and executing investment strategies. As part of BBVA's strategic growth plan, the Institutional business has been identified as a key priority, with a strong focus on expanding distribution capabilities, increasing client penetration and strengthening the bank's position across priority markets and client segments . The eFX team forms part of this strategy, connecting Global Markets' FX capabilities with the evolving execution needs of Institutional clients. Working in a highly collaborative environment across Sales, Trading, Product, Technology and Client Management, the team contributes to the continued development of BBVA's electronic FX franchise, leveraging the bank's strong presence in Latin America and its global distribution network. Role Overview BBVA is seeking an eFX Sales professional at Senior Associate level to support the growth of its electronic FX franchise across Institutional clients in the Americas. The role combines client coverage, electronic execution expertise and commercial development, with a focus on increasing eFX adoption, expanding client activity and improving execution outcomes. Based in New York, the successful candidate will work closely with regional Sales and Trading teams, as well as the global eFX organization, contributing to the expansion of BBVA's electronic distribution capabilities and Institutional client franchise. Key Responsibilities Act as a client-facing electronic FX execution specialist for Institutional clients, serving as a key point of contact for platform usage, trade-related queries, execution analysis and market best practices. Drive the growth and adoption of electronic FX solutions across Institutional clients, increasing trading volumes, active clients, wallet share and revenue generation. Embed BBVA's Radical Client Perspective in client interactions by understanding execution needs, anticipating opportunities and delivering consistent, high-quality solutions that create long-term value. Partner closely with FX Sales and Relationship Managers to identify and develop commercial opportunities, supporting client engagement, prospecting and revenue generation. Collaborate with FX Trading and eTrading teams to monitor execution quality, pricing competitiveness and client trading behavior, contributing to targeted actions that improve client outcomes and business performance. Leverage execution data and commercial metrics, including volumes, spreads, hit ratios, markouts and client activity trends, to proactively identify opportunities and support commercial initiatives. Develop and maintain client-level analysis and reporting, providing actionable insights to Sales, Trading and other internal stakeholders. Support the rollout and commercialization of new electronic trading platforms, connectivity solutions, features and functionalities, ensuring effective client adoption. Contribute to the evolution of BBVA's electronic FX offering by providing client-driven feedback to Product, Technology and Quant teams to enhance pricing, execution workflows and platform capabilities. Coordinate with onboarding, operations and support teams to ensure efficient client activation and integration across internal and external electronic trading platforms. Engage with external electronic trading venues and platform providers to address client-related matters, identify new commercial opportunities and strengthen BBVA's electronic distribution footprint. Collaborate with regional and global teams to support the distribution of BBVA's FX capabilities, particularly in Latin American and Emerging Markets currencies, to Institutional clients across the Americas. Monitor key business indicators, including electronic revenues, trading volumes, active clients and activation rates, contributing to commercial action plans and business performance reviews. Ensure adherence to internal policies, applicable regulatory requirements and risk frameworks, escalating issues when appropriate. Qualifications & Experience Bachelor's degree in Business Administration, Finance, Economics, Engineering or a related field. 5+ years of experience in FX markets , with relevant exposure to electronic FX execution, eFX Sales, FX Trading, FX Product or related Global Markets roles. Demonstrated client-facing experience with Institutional clients, including banks, asset managers, hedge funds or other financial institutions, with a strong commercial mindset. Hands-on experience with electronic FX trading platforms, such as FXall, Bloomberg FXGO, 360T, FX Connect, BidFX, FXSpotStream or other relevant electronic trading venues. Good understanding of FX products, particularly Spot, Forwards, FX Swaps and NDFs, as well as electronic execution workflows and market structure. Familiarity with electronic FX pricing, liquidity provision, execution quality and client trading behavior. Strong analytical mindset, with the ability to translate client activity and execution data into actionable commercial insights. Excellent communication and interpersonal skills, with the ability to engage effectively with clients, traders and internal stakeholders. Proactive and commercially driven approach, with the ability to identify opportunities, take ownership of initiatives and contribute to business growth. Ability to work effectively within a global organization, coordinating with colleagues across different regions, products and functions. Languages English: Required. Spanish: Preferred. Portuguese : Valued Preferred Qualifications Experience covering Institutional clients in the US or broader Americas region. Knowledge of Latin American and Emerging Markets FX products, liquidity and market structure. Experience in electronic FX platform implementation, client onboarding, execution optimization or electronic flow migration initiatives. Familiarity with execution analytics, transaction cost analysis (TCA), client segmentation and business intelligence tools. Experience working with FX Trading desks on pricing, spreads, markouts and client profitability. Experience using analytical tools such as Excel, SQL, Python, Tableau or Power BI.

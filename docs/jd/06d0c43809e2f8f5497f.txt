Location: New York, San Francisco, Charlotte At EY, we’re all in to shape your future with confidence. We’ll help you succeed in a globally connected powerhouse of diverse teams and take your career wherever you want it to go. Join EY and help to build a better working world. The Enterprise Architecture team helps clients improve how they work with customers, employees, and partners. We modernize applications, add AI capabilities to new and existing platforms, and improve business efficiency across platforms and channels. Your key responsibilities You’ll work directly with clients to address business challenges through architecture solutions. You will lead standalone projects or multiple client engagements, including service delivery, identification of future sales opportunities, and practice development. You will also lead and motivate teams with diverse skills and backgrounds, monitor progress to ensure high-quality client service, and demonstrate deep technical expertise and professional knowledge. Skills and attributes for success Effectively lead and motivate client engagement teams with diverse skills and backgrounds while fostering an innovative, inclusive work environment. Contribute actively to practice management by counseling and mentoring junior consultants and developing new offerings. Demonstrate hands-on technical capabilities that support enterprise architecture solution development. Research client questions and developments in regulation, including AI governance and model risk, industry practices, and technologies such as generative AI. Build and maintain long-term client relationships, expand professional networks, and cultivate business development opportunities. Deliver high-quality client service by directing daily engagement activities, keeping engagement leadership informed of status, and managing staff performance. To qualify for the role, you must have Bachelor's degree and approximately 8 years of related work experience at a financial services company or consulting firm; or a graduate degree and approximately 7 years of related work experience. Ability to lead teams of technical advisors through all phases of the project lifecycle, including requirements definition, architecture design, migration, and testing, using a defined methodology. Experience helping financial services clients deliver data and AI initiatives by working with data engineers, data scientists, machine learning engineers, analysts, and business stakeholders to build scalable platforms. Hands-on experience architecting cloud data platforms using technologies such as Databricks, Snowflake, Microsoft Fabric, Apache Spark, Apache Iceberg/Delta Lake, and cloud-native services on Azure, AWS or GCP. Hands-on experience architecting generative AI solutions, including LLM integration, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), vector databases, prompt and agent orchestration, and AI guardrails and evaluation. Strong experience with event streaming and real-time processing technologies such as Kafka, Flink, Spark Structured Streaming, and cloud-managed streaming services. Implementation and development experience in event-driven and microservices architectures (for example, domain-driven design, saga/choreography patterns, messaging and event brokers). Experience with at least one programming language, such as Python, Java, Scala, or TypeScript. Experience architecting distributed solutions that handle high data volumes. Hands-on experience with modern data ingestion and integration patterns (CDC, ELT, streaming pipelines, data contracts). Experience with enterprise integration patterns. Functional exposure to risk systems, regulatory systems, or customer 360 platforms. Ability to design API-first solutions and experience with at least one relevant API management tool. Expert understanding of modern technology stacks, decision automation and rules engines, and real-time event processing. Excellent written and verbal communication skills, including technical writing and client presentations using PowerPoint, Excel, Word, and modern diagramming tools. Willingness to travel as needed to meet client needs. Ideally, you’ll also have Strong troubleshooting and performance tuning skills. Understanding of cloud infrastructure and platforms, data mesh and data product design patterns, graph and vector databases, and source control. Experience developing schemas and related data structures. Experience with service design and governance, CI/CD, DevSecOps, infrastructure as code, MLOps/LLMOps and FinOps. Experience implementing security and identity such as OAuth 2.0/OIDC, SAML, SSO, zero trust, and responsible AI and data privacy controls. Familiarity with AI-assisted development tools and agentic workflows. What we look for We’re looking for self-motivated individuals who can work effectively in a demanding, performance-focused environment. Candidates should recognize team accomplishments, support the development of others, and lead people effectively. The role requires sound judgment, practical problem-solving skills, and a focus on client needs.

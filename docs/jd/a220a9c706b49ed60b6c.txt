Job Title: Senior Specialist, Corporate Venture Capital Job Description: Here at Entegris, we use advanced science to enable technologies that transform the world, and we are seeking employees who have the drive to continue that mission. The Role: Entegris is seeking a Senior Analyst, Corporate Venture Capital, to support the identification and evaluation of growth opportunities through strategic investments and partnerships. The role focuses on opportunities aligned with Entegris’ materials science innovation culture, high-tech go-to-market channels, unit-driven business models, sustainability objectives, and PACE values. This individual will be a key contributor within a small, nimble team, supporting venture investment activities, deal evaluation, due diligence, financial analysis, portfolio reviews, and investment recommendations. The role works closely with senior business development leaders, business unit stakeholders, and external partners throughout the investment lifecycle, with increasing responsibility over time. What you'll do: Corporate Ventures Support the Entegris Ventures team in evaluating strategic investment opportunities and preparing management and Investment Committee presentations. Responsibilities may include assessing strategic fit, supporting market and technology diligence, coordinating with internal stakeholders, monitoring portfolio activity, and developing investment recommendations. Deal Sourcing & Market Analysis Support the identification and evaluation of potential investment and partnership opportunities through market research, competitive analysis, and strategic screening. Assist in maintaining relationships with ecosystem partners, industry contacts, and internal stakeholders. Help convert inbound and outbound opportunities into structured evaluation projects. Due Diligence Support Support end-to-end due diligence activities, including data gathering, analysis, and coordination of cross-functional diligence workstreams with internal subject-matter experts. Assist in managing diligence requests, synthesizing findings, and drafting investment memoranda and other materials for internal deal reviews, the Investment Committee, and other decision forums. Portfolio Management Support portfolio reviews and ongoing monitoring of portfolio company performance, financing activity, key milestones, and strategic developments. Assist with portfolio reporting and coordination of strategic engagement between portfolio companies and relevant Entegris stakeholders.

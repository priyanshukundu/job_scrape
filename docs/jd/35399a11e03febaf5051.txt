Work you’ll do As a Senior Consultant on the Capital Projects team, you will be responsible for: Advising clients on data center capital projects across the project lifecycle, including feasibility and site assessments, utility coordination, design, procurement, construction, commissioning, and operational handover. Leading workstreams for data center development, expansion, and upgrade programs, helping clients manage delivery, cost, schedule, risk, and project governance. Assessing project plans, controls, schedules, and risks, including dependencies on utility power and long-lead equipment such as substations, switchgear, UPS, generators, chillers, and cooling systems. Reviewing and analyzing project schedules, construction documents, and design information to identify constructability, coordination, sequencing, and delivery risks; partnering with technical design authorities as needed. Supporting project cost and schedule assurance, risk analysis, procurement planning, change management, data analytics, and actionable performance reporting. Supporting commissioning and operational readiness by reviewing plans, test documentation, integrated systems testing, and turnover processes. Working with client stakeholders, design teams, contractors, equipment vendors, utility providers, and operations teams to resolve issues and advance project outcomes. Leading teams and workstreams; providing coaching, oversight, and support to delivery teams and staff. Supporting proposal development, client relationship development, business development, and practice recruiting and staffing efforts.

Job description limited partners on portfolio and fund management objectives and liquidity solutions, including structured capital solutions for private markets issuers and investors. What is the Position? Evercore PCA seeks an Associate to support the day-to-day operations of its Structured Capital Solutions (SCS) team in New York. The position will report directly to the head of PCA’s SCS group and collaborate with the PCA Operations team, using shared processes, systems and reporting standards. Role Focus: The Associate will manage SCS activities outside of deal execution, including project management, pipeline reporting, recruiting and people management, and leading technology initiatives, in close collaboration with the PCA operations team. Primary Responsibilities Project Management and Execution Discipline Establish and maintain project-management standards and tools for the team, including project charters, integrated schedules, work breakdown structures, critical-path analysis, risk and issue logs, dependency tracking, and regular status reporting. Enhance execution discipline by translating priorities into deliverables, owners, milestones, dependencies and deadlines; identifying slippage and bottlenecks, and escalating issues promptly as needed Pipeline Ownership and Management Reporting Own the SCS pipeline reporting process end to end, including collection, validation, reconciliation and timely circulation of weekly updates and leadership materials Maintain DealCloud as the core pipeline record, working with bankers to keep all data entry current; reconcile reports to underlying records and confirm commercial assumptions with responsible bankers Prepare weekly senior banker meeting materials, own meeting agenda and follow up actions; provide consistent SCS inputs to PCA-wide reporting Business Initiatives and Process Improvement Own AI initiatives of streamlining SCS deal processes: confirming scope, accountable owners, target dates, dependencies and completion criteria with SCS leadership Partner with PCA Operations and Technology on practical DealCloud improvement; coordinate testing, user feedback, documentation and adoption, with substantive output reviewed by the relevant specialists Team Coordination and PCA Operations Partnership Work closely with PCA Operations to align SCS reporting, workflows and operating practices with the wider platform, avoiding duplicate trackers and parallel processes Support SCS resource and capacity reporting, recruiting coordination, onboarding and training logistics in partnership with PCA Operations and relevant internal teams, with scope prioritized by SCS leadership Specific Qualifications 3+ years of relevant experience in business operations, project coordination or management, implementation, consulting, financial services or a similarly demanding environment Demonstrated ability to own recurring reporting and multiple concurrent projects, maintain detailed schedules, follow up across stakeholders and deliver accurate work on time Strong Excel and PowerPoint skills, including data reconciliation, clear reporting and preparation of concise management materials; familiarity with CRM systems such as DealCloud is helpful Working knowledge of project-planning tools such as Microsoft Project, Primavera or comparable platforms, with an understanding of milestones, dependencies and critical paths Clear written and verbal communication, sound judgment, discretion and the confidence to follow up with senior colleagues and escalate issues appropriately Collaborative, practical approach and willingness to handle detailed execution; financial services or private markets experience is helpful but not required Expected Base Salary Range: $150,000 - $160,000 In addition to a competitive base salary, employees may be eligible to receive a discretionary bonus delivered in the form of cash and/or deferred equity.

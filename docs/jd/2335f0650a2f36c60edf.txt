About the Role We're looking for a Senior Manager Servicing Financial Transaction and Portfolio Management to join our growing team! As Senior Manager Servicing Financial Transaction and Portfolio Management, you'll own servicing financial transaction management, loan portfolio performance, and forecasting. You'll lead a team of analysts on monthly servicing transaction reconciliations and act as the main Servicing Operations partner to the leaders of Finance, Credit, Loss Forecasting, Capital Markets, and Investors. Your work shapes decisions on sub-servicer performance, portfolio recovery, and risk mitigation. If you want to own the numbers that drive servicing results and are ready to turn them into decisions, this role is for you.

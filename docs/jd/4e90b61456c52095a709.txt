About This Job The main purpose of this job is to support the daily management, adoption, and continuous improvement of IT Portfolio Management platforms such as Planview. This role combines platform administration with user support, training, business analysis, and stakeholder partnership to help users effectively leverage the platform while maintaining data integrity and operational efficiency. Essential Functions Assist with system configurations, including fields, views, forms, and security settings Manage user accounts, permissions, and access requests Support workflow setup and optimization for project, portfolio, and operational processes Serve as a primary point of contact for user questions, support requests, and system troubleshooting Build strong relationships with business and technology partners to understand their needs and improve the user experience Facilitate user onboarding and provide training to new and existing platform users Create and maintain user guides, training materials, knowledge articles, and process documentation Conduct requirements-gathering for routine to moderately complex work sessions with stakeholders and document business needs, process flows, and enhancement opportunities Analyze business processes and recommend platform solutions that improve efficiency, visibility, and adoption Import, export, validate, and maintain data to ensure accuracy and consistency across the platform Generate reports, dashboards, and insights to support project, portfolio, and operational decision-making Partner with vendors and internal stakeholders to evaluate routine to moderately complex enhancements, resolve issues, and adopt new capabilities Monitor system performance and support routine maintenance activities Assist with routine to moderately complex testing, validation, and rollout of new features, workflows, and platform enhancements Perform other duties as assigned Knowledge, Skills & Abilities Required Basic understanding of Planview or similar portfolio management, work management, or enterprise SaaS platforms Strong customer service mindset with the ability to build trust and positive relationships across all levels of the organization Ability to translate business needs into system solutions through requirements gathering, analysis, and documentation.

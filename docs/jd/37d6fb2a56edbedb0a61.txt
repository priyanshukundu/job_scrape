About this role: Wells Fargo is seeking a Senior Software Engineer to join the Workbench Wizards AI Engineering team , responsible for designing, developing, and supporting enterprise-scale Generative AI and intelligent automation solutions. The role will contribute to moderately complex AI initiatives and deliverables supporting business, regulatory, engineering, and operational use cases. The position requires strong hands-on software engineering expertise combined with practical experience building Generative AI applications, LLM-based solutions, AI agents, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), and enterprise AI integrations . We are seeking a highly skilled Senior Software Engineer with strong Python and Generative AI engineering expertise to design, build, and productionize scalable AI solutions. The ideal candidate will have hands-on experience developing applications using Large Language Models, prompt engineering, RAG architectures, vector search, agentic workflows, APIs, and modern AI frameworks. This role requires strong engineering fundamentals, experimentation and problem-solving skills, and the ability to translate business requirements into reliable, secure, and scalable AI capabilities while operating within enterprise risk, security, privacy, and compliance standards. In this role, you will Lead moderately complex AI engineering initiatives and technical deliverables. Design, develop, test, deploy, and support Generative AI and LLM-powered applications . Build AI agents and agentic workflows capable of reasoning, tool invocation, workflow automation, and integration with enterprise systems. Design and implement RAG solutions , including document ingestion, chunking, embeddings, vector search, retrieval, reranking, and response generation. Develop reusable AI components, APIs, services, and frameworks that can support multiple enterprise use cases. Perform prompt engineering and prompt optimization , including evaluation and testing of LLM responses. Build mechanisms for LLM evaluation, observability, monitoring, guardrails, and responsible AI controls . Integrate AI solutions with enterprise data sources, APIs, databases, and internal applications. Evaluate AI models, frameworks, and implementation patterns based on accuracy, latency, scalability, security, and cost. Troubleshoot complex AI and software engineering issues across development and production environments. Participate in code reviews and contribute to engineering standards, reusable patterns, and development best practices. Collaborate with product owners, business partners, architects, data engineers, AI engineers, and technology leaders to translate use cases into scalable AI solutions. Provide technical guidance and mentoring to less experienced engineers. Ensure solutions comply with enterprise security, privacy, model risk, data governance, and responsible AI requirements . Required Qualifications 4+ years of software engineering experience , with strong hands-on development experience. Strong understanding of software engineering fundamentals, including modular design, testing, debugging, performance, scalability, and secure development. 3+ years of Python development experience building production-grade applications, APIs, or data/AI services. 2+ years of experience with Generative AI or LLM-based applications . 2+ years of experience working with LLM APIs and AI application frameworks , such as LangChain, LangGraph, Semantic Kernel, or equivalent. 1+ years of experience implementing Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) patterns. 1+ years of experience with embeddings, vector search, and vector databases or equivalent enterprise search technologies. 2+ years of experience developing REST APIs and integrating applications with enterprise services and data sources. 2+ years of experience with Git, CI/CD, automated testing, and Agile software development practices . Desired Qualifications Experience designing or implementing agentic AI solutions and multi-step AI workflows . Experience with LLM evaluation techniques, including quality, grounding, hallucination, relevance, latency, and cost evaluation . Experience with AI observability, tracing, monitoring, prompt/version management, and production support. Experience implementing AI guardrails, content safety controls, human-in-the-loop workflows, and responsible AI practices . Experience with libraries and frameworks such as PyTorch, Hugging Face Transformers, LangChain, LangGraph, Semantic Kernel, or similar technologies . Experience with cloud AI platforms and services in Azure or AWS . Understanding of modern data architectures, structured and unstructured data processing, and data integration. Experience working with enterprise authentication, authorization, secrets management, and secure API integration. Experience developing AI solutions within financial services, regulatory reporting, risk, or other highly controlled environments . Strong analytical, problem-solving, communication, and collaboration skills. Demonstrated ability to independently evaluate technologies, prototype solutions, and move successful prototypes into production. Experience mentoring engineers and contributing to reusable engineering standards and frameworks. Bachelor’s degree in computer science, engineering, artificial intelligence, machine learning, or a related field, or equivalent practical experience.

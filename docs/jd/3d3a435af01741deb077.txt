Introduction to the Team Expedia Group Advertising helps Expedia Group and its partners connect with travelers and understand the impact of advertising across Expedia Group brands and partner channels. Within Emerging Business Technology, the Measurement, Attribution & Data Platform organization builds the data, measurement, and attribution capabilities that translate advertising interactions and outcomes into trusted insights. As a Data Scientist III, you will shape how we measure advertising performance and outcomes. You’ll apply statistical methods, causal inference, experimentation, and predictive modeling to complex, large-scale datasets—developing approaches that help distinguish the impact of advertising from other factors in a traveler’s journey. Your work will inform attribution, measurement, and reporting experiences used by advertisers and internal teams to make better decisions. You’ll partner closely with engineering, product, and analytics stakeholders to define measurement problems, evaluate data quality and assumptions, and turn analytical methods into scalable, reliable solutions. You’ll bring scientific rigor to ambiguous questions, communicate findings and tradeoffs clearly, and help ensure that insights are actionable and trustworthy in production. We’re looking for a data scientist who takes ownership of high-impact work, exercises sound judgment, and raises the bar for analytical quality. At this level, you’ll lead projects from problem framing through validation and adoption, influence measurement strategy, and help evolve the platforms and methods Expedia Group uses to understand advertising outcomes. In This Role, You Will Analyze large, complex datasets to solve business and product challenges. Develop and apply statistical, machine learning, and experimentation methodologies to generate actionable insights. Build scalable analytical solutions, dashboards, and measurement frameworks. Communicate findings and recommendations clearly to technical and non-technical audiences. Mentor junior team members and contribute to analytics best practices. Minimum Qualifications Bachelor's degree in Data Science, Statistics, Mathematics, Computer Science, Economics, Engineering, or a related quantitative field. 5+ years of experience in data science, analytics, or a related field applying statistical analysis, experimentation, machine learning, or predictive modeling to business problems. Advanced proficiency in SQL and Python, R, or similar programming languages. Strong depth in experimental design, causal inference, forecasting, optimization, or other advanced analytical methods, with a record of using data to guide product or business decisions. Ability to independently manage analytical projects from problem definition through implementation and stakeholder adoption. Experience applying statistical modeling and machine learning concepts to real-world products; familiarity with AI-driven systems, tools, or workflows and applying AI/ML concepts to real-world products. Strong communication skills with experience presenting insights to both technical and non-technical audiences. Preferred Qualifications Master's degree or PhD in a quantitative field. Experience developing, deploying, or monitoring machine learning models in production environments. Expertise in experimentation, causal inference, A/B testing, and advanced statistical methods. Demonstrated ability to influence business strategy and decision-making through data-driven insights. Experience mentoring junior team members and partnering with senior stakeholders. Familiarity with AI/ML applications, generative AI, and large-scale data environments. Please note that this role is only available in Chicago, IL .

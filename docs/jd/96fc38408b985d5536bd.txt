Senior System Software Engineer - AV Platform
NVIDIA
Location: US, CA, Santa Clara
Link: https://nvidia.wd5.myworkdayjobs.com/NVIDIAExternalCareerSite/job/US-CA-Santa-Clara/Senior-System-Software-Engineer---AV-Platform_JR2030679

NVIDIA’s Autonomous Vehicles Platform team is seeking a Senior System Software Engineer with a strong background in embedded systems software design and development. You will design and build software that enables the bring-up, integration, and validation of next-generation autonomous vehicle platforms! In this hands-on role, you will own technical solutions from requirements and architecture through implementation and delivery. You will collaborate with hardware, firmware, platform software, and validation teams to solve complex system challenges and deliver reliable, maintainable software for NVIDIA DRIVE platforms. What you’ll be doing: Architect and develop embedded system software for NVIDIA DRIVE platforms, with well-defined interfaces and modular design. Develop software components that support platform bringup, hardware interaction, and system validation. Build reusable platform software and tools that streamline development and integration. Collaborate across engineering teams to guide design decisions and deliver robust, high-quality software. Provide technical leadership, mentor engineers, and lead a team to design and deliver high-quality embedded software. What we need to see: BS or MS in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, Electrical Engineering, or a related field (or equivalent experience). 5+ years of experience in embedded system software design and development. Demonstrated ability to own software architecture and detailed design, evaluate technical tradeoffs, and deliver production-quality implementation. Expertise in low-level debugging and system-level optimization within complex embedded hardware-software ecosystems. Proficiency in C/C++/Python. Ways to stand out from the crowd: Hands-on experience designing and deploying HIL/SIL systems for embedded automotive or robotics platforms. Familiarity with automotive sensors and AV software stack. Experience with the NVIDIA DRIVE platform. Background with CI/CD tooling (e.g., Jenkins, Docker) for system software. Experience using AI tools to accelerate software development. Your base salary will be determined based on your location, experience, and other objective job-related factors. The expected base salary ranges for this position are: 157,500 USD – 299,000 USD for Level 3; and 187,500 USD – 364,000 USD for Level 4. You will also be eligible for equity and benefits .

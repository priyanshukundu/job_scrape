Turner & Townsend is seeking an experienced Associate Director, US Assurance to join our US Assurance team. This senior role will lead assurance reviews across strategic accounts, major programs, and complex projects throughout the United States. We are looking for an experienced professional with a strong project controls background who can apply expertise across cost, schedule, risk, change, forecasting, performance management, and governance to independently assess delivery performance and identify areas of business exposure. The successful candidate will combine strong technical knowledge with analytical thinking, independent judgment, and the ability to engage credibly with senior stakeholders. The role will lead complex assurance reviews, challenge project and program performance, identify root causes and systemic risks, and translate findings into clear recommendations and business insights that support executive decision-making. Working closely with the US Assurance Lead, regional leadership, and account teams, the Associate Director will play an important role in strengthening delivery performance, protecting client confidence, and reducing business risk. Responsibilities Assurance Leadership Support the US Assurance Lead in delivering the US assurance strategy and annual review program. Lead complex assurance reviews, deep dives, and independent assessments of high-risk or strategically significant accounts and programs. Provide senior technical direction and assurance support across the review portfolio. Act as a trusted assurance and project controls advisor to regional, account, and functional leadership. Deputize for the US Assurance Lead when required. Promote an independent, evidence-based, and improvement-focused assurance culture. Assurance Reviews & Performance Assessment Independently assess governance, project controls, commercial management, delivery performance, resourcing, client risks, and operational effectiveness. Facilitate review interviews and challenge sessions with account leadership, delivery teams, and Subject Matter Experts. Identify root causes and determine whether issues represent isolated delivery concerns or wider systemic weaknesses. Develop clear, practical recommendations that improve performance and reduce business exposure. Provide assurance leadership and review facilitation when additional senior support is required. Project Controls & Performance Assurance Assess the effectiveness of project and program controls, including cost management, forecasting, planning and scheduling, baseline management, change control, risk and contingency management, earned value, and performance reporting. Analyze performance against approved cost and schedule baselines to identify adverse trends, control weaknesses, and emerging risks. Assess alignment between cost forecasts, schedules, risk exposure, contingency, change, and reported performance. Challenge the adequacy of forecasts, recovery plans, corrective actions, and management responses where performance is outside agreed tolerances. Apply project controls expertise to independently challenge project and program information and provide meaningful performance insight. Risk, Governance & Business Insights Provide independent oversight and challenge of material project, program, and account risks. Assess the effectiveness of governance, management controls, decision-making, and risk escalation. Review and challenge assurance findings, risk logs, RAG assessments, mitigating actions, and management responses. Ensure material risks are clearly articulated, appropriately rated, owned, and supported by credible mitigation plans. Monitor assurance risks and actions through 30-, 60-, and 90-day follow-up and escalate significant, overdue, or deteriorating exposure as appropriate. Identify recurring themes, trends, systemic control weaknesses, and emerging risks across the assurance portfolio. Lead or support root cause analysis and translate assurance findings into actionable business intelligence. Identify and communicate lessons learned, successes, and business or program opportunities arising from assurance activity. Reporting & Quality Assurance Quality assure assurance reports to ensure findings are evidence-based, appropriately rated, concise, and supported by clear business impacts and actions. Review quarterly and annual assurance reports, executive summaries, dashboards, and leadership presentations. Ensure consistency and quality of reporting across the US Assurance program. Support development of portfolio-level trend analysis, thematic reviews, annual assurance insights, leadership briefings, and business intelligence outputs. Subject Matter Expert Leadership Identify and assign appropriate Subject Matter Experts based on technical expertise, sector experience, independence, and availability. Establish clear review objectives, assessment criteria, evidence requirements, deliverables, and timelines. Review and challenge Subject Matter Expert outputs to ensure findings are evidence-based, appropriately rated, and consistent with assurance methodology. Assume Subject Matter Expert responsibilities where additional technical expertise or capacity is required. Promote consistency and calibration across assurance assessments and RAG ratings. Coach and support Subject Matter Experts to strengthen the quality of analysis and reporting. Executive & Stakeholder Engagement Engage with Regional Leads, Cost Center Leads, account leadership, and other senior stakeholders on assurance matters. Present significant findings, risks, root causes, and recommendations clearly and credibly. Provide constructive independent challenge while maintaining productive relationships with account and business leadership. Facilitate discussions involving material risks, disputed findings, inadequate mitigation, and overdue actions. Participate in Regional Sync and Operations Risk Meetings as required. Build leadership confidence in assurance through sound judgment, technical credibility, and high-quality insight. Assurance Operations & Continuous Improvement Support the planning, prioritization, and coordination of the US assurance review program. Help prioritize assurance activity based on business risk, account significance, client exposure, and performance indicators. Act as a senior escalation and support point for the Assurance Manager and Assurance Coordinator. Support internal assurance planning, calibration, and review closeout meetings. Continuously improve assurance methodologies, assessment criteria, review tools, scoring approaches, and reporting standards. Support the integration of AI, analytics, automation, dashboards, and other digital capabilities into assurance processes. Promote knowledge sharing and adoption of project controls and assurance best practices across the business.

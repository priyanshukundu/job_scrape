Associate Director FC Strategy
Chewy
Location: Jessup, PA
Link: https://chewy.wd5.myworkdayjobs.com/External/job/Jessup-PA/Associate-Director-FC-Strategy_R31483-1

Job Description: Chewy is seeking an Associate Director, FC Strategy, to lead the development of new operational capabilities across our Core Fulfillment Center network. With a primary focus on the next 12–24 months, this leader will identify structural constraints, develop practical solutions and business cases, and lead selected strategic initiatives through validation, implementation, and operational handoff. This role leads an existing team and works through influence across Operations, PMO, Engineering, Technology, Finance, Maintenance & Reliability, and other functional partners. The successful candidate will combine deep fulfillment-operations experience with the ability to evaluate alternatives, make clear recommendations, and translate strategy into measurable operational results. The position is based at one of Chewy’s Core FCs and requires approximately 25–30% travel. What will you do · Define the future capability roadmap. Identify gaps in existing FC processes, systems, and physical capabilities that could constrain future business needs. Develop a sequenced 12–24-month roadmap with clear problems to solve, expected benefits, and dependencies. · Develop decision-ready business cases. Establish current-state baselines, quantify the cost of each gap, compare alternatives, and recommend solutions with Finance-validated benefits, resource requirements, risks, and investment needs. · Validate new capabilities. Partner with technical and operational teams to define requirements, pilot plans, and success criteria. Use results to recommend whether to scale, modify, or stop an initiative. · Lead assigned initiatives through implementation. Remain accountable for the operational solution and agreed business outcomes, while coordinating delivery with PMO and the functional teams responsible for execution. · Establish sustainable operational ownership. Engage Operations, Quality, Learning, and Maintenance & Reliability early. Define acceptance criteria and ensure that standards, training, support requirements, and ownership are established before handoff. · Lead and develop the team. Set focused priorities, delegate clear ownership, coach strategic thinking, and build the team’s ability to connect operational detail with network-level decisions. How Success Will Be Measured · Roadmap quality: A leadership-aligned roadmap that connects quantified operational gaps to business needs, proposed solutions, dependencies, and decision dates. · Decision quality: Business cases with credible baselines, evaluated alternatives, Finance-validated assumptions, and clear recommendations. · Capability validation: Pilots evaluated against predefined operational and financial criteria, resulting in timely scale, modify, or stop decisions. · Initiative outcomes: Delivery against agreed capability and benefit targets, such as throughput, usable capacity, productivity, or cost improvement, with safety, quality, and customer-experience guardrails. · Sustained operational handoff: Receiving teams accept the capability, have the required standards and support, and sustain agreed performance over a defined stabilization period. · Team effectiveness: Clear ownership, dependable delivery of commitments, and demonstrated growth in team members’ strategic and operational capabilities. Specific targets will be established for the assigned initiatives. Performance will reflect the leader’s contribution and agreed accountability, rather than overall network results outside the role’s control. Minimum Qualifications · Bachelor’s degree or equivalent practical experience. · At least 7 years of fulfillment-operations experience, with a strong understanding of FC processes, constraints, and performance drivers. · Demonstrated strategic thinking: the ability to connect operational problems to longer-term business needs, evaluate tradeoffs, and recommend practical solutions. · Experience leading cross-functional initiatives from problem definition through implementation and measurable results. · Experience developing operational business cases and partnering with Finance to validate benefits. · Experience leading and developing teams and influencing stakeholders without direct reporting authority. · Strong analytical, written, and executive communication skills. · Ability to be based at a Core FC and travel approximately 25–30%. Preferred Qualifications · Experience introducing automation, material-handling systems, WMS capabilities, or new operating processes into fulfillment operations. · Experience scaling a proven solution across multiple sites. · Familiarity with capital evaluation, pilot design, and benefits realization.

Model Development and Fine-Tuning:** Evaluate, select, and where appropriate guide the fine-tuning of foundation models. Clinical and Product Partnership:** Collaborate closely with product managers, designers, clinicians, and data stakeholders to understand care delivery workflows and translate them into well-scoped, high-impact AI features.

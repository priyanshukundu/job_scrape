Become a part of our caring community The Senior Software Engineer codes software applications based on business requirements. The Senior Software Engineer work assignments involve moderately complex to complex issues where the analysis of situations or data requires an in-depth evaluation of variable factors. The Senior Software Engineer standardizes the quality assurance procedure for software. Oversees testing and debugging and develops fixes. Research complaints and makes necessary adjustments and/or recommendations to resolve complex software related issues. Begins to influence department’s strategy. Makes decisions on moderately complex to complex issues regarding technical approach for project components, and work is performed without direction. Exercises considerable latitude in determining objectives and approaches to assignments. Responsibilities Design, develop, test, deploy, and support scalable software applications and cloud-based solutions for large-scale enterprise environments. Build and maintain .NET, Java/Spring Boot and Spring Batch applications, ensuring high performance, reliability, and maintainability. Design and develop RESTful APIs and microservices using enterprise architecture standards and cloud design patterns. Contribute to technical strategy by recommending solution approaches that align with business objectives and departmental goals. Analyze moderately complex technical problems, evaluate solution options, and provide sound recommendations involving cross-functional teams. Work independently with limited guidance while managing day-to-day development activities, priorities, and delivery timelines. Apply strong knowledge of software architecture, design patterns, and development methodologies to create robust and reusable solutions. Collaborate with product owners, architects, developers, QA engineers, and other stakeholders to deliver high-quality software in an Agile/Scrum environment. Develop and support cloud-native or cloud-enabled applications on Azure or other public/private cloud platforms such as AWS, GCP, or PCF. Implement and optimize solutions using NoSQL databases, preferably MongoDB, and integrate with other enterprise data platforms as needed. Participate in all phases of the software development lifecycle, including requirements analysis, design, coding, testing, deployment, and production support. Create automated unit, integration, and functional tests to ensure code quality and application stability. Support and improve CI/CD pipelines, release automation, and DevOps processes to enable efficient and reliable software delivery. Follow clean code, secure coding, and enterprise engineering best practices to ensure software quality, maintainability, and compliance. Troubleshoot production and non-production issues, perform root cause analysis, and implement sustainable fixes for complex technical challenges. Mentor junior engineers by providing technical guidance, code review feedback, and best practice recommendations. Promote engineering excellence through effective communication, peer collaboration, and continuous improvement of team development practices. Leverage AI-assisted development tools such as GitHub Copilot, Claude, Windsurf, or similar platforms to improve productivity in coding, testing, and documentation. Ensure responsible use of AI-assisted engineering practices while maintaining code quality, security, compliance, and enterprise standards. Continuously learn and stay current with emerging technologies, tools, frameworks, and industry trends to enhance team capability and solution quality. Contribute to improving consumer experiences by delivering reliable, secure, and user-focused digital solutions. Operate with independent judgment on moderately complex assignments and establish work priorities aligned to project objectives. Regularly use new technologies, concepts, and tools to solve business and technical problems effectively. Communicate technical concepts clearly to both technical and non-technical stakeholders and contribute to team decision-making.

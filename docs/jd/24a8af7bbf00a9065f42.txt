Job Title Sr. Financial Analyst Job Description Summary As the Senior Financial Analyst, Real Estate, you will independently deliver advanced financial analysis and decision support across complex client real estate portfolios and transactions. You will own sophisticated models and scenario analyses, maintain analytical integrity, synthesize findings into recommendations, and improve the tools and processes used to evaluate real estate alternatives. As a member of Cushman & Wakefield’s Portfolio Advisory Group, the Senior Financial Analyst, Real Estate will be dedicated to one or more of Cushman & Wakefield’s most dynamic client accounts. In this role, you will collaborate closely with peers and the account team to support a client's strategic real estate vision. The Senior Financial Analyst, Real Estate will embody our team philosophy “Client First, Insight Always, Execution Excellence”, ensuring every deliverable reflects the team’s commitment to innovation, collaboration, and measurable impact. Job Description This role will primarily: Own complex financial modeling and analysis for lease, transaction, investment, and portfolio decisions. Establish analytical rigor through documented assumptions, quality review, and reliable financial data. Advance automation, AI-enabled analysis, and standardized processes that improve speed, consistency, and scalability. Translate complex financial findings into executive-ready recommendations, business cases, and decision materials. Success in this role looks like: Complex financial analyses and business cases are accurate, transparent, timely, and decision-ready. Analytical assumptions, methodologies, risks, and limitations are clearly documented and withstand senior stakeholder review. Automation and process improvements reduce manual effort and improve consistency across recurring deliverables. Senior stakeholders understand the financial implications, risks, and tradeoffs of real estate alternatives and can act with confidence. Key Responsibilities Financial Modeling & Real Estate Decision Support Develop and maintain complex financial models that evaluate leases, acquisitions, dispositions, developments, workplace investments, and portfolio optimization opportunities. Conduct sale-versus-buy, scenario, sensitivity, stress-testing, and risk analyses that clarify financial implications and tradeoffs. Synthesize portfolio, transaction, market, and financial information into findings, recommendations, and business cases that support real estate decisions. Provide analytical guidance on model structure, assumptions, methodologies, and interpretation of results. Data Accuracy, Analytical Integrity & Documentation Own quality review of source data, model logic, assumptions, calculations, and outputs for assigned analyses. Establish and maintain clear documentation standards for data sources, financial methodologies, assumptions, metrics, and model limitations. Resolve complex data or analytical issues and communicate material risks, gaps, and limitations to stakeholders. Promote consistent analytical practices that improve confidence in financial reporting and decision support. Automation, AI Enablement & Process Improvement Design standardized tools, templates, and quality-control practices that improve the consistency and scalability of financial analysis. Apply AI-enabled solutions and automation to reduce manual analysis, accelerate insight generation, and strengthen reporting quality. Lead continuous-improvement efforts for recurring models, analyses, reporting workflows, and analytical documentation. Guide teammates in the effective use of approved analytical methods, automation, and shared resources. Stakeholder Communication & Executive-Ready Deliverables Partner with account teams and cross-functional stakeholders to define business questions, analytical requirements, assumptions, and decision criteria. Translate complex quantitative and qualitative findings into concise recommendations, executive-ready narratives, and presentation materials. Present analytical conclusions, risks, alternatives, and tradeoffs to senior stakeholders and respond to review questions with supporting evidence. Manage multiple high-priority analyses, review cycles, and stakeholder expectations in a fast-paced client environment. Portfolio Reporting & Visualization Design and maintain financial and portfolio reporting solutions, dashboards, and visualizations that support recurring and ad hoc decision-making. Define and apply consistent financial metrics and reporting logic across assigned analyses and deliverables. Evaluate reporting outputs for usability, relevance, and reliability and incorporate stakeholder feedback into future iterations. Qualifications Must-Have Requirements 5-8+ years of experience in financial analysis, corporate real estate, portfolio strategy, investment analysis, or a related analytical role. Demonstrated experience independently developing complex financial models, cash flow analyses, business cases, and scenario evaluations. Strong knowledge of commercial real estate fundamentals, lease economics, valuation measures, and investment decision frameworks. Demonstrated ability to synthesize complex data into clear findings, recommendations, and executive-ready deliverables. Experience establishing analytical assumptions, reviewing model quality, and resolving data or methodology issues. Ability to manage multiple high-priority assignments and influence cross-functional stakeholders without direct authority. Exceptional written, verbal, presentation, and stakeholder-management skills. Preferred Experience Experience supporting enterprise corporate real estate portfolios, major transactions, capital planning, or portfolio optimization. Experience in a client-facing consulting, advisory, or outsourced-services environment. Experience guiding junior analysts or reviewing the analytical work of others. Technology & Tools Advanced proficiency in Microsoft Excel and/or Google Sheets, including complex financial models, scenario analysis, and quality-control techniques. Advanced proficiency in Microsoft PowerPoint and/or Google Slides. Experience with data visualization or reporting tools such as Power BI, Tableau, or similar platforms. Demonstrated ability to apply AI-enabled analytical and workflow-automation tools to improve speed, quality, and consistency. Capabilities Advanced financial judgment, analytical rigor, and structured problem solving. Executive communication and the ability to explain complex financial concepts clearly. Independent ownership, prioritization, and sound judgment in ambiguous situations. Collaboration, stakeholder influence, continuous improvement, and accountability. Education Bachelor’s degree in Finance, Real Estate, Economics, Business, Data Analytics, or a related field preferred; equivalent relevant experience may be considered based on role and account requirements.

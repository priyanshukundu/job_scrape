Role Overview McKesson is seeking a Sr. Manager, FP&A – Generics Injectables to join the North American Pharmaceutical Distribution team and serve as the lead financial partner for the Generics Injectables portfolio. Reporting to the Director of Generics FP&A, this highly visible individual-contributor role will lead financial planning, profitability analysis, growth strategy, forecasting, annual operating planning, long-range planning, and strategic decision support. The role partners closely with Commercial, Sales, Operations, Sourcing, Pricing, and Finance leadership. The ideal candidate combines deep FP&A expertise, advanced financial judgment, and strong executive communication skills. This individual will translate ambiguous business questions into actionable recommendations, connect end-to-end Injectables economics, and influence decisions that support sustainable growth and profitability. The successful candidate will operate with significant autonomy, lead complex cross-functional initiatives without direct authority, and provide informal leadership across the broader FP&A organization. *This is an individual contributor role* Key Responsibilities Lead end-to-end FP&A for Generics Injectables, including forecasting, annual operating planning, long-range planning, performance analysis, profitability, variance analysis, and executive reporting. Own the Injectables financial forecast and underlying assumptions, ensuring alignment across Commercial, Sales, Sourcing, Pricing, Operations, Accounting, and Finance and providing a clear view of expected performance Lead monthly financial performance management, partnering with business teams to validate results, identify underlying drivers and one-time impacts, explain actual-to-forecast variances, and translate performance into forward-looking implications Serve as the lead financial advisor for Injectables, providing independent recommendations on business performance, growth opportunities, investment decisions, emerging risks, and strategic trade-offs. Lead complex financial analyses across product, program, customer, supplier, sourcing, channel, and commercial economics, with clear linkage to key performance indicators. Partner with Commercial, Sales, Operations, Sourcing, Pricing, Accounting, and Finance leadership to shape growth strategy, align financial assumptions, and evaluate strategic opportunities. Lead financial modeling and business cases supporting program expansion and conversion, sourcing opportunities, channel growth, emerging generics, and evolving Injectables priorities. Establish and enhance driver-based forecasting, KPIs, management reporting, and financial frameworks that improve visibility and decision quality. Anticipate changes in market, product, customer, channel, and sourcing dynamics and proactively redirect analysis and recommendations. Translate complex financial and operational information into concise executive-level choices, risks, trade-offs, and recommendations. Lead cross-functional strategic workstreams requiring alignment, coordination of significant resources, and influence across multiple organizations. Drive continuous improvement through standardized processes, automation, advanced analytics, Power BI, and AI-enabled decision support. Provide informal leadership, mentorship, and financial expertise to the broader FP&A organization while helping strengthen analytical capability and bench strength. Independently prioritize multiple high-impact initiatives while maintaining appropriate financial controls, analytical rigor, accuracy, and timeliness.

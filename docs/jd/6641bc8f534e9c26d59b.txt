Job Summary: The Third-Party Risk Manager, Audit & Regulatory will lead audit, regulatory, and examination management for the First Line of Defense (FLOD) execution of PayPal’s Third-Party Management Program. This individual contributor role is accountable for responding to internal audits, regulatory exams, regulator and partner requests, and regulatory reporting (such as the DORA register) accurately and on time, backed by well-organized data and due diligence evidence. The role is also the primary FLOD point of contact for Second Line of Defense (SLOD) control partners and policy owners across each risk domain, as well as Entity Service Owners (ESOs), and participates in risk forums to share feedback, trends, and change requirements. The successful candidate will offer a data-driven strategic perspective, sound business judgment, agility to adapt to changing priorities, and the ability to work and communicate collaboratively across multiple teams and geographies. Job Description: Essential Responsibilities: Lead and refresh risk assessments, including risk identification, documentation, rating, and control evaluation. Oversee the documentation of procurement processes and controls within a new system of record, ensuring regular revalidation. Support and manage enterprise-wide risk management programs, including the documentation and resolution of issues and incidents. Develop and maintain comprehensive risk and control inventories, including processes, risk mitigations, risks, and controls. Ensure accurate maintenance of governance, risk, and compliance tool data for areas of responsibility. Manage and execute risk programs for specific functional areas. Provide detailed assertions on risk exposure and present escalated risks to senior management. Maintain a strong understanding of risk management principles and PayPal products within areas of responsibility. Represent the business line in first-line risk forums. Lead global and cross-functional initiatives to drive change in a fast-paced environment. Minimum Qualifications: 5+ years relevant experience and a Bachelor’s degree OR Any equivalent combination of education and experience.

Position Purpose: The Sr. Software Engineer is responsible for independently developing and consistently redesigning/refactoring a product to adapt to our customers’ and associates’ needs. This role will support the continued evolution of The Home Depot mobile application and customer shopping experience, including the transition from legacy WebViews to native iOS implementations. The Sr. Software Engineer will contribute to the design and evolution of Server-Driven UI (SDUI), Backend-for-Frontend (BFF), and Kotlin Multiplatform (KMP) layers to enable flexibility while maintaining native performance. As a Sr. Software Engineer, you will be part of a dynamic team with engineers of all experience levels who help each other build and grow technical and leadership skills while creating, deploying, and supporting production applications. In addition, Sr. Software Engineers may be involved in product and tool selection, configuration, security, resilience, performance tuning, and production monitoring, as well as mentoring less experienced engineers. This role will also leverage AI-enabled development tools, such as Claude Code, as part of the engineering workflow to improve efficiency, reduce manual effort, and accelerate the delivery of customer value.

Analyst/Associate - Centralized Business Services
Neuberger Berman
Location: New York, NY
Link: https://nb.wd1.myworkdayjobs.com/NBCareers/job/New-York-NY/Analyst-Associate---Centralized-Business-Services_R0012836-1

As a member of the Private Wealth Centralized Business Services group, the Associate/Sr. Associate will provide onboarding and business support to Neuberger Berman Private Wealth Teams. The Associate/Sr. Associate will partner with Compliance, Supervision, Business Control and Operations as a point of service contact to ensure a highly efficient and rewarding client experience. Responsibilities: Coordinate with Neuberger Berman’s Private Wealth Teams to provide administrative and holistic support services to new and existing clients. Establish new accounts for natural person and entity clients. Duties include: document preparation, entity documentation review, KYC data gathering and review, performing quality control check before submission for approval, documentation, account funding and information follow up. Assist Teams with the NB Investment Advisory framework, mastering all aspects of account opening, generating necessary documentation, and advisory program monitoring and maintenance. Work closely with business partners to determine outcomes and requirements on a case by case basis, the candidate will be responsible for mastery of the program rules as well as point in time consultation with PW teams to assist their clients. Candidates are comfortable with varied workflow lifecycles and can pivot between many different cases at once in various stages of completion to maintain efficient operation within program compliance. Collaborate with cross functional teams in setting up Investment Policy Statements and maintenance related to any changes in client investment planning Review and process requests for fund disbursements, gifting securities, ACATs and DTC transfers. Process account maintenance requests including: KYC updates (name changes, personal data updates, address changes, investment objective updates, etc.), control and interested party updates, and account feature updates such as margin, check writing, options, etc. Serve as subject matter expert on complete end to end alternatives investment lifecycle including fund launch, subscription document fulfillment, review of KYC/documentation requirements and case workflows Act as primary point of contact for Private Wealth teams, addressing questions related to subscriptions, transfers and required documentation Review subscription materials for completeness and accuracy, ensuring alignment with product, legal, and regulatory requirements Coordinate with Legal, AML and other internal teams to facilitate timely and accurate review and approval of subscriptions Provide guidance, education and training to Private Wealth teams on subscription procedures, documentation standards and operational procedures Demonstrate a strong understanding of complex entity and ownership structures, with the ability to identify required documentation for each entity type Address internal/external client service inquiries/requests from PW Teams and resolve issues in a timely manner and escalate complex issues as necessary Assist with the Employee Investment Solutions platform with account openings, maintenance and trading Identify and flag suspicious activity to mitigate risk and prevent fraudulent account behavior and potential fraud exposure Ad hoc projects as assigned by management Qualifications: Undergraduate degree Minimum 2 years of experience in Private Equity or Wealth Management client service SIE, Series 7 and 66 licenses preferred or ability to obtain Excellent communication skills with a client service mindset Proficiency with Excel and PowerPoint Strong organizational skills and excellent attention to detail Ability to prioritize workload based on evolving needs and circumstances Strong multitasking skill Location: New York City; hybrid with a minimum of three days per week in office. Applicants must be authorized and have the right to work in the country where the role is located without the need for current or future sponsorship.

The AVP Data Engineering – Business Insurance Ontology & Knowledge Graph is an executive-facing leadership role accountable for defining, building, and driving enterprise adoption of the Business Insurance ontology, semantic layer, and knowledge graph. This leader establishes the governed business meaning, context, and relationships that power trusted analytics, AI, agentic experiences, and decision automation across Small Business, Middle & Large Business, and Global Specialty. Operating at the intersection of commercial P&C insurance, business semantics, data architecture, graph engineering, and AI enablement, the role translates complex insurance domain knowledge—submissions, quotes, accounts, policies, coverages, exposures, risks, claims, producers, and portfolio performance—into production-ready semantic assets consumable by people, data products, LLMs, and agents. The AVP serves as the senior technical and domain authority for semantic and AI-ready data in Business Insurance, leads and leverage team of ontologists, graph modelers, semantic and AI data engineers, and business data analysts, and partners directly with the heads of Small Business, Middle & Large Business, and Global Specialty to align the ontology roadmap to growth, underwriting profitability, operational efficiency, and innovation outcomes. Must be eligible to work in the US without company sponsorship.

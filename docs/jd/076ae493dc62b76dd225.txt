Hands‑on experience developing, deploying, and testing solutions in cloud environments (AWS preferred). Strong experience debugging, optimizing, and performance‑tuning high‑volume data pipelines. Financial‑services data domain expertise is preferred. Excellent communication and collaboration skills; ability to work with cross‑functional teams including data scientists, researchers, and business partners.

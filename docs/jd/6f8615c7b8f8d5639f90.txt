About the Role: We are seeking a talented and motivated, best-in-class Data Engineer II. This role presents an exciting opportunity to thrive in a dynamic, fast-paced environment within a rapidly growing team, with abundant prospects for career advancement. This position will own the data pipelines, transformations, and reporting processes that support accurate, complete, and timely furnishing of customer account information to credit bureaus. This role presents an opportunity to work in a fast-paced, high growth environment while taking hands-on ownership of a critical customer-facing data function. You will dig into account data and code, debug SQL and Python workflows, and carry issues from investigation through verified resolution. You will partner with Customer Service to resolve technical dispute escalations, with Compliance to apply approved reporting requirements, and with Engineering when a fix is needed in production services or the consumer app. We are looking for an independent problem solver who can manage recurring reporting operations while improving the systems behind them.

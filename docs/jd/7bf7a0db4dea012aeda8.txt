Job Family : Data Science Consulting Travel Required : Up to 10% Clearance Required : Ability to Obtain Public Trust What You Will Do: We are seeking an experienced Data Governance Workstream Lead to support a large-scale State Health data modernization initiative. This individual will lead the development and implementation of practical governance, stewardship, data quality, metadata, and data management practices that improve the consistency, quality, and trusted use of enterprise data and assist in a broader digital modernization initiative. This is a hands-on leadership role working across agency programs, business stakeholders, IT, and data teams. This role will lead a data workstream, facilitate key decisions, manage deliverables and team members, and help integrate governance requirements into data platform modernization, source onboarding, reporting, and ongoing operations. What You Will Do: Lead the data governance workstream for a large-scale State Health data modernization program, partnering with agency leadership, business stakeholders, data owners, architects, engineers, and other project workstreams. Establish and operationalize the data governance operating model, including roles and responsibilities, decision rights, governance forums, escalation paths, and processes for resolving data quality, access, definition, and reporting issues. Establish and mature data ownership and stewardship, including identification and onboarding of data owners and stewards across key health and Medicaid data domains. Develop and implement data governance policies, standards, business rules, definitions, and controls covering data quality, metadata, classification, access, security, retention, and data sharing. Partner with business and technical teams to define and maintain data domains, critical data elements, authoritative sources, business definitions, metadata, and lineage as data sources are integrated into the enterprise platform. Define and operationalize data quality and issue-management practices, including data quality rules, monitoring, remediation, ownership, escalation, and reporting. Integrate governance requirements into source onboarding, data integration, reporting, and enterprise identity activities, helping ensure governance is part of day-to-day delivery rather than a separate compliance exercise. Develop practical governance artifacts such as governance charters, RACI matrices, stewardship guidance, data inventories, business glossaries, standards, issue-management processes, and governance reporting. Facilitate governance working sessions and decisions with stakeholders across agency programs, IT, privacy, security, data and analytics, and leadership. Lead and mentor the governance team, manage workstream priorities and deliverables, and communicate progress, risks, dependencies, and decisions to client and project leadership. What You Will Need: Bachelor’s degree from an accredited college or university. Based on contractual obligations, candidate must be located within the United States and be a U.S. Citizen. Must be able to obtain and maintain a Federal or DoD Public Trust. Minimum seven (7+) years of overall work experience. Experience in data governance, data management, analytics, data engineering, or enterprise data modernization, including experience supporting public-sector organizations. Demonstrated experience leading data governance initiatives or workstreams, including governance operating models, policies, standards, stewardship, and organizational roles. Strong knowledge of data ownership and stewardship, data domains, metadata, business glossaries, lineage, data quality, master and reference data, classification, privacy, and access controls. Experience supporting enterprise data modernization, including data warehouses, cloud data platforms, data integration, or analytics environments. Ability to work closely with architects and engineers to incorporate governance into source onboarding, data integration, transformation, data models, quality controls, metadata, lineage, and reporting. Experience establishing data quality and issue-management processes, including rules, ownership, monitoring, remediation, escalation, and reporting. Experience facilitating cross-functional governance discussions with business programs, executives, IT, privacy, security, and data and analytics teams. Strong client leadership, facilitation, written communication, and team management skills. Ability to travel up to 10% during key project periods. What Would Be Nice To Have: Experience leading data governance or modernization initiatives for State Health, Medicaid, or other health and human services agencies. Experience with Medicaid or health data, including claims and encounters, eligibility, provider, managed care, pharmacy, behavioral health, or clinical data. Experience with enterprise data warehouses, cloud data platforms, lakehouses, or enterprise analytics environments. Experience with data governance and catalog tools such as Microsoft Purview, Collibra, Alation, Informatica, OneTrust, or similar platforms. Experience establishing data catalogs, business glossaries, metadata repositories, lineage, data quality scorecards, or stewardship workflows. Familiarity with HIPAA, health data interoperability, public-sector privacy and security requirements, and data-sharing agreements. Data governance or data management certifications such as DAMA CDMP, or relevant platform certifications. Experience supporting organizational adoption, stakeholder engagement, and training associated with new governance practices. The annual salary range for this position is $149,000.00-$248,000.00.

The Brand Partnerships team meets this audience where they already are, partnering with premium sports, gaming, and lifestyle properties to build cultural relevance and shift brand perception through activations that feel unconventional yet native. As a Brand Partnership Associate - Sports, you'll own the coordination and operational execution across a multi-sport portfolio that includes Aston Martin F1, Golden State Warriors, Valkyries, New York Liberty, and emerging properties, managing timelines, partner deliverables, and hospitality logistics across some of the most visible partnerships in the industry. What you’ll do: Develop and maintain relationships with key brand partners and support Sr. Manager and cross-functional teams on brand initiatives leveraging the sports portfolio. Own asset inventories, activation trackers, and timeline management across sports partnerships, and maintain a tracker for emerging sports pilot opportunities. Lead project management for sports activations, including weekly status updates, meeting notes, creative approvals and coordination with external partners. Drive budget tracker upkeep, purchase order processing, and invoice reconciliation across the sports portfolio. Manage hospitality operations for Warriors, Valkyries, and Liberty: ticket inventory, guest lists, internal distribution, and on-site hosting. Support GTM strategy and briefing/ideation strategies, mid and end-of-season recaps, and materials for renewals/decisions. Required Skills and Experience: 2+ years of experience in sports marketing, partnerships or sponsorship management. Demonstrated ability to manage multiple concurrent project timelines, deliverables, and external partner relationships simultaneously. Hands-on experience with budget tracking, purchase orders, and invoice reconciliation in a marketing or partnerships context. Proven success working cross-functionally with creative, social, communications, and analytics teams to deliver activations on time. Familiarity with sports partnership ecosystems, including rights holders, agencies, and hospitality logistics, with direct experience in professional basketball, Formula One, tennis, or golf preferred. A pulse on emerging cultural trends, with a track record of identifying moments early and connecting them to brand opportunities. Agency-side experience in sports marketing or brand partnerships is a plus. Utilizes generative AI responsibly, maintaining human oversight to deliver business-ready outputs and drive measurable improvements in workflow efficiency, cost, and quality.

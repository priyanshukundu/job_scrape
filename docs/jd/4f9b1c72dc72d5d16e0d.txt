We are seeking a Senior Machine Learning Engineer / Applied Research Scientist to build and scale AI/ML systems for complex healthcare utilization management problems. This is a hands-on senior IC role requiring deep technical expertise, scientific rigor, healthcare domain knowledge, and ownership from problem definition through production and measurable business outcomes. Responsibilities: Own ML problems end-to-end : problem formulation, data analysis, experimentation, modeling, validation, deployment, monitoring, and iteration. Build and optimize solutions using Generative AI, LLMs, deep learning, classical ML, NLP, RAG, and retrieval technologies . Train and fine-tune models, diagnose model behavior and failure modes, and debug issues across data, model, inference, retrieval, and production systems . Design statistically rigorous experiments, establish baselines and success metrics, perform validation/error analysis, and draw defensible conclusions from quantitative and qualitative evidence. Build scalable production ML systems , identifying and resolving bottlenecks across quality, latency, cost, reliability, and infrastructure. Perform large-scale data analysis using Python, SQL, and appropriate data/ML tooling , including building custom solutions where existing tools are insufficient. Apply strong knowledge of healthcare utilization management , clinical workflows, medical necessity, authorization processes, and healthcare data. Partner with product, engineering, clinical, and business stakeholders to translate ambiguous problems into scalable technical solutions and measurable outcomes. Operate with high agency , making sound technical decisions under uncertainty and driving complex initiatives from concept through stable production. Qualifications: BS in Computer Science, ML/AI, Statistics, Engineering, or a related quantitative field ; MS with ML/AI specialization preferred . Significant hands-on experience building and operating ML/AI systems at production scale . Strong expertise in Python, SQL, ML/deep learning, LLMs/GenAI, NLP, RAG, experimentation, and distributed systems . Demonstrated ability to train/fine-tune models, debug model behavior, analyze large datasets, and design rigorous experiments and evaluations . Strong healthcare experience, preferably in utilization management or related clinical decision-support workflows . Publications in reputable peer-reviewed conferences or journals , demonstrating scientific reasoning, independent research, and technical writing capabilities. Proven ability to operate independently in ambiguous environments and deliver quantifiable clinical, operational, or business impact . What Distinguishes This Role: We are looking for someone who can operate across the full lifecycle: Healthcare problem → data → hypothesis → experiment → model → validation → scalable production system → measurable outcome This is not a research-only or prototype-focused role. The ideal candidate combines the rigor of an applied scientist , the depth of an ML engineer , and the ownership expected of a technical leader . If you will be working at home occasionally or permanently, the internet connection must be obtained through a cable broadband or fiber optic internet service provider with speeds of at least 10Mbps download/5Mbps upload.

Monitoring and analysis of global financial markets, economic indicators, news, and geopolitical events to identify trading opportunities and assess market risks. Management and monitoring of the risk associated with the trading portfolio and ensuring compliance with regulatory and internal risk management guidelines. Collaboration with research analysts and other teams to gather insights and information for trading decisions. This is an exciting opportunity to join Barclays as an Exotic Rates Trader. In this role, you will contribute to the pricing, risk management, and lifecycle management of multi-layered rates products while partnering closely with Sales, Structuring, Technology, and Risk teams. You will gain exposure to a broad range of market dynamics, quantitative analysis, and product innovation, playing a key role in delivering solutions for clients and driving business growth. To be successful as an Exotic Rates Trader, you should have experience with: Exotic derivatives, structured products, or rates trading and risk management Quantitative modeling, pricing methodologies, and financial market analysis Managing product lifecycle events, including expiries, resets, and other key risk events Some other highly valued skills may include: Knowledge of macroeconomic trends and their impact on rates markets Experience developing new products, trading strategies, or quantitative analytics Ability to collaborate effectively with stakeholders across Sales, Trading, Structuring, Technology, and Risk, with experience in Rates, FX, or Equities markets being advantageous You may be assessed on the key critical skills relevant for success in role, such as risk and controls, change and transformation, business acumen, strategic thinking, digital and technology, as well as job-specific technical skills.

Overview Prime Finance operates in a highly competitive marketplace, delivering Prime Brokerage services to institutional clients such as hedge funds, backed by robust financing and stock borrow capabilities. Prime Brokerage Technology provides the platforms and solutions that support our clients and traders - the foundation of the Prime Brokerage business. Our clients depend on us to deliver consistently high levels of service, ensuring transactions, payments, and reporting are processed accurately and without failure, every single day.

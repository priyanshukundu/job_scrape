You will support multiple concurrent initiatives that require extensive cross-functional collaboration with business partners and other IT teams. Success in this role requires creativity, strong problem-solving and communication skills, and meticulous attention to detail. The position comes with an expectation to continuously learn and adopt new technologies. Gathers information from existing systems and analyzes program and time requirements. Designs programs for projects and enhancements to existing programs. Stays current on industry trends and new developments to maintain skills and align with industry standards. Works directly with business partners to prioritize work and assign tasks to team members. Conducts quality assurance activities, such as peer reviews. Stays current on business processes and partners with business leaders to ensure system alignment. Maintains a positive team culture built on collaboration and trust.

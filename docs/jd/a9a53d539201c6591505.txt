The role We are seeking a Senior Quantitative Researcher to join GRM at a pivotal moment in its evolution - from compliance-oriented risk enforcement to a research-driven risk advisory function. This is the first dedicated research hire in GRM's history, and a rare opportunity to define what rigorous, independently credible risk governance looks like at a sophisticated, multi-product options firm. The role is hands-on and consequential. You will build, validate, and deploy production-quality models across limits calibration, scenario analysis, and firmwide risk measurement and your findings will translate directly into governance decisions, not disappear into a report. Working closely with the Head of GRM and in active collaboration with the broader quantitative research team, you will shape a modeling agenda that is wide open and immediately impactful: firmwide convexity, correlation misestimation, limits calibration, and beyond. The ideal candidate combines deep statistical rigor with genuine options market intuition. GRM is small by design, and the Head of GRM is actively building the function which means the Senior Quant will have real agency over what this team becomes, what problems it prioritizes, and how research ultimately informs the way the firm manages risk. What you'll do Own the quantitative modeling agenda for GRM - designing, calibrating, and validating the risk models that underpin governance decisions, including a new and evolving in-house VaR model, with significant autonomy over methodology. Research and quantify risks not captured by the canonical model - correlation misestimation, term structure concentration, basis risk, OI/liquidity dynamics, and firmwide convexity exposure - and develop principled calibration frameworks for limits parameters currently set by negotiation or convention Translate research findings into actionable governance recommendations and evaluate existing framework assumptions against empirical data, ensuring every project connects to a limit, a metric, or a decision Participate in peer review practices with the broader quantitative research community to ensure GRM's modeling work is independently credible What we're looking for 5+ years of experience in quantitative research or risk modeling, with a proven track record of building production-quality risk models - including vol surface dynamics and complex cross-product exposures Deep statistical foundation and programming skills (Python or similar), with strong options market structure intuition - including how vol surfaces, term structure, and Greeks behavior across regimes relate to how risk actually manifests in practice Experience contributing to limits frameworks, risk attribution pipelines, or production monitoring infrastructure; track record of leveraging frontier AI tools and LLM-assisted workflows to accelerate research and code development Builder mentality combined with independent judgment: able to take a concept from whitepapers to production tool, comfortable with messy real-world data, and confident enough to form and defend model assumptions in front of experienced traders and quants with strong priors Clear communicator with a high-ownership attitude and a genuine orientation toward connecting research to governance decisions, not just methodological elegance Nice to have Advanced degree (PhD preferred) in Mathematics, Statistics, Physics, Engineering, Computer Science, Operations Research, or a related quantitative field Familiarity with options market making or systematic volatility strategies across equities, indexes, rates, commodities, or VIX, with exposure to cross-product risk measurement challenges Most teams at CTC, with the exception of Trading, follow a hybrid workplace model, subject to change based on business need.

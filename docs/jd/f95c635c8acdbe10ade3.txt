Join a Company that Empowers you to Build your Future We are seeking a detail-oriented Software Engineer in Test to join our engineering team building our flagship digital web presence. The ideal candidate will bring deep expertise in both manual and automated testing, a strong understanding of the software development lifecycle, and a passion for quality. As a Software Engineer in Test, you will serve as the dedicated testing resource across multiple product pods, owning end-to-end quality efforts, designing and maintaining a robust suite of automated checks, leveraging AI tools to drive efficiency and coverage, and collaborating closely with cross-functional teams to help foster a culture of continuous improvement and fast feedback. A career with purpose. A career built on making dreams come true. A career built on building zero defect homes, cost management, and adherence to schedules. This is a full-time HYBRID position for Dallas, TX or Miami, FL. Candidates must be authorized to work in the United States and be a resident in a United States time zone. Your Responsibilities on the Team Serve as the testing resource across multiple product pods, owning both manual and automated testing efforts to ensure consistent quality standards team-wide. Design, create, and maintain a robust suite of automated checks using front-end automation tools, ensuring broad coverage across unit, API, and UI levels. Coordinate with the team to prioritize and manage a running list of checks to automate. Collaborate with engineers to determine what requires automated checks and at what level of the testing pyramid. Work with the team to effectively test features and fixes, providing timely and actionable feedback. Create innovative solutions to complex testing problems, empowering the team with fast, reliable feedback loops. Assist with building and maintaining testing documentation to support team knowledge and consistency. Utilize AI tools to accelerate testing workflows and improve overall software quality. Promote a team culture that is always learning, motivated, and collaborative. Help troubleshoot and resolve quality-related incidents as needed. Requirements Education: Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field, or equivalent practical experience. Test Automation: Proven experience writing code for automated checks and/or building tools to aid in testing.

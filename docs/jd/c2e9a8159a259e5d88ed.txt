We believe each of us is here to help others succeed and this has led to PIMCO being recognized as an innovator, industry thought leader and trusted advisor to our clients. PIMCO is seeking a highly motivated Senior Associate to join our global Legal & Compliance team. This role plays a key part in supporting and enhancing the firm’s broker-dealer compliance program, helping to ensure regulatory compliance, strengthen governance, and promote a culture of integrity across the organization. The successful candidate will join a team responsible for the ongoing oversight and continuous improvement of PIMCO’s broker-dealer compliance program, with responsibilities spanning compliance monitoring and testing, training, electronic communications monitoring, supervisory inspections, data analysis and reporting, and other broker-dealer compliance activities. This is an excellent opportunity for a driven, self-motivated compliance professional with strong analytical and interpersonal skills who thrives in a fast-paced, dynamic environment and can effectively navigate shifting priorities and evolving business needs. Key Responsibilities Core Responsibilities Manage compliance reviews of sponsorships and training or education-related events, including evaluating requests for compliance with applicable cash and non-cash compensation requirements and Firm policies. Work with business partners to obtain required information and documentation and address compliance questions as they arise. Support the compliance training program by coordinating with the Firm’s dedicated Training team on training for registered representatives and supervisory principals, including Firm Element, Annual Compliance, Regulation Best Interest, and other applicable training. Assist with identifying and tracking applicable training populations and requirements, provide input on training content, and develop and deliver ad hoc compliance training for registered representatives as needed. Support the Firm’s Regulation Best Interest compliance program, including monitoring client-facing activities, documentation, and records related to potential recommendations. Perform testing to assess compliance with applicable documentation and recordkeeping requirements, with results informing broader compliance assessments and program reviews. Compile and analyze compliance data to support board and other governance reporting, including identifying trends, exceptions, and other notable developments. Perform ongoing monitoring and review of electronic communications across Firm-approved communication channels in accordance with applicable regulatory requirements and Firm policies. Support the Firm’s supervisory inspection program for OSJs, branch offices, and non-branch locations, including coordinating and conducting inspections, performing risk assessments and testing, and documenting potential findings. Assist with other areas of the Firm’s conflicts of interest compliance activities, including reviewing and monitoring matters that may present actual or potential conflicts of interest and related compliance requirements. Program Development & Strategic Initiatives Support the development and continuous improvement of the broker-dealer compliance program, working closely with members of the Compliance Department to drive consistency and operational efficiency. Leverage compliance technology and collaborate across departments to identify, implement, and enhance processes supporting pre-approval requirements, surveillance, reporting, and other compliance activities. Proactively respond to regulatory changes, business developments, and information requests arising from regulatory examinations, audits, and compliance program annual reviews. Coordinate internally to gather, review, and deliver accurate and timely responses. Manage projects to enhance broker-dealer compliance processes, including coordinating technology resources, driving timelines, managing cross-functional stakeholders and vendors, and overseeing communications to support successful delivery. Demonstrate ownership of assigned functions by developing and maintaining subject-matter expertise, staying current on applicable requirements, and proactively identifying the activities and obligations needed to manage those responsibilities effectively. Demonstrate a strong sense of integrity and behave consistently with PIMCO’s values and ethical principles. Qualifications Required A bachelor’s degree is required; a Juris Doctor (J.D.) or other relevant advanced degree is a plus. Minimum 5 years of relevant professional experience, including at least 2 years of financial services compliance experience, with direct broker-dealer compliance experience required. Demonstrated knowledge of FINRA rules and regulatory requirements applicable to broker-dealers, including those related to supervision, Regulation Best Interest, cash and non-cash compensation, electronic communications, and branch and office inspections. Excellent problem-solving skills and exceptional attention to detail, with a strong focus on accuracy in a regulatory environment. Strong written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to articulate compliance requirements clearly to technical and non-technical audiences. Service-oriented, with the ability to balance regulatory and policy standards with business needs. Highly motivated self-starter with strong organizational skills and the ability to manage competing priorities and meet strict deadlines in a fast-paced environment. Experience with compliance technology platforms supporting pre-approval workflows, electronic communications monitoring, compliance testing, and other regulatory compliance processes. Demonstrated proficiency with AI-enabled tools and the Microsoft Office Suite, including Word and Excel. Collaborative team player with the ability to work effectively across functions, global teams, and all levels of the organization, including the firm’s senior leadership. Licensing Requirements Candidates must hold, or be prepared to obtain within a reasonable period following hire, the FINRA Series 7 (General Securities Representative) and Series 24 (General Securities Principal) registrations. Preferred Background in fixed income, alternatives, or multi-asset strategies offered across registered funds, ETFs, separate accounts, and private funds.

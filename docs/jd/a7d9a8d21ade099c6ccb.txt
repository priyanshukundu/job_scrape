Job Description About Aberdeen Aberdeen Group plc is one of Europe’s largest investment companies, and we are built on a long-standing culture of caring about the future and making a positive impact. Together we invest for a better future. We do it to make a difference to the lives of our clients and customers, our employees, society, and our shareholders. Our business is structured around three distinct areas focused on our clients' changing needs: Aberdeen Investments, Aberdeen Adviser, and interactive investor. As part of Aberdeen Adviser, this role helps deliver the insight, governance and operational excellence that enable advisory businesses to thrive and supports our commitment to Invest in your tomorrow. As a Portfolio Value Lead, you will help turn this purpose into action by ensuring investment in change delivers measurable outcomes while helping colleagues and clients invest in their tomorrow. About the Department The Adviser business supports financial advisers across the UK with expertise, technology and services. The team provides portfolio oversight across resources, workforce capability and value management, ensuring strategic investment decisions are supported by robust insight. Working collaboratively across Product, Technology, Finance and PMO teams, it helps deliver sustainable outcomes that enable advisers and clients to prosper. About the Role As a Portfolio Value Lead in Edinburgh, you will ensure portfolio demand is aligned to capacity, capability and value. You will lead workforce planning, resource modelling, benefits realisation and portfolio insight, helping leaders make informed investment and prioritisation decisions. This role offers significant visibility and influence across Product, Technology, Finance and delivery teams within a blended working environment. Key Responsibilities Own and develop the portfolio resourcing model and capacity planning approach. Balance demand and capacity through effective forecasting, sequencing and resource recommendations. Lead workforce and capability planning, identifying future skills needs and capability gaps. Define and operate portfolio benefits and value management frameworks. Provide decision-grade resource, cost and value insight to support investment decisions. Partner with Finance, governance and reporting teams to align portfolio information and outcomes. About the Candidate - Essential skills Proven experience in PMO role; resource, workforce, capacity or benefits management within complex portfolios. Strong capability in workforce planning, demand management and scenario modelling. Experience defining and monitoring benefits, value metrics and realisation plans. Experience with tools including Workday, ServiceNow, Jira, SharePoint, Excel or Power BI. Desirable skills Excellent analytical and decision-support skills.

Wells Fargo is seeking a Senior Business Execution Consultant (Sr. Assistant Vice President) within the Product Risk Management & Enablement team of Wells Fargo Auto. This role will serve as a key risk management partner supporting the Auto Product, Analytics and Digital Innovation (PADI) organization, with primary responsibility for leading the execution of the Impact Analysis process. This individual will independently lead complex assessments, partner across Product, Technology, Risk, Control, Compliance, and Operations, and apply sound judgment to determine business and product impacts, root cause considerations, and appropriate disposition. The role requires the ability to challenge assumptions, influence outcomes, and translate complex issues into clear recommendations for senior leadership. In this role, you will: Lead end-to-end execution of complex Impact Analysis assessments, from intake and research through disposition, documentation, and closure. Evaluate incidents and risk events to determine product, customer, operational, compliance, financial, and control impacts. Conduct fact-finding, data analysis, and root cause evaluation to develop clear, defensible conclusions and recommendations. Challenge incomplete or unsupported conclusions and influence cross-functional stakeholders toward appropriate resolution and accountability. Facilitate disposition discussions and escalate complex matters when impact, ownership, root cause, or the appropriate path forward remains unclear. Manage the Impact Analysis pipeline, including aging, dependencies, deadlines, follow-up actions, and reporting. Develop concise, executive-ready documentation that clearly communicates the incident, root cause, business impact, resolution, and disposition. Identify trends and recurring themes across Impact Analyses and recommend opportunities to strengthen products, processes, controls, testing, or governance. Serve as a subject matter resource to business partners and peers while driving continuous improvement of the Impact Analysis process. Support broader Product Risk Management & Enablement priorities, including governance, issue management, and strategic initiatives. Required Qualifications: 4+ years of Business Execution, Implementation, or Strategic Planning experience, or equivalent demonstrated through one or a combination of the following: work experience, training, military experience, education Desired Qualifications: Experience leading complex incident, impact, risk, or root cause assessments through final disposition. Demonstrated ability to investigate ambiguous or incomplete information, challenge assumptions, and develop defensible conclusions. Experience assessing product, customer, operational, technology, compliance, or control risk within a complex or highly regulated environment. Strong understanding of root cause analysis, issue management, risk assessment, and control concepts. Ability to exercise independent judgment, influence without direct authority, and effectively navigate ambiguity. Strong analytical, written, and verbal communication skills, including the ability to translate complex information into concise recommendations for senior leadership. Experience managing multiple concurrent assessments with competing priorities, dependencies, and deadlines. Experience building partnerships across business, risk, compliance, technology, operations, and control functions. Experience identifying trends and translating findings into meaningful process, product, control, or governance improvements. Experience supporting audit, issue management, governance, or regulatory initiatives. Job Expectations: Ability to travel up to 5% of the time. This position offers a hybrid work schedule.

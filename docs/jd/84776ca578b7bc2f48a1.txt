Job Description Job Summary Software Development Engineers are expert problem-solvers and builders who design, implement, and improve software applications and systems. In this role, engineers spend approximately 60-75% of their time coding, working hands-on with code, data, and modern tools including AI-assisted development, cloud services, automation frameworks, and mobile technologies to deliver secure, scalable, and high-quality solutions that drive business outcomes in the fintech sector. This position focuses on developing innovative mobile applications, SDKs, and platform capabilities that enable secure authentication, digital identity, payment security, fraud prevention, and customer engagement experiences across Android and iOS platforms. Engineers collaborate with product managers, architects, designers, QA engineers, operations teams, and compliance partners to deliver resilient, secure, and scalable mobile solutions used by millions of consumers worldwide. All roles require digital fluency, including the ability to effectively leverage emerging technologies such as Generative AI tools (e.g., Microsoft Copilot, GitHub Copilot, ChatGPT, and Agentic Development workflows) to improve engineering productivity, software quality, and developer experience.

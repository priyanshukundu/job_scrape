Job Title: Associate Director, Patient Support Service Operations – Affordability Programs Location: Morristown, NJ About the Job As the Associate Director, Patient Support Services Affordability Programs and Partnerships within OneSanofi PSS (One Sanofi Patient Support Services) supporting the Insulins & Established Products at Sanofi, you will pivotal manage Sanofi’s copay and cash offer programs to drive consistency amongst our brands, maximize access and operating performance while ensuring compliance with laws. In this role, you will set KPIs (Key Performance Indicators), manage the execution of various copay programs and contracts for all Sanofi I&EP, be the Patient Support Program Lead for all programs, and indirectly manage internal stakeholders and vendors to ensure enhanced results. You will work closely with the other GenMeds PSS program teams, including hub and case management, field reimbursement, digital and patient engagement, and PSS operations, so affordability solutions connect seamlessly to the broader patient journey and are applied consistently across brands. The position requires an individual who possesses a proactive, customer centric, multidisciplinary approach to business management, while leveraging organization and project management expertise to support the development and implementation of programs and external partnerships. Join the team transforming how healthcare is delivered for chronic and specialty conditions worldwide. In General Medicines, you’ll help drive meaningful outcomes in diabetes, transplant, and immunology - with the scale and urgency patients deserve. About Sanofi We’re an R&D-driven, AI-powered biopharma company committed to improving people’s lives and delivering compelling growth. Our deep understanding of the immune system – and innovative pipeline – enables us to invent medicines and vaccines that treat and protect millions of people around the world. Together, we chase the miracles of science to improve people’s lives. Main Responsibilities: Program management: Own end-to-end management of the copay and cash savings programs, ensuring project plans, deliverables, budgets, and timelines are coordinated across functions and communicated clearly. GenMeds PSS partnership: Partner with GenMeds PSS program leads integrating affordability solutions into the broader support experience (e.g., first-fill copay activation, patient assistance, and hub and field touchpoints). Align on shared KPIs, handoffs, and patient communications, and share insights across programs to improve consistency and efficiency. Stakeholder partnership: Collaborate with brand and internal stakeholders on strategy, program design, and analytics. Involve all responsible stakeholders in design and evaluation, with a full view of the resources and investment needed to achieve brand objectives. Vendor management: Lead vendor management for copay programs, including RFP requirements, vendor selection, contracting, quarterly business reviews and relationship management. Evaluate vendors continually against KPIs and ensure alignment with legal and compliance requirements. Patient Support Program Lead accountability: Ensure compliance with the Company's PSP SOP during program development, before launch, and throughout each program's duration. Work with Legal, Privacy, Pharmacovigilance, Medical, and Regulatory, and ensure any service provider executes programs consistent with Company compliance policies and procedures. Content and activation: Partner with stakeholders on program content design, promotional content development, printing and distribution, and digital activities. Reporting and analytics: Establish formal reporting and enhanced in-house analytics to measure program value and inform decisions on program viability. Summarize results and performance for stakeholders. Compliance monitoring: Establish a formal process to monitor vendor performance and compliance with Federal and State Healthcare Program laws and requirements. Budget: Support development of the centralized copay program budget and brand-specific copay budgets, including administrative fees and reimbursement costs. Best practices and benchmarking: Benchmark Sanofi programs against competitors to generate insights and recommendations. Share best practices with internal stakeholders and across GenMeds PSS programs through periodic forums.

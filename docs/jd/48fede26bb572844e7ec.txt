Senior Associate Scientist - Assay Development
Regeneron
Location: TARRYTOWN
Link: https://regeneron.wd1.myworkdayjobs.com/Careers/job/TARRYTOWN/Senior-Associate-Scientist---Assay-Development_R51753

Build our future together: At Regeneron, we use science and innovation to develop life-changing medicines for people with serious diseases. We are seeking a Senior Associate Scientist to join our team, supporting our pipeline in a hybrid work mode. In this role, you will develop and validate immunoassays while collaborating with Assay Development Group scientists and study directors. This position offers the opportunity to contribute to a fast-growing, science-driven organization making a meaningful difference to patients worldwide. When & where: Tarrytown, New York On-site Discover your role: Design, execute, and troubleshoot immunoassays, including enzyme-linked immunosorbent assays, with minimal supervision. Develop immunoassay methods and write validation protocols for study director review. Perform validation experiments and prepare tabular results, reports, and procedure documents. Process samples, run assays, and analyze data under current GxP guidance. Evaluate new immunoassay technologies, formats, and techniques. Operate laboratory instruments and oversee verifications or maintenance when required. Oversee reagent preparation, laboratory supplies, cleanliness, and safety compliance. You manage multiple projects and clearly present results through reports or presentations. This role requires: Bachelor’s degree in biology or a related field and three to six years of experience. Hands-on immunoassay knowledge using multiple detection systems. Understanding of methodologies measuring proteins and immunogenicity. Knowledge of Good Laboratory Practice, FDA regulations, and company standard operating procedures. Prior bioanalytical assay development experience is highly desirable. Master’s degree is preferred.

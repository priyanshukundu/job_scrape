Job Description Specific Job Responsibilities: Research Program Support Serve as an FPD representative to assigned small-molecule Research project teams, supporting multiple programs across the discovery portfolio. Partner with Research scientists and other cross-functional colleagues to understand program objectives and identify appropriate formulation and pharmaceutical development activities to support compound evaluation and progression. Design and execute formulation and drug product studies supporting pharmacokinetic, efficacy, toxicology, and other preclinical studies. Develop fit-for-purpose formulations based on compound properties, study objectives, and stage of Research. Support assessment of physicochemical properties, formulation behavior, and other developability considerations relevant to candidate selection and nomination for clinical development. Integrate experimental results and available cross-functional data to identify formulation, drug-delivery, and development risks and recommend appropriate follow-up activities. Communicate FPD results, conclusions, and recommendations to Research project teams and other cross-functional stakeholders. Collaborate with FPD colleagues to bring appropriate technical expertise and capabilities to individual Research programs. Scientific Investigation and Problem Solving Independently design and execute moderately complex experiments and scientific investigations. Analyze experimental data, identify trends and anomalous results, develop hypotheses, and recommend appropriate follow-up experiments. Troubleshoot formulation, analytical, and experimental issues. Apply scientific literature, prior internal experience, and experimental data to develop practical solutions to formulation and drug-delivery challenges. Evaluate new experimental approaches and technologies that could improve support of Research programs. Cross-functional Collaboration and Communication Serve as an effective scientific partner to Research colleagues and contribute FPD perspectives to project discussions. Clearly communicate experimental results, conclusions, risks, and recommendations to cross-functional audiences. Prepare technical summaries, reports, presentations, and project documentation. Help facilitate knowledge transfer between Research and FPD and capture relevant learnings for application across the portfolio. Contribute to a collaborative working model between FPD and Research that enables timely and effective scientific support. Basic Qualifications: 5+ Years with BS degree in related scientific discipline. 3+ Years with MS degree in related scientific discipline. Preferred Qualifications: 6+ years of experience with BS degree in related scientific discipline. 4+ years of experience with MS degree in related scientific discipline.

Job Description This role focuses on Internal Liquidity Stress Testing (ILST), liquidity risk strategy, quantitative analysis, and emerging risk identification. The Analyst will assess how changes in the balance sheet, funding markets, customer behavior, and broader financial conditions affect the firm’s liquidity position under normal and stressed environments. The role combines financial analysis, data analytics, and risk management, with exposure to senior Treasury and Risk stakeholders. It is well suited for someone who enjoys working with data, investigating why results are changing, solving complex problems, and translating quantitative analysis into clear business insights. Key Responsibilities Support the execution, analysis, and ongoing enhancement of the firm’s Internal Liquidity Stress Testing (ILST) framework, including assumptions, scenarios, documentation, controls, and data validation. Analyze liquidity sources and uses, balance-sheet activity, and funding needs, including how changes impact liquidity metrics and stressed results. Perform scenario, sensitivity, trend, and period-over-period analysis to identify key drivers and emerging liquidity risks. Monitor balance-sheet and financial-market developments, including deposit behavior, wholesale and secured funding, collateral, and market liquidity. Forecast liquidity needs on a daily, weekly, and monthly basis to help inform funding decisions. Work with large financial and regulatory datasets to identify trends, anomalies, concentrations, and unexpected movements. Use tools such as Excel, SQL, Python, and Power BI to support analysis, reporting, and process improvements. Investigate complex or unexpected results, challenge assumptions using data, and translate findings into clear conclusions for technical and non-technical audiences. Identify opportunities to automate recurring analytics and improve the efficiency and repeatability of existing processes. Basic Qualifications Bachelor's degree, or equivalent work experience Typically two or more years of related experience Preferred Skills/Experience Strong quantitative and analytical skills with the ability to work through complex problems and investigate unexpected results. Strong Excel skills and comfort working with large datasets. Strong attention to detail and commitment to data accuracy. Ability to communicate analytical findings clearly in written and verbal formats. Intellectual curiosity, willingness to learn, strong work ethic, and ability to work effectively as part of a team. Internship, coursework, or experience in banking, Treasury, balance-sheet management/ALM, financial markets, risk management, financial analysis, or data analytics. Exposure to SQL, Python, Power BI, VBA, coding, or other analytical tools. Familiarity with bank balance sheets, deposits, securities, wholesale funding, capital markets, or liquidity risk concepts such as ILST, LCR, HQLA, and liquidity stress testing. Familiarity with regulatory or financial datasets such as FR Y-9C, FR Y-15, FR 2052a, Call Reports, or Federal Reserve H.8 is helpful but not required.

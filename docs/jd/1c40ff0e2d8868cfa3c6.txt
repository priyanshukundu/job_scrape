Senior Cosmos Research Scientist, Agentic AI for 3D and Robotics
NVIDIA
Location: US, CA, Santa Clara
Link: https://nvidia.wd5.myworkdayjobs.com/NVIDIAExternalCareerSite/job/US-CA-Santa-Clara/Senior-Cosmos-Research-Scientist--Agentic-AI-for-3D-and-Robotics_JR2030744

NVIDIA is seeking a world-class researcher to advance agentic AI for 3D and robotics on the Cosmos team. You will develop AI agents that write and execute code, build and manipulate 3D environments, and solve robotics tasks through simulation and interaction. A central focus of this role is recursive self-improvement: developing agents that can evaluate their own work, learn from failures, and improve their code, tools, and problem-solving strategies. You will investigate how generative models, coding agents, and simulation can work together to accelerate this process, with rigorous evaluation of capability gains and generalization. You will work with a team of researchers to turn new ideas into research prototypes, scale promising approaches with large datasets and compute, and collaborate with product teams to bring your research into real-world applications. What you’ll be doing: Conduct original research in agentic coding for 3D content creation, simulation, and robotics. Develop agents that generate, execute, debug, and refine code to construct 3D worlds, build simulation workflows, and solve robotics tasks. Design recursive self-improvement methods that enable agents to improve their implementations, tools, and strategies through experimentation, execution feedback, and experience. Build training and evaluation pipelines using simulated environments, synthetic data, and automated task generation. Develop benchmarks and controlled experiments to measure task success, reliability, generalization, and sustained improvement across iterations. Collaborate with researchers and product teams to scale successful methods and transfer research into products. Communicate research findings through publications, prototypes, and technical demonstrations. What we need to see: Ph.D. in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, Electrical Engineering, or a related field, or equivalent experience. 5+ years of relevant research experience. A track record of research excellence or significant technical contributions to deployed systems. Strong knowledge of deep learning, with expertise in one or more of the following: coding agents, reinforcement learning, program synthesis, generative models, computer graphics, 3D vision, or robotics. Experience building systems that connect models with executable tools, environments, or simulators. Excellent Python programming skills, proficiency in C++, and experience with modern deep learning frameworks. Experience training and evaluating large-scale models and building or curating datasets. Excellent collaboration, communication, and problem-solving skills. Ways to stand out from the crowd: Research on self-improving agents, automated discovery, code generation, or learning from execution feedback. Experience with 3D simulation, procedural environment generation, robot learning, or embodied AI. Experience designing evaluations that distinguish robust capability improvements from benchmark overfitting. Demonstrated ability to build end-to-end research systems that combine model training, tool use, simulation, and evaluation.

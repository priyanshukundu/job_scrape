Senior Deep Learning Scientist, AI Security

NVIDIA helped ignite the modern AI era through GPU accelerated deep learning. Now, we’re helping shape what comes next—and the security that makes it possible. AI will do more than respond to questions. It will use tools, navigate sophisticated environments, and take on greater responsibility in the world. Securing these capabilities is one of the defining technical challenges of our time. Join us to turn scientific discovery into security breakthroughs that help people build and use AI with greater confidence. We’re looking for Deep Learning Scientist to join our AI Security and Responsibility efforts. You’ll advance security alignment for large language models and develop guardrails that help the systems they power resist adversarial manipulation and operate within trusted boundaries. NVIDIA’s work creates opportunities to carry new ideas from experiments into practice. If you’re driven to explore how powerful systems can fail and determined to make them stronger, bring that curiosity and persistence to Nvidia. What you will be doing: Apply fundamental and applied security research to develop, train, fine-tune, and deploy large language models for multimodal agentic reasoning systems and guardrails encompassing text, audio, image, video, and tool usage. Advance post-training and alignment methods including instruction tuning, preference optimization, and RLHF/RLVR/MOPD to improve security of multimodal agents for complex use cases. Model and System Security: Research and deploy techniques beyond post-training, such as instruction hierarchy, risk detection, and new cyber gyms. Lead the collection, development, and benchmarking of multimodal security datasets, ensuring high-quality evaluation of model accuracy, safety, and task completion success. Partner with engineers, data scientists, research teams, and product managers across NVIDIA to scale solutions for LLM security and agentic safety. What we need to see: Master’s degree (or equivalent experience) or PhD in Computer Science, AI, or Applied Math with 8+ years of relevant work experience. Excellent programming skills in Python with strong fundamentals in scalable model development and deep learning frameworks like PyTorch. Strong knowledge of ML/DL techniques and modern foundation model architectures, including Transformers and mixture-of-experts models. Core Safety Expertise: 1+ years of dedicated experience or research in at least one of the following areas: LLM Security: Focus on backdoors, data poisoning, latent malicious behavior, and structural model vulnerabilities. Frontier Risks: advanced alignment challenges, including model deception, manipulation, and loss-of-control scenarios. Agentic Safety: Focus on LLM-level security for autonomous systems, including multi-turn tool-calling, orchestration, and execution risks. Multi-turn Security Evaluation: Focus on robust, scalable automated evaluation methodologies for conversational and iterative multi-turn use cases. Hands-on experience in post-training multimodal models for multimodal reasoning. Ways to stand out from the crowd: Strong record of publications in top-tier AI and machine learning venues such as NeurIPS, ICML, ICLR, CVPR, LLMSEC, OMMM, or WAITI Community Contributions: Active contributions to open-source AI safety tools, benchmarks, datasets, or models. Multilingual Security: Experience with multilingual AI security. Experience with auto red-teaming.

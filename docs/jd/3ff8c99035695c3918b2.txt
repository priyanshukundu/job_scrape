Who You’ll Work With Institutional Markets (IM) is the division within Corebridge Financial that manages funded and unfunded business with institutional clients. The Global Assumed Reinsurance business is a core part of IM and partners with institutional clients to deliver risk-transfer solutions across life insurance, annuity and pension products. The business combines disciplined underwriting, structuring expertise, balance-sheet strength and global asset sourcing to generate attractive risk-adjusted spread earnings. Asset-intensive reinsurance is a central focus of the growth strategy in Asia. About The Role We are seeking a detail-oriented, analytical and highly organized Business Analyst to support the Head of Sales, Asia and the regional Global Assumed Reinsurance team . R eporting to the Head of Sales, Asia and working closely with the Associate Project Manager, this role will deliver business analysis and reporting, support client and internal meeting preparation, and manage the day-to-day administration and logistics that keep the regional business running smoothly. The ideal candidate is proactive and agile, enjoys working with data and information, and is comfortable supporting a fast-paced, globally distributed team across multiple time zones. This is an excellent opportunity for an early-career professional to build expertise in reinsurance and institutional markets while gaining exposure to senior leaders and clients in Japan and other Asian markets. Responsibilities: Data and Reporting: Gather, organize and analyze business data to identify trends, patterns and areas for improvement. Prepare regular reports and dashboards on client activity, pipeline status and regional business performance. Pipeline and Client Information Support: Maintain client, intermediary and opportunity records in the CRM and tracking tools, ensuring information is accurate, complete and up to date. Strategic Initiatives: Collaborate with the Associate Project Manager and the broader team on regional strategic initiatives, and track progress, action items and deadlines through status reporting. Market Research: Conduct research on Japanese and other Asian life insurers, competitors, industry trends and regulatory developments. Prepare concise summaries and background materials for the Head of Sales, Asia. Meeting Preparation: Support client meetings, internal meetings and management reviews in a proactive, agile manner based on priorities. Prepare presentations, briefing books, agendas and meeting notes, and track follow-up actions. Calendar Management: Manage calendar logistics and meeting scheduling across multiple time zones, including finalizing and distributing meeting materials. Travel Management: Manage travel arrangements for the Head of Sales, Asia and team members, including air and ground transportation, hotels, client dinners, social events and expense reports. Events and Visitor Support: Support logistics for industry conferences, client events and visiting delegations, including venue arrangements, attendee lists, materials and follow-up. In-Office Management: Provide administrative support for day-to-day operations, including correspondence, document filing, supplies, printing, invoice processing and vendor coordination. Collaboration: Establish strong relationships throughout the organization and partner with executive assistants, IT Executive Support, Facilities and other team members. Organizational Agility: Navigate the organization effectively by learning and mastering internal systems, protocols and culture. Skills and Qualification s 2 plus years of professional experience in the financial services or insurance industry required Proven experience in business analysis and/or administrative support, preferably at an executive level. Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to turn data into clear, actionable insights. Solid organizational and time management skills, with keen attention to detail. Excellent written and verbal communication skills in English. Understanding of the Japanese language and business culture is a plus. Strong interest in Japan and the broader Asia region, with a genuine passion for cross-cultural exchange and international business. Basic understanding of, or willingness to learn about, life insurance, annuities and reinsurance; prior exposure to institutional markets is a plus. High degree of discretion and professionalism in handling confidential information. Highly proficient in Microsoft Office applications (Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook, Teams, SharePoint and Visio). Willingness to work flexible hours across multiple time zones, including early-morning and evening calls with Japan and other Asian markets, and to travel occasionally as business needs require. Bachelor’s degree in business administration, finance, economics or a related field required. Analytical and intellectually curious, eager to grow in a global business. Highly organized and dependable, with strong follow-through. Service-oriented and agile, adapting smoothly to shifting priorities in a fast-paced environment. Culturally aware and comfortable working with colleagues and counterparts across geographies. Proactive, assertive and highly responsive, taking initiative, anticipating needs, reacting quickly to requests, and confidently voicing ideas, concerns and recommendations in a constructive and respectful manner. Collaborative team player who contributes to a culture of teamwork, innovation and inclusion.

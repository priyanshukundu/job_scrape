About the Opportunity We are looking for a seasoned engineer to build and improve the ML and data infrastructure behind our risk and fraud prevention systems. You will work hands-on to move models from experimentation into reliable production services, improve model execution performance, and develop data pipelines that support training, evaluation, and real-time decisions. You will take ownership of your work, identify improvements proactively, and deliver with limited guidance What You’ll Be Doing Improve model inference latency, throughput, resource utilization, and cost. Profile the prediction path—from feature retrieval and preprocessing to model execution—and implement measurable improvements while preserving model quality. Build and maintain infrastructure for model packaging, versioning, deployment, serving, monitoring, and rollback across cloud platforms and regions. Develop and optimize offline data pipelines using Databricks, Apache Spark , and warehouse or lakehouse technologies. Address data modeling, partitioning, joins, shuffles, skew, incremental processing, and cost. Work on offline and online feature pipelines, supporting point-in-time correctness, consistent feature definitions, freshness, lineage, and reproducible backfills. Build and maintain reliable feature services and data integrations for risk models and decision systems. Take ownership of projects and operational issues, communicate progress and tradeoffs, and collaborate with ML, data engineering, cloud infrastructure, security, and product teams. Contribute to technical discussions, share knowledge, and help teammates through code reviews and collaboration. What We Look For In You Experience building and operating distributed systems, ML infrastructure, or data platforms, with ownership of meaningful production components. Hands-on experience improving production model performance, such as latency, throughput, resource utilization, or cost. Strong experience with Databricks and Spark, or comparable offline data platforms, including execution plans, distributed query optimization, storage layouts, data correctness, and workload cost management. Experience with the model lifecycle, including training and serving integration, validation, deployment, observability, and rollback. Strong Python and SQL skills, plus proficiency in at least one backend or systems language such as Java, Scala, Go, or C++. Ability to work independently through loosely defined problems, make sound implementation decisions, and deliver maintainable systems with limited guidance. Nice to Haves SageMaker, Alibaba Cloud PAI/EAS, or comparable ML platforms. Inference optimization using tools such as ONNX Runtime, TensorRT, or Triton. Delta Lake, MLflow, feature stores, Kafka, Flink, and low-latency data stores. Fraud detection or financial risk systems.

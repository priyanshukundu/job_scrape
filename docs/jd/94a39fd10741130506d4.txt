Reporting to the Senior Vice President and Chief Information Technology Officer, the Director, IT Portfolio Governance and Vendor Management serves as the strategic bridge between business demand, IT capacity, financial investments, and project delivery. They establish and maintain governance frameworks, drive prioritization, manage resource allocation, and provide executive visibility into portfolio health, risk, and value realization. This position leads the delivery of the enterprise technology portfolio, sets investment and intake guardrails, and ensures IT projects align directly with business strategy, capacity constraints, and financial targets. They work with the other IT areas to ensure the assignment and mobilization of all necessary technology resources are coordinated and integrated to support the timely and cost-effective delivery of services to the business areas. This position is also responsible for development of, training on, and oversight and compliance with all portfolio delivery methodologies (i.e., Waterfall, Agile, etc.) used in the delivery of projects and other work types. This includes the development, delivery and adherence to Testing and Quality Assurance standards, Enterprise Monitoring standards, and Robotics Process Automation standards in support of the delivery of IT services to ensure quality delivery and monitoring of technology solutions. In collaboration with Corporate Finance, the Director, Portfolio Governance and Vendor Management is responsible for capital and operating expense reporting. This includes ensuring project approvals are done in compliance with company policies and regulatory requirements. It also includes ensuring compliance with company delegation of authority (DOA) approvals. The Director will also lead the responses to all IT regulatory requests and audits in support of regulatory proceedings. In collaboration with Procurement, the Director, Portfolio Governance and Vendor Management is responsible for overseeing the full lifecycle of technology agreements, software licenses, cloud/SaaS subscriptions, hardware vendors, and IT service providers. This includes coordinating with the CIO and other IT areas around strategy, acquisition, and management of technology suppliers, software licenses, hardware, and IT services.

Role Summary: Job Title : Senior Data Scientist - Strategic Pricing Job Duties: Lead strategic pricing initiatives by partnering with business leadership to define pricing strategy, governance, and competitive positioning; drive cross-functional alignment across Marketing, Food and Beverages, Finance, Film and Operations teams. Develop and enhance pricing models including price elasticity, demand forecasting, promotions, and revenue optimization, to maximize revenue, margin, and total guest spend. Quantify and forecast pricing impact on key metrics including attendance, Average Ticket Price, Food & Beverages performance, and overall profitability to inform enterprise decision-making. Design and execute experiments including A/B tests and pilots, and apply causal inference methods to measure incremental impact and isolate true pricing effects on customer behavior and revenue. Leverage advanced Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning (AI/ML), including Large Language Model (LLM) based workflows and Generative AI integration, to build scalable pricing frameworks, including scoring, prioritization, and decision-support tools that drive consistent, data-driven pricing actions. Translate analytics into actionable insights through dashboards, data storytelling, and executive communication to influence strategy and enable adoption across stakeholders. Some telecommuting is allowed, 1 day per week. Minimum Requirements: Master’s degree in Statistics, Economics, Business Analytics, or related field and five (5) years of experience in data analysis. Experience must include: AI/ML modeling such as tree-based models, time-series, API integration and consumption and building end-to-end Machine Learning lifecycle; Business and pricing strategy, revenue optimization, and pricing data science; Pricing analytics including price elasticity modeling, promotion effectiveness measurement, revenue forecasting, and financial impact quantification; Customer segmentation, competitive analytics, and market insights including analyzing competitor pricing, public company disclosures, market positioning, customer feedback, and market share trends to identify revenue growth opportunities; Pricing experimentation and causal inference using A/B testing; Statistical techniques including complex regression modeling such as multivariate, and mixed-effects models, correlation, and hypothesis testing; Applying AI/ML to business problems, including Clustering, AI-driven scoring, prioritization models, LLM-based sentiment analysis, explainable AI, Natural Language Processing (NLP), and text analytics to derive insights; Computational programming and analytics using SQL, Alteryx, Python including Pandas, scikit-learn, PyTorch, and TensorFlow; Azure or Fabric to lead end-to-end model development and deployment; Building data visualizations and executive-facing dashboards using Power BI and Streamlit; and Must also possess Alteryx Designer Core Certification.

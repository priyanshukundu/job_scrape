Analyst / Associate, Derivatives Risk Solutions Americas (Corporate Interest Rate Solutions) The Role Mizuho is seeking a Senior Analyst or Associate to join Derivatives Risk Solutions Americas (DRSA), a team of salespeople and structurers that advises global corporations and financial sponsors on interest rate and foreign exchange risk. This seat sits within the team’s corporate coverage effort and is focused primarily on interest rate solutions: helping corporate treasury and finance teams manage the rate risk that comes with their balance sheets, their debt financings and their strategic transactions. DRSA works where the bank’s lending, capital markets and M&A relationships meet the trading desk. A corporate client refinancing a term loan, pre-funding a bond issue, closing an acquisition or reshaping its fixed/floating mix needs a hedge that fits its financing, its accounting and its risk appetite. The team’s job is to spot those needs early, design the right solution, price it competitively with trading, and see it through execution and the life of the trade. The team runs a lean, entrepreneurial model with a high bar. Junior team members are not confined to one lane: you will work directly with senior marketers on live pitches and transactions, build and price structures yourself, join client meetings, and over time take on coverage responsibility of your own. The role is client-facing and commercial. It suits someone who wants to grow into a derivatives marketer, not a pure modeler or risk analyst. This version of the role is weighted toward corporate interest rate products, but we welcome candidates from across the derivatives solutions universe (sponsor and project finance hedging, corporate FX solutions, macro structuring) who want to build depth in corporate rates. Level will be set at Senior Analyst or Associate depending on experience. Key Responsibilities Client coverage and idea generation Partner with senior DRSA marketers to cover a portfolio of corporate clients, building a working understanding of each client’s capital structure, debt maturities, hedging policy and existing derivatives positions. Track client financing activity (new issuance, refinancings, M&A, maturities) with coverage colleagues in corporate banking, debt capital markets and leveraged finance, so that hedging needs are identified before mandates are awarded. Prepare client-ready pitch materials, hedging analyses and recommendations; present them in client meetings and, as experience builds, lead technical discussions with treasury teams. Produce market commentary on interest rates (and FX where relevant), covering Fed policy, curve shape, swap spreads, SOFR dynamics and volatility, and turn it into actionable ideas for clients. Structuring, pricing and analytics Model, structure and price interest rate derivatives, including vanilla, forward-starting, amortizing and accreting swaps; caps, floors and collars; swaptions; treasury locks and rate locks; cross-currency swaps; and deal-contingent hedges linked to M&A and financing. Build hedging analysis for clients: debt portfolio reviews, fixed/floating mix optimization, scenario and sensitivity analysis, breakevens, carry and mark-to-market projections. Work alongside rates trading to price transactions, understand the risk and costs the desk takes on (credit, funding and capital charges), and optimize structures for both the client and the bank. Assess hedge accounting (ASC 815) and documentation considerations with accounting, credit and legal partners, and explain the implications clearly to clients. Execution and lifecycle management Support transactions end to end: pricing runs, term sheets, client onboarding, ISDA/CSA and credit approval coordination, execution and confirmation. Manage existing client positions after trade: periodic mark-to-market reporting, restructuring and unwind analysis, novations and rollover opportunities. Take ownership of the operational and administrative work that keeps a lean team running, including pipeline tracking, CRM, trade records and regulatory and compliance requirements. Team and platform Contribute to the team’s pricing templates, pitch libraries and analytics tools, including automation and AI-enabled workflows where they make the team faster. Collaborate across DRSA’s corporate, sponsor/project finance and FX efforts, and with colleagues in EMEA, LatAm and Asia on cross-border clients. Required Experience and Background 2 to 5 years in a front-office or client-facing derivatives role at an investment bank, commercial bank or hedging advisory firm. The most relevant backgrounds are corporate derivatives marketing or risk solutions, sponsor and project finance hedging, corporate FX solutions, macro structuring on the liability side, and rates sales. Solid technical grounding in interest rate derivatives: swap pricing and valuation, curve mechanics, DV01, mark-to-market and option basics. Candidates should be able to reason through MTM and risk questions quickly and intuitively, including mental math, and should expect technical problems as part of the interview process. Strong quantitative aptitude combined with the ability to explain complex structures simply to CFOs, treasurers and finance teams. Advanced Excel modeling and PowerPoint skills; working knowledge of Bloomberg pricing and market data tools. Bachelor’s degree in Finance, Economics, Mathematics, Engineering, Statistics or a related field. SIE, Series 7 and Series 63, or the ability to obtain them within a set period after joining. Preferred Experience covering corporate clients, with an understanding of corporate debt structures (term loans, bonds, credit agreement hedging requirements) and how financing events drive hedging. Exposure to deal-contingent hedging, pre-issuance hedging and M&A-linked risk management. Working knowledge of hedge accounting under ASC 815. FX hedging experience, including cross-currency swaps, for multinational corporates. Programming or automation skills (Python, VBA) or practical experience using AI tools to speed up analysis and pitch work. Language skills relevant to the bank’s client base (for example Japanese, Spanish or Portuguese). Progress toward the CFA or a quantitative Master’s degree. What we look for Commercial drive and a genuine interest in client-facing derivatives work, not simply a move away from a current role. An ownership mindset: willingness to do whatever a lean, high-performing team needs, from pricing a live trade to the operational details behind it. Composure and accuracy under pressure in live deal environments. A collaborative style and good judgment working across sales, trading, banking and control functions.

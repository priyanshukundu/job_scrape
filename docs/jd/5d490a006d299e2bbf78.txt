Job title : Senior Associate Scientist, Analytical Sciences Location: Waltham, MA About the job As a Senior Associate Scientist, Analytical Sciences, you will participate in laboratory activities in support of process development and industrialization, and ensure the characterization of mRNA vaccines in development. Lab activities include, but are not limited to, the development, qualification, and execution of analytical methods related to immunology, including immunochemical methods (e.g., ELISA, Flow Cytometry), molecular assays, and cell-based assays, to assess the identity, potency, and biological activity of mRNA-based products and related impurities. Join the engine of Sanofi’s mission — where deep immunoscience meets bold, AI-powered research. In R&D, you’ll drive breakthroughs that could turn the impossible into possible for millions. About Sanofi: We’re an R&D-driven, AI-powered biopharma company committed to improving people’s lives and delivering compelling growth. Our deep understanding of the immune system – and innovative pipeline – enables us to invent medicines and vaccines that treat and protect millions of people around the world and are supported and enhanced through our diverse portfolio of medical device and diagnostic products. Together, we chase the miracles of science to improve people’s lives. About You Basic Qualifications: Bachelor's degree in Biology, Biochemistry, Biotechnology, or a closely related scientific discipline with 4+ years of professional experience in R&D and/or QC analytics within the vaccine or pharmaceutical industry. OR Master's degree in Biology, Biochemistry, Biotechnology, or a closely related scientific discipline with 2+ years of professional experience in R&D and/or QC analytics within the vaccine or pharmaceutical industry. Preferred Qualifications: Technical Skills Practical experience or knowledge of bioanalytical techniques (ELISA, Western Blot, qPCR/ddPCR, Cell Culture, Flow Cytometry etc.) Hands-on experience in high throughput automate analytics is a plus. Good knowledge of analysis software (SoftMax, Prism/JMP, and Flowjo etc.) GMP experience is a plus. Soft Skills Demonstrated ability to work independently as well as part of a team to complete assignments within defined time constraints. Excellent verbal and written communication skills Essential qualities include ability to work in a fast paced, matrix environment; agility to respond to urgent requests, and initiative to lead new tasks. Ability to work full time onsite in an analytical lab environment. Main Responsibilities: Participate in the selection, development of new analytical methods of mRNA products in collaboration with the scientists of the team Participate in qualification and transferring of analytical methods Perform analytical testing in the lab, data analysis, and related document writing with a critical look at the relevance of the results. Develop and write work plans/protocols Summarize and communicate the progress of your work Participate in laboratory automation to set up high-throughput analyses Accompany and train newcomers in analytical technologies and methods Participate laboratory organization activities (orders, management/maintenance of equipment, reception & verification of reagents and receipt of samples) Participate in or lead analytical working groups Participate in GMP and non-GMP lab set up and continuous improvement of laboratory safety and quality compliance. Perform GMP sample collection, analysis, and data verification. Compliant to Sanofi Quality standards and HSE policy.

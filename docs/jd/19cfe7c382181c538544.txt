The Role: A great entry point into quantitative finance for a motivated recent graduate or final-year student (Master's, Ph.D. or strong Bachelor's) with a solid quantitative and programming background. You will support the Fundamental Review of the Trading Book (FRTB) implementation and analytics for our Exotics desks, working alongside senior quants, traders, market risk managers and technology partners to build, test and improve regulatory models, capital calculations and pricing libraries. What you will do: Library development: Help build and maintain the C++ libraries behind exotics pricing, risk and regulatory calculations. FRTB delivery: Contribute to modelling and implementing FRTB frameworks (SA, IMA, NMRF, DRC) across exotic products. Numerical methods: Implement and benchmark techniques such as Monte Carlo, PDE solvers, trees/lattices and sensitivities/AAD. Collaboration: Partner with traders, structurers, risk quants and technology to integrate models and roll them into production. Quality & documentation: Write unit tests, regression suites, performance benchmarks and clear technical documentation. WHAT WE'RE LOOKING FOR · Programming: Comfortable coding in C++ and/or Python. A genuine interest in high-performance C++ matters more than being an expert today — we will help you grow your skills on the job. Tools: Exposure to Python (NumPy, SciPy, Pandas), Git, Linux and build systems like CMake is a plus. Numerical skills: Some hands-on experience implementing numerical algorithms, linear algebra or simulations — from coursework, projects or internships. Mathematics: Good grounding in probability, statistics, stochastic calculus, linear algebra and numerical analysis. Finance (nice to have): Interest in or academic exposure to derivative pricing and market-risk concepts (FRTB, VaR, Expected Shortfall, Greeks). YOU'LL THRIVE HERE IF YOU ARE ·Analytical and detail-oriented, with a strong problem-solving mindset. Curious and eager to learn complex products and regulation. A clear communicator who can explain quantitative ideas simply. A collaborative team player who enjoys a fast-paced, global environment. EDUCATION PhD preferred in a STEM discipline, including but not limited to Mathematics, Statistics, Physics, Computer Science, Data Science, Machine Learning, Artificial Intelligence, Operations Research, Quantitative Finance, Econometrics, Engineering, or a related quantitative field.

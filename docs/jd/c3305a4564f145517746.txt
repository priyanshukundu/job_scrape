Job Description Role Summary: The Associate Director, Healthcare Quality Improvement (AD, HQI), Quality Insights & External Response, position resides in the Value & Implementation (V&I) organization, which includes Global Medical and Scientific Affairs as well as Outcomes Research. The AD, HQI, Quality Insights & External Response, is a core member of the Healthcare Quality Improvement Center of Excellence (HQI CoE) and reports directly to the HQI CoE Team Lead. The AD, HQI, Quality Insights & External Response, executes the monitoring, assessment and development of external responses related to healthcare quality, including quality measurement, standards, accreditation, quality improvement, and other priority healthcare quality and policy topics, in alignment with the HQI CoE external response strategy. The AD, HQI, Quality Insights & External Response, partners cross-functionally to assess the implications of external healthcare quality developments, translate insights into organizational perspectives and positions, and contribute to coordinated, evidence-based external responses. The AD, HQI, Quality Insights & External Response, engages with assigned external organizations to build relationships, gather and translate stakeholder insights, and support initiatives and partnerships aligned with HQI priorities. Responsibilities and Primary Activities External Response and Insights: Executes the HQI CoE external-response capability, including monitoring, evaluating and prioritizing U.S. healthcare quality public commenting opportunities, proposed quality measures, standards, accreditation requirements, and other relevant developments, in alignment with the HQI CoE external response strategy. Drafts evidence-based positions, public comments, and other external responses within established HQI CoE strategy and guidance, synthesizing scientific evidence, healthcare quality perspectives, and cross-functional stakeholder input. Coordinates cross-functional input and facilitates appropriate review and approval of external responses, partnering with U.S. Policy on rulemaking-related opportunities and supporting finalization and submission through appropriate organizational pathways. Makes decisions within defined parameters and established HQI CoE processes; aligns with the HQI CoE Team Lead and other leadership on broader, higher-impact or higher-risk decisions, including external positions and engagement priorities. Evaluates the implications of evolving quality measures, standards, and policy for healthcare stakeholders and quality improvement, translating external developments into actionable insights to inform HQI CoE, V&I organization, and therapeutic-area team planning. Maintains processes, resources, and a repository of organizational comments and positions to promote consistency, efficiency, and knowledge-sharing. External Engagement: Builds and maintains relationships with assigned priority healthcare quality organizations, measure developers, professional medical societies, and other relevant external stakeholders. Represents the HQI CoE in assigned external meetings, working groups, and stakeholder engagements, consistent with HQI CoE priorities and approved positions. Executes engagement plans for assigned organizations in alignment with HQI CoE priorities and in coordination with relevant internal stakeholders and therapeutic-area teams. Gathers and translates external stakeholder insights to understand the implications of evolving quality measures, standards, and policy for healthcare delivery and support quality improvement and implementation efforts. Manages defined components of strategic external partnerships, in alignment with HQI CoE leadership, that advance healthcare quality, quality improvement, and implementation priorities. Other: Supports vendor, budget, and resource management related to assigned activities. Maintains knowledge of healthcare quality trends, quality measurement, policy developments, and relevant internal and external requirements. Ensures activities comply with applicable Legal, Compliance, regulatory, and company requirements. Contributes to HQI CoE strategic planning, impact reporting, and continuous improvement. Required Qualifications, Skills and Experience Minimum: BA/BS degree in life sciences, public health, health policy, healthcare administration, health services research, or a related field, with seven or more years of relevant experience; or an advanced degree with five or more years of relevant experience. Demonstrated experience developing or coordinating evidence-based organizational positions, public comments, external responses, quality-measure feedback, policy submissions, or similar external-facing materials. Strong understanding of the U.S. healthcare quality environment, including quality measurement, quality improvement, and relevant healthcare policy. Demonstrated ability to interpret and synthesize complex scientific, clinical, quality, or policy information into clear recommendations and external-facing materials. Strong strategic-thinking, communication, and project-management skills, with demonstrated ability to lead cross-functional initiatives and influence without direct authority. Ability to work effectively in a complex, matrixed organization and manage multiple priorities within externally defined timelines. Preferred: Advanced degree, such as MPH, MHA, MBA, MS, PhD, PharmD, MD, or other relevant graduate or healthcare professional degree. Experience conducting external landscape monitoring and assessments to identify emerging healthcare quality trends, opportunities, and strategic implications. Experience with U.S. healthcare quality programs, including Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS) quality programs, value-based initiatives, health employment data information set (HEDIS), quality measurement, or accreditation. Experience developing and managing cross-functional processes to gather, reconcile and incorporate stakeholder input into external responses or organizational positions. Experience in healthcare quality improvement, healthcare delivery, or clinical practice, with demonstrated ability to translate changes in healthcare policy, quality measures, standards, or guidelines into implications for clinical practice and quality improvement. Experience working with healthcare quality organizations, measure developers, professional medical societies, government agencies, or policy organizations. Experience partnering with Medical Affairs, Policy, Legal, Compliance, Outcomes Research, or related functions within the pharmaceutical or healthcare industry.

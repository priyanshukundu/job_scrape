About the Role Northwestern Mutual is seeking a Software Engineer II to join our Underwriting Technology team and help modernize the systems that support our underwriting experience. In this role, you will design, build, test, and support software solutions as the team continues moving Disability Insurance (DI) capabilities from legacy applications to the newer underwriting platform. A major focus for the team is DI Foundational work and the migration of DI cases from Workbench to the underwriting platform, while continuing to maintain the applications and services that support today's underwriting processes. You will work across backend and frontend technologies, including Java, Spring Boot, Node, React, and JavaScript, to deliver reliable and scalable solutions. You will collaborate closely with engineers, product partners, and underwriting stakeholders throughout the software development lifecycle. This role is ideal for an engineer who enjoys solving technical problems, learning across the technology stack, and contributing to the modernization of business-critical applications. What You Will Do Design, develop, test, deploy, and support software applications that enable underwriting operations. Contribute to DI Foundational work and the migration of Disability Insurance capabilities from legacy applications to the underwriting platform. Develop and maintain applications and services using technologies including Java, Spring Boot, Node, React, and JavaScript. Integrate applications with APIs, databases, and internal and external data sources. Collaborate with engineers, product partners, underwriting stakeholders, and other cross-functional teams to understand requirements and deliver effective technical solutions. Troubleshoot application and production issues, identify root causes, and implement sustainable solutions. Develop unit, integration, and end-to-end tests that support software quality and reliability. Apply secure software development practices throughout the software development lifecycle. Create and maintain technical documentation for applications, integrations, and development processes. Participate in Agile development practices including planning, development, testing, and continuous improvement. Share knowledge and collaborate with other engineers to improve development practices and team capabilities. What You Will Bring to the Role Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or equivalent experience. 2-4 years of professional software engineering or software development experience. Experience developing applications using JavaScript, Node, React, or similar technologies. Knowledge of Java and Spring Boot. Experience working across the software development lifecycle within Agile or Scrum environments. Familiarity with cloud platforms such as AWS or Azure and microservices architecture. Experience working with APIs, SQL, NoSQL databases, ETL processes, or other data integration technologies. Strong software and technical problem-solving skills, including troubleshooting and root-cause analysis. Ability to learn new technologies, systems, and business domains and apply critical thinking to technical challenges. Strong communication skills with the ability to collaborate with both technical and non-technical partners. Ability to work effectively within a collaborative, cross-functional engineering environment. Nice to Have Experience with Python or C#. Experience within insurance, financial services, underwriting, or another regulated environment. Familiarity with insurance underwriting processes, terminology, applications, or platforms. Experience supporting application modernization or legacy system migrations. Familiarity with DevOps and CI/CD practices. Experience with automated testing frameworks. Knowledge of insurance regulatory or compliance requirements.

Work Flexibility: Hybrid or Onsite What You Will Do – Lead Integrated Business Planning Facilitate monthly IBP/IBPR activities across assigned business units, ensuring risks, opportunities, assumptions, and decisions are clearly identified and addressed. Bring together demand, supply, commercial, operational, and financial inputs to create an integrated view of the business and support leadership decisions. Lead Regional Requirements Meetings to align demand signals, regional needs, and supply-planning inputs. Prepare and present clear, executive-ready insights, recommendations, and actions. Drive Cross-Functional Alignment Partner with Commercial, Demand Planning, Supply Planning, Finance, Marketing, Manufacturing, and regional teams to align business priorities and planning assumptions. Facilitate fact-based discussions around supply-demand trade-offs, business risks, and financial commitments. Build alignment across global stakeholders and drive accountability for decisions and follow-up actions. Manage Supply, Risk & Product Lifecycle Develop allocation recommendations during supply constraints, balancing customer needs, revenue, inventory, and business priorities. Communicate supply risks, recovery plans, and mitigation strategies and coordinate actions to minimize business impact. Lead Last Time Buy, phase-in/phase-out, end-of-life, and other product lifecycle planning activities. Support inventory decisions that minimize excess and obsolete inventory while maintaining customer commitments. Deliver Insights & Execute Analyze demand, supply, inventory, service, and financial data to identify trends, risks, and opportunities. Support scenario planning and supply-demand balancing and recommend actions based on business impact. Provide hands-on planning and analytical support as needed to keep the monthly planning cycle moving.

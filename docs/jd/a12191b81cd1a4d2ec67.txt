The opportunity EY has curated a dynamic ecosystem of Alliance and Ecosystem relationships which creates new forms of customer value to help our clients rapidly and digitally transform. Our ecosystem consists of more than 100 relationships which allow us to provide a wide range of collaborative opportunities and solutions to our clients. We help them tap into the latest technologies to achieve transformational outcomes and be more agile, innovative, resilient and better equipped to respond to disruptive change. The BMC function has an important role to play in helping to achieve EY’s ambition to become the leading global professional services organization. We work hand in hand with the business to take services and solutions to market; bring our purpose, building a better working world, to life for our people, clients and communities; engage our people and build a high performing culture; and to build the commercial value and emotional resonance of the EY brand. The BMC Global Alliance Strategic Activations Lead is focused on supporting key marketing and communications programs and campaign initiatives that span the strategic alliances and wider ecosystem. The role is critical to support the positioning of EY as the master of unlocking ecosystem value and opportunity for clients. This role will be responsible for managing a consistent approach and delivery of our strategic programs and enabling their activation in the 10 EY regions globally. The role combines collaboration with strategy and execution to deliver programs that engage buyers, create demand and support revenue growth. In this role within the Global Alliances and Ecosystem marketing team, you will be working with, and reporting into, the Global Alliances and Ecosystem BMC leader. You will also work across the Global Alliances marketing team to support the marketing efforts for our strategic alliances. The role will have one direct report and also needs to work with the wider EY Global BMC team to facilitate delivery of multi-channel initiatives, collaborating with Industry and Service Line as needed.

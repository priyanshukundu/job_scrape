General Summary: Mainframe expertise still powers some of the most important technology in our business, and we’re looking for someone who wants to help shape what comes next. As a Software Engineer II supporting Colonial Life, you’ll be hands-on with the mainframe applications that power our go-to-market business. From COBOL, JCL, VSAM, and DB2 to batch and online processing, you’ll use your technical expertise to enhance critical applications, troubleshoot complex challenges, and deliver new functionality. And this role isn’t only about maintaining what exists today. As we explore longer-term modernization opportunities, you’ll have the chance to bring your experience, curiosity, and ideas to how our mainframe environment continues to evolve. Job Specifications: Bachelor’s degree in computer science, information technology, or a related discipline preferred, or an equivalent combination of education and relevant experience. Four or more years of software development experience, including hands-on experience supporting mainframe applications. Strong working experience with: COBOL, JCL, VSAM and DB2 Experience supporting both batch and online mainframe processing. Experience with either CICS or IMS. Experience with both is not required. Ability to understand and work within a broader mainframe ecosystem, including application dependencies and data flows. Experience designing, coding, testing, troubleshooting, and supporting software in a production environment. Experience working with Agile development practices and collaborating within a cross-functional team. Strong analytical, problem-solving, written communication, and verbal communication skills. Ability to work with limited oversight, manage assigned priorities, and deliver high-quality software. Principal Duties and Responsibilities: Design, develop, enhance, implement, and maintain Colonial Life mainframe applications. Develop and maintain COBOL programs, copybooks, JCL, VSAM structures, and DB2 components across batch and online processing environments. Analyze business and technical requirements to determine appropriate solution designs and implementation approaches. Collaborate with team members, architects, and business partners to deliver solutions that meet functional, technical, and performance expectations. Write, configure, unit test, troubleshoot, document, and maintain software using established standards, processes, and tools. Estimate the effort required to complete assigned work and communicate risks, dependencies, and potential delivery impacts. Participate in controlled production releases and provide post-release support, including issue analysis, troubleshooting, and defect resolution. Analyze complex systems, data flows, integrations, and dependencies across applications. Contribute to technical documentation and knowledge sharing to support long-term application maintainability. Work with Principal Software Engineers and architects to apply coding standards, development practices, tools, and frameworks. Support modernization efforts when applicable, including APIs, service-based integrations, and proof-of-concept work related to the mainframe environment. Demonstrate a willingness to learn unfamiliar tools, technologies, and areas of the application through self-study and hands-on experience.

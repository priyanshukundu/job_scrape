FHLB San Francisco

Job Description: Bring your organizational skills, attention to detail, and interest in data and process improvement to FHLBank San Francisco. As an Administrative Operations Coordinator supporting Credit and Collateral Risk Management (CCRM), you’ll help the department maintain accurate information, execute operational controls, and coordinate work across business and technology teams. Your responsibilities will combine advanced administrative support with reporting, process documentation, testing, and project coordination. This role is well suited to someone who enjoys working with data, keeping complex activities organized, and finding practical ways to improve how work gets done. What you’ll do Support accurate data and reporting. Maintain departmental databases, business records, and customer profiles. Process membership and counterparty portfolio changes, reconcile information, and prepare recurring operational, credit agreement, control, and regulatory reports. Contribute to credit and collateral operations. Gather source data, maintain customer files, compile reporting inputs, and support analysts with risk monitoring, research distribution, and compliance activities. Document and improve processes. Maintain procedures, workflows, job aids, reporting tools, and process inventories. Identify opportunities to improve efficiency, strengthen controls, and reduce manual work for process-owner review. Support system and operational changes. Help gather and document business requirements, coordinate stakeholder follow-up, and support user acceptance testing by recording results, tracking defects, and coordinating resolution. Coordinate departmental business activities. Support vendor requests, purchase requisitions, disbursements, expense tracking, records retention, and business unit onboarding. Support leadership and committee operations. Schedule meetings, prepare management and committee materials, coordinate Credit Committee and Credit Review Working Group attendance, track action items, and prepare minutes for review. Handle sensitive information with care. Maintain appropriate controls over confidential business records and regulatory materials in accordance with Bank policies and procedures. What you’ll bring Strong Microsoft Office skills, including Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook, and SharePoint or comparable collaboration tools. Strong Excel capabilities, including formulas, lookup functions, pivot tables, data validation, charts, and controlled spreadsheet design. Ability to compile, validate, reconcile, and analyze information from multiple sources, identify exceptions and trends, and communicate findings accurately. Ability to clearly document processes, controls, requirements, test scenarios, and implementation results. Working knowledge of data quality, reconciliation, exception management, process improvement, root-cause analysis, user acceptance testing, and change implementation. Strong organization, judgment, discretion, and problem-solving skills, with the ability to manage competing priorities, meet deadlines, and escalate issues appropriately. Clear written and verbal communication skills and the ability to prepare accurate materials for leadership, committees, auditors, regulators, and other stakeholders. Knowledge of records management, confidentiality requirements, and administrative controls. Ability to work independently and collaborate across teams. Familiarity with Power Query, Power BI, SQL, Access, or comparable tools is preferred. Why FHLBank San Francisco Here, your expertise connects to a broader purpose. You’ll collaborate with skilled colleagues in an organization that values individual contributions and supports employees’ professional growth and work and life goals. Medical, dental, vision, and mental health support. A 401(k) with company match, life insurance, and disability coverage. Vacation, sick leave, holidays, wellness, and volunteer days. Backup care for children, elders, and pets, plus parental and family leave. Annual wellness and technology stipends, a transit subsidy, and pretax commuter benefits.

The Quantitative Trading & Research team supports the Digital Agenda by building quantitative models and agentic workflows that automate the Access lifecycle and strengthen how Sales and Research teams engage with clients across asset classes. The team applies statistical, machine learning and large language models to client profiles, interactions, coverage efforts and Access activity, including corporate and analyst meetings, roadshows, conferences and events—to improve investor and client targeting, “who to call” recommendations, Access monetization and wallet predictions. Job Summary As an Associate on the team, you will develop and operationalize quantitative models and agentic workflows that help automate the Access lifecycle and improve client engagement. You will work across statistical, machine learning and LLM-based approaches, taking solutions from initial problem definition and data preparation through evaluation, production deployment and ongoing monitoring. You will also partner with Technology, Data and Product teams to integrate your work into tools and workflows used by Sales and Research. Job Responsibilities: Research, develop and validate statistical, machine learning and LLM-based models for client targeting, opportunity prioritization, signal identification and Sales and Research analytics. Build propensity, recommendation and ranking models that identify investors and clients for Access opportunities, with clear rationale and appropriate baselines. Develop investor-to-corporate and investor-to-analyst matching models for Access events using client profiles, interests, holdings, flows, research engagement, participation and feedback. Create wallet and monetization models to estimate client wallet and share-of-wallet, and assess the impact of Access activity, interactions and coverage on revenue and client value. Model client engagement and Sales and Research coverage using interaction, meeting, event and research-consumption data, including time-to-event and attrition signals; build reusable cross-asset client profiles and embeddings. Design agentic workflows to coordinate multi-step Sales, Research and Access tasks, including demand capture, event invitations, scheduling, meeting preparation, summaries, follow-ups and inbound requests. Build workflows that use targeting and matching models to support Access origination and scaling, personalize outreach, coordinate scheduling, capture feedback and update client profiles, with human review where appropriate. Develop agentic coverage workflows that monitor client activity and surface “who to call” recommendations with relevant context and suggested next steps. Create reusable analytical tools and agent capabilities, and design architectures with appropriate planning, retrieval, tool selection, guardrails, human oversight and auditability. Use LLMs to generate personalized client content grounded in source data, with controls for attribution, accuracy, tone and compliance; evaluate models and workflows through benchmarks, backtests, experiments and user feedback. Build reproducible datasets and feature pipelines, maintain production-quality code and monitoring, and partner with Sales, Research, Access, Technology, Data and Product to integrate solutions and measure their impact.

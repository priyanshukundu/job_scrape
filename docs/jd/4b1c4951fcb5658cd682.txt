Applied Scientist III Our Technology Team partners with teams across Expedia Group to create innovative products, services, and tools to deliver high-quality experiences for travelers, partners, and our employees. A singular technology platform powered by data and machine learning provides secure, differentiated, and personalized experiences that drive loyalty and traveler satisfaction. EFR builds machine-learning and AI systems that help Expedia Group protect travelers, partners, and transactions from fraud—while reducing friction for legitimate activity. The EFR ML team works across the full lifecycle, from discovering useful signals and developing models to deploying, monitoring, and evaluating decision systems. Its work spans booking fraud, account protection, and supply risk, combining established machine-learning approaches with emerging LLM and agent capabilities to detect evolving patterns and support investigation. Working closely with product, engineering, and operations partners, the team turns complex, adversarial problems into measurable improvements in fraud prevention, customer experience, and analyst effectiveness. It’s an opportunity to take ideas from experimentation into production and make a meaningful impact at Expedia Group scale. In this role, you will: Develop, evaluate, and productionize applied science solutions that address complex business and customer problems across Expedia Group. Translate ambiguous product and business needs into well-defined scientific approaches, measurable outcomes, and deployable solutions. Apply advanced statistical modeling, machine learning, experimentation, and data analysis techniques to generate actionable insights and improve product outcomes. Design rigorous experiments and evaluation frameworks, interpret results, and communicate recommendations to technical and non-technical stakeholders. Partner effectively across science, engineering, product, and business teams while contributing expertise across multiple domains and problem spaces. Safely integrate and operate AI/ML-enabled solutions that improve outcomes, while demonstrating familiarity with AI-driven systems, tools, or workflows and applying AI/ML concepts to real-world products. Minimum Qualifications: Masters in a quantitative, technical, or related field and relevant professional experience applying advanced data science, statistics, machine learning, or scientific research methods. Demonstrated experience owning applied science work from problem definition through analysis, validation, and delivery, with scope spanning a product area, service, or related domain. Strong programming and technical modeling skills, including the ability to work with large and complex datasets and develop reliable, reproducible analytical solutions. Experience designing experiments, evaluating models or product interventions, and using data to make evidence-based decisions. Ability to communicate complex technical findings clearly and collaborate with cross-functional partners to influence product or business outcomes. Preferred Qualifications: PhD in a quantitative, technical, or related discipline, or equivalent depth of applied research and industry experience. Experience developing and operating machine learning or statistical solutions at scale, including attention to model performance, reliability, monitoring, and iterative improvement. Demonstrated ability to provide technical leadership for applied science initiatives within a product area or scientific domain and to influence architecture or solution direction. Experience applying causal inference, experimentation, optimization, forecasting, recommender systems, or other advanced analytical methods to complex real-world problems. Experience using AI-driven tools and workflows to accelerate scientific discovery, improve model development, or enhance the delivery of production AI/ML solutions.

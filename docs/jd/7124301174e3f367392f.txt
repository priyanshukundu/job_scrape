Working within a cross-functional product team, this role owns features and moderately complex outcomes from refinement through production, translates product and technical requirements into effective designs and implementations, and makes sound engineering decisions within established architecture and standards. Applies proficient knowledge of the team's programming languages, frameworks, APIs, data patterns, automated testing, CI/CD, cloud-native engineering, observability, DevSecOps, and production support practices; diagnoses moderately complex defects and operational issues; and evaluates trade-offs involving quality, security, performance, reliability, maintainability, and cost. Responsibly applies AI-enabled engineering tools, Agentic AI patterns, and AI agents to improve productivity and solution quality. Contributes technical judgment to team decisions, supports less-experienced engineers, and connects engineering choices to product objectives, client needs, operational risk, and business value. Advances technical mastery and engineering judgment by applying proficient knowledge of the team's programming languages, frameworks, APIs, data access and integration patterns, development tools, and software lifecycle.

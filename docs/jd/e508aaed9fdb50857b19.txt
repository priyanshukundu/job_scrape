Data Engineer The Opportunity: As a data engineer, you know that organizing data can yield pivotal insights when it’s gathered from disparate sources. Working with a team of dedicated developers, you will be involved in the full development lifecycle from gathering client requirements, developing and deploying with CI / CD, and driving mission impact. You will work with open-source frameworks to create accessible and maintainable sof tware, explore and deepen your understanding of these frameworks, and develop relationships with stakeholders who benefit from your work. Additionally, you will work with a multi-disciplinary team of data analysts, engineers, scientists, electrical engineers, test engineers, sof tware engineers, and project stakeholders. You will leverage your experience in analytical exploration and data examination to assess, design, build, and maintain scalable data platforms for your clients. You will be responsible for helping develop and maintain database systems, continually seek opportunities to optimize, and build custom ETL or ELT and CI / CD workflows in that pursuit. How You’ll Contribute: As a data engineer on our team, you’ll: Collaborate with cross-functional teams to understand business requirements and translate them into technical solutions. Implement and manage data governance practices, ensuring data quality, security, and compliance. Design, construct, install, test, and maintain highly-scalable data management systems, ensuring they meet business requirements and industry practices. Employ a variety of languages and tools to marry systems together or try to hunt down opportunities to acquire new data from other systems. Interpret and analyze data from various source systems to support data integration and data reporting needs. Identify, design, and implement internal process improvements, automating manual processes, optimizing data delivery, and re-designing infrastructure for greater scalability. Develop and optimize database schemas and data models to support analytics and reporting. Create data tools for analytics and data scientist team members that assist them in building and optimizing our data products. Grow your communication and technical skills by merging consultation and big data to create data-centric solutions across the testing and evaluation industry. Stay updated with industry trends and emerging technologies to propose strategic improvements. Join us. The world can’t wait. You Have: 2+ years of experience designing, building, and operating production ETL or ELT pipelines 2+ years of experience with relational databases such as PostgreSQL, including data modeling and schema design 2+ years of experience using Python for data processing and automation 1+ years of experience with cloud services such as AWS Experience integrating data from RESTful APIs and external data sources Experience with workflow orchestration and CI / CD for data workloads Experience implementing or contributing to logging, monitoring, alerting, and data validation for production pipelines Ability to obtain a Secret clearance Bachelor's degree Nice If You Have: Experience with container orchestration platforms such as Docker Experience with PySpark or distributed data processing frameworks Experience with data quality frameworks or automated testing for data pipelines Knowledge of security and access controls for cloud-based data systems Secret clearance Clearance: Applicants selected will be subject to a security investigation and may need to meet eligibility requirements for access to classified information.

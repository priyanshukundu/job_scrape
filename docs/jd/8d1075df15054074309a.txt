Leidos seeks a CRM Business Analyst to support the Digital Solutions, Inc. program for our Federal, State, Local, and Tribal customers. The selected candidate will be part of a project team of project managers and consultants responsible for a core group of customers, providing analysis, consulting, and day-to-day support in the IQ CRM. This position is primarily remote with up to 20% travel possibility if customers request it. For this reason, the candidate must reside in Washington DC / metro area. The Challenge: · Analyze and evaluate user business problems and develop system recommendations to meet requirements · Provide day-to-day support and troubleshoot existing issues for customers in the IQ CRM · Interact directly with customers and stakeholders to analyze current business and technical processes and formulate new and modified business information processing systems in the IQ CRM · Coordinate with business and technology teams to ascertain customer system requirements, such as program functions and user stories, output requirements, and input data acquisition · Execute user acceptance testing (UAT) and, as needed, coordinate testing and system controls · Address complex technical issues/problems using internal best practice and through collaboration with colleagues · Communicate with colleagues across multiple areas (project managers, engineers, developers and other technical resources, etc.) and explain technical solutions, practices and procedures to others within the organization What Sets You Apart: · Bachelor’s degree and 2 – 4 years of prior relevant experience. May consider more years of relevant experience in lieu of degree. · Ability to work independently on larger, moderately complex projects/assignments. May assist other technical staff with tasks and assignments. · Ability to learn proprietary CRM software · Basic knowledge of HTML and troubleshooting skills · Ability to clearly/concisely/effectively communicate in writing or in verbal exchange with varying audiences. · Two plus (2+) years of relevant experience with Microsoft 365 Suite products such as Word, Excel, Teams, OneDrive and SharePoint · Candidate will need to be a U.S Citizen and able to secure and maintain a Public Trust Security Clearance for most customers and be eligible to secure and maintain a Confidential, Secret and Top Secret if required You Might Also Have: · One plus (1+) year of experience working in a Governor’s, Senator’s or Congressman’s office using an IQ CRM would be beneficial · Experience reading requirements documentation and user stories to create process mapping and UAT planning · Ability to effectively synthesize clear explanations of complex technical or business processes to audiences with varying levels of background and expertise · Previous helpdesk/onsite support experience · Working knowledge of Technical Support Ticketing systems · Oracle and SQL knowledge and experience We take care of our employees.

Job Description Summary This job focuses on the design and development of software for a medical product. This is a hands-on engineering role where you will own feature development, actively contribute code, and support team members in delivering high-quality product capabilities. You will own UI/software feature implementation while contributing to overall design decisions. This is a position in GE HealthCare’s Invasive Cardiology (ICAR) business, which makes Hemodynamic and Electrophysiology recording system Mac-Lab/CardioLab. These systems help treat the most difficult cardiac conditions through enhanced signal-processing, clinical capabilities, and algorithms. By creating smoother workflows and by facilitating more accurate and complete documentation, the Mac-Lab/CardioLab recording systems provide the end user with the data needed to deliver exceptional patient care. GE HealthCare is a leading global medical technology and digital solutions innovator. Our purpose is to create a world where healthcare has no limits. Unlock your ambition, turn ideas into world-changing realities, and join an organization where every voice makes a difference, and every difference builds a healthier world.

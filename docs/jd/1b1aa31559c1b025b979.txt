Grant Thornton is seeking a Manager, Partner Equity & Finance to join the team Position Summary The Manager, Partner Equity & Finance (Internal Finance Manager Role) will lead and execute equity holder transactions, equity administration & capitalization reporting, and lifecycle activities while coordinating across various teams such as People & Culture, Accounting & Finance, Tax, Legal, Payroll, Treasury, external counsel, and third-party providers. The candidate will bring strong accounting, reconciliation, and financial reporting capabilities, together with working knowledge of equity, payroll, tax, legal, and partner-lifecycle processes. This is a hands-on working manager role. The Manager/SM will execute and review recurring deliverables, manage exceptions and deadlines, maintain key stakeholder relationships, create documentation and controls, and contribute to process standardization and improvement. Key Responsibilities Partner with Director and existing team members to manage the global equity management program which would include equity award issuances, maintenance of the capitalization table, reporting coordination, and maintaining the global equity management tracking tool. Maintain and reconcile equity records, ownership schedules, equity holder executed documents, etc. Lead PFM responsibilities for partner separations, including equity treatment, vesting and forfeiture updates, agreements, and partner communications, while coordinating with People & Culture, Legal, Tax, Payroll, Treasury, and Accounting. Support month-end and quarter-end reporting for Accounting, executive stakeholders, investors, valuation providers, country stakeholders, and other approved recipients. Respond to ad hoc capitalization table, transaction related, and historical data requests. Coordinate with People & Culture and other stakeholders on equity holder lifecycle events, including offers, onboarding, grants, separations, and estate administration. Oversee and support PFM responsibilities relating to the capitalization table for tax reporting purposes. Support recurring regulatory and compliance reporting requirements. Preferred Qualifications Bachelor’s degree in Business, Finance, or equivalent. Minimum 5 years at a supervisory level of accounting or financial management experience. Proficient with MS Excel spreadsheets, including pivot tables and complex formulas. Outstanding analytical skills requiring an aptitude for detail, precision, and logic. Exhibit a high degree of initiative and critical-thinking skills in exercising independent judgment and making decisions in establishing and managing multiple priorities in a fast-paced, deadline-driven, detail-oriented work environment. Ability to function in stressful situations and think strategically and analytically to troubleshoot and resolve issues quickly. Supervisory/managerial skills necessary in order to interact and communicate with subordinates, provide instruction/direction/training, determine work priorities, and coordinate activities of the team. Ability to understand and support firm goals, culture, and strategy to represent the firm in a positive, professional manner. Demonstrated literacy in AI concepts and applications.

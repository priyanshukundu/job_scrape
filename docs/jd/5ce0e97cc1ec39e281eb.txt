The Wealth Planner I will build a foundation of knowledge of the wealth planning process. You will work closely with colleagues in the Wealth Strategies Group and collaboratively with wealth advisors and teams within Private Wealth Management (PWM). You will gain an understanding of how to develop wealth plans and provide education and analyses to clients related to all facets of the wealth planning process. You will assist senior team members in preparing basic documentation to be used in current and prospective client meetings. Essential Responsibilities: Build knowledge of the financial planning process including investment, estate, tax, retirement and insurance planning for high-net-worth and ultra-high-net-worth individuals. Become proficient at using a suite of software tools related to the financial planning process. Perform wealth planning analyses including net worth statements, cash flow plans, investment/asset allocation, risk mitigation/insurance, and retirement projections. Assist the Wealth Planning team with data gathering and data input. Perform research as directed by senior Wealth Strategies professionals. Consult with outside professionals when necessary. Synthesize findings into relevant content for wealth plans. Respond to client inquiries/service requests in a timely and proactive manner. Identify and communicate issues/potential problems that may require escalation. Collect and maintain client data according to established policies and requirements. Ensure all wealth planning activities are in compliance with applicable rules, regulations, policies and procedures. Provide internal support and troubleshooting for advisor financial planning applications. Additional responsibilities as requested. Qualifications: Bachelor’s Degree required, CFP (or working towards CFP) preferred. 1+ years of experience in financial services required. Will consider recent college grad for the right candidate.

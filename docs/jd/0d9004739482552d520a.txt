The Senior Software Engineer (Technical Lead) for Bond & Specialty Insurance (BSI) will help embed GenAI technologies and practices into our engineering ecosystem—enhancing productivity, accelerating innovation, and empowering engineers to leverage AI responsibly and effectively in daily workflows. This technical leader will: • Elevate engineering maturity by championing modern software delivery patterns, automation, and best-in-class resiliency practices. • Enable domain-aligned teams to operate with greater autonomy and velocity through modular, API-driven, and decoupled solutions. • Drive GenAI adoption and skill-building across our engineering community, equipping teams to innovate faster and make data-driven decisions. Ultimately, this investment helps to ensure our teams can deliver adaptable, intelligent, and efficient technology capabilities that directly support business growth, speed to market, and long-term operational resilience.

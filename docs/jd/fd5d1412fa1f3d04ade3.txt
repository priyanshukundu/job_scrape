Job Summary The Enterprise NERC Reliability Assurance (ENRA) team at AEP provides coordination and decision-making authority for NERC (North American Electric Reliability Corporation) compliance obligations. The team leads cross business unit teams, executes process improvements, performs root cause and incident analysis, provides independent oversight and technical support, project and risk management, metrics development, and engagement and outreach. The team operates in a matrix environment that interfaces directly with federal and regional regulators. This position will be focused on risk assessment and risk management of AEP’s NERC compliance program. Job Description What You'll Do: Develop policies and practices for performing research, data management and studies related to the analysis of potential and existing risks for AEP’s NERC compliance program. Conduct and assist with departmental risk analyses across the enterprise to enhance risk identification, prioritization, and NERC reporting effectiveness. Develop reliability remediation plans and programs and ensure they are effective in improving reliability. Oversee the effort to gather, review and document internal and external data associated with the identified risks. Interview executives, directors, and other key stakeholders to gain their perspectives on departmental goals and risks in attaining those goals. Lead assessment discussions and drive towards consensus on areas of risk and their relative prioritizations. Ensure that the NERC compliance risks are documented with appropriate detail and in accordance with the corporate standards. Prepare and present reports to internal management as well as senior management as requested. Produce and communicate assessments of compliance-related materials. Produce analytics to assess health of ENRA program; develop new metrics and reports; analyze program metrics and communicate results to stakeholders. An expert knowledge of risk identification and mitigation techniques is required. Lead, communicate, and advocate process improvement efforts. Manage projects; manage and advocate for phases of implementation projects. Engage with other utility stakeholders and industry organizations in the consideration of reliability compliance issues and associated regulatory matters. Contribute in governance-related activities and provide program-wide perspective to coordinate achieving strategic goals focused on risk, controls, and operational excellence. Lead risk compliance training and awareness program; ensure message content consistency & maximize delivery effectiveness. Track and monitor newly developed standards for inclusion in NERC risk assessment. Engage in periodic Regional Entity (RE) and Regional Transmission Operator (RTO) audits and consult and advise internal audits as needed. What We're Looking For: Education Requirements Listed Below: Bachelor's degree in engineering, mathematics, communications, computer science, business, or other related field of study; or an Associate's degree in one of the aforementioned fields of study and two (2) years of relevant work experience; Experience Requirements Listed Below: In addition to any experience required above, seven (7) years of experience in electric utility or similarly regulated industry. Preferred Qualifications: Master’s Degree is desirable. Certification/Licensure is preferred. Demonstrate the highest standards of ethical behavior and support of Company’s Corporate Compliance policy. Demonstrate strong problem-solving skills and ability to initiate solutions to complex technical issues. Demonstrate ability to effectively and efficiently develop functional relationships on all levels as well as external partnerships with various regulatory agencies/industry organizations. Proficient in oral and written communication skills, including the ability to articulate strategy and provide guidance to management and witnesses on case as required. Proficient in navigating and creating spreadsheets is required. Demonstrated ability to manage multiple projects and coordinate deadlines to enable the organization to not miss deadlines. Occasional travel will be required.

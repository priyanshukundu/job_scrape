The Assistant Vice President, Trade Finance Business Analyst will serve as the GPS business-analysis lead supporting Trade Finance products, Trade Services Operations, T360 delivery, maintenance releases, periodic upgrades, and global implementations. The role will translate business, operational, compliance, data, and downstream needs into controlled requirements and delivery artifacts, while coordinating stakeholders across Operations, Technology, Product, Finance, Risk, Compliance, vendors, and global teams. GPS Business Analyst Responsibilities · Lead requirements elicitation, analysis, documentation, validation, traceability, and approval across functional, non-functional, interface, reporting, data, and control requirements. · Own or coordinate BRDs, DRDs, current-state and future-state workflows, business rules, requirements traceability, RAID, decisions, assumptions, dependencies, and acceptance criteria. · Create and maintain the GPS WBS, Jira stories and subtasks, deliverable ownership, target dates, supporting evidence, and executive-level status reporting. · Facilitate workshops with Trade Finance and Trade Services Operations, Front Office, Technology, Finance, Risk, Compliance, Data, Home Office, regional teams, CGI, Finastra, and system integrators. · Review functional and detailed design documentation for alignment with approved business requirements, operating workflows, controls, and downstream impacts. · Coordinate stakeholder reviews, comments, approvals, scope changes, issue resolution, and governance evidence in accordance with project and audit requirements. Trade Finance Products & Trade Services Scope · Support T360 implementation, maintenance releases, periodic upgrades, product enhancements, and global rollout activities across Trade Finance and Trade Services Operations. · Provide business-analysis and product support across Documentary Collections, Documentary Letters of Credit, Standby Letters of Credit, Bank-to-Bank Reimbursements, Guarantees, Trade Payments, and related Trade Services workflows, controls, interfaces, and reporting. · Document end-to-end requirements and integration flows between T360, Deposits, Payments, Markets, Finance, data platforms, compliance systems, and other upstream and downstream applications. · Partner with Trade Finance product and Operations SMEs to align T360 and related platform capabilities with product processing, regional workflows, controls, and regulatory requirements. · Support data-field inventories, source-to-target mappings, transformations, data lineage, reconciliations, cleansing, migration, control totals, and exception handling. Testing, Readiness & Implementation · Develop or coordinate UAT strategy, plans, scenarios, scripts, test data, requirements coverage, execution tracking, evidence, defect triage, retesting, and business sign-off. · Support SIT and vendor-testing alignment by clarifying business intent, expected outcomes, interface behavior, and operational exceptions. · Coordinate operational-readiness activities including procedures, runbooks, training inputs, access, cutover, production validation, stabilization, and handover to BAU teams. · Track defects, risks, issues, dependencies, decisions, and workarounds through closure, escalating material impacts to GPS and project leadership. Cross-Functional Accountabilities · Partner across Trade Finance, Loans, Deposits, Markets, Payments, Finance, Risk, Fraud, Sanctions Screening, Transaction Monitoring, Compliance Data Quality, and Regulatory Reporting. · Coordinate global and regional requirements with Americas, Home Office, EMEA, APAC, and technology stakeholders while preserving local regulatory and operational needs. · Ensure downstream system owners and data consumers are engaged, impacts are assessed, and requirements are reflected in the appropriate BRD, DRD, design, and testing artifacts. · Support process optimization, automation, control improvement, and AI-enabled opportunities where they improve delivery quality, traceability, and operational sustainability. Leadership & Delivery Expectations · Operate as the Tampa-based GPS point of contact for assigned Trade Finance, Trade Services Operations, T360, and global implementation workstreams. · Provide structured work direction to supporting analysts and offshore resources; review deliverables for completeness, quality, consistency, and audit readiness. · Maintain transparent reporting of progress, scope, milestones, risks, dependencies, decisions, resource needs, and stakeholder actions. · Build reusable knowledge through templates, procedures, process flows, data dictionaries, decision logs, Jira dashboards, and lessons learned. Required Qualifications · Extensive business analysis experience in Trade Finance, Trade Services Operations, or a large-scale banking transformation supporting Trade Finance products. · Deep Trade Finance and Trade Services Operations knowledge, including Documentary Collections, Documentary Letters of Credit, Standby Letters of Credit, Bank-to-Bank Reimbursements, Guarantees, Trade Payments, and related operational controls. · Strong knowledge of end-to-end Trade Finance processing, including transaction initiation, document examination, settlement, reimbursements, fees, accounting, risk controls, exceptions, and downstream reporting. · Demonstrated experience producing BRDs, DRDs, process flows, business rules, traceability, data mappings, UAT artifacts, RAID, and governance documentation. · Ability to bridge Operations, Product, Technology, Data, Risk, Compliance, Finance, vendors, and global stakeholders in a complex delivery environment. · Strong communication, facilitation, analytical, organizational, and stakeholder-management capabilities. Preferred Qualifications · Hands-on experience with T360 or another Trade Finance processing platform and its integration with payments, accounting, limits, screening, monitoring, data, and downstream systems. · Experience with another major Trade Finance platform, including implementation, requirements, interfaces, data migration, reconciliations, UAT, cutover, or production stabilization. · Knowledge of Supply Chain Finance, SWIFT Trade Finance messaging, sanctions screening, transaction monitoring, compliance data quality, and operational controls. · Experience using Jira, WBS-based delivery tracking, SharePoint documentation, dashboards, and structured evidence management. Key Success Outcomes · Complete, approved, and traceable business, data, interface, control, and testing requirements. · Clear alignment between Trade Finance products, Trade Services Operations, T360, Technology, Compliance, and downstream stakeholders. · Predictable delivery of WBS milestones with transparent Jira tracking, evidence, risks, dependencies, and decisions. · High-quality UAT, migration readiness, operational readiness, and sustainable transition to BAU support. Education Bachelor’s degree or equivalent work experience equally preferable.

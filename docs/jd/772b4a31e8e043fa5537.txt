Augmented Reality Software Engineer The Opportunity: Develop and debug Virtual Reality (VR) and Augmented Reality (AR) software or applications for various devices. Apply advanced skills or extensive technical expertise, including full industry knowledge. Develop innovative solutions to complex problems. Work without considerable direction, and mentor and supervise team members. Join us. The world can't wait. You Have: 3+ years of experience with software development or research in industry settings 1+ years of experience with Unity3D in a professional environment 1+ years of experience developing features within VR, AR, or 3D real-time interactive products in industry settings Experience with programming languages such as C# or C++ Knowledge of interfaces, abstracting, inheritance, and polymorphism Ability to solve problems effectively and work independently and collaboratively Ability to obtain a Secret clearance Bachelor's degree in STEM Nice If You Have: Experience developing SDKs or shared technology that enhances extensibility and reuse Experience building for VR, including Quest or VIVE, or mixed or AR, including HoloLens, Magic Leap, ARKit, or ARCore Experience with performance optimization of real-time applications, including CPU, GPU, or Memory Experience with ArcGIS and Cesium Knowledge of MVVM architecture Knowledge of real-time engine components, including rendering, input processing, serialization, UI, or networking Knowledge of 3D math, linear algebra, and data structures Possession of excellent communication and interpersonal skills Clearance: Applicants selected will be subject to a security investigation and may need to meet eligibility requirements for access to classified information.

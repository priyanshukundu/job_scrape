What We Need to See: Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field, or equivalent experience. 12+ years of experience designing and building distributed systems. Advanced programming skills in one or more languages, including C, C++, Go, or Rust. Proven experience building and maintaining APIs.

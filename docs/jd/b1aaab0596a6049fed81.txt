About the Role: The Enterprise Risk Officer will play a critical role in supporting and advancing the Company's Enterprise Risk Management (ERM) framework through the governance, maintenance, and enhancement of enterprise-wide risk policies and standards. This role helps ensure a cohesive, consistent, and effective risk governance structure that aligns with the Company's risk appetite, strategic objectives, regulatory expectations, and industry best practices. The Enterprise Risk Officer will partner across business lines and risk disciplines to strengthen risk management practices, promote accountability, and support the ongoing maturation of the Enterprise Risk Management program. Lead and support initiatives aimed at enhancing risk management practices. Collaborate with cross-functional teams to implement risk mitigation strategies. Engage with stakeholders across the Company to provide critical insights into key risks. Facilitate communication and collaboration across the Company to ensure effective risk management. Coordinate the development, review, approval, and periodic maintenance of ERM policies and standards to ensure they remain current and effective. Promote consistency, clarity, and alignment across risk-related policies and standards while ensuring adherence to the Company's Enterprise Risk Management Framework and Risk Appetite Statement. Provide advice and guidance to stakeholders on the identification, assessment, management and mitigation of risks within the Company. Provide independent and objective challenge to ensure the effectiveness of risk management practices. Demonstrates compliance with all bank regulations for assigned job function and applies to designated job responsibilities – knowledge may be gained through coursework and on-the-job training. Keeps up to date on regulation changes. Follows all Bank policies and procedures, compliance regulations, and completes all required annual or job-specific training. Maintain a working knowledge of Bank's written policies and procedures regarding Bank Secrecy Act, Regulation CC, Regulation E, Bank Security and other regulations as applicable to this job description. May be asked to coach, mentor, or train others and teach coursework as subject matter expert. Actively learns, demonstrates, and fosters the Columbia corporate culture in all actions and words. Takes personal initiative and is a positive example for others to emulate. Embraces our vision to become “Business Bank of Choice” May perform other duties as assigned. About You: 5+ years of banking, risk management, compliance, finance, or IT/Ops experience required. Expert knowledge of tools and techniques employed in risk identification, risk assessment, monitoring, and reporting. Strong knowledge of operational and enterprise risk management concepts, best practices, and regulatory guidance. Demonstrate ability to independently manage multiple and complex work efforts, which involves directing other key stakeholders across the bank. Possess strong oral and written communication skills, critical thinking, and analytical skillset. Ability to present complex information to a variety of different audiences. Advanced skills in Excel, Microsoft PowerPoint, Microsoft PowerBI and knowledge of GRC systems helpful. Occasional travel may be required. The pay range for this role is $90,000 - $120,000.

Overview: This role is responsible for leading the analysis, design, and delivery of business and technology solutions that support Talcott's retail annuity operations, New Business, Policy Administration, Contact Center, and downstream Finance and Actuarial functions. This role partners closely with cross-functional teams (working closely with Finance and Actuarial teams), in identifying and building requirements working with upstream teams, influencing decision-making that will best suit the application ecosystem while adhering to enterprise standards, governance, and risk management practices. The ideal candidate for the retail Senior Business Analyst position will possess a deep understanding of the Annuities value chain, and lifecycle. They will have the ability to analyze how annuity submissions, case setup, policy issuance, and post‑issue events flow across upstream intake systems, policy administration platforms, and downstream financial and reporting systems. This position can be a hybrid work arrangement in our Hartford, Connecticut office. Remote work arrangements will be considered.

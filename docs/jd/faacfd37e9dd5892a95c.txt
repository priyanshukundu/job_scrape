Job Summary: PayPal Marketing Technology team is dedicated to creating a best-in-class platform. We are looking for highly talented, professional, and motivated engineers to join our team. As a Staff Data Engineer on our Marketing Technology Platform, you will own the architecture and delivery of our data pipelines and data services on GCP (BigQuery, Bigtable), Python, and Kafka, evaluate and introduce the right technologies, and drive the hardest data challenges independently. You will remain hands-on in design, coding, and production releases, while leading and overseeing the work of a team of engineers, providing technical leadership across definition, development, integration, testing, documentation, and support. Job Description: Essential Responsibilities: Lead and manage data engineering projects, ensuring timely delivery and alignment with business goals. Develop and maintain data architectures and pipelines to support data analysis and decision-making. Analyze complex datasets to identify trends, patterns, and insights that drive strategic initiatives. Collaborate with cross-functional teams to understand data needs and provide actionable insights. Ensure data quality and integrity through regular audits and validation processes. Mentor and guide junior data engineers, fostering a culture of continuous learning and improvement. Minimum Qualifications: 5+ years relevant experience and a Bachelor’s degree OR Any equivalent combination of education and experience. In your day-to-day role you will Lead the design and development of complex, high-scale data platforms and pipelines in GCP, primarily batch, with streaming integrations via Kafka. Own BigQuery-backed datasets and pipelines that support complex Marketing use cases, with ingress and egress at scale, and set the design for data models and access patterns that are adaptable to evolving business needs. Optimize BigQuery performance, cost, and reliability using partitioning, clustering, and query tuning. Build agentic services end to end on top of our data and pipelines, covering data access, orchestration, guardrails, evaluation, deployment, and monitoring, leveraging Google Gemini, Claude, and other AI platforms. Use AI-native development practices to accelerate design, coding, testing, and operations, and set the patterns the team follows. Build and operate real-time and near-real-time pipelines using Pub/Sub, batch or streaming frameworks, and Java services. Take on ambiguous, complex data challenges and drive them to completion independently as a hands-on technical lead. Lead and oversee the work of a team of engineers: set technical direction, plan and estimate work, review design and code, and mentor team members. Evaluate technologies and architectural approaches for data storage, processing, and orchestration across OLTP and OLAP systems, and document sound design decisions. Champion SDLC best practices across design, coding, testing, CI/CD, release, and operations to raise quality across multiple projects. Define standards for data modeling, schema design, pipeline instrumentation, data quality, and end-to-end data lineage. Scale data services and pipelines for very high data volumes, solving performance, reliability, and cost bottlenecks. Deliver complete solutions across all phases of the SDLC and advise leadership on project-level issues and risks. Partner with product, engineering, and business stakeholders in a global, matrixed organization to shape data solutions. Take part in the interview process to hire and build a world-class engineering team. What do you need to bring A bachelor's degree in computer science or an equivalent combination of technical education and work experience. 10+ years of data engineering experience, with deep expertise in ETL/ELT using advanced SQL and Python. An AI-native mindset, with hands-on experience building agentic services end to end using Google Gemini, Claude, and other LLM platforms and agent frameworks, and the ability to set patterns and best practices for the team. Expert-level data fundamentals: data warehouses, data marts, data lakes, data modeling, and schema design, with strong command of OLTP and OLAP systems. Strong hands-on experience with event-driven architecture and messaging systems such as Kafka or Pulsar, and streaming integrations. Hands-on experience with databases, both SQL and NoSQL. Experience with cloud platforms such as AWS, GCP, or Azure and Infrastructure as Code (Terraform). Proven experience designing and building highly scalable data pipelines and platforms in GCP (BigQuery, Bigtable) handling very high data volumes. 4+ years of hands-on experience on Google Cloud Platform, particularly BigQuery and Pub/Sub. Depth and breadth of expertise across the data stack, including technology evaluation and architectural decision-making. Strong command of SDLC best practices, CI/CD for data pipelines, automated testing, observability, and workflow orchestration (Airflow, UC4). Experience with pipeline instrumentation, data quality, and data lineage at scale. Experience leading or technically guiding a team of engineers while staying hands-on, ideally within a global, matrixed organization. Experience working on SaaS marketing platforms such as Braze, Simon Data, or Adobe is a plus. Experience using Atlassian JIRA, ServiceNow, and Confluence. Experience delivering projects using Agile methodology.

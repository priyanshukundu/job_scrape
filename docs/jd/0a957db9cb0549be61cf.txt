Title: Sr Ratings Associate Location: 7 World Trade Center, 250 Greenwich Street, New York, NY 10007 Duties: Perform portfolio credit analysis, research, presentations, and investor outreach. Proficient and in-depth knowledge of proprietary models and projections as applicable, identifying trends in data and applying analytical skills and judgment to form well-reasoned credit conclusions. Review financial spreadsheets and charts, analyze data and financial documents, building and updating financial forecasts, and writing analytical commentary in accordance with internal methodologies. Interpret and analyze financial data and other credit inputs provided by internal and external parties. Attend investor briefings and issuer meetings as required; help prepare presentation materials in some cases; arrive prepared to engage in discussions; present certain sections in some cases. Prepare first draft of rating committee memos and materials, in collaboration with lead analysts. Actively participate in rating committee discussions and interact with issuers and other market participants, in collaboration with lead analysts. Collaborate with lead analysts in preparing the first draft of core research (such as issuer or sector comments, press releases, pre-sale and new issue reports). Apply proficient knowledge of credit fundamentals through review of transaction documentation and cash flow analysis. Develop and apply strong working knowledge of internal methodologies and credit rating standard processes with moderate guidance from lead analysts. Contribute to training and onboarding of new staff, serve as a ‘buddy’ where applicable. Actively participate in special projects while concurrently maintaining consistent dedication to core responsibilities. Provide significant input into various analytical and statistical projects. Provide analytical oversight, document and implement workflow processes and conduct quality checks. Work with lead analysts to perform day-to-day credit analysis related to ratings with increasing complexity, support research, and prepare presentations. Serve as a resource on more complex credit questions and issues for less experienced staff. Act as a mentor to more junior staff, on topics such as research writing, issuer relationship management, and credit process. Monitor a full and diverse portfolio with growing independence. Demonstrate ability to understand and identify key factors that affect issuer credit ratings. Long Description Requirements: Requires a Bachelor’s degree or foreign equivalent in Finance, Economics or a related quantitative field plus two (2) years of experience as an Analyst or in a related position performing credit and ratings analysis in various rating groups/asset classes. Must have experience with the following: performing credit and ratings analysis in various rating groups/asset classes; applying knowledge of rating process and services, analyst workflow, compliance policies and procedures, and rating business regulatory requirements; reviewing quantitative and qualitative assessments of financial documentation and economic/market data; reviewing research produced on specific issuers, general credit trends, or sector related issues; providing analytical oversight, including documenting and implementing workflow processes and conducting quality checks; performing project management; and conducting quantitative financial modeling and statistical analysis.

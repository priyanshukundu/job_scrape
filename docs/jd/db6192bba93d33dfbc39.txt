GTM Strategy & Operations Associate — Salesforce Security Practice Target Start Date: January 2027 What You’ll Be Doing & Why It Matters The Salesforce Security Practice is hiring a GTM Strategy & Operations Associate to help build and scale how we go to market — supporting GTM execution, pre-sales, and business operations, with hands-on learning and coaching from experienced teammates. You’ll partner with Account Executives (AEs), Customer Success Managers (CSMs), Professional Services (PS) sellers, and product and delivery teams to identify customer needs, prepare compelling pursuits, and keep the business running smoothly. We’re looking for someone who brings curiosity, commercial judgment, and a practical approach to using data and AI to make the team more effective. This is a role you grow into. You’ll own defined analyses, deliverables, and coordination workstreams with coaching along the way. Success looks like well-qualified opportunities, clear customer-facing materials, timely proposals, reliable pipeline reporting, and clean sales-to-delivery handoffs. Core Responsibilities GTM Strategy & Account Targeting Research and prioritize target accounts using market trends, account plans, customer initiatives, and service gaps. Prepare account briefs and opportunity hypotheses for seller validation. Support targeted campaigns, repeatable sales plays, and follow-up cadences. Tailor messaging to customer priorities, coordinate with AEs, CSMs, and PS sellers. Track engagement, meetings, and qualified pursuits, and use response and conversion data to recommend improvements. Help build and pilot lightweight AI-native tools — with technical guidance — that surface opportunities across sellers from approved CRM, customer-success, and public market signals, always with source evidence and recommended next steps. Sales Enablement & Deal Execution Develop pitch decks, battle cards, case studies, and business cases that explain the value of our security services. Prepare account research, discovery questions, presentation storylines, and demo briefs. Participate in customer conversations alongside experienced teammates. Coordinate inputs for proposals and statements of work using approved templates. Help articulate customer outcomes, deliverables, assumptions, dependencies, and timelines, and support effort estimates and business cases with delivery leads. Business Operations & Reporting Maintain CRM data, pipeline dashboards, forecast inputs, and operating-review materials. Track key metrics such as pipeline, ACV, conversion, stage progression, and deal aging; flag bottlenecks and pursuit risks. Support weekly forecast and pipeline-inspection cycles, and coordinate sales-to-delivery handoffs. Help prepare analyses and presentations for leadership reviews, including QBRs, building the supporting materials that senior teammates present. Recommend practical process improvements that increase data quality, shorten turnaround times, and help opportunities progress. Minimum Requirements 0-2 years of experience, Bachelor’s degree completed or expected by the start date, or equivalent practical experience. Analytical foundation: Comfort with spreadsheets, basic quantitative analysis, and presentations. Ability to organize information, spot patterns, and explain a business recommendation clearly. Disciplined operator: Keeps data accurate, manages competing priorities, and follows through on actions and deadlines. Looks for simpler, more repeatable ways of working including appropriate use of AI and automation. Strong communicator: Communicates insights clearly in writing, slides, and conversation. Translates analysis and technical input into concise, audience-appropriate business messages, and supports recommendations with evidence. Nice to Have (Not Required) Salesforce ecosystem: Interest in CRM, SaaS, and professional services, and in how sales, customer-success, product, and delivery teams work together. Familiarity with Salesforce, Agentforce, or AI and low-code tools is a plus — willingness to learn and apply them thoughtfully matters more. Security fluency: Curiosity about secure SaaS, identity, and data protection. Advanced security experience or certifications are not expected. Analytical tooling: Exposure to tools such as SQL, Python, Tableau, or R is welcome but not required. Relevant experience: Internships or project work in consulting, finance, or product management is a plus.

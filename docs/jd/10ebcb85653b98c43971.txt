Our location in Lake Forest, IL , currently has an opportunity for a Business Analyst. This role requires an individual with a passion for analyzing data, extracting needs for business decisions, and assisting in creating products which deliver value to our customers. The Business Analyst will work as part of cross-functional teams including Global Marketing, Research & Development, Information Technology, Quality, Operations, Regulatory and Business Development to deliver software solutions. They are responsible for understanding the business problems, extracting the user needs and requirements, transforming them into actionable user agile stories and requirements, and then working with the R&D development and testing teams through execution. Continuing product support, customer interactions, and on-market support assistance is also expected. The ideal candidate will have a demonstrable track record of success delivering new features or products. The candidate will be a critical contributor to defining products within the Diagnostics Division.

About the Job: We are seeking a Software Engineer III to help design, develop, and support scalable software and data solutions. In this role, you will work across product, engineering, and business teams to deliver reliable solutions using technologies such as Python, Databricks, Amazon S3, SQL, and Node.js. You will contribute to technical discussions, improve development and continuous integration practices, and help resolve technical challenges throughout the software development lifecycle. This position is well suited for an engineer who demonstrates strong technical ownership, sound judgment, and a commitment to continuous learning. What You'll Do: Design, develop, test, and maintain reliable software and data solutions using Python, Databricks, Amazon S3, SQL, Node.js, and related technologies. Recommend improvements to established development, deployment, and continuous integration practices. Troubleshoot and resolve technical issues that arise during the development and deployment of software solutions. Conduct thorough code reviews to promote quality and ensure alignment with established coding standards and engineering best practices. Apply an evolving understanding of system-wide architecture when developing solutions and addressing technical challenges. Contribute to technical discussions, solution design, and team decision-making. Collaborate effectively with product, engineering, and business partners to translate requirements into practical technical solutions. Develop and deliver solutions using Agile methods and iterative development practices. Take ownership of assigned work from initial development through implementation and production support. Provide guidance, knowledge sharing, and technical support to junior engineering talent, as needed. What You'll Bring to the Role: Bachelor’s degree in computer science, engineering, information technology, or a related field, or equivalent professional experience. At least 3 to 4 years of experience working with modern software engineering tools, programming languages, and development practices. At least 3 to 4 years of development experience using Python, Databricks, Amazon S3, SQL, and Node.js. Strong understanding of software engineering principles, solution design, coding standards, and development best practices. Experience developing and delivering solutions within an Agile environment. Demonstrated critical-thinking and problem-solving capabilities. Demonstrated ownership mindset and commitment to continuous learning. Ability to communicate technical concepts effectively across product, engineering, and business teams. Evolving analytical skills and the ability to evaluate technical challenges from multiple perspectives. Skills you have: Software Engineering- Designs, develops, tests, documents and maintains software applications for the organization to use to process complex technical information. Demonstrates the ability to convert design specifications into functional software solutions that meet customer needs. Includes planning, designing, programming, debugging, testing, documenting and maintaining applications and services, using established coding standards and methodologies. Ensures that the code delivered is reusable and modular. Uses software development processes in which the smallest testable parts of an application (units) are individually and independently scrutinized for proper operation. Software Development- Creates and maintains applications, frameworks, and other software components via designing, programming, testing, and bug fixing; writes and maintains source code for all processes in the software development life cycle and facilitates the automation and orchestration of disparate systems. Leverages TDD (Test Driven Development) and BDD (Behavior Driven Development) methodologies to ensure unit, integration, and end-to-end testing with appropriate level of coverage is met. Cloud Platform Engineering- Applies knowledge of cloud service platforms and delivery models to support, develop, adopt, secure, implement and evaluate end-to-end cloud solutions delivery and data storage instead of through traditional physical servers in a back office. DevOps & Delivery - Automates and manages continuous integration, testing, release, deployment, and delivery pipelines. Coordinates release readiness, timing, dependencies, and deployment approaches; monitors outcomes and improves delivery speed, reliability, quality, and user impact. Application Performance & Maintenance- Operates and maintains applications to meet defined performance, availability, resilience, and user-experience requirements. Monitors capacity and reliability, resolves issues, and implements evidence-based improvements to sustain dependable service and business operations. Technical Problem Solving- Conducts in-depth analysis of technology needs, issues, and roadblocks within assigned scope, applies best practice based on experience and expertise, explores the "art of possible." Solves problems effectively and gains alignment on the path forward. Defines, understands, and documents what is being solved for.

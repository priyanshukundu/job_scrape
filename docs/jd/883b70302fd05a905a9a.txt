TITLE: Lead Analyst, Treasury Operations EMPLOYER: AES Clean Energy Services, LLC LOCATION: 1 Monument Circle, Floor 9, Indianapolis, IN 46204 (100% remote; must work within commuting distance of an AES office) DUTIES: Support AES financial operations by executing daily cash management activities across domestic and international bank accounts, administering payment processes, and maintaining banking infrastructure with a high degree of accuracy, independence, and cross-functional coordination to support liquidity, compliance, and operational efficiency across AES business units and legal entities. Monitor daily cash positions and execute intercompany and external fund transfers through banking platforms to ensure adequate liquidity, timely settlement of obligations, and efficient cash concentration across AES entities. Maintain and update short-term cash flow forecasts using data from multiple internal and external sources, including SAP, banking platforms, AP/AR activity, debt schedules, and business unit projections, to support liquidity planning and treasury decision-making. Oversee end-to-end treasury operations workflows, including payment initiation and approvals, transaction tracking, bank confirmations, and treasury system updates while ensuring compliance with AES policies and banking requirements. Manage bank account administration processes, including coordinating account openings, closures, signer updates, KYC documentation, and maintenance of banking records across treasury management systems and bank portals. Administer Treasury Management Systems, bank portals, and other treasury platforms with full access and release authority. Ensure adherence to AES internal controls, SOX compliance requirements, treasury policies, and audit standards by maintaining proper documentation, segregation of duties, and supporting internal and external audit requests. Mentor and support junior treasury team members by providing guidance on treasury operations, banking processes, internal controls, and system usage while helping drive consistency and operational excellence across the team. REQUIREMENTS: Bachelor’s degree or foreign equivalent degree in business administration, finance, accounting, or related field and 3 years of experience in treasury operations or a similar financial role.

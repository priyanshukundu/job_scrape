THE OPPORTUNITY The Senior Treasury Management Operations Specialist provides advanced technical and operational support across Treasury Management products and services, serving as a subject matter resource to internal team members and commercial clients. The role provides cross-functional coverage, resolves complex operational and technical issues, and supports management with procedure development, testing, quality assurance, operational controls, audits, and departmental initiatives. This position partners closely with internal teams to ensure Treasury Management services are delivered accurately, timely, and in accordance with established service level agreements, policies, procedures, and regulatory requirements. Performs all duties in accordance with the Company’s policies and procedures, all U.S. state and federal laws and regulations, wherein the Company operates. HOW YOU’LL MAKE A DIFFERENCE Serve as a senior subject matter resource, providing advanced technical and operational support to internal team members and commercial clients utilizing Treasury Management products and services, including Online/Mobile Banking, Positive Pay, Remote Deposit Capture, Bill Payment/Integrated Payables, Wire Transfers, ACH, Secure File Transfers/Enhanced Reporting functions, ICS/CDARS, and Treasury Management risk and fraud-related processes. Research, troubleshoot, and resolve complex Treasury Management product, service, and operational issues, escalating matters as appropriate. Partner with internal teams and department managers to ensure customers have an outstanding and reliable experience. Ensure that the implementation and delivery of products, inquiries, and services are completed within agreed-upon SLAs, adhering to policies, procedures, operational controls, and regulatory requirements. Provide training and guidance to clients on Treasury Management products and services, including product usage and best practices. Collaborate with teammates and management in a resourceful and positive manner. Process requests for Treasury Management products and support the maintenance and modification of existing products and services. Provide cross-functional operational coverage based on business and staffing needs. Assist with annual audit preparation and remediation processes. Assist with department projects such as acquisitions and new product offerings. Create or modify, test, and validate department procedures. Perform quality assurance reviews and support operational controls. Provide internal phone, email, and client support, as applicable. Follow all established policies and procedures. Perform other duties and projects as assigned. WHAT YOU’LL BRING 3+ years of Treasury Management, banking operations, commercial banking, or related financial services experience, with demonstrated knowledge of Treasury Management products and services. High School diploma or equivalent required. Advanced knowledge of Treasury Management products, operational processes, systems, and client service practices. Proficient in Microsoft Word and Excel. Self-motivated and team-oriented, with the ability to work independently, exercise sound judgment, prioritize competing responsibilities, and appropriately escalate issues. Excellent interpersonal, verbal, and written skills. Excellent time management and organizational skills.

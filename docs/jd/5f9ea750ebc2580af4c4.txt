WHAT YOU WILL DO

As a Principal Software Engineer on the Outer Loop team in the Engineering Systems organization, you will set the technical direction for how Snowflake tests, validates, and releases software in an AI-first world. You will build the frameworks, tooling, and practices that let our engineering organization scale its velocity by an order of magnitude without compromising quality.

 - Build agent-first test frameworks:  Extend test frameworks from humans to agents. Build layers of verifiers that agents can autonomously hill climb on correctness and performance. Extend observability for agents to detect, diagnose and auto fix test or product issues.

 - Improve release tooling and process: Re-imagine our release pipelines and processes to drive faster, more frequent releases with higher quality. Shorten commit-to-production cycle time, reduce release friction and toil, and build the automation, gating, and observability needed to ship confidently at scale.

 - Identify quality escapes and drive systemic fixes: Analyze production incidents, regressions, and customer-impacting defects to identify quality escapes and recurring themes. Turn those insights into concrete changes in testing strategy, tooling, and engineering practices that measurably reduce escapes across the organization.

 - Set strategy and lead across organizations: Drive multi-quarter technical strategy for the outer loop, influence engineering-wide standards, and partner with product managers, architects, release management, and product engineering teams to deliver end-to-end solutions.

 - Measure what matters: Define and own metrics for test signal quality, flakiness, release velocity, change failure rate, and escape rates, and use data to prioritize the highest-leverage investments.

 - Raise the bar: Mentor senior and junior engineers, set the bar on AI era talent, set ambitious goals and understand the art of what is possible with AI transformation.

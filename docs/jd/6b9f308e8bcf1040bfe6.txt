Job Description: Job Title: Senior Data Scientist Corporate Title: Assistant Vice President Location: New York, NY Overview As a Senior Data Scientist, you will join a global Data Science team supporting AI-driven transaction monitoring and analytics for Wealth Management. You will work closely with U.S.-based product users and stakeholders to understand customer needs, how analytical products are being used, and opportunities to enhance those products. You will combine hands-on Data Science expertise with product thinking and stakeholder management to translate business problems into practical machine learning and AI solutions. Working with a broader global team, you will help deliver measurable business value while advancing both established machine learning and emerging generative AI capabilities.

Associate Scientist, Biomolecular InterAction Core
Regeneron
Location: TARRYTOWN
Link: https://regeneron.wd1.myworkdayjobs.com/Careers/job/TARRYTOWN/Associate-Scientist--Biomolecular-InterAction-Core_R53212

Build our future together: At Regeneron, we use science and innovation to develop life-changing medicines for people with serious diseases. We are seeking an Associate Scientist to join our Biomolecular InterAction Core group, supporting our Therapeutic Proteins team in an on-site capacity. In this role, you will implement methods to characterize protein interactions and biochemical properties in support of our antibody pipeline, while collaborating with colleagues across the group and the wider organization. This position offers the opportunity to contribute to a fast-growing, science-driven organization making a meaningful difference to patients worldwide. When & where: Location: Tarrytown, NY Work model: On-site Discover your role: Characterize antibodies, develop methods, and provide analytical support for biologics development Implement methods to characterize protein interactions and biochemical properties Troubleshoot established test methods used in development and release testing of screening reagents Tabulate results and write summary reports of assay development Present findings at group meetings and, possibly, interdepartmental meetings Maintain lab supplies and equipment Produce and maintain accurate records This role requires: BS/MS in Molecular Biology, Cell Biology, or a related field and 0-3 years of relevant laboratory experience Knowledge of protein chemistry and biophysical methods, including spectroscopic and calorimetric techniques, SPR (Biacore), BLI (Octet), and ITC Experience with chromatography and electrophoretic analysis of proteins, including HPLC, FPLC, SDS-PAGE, and Western blot Experience developing immunoassays (ELISA and RIA) Strong oral and written communication skills and proficiency in word processing, spreadsheet, and database management Excellent organizational and interpersonal skills Does this sound like you?

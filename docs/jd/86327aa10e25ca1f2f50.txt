Role Overview We're looking for an engineer who thinks in systems, leads with vision, and sees AI as a force multiplier for everything we build and ship. As part of our Build and Release Management team, you'll own the future of how Cboe tests and delivers software — defining where our engineering practice is headed, architecting a next-generation CI/CD pipeline, and embedding AI across the DevOps lifecycle. At the heart of this role is Cboe Titanium (Cboe Ti), our high-performance, globally consistent and locally optimized exchange platform designed to support Cboe's trading operations across options, futures, and equities markets worldwide. You'll be a key driver in shaping how Cboe Ti is built, tested, and released and ensure the platform continues to meet the exacting demands of global markets with speed, precision, and reliability. This is a hands-on and highly technical role focusing on managing the software build process, providing oversight for build/test environments, developing and supporting testing tools and status dashboards, influencing and shaping our continuous integration process, and ensuring our weekly release process is successful. You'll set the standard for what great looks like at a company where reliability is measured in microseconds and releases happen every week. Your responsibilities will be: Define and drive the vision for Cboe's build and release engineering practice — from pipeline architecture to tooling strategy to long-term roadmap Lead AI adoption across the DevOps lifecycle — deploying intelligent automation for changelog generation, incident summarization, deployment workflows, and infrastructure-as-code generation.

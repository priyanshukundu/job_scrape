The Crown Is Yours As an Associate Problem Manager, you’ll help strengthen the reliability and performance of our platforms by identifying recurring issues and driving long-term solutions. You’ll work closely with Engineering and cross-functional teams to investigate underlying causes, track corrective actions, and prevent incidents from happening again. Through data analysis, structured problem investigations, and continuous improvement initiatives, you’ll help turn operational challenges into meaningful improvements that enhance platform stability and the customer experience. What you’ll do as an Associate Problem Manager Support end-to-end problem management processes, from identifying recurring incidents through root cause analysis and resolution tracking. Partner with Engineering and cross-functional teams to investigate underlying technical issues, identify contributing factors, and develop long-term solutions. Coordinate post-incident reviews, document key findings, and track corrective and preventive actions through completion. Analyze incident and problem data to identify trends, recurring issues, and opportunities to improve platform stability and operational performance. Maintain accurate problem records, including investigation findings, known errors, action items, and resolution progress. Communicate problem investigation updates, risks, and business impacts to technical teams and stakeholders, ensuring visibility into progress and next steps. Contribute to continuous improvement initiatives that strengthen problem management processes, reporting, and overall service reliability. What you’ll brin g Experience in Incident Management, Problem Management, or Technology Operations within a technology-driven environment. Familiarity with root cause analysis, post-incident reviews, and problem management processes focused on preventing recurring issues. Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to identify patterns in incident data and support investigations into underlying causes. The ability to collaborate with Engineering and cross-functional teams to coordinate investigations, track action items, and drive progress toward resolution. Strong written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to explain technical issues, investigation findings, and business impacts to different audiences. Excellent organizational skills and attention to detail, with the ability to manage multiple investigations, prioritize competing tasks, and maintain accurate documentation. Familiarity with incident management and observability tools such as Jira, PagerDuty, Datadog, or similar platforms.

PURPOSE Ensures that all changes (application and/or infrastructure) adhere to high quality standards for integrity and performance prior to release, and upon delivery to internal/external customers. PRIMARY DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES The following statements describe the general nature of work performed by people assigned to this job, may not be performed by all individuals in this job, and are not a complete list of all job duties required. · Develops a formalized testing methodology and standardize techniques. Develops new testing techniques for various OutSystems Low Code Platform. · Construct and facilitate development of test plans and environments to identify software defects at all stages of development. Lead defect triage and communicate release risks to technical and business stakeholders. · Define risk-based coverage and determine what should be tested manually versus automated. Identifies reusable test plans for regression type testing. · Conducts a wide range of quality control tests and analyses to ensure that information systems, applications, and software meet specified standards, proper operations, end-user requirements, and are defect-free. Review requirements and solution designs for testability. · Coordinates with development team and users for user acceptance testing and ensures testing is complete, successful, and documented. · Document formalized procedures for program evaluation, testing and correction. Document all defects and work with business analysts and developers to track them through resolution. · Coordinates, configures, and/or participates in the implementation of automated testing tools. Researches and reviews additional testing tools, alternative testing techniques. · Takes an active role in directing and managing his/her own knowledge and experience base with an explicit focus of OutSystems’ tester learning path · Collaborate effectively with developers, architects, product owners, DevOps engineers, security teams, and platform administrators. · Mentor testers and establish reusable standards, test assets, metrics, and quality gates. EXPERIENCE / BACKGROUND · Bachelor’s degree in computer science, information systems, engineering, or equivalent practical experience. · Technical Architecture · Automated testing and programming tools with focus on Data driven automation testing methodology · OutSystems Testing Best Practice · Open Source/Off the Shelf Automated testing and programming tools · OutSystems training or certification is preferred. · Demonstrated OutSystems lifecycle knowledge, automation capability, integration testing, CI/CD participation, and leadership of quality practices preferred.

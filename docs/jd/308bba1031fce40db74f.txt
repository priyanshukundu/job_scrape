HP PageWide Web Press R&D Software Engineer Description - Job Summary HP PageWide Web Press technology powers some of the world’s most productive industrial printing systems, operating 24x7 in demanding customer production environments. This role develops the software and firmware that directly control and optimize large-scale electromechanical systems where milliseconds matter and software quality directly impacts uptime, print quality, and customer revenue. You will work across the full product lifecycle, delivering Digital Front End (DFE), Press Software, and Firmware solutions—from architecture and design through on-press validation and customer deployment. Key Responsibilities Design, develop, test, integrate, and maintain software and/or firmware for HP PageWide Web Press platforms. Build distributed, multi-threaded, and event-driven systems interacting with real-time hardware and industrial controls. Translate product and system requirements into robust, maintainable implementations. Collaborate with writing systems, industrial controls, mechanical, electrical, quality, service, and product teams. Execute automated and manual test strategies, including lab and on-press validation. Participate in architecture, design, and code reviews. Investigate and resolve issues found in development and customer environments. Role Focus Areas (depending on background and interests) Digital Front End (DFE): Print pipelines, workflow, performance optimization. Press Software: Press orchestration, configuration, and state management. Firmware / Embedded: Real-time device control, interactions with ASIC and FPGA, and diagnostics Innovation & AI-Assisted Capabilities (Optional) Apply AI-assisted techniques to improve engineering productivity and diagnostics where appropriate. Ensure deterministic and safety-critical behavior remains intact. Education & Experience Recommended Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, Software Engineering or related field. Typically has 4-6 years of work experience, preferably in software designing & development, software architecture, programming languages, or a related field. Industrial automation, embedded systems, robotics, or manufacturing experience valued. Knowledge & Skills Proficiency in software and/or firmware development languages such as C/C++, Java, Groovy, Scala, JavaScript. Experience with distributed, multi-threaded, or event-driven systems. Understanding software, firmware, and hardware interaction. Familiarity with Agile development and CI/CD practices. Strong debugging, analytical, and problem-solving skills. Experience with automated testing frameworks and quality-driven development. Experience with industrial, embedded, or high-reliability systems. Exposure to commercial or industrial printing, automation, or press systems. Experience with open-source software, third-party integration, or cybersecurity practices. Ability to work across multiple software and firmware domains. Applied AI / Agentic Systems (Preferred): Familiarity with agent-based or tool-orchestrated AI systems. Understanding of evaluation, observability, and safety considerations. Experience integrating AI into existing production systems. Cross Organization Skills Ability to collaborate effectively across teams, functions, and geographies. Strong written and verbal communication skills. Collaborative mindset with commitment to mentoring and knowledge sharing. Results-oriented approach with a bias for action. Impact & Scope Direct impact on product quality, system reliability, and customer experience. Influences multiple subsystems or components within a platform or solution. Operates with moderate autonomy within established architectural and product frameworks. Complexity Addresses complex technical challenges in distributed systems and hardware-software integration. Balances performance, reliability, scalability, and maintainability. Operates in an environment with evolving requirements and cross-team dependencies. Why Join HP PageWide Web Press Work on real systems producing products at scale for global customers. See your software directly impact productivity and quality. Work hands-on with hardware, presses, and production environments. Grow as a technical leader in a world-class industrial R&D organization.

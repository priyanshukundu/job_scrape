Job Description Purpose of the role To produce and deliver Research with differentiated market insights and actionable ideas to Barclays Clients. Accountabilities Analysis of market, sector, corporate and/or economic data to help develop investment theses for your coverage universe to produce best in class Research. Research may range from individual company or sector notes, through to long dated thematic reports. Presentation of Research views to Barclays Clients; this can be through direct, face to face and virtual interactions, Research hosted events and written communications. Engagement with Markets, Client Strategy and other stakeholders, to raise awareness of your Research both to Clients and internally. Prioritise interaction with the most relevant and valuable Clients for your Research. Provision of insights and Research views to internal Clients to help them navigate financial markets and risks. Collaboration with the Supervisory Analyst, Compliance and other stakeholders to ensure Research is produced and delivered to Clients and internal stakeholders in a compliant manner. Join Barclays as a High Yield Credit Research, VP - Retail & Consumer, where you will conduct in-depth credit research across Retail and Consumer sectors. You will also prepare and deliver research insights while building ample relationships with internal and external stakeholders. To be successful as a High Yield Credit Research, VP, you should have experience with: Conducting credit research across High Yield and/or Investment Grade markets Evaluating companies within the Retail and Consumer sectors, including financial performance, capital structures, and credit profiles Developing investment recommendations and communicating research findings to internal and external stakeholders Monitoring industry trends, market developments, and emerging credit risks Some other highly valued skills may include: Experience in Credit Research, preferably within a sell-side or buy-side environment Direct Retail and/or Consumer sector experience, with the ability to take care of of coverage Financial modeling and research writing skills Experience working with institutional clients, trading, and other key stakeholders You may be assessed on the key critical skills relevant for success in this role, such as risk and controls, change and transformation, business acumen, strategic thinking, digital and technology, as well as job-specific technical skills.

Join Barclays as an Equity Derivatives Structuring, AVP and help deliver structured investment solutions for clients through innovative products and technology. In this role, you will structure and price complex equity derivatives, including exotics, structured notes, and QIS strategies, while partnering with Sales and Trading teams to develop tailored client solutions. You will apply quantitative expertise across pricing, volatility modelling, and risk analytics, while leveraging technology and AI tools to enhance client outcomes.

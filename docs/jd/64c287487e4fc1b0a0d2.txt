Job Description This position will be responsible for the analysis, design, testing, development and maintenance of best in class software experiences. The candidate is a self-motivated individual who can collaborate with a team and across the organization. The candidate takes responsibility of the software artifacts produced adhering to U.S. Bank standards in order to ensure minimal impact to the customer experience. The candidate will be adept with the agile software development lifecycle and DevOps principles. Essential Responsibilities: Design, build, and enhance technology health analytics and automation solutions that support enterprise engineering and operational excellence initiatives. Develop and maintain scalable data pipelines, data models, and metric frameworks that drive consistent reporting and insight generation. Create and optimize Power BI dashboards, scorecards, and executive reporting solutions that enable informed decision making. Develop workflow automation solutions using Power Apps, Power Automate, APIs, and modern programming languages . Design and implement AI-powered workflows for issue triage, summarization, anomaly detection, metric interpretation, and recommendation generation. Build reusable prompt frameworks, AI agents, guardrails, and quality controls to support responsible AI adoption at scale. Implement monitoring, alerting, and exception management capabilities to proactively identify technology risks and operational issues. Collaborate with cross-functional partners to drive adoption of health metrics, influence decisions, and deliver measurable business outcomes. Basic Qualifications - Bachelor’s degree, or equivalent work experience - Five to six years of relevant experience Preferred Skills/Experience • Demonstrated senior-scope delivery as an individual contributor on complex, cross-team initiatives. • Advanced SQL skills for data extraction, transformation, and performance-aware analysis. • Experience with technology health domains such as incidents, changes, vulnerabilities, and obsolescence. • Hands-on data analysis experience, including trend analysis, anomaly detection, and root-cause analysis. • Advanced experience building and scaling Power BI reporting products. • Practical experience using AI prompting and AI agents in analytics or engineering workflows. • Hands-on experience with Power Apps and Power Automate. • Proficiency in one or more relevant programming or scripting languages (for example Python, Java, C#, JavaScript/TypeScript, etc.). • Experience with Git or GitLab and CI/CD practices.

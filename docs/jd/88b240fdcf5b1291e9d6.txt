Manager, Treasury Product Management
American Express
Location: New York, NY, United States
Link: https://egug.fa.us2.oraclecloud.com/hcmUI/CandidateExperience/en/sites/CX_1/job/26016448

The Manager, Treasury Product Management is a key role within the Global Treasury team, responsible for translating Treasury business needs and a prioritized roadmap into well-defined product requirements and supporting delivery of platform capabilities. This individual will work closely with Treasury and Technology teams across the product lifecycle to enable effective, controlled and scalable solutions that improve Treasury processes, data and execution. We are at a critical juncture of transformation across a number of our key platforms, and the Manager will have a significant opportunity in supporting that modernization. Translate Business Needs into Product Requirements – Partner with Treasury stakeholders to understand business processes and translate requirements into clear capabilities, features, user stories and acceptance criteria. Manage Product Backlog – Maintain and refine the product backlog, ensuring requirements are clearly documented, prioritized and ready for Technology execution. Drive Agile Execution – Partner closely with Technology through refinement, sprint planning, demonstrations and delivery to provide business clarification and resolve requirements gaps. Support Product Delivery – Track delivery of assigned capabilities, manage requirements and dependencies, and escalate risks or issues impacting business outcomes. Enable Data & Process Improvements – Support data, reporting, automation and AI requirements that simplify Treasury processes and improve controls and decision-making. Lead Testing & Implementation Readiness – Coordinate UAT, business acceptance, implementation readiness and post-production validation for delivered capabilities. Embed Risk & Controls – Incorporate regulatory, risk, control and audit requirements into product requirements, testing and delivery. Partner Across Teams – Work across Treasury, Finance, Risk and Technology to maintain alignment on requirements, priorities, delivery status and issues. Minimum Qualifications: 3+ years of experience in product management, Treasury, Finance, payments, banking platforms or related financial services roles. Understanding of Treasury, Finance or related financial services processes and ability to translate business, data and control needs into technology requirements. Experience defining product requirements, capabilities, features, user stories and acceptance criteria in an Agile environment. Experience partnering with Technology teams through development, testing and implementation. Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with a risk and control mindset. Strong communication and collaboration skills with the ability to work effectively across business and Technology teams. Preferred Qualifications: Experience in Treasury areas such as cash management, liquidity, payments, forecasting, Treasury data or financial/regulatory reporting is preferred. Depending on factors such as business unit requirements, the nature of the position, cost and applicable laws, American Express may provide visa sponsorship for certain positions.

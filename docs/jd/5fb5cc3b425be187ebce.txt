Your Role We're looking for a Senior Data Scientist, Research who is versatile: you model high-stakes business problems—revenue optimization, risk management, and related questions—and you use AI to scale that work. You'll be a senior individual contributor who can go deep statistically, learn a new domain quickly, and rethink how data science problems get solved in the age of AI: not only classical models, but agents and other AI systems that multiply what a small research team can do. This role is for someone who is deeply curious, strong at analytical thinking and problem solving, and a fast learner—with formal training and a strong statistical foundation. You have tackled revenue and risk problems with modeling before, and you are excited to solve them differently now: building AI agents, automating research workflows, and using modern tools so the science scales. If you want ownership over modelizable, revenue- and risk-critical problems and you already leverage AI to move faster, this is the role. What You'll Do Tackle revenue and risk problems with modeling. Frame ambiguous business questions as quantitative problems; build models that improve revenue, manage risk, and inform decisions. Solve them differently in the age of AI. Use AI—including agents and other automation—to scale data science work: accelerate research, extend coverage, and change how models get built, tested, and used. Stay statistically rigorous. Bring a strong foundation in statistics and inference so AI-accelerated work is still trustworthy—you know when a result is solid and when it is not. Learn the business fast. Ramp quickly across new domains and problems; stay versatile rather than attached to a single method or subject area. Turn research into decisions. Communicate findings and trade-offs clearly to technical and non-technical partners, including senior leadership. Raise the bar. Share practices that help the team use AI to scale DS work without lowering the quality of the science. Mentor and set standards. Mentor other data scientists and help grow a culture of curiosity, rigor, and high leverage. What We're Looking For Versatile applied data scientist with experience modeling business problems such as revenue optimization and risk management—not just analyses or dashboards. Strong statistical foundation from formal training (statistics, mathematics, or a related quantitative field) and the judgment to apply it under real-world, messy conditions. Deeply curious, with strong analytical thinking and problem-solving skills; a fast learner who picks up new domains, tools, and methods quickly. Practical experience leveraging AI to scale data science work—for example building AI agents, automating research workflows, or otherwise changing how modeling work gets done. Strong programming skills in Python and SQL; able to go from question to working analysis or model without a large support structure. Strong communication and collaboration skills; able to work with Finance, Risk, Product, and Engineering. Ability to thrive in a fast-paced environment and ship work that gets used. Master's or PhD in a quantitative field such as statistics, mathematics, engineering, economics, physics, or a related discipline, and at least 6–10 years of relevant experience. Equivalent industry experience is welcome. Nice to Have Background in brokerage, trading, fintech, or another domain where revenue and risk decisions have real consequences. Experience putting models or agents into production with engineering partners.

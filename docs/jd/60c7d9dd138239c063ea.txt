Job Description Columbia Threadneedle Investments, the global asset management subsidiary of Ameriprise Financial, is seeking a Technology (Software/Hardware) Analyst to be based in Boston, MA or New York, NY. Responsibilities: Conduct primary research on the broader technology industry and specific companies through expert and channel checks, earnings calls, management interactions, company visits, sell-side or industry conferences, and investor and analyst days. Identify technology innovations and secular shifts (e.g., AI, cloud infrastructure, cybersecurity, semiconductors, data center and enterprise hardware) that could lead to large market opportunities. Formulate hypotheses on technology trends and competitive dynamics, and identify investment ideas that best leverage those hypotheses. Identify and maintain a catalyst calendar covering earnings, product launches, customer events, regulatory developments, M&A, and other events relevant to covered industries. Build and maintain detailed financial models and keep them current. Proactively work with Technology team analysts and portfolio managers to develop and refine investment theses. Share field intelligence from conferences, expert calls, and management interactions with the broader investment team promptly. Required Qualifications: Bachelor's degree in engineering, computer science, finance, economics, or a related field, or equivalent experience. 7-10 years of relevant experience in equity research, investment banking, consulting, or an operating role at a software, hardware, or semiconductor company. Strong understanding of software business models (SaaS, subscription, consumption-based pricing) and hardware supply chains and product cycles. Ability to analyze company fundamentals, including unit economics, ARR and retention metrics, margin structure, and capital intensity. Strong analytical, quantitative, and financial modeling skills. Clear and concise written and verbal communication skills. Preferred Qualifications: MBA and/or CFA. Technical background in software engineering, systems architecture, or electrical engineering. Ability to create detailed financial models, valuation analyses, and written reports to support stock recommendations.

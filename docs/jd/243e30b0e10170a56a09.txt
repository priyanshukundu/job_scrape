Our Impact You will join Goldman Sachs Global Investment Research as an Equity Research Associate supporting the Healthcare and Pharmaceuticals team in New York. The team conducts fundamental equity research across the large-cap pharmaceutical and biotechnology sectors, evaluating company fundamentals, earnings trends, product portfolios, pipelines, competitive dynamics and broader industry developments. Your Impact Associates play an integral role in the research process, with significant responsibility for financial modeling, company and industry analysis, earnings preparation and the development of differentiated investment research. The role provides exposure to senior research analysts, institutional investors and corporate management teams and is well suited for an analytically rigorous, intellectually curious individual interested in building a long-term career in Equity Research. Job Summary and Responsibilities Support fundamental equity research across the large-cap pharmaceutical sector, developing a deep understanding of company fundamentals, therapeutic franchises, product portfolios and industry dynamics. Build, maintain and enhance detailed financial and valuation models, including company forecasts, earnings estimates, product-level revenue assumptions and scenario analyses. Analyze company earnings, guidance, financial statements and operating trends and assess their implications for forecasts, valuation and investment views. Evaluate key drivers of pharmaceutical company performance, including commercial trends, product launches, market share, competitive positioning, pipeline developments and patent or exclusivity dynamics. Conduct primary and secondary research using company filings, industry data, clinical and regulatory information, management commentary and other relevant research sources. Monitor company-specific and sector developments and proactively identify issues, catalysts and emerging themes that may affect earnings expectations or investment theses. Contribute to company research, earnings notes, thematic reports, industry analyses, client presentations and other research publications. Synthesize complex financial, industry and scientific information into clear, investment-relevant conclusions. Support communication of research views and findings to institutional investors, the Equities Sales force, company management teams and internal stakeholders. Work collaboratively across the Healthcare research franchise while developing increasing ownership of individual companies and areas of coverage. Desired Qualifications Bachelor’s degree in Finance, Economics, Accounting, Business, Life Sciences or a related field. 2 plus years of relevant experience in sell-side equity research, buy-side investment research, investment banking, valuation or another highly analytical finance role. Prior exposure to pharmaceuticals, biotechnology or the broader healthcare sector is strongly preferred, particularly experience analyzing large-cap pharmaceutical companies. Strong financial modeling skills with the ability to build, navigate and maintain detailed company models and translate operating assumptions into earnings and valuation outcomes. Strong understanding of accounting, corporate finance and valuation methodologies. Ability to analyze both financial and industry-specific drivers and understand how product, pipeline, competitive and market developments affect company forecasts and valuation. Strong research skills with the ability to independently gather, evaluate and synthesize information from company materials, industry sources and relevant pharmaceutical data. Exceptional attention to detail and a disciplined approach to validating assumptions, reconciling data and maintaining accurate financial models. Intellectual curiosity and the ability to connect disparate pieces of information, ask thoughtful questions and develop differentiated investment insights. Proactive, self-directed approach with the ability to identify relevant developments and pursue research questions without waiting for direction. Strong written and verbal communication skills and the ability to explain complex analysis clearly and concisely. Collaborative, coachable and comfortable working in a fast-paced, team-oriented research environment. Demonstrated interest in fundamental investing and a desire to build a long-term career in Equity Research.

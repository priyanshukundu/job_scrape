JPMorganChase's Firmwide Financial Control organization plays a critical role in safeguarding the integrity of the firm's financial and regulatory reporting. Our Banking Asset Class Control team partners across technology, operations, and business lines to deliver scalable control solutions that protect the firm's books and records. Join a high-visibility team where your analytical expertise and control mindset will directly influence how we manage risk and drive continuous improvement across loans and deposits products. As a Banking Asset Class Control Associate within the Firmwide Financial Control organization, you will provide subject matter expertise to ensure robust financial control processes across general ledger and regulatory reporting for loans and deposits products. You will partner with technology, operations, and front-to-back stakeholders to investigate and resolve data breaks, strengthen control design, and deliver scalable solutions that enhance the firm's control framework. Additionally, you will support balance sheet and profit and loss reconciliations, manual accounting entries, month-end close coordination, and executive-ready control reporting.

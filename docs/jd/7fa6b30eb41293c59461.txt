Your Team, Your Impact Marvell’s Central Engineering Foundational IP team is seeking a Senior Staff SRAM Software Engineer to join our Burlington, VT custom SRAM team. Our Custom SRAMs are enabled with the support of a software focused team to develop infrastructure, tooling, and views for memory IP, as well as create memory optimization solutions for chip designs. The ideal candidate will have a strong computer science background along with EE fundamentals, preferably in the semiconductor industry. What You Can Expect In this position, you will: Work closely with SRAM circuit designers to implement PPA (Power, Performance, Area) data to create design kits. Create systems to optimize SRAM memory solutions for SoC designers. Develop new solutions to meet emerging market needs for semiconductor IP. What We're Looking For Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Engineering, Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, or a related field. 5+ years of hands-on software development experience, preferably in the semiconductor industry. Functional programming (Clojure desirable), and Java programming. Scripting (Perl, Python, Bash) in a command line environment. Strong background developing in a Linux environment. Well versed with version control systems (Git, GitHub). Ability to work effectively in a collaborative team environment. Ability to work independently and manage project deadlines effectively. Strong verbal and written communication skills. Preferred Qualifications Experience with SRAM architecture. Proficiency with EDA views (e.g. ATPG, TCD, Verilog). Experience with software system architecture.

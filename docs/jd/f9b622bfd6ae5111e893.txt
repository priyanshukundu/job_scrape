Lead Software Engineer, Developer Experience
Nasdaq
Location: USA - Atlanta - Georgia
Link: https://nasdaq.wd1.myworkdayjobs.com/Global_External_Site/job/USA---Atlanta---Georgia/Lead-Software-Engineer--Developer-Experience_R0027325

As a Lead/Principal Software Engineer reporting to the Sr. Director - Software Engineering, you'll play a critical role in shaping the technical direction of Nasdaq's new Developer Experience team — building AI-powered tools and capabilities that help engineers plan, build, test, and ship software faster and with greater confidence. You'll thrive in this position if you're curious, hands-on, and collaborative, with a passion for solving complex technical challenges and elevating the engineers around you in a fast-paced, high-impact global environment. Key Responsibilities Set the technical direction and engineering standards for AI-assisted workflows across planning, building, testing, and shipping software, with approaches that scale across the organization. Stay hands-on by building prototypes and production capabilities alongside the team, using real engineering problems to identify opportunities for speed, efficiency, and quality improvement. Lead the design of engineer-centric AI agents — including defect triage and code quality tooling — keeping engineers in the decision-making seat. Build and maintain a shared view of applications, their interdependencies, and ownership, so AI agents have the context needed to effectively support engineers. Mentor engineers and expand Developer Experience capabilities over time into adjacent areas such as client service, legal, and operations. Required Qualifications Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field, or equivalent practical experience. 10+ years of software engineering experience, including leading technical teams or large-scale initiatives and serving as a subject matter expert who sets standards and shapes key decisions. Proven ability to design and deliver complex solutions end to end, applying sound judgment in ambiguous situations and identifying dependencies and risks across teams early. Deep expertise in modern engineering practices, including AI-assisted development, Git, CI/CD, and Agile delivery. Clear communicator with the ability to influence senior stakeholders and collaborate effectively across global, matrixed environments. Preferred Qualifications Hands-on experience building and operating engineer-centric generative or agentic AI solutions in production. Experience in capital markets, fintech, or a regulated industry. Track record of driving adoption of new tools or practices across engineering organizations. This position will be located in Atlanta, Georgia and offers the opportunity for a hybrid work environment at least 3 days a week in-office, subject to change, providing flexibility and accessibility for qualified candidates.

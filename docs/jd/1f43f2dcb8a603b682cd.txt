City San Mateo State California Job Location San Mateo Corp Office Position Type Regular The Private Equity Accountant will be a key member of Essex's co-investment team based in our San Mateo, California office and will report directly to the Private Equity Manager. This role provides hands-on responsibility for accounting, financial analysis, and reporting related to Essex's co-investment activities, with a primary focus on supporting internal stakeholders and business operations. In this role, you'll work closely with a small, collaborative team and gain exposure to the accounting and financial reporting processes that support Essex's multifamily investment portfolio. WHAT YOU WILL DO: Review and analyze property-level financial statements to ensure accuracy, completeness, and compliance with accounting standards. Prepare joint venture accounting entries, including fair value adjustments and investment-related transactions. Assist in the preparation of quarterly joint venture financial reporting packages for internal and external stakeholders. Develop a working knowledge of partnership and joint venture agreements to support investment-related accounting activities. Perform internal control and audit-related procedures to help maintain the integrity of financial reporting. Monitor and manage cash activity across investment structures and partnerships. Prepare capital call and distribution notices while supporting investor reporting requirements. Partner with external auditors and tax advisors during annual audits and tax return preparation. Assist in the preparation and review of annual property budgets and forecasts. Contribute to special projects and cross-functional initiatives for the co-investment team, investors, and lending partners. WHAT THE JOB REQUIRES: Bachelor's degree (B.A./B.S.) in Accounting, Finance, or a related field. 3+ years of progressive accounting experience, preferably within real estate, private equity, investment management, or a related industry. Experience collaborating with internal and external auditors. Strong understanding of accounting principles and working knowledge of GAAP. Advanced Excel skills required; experience with Yardi or similar real estate accounting systems preferred. This position requires the ability to work Monday through Friday. It includes a hybrid work arrangement with in‑person office requirements for a minimum of four days per week (Monday through Thursday) at Essex’s corporate office in San Mateo. The hybrid policy is subject to change at Essex’s sole discretion. Work is primarily conducted in an office environment and requires regular use of computers and standard office equipment. Requires the ability to sit or stand for extended periods and perform standard office-related activities. No travel is required for this position. WHAT YOU WILL BRING TO THE TABLE: Strong analytical and critical-thinking skills with the ability to identify issues, interpret financial information, and recommend solutions. Exceptional attention to detail and organizational skills, with the ability to manage multiple priorities in a deadline-driven environment. A collaborative mindset and a desire to build strong working relationships across accounting, finance, operations, and investment teams. Effective written and verbal communication skills, including the ability to clearly explain financial concepts to a variety of audiences. Ability to work independently, prioritize competing deadlines, and maintain a high level of accuracy. A continuous improvement mentality with a willingness to learn, adapt, and contribute to process enhancements.

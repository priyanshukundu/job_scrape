Job Description: Job Title CLO & Private Credit Services – Account Administrator Corporate Title Analyst Location Jacksonville, FL Overview As an Account Administrator within CLO & Private Credit Services, you will support a global business that provides trustee, calculation agent, collateral administration, custodial, loan administration, and paying and transfer agent services for more than 400 SPVs across the United States, Europe, and Asia. You will oversee cash management, mutual fund investments, FX trades, non-USD wires, cash ledger reconciliations, and the identification of uninvested cash across our CLO products. By managing the work of four to six team members and maintaining strong operational controls, you will help ensure activity is completed accurately, on time, and in line with client expectations. Through close partnership with Account Managers and your team, you will resolve client inquiries and escalations promptly while strengthening the reliable service that supports our clients and the organization. What We Offer You · A diverse and inclusive environment that embraces change, innovation, and collaboration · A hybrid working model, allowing for in-office / work from home flexibility, generous vacation, personal and volunteer days · Employee Resource Groups support an inclusive workplace for everyone and promote community engagement. Competitive compensation packages including health and wellbeing benefits, retirement savings plans, parental leave, and family building benefits · Educational resources, matching gift and volunteer programs What You’ll Do · Support cash administration, mutual fund investments, FX trades, non-USD wires, cash ledger reconciliations, and the identification of uninvested cash for our CLO products · Coordinate assigned work across four to six team members and help ensure all tasks are completed accurately and on time · Review wire processing and support the accurate journalization and reconciliation of daily cash and investment activity · Support day-to-day operational and client service activities while monitoring key controls, including reviews of overdrawn accounts and break reports · Partner closely with Account Managers to ensure client inquiries and escalations are addressed accurately and promptly · Collaborate closely with team members and Account Managers supporting various deal families. As required, support the monitoring of timesheets, completion of mandatory training, and red-flag scenarios Skills You’ll Need · Cash administration experience in the CLO industry or relevant operations, including payments, settlements, reconciliations, and stakeholder management. · Experience supporting team workflows, operational reconciliations, and stakeholder engagement · Experience using the SOLVAS operating system · Working knowledge of general accounting, financial reporting principles, and structured credit products · Strong Microsoft Excel skills, including formulas and financial modelling techniques, with proficiency in Microsoft Office applications Proven ability to leverage AI tools to enhance productivity, optimize workflows to solve business problems, while applying critical judgment to ensure responsible and ethical use of data and AI outputs Skills That Will Help You Excel · Strong written and verbal communication skills, with fluency in written and spoken English · Strong organizational skills and the ability to manage competing priorities accurately and on time · A collaborative approach to supporting team members, Account Managers, and other stakeholders · A detail-oriented and control-focused approach to cash, investment, and reconciliation activities · Bachelor’s degree in Accounting, Finance, Business, Economics, or a related field preferred Expectations It is the Bank’s expectation that employees hired into this role will work in the Jacksonville office in accordance with the Bank’s hybrid working model.

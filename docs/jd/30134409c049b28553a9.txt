At Cadence, we hire and develop leaders and innovators who want to make an impact on the world of technology. A T2 Software Engineer is responsible for developing and validating software components under moderate supervision. This role focuses on implementing assigned features, debugging issues, and ensuring code quality by following established engineering standards and development processes. The engineer works on well-defined modules within a larger system, collaborates closely with senior team members for design guidance, and contributes to testing, integration, and continuous improvement of the product. The position requires solid programming fundamentals, problem-solving skills, and the ability to learn and apply new technologies within a structured engineering environment.

As a Data Engineer on the Schwab Technology Services Insights team, you will build and support enterprise data warehouse solutions that enable reliable analytics and reporting. You will use Informatica, advanced SQL, and dimensional data modeling to develop scalable data pipelines, improve data quality, and deliver accurate information to business partners. Your work will include designing and optimizing ETL and ELT processes, validating production results, resolving complex data issues, and strengthening monitoring, audit, lineage, and recovery controls. You will collaborate with business analysts, architects, quality assurance teams, product owners, and production support partners to translate requirements into effective technical solutions. Success in this role requires analytical thinking, sound decision-making, adaptability, and a commitment to continuous improvement. What you have Required Qualifications Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, Engineering, a related technical field, or equivalent practical experience. 5+ years of experience in data engineering, data integration, ETL development, or data warehousing. 3+ years of hands-on experience designing, developing, and supporting enterprise data pipelines using Informatica. Experience developing mappings, mapping tasks, taskflows, reusable components, parameterized processes, scheduling, monitoring, and error-handling solutions. Advanced SQL skills with experience developing complex transformations, reconciling data, tuning performance, and validating production results. Experience with dimensional data modeling, fact and dimension design, slowly changing dimensions, and ETL or ELT patterns. Demonstrated analytical thinking and problem-solving skills used to diagnose data issues, perform root-cause analysis, and implement sustainable solutions. Experience translating business requirements into technical designs, transformation rules, source-to-target specifications, and test scenarios. Experience with Google Cloud data services such as BigQuery, Cloud Storage, Dataflow, Cloud Composer, Pub/Sub, Cloud Run, or Dataproc.

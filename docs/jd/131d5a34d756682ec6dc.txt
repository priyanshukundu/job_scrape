Role Overview Western Union is seeking a talented Senior Software Engineer to design, develop, and deliver high-quality APIs, microservices, and cloud-native solutions that power our core money transfer platform (1P). In this role, you will work closely with product managers, architects, UX teams, and fellow engineers to build scalable, secure, and resilient systems that support critical business capabilities and customer experiences. The ideal candidate combines strong Java and cloud engineering expertise with a passion for modern software development practices, distributed systems, and continuous innovation. Experience in the fintech, payments, or regulated financial services industry is highly desirable. Key Responsibilities Design, develop, test, and deploy large-scale distributed systems, microservices, APIs, cloud-native platforms, and event-driven architectures on AWS using Java, Spring Boot, and related technologies. Collaborate with product, architecture, UX, and engineering teams to deliver end-to-end solutions that meet business and customer needs. Build secure, scalable, and highly available services that support enterprise-level transaction processing and business operations. Participate in system design, architecture reviews, and technical planning activities. Ensure software quality through test-driven development (TDD), automated testing, code reviews, and engineering best practices. Contribute to CI/CD pipelines, deployment automation, observability, monitoring, and operational excellence. Troubleshoot production issues and participate in root cause analysis and continuous improvement efforts. Leverage AI-enabled engineering tools aNd practices to improve software quality, developer productivity, code generation, testing, documentation, and delivery effectiveness. Mentor junior engineers and contribute to a culture of technical excellence, collaboration, and continuous learning. Role Requirements Master's degree with 4 years of relevant experience, Bachelor's degree with 6 years of relevant experience, or equivalent practical experience. 4+ years of hands-on software engineering experience developing secure enterprise applications using Java and Spring Boot. 3+ years of experience designing, developing, and debugging RESTful APIs and microservices in distributed systems, including implementing secure application design principles such as authentication, authorization, and data protection. 3+ years of experience designing and operating cloud-native applications on AWS using services such as ECS, Lambda, API Gateway, S3, and related technologies. 3+ years of experience with CI/CD pipelines, Docker, and modern DevOps practices in Agile development environments. 1+ years of experience leveraging AI-enabled engineering practices to improve software quality, developer productivity, code generation, automated testing, documentation, and delivery effectiveness. Preferred Qualifications Experience in the Financial Services industry. AWS certification(s) preferred (e.g., AWS Certified Solutions Architect, Developer, or equivalent). Hands-on experience building and operating cloud-native platforms in AWS, Azure, or hybrid environments. Experience with containerized microservices, Kubernetes, and modern API platforms. Awareness of emerging AI development tools and practices. Work Shift HYBRID - Western Union values in-person collaboration, problem solving, and ideation whenever possible. We believe this fosters common ways of working and supports how we execute initiatives for our customers. The expectation is to work from the office a minimum of three days a week.

Company: Marsh Description: Associate II – Broking We are seeking a talented individual to join the Broking team at Marsh as an Associate II – Broking . This role will support clients by analyzing risks, developing tailored insurance solutions, negotiating transactions, and managing relationships with clients, insurers, reinsurers, and colleagues. The Associate II will independently manage assignments involving moderately complex risks while supporting senior Brokers on larger or more complex client needs. We will count on you to: Review risk analyses prepared by junior Brokers and develop innovative solutions for client needs. Advise clients on moderately complex risks and negotiate transactions on their behalf. Respond independently to client questions and concerns, resolving issues promptly. Oversee the timely preparation and follow-up of account, program, and policy documentation. Identify and pursue opportunities to win new clients and projects. Independently manage assignments and support senior Brokers serving larger or more complex clients. Build strong relationships with clients, insurers, reinsurers, and colleagues. Apply knowledge of coverage lines, insurance markets, and specialized products to develop client solutions. Review contract documents, bond forms, and banking terms and conditions as needed. Educate clients on the terms and conditions of General Agreements of Indemnity supporting surety transactions. Review financial statements and apply knowledge of industry-specific financial metrics. Perform other job-related duties as assigned. What makes you stand out? Experience analyzing moderately complex risks and developing insurance solutions. Knowledge of coverage lines, insurance markets, and specialized insurance products. Experience with surety transactions, bond forms, or General Agreements of Indemnity. Ability to analyze financial statements across multiple industries. Experience building relationships with clients, insurers, and reinsurers.

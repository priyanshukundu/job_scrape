Responsibilities The Property & Casualty Data team is seeking an experienced Data Engineer/Lead Data Engineer to join us! The Data Engineer will support the Property & Casualty Data tool for z/OS platform and associated data modernization initiatives. This role is responsible for engineering, maintaining, and supporting reliable data pipelines that extract data from legacy systems, make it available through tools, and deliver enterprise data into AWS for analytics and downstream consumption. This role plays an important part in the continued modernization of Property & Casualty data capabilities, helping transition critical data from legacy platforms to scalable cloud-based solutions while maintaining data accuracy, availability, security, and operational reliability. This position sits within Enterprise Technology and offers the opportunity to work in a highly collaborative environment focused on delivering meaningful business impact. You will partner across teams, solve complex data challenges, and help shape modern data solutions aligned to enterprise goals. It is a strong fit for someone who wants to expand their technical skills, work on high-visibility initiatives, and contribute in a fast-moving, innovative setting. This is a great opportunity to apply skills in S3, Glue, Athena, and enterprise data lake capabilities to create secure, scalable, and maintainable data solutions to simplify legacy processing, improve performance, automate operational activities, reduce technical debt, and transition appropriate workloads from Mainframe-based solutions to modern cloud architectures.

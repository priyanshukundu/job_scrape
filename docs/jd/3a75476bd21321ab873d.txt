ABOUT THE ROLE: The Associate, Human Capital Strategy role works closely with leaders and key internal stakeholders to drive targeted Human Capital strategies and bank-wide initiatives to ensure Fifth Third has the right workforce to achieve business goals and remain competitive in a dynamic marketplace. This role is responsible for curating structured external research and internal analysis to support the development of the organization’s Human Capital strategy/strategies. This position works with and influences division leaders to gather and synthesize data on their current and historical performance and operations to identify strategic and operational improvement opportunities, develop recommendations, and may collaborate to develop and deploy the initial implementation plan, particularly for net new strategic initiatives. Responsible and accountable for risk by openly exchanging ideas and opinions, elevating concerns, and personally following policies and procedures as defined. Accountable for always doing the right thing for customers and colleagues, and ensures that actions and behaviors drive a positive customer experience. While operating within the Bank's risk appetite, achieves results by consistently identifying, assessing, managing, monitoring, and reporting risks of all types. YOUR RESPONSIBILITIES: The responsibilities for this role are below; you may support projects of varying complexity based on skill and organizational priorities. Research Planning: Identify and analyze opportunities and develop and execute thoughtful, targeted internal and/or external research plans given a broad research task with loosely defined objectives. Quantitative Analysis: Select and effectively execute the most appropriate quantitative analysis techniques for the problem at hand and craft compelling narrative summaries of the results to aid in data-driven decision making. Problem Solving and Recommendations: Structure problems, synthesize findings, develop creative and innovative options, and make logically supported recommendations tailored to the Fifth Third environment. Cross-Functional Partnership: Partner across teams within and outside of Human Capital in a collaborative way on key initiatives to create the best value proposition for the organization through disciplined planning and execution. Processes, Tools and Frameworks: Create processes, tools and frameworks to enable structured and efficient collaborative work. Tailored Communication: Tailor communications in method and tone based on the intended audience and purpose, ensuring communications are effective in achieving the intended goal. Meeting Facilitation: Facilitate meetings and working sessions with mid- to senior-level leadership with confidence and presence. Other Duties: Performing other duties as assigned. MINIMUM REQUIREMENTS: This position requires an experienced level of proficiency. We require a Bachelor’s degree, along with four to six years of professional experience, or an equivalent combination of education and progressive business leadership experience.

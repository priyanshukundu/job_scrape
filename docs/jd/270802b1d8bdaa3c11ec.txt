Principal AI/ML Research Scientist — Investment Management Fintech Strategies (IMFS) About the role: IMFS is Vanguard's applied AI and machine learning research team for investment management. We adapt state-of-the-art ML and AI to modernize how Vanguard invests, with a focus on fixed income and systematic credit. You'll work on hard, open problems with direct investment impact: Predictive signals from bond time series and unstructured data A knowledge layer that links issuers, macro themes and market events into structured intelligence. Autonomous research systems that generate, test and evaluate investment hypotheses on their own. This is a hands-on research role for someone who wants to build, test and see their work used in real investment decisions. What you'll do: Own research end to end: hypothesis, experiment design, validation, overfitting controls and deployment. Develop ML and deep learning models on large, noisy financial datasets to find and back-test predictive signals. Build NLP, information extraction and knowledge-graph methods that turn research and news into decision-ready intelligence. Prototype agentic AI for multi-step research tasks, including reasoning, planning, tool use and memory. Design benchmarks and evaluation frameworks that make AI-assisted research reliable, grounded and interpretable. Write production-quality code and partner with investment and technology teams to put models into production. What you'll bring: 6+ years of hands-on ML/AI research experience. PhD preferred; Master's required. A track record of taking ML ideas from theory to measurable impact. Deep expertise in modern ML: transformers, sequence models, representation learning and robust validation. Hands-on experience and deep expertise with LLMs, agentic systems, retrieval or knowledge graphs, and AI evaluation. Expert Python skills with modern ML frameworks and sound engineering practices. The ability to explain complex ideas clearly to investment professionals. Fixed income, credit or investment management experience preferred, not required. Curiosity about markets is essential.

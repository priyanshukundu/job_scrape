Leidos Energy is seeking a Regional Portfolio Manager to lead the Mountain & Pacific Region of the Electric Distribution Solutions Division . Reporting directly to the Division Manager, you will be the single accountable leader for the region, owning its operational and financial results, its client relationships, and the engineering teams serving a portfolio of electric utility clients across the West. You will set the region’s direction and deliver against it. That means growing and retaining clients through one unified client strategy, matching staffing, and resources to demand, holding programs to disciplined forecasting and utilization, and ensuring every program is executed safely, to a high standard of quality, and achieving financial metrics. You will run a regular portfolio review rhythm, drive adoption of our digital tools and standard practices, and build a team culture grounded in safety and accountability. The successful candidate will have a strong background in electric utility engineering, project and program management, business development, and operational leadership, with experience supporting electric and gas distribution projects. Key Responsibilities Regional Leadership & Decision-Making Authority Exercise delegated Authority to Operate (ATO) in accordance with Leidos' Proposal and Change Order Approval Policy. Provide strategic direction and operational leadership for the region, ensuring alignment with divisional objectives. Lead regional project engineering teams and oversee successful execution of engineering and construction support programs. Client Relationships & Business Development Own and grow relationships with utility clients throughout the assigned region, including executive-level engagement. Partner directly with electric utility clients to understand project requirements, from engineering design through construction support. Lead regional business development efforts, identify growth opportunities, and provide ongoing sales support. Oversee client relationships, project scope development, and contract execution. Identify, assess, and mitigate program and business risks while ensuring client satisfaction. Proposal Development & Financial Management Lead proposal development and preparation efforts, including final approval authority within established delegation limits for the Power Delivery Services Division. Drive regional revenue growth, financial profitability, and overall business performance. Oversee project budgets, financial forecasting, resource utilization, and operational efficiency. Ensure projects and programs meet established financial, schedule, and quality objectives. Engineering Leadership & Resource Management Lead and develop multidisciplinary project engineering teams, including direct-report management responsibilities. Make staffing decisions, oversee workforce planning, and allocate resources to support regional project demands. Provide leadership and oversight for electric and gas distribution engineering projects, including the development of construction work packages utilizing utility-specific software systems, design tools, and construction standards. Champion employee development, performance management, and succession planning to build a high-performing regional team. Operational Accountability & Program Execution Hold full accountability for regional performance across safety, quality, schedule, financial results, and client satisfaction. Oversee all aspects of day-to-day regional operations and project execution. Ensure engineering deliverables, construction support activities, and project management practices meet client requirements and industry standards. Drive continuous improvement initiatives that enhance operational efficiency, project delivery, and profitability. Foster a culture of safety, accountability, collaboration, and technical excellence. Location & Travel Requirements Location: Candidates must reside within the Mountain or Pacific region to effectively support regional operations, utility clients, and business development activities. Travel: Anticipated travel of 25–50% throughout the assigned region to support client engagements, project oversight, business development, and operational leadership responsibilities. Basic Qualifications: Bachelor's degree in Engineering (Electrical, Mechanical, Civil, or Industrial preferred) with 10+ years of relevant experience; or Master's degree with 8+ years of relevant experience. May consider additional experience in lieu of degree. 6+ years of experience supervising or leading teams or projects. Experience with managing P&L. Desired Skills and Attributes Professional Engineering Registration (NCEES Record preferred). Experience with complex projects in distribution design (Overhead and Underground) Capability to lead all aspects of a large engineering team with ability to develop staff through mentoring Sales pipeline creation. Work effectively in team environment with the ability work also independently. Proven ability to communicate with clients and project teams. Successful project management experience (initiate, plan, execute, monitor & control, closeout) Knowledge of engineering estimates, construction cost estimating, specification, material procurement process, project schedule creation, and construction work packages.

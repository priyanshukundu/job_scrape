Financial Analyst, Environmental Projects Reporting to the Finance Project Manager, the Financial Analyst will be an integral member of the Environmental Projects Group performing both accrual-based and cash-flow forecasting and analysis, monthly project financial reporting, monthly accruals, KPIs, financial modeling and various other project and FP&A activities. This role will also support the annual budgeting process and will require some travel to the project sites. The role is an excellent opportunity to learn about a wide variety of business activities while interacting with multiple teams including Plant Projects, Environmental, Accounting, and FP&A. The Business Analyst should have an ability to collaborate across the company and develop and maintain working relationships at the plants to facilitate project costing and reporting. The preferred location for this position is either Berwick or Allentown, Pennsylvania. Primary Responsibilities · Work alongside the Project Finance Manager to collect and consolidate data to support development of the annual budgets for Environmental Projects at Talen sites. Coordinate as necessary with Environmental Department team members, plants, contractors, consultants and other corporate groups. · Perform analysis of actual financial performance and variances to plan and prior forecasts to prepare the monthly forecast updates. · Develop new and maintain / optimize existing reporting tools and processes (financial, project and others) necessary for preparation of reports, analysis and other materials for Environmental Projects Leadership and external parties. · Perform detailed analysis on potential project options (including modeling, sensitivity analysis, costing) and provide reporting that outlines those options along with potential outcomes and any associated risks and opportunities. · Partner with various internal and external parties, including but not limited to project managers, plant personnel, accounting staff, auditors, consultants, and contractors to ensure strict and quick-turn around deadlines are met. · General support for day-to-day project and financial activities. · Complete other work as necessary to support Talen Finance. · Travel as required to job sites, meeting locations, conferences and corporate offices. Minimum Qualifications · Bachelor’s degree in a Business-related field with a minimum of 2 years experience in a professional environment. · Must be technologically proficient and have advanced Excel knowledge and experience (charts, pivot tables, macros, advanced functions) and be capable of creating executive-level presentations in PowerPoint. · Experience using Business Intelligence software for reporting is a plus. · Have a general understanding of project controls including Procurement, Scheduling, and Resource Loading. · Strong attention to detail, organizational skills, communication and reporting skills, and time management methods. · A proven track record of working with and understanding complex projects with very large budgets. · A proven track record of coordinating with various stakeholders to achieve strict deadlines. · An ability to gather facts and data and make informed decisions and recommendations while facing project uncertainty and time pressure. · Must have a general understanding of Accounting concepts such as assets and liabilities, debits and credits, accrual process, etc. Ability to travel within Talen Energy system and, on occasion, outside of Pennsylvania. Must have a valid driver’s license. Note: You will have an opportunity to add attachments to your application.

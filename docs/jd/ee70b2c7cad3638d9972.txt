The Secondary Marketing Analyst is an entry-level member of the Mortgage Capital Markets team responsible for supporting daily mortgage pricing, rate lock administration, and Capital Markets processes and reporting. The role provides hands-on exposure to mortgage pricing, secondary market execution, pipeline management, and interest rate risk while supporting critical daily activities within the Capital Markets function. Primary responsibilities include: Support daily lock desk activities, including new locks, extensions, relocks, loan changes, pricing exceptions, and related inquiries. Monitor the locked mortgage pipeline and assist with resolving pricing, data, and system exceptions. Support daily mortgage pricing and rate sheet generation, including maintenance and validation of pricing inputs, margins, adjustments, and product parameters. Perform pricing quality-control checks and assist with intraday repricing based on changes in market conditions. Execute and monitor recurring processes within QRM, including data loads, process runs, reconciliations, and validation of outputs. Identify QRM processing exceptions, data issues, or unusual results and coordinate resolution with appropriate partners. Produce recurring Capital Markets reports covering pricing, rate locks, pipeline activity, fallout, pull-through, and other secondary marketing metrics. Reconcile QRM and secondary marketing reporting to source systems and investigate discrepancies. Support testing and implementation of changes to QRM, pricing systems, data feeds, and Capital Markets reporting. Maintain process documentation and controls and identify opportunities to improve or automate recurring processes. Perform ad hoc analysis in support of Mortgage Capital Markets management. Qualifications, Education, Certifications and/or Other Professional Credentials Required Qualifications Bachelor’s degree in Finance, Economics, Accounting, Business, Mathematics, Data Analytics, or a related field. Strong analytical aptitude, attention to detail, and problem-solving skills. Proficiency in Microsoft Excel. Ability to execute accurately in a deadline-sensitive environment and manage multiple daily priorities. Strong written and verbal communication skills and ability to work effectively across business functions. Preferred Qualifications Prior mortgage banking, financial services, or analytical experience through employment or internship. Familiarity with mortgage products, secondary marketing, or QRM (Quantitative Risk Management). Experience with SQL, Power BI, VBA, Python, or other data and reporting tools.

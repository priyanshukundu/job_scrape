ABOUT THE ROLE 

Global Marketing at Nu is growing quickly, from the launch of our global partnerships with Mercedes-AMG PETRONAS F1 and Inter Miami at the beginning of this year to launching our Nu US business and our Nu Global product in September. As Business Analyst in Global Marketing you will build the measurement capability from the ground up and own data and insights for the team. This is a senior individual contributor role. You will choose the methods, set the standards, and decide what we measure and how. You will work closely with the Global Partnerships Managers for MF1 and IMCF, the Paid Media Specialist, the Growth lead, the Research team, and Product Business Analysts.



YOU’LL BE RESPONSIBLE FOR 

 - Lead Media Mix Modeling: Scope, help build and run MMM for middle and upper funnel paid media together with an agency. Actively contribute to the model design, own data inputs and turn the outputs into budget and optimization recommendations the Global Marketing team can act on.

 - Set up geo testing: Design and run geo experiments to measure incrementality Define hypotheses, market selection, sample sizes, duration and guardrails, and use the results to calibrate the MMM

 - Build ROAS and ROI models: Create models for marketing activities across Nu US, Nu Global and our global partnerships, so each investment can be compared on the same basis

 - Build full-funnel attribution: Define how marketing activity connects to customer acquisition and activation, from awareness through conversion. Agree one methodology with the product BAs and the Growth Lead

 - Connect research to business impact: Work with the research team to link brand, audience and customer insights to measurable outcomes, so qualitative findings influence marketing investment decisions

 - Own data and insights for Global Marketing: Be the single point of ownership for core marketing metrics and the dashboards leadership uses. Define the metrics, keep them consistent across teams, and make sure the team works from a single source of truth

 - Turn analysis into decisions: Take ambiguous business questions, structure the data, and give clear, executive-ready recommendations on where to invest and A/B test

 - Partner across the business: Align with research, product BAs, paid media and Finance on definitions, methods and priorities

 


WHAT WE'RE LOOKING FOR SOMEONE WHO HAS

 - Marketing measurement depth: Proven hands-on experience building or leading Media Mix Modeling, geo or incrementality testing. You have taken models from design to a decision someone acted on, and you can communicate and show the results.

 - ROI/ROAS ownership: You have built the models that show where marketing spend works and where it needs adjustment and testing. You can explain the assumptions, limitations and results to a diverse audience.

 - Technical skills: Knowledge in Python, SQL, Databricks, Airflow, AWS or other Cloud.

 - Senior judgment: You are proactive, set your own priorities and behave like an owner not a renter.

 - Research and data fluency: You are comfortable combining qualitative and quantitative inputs, and you know how to turn brand and customer research into something testable and actionable.

 - Executive communication: You turn complex findings into short, clear stories that influence senior stakeholders.

 - Cross-functional partnership: Experience working with marketing and product teams in a fast-moving environment, ideally across more than one market or region.

 - Comfortable with AI-enabled workflows: You already use AI tools in your work, you know its limitations and use it to your advantage while exercising critical thinking

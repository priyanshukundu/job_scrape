Wise is a global technology company, building the best way to move and manage the world’s money. Min fees. Max ease. Full speed. Whether people and businesses are sending money to another country, spending abroad, or making and receiving international payments, Wise is on a mission to make their lives easier and save them money. As part of our team, you will be helping us create an entirely new network for the world's money. For everyone, everywhere. More about our mission and what we offer . Wise is building the infrastructure for money with a clear ambition: to become the everyday account people rely on, whether they're managing money at home or across borders. Customers should be able to receive their salary, pay bills, earn on their balances, and move money internationally—all from a single account that is simple & transparent. As a Software Engineer on the North America Account team, you'll work on products that customers interact with every day. You'll own meaningful problems from discovery through production, improving the reliability and experience of receiving money, expanding access to local account capabilities, evolving our interest offering, and building the platform that powers Wise's everyday account experience in North America.

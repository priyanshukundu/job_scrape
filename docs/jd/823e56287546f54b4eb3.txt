About the Division Corporate Treasury at Goldman Sachs manages the firm's liquidity, funding, and deposit franchises. The Deposits Sweep Eng team engineers and operates the platform powering the firm's Corporate Deposits business - enabling institutional broker-dealers to place, manage, and reconcile deposit accounts through a suite of automated, event-driven systems. The Role We are seeking a Software Engineer to join the Deposits Sweep Eng team and contribute to our institutional deposits execution platform - microservices that handle broker account management, daily balance reconciliation, and payment processing. You will work across the stack to build, test, and maintain production-grade components that process time-sensitive financial instructions every business day. Over time, you will also contribute to a multi-year platform modernization effort, gaining exposure to greenfield architecture work alongside day-to-day delivery.

Role Summary We are seeking a Lead Software Engineer with deep expertise in Oracle Cloud technologies, including ERP, EPM, Oracle Integration Cloud (OIC), OTBI, BI Publisher, and APEX. This role will lead the design, development, integration, optimization, and support of Oracle Cloud ERP and EPM solutions, with responsibility for scalable integrations, reporting capabilities, custom application development, and enterprise solutions aligned with business objectives. Responsibilities Solution Architecture and Development: Design and build custom extensions, workflows, and integrations across Oracle Fusion Cloud ERP modules, including General Ledger, Accounts Payable, Accounts Receivable, Fixed Assets, Cash Management, and Financial Data Hub. Integration and Data Pipelines: Architect secure, scalable integrations using OIC, REST/SOAP APIs, file-based integrations, and event-driven patterns while supporting data migration, conversion, and reconciliation activities. ERP and EPM Delivery: Build and enhance Oracle ERP and EPM solutions, including configurations, custom extensions, financial process automation, and integrations across finance, procurement, Financial Data Hub, and enterprise performance management domains. Reporting and Analytics: Develop and optimize OTBI and BI Publisher reports, dashboards, and data models to support operational reporting, real-time analytics, and business decision-making. APEX Application Development: Design and deliver Oracle APEX applications that improve user experience, automate workflows, and extend Oracle Cloud capabilities. Technical Leadership: Lead technical design discussions, code reviews, implementation planning, and engineering best practices while mentoring developers and promoting reusable frameworks. System Optimization and Upgrades: Assess Oracle quarterly releases, patches, and roadmap changes, and evaluate impacts on configurations, customizations, and integrations. Governance, Security, and Compliance: Partner with business, audit, and compliance teams to ensure implementations align with SOX, ITGC, data security, RBAC, SSO, OAuth, and segregation of duties requirements. Production Support and Technical Debt Management: Provide L2/L3 production support, troubleshoot complex system and data issues, improve system availability, and manage technical debt. Problem Solving and Delivery Ownership: Own end-to-end delivery across design, development, testing, deployment, and support while making sound technical trade-offs with minimal guidance. Business Knowledge Apply a strong understanding of finance, procurement, and enterprise business processes within Oracle ERP and EPM ecosystems. Translate business requirements into scalable technology solutions aligned with enterprise architecture standards. Partner with stakeholders across business and technology teams to drive modernization, automation, and process improvement. Aligning engineering decisions with business priorities, data integrity, operational efficiency, and enterprise control requirements.

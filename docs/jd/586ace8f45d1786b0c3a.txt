Join our growing Dental organization and help drive profitable growth. You’ll partner with Underwriting and Sales teams to evaluate risk, develop competitive pricing strategies, and support both new and existing business across the Dental and Vision portfolio. What You’ll Do Manage a Dental and Vision book of business focused on growth, retention, and profitability. Assess financial and underwriting risk for new and existing accounts. Develop rates and account earnings strategies. Provide insights on market competition, products, pricing, and financial performance. Partner with Sales on case strategy, customer negotiations, and financial considerations. Monitor market trends and identify opportunities to improve portfolio performance. Help shape underwriting best practices, product development, and pricing strategies. Qualifications: 5+ years health benefits underwriting experience Experience with manual and experience rating, funding options, plan designs, and financial analysis. Strong analytical, technical, and problem-solving skills. Ability to assess financial risk and make sound business decisions. Strong understanding of market and competitive dynamics. Effective written and verbal communication skills. Ability to work independently while collaborating and influencing across teams. Strong prioritization skills and comfort working through ambiguity. Bachelor’s degree or equivalent relevant experience strongly preferred.

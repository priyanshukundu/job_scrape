Industry/Sector Banking and Capital Markets Specialism Financial Risk Management Level Director Job Description & Summary The Opportunity As a Risk & Reg Consulting - Regulatory Reporting/Capital Director you will guide financial institutions through regulatory reporting and capital challenges within our Advisory practice. In this client-facing role, you will connect treasury risk management with capital and liquidity planning, financial risk analysis, and regulatory compliance. Working with colleagues in modeling, data, and technology, you will shape reporting and analytics approaches that help banking and capital markets clients strengthen risk oversight and make informed capital decisions. This role brings together regulatory change, quantitative insights, and practical improvements to treasury operations. As a Director, you will set the direction for regulatory reporting and capital engagements, develop business opportunities, and build relationships with senior client leaders. You will oversee multiple engagements and make strategic choices that balance immediate reporting demands with longer-term risk priorities. By drawing on capabilities across the network, mentoring emerging leaders, and encouraging thoughtful use of technology, you will help teams develop approaches to complex financial risk questions. You will uphold professional standards and independence requirements while fostering quality, integrity, and inclusion alongside the practice’s commercial goals. Responsibilities - Setting the strategic direction for regulatory reporting and capital risk engagements, aligning treasury risk analysis and reporting processes with financial institutions’ regulatory requirements - Overseeing client projects that assess liquidity, credit, market, and model risks through quantitative finance, statistical modeling, financial records, and data analytics - Directing the design of capital and liquidity reporting capabilities that integrate risk data, financial accounting, internal controls, and documented policies - Advising executive stakeholders on cash management, capital allocation, and portfolio decisions informed by macroeconomic conditions, lending activity, and securities exposures - Guiding analysis of foreign exchange risk and hedging strategies alongside liquidity and credit exposures in commercial lending and collateralized loan obligation portfolios - Shaping risk technology solutions using automation, digital tools, AI-supported analytics, and rapid testing to modernize reporting workflows and evaluate model outputs - Developing client relationships and business opportunities by translating financial risk findings into clear recommendations and shaping service approaches around regulatory reporting needs - Coaching engagement leaders and teams on analytical methods and project delivery while reviewing work against applicable professional standards and regulatory requirements What You Must Have - At least a Bachelor's degree - At least 9 years of experience What Sets You Apart - Preference for at least one of the following fields of study: Accounting, Analytics/Data Science, Business Administration/Management, Computer Science/Information Systems, Economics, Engineering, Finance, Financial Mathematics/Quantitative Finance, Law/Legal Studies, Mathematics/Statistics, Risk Management/Insurance, Science - Leading regulatory reporting production, quality assurance, conformance, and transaction testing, including experience with reports such as FR Y-9C, Call Reports, FR Y-14, and FR 2052a, and related reporting controls and regulatory requirement - Directing regulatory reporting and capital initiatives for financial institutions - Leading quantitative modeling and analytics across credit, market, and liquidity risk - Integrating risk technology and automation into regulatory reporting operations - Guiding executive stakeholders through evolving regulatory expectations and strategic risk decisions - Developing business opportunities in capital, treasury, and financial risk management - Coaching teams across complex client engagements and cross-functional delivery Travel Requirements Up to 60% Job Posting End Date The salary range for this position is: $155,000 -

Job Overview: The Assistant Vice President (AVP) of Third-Party Risk Management is responsible for the development, implementation, and oversight of enterprise standards, governance, and risk management frameworks that address third-party risks introduced through advisor relationships. This role leads the design and execution of scalable capabilities to inventory, assess, monitor, and report on advisor-supported third parties, ensuring alignment with enterprise risk standards, regulatory expectations, and organizational policies. The Assistant Vice President (AVP) of Third-Party Risk Management serves as a key leader in strengthening the organization's ability to identify, assess, and manage advisor-related third-party risk while enabling business growth and regulatory compliance. The role provides strategic guidance, education, and consultation to internal stakeholders and advisors to drive consistent adoption of third-party risk management requirements and foster a strong risk-aware culture across LPL Financials’ diverse advisor network. The AVP will ensure LPL effectively: Develops and maintains scalable capabilities to inventory, monitor, assess, and escalate risks associated with advisor third-party relationships. Establishes and enforces risk management standards, methodologies, and processes to support continuous evaluation of advisor-introduced third-party risks. Provides guidance, education, and consultation to internal stakeholders and advisors regarding third-party risk requirements, expectations, and best practices. Ensures adherence to enterprise policies, governance requirements, and regulatory expectations through effective oversight, reporting, and risk transparency. This role plays a critical leadership function in protecting the organization from operational, compliance, reputational, and regulatory risks arising from third-party relationships while enabling scalable, efficient, and resilient advisor support models. Responsibilities: Lead the development, implementation, and ongoing enhancement of scalable advisor third-party risk management capabilities, including processes to inventory, monitor, assess, and escalate risks associated with third parties leveraged by advisors. Communicate, educate, and advise internal stakeholders and advisors on third-party risk management standards, requirements, and expectations, ensuring consistent understanding and adoption across the organization. Ensure compliance with enterprise policies, governance frameworks, and regulatory expectations by providing leadership with timely, accurate, and transparent reporting on key initiatives, risks, issues, trends, and potential business impacts. Partner closely with business leaders, compliance, legal, information security, procurement, and risk teams to ensure advisor-related third-party risks are appropriately identified, managed, and escalated. Foster a strong culture of risk awareness, accountability, and collaboration by promoting proactive risk identification, assessment, issue management, and remediation activities across business functions. Support Third-Party Risk Management governance activities and regulatory engagement by participating in governance forums, preparing materials for leadership review, and partnering with stakeholders to address internal audits, regulatory examinations, and remediation efforts.

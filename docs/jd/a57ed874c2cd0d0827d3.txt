We are looking to hire a Vice President, Non-Financial Risk Controls Group Lead to provide first-line controls support within the platform by strengthening the control environment, identifying and assessing risk, supporting issue remediation, and ensuring day-to-day activities align with BNY’s risk and expectations. Typical responsibilities Partner with platform leadership and SMEs to design, implement, and enhance controls across processes, change initiatives, and operational activities. Lead the timely identification, escalation, remediation and governance of operational errors in line with BNY policy and control standards, ensuring thorough root cause analysis, documented corrective actions, stakeholder transparency and actions to reduce recurrence Perform or coordinate risk and control assessments, including identifying gaps, documenting control design, and assessing effectiveness. Support issue management, including root cause analysis, remediation planning, and tracking actions to closure. Monitor control performance, KRIs/KPIs, incidents, and themes to identify emerging risks and recommend improvements. Ensure controls are appropriately embedded in business processes, governance routines, and platform change delivery. Prepare materials for governance, audit, regulatory, and management reviews. Provide coaching, healthy challenge and advisory support to platform stakeholders on policy adherence, process risk, and control requirements. Contribute to a culture of accountability, transparency, and continuous improvement. What “working directly within a platform” usually means: You are aligned to the platform’s priorities and delivery agenda, not operating as a fully separate central controls function. Your goals are often tied to platform OKRs, delivery risks, process quality, resiliency, and remediation outcomes. You translate enterprise control expectations into practical actions for the platform team. To be successful in this role, we’re seeking the following: Preferred deep industry/business/product knowledge and expertise in managed accounts, or more broadly across the Wealth/Asset Management/RIA industry. Ability to work in a fast-paced environment and manage multiple deadlines and priorities Good judgment in balancing risk, control, and business enablement Influencing skills with the ability to advocate for decisions Ability to develop AI and advances analytics capabilities to support the platform to optimize risk return trade-offs for the business Strong Understanding of a Risk and Control framework, Control design and Control effectiveness: o Strong issue management, analysis, and reporting experience with process, governance, and change management o Detail-oriented, with the ability to follow up on items or determine appropriate steps for timely escalation and/or resolution o Requires business, regulatory, and operational risk and control management expertise, especially for large complex financial institutions o Ability to interpret and apply Risk & Compliance policies and procedures o Ability to plan, syndicate and implement balanced risk and control strategies

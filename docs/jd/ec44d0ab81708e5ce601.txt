Job Family : Data Science Consulting Travel Required : Up to 10% Clearance Required : None What You Will Do Perform data analysis and support the development of analytical solutions using tools such as Python, R, SQL, Databricks, Power BI, GIS, and other modern analytics platforms. Work with project team members and subject matter experts to understand client business needs, analytical objectives, and technical requirements. Support the design, development, testing, and implementation of data, analytics, and AI-enabled solutions for utility clients. Develop and maintain analytical workflows, datasets, dashboards, visualizations, and other decision-support tools. Analyze data, identify trends and insights, and contribute to the development of findings and recommendations for technical and non-technical audiences. Contribute to high-quality client deliverables while collaborating with multidisciplinary project teams. What You Will Need Bachelor’s degree from an accredited university. Minimum 0-2 years of overall work experience. Experience in applying data analytics, business intelligence, data science, or related quantitative methods is preferred. Exposure to one or more analytical or data management tools such as Python, SQL, R, Databricks, Power BI, Tableau, GIS, or similar technologies. Experience analyzing datasets and using quantitative methods to develop insights, solve problems, or support decision-making. Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with attention to detail and a willingness to learn new tools, technologies, and business domains. US Citizenship is contractually required. What Would Be Nice To Have Experience or demonstrated interest in electric, gas, water, or other energy-sector organizations and challenges. Strong written and verbal communication skills, including the ability to explain analytical concepts and findings clearly. Ability to work effectively on multiple assignments while collaborating with project teams and stakeholders. Exposure to cloud-based analytics platforms, data engineering, AI, machine learning, geospatial analytics, or optimization techniques. Experience developing dashboards, visualizations, analytical models, data products, or decision-support tools through professional, academic, or personal projects. Relevant coursework, certifications, or advanced education in analytics, cloud technologies, data engineering, or related fields. The annual salary range for this position is $68,000.00-$113,000.00. Compensation decisions depend on a wide range of factors, including but not limited to skill sets, experience and training, security clearances, licensure and certifications, and other business and organizational needs.

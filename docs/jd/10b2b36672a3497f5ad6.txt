About this role: Seeking a Software Engineer to design, develop, and support enterprise reporting, automation, and collaboration solutions leveraging Microsoft Power BI and SharePoint. In this role, you will: This role will partner with business teams to deliver scalable dashboards, workflow automation, and data-driven solutions that improve operational efficiency, reporting accuracy and decision-making across the organization. · Design, develop, and maintain Power BI dashboards, reports, and semantic data models. · Develop and support SharePoint Online sites, workflows, forms, and collaboration solutions. · Automate business processes using Power Automate and integrate solutions across Microsoft 365 platforms. · Partner with business stakeholders to gather requirements and translate them into technical solutions. · Develop data integrations and reporting solutions to improve operational efficiency and decision-making. · Ensure solutions meet security, governance, and compliance requirements. · Troubleshoot production issues and implement enhancements. · Participate in testing and deployment activities. Participate in Agile ceremonies and deliver assigned work items. Collaborate with team members to support automation, compliance reporting, and operational improvement initiatives. Continuously develop technical knowledge through training and hands-on experience. Required Qualifications: Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Systems, Software Engineering, or related field, or equivalent work experience. 2+ years of experience developing business applications, reporting solutions, or enterprise collaboration platforms. Hands-on experience with Microsoft Power BI, including dashboards, reports, data modeling, DAX, and Power Query. Experience developing and administering Microsoft SharePoint sites, lists, libraries, workflows, and permissions. Proficiency with Power Platform components including Power Automate and Power Apps. Experience with SQL and relational database concepts. Knowledge of data integration and ETL processes. Experience using REST APIs and integrating Microsoft 365 services. Strong analytical, troubleshooting, and problem-solving skills. Ability to communicate technical concepts effectively to business stakeholders. Desired Qualifications: Proficiency in one or more programming languages such as C#, JavaScript, TypeScript, or Python. Knowledge of SharePoint Framework (SPFx) development.

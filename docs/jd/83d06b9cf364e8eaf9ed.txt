A hands-on role spanning code, data analysis, ML and applied GenAI. You'll execute well-scoped projects, flex as priorities shift, and build healthcare UM expertise quickly. Responsibilities: Deliver ML and data projects from analysis and experimentation through deployment and monitoring. Move between analytics requests, model work and engineering tasks as priorities shift. Write complex SQL and Python . Prepare, validate and analyze large datasets, including claims, authorizations, CPT/ICD-10 codes and clinical documents. Build, fine-tune and evaluate models using ML, NLP and LLM/RAG techniques, with guidance on complex problems. Execute experiments with sound baselines, metrics, validation and error analysis. Write production-quality code using Git, testing, code review and CI/CD , and help support systems in production. Learn UM workflows such as prior authorization, medical necessity and clinical criteria. Build with HIPAA, privacy and human oversight in mind. Explain findings clearly to technical, clinical and business stakeholders. Qualifications: 6-8 years plus of industry experience in ML, data science, software engineering or applied AI. BS/MS in CS, Data Science, Statistics or a related field, or equivalent experience. Strong Python and SQL skills, plus practical experience with at least some of: ML, NLP, deep learning or LLMs. Solid grounding in statistics, model evaluation and experimentation. Healthcare experience is a plus. You must show you can learn complex domains quickly . Adaptable, curious and effective in cross-functional teams. Works on well-defined projects and clarifies requirements with guidance. Builds components within the existing architecture. Grows toward owning larger problems.

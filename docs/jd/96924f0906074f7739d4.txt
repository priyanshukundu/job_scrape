About the Job Northwestern Mutual is seeking a Senior Product Manager to join our Finance and Risk organization and help advance the products and capabilities supporting Enterprise Risk and Corporate Audit. This role will help drive product initiatives focused on strengthening risk and audit capabilities while advancing operational efficiency, automation, integration, and modernization. As a Senior Product Manager, you will work across a complex landscape of business partners, engineering teams, business analysts, technology stakeholders, and vendor solutions. You will partner across the organization to understand business needs, identify opportunities, translate product strategy into actionable priorities, and deliver measurable business outcomes. You’ll help drive strategic initiatives from concept through execution while balancing business needs, risk considerations, technology dependencies, vendor capabilities, and organizational priorities. This role is ideal for a product leader who thrives in complexity and ambiguity, enjoys solving complex business problems, and excels at building relationships across diverse stakeholder groups. Success requires strong stakeholder influence, business acumen, systems thinking, exceptional communication skills, and the ability to create alignment and lead through collaboration rather than authority. What You’ll Do Help shape product direction and roadmaps aligned with Enterprise Risk and Corporate Audit priorities and broader Finance and Risk objectives. Lead product discovery to identify business needs, pain points, opportunities, and potential solutions across multiple risk and audit capabilities. Translate product strategy into actionable plans, capabilities, features, and measurable business outcomes. Partner with engineering and technology teams to deliver product capabilities from concept through implementation. Build strong partnerships across a diverse stakeholder landscape, including Enterprise Risk, Corporate Audit, business analysts, engineering teams, technology leaders, and vendor partners. Navigate a complex vendor technology landscape, evaluating vendor capabilities, dependencies, integrations, and opportunities to maximize business value from technology investments. Define and track KPIs, success measures, and business outcomes to evaluate product effectiveness and inform future priorities. Prioritize product work based on business value, risk considerations, dependencies, organizational objectives, and available capacity. Drive stakeholder alignment and clearly communicate priorities, product decisions, tradeoffs, risks, dependencies, and progress. Guide product delivery through ambiguity, evolving priorities, competing business needs, and complex dependencies. Support adoption and continuous improvement following implementation, incorporating stakeholder feedback and business outcomes into future product decisions. Evaluate opportunities for automation, AI, data, and emerging technologies that can strengthen Enterprise Risk and Corporate Audit capabilities and improve business processes. Facilitate the Sprint Planning, Sprint Reviews and Retrospectives. Work daily with the Scrum Master. Experience supporting modernization, process improvement, or operational transformation initiatives. AI or automation exposure. Business Analyst partnership experience. Scrum Master experience. Experience working across multiple business functions and technology teams.

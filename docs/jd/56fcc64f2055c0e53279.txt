Job Overview The Risk, Performance & Management (RPM) team is located in the new LEED certified Carolinas campus (Fort Mill, SC). As a Senior Third Party Risk Management Assessor, you will assess LPL's third party’s control environments, including escalation and remediation of identified third party issues with line of business partners, while providing robust and challenging insight on business risk, and on the adequacy and effectiveness of the third party’s controls. RPM's mission is to 1) Deliver sustainable, ethical practices and leadership to enable the selection of external partners to drive the best service, quality, and value for LPL while appropriately mitigating risk. 2) Provide leadership to our business partners through LPLs center-led Vendor Management Program to ensure that any contractual and performance risks are effectively mitigated. Responsibilities: Perform third party risk assessments by analyzing questionnaires such as Standard Information Gathering (SIG) third-party engagement profiles and due diligence evaluations. Manage all aspects of complex third party risk assessments that may entail collaboration with cross-functional internal teams. Create comprehensive, articulate and objective reports to effectively communicate and relate security, compliance, or governance-related concepts and controls across a variety of audiences including technical and non-technical audiences. Maintain a current understanding of relevant third party risk management best practices, regulations and Information Security Principles. Develop positive working relationships to maintain an open environment for collaboration, risk identification and remediation. Conduct Risk Assessments: Lead and execute comprehensive inherent and residual risk assessments of third parties to identify potential security threats, vulnerabilities, and compliance gaps. Meet individual goals as assigned by your manager. Due Diligence and Evidence Validation: Collect and analyze information and evidence from third parties, including security questionnaires (e.g., SIG, CAIQ), SOC 2 reports, ISO 27001 certifications, penetration test results, and other audit artifacts, to determine the effectiveness of their controls. Identify and Document Findings: Document identified security gaps, vulnerabilities, and non-compliance issues. Translate complex technical findings into business-level risks for stakeholders. Develop and Track Remediation Plans: Collaborate with vendors and internal business leaders to develop risk mitigation strategies and action plans. Monitor and track the progress of remediation efforts to ensure timely closure of vulnerabilities. Monitor Continuously: Support ongoing monitoring activities to track third-party compliance and performance, using tools like Security Scorecard or BitSight to detect posture changes or emerging risks in real-time. Ensure Regulatory Compliance: Verify that third-party vendors adhere to applicable laws, regulations, and industry standards such as GDPR, HIPAA, NIST, ISO 27001, and organizational policies. Collaborate with Stakeholders: Partner with cross-functional teams, including Legal, Procurement, IT, Compliance, and business units, to ensure a cohesive and standardized approach to third-party risk management. Support Audits and Reporting: Assist in internal and external audits and regulatory reviews by providing documentation and evidence of TPRM program activities. Create reports and executive dashboards summarizing vendor risk posture for senior management. Process Improvement: Proactively contribute to the development and enhancement of TPRM policies, procedures, and tools, including exploring automation and AI solutions to improve efficiency and effectiveness.

The Franklin Templeton US Product & Specialty Sales team is seeking an exceptional candidate to join the team as a Senior Product Development and Implementation Associate. This solution-oriented individual will help advance Franklin Templeton’s US product platform by supporting and, where appropriate, leading product development and implementation initiatives, coordinating cross-functional execution, and partnering with Sales, Product, investment management, legal, compliance, risk, operations, marketing, and other business partners to move product actions through governance and implementation. US Product Implementation is accountable for implementation and project management oversight for product initiatives including new fund launches, mergers, closures, product changes, and the development of new vehicle types for Franklin Templeton and its investment groups. Members of the team lead cross-functional working groups to drive the successful implementation of product changes, identify potential execution challenges, help structure solutions, and inform stakeholders of outcomes. Vehicles covered by the team include 3c1s, 3c7s, 529s, Custom Indices, ETFs, Interval Funds, and Mutual Funds. What is the Senior Product Development and Implementation Associate responsible for? The Senior Product Development and Implementation Associate is responsible for supporting product initiatives and leading smaller product development and implementation initiatives across Franklin Templeton’s US product platform. The role provides product implementation, governance, and project management support for fund launches, mergers, closures, repositionings, product changes, pricing actions, and new vehicle development. This individual will partner with senior product leaders, distribution, investment management, legal, compliance, risk, operations, marketing, and other business partners to support successful execution of product initiatives. The role is responsible for managing project workflow and timelines, coordinating stakeholder engagement, preparing updates and reports for senior leaders, identifying implementation risks or dependencies, and helping ensure product changes advance through the appropriate governance and approval processes. What are the ongoing responsibilities of the Senior Product Development and Implementation Associate?

About the Team & Business Line The Structured Products team under the Loan Servicing umbrella is one of Citco’s core businesses, and our alternative asset and accounting service is one of the industry’s most respected. This team specializes in CLO administration, Rated Note Feeders, Collateral Administration and Investments Accounting services. Our continuous investment in learning and technology solutions means our people are equipped to deliver a seamless client experience. Your Role: This is an analytical and multi-faceted role that requires the team member to be able to analyze and maintain a range of complex structures, loan security types and lifecycle events in support of product administration functions and reporting preparation. You will be responsible for the oversight of daily tasks associated with the full lifecycle of loan products including but not limited to: Review credit agreements and other loan documentation to ensure proper setup and processing in the loan system Monitor accurate processing of loan lifecycle activities Identify, investigate and solve for accrual, position, and cash breaks to Loan Servicers and Agents Collaborate with internal teams to streamline and enhance processes Ensure the client service level agreements are closely monitored and monthly deliverables are met timely, accurately and completely Maintain standard processes within a robust control environment, with an eye to continually improving or modifying the same to support assigned clients Support / conduct training of new hires in standard processes including those without experience in loan products Assist in management of special projects as needed. About You: Bachelor’s Degree from an accredited college or university Professional Accounting/Finance designation considered an asset (CFA, CPA) 2+ years experience in Financial Services, Loan Administration and/or Loan Agency Experienced in a range of debt product types including, but not limited to, syndicated, bilateral, direct lending originations, commercial real estate loans, letters of credit Experienced in maintaining a control environment and adhering to standard operating procedures You are flexible and comfortable adapting to fluid operational requirements and deliverables Knowledge of Sentry PM is preferred CLO and/or Collateral Administration experience is preferred Accounting acumen is a plus Ability to manage time effectively, set priorities and meet deadlines Strong proficiency with Microsoft Office suite, especially Excel Our Benefits Your well-being is of paramount importance and central to our success here at Citco.

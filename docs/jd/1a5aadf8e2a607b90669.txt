Description Huntington National Bank has a new opportunity within Consumer and Regional Banking (CRB) for a Quantitative Risk Analytics Manager. This position will focus on quantitative analytic techniques to help support a variety of credit functions including credit analytics, PPNR analytics, fair lending analytics, and provide analytic support for other areas to help manage risks associated with Huntington’s balance sheet. This position will drive the analytic framework to help support the CRB analytics program and provide cross-functional support to different areas within the Bank. The Quantitative Risk Analytics Manager will lead the development of techniques to facilitate evaluation of risk and reward across a variety of lending products. The role will also work with a larger team that is responsible for forecasting key elements of Huntington’s balance sheet and income statement as part of our annual capital planning activities which include both commercial and consumer assets and liabilities. Duties and Responsibilities : Develop quantitative methods using SQL, SAS, R, and python for optimization of credit risk across consumer and regional banking portfolios. Using business intelligence software (i.e. Tableau) to design, develop, deploy, and maintain effective visualizations, dashboards, and scorecards that provide actionable information. Gather and assemble data for quantitative method development including loan characteristics from loan origination data sources as well as loan accounting systems. Partner with data analytics teams to ensure the validity of data, identify non-compliance and make recommendations for corrective action. Work with internal partners to allow efficient and effective use of analytics for fair lending, capital planning, and stress testing. Lead analytic approaches for credit portfolio management Performs other duties as assigned. Basic Qualifications : Minimum master’s degree required with an economics or other quantitative/statistics/finance concentration, related field, or equivalent experience. 5+ years of experience in quantitative analytics using SQL, SAS, R, and Python Preferred Qualifications : Quantitative method experience in the banking industry with an understanding of consumer loan products. 3+ years of experience in Tableau Experience working with statistical platforms (SAS, R, Python, Matlab, or Eviews). Excellent written and verbal (face-to-face and phone) communication skills including professional grammar and demeanor. Ability to interact with various levels of management and external regulators with the ability to communicate complex calculations into language that is clear and concise. Collaborate effectively with others while demonstrating effective challenge by constructively offering perspectives that question conventional thinking. Excellent PC software skills including all Microsoft Office Products. Strong organizational skills with attention to detail. Ability to multi-task.

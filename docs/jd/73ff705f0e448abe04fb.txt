Job Description: The Role As Fidelity expands the distribution of investment products into new markets and jurisdictions, the Senior Manager, Global Regulatory Reporting will lead the assessment, implementation, and ongoing support of new regulatory reporting requirements. This highly visible role partners across Legal, Compliance, Product, Technology, Data, Operations, and external service providers to determine reporting readiness, evaluate data availability and establish scalable compliance solutions. The successful candidate will translate complex regulatory requirements into practical operating models by evaluating business processes, data availability, technology capabilities, and third-party vendor solutions. They will develop plans for new reporting requirements, identify data and process gaps, and help design and execute reporting solutions that support Fidelity's expanding global distribution strategy. This role is ideal for a candidate who thrives in a dynamic environment, enjoys building new capabilities, and can translate regulatory requirements, business processes, data and technology capabilities into effective and sustainable solutions. It provides a unique opportunity to help shape Fidelity's global regulatory reporting capabilities as the firm's international footprint continues to grow. The Expertise and Skills You Bring Bachelor's degree or equivalent work experience (advanced degree or certification a plus). 8 plus years of experience within asset management, regulatory reporting, compliance, fund operations, product development, business analysis, or related financial services functions. Demonstrated ability to analyze and interpret complex regulatory requirements and translate them into operational, data, and business requirements. Strong understanding of the asset management industry , including investment products, fund structures, and supporting operational processes. Experience evaluating data requirements and working across multiple systems to assess data sourcing, lineage, quality, and governance considerations. Proven ability to synthesize complex information and develop clear assessments, recommendations, and implementation plans for senior leadership. Strong written and verbal communication skills with the ability to present complex topics to technical and non-technical audiences. Exceptional stakeholder management and relationship-building skills across diverse business groups. Ability to manage multiple priorities and navigate ambiguity in a rapidly evolving environment. The Team The Shareholder Reporting Filings team is responsible for supporting critical regulatory reporting obligations across Fidelity's investment management businesses. Historically focused on U.S. regulatory requirements, the team's mandate has expanded to include private fund and derivatives reporting regimes and continues to evolve alongside the firm's global growth strategy. As Fidelity expands product distribution into new jurisdictions, the team is increasingly responsible for assessing and implementing new reporting requirements, developing scalable operating models, and partnering with internal and external stakeholders to ensure regulatory compliance. This role will help define and build those future capabilities.

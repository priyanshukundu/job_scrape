About the team and role At eBay, our Engineering team strives to build world-class solutions that drive our ambitious vision of the future of ecommerce. As a Sr Software Engineer, you will be at the forefront of developing innovative technologies that improve the buying and selling experience on our platform. You will play a pivotal role in ensuring our marketplace remains a safe, fair, and trustworthy environment for all users. Your expertise will guide the development and implementation of scalable backend systems and AI-powered tools. These tools help detect and prevent prohibited or counterfeit items and ensure regulatory compliance. This role offers an excellent opportunity to invent solutions that protect customers and support a globally trusted marketplace. What you will accomplish Lead the technical build and delivery of large-scale applications, ensuring flawless execution of low-latency APIs, data pipelines, and foundational architectures. Define and evolve configurable platforms for ingesting and applying attributes, rules, models, policies, and derived aggregates. Guide the development and deployment of machine learning models and generative AI tools to generate insights and improve customer experiences successfully. Set technical direction for complex initiatives, balancing scalability, reliability, performance, and maintainability with outstanding precision. Partner with architects, business leaders, and industry experts to build strategy and deliver solutions across teams. Own the software development lifecycle for key initiatives, from architecture and implementation through testing, experimentation, and production operations. Raise engineering standards through build reviews, technical guidance, and mentorship of junior engineers. What you will bring Candidates should have a bachelor’s degree or equivalent experience in computer science or a related area with a minimum of 8 years of proven experience. Alternatively, a master’s degree or equivalent experience in computer science with at least 6 years of relevant software development experience is acceptable. Strong programming skills in Java, Scala, and Python, with hands-on experience using frameworks such as PyTorch or TensorFlow. Demonstrated experience crafting and delivering horizontally scalable APIs and distributed systems. Practical experience with data processing and streaming technologies such as Spark, Flink, and Kafka. Solid understanding of SQL, NoSQL, and data modeling. Experience building, deploying, and operating AI, machine learning, or generative AI models in production. A track record of leading technical initiatives, influencing build decisions, and helping other engineers succeed. Bonus qualifications Experience building AI agents or agentic workflows. Experience with harness engineering, including the tools, orchestration, and evaluation practices that support reliable agent behavior. Experience with context engineering, including selecting, structuring, and managing the context provided to AI models.

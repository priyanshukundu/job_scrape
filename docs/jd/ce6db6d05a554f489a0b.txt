The Role As a Senior Machine Learning Engineer, you will build the next generation of AI products at Capital Group, including agentic systems, LLM-powered workflows, and the platforms that help keep them safe, governed, and reliable in production. Working across production machine learning, generative AI, agentic workflows, and governed data infrastructure, you’ll help define how enterprise-grade AI systems are designed, deployed, and operated. You’ll work with autonomy, mentor junior engineers, and strengthen engineering standards across solutions built on Databricks, AWS, and agent-based architectures. Your work will support responsible innovation and durable AI capabilities for the firm’s long-term needs. The Team You’ll be part of the Machine Learning Engineering team, which builds and operates production AI capabilities across Capital Group. The team partners with product, business, data, and engineering groups to turn complex needs into well-scoped, reliable solutions. Its work spans platform engineering, model delivery, agentic systems, and the governance practices required to run AI responsibly at enterprise scale. Your Impact Architect and operate end-to-end production AI systems across design, deployment, monitoring, and lifecycle management. Build cloud-native environments and reusable platform components that support model training, serving, orchestration, and governance. Design scalable inference pipelines with retraining loops, drift detection, evaluation harnesses, tracing, replay, and observability. Deliver agentic systems with multi-step reasoning, orchestration, tool and function calling, MCP-based integrations, guardrails, and human-in-the-loop patterns. Drive the agentic software development lifecycle by defining standards for design, testing, evaluation, deployment, and monitoring. Build solutions on Databricks and AWS using Unity Catalog, MLflow, Spark, Infrastructure as Code, and cloud-native AI capabilities. Create REST and streaming APIs, retrieval and memory integrations, and optimized distributed workloads for downstream products and platforms. Embed data lineage, access controls, audit trails, and responsible AI practices while mentoring engineers and leading code reviews. What You’ll Bring 7+ years of professional software engineering experience, with strong proficiency in Python and core software engineering fundamentals. Experience building and operating production machine learning systems end to end, including deployment, monitoring, evaluation, and lifecycle management. Hands-on experience with AWS and/or Databricks, including native AI and machine learning capabilities and Infrastructure as Code. Experience integrating generative AI and LLMs using prompt engineering, evaluation techniques, RAG patterns, fine-tuning, and model selection. Experience developing REST and streaming APIs, plus familiarity with Model Context Protocol. Strong machine learning fundamentals, including algorithms, evaluation metrics, model tuning, data cleaning, feature engineering, preprocessing, and exploratory analysis. A bachelor’s degree in information technology, computer science, or a related field. Ability to operate autonomously on complex initiatives, with experience in CI/CD, DevOps, containerization, deployment pipelines, and testing frameworks. Preferred Experience with agentic architectures, including multi-step reasoning, orchestration frameworks, tool and function calling, and agent evaluation. Experience with AI data infrastructure such as vector stores, graph databases, Redis, DynamoDB, and ElastiCache. Experience with data governance tools and practices, including Unity Catalog, data lineage, access controls, and audit trails. Experience with Spark, distributed computing, and large-scale data processing. Experience designing human-in-the-loop systems, guardrails, LLM output evaluation, and responsible AI practices.

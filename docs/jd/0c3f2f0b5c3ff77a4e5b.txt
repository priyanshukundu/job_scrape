Are you ready to develop the future of AI and factory automation? At NVIDIA, we are seeking a Senior System Software Engineer to join our AI Factory Automation team. This is your chance to work with powerful technology and innovative solutions that will drive the next generation of AI infrastructure. Join us and contribute to building world-class automation platforms that will transform industries and improve lives! What you’ll be doing: Contributing to a comprehensive automation platform for GPU infrastructure provisioning, configuration, validation, observability, upgrades, remediation, and lifecycle management across different cloud environments. Supporting our automation platform and building customer-facing features that the industry uses to operate GPU capacity around the world. Working directly with product, engineering, and executive teams to understand what we build, when we build it, and why. Implementing monitoring and health management capabilities that enable industry-leading reliability, availability, and scalability of GPU Infrastructure. Harnessing multiple data streams ranging from GPU hardware diagnostics to cluster and network telemetry. Contributing to building and code reviews, automated testing, CI/CD, documentation, and production observability. Crafting consistent APIs through which customers, partners, and agents operate the whole AI factory. Shipping open-source software with predictable releases and extended service. Working hands-on with operators and partners to deploy and debug our tools in their environments, and bring field insight back into the roadmap. What we need to see: Qualified candidates must possess at minimum each of the skills and qualities listed below: Highly motivated with strong communication skills, the ability to work successfully with multi-functional teams, principles and architects and coordinate effectively across organizational boundaries and geographies. Working knowledge of AI Factory hardware (GPUs, CPUs, memory, PCIe, NICs, NVLINK, InfiniBand, DPUs, storage, and firmware) and of how that hardware fails. Strong agentic software development expertise in a systems language such as Go, Rust, or C/C++, proficiency in Python, and solid fundamentals in data structures, algorithms, and concurrency. Solid understanding of Linux internals and administration: boot, kernel and device drivers, networking and storage stacks, and tools such as perf, eBPF, gdb, and strace. A methodical approach to debugging across hardware, firmware, kernel, drivers, containers, and orchestration, and the habit of turning each fix into automation. Familiarity with automation patterns that involve autonomous components and the security practices that make such systems safe to run against production infrastructure. 5+ years in similar role and experience on large-scale production systems. Experience with common software engineering principles, tools and techniques. You possess a BS in Computer Science, Engineering, Physics, Mathematics or a comparable degree or equivalent experience. Ways to stand out from the crowd: Outstanding candidates will have deep knowledge in one or more of the following areas: Data center operations and NVIDIA technologies: GPUs and DCGM, NVLink and NVSwitch, InfiniBand or Ethernet AI fabrics, and BlueField DPUs. Kernel, device driver, firmware, or CUDA development, and performance profiling and tuning on GPU systems. Out-of-band management and firmware lifecycle at fleet scale: BMC, Redfish, IPMI, BIOS/UEFI, and safe update and rollback. Hardware diagnostics such as server screening, burn-in, failure localization, and hardware-aware remediation, including extending DPU fleet management platforms to GPU or other accelerator fleets. Kubernetes controllers or operators, GitOps, infrastructure as code, and durable workflow orchestration across multiple clusters, on premises or across cloud providers. #LI-Hybrid Your base salary will be determined based on your location, experience, and other objective job-related factors.

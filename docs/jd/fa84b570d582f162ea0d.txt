Job Summary Assist in the work scheduling process by assigning jobs to line servicer personnel and providing customer assistance for an assigned area. Job Description What You’ll Do: Perform duties assigned in the work scheduling process, including the assignment of jobs to line servicer personnel using management system applications. Develop schedules for line servicer and technician appointments. Communicate with customers and direct inquiries. Support adherence to standard work scheduling policies and procedures. Assist with outage management as needed. Provide administrative support as needed. Manage timekeeping responsibilities for line department personnel. This posting represents multiple positions that offer a range of opportunities for advancement. The minimum qualifications outlined below apply to the Work Scheduling Asst Assoc, with increased requirements for the Work Scheduling Asst - Sr. level. What We’re Looking For: Education requirements and work experience are listed below: Work Scheduling Assistant Associate High school diploma or GED. 9 months of post high school experience in utilizing administrative procedures and office equipment. Work Scheduling Assistant High school diploma or GED required. 3 years of post-high school experience in utilizing administrative procedures and office equipment or the equivalent, normally obtained by progression through the Work Scheduling Rep Assoc classification. Work scheduling Assistant, SR High school diploma or GED required. 6 years of post-high school experience in utilizing administrative procedures and office equipment or the equivalent, normally obtained by progression through the Work Scheduling Asst classification Other: Must be available to work overtime, call out overtime and shift work, as required. Travel requirements are expected 10-25% of the time Ability to demonstrate the following skills and competencies: Organizational and analytic/problem solving skills, Prioritization skills, Customer Focus and Teamwork. Demonstrated proficiency with computers and computer programs, effective verbal and written communication skills required. Strong verbal and written communication skills. Must be available for overtime when needed. Occasional travel may be required.

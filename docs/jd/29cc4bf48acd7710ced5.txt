Work Flexibility: Hybrid The Associate Manager, Market Insights & Analytics will be responsible for transforming customer, market, competitive, and business data into actionable insights that guide ENT portfolio strategy and commercial decision-making. This role will own and support primary market research, Voice of Customer (VOC), competitive intelligence, market sizing, forecasting, pricing research, and strategic analytics. The individual will partner closely with Marketing, R&D, Sales, Finance, Business Development and leadership to establish fact-based perspectives on market opportunities, competitive dynamics and customer needs. The ideal candidate combines strong analytical and market-research capabilities with the ability to turn complex information into concise, executive-ready recommendations. What you will do: Develop a deep understanding of ENT markets, customers, competitors, and industry trends to identify opportunities and risks for the business. Lead primary and secondary market research , selecting the right methodologies to answer critical business questions and translating findings into actionable insights. Assess competitive dynamics , including market positioning, points of differentiation, emerging competitors, product launches, and evolving customer needs. Build and maintain market models that evaluate market size, growth, procedure volumes, market share, customer segments, and other key market dynamics. Synthesize internal, third-party, and publicly available data to identify market trends and develop a clear fact base for strategic decision-making. Translate insights into recommendations , connecting research and analytics to portfolio strategy, commercial priorities, innovation, and business development decisions. Identify customer needs and unmet needs through Voice of Customer and other research to inform product development, positioning, claims, and portfolio strategy. Partner with cross-functional teams including Marketing, Sales, R&D, Finance, Strategy, and Business Development to define critical business questions and deliver insights that drive action. Identify knowledge gaps within the organization and proactively develop research and analytical approaches to address them. Manage external research partners and data providers , ensuring projects deliver high-quality insights on time and against business objectives. Develop executive-ready presentations and recommendations that clearly communicate what the data says, why it matters, and what actions the business should consider. Build strong relationships with internal stakeholders, customers, and industry experts to continuously strengthen our understanding of the market. Take ownership of multiple priorities in a fast-paced environment , consistently delivering rigorous, high-quality work. Minimum Qualifications (Required): Bachelor’s degree required 6+ years of professional experience required Preferred Qualifications (Strongly Desired): MBA or other relevant advanced degree 3+ years of experience in market research, market insights, analytics, strategy, consulting, or a related field Experience designing and executing primary and secondary market research , including Voice of Customer Strong analytical capabilities with experience in market sizing, forecasting, competitive intelligence, and/or market modeling Demonstrated ability to synthesize complex quantitative and qualitative information into clear, actionable business recommendations Medical device, healthcare, or life sciences experience preferred Experience with Power BI, syndicated market data, or competitive intelligence platforms is a plus United States of America Pay Ranges: USN : $89,300 - $148,800 USD Annual View the U.S. work location and transparency guide to find the pay range for your location.

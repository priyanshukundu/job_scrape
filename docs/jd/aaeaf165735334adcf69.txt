Title and Summary Lead Software Development Engineer Overview Mastercard is a global technology company in the payments industry. Our mission is to connect and power an inclusive, digital economy that benefits everyone, everywhere by making transactions safe, simple, smart, and accessible. The Cyber & Intelligence (C&I) organization is responsible for Mastercard's security and risk-related product development efforts, delivering innovative solutions focused on cybersecurity, authentication, fraud prevention, risk mitigation, and dispute resolution across the global payments ecosystem. We are seeking a highly skilled Lead Software Engineer to drive architecture, design, development, modernization, and operational excellence for mission-critical platforms. This role combines deep technical expertise, hands-on software engineering, architectural leadership, and mentoring to deliver secure, scalable, resilient, and high-performing solutions.

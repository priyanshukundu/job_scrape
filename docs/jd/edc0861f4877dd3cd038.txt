Duties & Responsibilities: Lincoln International's Capital Advisory Group is seeking to hire an Associate that has strong cognitive, technical and financial modeling skills, as well as good interpersonal abilities and a commitment to excellence. Associates at Lincoln International interact closely with senior bankers in the firm and with senior executives of the firm’s clients and lending relationships. An Associate at Lincoln International holds a demanding position, as the individual must be able to work on processing multiple transactions at any one time while also supporting new business development efforts. Key Responsibilities include: Responsibilities at Lincoln International are often less rigid and more entrepreneurial than at other firms, and key Associate responsibilities on transactions include: Performing financial analyses and building complex financial models in support of platform acquisitions, mergers and recapitalization transactions, among other capital events Identifying and contacting potential lenders/investors regarding a financing Leading the preparation of confidential information memoranda and management presentations Hosting visits of potential lenders/investors at client facilities Supporting senior bankers in negotiations Assisting during the due diligence and closing process Key responsibilities also include preparation of materials and active participation in new business origination and contributing to the internal strengthening of our firm, including a commitment to Analyst personnel development and the general growth of the firm. Qualifications: Fluent in accounting and finance and strong business acumen Strong verbal and written communication skills Assertive, self-motivated and desire to succeed in a fast-paced, entrepreneurial environment Prior banking, capital raising, lending or investment banking experience is required Additional Information Lincoln International is not currently hiring individuals for this position who now or in the future require sponsorship for employment visa status.

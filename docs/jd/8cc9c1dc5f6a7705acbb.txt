Revolution Medicines

Revolution Medicines is a global, commercial-state oncology company dedicated to discovering, developing and delivering innovative medicines for patients with RAS-addicted cancers. Leveraging its differentiated RAS(ON) tri-complex inhibitor platform, the company is advancing a broad, integrated portfolio of oral RAS(ON) inhibitors designed to directly target the active, cancer-driving state of RAS. Founded on rigorous scientific inquiry and a willingness to challenge long-held assumptions, Revolution Medicines is committed to changing the trajectory of disease for patients with RAS-addicted cancers worldwide. Our people are united by a shared way of working: follow the science, challenge assumptions, act with urgency and hold ourselves to a high standard of rigor—all in service of patients. The Opportunity: Revolution Medicines is seeking an Associate Director, Contract Specialist to join our Legal team. Reporting to the Director, Contract Management, with functional supervision of all legal work by a designated licensed attorney on the Legal team, this role will lead the activities of the contract management discipline within the Contracts function, including the drafting, review, negotiation, and management of a broad range of commercial and operational agreements that support the company’s commercial-stage business. The Associate Director will bring formal legal training and analytical rigor to complex contracting matters, operate with a high degree of independence in managing workload and contracting processes within attorney-approved positions and guidelines, and partner cross-functionally to drive efficient, consistent, and risk-appropriate contracting practices across the organization. Working under the direction of and in close collaboration with the company’s attorneys, this individual will be internally recognized as a contracts subject-matter expert, with a solid understanding of the overall organization and competitive environment, and will help shape the processes, templates, and tools that enable the Contracts function to scale. All legal analysis, legal advice, and negotiation positions in this role are performed under the direction and supervision of a licensed attorney on the Legal team. Key Responsibilities: Under the supervision of a licensed attorney on the Legal team: Lead the day-to-day activities of the contract negotiations for assigned contracts and serve as a resource for junior team members. Draft, review, redline, and negotiate a high volume of complex commercial, clinical, and operational agreements, including master services agreements (MSAs), statements of work (SOWs), work orders and change orders, clinical trial agreements and related site, CRO, and vendor agreements, consulting agreements, vendor and supply agreements, confidentiality and non-disclosure agreements, data processing agreements, software-as-a-service (SaaS) and technology licenses, agreements with specialty pharmacies, distributors, group purchasing organizations, and other trade partners, marketing and agency agreements, speaker and advisory board agreements, and related amendments, renewals, and terminations. Analyze contract terms to identify legal, commercial, operational, and compliance risks; resolve highly complex or unusual contracting challenges that impact commercial success, applying high-level analytical judgment and exploring new approaches; recommend practical, business-oriented solutions; and escalate novel or high-risk issues to the appropriate Legal team attorneys with clear analysis and proposed positions. Serve as a trusted contracts advisor to business stakeholders across Commercial, Market Access, Medical Affairs, Clinical Development, Research, Manufacturing and Supply Chain, Finance, IT, and other functions, providing guidance on contracting processes, business terms, and attorney-approved positions, routing legal questions to the Legal team attorneys, and contributing beyond the Contracts discipline in support of commercial advancement and broader corporate objectives. Ensure agreements align with company policies and applicable healthcare and life sciences requirements, partnering with Legal, Compliance, Privacy, and Finance on matters such as anti-kickback and transparency considerations, fair market value, data privacy, and revenue recognition. Develop, maintain, and continuously improve contract templates, clause libraries, negotiation playbooks, fallback positions, and contracting guidelines in partnership with, and subject to final approval of legal positions by, the Legal team attorneys. Translate Legal department and strategy into Contracts discipline plans and manage their execution; provide input on decisions and implementation strategy and consistently inform decision-making at the Director level and above, recognizing that contracting frameworks and process decisions may have a long-term (two to three year) impact on business activities. Develop and track contracting metrics and operational effectiveness, identify bottlenecks, and implement process improvements that increase efficiency, consistency, and turnaround time. Drive change initiatives within the Contracts discipline (e.g., contract lifecycle management implementations and new templates or playbooks), identifying stakeholders, developing and sharing communication and training content, and providing oversight to create transparency, buy-in, alignment, and successful adoption. Provide guidance, training, and mentorship to contracts team members and business stakeholders on contracting best practices, coaching team members and mentoring team members as the function grows. Champion ethical leadership and accountability, drive alignment between Contracts team performance, patient impact, and the company’s core values, and lead by influence, fostering a culture of adaptability, trust, inclusivity, and cross-functional collaboration during rapid organizational scaling and change. Additional Responsibilities and Role Evolution: As a growing organization, our legal, corporate governance, contracts, and intellectual property functions continue to evolve to meet changing business needs. Responsibilities may change, expand, or shift over time based on organizational priorities, business growth, process improvements, technology implementations, and evolving legal or regulatory requirements. The successful candidate will demonstrate flexibility, adaptability, and a willingness to contribute beyond the core responsibilities of the position, partnering collaboratively across functions to support the continued success and growth of the company. Required Skills, Experience and Education: A bachelor’s degree combined with formal legal training (e.g., a J.D., LL.B., LL.M., or foreign law degree) and substantial, progressive commercial contracts experience will be considered. Admission to practice law in a U.S. jurisdiction is not required for this role. 15+ years of progressive experience drafting, reviewing, and negotiating commercial and clinical agreements, including at least 10 years in the pharmaceutical, biotechnology, or broader life sciences industry, gained within an in-house legal department, a law firm, or a legal services or contracts organization supporting legal teams. Demonstrated expertise across a broad range of agreement types, including MSAs, SOWs, work orders, clinical trial agreements, confidentiality agreements, and vendor, consulting, services, technology, and commercial trade agreements, together with in-depth understanding of the legal positions underlying key terms and the ability to develop fallback language. Advanced knowledge of contract principles and commercial terms, including indemnification, limitation of liability, intellectual property, confidentiality, data privacy, subject injury, publication, insurance, warranties, and termination, with the analytical rigor to assess risk and recommend positions for attorney review. Proven ability to work independently within established playbooks and attorney guidance, manage a high-volume, fast-paced workload, resolve highly complex or unusual matters requiring high-level analytical thought and sound judgment, and escalate novel or high-risk issues to attorneys. Exceptional negotiation, written, and verbal communication skills, with the ability to regularly collaborate with senior management and executive levels to influence policy on contracting matters, influence the decisions of senior leaders, and negotiate with internal stakeholders and external counterparties on matters of significance; may represent the company at professional events. Demonstrated experience leading, developing, and managing contracts professionals, including through subordinate supervisors or team leads. Hands-on experience with contract lifecycle management systems (e.g., Ironclad, Agiloft, Icertis, Conga, Apttus, or similar), e-signature platforms, and Microsoft Office tools. Strong business acumen, a solid understanding of the commercial-stage biopharmaceutical competitive environment and how contracting integrates with commercial, clinical, and supply operations, attention to detail, and a demonstrated commitment to integrity and confidentiality. Preferred Skills: Experience supporting commercial-stage pharmaceutical operations. Familiarity with healthcare compliance frameworks relevant to contracting, such as the federal Anti-Kickback Statute, Sunshine Act transparency reporting, the PhRMA Code, HIPAA, CCPA, and GDPR. Experience with clinical, research, manufacturing, or supply-related agreements (e.g., CRO, CDMO, clinical trial, and quality agreements).

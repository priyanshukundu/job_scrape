Job Description Under the guidance of the Director, GRACS Quality Excellence & Governance (QEG), the Associate Director, GRACS Standards & Training independently plans, executes, and documents operational activities supporting the GRACS Quality Management System (QMS), including GRACS Standards, seeking guidance as needed. The role collaborates with GRACS Leadership and cross-functional stakeholders across our Research & Development Division , our Manufacturing Division , HH, our Research & Development Division 's Quality Assurance, Global Learning & Development, and other internal and external functions to develop and execute standards and strategies that strengthen quality, QMS alignment, compliance, and evolving business needs. The Associate Director also contributes to moderately complex cross-functional initiatives requiring functional expertise and a broad understanding of GRACS processes, SOPs, and the broader quality environment. Core Responsibilities include and are not limited to: Independently drives and supports GRACS QMS activities by leveraging advanced compliance knowledge and strategic judgment. Proactively identifies, analyzes, and resolves complex systemic quality issues, collaborating with GRACS and external leadership to implement robust corrective actions. Serves as an authoritative Subject Matter Expert (SME) on GRACS Standards and QMS processes, systems, and regulatory requirements. Champions continuous quality improvement and actively mentors and builds capabilities within the broader team, as appropriate. Supports the strategy, planning, and facilitation of regulatory inspections and internal quality audits. Oversee the coordination and/or management of documentation requests, ensuring overall GRACS audit readiness. Under the guidance of the Director, QEG, partners across GRACS, our Research & Development Division , our Manufacturing Division , and HH to align global quality standards, ensuring a unified understanding and execution of applicable regulations across all collaborating divisions. Oversee the design, development, and delivery of educational and training materials to elevate regulatory and QMS competencies across GRACS and partner divisions, where applicable Supports the GRACS Quarterly Documentation Release and execute the communication strategy for new or revised procedural documentation, ensure global training curricula are perfectly synchronized with released standards. May provide direct support to system users globally for Systems and tools used by GRACS in support of the QMS execution and/or explore automation-based solutions for further digital enhancement of the QMS processes. Works with Functional Area (e.g., RCPO) Process Owners to develop and maintain business process maps, associated documentation, and training for processes or process changes. Establish, lead, and champion key QEG Communities of Practice (CoPs) across GRACS and collaborating divisions. Foster a culture of continuous learning, break down operational silos, and drive the standardization of best practices, process efficiencies, and regulatory alignment across the global network. Conducts appropriate work in accordance with applicable and established Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs), applicable regulations (e.g., Good Regulatory Practice, Good Pharmacovigilance Practice, and Good Clinical Practice), safety guidelines and company policies and procedures. May assist with the preparation or review of relevant SOPs or policies relevant to GRACS or partnering our Research & Development Division / our Manufacturing Division /HH organizations. Qualifications: Education Minimum Requirement: Bachelor’s Degree with a minimum of 8 years of quality & compliance and/or Pharmaceutical (clinical, regulatory or safety) experience, OR An advanced degree and a minimum of 4 years of quality & compliance and/or Pharmaceutical (clinical, regulatory or safety) experience related experience. Required Experience and Skills: Collaboration and Partnering : Effectively partners with others (within GRACS and external to GRACS) to accomplish quality goals; possesses group leadership skills. Communication Skills : Demonstrates advanced communications skills to convey and receive information. Demonstrates advanced oral and written communications skills. Problem Solving & Continuous Improvement Mindset : Proficiently synthesizes multi-source data and stakeholder input to navigate ambiguous, complex, or systemic challenges and influence strategic enterprise decisions. Proven expertise in applying structured operational excellence and process improvement methodologies (e.g., Lean, Six Sigma, DMAIC, root cause analysis) preferably within pharmaceutical development and regulatory operations to drive sustainable quality outcomes. Strategic Thinking: Effectively applies skills to drive or participate in change efforts to enhance processes within GRACS or across areas within our Research & Development Division that improve quality and /or add value to the business. Project Management: Demonstrates advanced ability to organize work efforts, prioritization of tasks and utilize appropriate resources to deliver or support work products in accordance with timelines and appropriate regulations. Decision Making: Effectively applies skills to utilize knowledge, networks and data to support rapid decision making with support of GRACS Quality Excellence & Governance management. Business Operations/Knowledge of Field: Effectively applies knowledge of regulations and business trends and applies this knowledge to optimize daily activities and collaborates with GRACS Quality Excellence & Governance management to make appropriate decisions that improve the quality of business and functional area outputs. Regulatory and Compliance Management: Demonstrates advanced knowledge of regulations and regulatory trends to ensure appropriate functional areas within GRACS are compliant with regulations and appropriate procedures and policies and proactively manages and mitigates risks to achieve quality outcomes. #GRACS #EligibleforERP Required Skills: Business Process Design, Corrective Action Management, Cross-Functional Teamwork, Document Control Systems, Employee Training Programs, Good Clinical Practice (GCP), Project Management, Quality Auditing, Quality Control Systems, Quality Inspections, Quality Management System (QMS) Compliance, Regulatory Compliance, Regulatory Experience, Regulatory Inspections, Written Communication Preferred Skills: Current Employees apply HERE Current Contingent Workers apply HERE US and Puerto Rico Residents Only: Our company is committed to inclusion, ensuring that candidates can engage in a hiring process that exhibits their true capabilities.

We’re looking for a Data Scientist to join Etsy’s Payments Analytics team and help make payments more seamless and reliable. You’ll partner with Product, Engineering, and fellow analysts to evaluate opportunities and inform product decisions through analysis, experimentation, and foundational data work. This is a full-time position reporting to the Manager, Analytics. What’s this team like at Etsy? The Payments team improves buyer and seller financial experiences and the infrastructure that supports transactions across Etsy. Payments Analytics helps the team understand customer behavior, builds trusted data sources, and measures the impact of product decisions. What does the day-to-day look like? Own well-scoped analyses, from understanding product goals and questions to defining metrics that measure success and sharing findings. Analyze buyer and seller behaviors to identify ways to improve payments experiences. Design and analyze experiments to measure product impact. Build and maintain reliable datasets and dashboards, with clear documentation. Turn findings into actionable recommendations and work with partner teams to prioritize follow-up opportunities. Of course, this is just a sample of the kinds of work this role will require! You should assume that your role will encompass other tasks, too, and that your job duties and responsibilities may change from time to time at Etsy's discretion, or otherwise applicable with local law. Qualities that will help you thrive in this role are: 2+ years of experience in data science, analytics, or another quantitative role, using data to answer business questions. Proficient in SQL, with some experience in Python or R. Experience with experimentation, dashboarding, and foundational statistical methods. Ability to manage well-scoped analytical projects and collaborate with stakeholders from planning through delivery. Strong commitment to data quality, reproducibility, and clear documentation Ability to communicate insights clearly and translate findings into actionable recommendations Curiosity about customer behavior and a growth mindset. Experience in payments, marketplaces, or e-commerce is a plus.

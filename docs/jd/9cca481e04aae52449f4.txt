Job Description: The Associate MCC / MCS program is a 6 -9 months development program to complete all of the Associate MCC/MCS training components. In the role of Manager Commercial Credit (MCC)you are principally responsible for managing the credit and operational risk for a unit's commercial loan portfolio. You are a subject matter expert and have the ability to analyze and understand credit proposals from a variety of industries. You can anticipate and assess the risks associated with entering into a lending relationship under a wide range of economic scenarios and ensure loan agreements are structured o be relevant, supportive, and competitive for the bank and our clients on an ongoing basis. You will assess, analyze, and perform due diligence in support of all applications, approving credits within delegated limits and making recommendations on all others. You will interface with TD's risk management group on a regular basis to ensure that your understanding of the Bank's risk models, and pricing platforms allows you to effectively interpret and apply policy. Recognizing that your credibility and influence with them is based on the credit proposals that are crafted and presented by your team, you make the development of your Account Managers and Analysts risk evaluation skills your primary priority. Thus, you will establish yourself as a trusted business leader who is sought after for guidance regarding matters relating to the credit facilities that are supporting the client's business strategy. You support your Account Manager and Analysts efforts to meet their business development targets by recommending credit packaging alternatives and actively discuss credit solutions. The Manager commercial sales is responsible for leading a team of Relationship Managers in business development, including driving key sales programs and pipeline management. Provide guidance, leadership, coaching and development to ensure business results and professional / personal development objectives are achieved for the overall group. The Senior Manager, Commercial Sales is accountable for the Unit's growth through strong pipeline management and sales coaching, while delivering a superior customer experience to drive profitable business growth, in line with business strategy. Job Requirements : In addition to your undergraduate degree in business or relevant professional designation, you ideally have 2-3 years of experience as a Financial Analyst or Account Manager in a business or agricultural banking environment. Alternatively you may have experience in another profession that supports the needs of business and are looking for an opportunity to add value from a different perspective. You have well developed analytical, relationship management and business development skills and look forward to developing them further in this role.

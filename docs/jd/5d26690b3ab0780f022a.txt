The US Rates Structuring Desk based in New York is seeking an Associate or Vice President to help with our growing client facing business. The individual will work with senior salespeople and traders covering a broad array of client types. Key responsibilities: Structuring, pricing and execution of derivatives and investment solutions for Morgan Stanley’s client base. Coordinating the execution process across multiple desks, from sales to trading and other internal functions, as well as interface with our clients. Pricing and risk analysis for vanilla and exotic interest rate products including XVA and capital/liquidity optimization considerations. Establishing/maintaining regular contact with the sales desks, relationship managers and other bank businesses with a view to identifying business opportunities and enhancing Structured Products business with them. Generating new ideas and opportunities (and producing marketing material) for new and/or existing clients in response to market movements, desk interests and liability driven investment strategies. Qualifications: Collaborative mindset and commercially driven. Strong quantitative skills (ideally candidate should have experience in derivatives and/or developing/using analytical tools for pricing, risk management and simulation). Ability to think creatively and develop innovative solutions. Ability to work in a fast-paced environment and efficiently multitask. Strong written and oral communication skills.

SS&C Technologies is seeking a Software Development Engineer in Test (SDET) to support our strategic investment in AI-enabled quality engineering and automation. This role focuses on developing and maintaining scalable test automation infrastructure, integrating AI-assisted capabilities into quality engineering workflows, and improving the reliability and efficiency of software delivery across multiple engineering teams. The ideal candidate brings hands-on experience in software engineering, test automation, AI-assisted development tools, and CI/CD infrastructure, with an interest in advancing modern quality engineering practices across the organization. Why Join SS&C SS&C combines proprietary technology with deep industry expertise to support complex financial and healthcare operations. Our teams design, implement, and operate solutions that help clients manage data, automate processes, and scale their businesses with confidence. You will work with industry experts, modern platforms, and evolving technologies, gaining exposure to real-world operational challenges and large-scale enterprise environments. What You'll Do Use AI-assisted development tools (e.g., Claude Code, GitHub Copilot, Cursor) as a routine part of development, debugging, and automated test authoring, while establishing effective practices for their use across engineering teams. Build and integrate AI-assisted capabilities into quality engineering platforms, including automated test-generation scaffolding, failure categorization, root-cause analysis, and intelligent alert routing. Develop, extend, and maintain scalable API-level and UI-level test automation frameworks supporting multiple scrum teams. Build and maintain CI/CD pipelines (e.g., GitHub Actions, Jenkins) to support automated integration and regression testing, improve test selection, and reduce feedback-loop times. Troubleshoot CI failures, flaky tests, and environment-related issues to improve testing reliability and development efficiency. Develop and maintain test-reporting, monitoring, and alerting infrastructure using observability and data tools such as Grafana, InfluxDB, and integrations with PagerDuty, Slack, or Microsoft Teams. Integrate LLM APIs and AI-assisted automation capabilities into internal engineering tools, quality workflows, and CI pipelines. Collaborate with product development teams to identify testing needs and deliver automation frameworks, CI improvements, and AI-assisted engineering solutions. Advocate for and support the adoption of AI-assisted quality engineering practices through peer guidance, knowledge sharing, and documentation. Participate in code reviews with a focus on reliability, maintainability, and appropriate validation of AI-generated code. Develop documentation, onboarding resources, and runbooks to promote consistent adoption and effective use of quality engineering tools. Experience integrating LLM APIs or AI-assisted automation capabilities into developer tooling, automated testing infrastructure, CI/CD pipelines, or internal engineering services. Strong programming skills in Python, JavaScript/TypeScript, or a comparable programming language. Hands-on experience developing and maintaining automated API, integration, or UI testing frameworks. Willingness to advocate for, support, and educate peers on AI-assisted engineering practices.

JOB DESCRIPTION ABOUT THE ROLE We are looking for a Director of Data Engineering to provide technical and people leadership across strategic data initiatives supporting our Advertising Sales organization. In this role, you will lead the development of scalable data solutions that drive Business Intelligence (BI) automation and enable the next generation of agent-to-agent advertising experiences. You will be responsible for building and evolving a common context layer that brings together critical data and business context, creating a trusted foundation for analytics, automation, and AI-powered workflows. You will partner closely with engineering, product, data, and business stakeholders to translate complex Ad Sales needs into scalable data capabilities while leading and developing a high-performing engineering team.

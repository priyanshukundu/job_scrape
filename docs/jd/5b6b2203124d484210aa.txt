Regular or Temporary: Regular Language Fluency: English (Required) Work Shift: 1st shift (United States of America) Please review the following job description: The Business Risk Senior Consultant supporting Technology, Data & Operations, is responsible for operationalizing risk management and front-line risk activities across assigned areas. They will act as the connective tissue between AI/Product Engineering, business process owners, and independent risk functions, translating policy, standards, and risk requirements into processes, controls, and decision points that are implemented, evidenced, and monitored across the AI lifecycle. They will lead a team of risk professionals and partner with technology and business leadership to identify, assess, monitor, and escalate risks related to business operations, emerging technologies, AI adoption, and strategic transformation. They will establish monitoring, KRIs, issue management, and reporting to demonstrate effective AI risk management and promotes business ownership, accountability, and risk-informed decision-making. *** Role is NOT remote *** *** In office expectations are 5 days per week. *** Essential Duties & Responsibilities: Risk Culture & Operational Integration 1. Advise business managers and leadership on strategic execution of enterprise risk programs and governance practices, providing guidance on the identification, assessment, and escalation of operational, control, and regulatory risks within assigned business areas. 2. Shape resolution of risk and governance issues by influencing business ownership and accountability, reinforcing governance expectations and enterprise risk requirements through strategic stakeholder engagement and risk-based guidance. Governance, Reporting & Leadership Support 3. Advise on governance forums, reporting processes, and escalation routines, providing expert analysis and interpretation of KRIs, operational risk metrics, and governance reporting to inform leadership decision-making and enhance oversight 4. Guide the development and enhancement of governance deliverables and reporting outputs, including governance materials, action tracking, and remediation oversight, ensuring alignment with enterprise requirements and enabling accurate, timely insights for stakeholders. Business Coordination & Operational Support 5. Facilitate coordination of governance activities, stakeholder communications, and enterprise partner engagement, including collaboration with ECCO, Compliance, Audit, Legal, and other governance functions to support aligned risk management and oversight. 6. Guide audit, regulatory, and enterprise readiness efforts, providing subject matter expertise for examinations, reviews, remediation activities, and operational readiness for business initiatives and governance programs. Business-Specific Risk Support & Execution 7. Advise on enterprise risk program activities, including RCSA, issue management, risk monitoring, and remediation tracking, providing insight and interpretation across supported business areas. 8. Guide implementation of risk management requirements and governance practices, offering risk-based input on operational monitoring, business-specific governance needs, and corrective action planning to strengthen overall risk posture.

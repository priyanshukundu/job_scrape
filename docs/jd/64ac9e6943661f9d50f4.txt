JPMorgan Chase

Chase Auto serves as a leading provider of auto financing to dealers, consumers, and manufacturers, with a portfolio of over $80 billion in assets and relationships across 75% of U.S. franchised automotive dealers. The Commercial Solutions group within Chase Auto delivers a full suite of commercial banking solutions for auto dealers, including Treasury Services, floorplan, real estate and acquisition financing, liquidity management and other banking solutions. You will help turn business priorities into action for Chase Auto Commercial Solutions, building the field infrastructure, and driving field readiness and change execution to equip bankers with the materials, communications, and timely updates they need to execute. As a Senior Associate, Field Enablement & Change Lead in Chase Auto Commercial Solutions, you will play a pivotal role in connecting business priorities with field execution. You will build the communications and resource infrastructure that empowers the field sales organization and lead change management to prepare Bankers, Treasury Management Officers (TMOs), and Portfolio Managers to embrace and execute business changes. Your responsibilities span field communications and resources, change management, and representing the business voice with our Marketing and LRCC (Legal, Risk, Compliance & Controls) partners. This role offers the opportunity to build critical new and foundational capabilities to the business, bring clarity to evolving priorities, and strengthen collaboration across the field. You will report to the Head of Commercial Solutions Field Enablement, Chase Auto Commercial Solutions and partner with the Commercial Solutions business salesforce (e.g., National Bankers, Regional Bankers, Treasury Management Officers, and Portfolio Managers) to support business priorities and help grow our business. Job responsibilities Lead ongoing field communications for Commercial Solutions, bringing business priorities to life through clear, actionable messages that engage the field sales organization. Build the foundation for effective field communications through standardized materials, reusable templates, and a new field resource hub – a central home for communications and resources. Keep materials current, consistent, and easy to access so bankers have the resources they need at their fingertips. Partner closely with key business stakeholders, including the Head of Treasury Services and the Head of National Sales, to develop execution plans, and coordinate the materials and activities required to support implementation Bring leaders and the field together through townhalls – shaping agendas, preparing materials, coordinating speakers, and capturing feedback and follow-up actions. Drive and own the end-to-end field change management process from planning through implementation, coordinating readiness activities and delivering timely updates that give Bankers clarity on what is changing, required actions, and execution timelines. Equip bankers to execute business changes with practical guidance and resources. Use field feedback to identify and address readiness gaps, support adoption and consistent execution, and align change activities across functional areas. Surface risks early with clear asks and options. Connect field needs with marketing priorities, serving as the key interface to align business priorities, materials, messaging, and delivery timelines. Bring field events to life from planning through delivery – coordinating agendas, logistics, stakeholder participation, and follow-up activities. Lead business readiness for new product and capability launches, developing the communications, training, procedures, and other enablement required for successful implementation and adoption. Strengthen the business’ operating rhythms through clear cadences, effective meeting templates, and disciplined tracking of decisions, risks, and action items to drive accountability and timely execution. Serve as the key interface with LRCC, bringing together relevant materials, stakeholder input, and follow-up actions to support field initiatives and governance requirements. Required qualifications, capabilities and skills Bachelor’s degree or equivalent experience. Strong understanding of internal communications and change management, with the ability to translate business priorities into clear, actionable, fit-for-purpose communications and resources for the field. Strong content development skills, including proficiency in PowerPoint and Word, with the ability to synthesize complex business information into clear, concise, and visually effective communications, presentations, and field resources. Strong planning and organizational skills to independently build detailed execution and readiness plans, and manage communications, resources, events, and competing deadlines. Analytical skills and sound judgment to assess business impacts, identify readiness gaps, interpret feedback, recommend improvements, resolve issues within scope, and escalate complex or governance matters to appropriate partners. Strong relationship-building skills to advise field stakeholders and coordinate with sales, marketing, and governance partners on shared priorities. Strong verbal and written communication skills to simplify complex updates into clear, accurate materials and guidance, clarify required actions, and communicate progress and next steps.

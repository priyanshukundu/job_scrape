As t he Associate Retirement Client Advisor , you will provide ongoing support to the RPS team and manage certain account relationships within an assigned book of business. The Associate Retirement Client Advisor works with various aspects of the retirement plan management practice including operations, investment management, knowledge, the client service model and new business acquisition. How you’ll spend your time: Serves on the client service team to assist in managing client relationships to help deliver the client service model to clients. Acts as the primary contact on a select group of assigned accounts. Participates in client and prospect meetings as well as networking opportunities. Monitors and manages required plan and participant notifications and reports. Communicates with clients and follows up on advice delivery . Develops competency with internal and external software packages to manage client relationships. Prepares investment analysis and financial related projects as needed. Develops competency in the Customer Relationship Management (CRM) and manages workflows and related documentation requirements. Facilitates best practices and assists in monitoring and satisfying compliance requirements.

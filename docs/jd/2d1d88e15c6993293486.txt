The Senior Associate for Credit RFPs is part of Sales Enablement in HarbourVest’s Marketing team, and responsible for leading Request for Proposals (RFPs) and other prospective investor requests such as follow-up questionnaires and fundraising inquiries in support of our Credit products. The individual will work closely with teams across the firm to manage response processes. They will synthesize investment information and help deliver accurate, compelling, client-ready materials. This occurs in a fast-paced global environment. The ideal candidate is someone who has: A seasoned RFP professional who can independently quarterback sophisticated, high-stakes investor requests from intake through final submission. Fluent in Credit concepts and able to translate investment content into clear, compliant messaging. A strong writer and editor who produces concise, accurate, and persuasive narratives tailored to institutional audiences. An effective cross-functional partner who can influence senior team members, drive timely inputs, and resolve content gaps with minimal issue. Detail-focused, capable of improving RFP workflows, maintaining a high-quality content library, and applying improvements in an RFP platform/knowledge base. Comfortable operating in a fast-paced environment, prioritizing competing deadlines while maintaining exceptional attention to detail. What you will do: Lead end-to-end delivery of RFPs, DDQs, RFIs, consultant questionnaires, and ad hoc investor inquiries for HarbourVest’s Credit products, ensuring accuracy, responsiveness, and on-time completion. Serve as project owner for sophisticated submission including setting timelines, assigning responsibilities, running kickoffs, driving follow-ups, and removing roadblocks to keep team members accountable. Partner closely with investment professionals to craft and refine Credit-specific content (strategy, sourcing, underwriting, portfolio construction, risk management, performance drivers, ESG/DEI integration where applicable). Ensure all deliverables align with SEC marketing rules and internal marketing compliance standards; proactively identify and raise potential compliance issues. Own and continuously improve the RFP content library/knowledge base: establish standards for version control, source-of-truth data, attribution, and refresh cadences. Analyze themes across investor feedback and RFP trends to propose enhancements to messaging, data presentation, and supporting collateral. Build strong relationships across Sales, Investments, Operations, Legal, and Compliance to streamline inputs and improve turnaround times. Act as an individual contributor and subject-matter resource—providing peer review, informal mentoring, and best-practice guidance to junior team members to elevate quality and consistency across submissions. What you bring: Proficiency with MS Office, Adobe Acrobat, and RFP software can be considered an asset 5-8+ years of proven experience in investment management, institutional marketing/sales enablement, or roles focused on RFP and DDQ coordination; Credit experience strongly preferred. Demonstrated expertise writing about Credit strategies and portfolio construction, capable of synthesizing investment, risk, and operational details into investor-ready responses. Proven track record to lead multiple concurrent deadlines and handle sophisticated team member groups with minimal oversight. Exceptional writing, editing, and quality-control skills, including comfort with numerical/financial data and consistency checks across sources. Strong understanding of SEC marketing rule considerations and the rigor to partner effectively with Compliance and Legal. Strong proficiency in Microsoft Office (especially Word, Excel, PowerPoint) and Adobe Acrobat; experience with RFP/knowledge management platforms (e.g., Responsive, Loopio, Qvidian, or similar) is a plus. High attention to detail, excellent judgment, and capacity for protecting confidential information. Clear, professional communicator with the ability to interact credibly with investment teams and senior partners. Comfort with creating and maintaining process documentation, templates, and standard operating procedures. Education Preferred: Bachelor of Arts (B.A) or equivalent experience Bachelor of Science (B.S) or equivalent experience Experience: Typically, 5-8+ years of demonstrated experience in an institutional RFP/DDQ function or investment management environment is required.

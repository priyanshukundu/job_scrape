Contribute to the design, development, implementation, and support of enterprise applications, APIs, integrations, and automation solutions. Design, code, test, debug, document, and deploy high-quality software solutions following engineering best practices. Collaborate with product owners, architects, and business partners to translate business requirements into technical solutions. Troubleshoot and resolve complex production issues while ensuring application stability, performance, and resiliency. Participate in Agile delivery processes, including sprint planning, backlog refinement, estimation, and code reviews. Mentor junior engineers and promote engineering excellence through knowledge sharing and best practices. Lead projects and serve as an escalation point for technical issues while providing guidance and direction to less experienced team members.

Working within East coast hours required JOB BRIEF (PURPOSE) The Product Risk team within Commercial Bank is responsible for the governance and tactile execution of owning and managing risk, optimizing risk and return, and enabling sound growth with speed for the business. As part of this team, KeyBank is seeking a Digital Assets Sr. Risk Manager to support the build out and ongoing oversight of Key’s Digital Asset strategy, with a focus on tokenized deposits and stablecoin. The role has responsibility for partnering with Digital Assets product leadership to asses risk implications of Key’s digital asset strategy, development of the risk and control environment for digital assets across the bank, and maintaining oversight and reporting as required across products. This also includes understanding regulatory impacts, industry perspective and Key’s own risk policies. The role requires an individual who demonstrates the ability to influence others, build meaningful relationships across the organization, act with a sense of urgency, and has solid communication skills. ESSENTIAL JOB FUNCTIONS Partner with Product across Digital Assets to assess the inherent risk of product changes, both for new development and future enhancements/expansions develop an appropriate control environment to manage the business within Key’s risk appetite define KPIs and KRIs to monitor and manage the environment ensure all stablecoin and tokenized deposit initiatives meet applicable legal, compliance, and risk standards. Partner with 2nd line of defense risk management to maintain risk requirements in bank-wide policies and procedures Drive cross-functional alignment on risk management for digital assets Partner with Product and BRC teams on creation of controls and procedures Develop awareness and understanding of risk framework throughout Commercial Bank Maintain and understanding of regulations, regulatory guidance and network rules impacting supported products, including changes and trends in regulatory policies related to stablecoins and digital assets Create presentations and present as required Use a continuous improvement mindset to improve risk identification, mitigation, and process efficacy QUALIFICATIONS Education Bachelor’s degree required, master’s degree optional; Work Experience Minimum 5+ years industry experience in Payments Risk Management, Operational Risk or Compliance Skills Demonstrated knowledge of digital assets, stablecoins, blockchain infrastructure and monitoring Understanding of risk management principles, best practices and regulatory requirements related to compliance, Anti-Money Laundering (AML), credit, and operational risk Excellent written and oral communication, interpersonal, and organizational skills Embrace a client-focused, positive mind-set work ethic Have desire to work in a team environment and willingness to backup team members as needed Proven ability to build strong collaborative relationship with internal partners throughout the organization including business development teams, operations, compliance, and all other areas of risk Be self-motivated and results-oriented Be able to function effectively under pressure Proficiency with PCs and standard Microsoft Office software COMPENSATION AND BENEFITS This position is eligible to earn a base salary in the range of $96,000.00 - $181,000.00 annually.

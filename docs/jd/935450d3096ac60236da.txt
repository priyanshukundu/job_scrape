Job Description Innovation isn’t just what we do; it’s how we work. At FIS, you’ll collaborate with talented people across the globe to create solutions that shape the way the world pays, banks and invests. If you’re ready to grow your career while making a meaningful impact, we’d love to meet you. About the role As a Lead Artificial Intelligence/Machine Learning Engineer, you will join a growing AI Platform organization and provide technical leadership within the Evaluation & Compliance area. You will remain hands-on while setting direction and leading execution across complex production AI initiatives, with particular focus on GenAI agents, agent platforms, evaluation, performance measurement, CI quality gates, guardrails and engineering standards. You will partner across product, platform, data, risk, compliance and business teams. Your key responsibilities Set technical direction for production AI/ML systems and translate ambiguous business opportunities into architectures, workstreams, decision criteria and measurable outcomes. Lead the design, deployment and operation of generative AI and agentic architectures, including models, orchestration, tools, retrieval, data flows, safety controls and enterprise integrations. Establish evaluation strategy and quality gates spanning baselines, gold datasets, automated and human evaluation, statistical analysis, regression testing and production monitoring. Define reusable standards and reference patterns for observability, traceability, security, privacy, responsible AI, release management and operational readiness. Lead architecture and design reviews; identify failure modes, technical debt, scalability constraints and build-versus-buy tradeoffs before they become delivery risks. Guide optimization of AI systems across accuracy, latency, throughput, reliability, infrastructure consumption and model or token cost. Shape AI-agent platform capabilities and reusable services that enable teams to safely build, evaluate, deploy and operate agents at scale through APIs, event-driven services and protocols such as MCP or comparable patterns. Partner with Product, Platform, Risk, Compliance, Legal and domain leaders to align technical decisions with client needs, regulatory obligations and business priorities. Lead complex incident resolution and root-cause analysis across models, data, prompts, tools, infrastructure and evaluation systems. Create technical roadmaps, decompose work across engineers and teams, manage dependencies and communicate decisions and risks to senior stakeholders. Mentor senior and developing engineers, improve engineering review practices and build a culture of disciplined experimentation, documentation and accountable delivery. Evaluate emerging models, frameworks and vendors through evidence-based prototypes and recommend adoption, containment or retirement based on measurable value and risk. What you bring Required qualifications Deep hands-on experience designing, building, deploying and operating GenAI agents or agentic systems in production, including substantial ownership of AI-agent or AI-platform capabilities. Generic exposure to ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot or prompt engineering alone is not sufficient. Strong coding and software engineering expertise in Python, Java or another relevant production language, including architecture, APIs, distributed systems, testing, CI/CD, version control and production troubleshooting. Demonstrated experience leading AI/ML architecture decisions and establishing evaluation, deployment or operational standards used by other engineers. Experience with modern generative AI and agentic systems, including retrieval, tool/function calling, structured outputs, orchestration, prompting, context design and failure handling. Experience designing rigorous evaluation programs that connect offline quality, human review, production telemetry and business outcomes. Experience with cloud-native production environments, containers, Kubernetes, observability and secure integration with enterprise data and services. Ability to influence across organizational boundaries, communicate complex tradeoffs to executive and technical audiences and drive decisions without relying solely on formal authority. A university degree in computer science, engineering, data science or a related field, or equivalent practical experience. Preferred qualifications Experience building AI platforms, evaluation platforms, model gateways, agent runtimes or shared developer capabilities used across multiple teams. Experience with MCP or comparable tool-integration protocols, Langfuse or equivalent observability, Snowflake, GitHub Actions and Kubernetes. Knowledge of model risk management, responsible AI, information security, privacy and regulated financial-services environments. Experience with champion-challenger approaches, controlled rollouts, feature flags and measurable optimization of cost, latency and quality. Experience conducting technical due diligence and build-versus-buy analysis for AI models, platforms and vendors. History of mentoring senior engineers, leading technical communities or establishing engineering standards across teams. The impact you will make You will help FIS scale from individual AI implementations to durable enterprise capabilities. Your leadership will improve architecture consistency, evaluation discipline, producti on reliability and responsible adoption while shortening the path from experimentation to measurable client and business value.

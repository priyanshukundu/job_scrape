Robinhood's Platforms Governance team works to keep our platforms secure and trustworthy. We partner with business teams to design the structures, policies, and processes that put safety first, guide decisions, manage risk, and support compliance. Our work also covers fraud prevention, detection, investigation, and recovery. Together, these efforts protect the firm and our customers from the impact of fraud and protect the integrity of our operations.

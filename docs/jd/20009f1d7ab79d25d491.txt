Description Are you passionate about figuring out whether a new data source actually moves the needle on existing models? Then this is your next job. We are seeking a data scientist to deepen our expertise in third-party data evaluation to improve models built in USDS. You will own the end-to-end process of identifying, testing, and quantifying the incremental value of external data assets across USDS, and with underwriting and product stakeholders to ensure external data is used thoughtfully, responsibly, and in ways that measurably improve model performance and business outcomes. This role requires strong technical depth, sound statistical judgment, comfort managing vendor relationships and multiple concurrent tests, and the ability to translate complex analytical findings into clear, actionable recommendations for both technical and non-technical audiences. Candidates who live within 50 miles of Boston, MA; Portsmouth, NH; Seattle, WA; Columbus, OH; or Plano, TX will follow a hybrid schedule, coming into the office two days per week. Otherwise, this role is remote with occasional travel. Please note: Resume review will begin on 11/30/2026. Applications submitted in the meantime will be reviewed and you will be notified by email regarding next steps. Responsibilities: Lead the research, prioritization, and evaluation of third-party data sources for design and modeling teams across USDS (BL Design, PL Auto Design, PL Home Design, Claims, Service, Distribution, Marketing, Demand), maintaining and refining the vendor data prioritization framework (benefit, cost, strategic alignment) Own the vendor data acquisition process — preparing data samples, coordinating secure data transfer with vendors, and partnering with data teams on scrambling, PII handling, and joining vendor data to modeling datasets Design and execute rigorous test methodology to quantify the incremental lift, tradeoffs, and risk associated with third-party data in predictive models, including exploratory analysis, variable transformation, residual analysis, and scoring/CBA Partner with design and modeling teams across USDS to interpret vendor test results and translate them into clear purchase / no-purchase recommendations and readouts for design and business leadership Contribute third-party data and diagnostics into broader modeling efforts across USDS Serve as a technical consultant on projects involving third-party data, guiding model design decisions, testing standards, and best practices across teams Build and maintain effective relationships with external vendors and internal cross-functional partners Engage with the broader data science community to stay current on emerging third-party data sources, vendor testing tooling, regulatory considerations, and analytical methods Mentor and develop other data scientists on vendor evaluation methodology and technical best practices; independently manage ambiguous, multi-team scope; and help shape TPD strategy, prioritization standards, and testing methodology across USDS Qualifications Broad knowledge of predictive analytic techniques and statistical diagnostics of models. Advanced knowledge of predictive toolset; reflects as expert resource for tool development. Demonstrated ability to exchange ideas and convey complex information clearly and concisely. Ability to establish and build relationships within and outside the organization. Ability to give effective training and presentations to management and other groups. Ability to use results of analysis to persuade team, department management or senior management to a particular course of action. Broad knowledge of business drivers and market context. Has a value driven perspective with regard to understanding of work context and impact. Competencies typically acquired through a Ph.D. degree (in Statistics, Mathematics, Economics, Actuarial Science or other scientific field of study) and a minimum of 3 years of relevant experience, a Master`s degree (scientific field of study) and a minimum of 6 years of relevant experience or may be acquired through a Bachelor`s degree (scientific field of study) and a minimum of 8 years of relevant experience.

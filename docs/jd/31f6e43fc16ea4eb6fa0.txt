ABOUT THE ROLE: The Fixed Income Trading Director is responsible for driving trading strategy, managing risk, and delivering liquidity solutions across primary and secondary markets. This role combines deep market expertise with leadership in revenue generation, inventory management, and regulatory compliance. The Director partners closely with Institutional and Retail Sales teams, provides strategic trade ideas, and ensures operational excellence in trade execution and reporting. Additionally, this position plays a key role in mentoring team members and shaping Fifth Third’s competitive position in fixed income markets. Please see below for specific responsibilities and requirements for this role. YOUR RESPONSIBILITIES: The responsibilities for this role are below; you may support projects of varying complexity based on skill and organizational priorities. Execute Fixed Income Trades across assigned sectors to support Institutional and Retail Sales representatives, ensuring timely and accurate execution. Develop Revenue-Generating Strategies by providing innovative trade ideas and market insights to Sales teams. Manage Inventory Positions within established policy limits, balancing risk and profitability. Maintain Broker Relationships with national and regional dealers to optimize liquidity and market access. Ensure Regulatory Compliance by filing required reports and adhering to all applicable regulations and internal policies. Oversee Trade Processing and clearing system accuracy for all executed transactions. Provide Product Expertise through formal training sessions and daily collaboration with Sales teams. Contribute to Strategic Initiatives that enhance trading capabilities and client service delivery. MINIMUM REQUIREMENTS: Bachelor’s degree in Finance, Accounting, Economics, or a related field. 10 years of experience in Banking, Investment Banking, M&A, or Capital Markets (strong Analyst performance required for internal promotion). Securities Industry Essentials (SIE), Series 7, and Series 63 licenses (or ability to obtain within company policy). REQUIRED SKILLS : Qualified candidates will bring professional proficiency, generally acquired through 10 years of relevant work or internship experience in finance, accounting, or a related field. Deep knowledge of fixed income instruments, market dynamics, and trading strategies. Expertise in risk management, inventory control, and regulatory compliance. Advanced proficiency in Bloomberg and trade processing systems. Strong analytical skills with the ability to identify market opportunities and optimize execution. Exceptional communication and relationship-building skills with internal teams and external counterparties. Ability to lead projects and mentor team members in a fast-paced, high-pressure environment. SKILLS FOR SUCCESS: Successful candidates will demonstrate a higher degree of proficiency (typically from 10 years of practical experience or advanced academic exposure) across the following areas: Strategic Market Insight – Anticipate market trends and position the trading desk for success. Operational Excellence – Ensure flawless execution and compliance across all trading activities. Revenue Leadership – Drive profitability through innovative trade ideas and client engagement. Team Development – Build capability through mentorship and knowledge sharing.

Lead Software Engineer – Enterprise Design System
M&T Bank
Location: Buffalo, NY
Link: https://mtb.wd5.myworkdayjobs.com/MTB/job/Buffalo-NY/Lead-Software-Engineer---Enterprise-Design-System_R91611

Overview: Builds and maintains a shared front-end design system (component library and supporting tooling) used across the Bank’s web applications. This role focuses on TypeScript-based component engineering, Node.js-powered build/test/release pipelines and deployments, accessibility and quality standards, and enablement of internal product teams through documentation, support, and AI-assisted development practices. Takes end-to-end responsibility for substantial engineering initiatives, resolves complex technical problems, and strengthens team execution through coordinated contributions, code review, and coaching. Partners with the Principal Engineer, who sets system-wide architecture and technical direction. This is a hands-on individual contributor role with no people management responsibilities. Primary Responsibilities: Own substantial web engineering initiatives from requirements through delivery: clarify outcomes, review and approve technical requirements and implementation plans, break work into tasks, and coordinate contributions and dependencies. Align milestones with product, design, delivery, and the Principal Engineer; track blockers and risks and communicate tradeoffs early. Review and approve pull requests and component design proposals for web contributions, provide clear and constructive feedback, and merge approved changes into the release path within approved Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC) and change management processes. Resolve complex library, integration, build, and packaging issues with team engineers and consuming teams. Triage and route requests, unblock engineers through pairing and hands-on support, and turn recurring issues into reusable guidance and developer experience improvements. Guide and coach engineers on component API design, TypeScript, accessibility, testing, and maintainability, helping them grow their skills and take on more complex work. Develop and document reusable implementation patterns and coding conventions within established architecture. Help engineers apply them consistently and document significant implementation decisions and tradeoffs. Keep the team aligned on design system architecture by translating technical direction from the Principal Engineer into practical implementation guidance, and raise architectural concerns or proposed changes from the team. Shape the team’s peer review practices and help other engineers become effective reviewers. Review unit/integration, accessibility, and visual regression test approaches, coverage, and results within owned domains and initiatives. Address material gaps before release and coach engineers on test effectiveness and areas of opportunity. Write clean, efficient, secure, and resilient TypeScript code at an expert level. Deliver complex components and foundational patterns, ensuring related contributions integrate into maintainable, supportable solutions. Apply AI-assisted and agentic engineering practices across implementation, investigation, code review, testing, and documentation. Remain accountable for the correctness, security, accessibility, and maintainability of every merged change, whether an engineer or an AI agent wrote it. Review AI-generated code, plans, and analysis with the same rigor as human contributions. Check claims against the source, confirm behavior through tests and in supported browsers, and reject output that looks plausible but is wrong. Maintain the team’s agent guidance (e.g., repository instructions, skills, and review workflows) as a versioned engineering asset. Turn recurring AI errors into lasting fixes, such as lint rules, type checks, CI gates, or guidance updates, rather than one-off corrections. Coach engineers on effective, disciplined use of AI tools: scoping and briefing agents, recognizing when an agent is not the right tool, and weighing cost, review burden, and risk. Contribute to the AI-facing guidance the design system ships to consuming teams (e.g., Figma Code Connect mappings, agent skills, and machine-readable component documentation), so that their AI tools produce correct, accessible usage of the library. Translate UX/UI designs into robust, accessible components, resolving ambiguous or complex interactions with designers. Develop Web Components, component APIs, and framework integrations for consistent adoption across applications. Build components using accessibility (a11y) best practices (semantic markup, keyboard/focus behavior, appropriate ARIA), collaborate with the team’s Accessibility Lead on standards and conformance, and help engineers apply accessibility requirements in their contributions. Drive adherence to performance, resiliency, and optimization practices within the library (e.g., bundle size, rendering performance, browser support), and recommend metrics to track library health. Own agreed areas of Node.js build, test, lint, and packaging tooling and CI/CD infrastructure for packages, documentation sites, and related assets. Maintain reliable publishing and deployment workflows, including containerized builds and web server configuration; resolve failures and document recovery procedures. Partner with the Principal Engineer on architecture and platform choices. Build, maintain, and improve Node.js-based tooling for the library (build, test, lint, and packaging), with a focus on build reliability and developer experience. Plan and execute releases for owned packages or initiatives, coordinating dependencies, verification, and readiness within approved change management processes. Maintain semantic versioning, change logs, prerelease channels, and deprecation and migration guidance. Provide limited on-call support as needed for system outages and after-hours or late-night releases. Serve as an escalation point for complex integration, build, and packaging issues raised by consuming teams, and identify recurring issues to improve developer experience (DX). Review and approve technical requirements and implementation plans for design system work in alignment with product, design, and delivery partners, ensuring completeness, accuracy, and feasibility. Translate architectural direction into practical implementation guidance and recommend improvements to architecture, patterns, and practices with the Principal Engineer. Author technical proposals and present at architecture peer group and review board meetings as needed. Communicate technical progress and challenges to stakeholders, present the team’s work at internal meetings, and author internal blog posts or knowledge-sharing content. Understand and adhere to the Company’s risk and regulatory standards, policies, and controls in accordance with the Company’s Risk Appetite. Identify risk-related issues needing escalation to management. Use only approved AI tools and services in accordance with the Company’s information security and data-handling policies, and escalate AI-related risks as needed. Promote an environment that supports a culture of belonging and reflects the M&T Bank brand. Maintain M&T internal control standards, including timely implementation of internal and external audit points together with any issues raised by external regulators as applicable. Complete other related duties as assigned.

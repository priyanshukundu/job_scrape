The Role In this role you will help build cloud infrastructure and data pipelines that support a state-of-the-art, AI-based application used by our analyst teams in the Voice of the Customer workspace. In this role you will have direct exposure to and gain a better understanding of big data management and the LLM’s that integrate with them. The Expertise and Skills You Bring You have a working knowledge of SQL with some Snowflake experience. You have experience with Object Oriented Programming and can write easy to read, serviceable and maintainable code. You are familiar with Test Driven Development and put quality first through User Acceptance Testing best practices. You are familiar with basic code branch management and Continuous Integration and Continuous Development strategies. You are familiar with Python application development and basic package management. You possess the ability to problem solve and you can communicate effectively with others and work well together in teams. Nice to Have: Experience with deploying apps in AWS/Azure platforms Exposure to Docker and Kubernetes Knowledge in designing applications and databases Experience in designing and implementing Snowflake, SQL, Python Bachelor’s degree in a STEM related field. The Team Our team supports Fidelity by leveraging Voice of the Customer data to help identify customer friction and customer opportunities. We help our analyst community identify these opportunities by curating data layers specific to their needs and by making this curated data available in an AI-based platform used to separate signal from noise. Analysts report out signals to executive leadership, and our leadership teams help steer Fidelity in the direction best suited to our customers’ needs.

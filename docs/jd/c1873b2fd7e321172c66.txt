JOB DESCRIPTION ABOUT THE ROLE We are looking for a Staff Backend Software Engineer to lead delivery of reliable, scalable services for audio/video hosting, monetization, and analytics in the media and creator space at a global footprint scale. You will work primarily in Python on APIs, asynchronous background job orchestration, infrastructure-as-code, and data pipelines alongside Celery, PostgreSQL, Redis, RabbitMQ, and cloud primitives on AWS and GCP, partnering with product, frontend, and infrastructure engineers. A SNAPSHOT OF YOUR RESPONSIBILITIES Lead design and implementation of RESTful APIs and async workers(Celery) for publishing, media processing, SSAI and VAST-related workflows, imports, and reporting. Own reliability and performance of distributed systems and workloads: queue design, deduplication, chord and dispatch patterns, prefetch tuning, backpressure, and operational logging that supports incident response. Drive data and integration decisions across PostgreSQL, Redis-backed coordination, object storage, and systems such as data warehouse-backed reporting (batching, limits, and cost-aware query patterns). Lead or significantly contribute to Terraform and AWS changes affecting the application: EB tiers, IAM, networking, WAF and rate limits, observability hooks, and phased rollouts. Raise the bar on observability: structured logging, Prometheus-style metrics, Grafana and CloudWatch usage, and alarms wired to actionable on-call signals Define and evolve testing strategy for critical paths (unit and regression around task helpers and services, AI-driven testing), CI-friendly checks, and operational readiness for risky migrations. Represent backend concerns in planning, design reviews, and incident-style discussions; communicate trade-offs clearly to technical and non-technical partners. Anticipate scaling and complexity: propose phased migrations, safer defaults, and long-term maintainability over short-term hacks. WHAT YOU WILL NEED 10+ years of professional backend or backend-heavy engineering experience in platform systems, or equivalent depth, including meaningful ownership of large services or platforms. Strong Python and a track record designing service-oriented systems: APIs, background jobs, idempotency, failure modes, and capacity thinking. Deep hands-on experience with Celery (or similar) and message brokers (AMQP and RabbitMQ-class), including heartbeats, retries, queue splits, and operational debugging. Strong SQL (PostgreSQL) and practical Redis usage beyond simple caching: coordination, rate limiting, circuit breaking, and correctness under concurrency. Substantial cloud infrastructure experience with GCP and AWS (for example AWS: EC2, ALB, IAM, CloudWatch, S3, RDS and proxy patterns) and comfort leading Terraform changes. Container building, deployment (Docker, ECS, Kubernetes), and multi-cloud networking a strong plus. Substantial experience with CI/CD in cloud environment. Demonstrated leadership in security-minded engineering: scoped IAM, TLS for data stores, network egress discipline, WAF and rate-limiting concepts. Experience with authorization at scale (RBAC, ABAC, or policy engines) is a strong plus. Excellent judgment under ambiguity, written communication, and a record of raising team quality without blocking delivery. Experience in early start-up or otherwise fast moving, rapidly evolving environments, and have ideally built products from 0 to 1. Experience successfully driving cross-team alignment to get impactful work shipped, and work with influence over authority.

Role Overview The Cboe FX and UST Data Engineering team is hiring a Senior Software Engineer. This role will design, develop, and deploy large-scale, fault tolerant data systems. We record every event on our 24x5 FX (Foreign Exchange) and UST (U.S. Treasuries) trading platforms. Billions of events are processed daily for books and records, reporting, analytics and market data products. Our proprietary tick database, built from the ground up using Java, is highly available and performant, and is a critical differentiator for Cboe FX and UST. This role entails working in a fast-paced environment where you will build and maintain software applications related to data collection and analytics. We are a global team with Software Engineering based in the NYC area and are expected to report to the office 4 times per week. Responsibilities: Build/test/maintain performant and highly reliable (24x5) software to record and process data on billions of data points daily. Drive architectural decisions/implementations for resilient, maintainable, and scalable software Communicate with stakeholders to capture/refine requirements and iterate towards a final product Identify opportunities to improve user experience, reduce costs, and improve performance. Support APIs and tools to query, summarize, and analyze big data. Automate monitoring and alerting of anomalous market conditions. Generate rich reports that deliver actionable business insights. Document relevant details in the team wiki. Leverage AI responsibly (Claude/Copilot) to optimize productivity and system reliability.

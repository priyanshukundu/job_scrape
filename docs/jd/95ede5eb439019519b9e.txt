The Opportunity Abbott Neuromodulation is seeking a Senior Research Scientist to lead translational neuroscience research focused on understanding the neural and biological mechanisms underlying neuromodulation therapies. This position is located in Plano, TX. The successful candidate will design and execute preclinical and translational studies integrating electrophysiology, neural circuit analysis, behavioral neuroscience, biomarkers, molecular biology, and advanced analytical approaches to identify mechanisms of therapeutic response and support the development of future adaptive neuromodulation systems. Working closely with molecular biology, clinical, engineering, regulatory, and data science teams, the scientist will help connect neural activity, biomarker signatures, and therapeutic outcomes to enable precision neuromodulation approaches. This role requires a scientifically rigorous and execution-oriented leader who can effectively communicate complex scientific concepts, influence cross-functional teams, represent Abbott in external collaborations, and translate research findings into actionable development programs. What You'll Do Systems Neuroscience & Neuromodulation Design and execute studies investigating neural circuit mechanisms of neuromodulation. Develop electrophysiological, behavioral, and physiological measures associated with therapeutic response. Support development of adaptive and closed-loop neuromodulation concepts. Investigate relationships between stimulation parameters, neural activity, biological response, and clinical outcomes. Translational Biomarker Discovery Integrate electrophysiological, molecular, behavioral, and clinical datasets. Identify biomarkers associated with treatment response and mechanism of action. Collaborate with genetics, transcriptomics, and molecular biology teams to develop multimodal biological signatures. Contribute to precision medicine and patient stratification approaches for neuromodulation therapies. Program Leadership & Execution Establish study objectives, milestones, budgets, timelines, and key deliverables. Develop actionable research plans and drive successful execution against program goals. Identify technical, scientific, and operational risks and develop mitigation strategies. Deliver high-quality scientific outputs on schedule and within budget. Coordinate activities across internal and external collaborators. Scientific Communication & External Engagement Serve as a scientific representative for Abbott in internal and external collaborations. Present scientific findings, program updates, and strategic recommendations to technical, business, clinical, and executive stakeholders. Represent Abbott's scientific and strategic positions professionally and effectively in meetings with investigators, key opinion leaders, research organizations, and industry partners. Author scientific publications, conference presentations, technical reports, and invention disclosures. Clinical, Preclinical & Regulatory Documentation Prepare and contribute to study protocols, technical documentation, and regulatory submissions. Develop and maintain IACUC protocols, IRB protocols, and associated research documentation. Support clinical and preclinical study execution activities in compliance with applicable regulatory and ethical requirements. Contribute to generation of evidence supporting future product development and clinical initiatives. Design Controls & Quality Systems Work within Abbott's quality system and product development processes. Contribute to design control activities including requirements development, risk analysis, verification, validation, and traceability. Collaborate with engineering, quality, and regulatory partners throughout the product development lifecycle. Ensure research activities are conducted in accordance with applicable design controls, quality standards, and regulatory requirements. Required Qualifications PhD in Neuroscience, Neuroengineering, Biomedical Engineering, Physiology, Molecular Neuroscience, Biology, or a related discipline; or Master's degree in a relevant scientific or engineering discipline with 8+ years of directly relevant experience; or Bachelor's degree in Biomedical Engineering, Neuroscience, Biological Sciences, or related discipline with 10+ years of directly relevant industry experience. 5+ years of postdoctoral and/or industry research experience leading complex neuroscience research programs. Experience designing and executing preclinical and/or translational neuroscience studies. Demonstrated expertise in one or more of the following: Electrophysiology Neural circuits Neuromodulation Systems neuroscience Biomarker discovery Molecular neuroscience Neurophysiology Translational neuroscience Strong written and verbal communication skills with the ability to communicate effectively across scientific, engineering, clinical, regulatory, and executive audiences. Demonstrated ability to define objectives, build execution plans, drive deliverables, and achieve program goals. Preferred Qualifications Experience with electrophysiological methods such as patch clamp, MEA, EEG, ECAP, LFP, calcium imaging, or neural recording systems. Experience with neuromodulation technologies including SCS, DBS, VNS, TMS, or closed-loop systems. Experience with transcriptomics, spatial biology, genomics, biomarker discovery, or multi-omics approaches. Familiarity with AI/ML approaches applied to neuroscience or multimodal biological datasets. Experience in chronic pain, movement disorders, psychiatric disorders, epilepsy, or sensory neuroscience.

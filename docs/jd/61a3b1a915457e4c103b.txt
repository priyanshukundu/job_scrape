Associate / Senior Associate, OCIO Investment Analytics & Financial Analysis | Hybrid – 4 days per week in office ABOUT THE OFFICE OF THE CHIEF INVESTMENT OFFICER The Office of the Chief Investment Officer (OCIO) leads portfolio strategy and capital allocation for New York Life's $340+ billion General Account. As the steward of the company's balance sheet, the OCIO is responsible for asset allocation, risk oversight and long-term investment performance across public and private markets. Working in close partnership with New York Life Investment Management and affiliated investment teams, the OCIO ensures disciplined portfolio construction aligned with the company's financial strength and policyholder commitments. ROLE OVERVIEW We are seeking an analytical, finance-minded Associate to join the OCIO team. The Associate will work closely with senior team members to support the full analysis lifecycle—from data sourcing and validation through financial analysis, production analytics, and results reporting and interpretation. This position is part of the OCIO Development Pool, which offers exposure across the broader OCIO alongside deep development in financial analysis and asset projection. Associates in the Development Pool also work directly with business partners across the organization, building visibility and relationships beyond the immediate team. This role will have broad responsibilities for financial analysis and for supporting our asset projection capabilities. Responsibilities will include analyzing portfolio and asset performance across traditional and alternative fixed income and equity assets, interpreting model results and translating them into business insights, supporting model and platform improvements, and ongoing production responsibilities. The role requires strong financial and analytical skills, along with working proficiency in the quantitative and technical tools used to support the analysis. Also important is effective communication and close coordination with other members within the OCIO and business stakeholders. WHAT YOU'LL DO Support asset projection and cash flow analysis for fixed income, structured products, derivatives, and insurance liability portfolios using proprietary projection software and internal databases. Help maintain and enhance data infrastructure that supports production analytics, monitoring of investment results, and regular reporting workflows. Perform financial analysis to support portfolio analytics, risk measurement, and investment strategy decision-making. Analyze portfolio and asset class performance, including return attribution, relative value, and risk/return trade-offs, and summarize drivers for senior stakeholders. Review and interpret model and projection results, identify trends and anomalies, and translate findings into clear business insights and recommendations. Monitor market, interest rate, credit, and economic developments and assess their implications for the investment portfolio and strategy. Prepare financial reports, dashboards, and presentations, including management commentary on investment results and portfolio positioning. Support finance, actuarial, and asset management teams by providing analysis and data for budgeting, forecasting, and investment planning needs, with guidance from senior team members. Assist senior team members in evaluating model enhancements and platform improvements, providing financial and business perspective on requirements and results. Support the maintenance and quality assurance of existing production processes, including data validation and results reconciliation. Prepare clear, well-documented analytical outputs and communicate findings to senior stakeholders within the OCIO team. WHAT YOU'LL BRING Bachelor's degree in Finance, Economics, Accounting, Business, Mathematics, Statistics, Financial Engineering, or a related discipline. 2-3 years of quantitative or investment analytics experience with a good understanding of markets Solid foundation in financial analysis, including financial statement analysis, valuation concepts, and investment performance and risk measurement. Working knowledge of Python or a similar language for data analysis and automation is helpful; a willingness to build technical skills on the job is expected. Proficiency in SQL and relational databases, with the ability to write queries to extract, join, and validate data from multiple sources in support of financial analysis and reporting. Exposure to C++ or C# is not required but may be helpful in the context of financial modeling. Strong command of Excel for financial analysis, reporting, and modeling; VBA experience is a plus. Understanding of fixed income instruments, equities, structured products, derivatives, or insurance liabilities—through coursework, internships, or relevant experience. Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with high attention to detail. Excellent written and verbal communication skills; ability to document methodology and present results clearly. Effective time management and organizational skills, with the ability to manage multiple priorities in a deadline-driven environment. Ability to synthesize data and analytical results into clear business insights, recommendations, and management-ready reports and presentations. PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS Exposure to asset projection platforms such as Beacon, Intex, Andrew Davidson, or Aladdin. Proficiency using AI tools to build dashboards, automate processes and examine data Experience with stochastic modeling, scenario generation, or proxy modeling techniques is a plus but not required. Experience with financial data and analytics tools such as Bloomberg, FactSet, or Power BI/Tableau for investment research and reporting. Prior internship or project experience in asset management, insurance, or a related financial services environment. Familiarity with insurance company finance, liabilities, or ALM concepts. KEY CHARACTERISTICS Intellectually curious with a desire to develop deep expertise in investment and financial analysis. Business-minded and commercially curious, with an interest in markets and the economic drivers behind investment results. Self-starter who takes initiative and follows through with minimal supervision. Collaborative and collegial; comfortable supporting and learning from experienced team members. Adaptable and composed under pressure, with the ability to handle shifting priorities and tight deadlines.

At Broadridge, we've built a culture where the highest goal is to empower others to accomplish more. If you’re passionate about developing your career, while helping others along the way, come join the Broadridge team. Broadridge is hiring! We are seeking a Financial Analyst to join its FP&A team. In this individual-contributor role, you will turn financial and operational data into clear analysis, practical insights, and recommendations that support sound business decisions. You will partner with leaders across the organization, contribute to planning and forecasting, and help explain performance, risks, and opportunities. Responsilbilities: Prepare monthly, quarterly, and annual management reporting; explain results and key variances in concise, decision-ready commentary for senior leaders. Analyze financial and operating performance—including revenue, expenses, margins, and sales—using variance, trend, and root-cause analysis to identify material drivers and emerging issues. Develop, maintain, and improve operating budgets and forecasts. Gather and reconcile relevant inputs across Finance, Sales, Marketing, Human Resources, and other partner teams. Build and maintain financial models, scenario analyses, and other forward-looking analytics to evaluate business assumptions, potential outcomes, risks, and opportunities. Meet regularly with business partners to review performance, test assumptions, and help identify actions that support future results. Communicate findings and recommendations clearly in writing and presentations, adapting the level of detail to the audience. Identify opportunities to improve Finance processes, reporting, and analytical workflows; collaborate with stakeholders to implement practical, timely improvements. Promote accurate, consistent financial analysis by validating inputs, documenting key assumptions, and escalating data or process issues when appropriate. How success looks: You deliver timely, reliable analysis; make financial results and their drivers easier to understand; build trusted working relationships with business partners; and help turn insights into measurable, well-supported actions Qualifications: Bachelor's degree in Accounting, Finance, Economics, a related field, or equivalent experience. At least 2 years of financial analysis experience. Experience with continuous improvement, root-cause analysis, and project coordination or project management. Strong analytical judgment and the ability to work independently, manage competing priorities, and navigate open-ended questions. Clear, concise written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to present financial findings to varied audiences. A collaborative, accountable approach to working with business partners and cross-functional teams. Strong Microsoft Office skills, especially Excel and Power BI Compensation Range: The salary range for this position is between $75,000 - $80,000 Bonus Eligibility: Bonus Eligible Broadridge considers various factors when evaluating a candidate's final salary including, but not limited to, relevant experience, skills, and education.

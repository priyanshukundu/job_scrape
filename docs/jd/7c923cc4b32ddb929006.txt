(2026 Inc. 5000) If you want to help build the next era of lending, we’d love to meet you. About the Role We're looking for an Underwriting Manager, Construction to join our growing team! As Underwriting Manager, Construction, you'll serve as the operational bridge across cross-functional teams, driving alignment and execution throughout the loan lifecycle. You'll manage a specialized team of Construction Underwriters and own the construction vertical across policy, process, and product success. You'll be the primary escalation point for complex, stalled transactions, making decisive structural calls to unblock workflows. You'll also structure deals upfront, oversee the pipeline, and drive efficiency and production strategy across alternative product lines, including Multifamily. With a deep understanding of secondary-market expectations, new-construction legal and insurance requirements, and multifamily cash flow analysis, you'll make sure each project is sound at origination, monitored through completion, and ready for a successful loan sale. If you want to own construction lending end to end and are ready to unblock hard deals, this role is for you.

SMBC Group is a top-tier global financial group. Headquartered in Tokyo and with a 400-year history, SMBC Group offers a diverse range of financial services, including banking, leasing, securities, credit cards, and consumer finance. The Group has more than 130 offices and 80,000 employees worldwide in nearly 40 countries. Sumitomo Mitsui Financial Group, Inc. (SMFG) is the holding company of SMBC Group, which is one of the three largest banking groups in Japan. SMFG’s shares trade on the Tokyo, Nagoya, and New York (NYSE: SMFG) stock exchanges. In the Americas, SMBC Group has a presence in the US, Canada, Mexico, Brazil, Chile, Colombia, and Peru. Backed by the capital strength of SMBC Group and the value of its relationships in Asia, the Group offers a range of commercial and investment banking services to its corporate, institutional, and municipal clients. It connects a diverse client base to local markets and the organization’s extensive global network. The Group’s operating companies in the Americas include Sumitomo Mitsui Banking Corp. (SMBC), SMBC Nikko Securities America, Inc., SMBC Capital Markets, Inc., JRI America, Inc., SMBC Leasing and Finance, Inc., Banco Sumitomo Mitsui Brasileiro S.A., and Sumitomo Mitsui Finance and Leasing Co., Ltd. Role Description MBC Capital Markets Inc (CM) is a derivatives trading company based in New York City, with offices in Charlotte, NC, London, Frankfurt and Hong Kong. CM is an established derivatives dealer with a broad product portfolio focused on interest rates and foreign exchange products such as rates & FX swaps, options & exotics as well as exchange traded products. SMBC Nikko Securities America Inc (Nikko US) is a SEC-registered securities broker-dealer, US Primary Dealer and member of the Financial Industry Regulatory Authority (FINRA) and the Municipal Securities Rulemaking Board (MSRB). These entities provide coordinated services to our corporate and institutional investor clients as part of the SMBC Global Markets business unit. SMBC Global Markets is searching for candidates to lead a first line credit team covering hedge funds and non-bank financial institutions. The role, Director level, reports to the Head of Client Lifecycle within the Chief Operating Office of SMBC Global Markets front office. The role's function is to manage the team that performs credit underwriting in support of our Global Markets investor business and to help lead first line credit transformation initiatives. The position is on the trading floor and the work environment is fast paced involving direct contact with front office staff, second line credit officers and our clients. The individual will support activities within our swap dealer and broker dealer businesses including derivatives, FX, repo and securities lending. As part of the first line of defense, the candidate will be expected to maintain sound credit risk management activities by liaising with other departments to review and assess credit risk, monitor credit exposure and provide guidance on credit limits in accordance with established policies and procedures. Role Objectives: Delivery Manage team that performs credit analyses on hedge fund, asset managers, insurance companies and banks with emphasis on investment strategy, asset quality, leverage, liquidity, historical performance, portfolio exposures and risk control framework Approves credit applications for new counterparty credit limits and the renewal of existing limits; assesses applications for compliance with Policy requirements; ultimately defends justification and business appetite to second line while seeking approval Support the negotiation of master trading and financing agreements and provides ongoing monitoring for termination events Establish and maintain strong relationships with clients through due diligence meetings, portfolio reviews and periodic performance discussions Drive continuous improvement initiatives to enhance underwriting processes, portfolio management practices, reporting capabilities and workflow efficiency Support management with first line credit transformation activities, including process redesign, control framework improvements, technology implementations and data quality initiatives Qualifications and Skills BA/BS required, preferably with concentration in finance or business 7+ years of relevant credit work experience covering hedge funds, insurance, asset managers and financial institution client types Strong understanding of derivatives and SFT product Prior team management experience or demonstrated ability to be a strong people leader Ability to operate independently, manage multiple simultaneous priorities and perform well under pressure in a demanding environment, backed by strong analytics & attention to detail Experience supporting governance and process improvement initiatives strongly preferred Additional Requirements SMBC’s employees participate in a Hybrid workforce model that provides employees with an opportunity to work from home, as well as, from an SMBC office.

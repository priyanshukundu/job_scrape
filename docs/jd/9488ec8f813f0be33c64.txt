Position Overview ATLAS SP is seeking a Software Engineer to build applications and data solutions supporting our Residential, Commercial Real Estate, and Commercial Asset Finance businesses. You will work directly with portfolio management and operations users to improve efficiency, data quality, and controls. With guidance from senior engineers, you will own smaller initiatives from requirements definition through implementation and adoption. You will use AI throughout the software development lifecycle and help build AI-powered capabilities into our platforms. Primary Responsibilities Work with business users to understand workflows and translate their needs into requirements, business rules, data dependencies, and acceptance criteria. Analyze processes and data to identify automation opportunities, investigate issues, and highlight control gaps. Design, code, test, and deploy applications and data solutions using sound software engineering practices. Develop prototypes, incorporate user feedback, and support user acceptance testing and adoption. Support and enhance vendor and proprietary systems used for lending data management, portfolio reporting, and servicing. Required Qualifications and Experience 3+ years of experience in technical roles within financial services. Relevant technical university degree. Professional experience with object-oriented programming using Python, Java, or C#. Experience with SQL and NoSQL databases and REST APIs, with a sound understanding of software design principles. Hands-on experience using AI tools to develop, test, and debug software, including reviewing and validating generated code. Experience integrating AI capabilities into applications and evaluating their outputs. Ability to clarify ambiguous business problems, ask effective questions, and translate findings into working software. Strong written and verbal communication skills, including explaining technical tradeoffs to nontechnical stakeholders. Preferred Qualifications and Experience Experience with mortgages, commercial real estate, commercial loans, or other fixed income and credit products. Familiarity with loan financing structures and portfolio management, collateral management, or servicing workflows. Experience delivering workflow automation, process improvement, or digital transformation initiatives. Experience with financial data integration, reconciliation, or reporting.

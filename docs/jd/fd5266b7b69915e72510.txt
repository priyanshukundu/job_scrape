Assessments & Exercises Senior Associate - Penetration Tester
JPMorgan Chase
Location: Wilmington, DE, United States
Link: https://jpmc.fa.oraclecloud.com/hcmUI/CandidateExperience/en/sites/CX_1001/job/210810118

Drive the security of critical banking applications and platforms through hands-on offensive testing. As an Assessments & Exercises Senior Associate in the Cybersecurity and Technology Controls organization, you will play a key role in safeguarding the firm's most vital assets. Your primary responsibility will be to plan, execute, and report on penetration tests targeting high-impact applications, platforms, and services, including those that use AI and large language models (LLMs). You will use industry-standard methodologies, advanced techniques, and AI-assisted tooling to find vulnerabilities, work with application owners to understand root causes, and guide effective remediation to strengthen the firm's security posture. We are seeking candidates with a passion for offensive security, deep technical expertise in penetration testing, an interest in how AI is changing both the attack surface and the way testing is done, and a commitment to continuous learning and excellence. Job responsibilities Plan, scope, and execute penetration testing engagements across a variety of environments, including web applications, APIs, cloud platforms, infrastructure, thick-client, mobile, and AI/LLM-enabled applications. Collect and validate prerequisites for each engagement, making sure all necessary access, documentation, and approvals are in place. Perform manual and automated testing to identify vulnerabilities, misconfigurations, and security weaknesses, using industry-standard tools, custom scripts, and AI-assisted techniques. Assess AI/LLM-enabled applications for common risks such as prompt injection, sensitive data leakage, and insecure integrations with tools or plugins. Use AI tools to make testing more efficient and broader in coverage and contribute to building AI-enabled tooling and automation that scales testing across the team. Document and communicate findings through comprehensive reports that include technical details, risk assessments, and actionable remediation recommendations. Conduct peer reviews of penetration test reports to ensure accuracy, consistency, and quality of deliverables. Collaborate with development, infrastructure, and security teams to clarify findings, support remediation efforts, and provide subject matter expertise on offensive security. Stay current with emerging threats, vulnerabilities, and attack techniques, including those targeting AI systems, by using threat intelligence, security research, and relevant industry groups. Contribute to the continuous improvement of penetration testing methodologies, tools, and frameworks, including more continuous and automated testing approaches, in alignment with firm strategy and regulatory requirements. Required qualifications, capabilities, and skills 4+ years of hands-on penetration testing experience in offensive security, with a proven track record of scoping, executing, and reporting on complex engagements. Expertise in manual penetration testing of web, API, cloud (AWS/Azure/GCP), infrastructure, thick-client, and/or mobile (Android/iOS) applications, including the use of industry-standard tools (e.g., Burp Suite, Nmap, Metasploit). Strong understanding of security assessment methodologies such as the OWASP Top Ten, NIST Cybersecurity Framework, and other relevant standards. Familiarity with AI/LLM security risks and practical experience using AI tools to support offensive security work. Ability to identify and articulate systemic security issues related to threats, vulnerabilities, and risks, and provide clear, actionable recommendations for remediation. Exceptional organizational and communication skills, including the ability to write detailed technical reports and present findings to both technical and non-technical stakeholders. Experience conducting peer reviews of penetration test reports and mentoring junior testers. Continuous learner who keeps up with the latest offensive security trends, tools, and techniques. Preferred qualifications, capabilities, and skills Hands-on experience testing AI/LLM-enabled applications, and familiarity with frameworks such as the OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications or MITRE ATLAS. Experience developing scripts, tools, or automation (including AI-enabled tooling) to scale or streamline penetration testing. Knowledge of cybersecurity practices, operational risk management, and incident response methodologies within the US financial services sector, including relevant regulations, threats, and risks. Proficiency in penetration testing and security concepts for both Windows and Unix-like operating systems. Experience conducting security-focused source code reviews (e.g., Python, Java, Rust). Experience in reverse engineering thick-client and mobile applications to identify vulnerabilities. Relevant certifications such as OSWE, CREST (CRT, CCT), OSCP, OSCE, GXPN, GWAPT, GPEN, GMOB, OSAI, or BSCP. #CTC

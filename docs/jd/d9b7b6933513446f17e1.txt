Scotiabank’s strong U.S. presence provides our clients an important bridge to this key global market for trade and investment flows across the Americas and the world. Global Banking & Markets provides a full range of investment banking, credit and risk management products and services relevant to the financing and strategic development needs of our clients. Our products include debt and equity financing, mergers & acquisitions, corporate banking, institutional equity sales, trading and research, fixed income products, derivatives, energy, foreign exchange and precious & metals. We also cross-sell the full range of wholesale products and services offered by the Scotiabank Group. Purpose Contributes to the overall success of the GBM Business Risk Management in the U.S. assisting in the management of day-to-day responsibilities of the US Business Risk Management team. Ensures all activities conducted are in compliance with governing regulations, internal policies and procedures. This position leads and oversees Communications Surveillance in the US ensuring business strategies, plans and initiatives are executed / delivered in compliance with governing regulations, internal policies, and procedures. The incumbent works in a team environment with specified duties which relate to monitoring and investigating all types of communications generated by Global Banking and Markets (“GBM”) staff. The primary role of the position is to detect possible policy or human resources issues that could be embedded in a communication. This position interacts from time to time with the senior management of GBM. What You'll Do Champions a customer focused culture to deepen client relationships and leverage broader Bank relationships, systems and knowledge. Reviews electronic and voice communications generated by Global Banking and Markets (“GBM”) and Group Treasury staff for compliance with regulatory requirements and/or internal policies and procedures. Performs and manages investigations of electronic and voice communications (e.g., discussing the facts and circumstances surrounding a particular communication with an employee), documents findings and, where required, escalates to relevant stakeholders which communications include potential violations of regulatory requirements and/or internal policies and procedures. Performs market conduct related transaction monitoring for GBM sales and trading employees and manages related investigations, documents findings, and, where required, escalates to relevant stakeholders which transactions may have violated regulatory requirements and/or internal policies and procedures. Understand how the Bank’s risk appetite and risk culture should be considered in day-to-day activities and decisions. Actively pursues effective and efficient operations of his/her respective areas in accordance with Scotiabank’s Values, its Code of Conduct and the Global Sales Principles, while ensuring the adequacy, adherence to and effectiveness of day-to-day business controls to meet obligations with respect to operational, compliance, AML/ATF/sanctions and conduct risk. Champions a high-performance environment and contributes to an inclusive work environment. What You'll Bring Must be highly organized and diligent in tracking and documenting numerous parallel investigations at the same time. Communicates directly with the business regarding monitoring related investigations. Able to use judgment in managing highly sensitive information and/or situations. Creates and disseminates periodic reports emanating from activities performed by BRM to various stakeholders including senior management and Compliance. Periodically reviews and drafts Business Risk Management procedures. Participate in the periodic review of the parameters of communication and transaction-based reviews performed by BRM. Produces communication or transaction monitoring related evidence in connection with requests from external and internal auditors. Understands how the Bank’s risk appetite and risk culture should be considered in day-to-day activities and decisions. Actively pursues effective and efficient operations of his/her respective areas, while ensuring the adequacy, adherence to and effectiveness of day-to-day business controls to meet obligations with respect to operational risk, regulatory compliance risk, and conduct risk, including but not limited to responsibilities under the Operational Risk Management Framework, Regulatory Compliance Risk Management Framework and the Guidelines for Business Conduct.

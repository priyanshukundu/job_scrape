Data Engineer The Opportunity: Organizations today depend on high-quality, trusted data to drive mission-critical decisions. As data volumes continue to grow across structured and semi-structured sources, organizations need scalable data engineering solutions that transform complex datasets into reliable, actionable insights. As a data engineer at Booz Allen, you'll help design, build, and maintain modern data pipelines and analytical workflows that support enterprise reporting, reconciliation, compliance, and decision-making. You'll work with large-scale Department of Defense (DoD) data environments, leveraging cloud technologies and distributed computing platforms to automate ingestion, enforce data quality controls, and integrate data from multiple sources into governed data products. You'll collaborate with cross-functional teams of data engineers, analysts, developers, and mission stakeholders to translate requirements into scalable solutions. Using technologies such as Databricks, Python, Spark, SQL, and modern cloud-based platforms, you'll develop and optimize end-to-end data pipelines, troubleshoot processing issues, validate data integrity, and deliver analytical products that enable enterprise-wide insights. Here, you'll work in a fast-paced, collaborative environment where you'll help modernize data ecosystems, improve operational efficiency, and enable data-driven decision-making across complex mission spaces. Join us. The world can't wait. You Have: 3+ years of experience building and maintaining data pipelines, ETL or ELT processes, or data engineering solutions Experience using Python and SQL for data integration, transformation, and analytics Experience with Databricks, Apache Spark, or distributed data processing frameworks Experience developing and optimizing end-to-end data pipelines with data quality controls, validation frameworks, or anomaly detection processes Experience integrating structured and semi-structured datasets from multiple enterprise systems Experience automating data ingestion, transformation, reconciliation, or workflow orchestration processes Experience troubleshooting ETL workflows, diagnosing pipeline failures, and implementing corrective actions Ability to collaborate with cross-functional teams to develop governed data products and analytical solutions Ability to obtain a Secret clearance Bachelor's degree Nice If You Have: Experience supporting DoD programs or working with DoD datasets Experience with financial data structures, audit-support environments, or compliance-driven reporting Experience with Delta Lake, Unity Catalog, Auto Loader, or similar data governance technologies Experience developing dashboards, analytical products, or business intelligence solutions using Qlik, Tableau, or similar platforms Experience implementing data quality monitoring, validation rules, hash-based integrity checks, or automated compliance controls Experience working in cloud environments, including AWS services such as S3, distributed storage, or external tables Experience with enterprise-scale data governance and master data management practices Experience working in Agile delivery environments Secret clearance Clearance: Applicants selected will be subject to a security investigation and may need to meet eligibility requirements for access to classified information.

Job Description: Job Description - Memory Lab Manager We are looking for an energetic and passionate professional to be part of a team responsible for the silicon validation of memory compilers utilizing the latest process technology. Our team supports memory tests and validation for high performance and low power memory for use in the latest AI, networking, and mobile chips. The ideal candidate is hands-on, highly organized, and self-motivated, with a strong interest in leading laboratory operations, financial tracking, and resource management. In this role, you will be the cornerstone of our testing and validation environment, ensuring our facilities and resources run efficiently so our team can bring the most advanced memory architectures on the leading-edge process node into production. Job Responsibilities Manage the purchasing of lab equipment, maintain comprehensive equipment inventory tracking, and coordinate and track all equipment calibration schedules. Generate the overall annual lab budget and perform detailed cost accounting monthly to ensure financial targets are met. Manage contractors for both lab operations and memory design positions, acting as the primary liaison working with contractor agencies. Develop and execute vendor/contractor contracts while actively managing the overall contractor budget. Manage overall contractor resource usage within the memory group, providing regular and actionable feedback to contractor agencies regarding work performance. Coordinate the end-to-end onboarding and offboarding process for new contractors and employees, including IT/access provisioning, workspace and equipment allocation, and lab orientation. Manage expense reporting and coordinate travel arrangements for personnel within the organization. Serve as the shipping coordinator within the organization, overseeing domestic and international logistics for lab equipment, test silicon, and hardware materials. Required Skillset Strong financial acumen with demonstrated skills in annual budgeting, monthly cost accounting, and expense tracking. Proven expertise in lab management, equipment procurement, and inventory control systems. Experience in vendor and contractor management, including contract drafting, execution, and performance evaluation. Solid understanding of domestic and international shipping processes, courier logistics, and export/import compliance guidelines. Familiarity with human resources coordination, including onboarding and offboarding workflows, access control, and hardware provisioning. Education/Experience: High proficiency with spreadsheets, financial analysis tools, and resource planning software. Relevant professional experience in lab operations, project management, or contractor resource management, ideally within an engineering, hardware, or semiconductor environment. Experience negotiating with staffing agencies and managing external talent pools. Experience coordinating shipping logistics and managing customs clearance/export-import documentation. Bachelor’s degree and 2+ years of related experience or master’s degree are required. Communication / Intrapersonal Skills Good communication, interpersonal, and leadership skills. Motivated, organized, self-driven and detailed oriented, can multi-task and work within deadlines.

Job Description As a valued contributor to our team, you will support strategic Multifamily application and data-development initiatives as part of a cross-functional Scrum team. You will partner with Product Owners, Business Analysts, subject matter experts, governance partners, developers, and data consumers to translate business needs into data requirements; analyze source data and downstream impacts; define mappings, transformation rules, and validation criteria; coordinate governance and data quality activities; and test delivered data. Strong SQL analysis, documentation, and communication skills will be essential to delivering trusted, reliable data. THE IMPACT YOU WILL MAKE The Multifamily COO – Data Management, Senior Associate role will offer you the flexibility to make each day your own, while working alongside people who care so that you can deliver on the following responsibilities: Partner with Product Owners, Business Analysts, subject matter experts, and stakeholders to translate business and application needs into detailed data requirements. Profile and analyze source data for structure, completeness, validity, relationships, anomalies, and fitness for intended use. Perform impact assessments to identify how source, schema, or rule changes affect downstream data, applications, reports, processes, and consumers. Define or support business rules, transformation logic, mappings, and downstream data representation in collaboration with technical partners. Create process flows and concise documentation to communicate data requirements, findings, impacts, and recommendations. Collaborate with the Scrum team to refine scope, support SDLC documentation, and, based on experience, contribute to stories and acceptance criteria. Work with technology leads developers to translate data requirements and mappings into implementable specifications; production coding remains with engineering. Develop and execute data-focused test cases, using SQL where appropriate to validate transformations, reconcile results, document defects, and assess acceptance criteria. Maintain data requirements, source-to-target mappings, dictionaries, lineage, metadata, and related documentation for consistency and traceability. Use SQL, Databricks, Excel, Jira, and related tools for analysis, documentation, testing, and delivery support. THE EXPERIENCE YOU BRING TO THE TEAM Minimum Required Experience 2+ years of relevant experience. Demonstrates curiosity and adaptability by learning and responsibly applying new techniques, including artificial intelligence, to reimagine how we work. Desired Experience Bachelor’s degree or equivalent. Experience as a Data Analyst, Data Quality Analyst, Business Data Analyst, or similar data-focused role. Strong SQL and data-analysis experience; Databricks experience is preferred, or willingness to learn the platform. Experience profiling, reconciling, validating, and investigating data across multiple datasets or systems. Experience documenting data requirements, mappings, business rules, test cases, or acceptance criteria. Knowledge of relational databases, data warehousing, integration, lineage, metadata, and data quality concepts. Strong communication, problem-solving, documentation, and organizational skills across business and technical teams. Familiarity with Agile/Scrum and SDLC processes; multifamily, mortgage, or financial services experience is strongly preferred.

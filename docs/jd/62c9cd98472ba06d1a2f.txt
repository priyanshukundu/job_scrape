Become a part of our caring community The Senior Risk Management Professional supports the effective intake, assessment, and implementation of Medicare and PBM pharmacy regulatory guidance. Partners with key stakeholders to promote timely implementation, appropriate risk management, and ongoing compliance with applicable requirements. The Senior Risk Management Professional supports the intake and implementation of Medicare and PBM pharmacy guidance, including coordination of required responses, evidence, controls, and implementation activities. Validates high-ranked and major implementations for completeness and readiness and manages Risk Acceptances for identified gaps or residual risks. Partners with key stakeholders to ensure guidance is implemented accurately, documented appropriately, and completed within required timelines. Use your skills to make an impact Required Qualifications Bachelor’s degree OR 3+ years of experience in healthcare, regulatory compliance, risk management, pharmacy/PBM operations, Medicare, or a related field. Experience reviewing, assessing, or implementing regulatory, compliance, or business requirements. Demonstrated ability to coordinate cross-functional implementations, projects, or initiatives involving multiple stakeholders. Ability to manage multiple priorities and time-sensitive deliverables simultaneously. Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, including the ability to identify gaps, risks, and issues requiring follow-up or escalation. Strong written and verbal communication skills. Proficiency with Microsoft Office applications, including Excel, Outlook, Teams, and SharePoint. Preferred Qualifications Experience with Medicare Part D, CMS guidance, or Medicare regulatory requirements. Experience with PBM or pharmacy regulatory/compliance requirements. Experience working within a health plan, PBM, pharmacy, or other highly regulated healthcare environment. Experience collecting, reviewing, and maintaining regulatory implementation evidence, controls, and supporting documentation. Experience performing validation or oversight of regulatory implementations. Experience with Risk Acceptances, issue management, corrective actions, or other formal risk-management processes. Experience using regulatory, compliance, governance, risk, or implementation tracking systems. Additional Information This position requires the ability to work independently and collaboratively across multiple business areas in a highly regulated environment. The role requires effective management of multiple concurrent regulatory implementations with varying levels of complexity, priority, and implementation timelines. The individual must be comfortable identifying implementation gaps, escalating risks or barriers, and facilitating resolution with appropriate stakeholders. Strong attention to detail is required to ensure implementation decisions, supporting evidence, controls, validations, and Risk Acceptances are appropriately documented and maintained. The position involves regular collaboration with partners across pharmacy/PBM operations, Medicare, compliance, regulatory, legal, risk management, and other impacted business areas, as applicable. The individual should be comfortable working in an environment where regulatory requirements, priorities, and implementation timelines may change quickly.

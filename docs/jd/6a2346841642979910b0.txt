The Impact you will have in this role: The Regulatory Change Management (RCM) Associate Director serves a key role within the Compliance team and plays a vital part in ensuring DTCC's compliance with applicable laws and regulations as they relate to all businesses and functions. The primary focus of this position is to monitor for changes in the regulatory environment that may impact DTCC's businesses, and to collaborate with key stakeholders to analyze and assess the impact of existing and proposed regulations on those businesses. Your Primary Responsibilities: Supports the day-to-day operation of the Regulatory Change Management function, reporting to the Director of Regulatory Change Management. Contributes to the monitoring of the global regulatory environment for proposed, new, and changing key laws, regulations, standards, and guidance that will or may impact DTCC and its subsidiaries. Works with Aligned Compliance, the General Counsel's Office, and other key stakeholders, to conduct applicability reviews, impact assessments, and readiness for new or changed requirements. Maintains the team’s Project List to provide transparency, traceability, and tracking of regulatory developments and retains applicability review and supporting evidence in accordance with RCM procedures. Supports Requirements Library maintenance, including updates arising from Regulatory Change Assessments and Regulatory Matrices, maker-checker review, and audit log maintenance. May serve as a resource to other business areas as it relates to compliance with existing and proposed regulations and guidance. Additional responsibilities may be required. Qualifications: Minimum of 7 years of related experience in the Financial Service Industry, ideally in an RCM role Bachelor's degree preferred or equivalent experience Talents Needed for Success: Ability to effectively engage in significant contact with internal stakeholders across all lines of DTCC’s business. General knowledge of compliance principles and standards, including industry best practices and compliance controls. Excellent organizational and time management skills, with a strong attention to detail, are a must. Ability to multi-task and to adhere to established deadlines while working in a fast-paced environment required.

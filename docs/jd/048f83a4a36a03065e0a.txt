Citibank, N.A. seeks a Credit Risk 2LOD Sr. Group Manager for its Fort Lauderdale, FL location. Duties: Critically evaluate and monitor assigned credit portfolios, identifying and assessing emerging risks. Approve, reject, or coordinate the approval or rejection of lines of credit or commercial, real estate, or personal loans. Provide continuous improvement of credit risk processes and standards for assigned entities and portfolios. Evaluate financial reporting systems, accounting or collection procedures, or investment activities and make recommendations for changes to procedures, operating systems, budgets, or other financial control functions. Support and validate corrective action plans for identified issues, addressing concerns from control functions. Review reports of securities transactions or price lists to analyze market conditions. Review, challenge and provide approval for the assigned portfolio credits and/or recommendations to more senior credit officers as needed. Plan, direct, or coordinate the activities of workers in risk or credit departments. Recruit staff members. Provide independent assessments on the accuracy and appropriateness of risk ratings and classifications for assigned credit portfolios. Assist the Regional Risk Head in orchestrating regional and/or global tasks and projects using innovative techniques and methodologies. Develop or analyze information to assess the current or future financial status of firms. Collaborate with other credit risk families to support the best outcome for Citi, sharing and implementing best practices. Examine, evaluate, or process loan applications. Manage a team of risk credit officers that monitor assigned credit portfolios and provide consistent credible challenge to corresponding first line of defense stakeholders. A telecommuting/hybrid work schedule may be permitted within a commutable distance from the worksite in accordance with Citi policies and protocols. Requirements: Bachelor’s degree, or foreign equivalent, in Finance, Economics or a related field, and ten (10) years of experience in the job offered or in a related finance occupation evaluating financial statements. Ten (10) years of experience must include: Evaluating financial statements to perform financial reporting analysis; Utilizing Microsoft Excel to prepare financial and economic estimates; Performing financial risk management assessments, including assessing potential macroeconomic, industry, and legal risks that could affect financial results; and Evaluating quantitative and qualitative financial forecasts and assessing financial assumption models. 40 hrs./wk.

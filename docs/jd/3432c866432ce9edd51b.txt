Overview Susquehanna's Compliance Technology team builds and runs the firm's in-house trade-surveillance and regulatory-reporting systems, working with order, execution and market data from across our trading businesses. We are looking for a data engineer who loves data at real scale and cares about getting it right. The team's biggest current effort is a global data layer for middle and back office. Alongside it, we are building proprietary surveillance models and the data foundations our compliance applications depend on. You will design how large volumes of trading data are modeled, moved and validated, and help shape a platform that the firm's compliance function relies on every day. We know no one has every skill on this list. We care most about strong data modeling and engineering fundamentals, a quality-first mindset and curiosity about the business, and we shape the role around the strengths you bring.

The Senior Manager, FCRM Remediation Strategy will support the development of First Line of Defense Financial Crimes remediation strategies, with a focus on establishing clear success criteria, definition of done, and evidence requirements for Consent Order and other regulatory commitments. This role will translate complex regulatory requirements and identified gaps into actionable remediation expectations and lead cross-functional working sessions to drive alignment on scope, ownership, deliverables, and outcomes. The successful candidate will combine strong Financial Crimes and remediation knowledge with exceptional communication, facilitation, and stakeholder-management skills . Define success criteria: Translate regulatory requirements, findings, and gaps into clear, measurable definitions of done and completion criteria. Develop remediation requirements: Establish required outcomes, deliverables, milestones, evidence expectations, and dependencies. Facilitate stakeholder alignment: Lead cross-functional workshops to drive decisions and alignment across business, FCRM, Technology, Data, Operations, and control partners. Challenge remediation plans: Assess whether proposed solutions and deliverables sufficiently address regulatory requirements, root causes, and intended control outcomes. Drive clarity: Synthesize complex requirements and stakeholder perspectives into clear decisions, actions, and remediation expectations. Build traceability: Establish linkage across regulatory requirements, identified gaps, remediation actions, success criteria, and completion evidence. Support remediation execution: Partner with execution and assurance teams to resolve scope and requirements questions and support validation readiness. Strong analytical and critical-thinking skills with the ability to identify gaps and challenge assumptions.

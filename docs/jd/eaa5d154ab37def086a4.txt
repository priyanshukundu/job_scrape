Job Description What is the Opportunity? Credit analysis of potential Securitized Credit investments in CLO tranches covering all parts of the CLO capital structure . Analyzing CLO Managers and pools in CLO structures to determine future investment performance. Preparing and maintaining records of investment rationale, and subsequently monitoring portfolio performance versus expectations while also tracking aspects related to ESG and market regulations. Working as part of a small team within a large, established credit firm. What will you do? Credit Analysis : Conduct credit analysis on originators, asset pools and asset backed credit structures across various sectors and geographies. Cash Flow Modelling : Conduct cash flow modelling and stress testing for credit assessment, including cash flow scenario and breakeven analysis. Documentation of investment thesis : Maintain documentation for investment rationales and thesis to aid with investment reviews. Securitization regulation : Assist in the monitoring of investments with regards to compliance with Securitisation Regulations ESG analysis : Incorporate ESG analytics into investment rationale, maintaining and improving ESG related systems Portfolio Monitoring : Monitor existing portfolio holdings, track credit metrics versus expectations, and market developments that may impact credit quality. Market Intelligence : Stay informed on credit market conditions, technical factors, and sector-specific trends relevant to the securitized credit investment universe. Collaboration : Work closely with portfolio managers and analysts, and other stakeholders to support investment decision-making and portfolio management activities. What do you need to succeed?

The Associate Manager, Tenant position is responsible for managing tenant relationships and ensuring compliance with lease obligations across Pilot’s real estate portfolio. Serving as the primary point of contact for assigned tenants, this role supports day-to-day property operations, responds to tenant inquiries and requests, and helps drive operational efficiency and property performance. The position also contributes to risk mitigation, lease compliance, and the development of processes and strategies that support effective portfolio management and overall business objectives. Essential Functions: Serve as the primary point of contact for assigned tenants, fostering strong relationships and addressing tenant inquiries, requests, and disputes. Administer tenant leases, ensuring compliance with lease terms and managing renewals, extensions, assignments, terminations, defaults, and evictions as needed. Maintain accurate tenant records, lease documentation, and contact information. Negotiate and coordinate tenant-related requests, including facility improvements, financing documents, subordinations, and other lease-related matters. Facilitate tenant onboarding, including due diligence, information gathering, and coordination with internal stakeholders. Partner with asset managers and cross-functional teams to oversee occupancy, move-in/move-out activities, and strategies for vacant tenant spaces. Develop and implement tenant management processes, standards, and site inspection programs to ensure compliance and reduce organizational risk. Analyze tenant performance, financial and operational risk factors, and contractual obligations, providing recommendations and support for business initiatives and property management strategies. Model behaviors that support the company’s common purpose; ensure guests and team members are supported at the highest level. Complete other duties as assigned. Specialized Knowledge: Strong organizational and communication skills. Ability to effectively resolve disputes and manage tenant expectations. Understanding of lease agreements, contractual obligations and general real estate principles. Knowledge of property management tasks, specifically tenant management. Familiarity with property maintenance and facility management coordination Bachelor degree preferred. 1-3 experience with tenant relationship management preferred.

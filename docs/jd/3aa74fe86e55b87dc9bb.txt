Job Description General Summary: The Quality Product Complaints Associate Director provides leadership and oversight of product complaint management for the global small molecule portfolio across clinical development and commercial supply. The role is accountable for ensuring complaints are appropriately assessed, investigated and resolved with a focus on product quality, regulatory compliance and continuous improvement. The role leads the evaluation of complex complaints and emerging quality signals, ensuring scientifically robust investigations and drives timely, risk-based escalation and decision-making. The role partners across internal functions and external manufacturing partners to identify product and process improvements, prevent incurrence and translate complaint insights into actions that strengthen product performance and patient experience. Working collaboratively across the Product Complaints, Defect Notifications and Recalls function, the Associate Director also contributes expertise and operational support across combination products, cell and gene therapies. Key Duties and Responsibilities: Portfolio Leadership and Oversight · Lead product complaint management for the small molecule portfolio, establishing clear priorities and ensuring consistent application of global procedures and quality standards. · Provide technical expertise and oversight across complaint assessment, investigation, escalation and closure. · Act as the primary point of contact for complex or high-priority small molecule complaints, coordinating cross-functional input and driving resolution. · Anticipate changes in complaint management needs arising from new product launches, portfolio growth, acquisitions and manufacturing or supply chain changes. Complaint Investigation and Technical Assessment · Oversee complaint investigations involving internal manufacturing sites, contract manufacturers, testing laboratories and distribution partners. · Critically assess investigation scope, technical rationale, root cause conclusions and supporting evidence to ensure investigations are scientifically sound and proportional to risk. · Ensure investigations integrate relevant product and process knowledge to assess broader quality implications across the portfolio. · Ensure corrective and preventative actions address identified causes and that their effectiveness is appropriately evaluated. · Lead quality risk assessments for significant complaints and emerging trends. · Partner with key stakeholders such as Regulatory Affairs, Safety, Supply Chain and other relevant functions to support defect notification, field action and recall assessments. · Ensure prompt escalation of potentially significant product defects and provide clear, evidence-based recommendations to support decisions. · Establish clear expectations with external partners for investigation quality, responsiveness, technical depth and timely completion. Trending and Continuous Improvement · Lead review and interpretation of complaint data to identify recurring failure modes, emerging signals and opportunities for product or process improvement · Translate complaint insights into actionable recommendations for manufacturing, packaging, distribution and lifecycle management · Drive improvements that strengthen investigation quality, data consistency, reporting and the efficiency of complaint management. Compliance, Performance and Capability · Maintain oversight of portfolio complaint performance including investigation timeliness, aging, investigation quality and completion of associated actions · Prepare and present portfolio updates, significant trends and risk assessments for Quality Leadership and management review · Support audits and regulatory inspections, including preparation of complaint records and response to observations · Contribute to global procedures , training and standards, ensuring that small molecule requirements are appropriately addressed. · Provide technical coaching and guidance to colleagues, strengthening investigation capability and consistent quality decision making. Additional Key Responsibilities · Provide cross-functional expertise and support for complaint management across combination products and cell and gene therapies, partnering with modality leads to address complex issues and functional priorities. · Lead or support defect notification and recall activities, as required, coordinating cross-functional assessments, escalation and execution in accordance with applicable requirements and internal procedures · Identify and lead continuous improvement projects, incorporating AI and other technology initiatives as appropriate, that strengthen complaint management, defect notification and recall processes across the function · Driver collaboration and knowledge sharing across modalities to promote consistent practices and strengthen technical and operational capabilities. Knowledge and Skills: · Demonstrated working knowledge and application of Good Manufacturing Practice (GMP) and Good Distribution Practice (GDP) including US and international/global regulatory requirements and associated guidance's and standards across multiple modalities (e.g., small molecule, devices/ combination products, biologics, cell and gene). Strong working knowledge and experience in: · Product Complaints, Recalls, Field Alerts and Defect Notifications to Health Authorities. · Risk Management. · Event/Quality Issue Management process and requirements, including CAPA and Effectiveness Checks. · Root Cause Analysis and Investigation process tools and techniques. · Inspection Readiness. · Strong oral and written communication and interpersonal skills. · Project Management, Planning and Prioritization skills. · Technical writing skills. · Adaptability / flexibility and ability to work in a fast-paced environment and act with urgency. · Strategic “big picture” thinking while maintaining ability to execute at a tactical level / attention to detail. · Strong analytical thinking skills, with the ability to translate data into risk-informed insights that drive decision-making. · Experience advising, coaching, and mentoring junior staff, as well as leading high performing and effective teams. Education and Experience: · Master's degree or bachelor's degree in a scientific or allied health field (or equivalent degree). · 5-8 years of relevant work experience (with Master's Degree) or 10+ years of relevant working experience (Bachelor’s degree) or relevant comparable background.

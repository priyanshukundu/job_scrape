HOW YOU'LL MAKE IMPACT

Airwallex is looking for a seasoned revenue strategy leader to shape high-stakes commercial initiatives for our Enterprise business across the Americas. You'll connect corporate strategy with the practical realities of winning complex enterprise opportunities across the US, Canada, and LATAM.

 - Lead the long-range go-to-market strategy for Airwallex's Americas Enterprise business, aligning regional revenue targets with global product capabilities.

 - Design commercial structures, pricing models, and value-realisation frameworks with Enterprise Sales leadership to accelerate complex deal cycles and maximise customer value.

 - Advise on must-win Enterprise accounts, shaping bespoke value propositions and engaging C-suite stakeholders at Fortune 500-level organisations.

 - Orchestrate large-scale initiatives across Legal, Compliance, Product, and Finance to remove friction from Enterprise onboarding and strengthen service delivery.

 - Improve sales effectiveness through advanced enablement tools, data-driven methodologies, and coaching on competitive displacement and complex solution selling.

 - Translate Enterprise market requirements into prioritised Product roadmaps, and lead regional planning, budgeting, metrics, and go-to-market experiments across new verticals, partner ecosystems, and lead-generation channels.



You'll be based in San Francisco or NYC. CONFIRM: required days in the office.

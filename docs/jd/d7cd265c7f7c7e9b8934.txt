You will work on-site a minimum of 2 days a week. What you will do Let’s do this. Let’s change the world. In this vital role as part of the Data Sciences team in Global Customer Capabilities & Innovation, the Senior Data Scientist will partner with commercial insights and analytics, brand teams, sales operations, data teams, external vendor partners, media agencies, service owners, and IS partners to develop advanced analytics and data science solutions that address complex business problems across multiple therapeutic areas and commercial functions. This individual will independently lead analytical workstreams, apply deep technical expertise to large and complex healthcare datasets, develop and productionize machine learning and AI solutions, and translate sophisticated analyses into clear, actionable business recommendations. The Senior Data Scientist will also contribute technical leadership, methodological standards, and mentorship across the team. Specific roles and responsibilities could include: · Leading the development and application of data science, machine learning, and artificial intelligence capabilities across Amgen’s commercial organization. · Translating complex business, medical, or scientific questions into analytical frameworks and defining appropriate solutions, success measures, delivery plans, and business impact. · Identifying high-value business opportunities and independently recommending fit-for-purpose analytical, statistical, machine learning, or AI approaches. · Designing, building, validating, and deploying high-performance algorithms, predictive models, prototypes, and production-ready analytical solutions using Python, SQL, and PySpark, with a focus on performance, interpretability, scalability, and actionability. · Applying advanced measurement and experimentation approaches, including test/control design, causal inference, attribution, and other methods, to quantify business and promotional impact where appropriate. · Partnering cross-functionally with stakeholders to align on methodology, data requirements, assumptions, limitations, and interpretation of results. · Providing technical leadership and mentorship, contributing reusable methodologies and best practices, and staying current with developments in data science, machine learning, AI, and industry analytics. · Communicating complex analytical findings and recommendations clearly to technical, scientific, medical, commercial, and executive stakeholders through compelling storytelling and presentations. · Ensuring analytical solutions comply with applicable regulatory, security, privacy, data governance, and documentation requirements.

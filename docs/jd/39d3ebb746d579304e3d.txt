SUMMARY Q2's Relationship Insights helps financial institutions better understand their commercial banking relationships and make more informed decisions. As a Senior Software Engineer on the Relationship Insights Integrations team, you will design and build the APIs and connectors that move data between Relationship Insights and the systems our customers and partners rely on. You will work across the stack with a focus on secure, reliable, well-documented integrations, partnering with Product, Engineering, and customer-facing teams to deliver solutions for financial institution customers. What You’ll Do Design, build, and maintain the integration services and APIs that connect Relationship Insights to Q2 products, customer systems, and third-party platforms. Own features end to end, from design and implementation through testing, release, and production support. Develop secure, scalable, and observable backend services, with attention to performance, resilience, and data integrity. Partner with Product, Engineering, Security, and customer-facing teams to turn integration requirements into clear technical solutions. Drive technical excellence through code reviews, automated testing, CI/CD practices, and architecture and security reviews. Participate in on-call and incident response, including monitoring, troubleshooting, and root-cause analysis for integration issues. Write clear technical documentation and API guides for internal teams and external consumers. Mentor engineers and help raise engineering standards across the Relationship Insights organization. RESPONSIBILITIES Design, implement, integrate, and test software solutions of moderate complexity, including Windows- and Linux-based front-end and back-end components. • Deliver solutions in live production systems individually and collaboratively. • Support, maintain, and resolve issues for custom features in production systems; occasional on-call availability may be required. • Ensure adherence to all security, availability, confidentiality, and privacy policies and controls. • Assist and mentor teammates, providing technical leadership and advocating best practices. EXPERIENCE AND KNOWLEDGE • Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Information Systems, or equivalent experience. • 5–8 years of software development experience. • Full-stack .NET development (SQL Server, C#, HTML/CSS, JavaScript). • Familiarity with MVVM and front-end frameworks (Vue, Angular, React). • Experience with test automation frameworks and auto-mocking tooling. • Practical experience with multiple design patterns and SOLID principles. • Domain experience in banking is a plus. • Web development experience. • Strong analytical, problem-solving, and communication skills. • Experience in all aspects of the software development life cycle and service-oriented architecture. • Experience in Agile development environments. • Ability to work flexible hours and provide occasional on-call/emergency support. • Working knowledge of accessibility guidelines (WCAG) applied across product, design, and engineering to support inclusive user experiences. This position requires fluent written and oral communication in English.

We strive to protect the firm's assets, reputation, and financial stability by implementing robust risk management practices. Join our team and contribute to our proactive approach in managing risks, allowing us to make informed decisions and thrive in an ever-changing market environment. Key Responsibilities: An Associate-level position in Market Risk Management, covering a broad credit platform that spans leveraged finance, lending, securitized credit, private credit, EM structured financing, high yield secondary loans, and capital markets underwriting. Review and challenge new trades, running stress analysis to support an informed approval decision. Analyze the capital and risk appetite impact of new deals, so stakeholders understand how each trade affects limits and return on capital. Prepare daily and weekly risk updates for senior management. Monitor limits and risk capital, and work with the desks to resolve any breaches or disputes. Partner with Credit Risk to monitor issuer and sector trends and stay ahead of potential downside in the portfolio. Required Qualifications: Up to 4 years of experience in Trading or Market Risk, with strong knowledge of global capital markets, particularly Credit. Interest in Credit, Lev Fin, Private Credit, or EM Structured Credit. Strong analytical skills, including Greeks, Stress Testing, VaR, Basel III, and FRTB. Sharp Credit research skills and sound judgment on downside risk. Excellent written and verbal communication skills, including with senior management. Programming skills are a plus.

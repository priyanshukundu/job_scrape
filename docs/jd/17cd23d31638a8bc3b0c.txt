Strategic Risk Analytics and Governance provides analytical, research, and governance support across financial risk disciplines. The team develops risk based data-driven insights, monitors emerging risk and regulatory developments, supports peer and industry benchmarking, and strengthens independent risk oversight through high-quality analytics and reporting. We are seeking a detail-oriented Data Analyst with strong technical, analytical, and communication skills who can translate complex data into clear, actionable insights for risk partners and senior management. Summary Responsibilities · Collect, organize, validate, and analyze data from multiple internal and external sources to support strategic financial risk analysis and governance activities. · Support cross-risk analysis across liquidity, interest rate risk, capital, market risk, regulatory reporting, and new or changing products. · Monitor data quality, investigate exceptions, document findings, and partner with data owners and risk teams to resolve issues. · Prepare clear, concise presentations, governance reporting, and written analyses for risk committees, and senior management. · Automate recurring data preparation and reporting processes where appropriate, while maintaining effective review and quality-control procedures. · Collaborate effectively with Treasury, Balance Sheet Risk Management, and other business and risk partners. · Assist with ad hoc analyses, risk assessments, and strategic initiatives as needed. · Keep abreast of analytical techniques, data tools, financial risk trends, regulatory guidance, and industry practices, and apply relevant insights to the team’s work. Basic Qualifications - Bachelor's degree, or equivalent work experience - Typically more than five years of applicable experience Preferred Skills/Experience · Bachelor’s degree or higher in Data Analytics, Finance, Economics, Statistics, Mathematics, Computer Science, Information Systems, or a related field. · Three or more years of experience in data analytics, financial risk management, banking, financial services, or a related field; equivalent combinations of education and experience may be considered. · Proficiency in SQL and advanced spreadsheet analysis; experience with Python, R, or a comparable analytical programming language is preferred. · Experience with data visualization and business intelligence tools, such as Power BI or Tableau, and with Microsoft Office applications, including Excel and PowerPoint. · Strong ability to work with large or complex datasets, reconcile information across sources, identify data-quality issues, and document analytical logic and results. · Understanding of banking, financial markets, balance-sheet management, or financial risk concepts, including liquidity, interest rate, capital, or market risk, is preferred. · Familiarity with applicable banking laws, regulations, regulatory reporting, and risk-management expectations is a plus. · Strong analytical, problem-solving, organizational, and project-management skills, with close attention to detail and accuracy. · Excellent written, verbal, interpersonal, and presentation skills, including the ability to explain technical findings to non-technical audiences. · Ability to manage multiple priorities, work independently, collaborate across functions, and deliver high-quality work within established timelines. · Demonstrated intellectual curiosity, sound judgment, and a commitment to effective challenge, continuous improvement, and high standards of data integrity.

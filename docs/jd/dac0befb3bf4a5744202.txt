Job Description DUTIES: Implement design elements in conformance with established Oracle SOA standards. Conduct detailed analysis of the business processes and recommend architecture for Oracle SOA implementation. Estimate the efforts required to implement the Oracle SOA solution, design and prototype Interfaces. Make recommendations around Oracle SOA monitoring best practices and train team members on Oracle SOA best practices. Assist in resolving current Oracle SOA Suite 12c issues and providing Oracle SOA stability and testing. Coordinate development and testing efforts between different development teams during the project. Participate in the system testing and deployment processes. Interact with IT management regarding work assignments and status. Assist in production support and troubleshooting. Automate and optimize processes. Interpret and implement business requirements according to established department standards. Hybrid Work Policy - 3 days In-office, 2 days remote - Must be able to report to local office. REQUIREMENTS: Bachelor's degree in Applied Computer Science, Software Engineering, Information Systems, Mathematics, Data Analytics, or related field of study and Five (5) years of experience as a Software Engineer, Systems Engineer, or related role.

About Goldman Sachs Goldman Sachs Asset Management is one of the world’s leading asset managers with over $3 trillion in assets under supervision. As the primary investment area within Goldman Sachs, we provide investment and advisory services for pension plans, sovereign wealth funds, insurance companies, endowments, foundations, financial advisors, and individuals. About ACF The Alternatives Capital Formation (ACF) business manages capital raising, client servicing and strategy for alternative investments including private equity, private credit, real estate, infrastructure, growth equity, sustainability, secondaries, GP stakes, and hedge funds / liquid alternatives. Within ACF, team members work within investment product verticals and collaborate across strategies to identify portfolio-wide solutions that best meet the needs of clients. They also partner with other teams within ACF, and across the Client Solutions Group (CSG), Private Wealth Management (PWM), and investing teams. Professional development initiatives provide opportunities to enhance expertise across alternative investment products, and to sharpen market knowledge from industry experts investing across the Firm. This specific ACF role sits within the External Investment Group (“XIG”) on the Alternative Insights and Strategy team. About the Team The Alternative Insights and Strategy team brings together proprietary data, external data and insights from investment teams across the firm. It translates that information into clear views, portfolio allocation frameworks and client-ready content for alternative investments. The team works closely with investment teams, portfolio managers, strategists and client-facing professionals across the firm. Role Description This Vice President will be a senior writer for the Alternative Insights and Strategy team, responsible for turning investment views, research and data into clear, engaging and credible content. Working primarily with the team's investment strategists, and directly with portfolio managers and other investment professionals as needed, the writer will find the strongest narratives in the team's work and decide how best to structure, package and deliver them for different audiences. This person will also drive written projects from initial concept through final copy, coordinating inputs, reviews and revisions to keep the work on track. The role requires a strong grounding in investment markets and exceptional writing and editorial judgment. Prior alternatives experience is preferred but not required.

We are looking for outstanding Senior AI Researchers & Engineers to join NVIDIA's Agentic Systems Intelligence (ASI) team, a frontier research and engineering team advancing high-performance AI. Our team builds the next generation of agentic AI systems for the CUDA ecosystem, working across the full stack—from training and improving models, to designing agent architectures and multi-agent systems, to building the systems, runtimes, and evaluation frameworks that make them effective at scale. You will investigate open-ended research questions and turn promising ideas into working systems that can reason about, generate, optimize, and operate across NVIDIA's accelerated computing stack. Depending on your strengths and interests, your work may focus on advancing model and agent capabilities, building scalable agent systems and runtimes, or developing high-fidelity training and evaluation environments. You will own significant parts of these systems end to end and collaborate closely with NVIDIA software, model, and hardware teams to bring new capabilities into NVIDIA products. What you'll be doing: Research, build, and optimize agentic AI systems for the CUDA ecosystem, including agent architectures, multi-agent workflows, tool use, memory, and orchestration. Build the data pipelines, environments, verification tools, reward mechanisms, and evaluation frameworks needed to continuously improve model and agent capabilities through rigorous experimentation. Co-design and optimize agentic systems across NVIDIA's software and hardware stack, from models and inference through compilers, runtimes, libraries, kernels, and GPUs. Demonstrate measurable improvements in correctness, performance, or developer productivity. Take ideas from research prototype to production: own technical directions end to end, set a high bar for rigor, and work with partner teams to deliver them into NVIDIA products. What we need to see: Master's or PhD in Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, or a related field, or equivalent experience. 6+ years of relevant industry or research experience (including PhD research) in AI systems, agentic AI, machine learning, reinforcement learning, compilers, high-performance computing, or computer architecture. Hands-on research or development experience in agent systems, coding agents, model training, reinforcement learning, or modern AI inference systems. Strong Python programming skills and proficiency in C/C++ or Rust, with solid software engineering fundamentals. Experience with GPU programming and performance optimization using CUDA or comparable accelerator platforms, and the ability to work effectively across system boundaries. Ways To Stand Out From The Crowd: PhD research in a relevant area, with demonstrated ability to formulate research questions, build working implementations, and evaluate them rigorously. Track record of building high-impact coding agents, autonomous software-engineering systems, developer tools, or agent training and evaluation environments. Hands-on experience with GPU performance optimization, or with optimizing and deploying on TRT-LLM, SGLang, vLLM, or Transformer Engine. Evidence of technical depth and ownership through impactful research prototypes, deployed systems, benchmark results, or substantial open-source contributions.

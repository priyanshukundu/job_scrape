The RCSA Program team within Enterprise and Operational Risk Management provides oversight and governance for Schwab's Risk and Control Self-Assessment (RCSA) and Issue Management programs. Through the establishment of program standards, methodologies, and risk management practices, the team enhances risk transparency, strengthens governance, and supports effective decision-making. As part of the broader Corporate Risk Management organization, this team enables effective risk management in alignment with Schwab's strategic objectives and risk appetite. In this Senior Specialist role, you will serve as a key enablement partner supporting the execution, optimization, and ongoing evolution of Schwab's RCSA Program. You will help drive program effectiveness through project and change management, risk data monitoring and analytics, adherence monitoring, and technology enablement initiatives that strengthen risk governance and operational efficiency. Working across all three lines of defense, you will coordinate and execute RCSA and Issue Management program activities, facilitate reporting and certification cycles, support MyGRC enhancements, and provide guidance and training to stakeholders. Success in this role requires strong analytical thinking, attention to detail and data, thoughtful verbal and written communication, adaptability, and the ability to translate evolving program needs into practical solutions that enhance consistency, scalability, and the overall effectiveness of the risk management environment. *This is an individual contributor position.

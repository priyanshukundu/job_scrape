FP&A Analyst, Shared Services Position Summary Integrity is seeking an FP&A Analyst to join our Shared Services Financial Planning & Analysis team. This early-career role is designed for a recent graduate or a professional with up to two years of experience who wants to build a strong foundation in corporate finance through hands-on ownership of operating expense reporting, budgeting, and forecasting. You will partner directly with Shared Services department leaders and finance leadership to manage cost center performance, explain what is driving results, and support financial planning across the organization. The role offers early exposure to executive leadership, enterprise-wide cost visibility, and real ownership of recurring deliverables. You will also help shape how our team uses AI to work faster and smarter. This position reports to the Manager of Financial Planning & Analysis. Responsibilities Own monthly operating expense reporting for assigned Shared Services departments and cost centers, reconciling actuals to the general ledger. Prepare budget and forecast submissions for assigned departments, working with department owners to gather inputs and validate assumptions. Investigate budget-to-actual and forecast variances and write clear commentary explaining the drivers. Support headcount and workforce planning, including tracking open roles, hiring, and compensation spend against plan. Complete ad hoc analyses and deep dives on cost drivers, vendor spend, and department performance to support leadership decisions. Build and maintain financial models, reporting packages, and dashboards, ensuring accuracy through consistent tie-out to source data. Partner with department leaders and cross-functional teams, asking the right questions to understand the business behind the numbers. Prepare presentations and reporting materials for finance leadership and management meetings. Maintain and improve the FP&A reporting and modeling platform, OneStream, and related reporting processes. Identify and implement process improvements that increase efficiency and data accuracy. Use AI tools to accelerate analysis, automate recurring reporting, and improve the quality and speed of variance commentary, while validating all outputs against source data. Requirements Bachelor's degree in Finance, Accounting, Economics, Business, or a related field. 0-2 years of experience in finance, accounting, FP&A, consulting, audit, or analytics. Internship, leadership, campus involvement, and relevant project experience are highly valued. Working knowledge of accounting fundamentals, including financial statements, general ledger structure, and accruals. Proficiency in Microsoft Excel, including SUMIFS, XLOOKUP, pivot tables, and building multi-tab files that tie to source data. Meticulous attention to detail and the discipline to check numbers against a source before sharing them. Ability to explain a variance in plain language, with effective written and verbal communication skills. Ability to manage multiple priorities and meet deadlines across recurring reporting cycles. Curiosity, initiative, and coachability, with comfort partnering with non-finance leaders. Proficiency with, or a strong desire to lean into, AI tools such as Claude and Copilot to streamline analysis and reporting, with the judgment to verify AI-generated outputs. Preferred Qualifications Internship experience in finance, accounting, banking, consulting, audit, or analytics. Exposure to budgeting, forecasting, or business analysis through coursework, internships, or work experience. Familiarity with financial reporting systems such as OneStream or NetSuite. Training will be provided. A demonstrated desire to build a career in corporate finance.

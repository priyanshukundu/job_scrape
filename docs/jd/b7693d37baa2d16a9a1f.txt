Manager, Third Party Risk Management
Charles Schwab
Location: Southlake, TX, US
Link: https://career-schwab.icims.com/jobs/130058/manager%2c-third-party-risk-management/job

Your Opportunity At Schwab, you’re empowered to make an impact on your career. Here, innovative thought meets creative problem solving, helping us challenge the status quo and transform the finance industry together. Within Enterprise Operational Risk Management, the Third-Party Risk Management team strengthens how Schwab identifies, assesses, monitors, and responds to risk across the third-party lifecycle. Reporting to the Director, Third Party Risk Management, you will connect program strategy with execution, improving how risk information, controls, issues, and oversight come together across the three lines of defense and business partners. You will lead cross-functional program enhancements that improve risk visibility, traceability, and decision-making. Your work will combine control assessment judgment with data, analytics, artificial intelligence, and workflow automation to reduce manual effort and strengthen processes. You will evaluate third-party control environments and their integration with Schwab processes, technology, and business services; translate findings into recommendations; and guide initiatives through requirements, milestones, testing, adoption, and transition. Success requires thoughtful problem solving, collaboration, adaptability, and the ability to influence technical and nontechnical stakeholders without authority. What you have Required Qualifications 3+ years of experience in operational risk, technology risk, compliance, internal audit, vendor management, control assessment, program management, or related discipline. Experience improving a risk, compliance, vendor management, or control assessment program through process design, technology enablement, data, analytics, or automation. Experience assessing control design and effectiveness and translating findings into implications for internal processes, dependencies, controls, and residual risk. Knowledge of the third-party lifecycle, including inherent risk assessment, due diligence, contracting, ongoing monitoring, issue management, renewal, and exit activities. Ability to critically evaluate policies, procedures, questionnaires, SOC reports, testing results, independent assessments, and remediation plans. Project Management skills demonstrated through leading complex, cross-functional initiatives with multiple stakeholders, dependencies, and competing priorities. Problem Solving and Decision-Making skills, including the ability to structure ambiguous issues, identify root causes, connect risk signals, and recommend practical solutions. Advanced proficiency with Microsoft Excel, PowerPoint, and Word, including producing clear analysis and executive-ready materials. Communication and Collaboration skills, with the ability to explain technical or risk concepts, facilitate decisions, and influence diverse stakeholders. Adaptability demonstrated through managing changing priorities and moving complex work forward across organizational boundaries. Preferred Qualifications Experience using Microsoft Power Automate and Copilot Studio to design workflows, automate repeatable activities, or develop governed business solutions. Experience using Tableau or a comparable business intelligence platform to build dashboards, monitor key risk indicators, and communicate trends. Working knowledge of Python and SQL for querying, validating, transforming, and analyzing aggregated risk data. Experience using GitHub Copilot or similar AI-assisted development tools within an enterprise environment and established governance expectations. Experience applying artificial intelligence, machine learning, natural language processing, or advanced analytics to risk management, control assessment, document review, workflow, or reporting. Knowledge of third-party risk regulatory guidance and industry practices, including risk-based due diligence and ongoing monitoring. Certified Third-Party Risk Professional, Certified Third Party Risk Assessor, Project Management Professional, or a comparable risk, audit, security, or project management credential.

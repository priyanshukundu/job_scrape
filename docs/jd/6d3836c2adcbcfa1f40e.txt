About the role We're looking for an outstanding team member to join Affirm’s Capital Markets team! Affirm Capital is responsible for raising the capital that funds our originations, a critical part of Affirm’s revenue strategy. As a Capital Markets Specialist I, you will own a significant portion of the platform’s day-to-day capital activities, which drive Affirm’s ability to scale its consumer point-of-sale lending business globally. This is a complex role that is essential to the functioning of a consumer finance and payments platform. The ideal team member will have a strong desire to grow their knowledge in structured debt finance or debt capital markets; strong technical and interpersonal skills; and the desire to work closely with both internal and external stakeholders.

Manager, Data Analysis - Commercial Risk At Capital One, data is at the center of everything we do. When we launched as a startup we disrupted the credit card industry by individually personalizing every credit card offer using statistical modeling and the relational database, cutting edge technology in 1988! Fast-forward a few years, and this little innovation and our passion for data has skyrocketed us to a Fortune 200 company and a leader in the world of data-driven decision-making. This position on Capital One’s Commercial Risk – Journey Analytics team offers the candidate a unique opportunity to drive dynamic reporting, analytics and automated solutions enabling risk insights for the Commercial Bank. An ideal candidate will be on the leading edge of analytical innovation, passionate about translating cutting-edge tools into scalable risk management solutions. Demonstrating robust analytical problem-solving and conceptual thinking alongside strong communication, interpersonal, and leadership abilities is essential. In this role, the candidate will lead high-impact initiative to modernize and automate the Commercial Control Testing framework, building resilient, continuous testing pipelines, automated exception-monitoring workflows and dynamic risk dashboards that eliminate manual friction, mitigate operational risk, and strengthen the overall Commercial risk and control environment. What you’ll do: Drive current and future risk analytics strategy across the Commercial Bank through advanced data modeling and insights. Manage, mentor, and elevate a team of high-performing data analysts to foster technical growth and career progression. Architect and scale well-managed data solutions, automated pipelines, and self-service analytics frameworks for Commercial Risk stakeholders. Partner closely with business leadership to translate complex risk management requirements into actionable key metrics, dashboards, and visual insights. Lead, prioritize, and execute end-to-end automation and process optimization initiatives to drive operational efficiency. Communicate complex analytical concepts and strategic recommendations effectively to executive leadership and governance committees. Evaluate and adopt cutting-edge cloud, open-source, and digital technologies to process, mine, and analyze voluminous structured and unstructured data sources. Leverage various AI tools and advanced techniques to streamline data workflows, accelerate development, and automate analytical tasks. Collaborate across business units and work with top talent in a fast-paced, entrepreneurial environment where analytics and good data are a top priority. Ensure all data solutions, reporting pipelines, and automated workflows strictly adhere to enterprise data governance, model risk management, and well-managed standards. Act as a primary liaison between Commercial Risk stakeholders and technical engineering teams to translate business requirements into scalable data products. Our ideal candidate will have: An ideal candidate will be on the leading edge of Analytical technology with a passion for the newest and most innovative tools. Strong problem solving and conceptual thinking abilities in addition to communication, interpersonal and leadership skills. Proficiency in at least one scripting language (SQL, Python, Shell, or Google Apps Script) alongside a solid foundation in data management and data governance principles. Experience working in AWS, Snowflake, or Databricks environments. A strong Business Intelligence focus, with a proven ability to leverage modern technologies to solve complex business challenges. Familiarity with AI-assisted coding tools (e.g., Gemini, Claude, GitHub Copilot). Basic Qualifications Currently has, or is in the process of obtaining a Bachelor’s Degree in quantitative field (Statistics, Economics, Operations Research, Analytics, Mathematics, Computer Science or a related quantitative field) plus at least 6 years of experience performing data analytics, or currently has, or is in the process of obtaining a Master’s Degree plus at least 4 years of experience performing data analytics with an expectation that required degree will be obtained on or before the scheduled start date.

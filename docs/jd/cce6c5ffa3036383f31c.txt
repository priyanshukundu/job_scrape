About the role Reporting to the Head of Equity Operations in Total Rewards, you’ll run day-to-day equity operations for Nebius’s global equity programs, from grant to settlement, and report on how those programs perform. You’ll work closely with Legal, Payroll, Finance, Accounting and our external partners, and you’ll own the accuracy of our equity records. What you’ll do Administer equity awards end to end: review grant requests against plan documents, eligibility rules and the share reserve, prepare grant packages, track approvals, load awards to the equity platform and send award notices. Run vesting, release, exercise and settlement cycles with Payroll, the broker and the transfer agent, and reconcile the results. Reconcile equity data across the equity platform, HRIS, payroll, general ledger and share reserve each month and quarter, and fix the root causes of differences. Manage the equity inputs for tax withholding and reporting across multiple jurisdictions, including cross-border cases. Resolve escalated participant cases and award exceptions, such as terminations, leaves, country transfers and corrections to prior transactions, by applying plan terms and documenting each decision. Work with Legal and local counsel to keep award agreements and plan terms current across countries. Prepare equity data for Finance and Accounting, including stock-based compensation inputs and quarter-end close. Build and maintain reporting on share usage, burn rate, dilution, outstanding awards and vesting forecasts, and present findings to Finance, Legal and HR leaders. Prepare accurate, confidential materials for Board and Compensation Committee meetings, and provide documents to internal and external auditors. Support equity audits and internal control testing. Manage our equity plan administrator, broker and transfer agent by tracking service issues and reviewing fees, and lead platform and vendor projects. Write and keep current our equity procedures and control standards, improve our processes, controls and reporting, and automate manual work. Educate employees, managers and executives through clear guides, FAQs, announcements and sessions on equity programs. Guide less experienced team members by reviewing their work and sharing procedures. What you bring Bachelor’s degree in Accounting, Finance, Business Administration, Human Resources or a related field, or equivalent experience. 8+ years of progressive experience in equity or stock plan administration, ideally at a public company. Hands-on experience with an equity administration platform and with broker and transfer agent portals. Solid knowledge of RSUs, stock options and PSUs, and of public company requirements such as trading windows. Knowledge of Rule 10b5-1 trading plans and Form 4 filings. Experience with equity programs in more than one country, including cross-border tax and reporting. Experience supporting stock-based compensation accounting, equity audits and internal control testing. Strong Excel and data skills, including lookups and pivot tables, and comfort with burn rate and dilution analysis. Knowledge of AI tools. Hands-on experience isn’t required. Close attention to detail, and good judgment with confidential information. Clear writing and the ability to explain equity to people who aren’t specialists, including executives. Nice to have Certified Equity Professional (CEP) designation, or at least Level 1 certification. Experience in a fast-growing technology company. Experience moving between equity administration platforms. Pay Transparency We offer competitive compensation and benefits packages.

Senior/Medior Data Engineer
Zurich Insurance
Location: SI
Link: https://www.careers.zurich.com/job/SeniorMedior-Data-Engineer/773697102/

Are you a motivated and innovative person, interested in driving strategic projects for one of the largest global insurers? Then this job is definitely something for you. The Data Engineer will be responsible for helping to power data-driven decisions and advancing the data and analytics tools of the Zurich organization. By leveraging new technology, the ZCAM (Zurich Customer Active Management) data engineering team ensures that all stakeholders in the organization can efficiently access reliable and accurate data. Data engineers develop, implement, and refine the standards, tools, and methodologies that make up the desired analysis environment within the organization. Their expertise helps create a strong data foundation within Zurich that keeps everyone informed and moving forward. What you will do Design, build and maintain scalable batch data pipelines that extract, transform and load data from structured and unstructured sources Develop and optimize data processing workloads using Spark on Databricks Implement data cleansing, validation and quality checks to ensure downstream consumers can rely on accurate, consistent data Write and tune advanced SQL, and optimize data models and data retrieval for performance and cost efficiency Monitor pipelines and environments, troubleshoot issues and continuously improve observability and alerting Lead data engineering projects from scoping through to delivery, working closely with technical and business stakeholders Mentor junior and mid-level engineers through code reviews, pair programming and knowledge sharing Evaluate and recommend new tools and technologies, and contribute to architectural decisions for the data platform Represent the data team in technical and business meetings, reporting on progress, outcomes and priorities What you bring 5+ years of experience in data engineering or a closely related field (or 3-4 years with clear senior-level ownership), plus a degree in Computer Science, Informatics, Natural Sciences or equivalent practical experience Strong Python skills with solid software engineering practices (OOP, testing, version control, CI/CD), and advanced SQL, including query and data model optimization Production experience with Spark and Databricks in the Azure ecosystem (e.g. Data Factory, Synapse), orchestration tools such as Airflow, and a solid grasp of data modelling, warehousing and Lakehouse concepts Proven track record of delivering data projects, a structured approach to problem solving, and the ability to explain technical topics clearly to both technical and non-technical stakeholders Fluent Slovenian and English, written and spoken You belong here.

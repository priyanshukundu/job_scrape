Job Description: The Oil and Gas Risk Officer (Petroleum Engineering Technician) will be responsible for managing engineering databases, and preparing accurate, timely outputs for use in reserve evaluations and credit underwriting. This role is responsible for setting up and maintaining engineering databases in accordance with established bank parameters, policies, and procedures, primarily using ARIES and PHDWin. The position supports the bank's reserve-based lending activities, including new credit facilities, semiannual borrowing base redeterminations, and ongoing portfolio monitoring. Working closely with Petroleum Engineers, the technician will assist in evaluating oil and gas assets by maintaining accurate reserve databases, preparing economic forecasts, generating cash flow projections, and supporting the technical analysis required for lending and credit risk decisions. This role helps ensure that engineering data, economic assumptions, and model outputs are complete, consistent, and reliable. The position requires strong technical aptitude, exceptional attention to detail, and the ability to manage multiple assignments in a fast-paced environment with competing priorities and time-sensitive deadlines. Specific duties include: import, convert, validate, and maintain oil and gas reserve databases received from borrowers, third-party engineering consultants, and other external sources. Apply bank-prescribed economic parameters, including commodity price decks, price differentials, operating expenses, capital costs, ownership interests, and other applicable assumptions. Support the preparation of reserve valuation summaries, cash flow schedules, and supporting documentation used in the borrowing base determination process. Required Qualifications: 3+ years of experience in petroleum engineering technical support, reserves evaluation, petroleum economics, or a related oil and gas technical role. Hands-on experience using ARIES and/or PHDWin for oil and gas reserves and economic evaluations. Experience maintaining, modifying, and troubleshooting petroleum engineering databases. Experience preparing economic cash flow projections and reserve valuation reports. Strong proficiency in Microsoft Access, Microsoft Excel, including formulas, data manipulation, and analytical reporting. Familiarity with oil and gas production data, reserve classifications, and basic petroleum economics. Proficiency with ARIES and PHDWin petroleum economic evaluation software. Familiarity with economic inputs, including commodity pricing, operating expenses, capital expenditures, and ownership interests. Ability to interpret reserve reports and supporting engineering data. Strong analytical skills and attention to detail. Desired Qualifications: Previous financial institution experience in petroleum engineering technical support area Locations: Houston, Dallas or Ft.

Requirements: Typically requires a minimum of 8 years of related experience with a Bachelor’s degree; or 6 years and a Master’s degree; or a PhD with 3 years of experience; or equivalent experience. Strong proficiency in one or more low-level programming languages, such as C++ or Rust , with a deep understanding of language internals, performance optimization, and memory management Experience with SaaS multitenant database architectures using RDBMS, and noSQL stores such as Elastic Search, Cassendra, and MongoDb Deep understanding of SOA, web security architecture, REST APIs, event-driven architecture, and other architectural patterns Experience with client-side MVC Frameworks (i.e., AngularJS, KnockoutJS) is a plus Proficient with source control management repositories (such as GIT, SVN, etc.) and CM practices Extensive experience writing high quality unit tests against mock objects Good understanding of object oriented analysis and design Demonstrated success in collaborating in a fast-paced team environment and delivering high-quality software products using established agile development methodologies (such as Scrum, Kanban, etc.) against formal schedules Desired: Possess proven troubleshooting skills. Experience debugging complex issues using low-level debugging and profiling tools (e.g., GDB, LLDB, perf, Valgrind, or equivalent).

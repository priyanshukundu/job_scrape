What we need to see: Bachelor's or Master's degree in Electrical Engineering, Computer Science, or Computer Engineering (or equivalent experience). 5+ years of proven experience in relevant roles. Proficiency in bare-metal firmware development, particularly with PCIE, ACPI and ARM/RISC-V technologies. Familiarity with computer system architecture, microprocessors, and microcontroller fundamentals (caches, buses, DMA, etc). Expertise in C programming languages, coupled with outstanding debugging skills. Defensive security programming skills We are seeking candidates with excellent communication abilities, both verbal and written. Ways to stand out from the crowd: Familiarity with Boot Process intricacies on sophisticated SoCs.

Position Overview Apollo is seeking an individual to join the Client Portfolio Management & Innovation (CPMI) team to contribute to market strategy and cross-platform communication efforts internally. As a part of the Client & Product Solutions division, CPMI is responsible for driving strategic and sustainable capital formation efforts via high-impact client engagement, innovative products and their adjacencies, as well as delivering perspectives on current market developments and trends to private markets. The individual will assist in crafting insights and commentary, primary in support of Apollo’s Chief Strategist, to help investors contextualize market and macro-economic themes affecting their portfolios as well as supporting internal communication initiatives to promote private markets adoption. Your responsibilities will include: Support cross-platform communication initiatives around the benefit of private markets/alternatives and Apollo’s platform of products through market research, insights development, and content creation (via commentaries, short-form text, and presentations for use by Apollo’s Global Wealth Strategist, sales teams, competitor intelligence teams, etc) Monitor, analyze, and synthesize capital market trends and industry developments Assist Global Wealth Strategist in creating a range of content – including market commentary, conference/webinar presentations, and market analytics – with messaging tailored to the Global Wealth internal teams/audience in a clear and thoughtful manner Maintain all house economic and asset class views which will be used for the CPMs and GWMS leadership Assist in streamlining and centralizing cross-platform internal communications (around products and markets) Manage and meet project deadlines effectively: juggling multiple assignments and ensuring timely delivery Collaborate with various teams (including marketing, sales, product management) on firmwide thought leadership initiatives Work closely with the content strategy team in client marketing for any relevant client facing materials and publications Qualifications & Experience 4+ years of prior experience in investor relations, product marketing & management, or financial writing/research role at a financial services firm, (e.g., asset management, wealth management or equity research) or financial journalism firm Strong business acumen and understanding of market dynamics and trends. Excellent written and verbal communication skills with the ability to present complex information in a clear and concise manner Self-motivated and resourceful with the ability to multi-task and focus on delivering results Detail-oriented with a focus on accuracy and precision in analysis. Ability to work independently and collaboratively (with an energetic, enthusiastic, and personable attitude) in a rigorous and fast-paced team-oriented work environment Series 7, 63, SIE AI Fluency: Demonstrates curiosity and fluency with AI and emerging technologies, leveraging available firm-provided tools to solve problems, improve decision-making, and enhance the quality of work while operating within firm policies. Efficiency & Innovation: Proactively identifies individual and team opportunities to use AI and technology to streamline processes, increase efficiency, and develop better ways of working.

Senior Product Manager - Alternatives and Derivatives
Clearwater Analytics
Location: Office - New York
Link: https://clearwateranalytics.wd1.myworkdayjobs.com/Clearwater_Analytics_Careers/job/Office---New-York/Senior-Product-Manager---Alternatives-and-Derivatives_R12663

The Senior Product Manager - Alternatives and Derivatives is the go-to product leader for all of Clearwater’s alternatives offerings (private credit and private funds) and derivatives. They are acutely aware of the investment products front to back and the technical specifications of both existing and roadmap products. They work with prospects and clients to review their operating models, map them against Clearwater, and shape a clear roadmap. They partner with market product leaders, professional services, go-to-market (GTM) and operations teams to ensure new products developed are ready for scale. Responsibilities Keep current all market and technical product specifications for existing and roadmap items across private credit, private funds and derivatives. Prepare the approach for, and conduct, operating model reviews of clients’ alternatives infrastructure across people, process and technology. Line each client’s operating model up against Clearwater’s capabilities and provide a roadmap for clients Partner with market product leaders on product development, and secure client engagement on the roadmap. Respond to RFPs related to alternatives and derivatives, and lead discussions with prospects and clients on operating model reviews. Recommend product opportunities to product leaders and professional services, and take them to clients. Develop a Alternative Investments Agentic operations solution working closely with market product leaders and professional services. Work with GTM and operations to enable cross-functional training and readiness for new product launches. Track market, regulatory and competitive developments, and bring that insight into the roadmap. Required Skills Deep knowledge of alternatives, including private credit and private funds, covering areas such as capital calls and distributions, NAV and valuation, loan servicing and fund accounting. Strong working knowledge of derivatives, including OTC and cleared instruments, valuation, collateral, and hedge accounting. Proven product management experience, including writing market and technical specifications and managing a roadmap. Experience assessing operating models and mapping client processes and systems to a solution. Experience responding to RFPs and leading client-facing discussions. Ability to lead and influence across product, professional services, GTM and operations without direct authority. Strong data and technical fluency, including data models, integrations and workflows. Excellent verbal, written and presentation skills, with strong documentation skills. Strong organizational, problem-solving and interpersonal skills. Education and Experience Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in finance, accounting, business or a related field. 8+ years of relevant experience at an asset manager, insurer, fund administrator, bank or fintech, with hands-on alternatives and derivatives exposure.

Job Description The Associate Director, Contract Management will be the site-level single point of governance accountable for the Jones Lang LaSalle (JLL) Integrated Facilities Management (IFM) scope of work for Cell Therapy Facilities at the Summit West, NJ campus. This position will oversee JLL delivery of hard services (HVAC, electrical, elevators, fire and life safety, plumbing, and building envelope), soft services (housekeeping, waste management, pest control, security, food service, mail, shipping and receiving, and space planning), Laboratory Services (biological and chemical waste management and cleanroom garment management) and the site utility infrastructure, including the boiler plant, the chiller plant and the high-voltage electrical yard. The position is responsible for the governance, monitoring, and assurance of all aspects of the facilities management scope of services to ensure consistent, safe, reliable and cost-effective delivery service that supports a GxP cell therapy manufacturing and laboratory environment, and will develop and manage the associated annual budgets. The position will report to the Senior Director, Facility & Engineering, Cell Therapy Operations. The position will be site-level single point of governance accountability for Site Utilities. The position will act as relationship manager for Cell Therapy with the Site Landlord. Key Responsibilities Align objectives, implement, and govern the JLL Integrated Facilities Management (IFM) contract for the Summit West campus, acting as the single point of contact to develop and maintain positive relationships with JLL leadership and site teams Oversee JLL delivery of hard services, including HVAC and building automation, electrical systems (switchgear, UPS, generators, data center support), elevators, fire and life safety, plumbing, and roof and building shell maintenance Oversee JLL delivery of soft services, including housekeeping, waste management (general, recycling, regulated, universal and shredding), pest control, security and emergency response, food service and coffee service, fitness center, mail, shipping and receiving, and space planning and moves.

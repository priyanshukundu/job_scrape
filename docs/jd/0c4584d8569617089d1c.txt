Start your Voyage – Apply Now Get to Know the Opportunity: This role is part of the Voya Investment Management Entry Level Analyst Program beginning in July 2027. The program is designed to develop entry-level employees and provide a unique opportunity to begin an investment career through hands-on experience, on-the-job development, and training. The Jr. Research Credit Analyst will support senior research analysts and traders in the day-to-day business of the Investment Grade Team. The analyst will contribute to fundamental credit research, financial modeling, earnings analysis, performance attribution, sector reviews, and investment-related reporting. This role is based in our Atlanta office and follows an Office Centric approach requiring on average 4 days onsite each week. The Contributions You’ll Make: Support fundamental credit research, analysis, and reporting for industries and issuers under coverage. Communicate the results of fundamental credit research in a timely and effective manner. Complete issuer tear sheets and post research to Aladdin. Keep the Investment Grade Team informed of relevant sector news through Bloomberg chat. Review rating agency and sell-side research and communicate key insights to senior analysts. Listen to company earnings calls and summarize calls and earnings releases, particularly during periods when multiple companies report within the same day or week. Draft earnings commentary for analyst review. Under the direction of the sector analyst, develop summary industry financial models that include income statements, cash flow statements, and balance sheets. Project key coverage and leverage ratios. Assist senior analysts with performance attribution for issuers and sectors under coverage. Support the preparation of Industry Review Meetings by collecting relevant data, completing assigned analysis, and developing presentation slides for analyst review and inclusion. Support environmental, social, and governance research by accessing third-party ESG research, incorporating ESG analysis into issuer tear sheets, and monitoring alerts from Voya’s internal ESG team. Run positioning reports and provide market data as needed. Coordinate weekly and semiannual team meetings. Maintain the weekly new-issue monitor and provide related analysis. Help coordinate sell-side events, attend industry conferences, and use research and market resources to support investment idea generation. Perform other responsibilities aligned with the needs of the Investment Grade Team. Minimum Knowledge & Experience: Applicants must be graduating with an undergraduate degree in December 2026 or spring/summer 2027. Pursuing a bachelor’s degree in finance or a related field. High School diploma or GED. Proficiency with Microsoft Word, Excel, and PowerPoint. Strong quantitative and analytical skills. Ability to work independently in a high-activity environment. Ability to manage multiple priorities and communicate findings clearly and promptly. Flexible and assertive approach to responsibilities and changing business needs. Demonstrated interest in asset management. Preferred Knowledge & Experience: Experience with or knowledge of Bloomberg. Familiarity with credit research, financial statement analysis, financial modeling, or fixed-income markets. Interest in investment-grade credit, issuer analysis, and portfolio performance. How This Role Aligns to Our Core Four: Voya’s Core Four guide how we work together and deliver meaningful results, and this role supports those expectations through disciplined research, thoughtful analysis, and strong partnership across the Investment Grade Team. Instill Customer Obsession: Deliver timely, thoughtful credit research and analysis that supports informed investment decisions and strong outcomes for Voya’s clients. Transform with Automation/AI: Use tools such as Aladdin, Bloomberg, Excel, and approved automation or AI capabilities to improve research efficiency, analytical quality, and information sharing. Drive Higher Performance Through Our People: Partner closely with senior analysts, traders, and other team members while building investment knowledge through feedback, collaboration, and hands-on development.

Role Overview We are seeking an experienced Development Lead to provide development leadership across the Core Modernization (Core Mod) Program. The Core Modernization Program is New York Life’s multi-year transformation to replace aging, mainframe-based core insurance capabilities with modern Verisk Life FAST SaaS implementation. This role is responsible for establishing and driving consistent engineering practices, development standards, code quality, and technical discipline across Agile development teams. The development lead will work closely with Solution Leads, Architects, Product Owners, QA, DevOps, and development teams to ensure solutions are implemented consistently with the program's architecture and engineering standards. The role will provide technical oversight, conduct and govern code reviews, mentor development teams, and continuously improve development practices across the program. What You'll Do: Development Leadership Provide development leadership and technical guidance across Core Mod Agile teams. Establish clear expectations for software engineering quality and development discipline Drive adoption of AI-enabled development practices to enhance code quality, automate code reviews, and improve developer productivity. Guide developers on implementation approaches for complex capabilities without serving as the primary developer. Mentor and develop senior engineers and technical leads to improve engineering capabilities and technical leadership across the program. Provide hands-on technical escalation and production support, troubleshooting complex issues, analyzing logs and integration failures, identifying root causes, and determining required code or configuration changes. Promote knowledge sharing and consistency across onshore and offshore development teams. Engineering Standards & Governance Define and govern development standards, coding practices, design patterns, and engineering guidelines to improve maintainability and promote consistency across Core Mod teams Partner with Architects and Solution Leads to establish development standards and practices aligned with the broader Core Mod architecture and technical roadmap. Establish guardrails for APIs, integrations, error handling, logging, security, configuration, and other common engineering concerns. Identify and drive opportunities to improve engineering efficiency, reuse, and standardization. Collaborate with Cross-Technology Architects and Solution Leads to drive the implementation and validation of enterprise solutions. Code Quality & Review Establish and govern code review standards and processes across development teams. Conduct or facilitate reviews of critical or complex implementations. Ensure code reviews address maintainability, security, performance, resiliency, testability, and adherence to architectural standards. Define expectations for static analysis, automated quality gates, code coverage, and other engineering quality controls. Identify recurring code-quality issues, development defects, and systemic engineering problems, and drive preventative improvements to development practices and technical implementations. Ensure technical debt is visible, appropriately prioritized, and actively managed. Development Lifecycle & Quality Promote strong engineering practices throughout the software development lifecycle. Drive adoption of unit and component testing, automated testing, CI/CD, and DevOps practices. Partner with QA and Test Automation leadership to shift quality earlier into development. Ensure development teams meet established standards for production readiness, supportability, observability, and resiliency.

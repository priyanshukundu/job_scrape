Work Schedule: Monday – Friday; 8:00am-5:00pm EST Contract Management: Contract Associate I The Contract Associate I is responsible for the end-to-end contracting of divisional client contracts, including drafting, reviewing, negotiating, and ensuring compliance with internal policies and legal requirements. The role plays a key part in supporting business objectives with urgency by facilitating timely contract execution, managing exceptions, and maintaining strong client relationships through clear and effective contract processes. Job Responsibilities Client Contract Management and Engagement Works with a team of contracts professionals in all aspects of contract creation and management. Draft, review and negotiate high profile, complex client contracts using pre-approved corporate templates, ensuring consistency and adherence to company policies and risk standard, escalating as needed. Review redlines using pre-approved language (approved deviations) and make determinations.. Address client questions, and drafts emails and or participates on calls related to client questions. Escalates client concerns on information requests, compliance confirmations, pricing and business team questions, and requests for client calls as needed. Review, analyze, and negotiate high profile, complex client-provided contract documents (“client paper”) to align with company requirements and minimize legal and commercial risk. Prepares requests for Law Department review and sends contracts for Law Department review. Secures contract signatures, returns executed documents to the client and returns contracts to corporate teams. Loads final documents into the contract management system. Clearly and accurately informs clients and management of the status of ongoing projects, including time frames, changes to plans, problems, solutions, etc. Works with management on annual reporting per law department and compliance policies. Provides status tracking of unique, challenging client contracts inside the life cycle (e.g., chasing signatures, etc.). Escalate, as needed. Collaboration with Legal and Business Teams Partner with the Law Department to interpret contractual terms, resolve disputes, and ensure adherence to applicable laws, regulations, and internal standards. Work closely with business units to understand client requirements, operational objectives, and service expectations to translate them into contractual terms. Advise internal stakeholders on contract structure, process, and risk considerations. Coordinate with stakeholders to identify and resolve contract exceptions and escalations. Client Record Management and Data Integrity Create, update, and maintain client records within the contract management system, ensuring data accuracy and completeness. Ensure accuracy, consistency, and completeness of client records in the contract management system. Monitor for duplicate, missing, or inconsistent client data and oversee resolution of discrepancies. Provides monitoring of client bid opportunities and communicates such opportunities to management for assignment. Assists in the preparation and submission of bid proposals in response to RFPs, RFQs, and other solicitation documents, including crafting responses to bid questions utilizing standard pre-approved language. Coordinates with cross-functional teams to gather necessary documentation, pricing, and compliance information. Reviews bid terms and conditions to identify contractual obligations, risks, and compliance requirements, as assigned by management. Tracks bid timelines, deadlines, and approvals to ensure timely submission. Packages proposal for legal review and submission out to routine clients. Secures signatures, returns executed documents, and loads into contract management system. Supports post-bid clarifications and contract award negotiations. Communicates bid submissions and results to the Law Department for Monthly Reports. M&A/Integrations: Attends calls, as assigned, with M&A Project Team to learn about Target Company, clients, service offerings, etc. Performs reviews of the client contracts in the Project data room, to look for service offerings, etc., that are non-standard for Labcorp, and which should be brought to the Project Team calls. Creates new contracts/assignments of contracts, where needed. Minimum Qualifications: High school diploma or equivalent with 2 years of relevant experience Additional Job Standards: Organization skills and having the ability to adapt to high pressure situations is a must. Prior Contract experience Good communication skills, verbal and written, necessary. Experience with Microsoft Office and Adobe Ability to change directions in workload based on required timeframes. Work independently, and as a team, with little to no supervision.

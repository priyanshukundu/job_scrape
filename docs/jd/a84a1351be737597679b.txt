Launch Your Career in Procurement Start your career at Aon as an Associate Manager, Procurement in our Launch Program for recent graduates. You will join the Global Spend Management (GSM) Category Management team and gain hands-on experience while working with colleagues across the business. This opportunity is designed to help you learn, contribute and grow. You will develop procurement and business skills through real projects, supportive relationships and opportunities to take on new responsibilities. Aon is in the business of better decisions At Aon, we shape decisions for the better to protect and enrich the lives of people around the world. As an organization, we are united through trust as one inclusive team and we are passionate about helping our colleagues and clients succeed. Skills and experience that will lead to success We welcome candidates who are ready to learn and build their skills. You may be a great fit if you: Enjoy learning, asking questions and exploring new ideas. Communicate clearly and enjoy sharing ideas with others. Stay organized and pay attention to details. Enjoy solving problems and working with information and data. Work well with others and value different perspectives. Are interested in procurement, sourcing or business. Have experience using Microsoft Excel, Word and PowerPoint. Previous internship experience in procurement, supply chain or an analytical role is helpful but not required. Familiarity with data, procurement technology or AI tools is also a plus. What the day will look like Contribute to sourcing projects, supplier renewals and new supplier opportunities. Research suppliers and market trends to help inform business decisions. Review data to identify savings opportunities and useful insights. Help coordinate project plans, milestones and priorities. Support supplier reviews and procurement activities. Prepare clear information and recommendations for colleagues and leaders. Build relationships with colleagues, business partners and suppliers. Grow your sourcing and negotiation experience with support from the team. Qualifications College Seniors graduating between Spring 2026 to Spring 2027 with a minimum cumulative GPA of 3.0 Preferred majors are Business, Economics, Finance, Supply Chain Management, Procurement or another quantitative or analytical field.

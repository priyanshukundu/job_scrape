As a Senior Software Engineering Lead, you will drive the design, development, and delivery of enterprise applications that power Citi's technology platform. You will lead applications systems analysis and programming activities, partnering with the Technology team to translate business needs into scalable software solutions. This is an opportunity to shape architecture decisions, mentor engineering talent, and deliver high-impact system enhancements at scale. Responsibilities: Lead applications systems analysis and programming activities, partnering with multiple management teams to deploy new products and process improvements. Design and implement system enhancements that resolve high impact technical and business problems, drawing on deep knowledge of industry standards. Ensure application designs align with Citi's overall architecture blueprint, applying advanced expertise in applications programming. Define coding, testing, debugging, and implementation standards that improve system flow and software quality. Build expertise across architecture and infrastructure to connect technical solutions with broader business goals. Analyze complex technical issues and develop innovative solutions through in-depth evaluation of system and business processes. Mentor and guide mid-level developers and analysts, allocating project work to strengthen team delivery. Evaluate risk in technology decisions and escalate control issues transparently to protect Citi and its clients. Required Qualifications and Skills: Brings 6+ years of experience in applications development or systems analysis, including hands-on software programming. Holds a bachelor's degree or equivalent practical experience in a related field. Operates as a subject matter expert in at least one area of applications development, resolving complex technical challenges. Manages and delivers technology projects successfully, from design through implementation. Demonstrates leadership and project management skills, communicating clearly with both technical and business stakeholders while adjusting priorities quickly as business needs shift. Builds applications using Python, Java, Angular, or equivalent reactive frameworks within microservices, event-driven, and cloud-native architectures. Integrates systems through REST APIs and Kafka, applies CI/CD and containerization practices such as Docker and Kubernetes, and develops real-time reporting, dashboards, and ETL/ELT data pipelines. Experience with agentic AI, large language models, retrieval-augmented generation, or generative AI platforms. Bachelor’s degree/University degree or equivalent experience Master’s degree preferred What We Offer You will work in a hybrid model that combines in-office collaboration with the flexibility to work from home, giving you the structure to connect with global teams and the autonomy to manage your own schedule. Work in a hybrid arrangement with 3 days in the office and 2 days working remotely each week. Collaborate with technology teams across Citi's global network on large-scale application initiatives. Access ongoing learning and development programs to build advanced engineering and leadership skills. Take ownership of architecture decisions and mentor the next generation of engineering talent. Benefit from programs that support your financial wellbeing and long-term career growth. Build a career path toward senior technical and leadership roles within Citi's technology organization.

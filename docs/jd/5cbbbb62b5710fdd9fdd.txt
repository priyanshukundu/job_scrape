Job Description U.S. Bank is seeking a full-time Portfolio Analytics & Strategic Planning Manager to advance portfolio reporting, analytics, strategic planning, and prioritization capabilities. This role will lead the continued development and adoption of Power BI solutions, translate complex data into actionable insights, and deliver clear recommendations to leaders to support informed decision-making. This candidate will also build and maintain strategic roadmaps, support portfolio prioritization activities, and help ensure Jira data and workflows remain accurate, consistent, and decision-ready. Participates in the identification, design, development and implementation of assigned projects. Assigned projects may be general or systems related in nature. Interfaces with internal and external resources to ensure successful and timely completion in accordance with user needs. Participates in training users and evaluating the project. Manages performance of the project team and evaluates overall team performance. Key Responsibilities Power BI Development: Design, build, enhance, and maintain scalable Power BI dashboards, reports, semantic models, and supporting data solutions that provide timely visibility into portfolio performance, demand, capacity, delivery, financials, risks, and outcomes. Analytics and Insights: Analyze portfolio and operational data to identify trends, dependencies, risks, opportunities, and performance gaps; convert findings into concise, actionable insights. Leadership Reporting: Prepare and present recurring and ad hoc reporting to leadership, clearly communicating performance, key decisions, tradeoffs, and recommended actions. Strategic Recommendations: Develop evidence-based recommendations that improve portfolio outcomes, resource allocation, sequencing, governance, and execution. Strategic Roadmaps: Build and maintain integrated strategic roadmaps that connect business objectives, initiatives, milestones, dependencies, expected benefits, and delivery timelines. Prioritization Support: Assist with intake, evaluation, scoring, sequencing, and prioritization processes; support leadership discussions with transparent data, scenario analysis, and decision materials. Jira Administration, Data Quality, and Usage Standards: Maintain accurate and complete portfolio information in Jira to aid in data flows to Power BI for reporting and analysis. Power BI User Training and Enablement: Develop and deliver ongoing user training, guidance, and support to promote accurate and effective use of Power BI and related portfolio tools. Process Improvement: Identify opportunities to standardize, automate, and improve reporting, roadmap management, prioritization, and portfolio governance practices. Stakeholder Partnership: Collaborate with business, technology, finance, product, and delivery partners to validate data, align reporting needs, and support portfolio decisions. Documentation and Controls: Maintain definitions, data standards, reporting procedures, governance documentation, and quality controls to promote reliable and repeatable portfolio management practices. Basic Qualifications -Master's degree, or equivalent work experience 10 or more years of experience in project management activities Preferred Skills/Experience Bachelor’s degree in business, analytics, technology, or a related field, or equivalent work experience; 8+ years of experience. Demonstrated ability to build, enhance, and maintain Power BI dashboards, reports, data models, calculations, visualizations, and supporting data solutions; advanced expertise in Power Query, DAX, workspace management, security, refresh processes, and performance optimization preferred. Hands-on experience building and maintaining Jira and SharePoint solutions, structures, workflows, lists, sites, and reporting capabilities; experience integrating or analyzing data from Jira, SharePoint, Excel, financial systems, and other enterprise sources preferred. Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with demonstrated ability to interpret complex data and translate findings into actionable business insights and recommendations; experience with financial analysis, capacity planning, benefits tracking, scenario modeling, or executive scorecards preferred. Working knowledge of strategic roadmaps, portfolio prioritization. Excellent written and verbal communication and stakeholder-management skills, including experience developing executive-level presentations and communicating with senior leaders and cross-functional stakeholders; ability to influence decisions without direct authority and work effectively across business and technology teams preferred. Strong organizational skills and exceptional attention to detail, with demonstrated ability to train and support users, reinforce tool standards, and promote consistent adoption of common processes.

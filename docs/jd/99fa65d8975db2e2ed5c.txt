Job Description Summary As a Principal AI Software Engineer, you will define the technical architecture and drive the strategy for enterprise-grade AI solutions across our organization. You will lead the integration of LLMs, agentic workflows, and predictive analytics into our product ecosystem, ensuring these systems are secure, scalable, and cost-efficient. You will be a bridge between research and product, mentoring engineering teams and championing high standards in software craftsmanship, reliability, and security. Job Description Essential Responsibilities Architectural Leadership: Set the technical vision and architectural standards for AI initiatives, ensuring alignment with organizational goals. AI Integration: Lead the design and implementation of RAG architectures, complex agent workflows, and LLM orchestration. System Design: Build and maintain multi-tenant, high-availability AI systems with a focus on observability and performance at scale. Operational Excellence: Define and enforce standards for AI security, cost management, and model monitoring. Mentorship & Collaboration: Lead cross-functional initiatives by partnering with product, data science, and engineering teams to elevate technical standards through mentorship and collaborative design. Required Qualifications Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, or a related technical discipline. Minimum of 10 years of professional software development experience, including mastery of modern front-end frameworks (React, Vue, or Angular) and robust backend languages (Python preferred; Java, Node.js, or Go). Demonstrated experience architecting and deploying production-grade applications leveraging LLMs or AI/ML services. Strong practical experience with vector databases (e.g., Milvus, Pinecone, pgvector) and embedding model optimization. Expertise in AI orchestration frameworks such as LangChain, LlamaIndex, or AgentCore. Deep proficiency in cloud-native development (AWS, GCP, or Azure) and containerization (EKS/Kubernetes). Proven ability to communicate complex technical strategies to diverse stakeholder groups. Desired Characteristics 13+ years of experience across the full software development lifecycle. Advanced understanding of large-scale enterprise RAG, semantic search, and conversation memory management. Experience with multi-modal AI, model fine-tuning, and the deployment of custom AI models. Expertise in AI observability tools, performance monitoring, and rigorous cost-optimization strategies. A track record of influencing technology roadmaps and driving cross-organizational collaboration.

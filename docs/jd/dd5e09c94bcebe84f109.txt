As a Senior AI/ML Engineer in AI/ML Engineering, with a focus on AI research, you help shape the foundations of future AI-powered capabilities — from problem framing and experimental design through prototyping, validation, and translation into product. You’ll combine strong software engineering with hands-on expertise in modern AI, including foundation models, generative AI, and predictive modeling, to turn emerging research into reliable, safe, and valuable product capabilities. You’ll help innovate and build the foundations of future AI-powered capabilities, partnering with engineers, product managers, and clinical, regulatory, and commercial stakeholders to translate emerging technologies into real-world impact. In doing so, you’ll help position Dexcom as an industry leader in health AI and empower millions of customers to take control of their health. Where you come in: You research, design, develop, prototype and validate novel AI/ML technologies, translating emerging scientific advances into high-impact opportunities for future product innovation while building reusable, high-quality research and prototype code. You investigate and advance state-of-the-art approaches in areas such as foundation models, multimodal learning, and time-series modeling, assessing their potential to address challenging customer, clinical, and business problems. You own evaluation and safety for your systems, defining metrics, benchmarks, and monitoring, identifying model and system risks, and driving mitigations and responsible-AI practices across the team. You lead technical strategy that balances model performance, latency, cost, safety, and regulatory requirements. You publish and present research at leading conferences and journals, clearly communicate complex methods and results to technical and non-technical audiences, and contribute to the broader AI community and Dexcom’s leadership in health AI. You coordinate with cross-functional teams — including stakeholders from R&D, Commercial, Product, Legal, and Regulatory — to ensure development aligns with company strategy and meets the highest safety standards. You build and improve the research and training infrastructure, including data, experimentation, and CI/CD pipelines, applying reliability, observability, and cost-awareness to accelerate the team’s work. What makes you successful: You love being on the cutting edge of AI/ML, bring a strong research mindset that keeps you current with advances and eager to explore new ideas and methods, and share a passion for Dexcom’s mission and health-sensing applications. You have strong CS fundamentals and hands-on experience with Python and modern AI/ML libraries and frameworks, with depth in areas such as deep learning, generative AI, reinforcement learning, and multimodal learning. You have experience independently conducting rigorous AI/ML research, from problem formulation and experimental design through validation and communication of results. You have experience working with large-scale datasets and leveraging cloud-based and distributed computing environments to accelerate AI/ML research, model development, and experimentation. You combine strong software engineering practices with sound experimental methodology to produce reliable, reproducible, and scalable AI research. You bring a responsible-AI mindset — attentive to model safety, reliability, and risk in regulated, high-stakes settings. You have a demonstrated record of contributing to the broader scientific community through conference publications, journal articles, patents, invited talks, or open-source contributions.

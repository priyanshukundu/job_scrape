We are global private markets specialists delivering tailored investment solutions, advisory services, and impactful, data driven insights to the world’s investors. Leveraging the power of our platform and our peerless intelligence across sectors, strategies, and geographies, we help identify the advantages and the answers our clients need to succeed. Position Overview StepStone is hiring a Senior Associate to help our venture and growth portfolio companies grow by connecting them with large corporate customers and partners. This individual will play a key role in driving revenue and partnership outcomes for portfolio companies by building high-impact relationships, sourcing new strategic connections, and spotting where a startup and a corporate can create value together. This role is a fit for someone with 3–4 years of experience in business development, partnerships, or sales, ideally at a high-growth technology company and with exposure to large enterprises. The role offers significant visibility within the firm and the opportunity to work closely with founders, investors, and corporate leaders shaping the future of technology and innovation. The ideal candidate is naturally curious and has sharp commercial instincts—quick to understand what a startup does, what a large enterprise needs, and where the two meet. They pair that judgment with executional rigor and are comfortable engaging with senior executives and originating new relationships across industries. Key Responsibilities Deliver commercial support to StepStone’s portfolio companies by connecting them with relevant corporate customers, partners, and strategic investors across key verticals including technology, healthcare, industrials, consumer, and defense. Own a vertical and a horizontal, developing real mastery of a specific industry and technology area, owning StepStone’s key relationships within it, and supporting the relevant portfolio companies. Source and manage executive relationships with technology, innovation, and corporate development leaders at large enterprises, as well as business unit heads, through cold outreach, referrals, events, and mutual connections. Support founders and investment teams on partnership development and go-to-market, understanding each company’s priorities so that introductions are targeted and well-timed. Represent StepStone at industry conferences and events, including firm-hosted gatherings, to build relationships and expand the team’s network within key industries. Desired Qualifications 3–4 years of professional experience in business development, partnerships, or sales, ideally at a high-growth technology company. Deep understanding of enterprise technology markets, with expertise in one or more of the following: enterprise IT, cybersecurity, and artificial intelligence. Proven ability to source, originate, and manage strategic relationships with senior corporate stakeholders through both warm and cold channels, with the presence and credibility to earn their time. Strong written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to explain a company’s value crisply and tailor it to the audience. Outstanding commercial intuition, with a knack for spotting where a startup’s product meets a real corporate need and what it would take to turn that into a partnership or customer relationship. Deeply curious, with a genuine appetite for new technologies, markets, and business models, and the drive to get up to speed quickly.

Regular or Temporary: Regular Language Fluency: English (Required) Work Shift: 1st shift (United States of America) Please review the following job description: Serves as the overall accountability office for the TD&O portfolio — anchored in the Measurement stage of the TD&O Investment Lifecycle. Manages realized savings and where they are reallocated, measures the financial outcomes of investments to confirm whether targeted savings were delivered, and manages reallocation of forecast based on anticipated savings and opportunities. Carries forward the TD&O's self-funding engine, lifecycle governance, and Line-of-Credit mechanism.

As a Controls Room Product Owner (Associate) within the Controls Room Team in Control Management, you will support the product strategy and delivery for our solutions, enabling effective oversight and execution across the control environment. You will promote measurable improvements in how test plans are designed, monitored, and executed by leveraging AI-enabled and agentic workflow patterns to enhance efficiency, consistency, and transparency while maintaining appropriate controls and human oversight. You will operate with a speed-to-production mindset, using an iterative delivery approach (e.g., Pizza POD model) to rapidly prototype, validate with users, and scale durable capabilities. Partnering closely with Software Engineering, Data Science, Design, Control Management, and Compliance stakeholders, you will help promote an outcome-focused roadmap, balance risk and value trade-offs, and ensure solutions are scalable, resilient, stable, and adoptable. As part of Control Management, you will contribute to maintaining a strong and consistent control environment across the firm through comprehensive coverage and joint accountability with business executives. You will help promote early operational risk identification and assessment, effective design and evaluation of controls, and sustainable solutions to mitigate operational risk. Within the Controls Room Team, you will collaborate with other control disciplines to oversee existing control functions and support the development of new control functions and protocols. You will help empower the three lines of defense with risk-based information to manage their control environment. You will support the collection, analysis, and monitoring of firmwide risk-based intelligence across all lines of business and corporate functions. You will also help gather information from key business and technology partners to facilitate better decision making and actionable insights, create cost-effective and automated reporting, and provide clear views into risks and issues. The Controls Room gathers information from key business and technology partners in order to: • Facilitate better decision making and actionable insights across the Firm. • Create cost-effective and automated reporting; and • Provide views into risk and issues.

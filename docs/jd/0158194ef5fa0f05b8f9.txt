Job summary As a Wholesale Credit Loss Forecasting Associate in the Corporate and Investment Bank Stress Testing team, you will analyze credit loss estimates for the commercial real estate portfolio and translate results into clear insights for stakeholders. You will support quarterly allowance and stress testing exercises by explaining key assumptions, drivers, and outcomes. Working with partners across risk, finance, modeling, and technology, you will help strengthen processes, improve reporting, and monitor portfolio risk as market conditions evolve. Job responsibilities Deliver portfolio analytics and reporting to support allowance and stress testing forecasts for commercial real estate exposures. Analyze scenario results and key loss drivers across stress scenarios and monitoring cycles. Calculate and explain model behaviors and parameters and how they translate into loss estimates. Track risk limit usage with credit officers and escalate emerging trends as needed. Support stress estimates for large transactions by compiling inputs, running analyses, and summarizing results. Run scenario analyses for senior management in response to evolving market conditions. Partner with teams across lines of business, risk, finance, model risk, quantitative research, technology, and data to align on assumptions and deliverables. Build a strong understanding of allowance and stress testing processes and apply it to improve day-to-day execution. Improve analytical workflows to increase efficiency, accuracy, and consistency. Contribute to model enhancements and results platform updates through testing, documentation, and feedback.

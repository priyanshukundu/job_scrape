Start your Voyage – Apply Now Get to Know the Opportunity: The Lead Financial Analyst is primarily responsible for the accounting and financial oversight of external software costs. This role supports the financial close, including preparing and reviewing journal entries and account reconciliations, analyzing financial results, and ensuring appropriate accounting treatment. The role also supports the quarterly forecasting process by preparing and providing relevant financial data and analysis. In addition, this position develops and maintains process documentation, helps drive process improvements, and provides training and guidance to associates on established accounting and financial processes. This role is remote; however, candidates must be located within commutable proximity to our Atlanta, Boston, Windsor, or New York City office to support periodic onsite collaboration. The Contributions You’ll Make: Contribute to monthly and quarterly financial close cycles, including the preparation of journal entries, account reconciliations, accruals, and supporting analyses related to external software costs. Analyze actual external software expenses, identify significant trends and variances, and clearly communicate findings to management and other stakeholders. Partner with appropriate stakeholders to understand changes in software spending, contracts, timing, and other business activities that may impact actual and forecasted results. Develop, maintain, and update process documentation, including procedures, process flows, desk-level documentation, controls, and other supporting materials to promote consistency and continuity. Coordinate, review, and document efforts involving financial close processes, analysis, GAAP and Statutory reporting requirements, ad hoc requests, research, and special projects. Direct and/or review the work of financial analysts and train associates on accounting and financial processes, providing clear instruction, guidance, and ongoing support to help ensure activities are completed accurately, consistently, and timely. Conduct or participate in complex financial analysis projects. Provide advice and direction to less experienced financial analysts. Evaluate existing processes and systems and make recommendations to management related to automation, standardization, control enhancements, and process efficiencies. Develop and maintain financial metrics, reporting, and tracking tools to support financial review and analysis of results. Analyze statistics and recommend improvements to workflows and systems. Deliver succinct and clear summaries of financial analysis tailored to the needs of different stakeholders. Manage multiple, often competing, priorities effectively. Perform other duties as assigned. Minimum Knowledge & Experience: Bachelor’s degree in accounting or a related field required. Good understanding of accounting principles. Proficiency with Microsoft Office Suite. Preferred Knowledge & Experience: 5+ years of accounting experience preferred. Experience with Oracle or SAP ERP preferred. Experience with SmartView or Hyperion preferred. Experience supporting expense accounting, financial close activities, reconciliations, accruals, and financial analysis preferred. How This Role Aligns to Our Core Four: Voya’s Core Four guide how we work, collaborate, and deliver results, and this role brings those expectations to life through strong financial discipline, thoughtful analysis, and partnership across the business. Instill Customer Obsession: Deliver accurate and timely financial analysis that supports informed business decisions and helps strengthen outcomes for the customers and stakeholders we serve. Transform with Automation/AI: Identify opportunities to use automation, AI, and process improvements to streamline financial activities, strengthen reporting, and reduce manual effort. Drive Higher Performance Through Our People: Provide clear guidance, training, and support to financial analysts and business partners while promoting accountability, consistency, and continuous improvement.

Embedded Software Engineer, Senior

Company: Qualcomm Technologies, Inc. Job Area: Engineering Group, Engineering Group > Software Engineering General Summary: The Products team within the Core Platform development group is seeking talented software engineers to help deliver current and future Data Center AI products . This role is suited to a versatile engineer who can work across platform software, architecture, system-level debug, and product execution. The successful candidate will work in a highly interactive, cross-functional environment, helping shape platform and software architecture, investigate complex system issues, and drive solutions from planning through product delivery. The role offers opportunities to work on cutting-edge data center technology and collaborate closely with software, hardware, systems, and architecture teams. Skills and Experience Experience with embedded software, platform software, or device drivers Familiarity with software source control, testing, and release workflows Working knowledge of real-time operating systems (Linux,Zephyr) and CPU architecture (ARM,RISC-V) Proficiency in C and C++; assembly experience is a plus Scripting experience in languages such as Python. Strong critical-thinking, system-level troubleshooting, and debugging skills Experience with JTAG or other on-chip debugging tools Excellent communication and collaboration skills Experience with data center, server, or compute platforms is a plus Responsibilities Work with cross-functional software teams to investigate, debug, and resolve platform stability and feature issues. Contribute to platform software and system architecture, translating product requirements into practical solutions Support product execution across planning, development, validation, and delivery Collaborate with program management and product image leads in developing software product roadmap and delivery plan to reduce time to market Partner with program management and product leads to shape software roadmaps and delivery plans, helping reduce time to market Minimum Qualifications: • Bachelor's degree in Engineering, Information Systems, Computer Science, or related field and 2+ years of Software Engineering or related work experience. OR Master's degree in Engineering, Information Systems, Computer Science, or related field and 1+ year of Software Engineering or related work experience.

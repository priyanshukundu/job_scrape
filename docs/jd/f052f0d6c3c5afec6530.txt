The Role As a Data Scientist at Kyndryl, you will play a critical role in transforming complex business challenges into measurable, high-value outcomes through advanced analytics and machine learning solutions. Partnering closely with business stakeholders, data engineers, and AI engineers, you will identify high-impact opportunities across areas such as forecasting, propensity modeling, optimization, and customer analytics—driving innovation that shapes the future of enterprise operations. In this role, you will own the end-to-end data science lifecycle, taking solutions from initial problem definition and hypothesis development through exploratory data analysis, feature engineering, experimentation, and rigorous model validation. Utilizing tools such as Python, SQL, scikit-learn, TensorFlow, and PyTorch, you will build predictive and prescriptive models while working alongside Data Engineering to establish reliable, governed datasets and reusable features. You will also partner with AI Engineering to productionize your solutions through scalable pipelines, APIs, and enterprise applications. Beyond model development, you will champion responsible AI practices—ensuring explainability, bias assessment, privacy, security, and strong model governance—while continuously monitoring deployed models for drift and performance to recommend timely optimizations. You will serve as a trusted analytical advisor, translating complex statistical findings, model assumptions, and expected business value into clear, actionable insights for both technical teams and executive stakeholders. If you are passionate about leveraging data and machine learning to drive operational efficiency, revenue growth, and superior customer outcomes, this role is for you. Your Future at Kyndryl Kyndryl’s focus on providing innovative IT and AI solutions to its customers means that as a Data Scientist, you will work with cutting-edge cloud and enterprise technologies while continuously learning and growing your technical skills. You will have the opportunity to contribute to large-scale, transformative projects and collaborate with a global network of data, AI, and technology professionals. Required Skills and Experience 7+ years of experience developing statistical, predictive, and prescriptive machine learning models to solve complex business problems. Advanced proficiency in Python and SQL, alongside practical experience with core data science and machine learning libraries (such as Pandas, NumPy, scikit-learn, TensorFlow, or PyTorch). Deep understanding of statistics, probability, experimental design, feature engineering, model selection, and rigorous model evaluation. Demonstrated experience working with, cleaning, and extracting insights from large, complex, and imperfect enterprise datasets. Practical familiarity with cloud data and AI platforms, such as Microsoft Azure, AWS, Databricks, or Microsoft Fabric. Solid understanding of MLOps principles, including model versioning, testing, deployment, drift monitoring, and end-to-end lifecycle management. Proven ability to translate ambiguous business challenges into structured analytical approaches and measurable success criteria. Strong communication and data storytelling skills, with the ability to clearly articulate analytical findings, model limitations, and business value to both technical and executive stakeholders. Demonstrated ability to collaborate effectively across business, data engineering, analytics, and AI engineering teams. Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Data Science, Computer Science, Statistics, Mathematics, Engineering, or a related quantitative discipline. Preferred Skills and Experience Hands-on experience deploying and scaling machine learning solutions within an enterprise production environment. Direct experience working with Microsoft Azure Machine Learning, Azure AI Foundry, or comparable enterprise AI platforms. Exposure to Generative AI, Large Language Models (LLMs), Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), and AI agents—particularly where predictive models complement agentic solutions. Relevant professional certifications in Data Science, Machine Learning, Microsoft Azure, AWS, Google Cloud, or NVIDIA.

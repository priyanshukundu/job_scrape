Job Description Initiates the design, development, execution, and implementation of technology research projects. Plans and executes research on Generative AI and long-term research agenda in large-scale Gen AI and machine learning. Delivers innovative technologies. Participates in development and integration of Gen AI infrastructure. Collaborates with industry and government on key initiatives to advance Gen AI research agenda. May participate in development of patent applications. All roles require digital fluency, including the ability to work with emerging technologies such as Generative AI tools (e.g. ChatGPT, Microsoft Copilot) to support everyday work. Key Responsibilities: Conduct research in Generative AI , with focus areas including agentic coding, agentic commerce, AI agents, large language models, reasoning, planning, and retrieval-augmented generation. Design and explore innovative AI model architectures , algorithms, and agentic workflows to improve reasoning, autonomy, reliability, efficiency, and task performance. Develop prototypes and proof-of-concepts that translate research ideas into practical AI capabilities for developer productivity, commerce experiences, and intelligent workflow automation. Review academic literature and emerging industry work to identify relevant methods, tools, benchmarks, and research opportunities in GenAI and agentic systems. Collaborate with senior researchers, engineers, and product teams to advance research projects from early exploration toward scalable product or platform opportunities. Contribute to technical documentation, research reports, publications, and patents , clearly communicating methods, results, limitations, and recommendations. .

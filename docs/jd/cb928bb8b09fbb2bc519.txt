The Strategic Delivery Director, Customer Portfolio for the US iGaming division is the senior delivery leader, accountable for the delivery of new customer go-lives and ongoing product roll-outs for existing customers. The role will oversee the coordination of multiple projects within project portfolio management, guiding prioritization, resource alignment, and delivery across strategic client programs. Reporting to the VP Operations, iGaming & Sports, the Director will translate commercial commitments and product updates into new customer go-lives and ongoing product rollouts for existing customers. They will manage senior customer collaborators through the delivery lifecycle. This is a highly cross-functional role requiring deep partnership with Commercial, Product, Engineering, and Compliance teams, and strong influence at the executive level. Success requires enterprise-level delivery leadership, strong digital/ platform delivery expertise, customer relationship management, organizational influence, and disciplined execution. What You'll Do Overseeing project management delivery across multiple client programs at once, aligning portfolio priorities with strategic objectives Leading portfolio selection, prioritization, and resource allocation activities to support timely program delivery Developing program management approaches that improve transparency, accountability, and milestone tracking Elevating end-to-end delivery excellence across iGaming products and platforms Implement scalable delivery frameworks, governance, and tooling to improve predictability, quality, and speed to market Define and track KPIs (e.g., release predictability, cycle time, defect rates, SLA adherence) Proactively identify and resolve delivery risks, dependencies, and systemic bottlenecks Build a high-performing delivery team through clear accountability, coaching, talent development, succession planning and strong delivery management standards Building senior customer relationships and shaping engagement direction through informed decision making Build alignment across broad stakeholder groups (including customer stakeholders) to drive consensus and create transparency, simplifying nuanced issues and communicating progress, risks and outcome. Driving process planning, continuous improvement, and cross-functional coordination across managed services delivery Use AI-enabled tools and modern delivery practices to improve the effectiveness and productivity of the Delivery organization itself What We're Looking For Proven Delivery leader with experience in high-growth, global, software or technology businesses; +10 years experience Experience translating strategic objectives into program delivery roadmaps A visible, curious, and collaborative leadership style that creates clarity, raises standards and develops talent.

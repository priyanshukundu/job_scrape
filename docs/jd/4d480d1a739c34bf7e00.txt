What You’ll Work On Responsible for implementing and maintaining the effectiveness of the quality system. Manage and maintain the site Validation Change Control system (M-files). Manage reviews / requests for changes and monitor compliance to validated systems such as Facility/Utilities/Equipment qualifications, processes validations, procedures, drawings, calibrations and software. Manage and maintain compliance to CRF 21 Part 11 requirements for computer validation Assure review/approve/close validation docs, Eng. Studies, policies, procedures, and forms. Liaison between Engineering, Quality Engineers, and Quality Systems, plant/division/Corporate, on all issues related to change control, certification and plant validated systems. Manage site Validation Master Plan. Manage the Validation Review Board. Manage the Validation Management System (validation file systems, audits, etc.) Represent plant validation metrics to Division validation and peers. Manage special projects as assigned. You’ll be accountable for meeting our compliance standards including FDA, OSHA and Abbott policies and procedures. Required Qualifications Bachelor’s degree in an engineering science or closely related discipline with five years progressive work experience, or sufficient practical experience to demonstrate functional equivalence. Strong computer and technical writing skills. Preferred Qualifications Three years of experience in validating equipment, facility, utility, computer, and cleaning and process systems. Knowledge of applicable regulatory standards and requirements for infant formula and nutritionals. Extensive understanding of cGMP and FDA requirements for validation.

Our Corporate IT Group is looking for an NX CAM Software Development Engineer to join them. This role requires access to data subject to U.S. government citizenship restrictions as well. To be considered for the NX CAM Software Development Engineer role, here’s what you’ll typically need to bring with you: Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering discipline, or related field or Equivalent professional experience may be considered in lieu of a degree Proven aptitude and experience in the development of software solutions Experience with coding languages at least 2 of the following: TCL, NX Open, Python, G-Code ISO 6983 … or similar Experience using version control software, i.e Git, Subversion, or similar Helpful but not required, experience using Post Builder/Post Configurator to create and maintain NX post processors Helpful but not required, experience with NX CAD and/or Siemens Teamcenter Excellent written and oral communication skills;

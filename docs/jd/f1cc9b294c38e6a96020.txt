What You'll Do : This role will work closely and collaboratively with Development, Technical and Business teams to support effective incident management processes. The primary responsibility will be supporting the identification, response, and resolution of incidents that impact OCC applications and systems, minimizing business disruption and restoring normal service as quickly as possible. This incident management role will help drive faster response, clear communication, and continuous improvement across the incident lifecycle. Primary Duties and Responsibilities: To perform this job successfully, an individual must be able to perform each primary duty satisfactorily. Lead the end-to-end incident management process, from detection and triage through resolution and closure Serve as incident commander for major and critical incidents, coordinating cross-functional response teams Support the design, development, delivery and management of incident communications Conduct impact and severity assessments to help prioritize response efforts and identify key stakeholders Assist in facilitating post-incident reviews and root cause analyses, documenting lessons learned Track and report incident metrics, trends and issues Support incident management practices across the team and broader organization Serve as an advocate for incident management, communicating processes and procedures throughout the company and assisting teams with response and resolution Coordinate with Problem, Change and Service Management teams to help prevent recurrence Contribute to ongoing improvements in the incident management process Stay current on trends and regulations to help ensure effectiveness and compliance Other job-related duties as assigned Supervisory Responsibilities: N/A Qualifications: The requirements listed are representative of the knowledge, skill, and/or ability required. Founded in 1973, OCC is dedicated to promoting stability and market integrity by delivering clearing and settlement services for options, futures and securities lending transactions. As a Systemically Important Financial Market Utility (SIFMU), OCC operates under the jurisdiction of the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC), the U.S. Commodity Futures Trading Commission (CFTC), and the Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System. OCC has more than 100 clearing members and provides central counterparty (CCP) clearing and settlement services to 23 exchanges and trading platforms.

What You Will Do Business partnership and solution design Act as the primary technology partner for Private Credit collaborators and the business product manager. Lead discovery through workshops, interviews and data analysis; translate business needs into requirements, process flows, data mappings, functional designs and user stories. Challenge assumptions, identify requirement and control gaps, evaluate solution options and drive clear business and technology decisions. Design end-to-end Private Credit workflows spanning deal and facility setup, trade execution, funding, interest and fee accruals, rate resets, paydowns, amendments, valuations, restructurings and reporting. Translate Private Credit operating requirements into scalable platform configurations, integrations and data solutions. Systems, Data and Delivery Configure Allvue workflows, reference data, business rules and application behavior. Use sophisticated SQL for data profiling, reconciliation, impact analysis, data lineage and root-cause investigation. Trace data across front-office platforms, Security Master, IBOR, investment accounting and enterprise data platforms to identify and resolve discrepancies. Understand and map relationships among sponsors, borrowers/issuers, deals, facilities/assets, trades, portfolios, positions/holdings and accounting records . Validate REST APIs, JSON payloads, batch/file interfaces and data pipelines; define reconciliations, exception handling and data-quality controls. Partner with engineering teams on integration build, implementation and fixing issues. Lead system, integration and user-acceptance testing with particular focus on business outcomes, accounting impact, financial calculations and data integrity. Support implementation through production stabilization and ensure sustainable operational handoff. Credit Data and Reporting Design, configure and maintain Private Credit loan tapes, portfolio extracts and credit reporting datasets for operational, investor, lender and other downstream requirements. Define source-to-target mappings and calculations covering borrower/issuer, facility type, commitment, funded and unfunded amounts, outstanding principal, benchmark/reference rate, spread, floor, maturity, PIK/cash-pay terms, seniority, lien, financial indicators and portfolio/holding information. Build configurable templates capable of supporting different reporting requirements while maintaining consistent definitions, lineage and controls. Reconcile loan-tape outputs to Allvue, investment accounting and other systems of record; investigate and resolve breaks. Understand how we use term sheets and completed credit documentation and translate into structured system attributes, transaction terms and downstream reporting. Build and maintain tear sheets or credit snapshots used for portfolio monitoring and investment review. Platform, vendor and team leadership Partner with Allvue engineering, product and support teams to solve defects and influence product enhancements. Evaluate trade-offs among native configuration, custom development, integration, data solutions including Snowflake, and business-process change. Mentor junior and mid-level analysts and establish standards for requirements, solution build, testing, documentation and production readiness. Give to the Private Credit technology roadmap with an emphasis on platform simplification, automation, reduced manual processing and stronger data quality and controls.

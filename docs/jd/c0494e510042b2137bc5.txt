Job Description: Job Title FX Derivatives Trader Corporate Title Analyst Location New York, NY Overview In the world’s financial markets, the Deutsche Bank Foreign Exchange team is recognized as a leader. The Business combines sales, trading and research teams who structure a range of financial market products and market these to our targeted client base. Deutsche Bank trades all currencies, including G10, Emerging Markets and Precious Metals, in all tenors. Working in a period of economic and regulatory uncertainty, the team places emphasis on innovation, collaboration, and risk management. The Global Foreign Exchange (GFX) business is looking to hire a Trader within the Foreign Exchange (FX) Derivatives global complex.

As a Quality Lab Associate I, you will perform chemical and analytical testing to support product quality, regulatory compliance, and operational excellence. This position plays a critical role in ensuring the safety, effectiveness, and quality of Baxter products through adherence to established procedures, quality systems, and Good Manufacturing Practices (cGMP). Essential Responsibilities Perform quantitative and qualitative chemical analyses of intravenous solutions, administration sets, devices, raw materials, and plant water systems using wet chemistry techniques and analytical instrumentation. Utilize laboratory instrumentation, computerized systems, and software applications to collect, analyze, and accurately document test data. Apply scientific principles and analytical techniques to perform testing and interpret results with a high degree of accuracy. Support laboratory investigations through effective troubleshooting, root cause analysis, and problem-solving methodologies. Prepare, execute, and document protocol testing and special projects in accordance with approved procedures. Investigate deviations, document findings, and assist with the completion of exception and quality system documentation. Maintain compliance with applicable regulatory requirements, internal quality standards, Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs), and current Good Manufacturing Practices (cGMP). Complete testing activities within established timelines while maintaining data integrity and documentation accuracy. Perform peer review of laboratory data and documentation as required. Escalate safety, quality, equipment, or compliance concerns to supervision and support appropriate corrective actions. Maintain a clean, organized, and inspection-ready laboratory environment through 6S and continuous improvement practices. Demonstrate knowledge of and compliance with all applicable environmental, health, and safety requirements. Support business needs through flexibility in work schedules, including off-shift and weekend coverage when required. Perform additional duties and special assignments as needed. Qualifications and Skills Strong computer proficiency, including Microsoft Office applications and laboratory computer systems. Ability to work independently and effectively manage multiple priorities in a fast-paced manufacturing environment. Strong organizational, analytical, and problem-solving skills. Effective written and verbal communication skills. Demonstrated attention to detail and commitment to data integrity and quality compliance. Ability to collaborate effectively within a team environment while supporting departmental objectives. Education and Experience Bachelor's degree in Chemistry, Chemical Engineering, Physical Chemistry, Biology, or a related scientific discipline. Chemistry-focused coursework preferred. Laboratory experience gained through academic coursework, internships, co-ops, or professional experience. Exposure to quantitative analysis and analytical laboratory techniques required. Experience with analytical instrumentation is preferred. 0 to 3 years of relevant laboratory or quality experience. Physical Requirements Must successfully meet color vision requirements as determined through the Ishihara Test for Color Deficiency. Must meet near-vision visual acuity requirements as defined by Baxter's Visual Acuity Procedure CL-01-01-037. Ability to work in a laboratory and manufacturing environment while following all required safety practices and procedures.

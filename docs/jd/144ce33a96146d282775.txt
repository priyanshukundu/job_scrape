Senior Data Scientist Brooklyn, NY (Rent the Runway HQ) About Us: Rent the Runway (RTR) is transforming the way we get dressed by pioneering the world’s first Closet in the Cloud. Founded in 2009, RTR has disrupted the $2.4 trillion fashion industry by inspiring women with a more joyful, sustainable and financially-savvy way to feel their best every day. As the ultimate destination for circular fashion, the brand now offers infinite points of access to its shared closet via a fully customizable subscription to fashion, one-time rental or ownership. RTR offers designer apparel and accessories from hundreds of brand partners and has built in-house proprietary technology and a one-of-a-kind reverse logistics operation. RTR has been named to CNBC’s “Disruptor 50” five times in ten years, and has been placed on Fast Company’s Most Innovative Companies list multiple times. About the Team: Data is core to our growing business and has been ingrained in the company's DNA since its founding. As a member of the Data Analytics team, you will partner closely with Product, Engineering, Finance, Inventory, and other business stakeholders to identify opportunities where machine learning, AI, and advanced analytics can create meaningful customer and business value. The Data Science team develops machine learning solutions, predictive models, recommendation systems, experimentation frameworks, and AI-powered capabilities that directly influence how Rent the Runway operates and how customers discover, engage with, and rent and buy inventory. Our work spans pricing optimization, inventory intelligence, personalization, predictive modeling, and intelligent customer experiences. As RTR continues investing in Data Science and AI, the team plays a critical role in driving business performance and bringing new customer experiences to life — from intelligent pricing and inventory optimization to recommendation engines, personalized experiences, and future AI-powered products. About the Job: We are looking for a hands-on Senior Data Scientist to independently own and deliver high-impact machine learning and optimization solutions that drive measurable business results. While this role will initially focus heavily on pricing optimization, predictive modeling , and inventory-related decision systems , you will also have the opportunity to contribute to a broad range of Data Science initiatives, including personalization, recommendation systems, and AI-powered customer experiences as business priorities evolve. You will partner closely with Product, Engineering, Finance, Inventory, and business stakeholders to translate ambiguous business problems into scalable, production-ready machine learning solutions. You will take ownership of projects end-to-end, from identifying opportunities and defining the analytical approach to building, deploying, monitoring, and continuously improving models. This is a highly hands-on individual contributor role with significant ownership and visibility. You will work closely with the Senior Data Science Manager while independently driving technical decisions, challenging assumptions, and helping shape how Data Science creates value across Rent the Runway. We're looking for someone with strong technical expertise, business intuition, analytical rigor, and the ability to operate independently in a fast-paced environment. What You’ll Do: Own end-to-end Data Science initiatives, from problem definition and exploratory analysis to model development, deployment, monitoring, and continuous improvement. Design, build, and optimize machine learning and statistical models that improve pricing decisions, revenue performance, inventory efficiency, and customer outcomes. Partner closely with Finance, Inventory, and business stakeholders to develop and continuously improve pricing strategies and optimization models across our rental and resale businesses. Analyze customer, product, transaction, and inventory data to identify opportunities where machine learning, predictive modeling, and advanced analytics can drive measurable business impact. Develop forecasting, optimization, and predictive modeling solutions to support inventory planning, demand estimation, profitability, and other business-critical decisions. Design and analyze experiments, A/B tests, and statistical evaluation frameworks to measure the incremental impact of Data Science initiatives. Build, maintain, and continuously improve production machine learning models, ensuring reliability, accuracy, scalability, and ongoing business relevance. Contribute to personalization, recommendation systems, customer segmentation, and AI-powered experiences as the team's roadmap evolves. Partner across Product, Engineering, Finance, Inventory, and the broader Data organization to translate complex analytical solutions into scalable, production-ready capabilities. Independently evaluate different modeling approaches, make thoughtful technical trade-offs, and communicate the reasoning behind methodological decisions. Establish and follow best practices around model validation, monitoring, experimentation, documentation, and reproducibility. Communicate insights, model performance, trade-offs, and recommendations clearly to both technical and non-technical stakeholders. Contribute to the technical development of the Data Science function by sharing knowledge, improving processes, and supporting a collaborative, high-performing team. About You: 5+ years of hands-on experience in Data Science, Machine Learning, Applied Statistics, Operations Research, or a related quantitative field. Bachelor's degree in a quantitative field (e.g., Computer Science, Statistics, Mathematics, Economics, Physics, Operations Research), or equivalent practical experience; advanced degrees and/or specializations are a plus. Strong proficiency in Python and SQL , with experience working with large-scale datasets and developing production-ready machine learning models . Strong foundation in statistics, experimentation, hypothesis testing, causal inference, and model evaluation. Proven experience independently designing, building, and deploying machine learning or optimization solutions that solve real-world business problems. Experience with pricing optimization, demand forecasting, inventory optimization, or revenue management is a strong plus. Experience with cloud AI/ML platforms and services (e.g., GCP, AWS, or Azure). Experience with GCP (including BigQuery and Vertex AI) is a strong plus. Experience with cloud data warehouses (e.g., BigQuery or Snowflake). Experience with dbt and modern analytics engineering practices is a strong plus. Strong business intuition and ability to translate ambiguous problems into scalable analytical solutions with measurable outcomes. Ability to critically evaluate results, challenge assumptions, troubleshoot unexpected model behavior, and clearly explain technical decisions. Self-driven, curious, proactive, and comfortable operating with ownership and ambiguity.

Senior Software Engineer
GE HealthCare

Job Description Summary As a Senior Software Engineer in GE HealthCare's Science & Technology organization, you will develop scalable, cloud-based software solutions that support healthcare innovation and digital transformation. You will own software components and contribute to complex technical projects that enable early-stage products to evolve and scale. Working with cross-functional teams, you will help deliver reliable, secure, high-quality software across cloud and edge environments. Job Description Responsibilities Develop software products in collaboration with cross-functional teams, following approved architectures and product roadmaps. Own the implementation of software components, applying test-driven development and engineering best practices to ensure quality and maintainability. Design, build, and maintain user-facing applications, APIs, scalable distributed services, and supporting systems. Implement, test, and deploy software solutions that improve performance, durability, cost efficiency, and security. Contribute to scoping and leading complex technical projects that support early-stage product development and scalability. Conduct software design and peer code reviews, providing constructive feedback to improve engineering quality and team deliverables. Deliver high-quality software within demanding development schedules. Required Qualifications Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or a related discipline. 4+ years of professional full-stack software development experience, including front-end engineering and modern design principles. 1+ year of experience building scalable, distributed systems using a modern cloud framework or platform such as AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud Platform (GCP). Strong experience with Java/J2EE, Spring Core, Spring Boot, and Java Persistence API (JPA), including frameworks such as Hibernate. Preferred Qualifications Master's or PhD degree in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, or a related field. 4+ years of professional software development experience. Experience with Spring Framework, including MVC, Integration, and Data, in addition to Java/J2EE and Spring Core. Experience designing large-scale distributed systems, preferably using AWS technologies. Experience with big data, analytics, containerized microservices, serverless functions, and event-driven architecture. Expertise across public cloud and on-premises deployment models, including supporting production deployments. Experience creating, documenting, and communicating software architectures for complex products. Experience improving testing and validation automation, production availability, conversion automation, distributed computing, and large-scale system design. Experience building, tracking, and communicating plans using Agile processes. Experience engaging directly with customers to assess needs, identify solutions, and constructively resolve disagreements. Strong technical problem-solving skills and ability to learn new technologies and methodologies. Why Join GE HealthCare At GE HealthCare, you will contribute to cloud and edge technologies that help make healthcare more precise, personalized, and accessible. Join a collaborative environment focused on innovation, ownership, transparency, trust, and integrity.

Process Data Scientist
Regeneron
Location: Limerick
Link: https://regeneron.wd1.myworkdayjobs.com/Careers/job/Limerick/Process-Data-Scientist_R53165

At Regeneron, we use science and innovation to develop life-changing medicines for people with serious diseases. Regeneron is currently seeking a Process Data Scientist to join our process monitoring and analytics team. This position offers the opportunity to contribute to a fast-growing, science-driven organization making a meaningful difference to patients worldwide. When & where: Location: On-site Shift Requirements: Monday to Friday, 8:00 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. What you’ll do: Lead and enhance process monitoring programs and systems. Source and verify process data from Laboratory Information Management Systems, Qumas, OPM, Historian, and paper records. Apply statistical process control and quality engineering to monitor process capability, identify trends, and support investigations. Determine root causes of process-related deviations using scientific and analytical methods. Develop automated analytical solutions to support reporting and data distribution. Manage data integrity investigations, Corrective and Preventive Actions (CAPAs), and change controls. Analyze large datasets using Principal Component Analysis (PCA), Partial Least Squares (PLS), curve metrics, and batch analysis to develop predictive models. Lead cross-functional investigations, present findings, issue technical reports, and mentor junior team members. Let’s find out if we’re a fit: Associate: Bachelor’s degree in Mathematics, Statistics, or a related discipline. Intermediate: Bachelor’s degree in Mathematics, Statistics, or a related discipline with 2+ years of experience; or an equivalent combination of education and experience. Knowledge of statistical process control, quality engineering, and process capability analysis. Experience working with manufacturing, process, and quality data systems. Experience applying multivariate analysis techniques, including Principal Component Analysis (PCA) and Partial Least Squares (PLS). Ability to develop data acquisition, aggregation, reporting, visualization, and automated analytics solutions. Experience supporting investigations, data integrity activities, regulatory audits, submissions, and continuous improvement initiatives. Experience in a biopharmaceutical current Good Manufacturing Practice (cGMP) manufacturing environment is an advantage. Strong collaboration, communication, presentation, coaching, and problem-solving skills. Does this sound like you? Apply now to take your first step towards living the Regeneron Way!

Job Description This position involves working in a fast-paced inbound phone support environment for the Auto Claims Department where customer focus, attention to detail, and strong communication skills are essential. You'll be responsible for handling a wide range of calls that support auto claim operations, including inbound customer interactions, data validation, claim-related task work, and process documentation. You'll collaborate with various teams and use multiple tools to ensure business needs are met efficiently and accurately. As a Processing Support Associate III , you'll be a key contributor in supporting internal and external customers, agents, repair facilities, and business partners through inbound phone support and claim-related task work. This role requires a high level of accuracy, adaptability, and the ability to work across multiple systems and communication channels. You'll help ensure smooth auto claim operations by managing information, resolving inquiries, documenting claim activity, and identifying opportunities for process improvements. In This Role, You Will Handle a high volume of inbound phone calls related to Auto Claims. Provide service, information, and procedural guidance to customers, agents, repair facilities, and business partners. Handle complex or unique customer interactions with professionalism, empathy, and care. Complete claim-related task work, including reviewing, updating, validating, and processing information across systems. Research claim information and utilize multiple tools while assisting callers. Validate, edit, and correct advanced data entries across systems. Communicate effectively with internal teams, external partners, agents, and consumers. Select appropriate tools and procedures from established options to resolve issues. Process data in alignment with internal policies and external business requirements. Identify and recommend process improvements; assist in maintaining standard operating procedures. Schedule: Monday-Friday, 9:00 AM - 5:30 PM CST You're a Great Match for This Role If You Have Strong verbal communication skills and comfort helping customers over the phone. Strong attention to detail and accuracy in data handling, data entry, and documentation. Excellent communication and interpersonal skills. Ability to work independently and manage multiple tasks efficiently. Ability to navigate multiple systems while assisting customers in a fast-paced environment. A problem-solving mindset with a focus on continuous improvement. A team-oriented attitude with a commitment to delivering high-quality service. Professionalism, empathy, and adaptability when handling challenging customer interactions. Other Things to Consider This role does not include supervisory responsibilities. Flexibility and adaptability are key, as priorities may shift based on business needs.

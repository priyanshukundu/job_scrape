(RTP) Meet the Team You will be a member of the Global Certification Team (GCT), which consists of experts from the ComSec team, Defense Programs team, CC team, FIPS team, and IPv6 team. You'll have the opportunity to architect, design, develop, and test some of the world's newest and most popular systems. Your Impact As a Junior Engineer on the India ComSec team, you will help bring Cisco products through National Centre for Communication Security (NCCS) “India Comsec” certification. You will build and maintain test environments, implement security and network test procedures, collect and organize evidence, analyze findings, and partner with Cisco engineering business units and accredited test labs. You will also use automation frameworks and responsible AI-assisted engineering tools to improve repeatability, documentation quality, and program visibility. You will work independently and as part of a team, managing multiple priorities while developing expertise across networking, cybersecurity, and product certification. Maintain and manage the lab infrastructure that supports India ComSec certification activities. Build test networks, including the configuration, installation, and setup of routers, switches, wireless products, and supporting systems. Implement internal readiness and certification testing against applicable security requirements, recording clear and reproducible results. Collect, organize, and review technical evidence and certification documentation; maintain test records, trackers, and status updates. Triage test findings and coordinate with engineering business units and accredited labs to reproduce issues, gather technical recommendations, and support resolution. Perform remote testing with accredited test centers, with travel up to 10% of the time. Research, design, develop, and document technical solutions and repeatable procedures using scripting, automation, and responsible AI-assisted tools. Minimum Qualifications Experience working with Cisco products. Working knowledge of IP routing protocols and switching technologies (e.g., LAN/WAN, STP, OSPF, BGP, VRRP/HSRP, IPv4, IPv6). Ability to build test networks, including configuration and setup of routers, switches, wireless controllers or firewalls. Understanding of security concepts and frameworks: SSHv2, PKI, IPSec, TLS, AAA, Zero Trust, NIST 800-53. Community college or higher education in Information Technology/Systems, Computer Science, or engineering, or a similar background, or 4 years additional work experience in a related field. Preferred Qualifications Direct experience supporting product security certification, compliance testing, or regulated laboratory testing; familiarity with NCCS/ITSAR/Comsec, FIPS 140, Common Criteria, NIST guidance, or related security standards. CCNA, CCNP Enterprise, CCNP Security, CyberOps Associate, or a comparable networking or cybersecurity certification. Familiarity with APIs and data models (REST, NETCONF/RESTCONF, YANG, JSON/YAML), and Git. Familiarity with Linux or Windows administration and virtualized lab environments used to host test tools and supporting services. Familiarity with AI-assisted tooling and responsible, secure AI usage; ability to document environments with Visio, draw.io, or diagrams-as-code. Excellent interpersonal, verbal, and written communication skills; detail oriented, self-motivated, strong time management, and able to work independently and in a team. S&TO Why Cisco?

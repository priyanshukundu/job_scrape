Your Impact As a CX Software Engineer, you will design, develop, and deploy production-grade software and automation solutions that directly enhance customer network reliability and operational agility. You will build and maintain robust microservices, custom APIs, and telemetry tools in Python or Go to streamline complex infrastructure orchestration across hybrid-cloud environments. You will implement modern NetDevOps and CI/CD pipelines to automate repetitive operational tasks and eliminate manual deployment errors for enterprise clients. You will partner closely with Lead Technical Architects and customer engineering leaders to translate multifaceted business requirements into scalable, test-driven software architectures. Success in this role means delivering resilient, automated codebases that significantly reduce incident resolution time and accelerate our customers' time-to-value. Minimum Qualifications Meet one of the following (completed within the past 3 years or expected to be completed within the next 12 months): A Master's degree with 0 years of work experience; or A Bachelor's degree with 2 years of work experience; or An educational or workforce development program (e.g., Associate’s, Apprenticeship, Boot camp, or Certification) with 3 years of work experience; or A High school diploma (or equivalent) with 4 years of work experience. Qualifying education and work experience should be in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, Software Engineering, Information Technology, Electrical Engineering, Mathematics, or a related technical field. Demonstrated proficiency in at least one modern programming language, such as Python, Go, or Java, with practical experience in object-oriented design and data structures. Hands-on experience developing, testing, or integrating RESTful APIs within Linux/Unix environments. Practical experience using version control systems such as Git/GitHub for code management, code reviews, and collaborative development. Experience in automated scripting, test automation, or software tooling to optimize workflows and reduce manual tasks. Preferred Qualifications Experience with Infrastructure as Code (IaC) and container technologies such as Docker, Kubernetes, Ansible, or Terraform. Working knowledge of networking fundamentals (TCP/IP, routing, switching, SDN) or network automation libraries/frameworks (e.g., pyATS/Genie, Netmiko, NSO). Exposure to deploying and monitoring software applications on public cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, or GCP). Active industry or developer certifications, such as Cisco DevNet Associate / Professional or Cloud Practitioner / Associate certifications.

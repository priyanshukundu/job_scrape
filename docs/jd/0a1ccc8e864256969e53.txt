To usher in this new era, we seek AI-native thinkers across every function who are energized by the opportunity to reinvent how they work. The Billing & Payments Platform team builds Snowflake's central systems for customer resource consumption, revenue processing, invoicing, and reporting. Our systems power Snowflake's business and enable every other engineering team. The architectures we ship also serve as reference patterns for customers building on Snowflake.

Computing Snowflake's bills is a distributed systems problem. Usage is metered across every cloud and region. The pricing catalog keeps changing, including new commercial constructs for Cortex AI, Snowflake Intelligence, and the broader agentic portfolio. The same systems have to catch abuse and fraud on a usage-based product, then produce a bill Finance can audit. The bar is accuracy, auditability, and processing that keeps up with usage as it happens.

This is a deeply cross-functional role. You will partner with Product, Finance, Legal, Growth, Go-to-Market Systems, Snowsight, Cortex AI, and product engineering teams to deliver experiences that customers and internal stakeholders depend on every day.




AS A STAFF SOFTWARE ENGINEER ON BILLING PLATFORM YOU WILL :

 - Drive multi-team initiatives from an ambiguous business problem through design, launch, and operation, and set the technical approach other engineers execute. Own a bounded project end-to-end when that is the right scope.

 - Build the billing systems that meter, rate, and invoice usage across Snowflake services, on Snowflake's own product. Usage is collected across clouds and regions, then reconciled into an auditable bill.

 - Shape how Snowflake meters, prices, and bills AI products with Cortex AI, Intelligence, and Apps, including token-based inference, agent workflows, and AI-native subscriptions.

 -  Be the go-to expert for a primary billing domain — metering, rating, invoicing, contracts, payments, threshold billing, fraud, or reporting — and influence the systems next to it.

 - Own reliability and correctness for what you build. Participate in on-call, debug across unfamiliar areas, and drive systemic fixes to monitoring, alerts, and quality rather than one-off patches.

 - Mentor senior engineers. Review designs, raise the bar on testing and rollout, and help others operate these systems.

 - Partner with Product, Finance, GTM Systems, Growth, Trust, and Legal. Write designs that both engineering and Finance can execute.




OUR IDEAL STAFF SOFTWARE ENGINEER WILL HAVE:

 - Significant experience building and operating large-scale production distributed systems, typically 10+ years or equivalent impact.

 - A bachelor's or master's degree in computer science, a related technical field, or equivalent practical experience.

 - Strong fundamentals in distributed systems: consistency, fault tolerance, idempotency, exactly-once processing, queueing, and stream and batch pipelines.

 - Strong fundamentals in databases and data modeling. SQL fluency, schema design, performance tuning, and an appreciation for the operational realities of large-scale data systems.

 - Proficiency in one or more of Java, Scala, Python, or Go, and the practices that make systems operable at scale: testing, observability, CI/CD, and incremental rollout.

 - A track record of driving outcomes that span teams. Framing the problem when Product, Finance, and engineering disagree. Writing the design. Making the tradeoffs. Landing the system in production.

 - Depth in at least one domain adjacent to billing — metering, rating, invoicing, contracts, payments, revenue, or financial reporting — with enough range to debug and influence the systems next to it.

 - Hands-on use of AI as a builder's tool in design, coding, debugging, and incident response. On this team that includes Cortex Code and Snowflake Intelligence, but prior use of those specific tools is not required.

 - Clear writing and communication with non-engineering partners. You can explain a pricing or contract change so Finance can audit it and engineering can build it.



Bonus points for:

 - Prior experience in billing, payments, metering, revenue, or financial systems at a SaaS or cloud company.

 - Experience with fraud detection, abuse mitigation, or trust and safety systems in a usage-based product.

 - Experience designing or evolving pricing models for AI/ML products, including inference, fine-tuning, and agentic workloads.

 - Experience building on Snowflake or comparable cloud data platforms, including Snowpark, Streams and Tasks, Iceberg, Streamlit, or Cortex.

 - Contributions to internal AI developer-productivity tooling, agentic skills, or similar coding-agent workflows.

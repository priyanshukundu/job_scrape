Company Federal Reserve Bank of Chicago The Risk Management job family focuses on risk driven supervision and analytics for a broad array of risk areas, products areas, and institutions or service providers engaged in moderately complex to complex activities. More specifically, this role will innovate, evaluate, and enhance data solutions enabling seamless delivery of data and analytical products to constituents of the Wholesale Credit Risk Center (WCRC). This individual will be part of a team that is responsible for coordinating and maintaining critically used licensed and regulatory wholesale credit data on behalf of the Federal Reserve System, including management of the WCRC’s data mart and related infrastructure. The team creates and maintains wholesale credit analytics to support risk analytics and supervisory stress testing and participates on supervisory assessments of risk management and modeling performed by financial institutions. The level of work required is advanced and staff must be able to work under minimal supervision. This job does not have any direct reports but may be assigned technical resources on projects when appropriate that would need direction. This job requires the ability to advocate for and upskill colleagues in technical processes. Department Overview: Supervision and Regulation plays a critical role in the Bank's core mission, supervising and regulating state-chartered banks that are members of the Federal Reserve System, bank holding companies and financial holding companies. Our duty is to foster the stability, integrity and efficiency of the nation's monetary, financial and payment systems to promote optimal economic performance. Wholesale Credit Risk Center Overview: FRB Chicago’s Wholesale Credit Risk Center serves as the Federal Reserve System’s leading provider of wholesale credit expertise. The WCRC maintains the Federal Reserve System’s largest collection of wholesale credit data and the related infrastructure. While physically located in Chicago, the WCRC provides national support for corporate and commercial real estate analytics to monitor credit conditions and inform supervisory teams and senior management on risk levels and emerging trends across industry, market, and supervisory information. In addition, the group directly performs/influences wholesale credit supervisory work at the nation’s largest banks and develops and maintains wholesale credit expected loss models for Dodd Frank Act capital stress testing efforts.

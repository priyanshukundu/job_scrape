Job description Group Description: PFG provides comprehensive, global advisory services on capital raising for select private fund managers. PFG’s clients include investment firms pursuing a spectrum of alternative investment strategies including private equity, private credit, and infrastructure / real assets. The team has an over fifteen-year track record in fund placement and has developed a reputation for representing some of the highest quality managers in the market. PFG advises and executes on all aspects of the fundraising process, including competitive positioning and market assessment, preparation of marketing materials, investor introductions and capital closings. The group serves a vital function in the fundraising market by identifying and executing on sources of capital that may not be readily accessible to its clients. Job Duties: Own day-to-day execution of assigned workstreams under the direction of senior team members across all phases of private fund capital raising engagements, including new business pitches, fund positioning, marketing process and material preparation, investor outreach, due diligence, and fundraising closings Serve as a day-to-day point of contact for general partner clients and institutional investors, coordinating fundraising activities, responding to information requests, and facilitating communication between fund managers, investors, and internal deal teams Review and provide guidance on analyses and materials prepared by Analysts to ensure accuracy, completeness, and consistency, including: Exercise independent judgment in developing analytical approaches to evaluate prospective fundraising mandates, including assessment of fund manager track records, investment strategies, market positioning, and potential investor demand Support development and execution of fundraising strategies, including identification and prioritization of prospective limited partners, investor segmentation, marketing timelines, and recommendations regarding investor engagement Manage investor outreach and due diligence workstreams, including coordination of general partner and limited partner meetings, review of investor briefing materials, preparation and review of due diligence responses, and escalation of substantive issues to senior team members Maintain regular dialogue with institutional investors and other market participants to gather intelligence regarding investment preferences, allocation priorities, fundraising conditions, and investor feedback Analyze and synthesize investor feedback, fundraising activity, and prospective capital commitments to evaluate fundraising progress and support the development of Evercore’s advice regarding marketing strategy, process execution, and investor allocations Oversee maintenance of internal CRM systems and fundraising tracking materials, ensuring accurate reporting of investor interactions, due diligence activity, prospective commitments, and fundraising milestones Coordinate with fund managers, investors, legal counsel, and other relevant parties to facilitate investor subscription processes, documentation requirements, and fund closings Review and direct Analysts’ preparation of financial analyses, charts, graphs, and presentations in Microsoft Excel, PowerPoint, and Word, ensuring materials accurately support client recommendations and fundraising execution Minimum

Job Description Job Title: Associate Manager, Fund of Funds Accounting Locations: New York City (hybrid), Union, NJ (hybrid), Newtown, CT (hybrid) About the Role Take the next step in your accounting career by joining a Fund of Fund team supporting a multi-asset class client that focuses heavily on private markets (private equity, venture capital, real estate) alongside hedge funds and public equities while developing your leadership and client management skills. You will have exposure to the Monthly NAV process (including investor allocations, management fees, and incentive allocations), quarterly investor statements and annual reporting requirements. As an Associate Manager, you will play a key role in delivering exceptional client service, mentoring team members, and driving operational excellence in a collaborative, fast-paced environment. Why Join SS&C SS&C combines proprietary technology with deep industry expertise to support complex financial and health care operations. Our teams design, implement, and operate solutions that help clients manage data, automate processes, and scale their businesses with confidence. You will work with industry experts, modern platforms, and evolving technologies, gaining exposure to real-world operational challenges and large-scale enterprise environments. How You Will Make an Impact Coordinate the day-to-day activities of the U.S. Client Service Fund Accounting team, helping manage workflow, priorities, and deliverables. Supervise, coach, and mentor accounting professionals while supporting their growth and development. Review fund accounting deliverables, including P&L reporting, NAV calculations, pricing, and economic allocations, ensuring accuracy and timeliness. Assist in the preparation and review of financial statements, partner capital statements, and other client reporting. Build and maintain strong client relationships by serving as a key point of contact and ensuring high-quality service. Support new client onboardings, fund launches, reorganizations, and platform conversions. Resolve fund accounting and operational issues while escalating complex matters as appropriate. Provide technical accounting guidance and support to team members and clients. Coordinate audit requests and support the external audit process. Contribute to process improvement initiatives, system enhancements, testing, and implementation projects. Required Experience Bachelor’s degree in Accounting, Finance, or a related field 4+ years of accounting experience 1+ years of leadership, supervisory, or team lead experience Strong verbal and written communication skills Experience preparing and reviewing financial statements, audit support, and investor allocations Solid understanding of corporate actions Strong knowledge of U.S. GAAP and hedge fund accounting principles Advanced proficiency in Microsoft Excel Experience with a broad range of financial instruments, including equities, fixed income, repurchase agreements, futures, foreign exchange, mortgages, or options What Sets You Apart Ability to manage multiple priorities in a fast-paced, deadline-driven environment Master’s, CPA, or CFA a plus Fund accounting experience at a fund administrator preferred Demonstrated success improving operational processes or mentoring junior team members Join SS&C, where innovation meets global opportunities.

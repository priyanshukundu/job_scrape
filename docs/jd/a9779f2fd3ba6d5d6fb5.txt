Job Description Visa is seeking an experienced technology risk management analyst that will work with our internal teams to assess and manage key technology risks. Specifically, the candidate will be responsible for the oversight and execution of a process designed to capture risks, ensure the correct stakeholders evaluate the risk, and then present the risks to decision-makers. The candidate will collaborate with other team members involved in the overall process and will help to ensure that risks are processed and evaluated in a timely manner. The selected candidate should have an understanding of technology risks and controls and be able to effectively collaborate with multiple stakeholders. The position reports to the Senior Director of Technology Risk (within our Strategic and Corporate Risk organization). RESPONSIBILITIES Execute the exceptions management process Follow-up with stakeholders to answer additional questions or to get clarifications Track status of exceptions, provide reporting to management and stakeholders Collaborate with other teams involved in the process (e.g. Cybersecurity, Risk Assessment, etc.) Run/facilitate governance meetings end-to-end (scheduling, execution, follow-up) Have a basic understanding of technical and cybersecurity controls (expertise not required) Provide the first level of review of exceptions to ensure sufficient information is included in requests Continuously evaluate and propose enhancements to the process Execute governance responsibilities including acting as Secretary for meetings This position is a Hybrid role is to be located in our Denver, CO office location.

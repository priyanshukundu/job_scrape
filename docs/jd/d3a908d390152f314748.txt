The AML and Fraud Prevention team works with all areas of IIS as well as other Invesco US entities and the Invesco US Legal and Compliance Department to ensure regulatory compliance. Additionally, the team monitors reports on a daily basis to proactively identify suspicious activity and fraud. Your Role In this role, you will be responsible for reviewing reports on a daily basis for suspicious activity, fraud, CIP and AML purposes. This role also supports other departments, responding to questions and reviewing accounts and paperwork to provide a risk assessment. You Will Be Responsible For: • Trains IIS associates on fraud prevention techniques. · Reviews shareholder/broker requests to evaluate risk and determine legal requirements. · Reviews all foreign account activity to determine AML risk. · Monitors for and reports attempted money laundering/suspicious activity. · Ensures compliance with the US Treasury's OFAC sanctions program and other applicable sanctions lists and programs. · Ensures compliance with the USA PATRIOT Act, including the Customer Identification Program and 314(a) searches. · Investigates reports of account takeover and fraudulent transactions and takes appropriate actions. · Reviews and assesses privacy breaches. · Responds to compliance inquiries and reviews conducted by Internal Audit and various external regulatory authorities including the SEC, US Treasury and FINRA. · Responds in a professional manner to inquiries from internal/external customers and from regulatory agencies. · Remains current on all internal/external policies and procedures that affect Invesco Investment Services. · Identifies and resolves all items/issues within standard. · Assists in the development of various procedures, forms, project plans, and policies. · Performs other duties as assigned. The Experience You Bring: 3-5 years directly related work experience in the mutual fund industry, financial services or similar experience is preferred. · Intermediate analytical and problem solving skills to analyze financial implications of transactions and to investigate and resolve problems. · Excellent organizational and prioritization skills in order to handle multiple tasks within an extremely dynamic environment. · Ability to work within defined time lines. · Determined, goal oriented individual with an accomplished attention to detail. · Proficient communicator with strong interpersonal skills to secure and manage the confidence of the company's management team. · Works well with minimal supervision.

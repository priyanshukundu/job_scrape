Understanding of NLP techniques and pipelines. Fundamental knowledge of LLMs. Strong Microsoft Office skills. Good verbal and written communication skills. Strong problem-solving ability and a proactive, curious mindset. Ability to build stakeholder management and reporting skills over time. Nice to Have Exposure to AI tools, automation platforms, or dashboarding tools such as Power BI. Experience working on end-to-end tool development or analytical delivery. Interest in business transformation, process improvement, and innovation.

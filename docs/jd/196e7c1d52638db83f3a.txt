Overview United Community is seeking a Manager of Data Engineering to lead the team that builds and operates the pipelines and platforms every analytics product in the bank depends on. This is both a leadership and a hands-on role: you will manage and mentor a team of data engineers and data platform engineers while staying close enough to the work to set technical direction, review builds, and unblock complex problems. Sitting within Enterprise Data, you will own our ingestion, transformation, and platform layer end to end, from source system connectivity and pipeline orchestration through modeled, historized data the analytics layer can consume without rework. You will partner with the analytics and data governance functions so that the data the bank relies on is accurate, timely, and traceable to its source.

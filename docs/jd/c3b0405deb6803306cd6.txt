Salary Range $129,600.00 - $213,800.00 Target Openings 1 What Is the Opportunity? Travelers is seeking an Architect to join our organization as we grow and transform our Enterprise Technology Solutions organization. Underwriters and agents count on fast, reliable tools to evaluate risk and put coverage in place for business customers. As an Architect on our underwriting technology team, you will design the systems behind that work, including a new generation of AI assisted capabilities. You will help shape the architecture of our Flow Underwriting Platform and the online portal agencies use to submit and manage business with us, including AI capabilities that read submission documents and create customer and account records automatically. Working alongside senior architects, engineers and business partners, you will take designs from first concept through production. This role offers direct exposure to enterprise architecture, AI governance and vendor evaluation, with room to grow your scope over time. What Will You Do? Scope: Design solutions from concept to production, document architecture decisions and support releases through production cutover and ongoing support. Contribute to the current and future state architecture of our underwriting platform and agent portal, aligned with enterprise technology priorities. Prepare solution designs for architecture review and AI governance before they are final, with guidance from senior architects. Support build, reuse and buy evaluations, including vendor assessments and proofs of concept, and identify chances to reuse existing capabilities. Partner with teams across business units, such as billing, specialty insurance and rapid prototyping, on integrations, shared capabilities and design fixes. Lead design discussions within your scope and take part in architecture forums to align on shared capabilities. Keep architecture documentation current so leaders and future teams can find and build on your decisions. Set architecture milestones, meet them and communicate early with a new date when risks arise. Perform other duties as assigned. What Will Our Ideal Candidate Have? Technical Expertise & Education: Bachelor’s in technology related field or equivalent with 6 years of demonstrated engineering experience or 2 to 3 years of technology architecture experience. Experience designing integrations across multiple systems using APIs and cloud services. Familiarity with AI and machine learning solutions, including document processing or AI agents, and the governance they require. Experience evaluating vendors or running proofs of concept. Knowledge & Experience of property and casualty insurance, especially underwriting. Clear writing and presentation skills, with the ability to explain technical designs to business partners. Comfort leading design conversations and guiding teams toward a decision. Hands on experience designing and documenting software solutions that reached production. Experience in the property and casualty (P&C) insurance industry, working on technology that supports insurance operations. What is a Must Have? Bachelor’s degree in computer science, related STEM field, or its equivalent in education and/or work experience. 4 additional years of architecture/software engineering experience.

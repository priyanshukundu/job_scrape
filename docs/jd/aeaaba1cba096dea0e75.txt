Senior Financial Analyst
Marqeta
Location: Remote - USA
Link: https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/marqeta-inc/ea883236-c606-4a2f-8844-1e68e2b882d1

As Marqeta’s Senior Financial Analyst, you will be the financial partner to sales teams and executive leaders for a portfolio of our customers, turning customer performance data into forecasts, deal recommendations, and clear explanations of how the business is doing. You will join the FP&A team that owns customer economics across Marqeta’s card issuing business, including deal economics for customers under your portfolio. In this role, you will forecast customer results through to gross profit, model pricing and contract negotiations, present monthly results to sales and leadership, and use SQL in Snowflake and AI tools to answer financial questions from the data and to automate how the team gets this work done.



We work Flexible First https://www.marqeta.com/blog/2022/05/10/flexible-first. This role can be performed remotely only in one of our National locations, which you can review here.




THE IMPACT YOU’LL HAVE

 - Conduct in-depth financial analysis and give executive leadership clear insights on the performance of customers within your scope.

 - Build models to forecast the performance of your customers, from top-line drivers through to gross profit, including deal models for negotiations on existing and new customer programs.

 - Communicate monthly results of customer performance to sales teams so they are prepared to manage customer relationships, including building and presenting decks and providing support files.

 - Assist cross-functional teams in building customer-facing review presentations that help customers understand the value of their relationship with Marqeta.

 - Write and maintain SQL queries in Snowflake to pull, join, and reconcile customer, transaction, and billing data, and turn them into reusable datasets and dashboards that finance and sales partners can use without asking for a new pull each time.

 - Use AI tools alongside SQL to automate recurring work such as data pulls, forecast refreshes, variance commentary, and first drafts of decks, then document what works so the wider team can adopt it.

 - Use AI tools to help answer financial questions from sales and executive leaders, such as why a customer’s gross profit moved versus forecast or how a pricing change would affect margin, and confirm each answer against the underlying data before it is shared.

 - Continuously refine finance processes for speed and accuracy, finding manual steps that can be automated and building the checks that keep automated and AI-assisted output reliable.

 - Stay current on payments industry trends and regulatory changes, and assess how they could affect Marqeta’s business and customers.
   
   


WHO YOU ARE

 - 5+ years of experience in financial analysis, with a deep focus on customer forecasting and deal modeling.

 - Bachelor’s degree in Finance, Accounting, or a related field, and a solid grasp of financial principles and their application in a dynamic business environment, including GAAP accounting practices.

 - Advanced SQL skills, ideally in Snowflake. You use SQL to pull and check the data behind customer forecasts, margin analysis, and pricing models, so you can answer finance and sales questions yourself and tie your numbers back to the financial statements.

 - Hands-on experience using AI tools in your own finance work, such as automating a recurring report, answering ad hoc financial questions, speeding up analysis, or drafting commentary, along with the judgment to check AI output before it reaches decision makers.

 - Experience using AI to support financial analytics: giving the tool the right data and business context, writing clear prompts, and testing its answers against SQL results or your source model so that the numbers you share are accurate and traceable.

 - Proven ability to manage multiple cross-functional stakeholders, particularly in supporting sales teams in deal negotiations and understanding customer performance.

 - Exceptional communication skills, with proven expertise in crafting and presenting financial decks to a diverse audience, including senior leadership.

 - Strong analytical mindset, coupled with an emphasis on accuracy and attention to detail.

 - Ability to thrive in a fast-paced setting, readily adapting to evolving business requirements.
   
   


NICE-TO-HAVES

 - Prior experience with the financial details of the card issuing space and payment networks, such as interchange, network fees, and bank partner economics.

 - Experience building automated workflows for finance, such as reusable AI prompts or agents, Python scripts, or self-serve dashboards in BI tools like Sigma.

 - Experience building governed data models in Snowflake (for example with dbt or Snowflake’s built-in AI features) so colleagues can ask questions of customer data in plain English and get consistent answers.

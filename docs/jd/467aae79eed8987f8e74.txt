Job title: Associate Director, Government Contracting and Analytics Location: Morristown, NJ About the Job The Associate Director is responsible for the compliant management of Government contracts primarily focused on the Federal Supply Schedule (FSS). This position develops the business case analysis and contracting for FSS and related voluntary strategies. The Associate Director will recommend plans of action that meet strategic objectives. This position performs financial analysis of commercial strategies and their impact on Government prices, and analysis pertaining to Government pricing regulation changes. This role also manages the Government markets gross to nets (GTN) and budget process and performs financial analysis and provides support for product acquisitions, divestitures, and launches. Join the team transforming care for people with immune challenges, rare diseases, cancers, and neurological conditions. In Specialty Care, you’ll help deliver breakthrough treatments that bring hope to patients with some of the highest unmet needs. About Sanofi We’re an R&D-driven, AI-powered biopharma company committed to improving people’s lives and delivering compelling growth. Our deep understanding of the immune system – and innovative pipeline – enables us to invent medicines and vaccines that treat and protect millions of people around the world. Together, we chase the miracles of science to improve people’s lives. Main Responsibilities: Responsible for the efficient, timely, and compliant management of the Federal Supply Schedule. Ownership of the Federal Supply Schedule, including contract renewals, requests for quotes, temporary price reductions, and modifications. Build and present business cases and financial analyses to substantiate strategic contract offers. Solicit cross-functional stakeholder approvals. Be the primary point of contact with external Federal Contracting Officers to submit offers and resolve issues. Identify and analyze risks and opportunities relative to government pricing and recommend plans of action to meet strategic objectives. Key stakeholders include Strategic Pricing, Government Pricing, Contract Development, Value & Access, Account Teams, Finance, GTN, Legal. Opportunity to assist with other Government Programs such as Medicaid Supplementals and State Pharmaceutical Assistance Programs (SPAPs). Assist Contract Development and Pricing team for assessing government program implications of commercial contracting activities and ensure the appropriate quantification of potential liability and risk. Ownership of government channel analysis of best price and tracking customer impacts. Interpret regulatory requirements and provide financial impact analysis when necessary. Support the development of corporate financials for government markets across Sanofi’s Specialty Care and General Medicines portfolio including bi-annual GTN, budget, and monthly accruals forecasting. Present during monthly GTN review meetings with Finance, as necessary. Provide contracting and analytical support for product acquisitions and divestitures. Recommend and implement process improvement measures to enhance operating performance, including driving the adoption of AI and technology solutions to enable efficiency and scalability. Work closely with various stakeholders to provide documentation, analysis and expertise in support of audit and litigation requests, as well as other cross-functional projects.

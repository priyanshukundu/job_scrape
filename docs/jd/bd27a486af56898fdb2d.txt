The Opportunity The Quantitative Modeling team is seeking a dynamic individual contributor with a skillset that includes technical quantitative modeling balanced by business knowledge and experience in auto loan acquisition, originations, pricing, approvals, and loss forecasting. The applicant should have excellent communication skills, creative application of quantitative methodologies to solve complex business problems, and the desire to work on a high performing team. The Work Itself Creation of new analysis to progress the pricing, decisioning, underwriting, and loss forecasting aspects of Ally’s auto lending business Utilization of advanced statistical and data science techniques (i.e. logistic regression, machine learning, decision trees) to develop quantitative models and tools to support the business Extraction, manipulation, management, and analysis of large datasets using Snowflake SQL, Python, and R Understanding how the business works and how the team can support the business using quantitative methodologies Clear communication of technical and business concepts with business stakeholders and model risk partners The Qualifications We’re Looking For Preferred Qualifications: 5+ years of quantitative modeling and business analytics experience preferred Master’s degree in quantitative field preferred Experience with logistic regression, decision trees, survival analysis, and loss forecasting Strong application of Python, Snowflake SQL, and R Experience with data visualization tools such as power PI Experience working in the line of business and applying advanced statistical / analytical techniques to solve complex business problems Ability to prioritize workload and balance multiple initiatives and efforts Minimum Qualifications 7+ years of relevant experience or equivalent combination of education and experience High school Diploma or GED equivalent How We'll Have Your Back Ally's compensation program offers market-competitive base pay and pay-for-performance incentives (bonuses) based on achieving personal and company goals.

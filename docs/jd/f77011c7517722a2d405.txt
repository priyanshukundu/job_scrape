Your Role Wind River, An Aptiv Company, is seeking a Senior Member of Technical Staff – Software Development in Test to develop automated tests for safety-critical embedded real-time operating systems and embedded virtualization solutions. You will combine hands-on software development, test automation, and technical leadership while collaborating with engineering management and technical authorities to support the team’s commitments and deliverables. Roles and Responsibilities Research new product features and translate technical requirements into effective validation strategies. Develop end-to-end automated tests to validate new product features and embedded system capabilities. Maintain and improve the existing automated test suite, including issue investigation and triage. Expand automated test coverage for new hardware, peripherals, and product functionality. Develop comprehensive test plans and test strategies for safety-critical embedded software. Perform manual testing when required to support product validation and release activities. Provide technical leadership, guidance, and mentoring to junior members of the engineering team. Collaborate with engineering management and technical authorities to deliver the team’s commitments according to agreed timelines. Your Background Bachelor’s degree or higher in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Software Engineering, another STEM field, or equivalent work experience. 6+ years of software development experience, including a solid understanding of Agile software development and testing methodologies. Strong programming experience with Python, C, and shell scripting. Experience with test automation frameworks such as pytest and Continuous Integration tools such as Jenkins or Travis CI. Strong knowledge of Linux, software architecture, containerization, and software configuration management tools such as Git or Bitbucket. Experience with defect-tracking tools such as Jira, combined with strong technical leadership, communication, documentation, and time-management skills.

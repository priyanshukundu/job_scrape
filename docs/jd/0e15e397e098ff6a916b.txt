The Associate, Transactional Services serves as the primary service partner for a portfolio of corporate and institutional banking clients. This role is responsible for managing client requests, account maintenance, onboarding activities, operational support, investigations, and transaction execution while delivering a high-touch service experience. Working closely with Commercial Banking Relationship Officers, the Associate acts as a trusted liaison between clients and the Bank, helping businesses navigate their day-to-day banking needs while ensuring timely resolution, operational accuracy, and exceptional client satisfaction. This hybrid position is based in the Oriental Center building in San Juan, Puerto Rico. What You'll Do As a Associate, Transactional Services, you will: Serve as the primary service contact for a portfolio of corporate and institutional clients. Build strong relationships with clients while providing proactive support and timely follow-up. Act as a liaison between clients, Relationship Officers, branches, and internal operational teams. Support account openings, onboarding activities, account maintenance, and documentation requirements. Investigate and resolve client requests, inquiries, complaints, and service-related issues. Coordinate banking transactions and account servicing activities for commercial customers. Monitor daily operational and production reports to ensure timely execution of client requests. Maintain electronic account records and documentation with a high degree of accuracy. Assist clients with cash management and deposit account-related needs. Coordinate branch visits and facilitate a seamless service experience for business customers. Support process improvement initiatives and provide operational recommendations. Collaborate across multiple banking departments to deliver efficient and effective client solutions. Manage a high volume of requests while prioritizing client needs and meeting service expectations. What We're Looking For Required Qualifications Bachelor's Degree in Business Administration, Finance, Accounting, or a related field. Minimum of two (2) years of experience in banking, customer service, operations support, sales and service, or related functions. Banking industry experience is required. Excellent verbal and written communication skills in both Spanish and English. Strong interpersonal and relationship-building skills. Ability to multitask and manage competing priorities. Proficiency in Microsoft Office applications, including Excel, Word, and Outlook. Strong organizational and problem-solving abilities. Ability to work independently while maintaining collaboration across teams. Preferred Strengths Commercial Banking or Business Banking experience. Teller, Personal Banker, Branch Operations, or Banking Service Representative experience. Experience supporting high-value clients or business customers. Cash Management, Treasury Services, or Deposit Operations exposure. Strong client relationship management skills. Experience handling investigations, escalations, or service resolutions. Customer retention and client service excellence mindset. Ability to think critically and identify practical solutions quickly. WORK AUTHORIZATION & ELEGIBILITY: Legally authorized to work in the US is required. This position is of indefinite duration and requires candidates to have permanent or ongoing work authorization.

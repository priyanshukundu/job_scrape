VP, Java & Big Data Engineering Lead – Markets Technology As Java & Big Data Engineering Lead, you will design and build the high-performance applications and data platforms that power Citi's Markets business. Sitting within Markets Technology, this role combines hands-on engineering with technical leadership, giving you the opportunity to deliver mission-critical systems that support trading, risk, regulatory, and data-driven decision-making at enterprise scale. You will stay close to the code while shaping the strategy, standards, and people behind some of Citi's most important platforms. Responsibilities Lead the design, development, and delivery of enterprise-grade applications using Java, Spring Boot, Python, and modern distributed architecture patterns. Build high-volume, low-latency applications that support front-office, risk, regulatory, and operational workflows. Develop and enhance microservices-based architectures, REST APIs, event-driven applications, and cloud-native services. Lead the development of large-scale data processing platforms that support real-time and batch analytics workloads. Design data pipelines and streaming solutions using technologies such as Apache Spark, Kafka, Flink, Hadoop, Iceberg, Delta Lake, Databricks, and Snowflake. Build data ingestion, transformation, and analytics frameworks that process billions of records efficiently. Drive cloud and platform modernization using AWS, Azure, Kubernetes, containerization, and modern DevOps practices. Mentor and coach engineers to build a strong engineering culture focused on innovation and quality. Required Qualifications & Skills You bring 6+ years of software engineering experience with hands-on expertise in Java. You hold a Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Data Science, or a related field. You have extensive experience with Spring Boot, REST APIs, microservices, and distributed systems. You write strong production code in both Java and Python. You have hands-on experience with Big Data technologies including Spark, Kafka, Iceberg, Delta Lake, Hadoop, and streaming architectures. You have designed and optimized high-throughput, large-scale data processing systems. You understand cloud platforms, Kubernetes, containerized deployments, and DevSecOps practices, and you have led engineering teams while staying actively involved in technical design and implementation within highly regulated, mission-critical environments. Beneficial Skills & Qualifications You have experience in Markets, Capital Markets, Fixed Income, Equities, Risk, Regulatory Reporting, or Financial Data Platforms. You understand Data Lakehouse architectures, real-time analytics, and AI/ML data pipelines. You have built event-driven architectures and large-scale streaming ecosystems. You bring strong performance tuning, data modeling, and distributed computing expertise. What We Offer This role gives you the chance to shape both the application and data platforms behind Citi's Markets business, combining strategic influence with genuine hands-on engineering impact. You will work in a hybrid model, spending 3 days in the office and 2 days working remotely each week. You will tackle complex, large-scale engineering problems across application and Big Data platforms, working alongside experienced technologists in a global organization. You will lead and mentor engineers, shaping standards and architecture decisions that influence the wider technology organization. You will gain access to ongoing learning and development opportunities to deepen your expertise in cloud, data, and distributed systems. You will benefit from financial wellbeing programs and resources that support your long-term goals.

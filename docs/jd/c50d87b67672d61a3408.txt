Senior System Software Engineer, Telemetry at Scale
NVIDIA
Location: US, CA, Santa Clara; US, NY, Remote; US, OR, Remote; US, WA, Remote; US, CA, Remote; US, MA, Remote
Link: https://nvidia.wd5.myworkdayjobs.com/NVIDIAExternalCareerSite/job/US-CA-Santa-Clara/Senior-System-Software-Engineer--Telemetry-at-Scale_JR2030471

Build the telemetry infrastructure behind NVIDIA’s newest GPU clusters. You will own how metrics and logs are collected, stored and queried across GPUs, networks, storage, power and cooling, giving engineers reliable data to validate systems and diagnose failures from early bring-up through production. What you’ll be doing: Develop collectors and exporters for hardware and Linux telemetry; validate metric definitions, labels, timestamps and coverage. Scale metrics and log pipelines, balancing ingestion rate, label cardinality, retention, query latency and cost. Engineer high availability, backups and recovery; detect missing, stale or dropped data and verify collection through failures and upgrades. Correlate metrics, logs, hardware topology and scheduler events to identify failure patterns and measure availability, utilization and workload impact. Provide query APIs, data exports, dashboards and actionable alerts for engineers and automated analysis. Automate deployment and testing across clusters; work with hardware, firmware and software teams to fix instrumentation and data-quality issues. What we need to see: 8+ years building and operating production infrastructure, distributed systems or data platforms, including direct ownership of telemetry services. Strong Python or Go programming, Linux debugging, and experience shipping collectors, data pipelines or infrastructure services. Hands-on experience with Prometheus or VictoriaMetrics, and a log platform such as Splunk, Loki or Elasticsearch. Ability to design metric schemas, control cardinality, write efficient queries and diagnose ingestion or query bottlenecks. Experience with containers, configuration management and CI/CD; a record of testing changes, handling incidents and explaining technical tradeoffs. BS or MS in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, Electrical Engineering or a related field, or equivalent experience. Ways to stand out from the crowd: GPU and hardware telemetry experience with DCGM, Redfish, NVLink, InfiniBand, or power and cooling sensors. Experience joining Slurm job data with metrics and logs to investigate application failures and cluster reliability. Experience with distributed time-series storage, telemetry migrations, or controlled API access for automated and AI-assisted investigations.

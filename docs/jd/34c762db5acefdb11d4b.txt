Program Evaluation Senior Associate
Guidehouse
Location: US - GA, Atlanta
Link: https://guidehouse.wd1.myworkdayjobs.com/External/job/US---GA-Atlanta/Program-Evaluation-Senior-Associate_46512

Job Family : Operational Effectiveness Travel Required : Up to 10% Clearance Required : Ability to Obtain Public Trust What You Will Do : Guidehouse is seeking a Program Evaluation Senior Associate to support the federal health clients in evaluating public health programs, measuring performance, and translating evidence into actionable improvements. The candidate will: Design and implement formative, process, outcome, impact, and other program evaluations for federal public health programs and initiatives. Apply program evaluation frameworks to assess program context, describe programs, focus evaluation questions and designs, gather credible evidence, generate conclusions, and support use of findings. Develop evaluation plans, logic models, evaluation questions, performance measures, indicators, data collection instruments, and analysis plans. Conduct and analyze quantitative and qualitative data collection, including surveys, interviews, focus groups, assessments, and document or literature reviews. Engage collaboratively with federal health programs, partners, funding recipients, and other stakeholders throughout the evaluation lifecycle. Translate evaluation findings into actionable recommendations, reports, presentations, data visualizations, and briefings that support evidence-based decision-making and continuous improvement. Apply rigorous evaluation practices that promote relevance and utility, rigor, independence and objectivity, and transparency. Independently manage assigned evaluation activities, timelines, stakeholder coordination, and client deliverables. What You Will Need : Bachelor's degree. FOUR (4) or more years of relevant experience in program evaluation, performance measurement, public health research, or a related field. Must be able to obtain and maintain a Federal Public Trust; Candidates with an active Public Trust or suitability are preferred. Experience designing and conducting program evaluations using quantitative, qualitative, and/or mixed-methods approaches. Experience developing evaluation plans, logic models, evaluation questions, performance measures, data collection instruments, and/or analytic approaches. Experience analyzing and synthesizing data and translating findings into actionable, client-ready recommendations and deliverables. Strong analytical, project management, stakeholder engagement, and written and verbal communication skills. What Would Be Nice To Have : Master's degree in Public Health, Program Evaluation, Epidemiology , or a related field. Experience supporting federal public health agencies (i.e., CDC). Knowledge of and experience applying agency-specific evaluation frameworks (e.g., CDC Program Evaluation Framework). Experience evaluating federal grants, cooperative agreements, or state, local, tribal, and territorial public health programs. Experience providing evaluation capacity building or technical assistance. Experience with tools such as R, SAS, SPSS, Python, NVivo, MAXQDA, Power BI, or Tableau.

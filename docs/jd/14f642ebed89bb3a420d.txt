The Associate supports the Talent Management and Skills team in turning agreed priorities into well-planned, high-quality learning and development experiences. The role has a primary focus on Skills at BNY and the Talent Accelerator Programs, partnering with the respective program leads on curriculum, facilitation, project coordination, event readiness, and continuous improvement. Support Across the Talent Management and Skills Team Program planning and project management: Build and maintain workplans, calendars, milestones, agendas, and action trackers. Coordinate dependencies, keep deliverables moving, and flag risks or readiness gaps early. Learning design and facilitation: Develop and refine workshop content, session objectives, facilitator guides, participant resources, and activities. Facilitate or co-facilitate sessions for employees and managers in virtual and in-person settings. Event and session coordination: Coordinate logistics and prepare for workshops, showcases, and program sessions. Align with facilitators, speakers, site contacts, and other stakeholders on roles, timing, technology, venue needs, communications, and materials. Branding and participant materials: Partner with the Branding team and program leads to coordinate branded swag and other program materials, ensuring they are ready for events and consistent with the experience. Measurement and continuous improvement: Track participation and feedback, summarize insights, and work with the program leads to apply lessons to future sessions and experiences. Team-wide support: Contribute to other Talent Management and Skills priorities by preparing presentations, communications, and resources; coordinating stakeholder input; and keeping plans, decisions, and follow-up actions organized. What It Takes to Be Successful Skills at BNY Own It: Take accountability for assigned work, follow through on commitments, and raise questions or risks early. Be Client-Obsessed: Design and deliver learning and program experiences that are clear, practical, inclusive, and responsive to the needs of participants, facilitators, and stakeholders. Thrive Together: Build dependable working relationships, communicate openly, and coordinate effectively across program leads, business partners, facilitators, and site teams. Additional Skills Learning design and facilitation: Develop useful learning content and lead engaging sessions for different audiences and formats. Project and event coordination: Organize multiple moving parts, track details and deadlines, and keep people prepared and informed. Communication and presentation: Write clearly and create polished, accurate materials that help people understand what is needed and what happens next. Judgment and adaptability: Balance consistency with audience needs, respond calmly when plans change, and know when to resolve an issue or escalate it.

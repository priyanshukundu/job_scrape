Job Description A high-impact role at the intersection of corporate risk, financial strategy, insurance markets, and enterprise partnership. Our Company aspires to be a premier research-intensive biopharmaceutical organization, working at the forefront of research to deliver innovative health solutions that advance the prevention and treatment of disease in people and animals. In this role, you will work on significant global risks facing a leading multinational biopharmaceutical company while contributing to the advancement of the Corporate Insurance & Risk Management function. We are seeking an intellectually curious and commercially minded professional who combines strong risk management and financial fundamentals with sound judgment and initiative. This role blends enterprise partnership, insurance market engagement, financial analysis, problem-solving, and innovation. It is well suited for someone seeking to deepen their risk management expertise, tackle complex business challenges, and contribute to initiatives that strengthen organizational capabilities, productivity, and business outcomes. What You Will Do Develop a strong understanding of the business and evolving risk environment and help shape the organization's approach to risk management and insurance. Manage assigned insurance programs from strategy and design through placement, implementation, and ongoing oversight. Partner with stakeholders across multiple areas of the organization, as well as external brokers, insurers, legal counsel, and business leaders, on significant global risk decisions. Oversee internal financial models and technology applications used to collect, report, and analyze global exposures. Lead special projects and contribute to a collaborative, high-accountability team environment. Identify practical opportunities to leverage data, automation, and emerging technologies, including AI, to improve decision-making, productivity, and business outcomes. Key Areas of Responsibility Financial and Risk Analysis Assess and model property, casualty, business interruption, and other insurable exposures. Identify risk and exposure trends and recommend effective risk-transfer and risk-financing strategies. Evaluate financial and risk trade-offs among alternative insurance structures. Recommend practical solutions that balance cost, coverage, and risk mitigation. Insurance Program Oversight Oversee global property insurance programs, including construction-related coverage. Oversee casualty insurance programs, including:General liability Clinical trial liability U.S. automobile liability Workers' compensation Support insurance renewal activities, including:Exposure data collection Coordination with brokers and insurers Underwriting presentation preparation Placement support and negotiations Pricing, capacity, and coverage analysis Placement recommendations Verify that issued insurance policies accurately reflect agreed-upon terms and conditions. Deliver high-quality work within established deadlines. Risk Management Information and Data Own the processes and technology platforms used to collect and analyze global property values. Lead annual global data collection efforts across sites and markets. Coordinate stakeholder communications, data validation, variance analysis, reporting, and corporate adjustments. Recommend system improvements and translate exposure data into actionable business insights. Advancement of AI and Technology Evaluate opportunities to apply artificial intelligence and other technologies to improve productivity, analytical capabilities, and decision support. Support implementation of fit-for-purpose technology solutions, including agentic AI where appropriate. Ensure appropriate consideration of: Data quality Security Governance Human oversight Claims and Recovery Help maximize insurance recoveries for assigned insurance lines. Coordinate activities across business functions, local entities, brokers, insurers, internal and external legal counsel, and third-party administrators. Analyze insurance coverage issues and monitor significant claims matters. Provide concise management updates regarding recovery activities. Work with external actuaries to assess loss development and recommend reserve levels. Business Partnership Serve as a trusted advisor to divisional and functional teams regarding insurance implications of operational and transaction-related risks. Support:Vendor agreements Acquisitions Licensing transactions Business development initiatives Integration activities Partner with internal and external subject matter experts on loss-prevention initiatives. Communicate program status and recommendations to senior management. Planning and Reporting Monitor performance against budget and forecast assumptions for assigned insurance lines. Maintain planning and forecasting models. Analyze key drivers of financial variances. Support clear, decision-focused reporting to management. What You Bring Required Qualifications Bachelor’s degree. Minimum of three (3) years of relevant experience in risk management, finance, analytics, or a related business discipline. Strong financial and quantitative analysis skills, including advanced Excel proficiency and the ability to translate analysis into practical business recommendations. Demonstrated ability to manage complex responsibilities, balance competing priorities, meet deadlines, and operate independently using sound judgment. Excellent written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to communicate complex topics clearly and effectively to multiple audiences. Strong attention to detail and commitment to high-quality execution. Ability to build productive relationships and influence business partners, brokers, insurers, and external stakeholders. Intellectual curiosity and a demonstrated willingness to learn and explore innovative approaches to business challenges. Preferred Qualifications MBA, CFA, CPCU, ARM, or another relevant finance, insurance, or risk management credential. Experience with commercial property and casualty insurance programs, risk financing, claims management, or risk analytics. Experience leading projects or process improvement initiatives across multiple functions and geographies. Experience using technology, artificial intelligence, data visualization, business intelligence, or workflow tools to improve analysis, processes, and decision-making.

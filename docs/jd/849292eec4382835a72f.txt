This position facilitates the creation and maintenance of performance measures, reports and analyses that provide corporate and business area leaders with the decision support information needed to achieve their goals. Works collaboratively with other team members and departments on performance management processes and supports various process improvement projects. Essential Functions Designs, builds and maintains performance reporting processes used to ensure updated business results are available throughout the year. Collaborates with external business partners and internal team members to obtain performance data and information, keeping all reporting tools updated throughout the year. Coordinates with business contacts to ensure timely submission of complete and accurate information, taking proactive steps to resolve issues encountered. Streamlines data collection routines used for metrics, minimizing manual processes and duplicative work. Creates and maintains appropriate documentation for performance metrics and reporting processes to enable cross-training and business continuity. Prepares supporting analysis, reports and/or presentations for improvement initiatives, regulatory data/information requests, support for operational decision making, and the annual planning processes. Conducts research to identify best practices and performance comparisons, involving utilization of existing external benchmarking resources. Proactively learns about the company, industry and in particular the business areas being supported to understand, validate and communicate performance results and progress of business initiatives. Qualifications Technical Knowledge/Skill: Ability to design, build, analyze and interpret data models (relational tables or databases) used in the measurement and explanation of business performance. Advanced conceptual and analytical capabilities, including experience with Excel, Power BI & other reporting tools, as well as work with IT system extracts to build and modify reports, analytics and databases. Detail orientation to ensure data quality and accuracy. Excellent interpersonal and communication (verbal and written) skills. Must be able to work collaboratively with internal and external parties of varying levels. Demonstrated self-directed work ethic and abilities, capable of taking lead on action items or initiatives. Ability to recognize and work with sensitive and confidential information. Education: Bachelor’s Degree in Information Technology, Engineering, Business Administration, Finance, Accounting, Economics, or Mathematics, related discipline or equivalent experience. Experience : One (1) plus years related experience.

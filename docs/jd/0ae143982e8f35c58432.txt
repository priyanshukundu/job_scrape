Job Description Summary We are seeking an experienced Senior Software Engineer to join our team in Haifa. The role focuses on designing and developing high-quality, performance-critical software in modern C++ for complex healthcare systems. The ideal candidate brings strong hands-on C++ expertise, solid software engineering fundamentals, and the ability to work effectively across software, hardware, product, and verification teams. Experience in embedded or hardware-integrated systems is a strong advantage. Job Description Responsibilities · Design, develop, and maintain robust software components in modern C++. · Own features and technical tasks from requirements and design through implementation, integration, testing, and release. · Participate in software architecture, technical design discussions, and code reviews. · Develop reliable multithreaded and performance-sensitive software. · Investigate complex software and system issues, identify root causes, and implement sustainable solutions. · Integrate software with hardware, devices, algorithms, and other system components. · Write clean, maintainable, testable, and well-documented code. · Collaborate with cross-functional teams, including system engineering, AI/algorithm, product, and verification. · Mentor engineers and contribute to engineering practices and knowledge sharing. Mandatory Skills and Experience · B.Sc. or M.Sc. in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Electrical Engineering, or a related field, or equivalent relevant experience. · 5+ years of hands-on software development experience, with strong expertise in C++. · Strong understanding of object-oriented design, software architecture, design patterns, and clean-code principles. · Experience developing multithreaded applications and troubleshooting concurrency issues. · Experience with performance analysis, debugging, and optimization of complex software systems. · Experience with unit testing, code reviews, source control, and standard software development practices. · Excellent analytical and problem-solving skills, with strong attention to quality and detail. · Strong communication skills and demonstrated ability to work effectively in a collaborative environment. Bonus Skills · Hands-on experience in embedded software, real-time systems, or hardware-integrated products. · Experience with hardware/software interfaces, device communication, drivers, or low-level system components. · Experience working in Windows development environments and with Visual Studio. · Experience with C# in desktop, tooling, or system-level applications. · Knowledge of operating-system concepts, networking, inter-process communication, or Windows internals. · Experience with CI/CD pipelines, automated testing, and modern development workflows. · Experience working in Agile environments such as Scrum or Kanban. · Experience in medical devices, healthcare software, medical imaging, or regulated development environments. · Familiarity with medical standards or protocols such as DICOM or HL7.

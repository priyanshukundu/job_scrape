The Impact You will have in this role : As an Business Analysis Senior Associate - FICC , you will serve as a key liaison between business stakeholders and technology teams supporting critical clearing and settlement applications across FICC. You will translate complex business needs into clear technology requirements, help drive solutions through the software development lifecycle, and ensure that technology changes meet business, operational, and regulatory needs. The role will work closely with Product Owners, business SMEs, developers, QA/testing teams, and other technology partners to deliver high-quality solutions in a complex financial services environment. Your Primary Responsibilities : Partner with business stakeholders, Product Owners, technology teams, and subject matter experts to understand business objectives, processes, pain points, and technology needs. Elicit, analyze, document, and validate functional and non-functional requirements for technology initiatives supporting FICC. Translate business needs into clear business requirements, functional requirements, user stories, use cases, process flows, and acceptance criteria . Facilitate requirements-gathering sessions, workshops, walkthroughs, and solution discussions with business and technology stakeholders. Work with Product Owners to refine and prioritize the product backlog and ensure requirements are ready for development. Create and maintain user stories and requirements within JIRA , ensuring appropriate traceability from business requirements through implementation. Collaborate with developers and solution/technical teams to clarify requirements and evaluate proposed solutions and design approaches. Identify requirement gaps, dependencies, risks, assumptions, and potential impacts to upstream and downstream applications. Analyze existing business processes and system functionality and recommend opportunities for simplification, automation, or process improvement. Support technology teams throughout design, development, SIT , defect resolution, and implementation. Partner with business and QA teams to develop test scenarios and ensure appropriate coverage of business requirements. Participate in Agile ceremonies including backlog refinement, sprint planning, daily stand-ups, retrospectives, and demonstrations. Maintain clear documentation and effectively communicate project status, requirements, risks, decisions, and dependencies to stakeholders. Build a strong understanding of FICC business processes and the applications and data flows supporting clearing and settlement activities. **NOTE: The Primary Responsibilities of this role are not limited to the details above. ** Qualifications: Bachelor’s degree preferred or equivalent experience. 6 years of related experience in Business Analysis, Business Systems Analysis, Technology Business Analysis, or a related technology delivery role. Experience working within the software development lifecycle (SDLC) and Agile delivery environments. Hands-on experience gathering and documenting complex business and functional requirements. Experience developing user stories, acceptance criteria, use cases, process flows, and business requirements documentation. Experience with JIRA or comparable Agile/project-management tools. Financial services experience is strongly preferred. Experience with clearing, settlement, capital markets, securities processing, or financial market infrastructure is highly desirable.

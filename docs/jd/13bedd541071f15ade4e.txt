You will be responsible for overseeing activities related to electrophysiology and cardiac rhythm management within the Global Data Science & Analytics team. You will lead and execute data analytics projects that generate impactful real-world evidence and insights related to medical device technology, relevant disease states, and healthcare economics. In addition, you will support digital health initiatives. You will work with different types of real-world data, developing creative ways to analyze and present your results. As a leader of the GDSA team, you will collaborate with internal and external stakeholders to identify business needs, develop strategy, identify appropriate data sources and analytic methods, and execute projects on specified timelines. You will lead a cross-functional team of clinical researchers, analysts, data scientists, and data engineers in project planning and execution. You will interact with key opinion leaders in the healthcare space, including physicians, regulators, and payors. Your work will support healthcare decisions by patients, physicians, health authorities, payers, and policy makers. Responsibilities Collaborate with counterparts across functional domains to understand business needs and develop evidence generation strategy, to support evidence generation across the product lifecycle through the appropriate use of real-world data. Lead the design and execution of observational studies utilizing epidemiologic, biostatistical, and causal inference methods to address clinical, regulatory, and business questions. Understand and analyze varied healthcare data types, including electronic health records, remote device monitoring data, and insurance claims data. Serve as a subject matter expert for real-world evidence studies across the business. Partner with leaders in clinical affairs, regulatory, medical affairs and marketing for project selection and prioritization. Support the development of real-world evidence strategies for regulatory submissions, post-market surveillance activities, and payer evidence generation. Communicate with regulatory agencies and payer organizations on real-world studies. Cultivate relationships with key opinion leaders in the healthcare industry. Prepare and oversee preparation of conference presentations and scientific manuscripts. Prepare and oversee preparation of real-world evidence protocols and reports for regulatory agencies. Support digital health and data science initiatives that deliver clinical and business insights. Manage timelines and deliverables for a portfolio of projects. Serve as a functional manager to scientists and analysts.

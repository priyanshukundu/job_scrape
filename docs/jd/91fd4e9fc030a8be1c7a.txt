Senior Risk Mitigation Leader

Job Description Summary Essential Functions / Responsibilities: Responsible for driving operational excellence by steering the Inquiry to Order (ITO) teams and a strong risk review process. Lead complex negotiations to grow capabilities and efficiency of bid and customer-facing teams to enhance competitiveness in all markets. Drive change through adaptation, improved rigor and quality of deal making and -closing to ensure better customer outcomes while protecting the company. Own the Commercial Operation and respective Risk Review process and function ensuring consistent operations in the ITO phase, adherent to policies, processes and metrics. Steer teams through bid build, risk assessment and mitigation, negotiations and escalations to ensure top bid quality and risk / reward balance. Build and operate “Go! & Deal Desk 1-4” for Power Transmission-region, while driving early engagement and strategy development with other Business Line region teams for faster decision making. Take corrective action, based on LEAN tools, via post-mortem, win-loss analysis and customer feedback, to support word-class offer quality. Negotiate complex contracts terms, while mapping customer needs and understanding Grid Solutions key quality goals and strategic imperatives. Drive empowerment through standardized or deal-specific Terms & Conditions, engagement with GSMM, and people capability development. Negotiate Consortium Agreements with Partners and help drive the qualification of partners, working alongside Sales and Legal. Establish training and on-the-job coaching to ensure an optimal level of proficiency and knowledge of customer-facing teams in risk and negotiation. Lead change management, interface with all functions (i.e., Sales, Tendering, Legal, Finance & Operations, and also with other Grid Solutions Business Lines) to optimize sales solutions. Maintain knowledge of market trends and actions, customer requirements, and customer base, along with local particularities of sub-markets across region. Job Description Qualifications / Requirements: Bachelor’s degree or equivalent in Engineering, Business or related. Ten (10) years of experience in Commercial Operations and Risk Mitigation within the energy industry. Work experience to include: Five (5) years of experience in: negotiation of complex contracts including commercial, financial, and legal terms; Supporting at least two (2) Engineering, Procurement, and Construction (EPC) projects involving equipment, software solutions, or systems and services; Analyzing market trends, customer requirements, and customer base to drive strategy decision-making; SPEAK, GO Process, or RPO (Remaining Performance Obligation) policy; Applying Delegation of Authority (DoA) framework to support critical risk management (RAM) and delegation of risk approvals; and Continuous improvement through Lean methodology and participation in Kaizen problem solving events. Any and all experience may be gained concurrently. This position allows for a hybrid work arrangement. Candidate may work remotely 2 days per week, within 15 miles of company location in Clearwater, FL. Approximately 20% domestic travel required to visit unanticipated customer sites for proposals management, deal negotiations, and risk review throughout commercial processes.

THE ROLE The Investment Data Management Office is seeking a Senior Investment Data Engineer with strong SQL and data quality assurance experience to support the implementation, testing, and maintenance of modern data engineering and analytics solutions. This role will contribute to the development, validation, enhancement, and support of investment data platforms and pipelines while partnering closely with data architects, platform engineers, investment teams, and vendor partners. The ideal candidate brings a strong QA mindset, advanced SQL skills, and experience validating complex data transformations and pipelines. Are you a hands-on and detail-oriented individual interested in financial instruments, investment data, and analytics? Do you enjoy solving complex data challenges and building scalable data solutions? Are you interested in working across a broad range of traditional and alternative asset classes while collaborating with business and technology teams in a fast-paced environment? PRINCIPAL RESPONSIBILITIES: Develop and maintain data models in dbt within Snowflake, implementing business logic and adhering to established data standards and architectural principles. Design, develop, and execute SQL-based data validation and QA testing for data pipelines, transformations, data models, and warehouse solutions. Develop complex SQL queries to validate data accuracy, completeness, consistency, and integrity across source and target systems. Perform data reconciliation, regression testing, root-cause analysis, and validation of ETL/ELT processes and business rules. Contribute to automated data quality and testing capabilities, including validation of dbt models and transformation pipelines. Assist in improving data quality, resiliency, controls, monitoring, and operational efficiency across the investment data platform. Troubleshoot data and system issues, identify root causes, and support resolution efforts in partnership with engineering and business teams. Partner with engineers, platform leads, architects, and business stakeholders to define test scenarios, acceptance criteria, and data validation requirements. Support integration and testing of data ingestion, transformation, orchestration, and validation tools within the unified data platform. Provide operational and production support, including validation and troubleshooting during unexpected outages or incidents. JOB REQUIREMENTS: Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science or related discipline. 3–5+ years of experience in data engineering, data quality assurance, data testing, or supporting data-oriented applications and platforms. Strong hands-on SQL experience, including advanced SQL concepts and the ability to develop complex queries for data validation, reconciliation, and troubleshooting. Demonstrated experience performing QA/testing of data pipelines, ETL/ELT processes, data warehouses, and/or analytics platforms. Experience validating data transformations, business rules, source-to-target mappings, data completeness, accuracy, and integrity. Experience developing, maintaining, or testing data models using dbt (Data Build Tool). Experience with Snowflake and/or other cloud-native databases is preferred. Development or testing experience with cloud-based PaaS platforms such as Microsoft Azure, Google GCP, or Amazon AWS. Familiarity with Agile SDLC, QA methodologies, DevOps practices, CI/CD processes, and automated testing. Exposure to financial services, investment management, or investment data domains is preferred. Strong analytical, problem-solving, interpersonal, and communication skills. PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS Experience working within the asset management industry and investment data domain. Understanding of financial instruments, investment data, transactions, positions, and multi-asset investment platforms. Certifications in Snowflake, dbt, cloud data engineering technologies, or related data platforms are a plus. Interest in emerging AI technologies and understanding of how AI-driven tools can improve engineering workflows, operational efficiency, and analytics processes. Experience using automation or AI-assisted development tools to support continuous improvement initiatives is beneficial.

We offer corporate and investment banking, treasury management, as well as research and advisory services to clients around the world. #bmocapitalmarkets Role Summary The Director, Non-Financial Risk Assessment Programs leads how Capital Markets carries out BMO's main non-financial risk identification and assessment programs: PRC (Process, Risk & Control) and Risk ID inventories and RCSA (Risk & Control Self-Assessment) and RCRAs (Regulatory Compliance Risk Assessment). The Director is the main point of coordination between the first line (1LOD) Business Control teams and the second line (2LOD) enterprise program owners. They make sure assessments are accurate, defensible, delivered on time, and meet ONFR and ECP requirements. The Director brings a deep understanding of how risks, controls, processes, products, business units and legal entities connect across Capital Markets. They advocate with enterprise program owners for structures and methodologies that fit the business. The role improves inventory quality, makes execution consistent across business units (BUs), reduces the workload on Business Controls and Business Units and improves portfolio level assessments Key accountabilities 1. Inventory governance and PRC management Own year-round governance and data-quality oversight of BU PRC inventories (products and services, internal activities, processes, specific risks and controls). Lead the annual PRC Inventory Confirmation. Manage organizational and ownership changes, including BU realignments, lift-and-shifts, new inventory builds, MCC/ME updates and inventory rationalization. Monitor inventory health through Risk Mart and Power BI. Find missing attributes, linkages and data-quality issues, then drive remediation and escalate unresolved gaps. Make sure applicable Master Common Controls (MCCs) and shared controls are adopted correctly in BU control inventories, including SOX designations. 2. Capital Markets risk structure and cross-program linkages Act as the authoritative interpreter of PRC, RCSA, RCRA for Capital Markets. Act as Capital Markets' expert on how products and services, processes, risks, controls, business units and legal entities connect, and keep that view current as the business and organization change. Advocate with the enterprise program owner for the data structure, taxonomy and linkage design that best fits Capital Markets, including how KRIs, Issues and Events connect to PRC risk and control records. Give business requirements and practical input on changes to the GRCE data model, workflow and future-state integration, including aggregation at the Legal Entity level. Make sure Capital Markets inventories apply the agreed linkage standards consistently. This includes keeping Issues linked to Key Controls complete and accurate Find structural gaps or misalignments, such as missing, orphaned or inconsistent linkages, and bring them to the program owner with recommended fixes. Use the linked data to support RCSA residual risk ratings, overrides and Targeted Risk Evaluations (TREs) with evidence, and to show where control weaknesses, KRI breaches and loss events cluster. 3. Risk assessment execution (RCSA and RCRA) Lead the PRC/RCSA/RCRA Annual Plan. Oversee risk categorization (key vs. non-key) and ensure assessments are right sized taking a risk based approach. Oversee reviews of TREs, including inherent risk, control effectiveness, residual risk, overrides and rationales. Ensure High Residual Risk ratings have corrective actions to improve control effectiveness. Lead RCRA execution and work with Business Regulatory Control teams to maintain and accurate inventory of risk evaluation points (REPs). Reconcile RCRA results with the regulatory and privacy TREs in the RCSA so regulatory compliance risk feeds into the BU risk profile. Manage slippage and deferral requests, and exception and exemption requests, in line with Directive requirements. Coordinate Effective Challenge from Operational Risk Officers (OROs), Risk Specialty Areas (RSAs), Enterprise Control Functions (ECFs) and other programs. Track comments, how they're resolved, and due dates. 4. Advocacy with 2LOD program owners Act as Capital Markets' lead advocate with the 2LOD program owners (ONFR, ECP and Regulatory Compliance, and Risk Specialty Areas). Represent the business's interests in methodology, Policy, Standard & Procedure changes. Influence program design so requirements suit Capital Markets' products, trading activities and legal entity structure and aid towards reducing risk. Negotiate realistic timelines, scope and sequencing for program deliverables. Prepare well- supported rationale for exceptions, exemptions or alternative approaches when they're justified. Bring the business's perspective, practical constraints and lessons learned to enterprise working groups and consultations. Share upcoming program changes with Director, Risk Programs for inclusion in broader communications across Capital Markets. Build trusted, credible relationships with 2LOD so issues are resolved collaboratively and escalated only when needed. 6. Reporting and executive enablement Produce AE summary materials and approval packages, and keep approval evidence in GRCE. Provide risk profile insights and committee reporting (for example, VRC). 7. Continuous improvement and future-state capability Lead improvements to methodology, process, reporting and systems with ONFR. Standardize tools, templates and procedures. Represent Capital Markets in working groups, user acceptance testing (UAT) and platform evaluations to shape GRCE and future assessment capabilities. 8. People leadership Lead, develop and set priorities for team members and Business Control teams across concurrent assessment cycles. Build a culture of accountability, data quality and practical, business-friendly risk partnership. Key relationships 1LOD: Business Controls, Risk Domain Leads, Business Unit Leads, Accountable Executives, Operational Resilience & Risk Programs, Portfolio Analytics, Capital Markets Assurance Role, Enterprise Control Functions 2LOD: ONFR and ECP program & methodology owners, OROs, RSAs, Regulatory Compliance and Privacy Qualifications 10+ years in operational or non-financial risk, risk and control self-assessment, or governance at a large financial institution, including people leadership. Capital Markets experience strongly preferred. Deep understanding of how Capital Markets products, processes, risks, controls, business units and legal entities relate, and the ability to turn that into clear structural requirements for enterprise program owners. Deep knowledge of the three lines of defence model, risk and control taxonomies, residual risk methods and Effective Challenge. Proven ability to influence and negotiate with 2LOD and enterprise program owners, representing business interests credibly and constructively. Hands-on experience with a GRC platform (Archer preferred) and with data and reporting tools (Power BI, Risk Mart). Track record of leading enterprise programs on regulatory deadlines and producing audit- ready, AE-ready and committee-ready materials. Strong judgment and executive presence, and the ability to escalate issues diplomatically with senior stakeholders.

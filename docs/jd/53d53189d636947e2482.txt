Job Description Summary The North America Customer Portfolio Leader is responsible for the comprehensive portfolio optimization and strategic direction of 12,000 wind turbine generators, generating $1 billion in revenue across 400 FSA contracts. Serves as the primary escalation point for complex customer disputes, spearheads liquidated damage offsets and settlements, and leads the renegotiation and renewal strategy for contracts valued at over $6B in backlog. High level of commercial acumen and strategic judgment required to shape long-term strategies, influence broad customer portfolios, and drive superior financial outcomes. Leadership over high-stakes contract renewals, dispute resolution, and commercial risk assessment. Impacts the business unit's ability to achieve financial, service, and operational objectives while operating under commercial policy objectives. Possesses high autonomy to execute commercial arrangements, navigate non-standard problems, and coordinate across internal functions and external sources to establish best practices and deliver elevated customer service. This role is responsible for the execution, optimization and escalation of North America services contracts. The individual will lead a team of individuals who will work closely with customers and internal stakeholders to achieve the best outcome for our customers and GE Vernova. Job Description Roles and Responsibilities: Owns the optimization of 12,000 WTG, $1B revenue and 400 FSA contracts. Escalation point for customer disputes and resolutions Responsible to resolve liquidated damage offsets and settlements Owns the renegotiation and renewal strategy of contracts valued at $6B+ in backlog. Requires strong commercial awareness and is expected to influence the development of strategy for their broad customer portfolio Interprets simple internal and external business challenges and recommends best practices to improve financial outcomes or customer service. Stays informed of industry trends that may inform work. Uses high level of judgment to make decisions and handle complex tasks or problems within projects, product lines, markets, disputes, campaigns or customers issues. Has ability to assess quality of information given and ask pertinent questions to stakeholders or customers. Able to offer new solutions to problems outside of set parameters and is able to construct and provide recommendations.

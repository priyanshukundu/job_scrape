About this role: Wells Fargo is seeking a forward-thinking, hands-on Software Engineering Senior Manager to lead the modernization of our banker-facing servicing platforms. This role will operate in an AI-native engineering environment, leveraging AI-powered capabilities to accelerate delivery, modernize technology, and reimagine how bankers serve customers. It offers a unique opportunity to shape the future of both customer and employee experiences at one of the world’s leading financial institutions. In this role, you will: Manage, coach, and develop teams of engineers and engineering leaders responsible for delivering high-quality capabilities using modern engineering practices and technologies. Lead the modernization of banker-facing servicing platforms, applying modern architecture, cloud, automation, and AI-native engineering practices to improve developer productivity, solution quality, and speed to market . Provide strong technical leadership and remain close to the engineering work, including solution design, code and technical reviews, engineering quality, architecture alignment, and resolution of complex technical challenges. Partner with architects, product leaders, and senior engineers to advance technology strategy and next-generation architecture, including application modernization, cloud readiness, APIs, data, security, resiliency, and scalability. Champion AI-assisted and agentic engineering capabilities , identifying opportunities to use AI across the software development lifecycle to accelerate delivery, improve quality, automate engineering activities, and create business value. Lead execution across the software development lifecycle, removing technical roadblocks, managing technology risk, and fostering a culture of experimentation, continuous learning, DevOps, and engineering excellence. Manage people and financial resources, third-party partnerships, and delivery commitments while ensuring investments align with strategic technology and business objectives.

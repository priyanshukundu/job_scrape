We have an exciting and rewarding opportunity for you to take your software engineering career to the next level. As a Automation Testing Software Engineer III at JPMorganChase within the Consumer and Community Bank- Business Banking, you serve as a seasoned member of an agile team to design and deliver trusted market-leading technology products in a secure, stable, and scalable way. You are responsible for carrying out critical technology solutions across multiple technical areas within various business functions in support of the firm’s business objectives. The Automation Engineer will design, build, and maintain automated test solutions that improve product quality and delivery speed, with a primary focus on digital accessibility/ADA compliance testing and test automation engineering. The ideal candidate has strong QA engineering fundamentals, hands-on automation experience, familiarity with desk check practices and modern QA processes, and of supporting full-stack engineering capabilities to contribute to accessible user interfaces, backend services, and end-to-end quality improvements. Job responsibilities Design, develop, and maintain scalable automated test frameworks (UI, API, integration, regression). Create and execute automated test suites aligned to product requirements, risk, and release timelines. Partner with developers, product owners, and QA to define acceptance criteria and test strategies early in the SDLC, and contribute targeted frontend and backend code changes that improve accessibility, testability, and automated coverage. Perform and document desk checks (e.g., peer review of test cases/automation code, requirements walkthroughs, defect prevention reviews) to catch issues early. Support ADA / accessibility testing by validating accessibility requirements and integrating accessibility checks into QA workflows where possible; collaborate on remediation of accessible UI components, semantic markup, keyboard interactions, and focus management. Analyze test failures, troubleshoot root causes across frontend components, backend services, APIs, and data flows, and drive defects to resolution with clear reproduction steps and evidence. Improve CI/CD quality gates by integrating automated testing into pipelines and reporting results. Maintain test data, test environments, and test documentation to ensure repeatable, reliable testing. Track and report quality metrics (coverage, pass rates, defect trends) and recommend improvements. Proficiency in at least one programming language used in automation (e.g., Java, Python, JavaScript/TypeScript, C#), with working knowledge of HTML, CSS, JavaScript/TypeScript, and a modern frontend framework (e.g., React or Angular) to support accessible UI development and testing. Experience with test automation tools/frameworks (e.g., Selenium, Playwright). Strong understanding of QA methodologies, test design techniques, and defect lifecycle management. Experience testing APIs (REST/JSON), including validation of contracts, schemas, and edge cases; working knowledge of backend service development, frontend-to-API integration, and SQL/data validation to support end-to-end automation and defect remediation.

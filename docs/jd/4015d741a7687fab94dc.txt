Position Purpose: The Field Capital Business Analyst position is responsible for providing dedicated support to Home Depot Capital Projects through project readiness, driving execution and issue management/resolution. This position will be responsible for supporting field execution teams through project life cycle including: readiness, material and labor order entry/management, communication to responsible parties, issue resolution prior to and during execution, and project status. The Field Capital Business Analyst will be responsible for generating reports on project health for in-progress and completed projects, processing project deliverables, and finalizing all closeout procedures. Key Responsibilities: 25% -Review store specific project plans to create and validate all material orders to support specific project; react to plan changes to ensure orders are updated to support changes; manage tracking and shipping information to ensure materials arrive on location to support project execution. 25% Through direction of Field Capital leadership enter and manage labor orders for 3rd Party Suppliers and Installers supporting project execution. Position will be responsible for managing and closing out all labor orders supporting specific projects. 25% Serve as liaison between field execution teams and cross-functional business partners to ensure clear communication and follow up driving project readiness prior to project execution. 25% Manage and drive issue resolution for opportunities that arise before and during project execution. Position will need to partner with appropriate groups to resolve issues in a timely manner to ensure minimal impact to project rollout.

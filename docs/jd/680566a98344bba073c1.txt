The Opportunity At USAA, technology plays a critical role in delivering on our mission to serve the military community and their families. Working alongside experienced engineers, you'll help develop automation, improve platform operations, support production environments, and contribute to solutions that enhance reliability, scalability, and the developer experience across USAA's OpenShift ecosystem. This role is ideal for an engineer who enjoys learning, troubleshooting complex technical challenges, and building practical solutions that create lasting impact. Whether your background is in software development, cloud engineering, or platform operations, you'll have the opportunity to grow your skills in, automation, and large-scale platform engineering while demonstrating USAA's behaviors of taking ownership, working horizontally across teams, and acting decisively to drive outcomes that matter.

About the Role
As a Data Engineer on our Natural Gas team, you will be dedicated to building and scaling a brand-new intelligence product covering the US natural gas markets. Operating within our backend engineering squad, you will expand our data acquisition capabilities, optimize high-throughput data distribution, and support the development of tailored analytics. Your work will directly equip global energy traders and market analysts with reliable, real-time insights into North American gas flows.

 Key Responsibilities Scale and structure data acquisition: Codify, structure, expand, and maintain robust data scrapers and ingestion pipelines to capture US natural gas market datasets. Enrich product capabilities: Collaborate across the team to design and ship new data features and tailored analytics for our expanding natural gas offering. Optimize API performance and distribution: Ensure high availability, speed, and reliability across our RESTful APIs to distribute datasets seamlessly to end users. Build observability and alerting tools: Develop and enhance monitoring dashboards and proactive alerting systems to safeguard data integrity and pipeline health. Shape sustainable product roadmaps: Partner closely with Product Managers, Engineering Managers, Analysts, and DevOps teams to build ambitious yet sustainable technical plans . Experience & Background What you'll need (Must-haves) Data engineering foundation: Circa 3–5 years of experience as a data-focused Software Engineer building and maintaining production data pipelines. Strong Python & API expertise: Significant experience working with Python (FastAPI is a plus) alongside proficiency in building and consuming RESTful APIs. Database design & time-series data: Hands-on experience with PostgreSQL or similar data stores, encompassing database design, data normalization, and handling time-series or event data. Performance optimization: Solid understanding of API performance optimization and caching strategies. Modern engineering workflows & communication: Comfortable working with Git, peer code reviews, and Agile methodologies, backed by a strong command of written and spoken English and a BSc/MSc in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, or equivalent experience . Nice-to-haves Cloud & orchestration tooling: Experience with AWS (or another cloud provider) using Infrastructure as Code (Terraform) and production orchestration tools like Apache Airflow. Containerization & architecture: Practical experience with Docker and Kubernetes, as well as familiarity with hexagonal and medallion architecture patterns. Observability & NoSQL databases: Experience utilizing monitoring solutions (e.g., Datadog, Grafana) and working with NoSQL data stores .

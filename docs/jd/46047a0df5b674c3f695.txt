About the Job Seeking a Software Engineer IV (Senior) to lead the design, development, modernization, and support of enterprise applications and automation platforms, including custom legacy tools. This role works within an Agile support team to deliver reliable solutions, improve application performance and user experience, and resolve issues through hands-on engineering and cross-functional partnership. What You'll Do Design, develop, test, deploy, secure, support, and maintain custom enterprise applications and services to deliver reliable business capabilities. Lead modernization efforts across legacy applications and platforms, and support and enhance existing applications to improve maintainability, security, and performance. Monitor application health and drive performance improvements to increase stability, availability, and operational visibility. Drive resolution of complex enterprise problems with minimal direction, partnering across teams to restore service and prevent recurrence. Partner with stakeholders and product teams to understand business requirements and user feedback, then translate them into effective technical solutions. Apply object-oriented programming principles and approved development practices to produce clear, reusable, and supportable code. Use Service Management tools and best practices to support incident, problem, and change management processes. Document solutions, testing results, deployment procedures, support requirements, and changes to enable sustainable operations. Participate in Agile ceremonies, software delivery activities, code reviews, source control, and continuous improvement efforts, collaborating through clear written and verbal communication to maintain alignment, surface risks, and support continued progress. Lead technical design discussions and architectural reviews, as well as mentor and coach junior engineers. Demonstrates technical leadership to team. Establishes, aggregates, and shares team standards and best practices within department. Utilizes working relationships across teams within their division. Assesses and provides solutions to system-wide architectural problems. What You'll Bring to the Role Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Information Systems, Software Engineering, or a related field, or equivalent practical experience. 5+ years of software engineering experience. 5+ years of experience working with modern engineering tools, languages and practices. Knowledge of object-oriented programming principles and modern software development practices. Advanced experience with C# and .NET; experience with Java, C++, or another modern programming language. Experience building enterprise applications and REST APIs. Experience with SQL Server, Git-based source control, and CI/CD pipelines; familiarity with Visual Studio 2019 or newer. Ability to resolve complex issues using structured testing, debugging, and problem-solving techniques. Knowledge of Agile software processes, change management, and application support practices. Demonstrated critical thinking, ownership mindset and continuous learning. Experience developing solutions using agile methods. Mentor for the team and department. Advanced skillset in one or more technical platforms. Capable of communicating between product, engineering, and the business. Manages own time to meet objectives. Ability to perform peer reviews on code and design. Solid understanding of design patterns and architecture.

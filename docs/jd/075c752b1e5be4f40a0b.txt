Our vision is to transform how the world uses information to enrich life for all . Key Responsibilities: Work closely with multiple teams across IT, Information Security, Technology, and business functions to drive impactful change. Design, develop, integrate, and support scalable enterprise solutions across Kinaxis, SAP, Blue Yonder (BY), and related enterprise planning applications . Analyze and troubleshoot complex system and integration issues across Kinaxis, SAP, and BY , leveraging Artificial Intelligence, Generative AI, and AI-Enabled tools where applicable to accelerate problem solving and improve productivity. Understand end-to-end business processes, system integrations, and data flows across enterprise planning platforms, and assess upstream and downstream impacts of application and software changes. Leverage data and AI Assisted analysis to support impact assessment, root-cause analysis, solution design, testing, and continuous improvement. Collaborate closely with cross-functional teams on end-to-end integration testing, system validation, and performance analysis; resolve issues, proactively identify opportunities for improvement, and drive initiatives through completion. Leverage AI Assistants and Large Language Models (LLMs) , where appropriate, to support software development, analysis, automation, testing, troubleshooting, and technical documentation while maintaining appropriate human review and accountability. Identify opportunities for AI-Enabled and Agentic AI solutions , automation, and process improvements across enterprise planning and supply chain applications.

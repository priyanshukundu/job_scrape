The Senior Risk Manager runs one risk picture across the four streams: one consolidated register, one scoring method, clear owners and follow-up, with the decision log kept current. The position sits in the Governance, Risk and Compliance function of an integrated project management consultancy (PMC) team serving a master developer in Makkah across four business streams: the Al Mashaer upgrade projects; operations and maintenance with Hajj seasonal readiness; long-term investment projects; and short-term build-operate-transfer (BOT) projects that must be fully operational for the Hajj season. The Senior Risk Manager reports to the Governance, Risk and Compliance Lead. Appointment is subject to the client's approval. What You'll Be Doing: Run the consolidated risk register and one scoring method across the four streams. Facilitate risk workshops. Keep the decision log up to date. Escalate risks and issues under the risk and escalation procedure. Provide risk data for the integrated dashboard and the improvement program reviews. What Required Skills You'll Bring: Bachelor's degree in Engineering, Architecture, Construction Management or a related field. PMI-RMP preferred; ISO 31000 is an advantage. Minimum of 12 years of progressive experience in risk management on capital programs. Risk registers and risk and issue escalation (required). Primavera Risk Analysis or a similar tool. Client-side PMC or PMO experience (preferred). Experience on Hajj or Holy Sites programs (preferred). Based in Makkah: candidates must meet the contract staffing conditions, including being Muslim and having experience in Makkah or Al Mashaer. Analytical, organized and accurate; works well across disciplines. Clear communicator in writing and in meetings. Delivers to deadlines under pressure. Fluency in English and professional fluency in Arabic required.

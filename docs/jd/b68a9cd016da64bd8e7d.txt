THE ROLE

Build the systems that turn blockchain activity into data developers can use. Alchemy’s Data Services work includes token, portfolio, and transfer APIs, with streaming in the updated roadmap.

You will own backend projects from ingestion through serving, making data correct, fresh, and fast to query. You will work with Product and customer-facing teams to understand what developers need, then ship improvements and measure their effect on reliability, performance, and cost.





WHAT YOU WILL DO

 - Build and operate blockchain ingestion, indexing, storage, and API-serving systems.

 - Handle reorganizations, retries, duplicate events, and historical backfills without silently losing or corrupting data.

 - Improve query performance and data freshness. Make those properties observable to the team and understandable to customers.

 - Extend API capabilities and chain coverage, with tests for differences in chain behavior and data semantics.

 - Develop streaming and event-delivery capabilities with explicit guarantees for ordering, replay, and failure recovery.

 - Reduce the cost of storing, processing, and serving data through measured changes to architecture and query execution.

 - Participate in production support and incident response for the systems you own. Maintain monitoring, runbooks, safe rollouts, and fixes that prevent recurrence.
   
   
   


WHAT WE ARE LOOKING FOR

 - A track record of independently designing, shipping, and operating production backend or distributed data systems.

 - Strong programming skills in a production backend language, plus experience with databases, indexing, and query performance.

 - Experience reasoning about consistency, partitioning, asynchronous processing, and failure recovery.

 - Evidence that you can diagnose a production problem and improve the system beyond the immediate fix.

 - Sound API design judgment, including compatibility, pagination, error handling, and data contracts.

 - Clear communication and the ability to turn an ambiguous customer problem into a scoped engineering project.

Blockchain experience is helpful, but not required. You should be interested in learning how chain behavior affects data correctness.





HELPFUL EXPERIENCE

 - ClickHouse or other analytical databases; Kafka or similar streaming systems.

 - Blockchain indexers, EVM logs and traces, finality, or non-EVM data models.

 - Developer-facing data APIs, real-time delivery, or large historical datasets.

 - Storage migrations and performance or cost optimization under production traffic.
   
   
   


WHAT SUCCESS LOOKS LIKE

Within three months:

 - You have shipped a customer-facing improvement and can show its effect on data quality, freshness, latency, or cost. 

 - You own a production component, have tested its recovery behavior, and have proposed the next improvement using operational evidence and developer feedback.

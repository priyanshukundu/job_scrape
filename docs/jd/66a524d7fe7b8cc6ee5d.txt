Senior Software Engineer, Release Engineering
Toast
Location: Remote, US

Toast creates technology to help restaurants and local businesses succeed in a digital world, helping business owners operate, increase sales, engage customers, and keep employees happy. The mission of the Release Engineering team is “to empower Toasters to build and deploy software with speed, confidence, and joy.” We create digital assembly lines that transform code into polished products. We build scalable, secure, and automated release pipelines that turbocharge our software delivery and ensure compliance and reliability from first commit to production deployment. We are looking for a software engineer to join in our mission! A day in the life (Responsibilities) Be part of a team working collaboratively to optimize build and deployment automation tools for developers Create and implement CI/CD solutions that ensure release quality and security Lead complex projects with multiple contributors and stakeholders Participate in team on-call rotation to respond to production alerts for the team’s systems Mentor and coach other team members Promote technical excellence What you'll need to thrive (Requirements) 5+ years of software engineering experience Proficiency in Go, Java/Kotlin or other modern language(s) with a strong understanding of testing, quality and best practices Ability to dive into a breadth of problems in various programming languages Passion for improving the software delivery process and making developers happy Hunger for leveraging AI tools efficiently for our team and our customers Agile mindset focused on delivering usable software Special Sauce* Experience with managing AWS/Cloud infrastructure Experience building CI/CD solutions Our Tech Stack A core of Java/Kotlin and Golang based backend services GitHub Actions workflow platform and custom actions written in TypeScript PostgresQL and DynamoDB as core persistence Backstage developer portal Deep AWS integrations and componentry Claude Code and similar AI development tools as part of daily engineering Many more technologies that help us build a world-class platform AI at Toast At Toast, one of our company values is that we're hungry to build and learn.

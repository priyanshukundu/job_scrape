We work together to drive ambition for every future! Overview The Associate is a member of the Warehouse Finance team within Commercial Real Estate Finance (CREF). CREF provides secured debt financing solutions to public corporations, private equity sponsors, REITs, real estate credit funds, and institutional asset managers. This role supports the origination, structuring, underwriting, execution, and ongoing management of commercial real estate warehouse lending facilities and other asset-backed financing transactions. The position will work closely with Capital Markets, Credit Risk Management, Loan Transaction Management, Securitization, Legal, Asset Management, and other internal stakeholders to deliver financing solutions while ensuring adherence to the Bank’s risk appetite, policies, and regulatory requirements. What You’ll Do: Support a customer-focused culture across the team to deepen client relationships and leverage broader Bank relationships, systems, and knowledge. Acts as an ambassador of the Bank’s values. Support the origination, structuring, and execution of commercial real estate warehouse financing facilities, including building cash flow models, performing collateral and sponsor analysis, and reviewing term sheets and legal documentation. Ensure all transactions adhere to the Bank’s credit policies, risk profile, and regulatory requirements, and support the credit approval process through closing. Underwrite underlying collateral, including review of appraisals, third-party reports, and business plans; run sensitivity and stress analysis on leverage, debt yield, and DSCR; and prepare credit approval materials and committee memos. Manage day-to-day transaction execution from initial screening through closing and funding, coordinating with clients, counsel, and internal teams. Monitor portfolio performance, including monthly collateral tapes, covenant compliance, lender consent requests, and risk ratings; flag and help mitigate credit, collateral, operational, and market risks. Support REGAL Corporate Banking and other business units on client coverage, business development initiatives, and cross-sell opportunities across the commercial real estate sector. Contribute to a collaborative team environment and help train and support Analysts. Perform responsibilities in accordance with Scotiabank’s Values, its Code of Conduct, and the Global Sales Principles, ensuring adherence to day-to-day business controls to meet obligations with respect to operational, compliance, AML/ATF/sanctions and conduct risk. What You’ll Bring 2–5 years of experience in CRE underwriting, financing, and/or structuring; warehouse or asset-backed lending experience a plus BA/BS degree in finance, economics, real estate, or related discipline preferred Strong financial modeling and Excel skills Strong valuation, analytical, and problem-solving skills Strong attention to detail and ability to manage multiple transactions at once Excellent interpersonal, communication, and presentation skills Interested?

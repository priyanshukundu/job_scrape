Citibank, N.A. seeks a Model Validation 2nd LOD Lead Analyst for its Irving, TX location. Duties: Validate and provide independent review of consumer fraud and credit risk models, including internally developed and vendor models, ensuring compliance with Model Risk Management (MRM) policies, procedures, and guidelines. Provide effective challenges and assessments of model structure, design, data relevance, and modeling techniques, including machine learning and regression methods for fraud and credit risk models. Assess the completeness and quality of fraud and credit risk modeling data, including transaction, application, and credit bureau data, to ensure suitability for model development. Perform MRM independent testing of model and data soundness for fraud and credit risk models and build confirming models to challenge the selection of modeling approaches. Prepare detailed, structured, and clear Model Validation Reports documenting validation results and conclusions for fraud and credit risk models. Conduct ongoing monitoring activities, including ongoing validation, annual model reviews, and performance assessments for existing fraud and credit risk models. Conduct ad hoc research and studies of emerging modeling techniques and statistical methods relevant to fraud detection and prevention to support enhancements to MRM guidelines and validation practices. A telecommuting/hybrid work schedule may be permitted within a commutable distance from the worksite in accordance with Citi policies and protocols. Requirements: Bachelor’s degree, or foreign equivalent, in Computer Engineering, Business Analytics, Information Systems, Mathematics, or a related field, and six (6) years of experience in the job offered or in a related occupation conducting data analysis or modeling activities. Six (6) years of experience must include: Applying technical and practical knowledge of modeling techniques including regression analysis, time series analysis, and machine learning models in the modeling process; Utilizing statistical software tools and programming languages including SAS/4GL, Python, SQL, R, Hive, PySpark, Oracle, and Tableau to conduct data analysis; Gathering requirements, preprocessing, and analyzing data as well as performing data quality checks to identify trends and anomalies; Performing statistical testing including Partial Dependence Plots (PDP), Kolmogorov-Smirnov (KS) tests, Population Stability Index (PSI), lift and gain charts, and AUC-ROC analysis used to assess model soundness and performance; and, Providing effective challenge to the model development process which includes identifying model limitations, data and model soundness issues, and performance weaknesses. In the alternative, employer will accept a Master’s degree and four (4) years of experience. Employer will accept pre or post-master's degree experience. 40 hrs./wk.

About the Role We are looking for a technically capable, detail-oriented Treasury Associate to join our Hong Kong Treasury team. You will help run daily treasury operations for a regulated, multi-currency broker and use Python and AI tools to improve how the team works, for example by automating local reporting, reconciliation support and data checks. You will work alongside our in-house treasury management system (TMS), which is built and maintained by a separate technology team. This is not a software development role. Your technical skills will be used to make the treasury team faster and more accurate around the system. Treasury experience is not required, because we will teach it. Recent graduates are welcome. What matters most is accuracy, sound judgment under time pressure, and curiosity about using technology, especially AI, to work better. You will have a dedicated mentor and full treasury training. Strong performers can grow into ownership of a currency or process area, lead process-improvement initiatives, and move into more senior treasury roles. Key Responsibilities Support day-to-day treasury operations (cash management, liquidity monitoring, reconciliations, reporting) across multiple currencies, meeting daily bank and payment-system cutoffs. Identify and deliver automation around our treasury processes and TMS, especially local Hong Kong needs such as reporting, reconciliation support, bank file handling and data validation. Build tools mainly in Python. Replace manual Excel/VBA steps with documented, version-controlled code, and maintain existing macros where needed. Use AI tools and agent frameworks to streamline workflows (e.g., break classification, data validation, report drafting), within approved tooling and data-handling policy. AI assists the process. People remain accountable for outputs, and AI is never used to approve or release payments. Work within our controls: maker-checker review, segregation of duties, code review and end-user computing governance. Tools use approved, read-only data sources and do not alter TMS data or bypass its controls. Escalate breaks, exceptions and issues promptly. Work with the TMS and technology teams as a business user, raising requirements, testing changes and flagging defects. Develop working knowledge of the regulatory framework we operate under (e.g., SFC Client Money Rules and Financial Resources Rules) and how daily activity supports compliance. Perform data analysis with a high degree of numerical accuracy and attention to detail. Partner with Accounting, Operations, Compliance and external banks to troubleshoot and resolve issues. Document processes and tools so that coverage and handover are smooth. Manage multiple tasks and projects, prioritising effectively under time pressure.

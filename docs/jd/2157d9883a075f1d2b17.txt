The Global Business Controls team is part of the first line of defense and partners across the organization to strengthen the control environment, manage risk, and support responsible business growth. We are seeking an experienced Assistant Vice President (AVP) who can operate independently, exercise sound judgment, and confidently manage complex risk and control matters. The successful candidate will bring strong risk, control, compliance, or audit experience and the ability to translate that expertise into practical business solutions. They will seek to understand the underlying risk and intent behind a requirement, critically assess whether the existing control remains effective and proportionate, and exercise independent judgment to determine the right outcome. This role requires someone who demonstrates confidence to question precedents, constructively challenge existing practices, and recommend practical solutions that appropriately balance risk management, regulatory obligations, and business objectives The successful candidate should be comfortable with ambiguity, able to develop a well-reasoned point of view, and capable of leading initiatives and influencing outcomes across functions, cultures, and geographies. Key Responsibilities Provide risk and controls expertise and guidance across a broad range of business, regulatory, and governance matters. Identify and assess underlying risks, control gaps, and emerging issues; evaluate whether existing controls appropriately address those risks; and develop practical, sustainable, and proportionate solutions. Lead complex issues and initiatives from identification through resolution, exercising independent judgment and escalating appropriately. Support control design and effectiveness, issue remediation, root-cause analysis, policies and procedures, regulatory change, and audit or compliance reviews. Partner effectively with business leaders and colleagues across Compliance, Risk, Legal, Audit, Operations, Product, Sales, and other functions. Confidently advise, influence, and appropriately challenge stakeholders, including senior leaders, using risk-based analysis and sound judgment to distinguish between procedural requirements, control intent, and the appropriate business outcome. Support supervisory and governance responsibilities, including regulatory registrations, complaints, sales and marketing practices, client obligations and attestations, and related controls. Identify opportunities to strengthen processes through simplification, technology, automation, and improved controls. Qualifications Bachelor's degree or equivalent relevant professional experience. 7+ years of relevant financial services experience , ideally within investment management, risk management, business controls, Compliance, Internal Audit, Legal, governance, or a related discipline. Strong knowledge of risk and control principles, including risk identification, control design and effectiveness, issue management, root-cause analysis, and remediation. Proven ability to independently analyze complex and ambiguous issues, identify the underlying risk, evaluate the effectiveness and proportionality of existing controls, and recommend a clear, risk-informed course of action. Demonstrated ability to lead initiatives and influence outcomes without relying on formal authority. Strong written, verbal, and executive-level communication skills. Ability to work effectively across functions, cultures, geographies, and organizational levels. Experience operating successfully in a complex, highly regulated, and matrixed environment. FINRA Series 7, Series 63, and Series 24 registrations, or the ability to obtain the required registrations within six months of assuming the role. What Will Make You Successful We're looking for someone who brings: Independent judgment and confidence making risk-informed decisions. Leadership and ownership , with the ability to take a problem from identification through resolution. Intellectual curiosity and a desire to understand the risk and business rationale behind a process or control. Flexibility and adaptability in an environment where priorities and requirements evolve. Critical thinking and problem-solving skills , particularly when there is no established playbook. Stakeholder credibility , including the confidence to advise and constructively challenge senior colleagues. Cross-cultural effectiveness and the ability to build relationships across a global organization. Pragmatism , balancing effective risk management with the need to enable the business.

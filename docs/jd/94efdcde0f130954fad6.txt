Job Description: HPE is seeking a Sr. Systems Engineer to design, develop, troubleshoot and debug software programs for software enhancements and new products. This person will develop software including operating systems, compilers, routers, networks, utilities, databases and Internet-related tools. Determines hardware compatibility and/or influences hardware design. We are seeking this candidate to contribute impact technical components of HPE products, solutions, or services regularly and sustainable. Applies advanced subject matter knowledge to solve complex business issues and is regarded as a subject matter expert. Provides expertise and partnership to functional and technical project teams and may participate in cross-functional initiatives. Exercises significant independent judgment to determine best method for achieving objectives. May provide team leadership and mentoring to others. Location: Candidates will work on a hybrid scheduled in our Roseville, CA office. Key Responsibilities • Own a feature area end‑to‑end, including architecture, design, implementation, and delivery.. • Design, develop, debug, and maintain embedded software using C (required) on Linux‑based systems; Python for tooling and testing as needed.. • Perform architectural design, system planning, and feature‑level ownership aligned with overall product architecture.. • Break down work into tasks, coordinate execution, and lead feature delivery across the team.. • Conduct code reviews, ensure software quality, and mentor junior engineers.. • Write and execute test plans; debug integration issues and drive solutions.. • Collaborate with management and cross‑functional teams to communicate design status and resolve issues.. Required Qualifications • Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science or related field (or equivalent experience) • Strong proficiency in C and experience developing embedded software on Linux.. • Experience with system‑level design, architectural decision‑making, and feature ownership.. • Proven ability to lead technically while remaining hands‑on.. • Strong analytical, debugging, and communication skills.. Preferred Qualifications • 10+ year of experience in software development experience (strongly desired). • Networking experience in wired networking or switch platforms.. • Experience mentoring engineers and leading technical initiatives.. • Ability to effectively use AI-assisted development tools to improve software design, coding efficiency, debugging, testing, and overall engineering productivity.. • Experience leveraging AI technologies to create, automate, or enhance software development workflows, tools, and processes while maintaining high standards of code quality and reliability..

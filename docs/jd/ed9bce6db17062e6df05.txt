We are looking for an experienced Senior Software Engineer to design and develop scalable, high-performance software solutions for complex, data-intensive applications. The ideal candidate combines strong software engineering fundamentals with experience in distributed systems, large-scale data processing, databases, microservices, and modern software architectures . This is a hands-on engineering role with opportunities to influence architecture and solve challenging problems across the software stack. Key Responsibilities Design, develop, and maintain scalable, production-quality software. Build solutions for large-scale data processing, storage, distribution, and visualization . Design microservices, APIs, and distributed applications. Develop efficient data-processing pipelines for compute- and data-intensive workloads. Work across Windows and Linux environments. Optimize software for performance, scalability, reliability, and maintainability. Diagnose complex problems across applications, services, databases, networking, and infrastructure. Participate in architecture, design, and code reviews. Collaborate with multidisciplinary and globally distributed engineering teams. Provide technical guidance and mentorship when appropriate. Technical Skills Strong experience in several of the following: C++ or Java or C# Microservices and distributed systems Multithreading, concurrency, and performance optimization Apache Spark, Kafka , or similar data-processing technologies PostgreSQL, MongoDB , or comparable database technologies Windows and Linux React and/or Angular REST APIs and service-oriented architectures CI/CD, automated testing, monitoring, and diagnostics Preferred Qualifications Experience developing large-scale distributed systems or data-intensive applications . Strong software architecture and system-design skills. Experience with performance optimization and complex system troubleshooting. Experience with containerization and modern deployment technologies. Experience working with globally distributed engineering teams. Domain knowledge or prior experience in semiconductor inspection, semiconductor manufacturing, or related semiconductor technologies is a plus. Education and Experience : BS/MS or equivalent experience in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, Electrical Engineering, or a related technical discipline. 7+ years building production software, including large-scale or data-intensive systems with MS, 10 with BS and 5 with PH.d.

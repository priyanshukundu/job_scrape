Extract, transform, and analyze data from BILL source systems databases and data lakes. Support Onboarding team to Implement automation frameworks for solutions by leveraging data analytics solutions, business intelligence software, and custom programming. Reverse engineer existing dashboards and analytics and enhance them based on updated requirements Contribute to tuning and maintenance of Compliance systems and solutions Create and enhance documentation of existing Compliance systems and processes.

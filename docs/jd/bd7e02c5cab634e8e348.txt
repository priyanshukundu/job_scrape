Zurich E&S is looking to hire an Underwriting Clearance Manager to lead the central clearance team! Hiring preference in Schaumburg, IL; Chicago, IL; or Atlanta, GA. This position is accountable for the people management, process management, and financial management of the Zurich E&S Underwriting Clearance team. This team is responsible for clearing and registering submissions from Wholesale brokers, basic triage of these submissions, and providing customer service to Zurich E&S Underwriters and Wholesale Brokers. As a leader, you will be expected to assess performance of direct reports, ensuring meaningful on-boarding experiences and provide regular, actionable coaching and feedback to employees to review progress, recognize accomplishments, support development and/or improve performance. Additional Responsibilities include: Recruit, train, develop, manage, and motivate a talented team of clearance specialists supporting Underwriting teams in achieving a balanced and profitable book of

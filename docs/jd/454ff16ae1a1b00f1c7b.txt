We are seeking an ETF & Mutual Fund Implementation, Officer to support the launch and ongoing management of ETFs and Mutual Funds. This highly visible role works across State Street Investment Management and State Street Bank and Trust to bring new investment products to market, execute product changes and lifecycle events, and improve the fund operating ecosystem. The ideal candidate brings strong ETF and/or Mutual Fund experience along with the ability to coordinate complex initiatives across multiple teams. Key Responsibilities: Support ETF and Mutual Fund launches, product changes, and lifecycle events from planning through execution. Maintain a strong understanding of ETF, Mutual Fund, and commingled fund structures. Partner with cross-functional stakeholders on scope, requirements, planning, and execution. Identify and escalate risks, dependencies, gaps, and implementation issues. Participate in Product Innovation discussions to understand upcoming product pipelines and priorities. Support process improvements across the ETF and Mutual Fund operating model. Skills & Qualifications 5+ years of ETF, Mutual Fund, or related asset management/fund industry experience Strong understanding of fund structures, launches, lifecycle events, and operational processes Experience with fund implementation, product management, fund operations, or similar cross-functional initiatives Strong communication, analytical, problem-solving, and organizational skills Ability to manage multiple priorities and collaborate across complex organizations Sound judgment and ability to work independently Bachelor’s degree preferred Proficiency with Microsoft Office Interest in emerging technologies, including AI, and their potential to improve business processes Salary Range: $60,000 - $101,250 Annual The range quoted above applies to the role in the primary location specified. If the candidate would ultimately work outside of the primary location above, the applicable range could differ.

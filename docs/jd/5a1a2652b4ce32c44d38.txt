In this role, you will help hospitals and health systems strengthen the financial planning, budgeting, forecasting, performance reporting, and analytical capabilities that support informed decision-making. You will often work directly with CFO organizations, finance leadership teams, and operational stakeholders. You will work across the financial planning and performance-management cycle, helping clients establish financial expectations, monitor results, understand performance drivers, and determine where management action may be needed. Depending on the engagement, you may support annual operating budgets, rolling forecasts, long-range financial plans, labor and compensation models, capital budgets, monthly performance reporting, KPI development, scenario modeling, and other strategic or operational analyses. Your work will involve translating operational assumptions, including patient volumes, staffing levels, compensation, reimbursement, revenue, and departmental expenses, into integrated financial plans and forecasts. You will also combine financial and operational information to evaluate actual performance, identify the underlying drivers of revenue, labor, operating expense, and margin results, and assess the potential implications for future performance. Working alongside Huron project and relationship leaders and client finance and operational leaders, you will collect and validate assumptions, develop financial models, perform budget-to-actual and forecast-to-actual analyses, and translate complex findings into clear, decision-ready insights. Engagements may also include implementing or improving enterprise planning processes, management reporting tools, and performance-management platforms. You will also contribute to reusable financial models, planning methodologies, reporting tools, and engagement approaches that strengthen Huron’s broader healthcare financial planning and analysis capabilities. Candidates do not need equal depth across every area. We encourage applicants with strong experience in budgeting and forecasting, performance analysis and reporting, or a combination of these capabilities to apply .

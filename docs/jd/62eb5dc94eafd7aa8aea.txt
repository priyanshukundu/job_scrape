The role As a Global Finance Associate Manager, you will lead high-impact financial and strategic analysis that informs decisions across Capital Group. Your work may include leading complex projects from framing the problem through recommendation and execution. It may also include core finance work such as planning, forecasting and staffing management. You will partner with business leaders across Global Finance and the broader firm to frame business questions, build financial models, evaluate alternatives and trade-offs, and turn analysis into clear, actionable recommendations. Your work will strengthen long-term planning and help the firm respond thoughtfully to changing market and business conditions. The team You will join Global Finance, which partners closely with leaders across Capital Group to evaluate business questions through a financial lens and connect financial and operational insights to business priorities. You will work with subject-matter experts and business leaders across the firm. You will present findings and recommendations to Global Finance directors and, when appropriate, to senior leaders across the business. Your impact Lead complex projects end to end with limited guidance. You will frame ambiguous problems, define the analytical approach, and deliver rigorous analysis, a clear recommendation and an executive-ready presentation. You will be accountable for quality and timelines. Lead or support planning, forecasting and staffing management. You will deliver insights that inform business priorities, resource allocation and decisions. Quantify the economics of key decisions. You will develop business cases, financial models and scenario analyses to evaluate alternatives, investments, costs, ROI and trade-offs. Your work will rest on validated data and well-tested assumptions. Turn analysis into clear recommendations. You will lead discussions with senior leaders, present findings and respond to questions and challenges. Build trusted relationships with business partners and cross-functional stakeholders at all levels. You will gather input, navigate differing perspectives, build consensus, influence decisions and drive outcomes. Improve how we work. You will lead initiatives that improve processes, tools and data, including adopting new technologies and AI-enabled capabilities. Strengthen team capabilities. You will lead project teams, review analytical work, coach junior associates, share knowledge and develop best practices. What you'll bring A bachelor's degree in finance, accounting, economics, business or a related field. 6+ years of relevant experience in corporate finance, strategic finance, FP&A, consulting, investment banking or a related analytical field. This includes independently leading projects and guiding the work of junior team members. Advanced financial modeling skills and experience using forecasting, scenario analysis and other analytical approaches to support decisions. Strong data literacy and advanced Excel and PowerPoint skills. You have experience with financial planning, reporting and data visualization tools. Intellectual curiosity and sound business judgment. You thrive in ambiguity, work independently with a high level of responsibility, and know which issues deserve deeper analysis. Executive presence and strong communication skills. You can turn complex analysis into concise executive-level summaries and adapt your message for different audiences. A collaborative style. You build strong relationships and consensus among diverse stakeholders, know when and how to engage others to build alignment, and are excited to be part of an agile, tight-knit team.

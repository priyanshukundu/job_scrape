Job Description: The Role This position sits within the Center of Enterprise Technology Analytics team primarily supporting Enterprise Technology Business Units (BUs). This role is highly analytical and requires an individual who can multi-task in a fast-paced and highly sophisticated organization. The ability to partner with Decision Support, IT Finance leaders, IT product management, Controller, and other finance partners is a necessity. The Skills and Expertise You Bring Bachelor’s degree in finance, Accounting, or Business with 2-to-3 years of related Finance experience Solid knowledge of financial principles, experience, tools, processes, and financial modeling. Leverage knowledge to proactively provide insightful analytics and practical recommendations. Ability to become a valued partner to Decision Support and make connection between business conversations and financial results. Outstanding analytical, conceptual thinking, and problem-solving capabilities. Flexibility and skill to work both independently and as part of a team. Maturity and presence to effectively interact across functions and different levels of the organization. Excellent communication and interpersonal skills with the confidence to deliver financial results. Keen ability to envision the big picture when telling the story while maintaining close attention to detail. Build strong business relationships and partnerships. Develop financial analysis and reporting for BUs, month-end close processing, variance analysis and related business commentary. Supporting ongoing/ad-hoc analysis and make well thought out recommendations. Evaluate and improve IT financial management processes to meet changing business. Be an integral and engaged participant in helping the team achieve goals and objectives. The Team The Financial Analyst is aligned to one, or more, business unit(s) and tasked with providing excellent finance support and analysis. The role is responsible for expense management, providing a broad range of financial analytics, reporting on and delivering financial reviews, and executing on financial controls in a matrix environment. In addition, this position plays a lead role in month-end close activities and telling the story to leadership.

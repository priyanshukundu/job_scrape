Grant Thornton is seeking a GTech Financial Management & Reporting Associate Director to join the team. Approved office locations can be found below. Position Summary The Associate Director, GTech Financial Management & Reporting serves as the GTech Technology Investment Partner to Global Finance and is accountable for management, forecasting, analysis, and executive reporting of the global GTech budget, including Rest of World (ROW) activity. The role leads a team of financial professionals responsible for purchase orders, invoice approvals, monthly reconciliation, headcount and workforce cost management, and high-quality financial reporting. This leader will strengthen the technology investment management framework, translate financial and operational data into decision-ready insights, and shape the narrative for budgeting, forecasting, investment decisions, and executive reporting. Key Responsibilities Own the global GTech budgeting, forecasting, and multi-year planning process, including coordination of inputs across regions, functions, and technology portfolios. Serve as the primary GTech Technology Investment Partner to Global Finance, providing clear financial analysis, investment insights, and timely responses to leadership and ad hoc requests. Manage the global and ROW technology budget, including actuals, forecasts, accruals, commitments, allocations, charge-ins, charge-outs, and variance analysis. Lead headcount and workforce cost management, including position tracking, compensation and related expense forecasting, vacancy impacts, hiring assumptions, and alignment to approved investment plans. Oversee a team of approximately 3 to 5 professionals supporting purchase orders, invoice approvals, vendor payment activities, monthly financial reconciliation, and reporting deliverables. Ensure purchase orders, invoices, accruals, and vendor charges are validated against contracts, statements of work, budgets, and approved commitments, with discrepancies resolved to completion. Develop and maintain a centralized technology investment reporting framework that provides consistent views of spend, commitments, forecast, headcount, benefits, risks, and key performance indicators. Produce data-driven executive reporting and presentations that clearly explain financial performance, material variances, cost drivers, investment trade-offs, emerging risks, and recommended actions. Shape the financial narrative for annual budgeting, monthly forecasting, leadership reviews, investment decisions, and other executive or governance forums. Provide financial analysis for new GTech initiatives, business cases, managed services, supplier relationships, and portfolio investment decisions. Apply benchmarking, market intelligence, and industry insights to assess cost efficiency, investment levels, and opportunities to improve financial performance. Partner with Finance, Accounting, Procurement, Vendor Management, Human Resources, and GTech leaders to improve data quality, controls, planning assumptions, and decision-making. Evaluate allocation and chargeback methodologies and recommend more transparent, evidence-based, and data-driven approaches. Identify and implement process, reporting, and control improvements that increase efficiency, accuracy, accountability, and scalability. Promote a culture of ownership, continuous improvement, and disciplined execution while giving team members appropriate autonomy and maintaining clear accountability. Develop team capability through coaching, cross-training, succession planning, and thoughtful alignment of work to individual strengths and development needs. Support internal and external audit requirements, including ISQM-related activities, as they relate to GTech financial systems, processes, controls, and reporting. Leadership Expectations Lead an experienced finance team with a balance of autonomy, ownership, performance accountability, and active people development. Continuously challenge whether financial processes, reporting, and controls are operating in the most effective and efficient manner. Build cross-functional trust and influence decisions through concise communication, sound judgment, strong analytics, and credible financial stewardship. Create resilient team coverage through cross-training and flexible deployment of skills across recurring and emerging priorities. Qualifications Bachelor’s degree in Accounting, Finance, Business, Economics, or a related field; advanced degree or relevant professional certification is preferred. Minimum of 10 years of progressive financial management, FP&A, technology finance, or related experience, including substantial people leadership responsibility. Experience managing large, complex global budgets and portfolios; experience with technology investments and budget responsibility at approximately $200 million scale is strongly preferred. Demonstrated expertise in budgeting, forecasting, long-range planning, variance analysis, management reporting, and executive-level financial presentations. Proven experience with headcount planning, workforce cost analysis, allocations, charge-ins, charge-outs, and expense management. Experience leading teams responsible for core financial operations such as purchase orders, invoice approvals, accruals, reconciliation, and reporting. Experience with outsourcing, vendor management, and the financial management of commercial managed services contracts is preferred. Strong analytical capability with the ability to convert complex financial and operational data into clear insights, recommendations, and executive narratives. Ability to partner effectively with Global Finance and senior technology and business leaders across regions and functions. Demonstrated ability to improve processes, strengthen controls, lead change, and drive continuous improvement. Advanced proficiency in Microsoft Excel and PowerPoint; experience with enterprise financial, procurement, planning, and reporting platforms such as Ariba is preferred. Excellent communication, organizational, stakeholder management, and people leadership skills. Preferred Profile The successful candidate will combine strong FP&A and technology finance expertise with practical operational discipline, executive presence, and a people-centered leadership style. The individual should be comfortable operating across a global organization, managing significant investment portfolios, improving financial transparency, and using analytics and insight to influence decisions.

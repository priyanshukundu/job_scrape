Job Family : Employee Experience Travel Required : Up to 10% Clearance Required : None What You Will Do : Support the design, execution, and continuous improvement of People Experience/Employee Engagement operational programs, processes, and services. Coordinate and improve operational processes supporting employee engagement programs, events, sponsorships, grants, awards, and other People Experience initiatives. Manage and coordinate operational workflows from initial intake through approval, execution, tracking, and closeout, ensuring requests are appropriately documented, routed, and completed. Coordinate program budgets. Identify opportunities to simplify, standardize, and automate manual processes to improve efficiency, visibility, and the employee experience. Partner with Human Capital leaders, People Experience teams, employee groups, and cross-functional stakeholders to translate business needs into practical and scalable solutions. Administer Guidehouse Networks & Neighborhood’s leadership and volunteer role placement: including development of resources, communications, processes, and tools that provide leaders with consistent experience and governance. Develop and maintain process documentation, job aids, standard operating procedures, templates, trackers, and other operational resources. Support shared mailboxes and centralized intake processes, including request triage, routing, follow-up, and identification of recurring issues or opportunities for improvement. Develop reports, dashboards, and operational insights that help stakeholders understand request volumes, turnaround times, workflow performance, trends, and areas requiring attention. Coordinate with cross-functional teams such as Human Capital, Finance, Procurement, Communications, and Technology to resolve operational issues and facilitate program execution. Apply strong attention to detail and sound judgment when handling employee information, approvals, financial processes, and other sensitive or time-critical activities. Manage multiple priorities and projects simultaneously in a fast-paced, evolving environment while maintaining high standards for accuracy and customer service. Contribute to broader operational modernization and transformation initiatives, including pilot testing, stakeholder feedback, implementation, and adoption. Contribute to broader Human Capital functional responsibilities, as needed, including providing support across various stages of the employee lifecycle, with a focus on onboarding and the new hire experience. Responsibilities include assisting with onboarding activities, supporting and facilitating new hire Orientation, and helping employees navigate key processes, resources, and requirements. What You Will Need : Bachelor’s Degree; Four (4) years additional professional experience may be substituted in lieu of degree. 4+ years of experience in Human Resources, Employee Engagement, Program Operations, or a related field. Demonstrated experience managing operational processes, projects, or programs involving multiple stakeholders and competing priorities. Strong organizational and project-management skills, with the ability to take work from concept through implementation and ongoing operational support. Experience identifying process gaps and developing practical solutions that improve efficiency, consistency, controls, and user experience. Strong written and verbal communication skills, including the ability to develop clear communications, process documentation, presentations, and stakeholder-facing materials. Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with the ability to identify trends, evaluate operational challenges, and recommend solutions. Strong attention to detail, follow-through, and commitment to delivering high-quality work. High proficiency with Microsoft 365, particularly Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook, Teams, SharePoint, and related collaboration tools. Ability to build effective working relationships across functions and organizational levels. Demonstrated ability to handle sensitive or confidential employee and organizational information with appropriate discretion. What Would Be Nice To Have : Experience with Benevity and Achievers software/platforms. Experience creating dashboards, metrics, or operational reporting using Excel, Power BI, or similar reporting tools.

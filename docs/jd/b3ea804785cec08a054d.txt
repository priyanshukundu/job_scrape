What You’ll Do: Weave is CoreWeave’s observability, evaluation, and monitoring platform for AI agents and GenAI applications. Our customers rely on Weave to build the auto-improving loop for their AI agents: including production tracing, offline evaluations, online monitoring and alerting. About the Role: We’re looking for Software Engineers to help build and scale Weave. In this foundational role on a small but growing team, you’ll design and ship core functionality, gather feedback directly from users and customers, and continuously improve the product experience. You’ll work closely with cutting-edge AI builders, developing the next generation of tools that help teams understand, evaluate, and iterate on their AI agents. Implement robust and ergonomic APIs. Design and build integrations with popular agent harnesses and SDKs such as Claude Code, Codex, pi.dev, OpenAI Agents SDK, etc. Scale and improve backend performance to support low-latency, high-volume production use cases. Build polished and intuitive features such online LLM judges, playgrounds, evaluations, labeling, and tracing UX. Partner directly with users to understand workflows, gather feedback, and rapidly iterate on solutions.

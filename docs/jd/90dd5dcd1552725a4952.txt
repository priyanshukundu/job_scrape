ML Architecture & Engineering: Design and build scalable, production-grade ML systems, APIs, feature pipelines, and reusable engineering components. Databricks & AWS Engineering: Develop reliable data and ML pipelines using Python, PySpark, SQL, Databricks, and AWS. MLOps: Implement model deployment, serving, versioning, monitoring, CI/CD, and retraining workflows. Agentic AI: Architect and develop production-grade agentic AI applications, including LLM orchestration, tool integration, evaluation, and guardrails. Technical Leadership: Lead architecture decisions, establish engineering standards, mentor engineers, and collaborate across technical and business teams.

Vice President, Risk Management (Affinity Healthcare)
Aon
Location: Fort Washington, Pennsylvania
Link: https://jobs.aon.com/jobs/112952

Aon is looking for an experienced Vice President, Risk Management (Affinity Healthcare) to own and advance risk management strategies and best practices for the organization’s internal and external clients. This person is accountable for identifying, evaluating and developing risk-related mitigation education and techniques to support Aon Affinity’s Healthcare Division business and clients of Aon Affinity’s Healthcare Division brands. The Vice President, Risk Management will manage a Senior Risk Consultant as well as a Junior Risk Consultant. The Vice President, Risk Management will collaborate with Aon Affinity’s Healthcare Division business leaders regarding professional liability insurance programs to plan, organize, and direct all aspects of risk management activities for internal and external clients that comply with state laws, regulations, standards of the profession, and accrediting and approval agencies. This position also manages the Business Continuity program. This role is virtual anywhere is the US Aon is in the business of better decisions At Aon, we shape decisions for the better to protect and enrich the lives of people around the world. As an organization, we are united through trust as one inclusive team and we are passionate about helping our colleagues and clients succeed.

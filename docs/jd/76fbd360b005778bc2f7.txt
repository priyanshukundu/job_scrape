SEI Investments
Location: USA - PA - Oaks

Treasury Management Analyst is responsible for performing various cash, disbursement and reconciliation activities for the SEI Wealth & US Operations Trust Platforms within SPTC. This individual will be a primary contact for internal and external clients, supporting and assisting in exception & problem resolution with regards to demand deposit accounts, money movement, settlement transactions and payment system issues. The analyst will be required to be a self-motivated, goal-oriented individual with the ability to manage multiple tasks in a time sensitive delivery schedule. What you’ll do : Basic processing and/or service activities specific to team function Work with High Level, Complex Clients Manages larger accounts Reviewing, analyzing, and entering client information and transactions into multiple systems Setting up securities, general account level maintenance, and some level of client contact. Respond to client inquiries in a timely, accurate and professional manner. Working on special projects as needed. Perform research & analysis of root cause conditions. Implement solutions as assigned. Interact effectively with multiple operational processing teams. Maintaining the highest level of confidentiality, quality and privacy. Manage a time-sensitive delivery schedule. Regular and reliable attendance in accordance with position, business and/or client needs. Understanding Cash flows within SPTC Other duties as assigned What we need from you : Undergraduate degree and/or equivalent work experience required. Preference in: Accounting, Finance or Business related field. Minimum of 2 years industry experience; Operations Analyst experience strongly preferred. What we would like from you: Demonstrate effective communication skills as well as set clear expectations to all clients, service partners, and internal teams both in written and verbal communication. Concise writing skills Effectively coordinate, manage and complete multiple duties and assignments concurrently and within acceptable timelines. Ability to learn and become an expert in field Ability to analyze processes and suggest process improvements as well as assist in documenting procedures Ability to solve complex problems Able to train new team members Ability to handle project work Must be able and willing to work effectively independently, as well as in a team, in an open floor environment Must be knowledgeable in all areas of Microsoft Office including Word, Excel, Access and Outlook Other duties as assigned Someone who will embody our SEI Values of courage, integrity, collaboration, inclusion, connection and fun.

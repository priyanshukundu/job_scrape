Job Duties: Associate, Data Analytics & Reporting with Goldman Sachs Services LLC in Salt Lake City, Utah. Support data, analytics and reporting for various global Talent projects/processes such as Recruiting, Goal Setting, Talent Development and Engagement, Employee Surveys, Promotions, Feedback360, Ongoing Feedback, and Performance Ratings. Contribute to impact analyses and measure the success of various Talent programs, including Inclusion, Recruiting and Survey initiatives. Utilize data analytics and statistical analysis to deliver compelling narratives on Talent at the organization. Drive automation and efficiency of reporting internally within Talent Insights and with partners in our Engineering and Business Intelligence teams through data transformation and visualization using tools like Alteryx, Tableau and PowerBI. Help organize, govern, and design Key Performance Indicators to measure process return on investment, interpret results and present meaningful business insights to various leaders and stakeholders. Assist in stakeholder analysis and organizational needs assessments using established Industrial/Organizational psychology diagnostic methods to ensure analytical outputs align with workforce and talent priorities. Job Requirements: Master’s degree (U.S. or foreign equivalent) in Psychology, Industrial/Organizational Psychology, Human Resource Management, Business Management or communication related field, such as Counselor Education, with one (1) year of experience in the job offered or in a related analytical or research role OR Bachelor’s degree (U.S. or foreign equivalent) in Psychology, Industrial/Organizational Psychology, Human Resource Management, Business Management or communication related field, such as Counselor Education, with three (3) years of experience in the job offered or in a related analytical or research role. Prior experience must include one (1) year of experience (with a Master’s) or three (3) years of experience (with a Bachelor’s) in the following: helping drive multiple time-sensitive analytics projects requiring solving analytics problems, performing data governance and controls, successfully adapting to ambiguity and unexpected change throughout project lifecycle; organizing and analyzing large datasets, interpreting results and presenting summarized analytical findings to various levels of detail, both verbally and in writing; working with advanced Excel functionalities for mass data manipulation and scenario modeling and generating randomized sample sets; utilizing Microsoft PowerPoint for data visualization to produce structured analytical presentations for stakeholder communication; conducting project management and assisting with planning, including risk management, and escalation handling; supporting analytics projects from conceptualization to completion, including defining business objectives, identifying data requirements, determining appropriate analysis method, data preparation, exploratory analysis and modeling, evaluating results, visualizing and presenting results; working with predictive, descriptive, and inferential analysis, using statistical modeling techniques, such as correlation, regression, odds-ratio, statistical significance, standard deviation, mean, median, mode, variance; and working with Business Intelligence tools, including experience with one of the following: Python, Alteryx, or Tableau. ©The Goldman Sachs Group, Inc., 2026.

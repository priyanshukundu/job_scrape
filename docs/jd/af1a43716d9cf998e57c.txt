Associate Manager Global Procurement COE
Regeneron
Location: SLEEPY HOLLOW
Link: https://regeneron.wd1.myworkdayjobs.com/Careers/job/SLEEPY-HOLLOW/Associate-Manager-Global-Procurement-COE_R51991-1

Build our future together: At Regeneron, we use science and innovation to develop life-changing medicines for people with serious diseases. Regeneron is currently looking for an Associate Manager Global Procurement Center of Excellence to join our team. In this role, you will evaluate, select, and help implement procurement technologies that expand the capabilities of the Global Procurement organization and drive a digital agenda. You will engage with technology suppliers, conduct assessments, and translate findings into an actionable technology roadmap across a highly integrated systems environment spanning procurement technologies and third-party risk management. You will think strategically and act consultatively, while remaining ready for hands-on delivery when direct execution is required. You will build trusted, influential relationships with procurement stakeholders and technology teams, using data-driven justification alongside strong relationship skills to drive adoption of new tools and manage change as processes and systems evolve. This position offers the opportunity to contribute to a fast-growing, science-driven organization making a meaningful difference to patients worldwide. When & where: Location: Sleepy Hollow, NY Work model: Onsite Discover your role: Identifies, evaluates, and implements procurement technology solutions, including procurement AI productivity solutions and platforms, to expand Global Procurement's digital capabilities across a highly integrated systems environment Engages technical resources to develop AI solutions at both the productivity/tool level and the agentic level, partnering with IT to align technology strategy with enterprise AI infrastructure Acts as a trusted advisor and active listener to procurement leaders and stakeholders, identifying opportunities to optimize work through automation and translating business needs into practical solution designs Leads change management efforts to transition teams from legacy processes and systems to modern, adaptive procurement technologies, ensuring sustained adoption Maintains a thorough understanding of the end-to-end source-to-pay (S2P) digital landscape and processes, and monitors emerging procurement technology trends Balances strategic roadmap thinking with hands-on execution, personally stepping in on detailed, tactical work when direct involvement is needed to keep initiatives on track Works collaboratively within a dedicated team of subject matter experts, contributing to and constructively challenging ideas to reach the best possible solutions Supports the broader Global Procurement Center of Excellence mission of continuous improvement, operational excellence, and consistent adoption of best practices This role requires: Bachelor's degree in Supply Chain, Information Systems/MIS, Business Administration, Computer Science, Industrial Engineering, Finance/Economics, or equivalent combination of education and relevant experience Associate Level: 5+ years of relative experience Experience working in the pharmaceutical, life sciences, or another progressive industry, with a demonstrated entrepreneurial mindset. Experience identifying, selecting, and/or implementing procurement technologies and third-party risk management (TPRM) systems is a plus Does this sound like you?

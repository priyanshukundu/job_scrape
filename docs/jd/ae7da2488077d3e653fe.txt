Citibank, N.A. seeks an FP & A Sr. Lead Analyst for its New York, New York location. Duties: Perform financial analysis and reporting, including strategic financial forecasting and product analytics focusing on payment rates, net credit loss ratios, fees, and balance growth. Leverage data analytics platforms, AI tools and Excel Advance, SQL Server, Query interface, Access, PowerPoint, Macros, Tableau, Knime, Power BI, Essbase, Hyperion, InquiryFrameWork and Resource Planning. Analyze annual 3-year Strategic/Operating Plans, CCAR Pre-Provision Net Revenue (PPNR) forecasts, mid-year reforecasts, and bi-monthly outlooks. Prepare financial reports summarizing trends. Prepare presentations and analysis to support business initiative reviews. Lead analysis of product profitability. Provide guidance on business and product decisions for US Services on expected ROA, ROI, capital requirements, operating margin, required investments and productivity. Prepare presentations and analysis for Monthly and Quarterly Business Reviews, Risk and Opportunities assessments, Outlook, Earnings, Quarterly Multi-Year Multi-Forecast and Strategic Operating Plan reviews. Execute the Expense Plan through financial tracking, identification of potential risks, and timely escalation to management. Conduct reviews of key accounts, of both direct and indirect costs, P&L composition and revenue correlation. Advise on CCAR, Plan, Outlook and P&L-based analysis. Provide insights to stakeholders on revenue and expense trends, cost of credit correlation, balance, and business drivers. Develop analytics of current trends in collaboration with data owners and functional teams to present a cohesive narrative to support and influence behavior on leaders’ decision making. Provide insight on forecasting and planning activities, including Outlook, Flash/Actuals, Quarterly Earnings, Monthly Closes, long-term regulatory planning, and ad hoc deliverables. Work with Business partners to drive Investment & Productivity initiatives and transformation programs, ensuring consistency across forums and deliverables. Offer solutions for revamping and simplifying planning processes using digital tools and capabilities Tableau, Knime, Power BI, and Essbase. Enhance governance and controls, including re-engineering and automation efforts supporting FP&A transformation initiatives. A telecommuting/hybrid work schedule may be permitted within a commutable distance from the worksite, in accordance with Citi policies and protocols. Requirements: Requires a Bachelor’s degree, or foreign equivalent, in Finance, Accounting, Economics or related field and 6 years of progressive, post-baccalaureate experience as a Financial Planning & Analysis Sr Analyst, Financial Planning & Analysis Manager, Global Expense Management Team Lead or related position involving financial planning and analysis for the financial services and consumer lending business. 6 years of experience must include: Financial analysis and reporting; Financial forecasting and budgeting; Product driven analysis of payment rates, net credit loss ratios, fees, and balance growth; Data analytics; Excel Advance, SQL server, Query interface, Microsoft Access, Power Point, and Macros; Tableau, Knime, and Power BI; Essbase, Hyperion, InquiryFrameWork, and Resource Planning; Enterprise applications to manage and integrate corporate data; Applying regulatory knowledge for CCAR framework; Chart of accounts; and Ruby forecasting models.

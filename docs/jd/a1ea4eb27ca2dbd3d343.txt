The Opportunity As a Senior Software Engineer at Ally, you will design, build, test, and support software that powers critical customer and business experiences. You will own meaningful technical solutions from design through production, work independently within squad priorities, and mentor less-experienced engineers. This is a hands-on role for an engineer who balances near-term delivery with quality, security, reliability, and long-term technical health. We value the ability to solve problems, learn new technologies, and use AI development tools effectively. At this time, Ally will not sponsor a new applicant for employment authorization for this position. The Work Itself Design, develop, test, deploy, and support production software. Own features and technical solutions from design through implementation and support. Write and review code using traditional, automated, and AI-assisted development approaches. Collaborate with Product, Architecture, SRE, Cybersecurity, Risk, and partner engineering teams. Improve software quality, reliability, maintainability, and developer productivity. Mentor junior and mid-level engineers through code reviews, pairing, and technical guidance. Why Ally : At Ally, you will solve meaningful engineering problems at enterprise scale while staying close to the code. You will work across modern and established platforms, use advanced AI-assisted development tools, and help shape how our technology evolves. The Qualifications We’re Looking For Minimum qualifications: 3+ years of relevant experience Bachelor’s degree in computer science, computer engineering, or equivalent Preferred qualifications: Professional experience building and supporting production software in a complex environment. Strong engineering fundamentals with depth in at least one programming language or technology domain. Ability to independently drive solutions, raise risks, and seek alignment when needed. Strong communication, collaboration, and mentoring skills. A production mindset that considers security, reliability, observability, maintainability, and customer impact. Core Technologies Experience with some of the following: Python, Java, Node.js, JavaScript/TypeScript, React, React Native, Angular, Spring, REST APIs, automated testing, CI/CD, Git, AWS or Azure, and connecting to backend SQL databases. Qualified candidates may be considered for multiple software engineering opportunities supporting Bank and Corporate Finance Tech or Dealer Financial Services Tech. Team placement will be determined based on candidate experience, business need, and overall fit.

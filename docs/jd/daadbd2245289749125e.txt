Background Details As a Lead Business Analyst, you will serve as a trusted business partner and subject matter expert for the Producer Solutions Operations business, leading complex business analysis initiatives from discovery through implementation. You will leverage strong domain knowledge and business stewardship to understand stakeholder needs, challenge assumptions, evaluate options and recommend solutions that best support business objectives. Rather than simply translating requests into requirements, you will help stakeholders understand what is possible, identify alternative approaches and guide decisions toward the most effective business outcomes. In this role, you will also bring a Product Owner mindset to the team, helping prioritize work based on business value, impact, dependencies and release commitments. You will partner across business and technology teams to develop requirements and acceptance criteria, coordinate and complete user acceptance testing (UAT), address issues and defects, and ensure work remains on track for scheduled releases. You will provide direction throughout the delivery lifecycle while balancing scope, quality, risk, timing and business priorities. If you enjoy connecting business strategy with execution, influencing decisions and helping teams deliver meaningful solutions, please read on! What you'll be doing You will serve as a trusted advisor and subject matter expert, partnering with key stakeholders to understand complex business challenges, uncover root causes and identify needs and opportunities. You will leverage strong domain knowledge and business stewardship to challenge assumptions, evaluate alternatives and recommend solutions, rather than simply executing against initial requests. You will lead the development of complex business requirements, translating business needs into clear, actionable requirements, user stories and acceptance criteria. You will help prioritize and sequence work based on business value, urgency, risk, dependencies, capacity and scheduled release commitments. You will partner closely with business, technology and other stakeholders throughout the delivery lifecycle to ensure solutions remain aligned with intended business outcomes. You will own and/or coordinate user acceptance testing (UAT), including developing test scenarios and acceptance criteria, validating results and ensuring solutions meet defined business needs. You will monitor progress toward scheduled releases, identifying risks, dependencies, issues or competing priorities that could impact scope, quality or delivery timelines and helping drive them to resolution. You will assess complex issues and defects, determine business impact and recommend appropriate prioritization and resolution. You will facilitate discussions and decision-making when competing requirements, priorities or stakeholder perspectives arise. You will conduct complex research and analysis to support business decisions and present recommendations and findings to management, project leaders and key stakeholders. You will identify opportunities to improve business processes, practices and solutions, and help lead change through analysis, design, testing and implementation. You will provide leadership and mentorship to peers and junior team members by sharing knowledge, best practices and domain expertise. What we’re looking for 4 Year/Bachelor's degree or equivalent work experience (4 years of experience in lieu of Bachelor's) 5+ Years of experience in business analysis that directly relates to the specific responsibilities of this role Producer Solutions Operations knowledge and experience is preferred Excellent written and verbal communication skills Excellent organizational skills with the ability to prioritize workload and multitask while maintaining strict attention to detail Ability to evaluate information and data trends, solve challenging but non-complex problems, apply sound judgment to draw conclusions and act effectively in situations with limited standardization Effectively and responsibly uses AI tools in daily work, validates AI-generated outputs, and identifies opportunities to improve productivity, decision-making, and outcomes for themselves, colleagues, and the organization Remains current in profession and industry trends Proficiency with Microsoft Office Suite (Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook).

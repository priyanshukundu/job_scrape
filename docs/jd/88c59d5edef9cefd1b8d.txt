Responsibilities We are seeking a Data Enginee r with strong data modeling, governance and data quality capabilities to transform a fragmented data estate into a trusted enterprise foundation. This role will design ODS and DWH models, establish consistent data definitions and quality controls, and ensure that governance and SOX requirements are incorporated into both the current SQL Server environment and the future Databricks platform. Candidate must sit in the Jacksonville, FL office. Key Functions/Duties of Position: · Analyze existing databases, marts and reporting structures to identify duplication and inconsistent models. · Develop conceptual, logical and physical models for ODS and DWH solutions, including dimensional models. · Define source-to-target mappings, business keys, historical data handling and transformation rules. · Help establish data ownership, stewardship, metadata, lineage and critical data element standards. · Define and automate data quality metrics for completeness, accuracy, validity, consistency, timeliness and reconciliation. · Build and maintain SQL-based pipelines supporting governed ODS and DWH models. · Support migration of models and pipelines to Databricks using SQL and, where appropriate, Python or PySpark. · Document control points and evidence supporting traceable, SOX-compliant data processes. Perform other duties and/or projects as assigned Qualifications Education Requirement Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Information Systems, Data Analytics, Data Engineering, or a related field Qualifications: Include necessary skills/experience and core competencies required · Advanced SQL and Microsoft SQL Server experience. · Strong relational and dimensional data modeling capabilities. · Experience with ETL/ELT, ODS and enterprise data warehouse solutions. · Experience defining data quality rules, reconciliations and source-to-target mappings. · Ability to translate business concepts into scalable technical data models. · Strong collaboration, documentation and problem-solving skills. Preferred Qualifications · Databricks, Python, PySpark or Spark experience. · Experience implementing data governance, metadata, lineage or data catalog practices. · Experience with automated data quality frameworks. · Experience in a SOX-controlled or regulated environment. · Property and casualty insurance industry knowledge, including policy, billing, claims or underwriting data, is a strong plus. · Experience working with Guidewire applications or data, including Guidewire ClaimCenter (GWCC), Guidewire BillingCenter (GWBilling) or related policy systems. · Experience designing governed, feature-ready data for predictive modeling or machine learning. · Understanding of quality, lineage, privacy and control considerations for analytical models and AI solutions. · Familiarity with generative AI, LLMs, RAG, vector data or unstructured data governance.

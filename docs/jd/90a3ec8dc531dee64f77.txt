Position Purpose: The Finance Analyst IV, Marketplace FP&A supports financial planning, forecasting, reporting, and decision-support activities for Marketplace Finance, with a primary focus on Marketplace G&A. The role partners with finance and business stakeholders to deliver accurate analysis, identify risks and opportunities, and support the transition to a centralized Marketplace Finance operating model. Key Details : Fully Remote anywhere in the continental United States Support annual budgeting, quarterly forecasting, variance analysis, and monthly performance reporting for Marketplace G&A. Partner with cross-functional finance and business teams to gather inputs, validate assumptions, and provide financial insights that support business decisions. Develop and maintain financial models for operational initiatives, staffing plans, investments, and other business needs. Partner with Accounting during month-end close to review results, support accruals, and align actual performance with forecasts. Prepare clear financial reporting, commentary, and presentations for finance leaders and business partners. Identify risks and opportunities, recommend actions, and help improve forecast accuracy and scalable finance processes. Protect confidential compensation, benefits, workforce, and financial data. Handle complex and high-level financial analysis. Present and discuss analysis with management. Perform other duties as assigned. Comply with all policies and standards. Preferred Skills: Managed Care experience Experience in budgeting, forecasting, financial planning, and performance management. Strong financial modeling, analytical, communication, and organizational skills. Advanced Excel and financial planning/reporting systems proficiency. OneStream, PeopleSoft, or similar enterprise planning systems experience. Education/Experience: Bachelor’s degree in related field or equivalent experience. 6+ years of financial or data analysis experience .

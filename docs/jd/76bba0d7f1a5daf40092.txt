Role Overview Join New York Life’s Enterprise Intelligence team as a Product Delivery and Execution Orchestrator. This role supports AI & Data project teams from ideation through production implementation and business success. You will engage cross-functionally, apply core Agile and Kanban principles, and use established delivery frameworks to organize, define, measure, and accelerate work. Working across Enterprise Intelligence product teams, AI & Data teams, and other technology teams as needed, you will help eliminate, reduce, or avoid challenges that impede progress and facilitate successful delivery. What You'll Do Drive product delivery: Spearhead kickoff, tracking, and progress across the entire analytics product lifecycle. Partner closely with Product Managers, Business Product Managers/Owners, Tech Leads, Analytics Leads, and Product Activation teams to define, create, refine, maintain, and launch analytical products. Guide products from early concept through refinement, prototyping, pilot, broader release, and ongoing maintenance—shaping the work and helping teams move ideas toward mature, scalable delivery. Track execution: Maintain Asana boards, capture business needs and product feedback, support prioritization and task allocation, map work to upcoming releases, and facilitate standups, business-team cadences, and backlog grooming sessions. Maintain reporting and visibility: Manage weekly, monthly, and quarterly reporting aligned with current expectations, with an emphasis on business impact and the value of work and features being delivered rather than purely technical updates. Keep key wins, impact slides, and other periodic content current; tailor reporting to the target audience; and provide visibility into progress, impediments, risks, and other areas of importance while helping remove or mitigate barriers to delivery. Coordinate release and change management: Prepare release management and ServiceNow documentation in accordance with Technology (TDAV) processes and standards through the TDLC ServiceNow lifecycle. Coordinate with change teams, impacted parties, and support teams; work to resolve conflicts before deployment; and represent production changes in Change Approval Board meetings as needed. Facilitate Product Increment quarterly planning and maintain delivery discipline: Support adherence to applicable AI & Data planning processes and partner with Product Owners, Tech Leads, and team members to identify and document dependencies during Pre-PI planning. Facilitate dependency discussions, confirm scope, capacity, timelines, and tracking, and support appropriate Enterprise Intelligence participation and representation during PI events and readouts. Ensure JIRA epics, stories, dependency logs, and related tracking remain current and aligned with AI & Data planning processes. Work collaboratively across product and technology teams to clarify dependencies, surface risks and issues, and support consistent execution across products and workstreams. What You'll Bring Required Skills 6+ years of experience in technical project management, program management, or Agile delivery management. 5+ years of experience in product ownership or product delivery, working across business and technology teams with the ability to represent the product seamlessly across both. Familiarity with data architecture, data warehousing concepts, and analytics best practices. Strong attention to detail with the ability to proactively identify, capture, and communicate risks and issues. Ability to collaborate effectively across product, business, data, and technology teams in a fast-paced, team-oriented environment with flexible goals and changing priorities. Well-versed in AI/LLM tool usage (Claude, OpenAI, Copilot, Gemini) to optimize productivity. Preferred Skills Exposure to cloud data platforms such as GCP, AWS, Databricks, or BigQuery. Interest in AI, machine learning, and enterprise intelligence applications. Experience with dbt or similar transformation tools. Familiarity with data architecture, data warehousing concepts, and analytics best practices.

Our employees are at the heart of this innovation. We’re looking for bold thinkers and collaborative doers. At FMC, your ideas matter. Responsibilities: Working in a team environment, the successful candidate will have job responsibilities which may include but are not limited to the following: Maintain safe laboratory practices in alignment with organizational and regulatory guidelines. Operate and support laboratory automation systems to enable efficient, high-quality workflows. Prepare compounds for High Volume Screens. Utilize Laboratory Information Management Systems (LIMS), including Cenevo Mosaic or similar platforms, for sample tracking, workflow execution, and data integrity. Execute, optimize, and troubleshoot automated liquid handling protocols on platforms such as, Analytik-Jena, or comparable systems. Manage sample storage and retrieval workflows, ensuring proper handling, organization, and tracking of materials across automated and manual storage environments Oversee sample data management, ensuring accurate metadata entry, timely updates, audit-ready documentation, and compliance with internal data governance standards. Perform routine sample management tasks, including chemical handling and coordination of sample inventory. Collaborate with scientists, engineers, and cross‑functional partners to support lab operations and project needs. Manage scheduling, task prioritization, and timely completion of laboratory activities. Communicate effectively with stakeholders while maintaining meticulous attention to detail in reporting and documentation. Education: B.S. with 5+ years of experience or a M.S. in Chemistry, Biochemistry, Biotechnology, Pharmaceutical, Bioinformatics, and Automation related discipline with a knowledge of compound management and laboratory automation in an industrial set up. Required Experience: Hands-on laboratory experience with laboratory safety knowledge. Working with automated compound storage systems. Experience using LIMS platforms, especially Titian Mosaic or comparable systems. Practical operation and troubleshooting of automated liquid handlers. Basic scripting or programming experience (Python, VB.NET, Oracle/SQL Developer or similar). Strong proficiency with Microsoft Office (Word, Excel, PowerPoint). Sample management and chemical handling experience.

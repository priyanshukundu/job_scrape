Manager, Financial Planning & Analysis - Card Customer Service & Strategy In alignment with Capital One’s strategic expansion, the Operations, Servicing, and Fraud organizations serve a critical function in driving operational efficiency, enterprise scalability, and an enhanced customer experience. This position will directly support the Customer Service and Strategy (CS&S) organization, with primary accountability for enterprise-level CS&S performance reporting and strategic horizontal initiatives. Serving as Manager, Financial Planning & Analysis (FP&A), the individual will oversee comprehensive financial modeling, management reporting, and executive communication for CS&S leadership. The successful candidate will deliver actionable financial insights to executive stakeholders, directly shaping strategic decision-making and key business objectives. An ideal candidate will be effective at building relationships with senior executives, creating analytical storylines, and effectively communicating these storylines to a broad set of stakeholders Communicate analytical outcomes, evaluate strategic option sets, and present data-driven recommendations to executive leadership Navigate ambiguous strategic areas with high autonomy and build out analytical support for recommendations Collaborate effectively with cross-functional business partners and manage successful execution of key deliverables through indirect leadership. Synthesize information to prepare and deliver executive-level presentations for senior Finance and business leaders. Conduct financial and quantitative analyses in close partnership with senior leadership to guide strategic decision-making Drive continuous process optimization and automation to enhance operational efficiency and strategic impact Basic Qualifications : Bachelor’s Degree or Military experience At least 5 years of experience in financial analysis At least 5 years of experience with financial modeling At least 1 year of experience managing a Financial Planning and Analysis (FP&A) function At least 1 year of people management experience Preferred Qualifications : Master’s Degree in Business Administration (MBA) or Master’s Degree in Finance Chartered Financial Analyst (CFA) license or Certified Public Accountant (CPA) license 7+ years of experience in financial planning and analysis (FP&A) within a B2B SaaS, or enterprise software, or technology environment 2+ years of experience managing both direct and indirect reports At this time, Capital One will not sponsor a new applicant for employment authorization for this position.

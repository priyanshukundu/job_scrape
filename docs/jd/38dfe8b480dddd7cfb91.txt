Associate, ABS Trading
Obra Capital
Location: New York, NY, USA

Position Overview Obra Capital is seeking an Associate, ABS Trading to join our New York office. In this role, you will work alongside Senior Traders to support the valuation and trading of Asset-Backed Securities across consumer and esoteric sectors. You will develop analytical tools, manage daily trading tasks, and collaborate with internal groups to ensure optimal execution. The ideal candidate has 3+ years of experience in a trading or structured credit capacity and a strong command of ABS products including but not limited to auto loans, consumer loans, solar loans, home improvement loans, credit card receivables, and small business loans. Responsibilities Proactively trade across ABS sectors to increase the firm’s market share and expand client outreach. Formulate trade ideas for the firm and clients based on appropriate risk/reward parameters. Size, understand, and manage portfolio risk commercially, considering credit costs and the market environment when pricing. Manage client risk to minimize exposure and capture client value efficiently. Run risk with educated views on underlying markets to create added value. Perform valuations of public and privately traded structured credit products. Conduct rigorous qualitative and quantitative analysis on ABS and other structured credit products to support trading decisions. Analyze data tapes and loan files to assess credit quality and collateral performance. Draft and present investment screening memos and distill complex ideas or structures into clear, concise communications. Develop and maintain cash flow models; perform ongoing portfolio and performance analytics. Drive communication with counsel and rating agencies on documentation and transaction execution. Interface with multiple functional groups including Operations, Investor Relations, and Back Office. Leverage rating agencies’ websites and models to efficiently source credit reports, peer analysis, and methodologies. Enhance the firm’s reputation through professionalism and expertise in all verbal and written communications. Qualifications Bachelor’s degree with a high GPA in finance, mathematics, or another quantitative field from a top-rated institution. 3+ years of experience in ABS trading, structured credit, or a related role from a buy-side or sell-side firm. Deep knowledge of securitized products including auto, consumer, solar, home improvement, credit card, and small business loan ABS; familiarity with esoteric ABS sectors is a plus. Demonstrated ability to size, understand, and manage portfolio risk; experience pricing with consideration for credit costs and market conditions. Track record of formulating and communicating trade ideas based on risk/reward analysis. Experience conducting qualitative and quantitative credit analysis, including review of data tapes, loan files, and collateral performance. Proficiency in building and maintaining cash flow models and performing ongoing portfolio analytics. Strong computational skills including proficiency in Excel, Intex, and Python. Ability to interface effectively with external parties including counsel, rating agencies, and clients, as well as internal groups such as Operations, Investor Relations, and Back Office. Meticulous attention to detail with the ability to work well under pressure and demonstrate a high degree of initiative. Clear and concise written and verbal communication skills; ability to distill complex ideas or structures into meaningful communications for a broad audience. Comfort working within a dynamic team in a fast-paced, evolving environment. Database experience including proficiency with SQL, R, or similar is preferred.

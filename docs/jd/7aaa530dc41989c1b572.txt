Start your Voyage – Apply Now Get to Know the Opportunity: This role is part of the Voya Investment Management Entry Level Analyst Program. Beginning in July 2027, the program is designed to support the development of entry-level employees and provide a unique opportunity to begin an investment career through hands-on experience, on-the-job development, and training. The Risk Analyst will work closely with senior risk professionals to measure and monitor operational risks and help maintain the risk management governance framework. The role will support incident management, risk assessments, data analysis, and oversight of risk remediation and mitigation activities. In addition, the analyst will support Risk Analytics and Reporting through data management and artificial intelligence initiatives. This role is based in our Atlanta office and follows an Office Centric approach requiring on average 3-4 days onsite each week. The Contributions You’ll Make: Support senior risk managers in coordinating risk event and incident response activities, including impact assessment, resolution, root cause analysis, preventive actions, and information gathering for recordkeeping. Conduct risk assessments and scenario analyses, evaluate operational processes and risk events, and recommend appropriate risk mitigation measures. Perform data validation, quality control, and outlier identification; correct data errors and transform raw data into usable formats for analysis. Apply statistical methods, mathematical models, and data-mining techniques to identify insights, trends, and potential outcomes. Create charts, dashboards, and other visual representations of data to clearly communicate observations to stakeholders. Contribute to risk management reports and updates provided to senior management, risk committees, and other stakeholders. Stay current on regulatory changes, industry trends, and operational risk management best practices. Support Risk Analytics and Reporting data flows, automation opportunities, and artificial intelligence initiatives. Minimum Knowledge & Experience: Applicants must be graduating with an undergraduate degree in December 2026 or spring/summer 2027. Pursuing a bachelor’s degree in Finance, Business Administration, Risk Management, or a related field. High school diploma or GED. Prior internship experience in financial services, risk management, asset management, or a related area. Excellent written and verbal communication skills, including the ability to clearly and concisely communicate findings to technical and non-technical audiences. Strong attention to detail and commitment to data quality and accuracy. Demonstrated ability to analyze data, identify trends and patterns, and draw well-supported conclusions. Ability to manage multiple priorities in a dynamic environment. Strong proficiency in Microsoft Excel, Word, and PowerPoint. Preferred Knowledge & Experience: Familiarity with Tableau, Power BI, or other standard reporting and data-visualization tools. Familiarity with programming languages and data-analysis tools, including Python and SQL. Exposure to operational risk assessments, incident management, scenario analysis, risk reporting, or risk remediation processes. Interest in using data, automation, and artificial intelligence to improve risk analytics and reporting.

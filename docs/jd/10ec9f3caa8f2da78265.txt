Overview Scotiabank’s Commercial Real Estate Finance (CREF) Large Loan team provides secured commercial real estate financing to institutional sponsors, including private equity firms, asset managers, REITs and other leading owners and investors in commercial real estate. The team originates, structures, underwrites and executes large-balance commercial real estate financings, including balance sheet loans, note-on-note financings, and single-asset/single-borrower (SASB) CMBS loans. The Associate will play an integral role across the full lifecycle of transactions, from initial opportunity evaluation and underwriting through credit approval, documentation, closing, and asset management. This role provides significant exposure to commercial real estate underwriting, structured lending, capital markets execution and institutional client coverage. This position will work closely with Capital Markets, Credit Risk Management, Loan Transaction Management, Securitization, Legal and Asset Management throughout the transaction lifecycle. What You’ll Do: Support loan origination and client coverage efforts with institutional real estate owners, private equity firms, asset managers, REITs and other commercial real estate market participants. Evaluate and underwrite large-balance CRE financing opportunities across the major property types and transaction structures, including balance sheet, note-on-note financings, and SASB CMBS loans. Build and analyze detailed financial models, including property-level cash flows, valuation analyses, debt sizing, financing metrics and sensitivity analyses. Conduct comprehensive property, market, sponsor and structural analysis, including review of historical and projected operating performance, leases, third-party reports, market data and comparable transactions. Participate in structuring transactions and developing financing solutions that balance client objectives, transaction economics and the Bank’s risk parameters. Prepare credit approval materials, transaction memoranda and presentations for senior management and internal credit committees. Work closely with the Capital Markets and Securitization teams to evaluate financing strategy, market execution and distribution considerations for CMBS transactions. Coordinate transaction execution from underwriting through closing, including due diligence, third-party reports, credit approvals, legal documentation and closing requirements. Monitor existing transactions and portfolio exposures and assist in identifying and evaluating credit, market and execution risks. What You’ll Bring Relevant experience in commercial real estate finance, investment banking, real estate private equity, commercial real estate lending, CMBS or a related field. Strong commercial real estate underwriting and financial modeling skills, including experience with property-level cash flows, valuation, debt sizing and sensitivity analysis. Analytical and problem-solving skills with the ability to identify and communicate key credit and transaction risks. Attention to detail and the ability to manage multiple transactions and priorities simultaneously in a fast-paced environment. Ability to work collaboratively across internal teams and interact professionally with clients, advisors, legal counsel and other transaction counterparties. BA/BS degree in finance, economics, real estate, or related discipline preferred Interested?

Job Description As a Software Engineer, Applications, you will help shape the future of Trusted Content within the Trusted Content & Standards team. In this role, you will work with product management, operating segment stakeholders, customer co-innovation teams, and other Rockwell Automation engineering teams to define, develop, and scale Trusted Content development capabilities and standards that accelerate autonomous operations across design, operations, and maintenance workflows. You combine strong systems and automation engineering experience with curiosity, collaboration, and execution discipline. You are an expert in Rockwell Automation technologies and Rockwell Automation product applications. You should be comfortable working across ambiguity, distilling organizational knowledge into Trusted Content methods and standards, and turning emerging ideas into repeatable solutions, proofs of concept, and scalable practices that support both internal teams and customer-facing efforts. You will report to Engineering Manager, Trusted Content & Standards and will work Hybrid in Milwaukee or Mequon, WI, Austin, TX or Mayfield Heights, OH. PRIMARY RESPONSIBILITIES Standards Development Work with the Trusted Content Organization in addition to Operating Segments, Commercial Engineering, Technical Consultants to define best practices as standards Drive decisions through collaboration and cooperation of cross-functional teams Develop methods to check and grade adherence to defined standards through automated reviews and peer checks. Identify new areas where standards are needed to drive cohesiveness in development practices and consistency in delivered products. Library and Application Development Define Trusted Content Applications & Software leveraging Logix Designer, View Studio, View Designer, Optix Studio, Future Rockwell IDE and other Automation centric tools to create optimized Application Architectures for applications and work cells. Define and optimize Libraries that support creation of applications and software in Rockwell Automation’s Automation Development Environments. Participate, keep informed, and drive awareness in Rockwell Automation around the evolution of Industry Standards that support Trusted Content (including OPC-UA Companion Specifications, ISA, specific manufacturer requirements, and others). Support Development and Prioritization of Firmware and Software functions supporting Trusted Content application integration in controllers, drives, and other products. Help define Digital Engineering Strategies (Digital Twins, Automated Code, and Automated Library creation principles) that assist Development of Automotive Applications Manage intellectual property as it is being created and become part of Trusted Content patent development.

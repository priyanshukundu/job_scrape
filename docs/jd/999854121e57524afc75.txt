Learn more about RayzeBio: https://careers.bms.com/rayzebio/ Summary The Senior EHS Associate will support the Manager of Environmental Health and Safety (EHS) in ensuring safe operations at our Indianapolis manufacturing site. The candidate will be responsible for assisting with compliance of all federal, state, and local environmental, health, and safety (EHS) regulations. The candidate will also provide technical EHS support to operations, quality, engineering, facilities, and supply chain functions. Job Responsibilities Essential duties and responsibilities include the following. Other duties may be assigned. Assists in the development, administration, and implementation of EHS policies, programs, and safety at the manufacturing site. Perform Industrial Hygiene evaluations, audits, and initiatives. Provide, prepare, and coordinate EHS training, as required. Prepare/assist in composing internal and external reports for governmental agencies (EPA, IDEM, IDHS, OSHA, etc.). Lead incident investigations identifying root cause and corrective actions. Drive a culture of radiation safety and compliance across all operational areas at the site. Education and Experience BS or MS in Safety, Environmental Science, Engineering, or relevant scientific field. 2+ years’ experience with manufacturing and/or pharmaceutical industrial hygiene practices. Professional certification (CSP, CIH, etc.) is desired. Demonstrated knowledge of Environmental, Health, and Safety and government regulations. Knowledge of Nuclear Regulatory Commission and/or Agreement State regulations is beneficial. Skills and Qualification Knowledge of Industrial Hygiene Programs, including air sampling, monitoring, and exposure assessments, is a plus. Highly motivated and organized scientific professional with strong interpersonal and communication skills with ability to interface with other departments in a team environment. Strong assessment and troubleshooting skills. Ability to efficiently use MS office, LabLogic, SAP, and other associated software systems. Excellent professional ethics, integrity, and ability to maintain confidential information. Uses AI tools to enhance individual productivity and quality of work Physical Demands While performing the duties of this job, the employee is regularly required to sit, reach with hands and arms, and talk or hear. The employee must occasionally lift and/or move up to 50 pounds. Specific vision abilities required by this job include close vision and distance vision. This is a position that requires working in laboratory and pharmaceutical production environments that contain chemicals and radioactive materials. Protective clothing, gloves, and safety glasses are required while performing work in these areas. The employee must be able to pass all aspects of respirator fit testing, including medical clearance.

Job Description: The Role The Senior Associate, Outside Trustee, is responsible for executing and overseeing financial trade activity, cash movements, and account reconciliations for a book of low-medium complexity WI Outside Trustee clients. This role serves as a primary point of coordination among WI internal teams, clients, Outside Trustee banks, and fund companies to ensure the accurate, timely, and seamless processing of all financial transactions. Duties: 1. 30% Process financial trades and cash transactions for Outside Trustee client accounts by following established procedures and instructions. 2. 25% Reconcile account activity by comparing transactions and balances across internal systems, Outside Trustee banks, and fund company statements. 3. 20% Communicate with internal teams and external partners to confirm transaction details and assist with resolving routine issues. 4. 25% Maintain accurate records and documentation related to transactions, reconciliations, and client activity in accordance with department standards. The Outside Trustee Senior Associate is involved with medium-too low complexity clients and is responsible for following established procedures to identify and resolve routine transaction and reconciliation issues. The Senior Associate will Investigate discrepancies by reviewing account activity, documentation, and system information to determine root causes. Escalates complex or unresolved issues to senior team members or management in a timely manner.

Job Description The Materials and Biophysical Characterization group in the Pharmaceutical Analysis and Digital Technologies organization is seeking a highly self-motivated and innovative scientist with deep knowledge and problem-solving skills in biotherapeutics characterization. The Materials and Biophysical Characterization team is responsible for building the mechanistic understanding of the interplay between formulation composition, stability, device, and process to ensure robustness and quality of drug substances and drug products in the biotherapeutics pipeline. As an Associate Principal Scientist, you will be contributing to projects in a diverse biotherapeutics pipeline by developing and implementing biophysical and particle characterization tools and collaborating with process, formulation and device functions as part of the CMC development team. You will apply a broad range of biophysical, analytical, and particle characterization approaches to identify critical degradation pathways, assess molecular interactions and physical stability, and provide mechanistic insight into formulation and process challenges. This work is integral to formulation selection, process development, product characterization, and development of robust control strategies. Additional responsibilities include authoring technical reports and regulatory documents, presenting technical data, and project management. You will have the opportunity to conduct independent research, publish research findings, present at scientific conferences, and engage with various external scientific societies/organizations. This is an excellent learning opportunity to work in an exciting, collaborative environment with a wide array of scientists from different divisions and make significant contributions towards our life-saving medicines. Key Responsibilities: Establish mechanistic understanding of protein stability, aggregation, protein-protein interactions, and degradation pathways. Evaluate the impact of pH, ionic strength, excipients, concentration, temperature, freeze-thaw, agitation, interfaces, and other formulation or process stresses on protein therapeutics. Apply complementary biophysical and analytical techniques to characterize conformational and colloidal stability. Partner with formulation and process development scientists to support formulation selection, process optimization, and drug product development. Integrate high-throughput experimental approaches where appropriate to enable rapid assessment pipeline assets. Contribute to technical reports, regulatory documentation, presentations, and scientific publications. Education Minimum Requirement: Ph.D. in Biophysics, Biochemistry, Chemistry, Chemical Engineering, Biomedical Engineering, Pharmaceutical Sciences or related discipline with a minimum of 4 years of relevant industry experience. OR M.S. in Biophysics, Biochemistry, Chemistry, Chemical Engineering, Biomedical Engineering, Pharmaceutical Sciences or related discipline with a minimum of 8 years of relevant industry experience. Required Experience and Skills: Strong fundamental understanding of protein science, protein stability, protein aggregation, and intermolecular interactions and related mechanisms. Experience designing experiments to understand the effects of formulation and environmental conditions on molecular stability. Experience with biologics formulation development and characterization. Capable of leading formulation characterization activities in matrix team environments. Recognized as a technical expert and scientific contributor with proven ability to identify and develop innovative ideas. Ability to deliver complex objectives under aggressive timelines in a rapidly changing environment. A strong team player with excellent oral and written communication skills; ability to work both independently and cross-functionally. Desired Experience and Skills: Experience with high-throughput experimentation, automation, or multivariate data analysis. Experience translating mechanistic scientific understanding into formulation, process, or product-development decisions. Deep understanding of one or more of the following: surface science, colloid science, particle engineering, ADC and/or high concentration formulations. Experience with peer-to-peer and technical mentorship and/or supervision. Required Skills: Analytical Method Development, Assay Development, Biochemistry, Biological Assay Development, Biophysical Characterization, Biophysics, Cell-Based Assays, CMC Development, High Resolution Mass Spectrometry (HRMS), Liquid Chromatography-Mass Spectrometry (LC-MS), Mass Spectrometry Analysis, Particle Engineering, Process Analytical Technology (PAT), Protein Aggregation, Protein Analysis, Protein Biology Preferred Skills: Current Employees apply HERE Current Contingent Workers apply HERE US and Puerto Rico Residents Only: Our company is committed to inclusion, ensuring that candidates can engage in a hiring process that exhibits their true capabilities.

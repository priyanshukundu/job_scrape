About the Role The Remitly Transaction Experience (RTE) team owns the systems at the very heart of Remitly: the platform that accepts, orchestrates, and tracks every remittance our customers send. Every transaction, across millions of customers and 175+ countries, flows through RTE services. We provide the transaction gateway, processing, delivery estimates, and search/read surfaces that customer-facing products and internal teams across Remitly build on. On systems where correctness directly affects customers' money, we prioritize reliability and operational excellence at scale. This is a pivotal moment for the team. Remitly is moving to a future-ready, API-first Transaction Platform that lets diverse product offerings integrate on their own, at scale. You'll help peer teams across Remitly build better transaction experiences on that platform, while operating the Tier-1 systems that Remitly's business runs on today. As a Senior Software Development Engineer, you'll own one or two key pillars of the transaction experience. From optimizing delivery estimates, which forecast when a customers’ money will arrive using advanced ML models, to building new platform capabilities, you’ll partner with peer teams to build new transaction types that are reliable, transparent, and seamless for our customers. You'll lead the design and delivery of initiatives that span multiple engineers and teams, and raise the bar for quality and operational excellence across the team. You'll work in an AI-forward engineering culture. We expect engineers to use AI tools effectively across coding, testing, debugging, and documentation to move faster, while owning the correctness of everything they ship, and we expect senior engineers to guide peers on using AI responsibly. Verifying and fully understanding AI-assisted output matters even more on systems that move money.

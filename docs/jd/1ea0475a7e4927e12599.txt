Role Overview Cboe Clear U.S. (CCUS) is a regulated clearinghouse. We clear the event contracts and prediction markets listed on Cboe's exchanges, and we are building toward tokenized collateral, 24x7 operation, and the infrastructure that carries traditional finance onto DeFi rails. CCUS Software Engineering is a high trust environment. Failure leads to inquiry rather than blame. Working across team boundaries is expected rather than tolerated. We solve the problem, then we write the code. Design documents and architecture decision records come first. The engineers we value most close their feedback loop. They write tests, and they instrument what they ship so it fails fast, explains itself in production, leaves an audit trail and rolls back cleanly. The Software Engineer builds and runs the services that clear trades. You would own features inside a design your team sets, write the tests and observability that ship with them, and carry the on-call that comes from owning your own code. Java is our stack choice, but we hire polyglots. The engineers who do well here grow into shaping the design rather than working inside it. Your responsibilities will be: Designing, developing and maintaining scalable microservices in Java. Optimizing PostgreSQL schemas and queries. Building services that support clearing workflows including trade processing, margin and settlement. Ensuring data integrity, consistency and reconciliation across integrated systems. Operating what you build, including the metrics, logs and traces that let you diagnose it in production. Helping define the service level objectives for your services and the alerting that defends them. Contributing to architecture decision records and design documents before implementation begins. Leveraging AI responsibly, using tools such as Claude or Copilot, to improve code quality and productivity. Documenting relevant details in the team wiki.

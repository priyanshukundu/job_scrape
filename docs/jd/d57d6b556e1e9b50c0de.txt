DESCRIPTION: The Senior Manager – Data Engineering & Applied Data Science is responsible for leading the strategy, design, and delivery of enterprise data products, applied data science, and AI solutions that enable business growth and digital transformation. This role leads a multidisciplinary team responsible for Data Engineering and Applied Data Science, transforming business challenges into scalable, production-ready data and AI products. The successful candidate combines strong technical leadership with business acumen to design data products that address business needs, build AI-ready data foundations, develop advanced analytics and machine learning solutions, and continuously evolve capabilities through the adoption of emerging technologies and engineering best practices. This role is also accountable for ensuring enterprise data is AI-ready—standardized, contextualized, governed, and optimized for analytics, machine learning, and Generative AI use cases. Lead Data & AI Solution Delivery – Translate business priorities into scalable enterprise data products, advanced analytics, machine learning, and AI solutions that deliver measurable business value. Drive Data Engineering Excellence – Lead the design and delivery of reliable, secure, and scalable data pipelines and products while establishing engineering standards across DataOps, CI/CD, automation, observability, and cloud-native development. Advance Applied Data Science & AI – Guide the development and production deployment of machine learning, Generative AI, and advanced analytics solutions, including reusable frameworks and scalable AI capabilities. Build AI-Ready Data Foundations – Ensure enterprise data products are trusted, governed, contextualized, and optimized for analytics and AI through strong data quality, metadata, lineage, semantic models, and accessibility. Shape Technology Strategy & Innovation – Develop technology roadmaps and evaluate emerging technologies and architectural patterns that accelerate AI adoption, improve engineering productivity, and strengthen enterprise data capabilities. Lead Teams & Strategic Partnerships – Build and develop high-performing Data Engineering and Applied Data Science teams while partnering across business, architecture, platform, security, and technology functions to align priorities, communicate outcomes, and drive execution.

About this role: Wells Fargo’s Fund Finance Group (FFG) is seeking an Analyst to join its Portfolio Management team, supporting the management and oversight of a diverse portfolio spanning subscription facilities, net asset value (NAV) financing, and structured products. The Analyst will play an important role in the ongoing management of the portfolio, with exposure to collateral analysis, credit and covenant monitoring, ad-hoc projects, portfolio data and reporting, and transaction-specific surveillance. This position provides an opportunity to develop a strong foundation in fund finance and portfolio management while helping enhance the processes, data, technology, and automation capabilities used to manage a growing and increasingly sophisticated portfolio. In this role, you will: Manage portfolio data intake and controls: Coordinate the collection, entry, and validation of facility, collateral, and reporting information; partner with support teams on processing, reconcile key data, and ensure high data integrity Support collateral underwriting and oversight: Support collateral scoring and underwriting, ongoing borrower and collateral risk ratings, and performance monitoring; oversee borrowing base and covenant reporting processes Transaction-specific surveillance: Track outstanding requirements and ensure new and amended facilities are accurately incorporated into portfolio systems and ongoing monitoring processes Deliver portfolio surveillance and reporting: Prepare and validate recurring and ad hoc outputs, including management reporting, internal and external exam materials, collateral and credit quality surveillance, stress loss analysis, and securitization reporting Coordinate portfolio workflows: Partner with India-based support teams and internal stakeholders to manage reporting calendars, track required deliverables, and review completed work Support newer and more complex transactions: Take a more direct role in transaction-specific monitoring, collateral tracking, and reporting while helping establish repeatable processes as these products develop Improve processes and infrastructure: Refine procedures, strengthen controls and data quality, and identify practical applications for automation and artificial intelligence Required Qualifications: 6+ months of Corporate Banking Relationship Management experience, or equivalent demonstrated through one or a combination of the following: work experience, training, military experience, education Desired Qualifications: Experience in fund finance, portfolio monitoring, collateral management, credit operations, securitizations, or related financial services activities. Strong organizational skills and attention to detail, with the ability to manage recurring deadlines and coordinate activities across teams. Strong Microsoft Excel skills, including organizing, reconciling, and reviewing data; experience with Power BI, automation, or AI-enabled tools is a plus. Ability to translate transaction requirements into practical monitoring, reporting, and escalation processes. Clear communication skills, sound judgment, and a proactive approach to resolving issues and improving execution.

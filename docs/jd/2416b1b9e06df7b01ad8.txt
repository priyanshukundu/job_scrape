Work Flexibility: Onsite Position Summary The Associate Manager, PMO coordinates assigned projects and supports the Mako Robotics site project portfolio across functions. The role develops and maintains project plans, manages scope, schedules, budgets, resources, risks, and dependencies, and provides reporting that supports timely decision-making and execution of site and business-unit priorities. Working with project sponsors, functional owners, and Finance, this role monitors project outcomes and escalates issues or proposed changes requiring approval. This is an individual contributor role that applies professional judgment and works independently within established project-management practices and approval authority. The position does not include direct people-management responsibility. Project & Portfolio Management Office Support the planning, prioritization, execution, governance, and reporting of site and business-unit project portfolios. Provide project and portfolio visibility through standardized reporting, risk management, and resource planning. Partner with project sponsors, functional leaders, and Finance to track project performance, benefits realization, and portfolio health. Key Areas of Responsibility Execute assigned projects and PMO priorities in alignment with site objectives, business-unit priorities, and approved operating plans. Partner across Manufacturing, Engineering, Quality, Supply Chain, Finance, Sourcing, Product Transfer, Advanced Operations, EHS, and divisional teams to coordinate cross-functional project deliverables and resolve execution issues. Develop and maintain project charters, schedules, budgets, resource plans, risk registers, and status reports for assigned initiatives. Support project intake, business-case development, prioritization, stage-gate reviews, change control, closure, and handover activities. Lead assigned cross-functional projects or workstreams within approved scope and authority, escalating decisions requiring additional approval. Monitor project performance against approved plans, identify risks and issues, coordinate mitigation activities, and drive timely resolution of execution barriers. Collaborate with Finance, sponsors, and project owners to track costs, benefits, forecasts, and variances, escalating potential budget concerns as needed. Coordinate resource requirements with functional leaders, identify capacity constraints, and recommend tradeoffs when priorities compete. Maintain stakeholder communication plans, facilitate project reviews, and provide clear updates on progress, risks, dependencies, resource needs, and decisions required. Apply established PMO methodologies, governance processes, templates, and reporting standards while supporting continuous improvement of project-management practices. Partner with Quality and functional leaders to ensure project deliverables meet applicable quality-system requirements and documentation standards. Support procurement activities and coordination of external contractors or vendors, where applicable. Utilize digital tools and analytics to improve project visibility, portfolio reporting, decision-making, and executive communications. Ensure compliance with applicable quality, financial, EHS, cybersecurity, data governance, and company requirements. Perform other duties as assigned. Leadership & Business Expectations Connect day-to-day project priorities to broader site and business objectives. Exercise sound judgment, accountability, and appropriate escalation. Build stakeholder trust and influence outcomes through collaboration and effective communication. Communicate progress, risks, tradeoffs, and resource needs clearly to leadership. Foster an inclusive, accountable team environment focused on safety, quality, customer needs, and business results. Basic Qualifications · Bachelor's degree in Engineering, Business, Operations, Supply Chain, or a related discipline, or equivalent practical experience. · At least 6 years of relevant work experience in project coordination, project management, business operations, or a related field. Preferred Qualifications · CAPM or equivalent project-management certification. · Experience supporting cross-functional projects or PMO activities in medical device or another regulated environment. · Working knowledge of project planning, scheduling, scope and change control, risk management, stakeholder communication, and applicable quality-system requirements. · Experience using project-management and reporting tools to maintain schedules, action logs, dashboards, and portfolio information. · Ability to analyze factual information, monitor budgets and project benefits, explain variances, and prepare concise recommendations for decisions. · High learning agility, sound judgment, strong communication, and the ability to influence across functions and organizational levels. · Self-directed, analytical, and organized, with the ability to manage multiple priorities in a fast-paced environment. Success Measures · Assigned projects deliver agreed scope and milestones within approved budgets, with changes reviewed and authorized appropriately. · Accurate and timely reporting of project schedules, budgets, approved benefits, risks, and dependencies. · Clear project ownership, current action and decision logs, timely escalation, and effective follow-through on agreed actions. · Completed project acceptance, closure documentation, and handover to functional owners, with outcomes reviewed against approved success measures.

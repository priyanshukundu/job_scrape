Senior Machine Learning Engineer, Decision Intelligence
Humana
Location: Remote Nationwide
Link: https://humana.wd5.myworkdayjobs.com/Humana_External_Career_Site/job/Remote-Nationwide/Senior-Machine-Learning-Engineer--Decision-Intelligence_R-437992

Become a part of our caring community As a Senior MLOps Engineer on the Next Best Action (NBA) team, you will build, deploy, and operate the machine learning infrastructure and decisioning workflows that power our NBA platform. The NBA platform uses reinforcement learning to deliver personalized recommendations that improve healthcare experiences and outcomes for millions of members. This is a hands-on engineering role focused on production ML systems. You will build scalable pipelines and deployment workflows that move models and decision policies from development through testing, simulation, production deployment, monitoring, and continuous improvement. The ideal candidate combines strong MLOps and software engineering experience with an understanding of how production decisioning systems work. You should be comfortable working with model-training pipelines, feature engineering, batch and online inference, model registries, automated testing, observability, and production reliability. Key Responsibilities MLOps and Model Lifecycle Design and maintain end-to-end ML workflows covering model training, validation, deployment, batch and online scoring, monitoring, and retraining. Develop MLflow-based workflows for experiment tracking, model versioning, model registry management, and production promotion. Implement CI/CD pipelines for automated testing, deployment, rollback, and release management. Build automated validation and quality gates to ensure models, features, and scoring workflows are production ready. ML Platform and Pipeline Engineering Develop scalable ML and feature-engineering pipelines using Python, Databricks, PySpark, Delta Lake, and related technologies. Build reliable batch and online inference workflows supporting NBA decisioning use cases. Improve the performance, scalability, reliability, and resource efficiency of ML pipelines and scoring services. Support data quality, lineage, feature consistency, and reproducibility through Databricks Unity Catalog or equivalent technologies. Decisioning System Integration Integrate models and reinforcement learning policies with decision engines, business rules, and downstream systems. Build reliable interfaces and data contracts between feature pipelines, scoring services, Redis, event streams, and decisioning components. Partner with applied scientists and engineers to productionize recommendation models and sequential decision-making policies. Support testing, simulation, and controlled deployment of new models, policies, and decisioning strategies. Monitoring and Production Reliability Implement monitoring and observability for data quality, features, model inference, scoring, and decision-system performance. Detect and investigate data drift, model degradation, pipeline failures, latency issues, and scoring inconsistencies. Build feedback loops using event-driven systems such as Kafka to capture member interactions and outcomes for future training and evaluation. Support troubleshooting, root-cause analysis, and continuous improvement of production ML systems. Use your skills to make an impact Required Qualifications Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Data Science, Mathematics, or a related technical discipline. 5+ years of professional experience in software engineering, machine learning engineering, MLOps, data engineering, or data-intensive production systems, including hands-on experience deploying and operating machine learning models and pipelines in production environments. Strong Python programming skills and experience developing production-quality software. Experience with MLOps practices, including CI/CD, automated testing, experiment tracking, model versioning, deployment, and monitoring. Experience with MLflow or equivalent model lifecycle management platforms. Experience with Databricks, PySpark, Delta Lake, or equivalent distributed data-processing technologies. Experience building scalable training, feature-engineering, batch-scoring, or online-inference workflows. Experience implementing monitoring, data-quality validation, or observability for production ML systems. Strong software engineering fundamentals, including system design, automated testing, version control, troubleshooting, and production reliability. Preferred Qualifications Experience with recommendation systems, personalization platforms, Next Best Action, reinforcement learning, or production decisioning systems. Familiarity with sequential decision making, Markov Decision Processes, policy evaluation, or simulation-based testing. Experience with PyTorch, TensorFlow, Ray RLlib, or related ML frameworks. Experience with Kafka, Redis, event-driven architectures, APIs, or rules-based decision engines. Experience with Docker, Kubernetes, or cloud-native deployment technologies. Familiarity with OpenTelemetry or equivalent observability platforms. Experience with model-performance monitoring, drift detection, and automated model validation. Experience operating production ML systems in healthcare, insurance, financial services, or other regulated industries.

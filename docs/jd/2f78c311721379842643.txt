ABOUT THE ROLE

Homepage is the most visited surface in Ramp. It is where finance teams and employees land each day to see what needs their attention, from approvals and receipts to account setup and recommendations. Behind it sits a shared platform that brings work from teams across Ramp into one ranked, actionable feed.


Ramp's Product Foundations team builds shared product capabilities across Ramp’s product surfaces. Our goal is platforms that are so well defined and dependable that product teams can self-serve on them, while customers get a fast, focused, and consistent experience.


We're looking for a senior backend engineer to own a core part of that foundation. You'll take responsibility for an area such as Homepage performance and reliability, set its technical direction within the team, and keep it fast and correct as more teams and task types build on it. You'll work closely with frontend, product, design, and engineering partners across Ramp to raise the bar on quality, speed, and adoption.




WHAT YOU'LL DO

 - Own the design and evolution of a core part of Ramp's Homepage backend, from technical design through launch, monitoring, and iteration

 - Improve our product platforms, with a focus on latency, reliability, and graceful behavior at scale

 - Build shared frameworks, such as standard task actions and detail views, so every team's tasks behave consistently and get new capabilities for free

 - Evolve how key areas like Homepage surface work, including running and evaluating experiments that improve task completion and relevance

 - Extend the platform to support new items and suggestions relevant to users

 - Make the platform simple and safe for other Ramp engineers to adopt, with clear abstractions, strong defaults, documentation, and good developer tooling

 - Drive technical direction for your area, connect long-term system health to the team's roadmap, and raise the engineering bar through design reviews, code reviews, and mentorship

 - Share ownership of platform reliability with the team, including on-call and incident follow-up
   


WHAT YOU'LL NEED

 - Experience designing, building, and operating production backend systems, including APIs, data models, and asynchronous or event-driven workflows

 - A track record of owning a system or product area end to end and shaping its technical direction over time

 - Strong system design judgment, especially around performance, reliability, and correctness for high-traffic services

 - Experience building platforms, shared services, or abstractions that other engineers depend on

 - Product sense: you care about the experience of the people using what you build, and you balance speed with quality

 - Clear written and verbal communication, and comfort aligning engineers, product, and design across teams while working remotely
   


NICE TO HAVES

 - Experience with ranking, recommendations, or feed systems, including experimentation and measuring their impact

 - Experience diagnosing and improving latency in high-traffic product surfaces

 - Experience building task, workflow, notification, or collaboration platforms

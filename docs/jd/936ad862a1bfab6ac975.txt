The Role We are seeking a Senior Business Analyst of Performance and Attribution who will support initiatives across investment performance measurement, attribution, portfolio analytics, and reporting capabilities. In this role, you will partner closely with Product Management, Operations Analyst, Investment Operations, and Technology teams to understand business needs and help deliver effective solutions that support business objectives. Working in a highly collaborative environment, you will build strong partnerships across business and technology teams to deliver solutions that meet both strategic and operational needs. Responsibilities include gathering and documenting business and functional requirements, supporting user acceptance testing, and collaborating throughout the development lifecycle to ensure successful delivery of business and technology initiatives. You will apply business analysis expertise, investment management knowledge, and critical thinking to support initiatives across the Performance and Attribution organizations. You bring the ability to analyze and interpret data, uncover insights, and find opportunities to enhance processes, reporting, and business outcomes. An understanding of technology concepts and system architecture enables you to effectively translate business needs into actionable requirements. The ideal candidate combines analytical and problem-solving skills with a passion for continuous improvement while successfully balancing strategic objectives and operational priorities. The Expertise and Skills You Bring · Experience as a Business Analyst, Systems Analyst, or similar role; experience within financial services is preferred. · Understanding of investment products, portfolio management concepts, capital markets, and investment performance and attribution · Proven ability to gather, analyze, and document business requirements and translate them into functional and technical solutions · Experience working with investment data domains, including portfolios, benchmarks, holdings, transactions, pricing, and security reference data · Understanding of data flows and dependencies across systems, with the ability to assess upstream and downstream impacts of process and technology changes. · Strong analytical skills with the ability to interpret data and drive business insights. · Collaborative partner skilled at working across business and technology organizations · Effective communicator who balances strategic objectives with operational priorities. · Moderate to advanced knowledge of tools and techniques for presenting and analyzing data including; SQL, Access, Excel, and relational databases The Team The Performance Attribution and Product Disclosure team is responsible for calculating daily transaction-based attribution at the holdings level for use by various consumers including Senior Investment Staff, Portfolio Managers, Research groups and Distribution Channels. The highly complex system is continually enhanced in response to challenges posed by, among others, changes in investment management strategy, the introduction of new investment products, and changes to upstream or interfacing applications. The Performance and Attribution Business Analysis team supports strategic initiatives, system and calculation enhancements, data governance efforts, and operational improvements across the performance measurement and attribution ecosystem. The team serves as a critical link between business needs and technology execution, ensuring accurate, scalable, and high-quality solutions for investment professionals and clients.

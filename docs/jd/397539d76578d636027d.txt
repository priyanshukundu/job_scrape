The Role This role puts you at the center of the team that independently challenges the models Square Financial Services relies on, from the credit models that decide who gets a loan to the forecasting models that shape the bank's plans. You'll help work out how to validate modeling approaches that are new to banking, often before an industry playbook exists. And with broad access to AI tools that few banks offer, you'll help rethink how validation itself gets done, so our team can run a rigorous program efficiently. If you enjoy digging into data and code, forming your own view of how a model really behaves, and explaining that view clearly to developers, leaders and bank examiners, this is the place to be. As a Senior Model Risk Analyst, you will lead independent validations of models used across Square Financial Services (SFS), Block's bank, which lends to small businesses through Square Loans and to consumers through Cash App Borrow, and offers FDIC-insured deposits. Every SFS model must be independently reviewed and approved before it goes into use, so your work directly affects whether the bank can launch new products, grow lending and meet regulatory expectations safely and on time. You will report to the Head of AI & Model Risk and join the AI & Model Risk team, part of the bank's independent risk function. No two validations are alike: you will decide which tests matter for each model's data, methodology and intended use, with minimal day-to-day oversight. You Will Lead independent validations across the model lifecycle, scaling the depth of your work to each model's risk rating Validate models used across the bank, including credit underwriting and loss forecasting models for small business and consumer lending, and finance models used for business planning, stress testing and balance sheet management Evaluate conceptual soundness: methodology, data, assumptions, limitations and any management adjustments to model outputs Verify that models work as intended: reproduce developer code (typically Python), test input data for accuracy and completeness with SQL, confirm outputs, and review evidence of accurate implementation, data integrity controls and security Build independent benchmark or challenger models for high-risk models, and perform outcomes analysis such as backtesting and rank-ordering tests Independently review each model owner's proposed risk rating and recommend a final rating Assess ongoing monitoring plans and results, review vendor models and pre-approved model changes, and flag models that are deteriorating before they become a problem Write clear validation reports, rate findings by severity, agree on remediation with model owners and track it to closure Present your conclusions to model owners, senior management and the bank's risk committees, and support internal audits and FDIC examinations Help keep the model inventory and validation records accurate, and build AI-enabled tools that make the validation program faster and more scalable You Have A minimum of 5 years of related experience with a Bachelor's degree in a quantitative field such as mathematics, statistics, data science, economics, finance or computer science; or 3 years and a Master's degree; or a PhD without experience; or equivalent work experience in model development, model validation or quantitative analysis Hands-on experience developing or validating statistical and machine learning models, and in-depth knowledge of the quantitative methods used to test them Proficiency in Python and SQL, including working in and adapting code someone else wrote, plus familiarity with GitHub Experience with one or more of these model types: credit underwriting, loss forecasting, stress testing, asset liability management or interest rate risk, or financial planning and forecasting Strong technical writing, and the ability to explain complex model issues to non-technical partners, senior management and regulators Sound judgment to scope a validation, choose the right tests and manage several validations at once Nice to Have Model development or validation experience at a bank or fintech Knowledge of interagency model risk management guidance, including SR 26-2 Experience building challenger models, especially machine learning models Experience validating vendor models Prior second-line-of-defense or internal audit experience at a bank or financial institution Certifications such as FRM or PRM Technologies We Use and Teach Block is investing heavily in AI, and our workflows are evolving accordingly. You'll have broad access to AI tools, use them in your own validation work, help design how the team uses them, and see up close how the bank governs AI alongside traditional models.

The Trans Capture Assoc Analyst 2 is an entry level position responsible for providing analytic and administrative support to traders, in coordination with the Transaction Capture team.The overall objective of this role is to maintain position records and provide support with processes surrounding securities. Responsibilities: Provide analytic and administrative support to Front Office Sales and Trading on derivatives and securities transactions Support trade capture management, Front to Back reconciliation, trade confirmations and settlement forecasts, and maintain position records Make process improvement recommendations based on data, and support the resolution of complex problems or transactions Design and analyze complex reports, and participate in, and support the implementation of projects as assigned Recommend the launch of new products/services and upgraded platforms, minimize bank risk, and provide error reducing solutions Identify policy gaps and assist with formulating policies to support work process efficiency, and provide informal guidance/training Escalate, resolve, and support all requests, issues and errors, and disseminate or explain information to external clients Appropriately assess risk when business decisions are made, demonstrating particular consideration for the firm's reputation and safeguarding Citigroup, its clients and assets, by driving compliance with applicable laws, rules and regulations, adhering to Policy, applying sound ethical judgment regarding personal behavior, conduct and business practices, and escalating, managing and reporting control issues with transparency.

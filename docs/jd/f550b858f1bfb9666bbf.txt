The application window is expected to close on: 11/30/2026 Meet the Team Join our innovative Software Engineering team dedicated to building intuitive, high-performance software applications and platforms across Cisco. Our mission is to solve complex customer challenges through Cisco Design Thinking Principles, placing simplification, security, and exceptional user experience at the core of everything we build. We are a collaborative, agile group of developers, UX designers, and product managers who foster continuous learning, code quality, and technical growth. What makes our team exciting is the opportunity to work on modern, cloud-scale architectures where your code directly impacts enterprise workflows and millions of global users. Your Impact As a Software Engineer, you will develop software consistent with Cisco ‘Design Thinking Principles’ with a focus on simplification and UX (User Experience) at its core, using secure coding practices, ensuring user privacy, and following software development best practices. You will partner with other teams including design and product management to create the right solution for customers. You will create technical design documentation to be used by the team as well as contribute to user documentation to be used by end users. You will debug and address software issues during development and in production systems to support customers, bring new ideas for product innovation, and collaborate on small-to-medium features from technical design through completion. Key Responsibilities: Develop clean, functional, and well-tested code across front-end or back-end software components to deliver customer-facing features on schedule. Collaborate with cross-functional teams, including design and product management, to build intuitive user experiences aligned with Cisco Design Thinking Principles. Author clear technical design specifications and contribute to user documentation to ensure maintainability and seamless adoption. Execute automated unit, integration, and reliability tests within continuous integration (CI/CD) pipelines to maintain high software quality and prevent regressions. Debug and triage software defects in development and production environments to ensure high system uptime and rapid incident resolution. Apply secure coding standards and participate actively in peer code reviews to maintain robust security and privacy compliance. Minimum Qualifications Demonstrated hands-on software development experience gained through professional work, technical internships, academic coursework, or engineering projects. Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, Software Engineering, Electrical Engineering, or a related technical field or 2+ years of related professional experience, OR a Master’s degree in a related technical discipline (or equivalent practical work experience). Practical experience writing, debugging, and testing code in at least one modern programming language (e.g., Python, Java, C++, Go, or JavaScript/TypeScript). Preferred Qualifications Experience assisting in peer code reviews and executing tests for scale, reliability, and velocity within own code. Experience monitoring and triaging incident reports, participating in postmortems, and supporting potential on-call duties. Demonstrated ability to analyze information, solve straightforward operational or technical problems, and eliminate blockers to meet deadlines. Strong collaboration and communication skills to partner effectively across engineering, design, and product management teams.

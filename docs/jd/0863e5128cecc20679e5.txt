Senior Forward-Deployed Engineer, Risk & Capital Technology – Vice President

Location: Irving Texas United States

We are looking for a Senior Core Technology Engineer to design, build, and operate cloud-native microservices and large-scale data platforms for regulatory risk, credit calculation, and RWA aggregation and reporting under Optima. You will work in a forward-deployed style . You will sit close to Risk, Finance, and Product users, turn their problems into working software, and own delivery through production. You will also use AI coding agents to raise team productivity while keeping quality, security, and auditability high. What You'll Do Lead the design and delivery of fault-tolerant microservices and APIs on Spring Boot 4 and Java 21 / 25, deployed on OpenShift / Kubernetes. Own architecture for batch and streaming risk pipelines using Flink and Spark. Orchestrate them with Apache Airflow . Design the analytics layer. Serve low-latency risk and RWA analytics through Apache Pinot and expose them to users through Apache Superset dashboards. Design event-driven integrations across Kafka, RabbitMQ, and TIBCO. Architect data access and caching across Sybase, Oracle, MongoDB, PostgreSQL, and Ignite / GridGain. Work directly with Risk and Finance stakeholders. Frame problems, prototype quickly, ship iteratively, and gather feedback from real users. Build and review software using AI coding agents (such as Devin and Claude) . Define team guardrails: approved tools, prompt and context hygiene, mandatory human review, and test-backed acceptance of agent-generated code. Set engineering standards across BDD testing, SonarQube gates, DevSecOps, and CVE remediation. Lead modernization (Java upgrades, Maven to Gradle, Ksh / Bash to Airflow). Lead design and code reviews, mentor C12 and junior engineers, and serve as an on-call escalation point. Required Qualifications 8+ years of software engineering experience. Strong Java (17 required; 21 / 25 preferred), distributed systems, concurrency, and SQL. Deep Spring Boot experience (Batch, Integration, Security / OAuth2, Data, Kafka). Proven microservices and REST API design in production. Hands-on Flink and / or Spark , and production experience with Kafka plus one other messaging platform. Proficiency with Docker, OpenShift / Kubernetes, Helm, Gradle, and CI/CD . Experience with observability and production monitoring. Working experience with relational and NoSQL stores (Sybase / Oracle, MongoDB, PostgreSQL) and distributed caching (Ignite / GridGain). Experience with Apache Airflow , and working knowledge of Apache Pinot and / or Apache Superset (or comparable OLAP and BI tools). Strong testing practice (JUnit 5, Mockito, Cucumber) and DevSecOps awareness. Excellent communication skills. Able to work directly with business users, explain technical trade-offs clearly, and lead design discussions. Preferred Qualifications Demonstrated experience building and writing production software with AI coding agents such as Devin and Claude , including prompt and context design, reviewing agent output, and keeping quality controls in place. Experience in a forward-deployed, customer-embedded, or business-facing engineering role. Basel / Capital, RWA, counterparty credit risk (BA-CVA / SA-CVA), or CCAR experience. Experience leading modernization programs and operating batch platforms under regulatory deadlines. React / TypeScript working knowledge; Python for tooling and data work. Prior technical lead or team-lead experience. Education Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field (or equivalent experience). A Master's degree is a plus.

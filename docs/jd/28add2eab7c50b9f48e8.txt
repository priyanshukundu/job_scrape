About This Role The Senior Associate I, Quality Control Raw Materials ensures raw materials meet quality and compliance standards through laboratory testing, sampling, investigations, and quality system support. This role partners with cross-functional teams to maintain GMP compliance, support manufacturing, and drive product quality. What You'll Do Perform raw material sampling and testing Execute compendial and non-compendial analytical testing methods (particularly GC and LC/MS) Conduct method validations, qualifications, and laboratory studies Author investigation reports, protocols, change controls, and technical documents Support investigations, CAPAs, and quality system activities Review analytical data and documentation for accuracy and compliance Troubleshoot laboratory methods, instrumentation, and testing issues Partner with Manufacturing, Quality, and cross-functional stakeholders Support process validation and continuous improvement initiatives Mentor junior team members and provide technical guidance Other duties as assigned Who You Are You are a collaborative laboratory professional who thrives in a GMP-regulated environment and enjoys solving complex technical challenges. You combine strong scientific expertise with excellent organizational skills and can effectively balance multiple priorities while maintaining a high standard of quality and compliance.

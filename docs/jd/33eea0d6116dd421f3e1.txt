Regular or Temporary: Regular Language Fluency: English (Required) Work Shift: 1st shift (United States of America) Please review the following job description: Applies specialized Agile delivery knowledge to support and enhance Agile transformation efforts within teams. Develops and implements Agile practices, facilitates ceremonies, and contributes to continuous improvement initiatives to drive effective team performance and delivery outcomes. ESSENTIAL DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES Following is a summary of the essential functions for this job. Other duties may be performed, both major and minor, which are not mentioned below. Specific activities may change from time to time. 1. Develops and applies Agile delivery techniques and best practices within assigned teams. 2. Facilitates Agile ceremonies such as sprint planning, daily stand-ups, reviews, and retrospectives. 3. Identifies impediments and collaborates with team members to resolve issues impacting delivery. 4. Supports Agile coaching efforts by providing guidance on Agile frameworks like Scrum, Kanban, and SAFe. 5. Measures and reports on Agile implementation progress and team performance metrics. 6. Collaborates with cross-functional teams to promote Agile adoption and continuous improvement. 7. Assists in the development and delivery of Agile training materials and sessions. 8. Contributes to process improvement initiatives to enhance Agile delivery effectiveness. 9. Supports the integration of Agile tools and technologies to streamline delivery processes. 10. Shares knowledge and mentors junior team members on Agile principles and practices. Qualifications Required Qualifications The requirements listed below are representative of the knowledge, skill and/or ability required. Reasonable accommodations may be made to enable individuals with disabilities to perform the essential functions. 1. Bachelor’s degree or equivalent education, training, and work-related experience. 2. Minimum of 7 years of experience in Agile delivery or related technical roles. 3. Solid understanding of Agile frameworks such as Scrum, Kanban, and SAFe. 4. Experience facilitating Agile ceremonies and supporting Agile teams. Preferred Qualifications 1. Agile certifications such as Certified ScrumMaster (CSM) or SAFe Agilist (SA). 2. Experience with Agile project management tools (e.g., Jira, Rally). 3. Familiarity with continuous improvement and change management practices. 4. Technical background or experience in software development or related fields.

Citi is seeking a highly motivated Business Analyst and Product Manager for its Fixed Income - Spread Products (SP) Technology team that serves the Whole Loan Financing and Securitization transactions. We are looking for a talented professional with a balance of technical and business acumen to partner with the business and guide the development of end-to-end flow on lending transactions. The Forward Deployed Business Analyst / Technical Product Manager (FDE BA/PM) is a hybrid, high-impact role designed for individuals who combine deep financial domain expertise, product ownership rigor, and hands-on technical execution . The candidate will be a core member of technology team responsible for leading product technology and implementing project by working with multiple business, operations groups, and application owners. Excellent opportunity to gain exposure to global financing & securitization as well as wholesale credit risk processes and help resolve business' technology challenges. Responsibilities: Develop creative technical solutions to meet business needs and manage complex, high-volume applications in partnership with business partners and stakeholders Rapidly build production-grade POCs using internal AI tools to solve acute operational and analytical bottlenecks across the whole loan lifecycle as part of requirements gathering and validation Hands-on familiarity with modern development environments ( VS Code / Cursor ), version control systems ( Git/GitHub/Bitbucket ), and AI-assisted development tools (such as GitHub Copilot or LLM-driven query/code generation) is a strong plus. Lead to formulation of strategies/processes for Product Road map, Development and Governance and contribute to defining standards and new automation initiatives Develop communication to guide, influence and convince others, including colleagues in other areas and external customers Provide advice and counsel related to the technology or operations of the business and filter, prioritize and validate potentially complex material from multiple sources Transition requirements to QA teams across multiple global regions and assist in identifying test scenarios and work with business users to execute UAT, conduct business user training and mange technology implementation Deliver material/presentations to Senior Management and application owners and apprise key stakeholders with project health Appropriately assess risk when business decisions are made, demonstrating particular consideration for the firm's reputation and safeguarding Citigroup, its clients and assets, by driving compliance with applicable laws, rules and regulations, adhering to Policy, applying sound ethical judgment regarding personal behavior, conduct and business practices, and escalating, managing and reporting control issues with transparency.

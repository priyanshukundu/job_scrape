Company: Qualcomm Technologies, Inc. Job Area: Engineering Group, Engineering Group > Cellular Technologies Software General Summary: The Wireless R&D group is focused on developing a Test Base Station (TBS) for verification of QCT mobile chipset functionality. We are seeking a software engineer for an exciting opportunity to collaborate in the implementation and verification of complex wireless systems. The successful candidate will be part of a diverse, experienced team developing cutting edge features for 5G and 6G wireless technologies for the 5G/6G TBS product. Sophisticated multi-thread software is written in C++ and implemented as a Linux service. Fast paced development is supported by Continuous Integration processes and systems. Minimum Qualifications: • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, or related field. Responsibilities Own 5G/6G Radio Access Network (RAN) Layer 2 (MAC, RLC, PDCP, SDAP) responsibilities Drive 3GPP 5G/6G feature development from specification through commercialization Design and develop L2 functionality, including DL and UL channel configuration, data-plane and call flows Implement and optimize fixed and dynamic DL and UL channel schedulers, data-plane paths Interface with NodeB Layer 3 (RRC) and Layer 1 (PHY) components Develop real-time-sensitive, multi-core, and multi-threaded software for the NodeB Debug, profile, and optimize software for latency, throughput, and reliability on the target platform Required Skills 2+ years of experience in SW design and development with the C++ programming language, or strong C programming with familiarity in object-oriented concepts Experience with the full software development life cycle Familiarity with 3GPP 4G/5G specifications. Acumen to read and understand the 3GPP specifications and translate them to requirements Familiarity with developing and working in a Linux environment Knowledge of multi-core, multi-threaded programming, and associated OS concepts Familiarity with industry-standard software development and debugging tools (IDEs, build systems, git, gdb). Familiarity with AI based tools/agentic workflows to improve work productivity Strong analytical, debugging, and communication skills with the ability to work effectively across cross-functional distributed teams Preferred Skills Hands-on experience in designing and developing LTE/5G L2 (MAC, RLC, PDCP, SDAP) or RAN software Experience with real-time scheduling, DPDK, or performance tuning on multi-core platforms.

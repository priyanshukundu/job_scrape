*This job can be filled to align with any Cigna/Evernorth multifunction office location* The Account Management Senior Associate serves as the primary communications liaison between a dedicated client and ESI, leading the planning, development, coordination, and execution of client and member communications while ensuring contractual, compliance, accessibility, and delivery requirements are met. This position also manages a broad portfolio of client support activities, including enrollment initiatives, formulary and benefit communications, digital and educational content, reporting, Performance Guarantees, legislative research, vendor coordination, and operational meeting support. Essential Functions Serve as the primary communications liaison between the client and ESI, coordinating communication needs, priorities, requests, and deliverables across client and internal stakeholders. Lead client communication projects from planning through implementation, managing timelines, deliverables, approvals, dependencies, and status reporting. Develop and manage custom member communications and outreach in partnership with the CRP team, account management, operational teams, and client stakeholders. Manage contractually required member communications and outreach, ensuring deliverables are accurate, timely, and aligned with contractual requirements. Coordinate communication review and approval processes between client and internal business partners, including Marketing, Legal, Compliance, and other subject matter experts. Own and maintain the Formulary Guidebook (PDL) and coordinate member communications related to formulary, benefit, and prescription drug program changes. Lead annual and Open Enrollment communication planning and execution, including the development, review, approval, and distribution of member-facing materials. Plan and support Summer and Fall Benefit Fairs, including event coordination, communication materials, fulfillment, and on-site or virtual support, as needed. Develop and manage newsletters, social media content, member education campaigns, FAQs, benefit awareness materials, and other targeted member communications. Manage client website content updates and conduct periodic communication audits to ensure member-facing information remains accurate, current, and compliant. Coordinate accessibility and Section 508 compliance reviews to ensure member communications meet applicable requirements and are ready for publication and distribution. Manage communication inventories, document version control, templates, standards, and account-specific documentation to ensure consistent use of current, approved materials. Coordinate print production, mailing, fulfillment, promotional materials, and vendor services supporting client and member communications. Maintain project trackers, implementation schedules, milestones, outstanding requests, and status reporting to support the timely completion of communication deliverables. Manage client communication requests and inquiries, coordinating follow-up, approvals, and resolution with appropriate internal stakeholders. Support monthly Performance Guarantee preparation and coordinate communication deliverables associated with applicable Performance Guarantee requirements. Monitor legislative developments and support research and analysis of proposed legislation that may impact the client, benefits, operations, or member communications. Support recurring and ad hoc client reporting, including the coordination, preparation, validation, and delivery of requested information. Support operational meetings and other client calls by documenting key discussions, decisions, responsibilities, action items, and follow-up activities. Qualifications Bachelor’s degree in a related field or 2 to 4 years of relevant professional experience. Proficiency in Microsoft Office applications and other web-based business tools. Strong analytical, critical-thinking, and problem-solving skills. Excellent written and verbal communication, presentation, and interpersonal skills. Demonstrated ability to manage multiple projects, priorities, deadlines, and deliverables simultaneously. Demonstrated ability to collaborate effectively across cross-functional teams, proactively communicate with stakeholders, and resolve issues with an appropriate sense of urgency. Strong organizational skills and attention to detail, with the ability to maintain accuracy across multiple communication deliverables and workstreams. Ability to work independently while effectively collaborating with client stakeholders, account management, and cross-functional business partners. Ability to travel overnight within Texas to support benefit fairs and other client events, primarily during the June and July timeframe. If you will be working at home occasionally or permanently, the internet connection must be obtained through a cable broadband or fiber optic internet service provider with speeds of at least 10Mbps download/5Mbps upload.

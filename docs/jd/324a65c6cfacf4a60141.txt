Associate Scientist, Immunology & Inflammation
Regeneron
Location: TARRYTOWN
Link: https://regeneron.wd1.myworkdayjobs.com/Careers/job/TARRYTOWN/Associate-Scientist--Immunology---Inflammation_R52598

Build our future together: At Regeneron, we use science and innovation to develop life-changing medicines for people with serious diseases. We are seeking an Associate Scientist to join our Immunology and Inflammation group, supporting the development of therapeutics for autoimmune disease. In this role, you will engage in cutting-edge immunological research as part of our discovery efforts, contributing to human health while collaborating with a highly collaborative team working in autoimmunity, immune cell biology, and tissue immunity. This position offers the opportunity to contribute to a fast-growing, science-driven organization making a meaningful difference to patients worldwide. When & where: Location: Tarrytown, NY Work model/flexibility: Onsite Discover your role: Handle and care for mice, including procedures such as blood/tissue sample collection and injections (IP, IV, and subcutaneous) Process organs for immune cell isolation and analysis Maintain, expand, and/or modulate cells in vitro Evaluate cell phenotypes and function using ex vivo or in vitro assays such as flow cytometry and immunoassays Analyze data using biological and statistical software such as FlowJo, OMIQ, Excel, and Prism Document experimental procedures and results with care and accuracy Collaborate closely with teams across the organization Thrive in an innovative, team-driven environment, adapting readily to new techniques and shifting tasks This role requires: B.S. or M.S. degree in a relevant field with 0-2 years of experience in immunology or a related discipline Strong organizational skills, attention to detail, and a creative approach to problem-solving Open and frequent communication with manager and team members Preferred: experience in tissue culture, cell-based assays, flow cytometry, or immunoassays (e.g., ELISA, MSD, Luminex) Preferred: proficiency in basic computer applications and biological data collection/analysis Preferred: experience in mouse handling Does this sound like you?

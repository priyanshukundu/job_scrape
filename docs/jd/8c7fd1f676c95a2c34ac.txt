Data Scientist The Opportunity: We are seeking a hands-on Data Scientist to develop data-driven solutions that strengthen medical device supply chain resilience in support of federal health missions. You will create reusable analytical workflows, intuitive visualizations, and interactive dashboards using AI-assisted development environments. Working closely with technical teams, subject-matter experts, and clients, you will translate open-ended requirements and urgent requests into accurate, scalable solutions. Work with us as we use data science for good. Join us. The world can’t wait.

Senior Research Scientist - Nucleic Acid Payload Engineering
Vertex Pharmaceuticals
Location: Boston, MA

Job Description Company Overview Vertex is a biopharmaceutical company dedicated to developing innovative and transformative therapies for patients with serious diseases. Our Cell & Genetic Therapies Research & Development site in the Boston area brings together research, development, and manufacturing capabilities to advance emerging therapeutic modalities to treat—and potentially cure—serious diseases. At Vertex Cell & Genetic Therapies, multidisciplinary teams integrate cutting-edge biology, science, and technologies to accelerate the development of novel therapies. Within this organization, the Nucleic Acid Science team focuses on engineering innovative RNA and DNA payloads for non-viral delivery, collaborating across scientific disciplines to advance therapeutic programs across multiple disease areas. Role Summary We are seeking a Senior Research Scientist to design, engineer, and evaluate nucleic acid payloads for multiple therapeutic programs and disease areas. Working across RNA, DNA, and genome-engineering cargo, the Senior Research Scientist will develop payloads for non-viral delivery and investigate how molecular design affects potency, durability, immune activation, and tolerability. This hands-on role calls for an independent and creative scientist who can advance several projects while partnering closely with delivery, chemistry, immunology, pharmacology, and disease-biology teams. Key Responsibilities Design and test nucleic acid payloads, optimizing topology, structure, sequence, and chemistry for therapeutic performance. Investigate how nucleic acid cargo properties, including size, topology, structure, sequence, and chemistry, influence compatibility with non-viral delivery systems, including lipid nanoparticles (LNPs) and/or polymer-based nanoparticles, as well as delivery efficiency, biological activity, and tolerability. Collaborate with delivery scientists to identify whether performance limitations arise from cargo properties, delivery systems, or their interactions. Develop innovative cargo-engineering strategies to enhance expression, stability, potency, durability, and gene-editing efficiency while minimizing unwanted immune activation and improving tolerability and safety. Design and execute cell-based studies and collaborate on in vivo studies to assess payload function and biological response. Develop and refine analytical and biological assays to characterize nucleic acid identity, purity, integrity, and functional activity, and evaluate associated immune activation, tolerability, and safety-related responses. Analyze complex datasets, troubleshoot experiments, form clear hypotheses, and set next steps across multiple projects. Collaborate across scientific disciplines and therapeutic programs, incorporate emerging research into experimental strategies, and communicate findings through presentations, technical reports, publications, and patent contributions. Maintain rigorous experimental documentation in electronic laboratory notebooks (ELNs) and contribute to the development of SOPs and scientific reports. Mentor junior scientists and provide technical guidance on experimental design and troubleshooting. Required Qualifications Ph.D. or equivalent in molecular or cell biology, biochemistry, chemical biology, biological engineering, or a related discipline, typically with 2–5 years of relevant postdoctoral or industry experience; or Master’s degree or equivalent in a related discipline with 5+ years of relevant experience; or Bachelor’s degree or equivalent in a related discipline with 8+ years of relevant experience. Demonstrated research experience in nucleic acid engineering, therapeutic payload development, or related areas. Hands-on experience designing and producing nucleic acid payloads, including RNA and/or DNA, for therapeutic applications such as gene expression, gene editing, or genome engineering. Experience working with nucleic acid payloads in non-viral delivery systems, including lipid nanoparticles (LNPs) and/or polymer-based nanoparticles, with an understanding of how cargo properties influence delivery performance and biological activity. Experience evaluating and developing strategies to mitigate unwanted innate immune activation associated with nucleic acid payloads, with an understanding of how cargo design and non-viral delivery systems influence immunogenicity and tolerability. Ability to independently design complex experiments, interpret data, solve technical problems, and communicate results across teams. Technical Skills Required Experience in nucleic acid construct design, production, and characterization, using relevant molecular biology, biochemical, or synthetic approaches. Assessment of how payload size, topology, structure, sequence composition, and chemical modification affect performance. Demonstrated proficiency in relevant molecular and cell biology techniques for nucleic acid engineering and evaluation, with experience in methods such as cloning, transfection, cell culture, next-generation sequencing, quantitative expression assays, or analytical characterization. Working knowledge of RNA and DNA innate-sensing pathways and the effects of construct features, formulation, and process impurities on immune activation. Preferred Skills Experience designing and engineering DNA-based therapeutic payloads, including cDNA, plasmid DNA, single-stranded DNA, linear or circular DNA, and other large DNA constructs. Hands-on experience optimizing nucleic acid cargo properties for compatibility with nanoparticle formulations, delivery efficiency, and tolerability. Experience distinguishing cargo-driven immune responses from formulation -associated effects. Assessment of innate immune responses using approaches such as cytokine panels and immune signaling pathway signatures. A track record of developing new experimental methods, technologies and contributing to scientific publications or patents. Prior industry experience in nucleic acid therapeutics for non-viral drug delivery.

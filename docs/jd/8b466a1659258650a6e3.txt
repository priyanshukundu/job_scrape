Join us as a Senior Risk Advisor, Investor Services The Senior Risk Advisor will help drive Enterprise Risk Management (ERM’s) mission; to strengthen and protect the Firm and its clients by establishing a global framework for identifying and anticipating risks to the Firm; executing our program of measuring, managing, and monitoring those risks; and driving a culture of risk awareness, transparency and escalation. The Senior Risk Advisor will be responsible for proactive identification, assessment, monitoring, and mitigation of risks arising from evolving new business activities, processes, technologies, and regulatory requirements. Some of your key responsibilities include: New Business Activity Reviews Reviewing and assessing proposed new business activities to understand potential risk impacts and mitigants, ensuring there is no negative impact on the risk profile of Investor Services. Understanding and clearly communicating risk relating to the evolution of new and existing business activities. Communicating on proposed new business activities and approvals to a high standard. Risk and Control Reviews Performing targeted risk/control reviews of operational processes and workflows, and articulating findings and recommendations. Monitoring and tracking corrective action plans, analyzing trends and assessing root causes of audit/regulatory findings, risk and control self-assessment gaps and risk events. Assessing and escalating trends and control weaknesses, and supporting the development of sustainable solutions for the management of risk. Leveraging risk management tools, such as the Firmwide Risk Event Database and Risk and Control Self-Assessment database, to assist in causal analysis of risk matters/incidents. Advisory and Training Providing risk advisory support on key principles of risk management, control, policy and best practice. Designing and delivering training and awareness materials for Investor Services employees on key concepts of risk management, control, policy and best practice, to build risk awareness, risk culture and understanding of fundamental control principles. Fostering a culture of risk transparency, regulatory risk awareness and appropriate risk escalation. Providing support and insights on industry best practice to initiatives impacting the Investor Services line of business. Working closely with wider ERM teams, Office of General Counsel, Compliance and Internal Audit to ensure effective and proactive business risk mitigation. Qualifications: Bachelor’s degree in Finance, Accounting, Law, Risk Management, Business, and/or equivalent work experience. 7+ years relevant industry experience in the asset management or asset servicing industry; with control management or experience within a risk and control function (Audit, Risk, Compliance, Legal or Product Development). Knowledge of regulatory and risk management principles and frameworks inclusive of Risk and Control Self-Assessment, control testing and incident management practices. An inquiring mind with an eye for detail, and strong analytical skills pertaining to risk identification and solutioning. Excellent written and verbal communication skills, with strong collaboration and interpersonal skills. Self-motivation with ability to manage multiple tasks simultaneously, while meeting deadlines.

The position will develop, prepare, and analyze financial and operating data while performing complex cash and general accounting duties. Including, but not limited to performing extensive research and advanced problem-solving functions. JOB RESPONSIBILITIES: Research and resolve variances from reconciliations, with limited assistance using advanced problem-solving skills. Monitor and initiate accounting transactions. Monitor transactional activity at system level to ensure validity of data transmissions. Responsible for accuracy of data across a variety of processes and data flows from a reporting standpoint. Involved in cross-departmental projects and process evaluations, including audits. This includes testing data, training team and documenting changes. Provide guidance and training support to a variety of associates. Reconcile complex accounts, researching and resolving variances. Provide information to other areas of the company. Process and distribute reports as necessary and effectively communicate appropriate information to supervisor/manager. Research and respond to inquiries from internal departments, as well as outside agencies, on accounting and transactional issues. Research and respond to inquiries from customers and suppliers. Maintain files on problem accounts Provide backup support as directed by supervisor. Annual compensation is $24/hr This position offers a hybrid work option. Nelnet values flexibility and understands the importance of work-life integration. Our hybrid work environment allows associates living within 30 miles of an office location to work remotely for part of the week, while also fostering collaboration and team connection through in-office presence three days per week. Please note that we are unable to provide visa sponsorship for this position. To be considered, candidates must already be authorized to work in the United States without the need for current or future sponsorship. EDUCATION: 4-year degree in accounting preferred EXPERIENCE: 3+ years of accounting experience, including reconciliation experience. COMPETENCIES – SKILLS/KNOWLEDGE/ABILITIES: Proficient skills in Microsoft Office, including Excel, Word, and Outlook. Ability to update and create Excel documents using a variety of formulas and Excel tools. Ability to maintain accountability, confidentiality, ethics, integrity and share information with resources appropriately. Ability to work independently with initiative and appropriate supervision. Strong attention to detail. Possess good judgement in dealing with business duties. Possess good analytical skills. Able to be flexible and adaptable while managing multiple priorities. Strong oral and written communications. Strong knowledge of accounting practices and procedures.

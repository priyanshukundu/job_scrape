The Program Risk Manager will lead and manage risk and opportunity management activities across a large, complex defense program operating within a matrixed organization of engineering, manufacturing, supply chain, program management, finance, and other functional teams. This individual will be responsible for establishing and maintaining a comprehensive program risk management process, ensuring risks are proactively identified, assessed, quantified, mitigated, monitored, and appropriately escalated. The Risk Manager will work directly with program leadership, Integrated Product Teams (IPTs), Control Account Managers (CAMs), and functional stakeholders to maintain an accurate program risk register, develop effective mitigation strategies, and provide meaningful insight into technical, schedule, cost, and program execution risks. They will use stakeholder input during the risk management process to uncover potential synergies, resources, or efficient technologies that align with business objectives to create opportunities that offset program risks. This position will also lead the preparation and facilitation of program Risk Management Boards, coordinate risk reviews with internal and external stakeholders, and develop consolidated risk reporting to support timely and informed leadership decisions. Primary Responsibilities: Lead the program-wide risk and opportunity management process, ensuring consistent implementation across divisions, IPTs, and functional organizations. Develop, maintain, and manage the consolidated program risk register, including risk identification, assessment, mitigation, monitoring, and closure for technical. cost, and schedule risks. Facilitate recurring Risk Working Groups, Pre-Board reviews, and formal Risk Management Boards. Coordinate with risk owners to develop actionable mitigation plans, including clearly defined responsibilities, milestones, target dates, and measurable outcomes. Evaluate technical, schedule, cost, manufacturing, supply chain, and operational risks that may impact program performance. Monitor risk trends, mitigation progress, and emerging concerns; identify risks requiring leadership intervention or escalation. Integrate risk assessments with the Integrated Master Schedule (IMS), program cost performance, and major contractual milestones. Coordinate with program management and finance to evaluate risk exposure and support management reserve planning and recommendations. Develop basis of estimates that quantify mitigation strategies. Identify cross-functional and cross-division dependencies that may introduce additional program risk. Develop and maintain risk dashboards, metrics, trend analysis, and executive-level risk reporting. Prepare Risk Management Status Reports and other required internal and customer deliverables. Track Risk Management Board decisions, action items, and approved risk changes to ensure timely implementation. Continuously evaluate and improve risk management processes, tools, and reporting effectiveness. Basic Qualifications: Bachelor’s degree in Engineering, Systems Engineering, Business, Project Management, or a related discipline, with experience requirements aligned to the approved job level. Demonstrated experience performing risk management on large-scale Department of Defense programs throughout the system development or production lifecycle. Working knowledge of formal risk management processes, including risk identification, probability and consequence assessments, mitigation planning, and risk monitoring. Experience working in a matrixed organization and coordinating with multidisciplinary engineering, manufacturing, and program management teams. Demonstrated ability to facilitate risk reviews and communicate program risks and mitigation strategies to senior leadership. Strong analytical, organizational, written, and verbal communication skills. Ability to independently manage competing priorities and drive accountability across cross-functional teams. Willingness to work in or relocate to the Huntsville, Alabama area. Preferred Qualifications: Experience leading risk management activities for large-scale DoD development, integration, or production programs. Experience with Integrated Master Schedules (IMS), Earned Value Management (EVM), and program cost and schedule performance. Experience evaluating quantitative risk exposure and supporting management reserve assessments. Understanding of DoD acquisition processes, systems engineering lifecycle reviews, and program execution requirements. Experience managing risks associated with engineering development, manufacturing readiness, production transitions, or supply chain performance. Experience developing risk dashboards, executive reporting, and automated tracking tools. Familiarity with Risk Management Boards, customer risk reporting, and program governance.

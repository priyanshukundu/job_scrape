Position location: Austin, TX Applicants must be currently authorized to work in the United States on a full-time basis. Western Union will not sponsor applicants for work visas for this position. As a Senior Software Engineer at Western Union, you will play a key role in our platform modernization journey across North America (NA) and Latin America & Caribbean (LACA). Leveraging AI coding agents and modern engineering practices, you will design, develop, and enhance applications, internal tools, workflow automations, and platform capabilities that address customer, agent, operational, and business needs. This role focuses on delivering high-quality software solutions while applying engineering best practices, cloud technologies, and AI-assisted development to accelerate delivery and improve outcomes. You will contribute to the modernization of legacy platforms into scalable, secure, cloud-native solutions by developing AWS microservices, cloud data solutions, distributed caching implementations, and event-driven services. Working closely with engineering teams, product managers, architects, and business stakeholders, you will help build reliable, high-performing platform capabilities that improve operational efficiency, support business growth, and enhance customer experiences across the region. In this role, you will: Contribute to payment platform modernization initiatives across North America (NA), Latin America & Caribbean (LACA) regions. Design, develop, test, and support applications, workflow automations, internal tools, APIs, and platform services using AI-assisted engineering practices. Develop and modernize services using Java, Spring Boot, APIs, event-driven architectures, and cloud-native design patterns. Build and maintain AWS-based microservices and cloud-native applications that meet performance, reliability, and scalability requirements. Develop and support cloud-native data solutions using PostgreSQL, Aurora, DynamoDB, and related technologies. Implement and optimize distributed caching solutions using Redis, Valkey, ElastiCache, or similar technologies. Collaborate with product, operations, architecture, risk, compliance, QA, and customer care teams to deliver solutions from requirements through production deployment. Leverage AI-assisted development tools to improve software development, testing, documentation, and engineering productivity. Implement telemetry, monitoring, and observability capabilities to improve platform health, reliability, and customer experience. Review and validate AI-generated code to ensure adherence to security, quality, compliance, and engineering standards. Participate in code reviews, technical design discussions, and architecture reviews. Support continuous improvement initiatives by contributing reusable components, engineering standards, and best practices. Mentor junior engineers and help foster a culture of collaboration, learning, and engineering excellence. Experience contributing to application modernization, cloud migration, or legacy transformation initiatives . 1+ years of experience working in Agile software development environments. Preferred Qualifications 1+ years of experience with containerization, CI/CD pipelines, observability tools, and infrastructure automation. Experience building highly available, scalable, and resilient distributed systems. Hands-on experience using AI coding agents or AI-assisted development tools to improve engineering productivity and software delivery. Experience developing RESTful APIs and event-driven services using Kafka or similar messaging platforms. Experience in Payments, FinTech, Banking, Remittance, or other high-volume transaction processing environments. Experience supporting multi-region or globally distributed platforms. Understanding of cloud security, authentication, authorization, and secure software development practices. Experience participating in platform modernization and cloud migration programs.

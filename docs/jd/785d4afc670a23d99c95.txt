About this role At Gartner, we’re searching for passionate and versatile software engineers to join our User Platform (UPT) team. As a member of UPT, you’ll play a crucial role in shaping how our clients securely access Gartner’s web, mobile, and internal platforms—including G.com, Tools, GPI, and more. Our team is at the heart of authentication and authorization, leveraging industry-standard protocols like OAuth and SAML to ensure a seamless and secure user experience. We embrace a modern microservices architecture on AWS Cloud, and you’ll have the opportunity to work with cutting-edge technologies while collaborating with talented colleagues across the organization. If you thrive in a fast-paced environment and enjoy solving complex challenges that impact real users, we’d love to hear from you. What you will do: Build and maintain backend services using Java Spring or similar J2EE frameworks. Design and implement RESTful APIs/microservices, and work across the full stack to deliver robust web applications. Work with Authentication and Authorization protocols like OAuth, SAML Develop engaging, responsive user interfaces with ReactJS or similar JavaScript frameworks, and HTML/CSS. Take ownership of your work, ensuring successful and timely delivery. Thrive in a fast-paced Agile/Kanban environment, collaborating closely with teammates. Follow best coding practices, and create thorough unit and integration tests, as well as deployment scripts. Help define and improve software architecture, working with technical leads to identify opportunities for enhancement. Continuously seek ways to improve the user experience, software architecture, or adopt new technologies. Leverage your experience with open-source technologies and applications. What you will need: A bachelor’s or master’s degree in computer science or a related engineering field, along with hands-on software development experience. At least 6 years of experience building customer-facing web applications. 5+ years working with Java Spring or similar J2EE frameworks. Good understanding of Authentication and Authorization frameworks Deep understanding of RESTful API principles and security best practices. Solid experience with unit testing and end-to-end testing. Strong skills in front-end development (JavaScript, React, HTML, CSS) are a plus. Bonus points if you have experience with Docker, Any cloud AWS/GCP/Azure, CI/CD tools like Jenkins, or Kubernetes. Excellent communication and interpersonal skills. A genuine passion for learning, including DevOps and new technologies. What you’ll receive: Competitive compensation.

The Senior Risk, Money Desk & Cash Analyst supports the Bank's liquidity management, member funding operations, and regulatory compliance activities. This role is responsible for cash position analysis, advance and letter of credit processing, OFAC/AML reviews, safekeeping administration, and other critical operational functions that support the Bank and its members while ensuring compliance with regulatory and internal requirements. Accountabilities: Responsibilities/Duties/Functions/Tasks: Analyze and manage daily cash positions across Federal Reserve, Office of Finance, and correspondent bank accounts. Calculate daily investable cash balances and support liquidity management activities. Review and evaluate OFAC and AML monitoring results and take appropriate action when warranted. Process advance transactions, provide product support, and coordinate funding and prepayment activities. Administer letters of credit, including issuance, modifications, beneficiary maintenance, and related documentation. Process member and Bank safekeeping transactions. Perform record retention activities in accordance with Bank policies and regulatory requirements. Support operational improvements, risk mitigation efforts, and departmental projects. Support the accurate and timely resolution of customer and internal constituents’ questions and concerns. Offer assistance that complies with our policies, procedures, and business processes. Qualifications: Bachelor's degree in Business, Finance, Accounting, or a related field preferred. Three or more years of experience in banking, treasury, risk management, cash management, or related financial services. Knowledge of liquidity management, payment systems, regulatory compliance, OFAC, and AML practices preferred. Strong analytical, organizational, and problem-solving skills. Excellent attention to detail and ability to work in a highly regulated environment. Demonstrated ability to collaborate effectively and communicate professionally with internal and external stakeholders.

Job Summary In this role, you will provide technical leadership and architectural direction across the iOS ecosystem, building robust applications using Swift, SwiftUI, UIKit, and modern iOS frameworks. You will partner closely with Product, UX, Backend, QA, and Platform Engineering teams to translate complex retail and business requirements into intuitive, reliable, and seamless customer and associate experiences. The ideal candidate brings deep expertise in iOS application architecture, API integration, performance optimization, security, automated testing, and CI/CD, along with a strong understanding of engineering best practices and the complete mobile development lifecycle. As a Lead Engineer, you will define technical standards, influence architecture, lead design and code reviews, mentor engineers, drive engineering excellence, and take end-to-end ownership of solution delivery. Experience building enterprise-scale retail, commerce, checkout, payment, or transaction-oriented applications is highly desirable. II.

Job Description About Law & Regulations The Law & Regulations organization provides strategic legal and compliance services that protect the company and its customers while ensuring adherence to evolving legal, regulatory, privacy, and ethical standards. The team supports critical business functions including Claims Litigation, Legal Operations, Matter Management, Document Management, Regulatory Compliance, and Legal Technology Solutions. ________________________________________ Position Summary We are seeking an accomplished and highly motivated Software Engineer (Full Stack Developer) to join the Law & Regulations Technology team. This position is responsible for the design, development, testing, deployment, and ongoing support of enterprise applications that enable legal operations, matter and document management, compliance initiatives, and business process automation. The successful candidate will demonstrate strong full stack development expertise and hands-on proficiency in React, Java, Spring Boot, microservices, cloud technologies, APIs, and event-driven architectures. This individual will collaborate with cross-functional partners across Legal, Compliance, Product Management, Architecture, Data Engineering, and other business functions to deliver secure, scalable, and innovative technology solutions. This role provides an opportunity to contribute to the modernization of enterprise legal technology while working with contemporary cloud-native platforms and engineering practices. Key Responsibilities Full Stack Application Development Design, develop, test, deploy, and maintain enterprise-grade applications across the technology stack. Build modern, responsive, and scalable web applications using React and modern UI frameworks. Develop secure, high-performing backend services and RESTful APIs using Java and Spring Boot. Write clean, maintainable, reusable, and well-tested code following software engineering best practices. Participate in application modernization and cloud transformation initiatives. Legal Technology and Matter Management Solutions Develop and support Matter Management, Legal Operations, and Document Management solutions. Build integrations between legal platforms, claims systems, document repositories, and enterprise applications. Implement workflow-driven solutions supporting legal intake, matter lifecycle management, document processing, and regulatory compliance. Support litigation, claims management, legal reporting, and operational processes through technology solutions. Backend and API Development Design and develop cloud-native microservices using Java, Spring Boot, and RESTful architectures. Build scalable APIs and enterprise integrations supporting business-critical applications. Develop event-driven solutions utilizing technologies such as Kafka, Azure Service Bus, or equivalent messaging platforms. Implement authentication, authorization, and security controls aligned with enterprise standards. Optimize application performance, reliability, scalability, and availability. Frontend Development Develop responsive user interfaces using React, JavaScript, TypeScript, HTML5, and CSS3. Integrate frontend applications with backend APIs and microservices. Create reusable UI components and implement modern design patterns. Improve usability, accessibility, and overall user experience. Database and Data Management Design and support solutions utilizing MongoDB, SQL Server, Oracle, and document-based databases. Develop and maintain relational and NoSQL data models. Ensure data quality, integrity, performance, and scalability across applications. Cloud and DevOps Contribute to CI/CD pipeline development and deployment automation. Support cloud-native application deployment and operations. Work with containerized applications using Docker and Kubernetes. Participate in Azure and AWS cloud environments. Support monitoring, alerting, logging, observability, and incident management activities. Perform production support, troubleshooting, and root cause analysis. Agile Delivery and Collaboration Participate in Agile ceremonies including sprint planning, backlog refinement, daily standups, sprint reviews, and retrospectives. Collaborate closely with Legal, Compliance, Product Owners, Business Analysts, Architects, and Engineering teams. Translate business requirements into technical solutions. Support testing, releases, upgrades, and post-production activities. Contribute to peer code reviews and continuous improvement initiatives.

Join a Company that Empowers you to Build your Future We are looking for a mid-level Software Engineer to join a domain product team that designs and delivers full-stack business applications and ServiceNow solutions. Our product teams own their services from design through production and operate in a self-service model, consuming capabilities from central platform teams — golden-path IDP templates, shared CI/CD pipelines, and reusable infrastructure modules. You'll own well-scoped projects end-to-end and be expected to self-enable using this platform tooling rather than waiting on hand-offs. Your Responsibilities on the Team Design, build, and ship full-stack features using TypeScript/React on the front end and Go on the backend. Develop and maintain ServiceNow applications, integrations, and workflows that support your product domain. Scaffold and provision new services using IDP templates (e.g., Backstage golden paths) and platform-provided self-service capabilities. Build and maintain your team's CI/CD workflows on shared pipeline foundations (GitHub Actions or similar). Provision and manage your product's infrastructure using platform-provided Terraform modules and IaC practices. Design and build agentic and AI product capabilities — AI agents, MCP servers, and LLM-powered features — ideally leveraging AWS Bedrock. Provide constructive code reviews and help mentor junior engineers. Requirements Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or related field, or equivalent practical experience. 2–5 years of professional software development experience. Proficiency in TypeScript and React, with a track record of shipping production front-end code. Professional experience with a compiled backend language; Go strongly preferred. Solid understanding of REST APIs, relational databases, and distributed systems fundamentals. Comfort operating in a DevOps/self-service model: you build it, you run it. Strong communication skills and the ability to work independently on defined projects. Preferred Qualifications ServiceNow development experience (scripting, Flow Designer, integrations) — highly preferred. Hands-on CI/CD experience with GitHub Actions or similar — highly preferred. Hands-on infrastructure-as-code experience with Terraform or similar — highly preferred. Experience working with internal developer portals such as Backstage (consuming templates, service catalogs, golden paths). Experience building agentic or AI workloads (AI agents, MCP servers, LLM integrations); experience with AWS Bedrock is a significant plus.

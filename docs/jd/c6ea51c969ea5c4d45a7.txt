What you'll do: The Security Risk Officer (SRO) is a key member of the Bank’s security risk function in its second line of defense and is responsible for evaluating and monitoring the Bank's information security posture, physical security of the Bank’s offices and supporting locations, and safety of Bank staff against the Bank’s appetite for those risks. The SRO regularly utilizes strong written communication skills to concisely and effectively inform the Head of Enterprise Risk Management, Chief Risk Officer, and management of the Bank of its external threat environment, current security and safety practices, and the effectiveness of the Bank’s security and safety control design. This position works collaboratively with all business units in evaluating and identifying the Bank’s security, safety and technology risks and balancing those risks across the Bank’s entire risk landscape. This position requires strong verbal and written communication skills to effectively interact with key members of business unit management across the Bank and is expected to lead, participate and present when appropriate in Bank board and management oversight committees. The SRO partners with the business lines to keep technology, security, AI, and data risks within the Bank’s risk appetite and escalates elevated risks as warranted. This Assistant Vice President-level position reports to the Vice President, Technology Risk Officer (TRO) and serves as the TRO’s deputy, leading the Security Risk Program and sharing the Technology Risk Program, including AI and data risk, to prepare for succession to the TRO role. How you'll make an impact: Strengthens the Bank’s resilience and security posture by providing independent oversight of cybersecurity, physical security, staff safety, technology, AI, and data risks, helping ensure emerging threats are identified early and managed within the Bank’s risk appetite. Shapes critical risk decisions across the Bank by translating complex security and technology risks into clear business insights, constructively challenging risk decisions, and providing senior management and Board-level committees with the information needed to make informed decisions. Advances the Bank’s technology risk capabilities for the future by helping lead the Technology Risk Program, strengthening governance around emerging areas such as generative and agentic AI, cloud, data, and third-party technology, while serving as a key deputy and succession pipeline for the Technology Risk Officer. What you can expect: Design, implement, and report on the Security Risk Program as second-line owner of information security, physical security, and staff safety risk, including risk assessments, threat monitoring, and monthly reporting on whether security posture remains within risk appetite. Provide independent challenge to first-line security decisions, risk acceptances, and remediation plans; manage third-party penetration testing and observe cyber incident response, promptly notifying ERM leadership of potential regulatory reporting incidents. Serve as the TRO’s deputy: lead or co-lead components of the Technology Risk Program, including risk assessments, monitoring of technology initiatives, and the quarterly Technology Risk Monitoring Report, and act for the TRO in the TRO’s absence. Support the technology and security roadmaps, frameworks, and procedures used to assess technology risk, with deeper ownership of assigned domains (e.g., identity and access management, cloud, technology resilience); track key initiatives and inform management of changes to residual risk. Serve as the Operational Risk Management (ORM) contact with Information Technology, and represent ERM at the Technology Risk Subcommittee, and Board-level committees as the TRO delegates as necessary. Provide independent second-line oversight of AI/ML risk across the AI lifecycle, aligned to the NIST AI Risk Management Framework, FHFA Advisory Bulletin 2022-02, the Artificial Intelligence Usage Policy, and the Model Risk Management Policy. Participate in AI governance forums to challenge and escalate; with Information Technology, risk-rate the inventory of AI use cases and agents, map the NIST AI RMF to existing controls, and help define AI testing, monitoring, and thresholds. Assess and report on emerging AI risks, including agentic AI, AI embedded in third-party products, AI-enabled cyber threats, and the reliability of AI-generated code and outputs. Provide independent second-line oversight of data risk, including quality, classification, protection, retention, privacy, and regulatory compliance, aligned to the Data Governance and Management Policy. Assess data exposure risks through AI tools, analytics platforms, and data lakes, and partner with Third-Party Risk Management on vendor access to and use of Bank data, including in third-party AI and across borders. Develop and evaluate risk metrics, thresholds, and tolerances for technology, security, AI, and data risk, and maintain the Security Risk Methodology and contribute to the Technology Risk Methodology and related policies. Prepare concise, balanced reports and presentations on the threat environment, risk posture, and control effectiveness for the Chief Risk Officer, management, and Board-level committees.

#Software Engineer - Automotive Platform
Qualcomm
Location: San Diego, California, United States of America
Link: https://careers.qualcomm.com/careers/job/446722119778

Company: Qualcomm Technologies, Inc. Job Area: Engineering Group, Engineering Group > Software Engineering General Summary: ** This position is not eligible for Qualcomm immigration sponsorship. ** General Summary We are looking for a bright, highly motivated, hands-on Software Engineer who is passionate about coding and solving challenging technical problems. The ideal candidate has strong software fundamentals, enjoys writing and debugging code, learns new technologies quickly, and is comfortable working on complex systems. We are less focused on experience with a specific technology or software stack and more interested in finding engineers with strong technical aptitude, curiosity, problem-solving ability, and a genuine passion for software development. The engineer will work with experienced Qualcomm engineers across software, hardware, systems, and product teams to develop and deliver software for Qualcomm Snapdragon platforms. Responsibilities Design, develop, test, debug, and maintain high-quality software. Write clean, efficient, maintainable, and well-structured code. Analyze complex technical problems and develop practical solutions. Debug issues across different layers of the software stack and perform root-cause analysis. Participate in software design, implementation, code reviews, testing, and integration. Develop tools and automation to improve engineering productivity and software quality. Learn new technologies and software domains quickly as project requirements evolve. Work closely with software, systems, hardware, and validation engineers to solve cross-functional technical problems. Take ownership of assigned features and technical problems from initial investigation through implementation and validation. Continuously improve software quality, development processes, and engineering practices. Minimum Qualifications: • Bachelor's degree in Engineering, Information Systems, Computer Science, or related field. Preferred Qualifications Master's degree in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, Electrical Engineering, or a related technical field. Strong programming and software-development fundamentals. Proficiency in at least one general-purpose programming language such as C, C++, Java, Python, or Rust . Good understanding of data structures, algorithms, operating-system concepts, memory management, concurrency, and software design principles. Strong analytical and problem-solving skills. Ability and willingness to debug unfamiliar code and complex technical problems. Demonstrated passion for programming through academic work, professional experience, open-source contributions, personal projects, hackathons, or other hands-on development. Ability to learn new technologies and technical domains quickly. Strong communication and teamwork skills. Strong C/C++ programming and debugging skills. Experience with embedded systems, operating systems, Linux, Android, QNX, or other system software. Familiarity with multithreaded and concurrent software development. Experience developing software on ARM-based or other SoC platforms. Experience with Git, code reviews, CI/CD, unit testing, and automated testing. Exposure to hardware/software integration and system-level debugging. Experience with automotive, robotics, AI/ML, multimedia, graphics, camera, connectivity, or other complex embedded systems is a plus. Qualcomm is an equal opportunity employer.

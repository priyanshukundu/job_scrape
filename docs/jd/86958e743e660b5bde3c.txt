The Associate Furniture Planner is responsible for coordinating and managing furniture logistics, move management, and space change projects across a multi-site portfolio. This role oversees project execution from planning through completion, ensuring furniture installations, relocations, inventory management, and warehouse operations are delivered efficiently and in alignment with client requirements, project schedules, and workplace standards. The Planner serves as a key liaison between clients, vendors, facilities teams, and project stakeholders to ensure seamless project delivery and operational continuity. What you'll do Manage furniture logistics, space change, and workplace relocation projects from initiation through closeout. Coordinate project schedules, milestones, resources, and stakeholder communications to ensure successful delivery. Serve as the primary point of contact for clients, vendors, facilities teams, and project stakeholders. Oversee furniture procurement coordination, delivery scheduling, installation activities, and move execution. Manage inventory tracking, warehouse operations, asset records, and material logistics. Coordinate cross-functional teams to support workplace changes, occupancy initiatives, and operational requirements. Monitor project budgets, risks, issues, and performance metrics, providing regular status updates. Ensure compliance with workplace standards, safety requirements, and quality expectations. Support move planning, space utilization efforts, and workplace transformation initiatives. Identify and implement process improvements to enhance logistics efficiency and project execution. Bachelor's degree in Facilities Management, Supply Chain, Logistics, Business, Interior Design, Project Management, or a related field, preferred. 2+ years of experience in furniture logistics, move management, workplace services, facilities, or project coordination. Experience coordinating vendors, furniture installations, warehouse operations, and inventory management. Strong organizational, analytical, and stakeholder management skills. Proficiency in Microsoft Office Suite and workplace/project management systems. Excellent communication, problem-solving, and customer service abilities.

About the Team: The Global Servicing Decision Science (GSDS) team is the Decision Science engine for Global Servicing, bringing together advanced analytics, data science, AI/GenAI, and decisioning to transform servicing experiences, empower colleagues, and enable intelligent operations. GSDS serves as a centralized Decision Science Center of Excellence, partnering across Product/Capabilities, Control Management, Strategy, Operations, Technology, and enterprise analytics and data science teams. GSDS builds and scales servicing intelligence across Global Servicing—from predictive and proactive servicing, personalization, next-best-action and journey orchestration to conversational AI, agent assist, AI-powered coaching, conduct and quality monitoring, and complaint and dispute intelligence. Our mandate is to build durable, production-ready capabilities that continuously improve decisions through prediction, recommendation, optimization, experimentation, and learning. How will you make an impact in this role? As a Senior Manager, you will lead end-to-end Decision Science work that helps build the next generation of servicing intelligence at American Express. You will translate complex customer, colleague, and operational challenges into analytical strategies, models, experiments, and scalable AI-driven solutions, partnering across business and Technology teams to move capabilities from concept through implementation and optimization. This role combines hands-on technical leadership with strong business problem solving. You will help advance modern capabilities—including Agentic AI, transformer-based recommendation systems, and Conversational AI—while guiding project teams, influencing partners, and ensuring solutions deliver measurable customer and business outcomes.

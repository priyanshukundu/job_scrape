Ingredion is hiring an Associate Quality Analyst who will join our team and play a critical role in ensuring product quality, food safety, and customer satisfaction through laboratory testing and analysis. What you will do: Support manufacturing operations by conducting laboratory testing, maintaining accurate records, and assisting with quality investigations and special projects. Collect and test production samples within established timelines, accurately recording results in SAP and maintaining retained samples as required. Maintain complete, accurate, and legible laboratory records in accordance with company policies and record retention procedures. Perform routine cleaning and housekeeping activities for laboratory work areas and testing equipment. Assist with basic laboratory equipment troubleshooting, maintenance, and calibration activities. Support customer pre-shipment sample requests by obtaining, packaging, and shipping samples through the sample order system. Prepare and ship samples to external laboratories for specialized testing. Conduct testing and analysis in support of special projects, process improvements, and nonconforming product investigations. Provide laboratory coverage for open shifts and staffing gaps as needed. Assist in maintaining, reviewing, and updating laboratory procedures and work instructions. Support food safety, quality, and continuous improvement initiatives. Perform additional duties and responsibilities as assigned. Who you are: Associate degree or bachelor's degree in Chemistry, Biology, Food Science, Microbiology, Engineering, or a related technical field preferred. Previous laboratory, quality control, manufacturing, food, beverage, agricultural, or industrial experience preferred. Experience working with computerized systems and data entry; SAP experience is a plus. Strong attention to detail, organization, and recordkeeping skills. Ability to interpret procedures, follow test methods, and communicate results effectively. Basic troubleshooting and problem-solving abilities. Ability to work independently and collaboratively in a fast-paced manufacturing environment. Willingness to work flexible schedules and provide shift coverage when needed. Why Join Ingredion?

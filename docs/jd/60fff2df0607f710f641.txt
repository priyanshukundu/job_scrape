Role Description: In this role, you will solution design, build, and maintain cloud-based data pipelines and data warehouse to support the Sales organization. Working as part of a team of data engineers, you will interact with business stakeholders and other internal teams and take responsibility for leading projects, applying experience and knowledge to optimize pipelines for security, performance and resilience. You will be expected to collaborate with fellow team members, mentor fellow engineers if needed, and continuously learn new technologies and applications that impact our tech stack. A minimum of 3 days per week in the Philadelphia headquarters is required.

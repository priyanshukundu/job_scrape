MongoDB is seeking a Software Engineer 3 to join the Atlas Clusters team. The team is part of the organization responsible for building MongoDB Atlas, our database as a service offering and fastest growing product. Atlas allows users to deploy fault-tolerant, secure, globally distributed MongoDB clusters in just minutes. The Atlas Clusters Workload Management team develops the software that manages the Atlas cluster infrastructure on the three major cloud providers (AWS, Azure, GCP) to provide the best managed database performance experience. We are constantly challenged to improve the performance of Atlas Clusters by advancing our intelligent scaling algorithms, dynamically adjusting resources to optimize cloud provider capacity, automating fleet-wide observability, and working closely with the MongoDB Server team to design new systems at a humongous scale. We are looking to speak to candidates who are based in New York City for our in office or hybrid working model.

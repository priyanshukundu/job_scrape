Your Team's Impact The Real Time Price Development team is responsible for onboarding, maintaining, and enhancing the real-time market data that powers FactSet's products. Working closely with Engineering and Operations teams, the group helps ensure data is accurately integrated, monitored, and delivered to clients. As FactSet continues to grow its real time market data business, this team plays a critical role in delivering reliable, high-quality data that investment professionals rely on to make informed decisions every day. What You'll Do As an Associate Content Specialist, you will help maintain and enhance the real-time market data used across FactSet's products. You'll work closely with Engineering, Operations, and other internal teams to test new functionality, investigate data issues, and ensure data is accurate and functioning as expected. In this role, you will review market data provider notifications, assess the impact of market and content changes, validate newly developed functionality, analyze data anomalies, and collaborate with cross-functional teams to support data quality and process improvements. This is an excellent opportunity for someone interested in financial markets, data, and technology who enjoys problem-solving and wants to make a direct impact on the quality of information delivered to FactSet clients. The role also requires flexibility to work evening hours as needed to support Asia-Pacific market coverage. Responsibilities: Test and validate new real-time market data content and functionality to ensure data accuracy and quality. Support the onboarding, maintenance, and enhancement of real-time market data across FactSet products. Review market data provider notifications and assess the impact of content, market, and feed changes. Investigate data anomalies and partner with Engineering and Operations teams to identify and resolve issues. Define, document, and validate business rules and data requirements for content onboarding and maintenance. Collaborate with cross-functional teams to improve workflows, processes, and the overall client experience. Take ownership of assigned tasks and projects, ensuring deliverables are completed accurately and on time. Develop subject matter expertise in assigned content areas and serve as a resource for data-related questions. What We're Looking For Qualifications: Bachelor’s degree in Finance, Economics, Mathematics, Data Analysis or a related field. Demonstrated knowledge of financial markets and market data concepts. Experience using AI tools (e.g., ChatGPT, Copilot, Gemini, Claude) to support research, problem-solving, content creation, and day-to-day productivity. Experience with SQL, Python, or other programming languages, a plus. Demonstrated ability to learn and apply new concepts, technologies, and processes quickly. Attention to detail with a focus on data accuracy and quality. Strong analytical and problem-solving skills. Strong communication and collaboration skills. Must be legally authorized to work in the United States without the need for employer sponsorship now or at any time in the future.

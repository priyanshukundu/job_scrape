Job Description: Job Summary We are seeking an experienced Senior Financial Analyst to serve as a strategic financial partner across Finance, Operations, Procurement, Accounting, and Program Management. This role combines strategic financial analysis with hands-on financial execution, supporting forecasting, P&L management, commercial contracts, cost optimization, financial controls, and complex AI, automation, and operational initiatives. The ideal candidate brings significant professional experience, strong business judgment, and a demonstrated history of progressively increasing responsibility. Practical experience, sustained business impact, and the ability to influence decisions are considered more important than advanced degrees or professional certifications. Key Responsibilities Lead complex financial analysis, forecasting, variance analysis, P&L management, financial planning, and ROI evaluations. Partner with business and Operations leaders to identify financial risks and opportunities, challenge assumptions, and provide clear, actionable recommendations. Prepare and present Quarterly Business Reviews (QBRs) to business and operations leadership, communicating financial performance, key business drivers, forecast changes, risks, opportunities, cost initiatives, and recommended actions. Review Service & Support (S&S) agreements and third-party OEM contracts, evaluating multi-year financial commitments, recurring costs, pricing structures, contractual terms, and overall contract profitability. Partner with procurement to evaluate vendor proposals and contractual terms, identify cost-saving opportunities, and support sourcing strategies that provide the strongest overall financial and commercial value. Evaluate opportunities to consolidate products or services with a single vendor when doing so provides improved pricing, commercial terms, operational efficiency, or overall financial value. Partner with multiple program managers on complex AI, automation, technology, and operational transformation initiatives, developing business cases and evaluating investment requirements, ROI, ongoing costs, productivity opportunities, cost avoidance, risks, and cross-organizational financial impacts. Develop financial models and scenario analyses to support vendor selection, contract negotiations, sourcing decisions, technology investments, automation programs, and other strategic business initiatives. Support Sarbanes-Oxley (SOX) requirements, including execution of financial controls, supporting documentation, control testing, and audit requirements. Prepare, review, and enter Journal Vouchers (JVs) with appropriate supporting documentation and in accordance with accounting policies and internal-control requirements. Collect and review manual system and account reconciliations, investigate discrepancies, identify root causes, and partner with appropriate teams to drive timely resolution. Identify opportunities to automate manual reconciliations, Journal Voucher activities, and other financial processes and controls while maintaining appropriate accuracy, documentation, controls, and auditability. Identify and drive opportunities for cost reduction, operational efficiency, process improvement, automation, and stronger financial controls. Work cross-functionally across Finance, Accounting, Operations, Procurement, and Program Management to understand and communicate the broader financial impact of business decisions. Lead the financial aspects of complex initiatives and provide financial guidance and mentoring to other analysts as appropriate. Qualifications 10+ years of relevant professional experience with progressively increasing levels of responsibility in financial analysis, business finance, operations finance, procurement finance, commercial analysis, accounting, or related disciplines. Career progression should demonstrate increasing scope, complexity, ownership, accountability, and influence over financial and business decisions. Strong experience with financial modeling, forecasting, variance analysis, ROI, business-case development, and evaluation of long-term financial commitments. Demonstrated ability to prepare and present financial and business performance reviews, including QBRs, to business and operational leadership. Experience evaluating commercial contracts, S&S agreements, and multi-year vendor commitments, including the ability to understand the financial implications of pricing and contractual terms. Experience partnering with Procurement on vendor evaluations, sourcing decisions, contract economics, cost optimization, and vendor consolidation opportunities. Experience with SOX requirements, financial controls, Journal Vouchers, manual system or account reconciliations, and financial discrepancy resolution. Experience evaluating the financial impact of AI, automation, technology investments, or operational transformation initiatives is highly valued. Advanced Microsoft Excel, financial modeling, analytical, communication, presentation, and stakeholder-influencing skills. Strong business and commercial acumen, with the ability to operate independently, challenge assumptions, and make fact-based recommendations in a complex business environment. Experience Over Credentials Demonstrated professional experience, sound business judgment, progressively increasing responsibility, and a proven record of delivering financial and business results are considered more important than advanced academic degrees or professional certifications . An MBA, CPA, or other advanced degree or professional certification is valued but not required and does not substitute for relevant professional experience, demonstrated business judgment, and proven business impact. Preferred Experience Experience working within a large, global high-technology organization, including a Fortune 100 company, is highly valued but not required. Experience successfully navigating a large, complex, global, matrixed organization and partnering across Finance, Accounting, Operations, Procurement, and Program Management. A demonstrated history of sustained contribution, career progression, and increasing responsibility within organizations is highly valued. The ideal candidate demonstrates an interest in building long-term organizational knowledge, developing lasting business partnerships, owning multi-year initiatives, and continuing to grow their career within the company while assuming broader responsibilities over time. Work Environment and Schedule Expectations This role requires close partnership with Operations, Finance, and other cross-functional organizations. In-office presence may be required up to four days per week , with the specific schedule determined by operations partner needs, business and project requirements, collaboration needs, and management discretion. Due to the global nature of the organization and regular collaboration with Operations and Finance partners in India, candidates must be able to accommodate early-morning and late-evening meetings or working sessions when required to support global business activities and time-zone differences. The position may require occasional weekend work to support urgent deliverables, critical financial deadlines, quarter-end activities, high-priority projects, or other time-sensitive operational requirements. Occasional overnight business travel may be required to support operational partners, business reviews, projects, or other business requirements. Candidates should be comfortable operating in a fast-paced, global environment requiring flexibility, responsiveness, independent ownership, and collaboration across organizations, geographies, and time zones.

BUILDING THE FUTURE OF OPEN FINANCE


Payward - the parent company behind Kraken, NinjaTrader, Breakout,  xStocks, Payward Services and CF Benchmarks - has spent the last 15 years building one of the most modern and globally accessible financial infrastructure platforms in the industry, built to advance an open, global financial system.




THE TEAM

Founded in 2011, Kraken is one of the world's longest-standing crypto platforms, trusted by over 10 million individuals and institutions across the globe. It offers spot trading, margin, futures, staking, and OTC services, with products built for both individual investors and institutional clients.

Payward is seeking a Sr. Director, Enterprise Risk (Head of Enterprise Risk) to own the enterprise risk program end to end, from strategy and tooling through execution and Board reporting. We expect you to set the direction, build the machine, and deliver without needing us to define the work for you. It will interact with leaders and teams across the organization, including Finance, Legal, Compliance, HR, Operations, Sales, Engineering, and IT.



THE OPPORTUNITY

 - Own the ERM program end to end: framework, policy, governance, risk appetite, and a risk taxonomy built for a multi-entity, multi-jurisdiction crypto and fintech group

 - Define and secure Board and executive approval of risk appetite and tolerances, and embed them into how the business makes decisions

 - Design and run the enterprise risk assessment cycle and emerging-risk reviews (e.g., custody, liquidity, market structure, stablecoin and regulatory change, third-party concentration, AI, geopolitical), and own the enterprise risk register

 - Build Payward’s risk metrics, KRIs and analytics from scratch: data sourcing, thresholds, escalation triggers, and dashboards that leaders actually use

 - Lead the implementation and adoption of a company-wide GRC platform, owning vendor, budget, integrations and rollout

 - Run risk incident oversight: challenge root cause and remediation plans, ensure they are complete and durable, and track them to closure

 - Run ERM program management, including quarterly planning and cadence, and author executive, Risk Committee and Board-level reporting

 - Act as an effective challenger to first-line owners while keeping risk ownership clearly with the business

 - Partner with Finance, Legal and Compliance on IPO readiness: public-company risk disclosures, control environment expectations, and regulator and auditor interactions

 - Hire, coach and lead a team of risk professionals. You’ll set the team’s structure, standards and roadmap

 - Keep Payward ahead of evolving practice, regulatory expectations and industry loss events


WHAT YOU BRING

 - 12+ years in enterprise risk, risk and controls, or operational risk at a financial institution, exchange, fintech or Big 4 advisory firm, including 5+ years leading an ERM program or function

 - Track record of building or materially re-platforming an ERM program (framework, appetite, KRIs, reporting), not just operating an existing one

 - Demonstrated experience presenting to executives, Boards and Audit/Risk Committees, and influencing senior leaders who didn’t want to be influenced

 - Hands-on experience selecting and implementing a GRC platform at scale

 - Strong quantitative and data skills: SQL, BI tools (Tableau preferred), and the judgment to turn noisy data into decisions

 - Working knowledge of COSO ERM, ISO 31000 and the three-lines model, and how regulators examine risk functions

 - Crypto and digital-asset experience: you understand custody, trading, liquidity and on-chain risk at a practical level

 - Proven people leader: built and developed teams, set clear objectives and drove results

 - Exceptional written and verbal communication, with the ability to translate complex risk into clear business trade-offs

 - Excellent program management: you drive multi-quarter, cross-functional initiatives to completion

 - Bachelor’s degree in Accounting, Finance, Risk Management, Data Analytics or a related field preferred


NICE TO HAVES

 - Experience in a high-growth, pre-IPO fintech or crypto company, including IPO readiness and public-company risk experience

 - Experience with regulated financial entities across multiple jurisdictions (e.g., US, EU/MiCA, UK)

 - FRM, CRISC, CERA, CRM, CPA or equivalent

 - Experience with quantitative risk methods (scenario analysis, stress testing, FAIR)

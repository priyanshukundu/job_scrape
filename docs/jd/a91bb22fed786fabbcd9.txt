With a career at The Home Depot, you can be yourself and also be part of something bigger. Position Purpose: The Principal Data Scientist is responsible for leading high-impact data science and AI initiatives that drive business growth, improve operational efficiency, and create better customer and associate experiences. A key focus of this role will be advancing AI-powered customer support and agentic workflows that apply the reasoning, context, and decision-making patterns used by skilled associates to help customers resolve complex needs. These capabilities are designed to provide customers with faster, more effective self-service experiences while improving resolution quality and reducing the cost to serve. As a Principal Data Scientist, you will lead the design and development of end-to-end AI and data science solutions, including predictive models, generative AI applications, agentic workflows, decisioning and orchestration capabilities. You will identify opportunities to apply the right combination of machine learning, large language models, optimization, business rules, enterprise data, and APIs to create intelligent solutions that can reason across customer needs, take appropriate actions, and integrate with production systems. You will partner closely with Product, Engineering, Architecture, and business teams to translate complex customer and operational problems into scalable AI capabilities. You will help refine how AI workflows understand intent, make decisions, use enterprise tools and data, and determine the appropriate next action. You will guide solutions from experimentation and proof of concept through production deployment, while establishing rigorous approaches for evaluation, experimentation, monitoring, reliability, and continuous improvement. The Principal Data Scientist will also provide technical leadership across data science teams, mentor other data scientists, develop reusable AI capabilities and intellectual property, and help shape the adoption of emerging AI technologies across The Home Depot. This role contributes to building strong data science talent through mentorship, recruiting, and hiring while maintaining connections with academic and industry experts to advance the application of AI and data science to complex business problems.

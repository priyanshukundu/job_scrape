In this hybrid role based at our Chicago headquarters you will investigate debit and credit fraud disputes in accordance to REG E, REG Z, and FCRA. Identify fraud trends and report to leadership to help mitigate fraud gaps. Research and resolve member questions and support internal departments. Essential Responsibilities Analyze fraud dispute cases and support documentation received to determine if the transaction(s) is a valid dispute. Make sound conclusions on claims based on investigations performed. Ensure debit and credit dispute notifications are processed accurately and timely according to REG E and REG Z. Provide phone and email support to internal partners and members on debit and credit fraud claim matters. Work with the fraud strategy team to develop business rules to monitor, identify and remediate fraudulent trends. Research and assist management with member complaints regarding credit and debit card disputes. Assist with completing root cause analysis on account losses in order to identify possible fraud gaps.

Job Description Every day at FIS, innovators, problem solvers and changemakers work together to create technology that advances the world of finance. We embrace new ideas, challenge what's possible and empower our teams to make a lasting impact. If you're ready to help shape the future of fintech, unlock your next chapter with FIS. Role location: Required hybrid schedule (2-3 days in-office, 2-3 days virtual) in one of our talent centers. Jacksonville, Atlanta or Milwaukee About the role: As a Business Analyst II , you’ll provide high-level analysis and insights that help shape business and product decisions. You’ll evaluate complex business needs, identify market and operational trends, develop forecasts, and recommend solutions that improve performance and efficiency. Working with cross-functional stakeholders, you’ll help translate complex data and business challenges into clear, actionable strategies. Your key responsibilities: You will lead complex business analyses to identify trends, risks, opportunities, and areas for improvement. You will evaluate business requirements and recommend strategic solutions that align with organizational goals. You will analyze market, industry, competitor, and client trends to support business and product decisions. You will develop forecasts, models, and comprehensive analyses to evaluate potential business strategies and future outcomes. You will partner with cross-functional stakeholders to influence decisions and drive process, system, and operational improvements. You will independently manage complex assignments and support the successful implementation of recommended solutions. You will provide guidance and coaching to less experienced professionals while helping ensure high-quality, accurate analysis. What you bring: Required qualifications Strong experience in business analysis, business intelligence, data analysis, or a related field, including experience independently solving complex business problems. Advanced analytical and critical-thinking skills with experience translating complex data and business information into actionable recommendations. Experience with forecasting, trend analysis, business requirements, and developing comprehensive analyses to support strategic decisions. Demonstrated ability to partner with and influence cross-functional stakeholders while managing competing priorities with limited supervision. Strong written and verbal communication skills with the ability to clearly present complex findings and recommendations to business stakeholders. Preferred qualifications Experience supporting strategic business initiatives, process transformation, or system implementations. Experience with market, competitor, industry, or client trend analysis. Experience guiding, coaching, or reviewing the work of other analysts. The impact you’ll make: As a Business Analyst II , you’ll help teams make confident decisions by turning complex information into meaningful business insights. You’ll work across the organization to evaluate opportunities, anticipate trends, and identify solutions that improve business performance. Your expertise will help strengthen decision-making, improve processes, and support the successful execution of strategic initiatives. At FIS, you’ll have opportunities to grow your career, collaborate with talented colleagues, and make an impact that reaches far beyond your role.

About the Team/Role Our Team: The Global Risk Solutions and Strategy group is a fast-growing team optimizing risk solutions, models, and strategies, and we are a key function in enabling WEX’s strategic objectives. The Team combines data science, risk expertise, technology, and a wide range of data sources to develop solutions that inform decision-making across the client lifecycle. Our team helps the firm identify, measure, and manage credit and fraud risk while enabling scalable, data-driven risk strategies. As a member of the team, you will work at the intersection of data, risk strategy, data science, and technology. You will partner closely with data scientists, risk strategists, data engineers, technology teams, and business stakeholders to ensure our teams have trusted, scalable, well-understood data and features to support analytics, modeling, and risk decisioning. Our Company: WEX is a fast-growing multinational payments company based in beautiful Portland, Maine. WEX headquarters is situated in the heart of Portland amongst some of the best restaurants in the country and overlooking the ocean and lighthouses. When you are here, you know you are in Maine. We have generous paid time off and paid volunteering time, not to mention great benefits and a culture that values diversity and inclusion. Who You Are You are a data and risk subject matter expert who understands how data moves through complex source systems and how it ultimately needs to support analytics, data science, and risk decisioning. You are naturally curious about how data is generated, transformed, and consumed, and you are skilled at connecting technical teams with the business and risk context behind the data. You thrive in environments where data is distributed across multiple systems and teams, and where creating clarity, consistency, and trust is essential. You are comfortable challenging assumptions, establishing common definitions, identifying data quality issues, and working with data engineering teams to develop scalable solutions. You are also passionate about enablement and adoption. You see the feature store not simply as a technology platform, but as a critical capability for scaling data science and risk strategy. You will serve as the primary liaison between our team and partner organizations, helping them understand, adopt, and contribute to the feature store while ensuring that its capabilities evolve to meet the needs of data science and risk. How you'll make an impact Data & Risk Enablement Serve as a trusted data and risk subject matter expert for data scientists, risk strategists, engineers, and business stakeholders. Develop a deep understanding of critical source systems, data structures, business processes, and data flows that support credit, fraud, and other risk strategies. Translate business and risk requirements into clear data requirements, helping teams identify the right data sources, transformations, features, and analytical approaches. Establish and promote common definitions for key risk, customer, account, transaction, and performance measures to improve consistency across analytics and decisioning. Identify opportunities to improve the availability, usability, quality, and scalability of data used by risk and data science teams. Act as a bridge between data consumers and data engineering teams, helping prioritize and communicate data requirements and technical needs. Data Pipeline Optimization Partner closely with data engineering teams to design, optimize, and improve pipelines supporting risk analytics, modeling, and strategy. Identify bottlenecks, redundancies, data quality issues, and opportunities to improve the efficiency and reliability of existing data pipelines. Help establish scalable data patterns that reduce duplicated data preparation and enable data scientists and risk strategists to focus on higher-value analytical work. Participate in the design and review of data architecture and pipeline changes to ensure they meet downstream analytical and risk requirements. Proactively identify upstream data changes or source-system dependencies that could impact models, strategies, reporting, or monitoring. Develop strong relationships with source-system owners and downstream data consumers to improve transparency and accountability across the data ecosystem. Feature Store Optimization Translate and support the relationship between the Risk Solutions and Strategy organization and the broader feature store ecosystem. Serve as the primary liaison to external teams supporting or consuming the feature store, representing the needs of data science and risk strategy. Support the feature store across data science, risk, and partner organizations by identifying barriers and creating practical solutions. Establish and promote best practices for feature development, documentation, reuse, governance, and consumption. Partner with teams to retain and monitor feature definitions, metadata, lineage, quality, ownership, and lifecycle management. Work with data scientists and engineers to identify opportunities to migrate duplicated or manually maintained features into reusable, governed feature-store assets. Gather feedback from internal users and translate it into enhancements, capabilities, and priorities for the feature store. Promote reuse of existing features and reduce unnecessary duplication across models and analytical solutions. Help ensure that features are discoverable, well documented, trusted, and fit for use in production risk applications. Data Quality & Governance Establish a strong quality mindset around data used for risk decisioning and model development. Investigate and coordinate resolution of data issues across source systems, pipelines, and downstream consumers. Partner with data governance and technology teams to improve data lineage, ownership, documentation, and controls. Identify gaps in data definitions and work across teams to establish consistent business and technical definitions. Proactively communicate data risks, dependencies, and limitations that could affect analytical or risk outcomes.

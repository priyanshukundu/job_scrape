Citibank, N.A. seeks a Treasury Risk Senior Officer I for its Tampa, FL location. DUTIES: Provide independent risk oversight and analysis across multiple asset classes, including private equity, venture capital, and a portfolio of fixed income investments related to insurance activities. Conduct independent risk assessments of new and existing investment transactions by reviewing investment structures, business models, financial information, and key risk drivers to support risk review and approval processes. Develop new financial models, review and challenge existing financial models, including assessing assumptions, inputs and methodologies to determine the reasonableness of the model outputs and conclusions. Analyze corporate financial statements (including balance sheet, income statement, and cashflow statement) to assess the reasonableness of valuation projections and investment risk profiles. Perform ongoing portfolio level risk monitoring through periodic reviews focused on underlying asset class risk profile, performance trends, valuation dynamics, and emerging risks. Prepare independent risk views for governance and review forums, evaluate changes to internal risk classifications, identifies early warning indicators requiring escalation, and support the development and maintenance of investment and risk management policies to ensure consistent application of risk frameworks and alignment with governance standards and regulatory expectations. A telecommuting/hybrid work schedule may be permitted within a commutable distance from the worksite in accordance with Citi policies and protocols. REQUIREMENTS: Bachelor’s degree, or foreign equivalent, in Finance, Mathematics, Financial Engineering, or a related field, and ten (10) years of experience in the job offered or in a related finance occupation managing investment risks. Ten (10) years of experience must include: Applying multi-asset investment product expertise across fixed income, securitized products, public equity, and private equity to conduct risk assessments, including evaluating underlying assets, product structures, and key risk drivers to support investment and risk management decisions; Analyzing corporate financial statements, including income statements, balance sheets, and cash flow statements, to assess financial performance, liquidity, and capital structure for investment and risk evaluation purposes; Performing new quantitative financial models and reviewing and challenging existing financial models, including assessing assumptions, inputs, and methodologies to determine the reasonableness of model outputs and conclusions; Conducting portfolio risk monitoring and performance analysis, including evaluating portfolio-level risk exposures, individual positions, portfolio and individual performance, and emerging risks to support risk assessment and identify potential escalation matters; and Developing and maintaining investment and risk management policies, including drafting, updating, and implementing policies and procedures to support consistent risk assessment practices and governance. 40 hrs./wk.

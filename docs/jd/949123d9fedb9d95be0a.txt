Your Opportunity At Schwab, you’re empowered to make an impact on your career. Here, innovative thought meets creative problem solving, helping us challenge the status quo and transform the finance industry together. Technology Risk Management provides independent oversight of the firm’s technology, cybersecurity, data, and operational resilience risks. As Director of AI and Business-Led Development Risk Oversight, you will shape how Schwab identifies, assesses, and manages risks associated with employee-developed technology, generative AI, AI-enabled tools, automated workflows, and end-user solutions. You will develop practical risk frameworks, evaluate governance and control coverage, and provide credible challenge for higher-risk solutions throughout their lifecycle. Success requires sound decision making, analytical thinking, and the ability to translate emerging technology risks into clear recommendations. Working across technology, cybersecurity, data, risk, and business teams, you will influence senior leaders, strengthen governance, and help Schwab innovate responsibly while maintaining an independent Second Line of Defense perspective. This is an individual contributor role requiring strong technical risk experience, sound judgment, and the ability to influence across a large organization. The Director will develop the risk framework, assess governance and control coverage, review higher-risk solutions, and provide clear conclusions to senior leaders and governance committees. What You’ll Do Develop and maintain a risk framework for employee AI and Business-Led Development that aligns with the firm’s risk taxonomy, policies, standards, RCSA risks, and controls. Translate new technology capabilities into practical risk scenarios, control expectations, monitoring needs, and escalation criteria. Assess risks involving data exposure, inappropriate access, non-human identities, unreliable output, insecure development, operational resilience, insider misuse, third-party services, and unauthorized technology use. Determine which policies, standards, procedures, and controls apply to employee-built and AI-enabled solutions. Identify gaps, conflicts, or unclear ownership within the existing governance structure and recommend appropriate action. Provide independent review and credible challenge of higher-risk solutions from initial intake through retirement. Evaluate solution ownership, risk classification, data handling, access, connectors, testing, human review, monitoring, change management, incident response, and residual-risk decisions. Determine whether controls are appropriately designed, supported by evidence, and proportionate to the risk. Analyze trends, incidents, exceptions, control weaknesses, and emerging concentrations of risk. Prepare concise assessments and recommendations for senior management and governance committees. Monitor regulatory developments, supervisory expectations, industry practices, and changes in AI and business-developed technology. Work with technology, cybersecurity, data, operational resilience, Model Risk Oversight, Enterprise Risk Management, AI governance, and business leaders while maintaining an independent Second Line of Defense perspective. Help build practical knowledge of AI and Business-Led Development risk across Technology Risk Management. What Success Looks Like Establish a practical risk framework tied to the firm’s existing risks, policies, and controls. Clarify which governance requirements apply to employee AI and business-developed solutions. Create a consistent, risk-based approach for reviewing higher-risk solutions. Identify meaningful weaknesses in governance, control design, monitoring, or supporting evidence. Give senior leaders clear, defensible analysis of the firm’s risk position. Build effective relationships across the organization while maintaining the independence expected of a Second Line of Defense function.

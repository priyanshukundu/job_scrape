Helping us as we transform our workflows and organization into a centrally orchestrated configuration management framework, operating at scale across geographies. Owning and driving integrations with various service APIs such as Cloud Service Providers, to automate creation of environments and auto populate data sources in turn. Streamlining deployment mechanisms and life cycle operations Developing modern service architectures around streaming data and event pipelines.

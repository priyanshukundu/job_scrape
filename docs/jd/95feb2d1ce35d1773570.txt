About this role Company: BlackRock Financial Management, Inc. Job Title: Associate Location: 50 Hudson Yards, New York, NY 10001 Job Duties: Work on the implementation and maintenance of the model surveillance and performance monitoring infrastructure for the models supported by Single Security Pricing (SSP). Enhance the model documentation. Collaborate with the Risk & Quantitative Analysis (RQA) Group in model validation process for the models owned by SSP. Expand product and market coverage to address evolving client needs by researching, implementing and rolling out new models and analytics. Maintain and enhance existing interest rate, inflation, FX, synthetic credit and equity derivatives models. Refactor existing analytical code to make it more modular and scalable. Keep abreast of recent trends in technology, quantitative finance, capital markets and regulation. Bring the latest techniques to bear on the problems we face in our day-to-day work. Qualifications: Master’s degree in Financial Engineering, Business Administration, Economics, or a related field and two (2) years of experience in the job offered or as an Analyst, Financial Engineer, or related role. Must have two (2) years of experience with: Experience programming in Python, R, MATLAB, SQL for quantitative analysis, and using Bloomberg terminal and Aladdin suite; and Experience applying time-series analysis to derivatives modeling, including historical volatility estimation, GARCH models, PCA on yield curve dynamics, and mean-reversion parameter estimation. Must have one (1) year of experience with: Experience with model validation and performance monitoring metric design, including validation metric design, out-of-sample testing, parameter stability assessment, threshold analysis, residual analysis, rolling-window statistical diagnostics, structural break detection, technical documentation of methodology and assumptions, and remediation of model review findings; Experience pricing fixed income derivatives including swaps, swaptions, caps/floors, CMS and CMS spread options, callable and puttable bonds; Experience implementing and calibrating short-rate models, including Hull-White, Shifted Lognormal, and G2++; Experience constructing and calibrating interest rate volatility surfaces including caplet stripping, swaption matrix fitting, and SABR smile calibration; Experience constructing multiple yield curves, including OIS, LIBOR & Bank, treasury curve; Experience pricing interest rate derivatives using LIBOR market model, SABR-LMM stochastic vol extension, and forward market model; Experience pricing convertible bonds and equity-credit hybrid securities using jump-diffusion PDE; Experience applying stochastic calculus to derivatives pricing including Ito's lemma, change of numeraire, Girsanov theorem, and Feynman-Kac representations; Experience implementing numerical methods including Crank-Nicolson finite difference, trinomial trees, Longstaff-Schwartz American Monte Carlo, and variance reduction; and Experience developing production pricing analytics in C++ including pricing libraries, calibration routines, and bond/derivative pricing engines.

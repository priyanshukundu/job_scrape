Associate Director, Cost Management
Turner & Townsend
Location: Gilbert, Arizona, us
Link: https://jobs.smartrecruiters.com/turnertownsend/744000163764558

1. Lead the cost management team on specific clt commissions by overseeing multidisciplinary cost teams across complex construction & infrastructure projects. Ensure that all commercial deliverables incl. cost plans, procurement strategies, contract administration, & financial reporting are completed w/ technical accuracy & in alignment w/ clt expectations. Utilize tools incl. CostX & Compass to manage project controls & reporting. 2. Interface w/ the clt, stakeholders, & other consultants at all project stages by acting as the primary commercial rep. from pre-construction through to closeout. Provide strategic advice during feasibility & design stages, facilitate commercial meetings, & resolve disputes or queries w/ design teams, MEP consultants, & general contractors using tools incl. Procore, Bluebeam, & Excel-based forecasting models. 3. Identify opportunities to improve cost management procedures, templates, & products through the standardization & refinement of internal documents incl. cost reports, risk registers, & benchmarking tools. Promote automation using Power BI dashboards, Excel VBA, or process enhancements that improve efficiency & data quality across commissions. 4. Ensure that key info & learning from ea. commission is inputted into internal databases & shared by conducting formal project closeouts, gathering lessons learned, & uploading relevant cost, change, & performance data into organizational knowledge-sharing systems. 5. Identify & act upon ways to improve internal syst. & processes by auditing project workflows, identifying bottlenecks in the commercial lifecycle (e.g., change order approval or pay app cycles), & implementing corrective actions to streamline delivery & enhance quality standards. 6. Lead proposals for new work or variations for existing proj. by preparing technical & commercial proposals, incl. scope of work definitions, resource allocation plans, & fee breakdowns. Leverage internal cost databases to support accurate pricing & strategic positioning during pursuits. 7. Attend relevant networking events & other promotional opportunities to represent the firm w/in the industry, enhance market visibility, & build relationships w/ key clts. & stakeholders. Participate in industry panels, RICS events to share market intelligence & promote technical leadership. 8. Input into the formal management of staff & recruitment interviews by reviewing candidate qualifications, conducting technical interviews, & contributing to hiring decisions. Assess familiarity w/ relevant systems incl. CostX, Procore & Excel to ensure team members are aligned w/ proj. & clt needs. 9. Undertake staff performance reviews by leading formal evaluations, setting goals aligned w/ organizational & proj. KPIs, & developing individual growth plans. Support team members pursuing professional qualifications incl. RICS APC & provide regular mentorship & feedback. 10. Utilize the tools provided to keep track of the ongoing margin levels & monthly fee/resource forecasts for ea. commission by preparing fee burn reports, forecasting resource allocations, & managing commercial performance through tools inc. Excel & internal cost dashboards. Address potential fee overruns or resource constraints. 11. Review & participate w/ the design services team & general contractor in the development of cost estimates by validating quantity takeoffs, market pricing assumptions, & subcontractor quotes across all proj. phases (SD, DD, CD). Leverage tools incl. CostX & historical cost data to ensure estimates are robust & defensible. 12. Reconcile changes that occur over the planning phase & assist the general contractor to ensure their data is accurate by maintaining & managing a live change event register, reviewing contractor-submitted changes for accuracy, & ensuring alignment w/ proj. scope, schedule, & commercial strategy. 13. Ensure compliance w/ quality standards & participation in audits by conducting internal quality reviews on deliverables, enforcing use of standardized templates & processes, & actively participating in internal & external quality audits to demonstrate commercial compliance. 14. Communicate or meet w/ general contractor & owner project mgr to gather status info to prepare a cost est update by leading regular commercial meetings, reviewing schedule progress, validating earned value data, & using that input to develop updated Estimate-at-Completion (EAC) & Cost-to-Complete (CTC) summaries. 15. Prepare written comments to the general contractor’s submissions, incl the exec summary, & coordinate all sources of cost info by reviewing contractor pricing proposals & preparing formal clt-facing commentary. Consolidate data from A/E quantity takeoffs, subcontractor quotes, supplier pricing, & benchmarking tools to provide a comprehensive & technically sound cost review.

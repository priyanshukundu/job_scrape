Reference Data Product Owner – Associate
JPMorgan Chase
Location: Newark, DE, United States
Link: https://jpmc.fa.oraclecloud.com/hcmUI/CandidateExperience/en/sites/CX_1001/job/210805194

Join JPMorganChase's Reference Data team, the single authoritative source for strategically managed reference data across the firm's most critical data domains. This is your opportunity to sit at the intersection of business strategy and technology delivery, shaping how data products serve stakeholders across Finance, Credit Risk, Liquidity, and Treasury. If you thrive in fast-paced, cross-functional environments and want to make a tangible impact on data-driven decision-making, this role is for you. As a Reference Data Product Owner within the Reference Data Product Owner team, you will work closely with Line of Business stakeholders, data Subject Matter Experts, consumers, and technology teams to drive product vision, strategy, and successful delivery across multiple data domains. You will play a crucial role in project planning, execution, and tracking — collecting business requirements, facilitating UAT cycles, and ensuring on-time, high-quality outcomes. You will serve as a key connector between business and technology, translating complex needs into actionable product roadmaps while managing risks, training users, and continuously enhancing the product experience. Job Responsibilities: Collect and document detailed business requirements from stakeholders Facilitate User Acceptance Testing cycles by partnering with key stakeholders to validate business requirements and ensure quality deliverables Articulate the product vision and strategy in alignment with business goals and objectives. Organize, schedule, coordinate, and control project activities and resources. Provide training and support to users, helping them understand the data product. Identify issues and risks, define mitigation strategies, and manage risk communication or escalation processes. Gather user feedback on the product to enhance functionality and user experience. Required Qualifications, Capabilities, and Skills: Bachelor’s degree with 3+ years of experience in product, financial services, technology, project management, or other relevant fields. Ability to quickly learn and assimilate business and technical knowledge Strong data analysis and visualization skillsets Effective communication skills (both verbal and written) for engaging with business and technical stakeholders and management. Experience working on projects that involve interaction with various stakeholders and technology within a financial domain. Strong partnership skills and experience influencing key stakeholders. Ability to perform in high-pressure, fast-paced environments. Preferred Qualifications, Capabilities, and Skills: SQL/querying knowledge a plus Agile project management experience, including proficiency in agile project management tools (i.e., JIRA, Confluence, Lucid etc.) Knowledge of JIRA, SQL, Microsoft suite of applications, Databricks and data visualization/analytical tools (Tableau, Alteryx, Python) is a plus Experience with cross-functional team collaboration Ability to streamline workflows and improve project efficiency

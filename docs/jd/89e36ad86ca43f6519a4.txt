This G&I Finance Senior Associate role will support project control and financial management activities across multiple accounts within the G&I practice. Working under the direction of Project Finance leadership and designated account leads, this role will provide hands-on analysis, reporting and coordination. Your key responsibilities Support G&I Markets/Project Finance leadership and account leads in delivering consistent project finance support across multiple accounts in EY’s growing G&I practice. Develop strong working relationships within finance and engagement teams across assigned accounts, providing responsive financial support and escalating issues to the appropriate lead. Assist with preparing and maintaining EAC and ETC documentation, researching program financial issues and developing analyses and proposed solutions for review by Project Finance before presentation to engagement team leadership. Contribute to continuous improvement of the markets/project finance team by sharing observations, supporting process enhancements and applying consistent practices across accounts. Participate in team training and knowledge sharing and assist with cross training activities as needed. Maintain tools, processes, and procedures to monitor engagement financials and support financial management across assigned accounts. Assist with preparing annual engagement budgets and monthly, annual and multi-year forecasts by compiling inputs, updating financial models and analyzing variances for review by account leads. Support external project audits with outside independent auditors and regulatory agencies. Support the G&I Reporting lead and account leads by gathering reporting requirements , validating financial data and preparing reports for engagement and account financial management. Assist with coordination and follow-up between engagement teams, PMOs, accounting, contracts, subcontracts and project setup , escalating complex matters to the appropriate lead . Analyze engagement profitability and prepare financial scenarios to help account leads evaluate risks, variances and potential actions. Maintain financial tracking and coordination for borrowed and loaned practitioners supporting G&I engagements. Review contract awards and modifications , update tracking of contract value, funding and AR , and flag discrepancies or concerns to account leads. Assist with project setup and workforce management activities, gathering required information and referring complex questions to experienced team members. Assist with billing support, monitor unbilled balances and accounts receivable, and coordinate follow-up on outstanding items with engagement teams and billing/accounting contacts. Monitor funding utilization, forecast variances and other project financial risks, escalating emerging issues promptly to account leads. Maintain accurate, timely and well-documented financial analyses, trackers and supporting records across assigned accounts. Support other ad hoc financial reporting and special projects as assigned. Skills and attributes for success High degree of proficiency with Microsoft Office Suite, including advanced Excel skills and experience building and maintaining financial models in Excel to aid in efficient analyses Exposure to planning and engagement financial management tools Demonstrated analytical curiosity and sound judgment, with the ability to investigate discrepancies, identify underlying causes and propose practical solutions Ability to build strong collaborative working relationships with Project Finance colleagues, engagement teams and cross-functional business partners Ability to work effectively within a matrixed team across multiple functional areas, coordinating inputs and following through on assigned tasks Hands-on, detail-oriented contributor with the ability to manage competing priorities across multiple accounts, execute assigned responsibilities and seek guidance when needed Excellent oral, written and interpersonal skills with the ability to communicate effectively with all levels of management and external auditors To qualify, candidates must have A bachelor’s degree in accounting, Finance or a related field, or equivalent experience Understanding of CAS and FAR Part 31 and their application to day-to-day project finance activities Working knowledge of project accounting, including different contract types (CPFF, T&M, FFP) and indirect rate structures A minimum of 4 years of relevant government contractor finance experience in a CAS compliant environment A minimum of 2 years of experience supporting project control activities Working knowledge of Deltek Costpoint.

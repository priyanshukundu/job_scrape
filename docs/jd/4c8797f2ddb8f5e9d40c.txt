Become a part of our caring community The Associate Director, Data Science uses mathematics, statistics, modeling, business analysis, and emerging technologies to transform high volumes of complex structured and unstructured data into advanced analytic solutions. The Associated Director, Data Science requires a solid understanding of how organization capabilities interrelate across departments and excellent communication and relationship building skills. The Associate Director, Data Science develops, maintains, and collects structured and unstructured data sets for analysis and scenario testing. Creates reports, projections, predictive models, and presentations to support business strategy and tactics. Decisions are typically related to identifying and resolving complex technical and operational problems within department(s) and would lead multiple highly specialized professional associates. Use your skills to make an impact Required Qualifications Master's Degree in a quantitative discipline, such as Computer Science, Mathematics, Statistics, Actuarial Science, Finance, and/or related field and 6 or more years of experience Demonstrated experience with Python, R, SAS, QlikView, SQL, PL/SQL, MATLABR or similar statistical tools Experience using supervised and unsupervised machine learning (ML) techniques and NLP and LLM capabilities to solve business problems and inform solutions 3 or more years of people leadership experience in building, managing and/or developing high-performing teams Experience leveraging large sets of structured and unstructured data to develop tactical and strategic insights Experience developing and validating statistical forecasting models and tools Experience interpreting and communicating analytic results to analytical and non analytical business partners and executive decision makers Demonstrated ability to articulate ideas effectively in both written and oral forms Flexible, dynamic personality who works well in a team environment and is also able to lead individual contributors Must be passionate about contributing to an organization focused on continuously improving consumer experiences Preferred Qualifications Healthcare or managed care experience, especially utilization management (UM) and clinical operations Management consulting background (e.g., McKinsey, BCG, Bain, Deloitte, or similar), with a focus on data, analytics, or operations transformation Ability to anticipate and be proactive around next steps in large initiatives Experience performing data management, mining, and manipulation to feed analytics Additional Information This role is not eligible for work visa sponsorship.

The JLLT Data Engineering team owns the Enterprise Data Platform (EDP), the shared foundation of ingestion frameworks, data products, APIs, and developer-facing data services that every JLL data domain builds on. The platform is built on Databricks and is evolving to serve both people and AI agents as first-class consumers of trusted data. What this job involves As a Principal Data Engineer on the Platform team, you will own the technical direction of our Databricks-based data platform across ingestion, processing, data contracts, agentic enablement, and cost efficiency. You will architect the native capabilities that domain teams reuse, set the engineering standards that keep the platform reliable and economical at scale, and orchestrate delivery across multiple project teams. You will also drive innovation by evaluating third-party tools, building the standards that govern their adoption, and partnering with vendors such as Databricks to advocate for better outcomes for JLL and the teams who depend on the platform. We are looking for an expert engineer with deep Databricks experience, a track record of shipping large, complex platforms across multiple domains, and the influence to shape strategy beyond your own team. What you’ll get to do As a Principal Data Engineer, you will have ownership over the architecture, standards, and evolution of the core data platform. Your customers are the domain engineering teams, data scientists, analysts, product teams, and increasingly the AI agents that depend on the platform to ingest, process, discover, and safely use JLL’s data. Your mission is to make the platform the fastest, most trusted, and most cost-efficient way to turn data into business value across JLL. This is an opportunity to define how an enterprise-scale Databricks platform is contracted, governed, and made agent-ready, and to leave a lasting mark on how JLL builds with data and AI. Key responsibilities Databricks Platform Architecture: Shape and own the end-to-end architecture of the Databricks lakehouse, including workspace and catalog design, Unity Catalog structure, medallion layering, compute strategy, and multi-environment promotion. Make the build-versus-buy and native-versus-custom calls that keep the platform simple and scalable. Data Governance, Quality, and Security: Design governance into the platform using Unity Catalog for access control, lineage, classification, tagging, and auditing. Embed automated data quality checks, expectations, and observability so that trust is measured and enforced rather than assumed. Agentic and AI-Ready Data Platform: Make platform data and services consumable by AI agents and LLM applications. Design governed agent access through MCP and APIs, vector search and retrieval pipelines, and curated, well-documented data products, with guardrails, evaluation, observability. Partner with AI and product teams to move agentic use cases from prototype to production on the platform. Cost Optimization and FinOps: Own the cost-efficiency strategy for the platform. Drive cluster and SQL warehouse right-sizing, cluster policies, serverless versus classic compute decisions, Photon and Spark tuning, storage lifecycle and file layout optimization, and workload scheduling. Build cost attribution, tagging, dashboards, budgets, and alerting so every domain and pipeline has visible unit economics and clear accountability. Platform Engineering and Automation: Champion infrastructure as code, Databricks Asset Bundles, and automated CI/CD for pipelines, jobs, and platform configuration. Provide golden paths, templates, and developer tooling that let teams self-serve securely and consistently. Innovation and Vendor Strategy: Drive innovation on the platform by continuously scouting emerging Databricks features and third-party tools and running structured evaluations and proofs of concept that compare capability, security, integration fit, and total cost. Recommend adopt, defer, or retire decisions with clear evidence, and turn the outcomes into platform standards and reference implementations. Vendor Partnership and Advocacy: Work directly with Databricks and other strategic vendors on roadmaps, architecture reviews, support escalations, and enablement. Advocate for JLL's requirements and for the success of the teams adopting these tools, and hold vendors accountable for outcomes that matter to the business. Standards and Best Practices: Build and maintain the engineering standards, reference architectures, and selection criteria that govern how tools and services are adopted across the platform, so that teams get consistent guidance on what to use, how to use it, and why. Technical Strategy and Influence: Participate in strategic architecture decisions across the Data Engineering organization, set priorities across multiple project teams, and align platform roadmaps with business and domain needs. Evaluate emerging Databricks and market capabilities and translate them into pragmatic adoption plans. Mentorship and Knowledge Sharing: Mentor lead and senior engineers, lead architecture reviews, and run knowledge-transfer and brown-bag sessions across the organization. Raise the engineering bar through code review, design review, and clearly documented standards. Who you are You are a hands-on technical leader who thrives at the intersection of deep platform expertise and org-wide influence. You enjoy untangling hard problems in data movement, governance, and cost, and you turn what you learn into standards and reusable frameworks that other teams adopt willingly. You are curious about how AI agents change what a data platform must provide, and you balance that enthusiasm with a disciplined view of reliability, security, and spend. You are energized by evaluating new technology with rigor and by building productive, candid relationships with vendors that turn their roadmaps into success for JLL. You challenge the status quo with concrete proposals, and you bring partners and peers along with clear, expert-level communication. Qualifications and skills Required Qualifications Experience: 10+ years in data engineering, including multiple large, complex platform or data product implementations across several technology domains. Databricks Expertise: Expert-level, hands-on experience with Databricks, including Spark, Delta Lake, Unity Catalog, Workflows / Jobs, Lakeflow or Delta Live Tables, Auto Loader, Structured Streaming, and SQL warehouses. Ingestion and Processing: Proven record of designing and building scalable, reusable ingestion and processing frameworks for batch, CDC, and streaming data, including schema evolution, data quality, replay, and backfill. Data Contracts: Demonstrated experience defining, versioning, and enforcing data contracts or equivalent producer-consumer agreements, and operating contract-backed data products. Cost Optimization: Demonstrated experience reducing and governing cloud data platform cost, including compute right-sizing, cluster policies, Spark and Delta performance tuning, storage optimization, and cost attribution. Agentic and AI Exposure: Practical experience enabling LLM or agent-based applications on a data platform, such as retrieval and vector search pipelines, governed data access for agents, or MCP / API-based tool integration. Programming: Expert Python and SQL, with strong software engineering practices including testing, modular design, and code review. PySpark optimization and Spark UI proficiency. Cloud and Automation: Expert knowledge of a major cloud (Azure preferred) and its data services, plus infrastructure as code (for example Terraform) and CI/CD for data pipelines and platform configuration. Architecture and Modeling: Expertise in data modeling and architecting complex end-to-end data solutions with a holistic, cross-product view. Innovation and Vendor Evaluation: Track record of evaluating third-party data and AI tools, running proofs of concept, making build-versus-buy recommendations, and working with vendors to resolve issues, influence roadmaps, and drive adoption success. Leadership: Experience orchestrating multiple engineering project teams, setting priorities across teams, and influencing cross-functional stakeholders without direct authority. Preferred Qualifications Databricks certifications (for example Data Engineer Professional, or Generative AI Engineer). Experience with Databricks Mosaic AI, Vector Search, Model Serving, or agent frameworks, and with building MCP. Experience with Kafka, Azure Event Hubs, or comparable event streaming platforms. Experience with Databricks system tables, FinOps practices, and unit-economics reporting for data platforms. Familiarity with open table formats and interoperability (Delta UniForm, Apache Iceberg) and data catalog or lineage tooling. Experience with real estate, financial services, or other multi-domain enterprise data environments.

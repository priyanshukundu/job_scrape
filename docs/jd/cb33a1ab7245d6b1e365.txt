Job Description The Team & What They Do: The Measurement Science team sits at the center of high-impact decision support across the enterprise. This group is the go-to partner when the business needs to understand what truly drives outcomes. Working within a specialized analytics pod and closely aligned with a broader “core” measurement team, you’ll collaborate with peers who combine deep statistical rigor with a consulting mindset. Together, the team evaluates campaigns, product changes, and strategic initiatives to ensure leaders can make decisions grounded in well-reasoned statistical evidence. This is a high-visibility team that frequently engages with senior leadership to assess the effectiveness of major initiatives and quantify real business impact. Unlike traditional data science roles focused on prediction, this role is about decision science—producing defensible, evidence-based insights that shape strategy. Overall Work Scope: As a Lead Data Scientist on the Measurement Science team you will have the opportunity to work on high value, high visibility projects across the entire enterprise. This role is tasked with providing clarity to problems with high degrees of uncertainty. Your role will be responsible for the following: applying advanced statistical and machine learning techniques to evaluate business metrics, designing and analyzing business experiments, applying quasi-causal techniques, validating and evaluating key metrics, and engineering repeatable measurement methodologies and systems. This role will contribute to and manage projects of high complexity Key Responsibilities: Works collaboratively to improve the effectiveness of business decisions and business results through designing, building, and advising on appropriate measurement, experimental, or quasi-causal designs. Provides leadership clear insights into the strengths and limitations of measurement approaches Identifies and evaluates key performance metrics, including validation of proxies and leading indicators. Contributes to the design, review and engineering of repeatable measurement methodologies and systems. Identifies languages, tools, and methodologies that can bring efficiencies and rigor to the team. Uses best practices to develop statistical and machine learning/AI techniques to enable rigorous data-driven decisions Mentors and upskills junior data scientists Required Qualifications: A minimum of 3 years demonstrated experience applying causal inference methodologies (experimental design, propensity score matching/weighting, double ML, cluster randomization, doubly robust estimation) Comfortable consulting with and educating non-technical business partners on a variety of measurement methods Strong foundation in statistical inference including hypothesis testing, generalized linear modeling, variance reduction techniques (CUPED) and Bayesian inference Experience in statistical and machine learning fundamentals, including model evaluation, feature engineering, and supervised/unsupervised learning principles Proficiency in Python and/or R for data analysis, data manipulation, and model development. Preferred Qualifications: Experience with dashboards or visualization tools (Power BI, Tableau, etc.). Familiarity with software engineering best practices (version control, code review, testing frameworks). Experience using CI/CD pipelines or modern development workflows for deploying data products, experiments, or analytics automation. Experience with agentic coding tools and methodologies (i.e. Github copilot, Claude code, spec-kit, etc.) Experience evaluating LLM and agentic systems Proficiency in distributed compute systems (i.e. PySpark) #LI-JB1 Skills Artificial Intelligence Markup Language, Business Model Development, Causal Inference, Data Analytics, Predictive Modeling, Python (Programming Language), R Programming, Statistical Analysis, Statistical Models, Statistical Tools, Statistics Compensation Compensation offered for this role is 134,000.00 - 209,750.00 annually and is based on experience and qualifications.

Job Description Summary Oversee and coordinate the execution of key elements of Transamerica’s Operational Risk Management program for assigned areas, ensuring risk considerations are embedded in business activities and supported by effective team execution, stakeholder engagement, and consistent application of ORM practices. Lead others to a successful outcome of annual initiatives. Job Description Responsibilities Translate ORM strategy into execution. Partner with business and technology leaders to ensure effective oversight of operational risk assessments across business units and major initiatives, ensuring critical risks are identified, evaluated, and escalated appropriately. Co-lead the development and deployment of an intelligent, AI-enabled Model Risk Management platform supporting Transamerica’s operations globally Build and maintain strong relationships across the three lines of defense to embed a robust risk culture and support risk-aware decision-making enterprise-wide. Lead significant operational risk management initiatives, including framework enhancements and process improvements, to strengthen the organization’s overall risk posture. Manage and develop direct reports, setting performance goals, providing coaching and feedback, and overseeing the quality and consistency of risk management activities. Continuously evaluate and enhance ORM processes, policies, and controls to improve efficiency, effectiveness, and coverage across the enterprise. Oversee the end-to-end lifecycle of operational risk issues, including identification, root cause analysis, remediation tracking, and reporting outcomes to senior management. Drive data-driven insights into the enterprise risk profile, leveraging the GRC tool and contributing to the development of the quarterly risk profile and related reporting. Review, challenge, and influence risk mitigation strategies, partnering with process owners to assess the adequacy and effectiveness of management action plans. Oversee the execution of Process Risk Assessments (PRAs), including review and challenge of inherent and residual risk ratings, control design adequacy, and mitigation strategies. Ensure consistent adherence to the Global Operational Risk Management Policy and framework, providing guidance and training as needed and escalating systemic or enterprise-wide risk themes.

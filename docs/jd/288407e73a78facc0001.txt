Job Description: The Role We are seeking a Quantitative Analyst to join Strategic Advisers, LLC (SAI)'s Private Markets Investment Team. In this role, you will collaborate closely with fellow quantitative analysts, Managing Director of Quantitative Research, the private markets team, and the broader investment organization. You will support SAI’s expanding private market offerings by developing quantitative models and data-driven analyses that inform the management of private equity, private credit, and private real asset funds. This role is ideal for a motivated and intellectually curious individual who thrives in a collaborative environment. You will work alongside the Private Markets fund team on projects related to liquidity management, portfolio construction, and risk modeling. These projects will involve leveraging large-scale private market datasets and applying statistical techniques to systematize and enhance decision-making for complex fund structures. Success in this role requires strong quant modeling, programming, and financial analytics skills, as well as the ability to translate investment and research needs into well-defined quantitative projects. You should be comfortable presenting results in a clear, intuitive, and investment-relevant manner to range of audiences, both sophisticated and less sophisticated. Given the rapid growth of SAI’s private asset business, this position offers dynamic responsibilities and requires an entrepreneurial mindset. The Value You Deliver Working within the private markets investment team, you will develop quantitative models and analytical tools to support portfolio construction and investment decisions for SAI’s private market funds. Your work will directly contribute to enhancing risk-adjusted performance across private equity, private credit, and private real assets.

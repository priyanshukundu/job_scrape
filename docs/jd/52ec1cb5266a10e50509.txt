Associate Manager - Test Lab Operations
Stryker
Location: Portage, Michigan
Link: https://stryker.wd1.myworkdayjobs.com/StrykerCareers/job/Portage-Michigan/Associate-Manager---Test-Lab-Operations_R577017

Work Flexibility: Hybrid As an Associate Manager, Test Lab Operations , under the guidance of the department leadership, you will run the Test Lab Operations group and provide day-to-day leadership to team/teams tied to the commercialization of products through testing activities. Working in alignment with department leadership, you will lead a highly technical team to support development and verification testing activities for Stryker Instruments and OrthoTech divisions. The successful candidate will be required to build and sustain strong working relationships with the business units we support, maintain key department metrics, and make recommendations on resource allocations, forecasting, and ensuring their team is prepared to fully support the needs of the business. What You Will Do: Lead the Test Lab Operations team, providing strategic direction, personnel leadership, technical guidance, mentoring, and development while promoting technical best practices. Lead projects and initiatives across NPD Operations, including planning, budgeting, resource allocation, staffing, and execution to ensure project and business goals are achieved. Drive financial accountability across the team by supporting cost center budget development, projections, expense tracking, and overall fiscal responsibility. Build strong partnerships with business unit stakeholders and cross-functional R&D teams to foster collaboration, influence technical decisions, and support predictable and efficient New Product Development activities. Coordinate cross-functional NPD planning efforts to improve planning accuracy, operational efficiency, resource alignment, and execution on an ongoing basis. Partner with R&D and NPD Operations teams to provide input on current and future testing needs and continuously improve the overall product testing and verification process. Ensure consistent departmental practices for product verification, test equipment development, technical report writing, regulatory compliance, and communication methods. Collaborate with internal subject matter experts to manage test protocol assignments and Test Requests through established systems, including pre-review, assignment, post-approval, and final sign-off. What You Will Need: Required Qualifications: Bachelor of Science degree or higher in Engineering or applicable technical field. 6+ years of experience in a highly regulated environment (Med Device/Pharma/Automotive) with 0+ years direct people management experience Excellent mechanical/electrical/software problem solving skills. Demonstrated Project management skills Preferred Qualifications: Good level of understanding of, experience in, and ability to mentor in one's own technical / functional disciplines. Effective math and technical writing skills Talented performance coach who is skilled at providing actionable feedback to team members, setting clear performance objectives and managing team member performance.

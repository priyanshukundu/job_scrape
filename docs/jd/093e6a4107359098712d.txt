Job Title Software Development Engineering - Professional II PRODUCT SUPPORT ENGINEER (Analyst), FINXACT WEB APPLICATIONS What does a successful Product Support Engineer (Analyst) do? A Product Support Engineer at Finxact plays an important role in our Experience Organization, helping keep Finxact.io, our AI tooling, and our web applications reliable and our internal teams successful. You’ll be one of the first engineering resources engaged when something isn’t working as expected, investigating problems across our React front end, Go services, APIs, and data to understand what happened, identify the root cause, and help get things back on track. This is a great role for someone early in their career who enjoys solving problems, digging into how software works, and helping people. You are not expected to know everything on day one. You’ll be backed by an experienced engineering team while developing deep knowledge of Finxact, software engineering, and financial technology. As your knowledge grows, you won’t just identify problems. You’ll contribute code to fix them, automate repetitive work, improve our support tooling and documentation, and help make our products easier to operate and support. What you will do: Serve as a primary engineering point of contact for Finxact web application support during the business day, taking ownership of incoming technical issues through investigation and resolution. Triage and prioritize support requests based on urgency and business impact. Troubleshoot problems across our React front end, Go back end, REST APIs, data, and supporting services. Reproduce reported issues and analyze logs, API requests and responses, application behavior, configuration, and data to identify root cause. Resolve issues directly when possible, including developing, testing, and contributing code fixes to our production applications. Collaborate with experienced engineers to investigate and resolve complex issues, escalating with a clear problem statement, evidence, reproduction steps, and findings when additional expertise is needed. Verify fixes and workarounds to ensure reported problems are resolved and expected application behavior is restored. Communicate clearly with internal client-facing teams throughout an investigation, translating technical findings into information they can use with our clients. Occasionally participate in client conversations when additional technical expertise is helpful. Identify recurring problems and work with the broader engineering team to address underlying causes rather than repeatedly treating symptoms. Create and improve troubleshooting guides, runbooks, technical documentation, and other knowledge that helps the team resolve future issues faster. Improve the support experience by contributing to better diagnostics, logging, monitoring, alerts, and operational tooling. Use approved AI tools, scripting, and automation to accelerate investigations, identify patterns, improve documentation, and eliminate repetitive support tasks while following appropriate data-handling practices. Develop a strong understanding of Finxact’s web applications, architecture, APIs, data, and the banking workflows they support. Participate in root-cause reviews and use lessons from incidents to improve the reliability and supportability of our products. Participate in an on-call rotation, typically for one week every six to eight weeks, providing support for issues occurring outside normal business hours. Work alongside an experienced on-call engineer during your first six months, or until you are comfortable handling the rotation independently. Contribute actively to agile ceremonies, code reviews, and team workflows while continuously developing your software engineering skills. What you will need to have: Bachelor’s degree in Engineering, Computer Science, Information Technology, or a related technical field. Foundational software development experience through coursework, internships, personal projects, open-source contributions, or professional experience. Experience programming in Go, TypeScript, JavaScript, or similar languages, with an interest in working across both front-end and back-end systems. Familiarity with SQL and relational databases, including the ability to query and investigate application data. Familiarity with REST APIs, JSON, HTTP, and modern web application concepts. Familiarity with GIT source and version control Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with the curiosity and persistence to investigate problems that do not have obvious answers. Ability to read unfamiliar code, follow application behavior across multiple components, and systematically narrow down the source of a problem. Interest in contributing production code and learning professional software development practices including Git, code review, testing, and CI/CD. Strong written and verbal communication skills, including the ability to clearly document a problem, investigation, and solution for both technical and non-technical audiences. A collaborative mindset and willingness to ask for help, learn from more experienced engineers, and share what you learn with others. A customer-focused mindset and an interest in helping others successfully use the software we build. What would be nice to have: Experience with React and TypeScript. Experience with Go. Experience with Docker, containerized applications, or cloud-based software. Experience troubleshooting applications using logs, browser developer tools, API clients, monitoring tools, or similar diagnostic tools. Experience with Jira, GitLab, Slack, PagerDuty, or similar development and support tools. Experience using AI-assisted development tools, scripting, or automation to investigate problems or improve engineering workflows. Internship, co-op, project, or other experience developing or supporting production software. Familiarity with banking, financial technology, or financial-services concepts.

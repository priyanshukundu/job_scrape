Work Schedule Standard (Mon-Fri) Environmental Conditions Office Job Description Join Us as an Associate Compliance Director - Make an Impact at the Forefront of Innovation The Associate Compliance Director identifies, develops, and executes quality and compliance strategies and initiatives across the organization. This role leads quality initiatives throughout operational departments. Identifies and mitigates risk and provides input to senior management. The Associate Compliance Director updates tools and materials essential to the execution of quality management systems and provides the leadership and expertise necessary to advance the vision of the department. What You'll Do: • Lead, review, and approve reports for a variety of client, internal, or GxP audits and regulatory inspections. • Define and lead quality and compliance responsibilities for quality system processes and engages with a variety of cross-departmental staff to ensure appropriate execution and completion. • Provide GxP consultation and support to internal project teams and external clients. • Identify and mitigate risk through participation in risk management activities and/or the compilation and analysis of metrics and trends, and provide input to senior management. • Develop and maintain tools which facilitate the execution of the defined quality management systems. • Mentor less experienced staff and serve as a team lead for group projects. • Lead cross-functional process/quality improvement projects and initiatives. • Serve as consultant and spokesperson for the functional area with clients and across multiple departments. • Create formal networks internally and externally involving coordination among groups. • Identify complex issues impacting the quality and/or integrity of clinical research programs, determines root cause of non-conformance and develop strategies to address issues. • Develop training materials and programs and provide guidance to junior auditors. • Provide strategic input on audit procedures and programs, and review and modify auditing procedural documents. Additional supplier quality and audit program responsibilities: · Oversee supplier qualification, routine audits, and inspection support; execute supplier audits from planning through reporting, CAPA follow-up, and closure. · Develop and operate a risk-based audit program, including supplier categorization, audit frequency, annual planning, auditor qualification, and work assignment. · Monitor and report audit metrics, key risk and quality indicators, and data trends; use findings to guide the audit program and continuous improvement. · Identify, communicate, mitigate, and escalate supplier risks, deviations, and quality events; facilitate investigations and CAPA development, and escalate significant issues to the appropriate quality and senior leadership teams. · Provide GCP, GCLP, and GVP expertise for supplier agreements and oversight documents, including quality agreements, pharmacovigilance contracts, and PVAs, support relevant change controls and controlled procedures. · Support internal audits, inspections, and business partner audit activities; coordinate with stakeholders and consultant auditor firms, including supporting the auditor framework and related contract updates.

Associate Machinist, R&D
Stryker
Location: Irvine, California
Link: https://stryker.wd1.myworkdayjobs.com/StrykerCareers/job/Irvine-California/Associate-Machinist--R-D_R577312

Work Flexibility: Onsite Shift: Monday – Friday, 7:00 AM – 3:30 PM Location : 6001 Oak Canyon Suite #100, Irvine, CA Summary: The Associate R&D Machinist supports Stryker’s product development and engineering teams by fabricating precision components, prototypes, and test fixtures. This role requires strong technical machining expertise, problem-solving skills, and the ability to adapt machining processes to meet unique engineering and product development needs. The ideal candidate is detail-oriented, safety-conscious, and comfortable collaborating with engineers in a dynamic R&D environment. What you will do: Set up and operate manual machine tools, including lathes, mills, drill presses, and grinders, to fabricate precision components and prototypes. Interpret engineering drawings, sketches, and technical specifications to plan machining sequences and select appropriate materials, tools, and fixtures. Perform specialized and nonstandard machining operations, adjusting techniques to accommodate prototype designs and experimental requirements. Determine appropriate cutting speeds, feeds, and machining parameters based on material properties and tolerance requirements. Inspect finished components using precision measuring instruments, including micrometers, calipers, height gauges, and indicators, to verify dimensional accuracy. Perform routine equipment maintenance, troubleshoot machining and tooling issues, and maintain cutting tools to ensure reliable operation. Document machining processes, communicate technical challenges or deviations, and collaborate with engineering and project teams to support development objectives. Maintain a clean, organized, and safe work environment while adhering to established safety procedures and providing guidance to junior machinists as needed. What you need: Required: ​High school diploma or equivalent 2+ years of relevant machining experience Experience reading and interpreting blueprints and using precision measuring instruments, including calipers, micrometers, and gauges Preferred: 3-5 years of CNC experience $34.80/hr. per hour plus bonus eligible + benefits.

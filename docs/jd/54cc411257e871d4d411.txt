From airlines and hotels to financial institutions and other travel platforms, we power smarter, faster, and more impactful travel solutions through the most connected marketplace in the world. Digital Operations Supply Marketing (DigOps Engineering) sits at the intersection of supply marketing and engineering, organized into three streams: Data Engineering, Software Engineering, and Innovation. This role sits on Data Engineering, which owns the ETL pipelines moving data to/from Marketo, SFMC, paid media and advertising platforms, and other marketing platforms across our business verticals (Hotels, VR, B2B/Connectivity, OCF), including the paid media reporting pipelines that bring ad spend and performance data into our Partner Marketing Data Store. Software Engineering builds the self-service utilities that let marketers and designers move faster, and Innovation drives new capabilities for the team. Make An Impact! The Expedia B2B Digital Operations Supply Marketing team are looking for a Data Engineer III to join the team! This role will act as a key technical contributor on the team supporting the continuous improvement of our data pipelines and systems, to empower our marketing team to deliver best in class marketing campaigns. You will focus on paid media data: ingesting, modeling and monitoring ad platform spend, performance and attribution data so marketers can measure and optimize paid campaigns across our business verticals. If you are someone with a growth mindset and love solving complex and challenging problems, come join us on this exciting journey.

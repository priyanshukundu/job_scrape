To learn more about CIBC, please visit CIBC.com The Director of Corporate Security, Risk & Reporting, is responsible for Director, Corporate Security Risk and Reporting The Director of Corporate Security, Risk & Reporting, is responsible for leading and overseeing risk governance coordination, control management support, audit engagement, data stewardship, and management reporting activities across Corporate Security. This role serves as a key liaison between Corporate Security, First Line of Defense Risk and Control (1B), Second Line of Defense (2LOD), Third Line of Defense (3LOD), Internal Audit, and control testing teams to facilitate effective risk management, regulatory compliance, and adherence to enterprise governance standards. The Director partners with Corporate Security leadership and cross-functional stakeholders to strengthen the control environment, coordinate risk and deficiency remediation activities, enhance reporting capabilities, and provide actionable insights to support informed business decisions. Key Responsibilities Risk Governance, Audit & Control Testing Lead and coordinate Corporate Security engagement with 2LOD and 3LOD stakeholders, overseeing documentation and information requests, evidence collection, audit inquiries, process walkthroughs, and timely delivery of required information. Serve as a key liaison among Corporate Security, 1B, Internal Audit, and control testing teams to facilitate audits, control testing activities, and coordinated responses to identified findings. Support Corporate Security Risk and Control Self-Assessment (RCSA) activities in partnership with the 1B team, including risk identification, control assessments, documentation, stakeholder coordination, and evaluation of control effectiveness and residual risk. Control Governance & Deficiency Management Partner with the 1B team to support the control lifecycle, including the establishment, enhancement, maintenance, and retirement of controls, ensuring appropriate governance, documentation, and alignment with identified risks. Provide oversight, guidance, and coordination of Corporate Security risk and deficiency remediation activities, including action plan development, progress monitoring, stakeholder engagement, and timely resolution of identified issues. Collaborate with Corporate Security control owners and stakeholders to identify control gaps, evaluate remediation strategies, and support sustainable improvements to the control environment. Management Reporting & Analytics Lead Corporate Security management and executive reporting, including the development and delivery of dashboards, operational performance metrics, control effectiveness reporting, and remediation tracking. Coordinate reporting requirements across Corporate Security functions, ensuring accurate, consistent, and timely information is available to support management oversight and strategic decision-making. Analyze risk trends, control performance, and operational data to identify emerging concerns, highlight areas requiring management attention, and strengthen risk visibility.Identify opportunities to improve reporting processes, data quality, automation, and analytical capabilities to enhance operational efficiency and transparency. Stakeholder Engagement & Continuous Improvement Establish and maintain effective working relationships with Corporate Security leadership, 1B, 2LOD, 3LOD, Internal Audit, and other enterprise stakeholders. Identify and drive opportunities to enhance Corporate Security risk governance, control oversight, documentation practices, and reporting capabilities. Provide guidance and subject matter expertise on risk governance, control management, and reporting requirements to support consistent execution across Corporate Security.

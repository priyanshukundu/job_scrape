Senior Software Engineer, Financial Integrity We're hiring a Senior Software Engineer to join the Financial Integrity team within Consumer Foundations in the Consumer & Business group. This team builds and operates the systems that provide oversight and management of Coinbase's financial flows, ensuring that every transaction across the platform is accurate, reliable, and reconciled. You'll own critical backend infrastructure spanning reconciliation, capital flows, anomaly detection, and inventory management, directly protecting the financial integrity of Coinbase and its users across every product line. What you'll do: Own the design, development, and operation of backend services that power reconciliation, capital flows tracking, and inventory management across Coinbase's financial systems Build and scale anomaly detection pipelines that identify discrepancies and potential issues in financial flows before they impact customers or the business Partner with Finance, Treasury, and cross-functional engineering teams to identify control gaps and build the automated safeguards that keep Coinbase's financial operations accurate and compliant Drive data pipeline architecture using SQL, Python, and Airflow to generate operational insights that inform decision-making across financial operations Strengthen system reliability, observability, and engineering quality across the Financial Integrity stack, establishing patterns that scale as Coinbase launches new products and asset types Required Skills and Experience: 5+ years of backend software engineering experience building and operating production systems, with strong proficiency in Go, Python, or Java Demonstrated experience working with trading, payment, or financial systems where data accuracy and consistency are critical Proven ability to design and maintain scalable data pipelines and backend services, with hands-on experience using SQL, Python, Airflow, and BI tooling to analyze data for operational insights Track record owning complex systems end-to-end, from design through production operation, with cross-functional collaboration across engineering, finance, and operations teams Utilizes generative AI responsibly, maintaining human oversight to deliver business-ready outputs and drive measurable improvements in workflow efficiency, cost, and quality.

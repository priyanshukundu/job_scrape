About this role: At Wells Fargo, a Lead Cyber Security Research Engineer, leads complex research and offensive-security initiatives that identify vulnerabilities, emulate sophisticated attacker techniques, and strengthen security responses across company networks, applications, infrastructure, and emerging technologies. You will apply advanced expertise, work autonomously, and lead cross-functional teams or initiatives. In this role, you will: Lead complex investigations of Attack Surface Intelligence data to identify, contextualize, and respond to potential security exposures across company networks, applications, infrastructure, endpoints, and cloud environments. Analyze findings from attack surface discovery, vulnerability, exposure, and security-validation technologies to determine ownership, business context, exploitability, and potential impact. Investigate assets, vulnerabilities, configurations, attack paths, and related security data to distinguish actionable risk from informational findings and false positives. Correlate security findings across multiple data sources to develop a comprehensive understanding of an exposure and identify relationships or attack paths that may increase risk. Determine the criticality and priority of identified exposures based on technical severity, exploitability, asset context, existing security controls, and potential business impact. Coordinate response to significant attack surface findings by engaging asset owners, security teams, and other stakeholders to drive investigation, mitigation, remediation, or appropriate risk treatment. Communicate investigation results, supporting evidence, risk context, and recommended actions through clear technical documentation, briefings, and escalation processes. Identify patterns and recurring conditions within Attack Surface Intelligence data and recommend improvements to security controls, detection capabilities, engineering practices, and exposure-management processes. Develop and improve investigative methods, automation, and repeatable processes that increase the speed, consistency, and quality of Attack Surface Intelligence investigations. Provide technical leadership, mentoring, and direction to engineers performing attack surface investigation, exposure analysis, and response activities.

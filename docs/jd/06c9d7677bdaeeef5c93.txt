JOB FUNCTION / SUMMARY: Provides intermediate-level operational and technical support for Treasury Services products and services, serving as an escalation point for complex client and system issues. Independently manages advanced product setups, system troubleshooting, and client support while partnering with Sales, Implementation, IT, and vendors. Plays a key role in maintaining service quality, operational controls, and regulatory compliance. ESSENTIAL DUTIES & RESPONSIBILITIES: Independently implement, maintain, and support Treasury Services products and services, including complex or customized setups. Serve as Tier 2 escalation support for operational, system, and client service issues. Troubleshoot Treasury Services system issues, performing root-cause analysis and coordinating resolution with IT and vendors. Support and maintain advanced services such as Zero Balance Accounts (ZBAs), Repurchase Agreements, and other specialized products. Monitor Treasury Services systems and workflows to identify issues, trends, and potential service impacts. Coordinate cross-functional issue resolution with Sales, Client Support, Implementation, and Relationship Management teams. Perform system testing for new products, enhancements, upgrades, and fixes, including client-facing testing when required. Prepare and analyze operational metrics, exception reports, and performance data for management review. Provide client and internal associate training on Treasury Services products, services, and processes. Assist in updating procedures, controls, and documentation to support new products, regulatory changes, and process improvements. MINIMUM REQUIRED EDUCATION, EXPERIENCE & KNOWLEDGE: High School Diploma or GED required; Associate’s or Bachelor’s degree preferred. 1–2 years of Treasury Services, cash management, or banking operations experience. Demonstrated experience supporting Treasury Services systems and resolving complex issues. Strong analytical, problem-solving, and documentation skills. Proficiency in Microsoft Office, particularly Excel, for reporting and analysis. Ability to communicate effectively with clients, internal partners, IT teams, and vendors. Strong understanding of operational risk, controls, and regulatory requirements, including BSA/AML. ESSENTIAL MENTAL & PHYSICAL REQUIREMENTS: Ability to travel if required to perform the essential job functions. Ability to work under stress and meet deadlines. Ability to operate related equipment to perform the essential job functions. Ability to read and interpret a document if required to perform the essential job functions. Ability to lift/move/carry approximately 10 pounds if required to perform the essential job functions. If the employee is unable to lift/move/carry this weight and can be accommodated without causing the department/division an “undue hardship” then the employee must be accommodated; hence omitting lifting/moving/carrying as a physical requirement.

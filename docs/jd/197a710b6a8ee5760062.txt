ABOUT RAMP

Ramp is building the smart infrastructure for finance teams, embedded in the transaction flow of every dollar a business spends. We automate how over $275B in annualized spend flows in and out of 85,000+ companies: authorizing payments, flagging risk, categorizing spend, and closing books.

The problems are high-stakes, data-dense, and unforgiving.

We hire people with high agency and high urgency. We look for slope over intercept. We care less about where you trained and more about what you’ve built. At Ramp, everyone is a builder who owns problems end to end and makes consequential decisions that shape the outcome.

The median Ramp customer saves 7.5% and grows revenue 16% in their first year – far in excess of businesses operating without Ramp. We believe every ambitious company deserves the same.

If you want to build systems that directly shape how companies move and manage billions, Ramp is the place to do it.




ABOUT THE ROLE

We're looking for builders and former founders who want to take big swings at Ramp. You'll tackle important, open-ended problems, turn ideas into working products, and own what happens after launch. This is a backend engineering role for someone who wants to shape what we build, not just how we build it.


Ramp's AI bets span products like Glass, an AI assistant for getting work done; Inspect, a platform for coding agents; and AI Spend Management, which helps businesses understand and manage their AI and token spend. These are examples of the problems that excite us: changing how people work, how software gets built, and how businesses put AI to use.



You'll have the opportunity to build from 0–1 and scale from 1–100, bringing new AI products to life and turning early traction into lasting customer value. You'll work closely with product, design, and other engineers, stay close to users, and help find the next opportunities to accelerate Ramp's growth. You don't need to have started a company, but you should bring the same initiative, resourcefulness, and accountability.




WHAT YOU’LL DO

 - Own products and backend systems from problem discovery and technical design through the first launch, adoption, and ongoing iteration

 - Turn ambiguous ideas into focused experiments, ship quickly, and use customer feedback and evidence to decide what to scale, change, or stop

 - Build the APIs, data models, integrations, and workflows that bring new AI products to life

 - Take promising products from 1–100 by improving reliability, performance, and the systems needed to support growing usage

 - Partner with product, design, and go-to-market teams to connect technical decisions to customer value, adoption, and Ramp’s growth

 - Make thoughtful bets, challenge assumptions, and balance speed with security, data correctness, and production quality


WHAT YOU’LL NEED

 - Strong backend engineering fundamentals and experience designing, building, and operating production services, APIs, and data models

 - Product judgment: you can identify the real user problem, question a proposed solution, and make clear tradeoffs with incomplete information

 - The initiative to create momentum without a complete specification, learn unfamiliar domains, and work beyond a narrow job description

 - Clear communication and a collaborative approach to solving hard problems across engineering, product, design, and business teams


NICE TO HAVES

 - Experience building AI-powered products, agent systems or developer tools

 - Experience scaling an early product into a reliable business and learning from customer adoption, retention, or revenue

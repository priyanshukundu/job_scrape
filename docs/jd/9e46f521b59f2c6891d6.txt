Job Family : Finance & Accounting Consulting Travel Required : None Clearance Required : Ability to Obtain Public Trust What You Will Do: As a Consultant on Guidehouse’s Federal Financial Services team, you will support a federal client’s Risk and Controls Self-Assessment (RCSA) Program. Under project team guidance, you will help assess risks and controls at the office and process levels, document business processes, test controls, and report findings. Review governance documents and stakeholder input, prepare questionnaires and walkthrough materials, and document business processes in narratives and flowcharts. Maintain risk registers and control matrices that document risk drivers, inherent and residual risks, existing controls, and control gaps. Assist with tests of control design and authorized tests of operating effectiveness. Organize supporting evidence and prepare workpapers for review. Prepare assessment reports, presentations, and meeting minutes. Support analyses that inform the project team’s recommendations for Government review and decision-making. Support quarterly cyber risk and technology monitoring by tracking key risk indicators and organizing supporting evidence. Help track corrective actions, report remediation progress, and maintain the RCSA program annually. Contribute to business development by identifying client needs, assisting with proposals and white papers, and participating in client and internal account meetings. Participate in coaching and mentoring programs, help develop learning materials and presentations, and support other Guidehouse initiatives. You will receive guidance on applying the RCSA methodology, OMB Circular A-123, the GAO Green Book, and relevant National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) guidance. You will build experience with internal control over financial reporting (ICOFR), internal control over reporting (ICOR), operations and compliance controls, and audit remediation. You will also learn about the client’s role in housing finance and secondary mortgage markets, including mortgage-backed securities and issuer and servicer oversight. What You Will Need: Must be able to OBTAIN and MAINTAIN a Federal or DoD "PUBLIC TRUST"; candidates must obtain approved adjudication of their PUBLIC TRUST prior to onboarding with Guidehouse. Due to contract requirements, candidates must be U.S. Citizens and reside within the United States. Bachelor’s degree from an accredited university in accounting, finance, business, economics, risk management, information systems, public policy, or a related field. Minimum of ONE (1) year of relevant professional experience in RCSA, audit, internal controls, compliance, business process improvement, or a related discipline. Relevant internships and academic projects may demonstrate foundational skills. Ability to assist, under project team guidance, with risk assessments, process documentation, risk registers and control matrices, control testing, cyber risk and technology monitoring, corrective action tracking, and assessment reporting. Attention to detail and ability to organize evidence, follow established methods and templates, and prepare accurate, complete workpapers. Ability to work collaboratively, complete assigned tasks, incorporate feedback, and communicate progress, questions, and issues promptly. Proficiency in Microsoft Word, Excel, and PowerPoint, and ability to learn Visio and other client-approved documentation and risk management tools. What Would Be Nice To H ave: A relevant credential, such as the COSO Internal Control Certificate, Certification in Risk Management Assurance (CRMA), Certified in Risk and Information Systems Control (CRISC), Certified Internal Auditor (CIA), Certified Information Systems Auditor (CISA), Certified Public Accountant (CPA), Certified Government Financial Manager (CGFM), or Certified Fraud Examiner (CFE). Familiarity with federal government lending and financial assistance programs. Experience supporting business process controls, audit remediation, or internal controls over reporting, operations, or compliance. The annual salary range for this position is $56,000.00-$94,000.00.

Join us and transform the lives of patients while transforming your career. Senior Data Scientist What you will do Let’s do this. Let’s change the world. The Global Quality Analytics & Innovation (GQAI) team leads digital transformation and innovation across Amgen’s Quality organization. We develop and deliver data-centric digital tools, using automation, artificial intelligence (AI), and generative AI to advance end-to-end quality transformation. We are seeking an experienced, highly motivated Data Scientist to develop AI agents, integrate enterprise tools and data through the Model Context Protocol (MCP), and build data products aligned with FAIR principles—Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, and Reusable. The successful candidate will translate business needs into practical, scalable AI and data solutions that improve efficiency and support informed decision-making. Roles and Responsibilities Design, develop, and deploy AI agents and agentic workflows to support Quality processes and business priorities. Build and integrate MCP servers and tools to connect AI solutions with enterprise data, applications, and services. Develop FAIR data products with clear metadata, defined ownership, and reusable interfaces to support analytics and AI. Collaborate with business, product, technology, architecture, platform, and data teams to deliver solutions aligned with enterprise standards. Translate business concepts and scientific questions into working prototypes, production-ready code, and scalable digital capabilities. Evaluate and monitor AI solutions and data products for accuracy, reliability, security, and business value. Assess emerging AI technologies and engineering practices, recommending approaches based on technical fit and implementation impact What we expect of you We are all different, yet we all use our unique contributions to serve patients. The dynamic professional we seek is a collaborative partner with these qualifications. Basic Qualifications: Doctorate degree in computer science, mathematics, computer/electrical engineering, or a related quantitative field OR Master’s degree and 2 years of data science, machine learning, or software development experience OR Bachelor’s degree and 4 years of data science, machine learning, or software development experience OR Associate’s degree and 8 years of data science, machine learning, or software development experience OR High school diploma / GED and 10 years of data science, machine learning, or software development experience Preferred Qualifications: Experience developing AI agents, generative AI applications, and LLM-based solutions using relevant frameworks. Familiarity with MCP and integrating AI applications with enterprise APIs, tools, and data sources. Strong proficiency in Python and SQL, with experience building modular, reusable, and maintainable software. Experience building data products using FAIR principles, including metadata management, data quality, interoperability, and governed access. Experience with cloud platforms and data technologies such as AWS, Spark, or Databricks. Familiarity with API development, version control, automated testing, CI/CD, and AI evaluation and monitoring practices. Strong problem-solving, collaboration, and communication skills, with the ability to translate technical concepts for diverse audiences. Experience in the biotechnology industry is not required.

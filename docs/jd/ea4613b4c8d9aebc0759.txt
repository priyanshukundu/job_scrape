The Risk Engineering Forensics Laboratory seeks an experienced engineer to lead forensic investigations of fire, electrical, mechanical, equipment, appliance, product, and property failures. We welcome candidates ranging from experienced engineers to seasoned forensic leaders, and the role will be filled at a level commensurate with experience, credentials, and leadership capabilities. The forensic engineering consultant will own complex investigations, laboratory examinations, and failure analyses to determine root cause, advising Claims, Legal, Subrogation, Risk Engineering, and Underwriting partners. Their findings will drive claim resolution, recovery opportunities, customer risk mitigation, and understanding of emerging risks. The role will also help shape the future of forensic engineering by advancing investigative methods and applying new technologies, data, and analytics to expand the impact of forensic insights. Responsibilities · Lead assigned forensic investigations involving residential, commercial, industrial, and institutional losses. · Own the technical analysis of physical evidence to determine origin, cause, root cause, and contributing factors. · Plan and execute laboratory examinations, testing, and technical evaluations. · Deliver clear, concise, and technically defensible reports. · Provide technical leadership for assigned matters involving litigation, subrogation, mediation, arbitration, and expert witness activities, as required. · Guide Claims professionals and business partners through the technical dimensions of complex losses. · Strengthen forensic capability by mentoring technical staff and advancing best practices and technical resources. · Advance the future of forensic engineering by anticipating emerging risks, championing new investigative approaches and technologies, and translating innovation into practical capabilities that strengthen forensic outcomes and business value. Organizational Impact · Translate forensic findings into actionable insights that improve claims outcomes, advance subrogation opportunities, inform customer risk mitigation, and strengthen business decision-making. · Proactively identify and communicate emerging loss trends, product-performance issues, and failure patterns that could affect customer risk, underwriting confidence, and loss-prevention priorities. · Drive cross-functional follow-through with Claims, Subrogation, Risk Engineering, Legal, Underwriting, and other business partners to ensure technical findings inform decisions and actions. · Advance forensic practices and organizational capability through technical guidance, knowledge sharing, mentoring, and adoption of new investigative methods and tools. · Identify patterns and emerging insights across investigations and translate them into opportunities to improve forensic practice, risk mitigation, and business outcomes. · Lead continuous improvement within assigned areas of expertise through data-driven analysis, trend identification, and evidence-based problem solving. Qualifications · 5+ years of experience conducting and reporting forensic investigations is required.

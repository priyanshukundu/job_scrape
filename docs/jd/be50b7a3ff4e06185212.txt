Position Title Senior Underwriter & Portfolio Manager, Asset Based Finance Location New York, NY 10018 Job Summary The Underwriter and Portfolio Manager Sr leads the initial reviews and subsequent underwriting of new business and assists portfolio transactions for Asset Based prospects and clients while adhering to the Bank's Credit Policies and Procedures. Senior Underwriter and PM leads ABF efforts to meet and evaluate prospective clients and existing client requests, structure credit facilities, conduct due diligence, and close both new transactions and portfolio client amendments and extensions. Job Responsibilities: Reviews financial and collateral information. Initial analysis to determine if Flagstar Bank should proceed to a deal screen memo. Analysis includes review of borrowing base, A/R agings, Inventory reports, historical and projected financials. Prepares Deal Screen Memo. Based on initial review of financial and collateral information, prepare a summary of the Company, anticipated structure of the Asset based Loan, identify strengths and risks and make a recommendation of action. Prepares Credit Decisioning Document. Combine analysis of the company, collateral and financial information with third party due diligence to prepare a deal summary for senior management and final approval including risk/mitigants and recommendation. Meets with prospective clients. Either by phone or in-person meetings with prospective clients to ask questions regarding financial performance, industry analysis, collateral and working capital cycles. Partner with the Portfolio Manager -Lead to structure and document amendments, renewals, and increases as needed. Prepare and present internal reporting, including risk ratings, portfolio reviews, and watchlist updates. Participate in portfolio approvals with senior leadership. Engages field examiner and appraisers; provide guidance for scope of exam; review reports Manages loan documentation, closing and fundings of new and existing transactions. Uses independent judgement and discretion to make decisions and recommendations for structure, funding or declinations. May be responsible for talent management functions including staff development/training, ADDITIONAL ACCOUNTABILITIES Performs special projects, and additional duties and responsibilities as required. Consistently adheres to regulatory and compliance policies and standards linked to the job as listed and complete required compliance trainings. Accountable to maintain compliance with applicable federal, state and local laws and regulations. JOB REQUIREMENTS Required Qualifications: Education level required: High School / High School Equivalency (GED, HiSET, TASC) / Foreign Equivalent, Bachelor’s degree preferred in Accounting, Finance, Economics. Minimum experience required: 10+ Years in Asset Based Lending including 2+ years as an underwriter or portfolio manager Preferred Qualifications: Education level preferred: Undergraduate Degree (4 years or equivalent) in Accounting, Finance, Economics or equivalent work experience if applicable Bank Credit Training or Field Exam experience Job Competencies: Asset Based Lending Moody’s Risk Analyst Spread System or similar spreading tools Requires strong written communication and analytical skills. Ability to evaluate financial and collateral trends Ability to communicate effectively with prospects, intermediaries and other internal and external stakeholders. Demonstrates a strong ability to build and maintain effective relationships with internal stakeholders by communicating clearly, engaging in proactive collaboration, and leveraging cross functional insights. Aligns relationship building efforts with enterprise goals to accelerate performance and drive strategic results. Builds trusted client relationships, whether internal or external, by identifying needs and delivering tailored solutions to enhance the overall client experience. Fosters or supports a positive work culture and productive work environment, displaying importance of effective relationships with customers and stakeholders. Travel requirements, 3-4 trips within the US per year. Physical demands (ADA): No unusual physical exertion is involved.

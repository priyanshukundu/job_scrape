Title: Analyst, Mortgage Capital Markets, Global Capital Markets Global Banking and Markets Global Banking and Markets (GBM) is a leading Canadian Capital Markets and Investment Banking business with a growing platform in the U.S. and Latin America and a global presence spanning more than 100 years. Scotiabank’s strong U.S. platform provides clients with an important bridge to capital and investment flows across the Americas and globally. GBM provides a broad range of investment banking, credit, capital markets and risk management products and services to support the financing and strategic needs of our clients. Our capabilities include debt and equity financing, mergers and acquisitions, corporate banking, fixed income, institutional equity sales and trading, research, derivatives, foreign exchange and other capital markets products. Purpose Contributes to the overall success of the Mortgage Capital Markets team in the United States by supporting the team’s mortgage financing, warehouse lending, credit, and capital markets activities. This role is intended for an experienced Analyst-level professional who has already completed their undergraduate degree and brings relevant full-time banking or financial services experience. Candidates must have completed their undergraduate degree by December 2026 and must not be enrolled in an undergraduate degree program beyond that date. What You’ll Do: Champions a customer focused culture to deepen client relationships and leverage broader Bank relationships, systems, and knowledge. Prepares pitch books and marketing presentations in support of the mortgage financing and capital markets platform. Assists in structuring, negotiating terms and documentation with borrowers and other third parties culminating in the closing of transactions. Works on maintaining the existing portfolio by handling clients' requests related to the warehouse facilities, collateral, and customer funding requests. Also draft annual risk reviews and works on internal credit underwriting approvals. Work closely with credit officers to coordinate the requisite reviews by risk management. Work closely with trading team to ensure daily marking of positions and requisite margin call monitoring. Attends due diligence meetings. Reviews daily collateral reports and monitors asset performance trends. Coordinates a variety of activities with the mid-office and operations teams including the closing of new and existing transactions, daily draw requests, tracking and monitoring of collateral, and providing them with updates or changes to the terms or funding of deals as required. Understand how the Bank’s risk appetite and risk culture should be considered in day-to-day activities and decisions. Actively pursues effective and efficient operations of his/her respective areas, while ensuring the adequacy, adherence to and effectiveness of day-to-day business controls to meet obligations with respect to operational risk, regulatory compliance risk, AML/ATF risk and conduct risk, including but not limited to responsibilities under the Operational Risk Management Framework, Regulatory Compliance Risk Management Framework, AML/ATF Global Handbook and the Guidelines for Business Conduct. What You’ll Bring Bachelor’s degree in Business, Finance, Economics, Accounting, or a related field, completed no later than December 2026. Candidates continuing undergraduate studies beyond December 2026 will not be considered. Prior full-time professional experience in banking, lending, capital markets, or a related financial services environment required. This role is ideally suited for a Senior Analyst or experienced Analyst-level candidate. Demonstrated knowledge of mortgage financing fundamentals, with experience in agency mortgage warehouse lending strongly preferred but not required. Prior experience in mortgage capital markets, mortgage warehouse financing, structured lending, or a closely related area strongly preferred but not required. Experience in corporate or commercial lending, credit underwriting, or a related banking environment. Experience performing credit analysis and reviewing transaction or legal documentation. Strong proficiency in Microsoft Excel, PowerPoint, and Word, with excellent written and verbal communication skills. Ability to prioritize competing responsibilities, exercise sound judgment, and communicate clearly and concisely with senior management. Strong team orientation and willingness to take a hands-on approach in a growing business. MBA, CPA, CFA, or other relevant industry designation is considered an asset. SIE and Series 63 and 79 licenses to be obtained as required. Interested?

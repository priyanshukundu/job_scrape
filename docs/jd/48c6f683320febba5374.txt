Associate Data Analyst
Valley National Bank
Location: Morristown, NJ, USA
Link: https://myjobs.adp.com/vnbcareers/cx/job-details?reqId=5001239691506

Responsibilities include, but are not limited to: Assist auditors through hands-on analysis of firm data and provide insights. Seek audit areas of opportunity where analytics can be applied to conduct testing versus the sample approach. Generate a visual analysis with charts/graphs for audits and audit functions. Interact with business partners to identify data sources and the data elements required for analytics. Work closely with audit staff towards meeting department goals. Use data analysis tools to build and perform continuous monitoring tasks and evaluate the results. Support requests from external auditors related to data extracts and data validation of key financial reports. Assist with audit fieldwork as needed.

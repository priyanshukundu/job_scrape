Working here means having every opportunity to achieve your goals – and to help others accomplish theirs, too. Join our team as we help shape the future. We are seeking a visionary and hands-on Data Science Manager to lead the next wave of intelligent Underwriting solutions within our Middle and Large Business segment. This role is ideal for a dynamic technical leader who thrives at the intersection of AI innovation, ML modelling and data science excellence. You will drive the design and implementation of cutting-edge AI-augmented integration solutions, leveraging technologies such as AI agentic framework, Gen AI solutions, knowledge graphs, in cloud platforms to transform how we build, connect, and scale intelligent business applications. With a strong data scientist mindset, you’ll lead by example—providing AI solutions, Solving complex domain intensive problems, mentoring high-performing teams, and shaping the future of AI-driven data science solutions. Responsibilities: Research, experiment with, and implement suitable Generative and ML algorithms, tools and technologies. Turn complex business challenges into measurable impact by delivering machine learning models and multi-agent systems, and ensure they perform reliably and as intended in production. Review work with leadership, product owners and partners on an ongoing basis to calibrate deliverables against expectations. Accountable for deployment design, development and maintenance of both traditional ML and AI models. Work with junior engineers and provide mentorship and thought leadership. Be comfortable presenting new concepts to technical and business audiences. Collaborate with partners Enterprise Data, Transformation/Business teams, Governance/Platform Team, and Enterprise Architecture teams Adopt emerging AI/ML technologies and best practices, and promote them across the Tech and Applied AI community. Requirements: Master’s degree in a related field, or 8+ years of equivalent experience in research or ML/AI solution design, development, and delivery. Demonstrated experience designing, building, deploying, and operating production-grade autonomous AI agents, multi-agent systems, and machine learning models in real-world enterprise environments. Strong hands-on experience designing, building, and deploying software in Python or Java, or C++, including cloud-based APIs and agentic AI services. Deep understanding of Generative AI, Machine Learning models, Large Reasoning Models and Agentic AI architectures. Experience with AI agent frameworks such as: Google ADK, LangGraph, CrewAI, AutoGen, Semantic Kernel and OpenAI Agents SDK Experience implementing: Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), Vector databases, Knowledge graphs, Fine-tuning models, Embedding models and Memory management techniques Experience developing repeatable architectural patterns; ability to identify redundancies and eliminate them with these patterns. Strong understanding of machine learning, reinforcement learning, prompt engineering, knowledge engineering and AI evaluation techniques. Experience in insurance, financial services, or highly regulated industries. Candidate must be authorized to work in the US without company sponsorship.

About the Role We are seeking a Robot Simulation Engineer to develop advanced simulation environments that support the development, testing, and validation of autonomous robotic systems. In this role, you will build 2D and 3D simulators, create realistic testing scenarios, integrate robot software with simulation platforms, and enable teams to safely evaluate functionality and behavior before real-world deployment. You will collaborate cross-functionally with autonomy and artificial intelligence experts to support system-level validation and continuously improve system robustness and testing workflows. What You’ll Do Develop and enhance 2D and 3D simulation platforms for testing, validation, and performance analysis of autonomous robotic systems. Create and maintain simulation scenarios representing real-world use cases and edge conditions. Integrate robot navigation software with simulation platforms. Support debugging and testing of robot behavior in simulated environments. Analyze simulation results to identify software and system issues. Develop regression and progression testing workflows for simulation-based validation. Collaborate with cross-functional teams to improve simulation capabilities and validation processes. Required Qualifications Bachelor’s, master’s, or doctoral degree in robotics, computer science, electrical or mechanical engineering, or a related field. Proficiency in Python or C++. Experience with simulation platforms such as NVIDIA Isaac Sim or Gazebo. Knowledge of physics engines, such as PhysX, Bullet, or ODE, or 3D game engines, such as Unity or Unreal Engine, used for robotic simulation. Familiarity with ROS 2 or other robotics middleware. Understanding of core robotics modules, including localization, mapping, and planning. Preferred Qualifications Experience with simulation tools and hardware-in-the-loop (HIL) testing. Familiarity with CI/CD pipelines for simulation-based regression or performance testing. Experience with cloud-based simulation systems. Experience integrating autonomous vehicles, advanced driver assistance systems (ADAS), or industrial automation systems. This role is categorized as hybrid.

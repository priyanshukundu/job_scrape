About the Opportunity
MoonPay is building a unified identity and KYC platform that can support customers across ramps, cards, virtual accounts and enterprise products.
Today, many financial products treat identity as part of a single onboarding flow. We are building it as a reusable platform capability: a secure identity layer that allows customers to verify once, maintain control of their information and access different MoonPay-powered products without repeating the same journey each time.
This role will help define the architecture and technical direction of that platform. You will work across product, engineering, compliance and security to determine how identity is captured, verified, stored, linked and exposed safely through APIs and SDKs.
As a Staff Software Engineer, you will hold a hands-on technical position. The problem is not fully defined, and we are looking for someone who can shape the platform while building its most important components.
Lead through ambiguity
Turn a broad identity and KYC vision into a clear technical strategy, architecture and sequence of deliverable work.
Identify the boundaries between identity, authentication, verification, compliance and product-specific onboarding.
Set the technical bar through rigorous reviews, clear standards and lasting engineering habits.
Align teams across ramps, cards, virtual accounts and enterprise products around a shared identity model.
Build the identity platform
Design a unified identity layer that can support multiple MoonPay products, partners and regulated use cases.
Model the lifecycle of a customer’s identity, including creation, verification, linking, updates, consent and deletion.
Build secure APIs and SDKs that allow internal teams and external partners to use identity capabilities without understanding the underlying complexity.
Integrate KYC and identity-verification providers while keeping MoonPay’s internal model consistent and extensible.
Develop clear ownership boundaries between the identity platform and the products built on top of it.
Protect sensitive customer data
Define how identity data is stored, accessed and shared across products, jurisdictions and legal entities.
Design for consent, auditability, data residency, retention and deletion from the beginning.
Build clear authorisation controls around who and what can access verified customer information.
Ensure the platform degrades safely when external verification providers or internal dependencies fail.
Partner closely with Security, Privacy, Legal and Compliance without allowing the platform to become difficult for developers to use.
Create a platform developers trust
Build public-facing APIs, SDKs and documentation that make complex identity workflows straightforward to integrate.
Establish stable contracts, versioning strategies and migration paths for teams and partners using the platform.
Provide observability into identity journeys, verification decisions and integration failures.
Reduce the need for product teams to rebuild identity and KYC logic independently.

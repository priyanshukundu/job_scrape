STAFF SOFTWARE ENGINEER – FILE INGESTION (CUSTOMER-FACING SERVICES)


BUILD THE FUTURE OF THE AI DATA CLOUD

Snowflake’s File Ingestion team owns the core engines behind COPY INTO and Snowpipe — the primary arteries landing customer data into the AI Data Cloud at hundreds of petabytes per quarter.

We are expanding our core mission to build next-generation, external-facing ingestion services that directly expose file-ingest capabilities to external platforms, partner ecosystems, and customer applications. As a Staff Software Engineer, you will lead the architectural vision and execution for these mission-critical, customer-facing services. You will operate at the intersection of high-throughput distributed systems, API platform design, and direct customer feature delivery, defining how enterprise data seamlessly flows into Snowflake at global scale.


WHAT YOU’LL DO

 - Drive Technical Vision & Architecture: Define the multi-year architecture and technical strategy for high-throughput, external-facing ingestion services and APIs across multi-cloud environments (AWS, Azure, GCP).

 - Deliver Customer-Facing Innovations: Lead the end-to-end execution of enterprise-grade external features, ensuring low-latency data landing, high availability, and seamless ecosystem integration (e.g., Snowpark Connect, partner connectors).

 - Set Technical Direction: Author foundational design documents, establish technical standards for reliability, security, and scalability, and lead high-stakes architectural reviews across engineering orgs.

 - Lead Cross-Functional Alignment: Partner closely with Principal Architects, Product Management, Security, and external developer platforms to translate customer requirements into resilient system architecture.

 - Elevate Engineering Excellence: Mentor and sponsor Senior, Mid-level, and Junior engineers; raise the bar for system design, code quality, testing methodologies, and operational maturity.

 - Direct Customer & Strategic Impact: Engage with enterprise customer engineering teams to solve complex integration challenges, debug production edge cases at scale, and turn direct customer feedback into core platform capabilities.


WHAT WE’RE LOOKING FOR

 - 10+ years of experience designing, building, and operating large-scale distributed systems or cloud-native infrastructure in production.

 - Proven track record of architectural leadership, having successfully taken complex, customer-facing services or external APIs from initial concept to General Availability (GA) and global scale.

 - Expert proficiency in Java or C++ (strong preference for engineers comfortable working across both systems-level C++ and managed service Java frameworks).

 - Deep expertise in external API design, cloud service orchestration, and building resilient, multi-tenant customer-facing endpoints.

 - Deep domain knowledge in at least one of the following: high-throughput ingestion pipelines, distributed query execution engines, messaging/streaming systems (e.g., Kafka, Pulsar), or database internals.

 - Exceptional written and verbal communication skills with a demonstrated ability to drive technical consensus across cross-functional leadership, product partners, and external technical stakeholders.

 - BS, MS, or PhD in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, or equivalent practical experience.


BONUS POINTS

 - Experience building and scaling external developer platforms, SDKs, or public SaaS APIs serving external consumers.

 - Deep expertise with open table formats (Apache Iceberg, Delta Lake) and integration with ecosystems like Apache Spark or Ray.

 - Advanced performance engineering expertise: memory management, SIMD, vectorization, lock-free data structures, or I/O optimization at PB-scale.

 - Strong background in metadata management, transactional state machines, or consensus protocols (e.g., Raft, Paxos).

 - Active contributor to open-source data infrastructure, cloud-native compute systems, or query engines.


WHY THIS TEAM

 - Massive Strategic Impact: You will lead the expansion of Snowflake's most critical data-path surface into direct, customer-facing integrations that unlock new platform ecosystems and drive platform volume.

 - Broad Architectural Scope: Your work will bridge edge API layers, control-plane orchestrators, file scanners/parsers, query planners, and underlying storage engines.

 - High Customer Visibility: Work directly on features that power the external integrations for Fortune 500 enterprises, seeing the immediate revenue and performance impact of your architecture.

 - Culture of Technical Leadership: Partner directly with Snowflake’s top architects and leads in an environment that values deep technical autonomy, rigorous engineering, and long-term architectural stewardship.

JOIN TARGET AS A SR DATA SCIENTIST – 1:1 PERSONALIZATION, AUDIENCE and EXPERIMENTATION About Us: Working at Target means helping all families discover the joy of everyday life. We bring that vision to life through our values and culture. Learn more about Target here . A role with Target Data Sciences means the chance to help develop and manage state of the art predictive algorithms that use data at scale to automate and optimize decisions at scale. Whether you join our Applied Data Sciences or Machine Learning teams, you’ll be challenged to harness Target’s impressive data breadth to build the algorithms that power solutions our partners in Digital Marketing, Supply Chain Optimization, Advanced AI, Search and Personalization rely on. Every Scientist on Target’s Data Sciences team can expect modeling and data science, software/product development of highly performant code for model performance at scale. As a Sr Data Scientist you will support the development of machine learning models that power one-to-one personalization at scale. In this role, you will design, build, and deploy advanced models that deliver highly relevant, individualized experiences across marketing and digital channels. You will collaborate with cross-functional partners in product, engineering, marketing, and analytics to define strategy, lead experimentation, and ensure that personalization drives measurable impact for our guests and business. Responsibilities: Model Development: Design, build, and optimize ML models for one-to-one personalization, including recommendation systems, ranking models, customer propensity models, and reinforcement learning approaches Experimentation & Measurement: Develop testing frameworks (A/B, multi-armed bandits, causal inference) to rigorously measure the impact of personalization initiatives within marketing optimization Collaboration: Partner closely with product managers, engineers, and business stakeholders to translate business problems into data science solutions Scalability & Deployment: Ensure models are production-ready, scalable, and integrated into digital platforms with high reliability and performance Innovation: Stay current on advances in ML/AI (deep learning, embeddings, transformers, large language models, reinforcement learning) and evaluate their application for personalization use cases Core responsibilities of this job are articulated within this job description. Job duties may change at any time due to business needs.

Become a part of our caring community The Senior Software Engineer leads the design, optimization, and modernization of critical HIT solutions supporting eHub and related business teams. This role improves system reliability, operational efficiency, and provider experience through strong engineering leadership, secure design, automation, and modern platform practices. The Senior Software Engineer is responsible for designing, engineering, and optimizing Batch, EDI, and Clearinghouse technology solutions that support business processes. This role combines hands-on software and integration engineering with technical leadership to improve system performance, reliability, compliance, and provider experience. The position also drives modernization through automation, AI-enabled productivity, and scalable integration patterns. This role will exercise considerable latitude in determining objectives and approaches to assignments. This hybrid position is based in Louisville, KY or Nashville, TN and requires onsite presence approximately 1-2 times per week. Key Responsibilities Technology Leadership and Solution Delivery Design, build, enhance, and support scalable, secure, and reliable technology solutions in partnership with Development, Operations, Product, and Architecture teams. Contribute to modernization, simplification, and lifecycle improvement efforts across applications, platforms, and integrations. Lead the technical delivery of moderately complex to complex initiatives and provide input into solution design, implementation approach, and future-state improvements. Analyze recurring system issues, performance bottlenecks, and operational inefficiencies, and implement sustainable improvements. Troubleshoot and resolve complex application, connectivity, and processing issues in collaboration with internal teams and external partners. Support system stability, performance, and availability through proactive monitoring, root cause analysis, and corrective action planning. Automation, AI, and Engineering Productivity Develop and implement automation solutions that reduce manual effort, improve consistency, and increase delivery speed and quality. Evaluate and apply AI-enabled tools and intelligent automation where appropriate to improve engineering productivity and operational workflows. Contribute to engineering efficiency initiatives, including:Application and API rationalizationReporting simplification and reduction of redundant dataReduction of unnecessary application and system footprint Apply modern software engineering, integration, and DevOps practices to improve maintainability, deployment reliability, and operational effectiveness. Support continuous improvement of development workflows, testing practices, and deployment automation. Security, Compliance, and Quality Support technology lifecycle management activities, including patching, upgrades, decommissioning, and modernization efforts. Work with security partners to identify, assess, prioritize, and remediate vulnerabilities within established timelines. Follow secure engineering practices, including proper secrets management, removal of hard-coded credentials, and adherence to enterprise security standards. Build and maintain solutions in alignment with enterprise operational, compliance, and quality expectations. Contribute to testing, validation, production readiness, and audit support activities as needed. Collaboration, Mentorship, and Technical Guidance Collaborate effectively with engineers, product partners, architects, operations teams, and other stakeholders to deliver business value. Provide technical guidance to team members on design, troubleshooting, coding practices, and engineering standards. Mentor less experienced engineers through knowledge sharing, code reviews, and support of best practices. Contribute to architectural discussions and integration strategy by providing engineering input and implementation insight. Communicate technical issues, risks, recommendations, and solution options clearly to both technical and non-technical audiences.

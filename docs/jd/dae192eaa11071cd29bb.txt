Job Summary The Manager, Analytics Operations role is a unique opportunity to help shape how data and AI drive decision-making across Insulet as the company continues to expand its impact on people living with diabetes. This position plays a critical role in advancing Insulet’s analytics strategy, enabling smarter business decisions, accelerating innovation, and supporting the development of life-changing technologies like Omnipod. Working at the intersection of data, healthcare, and leadership, you will help transform complex information into meaningful insights that influence products, patients, and operations across the organization while contributing to Insulet’s mission of improving lives through technology. Position Overview: Insulet Corporation, maker of the OmniPod, is the leader in tubeless insulin pumps. The Manager Analytics Operations role is a highly technical leadership position in Insulet’s Analytics COE responsible for the execution of global analytics and data science initiatives, according to Insulet’s data analytics strategy. Working for the Senior Director, Artificial Intelligence, Analytics and Advanced Algorithms, you will mentor Data Scientists on the team, and guide the development of tools and processes to transform data into insights with cross-functional subject matter experts. Using data cleaning best practices, aggregation, and joining large datasets, your team will leverage cloud technologies to develop and deploy analytics reporting for a comprehensive view of the business, product, and patient. You will gain detailed knowledge of multiple facets of business operation and collaborate with senior staff in the Analytics COE to support cross-functional efforts spanning R&D, clinical, medical affairs, quality, and global manufacturing among others. This position is intended for an individual with a data science background, and with a demonstrated track record of effective leadership who is highly competent in both hands-on data science work and management responsibilities. We are a fast-growing company that provides an energetic work environment, and tremendous career growth opportunities. Responsibilities: Interface with IT, executives, and numerous cross functional leaders across the breadth of the organization to identify data science opportunities with extraordinary business value and then coordinate, mentor, guide and support the Analytics COE scientists to fully realize the potential value. Manage a team of data scientists to execute high quality data science and analytics efforts and provide excellent leadership to continually develop their potential and ensure retention of talented people. Own the data products and insights delivery portfolio of analytics and guide the use of cloud computing technologies for building and deploying automated machine learning and analytics pipelines, such as Azure, AWS, GCP, etc. Explore data patterns using existing data science tools and self-created programs. Perform statistical analysis, predictive modeling, and generate automated reporting as required. Clearly communicate findings to a broad audience, in relation to business objectives. Maintain a yearly budget with periodic updates and reforecasting. Perform other duties as required. Insulet Corporation (NASDAQ: PODD), headquartered in Massachusetts, is an innovative medical device company dedicated to simplifying life for people with diabetes and other conditions through its Omnipod product platform. The Omnipod Insulin Management System provides a unique alternative to traditional insulin delivery methods. With its simple, wearable design, the tubeless disposable Pod provides up to three days of non-stop insulin delivery, without the need to see or handle a needle. Insulet’s flagship innovation, the Omnipod 5 Automated Insulin Delivery System, integrates with a continuous glucose monitor to manage blood sugar with no multiple daily injections, zero fingersticks, and can be controlled by a compatible personal smartphone in the U.S. or by the Omnipod 5 Controller. Insulet also leverages the unique design of its Pod by tailoring its Omnipod technology platform for the delivery of non-insulin subcutaneous drugs across other therapeutic areas.

About the Team: Authentication Core decides how every user, admin, and integration signs in to Salesforce. We own login flows, session management, MFA, and the OAuth 2.0 / OpenID Connect and SAML endpoints that issue and validate credentials at global scale. We also own the defenses around them: binding sessions to devices (DBSC and Web Crypto) to prevent session hijacking, rolling out mTLS, enforcing PKCE and refresh-token rotation for connected apps, and retiring legacy login protocols. A mistake here is a security incident or a global outage, so we hold a high bar for correctness, security, and operations. Your Impact: Your work protects every Salesforce customer’s front door. You’ll ship security improvements to hundreds of millions of users without breaking the integrations enterprises depend on.

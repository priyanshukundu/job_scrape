Job Description The Finance and Accounting team is seeking an experienced Senior Associate to join the US Public Credit Financial Reporting team, with a focus on SEC reporting and technical accounting research. The US Public Credit Financial Reporting team oversees the external financial reporting function for multiple Business Development Companies (BDCs) and other investment vehicles subject to the Investment Company Act of 1940. The team is responsible for SEC reporting, technical accounting, and the preparation of financial statements and related disclosures, partnering closely with Fund Accounting, FP&A, Legal, Investor Relations, and external auditors. This role requires a highly hands-on, detail-oriented professional with strong SEC reporting experience, deep familiarity with Workiva (Wdesk), and a working knowledge of investment company accounting and reporting requirements under the Investment Company Act of 1940. Responsibilities: Prepare and file SEC reports using Workiva (Wdesk) including Forms 10-Q, 10-K, 8-K, earnings releases, N-CSR, N-CSRS, presentations and registration statements Prepare roll-forwards and drafts SEC documents within Workiva, ensuring accuracy and consistency Own XBRL validation, and submission, including ongoing maintenance and resolution of SEC validation errors Prepare and update financial statement disclosures in accordance with U.S. GAAP, SEC rules, and applicable investment company/BDC requirements Perform detailed tie-outs across all external reporting deliverables

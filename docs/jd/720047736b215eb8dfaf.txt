Relocation assistance is not available for this position. What you'll do: Design, develop, code, and test technical solutions. Write code to deliver features, and improvements, on a regular basis. Investigates and resolve technical problems and production issues through troubleshooting techniques. Continually improves operations by conducting systems analysis and recommending changes in policies and procedures. What you have: Bachelor's degree; OR 4 years of relevant education and/or experience. 2 years of software development experience specific to a discipline/technology. Experience with GitHub. Experience with the Spring Framework. 1 year of experience delivering technology solutions in all phases of the software systems and application development lifecycle. Basic understanding of one or more of the following: Java, JavaScript, C++, SQL, or Python. Knowledge and understanding of troubleshooting applications and systems within production issues. Ability to quickly understand applicable business acumen to provide appropriate engineering solutions. Ability to effectively articulate technical challenges and solutions. Basic understanding of cloud technologies and tools. What sets you apart: Experience designing and building APIs using Spring Boot . Experience with Kafka.

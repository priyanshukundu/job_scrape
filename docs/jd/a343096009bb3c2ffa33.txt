Role Location - Hybrid (three days in-office, two days virtual) Jacksonville, Florida About the role As a Senior Artificial Intelligence/Machine Learning Engineer, you will join a growing AI Platform organization within the Evaluation & Compliance area. You will design, build, deploy, evaluate and operate production AI systems, with particular focus on generative AI agents, agentic systems, performance measurement, CI quality gates and guardrails. You will work independently on complex engineering problems while partnering with data scientists, computer scientists, platform teams, product partners, risk and compliance stakeholders. Your key responsibilities Design, build, deploy and operate production GenAI and agentic applications, including tool-enabled agents, orchestration workflows, structured outputs and enterprise integrations. Develop evaluation frameworks for agents and LLM applications, including baselines, test datasets, automated graders, human review, regression testing and failure analysis. Measure and improve agent quality, reliability, latency, throughput, availability and cost using production traces, metrics, logs and evaluation results. Build data pipelines that prepare structured and unstructured data for model training, retrieval, inference and evaluation. Select and implement model and agent architectures based on accuracy, safety, explainability, scalability, maintainability and operational requirements. Create CI/CD quality gates, guardrails and engineering standards in partnership with Risk, Compliance and domain experts to support secure, privacy-aware and responsible AI delivery. Conduct design and code reviews, troubleshoot complex production issues, coach developing engineers and contribute reusable engineering patterns and documentation. Deploy and support containerized AI services using APIs, cloud platforms, Kubernetes or comparable orchestration technologies and modern software-engineering practices. Use AI-assisted development practices to accelerate analysis, implementation, testing and debugging while maintaining human review and strong engineering standards. What you bring Required qualifications Approximately 5+ years of software engineering experience is a useful guideline; demonstrated depth building production AI systems. Hands-on experience designing, building, deploying and operating GenAI agents or agentic systems in production. Generic exposure to ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot or prompt engineering alone is not sufficient. Strong coding and software engineering fundamentals in Python, Java or another relevant production language; the team is not limiting the search to one language. Experience evaluating AI/ML or LLM systems with meaningful metrics, controlled datasets, regression safeguards and production telemetry. Experience integrating models or agents with enterprise data, APIs, services and tools, together with strong communication and stakeholder-management skills and a university degree or equivalent practical experience. Preferred qualifications Experience with multi-agent systems, agent orchestration, retrieval-augmented generation, tool/function calling, structured outputs and failure handling. Experience with AI evaluation and observability capabilities such as Langfuse, MLflow, Arize, WhyLabs or comparable platforms, plus knowledge of model risk management and responsible AI. Experience with Kubernetes, GitHub Actions, Snowflake or comparable cloud-native production environments and optimizing agent workflows for quality, latency, throughput and cost. The impact you’ll make As a Senior Artificial Intelligence/Machine Learning Engineer, you will help FIS move AI solutions beyond prototypes into dependable production capabilities. Your work will strengthen agent evaluation, production readiness, engineering quality and governance while creating reusable practices that accelerate delivery across the AI Platform organization.

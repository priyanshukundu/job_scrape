Your Opportunity At Schwab, you’re empowered to make an impact on your career. Here, innovative thought meets creative problem solving, helping us challenge the status quo and transform the finance industry together. Technology Risk Management provides independent oversight that helps Schwab identify, understand, and address technology risk. As a Senior Manager, Technology Risk Management, you will evaluate first-line risk assessments for new products, services, and material changes across Information Security, Data, Information Technology, and Operational Resilience. Using Risk Management, Problem Solving, and Decision-Making skills, you will assess risk ratings, control design, evidence, and emerging exposures involving AI, cloud, third parties, access management, architecture, and data sharing. You will form clear, evidence-based positions and recommend risk-appropriate controls or conditions that support informed launch and governance decisions. Success requires Collaboration, Influencing, Communication, and Adaptability. You will provide constructive challenge, raise material concerns early, communicate decision-useful recommendations to senior audiences, and improve oversight practices while maintaining independent second-line judgment. This individual contributor role also supports major technology initiatives, standards reviews, testing, and audit or regulatory-exam readiness. What you have To ensure that we fulfill our promise of challenging the status quo, this role has specific qualifications that successful candidates should have. Required Qualifications Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Information Systems, Business Administration, Risk Management, or a related field, or an equivalent combination of education and experience. 8+ years of experience in technology risk management, technology governance, IT audit, information security, project or program risk, controls, or a related discipline. Risk Management skills demonstrated through evaluating risk assessments, control design, supporting evidence, and residual risk ratings to reach defensible conclusions. Problem Solving and Decision-Making skills demonstrated through identifying material gaps, evaluating incomplete or conflicting information, and recommending risk-appropriate action. Broad working knowledge of technology risks across Information Security, Data, Information Technology, and Operational Resilience, with expertise in at least one area. Communication skills demonstrated through preparing clear, audit-ready risk narratives and decision-useful recommendations for senior leaders and governance forums. Collaboration and Influencing skills demonstrated through constructively challenging business, technology, product, architecture, and risk partners while maintaining second-line independence. Familiarity with FFIEC, NIST, COBIT, or ITIL frameworks and established project management practices. Preferred Qualifications Experience providing second-line risk oversight, new-product governance, project or program governance, or audit support, including regulatory examinations or governance committee reviews. Experience in financial services or another regulated industry. Working knowledge of AI, machine learning, and generative AI risks, limitations, and control considerations. CRISC, CISA, CISSP, CGEIT, PMP, or PMI-ACP certification. Experience assessing cloud, API, third-party, identity and access management, or data-sharing integrations. Project Management skills applied to technology initiatives, risk remediation, implementation readiness, or evidence-based issue closure. Adaptability and Business Acumen demonstrated through learning evolving technologies, understanding business context, and applying sound judgment across a broad portfolio.

(2026 Inc. 5000) If you want to help build the next era of lending, we’d love to meet you. About the Role We're looking for a Senior Analyst, Portfolio Management to join our growing team! As Senior Analyst, Portfolio Management, you'll support our mortgage loan servicing oversight function with the analysis, reporting, and day-to-day monitoring that keep servicing efficient, compliant, and effective at reducing delinquency and losses. You'll work closely with Portfolio Management leadership to evaluate loss mitigation and default strategies, track how customers in financial hardship are engaged, and refine programs that put borrowers on a long-term path to cure. You'll also keep our data, processes, and systems aligned so key stakeholders (Customer, Company, and Investors) get clear, accurate visibility into portfolio performance. If you want to turn servicing data into decisions that cut delinquency and are ready to own the numbers, this role is for you.

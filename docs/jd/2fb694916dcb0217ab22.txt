Barclays Services Corp. seeks Quantitative Analyst Risk Modeler in New York, NY (multiple positions available): Design, develop, and optimize quantitative risk solutions supporting IFRS 9 and CECL expected credit loss (ECL) frameworks, ensuring seamless integration with enterprise risk platforms and data pipelines. Implement scalable, production-grade Python and SQL applications that automate data processing, model execution, and reporting while maintaining high performance, reliability, and operational stability. Evaluate system performance through back-testing, benchmarking, and monitoring of model outputs, identifying optimization opportunities to improve computational efficiency and business usability. Build risk solutions that integrate effectively into existing systems and processes while delivering strong and consistent performance. Create project designs; develop predictive models and strategies; and create production code for model implementation. Work closely with the technical disciplines of the Internal Governance and Control Team, Internal Validation Unit, Model Monitoring to ensure that the latest methodologies and constraints are incorporated in the acquisition, capital and impairment plans and to provide efficient documentation around the process to ensure smooth audit. Produce comprehensive documentation to ensure the models can be replicated and to indicate how the models adhere to internal, external and regulatory requirements.

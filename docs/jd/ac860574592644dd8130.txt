Role Overview: We are seeking a software engineer who is fluent in numerical computing to build the software foundation of our electromagnetic warfare product line. Our Spectrum Sensing team is transforming CHAOS radar systems into distributed sensing systems that detect, classify, and geolocate RF emissions — and the algorithms that do it only matter if they run correctly, quickly, and reliably in production. This is a software engineering role first. You will own how our numerical and signal processing code is architected, integrated, tested, and accelerated. You do not need a background in RF or radar; you do need to be the person on the team who knows why a Python implementation is slow, what to do about it, and how to build a codebase that other engineers can safely extend. You will work alongside signal processing and RF engineers who supply the algorithms, and you will be the reason those algorithms become dependable software. Responsibilities: As a key contributor to the Spectrum Sensing team, you will shape the software architecture and engineering practices behind our sensing stack. The day-to-day will be diverse, engaging, and intellectually stimulating, with direct impact on pushing the boundaries of sensing technology and application.

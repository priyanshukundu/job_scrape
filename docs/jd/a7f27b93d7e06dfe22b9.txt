Work you’ll do/Responsibilities Support business functions through the development, configuration, maintenance, and continuous improvement of Palantir Foundry applications. The work spans software development, data pipeline delivery, Ontology governance, application support, real-time operations, regulatory compliance, and infrastructure coordination. The role helps plan, deliver, and operate integrated capabilities that meet end-user business and performance needs. Build and maintain Palantir Foundry data pipelines, Ontology objects, and Workshop applications. Develop and test transforms under the guidance of senior engineers. Troubleshoot defects and support timely resolution. Help maintain production platforms supporting wildfire safety and emergency operations. Support regulatory reporting deadlines by monitoring data and application issues and escalating risks promptly. Communicate regularly with Engagement Managers (Directors), project team members, and representatives from various functional and / or technical teams, including escalating any matters that require additional attention and consideration from engagement management. Independently and collaboratively lead client engagement workstreams focused on improvement, optimization, and transformation of processes including implementing leading practice workflows, addressing deficits in quality, and driving operational outcomes. Qualifications Required 4 years of experience working as a Palantir Engineer 4 years of experience developing, sustaining, and maintaining applications in Palantir Foundry. 4 years of experience building and maintaining data pipelines, develop transforms, and working with Ontology objects and Workshop applications. 4 years of experience supporting IT operations and production environments, including troubleshooting defects and escalating issues in Palantir. Ability to work under the guidance of senior engineers and collaborate with technical teams. Ability to support applications and data processes subject to strict regulatory reporting deadlines. Familiarity with wildfire safety, emergency operations, or utility platforms is beneficial.

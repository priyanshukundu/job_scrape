Senior Associate Scientist, Transgenic Biology
Regeneron
Location: TARRYTOWN
Link: https://regeneron.wd1.myworkdayjobs.com/Careers/job/TARRYTOWN/Senior-Associate-Scientist--Transgenic-Biology_R53043

Build our future together: At Regeneron, we use science and innovation to develop life-changing medicines for people with serious diseases. We are seeking a Sr. Associate Scientist to join our Velocigene Assisted Reproductive Technology and Transgenic Biology group, expanding our in vitro and in vivo gene editing capabilities. In this hybrid molecular biology, cell culture, and in vivo role, you will run CRISPR experiments (Cas9, prime editing, and base editing), build sequencing and long-read libraries, and support mouse studies from injection through tissue collection, driving innovation in genome modification techniques and tools for therapeutic advancements. This position offers the opportunity to contribute to a fast-growing, science-driven organization making a meaningful difference to patients worldwide. When & where: Location: Tarrytown, NY Work model/flexibility: onsite Discover your role: Perform cell culture-based experiments, including transfection, electroporation, and viral transduction across immortalized cell lines and primary cells Design and execute CRISPR/Cas9 experiments, including guide design, delivery, and downstream analysis of editing outcomes Perform molecular biology techniques, including PCR, qPCR, cloning, and nucleic acid/protein handling Prepare DNA for sequencing applications, including long-read and ONT library prep where applicable Manage in vivo mouse studies, including tail vein and retro-orbital injections, tissue dissection, and sample collection Work independently to plan, execute, and troubleshoot experiments under the supervision of colleagues and manager Collaborate closely with team members and cross-functional partners Maintain thorough, timely records and summarize/present results in formal and informal settings This role requires: M.S. preferred, with 2-5 years of directly relevant hands-on laboratory experience Hands-on experience with cell culture technique, including transfection, electroporation, and viral transduction Prior hands-on experience working with mice Strong bench skills with a demonstrated ability to work independently and troubleshoot experiments A genuine eagerness to learn new techniques and a strong collaborative mindset Preferred: experience with CRISPR/Cas9-based genome editing Preferred: experience with Western Blot, ELISA, FACS, and flow cytometry (panel design and analysis) Preferred: experience with injections (IP, tail vein, and/or RO) and tissue collection, and long-read/ONT sequencing workflows Does this sound like you?

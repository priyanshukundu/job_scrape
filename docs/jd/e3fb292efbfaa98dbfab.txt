Morgan Wealth Management is focused on helping investors achieve their long-term financial goals. Field Performance & Incentives supports programs that reinforce our business strategy and help ensure incentive programs are administered accurately and effectively. You will join a collaborative team where you can apply your financial, analytical, and process improvement experience while contributing to meaningful programs that support our advisors and clients. As an Associate within the Field Performance & Incentives Programs Administration team, you will support the financial administration, reporting, and execution of key advisor programs. You will manage financial activity, analyze program data, support payments and reconciliations, and partner with Finance and other stakeholders to help ensure accurate execution. Your work will help strengthen financial controls, improve data quality, and provide clear reporting and insights to business leaders. You will also identify opportunities to simplify processes and develop more automated and scalable reporting solutions. This role provides an opportunity to combine financial management, analytics, controls, and process improvement while contributing to programs that support the advisor and client experience. Job Responsibilities Manage the financial administration of key advisor programs, including calculating and validating payments, maintaining financial records, tracking program activity, and coordinating timely and accurate execution. Reconcile financial activity across multiple data sources, research discrepancies, validate calculations and payments, and partner with stakeholders to resolve exceptions. Partner with Finance, Controllers, Compensation, and other business stakeholders on financial transactions, payment activity, reconciliations, and related program requirements. Develop and maintain recurring financial and management reporting and analyze program activity, revenue, payments, trends, and exceptions to provide insights to business leaders. Maintain financial controls and supporting documentation to help ensure calculations, adjustments, approvals, and payments are accurate, appropriately documented, and reproducible. Identify opportunities to improve processes, data, and reporting through standardization, automation, and effective use of technology. Support broader program administration, analysis, issue resolution, and ongoing program enhancements in partnership with team members and business stakeholders.

Job Description Summary Responsible for the day-to-day execution and ongoing development of Transamerica Inc.’s Enterprise Risk Management program. Assists with the oversight of the design, implementation, and maintenance of the Enterprise Risk Management framework, policies, risk appetite statements, risk tolerances, and related governance and reporting practices. Provides enterprise-wide coordination for monitoring, aggregation, escalation, reporting, and disclosure of key risks to support informed decision-making and ensure alignment with the company’s strategy, regulatory obligations, and corporate standards. Partners with business leaders, risk owners, and other key stakeholders across all Strategic Business Units to promote consistent risk management practices, clarify risk ownership and accountability, and strengthen the company’s risk culture. Job Description Responsibilities Support the development, implementation, and maintenance of Transamerica Inc.’s Enterprise Risk Management framework, policies, standards, risk appetite statements, risk tolerances, and related governance processes. Serve as a subject matter expert on ERM principles, frameworks, policies, risk appetite, risk tolerances, and governance practices. Develop and coordinate enterprise risk assessments (Emerging Risk Scans, Global Climate Risk Assessments, Risk Culture), risk profiles, dashboards, committee materials, and reporting for senior management, executive risk committees, and the Board, as appropriate. Monitor enterprise risk exposures against established risk appetite statements, risk tolerances, limits, and escalation thresholds, and coordinate appropriate reporting and escalation. Represent Enterprise Risk Management on cross-functional initiatives, and working groups, and participate in relevant risk committee discussions. Partner with business and risk leaders, risk owners, and other stakeholders across Transamerica and the other business units to promote consistent risk management practices and clear accountability. Lead and coordinate team resources in the execution of ERM activities, assessments, reporting cycles, projects, and program enhancements. Support ERM-related regulatory reporting (ORSA), audits, rating agency inquiries, and other internal or external reviews, as applicable. Perform other duties and responsibilities as assigned in support of the Enterprise Risk Management program.

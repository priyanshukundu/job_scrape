Job Description As a valued colleague on our team, you will act as a team lead to provide risk oversight for the Single-Family mortgage business by generating meaningful insights for executive leadership, providing effective challenge to key initiatives and applications, developing change recommendations, and improving or automating risk processes. You will coach, mentor, and guide the team in the application of data mining and data analysis methods, be adept with common large data processing techniques, computational programing capabilities, practical problem-solving skills, and have an ability to articulate solutions to non-technical consumers or partners. As a lead associate, you will partner and coordinate with business, data engineering, and data management stakeholders, and provide guidance to your team in the application of advanced data techniques and the production of effective risk reporting. THE IMPACT YOU WILL MAKE The Lead Data Scientist – Corporate Risk, Single Family role will offer you the flexibility to make each day your own, while working alongside people who care so that you can deliver on the following responsibilities: Coordinate product and/or business owners across divisions or product lines, data engineers, and platform teams to define business needs and advise on current capabilities, data availability, and alternative uses. Lead the implementation of new statistical modeling and analytic capabilities to support risk identification, measurement, and oversight. Apply advanced analytic capabilities to identify emerging risk patterns, measure exposures, and monitor changes in risk. Apply innovative research and testing practices to evaluate business initiatives and risk outcomes. Design analytic applications to support risk measurement, independent assessment, and effective challenge. Design data visualizations, technical documentation, and non-technical presentation materials to communicate new ideas and high-impact solutions to business partners. THE EXPERIENCE YOU BRING TO THE TEAM Minimum Required Experiences: 4 years Bachelors degree in Data Science, Business Analytics, Computer Science, Engineering Finance, Math, Physics, Statistics, or a related field Strong understanding of risk management and credit risk, with knowledge of mortgage finance and residential real estate Shows curiosity and adaptability in learning and responsibly applying new technologies, including artificial intelligence, to reimagine how we work. Desired Experiences: Masters or PhD* in Data Science, Economics, Math, Statistics, or a related field (*or comparable experience) Experience applying advanced data analysis, statistical modeling, machine learning, and generative SI tools. Experience producing executive-level risk reporting for senior leadership. Ability to advise senior stakeholders, challenge assumptions, and explain technical ideas clearly to non-technical audiences. Ability to provide independent oversight, review, or effective challenge of automated underwriting, credit decisioning, or credit risk models. Demonstrated proficiency in SQL and R; experience with Python is preferred.

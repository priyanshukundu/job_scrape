Job Overview: We are seeking an AVP Tech Lead to provide technical leadership for a development team focused on enhancing and evolving our advisor workstation experience. This role is responsible for designing, developing, and implementing scalable cloud-enabled web applications and services that support new business capabilities and digital experiences. The position partners closely with business stakeholders, architects, quality assurance, release management, and governance teams to deliver solutions aligned with enterprise standards and strategic objectives. Responsibilities: Provide technical leadership and hands-on development expertise for AWS-based applications and services. Guide development teams in implementing cloud-native architectures, web applications, and service-oriented solutions. Mentor and support developers in adopting new technologies, frameworks, and development practices. Analyze existing applications and systems to identify modernization and improvement opportunities. Assess technical risks and develop recommendations for issue resolution and solution delivery. Collaborate with architecture teams to create and maintain technical documentation in accordance with SDLC standards. Communicate technical concepts, designs, and delivery plans to both technical and non-technical stakeholders. Design, develop, and maintain enterprise-grade services and application components. Participate in code reviews and present solution designs to development peers and stakeholders. Troubleshoot complex production issues and provide advanced debugging support. Evaluate emerging technologies, design proposals, and process improvements to enhance scalability, performance, and reliability. Contribute to solution estimation activities, including ROM-level effort assessments. Maintain platform documentation and ensure alignment with enterprise governance requirements.

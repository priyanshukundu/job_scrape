Background Details As a Sr. Associate, Actuarial Product Specifications, you will help bring life insurance products, riders and enhancements from design through implementation. You’ll serve as an important connection between product and pricing actuaries and business partners, translating actuarial, regulatory and business requirements into clear, accurate product specifications. You’ll apply your knowledge of actuarial concepts and applicable regulations, including IRC Section 7702, to understand their impact on product features, calculations and specifications. Through collaboration with actuarial, legal, compliance, operations, administration and technology, you’ll help ensure product intent and requirements are consistently understood and implemented. If you enjoy combining analytical thinking, technical detail and cross-functional collaboration to bring products to life, please read on! What you'll be doing You will serve as a bridge between the team and pricing/product actuaries, providing technical and actuarial support during product development and implementation initiatives. You will develop and maintain product specifications using established standards and guidelines to support new products and changes to existing products. You will review product specifications for completeness, consistency, accuracy, and alignment with actuarial intent and identify issues requiring further review or resolution. You will advise stakeholders on product design decisions, calculation methodologies, and downstream impacts. You will provide actuarial input to support the development of product specifications, contract language, and related product documentation. You will develop an understanding of applicable regulatory requirements, including IRC Section 7702, and interpret and apply those requirements to life insurance product features and specifications. You will apply established guidelines to address challenging but non-complex specification issues and escalate matters requiring additional expertise. You will collaborate with actuarial, legal, compliance, operations, administration, and technology partners to support consistent interpretation of product requirements. You will support the translation of actuarial and business requirements into clear product specifications for implementation by internal and external stakeholders. You will assist with product development and implementation activities for life insurance products, riders, and product enhancements. You will explain product specifications, requirements, and established practices to stakeholders to support understanding and consistent execution. What we’re looking for 4 Year/Bachelor's degree or equivalent work experience (4 years of experience in lieu of Bachelor's) 1+ Years actuarial experience or has passed three (3) actuarial exams Excellent organizational skills with the ability to prioritize workload and multitask while maintaining strict attention to detail Remains current in procession and industry trends, including regulations Ability to evaluate information and data trends, solve challenging but non-complex problems, apply sound judgment to draw conclusions and act effectively in situations with limited standardization Effectively and responsibly uses AI tools in daily work, validates AI-generated outputs, and identifies opportunities to improve productivity, decision making, and outcomes for themselves, colleagues, and the organization Proficiency in Microsoft Office Suite (Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook) Application Deadline Applications for this position will be accepted through November 30, 2026, subject to earlier closure due to applicant volume.

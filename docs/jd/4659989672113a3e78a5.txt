WHO WE ARE LOOKING FOR We are looking for a Senior Software Engineer to design, build, and operate foundational services for our enterprise AI platform. You will combine strong software engineering fundamentals with cloud-native architecture to deliver secure, scalable, observable, and reusable capabilities that accelerate AI development across Nike. You will thrive in ambiguous problem spaces, apply sound technical judgment, and collaborate across engineering, product, data science, and machine learning teams. WHAT YOU WILL WORK ON As a Senior Software Engineer in AI Platforms, you will design, build, and operate scalable, reliable platform capabilities that enable AI applications and services across the enterprise. You will develop reusable services, APIs, and paved paths that simplify how teams build, integrate, deploy, and operate AI solutions, with a key focus on AI knowledge and retrieval capabilities such as knowledge ingestion, representation, vector search, and knowledge graphs. You will contribute across the software lifecycle, engineering for reliability, resilience, observability, performance, security, and operational excellence. You will develop robust Python services and APIs, provision and manage AWS infrastructure through Infrastructure-as-Code and GitOps workflows, and strengthen CI/CD and engineering automation. You will design for reliability, extensibility, and secure enterprise integration, including authentication and authorization using OAuth, OIDC, Okta, or similar SSO platforms. You will also improve platform instrumentation using OpenTelemetry and tools such as Splunk, participate in architecture and code reviews, and help establish engineering standards through agile and test-driven development practices. WHO YOU WILL WORK WITH You will work closely with AI and agent application developers, software engineers, ML engineers, product managers, security teams, and cloud platform teams to identify and deliver reusable AI platform capabilities. You will also partner with teams integrating AI services into Nike business applications. This position reports to the Director of AI Data and Knowledge Foundation, AI Platforms.

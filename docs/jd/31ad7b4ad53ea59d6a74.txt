Job Summary The Equipment Finance Portfolio Management Representative is responsible for handling all customer communications primarily in the collections division in a friendly, professional, and timely manner. Partner with the vendor and sales team to establish and maintain a cohesive team atmosphere to best serve the customer. Performs other projects and duties as assigned. Essential Functions, Duties and Responsibilities Handle incoming calls, e-mails, and written correspondence. Collect delinquent payments and handling administrative calls. Coordinate interdepartmental responsibilities to ensure a timely customer response. Process billing and insurance inquiries, customer complaints, payoffs, and other tasks assigned. Maintain a working knowledge of company policies and procedures. Accurate account documentation for record keeping. Required Knowledge, Skills and Competencies Ability to proactively manage daily responsibilities for assigned tasks. Ability to interact with management daily for problem resolution. Must be able to multi-task responsibilities. Strong verbal and written skills required. Microsoft Office (Word, Excel and Outlook). Industry and Work Experience A minimum of 2 years of experience in customer service/collections. Academic High school diploma or GED required. Bachelor’s degree preferred or equivalent education and related training or experience required. Salary range is $21.37 - $25.64 - $29.92.

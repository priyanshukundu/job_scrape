The Resiliency and Delivery Management (RDM) organization supports internal Consumer & Community Banking (CCB) Operation employees by ensuring knowledge and change-management communications are accurate, consistent, and aligned to applicable standards. We partner with business stakeholders to review, quality-check, and refine messages and materials used across multiple functions, helping improve clarity, reduce rework, prevent avoidable content errors, and support content that can adapt to evolving AI-enabled experiences. As a Business Analysis Associate in the Content Delivery and Change Management team, you will serve as a primary content reviewer and standards-focused quality gate for high-visibility communications and knowledge management procedures. You will leverage strong editorial judgment and use a structured review approach, you will identify gaps, inconsistencies, and non-compliant or unclear language; ensure materials meet internal standards; and provide clear, actionable feedback to content writers. This role requires influence, attention to detail, and the ability to drive continuous improvement in content governance and review processes. Job responsibilities Own end-to-end content review for internal communications and procedures, focusing on accuracy, completeness, tone, consistency, and adherence to defined standards Detect and correct content errors (e.g., inconsistent terminology, misaligned messaging, outdated references, unclear instructions, formatting/structure issues, and conflicting statements) before publication Establish and apply review checklists, templates, style/standards guides, and quality controls to improve consistency and reduce defects across communications deliverables Partner with business stakeholders and CCB Operations teams to translate standards into clear content requirements, providing guidance and recommended edits Coordinate review cycles and approvals, managing versions, incorporating feedback efficiently, and ensuring final content is publication-read Identify recurring content defects and drive process improvements (e.g., standard work, reusable language libraries, pre-brief review workflows) Mentor junior team members on editorial standards, review techniques, and quality expectations Maintain confidentiality and demonstrate a strong commitment to high-quality execution and organizational principles Apply evolving content standards designed to improve how content can be consumed and used by AI-enabled tools, partnering with internal machine learning subject-matter experts to learn and incorporate new requirements Use approved internal AI tools to improve review efficiency and consistency Required qualifications, capabilities, and skills Bachelor’s degree or equivalent work experience. 5+ years of related experience in content review, editorial/communications quality assurance, business management communications, or similar roles that incorporated evaluation of written content against established standards. Advanced written and verbal communication skills with strong editing and proofreading capability; able to provide clear, specific feedback. Demonstrated ability to evaluate content against standards and requirements, identify risks/issues, and recommend precise corrections. Strong judgment and ability to synthesize complex information into consistent, accurate messaging, identify trends across review findings, and translate those trends into practical recommendations. Proven stakeholder management skills; able to influence without authority and drive alignment on content standards Highly organized, detail-oriented, and able to manage multiple concurrent review queues under tight deadlines; experience mentoring or leading junior team members Foundational understanding of generative AI, large language models (LLMs), and prompt engineering, with experience or interest in using AI-enabled tools to support writing, editing, content analysis, and quality-review activities Standards-based content QA: Structured reviews, defect detection, consistency checks, and final “publication-ready” Strong editorial rigor: Clarity, brevity, tone, terminology control, logical flow, and the ability to manage review cycles, version control, and approval Continuous improvement mindset: Building templates, checklists, reusable language, and streamlined review processes.

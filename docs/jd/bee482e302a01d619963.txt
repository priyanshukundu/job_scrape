About this role Dow has an opening for an Associate TS&D Scientist located in Auburn, MI or Collegeville, PA . This role provides dedicated technical service and development support for Personal Care customers and distributors across North America. The position leads the development of new applications, provides technical guidance on the use, handling, and management of Dow products, and translates customer formulation needs into practical, application-ready solutions. In this role, you will apply in-depth conceptual and working knowledge of your discipline or functional area and understand how its best practices connect with adjacent disciplines. You may lead small projects with manageable risks and resource requirements, serve as a resource for less experienced colleagues, and communicate complex or sensitive information in a way that builds alignment and enables decisions. You will analyze and resolve complex problems, integrate information from multiple sources, and use sound judgment to evaluate established and emerging solutions. Success in this role requires close collaboration with field TS&D, R&D, Sales, customers, and distributors to improve responsiveness, accelerate opportunity advancement and new-product adoption, and strengthen Dow’s competitive position. Responsibilities Provides direct technical support to Personal Care customers and distributors across North America, including formulation guidance, troubleshooting, and application development Builds and applies fast-formulation capabilities that enable field TS&D and Sales to respond quickly with practical, application-ready solutions Supports customer engagement and technical training, including sample evaluation, launch follow-up, and distributor-led initiatives Coordinates regulatory and product stewardship support and helps convert technical activity into well-defined commercial opportunities Effectively implements new technologies, products, or applications to address technology needs for the business and comprehends test method scope and limitations and prepares internal test methods, designs overall testing protocol to deliver project objectives Convinces others about differentiated technical solutions. Articulates synergies between technologies to maximize value effectively and understands how business value is created for Dow and the customer Consistently applies the scientific method to solve technical problems Understands the connections of the scientific method to Six Sigma, ICO, DFSS, NPI, and other methodologies Develops new approaches and solutions to problems by considering issues from different points of view Manages secrecy agreements and joint development agreements to protect Dow technology for specific projects Provides constructive input to project participants Leads technical sub teams and defines scope of the larger project space Mentors junior level scientists and technologists Delivers presentations or reports that distill complex ideas into clear results and proposals; communicates appropriately to influence internal and external audiences Communicates and appropriately documents work in the form of internal reports/ROC's/presentations, technology manuals, knowledge management resources, external reports, and patents when appropriate Serves as principal author on papers and presentations and presents at industry conferences Qualifications A minimum of a bachelor’s degree or relevant military experience at or above a U.S. E5 ranking or Canadian Petty Officer 2nd Class or Sergeant. PhD in Chemistry or Chemical Engineering strongly preferred A minimum of 3 years of relevant experience in research and project management A minimum requirement for this U.S. based position is the ability to work legally in the United States. No visa sponsorship/support is available for this position, including for any type of U.S. permanent residency (green card) process. Skills Researching : Ability to define, design, and conduct scientific research using advanced principles, experimental design, and disciplined application of the scientific method to solve technically complex problems and generate new knowledge. Chemistry : Strong foundational and applied understanding of chemical principles relevant to research and development, enabling hypothesis formulation, experimental execution, and interpretation of results within Dow’s R&D environment. Innovation : Capability to propose and develop novel, proprietary technologies and technical solutions by applying creative problem-solving and scientific insight aligned to business and R&D objectives. Data Analysis : Skill in analyzing, interpreting, and drawing conclusions from experimental data to validate hypotheses, guide decision-making, and ensure technical rigor in research outcomes. Communication : Ability to clearly explain complex scientific concepts, experimental results, and project alignment to diverse internal and external audiences, while providing guidance and peer coaching within multifunctional teams.

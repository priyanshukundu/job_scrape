Senior Software Engineer
General Motors
Location: Warren, Michigan, United States of America
Link: https://generalmotors.wd5.myworkdayjobs.com/Careers_GM/job/Warren-Michigan-United-States-of-America/Senior-Software-Engineer_JR-202624297

Job Description DUTIES : Source, validate, and release electronic power steering (EPS) control system software for production. Address all controller and system safety concerns promptly. Lead and support product issue investigations (Problem Resolution Tracking System - PRTS), continuous process improvements, standard methodologies, lessons learned, and quality improvement initiatives. Gather all necessary documents for software sourcing and provide technical expertise during purchasing process. Hybrid Work Policy - 3 days In-office, 2 days remote - Must be able to report to local office. REQUIREMENTS : Bachelor's degree in Engineering or related field of study and Five (5) years of experience as a Software Engineer, Software Testing and Validation Engineer, Design Release Engineer, Sub-system Engineer, Component Engineer, or related occupation. Five years of experience with: Engineering theory and principles of mechatronics. Two years of experience with: Software and mechatronic hardware requirements; Software verification and validation process; Providing component-specific direction to new engineers and supplier resident engineers; and Reading a vehicle communications bus CAN log and diagnosing flash programing and diagnostic issues.

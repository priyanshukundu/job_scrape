About the Role: Enova is currently searching for a Capital Markets Analyst that will help implement Enova’s capital markets strategy. As a key member of Enova’s Treasury team, this person will work with internal and external stakeholders to ensure that existing debt facilities and capital markets transactions run smoothly, assist in the development of new financing to support Enova’s growth, and participate in special projects. The Capital Markets Analyst will build relationships across the business, develop knowledge and analytical skills associated with corporate finance, and work in a fun and motivated culture! Responsibilities: Work with a small team to evaluate and execute new debt facilities and capital markets transactions Manage the reporting and funding processes for Enova’s consumer and small business loan securitization facilities, including day-to-day operating activities and monthly investor reporting Partner with Product, Technology, and Business Intelligence teams to implement operational and reporting requirements of debt facilities and in support of other capital markets initiatives Build relationships with existing and potential investors through introductory meetings (phone calls and conferences), roadshows, and due diligence sessions Prepare data and analyses related to due diligence requests from lenders, investors, rating agencies, and auditors Assist in daily cash management and forecasting analyses Own the field exam and audit process for Enova’s debt facilities, ensuring timely, accurate responses, keeping documentation organized and moving open items towards resolution Perform analyses and create financial models and tools that assist in forecasting and monitoring to help drive capital markets intelligence and efficiencies Assist in managing the investor relations (IR) systems that support the IR program, including monitoring industry trends and tracking details of communication with existing and prospective investors Requirements: Bachelor’s degree or equivalent experience. A successful candidate may have a degree in Business, Finance, Accounting, or Economics, but can also demonstrate an interest in finance through prior work experience 2+ years of relevant experience in a corporate finance or data analysis setting Strong problem solving and analytical skills and the ability to work well in groups Demonstrates excellent written and oral communication Independent, proactive self-starter with strong organizational skills and attention to detail, able to meet deadlines Enthusiasm for learning new concepts and skills Prior experience in capital markets, treasury, investor relations, securitization, banking, fintech, auditing or lending is a plus Proficiency in Excel and PowerPoint Familiarity with data analysis tools Compensation: The budgeted annual salary range for this position is $50,000 to $70,000.

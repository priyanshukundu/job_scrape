Location: Douglas, Isle of Man Closing Date: 10th December 2026 The opportunity: Join us as a Business Analyst and play a key role in shaping solutions that improve experiences for our customers, colleagues and business. Working across a variety of initiatives, you'll help turn ideas and business challenges into practical, value-driven outcomes. You'll lead business analysis activities from initial concept through to delivery, helping teams break down complex initiatives into manageable releases that deliver benefits sooner. Working closely with stakeholders across the business, you'll identify needs, assess impacts and risks, and translate requirements into clear user stories and practical solutions. We work primarily in an agile environment, adapting our approach to suit the needs of each initiative. You'll collaborate with colleagues across the business, technology teams and senior stakeholders to help deliver meaningful change while ensuring solutions align with business objectives and customer outcomes. This is an excellent opportunity for an experienced Business Analyst who enjoys solving problems, building strong relationships and influencing positive change across an organisation. What you'll do: Facilitate informed decision-making by providing insight, analysis and recommendations to stakeholders. Gather, document and manage functional and non-functional requirements, ensuring they are clear and actionable. Write high-quality user stories and acceptance criteria to support successful delivery. Keep initiatives aligned with business goals and focused on measurable outcomes. Make product and solution recommendations within agreed scope, considering customer needs, business processes and existing products. Break large initiatives into smaller releases that deliver value quickly while maintaining focus on the overall business outcome. Build strong relationships with stakeholders and communicate effectively at all levels of the organisation. Support solution delivery through test planning, user acceptance testing, implementation and training activities where required. Contribute to the development and maintenance of product roadmaps, helping to assess priorities, value and delivery timelines. Review ongoing initiatives to ensure they continue to meet business needs, recommending changes where appropriate. What you'll bring: Proven business analysis experience, with the ability to gather, analyse and translate complex information into practical solutions. Experience delivering projects using a variety of methodologies and adapting your approach to suit changing business needs. Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to identify opportunities for improvement and drive outcomes. Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills, with the confidence to challenge constructively and influence at all levels. An understanding of product management and roadmaps, including balancing regulatory requirements, strategic growth initiatives and operational priorities. The ability to translate business needs into clear plans and requirements that delivery teams can successfully implement. Experience working within insurance or another regulated industry would be beneficial. A bachelor's degree, professional business analysis certification, or equivalent practical experience would be advantageous.

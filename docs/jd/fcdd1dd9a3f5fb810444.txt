Start your Voyage – Apply Now Job Purpose/Summary This role is a part of our Voya Investment Management Entry Level Analyst Program. This program, which will begin in July 2027, is designed to develop the careers of entry level employees and provide you with the unique opportunity to begin your investment career while gaining hands-on experience and on-the-job development and training. We're looking for an entry-level Risk Analyst to join our investment risk team supporting fixed income and alternatives investment platform. This role is ideal for a recent graduate with prior internship experience in financial services who wants to build a career in credit analysis, portfolio risk, or investment research. You'll work closely with senior risk professionals to measure and monitor credit and market risk, support model validation efforts, and help maintain the risk frameworks that guide investment decisions across corporate credit, structured credit, and broader fixed income portfolios. Key Responsibilities 1. Produce a suite of risk monitoring reports on a periodic basis and discuss findings with senior members of the risk team. 2. Help maintain and update credit risk models, scorecards, and internal ratings under the guidance of senior risk managers. 3. Conduct stress testing and scenario analysis and interpret results in terms of potential portfolio actions. 4. Improve efficiency of risk reporting processes by automating reports and eliminating redundancies. 5. Stay current on macroeconomic trends, sector developments, and regulatory changes affecting financial markets. Qualifications Applicants should be graduating with an undergraduate degree in December 2026 or spring/summer of 2027. Bachelor's degree in Finance, Economics, Accounting, Mathematics, Statistics, or a related field (graduating or recently graduated). Internships in financial services — credit risk, fixed income research, banking, asset management, or a related area. CFA or FRM designation or a progress towards similar certification preferred. Excellent written and verbal communication skills — comfortable summarizing analysis for both technical and non-technical audiences. High attention to detail and comfort working with large datasets. Strong analytical and problem-solving skills. Strong Excel skills; familiarity with SQL, Python, or R is a plus but not required.

IMC is looking for a talented and analytically driven Trading Analyst in New York to join our systematic equities business. Our Trading team is responsible for overseeing the real-time trading, performance, and risk of our systematic strategies. Working closely with quantitative researchers, senior leaders, execution engineers, and external partners, the team makes time-sensitive decisions to ensure strategies operate effectively, respond to changing market conditions, and identify opportunities to improve trading performance. Your Core Responsibilities: Real-Time Trading Oversight: Monitor live systematic trading strategies, identify anomalies, and take timely action to ensure operational stability and continuity. Risk Management: Monitor portfolio exposures, trading activity, and risk limits, escalating and responding to potential risks. Performance Analysis: Analyze trading performance, P&L, execution quality, and market behavior to identify opportunities for improvement. Research Collaboration: Work closely with quantitative researchers to investigate trading outcomes, evaluate strategy behavior, and support ongoing optimization. Technology & Automation: Partner with engineers to improve trading systems, monitoring tools, and automated operational workflows. Business Expansion: Support the deployment of new strategies and expansion into new markets, venues, and asset classes. Your Skills and Experience: 2+ years of experience in a trading analyst, trade support, or trading operations role at a bank or trading firm. Strong quantitative and analytical skills, with a degree in Mathematics, Computer Science, Physics, Engineering, or a related field. Genuine interest in financial markets, systematic trading, and quantitative research. Sound judgment and the ability to make effective decisions under pressure. Intellectual curiosity, attention to detail, and a proactive approach to problem-solving. Strong communication skills and the ability to collaborate across trading, research, and technology teams. Experience with Python, SQL, or other programming languages is advantageous. We value analytical ability, initiative, and a willingness to learn.

Toast
Location: Remote, US 
Link: https://careers.toasttab.com/jobs?gh_jid=8359240

Toast creates technology to help restaurants and local businesses succeed in a digital world, helping business owners operate, increase sales, engage customers, and keep employees happy.' As a Data Engineer on the Enterprise Data team, you’ll build and scale the data platforms and pipelines that power insights across the entire company. You’ll work closely with Analytics, Engineering, and Product teams to ensure high-quality, reliable data is accessible and actionable. This is a hands-on engineering role focused on delivering production-grade, scalable, and reliable data systems that support analytics, reporting, and operational decision-making across the company. A day in the life (Responsibilities) Build and maintain scalable, production-ready data pipelines end to end using modern data technologies. Ensure data quality, observability, lineage, and governance are built into every pipeline. Implement best practices for cost-efficient, performant cloud-based data infrastructure. Contribute to data platform tooling and frameworks that support self-service analytics and reusability across teams. Participate in code reviews, design discussions, and technical architecture planning to continually raise engineering standards. Build and support semantic and metrics layer foundations (e.g., dbt metrics, Snowflake semantic layer) for AI applications that enable consistent, reusable definitions of KPIs across reporting and analytics. Collaborate with cross-functional stakeholders to design and support the evolution of the enterprise data domain model, including Customer 360. What you'll need to thrive (Requirements) 2+ years of experience in data engineering, software engineering, or a related role building and maintaining data systems in production. Strong SQL skills and experience with a programming language such as Python, Scala, or Java. Familiarity with modern data warehouse/lakehouse technologies (e.g., Snowflake, BigQuery, Redshift, Delta Lake). Experience building and optimizing ETL/ELT workflows using tools such as Airflow, dbt, or similar orchestration frameworks. Comfortable understanding of data modeling, data warehousing, and pipeline design. Ability to write clean, maintainable, and well-tested code for data transformations and automation. Demonstrated ability to communicate technical tradeoffs and data concepts clearly to non-technical stakeholders, including operations and business leaders. Proven ability to work collaboratively with analytics, operations, and product teams. Strong problem-solving skills, attention to detail, and a drive to build scalable, reliable systems. What will help you stand out (Non-essential Skills/Nice to Haves) Hands-on experience with cloud-based data platforms (AWS, GCP, or Azure). Experience with CI/CD, infrastructure as code, or containerization (e.g., Docker, Terraform). Comfort using AI-assisted development tools (e.g., GitHub Copilot, Claude, Snowflake Cortex AI) to accelerate pipeline development, testing, and documentation and curiosity about how GenAI can enhance data product quality. Exposure to data governance, metadata management, and observability tools. Knowledge of data security, privacy, and compliance in cloud environments.

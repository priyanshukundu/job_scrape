Data Scientist
Vanguard
Location: Malvern, PA; Charlotte, NC; Dallas/Ft. Worth, TX; Philadelphia, PA; Scottsdale, AZ
Link: https://vanguard.wd5.myworkdayjobs.com/vanguard_external/job/Malvern-PA/Data-Scientist_183869

Vanguard is committed to continuous Client Experience improvement, and the Enterprise Intelligence group develops and supports a suite of internal CX analytical tools. These Data Science tools – spanning models, software, and interfaces – are used by internal Vanguard expert analysts to better understand CX outcomes. This position is for a full stack Data Scientist supporting the continued development of our internal suite of CX analytical tools. The role will be responsible for rapidly understanding the current state of those methods / models / systems, then working with existing Data Science and Machine Learning stakeholders to support and update that suite. Applicants should have a baseline understanding of the Product Development Lifecycle (PDLC), deployment of code through Python packages, integration of AI into code (both in development and application), and Natural Language Processing (NLP) fundamentals for text processing & analysis. Your work will connect with subject matter experts across Data Science, Analytics, Client Experience, and Business – you’ll need to be able to cater your technical communication across audiences. You will follow the Vanguard Model Governance process and adhere to AI usage policies, protecting client information and ensuring full compliance with safety & security best practices. You’ll be working with a highly-skilled team of Data Scientists and Machine Learning Engineers, on a suite of software that has moved beyond its initial development stages. This will demand rapid familiarization, the ability to quickly understand existing codebase, and finding opportunities for improvement. You will need to work with your management and stakeholders to prioritize tasking, autonomously deliver on that tasking, and proactively come back with proposed next steps. Core Responsibilities Deliver technology: Build and support existing software, models, packages, and other analytics tools used for data-driven business decision making. Implement AI prompt engineering, NLP text processing, and unstructured data modeling in systems & packages. Deploy software to internal consumers, adhere to the PDLC process, create and use monitoring & tracking features. Use technology responsibly: Follow the Vanguard Model Governance methodology & process, adhering to security policies around AI usage, software design, and model validation. Identify and diagnose data & code issues, always through continuous code review, and when necessary by performing root-cause analysis and post-mortems. Proactively surface found issues or risks. Manage stakeholders: Engage with internal stakeholders (DS, MLE, Business...) to understand and probe business processes in order to improve CX models, packages, and tools. Establish Definitions-of-Done with stakeholders, ensure that DoD is tracked and met through the PDLC process. Communicate complex findings and implications to leaders & teammates. Be a team player: Mentor and develop junior data scientists and analysts. Actively share information across workstreams, promote cross-pollination of findings & hurdles. Participate in special projects and perform other duties as assigned. Qualifications Minimum of five years related work experience in Data Science development & modeling roles. Undergraduate degree in Computer Science or related Data Science field of study, or equivalent combination of training and experience. Graduate degree preferred. Experience with the Product Development Lifecycle (PDLC), especially deploying Data Science tools to expert analyst users. Proven experience using AI and NLP methods & tools to extract information from unstructured data at scale. Strong background using quantified measures to evaluate text, developing models around those measures, and improving them over time. Advanced programming experience with Python, including deploying solutions as Packages that include telemetry feedback, safeguards, secure credentialing, and other User Access Controls (UAC). Ability to communicate technically complex concepts across varying audiences. Know when to dive deep, and when to keep things high-level. Some experience with data wrangling required – ability to extract, transform and load (ETL) large-scale data for statistical modeling. Preference for candidates who have worked with Client Experience & client outcomes, who have familiarity with terminology surrounding client feedback & stories.

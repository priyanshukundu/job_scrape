Databricks Data Engineer, Lead The Opportunity: As a lead Databricks Data Engineer, you will be hands-on designing, building, and maintaining robust data pipelines within Databricks and contributing independently while also providing technical leadership and mentorship within the team. Your key responsibilities will include creating and optimizing end-to-end data flows, handling extract, transform, load (ETL) processes, designing data models, ensuring data security and compliance, collaborating with cross-functional teams, and continuously improving performance and scalability. Your experience in both Databricks and AWS environments and services will position you as a technical team leader. Your expertise will bridge the gap between raw data and actionable insights, enabling efficient data processing and analytics across the business. Here, you’ll mentor a multi-disciplinary team of analysts, data engineers, developers, and data consumers in a fast-paced, agile environment. You’ll use your expertise in analytical exploration and data examination while you oversee the assessment, design, building, and maintenance of scalable platforms for your clients. Work with us to use data for good. Join us. The world can’t wait.

About the Job We are looking for a Software Engineer II to join our Publics Investment Technology team within Institutional Investments at Northwestern Mutual. Here you will play a key role in building and supporting technology (both Custom Developed and Vended) for our Publics Investment Team. In this position, you will be working with a group of engineers within the NM’s Publics Investment Technology team building high-quality applications, sourcing and integrating market data, and providing analytic capabilities to our Publics Investment Team that manages over $130B of investments. To support our growing team, we are seeking a strong developer, who is passionate about developing solutions that meet business needs, is a quick learner, enjoys daily interaction with business and technology teams and is comfortable with both vended applications and custom development. What You’ll Do Software Development: Contribute to the design, development, and maintenance of software applications that support Public Investments, ensuring scalability and performance. Data Integration: Source internal and external data sources in support of the end to end investment life cycle Collaboration: Collaborate with Institutional Investment Technology, Product and Business partners to understand requirements and deliver solutions that meet business needs. Quality Assurance: Implement testing strategies to ensure system reliability and data accuracy. Documentation: Create and maintain technical documentation for software solutions and processes. Continuous Improvement: Research and apply new technologies to continually enhance Public Investment systems. Continuous Integration & Delivery: Use and contribute to CI/CD pipelines to build, test, and deploy software changes safely and reliably. Code Quality : Participates in code reviews and provides feedback to other developers to improve and maintain code quality of team’s work. Support : Assists in troubleshooting production issues using code debugging and monitoring tools Mentorship: Provide support and mentorship to junior developers, fostering an environment of growth and learning. What You’ll Bring to the Role Education: Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or related field. Experience: 2-4 years of professional experience in software development, preferably in the investments or financial services industry. Experience with SDLC and agile methodology or scrum. Technical Skills: Proficiency working with modern languages and frameworks like Java, Spring/Spring Boot, TypeScript/JavaScript, ReactJS, API Development and SQL Experience with writing automated unit tests Problem-Solving: Strong analytical skills with the ability to translate complex business requirements into technical solutions. Communication: Excellent communication skills, with the ability to convey technical concepts to non-technical stakeholders. Team Player: Ability to work effectively in a collaborative environment. Additional Qualifications We’d Love: Experience with AWS, Snowflake and PowerBI is preferred Experience with Batch Scheduling, ELK, Control-M, Gitlab CI and Terraform is nice to have Industry Knowledge: Basic understanding of investment processes and terminology or experience with investment software or platforms is a plus. Familiarity with DevOps practices.

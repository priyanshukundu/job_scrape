About this role: Wells Fargo is seeking an Analyst to join our Fixed Income Strategy team to expand strategic support for corporate and public entity clients. The Strategy team provides custom solutions for institutional fixed income investors utilizing portfolio analytics, balance sheet and cash management consultation, investment strategies, and market intelligence. The Analyst will primarily focus on supporting senior strategists in producing a variety of analytical reports and performing data analysis and aggregation. The role includes two broad educational tracks that focus on learning all cash fixed income products while also developing a strong foundational knowledge of our clients’ balance sheets and investing strategies. The position requires the ability to work quickly and accurately with large amounts of data, and to present the results in a clear and concise format. Responsibilities also include participating in a collaborative process of generating ideas, developing tools, sharing market intelligence, and reviewing peer work product. This is a client-facing role that requires partnership with internal team members across multiple departments to provide best in class service.

Help teams make high-stakes migration decisions with clear, reliable analytics. In this role, you will turn complex portfolio and project data into insights that improve planning, execution, and risk visibility. You will partner with cross-functional teams to build practical reporting and dashboard solutions that support specialists and leaders. If you enjoy disciplined data handling and thoughtful storytelling, you’ll have opportunities to make a measurable impact. As a Migration Data Analytics Associate in the Client Migration Data & Analytics team, you translate business questions into clear analysis, reporting, and decision-ready materials that support client migration execution. You help identify trends, exceptions, risks, and data quality concerns, and you deliver recurring and ad hoc insights used by migration teams and leaders. You contribute to scalable analytics solutions while maintaining strong controls, documentation, and responsible use of approved tools.

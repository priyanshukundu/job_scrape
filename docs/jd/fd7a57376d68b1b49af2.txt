Location: 4910 Tiedeman Road, Brooklyn Ohio Senior BRC Risk Advisor Job Summary This position is within the Key Technology Services (KTS) organization and supports the IT Asset Risk and Control Assessment (IT-ARCA) program. The primary responsibility of this role is to partner with technology leaders, asset owners, risk partners, and other stakeholders to identify, assess, monitor, and communicate technology risks across the organization. This role contributes to the effective execution of the IT-ARCA program through risk assessments, data collection and analysis, quality reviews, control testing activities, governance oversight, and cross-functional collaboration. Leveraging risk management principles, knowledge of technology environments, and analytical expertise, the individual helps promote the consistent application of controls, policies, and risk management standards while supporting informed risk-based decision making. This role provides an opportunity for individuals to deepen their understanding of technology risk management, strengthen stakeholder relationships, and influence the identification and mitigation of risk across a diverse technology landscape. Essential Functions Monitor and respond to inquiries received through the team's shared mailbox and provide regular status updates to technology partners regarding assigned IT-ARCA activities. Facilitate and lead IT Survey QC reviews with asset owners and stakeholders to evaluate survey responses and supporting documentation, recording QC results in GRC, and updating IT Survey QC status within Microsoft Teams task management tools. Perform risk assessments of moderate to high complexity, including: Reviewing supporting documentation and evidence. Executing control testing procedures. Identifying and documenting control exceptions. Tracking assessment progress and completion. Perform independent QC reviews of completed risk assessments and provide constructive feedback to assessment owners. Identify deficiencies, inconsistencies, and improvement opportunities within control testing execution. Leverage working knowledge of technology platforms, infrastructure components, application architectures, configuration items (CIs), and associated operational processes to effectively challenge survey responses, validate supporting evidence, assess control applicability, and identify potential gaps, inaccuracies, or emerging risks during IT Survey QC reviews and Risk Assessments. Ability to analyze assessment and QC data to identify trends, and to improve reporting efficiency using approved tools Working knowledge of cloud, hosted, and hybrid infrastructure and associated control considerations Working knowledge of third-party technology risk considerations and how they affect control applicability Working knowledge of risk considerations associated with AI and emerging technology adoption Ability to interpret regulatory and policy changes affecting assessment scope Maintain and support team SharePoint resources, documentation repositories, and knowledge management content. Develop, update, and deliver training for team members and technology partners. Support and lead process enhancement efforts and contribute to operational efficiencies across the IT-ARCA program. Mentor Risk Advisors while supporting succession planning and team development.

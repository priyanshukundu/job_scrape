Software Developer The Opportunity: As a software sustainment engineer, you play a pivotal role in an application’s functional life. From troubleshooting to fixing bugs, you use your operations and maintenance skills to ensure that things run smoothly. We’re looking for a software sustainment engineer to apply technical knowledge to our clients’ challenges and their legacy enterprise systems. As a software sustainment engineer at Booz Allen, you’ll have an operational impact from day one. With a team of professionals, you’ll maintain applications and keep systems working. You and your team will use programming and scripting languages as you continuously enhance and maintain applications, troubleshoot operational issues, and support upgrades that help systems meet security and business requirements. Most importantly, you’ll advise clients on opportunities to improve and modernize existing systems. You'll collaborate with developers, engineers, and stakeholders to support web applications, databases, APIs, and enterprise software solutions in an Agile environment. Work with us to help secure crucial systems while watching them grow and modernize. Join us. The world can’t wait. You Have: 1+ years of experience with software development, software maintenance, application support, internships, academic projects, or technical work Experience developing, testing, debugging, or maintaining software applications using object-oriented programming principles Experience with web applications, APIs, databases, or user interfaces Experience using programming languages such as Java, Python, JavaScript, C++, C#, SQL, or technologies Knowledge of source control tools, including Git Ability to work in a collaborative Agile development environment Ability to obtain a Secret clearance Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Information Systems, or a technology field Nice If You Have: Experience with Java, Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, C++, HTML, CSS, PHP, or SQL technologies Experience with Agile methodologies, Scrum, Jira, or software development lifecycle processes Experience developing full-stack, web-based, database-driven, or cloud-enabled applications Experience integrating third-party APIs or external data sources Knowledge of data structures, algorithms, object-oriented programming, and software design concepts Knowledge of AWS, Microsoft Azure, or Google Cloud Platform Ability to support requirements gathering, software testing, and technical documentation efforts Secret clearance Master's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Data Science, or a related field Clearance: Applicants selected will be subject to a security investigation and may need to meet eligibility requirements for access to classified information.

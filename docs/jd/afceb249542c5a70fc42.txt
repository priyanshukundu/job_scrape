American Express Global Commercial Services (GCS) is a leading provider of payment solutions for corporations and businesses of all sizes globally. The US Small and Middle Enterprise (SME) segment is a significant growth opportunity and strategic priority, supported by continued innovation across products, capabilities, and B2B payment solutions. The US SME Managed Customer Marketing team deepens relationships with prospects and existing customers by bringing the powerful backing, financial support, and differentiated value of American Express to life through compelling marketing. We are seeking a strategic, creative, and execution-oriented leader to shape the field collateral strategy across US SME. This highly visible role sits at the intersection of content, sales, and product innovation—turning complex solutions and evolving business priorities into clear, persuasive marketing that helps field teams engage customers and drive growth. As Senior Manager , you will define the collateral roadmap, lead the development and optimization of customer-facing content, and shape how new products and B2B solutions are introduced to the field. You will also scale adoption, strengthen performance measurement, and build the operating processes that enable consistent, compliant execution. You will report to the Director of Sales Content Strategy and Marketing, lead a team of two analysts, and partner closely with leaders across Sales, Product, Legal, Compliance, and the broader marketing organization. Content Strategy & Development Own and evolve the US SME field collateral strategy and roadmap, translating business and product priorities into compelling, customer-centric marketing that supports field teams and advances business goals. Lead end-to-end content development, creative direction, optimization, governance, and execution of new and core product collateral in close partnership with Sales, Product, Legal, and Compliance. Bring strong editorial judgment and storytelling to materials that communicate the American Express value proposition clearly and persuasively. Product Innovation & Go-to-Market Serve as the field collateral thought leader for American Express product launches, enhancements, and refreshes, including B2B products and solutions. Translate new capabilities and complex product propositions into differentiated messaging and impactful field-ready assets. Adoption, Measurement & Business Impact Drive collateral adoption across priority US SME field audiences, ensuring content is accessible, relevant, and actively used in customer conversations. Evolve performance measurement beyond asset utilization to assess effectiveness and business impact, applying insights and field feedback to guide optimization and future investment. Creative Partnerships & Cross-Functional Delivery Lead external agency partners to develop high-quality, engaging, and compliant field marketing materials aligned with business objectives, brand standards, and regulatory requirements. Lead cross-functional execution across Sales, Product, Legal, Compliance, and Marketing; proactively oversee timelines, dependencies, approvals, and risks while providing executives and stakeholders clear visibility into milestones, decisions, and progress. Team Leadership & Organizational Influence Lead, coach, and develop a Senior Analyst and Marketing Operations Analyst, setting priorities and guiding execution, deployment, and ongoing enhancements. Ensure 100% compliance and asset availability to sales reps. Serve as the collateral subject matter expert across the broader GLAMM organization, sharing best practices and improving how teams develop and deliver effective field collateral. Minimum Qualifications 5-7+ years of relevant experience in marketing strategy, content development, sales enablement, product marketing, or related fields. Demonstrated hands-on experience developing compelling marketing content, shaping messaging, and translating complex products or solutions into clear, engaging customer-focused stories. Significant experience in marketing strategy, content development, and stakeholder or agency partnership management, supported by strong analytical and strategic thinking. Excellent project management skills and attention to detail, with the ability to independently advance multiple complex projects in parallel. Exceptional written, verbal, presentation, and training communication skills, with strong executive presence and the ability to engage stakeholders at all levels. A proactive, autonomous, growth-oriented mindset, creative judgment, and a collaborative approach to solving problems and working as a team. Preferred Qualifications Experience directly managing, coaching, or developing a small team. Experience supporting field sales organizations and creating B2B sales enablement content. Experience supporting product launches or go-to-market initiatives for complex products and solutions.

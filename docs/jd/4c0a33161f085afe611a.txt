Senior Associate - Commercial Solutions Pitch Strategy & Execution
JPMorgan Chase
Location: New York, NY, United States
Link: https://jpmc.fa.oraclecloud.com/hcmUI/CandidateExperience/en/sites/CX_1001/job/210811703

Chase Auto serves as a leading provider of auto financing to dealers, consumers, and manufacturers, with a portfolio of over $80 billion in assets and relationships across 75% of U.S. franchised automotive dealers. The Commercial Solutions group within Chase Auto delivers a full suite of commercial banking solutions for auto dealers, including Treasury Services, floorplan, real estate and acquisition financing, liquidity management and other banking solutions. You will work alongside Bankers, Treasury Management Officers (TMOs), and Portfolio Managers to support prospect conversations and bring the Commercial Solutions value proposition to life, gaining hands-on exposure to the commercial banking function through pitch development, meeting preparation, and follow-up. As an Associate within Chase Auto Commercial Solutions, you will partner with business stakeholders to develop compelling prospect materials and coordinate pitch support end-to-end, from intake through meeting preparation and follow-up. You will build reusable deck templates, tailor presentations to individual prospects, coordinate pitch preparation and follow-ups, and establish and manage the intake process. Through hands-on collaboration with Bankers, Treasury Sales, and Portfolio Managers, you will develop a strong understanding of how Bankers and Treasury Management Officers assess prospect needs, identify and bring together relevant banking solutions, and prepare for business development conversations – deepening your understanding of the commercial banking function while contributing to team priorities. You will report to the Head of Commercial Solutions Field Enablement, Chase Auto Commercial Solutions, and partner with the Commercial Solutions business salesforce (e.g., National Bankers, Regional Bankers, Treasury Management Officers, and Portfolio Managers) to support business priorities and help grow our business. Job responsibilities Bring the Commercial Solutions value proposition to life by developing and maintaining reusable pitch deck templates that combine compelling narratives, consistent messaging, and polished presentation materials. Develop prospect-specific pitch decks that connect Banker insights, prospect business context, relevant banking solutions, and supporting business analysis to create a tailored and compelling story. Synthesize complex business and prospect information, facilitate alignment with business partners, close information gaps, and resolve inconsistencies to deliver compelling, accurate, and cohesive presentations that are ready for use. Help Bankers prepare for effective prospect conversations by coordinating and joining pre-call meetings to align on meeting objectives and talking points, organizing preparation timelines, and incorporating feedback ahead of discussions. Keep momentum after prospect discussions by coordinating follow-ups across Bankers, TMOs, and Portfolio Managers, capturing requests and next steps, preparing follow-up materials, and tracking activities through completion. Build and manage a streamlined pitch support intake and delivery process, capturing requirements, prospect context, deadlines, and stakeholder inputs. Turn requests into clear priorities and delivery plans, organize assigned work, communicate progress and dependencies, and escalate complex issues or competing deadlines with clear options. Use Banker, TMO, and Portfolio Manager feedback to continuously improve pitch support – refining deck templates, prospect tailoring, and the intake and delivery process, and sharing effective approaches across the team. Required qualifications, capabilities and skills Bachelor’s degree or equivalent experience. Strong presentation development and business communication skills, with the ability to synthesize complex information into clear, compelling, and polished materials. Advanced PowerPoint skills and proficiency in Word and Excel, with the ability to develop polished presentations and perform business analysis to support prospect-specific materials. Strong planning and organizational skills to independently manage multiple concurrent deliverables, stakeholder inputs, and competing deadlines in a fast-paced environment. Analytical skills and sound judgment to synthesize information, identify gaps and inconsistencies, solve problems, and determine when to escalate or seek guidance. Strong business acumen and intellectual curiosity, with the ability to quickly understand prospect needs, business context, and relevant banking solutions. Strong communication and relationship-building skills to work effectively with business partners, incorporate differing perspectives and feedback, and drive work toward completion. Strong service orientation, with a proactive and responsive approach to understanding stakeholder needs, anticipating requests, and delivering high-quality support. High attention to detail and commitment to accuracy and quality, particularly when developing materials for prospect and client-facing conversations.

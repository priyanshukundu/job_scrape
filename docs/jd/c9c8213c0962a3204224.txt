At Lyft, our purpose is to serve and connect. To do this, we start with our own community by creating an open, inclusive, and diverse organization. Data Science is at the heart of Lyft’s products and decision-making. As the Director, Data Science for Lyft EU, you will work in a dynamic environment, where we embrace moving quickly to build the world’s best transportation. Data Scientists take on a variety of problems, ranging from shaping critical business decisions to building algorithms that power our business insights. We’re looking for a passionate, driven Director, Data Science with strong experience in two-sided marketplaces and production machine learning to take on some of the most interesting and impactful problems and lead some of the best scientists in the industry. This role will be overseeing the Product Science teams, which drive experimentation, metrics, and product insights across rider and driver experiences, and the ML Engineering teams, which build and operate the production models behind pricing, matching, ETAs, and marketplace efficiency. You will be working on one of the most profitable and fastest growing revenue centers in our company. We are the team that solves some of mobility’s hardest problems: balancing supply and demand in real time, setting prices that work for riders and drivers, and measuring the true impact of product changes in a two-sided marketplace. As a trusted leader in the broader Data Science org, you will define and drive the Data Science strategy, vision, and roadmap for Lyft EU, while ensuring that the team has all the necessary tools and infrastructure to perform their work. You will also be responsible for building and executing a strategy for self-service data science and analytics experiences across the organization, overseeing the Science, Product, and ML Engineering teams for Lyft Europe. In this role, you will be a key partner to Engineering, Operations, Regional leaders, and cross-functional stakeholders to ensure consistent delivery of what the organization and our partners need.

POSITION TITLE LTC Model Development Actuary POSITION LOCATION Richmond, VA or Remote YOUR ROLE The Long-Term Care (LTC) Model Development Team is primarily responsible for the development, maintenance and improvement of multi-purpose actuarial models in Moody’s AXIS. You will support LTC model improvements for Valuation, Projections, ALM, and Inforce Action model functionality. You will be involved in the quarterly model change process and model/process improvement initiatives. In addition, you will support other LTC teams such as Experience, Analytics and Inforce Management that rely upon the actuarial models for their work.

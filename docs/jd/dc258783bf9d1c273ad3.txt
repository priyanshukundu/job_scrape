About this role: Wells Fargo is seeking a Fraud & Claims Operations Associate in First Party Fraud, providing coverage for Consumer and Small Business Banking Operations. This role will support first-party fraud operations through the research and review of transactions, referrals, policy violations, and other account activity to identify potential fraudulent or suspicious activity within the contact center. Learn more about the career areas and lines of business at wellsfargojobs.com . The Associate will review fraud and claims matters using established procedures, investigative practices, and available resources. This position will gather and evaluate account and transaction information, identify potential patterns or anomalies, and collaborate with team members and leadership to support accurate and timely case outcomes. In this role, you will: Participate in researching transactions, referrals, policy violations, and merchant disputes to identify potential fraudulent activity Review first party fraud cases using established investigative practices, policies, and procedures Gather and organize financial or business information to support the identification of patterns, trends, anomalies, or potential fraud Review case activity and documentation to identify potential issues, provide guidance, and support accurate case resolution Exercise judgment within defined parameters while developing knowledge of fraud and claims processes, investigative techniques, and liability decisions Escalate higher-risk, complex, or unusual situations and provide supporting information to more experienced colleagues functional partners to exchange information and support accurate and timely case outcomes Support leadership with case follow-up, assigned projects, reporting, and opportunities to improve procedures

What you’ll do As Engineering Manager for FinCrime, you’ll directly manage a team of software engineers building fraud prevention and financial crime compliance systems at Brex. Your team will protect Brex and our customers while delivering secure, intuitive product experiences. Reporting to the Senior Engineering Manager for Risk, you’ll own your team’s delivery, technical quality, and engineers’ growth. You’ll partner closely with Product, Fraud, Compliance, and Operations to translate risk priorities and regulatory requirements into reliable systems and effective controls. This is a first-line management role focused on people leadership, technical judgment, and execution across fraud and compliance. Where you’ll work This role will be based in our San Francisco office. We are a hybrid environment that combines the energy and connections of being in the office with the benefits and flexibility of working from home. We currently require a minimum of three coordinated days in the office per week, Monday, Wednesday and Thursday. As a perk, we also have up to four weeks per year of fully remote work! Responsibilities Lead and develop engineers. Coach your direct reports, provide actionable feedback, support career growth, and recruit great talent. Drive team execution. Translate agreed priorities into clear plans, milestones, and deliverables. Manage dependencies, surface risks early, and drive projects to completion. Build fraud and compliance systems. Deliver services, controls, and customer experiences that prevent fraud and support compliance obligations while minimizing unnecessary friction. Provide technical leadership. Guide design and architecture decisions, balancing delivery speed with reliability, maintainability, and evolving requirements. Partner with domain experts. Work with Product, Fraud, Compliance, and Operations to clarify requirements, make trade-offs, and evaluate whether solutions achieve their intended outcomes. Improve operational effectiveness. Build tools and automation that reduce manual effort and make investigations and controls more effective and auditable. Own operational excellence. Hold a high bar for testing, observability, incident response, and safe changes to critical production systems. Contribute to planning. Partner with your manager on team priorities, staffing, and technical investments, bringing insights from day-to-day execution. Requirements 2+ years of engineering management experience directly managing software engineers. Experience building or leading engineering teams in fraud, financial crime, risk, or a closely related domain. Strong software engineering fundamentals and experience designing, delivering, and operating reliable production systems. A track record of delivering complex projects with cross-functional partners while setting clear expectations and managing dependencies. Experience coaching engineers, supporting career development, and building a collaborative, accountable team. Strong communication skills and the ability to translate ambiguous business requirements into practical engineering plans. Sound judgment balancing customer experience, risk reduction, operational needs, and technical quality. A hands-on leadership approach: comfortable engaging in technical details and helping engineers solve difficult problems without becoming a bottleneck.

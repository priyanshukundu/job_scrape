Department Overview W&D Affordable Equity (formerly Alliant Capital) is a leading tax credit firm focused on tax credit syndication for the development and financing of affordable multifamily rental housing. As a syndicator of Low-Income Housing Tax Credits (LIHTC), W&D Affordable Equity raises corporate equity and monitors the development, construction, lease-up, and permanent financing phases of its investments. With a dedicated team of experienced real estate, asset management, legal, and tax professionals, we provide the highest level of fully integrated real estate and investment support services. The Impact You Will Have As Associate Director, Proprietary Funds, you will play a key role in the execution and ongoing management of proprietary fund transactions, working closely with investors and internal teams to ensure seamless execution and a high-quality investor experience. You will support senior Investor Relations professionals in managing day-to-day investor relationships, coordinating transaction due diligence, fund closings, and ongoing investor requirements. As you develop deeper familiarity with the platform and its investor relationships, you will have the opportunity to assume greater responsibility for direct investor engagement and serve as the primary execution relationship contact for designated investors. Through strong transaction management, sound judgment, and effective cross-functional collaboration, you will help strengthen investor relationships, drive operational excellence, and support the continued growth of the proprietary funds platform. Primary Responsibilities Investor Relationship Management & Coverage: Support senior Investor Relations professionals in managing day-to-day relationships with proprietary fund investors, including transaction execution, due diligence, investor communications, and ongoing fund matters. As experience and capabilities develop, assume increasing responsibility for serving as the primary execution relationship contact for designated investors. Proprietary Fund Transaction Execution: Manage and coordinate day-to-day due diligence, investor engagement, and closing activities for proprietary and guaranteed fund transactions, partnering closely with Originations, Acquisitions, Credit, Legal, Accounting, Asset Management, Construction, and Investor Relations. Investor Due Diligence & Transaction Materials: Lead the preparation and coordination of investor due diligence materials, property information templates, risk assessments, and responses to investor inquiries. Review Property Acquisition Reports and other investor-facing materials to ensure accuracy, consistency, and alignment with investor expectations. Financial Analysis & Fund Structuring: Partner with Financial Modeling and Fund Management to develop customized financial analyses aligned with investor objectives, including term sheets, benefit schedules, upper-tier analyses, and related fund documentation. Investor Documentation & Negotiations: Coordinate with internal stakeholders and legal counsel on the review, negotiation, and execution of upper-tier investor documents, side letters, consent rights, transfer restrictions, and other investor-specific requirements. Fund Closing & Execution Management: Oversee investor-related closing processes, including diligence tracking, transaction timelines, data rooms, closing conditions, documentation, and post-closing deliverables, ensuring timely and coordinated execution across internal teams. Ongoing Fund Management & Investor Reporting: Support ongoing management of proprietary funds, including monitoring fund composition, investor requirements, reporting obligations, and material property-level developments. Partner with internal teams to prepare fund-level reporting packages, portfolio summaries, investor updates, and other recurring communications. Investor Consents & Portfolio Matters: Coordinate investor consent requests, amendments, waivers, transfer restrictions, and other fund- or property-level matters requiring investor communication or approval, working closely with Asset Management, Legal, Accounting, and other relevant teams. Cross-Functional Coordination & Investor Communication: Serve as a key liaison between investors and internal stakeholders to facilitate timely information flow, resolve transaction-related issues, and ensure investor communications accurately reflect fund strategy, transaction developments, and broader relationship considerations. Process Improvement & Operational Excellence: Identify and implement improvements to proprietary fund execution, investor due diligence, reporting, and relationship management processes, including the development of standardized workflows, templates, checklists, and internal controls.

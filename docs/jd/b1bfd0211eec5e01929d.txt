Securian Financial Group’s internal position title for this role is Engineering Analyst. Position Summary: Securian Financial is seeking a Software Developer to join a hybrid environment Scrum team that focuses on our shared Annuity Insurance technology platform and processes to implement Annuity and Retirement products. Technologies used by the team include Java, Spring, Spring Batch, SQL Server, and AWS. Responsibilities include but not limited to: Work to solve problems with a test and learn mindset. Provide ongoing support for business applications and respond to production needs. Provide Level 3 support off-hours through a team rotation. Perform analysis, design, development, testing, integration, and implementation activities of low to medium complexity on internal software integrations with vendor and enterprise systems. Support operational and modernization efforts with internal teams and in a global operating model. Develop and maintain Annuity and Institutional Retirement business knowledge. Develop and maintain relationships with key stakeholders. Develop knowledge of AI Software Development and Engineering as part of Securian’s Software Development Lifecycle. Qualifications: Experience with Java or other programming languages. Strong collaborator and communicator. Excellent problem-solving skills. Bachelor’s degree (preferably in Computer Science or related field). Preferred Qualifications: Experience with agile/scrum methodologies. Java batch & Spring batch experience. Experience with SQL Server, AWS, ALIP Contract Administration System. Experience with Software Engineering, Software Development Lifecycle, and AI Software Development concepts. #LI-Hybrid **This position will have a hybrid working arrangement, working in-office for a minimum of 3 days a week.** Securian Financial believes in hybrid work as an integral part of our culture.

We are looking for a Staff-level technical leader to architect and advance software automation, integration, and quality engineering across the full software development life cycle for safety-critical, connected medical devices. This is a hands-on software engineering leadership role centered on scalable test automation and continuous verification—not a traditional, script-heavy QA position. You will embed quality from requirements and design through implementation, integration, release, and maintenance. You will also apply AI tools responsibly in daily engineering work, using human oversight, traceability, privacy, security, and measurable evidence to improve—not replace—engineering judgment. As a Staff Engineer, you will set technical direction, raise the engineering bar across teams, and mentor others while remaining deeply hands-on. What you'll work on Set the automation and quality strategy. Define the vision, reference architecture, and roadmap for scalable automation and continuous verification across product lines, and drive adoption as a recognized technical authority. Architect lifecycle automation. Design and build reusable frameworks, services, simulators, and engineering tools that enable requirements-based and risk-based verification at the unit, component, integration, system, regression, and release levels. Integrate quality into delivery. Embed automated quality controls, traceability, diagnostics, and actionable quality signals into CI/CD pipelines so teams receive rapid, reliable feedback throughout the development life cycle. Engineer connected-system integration. Develop robust integration capabilities across embedded devices, clinician applications, interfaces, services, data flows, and representative hardware or simulated environments. Apply AI responsibly. Incorporate Abbott’s Responsible, Safe, Transparent, Fair, and Impactful AI principles into daily automation and quality engineering. Follow established governance and intended-use boundaries; protect sensitive data; verify generated outputs; preserve source traceability; document limitations; and maintain meaningful human oversight and accountability. Advance AI-enabled engineering. Use AI copilots, large language models, and agentic workflows to improve coverage analysis, automation development and maintenance, failure investigation, and engineering productivity where their use is appropriate, validated, reproducible, and auditable. Lead technical quality. Partner with software, systems, quality, cybersecurity, and product teams to improve architecture, prevent defects, close coverage gaps, strengthen design reviews, and make quality measurable across programs. Ensure safety, compliance, and continuous improvement. Keep automation and AI-assisted engineering aligned with applicable medical device standards, including IEC 62304, ISO 14971, ISO 13485, and 21 CFR 820. Evaluate emerging tools, measure their impact on product quality and delivery, scale proven approaches, and mentor engineers in their effective and responsible use.

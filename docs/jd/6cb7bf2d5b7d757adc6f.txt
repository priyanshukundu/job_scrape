Your Opportunity At Schwab, you are empowered to make an impact on your career. Here, innovative thought meets creative problem solving, helping us “challenge the status quo” and transform the finance industry together. As part of the Schwab Technology Services Insights team, you will partner with our internal Business stakeholders and Data Engineering team to design and develop data solutions for data science, analytics and reporting. We are a team of passionate data engineers and SMEs who bring a lot of energy, focus and fresh ideas that support our mission to provide value by seeing the world “Through Clients' Eyes”. ETL Developers and Data Analysts work with large teams, including onshore and offshore developers, using best-in-class technologies including BigQuery and Informatica. You will lead the use case analysis, design, solutioning for our enterprise data integrations that enable insights for our internal technology leadership. In this role you will have opportunities to grow in responsibility, work on exciting and challenging projects, train on new technologies and lead other developers to set the future of the Data Warehouse. Key Responsibilities Lead discovery with business and technical stakeholders to understand business capability enabled, use cases, data needs, business rules, and desired outcomes. Analyze source data and business rules; translate findings into clear business capability use cases, functional requirements, data definitions, source-to-target mappings, transformation rules, and data-quality expectations. Decompose business needs into well-defined features and stories, including acceptance criteria, dependencies, and traceability from requirements through delivery. Facilitate requirements, mapping, and solution reviews with business partners, product owners, developers, QA, and support teams; resolve gaps and confirm shared understanding. Create data warehouse models and produce ETL design patterns to support enterprise data engineering projects. Assist in the design and development of scalable enterprise data ingestion and integration workflows across batch, near-real time, and real-time patterns. Partner with engineers to evaluate solution options and clarify implementation details, ensuring that delivered data integrations meet business needs. Define data-validation and user-acceptance scenarios, coordinate stakeholder reviews, and document results, defects, and outstanding issues. Maintain requirements, mappings, decisions, and other analysis artifacts throughout the delivery lifecycle. Help product owners and delivery teams assess scope, prioritize work, identify dependencies, and manage changes to requirements. Support implementation readiness and production triage by clarifying expected business behavior, assessing data discrepancies, and coordinating follow-up. Use SQL and data-analysis techniques to investigate source data, validate mappings, reconcile results, and communicate findings. Contribute to technical artifacts or development activities when needed, while focusing primarily on business analysis and delivery alignment. Identify delivery, data, and technology risks early and work with stakeholders to define mitigation plans. Collaborate with onshore and offshore teams and shared functions to improve handoffs, support processes, and delivery practices. Promote clear communication, constructive feedback, accountability, and continuous improvement across the team.

Team & Role Overview About our Team: Franklin Templeton Investment Solutions (FTIS) is a leader in multi-asset, quantitative, and hedged solutions, leveraging a global network of investment teams to offer innovative and diversified strategies. With a focus on long-term investing, we combine deep research, active management, and data-driven insights to create solutions which seek to meet the evolving needs of investors worldwide. About the Role: The Portfolio Analyst joins a team managing $144B in assets and will work closely with Portfolio Management to help ensure client portfolios are positioned in accordance with the Portfolio Managers' investment strategies and views. These portfolios include a mix of single- and multi-asset-class strategies, investing across a range of instruments including stocks, bonds, ETFs, mutual funds, futures, and total return swaps. The Analyst will support day-to-day portfolio management activities, working closely with the investment, trading, and technology teams to ensure accurate positioning and reporting. How You Will Add Value Primary Responsibilities: The Analyst is a detail-oriented, proactive, and process-driven individual with an interest in multi-asset portfolio management and investment operations. The role entails: Support Portfolio Managers in the day-to-day management of multi-asset class portfolios, helping ensure positioning aligns with investment strategy and client guidelines. Assist with the implementation of trades, rebalancing activity, and cash management across portfolios. Monitor portfolio positioning relative to target allocations, escalating any deviations for review. Monitor and confirm daily subscriptions and redemptions across portfolios, resolving any discrepancies with trading, operations, or service providers. Conduct research and analysis to support asset allocation decisions and portfolio construction. Partner with trading, operations, and technology teams to help ensure accurate and timely execution of portfolio changes. Support the onboarding of new accounts, including setup and validation of account data across investment systems. Support ad hoc projects, analyses, and reporting requests from Portfolio Management, Client Service, and senior management. What Will Help You Be Successful in This Role Experience, Education & Certifications 5+ years of experience in portfolio management, investment operations, or a related role, ideally supporting multi-asset class strategies. Experience as a Portfolio Analyst supporting quantitative equity and multi-asset portfolios and strategies. B.S., B.A. or BBA degree in Finance, Economics, Business, or a related field. Knowledge of asset allocation concepts and portfolio construction principles in a multi-asset class framework. Familiarity with order management systems, including Aladdin and Charles River, and investment tools such as FactSet, Bloomberg, and Excel. Strong analytical mindset with a demonstrated ability to interpret and communicate investment data. Excellent written and verbal communication skills. Ability to manage multiple priorities and deadlines in a fast-paced environment. High attention to detail and strong organizational skills.

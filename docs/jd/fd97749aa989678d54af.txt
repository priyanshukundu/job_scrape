What the day will look like As a Sr. Business Analyst, your day is dynamic and collaborative, balancing critical thinking with hands-on problem solving. You’ll work closely with business partners and the technology team to build new business systems capability to support growth. Every day brings new challenges and opportunities to improve processes, encourage innovation, and deliver value. Typical Daily Activities Meet with business leaders to grasp strategic goals and determine how technology will aid growth and efficiency. Communicate product vision and strategy to internal and external collaborators. Analyze system requirements and build solutions to enhance business operations. Write user stories, refine with the product team and track through delivery. Lead project planning and execution for system improvements, ensuring timelines and budgets are met. Guide teams through collaborative problem solving and process improvement initiatives. Provide training and support to users, ensuring effective system utilization. Monitor product performance using critical metrics and objectives and key results and gather feedback for continuous optimization. Skills and experience that will lead to success Proven experience as a Business Analyst with a minimum of 3 years in the field. Proven experience in project management and system analysis.

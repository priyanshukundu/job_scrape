Skills and Competencies Excellent written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to understand and articulate business requirements and objectives to both technical and non-technical stakeholders Expertise in supervised and unsupervised machine learning algorithms and their implementations, including advanced concepts such as active learning, computer vision, and deployments in complex setups, beyond the standard approaches Expertise in Python programming, including machine learning-relevant libraries such as NumPy, Pandas, and PyTorch Experience in machine learning operations (MLOps) practices, including continuous integration and continuous deployment pipelines for machine learning models, model monitoring, and maintenance is a plus Expertise with tools in the modern machine learning stack, including Jupyter, Docker, Git, and cloud computing platforms such as Amazon Web Services or Google Cloud Platform Expertise in building data tools for extract, transform, and load processes, extracting data from SQL and NoSQL databases, and data analysis Experience with Geographic Information Systems is preferred Deep expertise in artificial intelligence, with a track record of implementing advanced AI solutions to drive strategic transformation and operational efficiency. Strong experience using AI tools to lead innovation initiatives. Demonstrated leadership in managing AI-related risks, ensuring ethical governance, and fostering a culture of responsible AI adoption across the organization Education Bachelor's degree in a Science, Technology, Engineering, or Mathematics field, or equivalent practical experience Responsibilities Build practical, robust, and scalable machine learning and computer vision solutions as part of a high-performing team, driving core product innovation and delivering measurable value to clients. Collect, clean, and preprocess data including imagery and geospatial data for computer vision applications to maximize data value, and conduct exploratory analysis to understand distributions, patterns, and data quality Create visual representations of complex findings to support both technical and non-technical audiences, and identify new and existing data sources to optimize and validate models Train and refine machine learning and computer vision models based on business objectives, improving performance by identifying and correcting failure modes Contribute to the design, implementation, and automation of large-scale model training, integration, and evaluation pipelines including computer vision workflows while ensuring responsible and ethical use of data Support a robust and secure computing environment for machine learning applications in coordination with engineering and information technology teams Collaborate with cross-functional teams including machine learning, software engineering, product development, and sales to communicate technical findings and contribute to the knowledge base Participate in project planning and prioritization discussions, contribute to quarterly planning by proposing projects, and document findings in a clear and accessible manner for all audiences Other duties and responsibilities as assigned About the Team Our Machine Learning Technology team sits within Moody's Insurance business and is responsible for the core technology behind our award-winning property intelligence solutions. We leverage machine learning, geospatial imagery, and computer vision to measure and monitor the built environment, delivering critical insights that help insurance professionals better assess and manage risk. By joining our team, you will be part of exciting work at the intersection of insurance and property intelligence, helping to better understand how our homes and workplaces can withstand the changing climate and economic risks of the 21st century.

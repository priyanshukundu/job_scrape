Back to search results Position Summary We are seeking a OpCon Workload Automation Administrator - software engineer III to support the design, development, testing, and deployment of technology solutions for Deloitte’s Government and Public Services practice. In this role, you will work with project teams to build and maintain applications, support delivery milestones, and contribute to client-focused outcomes. Serving federal, state, and local government clients, as well as public higher education institutions, the team helps organizations anticipate disruption, rethink what is possible, and advance their mission.

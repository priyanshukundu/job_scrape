Neuberger is an employee-owned global investment management firm with $613 billion in assets under management. The private equity group of the firm, Neuberger Private Markets, is a leading and growing investor in private markets, managing over $170 billion of client commitments since inception. The Private Investment Portfolios and Co-Investment (“PIPCO”) platform within Neuberger Private Markets oversees all commingled funds and custom portfolios consisting of a combination of primaries, co-investments, and/or secondaries, as well as primary-only and co-investment-only programs. The diverse suite of products includes a variety of strategies, ranging from highly diversified portfolios to those focused on a particular asset class (i.e., buyout, venture, and/or infrastructure), certain geographies, emerging managers, impact investments, or other customizations as requested by clients. Our New York and London offices are looking for an Analyst/Associate dedicated to the PIPCO team to reporting to the SVP of Product Management. The individual will coordinate private equity co-investment transactions end to end, partnering closely with the investment, sales, legal, finance and operations teams to move each deal from approval through funding and ensuring closings are completed accurately and on time. Responsibilities: · Manage work streams with internal and external stakeholders throughout the co-investment lifecycle, from investment committee approval through documentation, funding and post-close coordination. Set closing timelines, manage processing and issue tracking from start to finish and confirm deliverables are complete before funding. Coordinate with Operations to keep required items moving through internal systems, consistent with internal procedures and controls. Partner with General Partners, the Investment Team and internal/external Legal Counsel to confirm transaction structure, key documentation requirements, closing mechanics and allocation considerations. Compile and deliver complete funding packages, including wire instructions, funds flow details, closing notices, account-level funding amounts and required approvals. Coordinate with Finance and Treasury on funding sources and mechanics, such as credit facility draws and account-level cash needs, to ensure funds are delivered accurately and on time for each closing. Complete post-closing deliverables and organize deal documentation to ensure accurate, accessible records for relevant parties, including clients, custodians, administrators, auditors and other downstream partners. Work with client coverage teams to promptly address deal-specific inquiries and deliver coordinated service throughout the closing process. Independently manage relationships with cross-functional stakeholders, including investment teams, Operations, Technology and third-party vendors. Collaborate with senior team members and Technology to maintain the deal monitoring database and reporting tools. Evaluate deal terms across co-investment transactions to determine opportunities for standardization. · Communicate deal schedules, upcoming activity and deal-specific nuances to applicable internal and external parties to support execution Qualifications: · Bachelor’s degree in Finance, Accounting, or Economics with outstanding academic credentials · 3-5 years of relevant professional experience (e.g., private equity, management or strategy consulting, financial services operations) · Solid working knowledge of alternative investment strategies (e.g., Private Equity, Private Credit, Venture Capital, Distressed Debt) and familiarity with the private markets deal/fund lifecycle · Excellent written and verbal communication skills, including experience presenting to and advising senior stakeholders · Comfortable operating in a dynamic, fast-paced environment with shifting priorities · Direct experience applying Generative AI tools to business processes strongly preferred Applicants must be authorized and have the right to work in the country where the role is located without the need for current or future sponsorship.

The Pricing and Derived Data team within UP is seeking an outstanding Senior Software Engineer to join us in building a new data platform that provides intra-day and end-of-day prices across the firm. The team is responsible for pricing millions of instruments across many asset classes and exchanges in near-real-time. Clients range from trading systems needing low-latency updates to researchers working with large historical datasets. Our current architecture is written mostly in Elixir, which we enjoy and derive great value from. Inter-service streaming communication is over gRPC and the data layer comprises PostgreSQL, Kafka, and ClickHouse. Demand for our services is growing rapidly across a variety of access patterns, and we are now building a comprehensive data platform as an extension of our existing system to meet this demand. This position presents an opportunity to shape the future direction of our work. The successful candidate will play a key role in answering questions such as: How do we guarantee that a client sees the same prices whether they query the warehouse, subscribe to a stream, or call our gRPC API? How do we let clients load a snapshot and then switch to live updates with no gaps or duplicates? What parts of our systems should stay in Elixir, and where should we consider other technologies?

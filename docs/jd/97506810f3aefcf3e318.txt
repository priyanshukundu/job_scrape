Purpose In this role as Senior Manager, Office of the Monitor, you will act in a supporting role to the Group and assist in providing strategic direction, leadership and oversight for all activities and dealings with Scotiabank’s Independent Compliance Monitor (ICM). You will partner closely with all stakeholders across all three lines of defense, particularly in Global Banking and Markets and Global Compliance, to ensure the Bank meets both its obligations and the Monitors expectations on a proactive basis. You will ensure that the Bank maintains transparent, positive and open relationships with the monitor and their team. What You'll Do • Provides support in managing Scotiabank’s relationship with the Independent Compliance Monitor (ICM). • Provides and prepares content for regular updates and reporting to key stakeholders and senior management committees. • Coordinate interactions required by the monitor and their team, with applicable BNS employees as the ICM may request. • Ensure timely submission of all requested daily, weekly and monthly reporting. • Attending related meetings. • Maintaining the centralized repository where documents are stored and previous related communication and reports are archived. • Ensuring key communications to the Monitor is responsive and complete. • Track Monitor requests and coordinate with business stakeholders to meet required deliverable deadlines; ensuring that submissions maintain a consistent message and tone. • Supports oversight and tracking of identified issues and remediation activities, including: o Coordinating and tracking the institution’s responses to findings with the Regulatory Initiatives Group o Ensuring that communication regarding remediation of suggested enhancements is clearly articulated and transparent. What You'll Bring • Bachelor’s degree. MBA/JD a plus. • At least 5-7 years of experience at a regulatory agency such as the NFA or CFTC, or in a similar position in the financial services industry, a Regulatory Affairs background or related consulting experience • An in depth understanding of Commodity laws and trading procedures • Strong understanding of financial markets, products and control functions. • Strong written and oral communication, presentation and interpersonal skills to effectively guide internal partners. • Strong planning and organization skills. • Ability to work independently and effectively with both regulatory staff and senior firm personnel. • Sound judgment in identifying risks in order to proactively escalate with relevant senior management. • Demonstrated ability to operate at a strategic level. • Professionalism, sensitivity, and discretion required. • Ability to solve problems, multi-task and manage changing priorities. • Detail-oriented. • Collaborative team player. • Strong office productivity skills (Word, Excel, PowerPoint and SharePoint) Interested?

Job Summary As a Lead Data Scientist (VP) supporting the American Dream Initiative (ADI), you will lead high-visibility work that defines how the initiative understands customers, measures progress, and proves impact. You will partner with stakeholders across Product, Marketing, Research, Finance, Technology, and other analytics teams to build foundational customer outcome metrics (including a financial health indicator), develop data-informed customer personas that blend qualitative and quantitative insights, stand up automated reporting and OKR/target frameworks, and establish robust testing and readout practices to evaluate customer experiences and programs. This role requires strong technical depth, structured problem solving in ambiguous environments, and clear executive-ready communication. Job Responsibilities Lead development of ADI measurement strategies, including a financial health indicator and supporting customer outcome/engagement metrics that are scalable and decision-ready. Drive cross-firm data discovery and integration to enable measurement (identify sources, align definitions, resolve gaps, and coordinate with partner teams). Establish and own metric governance and automated reporting (KPI/OKR framework, target-setting support, dashboards/scorecards, and quality controls). Develop customer personas and segments by integrating qualitative customer research with quantitative behavioral, product, and engagement data; translate findings into actionable narratives and opportunities. Build and operationalize a testing and impact measurement framework for ADI experiences/programs (experiment design, readouts, and recommendations to improve future iterations). Execute complex analyses and models using SQL and Python in big-data environments to inform targeting, personalization, and customer engagement strategy. Communicate clearly to senior stakeholders and mentor team members; ensure work aligns with business goals, compliance requirements, and responsible analytics/model risk practices.

Reporting to the Sr. Director of Strategic Pricing, Contracting and Reimbursement (SPCR), the role of Director of Strategic Pricing, Contracting & Reimbursement - HIV Pipeline Portfolio is responsible for developing pricing, contracting & reimbursement strategies for payer, institutional & provider customer channels in the US for in-market product(s) specific to HIV Treatment. This position is an integral role within the US Market Access function and involves significant levels of matrix partnership to influence stakeholders and manage priorities as well as cross-functional teamwork to assess the external environment, derive customer insights, and develop tailored strategies across channels and amid a dynamic US payer landscape. This role is based at ViiV Healthcare’s U.S. headquarters in Durham, NC and follows a hybrid working model, requiring a minimum of 2 days per week onsite. Candidates who do not currently live within commuting distance of Durham will be required to relocate to North Carolina. This role is eligible for a domestic relocation package.

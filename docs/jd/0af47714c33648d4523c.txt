Job Description What is the Opportunity? The Associate Director, Operational Resilience Program & Governance Manager role sits within the 1LOD Cross-Business Controls & Enablement team under the Consolidated US Operations (CUSO) and Capital Markets Chief Controls Office (CCO). The mandate of the role includes coordinating and driving a complex portfolio of regulatory commitments, internal remediation initiatives, and other operational resilience/business continuity management (BCM) strategic priorities across 1LOD for CUSO and global Capital Markets. This role will also own or coordinate key governance and management reporting, providing senior leadership with a clear and forward-looking view of program progress, issues, dependencies, and emerging risks. This position has a high degree of interconnectedness across businesses (Capital Markets, US Wealth Management, City National Bank, RBC Bank (Georgia)) along with partnering closely with diverse stakeholders, including (but not limited to) operational resilience/BCM leads, service/process owners, 2LOD risk domain and governance leads, regulatory relations, and internal audit. What will you do? Create structure and management reporting around complex and strategic initiatives, establishing accountability, managing dependencies, and driving execution against measurable outcomes. “Connect the dots” across various program requirements, initiatives, controls, issues, risks, and timelines, translating these into practical execution strategies. Aggregate and distill disparate information into clear and concise messaging, including potential trade-offs and required management actions. Influence and build credibility without direct authority as well as constructively challenge assumptions, execution plans, and risk assessments. Review enterprise requirements and collaborate with key stakeholders to drive or influence policies, standards, and processes/reporting alignment as well as simplification across CUSO and Capital Markets. Lead 1LOD risk profile reporting and develop data-driven insights; also support RBC’s risk appetite reporting processes, including driving consistent risk narratives where applicable. Lead and/or contribute to Working Groups, governance fora, and targeted initiatives. Lead and/or coordinate responses to regulatory exams and internal reviews; assist with developing remediation plans as applicable. Drive consistency in issue management by setting clear expectations, documentation quality, and proactive reporting and oversight. Promote the use and integration of Technology tools/AI to increase efficiency and effectiveness. What do you need to succeed? 8 years of experience in Financial Services, with at least 4-5 years dedicated to supporting operational risk management (operational resilience/BCM experience is beneficial). Demonstrated ability to translate complex concepts into clear materials or briefings for senior management and/or risk governance, including themes, residual risk framing, and actionable recommendations. Strong analytical and problem-solving skills (including root cause analysis) with keen attention to detail. Demonstrated capability to work in a dynamic, fast-paced environment with multiple priorities. Demonstrated ability to lead structured and outcomes-oriented meetings. Excellent project management and communication skills with the ability to constructively challenge without alienating stakeholders. Continuous improvement orientation particularly around modernizing processes, controls, and tooling. What’s in it for you?

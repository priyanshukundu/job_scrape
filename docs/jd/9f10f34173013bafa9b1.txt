Do you enjoy working with data, identifying trends, and solving problems? Are you ready to put those skills to work in a role where your insights can make an impact? We are Atlantic Union Bank, the largest regional bank in the mid-Atlantic, and we are looking for a Credit Portfolio Management Associate to join our Caring, Courageous, and Committed team. In this role, you'll help monitor the performance of our credit portfolio, support portfolio management activities, and contribute to the ongoing success of our Wealth Management Operations team. If you're ready to bring your analytical skills to a team where you can contribute and grow your expertise, we welcome the opportunity to meet you. Your Impact As a Credit Portfolio Management Associate, you'll put your analytical skills to work, helping support the financial health and long-term success of the Bank. Working alongside Teammates, you'll use data and portfolio information to spot trends, identify areas that may need attention, and help determine when adjustments may be needed. You’ll have the opportunity to apply your skills, deepen your expertise in credit and risk, and take on increasing responsibility as you grow in the role. Along the way, you’ll make a measurable contribution to the Bank while building the experience and knowledge to support your continued career progression. What You'll Do Monitor and analyze portfolio performance Help monitor the credit portfolio to ensure it meets established standards. Review portfolio information to spot trends, exceptions, and areas that may need attention. Analyze financial statements Help maintain accurate records and documentation. Support strong risk management Work with Teammates to review existing credit facilities and help identify when risk assessments may be needed. Help identify and monitor potential risks while supporting efforts to optimize portfolio performance. Escalate questions or concerns to appropriate Teammates for review. Gain experience underwriting different credit products. Deliver operational excellence Support accurate and timely completion of assigned portfolio management activities. Partner with Teammates to gather information and support portfolio-related needs. Follow established policies and practices related to your area of responsibility. Be a Great Teammate Take ownership of your work by delivering accurate, timely results and contributing to team goals. Work collaboratively, communicate openly, take ownership of your actions, and support the success of our clients and Teammates. Manage tasks independently with limited supervision, demonstrating strong attention to detail. What You'll Need Bachelor's degree required; equivalent combination of education and experience may be considered. 5-6+ years of prior experience, with a broad understanding of the field.

Job Summary The Software Engineer I is an entry-level role focused on developing, configuring, testing, and supporting enterprise applications under direct supervision. The position provides opportunities to work with custom software, cloud platforms, integrations, databases, and low-code development tools. procedures and guidance Assists with monitoring applications, reviewing logs and alerts, and resolving routine support issues in accordance with established procedures Assists with developing and supporting integrations between various applications and external systems using REST APIs and other approved integration patterns Creates and maintains technical documentation for configurations, components, integrations, and support procedures Collaborates with business analysts, administrators, developers, and other stakeholders to clarify requirements and deliver solutions Develops and

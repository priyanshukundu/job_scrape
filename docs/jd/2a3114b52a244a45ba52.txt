Work You’ll Do Key Responsibilities: Applied Research & Written Product Development Conduct research, analysis, and synthesis of disaster management data and information to produce accurate, comprehensive publications aligned to client-specific style and content requirements, including international civil-military coordination contexts. Produce a wide range of disaster management and humanitarian assistance materials, including: Fact sheets, case studies, best practices, research articles, and research studies Disaster management reference country handbooks Briefings, presentations, memos, information/policy papers, planning documents, and conference materials Professional journal publications, situation reports, after action reports, and time-sensitive requests for information (RFIs) Case Study and Training Content Support Design and develop operationally relevant disaster response case studies for use in training modules. Support civil-military training, exercises, operations, short-/long-term planning, and assessments in alignment with the theater campaign plan by researching, sharing information, and exchanging best practices. Collaboration, Review Cycles & Quality Editing Collaborate with subject matter experts (SMEs) to collect and validate information, verify data integrity, and ensure analytic accuracy. Facilitate integration of external feedback into documents; review comments, recommend improvements, and ensure products meet target audience needs. Edit research products for logical flow, clarity, completeness, grammar, punctuation, and content quality. Coordinate with graphics/design staff to ensure drafts meet final production standards for web and print publication; directly support print production and inventory of information products. Academic Partnerships, Outreach & Knowledge Sharing Support the Academic Partnership Program by cultivating academic partnerships and facilitating information exchange with universities, students/interns, academic partners, international organizations, and related networks. Update data management and information-sharing plans to support program operations. Coordinate marketing and outreach activities as needed, including website updates, brochure/report development, social media and newsletter content, and engagement with external institutions. Provide guidance to students on their research papers; edit/revise academic papers to translate findings into practical DMHA recommendations. Coordinate with various staff to ensure students are fully integrated and resourced; answer academic inquiries; curate a list of strategic research topics to provide project options for incoming students. Stakeholder Engagement, Program Operations & Events Liaise with other branches and engage staff with relevant expertise, relationships, or country/subject knowledge. Organize and facilitate events, training sessions, and exercises tied to product development and dissemination. Prepare and deliver presentations/briefings at academic engagements, workshops, and exercises. Attend regular meetings, and maintain an updated task/to-do deliverables dashboard to leadership Directly support print production and inventory of information products, unpack boxes of products, event set up, and other duties as assigned.

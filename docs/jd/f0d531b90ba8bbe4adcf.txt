THE ROLE

Data at Confido feeds customer-facing reports, internal dashboards, and a lot of day-to-day decisions. Right now it lives in too many places and is owned by too many people. We’re hiring a VP of Data Engineering to own all of it: how data gets into our systems, how it’s stored and modeled, and how it shows up in the reports people use, inside the company and out.

You’ll be the single owner of data at Confido. You’ll manage our Senior Analytics Engineer to start, and you’ll build the team from there. Early on you’ll be hands-on. Over time, more of your time will go to hiring, setting direction, and working with the leadership team.


What You’ll Own

 - Ingestion. Every source that feeds our data, including product databases, event tracking, and third-party tools. You decide how data comes in, how often, and what happens when a source changes or breaks.

 - Warehouse and pipelines. Warehouse design, orchestration, and the infrastructure underneath, including its cost.

 - Modeling. One set of core models and metric definitions that the whole company uses.

 - Reporting. Internal dashboards and BI, plus the data behind customer-facing reports. If a number shows up somewhere at Confido, it’s yours.

 - Quality and governance. Testing, monitoring, access controls, and data privacy. You’re accountable when numbers are wrong, and you put fixes in place so the same problems don’t come back.


What You’ll Do

 - Build and lead the data org, starting with our Senior Analytics Engineer. Decide which roles to hire and when, run the interviews, and develop the people you bring on.

 - Set the data roadmap and explain the trade-offs to the leadership team and the rest of the company.

 - Make the big technical calls on architecture, tooling, and build vs. buy.

 - Work with Engineering, Product, Ops, and Customer teams to figure out what data they actually need, and say no when something isn’t worth building.

 - Set the standards the team works by: modeling, testing, docs, monitoring, and on-call.

 - Write SQL, review PRs, and build things yourself while the team is small.


What We’re Looking For

 - 10+ years in data engineering, analytics engineering, or similar, with 4+ years leading data teams.

 - You’ve owned data end to end before, from ingestion through reporting, not just one layer of it.

 - You’ve built a data function from an early or messy state, grown the team, and kept it running.

 - Strong SQL and data modeling skills, and experience with Snowflake, dbt, Airflow, Dagster, or similar tools.

 - You’ve hired well, including people senior to where the team started.

 - You can sit in a leadership meeting, turn “we need better data” into a plan, and defend it.

 - You’re comfortable being hands-on now and stepping back as the team grows.

Success in This Role

A year in, there’s no question about who owns data at Confido. People trust the numbers and know where to find them. Sources are documented, pipelines break less often, and when they do, the team catches it before a customer does. Internal and customer-facing reports run off the same definitions. The team has grown, it has a clear structure and standards, and it’s planned out for the next stage.

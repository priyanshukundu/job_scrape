About This Job The Commercial Underwriting Associate supports the evaluation, structuring, and underwriting of commercial lending opportunities across the Small Business and Technology & Life Sciences portfolios. This role performs financial and credit analysis, evaluates repayment capacity and transaction risk, and develops underwriting recommendations in partnership with Relationship Managers and Credit leadership. The ideal candidate has a strong foundation in commercial credit and financial analysis and is comfortable underwriting a diverse range of borrowers, from established small businesses to growth-oriented technology and life sciences companies with more complex or evolving financial profiles. Responsibilities: Analyze and underwrite commercial lending opportunities across the Small Business and Technology & Life Sciences portfolios Evaluate financial statements, tax returns, cash flow, liquidity, projections, collateral, and other financial information to assess creditworthiness and repayment capacity Assess borrower performance, industry dynamics, management strength, and key credit risks using appropriate metrics for each portfolio Build financial models and sensitivity analyses to support underwriting conclusions and transaction structures Evaluate loan structures, collateral, guarantees, covenants, and other terms to mitigate credit risk Prepare credit memoranda and present well-supported underwriting recommendations to Credit leadership Participate in client discussions and due diligence to identify key strengths, risks, and mitigants Partner with Relationship Managers, Credit, Legal, Portfolio Management, and Operations throughout the underwriting and closing process Monitor market and industry trends impacting Small Business and Technology & Life Sciences clients Qualifications: Bachelor’s degree in Finance, Accounting, Economics, Business, or a related field 2+ years of experience in commercial underwriting, credit analysis, small business lending, or commercial banking Strong knowledge of financial statement, cash flow, and commercial credit analysis Experience evaluating credit risk, repayment capacity, collateral, and financial projections Strong Excel, financial modeling, and quantitative analysis skills Strong analytical,

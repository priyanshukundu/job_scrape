About the Role:

Crusoe is building the world's favorite AI-first cloud, and the Identity & Access Management (IAM) team is the security bedrock our customers trust to run their most demanding AI and GPU workloads. IAM sits on the hot path for every request in the cloud, authorizing millions of requests at single-digit-millisecond latency, so reliability and performance are non-negotiable. We are seeking seasoned Senior Software Engineers to design, build, and scale this platform: the fine-grained authorization model for Crusoe Cloud resources, enterprise SSO and identity federation, multi-tenant organization hierarchies, identity for the agentic-AI era, and credential and secrets management. As a Senior Engineer, you'll own the design and delivery of significant IAM services end-to-end, partner across teams, and help raise the technical bar through mentorship and code review.



What You'll Be Working On:

 - Design and Deliver IAM Services - Own the end-to-end design, development, and operation of core IAM services - fine-grained authorization, authentication, identity federation, and identity lifecycle - that secure every request in Crusoe's AI cloud.

 - Solve Complex AuthN/AuthZ Problems - Design and build solutions to complex authentication and authorization scenarios at the platform level, including the fine-grained authorization model for accessing Crusoe Cloud resources.

 - Engineer for the Hot Path - Deliver low-latency, fault-tolerant services that remain highly available under load, since IAM is on the critical path for every API call.

 - Harden Security Posture - Apply security best practices such as OAuth2/OIDC, RBAC/ABAC, and session and credential management across the services you own.

 - Contribute to Architecture - Contribute to architectural decisions that support security, reliability, and maintainability across the company.

 - Collaborate Across Teams - Work with product, design, security, and SRE to evaluate tools, frameworks, and customer needs, aligning priorities to ship customer-first solutions.

 - Mentor and Elevate - Mentor engineers, enhance hiring and onboarding practices, perform code reviews, and contribute to a strong, inclusive engineering culture.

Quantitative Risk, VP

Location: Boston, Massachusetts

Who we are looking for We are seeking an experienced quantitative risk professional with strong analytical, modeling, independent ownership and stakeholder management capabilities to join our Model Validation Group as a Vice President. The successful candidate will play a key role in providing independent oversight and effective challenge of quantitative models used across the organization, while partnering with model developers, risk managers, business stakeholders, senior management and regulators. This role requires strong expertise in quantitative finance and model risk management, as well as the ability to communicate complex technical concepts to diverse audiences. The candidate will report to a Senior Model Validation Lead within the Model Risk Management Department. Why this role is important Models are critical to the firm's risk management, capital planning, valuation, trading and strategic decision-making processes. As part of the Model Validation Group, you will help ensure that models are conceptually sound, fit for purpose, compliant with regulatory expectations and appropriately governed throughout their lifecycle. You will contribute to safeguarding the firm's risk management framework by independently assessing model methodologies, assumptions, limitations, data quality, implementation and ongoing performance. Your work will directly support sound risk management practices, regulatory compliance and informed business decisions across the organization. This is an opportunity to work on some of the firm's most important quantitative models while helping shape validation standards, methodologies and best practices across the model risk management function. What you will be responsible for As a Quantitative Risk Vice President, you will independently lead end-to-end model validations for assigned models, with responsibility for validation timelines, deliverables and stakeholder engagement throughout the review process. The models may span areas including market risk, counterparty credit risk, valuation, capital stress testing and trading algorithms. Key responsibilities include: Execute comprehensive validation activities, including methodology review, theoretical assessments, data analysis, implementation testing, benchmarking, outcomes analysis and ongoing monitoring assessments. Provide effective challenge to model design, assumptions, limitations, data sourcing and model performance metrics. Assess whether models are conceptually sound, fit for purpose and appropriately governed throughout their lifecycle, and evaluate compliance with internal model risk policies and applicable regulatory expectations. Develop and execute quantitative testing frameworks, benchmark analyses, sensitivity analyses, stress testing and alternative model assessments to evaluate model risk. Analyze large and complex datasets using statistical, econometric, machine learning and data analytics techniques where appropriate. Identify model weaknesses, limitations and implementation issues, and clearly articulate associated risks and remediation recommendations. Review model development documentation, technical specifications, governance materials and ongoing monitoring results. Prepare comprehensive validation reports and present findings, conclusions and recommendations to model owners and other stakeholders. Partner with model owners across Enterprise Risk Management, Front Office and other relevant functions to facilitate timely execution of validation activities and remediation efforts. Represent Model Risk Management, as appropriate, in discussions with senior management, governance teams, Internal Audit, regulators and other key stakeholders on model risk matters. Contribute to continuous enhancement of validation methodologies, analytical frameworks, automation capabilities and model risk governance practices. What we value The successful candidate will demonstrate: Strong quantitative and analytical expertise with the ability to independently evaluate complex financial models and quantitative frameworks. Intellectual curiosity and professional skepticism, with a willingness to challenge assumptions and investigate underlying model risks. Sound understanding of model risk management principles, validation techniques and industry practices. Strong project management skills with the ability to independently manage multiple validations, timelines, deliverables and competing priorities. Excellent problem-solving capabilities and the ability to translate complex quantitative concepts into clear and actionable insights. Effective stakeholder management and collaboration skills, with the ability to work across model development, business, risk, technology, audit and governance functions and influence outcomes through constructive challenge and partnership. Strong written and verbal communication skills, including the ability to present technical findings to senior management and non-technical audiences. Commitment to continuous learning and staying current on emerging quantitative methodologies, regulatory developments and industry trends. Ability to mentor and support junior team members while independently leading complex validation assignments. Qualifications 6+ years of relevant experience in model validation, model development, quantitative risk or related quantitative disciplines within financial services. Advanced degree preferred in a quantitative discipline such as Finance, Financial Engineering, Statistics, Mathematics, Economics, Physics, Computer Science, Data Science or a related field; equivalent relevant quantitative experience will also be considered. Candidates with strong model development or quantitative risk backgrounds and demonstrated experience providing effective challenge or independent review will be considered. Strong understanding of financial products and markets, which may include derivatives, securities financing transactions, fixed income, foreign exchange, equities and structured products. Experience in one or more areas such as market risk, counterparty credit risk, valuation, stress testing, capital modeling or trading algorithms is preferred. Knowledge of applicable model risk management regulatory frameworks, supervisory expectations and model governance standards. Proficiency in quantitative programming and data analysis tools such as Python, R, SQL, MATLAB or equivalent analytical platforms. Experience developing or validating advanced statistical, econometric, machine learning or optimization models is preferred. Experience interacting with senior stakeholders, governance functions, Internal Audit and/or regulators on model risk matters is preferred.

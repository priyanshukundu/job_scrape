Data Scientist III
Chewy
Location: Bellevue, WA; Plantation, FL

Job Description: Our Opportunity: Chewy’s Inbound Technology tam is seeking a Data Scientist III to join our Inbound Tech team in Bellevue, WA or Plantation, FL. The team focuses on technology improvements in the inbound supply chain space, ranging from transportation decisions to informing labor planning. You will partner daily with data science, software engineering, business, and product teammates and partners. This role will act as an Individual Contributor to design, test, and implement solutions and analyses, lead initiatives, and mentor less experienced teammates. Millions of outbound shipments are only possible when we bring the right material into our facilities at the right time. In inbound logistics, you must be nimble and creative, able to balance various priorities, and be adept at looking at a situation and identifying ways you can apply your data science expertise to improve decision-making, efficiency, cost, or safety. This team has a unique culture of both working independently AND being available to brainstorm with or help teammates on gnarly problems. This is only possible when we all excel at our Operating Principles of Deliver Results and Earn Trust . What You’ll Do: Develop new and improve existing models and solutions to solve complex supply chain problems using machine learning, operations research, statistics, and relevant simulation or artificial intelligence (AI) techniques. Design, build, and monitor metrics to confirm impact of your solutions. Design and maintain data pipelines for large-scale modeling workflows, including feature engineering and data validation for large, complex datasets. Implement process improvement initiatives by developing new data sources, developing and testing model enhancements, and reducing computational complexity Deep dive performance and supply chain data to explain change, develop insights, and provide recommendations Establish strong working relationships with and partner with other functional teams across Fulfillment, Supply Chain, and Finance. Understand their needs, to maximize your ability to meet their needs with technical solutions. Write and present one-page proposals to gain stakeholder buy-in for new models, and white papers that clearly explain the assumptions, results, and business recommendations of implemented models. Design clear and concise updates and visualizations at detail levels appropriate to the audience. What You’ll Need: 5+ years of experience developing and deploying optimization, machine learning, and/or predictive models in fast-paced environment Advanced degree (MS or PhD) in Data Science, Industrial Engineering, Operations Research, Statistics, Applied Mathematics, or equivalent field Mastery in SQL and Python Ability to use collaborative programming tools (Git, Confluence, etc.), and assist and support junior colleagues’ use of those tools Hands-on industry experience developing, validating, and deploying machine learning models to solve complex business problems, including feature selection, model evaluation, and performance monitoring. Hands-on industry experience building and deploying large-scale mathematical optimization models (LP, MILP, etc.) in production using Gurobi (strongly preferred) or similar solvers. Ability to translate complex analysis and data science concepts into ‘plain English’ and simple business recommendations. Nice to Have: Experience in a supply chain, logistics, or e-commerce environment.

Location: 4910 Tiedeman Road, Brooklyn Ohio JOB TITLE: Lead Business Analytics Associate (II) – Deposit Product and Pricing Analytics ABOUT THE JOB (JOB BRIEF) A lead business analytics associate will support the Deposit Product Management and Pricing organization with a focus on predictive analytics and forecasting. This analyst will be tasked with creation, ownership and regular maintenance of one or more deposit portfolio forecasting tools, including Savings, Money Market or CD. The associate will use data and analytical tools to perform moderately complex diagnostic analyses to provide answers to questions such as “Why did it happen?” and “What strategies could we employ to drive desired business outcomes?”. The associate will also be expected to build ongoing and repeatable reporting, such as tableau dashboards. A successful analyst will leverage business acumen to become a domain expert in both the data and business strategy of the products and portfolios they support. They will exhibit clear and concise communication and storytelling with business leaders and stakeholders; and collaborate effectively with broader analytics and business partners. ESSENTIAL JOB FUNCTIONS Create and maintain deposit forecasting tools using SQL, Google Cloud Platform and Microsoft Excel Translate data into insights and answer the “why” question Analyze, identify, and recommend appropriate solutions to moderately complex problems Develop “client ready” work product Proactively communicate with business partners and ask the right questions to understand analytics needs Use visualizations to tell a compelling story with data and information, using PowerPoint and/or Tableau May guide or monitor the work of others REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS Bachelor’s degree (or its equivalent) and 2 years minimum of relevant experience; 1 year with Master’s or PhD Proficient in: Microsoft Excel / PowerPoint Query writing/coding with ability to perform queries of large datasets Developing data visualization skill set Ability to learn the process of consuming data from the data supply chain Building knowledge / experience with analytical programming languages EXPECTED COMPETENCIES Communication: Basic written and verbal communication skills; Asks the right questions; Understands upstream and downstream impacts Critical Thinking / Problem Solving: Demonstrates critical thinking; Can translate data and answer the “why” question; Starting to understand impacts / intersections with others Physical Demands General Office - Prolonged sitting, ability to communicate face to face in person or on the phone with teammates and clients, frequent use of PC/laptop, occasional lifting/pushing/pulling of backpacks, computer bags up to 10 lbs.

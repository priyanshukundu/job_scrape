Salesforce Full Stack Engineers About the role We are looking for Salesforce Developers to work in a diverse and fast-paced environment. This role will be responsible for developing solutions that are built on the latest technologies and patterns, including a strong understanding of AI-assisted development and harness engineering. This role requires partnering with the broader JLL teams based in the US and will provide the opportunity to interact directly with key business and leadership stakeholders. This role requires a background knowledge of commercial real estate and GSE Agency financing activities. Responsibilities Understand business requirements and implement them in Salesforce using up-to-date tools and technologies, current LWC frameworks and Claude Code (or similar) practices. Ensure that all the development work done aligns with Salesforce and JLL best practices. Prepare workflow documents, technical documentation and end-user guides. Work with business and product managers to do rapid prototyping and iterative development. Ensure that features developed are fulfilling the established acceptance criteria. Active participation in meetings with strong communication and presentation skills. Technical Skills & Competencies Effective written and verbal communication skills, including technical writing. Strong knowledge of out of box Salesforce functionality and best practices around Salesforce configuration and customization Expertise in designing custom objects, custom fields, picklists, page layouts, workflows approval processes, validation rules, custom tabs, reports, dashboards, and email generation according to application requirements Expert engineering skills with Force.com Platform (Apex Classes and Triggers, SOQL, Aura, LWC Components and Javascript skills. Experience designing solutions to overcome Governor Limits/HEAP Limitations, etc. Experience with web services (REST & SOAP, JSON & XML, etc.) Strong knowledge of deployment patterns with Github with Gearset integrations. Expertise with VS Code and AI assisted development tools such as Claude Code or Cursor. Experience with Excel, knowledge of VBA, Macros and Excel to Salesforce integrations. Experience with third-party API integrations. A desire to work within a fast-paced, collaborative, and team-based support environment. What we can do for you: Best in class salary.

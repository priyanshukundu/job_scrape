About the Role:

Crusoe is seeking a Senior Staff Software Engineer on our Architecture team to help shape how our infrastructure monitors, configures, and heals itself without human intervention. You will partner with the Physical Network Automation team to design the automation frameworks, self-healing workflows, and observability platforms that allow our global fleet to scale sublinearly with headcount.



This is a senior technical leadership role at the intersection of Architecture, Software Engineering, and Infrastructure Reliability. You will work side by side with the Physical Network Automation team, combining Architecture's design perspective with their operational and implementation expertise, to turn intent-based designs into production automation. You will also represent Architecture in cross-functional planning with Deployment, Operations, and Site Reliability. You're not writing scripts to replace manual tasks. You're helping design the control plane model that makes our infrastructure intelligent.



What You'll Be Working On:

 - Co-Develop the Automation Architecture: Partner with the Physical Network Automation team to shape the technical roadmap and reference architecture for Crusoe's automation stack, from source of truth and configuration generation through day-2 operations, telemetry, and closed-loop remediation.

 - Shape the Source of Truth: Work with automation and operations teams on the data model that drives configuration, validation, and operational state, and help establish how systems integrate with it.

 - Design Intent-Based Systems Together: Collaborate on declarative, model-driven configuration pipelines that treat infrastructure as code and make configuration drift impossible, contributing architectural direction while the automation team brings implementation and operational insight.

 - Contribute to Model-Driven Standards: Help define the approach to API-driven, schema-defined telemetry collection, configuration management, and state validation across heterogeneous hardware and vendors.

 - Design Self-Healing Workflows: Work jointly on the architecture for event-driven, auto-remediation systems that detect faults, correlate telemetry, and resolve known failure modes without human escalation.

 - Shape the Observability Strategy: Partner on the technical direction for telemetry, metrics, alerting, and dashboarding, including streaming pipelines and the data models behind them.

 - Advocate for Automation-First Design: Work with network architects and automation engineers so that designs are deployable, validatable, and operable programmatically at scale from day one.

 - Collaborate and Multiply: Participate in design reviews, architecture reviews, and technical discussions that raise the engineering bar across both teams, learning from the automation team as much as guiding them.



What You'll Bring to the Team:

 - 12+ years of engineering experience with a demonstrated focus on production software engineering, platform architecture, and infrastructure as code in hyperscale or internet-scale environments.

 - Demonstrated Technical Leadership: Proven track record of helping define architectures for automation platforms that a broader engineering organization builds on, and of influencing and aligning teams you don't directly manage.

 - Strong Software Engineering Foundation: Fluency in Python or Go, with a clear understanding of what makes systems testable, CI/CD-integrated, and production-ready. You can review and contribute to implementation, and prototype when needed.

 - Model-Driven Automation Fluency: Deep experience with declarative, schema-defined configuration and telemetry, and a clear view of why model-driven automation is the only path to hyperscale.

 - Source of Truth Design: Experience designing or contributing to an infrastructure source of truth, including the data model, integrations, and the pipelines that consume it.

 - Event-Driven and Self-Healing Systems: Experience architecting or building auto-remediation and closed-loop automation that detects, correlates, and resolves faults without human intervention.

 - Observability Expertise: Hands-on experience designing streaming telemetry and observability platforms at fleet scale.

 - Hyperscale Operational Context: Comfort designing for 10K+ devices and multi-region fleets, where the blast radius of a bug is measured in racks, not individual machines.

 - Cross-Team Partnership: Ability to build trust and shared direction with automation engineers, architects, and operations teams, and to keep designs and implementations aligned without relying on formal authority.

 - Education: Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, or a related field, or equivalent practical experience in hyperscale or internet-scale environments.

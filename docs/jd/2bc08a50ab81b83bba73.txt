Associate Director - Debt Capital Markets
Standard Chartered
Location: New York, US
Link: https://jobs.standardchartered.com/job/New-York-Associate-Director-Debt-Capital-Markets/1377419057/

Job Summary • The Associate Director, Debt Capital Markets - Americas will contribute to the development of the Americas DCM franchise through the origination and execution of debt capital markets transactions for North American corporate clients. • The role will support and, where appropriate, independently manage defined client relationships and transaction workstreams across sectors including Technology, Media and Telecommunications; Industrials; Energy and Chemicals; Metals and Mining; Agribusiness and Commodity Traders; Logistics; Transportation; and Airlines. • Working closely with senior members of the Corporate Investment Grade DCM team, Global Banking, Bond Syndicate, Financial Markets, Corporate Finance and other product partners, the role holder will develop and deliver financing strategies across the global capital markets, including the United States, Europe, Asia, the Middle East and Africa. • The Associate Director will be expected to originate actionable financing ideas, provide clients with informed advice on market conditions, pricing, transaction structure and issuance strategy, and lead the day-to-day execution of transactions. The role holder will also contribute to client development, revenue growth, cross-product collaboration, effective risk management and the coaching and development of junior team members. Key Responsibilities Strategy • Contribute to the development and execution of the DCM Americas business strategy in conjunction with Global Banking, Financial Markets, Corporate Finance and other product partners globally. • Support the growth of the Americas DCM franchise by identifying, developing and helping convert capital markets opportunities with existing and prospective clients. • Develop client-specific origination strategies based on funding requirements, debt maturity profiles, capital structure, credit and ratings considerations, market conditions and investor demand. • Identify opportunities to connect North American clients with the Bank’s global capital markets, distribution capabilities and cross-border investor base. Business • Contribute to the growth of DCM revenues, market share, client penetration and awareness of the Bank’s global DCM capabilities. • Maintain a forward-looking understanding of global fixed-income markets and translate material market developments into actionable client ideas. • Contribute to appropriate management of costs, resources, compliance requirements and risk controls in the operation of the DCM Americas business. • Assist senior team members with client prioritisation, business planning, pipeline management and the allocation of execution resources. Processes • Develop a deep understanding of processes and provide guidance to junior staff on the team • Help support the execution of all transactions with DCM legal and bond syndicate. • Provide clients with pitches, proposals, presentations and analyses related to potential bond issuance and liability management. • With Bond Syndicate and senior members of the DCM team, be jointly responsible for pricing updates and new issue executions. • Help cross sell interest rate and Foreign Exchange risk management with Financial Markets sales. People & Talent • Lead by example and help build the appropriate culture and values. • Help develop junior staff. • Contribute to continuous process improvement and sharing of best practices. • Contribute to sharing knowledge with junior staff. Risk Management • Display exemplary conduct and live by the Group’s Values and Code of Conduct. • Take personal responsibility for embedding the highest standards of ethics, including regulatory and business conduct, across Standard Chartered Bank. This includes understanding and ensuring compliance with, in letter and spirit, all applicable laws, regulations, guidelines and the Group Code of Conduct. • Contribute to achieve the outcomes set out in the Bank’s Conduct Principles: Fair Outcomes for Clients; Effective Financial Markets; Financial Crime Compliance; The Right Environment. • Effectively and collaboratively identify, escalate, mitigate and resolve risk, conduct and compliance matters. Governance • Ensure that responsibilities are discharged in accordance with the Bank’s Governance Manual, applicable policies, procedures, delegated authorities and control requirements. • Maintain appropriate governance over assigned client activities, pitches and transactions. • Ensure that material decisions, approvals, transaction records and supporting documentation are complete, accurate and appropriately retained. • Support senior DCM management in maintaining effective oversight of the client pipeline, live transactions, revenue opportunities and material risks. • DCM Legal and external-counsel management teams • Compliance and Financial Crime Compliance • Control Room • New Issues Group • Finance • Operations and Transaction Management • Other relevant product and control functions External • Corporate Treasury teams • Chief Financial Officers and other senior Finance stakeholders • Existing and prospective corporate clients • External legal counsel • Rating agencies • Institutional investors, where appropriate to the role • Trustees, agents and other transaction counterparties • Other relevant capital-markets participants Other Responsibilities • Embed Here for good and the Group’s brand and values in DCM Origination - Americas. • Perform other responsibilities assigned under Group, Country, Business or Functional policies and procedures. • Support strategic projects and initiatives relating to the development of the Americas DCM franchise. • Contribute to client-targeting exercises, business-planning processes, pipeline reviews, revenue analysis and management reporting. • Contribute to internal market updates, client publications, thought leadership and relevant marketing initiatives. • Represent the DCM team in internal and external meetings where appropriate. • Maintain current knowledge of fixed-income markets, interest rates, foreign exchange, credit conditions, monetary policy, regulation and investor trends affecting DCM clients. • Undertake any additional responsibilities reasonably associated with the role’s seniority, business purpose and technical remit. • Where the role holder performs multiple functions or operates under a dual-hatting arrangement, discharge all associated responsibilities in accordance with the applicable terms of reference, policies and governance requirements. Skills and Experience Financial Risk Management • Understands and applies relevant credit, market, interest-rate, foreign-exchange, conduct, reputational and execution-risk considerations when developing and executing financing solutions. Knowledge of Customers • Maintains a detailed understanding of assigned clients’ businesses, financial profiles, strategic priorities, capital structures, funding requirements, ratings considerations and relevant decision. Prospecting • Identifies and develops capital-markets opportunities with existing and prospective clients and supports the conversion of relevant ideas into active client dialogue and mandates. Understanding Customer Needs • Evaluates client objectives, constraints, financing requirements and decision criteria and translates them into appropriate capital-markets strategies. Addressing Customer Needs • Develops and presents practical, commercially relevant and executable financing recommendations that reflect market conditions, investor demand, pricing, structure, timing and client objectives. Capital Markets Origination and Execution • Demonstrates the technical capability and execution judgment required to originate financing ideas and coordinate debt capital markets transactions from initial engagement through pricing and closing. Qualifications Education • Bachelor’s degree or equivalent qualification required, preferably in Finance, Economics, Accounting, Business, Mathematics or a related discipline. • A relevant postgraduate degree or professional qualification is advantageous but not required. Experience • Relevant experience in Debt Capital Markets, investment banking, corporate finance, syndicate, leveraged or acquisition finance, credit markets, or a closely related capital-markets role at a leading commercial or investment bank. • Demonstrated experience supporting and executing fixed-income capital-markets transactions for corporate issuers. • Experience developing financing strategies, client presentations, pricing analyses and transaction recommendations. • Experience analysing corporate financial statements, credit profiles, capital structures, ratings considerations and debt maturity schedules. • Experience coordinating internal and external transaction stakeholders and managing multiple concurrent workstreams. • Experience presenting market, pricing and financing recommendations to clients or senior internal stakeholders. • Experience with international or cross-border capital markets is preferred. Technical and Professional Qualifications • Strong proficiency in Microsoft Excel and PowerPoint. • Proficiency in Bloomberg, Dealogic and other relevant capital-markets information platforms. • Strong understanding of global debt markets, interest rates, credit spreads, foreign exchange and fixed-income investor dynamics. • Formal credit training or equivalent credit-analysis experience is advantageous. • Strong quantitative, financial-modelling, analytical and presentation skills. • Ability to analyse comparable instruments, issuer curves, relative value, fair value and new-issue pricing. • Ability to work accurately and effectively under pressure, including during live transaction execution. • Strong written and verbal communication skills. • Strong attention to detail and commitment to producing high-quality client and transaction materials. • Demonstrated professional maturity, sound judgment, integrity and a strong work ethic. • Self-starter with a demonstrated record of operating successfully in a collaborative and international team environment. Licences and Certifications • Securities Industry Essentials Examination, or SIE. • FINRA Series 79. • FINRA Series 63. • Any other licences, registrations or continuing-education requirements applicable to the final responsibilities and location of the role.

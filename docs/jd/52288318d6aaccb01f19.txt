Job Description: Mainframe Software Engineer Job Description: Broadcom's Mainframe Software Division is looking for a talented and motivated Software Engineer with a passion for building high-quality applications using agile practices to join our team. We are building an ecosystem of tools and principles to help our Mainframe product teams deliver high-quality, accessible, and consistent software. This role is for a hands-on engineer who will work at the intersection of design and development to modernize our enterprise software products. People who do well on this team are self-motivated engineers with a passion for our business and for technology innovation. This position is concentrated on the mainframe platform, with development in Assembler language. #Broadcomsoftware In this role, you will: Design and develop: Create high-quality software design and architecture for applications on the mainframe platform. Build and implement: Produce, test, and debug functional software solutions, leveraging your Mainframe expertise to modernize our enterprise software. Collaborate: Partner with product management and fellow software engineers in an Agile environment to define software requirements and deliver solutions. Automate: Increase efficiency by automating tasks through appropriate tools and scripting. Drive innovation: Contribute to a fast-paced environment with a passion for continuous improvement and technology innovation. Key Responsibilities: Collaboration and Leadership: · Collaborate with other product SW Engineers to share knowledge, best practices, and lessons learned. · Performing validation and verification testing. Excellent written and verbal communication skills, ability to efficiently collaborate with multiple teams across geographically diverse areas. Experience working in an Agile development methodology and an understanding of DevOps principles. Engineering Excellence Initiatives: · Take the lead in engineering excellence initiatives, promoting best practices, code quality, and technical debt reduction. · Contribute to retrospectives and actively address impediments to team productivity. · Ensuring software is up-to date with latest technologies. Continue to build Mainframe expertise and keep abreast of new technology, processes, systems, and tools. Required Skills & Competencies: Proven Mainframe knowledge and skills. IBM Assembler Language (HLASM) proficiency. Proficiency in Metal C (or PL/X), and JCL. z/OS Operating system familiarity. Source control systems experience such as Endevor, ISPW, SMUF / APTS, SPA / VMX, STAR, or others. Strong analytical, problem solving and debugging skills. Optional Competencies: Experience working with Disk Backup and Restore, BMC FDR, CA 1 Flexible Storage, Vtape, DASD Storage Management, DFSMS, Allocate DASD Space and Placement, DFShsm, DFSrmm and/or other mainframe storage products and systems. Experience with online z/OS debugger such as XDC, IBM SLD, and/or IBM Debug for z/OS. Proficiency in Metal C (or PL/X), and JCL. Scripting language experience. Preferred Education: Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Electrical Engineering or equivalent in a related field. Work Experience: Preferably, five or more years of experience in software development or a related field.

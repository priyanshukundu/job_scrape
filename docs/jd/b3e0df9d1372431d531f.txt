Job Summary: This job identifies issues and recommends best practices for risk management, leads projects to enhance risk assessment and mitigation, collaborates with teams to analyze business trends, contributes to process improvements, and provides guidance to team members. Job Description: Essential Responsibilities: Identify issues and recommend best practices for risk management within the organization. Lead functional projects and programs that enhance risk assessment and mitigation efforts. Collaborate with teams to analyze business trends and their implications for risk management. Contribute to process improvements that enhance the effectiveness of risk management strategies. Provide guidance and support to team members in executing risk management initiatives. Minimum Qualifications: 5+ years relevant experience and a Bachelor’s degree OR Any equivalent combination of education and experience. Additional Responsibilities & Preferred Qualifications : Ability to manage cross functional teams to secure governance approvals for new credit products and strategy changes.

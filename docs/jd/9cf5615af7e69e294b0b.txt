The First Line of Defense US Issuing Financial Crimes Risk & Controls Center of Excellence provides an approach to strengthening Financial Crimes Risk Management (FCRM) across the first line, including anti-money laundering, sanctions, and anti-bribery and corruption. The Director, Regulatory Remediation Strategy will lead the definition and development of 1LOD Financial Crimes Regulatory Remediation strategies. This role will translate regulatory requirements and identified control gaps into clear remediation outcomes, bring stakeholders to the table to execute on actionable plans, and measurable success criteria, across 1LoD Financial Crimes control environment and program requirements. The role will partner closely with assurance, governance, Technology, business units, Global Financial Crimes Compliance, and other control functions to ensure remediation is comprehensive, sustainable, and positioned for successful validation. Translate regulatory requirements, examination findings, and control deficiencies into clear remediation outcomes, milestones, deliverables, owners, and completion criteria. Support the definition of success criteria and evidence requirements to ensure remediation addresses underlying regulatory expectations and supports validation and closure. Drive sustainable enhancements to 1LOD processes, procedures, controls, governance, data, and other capabilities required to address identified deficiencies. Oversee remediation of internal Financial Crimes control issues, ensuring actions address root causes and deliver sustainable risk reduction. Pressure-test proposed solutions and deliverables for completeness, sustainability, and alignment with regulatory expectations. Mobilize and hold stakeholder accountable, resolve issues and dependencies, and escalate material scope, design, ownership, or execution risks. Work closely with execution, assurance, governance, and 2LOD partners to support effective implementation, testing, validation, and closure.

Who You’ll Work With New Markets is a new team within Corebridge’s Institutional Markets business. We find new commercial opportunities in international markets where Corebridge does not operate today. These opportunities may take the form of reinsurance, retail, or M&A, and we partner directly with the Corebridge teams that own each of those areas. For every opportunity, we work out what the business is worth and make sure Corebridge enters each market better informed than competitors. When a transaction takes the form of assumed reinsurance, New Markets works hand in hand with the Assumed Reinsurance team, which owns origination, structuring, negotiation, and post-close management of the transaction, and with the Pricing team, which prices every deal and owns the pricing opinion. For opportunities that take other forms, we partner with Corebridge’s retail businesses and corporate development functions as appropriate. The team is deliberately small and senior. We use AI tools, a panel of external partners (consulting actuaries, local counsel, auditors, and licensed local carriers), and expertise from across Corebridge to do work that would otherwise need a much larger organization. You will report to the Head of New Markets and work closely with the Assumed and Ceded Reinsurance teams, Pricing, Corporate Actuarial, Finance, Risk, and Corebridge’s asset management team, as well as external consulting actuaries, advisers, and the actuarial teams of the insurers we work with. About The Role As Vice President, Actuarial Valuation & Quantitative Analysis, New Markets you will lead Corebridge’s New Markets view of what life and annuity business is worth. You will value each theoretical and real international opportunity on a consistent basis, so that the decision to strategically enter new markets has a strong foundational business case and transaction work has an analytical basis. At its core, this is discounted cash flow valuation of complex insurance liabilities: deeply understanding and driving any improvements needed in liability and asset cash flow projections, reflecting capital and collateral, and turning them into a clear, well-supported value. You will have the freedom to build the models and tools this work requires, drawing on best practices and partnering closely with Corporate Actuarial, Pricing, and other actuarial teams. This role is for a hands-on technical leader: someone who does the core analysis personally, sets the standard for others, and uses AI tools to work faster and at greater scale. The Pricing team signs the pricing opinion and the reinsurance teams negotiate treaties. Your job is to make sure every decision rests on a rigorous view of value before negotiation Responsibilities Opportunity Valuation & Transaction Support Value each prospective international opportunity set on a consistent basis, showing value, key drivers, sensitivities, and how value might translate to target prices. Build transaction-grade discounted cash flow valuations that reflect required capital, collateral, reserve credit, tax, and cost of capital. Translate a local market’s statutory, GAAP, or solvency view into Corebridge’s basis, and quantify the capital and reserve-credit effects of different structures. Act as an internal consultant on deals. As appropriate, represent Corebridge’s technical position in meetings with ceding companies’ actuaries, consulting appraisal teams, and advisers. Post-Close Look-Backs & Deal Insight Re-value each closed block at 12 and 24 months on the same basis used at signing, and explain differences from the original deal case. Keep the Assumed and Ceded Reinsurance teams informed on how each deal is evolving, including emerging experience, changes in value drivers, and early signals for treaty management, recapture, or follow-on opportunities. Share look-back findings with assumption-setting teams. Models, Collaboration & Governance Build and own the New Markets valuation models and tools, choosing the approach that best fits each opportunity, while drawing on industry best practices and on the models, assumptions, and data of Corporate Actuarial, Pricing, and other actuarial teams where useful. Make sure work is documented, follows Corebridge standards, and is independently validated before business cases are presented. Protect commercially sensitive results with appropriate access controls and information barriers. AI-Enabled Analytics Use AI tools hands-on for data preparation, document extraction, reconciliations, and drafting, with human review of every output. Technical Leadership Do the core analysis personally while setting standards for clear, reproducible work. Guide and develop analysts and future valuation hires, and share expertise across Corebridge. Join selected market visits and client meetings where valuation depth is needed. Other duties as assigned. Please note this is not an exhaustive list of all responsibilities of the position, and the requirements of the job may change in line with business needs. Skills And Qualifications Required Fellow of the Society of Actuaries (FSA), Fellow of the Institute and Faculty of Actuaries (FIA), or equivalent; or, for candidates from investment banking or corporate development, equivalent depth in insurance valuation. 10+ years of experience in life and annuity valuation, M&A, or appraisal work at an insurer, reinsurer, consulting firm, or investment bank. Has valued in-force blocks or insurance companies in completed transactions, for a buyer or a seller. Deep expertise in discounted cash flow valuation of insurance liabilities, including capital and cost-of-capital effects, and related methods such as embedded value and appraisal value. Strong working knowledge of U.S. GAAP (LDTI) and U.S. statutory reserving. Solid understanding of reinsurance structures (coinsurance, modified coinsurance, funds withheld, collateral) and their capital effects. Advanced financial modeling skills, including experience with an actuarial projection platform (e.g., AXIS, Prophet, or MG-ALFA) or equivalent. Demonstrated use of AI tools to improve the speed or quality of analysis. Track record of leading technical work while guiding others, and of explaining complex valuations clearly to senior leaders and counterparties. Preferred Investment banking experience covering insurance, or corporate development experience at an insurer or reinsurer. Working knowledge of a non-U.S. regime. Familiarity with the Bermuda economic balance sheet framework or experience at a Bermuda reinsurer. Awareness of current U.S. regulatory focus on asset-intensive reinsurance (e.g., VM-22, Actuarial Guideline 55) Python and SQL skills. CFA or MBA. Business proficiency in another language.

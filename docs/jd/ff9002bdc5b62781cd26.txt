About the role: This Senior Software Engineer role is a core individual contributor on one of three Scrum teams within the Peer Community product organization in Digital Product Technology (DPT). You will own feature delivery end-to-end, contribute to technical design, and help raise the overall quality and capability of your team. Gartner Peer Community is a peer-driven platform where thousands of verified enterprise leaders connect to share knowledge, ask questions, and provide peer feedback that helps inform technology and business decisions. What you will do: Design and build high-quality features end-to-end across web, and backend platforms with a focus on reliability, performance, and maintainability. Take ownership of stories and epics from refinement through delivery, driving clarity on scope and acceptance criteria. Participate actively in technical design discussions and contribute meaningful input on architecture and implementation approaches. Write well-tested, well-documented code and hold a high bar in code review. Contribute to observability, alerting, and on-call practices to keep production systems healthy. Support engineers on the team, sharing knowledge and helping others grow. Collaborate with Product and Design to refine requirements and surface technical constraints early. Collaborate with principal and lead software engineers and engineering managers to define technical tasks for user stories leading to efficient technical refinement and delivery. Identify opportunities to reduce technical debt and improve engineering tooling and processes. Work closely with DevOps to design, provision, and maintain cloud infrastructure on AWS using Terraform and other infrastructure-as-code tools. What you'll need: Must have: 4+ years of professional software engineering experience building production systems, including rich experience in end-to-end system design and delivery. Strong proficiency in Go (Golang) for backend development and React/Next.js for UI development; must have hands-on full stack engineering experience. Significant working experience with AWS cloud services and infrastructure-as-code using Terraform. Solid understanding of software design principles, testing practices, and delivery fundamentals. Experience contributing to production systems with observability, incident response, and on-call practices. Clear written and verbal communication with teammates, product managers, and stakeholders. Nice to have: Prior experience in distributed systems, high-traffic services, or data-intensive platforms. Familiarity with agile/scrum delivery models.

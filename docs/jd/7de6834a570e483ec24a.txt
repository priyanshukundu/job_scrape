Job Description The CT Engineering organization at GE HealthCare Japan is one of the key product development sites within GE HealthCare’s global CT organization. As a Staff Software Engineer in CT Image Reconstruction, this role provides technical leadership for the software and subsystems that integrate image reconstruction algorithms into products and consistently generate high-quality CT images. Software We Develop CT Image Reconstruction is a core product area that transforms acquired scan data into images used for diagnosis. Based on their area of expertise, the successful candidate will contribute to and lead software development in areas such as: · Image reconstruction software that executes CT image reconstruction algorithms in the product · Applications for image correction, image-quality improvement, image analysis, and post-processing · Software that optimizes image quality, reconstruction time, throughput, and resource utilization · Services, interfaces, virtualization, and container platforms supporting image reconstruction applications Key Responsibilities · Understand clinical, image-quality, product, and system needs and translate them into image reconstruction subsystem and software requirements and designs. · Define technical direction and software architecture across components and algorithms, and lead their introduction into new products and platforms. · Evaluate technical options based on image quality, performance, reliability, maintainability, scalability, and development cost, and lead key technical decisions. · Contribute directly to design, implementation, and performance analysis when needed, and lead root-cause identification and resolution of complex image reconstruction and system-level issues. · Collaborate with Image Quality & Algorithm, Software, Systems, Clinical, and other teams in Japan and globally, providing technical leadership for algorithm productization, system integration, and customer issue resolution. · Support the growth of engineers and strengthen the technical capabilities of the team through technical reviews, knowledge sharing, and mentoring.

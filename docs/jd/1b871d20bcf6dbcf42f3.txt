We are looking for a Senior Data Engineer to build and extend the framework and tooling that our telemetry and analytics run on: collection, transformation, aggregation, access and visualization. The aim is for product managers, project managers and engineers to answer most of their own analytics and research questions without going through a specialist. Over the past three years we have standardized how telemetry and product data are collected, made that data available for analysis in UI tools (Kibana, Tableau), and taught our product and project managers to use those tools and analyze data themselves. You will also take on the analyses that self-service cannot reach: how our services operate, perform and stay stable, and how our features are actually used. You will be part of A Telemetry/Data team that works closely with product owners and development teams. You will own the tools and pipelines that collect and expose data across the department, and you will manage two data engineers working on data collection, processing and analytics.

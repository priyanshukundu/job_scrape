About this role: Wells Fargo Credit Cards is seeking a Senior Lead Business Execution Consultant to support the continued transformation of the Branded Credit Card business. This role will lead complex business execution, risk and control, change governance, audit, and regulatory initiatives designed to strengthen the control environment and advance key priorities across Consumer Credit Cards. This role will work across business and risk functions to evolve governance processes, establish structure around complex initiatives, and drive work from strategy through execution. The Senior Lead Business Execution Consultant will partner closely with senior leaders and stakeholders across the business, Credit Risk, Controls, Independent Risk Management, Compliance, Technology, Operations, and other functions to assess risks, drive change, and ensure effective execution. The role requires strong business judgment, executive communication and storytelling, analytical capabilities, and the ability to influence and create alignment across a highly matrixed organization.

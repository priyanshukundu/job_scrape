The impact you will have in this role: The SDET family is responsible for creating, designing, deploying, and supporting applications, programs, and software solutions. This may include new development, maintenance, automation, enhancement, and modernization of software products used internally and externally across DTCC platforms. As a Software Development Engineer in Test (SDET) , you will help drive quality engineering practices across strategic initiatives and enterprise delivery streams. In this role, you will contribute to functional and automated testing, support shift-left quality practices, maintain end-to-end traceability, and validate complex workflows spanning user interfaces, APIs, databases, regulatory reporting, file-based ingestion, and cross-system integrations. You will partner closely with Software Engineers, Product Owners, Scrum teams, and partner platforms to understand complex business and regulatory requirements, identify quality issues earlier in the software development lifecycle, and ensure comprehensive test coverage. The role includes backend and database validation, structured file and XML testing, automation, regulatory and reporting validation, and end-to-end data verification across integrated systems. Your Primary Responsibilities: Apply software development and testing methodologies, tools, and technologies to validate business, functional, technical, and regulatory requirements across enterprise applications and platforms. Design, execute, and maintain manual and automated tests across the delivery lifecycle, including functional, regression, integration, system, exploratory, and automation-assisted testing. Partner with Software Engineers and Product Owners during refinement, story grooming, and scenario walkthroughs to clarify requirements, identify ambiguities and dependencies early, and improve testability before code completion. Perform backend and database testing, including SQL-based data validation, to verify data accuracy, completeness, business rules, transformations, and application outputs and troubleshoot data discrepancies. Validate API, UI, database, and cross-system behavior and perform end-to-end testing across integrated enterprise applications and platforms. Perform file-based and structured data validation, including XML and CSV processing, schema/XSD validation, field mapping, transformations, report verification, ACK/NACK handling, error validation, and simulator-driven testing. Perform end-to-end data validation and reconciliation across source data, database records, file outputs, transformations, regulatory reports, and downstream systems to identify inconsistencies and ensure data accuracy. Develop, maintain, troubleshoot, and enhance automated tests using Java and existing automation frameworks while identifying opportunities to expand automated coverage and reduce manual testing. Support regulatory and control-focused testing, including validation of transaction and reporting accuracy, reporting timeliness, privacy and data masking, entitlements, auditability, portal access, and downstream impacts. Maintain end-to-end traceability from user stories and acceptance criteria through test scenarios, test cases, automation assets, execution evidence, and defect tracking using tools such as JIRA and ALM/AQM. Contribute to continuous testing and release readiness by integrating automated checks with CI/CD pipelines and supporting smoke, sanity, regression, defect investigation, troubleshooting, and issue triage. Align risk and control processes into day-to-day responsibilities to monitor and mitigate risk, escalate issues appropriately, and support secure and compliant software delivery. **NOTE: The Primary Responsibilities of this role are not limited to the details above. ** Qualifications: Minimum of 7 years of overall related technology experience, including 4+ years of hands-on software testing, quality engineering, and test automation experience.

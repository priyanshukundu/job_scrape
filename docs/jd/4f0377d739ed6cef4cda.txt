Associate Director Product Management – Virtual Care
Chewy
Location: Bellevue, WA; Boston, MA
Link: https://chewy.wd5.myworkdayjobs.com/External/job/Bellevue-WA/Associate-Director-Product-Management---Virtual-Care_R31535

Job Description: Our Opportunity: Chewy's mission is to be the most trusted and convenient online destination for pet parents and partners everywhere. We are obsessed with meeting the needs of pets and pet parents and exceeding customer expectations through every interaction. Within Chewy, the Chewy Health team is focused on redefining how pet parents care for their pets and how veterinarians support them. We are on an exciting journey to build a comprehensive suite of virtual care solutions that deeply engage and interconnect vets and pet parents, making it easier for pets of all shapes and sizes to lead healthy, happy lives. To support our mission, we are seeking an Associate Director, Product Management – Virtual Care to join the Chewy Healthcare organization in Seattle, WA or Boston, MA . This high-profile individual-contributor role will be pivotal in defining and executing the vision for the technical infrastructure needed to support the scaling of our Virtual Care (Telehealth) platform. The role requires you to operate autonomously, bringing structure to ambiguous problems, influencing senior leaders, and balancing near-term customer learning with Chewy’s long-term product and technology strategy. You will collaborate cross-functionally with teams across Product, Design, Technology, Merchandising, Customer Service, Operations, and Executive teams to create, build, and execute product vision and roadmap. If you are a technically fluent, customer-obsessed product manager with a history of delivering impactful solutions, join us to shape the future of pet healthcare. What You’ll Do · Product Strategy and Vision: Own the end-to-end product strategy and roadmap to drive platform interoperability with the Chewy healthcare ecosystem, ensuring reliable, performant, and secure integrations across internal and external systems · Customer Discovery and Validation: Combine quantitative analysis, qualitative research, and customer feedback to anticipate needs that may not be readily apparent from existing data. Design pilots and experiments that validate customer value, operational feasibility, and business impact. · Roadmap Development and Prioritization: Develop and apply a prioritization framework based on customer impact, business value, complexity, feasibility, and economics. Manage tradeoffs, dependencies, critical paths, and product risks. · Cross-Functional Product Delivery: Mobilize a virtual team across Design, Engineering, Data Science, Analytics, Customer Care, Operations, Finance, Legal, Marketing, and other business teams to move initiatives from concept through launch and optimization. · Scalable Product Architecture: Partner with technical teams to determine where existing Chewy capabilities can be reused, extended, or connected and where new investments are required. Review customer and technical designs, identify risks, and proactively drive mitigation. · Executive Communication and Alignment: Write crisp, data-driven product documents—including vision documents, PRFAQs, business cases, and roadmap recommendations—that clarify customer needs, business requirements, and decisions for senior leadership. · Measurement and Optimization: Establish success metrics across adoption, customer experience, revenue, cost to serve, and operational performance. Use results to recommend whether solutions should scale, evolve, or stop. · Product Management Excellence: Strengthen product-development mechanisms, experimentation practices, delivery quality, and execution velocity across the initiative. Bonus Experience launching new technology products or services in virtual care, telemedicine, digital health, or medical practices. Experience working with healthcare data standards (e.g., HL7/FHIR, SNOMED, NCPDP), EHRs/EMRs (e.g., Epic, Cerner), PIMs, or other veterinary clinical or medical systems. In-depth experience with veterinary or human healthcare delivery in the U.S.; clinical degrees (DVM, MD, PharmD, RN) and certifications are a strong plus. Strong understanding of the pet care or healthcare industries and emerging trends. MBA or equivalent.

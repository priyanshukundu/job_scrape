NBCUniversal is seeking a Software Engineer to provide L1 and L2 support for our enterprise Atlassian product suite, including Jira, Jira Service Management (JSM), and Confluence. This role will be responsible for day-to-day platform support, configuration, troubleshooting, automation, and integrations while helping improve the reliability and overall user experience of the Atlassian environment. Key Responsibilities Provide L1/L2 support for Jira, JSM, Confluence, and related Atlassian applications, ensuring requests are addressed within established SLAs. Manage user access, permissions, projects, spaces, workflows, custom fields, dashboards, filters, and other platform configurations. Administer JSM capabilities including request types, queues, SLAs, portals, forms, approvals, notifications, and knowledge bases. Build, maintain, and troubleshoot Jira Automation rules, JQL queries, webhooks, and integrations. Troubleshoot platform and integration issues involving GitHub, Slack, Microsoft Teams, SSO, and other enterprise systems. Support Atlassian Marketplace applications and coordinate escalations with Atlassian and third-party vendors as needed. Maintain platform documentation, knowledge articles, and operational procedures. Identify recurring issues and implement automation, self-service, or configuration improvements to reduce support demand. Participate in platform maintenance, migrations, cleanup initiatives, and user training. Collaborate with engineering, security, procurement, and business teams to support platform governance and operational needs. Bachelor’s degree or equivalent education, training, and experience in Computer Science, Data Science, Information Systems, or a related field. 3+ years of experience administering and supporting Jira, JSM, and Confluence in an enterprise environment. Strong hands-on experience with Jira workflows, permissions, custom fields, schemes, JQL, dashboards, and automation. Experience configuring and supporting Jira Service Management and Confluence. Experience troubleshooting SaaS integrations, identity/access issues, and complex platform configurations. Familiarity with Atlassian Marketplace applications such as ScriptRunner, Tempo, Qmetry, or Gliffy. Experience with REST APIs, webhooks, scripting, or other automation technologies preferred. Strong troubleshooting, communication, documentation, and customer-service skills. Ability to work independently on support issues while collaborating with engineering teams, stakeholders, and vendors on more complex escalations.

Job Description Serving as a hands-on technology leader for Raymond James’ trading platforms, the Associate Director of Trading Technology will help shape, deliver, and continuously evolve mission-critical trader-facing systems that sit at the center of the firm’s order entry and trading workflows. This role combines passion for the markets, strong engineering judgment, and disciplined execution leadership to build scalable, resilient, intuitive, and high-performance technology used by traders and financial professionals every day. The Associate Director will operate at both strategic and hands-on levels—partnering closely with Trading, Product, Operations, Architecture, Engineering, QA, SRE, and Compliance teams to understand real trading workflows, define practical technology roadmaps, and deliver improvements across the full order lifecycle. This leader will play a key role in modernizing legacy capabilities into a more service-based, API-enabled, event-driven ecosystem while protecting the stability, performance, and regulatory integrity of production trading platforms. The ideal candidate is a technically curious, passionate, and accountable leader who enjoys getting close to business, engaging deeply with engineers and architects, and helping teams turn complex trading needs into reliable technology outcomes. They will mentor and develop high-performing teams, foster a culture of ownership, craftsmanship, collaboration, and continuous improvement, and bring the energy to build great platforms—not just manage them. Key Responsibilities: Own the Raymond James Trade Desk Order Entry (TDOE) platform, providing strategic and operational leadership for the trader-facing application supporting equity and options trading across the enterprise. Accountable for the delivery, enhancement, stability, and long-term evolution of TDOE, ensuring alignment with business objectives, trader productivity, regulatory requirements, and operational excellence. Partner closely with Trade Desk leadership, traders, operations, compliance, product management, and technology teams to understand business needs, gather requirements, and deliver solutions that improve trading efficiency and client service. Lead business analysis, requirements discovery, and solution design activities for trading workflows, ensuring business requirements are translated into scalable and maintainable technology solutions. Drive the strategic roadmap for TDOE, identifying opportunities to improve trader workflows, automate manual processes, reduce operational risk, and enhance integration with adjacent trading platforms. Provide technology leadership across order lifecycle management, including order capture, validation, routing, execution, allocations, exception management, and post-trade processing. Partner with OMS, COE, Rebalancing, Operations, and Architecture teams to ensure seamless end-to-end trading workflow integration across the trading ecosystem. Lead modernization efforts for TDOE, including UI modernization, API enablement, workflow automation, service-based architecture adoption, and platform simplification initiatives. Ensure platform reliability, resiliency, scalability, performance, observability, and security while maintaining support for critical trading operations. Oversee delivery across software engineering, business analysis, quality assurance, production support, SRE, and vendor teams to ensure predictable execution and operational stability. Build and lead high-performing teams, including Engineering Managers, Software Engineers, Business Analysts, Architects, and QA professionals Establish and reinforce a culture of accountability, ownership, customer focus, engineering excellence, and continuous improvement. Communicate platform strategy, delivery status, risks, and investment priorities to executive leadership and business stakeholders.

Job Description This role advises the adjusters and team management on the obtainment of adjuster licenses, maintains information for the license website, and works with the DOIs on licenses. The individual is also expected to deliver compassionate service that is fast, fair, and easy, to ensure customer retention. Key Responsibilities: Work with multiple business partners to obtain applicable information needed for newly hired adjusters. Organize, prepare, and facilitate completion of licensing forms for new hire classes Review documents needed for candidate exam prep and licensing Assists with licensing requests for new adjuster licensing. Manage/maintain existing records through license renewal applications and continuing education requirements. Works directly with the licensee and DOI to resolve any non-compliance issue. Analyze, review and process documents to renew licenses and appointments according to specifications Provide direction to licensee to update demographic information. Performs complex processes that requires clear understanding and interpretation of state specific-guidelines, and/or standard operating procedures Report, analyze, and make suggestions to processes and procedures to improve operations efficiency, timeliness, and accuracy Qualifications: Experience 1 or more years of licensing experience preferred. Functional Skills Proficient in Microsoft Office Perform multiple tasks in a confidential, fast paced environment with attention to detail Ability to use intermediate written and verbal communication skills Displays advanced interpersonal and time management skills Critical Thinking Excellent customer service skills Skills Collaborative Development, Insurance, Insurance Administration, Insurance Credentialing, Insurance Industry, Microsoft Office Compensation Compensation offered for this role is 17.98 - 25.66 per hour and is based on experience and qualifications.

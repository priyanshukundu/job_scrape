Job Summary Projects and initiatives include, but are not limited to, Privileged Access Management (PAM), CyberArk onboarding and integrations, Safes and Platform Management, Privileged Account Discovery and Cleanup, Password Management, Credential Rotation, Reconciliation, Application and Service Account Management, Access Reviews, Compliance Reporting, NERC CIP requirements, Privileged Session Management, Secrets Management, and associated Security Policies and Standards. The candidate will be involved in all aspects of CyberArk initiatives from planning through implementation and operational support. This individual will serve as a CyberArk and PAM subject matter expert (SME), partnering with security, infrastructure, application, and business teams to analyze requirements, evaluate technical options, identify risks, and recommend the most effective solution. The ideal candidate will possess strong communication and writing skills, analytical thinking, problem-solving abilities, and a customer-focused mindset. They should be a quick learner, adaptable, detail-oriented, and capable of working with both technical and non-technical stakeholders. Experience with data analysis, account remediation, process mapping, architecture documentation, integrations, reporting, database querying, and Agile methodologies is highly desirable. Job Description What You’ll Do: Partner with CyberArk, IAM, Infrastructure, and Application teams to gather, analyze, and document business and technical requirements for PAM initiatives. Support CyberArk onboarding efforts for applications, service accounts, privileged accounts, and infrastructure platforms. Analyze existing privileged account inventories and identify opportunities for account cleanup, consolidation, remediation, and compliance improvement Work with architects, engineers, administrators, and application owners to design and implement CyberArk solutions that align with security requirements and operational needs. Collaborate with Security and Compliance teams to ensure solutions meet corporate security standards, NERC CIP requirements, and regulatory obligations. Perform data analysis and account reconciliation activities to identify orphaned, duplicate, excessive, or non-compliant privileged accounts. Create and maintain technical documentation, including workflows, process maps, onboarding procedures, system integrations, operational runbooks, and architecture diagrams. Analyze processes, trends, and operational challenges to recommend improvements that increase efficiency, reduce risk, and improve account governance. Participate in testing activities, including system integration testing, user acceptance testing, and validation of CyberArk onboarding and password management processes. Create reports, dashboards, and metrics related to privileged account management, onboarding progress, remediation efforts, and compliance activities. Work directly with business units and technical teams to coordinate onboarding activities, account remediation efforts, and operational support. Perform data extraction, validation, and analysis using SQL and other reporting tools to support CyberArk onboarding and cleanup initiatives. Assist with ongoing operational support, issue resolution, and continuous improvement efforts within the CyberArk environment. Additional Responsibilities: Gather and document business and technical requirements for CyberArk and PAM initiatives. Analyze privileged account populations and identify cleanup, remediation, and onboarding opportunities. Develop detailed functional requirements, process documentation, and onboarding workflows. Coordinate with application owners, infrastructure teams, and security teams to facilitate CyberArk onboarding activities. Support testing, validation, and implementation of CyberArk solutions and enhancements. Prepare reports, dashboards, and presentations to communicate findings, recommendations, and project status. Track onboarding and remediation efforts through Jira and other project management tools. Provide ongoing support and guidance to stakeholders regarding privileged account management processes and CyberArk capabilities. What We're Looking For: Education requirements are listed below: Bachelor's degree in computer science, information systems, business or related field of study; Or Associate's degree in computer science or related field of study with 2 years of relevant work experience; Or High school diploma/GED with 4 years of relevant work experience. Work Experience requirement listed below: In addition to any experience required above, 7 years of relevant work experience is required. Additional Rrequirements: Must be able to attain NERC access (U.S. Citizen or meet applicable residency requirements and pass background screening). Strong SQL, reporting, and analytical skills who is eager to grow within cybersecurity and identity security. In depth analytical, problem-solving, and organizational skills. Experience analyzing and managing large data-sets. Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills. Ability to work independently and collaboratively across multiple teams. Preferred Skills: Experience with CyberArk Privileged Access Management solutions. Knowledge of Privileged Access Management (PAM) concepts and best practices. Experience with privileged account discovery, onboarding, remediation, and governance processes. SQL and data analysis experience. Knowledge of Active Directory, Windows, Linux, databases, and service account management. Familiarity with CyberArk Safes, Platforms, CPM, PSM, and Password Vault concepts. Experience with Jira, Visio, ServiceNow, and Microsoft Office products. Understanding of NERC CIP, SOX, and other regulatory or compliance frameworks. Knowledge of Agile methodologies and SDLC processes. Strong attention to detail and ability to manage multiple priorities.

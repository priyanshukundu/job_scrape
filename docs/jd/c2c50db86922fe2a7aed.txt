Job Description The Role: We are looking for a talented and motivated candidate to join our team in our journey of defining and executing the backbone of our company's future—the next generation Vehicle Electrical and Software Architecture. The Mechatronics Infrastructure organization is looking for a Software Engineer who will develop test scripts, execute test cases on infrastructure software builds across a family of microcontrollers, triage daily test results, and collaborate with our software and integration teams in the software quality lifecycle from defect root-cause through complete validation. The role will interface with both physical and virtual network test assets. The system functionality in scope covers the data-link layer, gPTP, ARP, TCP/IP stack, SOME/IP, Service Discovery, as well as several custom protocols for dynamic network configuration. The role will cross-collaborate with teams working on embedded software, system specifications, virtual models, test automation infrastructure, and architecture analysis. This is an excellent opportunity to make an impact to the infrastructure software quality of a brand-new embedded vehicle platform while building expertise on a full-stack automotive Ethernet solution. What You'll Do: Develop and maintain Python scripts and libraries to automate the test execution of key networking functions as mentioned in the role summary Validate test scripts in HIL and virtual test execution environments Deploy scripts to the overall test execution pipeline Monitor daily test results, perform root-cause analysis, and debug In close collaboration with system engineers, develop and iterate on behavioral technical specifications that define system functionality and test scenarios Support software quality assurance efforts for key vehicle program milestones Use AI tools and automation workflows towards to increase efficiency in development, root-cause analysis, and triage Your Skills & Abilities (Required Qualifications): Bachelor of Science in Systems Engineering, Computer Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Computer Science, or related experience. 2 years of experience Advanced Python programming knowledge Working knowledge of CI/CD pipelines and tools such as Github, Jenkins, BlueFlag, and related platforms. Experience developing software, troubleshooting computer networks, and analyzing logs. Knowledge of some computer networking protocols, network architecture, and communication standards (examples include networking stacks like TCP/IP and SOME/IP). Knowledge of and experience with software development lifecycle and testing methodologies. Strong teamwork skills to collaborate effectively with peers, cross-functional teams, and stakeholders. Strong knowledge of Git and other version control systems. Use of modern AI tools What Will Give You A Competitive Edge (Preferred Qualifications): Other programming languages like C/C++ or Rust Experience with automotive Ethernet switches and networks based on 100/1000BASE-T1 and 10BASE-T1S. Advanced knowledge of the internals of the data-link layer components (for example, switch internals and communication software stack). Master of Science in Systems Engineering, Computer Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Computer Science, or related experience. This role is categorized as hybrid.

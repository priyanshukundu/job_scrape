We’re bringing together the people, ideas and capital that nurture progress and growth, helping to create a better world – for ourcustomers, our people, our investors, our communities, and the planet we all share This position is responsible for managing and analysing the risks relating to a portfolio of Alternative Funds, primarily hedge funds but also private equity funds, and supporting the ICG relationship management team in all credit-related matters. ICG seeks to secure sound, profitable and durable business for the Global Banking and Markets division from alternative and traditional fund managers. Together with other internal stakeholders within Relationship Management, the business and WMR, the analyst will participate fully in the collective management of credit risk and undertake ad hoc projects as requested. The analyst will be responsible for composing, reviewing and recommending credit reviews for fund vehicles that are more complex, highly leveraged or less liquid. Such funds are difficult to analyse and require a high degree of analyst skill to evaluate the relevant risk factors. The analyst will also be responsible for ensuring the appropriate documentation of all legal, compliance and credit risks in accordance with guidelines and approvals. The analyst will need a thorough understanding of markets and the alternative funds industry, and ensure that HSBC’s credit policy, objectives and priorities are fully met within the alternative funds space. As our Vice President, Credit Analyst (Hedge Funds) you will: Recommend credit limits in partnership with Relationship Managers, based on the bank’s risk appetite and the client’s credit risk profile. Maintain a strong understanding of Markets products and associated risks and mitigants. Conduct periodic interim reviews and maintain clear, up-to-date documentation in all credit files, including financial statements, financial spreading, credit applications, validation records, and internal and external correspondence. Ensure appropriate documentation of all legal, compliance and credit risks in accordance with guidelines and approvals. Review and assess clients’ credit risk ratings, including assessments driven by special events, based on internal and external ratings and analytical tools. Report significant business events, financial trends, industry developments and other relevant issues relating to clients’ financial positions. Use initiative and independent judgement when analysing information and making recommendations to WMR. Recommendations must be clearly supported in credit write-ups and defensible in response to verbal challenges from Credit Risk approvers. Complete all credit reviews, fund research and NAV monitoring within the specified timeframe. Reduce product cycle times, decrease aged items and improve productivity month on month. Identify and communicate priorities, escalating issues where required. Provide a highly visible point of contact for Relationship Managers and clients in relation to fund renewals and new client credit requests. Meet the reasonable expectations of the business in relation to these activities. Maintain close contact with WMR to review credit issues relating to the portfolio. Develop meaningful relationships with external clients and implement the client engagement framework. Maintain regular interaction with and receive feedback from key stakeholders. Engage with the business daily to ensure that all issues, queries and priorities are understood and managed appropriately. Conduct activities and daily tasks in a way that supports alignment with the longer-term strategy. Ensure the most effective processes are used to support business requirements and meet required controls. Identify issues and gaps, and implement actions to resolve them. Adhere strictly to compliance and operational risk controls in accordance with HSBC and regulatory standards, policies and practices. Report control weaknesses, compliance breaches and operational loss events.

Join us and transform the lives of patients while transforming your career. Associate Director – Molecular Design What you will do Let’s do this. Let’s change the world. In this vital role you will play a key role in determining the strategy and developing computational solutions to generate and prioritize design ideas for small molecule pipeline projects. This role demands a strong blend of business acumen, technical expertise, and strategic thinking to adopt new technologies and communicate best practices for use in drug discovery. The Associate Director will primarily focus on the development, deployment, and support of AI/ML models for chemistry applications. This position is a senior scientific role with significant leadership responsibilities and opportunities. Amgen is seeking an experienced and accomplished Associate Director to join our drug discovery efforts in the Computational Chemistry team. Key responsibilities include: Own the continued development of software packages for the iterative design of small molecules on drug discovery projects. Support standard workflows and capabilities for molecular design, including reaction enumeration and AI/ML generated structures with LLM. Collaborate with teams across Research to implement and adopt solutions for the management of model artifacts and compute environments. Champion innovation and continuous evolution of Amgen’s AI/ML landscape through the adoption of new technologies and industry best practices. Partner closely with computational infrastructure teams to deploy models for use across Research. Directly supervise and lead computational scientists. Communicate AI/ML strategy and team deliverables to executive leadership and other non-technical stakeholders. What we expect of you We are all different, yet we all use our unique contributions to serve patients. The experienced professional we seek is a leader with these qualifications. Basic Qualifications: Doctorate degree and 3 years of experience in computer science, cheminformatics, computational chemistry, or related field; OR Master’s degree and 5 years of experience in computer science, cheminformatics, computational chemistry, or related field; OR Bachelor’s degree and 7 years of experience in computer science, cheminformatics, computational chemistry, or related field; OR Associate’s degree and 12 years of experience in computer science, cheminformatics, computational chemistry, or related field, OR High school diploma / GED and 14 years of experience in computer science, cheminformatics, computational chemistry, or related field Preferred Qualifications: PhD. in computer science, cheminformatics, computational chemistry, or related field. A thorough understanding of AI/ML applied to the drug discovery process, from screening to late-stage optimization, as evidenced by relevant industry experience. Expertise in cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, or GCP), programming languages (e.g., Python), and modern ML/deep-learning frameworks (e.g., PyTorch). Expertise in developing and adopting novel algorithms and AI/ML models with LLM, as demonstrated by a strong publication record and/or industry experience. Experience with standard cheminformatics tools and software packages/ platforms to analyze molecular structures and SAR data (e.g., rdkit, Schrodinger’s LiveDesign, etc). Experience designing and evaluating agentic AI architectures, including tool use, orchestration, planning, memory, guardrails, human oversight, and production monitoring. Experience building and leading high-performing technical teams, developing professionals, and informing resource allocation. Familiarity with standard tools for structure-based drug design (e.g., docking, FEP, MD) Familiarity with DevOps, CI/CD pipelines, and Infrastructure as Code (Terraform, CloudFormation).

NBCUniversal is looking for a Staff Data and Analytics Engineer focused on the studio business. In this role, you’ll have the opportunity to shape and configure the SAP Analytics Cloud tools and SAP BPC within Corporate Technology Engineering. You’ll help deliver a dynamic SAP Data and Analytical capability that caters to the unique needs of Studio business sectors. Join us! NBCU has deployed SAP to run financial, logistics and HR processes. SAP Data and Analytics tools are used to run planning processes at enterprise and division level. NBCU Entertainment division has globally developed SAP solutions to perform planning & accounting functions of high complexity. D&A manager is to provide feasible planning/accounting solutions by creating necessary blueprint documents for functional requirements, technical specifications, design & implement planning and reporting solutions, review the work performed by engineering team members as per established standards & requirements. It is also expected that manager will review current standards and revise them to ensure best practices. Responsibilities: Manage the day-to-day operations of SAP D&A solutions for Studio group, ensuring system reliability and performance. Lead the design and build of SAC, SAP BPC and other SAP D&A solutions for planning, budgeting, forecasting, and financial consolidation. Build Data models for studios using SAP technologies like SAP BDC, SAC, BPC and BW/4 HANA Deliver effective planning solutions in SAC Planning . Design and build new SAC Planning solutions in SAC and migrate existing BPC solutions into SAC Planning. Oversee and coordinate SAP Products (BPC, BW, SAC) maintenance, upgrades, enhancements, and troubleshooting. Build SAC models, stories and dashboards Lead the triage and resolution of business-reported data and analytics issues, collaborating with cross-functional teams to identify root causes, implement sustainable fixes, and improve overall data platform reliability and performance. Implement security, access control, and governance best practices for D&A applications. Provide technical leadership, mentoring, and best practices guidance to the SAP D&A team. Troubleshoot and resolve complex issues, ensuring system stability and performance. Write technical specifications from functional specifications and build solutions according to the technical specifications Review design recommendations to ensure quality and consistency across the footprint Perform fit-gap analysis via discovery by engaging with business units and other corporate systems team members Review and/or participate in solution build and test activities on BPC & SAC implementations, rollouts, or upgrade projects; may be asked to step in to conduct project management activities from time to time Advise on solution roadmap for studio business projects considering current BW4HANA footprint for BPC & SAC solutions. Conduct comparative research and recommend strategies for future roadmaps for BPC & SAC solutions. Collaborate with peers in Corp Tech to apply best practices to integrate between SAP applications (S4, BW4, BO and BPC) Become the trusted internal advisor for key business users Requirements: Hands-on experience with SAC, DSP, EPM Add-in, Analysis for Office (AO), and BPC reporting tools. Work on data modeling, blending, and transformation to ensure efficient and optimized reporting. Integrate SAC with SAP BW, SAP HANA, S/4, and other data sources for seamless analytics. Strong expertise in SAC planning design, Analytical Applications, and Data Modeling. Manage and mentor a team of engineers directly reporting to you. Bachelor’s or advanced degree in Computer Science, Information Systems, or Business from an accredited institution or equivalent 7+ years of progressive SAP BPC and/or BPC Netweaver hands on experience leading up to Architect level work in an IT capacity implementing TV accounting and planning solutions of high complexity and large scale. Experience with Studio planning functions such as Planning, Pacing, Amortization calculations. Migration experience from BPC Solutions to SAC platform. Self-starter to work on multiple projects simultaneously with a keen desire to learn and expand depth of knowledge Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills.

The Opportunity As a dedicated Treasury Management Analyst (Mid-Level), you’ll provide bank account administration, liquidity management, and cash flow support to USAA’s lines of business. You’ll help ensure daily liquidity needs are met while maintaining appropriate liquidity levels to support USAA’s overall financial strength. We offer a flexible work environment that requires an individual to be in the office 4 days per week. This position can be based in one of the following locations: San Antonio, TX, Plano, TX, or Charlotte, NC. Relocation assistance is not available for this position. What you'll do: Coordinate opening, modifying, and closing of bank accounts, as well as online banking platforms. Maintain authorized signers, and Know-Your-Customer (KYC) documentation in line with regulatory and internal control standards. Review monthly bank account analysis statements, validate fees, reconcile balances or data discrepancies, and coordinate with bank representatives to resolve discrepancies. Maintain a centralized register of accounts and user entitlements, and prepare operational or compliance reports. Act as a liaison between banking partners and internal groups such as Treasury, Accounts Payable, Accounting, Tax, Legal, and other lines of business. Resolve complex issues and assists team to resolve escalated issues. Provide support of internal, external and regulatory audits. Ensure that control documentation is well-maintained, and controls are properly implemented within the team. Maintain external banking relationships and communicate with banks as necessary on bank account administration. Support users on security administration tasks for bank platforms and treasury management system. Ensure risks associated with business activities are effectively identified, measured, monitored, and controlled in accordance with risk and compliance policies and procedures. May perform backup responsibilities to corporate cash positioning function which meets daily liquidity, regulatory requirements, and maintain optimal bank balances. What you have: Bachelor's degree; OR 4 years of relevant education and/or experience. 4 years of related experience in corporate treasury, finance, or accounting. Knowledge of Treasury Management products and services. Proficient level of business acumen in the areas of the treasury, industry practices and emerging trends required. Proficient knowledge of cash management practices and concepts, banking systems and familiarity with financial analysis. Proficient experience in financial statement review with a working knowledge of accounting, financial and treasury functions. Knowledge of risk management and compliance framework and guidelines. Proficient experience with treasury workstations, cash management techniques, liquidity structures, compliance with line-of-credit agreements and other treasury related aspects. What sets you apart: US military experience gained through military service or gained as a military spouse / domestic partner.

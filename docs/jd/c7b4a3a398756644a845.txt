Job Summary The Financial Structing Associate supports the evaluation and advancement of capital projects by developing cash flow models, business cases, and senior presentations. Working closely with project development, engineering, and finance teams, this role models project opportunities, evaluate alternatives, and provide the analysis needed to support investment decisions. Job Description What You’ll Do: Project Evaluation & Financial Analysis Support the development and evaluation of capital project opportunities from concept through approval. Develop Excel-based project cost, cash flow, and economic models. Analyze project costs across engineering, procurement, construction, and commissioning phases. Evaluate project alternatives, execution strategies, risks, and key business drivers. Maintain project assumptions, supporting analyses, and financial documentation. Business Case & Investment Support Prepare business cases, investment analyses, and capital approval packages. Assist in developing materials that support project funding and capital allocation decisions. Executive Communication Create senior presentations and investment review materials. Develop concise, visually effective slide decks that communicate project costs, assumptions, risks, benefits, and recommendations. Present analyses and insights to project teams, business stakeholders, and leadership audiences. What We’re Looking For: Education requirements are listed below: Bachelor's degree in: Finance Business Administration Economics Engineering Related discipline Experience requirements are listed below: Entry-level to three (0-3) years of experience in financial analysis, project development, engineering, project management, or related business support roles. Internship or co-op experience supporting capital projects, engineering, finance, or business operations is preferred.

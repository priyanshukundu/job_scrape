About the Role ClearBridge Investments is looking to add an Equity Research Analyst to our research team with coverage spanning AI and Software companies to its New York-based fundamental research team. Our research process focuses on using bottom-up fundamental analysis to identify and take advantage of mispriced securities. This requires due diligence on industry- and company-specific trends, analysis of company-reported financials, as well as extensive modeling and valuation work. Our Analysts then use this research to make specific company and stock recommendations for the portfolios managed by ClearBridge.

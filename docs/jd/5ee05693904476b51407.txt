Senior Manager - USCS Data Risk Management
American Express
Location: Charlotte, NC, United States
Link: https://egug.fa.us2.oraclecloud.com/hcmUI/CandidateExperience/en/sites/CX_1/job/26016267

The Senior Manager, Data Risk Management will lead execution and continuous improvement of a scalable, preventative, and insight-driven approach to data risk management across USCS. This role will provide dedicated US-based support for business areas served by globally distributed Data Office teams, strengthening stakeholder engagement, accelerating risk and control activities, and driving timely issue resolution. Leading a team focused on risk identification, control effectiveness, and remediation, this role will play a key part in accelerated Risk and Control Self-Assessment (RCSA) activities while advancing the use of automation, Artificial Intelligence (AI), and Generative AI to strengthen data risk management across critical business processes. Lead data risk management activities across assigned USCS business areas, providing dedicated US-based stakeholder coverage and coordinating execution with globally distributed teams. Build trusted relationships with Business, Technology, Risk, and Data Office partners to align priorities, influence decisions, resolve dependencies, and drive cross-functional outcomes. Lead and develop a team by establishing clear priorities, coaching talent, managing performance, and fostering accountability, collaboration, and continuous improvement. Drive accelerated RCSA activities, including business process assessments, risk identification, control evaluation, gap analysis, and remediation planning. Identify and assess data risks across business processes, applications, and data flows, evaluating business impacts and recommending effective mitigations. Drive issue remediation and root cause analysis while strengthening preventative and detective controls, including data quality monitoring and application controls. Analyze risk and control information to identify trends, assess effectiveness, and communicate actionable insights and recommendations that support business decisions and risk reduction. Minimum Qualifications: 4–6 years of experience in data risk management, operational risk, controls, technology, or a related discipline. Demonstrated people leadership experience, including directly managing colleagues, setting priorities, coaching and developing talent, and driving accountability for results. Strong stakeholder management and communication skills, with the ability to influence senior business and technology partners, resolve dependencies, and communicate business and risk impacts effectively. Practical experience identifying and assessing data risks, evaluating control gaps, performing root cause analysis, and recommending effective mitigations. Experience leading complex initiatives across multiple stakeholders, managing competing priorities, and delivering results in a fast-paced environment. Demonstrated ability to collaborate effectively across geographically distributed teams, including flexibility to work across US and India time zones. Preferred Qualifications: Bachelor's degree in Information Systems, Data Analytics, Computer Science, Business, Risk Management, Finance, or a related field. Familiarity with financial crimes risk management concepts, including Anti-Money Laundering (AML), Know Your Customer (KYC), sanctions, and related business processes and controls. Experience supporting Risk and Control Self-Assessment (RCSA) activities, including process mapping, risk identification, control assessment, and remediation planning. Familiarity with USCS business processes, applications, technology platforms, and underlying data flows. Experience designing or implementing preventative and detective controls, including automated data quality monitoring and application controls. Understanding of Artificial Intelligence (AI), Generative AI, and automation, including their practical applications in business processes, data governance, or risk management. Experience leading geographically distributed teams and familiarity with enterprise data management, operational risk, issue management, or internal control frameworks. Employment eligibility to work with American Express in the United States is required as the company will not pursue visa sponsorship for these positions.

Research Scientist in Genetics and Tissue Analytics

Job Family : Scientist Travel Required : None Clearance Required : Ability to Obtain Public Trust What You Will Do : We are currently searching for a Research Scientist in Genetics and Tissue Analytics to provide support services and deliverables through performance of support services within the National Institutes of Health (NIH). This is a full-time, on-site opportunity in Bethesda, MD. Perform a wide range of complex procedures and techniques including, cell culture, molecular biology, DNA/RNA analysis, DNA/RNA isolation, DNA/RNA purification, DNA/RNA next generation (Illumina) sequencing, flow cytometry, gene cloning. Perform a wide range of complex tissue analysis procedures and techniques including confocal microscopy and image analysis, tissue processing for single-cell suspensions, fresh-frozen embedding and FFPE embedding, tissue sectioning and staining workflows, spatial transcriptomics bench workflow. Develop and validate new pipelines for sequencing and tissue data analysis. Conduct research including planning and conducting experiments and data analysis. Design and implement improved methods to existing standard next generation sequencing and tissue analysis protocols. Maintain accurate, daily records of experiments. Monitor advance in scientific methods through peer-reviewed journals. Troubleshoot projects when standard workflow yields unexpected results. Interact with students, post-doctoral fellows and non-doctoral research technicians on experimental design and laboratory-based methodologies. Manage the use and upkeep of the Genome Analysis Core next-generation sequencers, library prep workstation and Tissue Analysis Core instruments. Develop protocols into SOPs for use in training material. Train students and other scientists on laboratory workflows and use of laboratory instruments, including qPCR instruments, liquid handling robots, thermal cyclers, and tissue analysis instruments. Collaborate with vendor technicians to troubleshoot instruments in the event of malfunction. Calibrate and operate a variety of laboratory instruments under GLP guidelines and follow SOPs to perform test, analysis, and other procedures. Incorporate various bioinformatic tools for data analysis. Familiarity running established bioinformatics pipelines from the command line/Linux environment. Familiarity with R, Python and/or Perl. Interact with scientists from other organizations or institutions involved in similar or related research projects. Communicate and present project progress to colleagues in group setting. What You Will Need : Master’s degree in microbiology or related field Minimum THREE (3) years of genomics, molecular biology, tissue analysis, or shared-resource research laboratory experience; FIVE (5) years of experience is preferred. Demonstrated proficiency in molecular biology and Illumina next-generation sequencing workflows, including DNA/RNA isolation, purification, and analysis. Experience with tissue preparation, sectioning, staining, confocal microscopy, single-cell processing, and spatial transcriptomics. Ability to operate, calibrate, maintain, and troubleshoot advanced laboratory instruments. Must be able to obtain and maintain a Federal or DoD “public trust”; candidates must receive approved adjudication prior to onboarding with Guidehouse. Candidates with an active public trust or suitability are preferred. What Would Be Nice To Have : Proficiency in MS Office Suite. Strong verbal and written communication skills. Ability to train researchers, collaborate across scientific teams, and clearly communicate project results. Familiarity with Linux command-line environments and R, Python, and/or Perl. Experience with developing SOPs and maintaining accurate experimental records consistent with GLP guidelines, strongly desired. The annual salary range for this position is $74,000.00-$124,000.00.

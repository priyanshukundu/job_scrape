Responsibilities In this job, your purpose is to contribute to reliable, traceable, and responsible AI quality assurance by evaluating AI models, workflows, data, and quality assurance evidence. You help stakeholders make informed decisions by interpreting findings, identifying risks or performance changes, and recommending improvements that strengthen AI quality and QA practices. You will be primarily responsible for: Evaluating AI Model Performance : Assessing model quality, interpreting performance patterns, and recommending improvements based on evaluation results Monitoring AI Model Drift : Noticing when the AI model starts behaving differently, finding possible reasons, and recommending actions to keep it reliable Supporting Ethical AI Governance : Applying ethical guidelines, recognizing potential risks, and implementing human review controls for AI outputs, exceptions, or high-risk workflow results before action is taken Validating Data and Test Evidence : Organizing, reviewing, and interpreting evaluation data and QA test evidence to confirm results are reliable, traceable, and aligned with quality assurance requirements Supporting QA Analytics : Preparing QA analytics outputs, supporting informed decision-making, and translating evaluation results for non-technical stakeholders Improving QA Processes : Identifying quality assurance opportunities, proposing process enhancements, and supporting continuous improvement across established QA practices Where you'll work: This is a hybrid position which allows you to spend some of your time working from home as well as some time working collaboratively in-office. During the job training period you may have a higher percentage of time spent in-office to support your learning experience. There is a 6 day a month minimum expectation in the office. State Farm Employees who are already classified as remote are eligible to apply. Existing remote employees who live near a hub or corporate location may be selected for a Hybrid work arrangement. If selected, all employees are expected to travel to Bloomington or another designated hub location for 25% of the month (approximately one week). Hours of operation are continually evaluated and may change based on business need. Successful candidates are able and willing to work flexible work shifts and may be asked to work overtime and/or irregular hours. This position may require travel via commercial transportation and driving motor vehicles to any location where the company does business. Business travel is required to perform the necessary job responsibilities. Qualifications Competitive candidates will demonstrate the following: Strong organizational skills with the ability to manage multiple priorities and meet deadlines. Effective written and verbal communication skills with attention to detail. Accountability, initiative, and commitment to delivering accurate, high-quality work. Resourcefulness and problem-solving skills with a focus on process improvement. Ability to work independently and collaborate effectively with team members and business partners. Ability to learn and navigate multiple systems and technology platforms. Preferred Skills and Experience: Experience in data visualization and the ability to take complex data and deliver information to stakeholders in a clear and intuitive manner. Proficiency with Microsoft Office applications, including Outlook, Teams, Excel, and PowerPoint. Ability to collaborate with various functional teams to gather reporting requirements, ask clarifying questions, understand stakeholder needs, and translate the business needs into solutions. Proven ability to quickly acquire and apply new technical and business knowledge and skills. Experience using and incorporating generative AI or agentic AI tools into daily workflows, as both a creative partner and a productivity enhancer, while maintaining human oversight, verifying accuracy, and exercising sound judgment about when and how AI-assisted output should be used. Working knowledge of how LLMs function, including their strengths, limitations, and common failure modes such as hallucinations, bias, and outdated information. Ability to write effective prompts, critically evaluate AI-generated content, and apply responsible-use practices around data privacy, security, and intellectual property.

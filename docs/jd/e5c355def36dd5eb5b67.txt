What You'll Be Doing Join a team that plays an important role in supporting Financial Advisors and their advisory business. As an Operations Advisory Trading Analyst, you’ll assist with advisory trading activities, research and resolve trade-related questions, and help ensure requests are processed accurately and efficiently. You’ll gain hands-on experience with advisory trading systems and workflows while developing knowledge of trading processes, investment products, account restrictions, and problem resolution. You’ll collaborate with experienced team members and other areas across the firm to address issues and provide timely support to Financial Advisors and branch associates. Success in this role requires attention to detail, strong analytical and problem-solving skills, a commitment to excellent service, and the ability to manage multiple priorities in a fast-paced trading environment. This is an excellent opportunity to build expertise in advisory trading while developing a broader understanding of the financial services industry.

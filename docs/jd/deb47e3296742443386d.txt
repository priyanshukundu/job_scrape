Role Overview: Could you be our next Sr. Manager, Financial Planning & Analysis ? Globe Life is looking for a Sr. Manager, Financial Planning & Analysis to join the team! In this role, you will be responsible for leading business analysis, forecasting, and planning processes to deliver timely, high-quality annual operating plans, long-term forecasts, and quarterly revisions to executive leadership. You will build and enhance complex financial models, reporting tools, and dashboards to improve the accuracy, efficiency, and visibility of financial data, while providing insights that help executive leadership make better business decisions. This role also car for one or more Financial Analysts. This is a hybrid position located in McKinney, TX. What You Will Do: Forecast and report financial results on a monthly, quarterly, and annual basis using performance management software, providing analysis and insight on current and forecasted results and their impact on the business. Provide comprehensive variance analysis, including performance to budget and forecast, identifying gaps, trends, and opportunities, and recommend course corrections to functional department heads and executive leadership. Support the design and optimization of Anaplan's planning modules, workflow, and dashboard capabilities to engage planners, reviewers, and executive approvers effectively. Partner with the sales organization and other business functions to provide financial reporting, analytical decision support, and recommendations that drive more efficient and profitable results. Organize complex information in a strategic and compelling way using strong Excel and PowerPoint skills, developing mock-ups quickly and iteratively for management presentations, reports, and dashboards. Establish and facilitate the company's annual planning cycle, including developing the calendar, coordinating deliverables, creating checklists, communicating expectations, and supporting system readiness. Identify, recommend, and lead change management efforts in collaboration with business partners and the Office of the CFO to improve reporting accuracy and eliminate inefficiencies and redundancies. Evaluate current financial planning processes and tools, and lead the implementation or refinement of new financial planning and reporting software applications. Serve as an active member of project and initiative teams, challenging assumptions and offering financial perspective to drive outcomes. Set team priorities aligned with business needs and shape roles and assignments to leverage team members' capabilities. Work extended hours, as needed, during the week and on weekends to meet quarterly reporting requirements. What You Can Bring: Bachelor's degree in business administration, finance, accounting, economics, or a related field required; MBA or other advanced degree preferred. 5 to 8 years of experience in corporate financial planning and analysis, with increasing levels of responsibility. 2 to 3 years of managerial experience preferred. Strong analytical, organizational, and problem-solving skills, with the ability to interpret data strategically rather than simply report it. Experience applying a broad range of data and analytical techniques to draw conclusions and optimize financial reporting. Knowledge and experience with coding and other technology-enabled tools for financial analysis and modeling. Experience synthesizing detailed analyses into clear, actionable financial and business insights grounded in an understanding of business drivers. Strong time management and organizational skills, with the ability to set and meet objectives independently or within a team. Strong interpersonal and communication skills, including experience presenting key data and findings to departmental managers and executive leadership. Ability to work effectively in a dynamic, fast-paced environment with strong project management skills and the ability to multitask. Demonstrated initiative with the ability to work independently, identify issues, and present solutions. In-depth knowledge of financial modeling, budgeting, forecasting, and variance analysis techniques. Expert-level knowledge of Microsoft Office, including Excel, Word, and PowerPoint.

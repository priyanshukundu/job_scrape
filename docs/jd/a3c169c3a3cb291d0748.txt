Job Family : Enterprise Risk Management Travel Required : Up to 10% Clearance Required : None What You Will Do : The Associate Director, Enterprise Risk & Strategic Programs will serve as a senior leader within the Integrated Risk & Quality function, under Strategy & Transformation, helping translate enterprise strategy into action and prepare the business for the future by building scalable capabilities, programs, and ways of working across the firm. A primary focus of the role will be advancing Guidehouse’s Enterprise Risk Management (ERM) capability. Working directly with the Director, the Associate Director will help establish and mature an advanced, forward-looking approach to enterprise risk identification, assessment, monitoring, governance, reporting, and decision-making. A critical component will be strengthening the connection between enterprise-level risk and engagement-level risk management, enabling insights from engagement delivery to inform the enterprise risk view while embedding enterprise risk considerations more effectively into how the firm manages its work. The Associate Director will also provide strategic leadership for key enterprise capabilities that connect strategy, risk, quality, and delivery, including Engagement Hub and the Guidehouse Delivery Framework (GDF). More broadly, the role will support the Strategy & Transformation agenda, helping the organization anticipate and prepare for future needs and translating emerging strategic priorities into practical enterprise capabilities. The Associate Director will help take initiatives from strategy and concept through design, implementation, adoption, measurement, and continuous improvement, partnering across corporate functions and business leadership to make change sustainable. Key Responsibilities Advance Enterprise Risk Management: Partner directly with the Director to mature Guidehouse’s ERM capability, including governance, enterprise risk identification and assessment, monitoring, reporting, escalation, risk response, and continuous improvement. Connect enterprise and engagement-level risk: Develop stronger mechanisms for identifying themes and emerging risks from engagements and using those insights to strengthen enterprise risk management, while translating enterprise risk priorities into practical engagement-level processes. Prepare the organization for emerging risk: Bring a forward-looking perspective to ERM by assessing trends, evolving business conditions, and emerging risks and translating those insights into actionable recommendations for leadership. Turn strategy into execution: Translate enterprise priorities into actionable roadmaps, governance models, operating processes, technology-enabled capabilities, metrics, and sustainable ways of working. Lead Engagement Hub strategy and governance: Provide business leadership for Engagement Hub, including governance, priorities, stakeholder alignment, adoption, risk and compliance trends, issue escalation, enhancements, and continued evolution. Advance the Guidehouse Delivery Framework: Provide strategic leadership and governance for GDF, supporting firmwide adoption, integration with engagement management, enablement, lessons learned, and continuous improvement. Lead strategic and transformation initiatives: Take ownership of emerging programs identified by the broader Strategy & Transformation function and help move them from concept through implementation and institutionalization. Establish scalable operating models: Define governance, decision rights, roles and responsibilities, performance measures, controls, and operating processes for new and evolving enterprise capabilities. Drive cross-functional execution: Partner with business leadership, corporate functions, technology teams, and process owners to align priorities, manage dependencies, resolve issues, and achieve enterprise outcomes. Provide executive-level insight: Translate complex quantitative and qualitative information into concise insights, recommendations, and decision materials for senior leadership. Drive adoption and organizational change: Help ensure new capabilities become embedded ways of working through stakeholder engagement, communications, enablement, measurement, and reinforcement. Drive continuous improvement: Use data, risk trends, stakeholder feedback, and lessons learned to measure effectiveness and continually improve enterprise capabilities.

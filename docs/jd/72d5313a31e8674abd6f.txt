Associate Scientist, Viral Delivery Technologies
Regeneron
Location: TARRYTOWN
Link: https://regeneron.wd1.myworkdayjobs.com/Careers/job/TARRYTOWN/Associate-Scientist--Viral-Delivery-Technologies_R52881

Build our future together: At Regeneron, we use science and innovation to develop life-changing medicines for people with serious diseases. We are seeking an Associate Scientist to join our Viral Delivery Technologies group, supporting the development and implementation of an antibody-retargeted adeno-associated virus (AAV) delivery platform. In this role, you will support the production and screening of antibody-retargeted AAV libraries, helping to expand the company's gene therapy delivery portfolio while collaborating with the bioinformatics team and other cross-functional groups. This position offers the opportunity to contribute to a fast-growing, science-driven organization making a meaningful difference to patients worldwide. When & where: Location: Tarrytown, NY Work model/flexibility: Onsite Discover your role: Execute rAAV production workflows and characterize production runs Perform basic molecular biology and protein analysis techniques (e.g., cloning, SDS-PAGE, Western blotting, ELISA, DNA extractions, PCR, qPCR, protein chromatography) Work closely with the bioinformatics team to organize data across screens Optimize AAV production protocols to enhance efficiency and yield Collaborate with cross-functional teams to coordinate and execute planned productions and screens Participate in team meetings and prepare regular presentations for departmental meetings Thrive in an innovation-driven, highly collaborative, fast-paced environment Work rigorously with keen attention to detail while balancing multiple projects This role requires: B.A./B.S. with 2+ years of relevant industry or academic experience, or M.S. in cell biology, molecular biology, biochemistry, bioengineering, antibody science, or a related field with 0-2 years of relevant experience Experience with mammalian cell culture Experience with virus or protein production and purification Familiarity with molecular biology and protein characterization techniques Experience maintaining accurate, up-to-date electronic lab records Preferred: familiarity with lab automation, key antibody properties, viral vector production, and relevant research software (e.g., Microsoft Office, GraphPad Prism, Benchling, MacVector) Does this sound like you?

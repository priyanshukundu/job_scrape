Business Analyst, Scheduling & Trading (Allegro ETRM) Location: Houston, Texas, US | Practice Area: Business Consulting | Type: Permanent Shape the future of energy trading. Turn complex scheduling and trading challenges into smarter commercial outcomes. The Role At Capco, we’re looking for a Business Analyst with expertise in physical natural gas scheduling, trading operations, and Allegro Energy Trading and Risk Management (ETRM) systems to join our Business Consulting practice in Houston. You’ll work at the intersection of energy markets, commercial operations, and technology, helping clients optimize their natural gas portfolios, streamline scheduling processes, and strengthen trading and risk management capabilities. Bringing together your knowledge of physical commodity transactions, pipeline transportation, storage optimization, and Allegro ETRM, you’ll translate complex business requirements into practical solutions that improve operational efficiency, strengthen controls, and support commercial decision-making. This is an opportunity to collaborate with energy trading professionals, business stakeholders, and technology teams to deliver meaningful transformation across the energy trading lifecycle. What You’ll Do Optimize Natural Gas Scheduling: Analyze and support daily natural gas purchases, sales, transportation, storage, and park-and-loan transactions across interstate and intrastate pipeline systems, ensuring operational efficiency and contractual compliance. Leverage Allegro ETRM Expertise: Support business requirements, process improvements, and system functionality across deal capture, position management, confirmations, settlements, inventory tracking, and transaction reporting. Connect Business and Technology: Partner with traders, originators, risk management, accounting, contracts, and operations teams to gather requirements, document workflows, identify process gaps, and translate business needs into effective solutions. Strengthen Trading Operations and Controls: Analyze transportation and production imbalances, pipeline constraints, maintenance events, weather impacts, and operational disruptions while supporting reconciliation, exposure reporting, and risk mitigation. Drive Commercial and Operational Improvements: Identify opportunities to enhance storage optimization, transportation capacity utilization, scheduling controls, and portfolio performance through data analysis, stakeholder collaboration, and process improvement. What We’re Looking For Energy Trading and Scheduling Experience: Practical experience supporting physical natural gas transactions, pipeline scheduling, transportation logistics, storage operations, and commodity trading activities. Allegro ETRM Knowledge: Experience working with Allegro ETRM across deal capture, trade lifecycle management, position tracking, confirmations, settlements, inventory balances, and reporting. Business Analysis Capabilities: Ability to gather and document business requirements, map processes, analyze operational challenges, and collaborate with business and technology teams to deliver improvements. Commercial and Risk Understanding: Knowledge of pipeline transportation agreements, capacity management, production imbalances, contractual obligations, risk exposure, and P&L considerations within natural gas markets. Analytical and Collaborative Skills: Proficiency in Microsoft Excel, data reconciliation, operational reporting, and stakeholder communication, with the ability to translate complex trading and scheduling requirements into clear business insights. Bonus Points For Experience supporting Allegro ETRM implementations, enhancements, integrations, testing, or business process transformation initiatives. Knowledge of interstate and intrastate pipeline operations, storage injections and withdrawals, capacity release management, and park-and-loan transactions. Familiarity with natural gas trading workflows, trade confirmations, settlements, month-end reconciliation, and accounting processes. Experience working with energy traders, marketers, producers, utilities, pipeline operators, or downstream commercial customers. Exposure to energy trading transformation programs, operational controls, risk reporting, or process automation initiatives.

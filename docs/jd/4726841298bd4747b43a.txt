Director, Treasury Product Management
American Express
Location: New York, NY, United States
Link: https://egug.fa.us2.oraclecloud.com/hcmUI/CandidateExperience/en/sites/CX_1/job/26016443

The Director, Treasury Product Management is a key product owner role within the Global Treasury team, responsible for developing the Treasury product strategy for select platforms and capabilities, translating that strategy into a clear roadmap, and driving end-to-end delivery. This individual will partner across Treasury and Technology to identify key business needs and deliver scalable, controlled and insight-driven capabilities that improve Treasury processes, data, decision-making and execution, including through automation and AI. We are at a critical juncture of transformation across a number of our key platforms, and the Director will play a leadership role in driving that modernization. Drive Product Roadmap & Delivery – Help develop and translate Treasury product strategy and business priorities into the product vision, roadmap and prioritized delivery plan for assigned platforms and capabilities. Own End-to-End Product Execution – Own the product lifecycle from requirements through build, testing, implementation and adoption, ensuring delivered capabilities achieve intended business outcomes. Lead Agile Product Management – Own and prioritize the backlog and translate business needs into clear capabilities, features, user stories and acceptance criteria, partnering closely with Technology through planning, refinement and delivery. Translate Treasury Needs into Solutions – Develop a deep understanding of Treasury processes and user needs and translate complex business, data, regulatory and control requirements into scalable platform capabilities. Drive Data, Automation & AI Capabilities – Identify data needs across platform initiatives along with opportunities to improve processes, reporting, controls and decision-making through automation, analytics and AI. Contribute to Treasury data strategy and partner with Finance data product owners to deliver on roadmap. Own Testing & Business Readiness – Drive Product Owner responsibilities for UAT, business acceptance, release readiness and post-implementation validation. Embed Risk & Controls – Ensure regulatory, risk, control and audit requirements are appropriately incorporated into product requirements and delivery. Drive Stakeholder Alignment – Partner across Treasury, Finance, Risk and Technology to manage priorities, dependencies and product decisions and communicate progress, risks and outcomes to senior stakeholders. 5+ years of experience in product management, Treasury, Finance, payments, banking platforms or related financial services roles. Strong understanding of Treasury, Finance or related financial services processes and demonstrated ability to translate business, data, regulatory and control needs into technology capabilities. Proven ability to own product roadmaps and deliver complex enterprise technology or data capabilities end-to-end. Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with a risk and control mindset and focus on measurable business outcomes. Excellent communication and influencing skills, with the ability to navigate ambiguity and drive complex initiatives across business and Technology. Experience translating business needs into product requirements and partnering with Technology to prioritize and deliver capabilities using Agile practices. Ability to operate effectively in a highly matrixed environment, navigate ambiguity and drive complex initiatives from requirements through execution.

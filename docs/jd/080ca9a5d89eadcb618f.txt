Here, you will find more than just a job—you will find purpose and pride. Your Role at Baxter This is where your work saves lives. Join Baxter's Materials Development Center of Excellence (COE) and help shape the future of healthcare through advanced materials innovation. In this highly visible role, you'll drive the selection, development, qualification, and lifecycle management of polymers and materials that support Baxter's global portfolio of life-saving products. Working at the intersection of materials science, digital innovation, and AI-enabled technologies , you'll partner with cross-functional teams across R&D, Product Development, Manufacturing, Quality, Regulatory, and Supply Chain to solve complex challenges and bring innovative solutions from concept to commercialization. This is a unique opportunity to influence next-generation healthcare technologies while advancing materials that improve product performance, sustainability, reliability, and patient outcomes worldwide. What You'll Do Lead the development, qualification, and optimization of polymeric materials across Baxter's product portfolio. Apply expertise in polymer science, chemistry, and engineering to solve complex technical challenges. Utilize advanced digital and AI-enabled tools to accelerate material development and data-driven decision-making. Design and execute experiments, analyze results, and communicate recommendations to key stakeholders. Define material requirements and specifications that align with product, customer, and manufacturing needs. Partner with global cross-functional teams to support product development, manufacturing implementation, and commercialization. Leverage material characterization techniques including mechanical, thermal, rheological, microscopy, and analytical testing. Ensure compliance with Quality Systems, Regulatory requirements, and GxP standards. Contribute to strategic innovation initiatives and future material technology roadmaps. What You'll Bring Strong foundation in Polymer Science, Materials Science, Chemistry, or Engineering . Proven ability to independently solve complex materials-related challenges. Experience designing experiments, interpreting data, and translating findings into practical solutions. Strong collaboration and communication skills in cross-functional environments. Curiosity and enthusiasm for leveraging emerging digital and AI technologies to drive innovation. Qualifications Bachelor's degree with 5-7 years of relevant experience Master's degree with 3-5 years of relevant experience PhD with 0-3 years of relevant experience Why Baxter?

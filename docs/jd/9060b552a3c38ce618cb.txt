Labcorp is a global leader in laboratory services, providing the insights and answers that help healthcare providers, patients, researchers, pharmaceutical companies and health systems make confident decisions and improve outcomes. Through our unparalleled science, data, technology and laboratory network, we advance diagnostics, accelerate innovation and help address some of the world’s most important health challenges. As we shape the future of healthcare, we are leveraging advanced technologies, intelligent digital solutions and data-driven innovation across our operations to enhance how work gets done and deliver greater value to customers and patients. With our global scale and deep expertise, you’ll have the opportunity to do meaningful work, grow your career and make a real impact on people’s health around the world. Together, we’re improving health and improving lives. Labcorp is seeking a Software Development Engineer for a dynamic team. This position will support high priority core instrument middleware projects, that requires travel to sites across the USA. Work Schedule This is a full‑time, exempt (salaried) remote position assigned to a First Shift schedule, with standard business hours of Monday through Friday, 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. Business needs may occasionally require flexibility in work hours, including earlier, later, or additional hours, with reasonable notice provided when possible. This position requires 25% travel. Job Responsibilities: The individual in this role will be responsible for: Preparing detailed specifications for instrument interfaces, middleware platforms, supporting services, programs and/or applications. Designing, coding, testing, debugging, and troubleshooting these interfaces, services, programs and applications. Analyzing, reviewing, and altering programs to increase operating efficiency or adapt new requirements. Assisting with the support and maintenance of systems and applications. Providing on call support for production systems. Providing support during and post system implementations and conversions. Consulting with users to identify current operating procedures and clarify program objectives. Minimum Qualifications: Bachelor's Degree 5 or more years of experience developing applications for the Windows Family of OSs, using Client Server or Web based architecture 7 or more years of JPA/ORM (Hibernate), Junit, SQL, XML/XSD, JSON experience, Cache’, Oracle, Iris for Health 2 or more years experience and solid troubleshooting skills for enterprise level production systems, to include instrument interfaces, middleware platforms and Laboratory Information Systems. 2 or more years experience with and ability to develop within InterSystems Cache’/Iris for Health and Windows applications. 2 or more years experience with and ability to develop within Laboratory Information Systems, instrument interfaces, and middleware platforms. 2 or more years experience and ability to develop technical design documentation using Microsoft Word, Visio and other tools. 2 or more years experience and ability to perform data modeling and system configuration. 2 or more years experience and good working knowledge with CSP and Web Services. 2 or more years experience in Healthcare applications and systems. Preferred Qualifications: Current or prior Labcorp experience Master's Degree 2 or more years experience with Java Messaging Service 2 or more years experience with WebLogic/WebSphere 2 or more years experience with RESTful Web Services 5 or more years experience with Java and Spring and Angular 2.X or later version 3 or more years of experience supporting HL7 interfaces, EDI transactions, instrument integrations, or laboratory data exchange processes Additional Job Standards: Ability to take initiative to learn existing applications through reviewing code and analyzing data. Excellent interpersonal and communication skills (verbal and written). Ability to travel 25% to sites across USA.

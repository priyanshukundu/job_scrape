Position Overview StepStone Group is currently seeking a candidate with excellent quantitative, qualitative and interpersonal skills for an Analyst/ Associate position within our Private Debt team in Charlotte, NC or New York. The Analyst/ Associate will work in a collaborative team environment, monitoring the private debt co-investment portfolio companies and contribute to the management and optimization of the co-investment portfolio. The Analyst/ Associate will have the opportunity to develop and articulate recommendations on ratings and valuations of single positions presented to the Firm’s Valuation Committee and on portfolio trends and findings to the Co-Investment and Risk Committees. The Analyst/ Associate will further help other stakeholders of the firm responding to requests from investors and leverage providers. StepStone prides itself on its collegial atmosphere, encouraging team-building initiatives and a collaborative approach to accomplishing projects. The Analyst/ Associate will engage with colleagues at all levels of the Firm, from Partners to peers, and will have the opportunity to interact with many of the leading fund managers in the private credit industry. This role requires adherence to the highest ethical standards, along with demonstrated maturity, reliability and strong attention to detail. Essential Job Functions Interpret financial reporting prepared by portfolio companies and/ or fund managers, including financial statement analysis Assess the financial situation of underperforming portfolio companies based on reporting received and discuss these situations in detail with the fund managers Form recommendations for ratings and valuations based on such completed assessment Help senior Research staff in the monitoring of complex restructuring situations Support senior Research staff in identifying and communicating portfolio trends and findings to the Co-Investment Committee and other firm stakeholders Help develop and optimize monitoring materials and recurring processes Use creativity to analyze data and test hypotheses regarding the attractiveness of specific sectors and investment opportunities Education and/or Work Experience Requirements BA/BS in Finance, Economics, Accounting or another relevant field Relevant internship experience or up to 2 years of professional experience in investment banking, consulting, asset management, private markets or a related field Demonstrated track record of academic success Strong analytical capabilities, including writing and quantitative/modelling skills Other Desired Skills/Attributes Ability to represent the Firm credibly in written and interpersonal communications Leadership potential High proficiency in Microsoft Word, PowerPoint and Excel StepStone is a dynamic and entrepreneurial organization, and all professionals are expected to perform multiple functions Ability to promote and demonstrate a positive, team-oriented attitude Exposure to valuation and investment analysis through academic, internship or professional experience Exposure to corporate restructurings is a plus Salary Range: $100,000 - $140,000 The salary range is an estimate of pay for this position.

A Day in the Life This team is responsible for rebates, accruals, balance sheet reconciliations, and reporting for Cardiovascular, Medical Surgical, and Neuroscience Portfolios. The U.S. Rebates team supports rebate accruals, financial reporting, balance sheet reconciliation, and analytical activities across the Cardiovascular, Medical Surgical, and Neuroscience portfolios. The team partners with Finance, Pricing, Contracting, Sales, Credit, Information Technology, and Systems teams to maintain accurate rebate data and provide financial insights that support business decisions. As a Senior Financial Rebate Analyst, you will support rebate accrual management, balance sheet reconciliation, reporting, and analysis across multiple Medtronic portfolios. You will help ensure source data accuracy, investigate rebate and reporting issues, provide actionable financial insights, and contribute to projects that improve rebate processes and reporting. Primary Responsibilities Ensure rebate source data accuracy through recurring accrual audits and reconciliation across Vistex, ECRS, CMS, GSR, and related reporting environments. Prepare balance sheet reconciliations, identify outstanding customer liabilities by contract and settlement period, and support remediation of aged items. Develop and distribute recurring rebate and operational reporting, including credit, issue logs, membership management, and annual true-up reporting. Research accrual, rebate, and reporting inquiries and provide analysis and insights to business partners. Support quarter-end rebate activities, including accurate and timely loading and accrual of Bulk Quarter End Deals (QEDs). Partner with Contracting, Sales, Pricing, Finance, Credit, Information Technology, and Systems teams on rebate-related initiatives and special projects. Apply Excel, Power Query, Power Automate, Business Objects, Access, Visual Basic, and other analytical tools to improve reporting and rebate processes. Support rebate system enhancements, agreement intake, membership, accrual processes, and other team priorities. Required Qualifications Bachelor's degree with 4+ years of finance or pricing experience. Experience using Excel, Business Objects, or similar data and analysis tools. Experience interpreting contract terms and applying them to financial or rebate analysis. Experience with accruals, financial analysis, reporting, or reconciliation activities. Preferred Qualifications Bachelor's degree in Finance or Accounting Medtronic experience in finance, sales support, pricing, or analytics. Experience with SAP, Vistex, Business Objects, Excel, Access, or related financial and rebate systems. Experience with revenue recognition, accounting flows, balance sheet reconciliations, or financial audits. Experience interpreting legal terms and conditions related to contracts, pricing, or rebates. Experience supporting process improvement, system enhancements, or change initiatives. For Baccalaureate degrees earned outside of the United States, a degree that satisfies the requirements of 8 C.F.R. § 214.2(h)(4)(iii)(A) is required.

About the team The Mobile Development Productivity team makes building, testing, and releasing Stripe’s mobile applications and device-integrated software fast, reliable, and secure. The team owns the mobile development platform end to end, including development environments, simulators and emulators, continuous integration, automated testing, builds, and releases. We support engineers working across iOS, Android, React Native, Kotlin Multiplatform, mobile SDKs, and device-integrated software. Our customers include engineers working on Stripe Dashboard Mobile, Link, Stripe’s mobile SDKs, and Terminal. We enable mobile engineers to use AI agents throughout the development process, including writing code, running tests, and validating changes across mobile platforms. Our work reduces manual release effort, improves test coverage, and makes mobile development faster and more reliable. What you’ll do You will improve the platform that Stripe engineers use to build mobile applications and device-integrated software. You will draw on your experience building mobile applications to understand where development is slow or unreliable, then build tools and systems that improve the experience across teams and platforms. You will also help make AI agents effective participants in mobile development. This includes giving agents access to realistic development environments, simulators and emulators, test frameworks, and the feedback they need to validate changes. You will work closely with mobile product teams and infrastructure teams, and you will operate the systems you build.

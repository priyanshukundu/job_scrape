We are seeking a Lead Data Engineer to design, build, and operate secure, scalable, and reliable data solutions for Scotiabank businesses and enterprise functions. This hands-on role spans batch and streaming data platforms, data products, cloud services, data quality, governance, and production operations. You will own the technical design and delivery of an application, coordinate the engineering work of the contributors alongside you, and make sound design decisions within enterprise architecture and control standards. You will work closely with product owners, business stakeholders, architects, data scientists, AI engineers, and the platform, cybersecurity, and governance teams. The role requires strong engineering judgment, comfort with ambiguity, and accountability for outcomes from design through production stabilization and continuous improvement What You'll Do • Lead technical discovery and design for the application you own, turning requirements into designs and estimates within enterprise patterns. • Design, build, and test streaming and batch data pipelines and services that are cloud-native and AI-native, using distributed computing and a modern technology stack. • Own planning and delivery of your application, guide contributors through code and design reviews, and mentor junior engineers. • Own production readiness and support for your application, including monitoring, runbooks, incident triage, and root-cause analysis. • Build security, privacy, and regulatory requirements into your application and escalate control gaps. • Enable AI and advanced analytics with governed, permission-aware data foundations and contracts for machine learning, generative AI, and agentic solutions. • Apply responsible AI practices, including privacy, explainability, auditability, and human oversight, in the data and AI applications you deliver. What You'll Bring • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related discipline, or equivalent experience. • 6+ years in data, software, or platform engineering, or equivalent experience. • Expert-level proficiency in Python or Java, with strong engineering practices in testing, code quality, and performance. • Strong expertise in large-scale distributed processing, such as Apache Spark and Apache Flink. • Strong experience building on cloud data and MPP platforms such as Databricks and Snowflake, including performance, reliability, and cost optimization. • Strong understanding of data governance, lineage, and security tools such as Unity Catalog, Snowflake Horizon, and Apache Polaris. • Strong knowledge of open table formats such as Delta Lake and Apache Iceberg, including schema evolution, transactions, and interoperability. • Proven ability to independently lead technical design and delivery of a complex application and coordinate contributors. • Experience mentoring junior engineers and guiding code and design reviews. • Ability to communicate designs, risks, and trade-offs to business and technical audiences. • Hands-on experience applying AI-native and agentic development techniques, with appropriate review, security, and control practices. Preferred Qualification • Strong cloud engineering expertise across Azure and AWS, including security, observability, and infrastructure automation. • Strong command of data engineering design principles, including batch and streaming, data modeling, ETL/ELT, data contracts, and quality. • Production-engineering experience, including CI/CD, automated testing, infrastructure as code, monitoring, and incident response. • Experience in financial services or another highly regulated environment. • Understanding of responsible AI and model risk management practices in a regulated environment. • Ability to evaluate technologies objectively based on workload, scale, security, and total cost rather than Interested?

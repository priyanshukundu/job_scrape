Title and Summary Senior Software Engineer Overview The ABU team enables issuers and acquirers to securely communicate account changes for credential-on-file and recurring payments to merchants. This helps increase approval rates for card-not-present transactions, improve customer satisfaction, and reduce customer service demand. We continue to strengthen platform performance, resiliency, and scalability to support evolving on-soil regulations and growing global demand. We are modernizing the ABU platform and looking for a Senior Software Engineer to build and enhance highly available, distributed systems. This role provides the opportunity to solve complex technical challenges, contribute to architectural decisions, and deliver secure, reliable software at scale. Role -Design, develop, and maintain Java-based backend services using Spring Boot, Spring Integration, gRPC, and distributed system patterns. -Translate business requirements and user stories into secure, efficient, and reusable software solutions. -Contribute to technical design and architecture decisions that support performance, resiliency, maintainability, and system functionality. -Create and maintain automated unit, functional, and integration tests to validate quality and interoperability. -Analyze application performance, identify bottlenecks, and implement optimized solutions that meet service-level objectives. -Apply secure coding practices and established engineering standards throughout the software development lifecycle. -Conduct code reviews focused on quality, maintainability, and technical standards while mentoring junior team members. -Troubleshoot complex issues across distributed systems, complete root-cause analysis, and implement lasting fixes. -Support operational readiness through monitoring, observability, recoverability, and production support practices. -Manage individual priorities, deadlines, user stories, and deliverables. -Participate in Agile ceremonies, including daily stand-ups, story refinement, estimation, sprint planning, and retrospectives. -Partner across Engineering, Architecture, Product Management, Testing, Release Management, and Application Support to deliver quality solutions.

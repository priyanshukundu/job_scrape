About this role Company: BlackRock Financial Management, Inc. Job Title: Associate Location: 50 Hudson Yards, New York, NY 10001 Job Duties: Rebalance portfolios for flows, benchmark changes and client directed events. Monitor Corporate Actions Manager dashboard and process corporate action elections for team in timely manner. Ensure that portfolios are in compliance with their investment guidelines and quickly raise and resolve potential issues. Develop strong business relationships with Lead Portfolio Managers, Product Strategists, and members from Compliance, Operations and Client facing teams to ensure smooth portfolio onboarding process, new account activation and to improve day to day investment operations. Contribute to corporate credit alpha, yield and idea generation through security selection and substitution trade idea recommendations. Develop analytics, reports, tools and framework to assist in better security risk monitoring and performance attribution that can be shared with broader investment team. Qualifications: Bachelor’s degree or foreign equivalent in Business, Finance, Economics, or related field and two years of experience in job offered or closely related field. Must have two (2) years of experience with: • Finance and Risk Management; • Monitoring positioning/risk, identifying investment opportunities, loading trades, and ensuring portfolios for Multisector portfolios; • Fixed income markets; • Liquidity, and trading various fixed income products including corporate bonds, CDX, and securitized products; • Capital structure nuances, including Senior, Subordinated, and Hybrid; • Corporate bonds trade on the secondary market, including bid/ask size, portfolio trading, and market technicals; • Excel tools, including VLOOKUP, to efficiently synthesize and summarize large data sets; • Knowledge of Excel formulas and capabilities and automation of tasks; • VBA; • Python Libraries including NumPy and Pandas; • Tableau; • Bloomberg; • SQL Database, including Sybase ASE; • R Libraries including tidyverse and ggplot; and • Aladdin Portfolio Construction, Portfolio Monitor, Dashboard, ATX, TradeEntry, and Explore.

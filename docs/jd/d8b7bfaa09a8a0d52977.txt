We are seeking a Software Engineer to design, develop, and enhance technology solutions across new and existing applications, systems, and infrastructure. In this role, you will translate business and technical requirements into reliable, secure, and scalable software, contribute technical expertise to defined projects, and collaborate with Agile teams to deliver system improvements. You will have the opportunity to contribute to cloud and generative AI initiatives, including agentic AI for proxy account opening, handwriting recognition using generative AI, a digital assets platform on AWS, and a mutual fund proxy project management platform on AWS. Responsibilities Design, develop, and implement software solutions that address short- and long-term business and technology needs. Review business processes and system requirements to inform technical designs and development plans. Write, test, debug, document, and deploy secure, scalable, and maintainable code. Develop application logic for new solutions and analyze, modify, and maintain existing applications. Collaborate with cross-functional teams in an Agile environment to deliver application enhancements and system improvements. Contribute technical expertise throughout the software development lifecycle to support successful implementation and reliable performance . Qualifications: 3+ years of experience in software development and application systems environments Demonstrated proficiency in some or all technologies like .NET, C#, Python, HTML, JavaScript, SQL, and Angular Knowledge of AWS application development would be a plus Requires an understanding and application of software engineering procedures and concepts within the discipline, along with basic knowledge of related technical disciplines. Understands key business drivers and applies that understanding to deliver effective technical solutions and complete assigned work. Has no direct supervisory responsibilities but provides informal technical guidance and support to new team members. Resolves problems in straightforward situations by evaluating potential solutions using technical experience, professional judgment, established practices, and precedents. Impacts the quality and timeliness of individual work and contributes to the work of the broader team while operating within established guidelines, standards, and policies.

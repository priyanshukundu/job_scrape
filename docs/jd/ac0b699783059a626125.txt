Job Description Summary Serves as a liaison between platform delivery teams, business stakeholders, and platform consumers to coordinate, analyze, validate, and improve platform capabilities, integrations, and operational processes. Attains subject matter expertise in platform functionality, business capabilities, quality assurance methodologies, operational workflows, and enterprise technology solutions. Maintains documentation supporting platform capabilities and operating procedures while driving platform adoption, quality, governance, and continuous improvement across enterprise platforms. Job Description Responsibilities Reviews and refines business requirements, platform capabilities, user stories, process documentation, and recommendations related to enterprise platform solutions. Develops functional specifications, workflow designs, operational models, process maps, and platform capability documentation.· Ensures issues, risks, dependencies, defects, and enhancement requests are identified, tracked, prioritized, reported, and resolved in a timely manner. Works with platform consumers, business stakeholders, product owners, architects, engineers, and operations teams to identify required changes and business impacts. Defines and refines testing strategies, test scenarios, validation procedures, user acceptance testing plans, and release readiness activities. Coordinates user acceptance testing, operational readiness validation, implementation support, and post-release reviews. Communicates platform enhancement requirements, operational impacts, and business objectives to product and engineering teams. Prioritizes multiple initiatives effectively and may assign and review project activities completed by other analysts. May lead stakeholder workshops, platform reviews, team meetings, and cross-functional planning sessions. Communicates effectively with stakeholders to identify needs, evaluate opportunities, and recommend platform-enabled solutions.· Identifies opportunities for automation, self-service enablement, workflow streamlining, and operational efficiency improvements.· Supports platform adoption, platform usage, stakeholder satisfaction, and business capability maturity. Maintains governance documentation, operating procedures, training materials, and business process artifacts supporting enterprise platforms. Solves complex business and operational challenges through analysis, innovation, independent judgment, and collaboration. Provides leadership across teams, mentors analysts, and may coordinate workloads of others. Qualifications Bachelor’s degree or equivalent education/experience. Minimum of 8 years of experience in testing and/or systems background. Thorough understanding of operational procedures and systems. Preferred Qualifications Programming and/or IT background helpful. Experience working within Agile delivery frameworks, including backlog refinement, sprint planning, iterative delivery, and user acceptance testing support. Experience utilizing Jira or similar Agile work management platforms for requirements management, workflow tracking, prioritization, and reporting. Experience supporting enterprise technology platforms, shared business capabilities, and cross-functional delivery teams. Experience facilitating requirements workshops, backlog grooming activities, business process analysis, and stakeholder engagement sessions.

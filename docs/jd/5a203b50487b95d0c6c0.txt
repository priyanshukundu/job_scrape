Job Description As a Senior Data Engineer, you will design, build, test, and operate scalable data pipelines, curated datasets, and data services. You will spend a significant portion of your time coding, debugging, and optimizing distributed data workloads using Apache Spark, Scala, SQL, cloud services, and engineering automation. You will independently own complex components and well-defined data initiatives from technical design through production support. Working with product managers, data scientists, quality assurance, operations, security, compliance, and other engineering teams, you will translate business requirements into reliable, maintainable solutions that meet data quality, performance, security, and governance expectations. You will contribute to technical design decisions, strengthen engineering practices within your team, and mentor junior engineers. You will use AI-assisted development tools responsibly, applying engineering judgment to validate generated code, tests, and documentation. Digital fluency is required, including the ability to work with emerging technologies such as Generative AI tools—for example, Claude, ChatGPT and Microsoft Copilot—to support everyday work within approved security and data-handling policies. Key Responsibilities Partner with stakeholders to clarify requirements, define acceptance criteria, and develop technical designs for assigned data pipelines, datasets, and services. Develop, debug, test, and optimize distributed data workloads using Apache Spark and Scala, working with Hadoop, Hive, Kafka, and distributed storage technologies as required. Build and operate high-volume ETL/ELT pipelines for batch and streaming workloads, including orchestration, incremental processing, change data capture, historical backfills, and recovery procedures. Create curated datasets, reusable data products, and secure data services accessible through tables, APIs, or other approved consumption interfaces. Own assigned pipelines and services end to end, including implementation, deployment, monitoring, incident investigation, root-cause analysis, documentation, and technical debt reduction. Implement automated data quality checks, schema validation, and data contracts to improve the accuracy, completeness, freshness, and consistency of delivered data. Write maintainable code, participate in design and code reviews, and apply automated unit, integration, and end-to-end testing, CI/CD, and secure coding practices. Use approved AI-assisted development tools, coding agents, and automation to support development, debugging, testing, and documentation; verify outputs for correctness, performance, and security. Improve performance, reliability, and cost efficiency through Spark tuning, observability, workload analysis, and operational automation. Apply data access, privacy, governance, retention, and applicable regulatory requirements; partner with relevant teams on remediation. Mentor junior engineers, explain technical trade-offs, coordinate delivery dependencies, and escalate risks that affect architectural direction, security, or delivery commitments.

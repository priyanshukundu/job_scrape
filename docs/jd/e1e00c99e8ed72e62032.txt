We Do Consulting Differently Associate – Turnaround & Restructuring (Corporate Finance) Location: New York, NY BRG’s Corporate Finance practice is seeking an Associate to join our Turnaround & Restructuring (T&R) team. Our professionals partner with companies facing financial and operational challenges to stabilize performance, restore profitability, and create long-term value. As an Associate, you will work closely with engagement teams to perform detailed financial and operational analyses that help clients navigate complex situations such as liquidity constraints, covenant breaches, or underperforming business units. You will build and analyze three-statement financial models , 13-week cash flow forecasts , and other analytical tools that support strategic decision-making and stakeholder communications. Typical responsibilities include: Developing and maintaining integrated financial models and cash flow forecasts Performing liquidity analyses, scenario testing, and sensitivity analyses Supporting preparation of lender and board presentations, management reports, and restructuring plans Conducting research and due diligence to identify operational drivers and improvement opportunities Collaborating with senior team members to assess strategic alternatives, develop turnaround strategies, and assist in implementation This role provides exposure to a broad range of industries and client situations, offering exceptional opportunities for professional growth. Successful candidates will demonstrate strong analytical skills, attention to detail, intellectual curiosity, and the ability to work collaboratively in a fast-paced, team-oriented environment. BRG fosters an entrepreneurial culture where Associates are encouraged to take initiative, develop technical expertise, and make meaningful contributions to client engagements from day one.

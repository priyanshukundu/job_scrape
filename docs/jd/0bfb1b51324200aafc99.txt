The Difference You Make: The Sr. Specialist, Underwriting works closely with the origination team and credit risk management in reviewing and analyzing financial statement reports, conducting due diligence, helping structure the transaction, assessing credit risk ratings, preparing deal screen presentations, writing credit approval memorandums, presenting recommendations to credit committee, reviewing legal documentation, and monitoring and managing the credit portfolio. Participates in projects that help improve and streamline the credit delivery process. Prepares high quality, detailed credit approval documents that contain an in-depth analysis and an understanding and identification of credit issues and risks. Decisions loan requests in accordance with the organization's loan policies and procedures. Recommends proper loan structuring that limits risk. Prepares commitment letters upon approval of loan request. Orders and reviews appraisals and valuations (real estate, equipment, business, etc.). Assists all partners (closing, portfolio, and operations) in the closing of business loans. Participates in projects that help improve and streamline the credit delivery process. Supports department manager by supervising all personnel within the department in case manager is absent.

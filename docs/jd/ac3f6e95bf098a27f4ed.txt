Business Resiliency Associate Incident Manager
JPMorgan Chase
Location: Columbus, OH, United States
Link: https://jpmc.fa.oraclecloud.com/hcmUI/CandidateExperience/en/sites/CX_1001/job/210810000

Bring your expertise to JPMorgan Chase. As part of a growing resiliency testing organization, you will play a key role in helping businesses strengthen preparedness, validate resiliency capabilities, and identify opportunities for continuous improvement through meaningful testing and simulation activities. As an Associate Incident Manager in Business Resiliency, you will coordinate response activities for major incidents and disruptions affecting customers, employees, and lines of business. You will bring business, operations, technology, and corporate partners together to clarify impact, advance restoration, and deliver concise updates in a high-stakes environment. You will also strengthen readiness by supporting post-incident actions, playbooks, governance, metrics, and process improvements that reduce risk and improve execution. Job Responsibilities Support the coordination of high-severity incidents, including Priority 1 and major issues, by helping establish and manage business bridge calls and situation rooms. Synthesize fast-moving information into clear customer, employee, operational, and business impact statements. Draft and maintain concise, executive-ready incident updates that communicate impact, restoration progress, risks, decisions, and next steps. Apply sound judgment and escalation discipline to advance restoration while maintaining control requirements and stakeholder alignment. Monitor incident tools, repositories, and partner channels to support timely triage, routing, documentation, action tracking, and follow-up. Coordinate response activities for technical and non-technical disruptions, including technology failures, cybersecurity events, natural disasters, facilities issues, and third-party incidents. Support cross-functional resiliency initiatives by defining scope, tracking milestones and dependencies, and reporting progress and risks. Contribute to post-incident reviews and translate lessons learned into corrective actions with clear owners, due dates, and verification steps. Analyze incident trends, operational data, and business metrics to identify friction points and recommend measurable improvements. Identify practical AI and automation opportunities and partner with technology and data teams to support controlled, governed implementation. Maintain playbooks, runbooks, training materials, and job aids so procedures remain current, clear, and usable during high-pressure events. Required Qualifications, Capabilities and Skills Bachelor's degree in Emergency Management, Meteorology, Atmospheric Sciences, Security, Business, Operations, or a related field. One or more years of experience in crisis response, incident management, business resiliency, business continuity, operations, or a related function within a complex organization. Experience working within an incident management framework under time pressure, including threat assessment support, stakeholder communications, and coordinated response. Knowledge of contact center operations, customer behavior, and the technology ecosystem that supports technical and non-technical incident response. Strong documentation, action tracking, operational follow-through, judgment, and escalation skills. Project management and cross-functional coordination skills, with the ability to prioritize and manage multiple concurrent workstreams. Process improvement experience, including workflow mapping, root cause analysis, change implementation, and outcome measurement. Strong written and verbal communication skills, including preparation of concise senior-leader updates and facilitation across diverse stakeholders. Preferred Qualifications, Capabilities and Skills Business resiliency or business continuity experience in a matrixed corporate environment, preferably within financial services. PMP, PRINCE2, Agile, Scrum, Kanban, or ITIL certification or training. Lean Six Sigma Yellow Belt, Green Belt, or higher, or demonstrated delivery of measurable operational improvements. Proficiency in Microsoft Excel and PowerPoint, with experience defining dashboard requirements and operational metrics. Experience using data, AI, or automation to improve the speed, accuracy, or consistency of operational processes. Work Schedule This role includes weekend rotation coverage as part of the normal schedule. Primary working hours are Monday through Friday, 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., with flexibility as needed to support incident response.

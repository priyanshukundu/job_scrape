Your Impact Help shape the technology that powers Lowe’s Marketplace and expands what customers can discover and shop across Lowe’s digital experiences. This Lead Software Engineer will be a key technical leader within Marketplace, solving complex engineering challenges, strengthening platform reliability, and helping the team deliver capabilities that support a growing digital assortment. This is a senior individual contributor role, not a people-management position. You’ll lead through technical expertise and influence: setting technical direction, making architecture and design decisions, unblocking complex problems, mentoring engineers, and driving delivery across a distributed U.S. and India team. You’ll stay close enough to the technology to troubleshoot, review code, and contribute hands-on when the work calls for it. This team works in an AI-enabled development environment, using tools such as Devin and Codex heavily in day-to-day engineering. Success requires strong engineering fundamentals and the judgment to evaluate, troubleshoot, improve, and take ownership of AI-generated code rather than simply accepting its output. What You Will Do Provide technical leadership for a six-engineer Marketplace pod distributed across the U.S. and India, guiding technical execution without direct people-management responsibility. Set technical direction and provide architecture-level guidance for complex enhancements, new features, and production issues across Marketplace systems. Remain hands-on as an engineer by contributing to delivery, reviewing solutions, troubleshooting issues, and stepping into code when needed. Drive reliable engineering solutions that help Marketplace capabilities and product information move effectively through the ecosystem and reach Lowe’s customers. Influence and align senior engineering managers, engineering and product directors, product managers, business leaders, and adjacent Marketplace teams to resolve issues and deliver outcomes. Provide technical input during Agile ceremonies and help the team execute effectively within two-week sprints. Respond to production issues with urgency, understand downstream business impact, determine the right engineering or business response, and coordinate action across teams. Use monitoring, alerts, reporting, and operational trends to proactively identify problems and improve system health. Mentor engineers, raise the technical bar, and build shared technical ownership so the team can make strong decisions and solve problems without depending on a single person. Bring forward new technical ideas and improvements that strengthen engineering execution, reliability, and Marketplace capabilities. What You Bring Demonstrated experience leading through technical influence, mentorship, architecture, and delivery ownership as a senior individual contributor; direct people-management experience is not required. Strong communication skills and the ability to align technical and non-technical partners across teams, functions, and time zones. Deep technical problem-solving and solution-design skills, with the ability to think at a solution-architecture level when evaluating complex systems and tradeoffs. Strong hands-on backend engineering experience with Java and/or Kotlin. Experience working with MongoDB or other NoSQL databases. Experience with CI/CD practices and modern software delivery. Ability to diagnose production issues, assess impact, identify root causes, and guide teams toward durable solutions under pressure. Strong engineering judgment when reviewing code, including the ability to evaluate correctness, efficiency, maintainability, and operational risk. Experience working in Agile software development environments and guiding technical execution across sprint-based delivery. It's a Plus if You Have Experience building or supporting marketplace, e-commerce, catalog, product-data, or other large-scale digital platforms. Experience working with AI-assisted or agentic software-development tools and reviewing AI-generated code in production engineering workflows.

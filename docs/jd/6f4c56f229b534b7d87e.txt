Citi's Markets business is a leading global financial institution, providing comprehensive financial services to clients worldwide. Within this dynamic environment, our Markets Quantitative Analytics team plays a critical role in developing cutting-edge analytical solutions and models that drive our trading and risk management capabilities across various asset classes. This role offers the opportunity to contribute directly to the innovation and strategic growth within this essential part of our organization. We are seeking a highly skilled and experienced Vice President, Quantitative Analyst, to join our Spread Products Structured Notes Quant Team in New York. This pivotal role involves supporting the business's expansion into making markets on new and innovative structured notes payoffs. You will be responsible for the development of sophisticated hybrid term structure models and their robust, efficient implementation within our C++ library to support these complex financial products. This is an exciting opportunity to contribute to cutting-edge financial product development, enhance quantitative analytics infrastructure, and collaborate with diverse teams to deliver innovative solutions in a fast-paced environment. Primary Responsibilities of the role Develop mathematical models for complex hybrid structured notes payoffs, such as callable credit range accrual CLNs, leveraged notes, and other innovative structures. Design and implement robust and efficient numerical methods for pricing, risk management, and valuation of structured notes within the existing C++ library. Enhance and maintain the quantitative analytics infrastructure, ensuring high performance, accuracy, and scalability of models. Collaborate closely with trading, structuring, and technology teams to understand business requirements and deliver cutting-edge quantitative solutions. Develop mathematical models to support the cross-asset retail structured notes framework, specifically focusing on calculating pre-trade risk metrics as required by PRIIP regulations. Conduct thorough model testing, validation, and documentation to meet internal standards and regulatory requirements. What we need from you Prior experience in quantitative modeling or analytics, preferably within the financial sector. Proven technical proficiency in Python and C++ for model development and implementation. Expertise in market data, statistics, and probability theory to evaluate financial instrument risks and develop analytical solutions, and design numerical schemes for complex contracts. Strong grasp of software design principles and best practices. Solid product knowledge in investments and quantitative methods. Excellent written and verbal communication skills.

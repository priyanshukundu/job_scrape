Associate Client Management Enablement
Standard Chartered
Location: Kampala, UG
Link: https://jobs.standardchartered.com/job/Kampala-Associate-Client-Management-Enablement/1378693657/

Job Summary The role of Associate Client Management Enablement at Standard Chartered offers a unique opportunity to contribute to the bank's operational excellence within the Chief Operating Officer (COO) function. This position primarily focuses on strengthening the control environment by designing, governing, and continuously refining end-to-end processes, operational controls, and risk governance mechanisms. By ensuring that key operational activities are resilient, well-managed, audit-ready, and compliant with regulatory standards, the role directly supports the bank's commitment to safe and efficient service delivery. This role acts as a vital nexus between various departments including COO, Risk, Compliance, Technology, Audit, and business units to ensure alignment and shared ownership of control effectiveness and risk mitigation. A successful candidate will bring expertise in operational risk management and governance, paired with the ability to drive change and inspire disciplined execution across the bank. Emphasising automation and data-driven insights, the role supports enhancing process efficiency, reducing operational risk, and fostering a sustainable culture of compliance and continuous improvement. Key Responsibilities Process Design and Control Enablement Design, implement and maintain fit-for-purpose processes and control frameworks that support safe, efficient, and compliant delivery of business activities Translate policy, regulatory, and governance requirements into practical operational processes embedded within business workflows Ensure controls are proportionate, clearly defined, and aligned to business activities, risk exposure and control standards Governance Frameworks and Standards Establish and maintain governance frameworks, standards, and operating procedures that promote consistency, transparency, and accountability Support the effective application of governance standards across functions, including documentation, approvals, and decision-making forums Enable clear ownership and escalation pathways for process, control and governance matters Data, Reporting and Control Monitoring Implement continuous control monitoring approaches and automate evidence capture where possible Develop dashboards / MI for control effectiveness, issue ageing, incident trends, and operational performance Support oversight and assurance activities by providing clear evidence, reporting, and analysis on control design and operation Partner with stakeholders to remediate control issues and strengthen process effectiveness where required Regulatory and management reporting Vendor and third party coordination and management Effective execution and control of client account administration activities, including dormant and unclaimed accounts and account mandate management Continuous Improvement and Operational Excellence Drive continuous improvement in processes, controls, and governance practices to enhance efficiency, effectiveness, and resilience Leverage insights from incidents, reviews, and operational feedback to strengthen frameworks and prevent recurrence Promote disciplined execution and consistent application of controls across the organisation Stakeholder, Change and Capability Enablement Act as a trusted partner to COO leaders and operational teams, translating governance expectations into day-to-day execution Drive communications, training and adoption plans to embed new standards, controls and process changes Influence cross-functional stakeholders (Risk/Compliance/Technology/Audit) and major change programmes to align on risk outcomes and practical implementation Skills and Experience Proven ability to work effectively across departments, balancing diverse stakeholder needs and perspectives. Strong understanding of banking operations, financial risk management, and regulatory landscapes to inform decision-making and process design. Skilled in engaging and influencing senior leaders and teams to drive control and governance initiatives. Experience managing complex change programmes and promoting adoption of new processes and controls. Ability to investigate issues, perform root cause analyses, and develop data-driven solutions and improvements. Regulatory Compliance and Policy Development. Process Optimisation and Performance Monitoring. Operational Risk Management. Strategic Planning and Trend Analysis. Negotiation and effective communication. Experience designing and embedding end-to-end operational processes, controls, and governance frameworks that ensure safe and compliant business delivery. Proficiency in translating complex regulatory and policy requirements into practical, auditable routines embedded within operational workflows. Strong analytical ability to leverage data and insights for enhancing control effectiveness and driving continuous process improvement. Excellent command of business English to communicate clearly and professionally across levels and functions. Qualifications A background in financial services, risk management, or a related field is advantageous, with relevant certifications being a plus.

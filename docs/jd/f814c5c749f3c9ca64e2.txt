Job Title Manager - Global Accounts Receivable Accounting & Securitization Job Description Summary The Manager – Global AR Accounting & Securitization is responsible for leading a global team to ensure proper analysis, quantification, and accounting treatment of the company’s securitization program. This includes management of accounts receivable pools, unapplied cash, debtor concentrations, DPP calculations, reserves, program health metrics, and both internal and external reporting. The role also oversees development and maintenance of the company’s Bad Debt and CECL methodologies, ensuring compliance with GAAP and consistency across regions. This leader will collaborate closely with the Global AR Director, Treasury, Controllership, and regional finance teams, as well as the Transformation and IT (TDS) teams, to drive system improvements, automation, and cost-saving initiatives. Success in this role requires strong analytical and communication skills, a deep understanding of accounting and securitization principles, and the ability to manage multiple priorities in a high-visibility environment. Job Description Key Accountabilities · Lead, mentor, and develop a high-performing team supporting the AR Securitization Program and Bad Debt/CECL methodology. · Manage and enhance processes related to program rollforwards, sales, collections, write-offs, and footnote disclosures for financial reporting. · Oversee development and deployment of Power BI dashboards and Alteryx workflows for program reporting and automation. · Prepare and present reporting packages to internal and external stakeholders on a daily, weekly, monthly, and quarterly basis. · Ensure accounting accuracy for all securitization program activities, including reconciliation review, journal entry approval, and program health monitoring. · Partner with cross-functional teams to identify process inefficiencies and implement enhancements aligned with best practices and internal controls. · Collaborate with IT and Transformation teams on system enhancements and automation projects. · Present key program insights and performance metrics to senior management and service line leaders. · Maintain strong working relationships with Finance Leadership, Treasury, and business partners across global regions. Basic Qualifications Bachelor’s degree in Accounting, Finance, or Business. 5–7 years of progressive experience in accounting or finance roles, with exposure to securitization, AR, or related financial programs. 1–3 years of people leadership experience. Strong understanding of GAAP, SOX, and AR securitization principles. Proven ability to lead process improvement and automation initiatives. Proficiency with financial systems and reporting tools such as Power BI, Alteryx, and Excel. Preferred Qualifications CPA or equivalent professional certification. Experience in shared services or multinational corporate environments. Familiarity with advanced data analytics tools and system integration. Exposure to large-scale finance transformation initiatives. Targeted Competencies Strong leadership, problem-solving, and decision-making skills. Excellent interpersonal skills with ability to influence cross-functional teams. Ability to manage dispute resolution and escalations effectively. High customer service orientation and negotiation skills. Comfortable working in high-volume, fast-paced, deadline-driven environments. Organized and able to prioritize deliverables effectively. Advanced proficiency in Microsoft Excel, Word and PowerPoint.

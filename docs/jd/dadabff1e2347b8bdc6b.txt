Associate Director, Sales
Fitch Ratings
Location: US
Link: https://careers.fitch.group/job/Associate-Director%2C-Sales/1456930533/

Fitch Solutions is currently seeking a Senior Sales Executive based out of our New York office. Fitch Solutions is a leading provider of insights, data and analytics. It informs investment strategies, strengthens risk management capabilities and helps identify strategic opportunities. Its analysts, lawyers, journalists and economists offer in-depth views on credit markets/risk and individual credits, ESG, developed and emerging markets, and industry sectors. Fitch Solutions is part of Fitch Group, a global leader in financial information services with operations in over 30 countries. Fitch Group is owned by Hearst. By becoming a part of the Fitch Solutions team, you will join a group of colleagues delivering critical data, insightful research, and comprehensive analytics that empower clients to make informed decisions. You'll work in a dynamic environment where innovation is encouraged, and collaboration is key to developing solutions that address the evolving needs of global markets. With a portfolio of best-in-class, award winning brands, we offer you the opportunity to advance your career while contributing to a company known for its expertise and commitment to excellence. About the Team dv01, a Fitch Solutions company, brings transparency to structured finance through a data analytics platform used by more than 400 of the largest financial institutions. The Senior Sales Executive will play a critical role in expanding dv01's & Fitch Solutions' Structured Finance business, working closely with our Head of Sales to acquire new clients and drive adoption of our industry-leading solutions. This role requires an established network of issuer and originator relationships across the Asset-Backed Finance market, with the ability to translate those relationships into strategic opportunities and meaningful revenue growth. You will connect dv01's data, analytics, and workflow solutions to clients' business priorities, with a particular focus on expanding adoption of our Loan Data Agent (LDA) capabilities. You are a seasoned sales professional who thrives in complex sales environments, understands the persistence required to navigate long sales cycles, and is comfortable operating with autonomy in a fast-paced, entrepreneurial environment. You don't need a playbook—you create one. How You'll Make an Impact: Already understand the market and dive in quickly. Work with your existing network of senior relationships across Structured Finance, particularly with issuers and originators in Consumer Unsecured, Residential Mortgage-Backed Securities (RMBS), Credit Cards, and Buy Now, Pay Later (BNPL), to open doors, engage decision-makers, and generate qualified opportunities. Drive new business and revenue growth. Own new business development across a defined portfolio of strategic prospects, with a primary focus on acquiring new LDA clients and expanding adoption of dv01's solutions. Identify opportunities to bring data and technology innovation to an established market and translate them into measurable revenue. Own the full sales cycle. Lead opportunities from prospecting and executive engagement through discovery, solution development, negotiation, and close, maintaining momentum throughout complex, multi-stakeholder sales cycles. Sell integrated workflow solutions. Partner closely with Product Specialists and Product teams to understand client challenges and deliver solutions that address business needs across the Structured Finance workflow, positioning dv01 as a strategic partner rather than a point-solution provider. Collaborate across the organization. Work closely with Sales, Account Management, Product, and other teams to advance opportunities, incorporate market feedback, and leverage dv01's technology and commercial resources to maximize sales effectiveness. You May be a Good Fit if: Significant experience in Structured Finance, ideally in capital markets business development, issuer-facing sales, or selling data, analytics, or technology solutions to the industry. An established network of issuer and originator relationships that can be leveraged to generate and advance strategic LDA opportunities from day one. Demonstrated success originating, managing, and closing complex sales, with a track record of delivering meaningful revenue growth. Strong understanding of issuer business models, securitization workflows, and the commercial dynamics of the Asset-Backed Finance market. What Would Make You Stand Out: Ability to engage senior decision-makers, build trusted relationships, and translate complex business challenges into differentiated solutions. Strong commercial instincts and an understanding of the art of selling, including the ability to build credibility, navigate organizational dynamics, and influence buying decisions. Entrepreneurial mindset and a demonstrated desire to help scale a growing business through innovative client solutions. Ability to work collaboratively across cross-functional teams while independently driving opportunities forward. Superior professional presence, business acumen, and executive communication skills. Excellent written and verbal communication skills.

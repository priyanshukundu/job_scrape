Investment Risk Analyst HYBRID schedule in our Baltimore office 3 days per week About the Department Investment Risk Management plays a critical role in providing independent oversight of the firm’s investment activities by delivering rigorous portfolio analytics and actionable risk insights. The team ensures that portfolio risks align with investment objectives, client expectations, and industry standards, with a strong focus on liquidity, market, and concentration risks. Working closely with Risk Managers, portfolio teams and other senior stakeholders, the group delivers investment risk insights and analytical capabilities. How You Will Add Value You will assess firmwide investment exposure to key risks and emerging trends, including through in-depth analysis of market events. You will translate complex analytical results into clear, concise insights for senior executives, risk managers, and portfolio teams. You will use AI and data visualization tools to identify trends and develop actionable insights. You will prepare and validate data, identify patterns and anomalies, and improve the quality and accuracy of analytics and reporting. What Will Help You Be Successful in This Role Experience, Education & Certifications 4+ years of experience in asset management, with a focus on investment risk. Bachelor’s degree in finance, Mathematics, Computer Science, or a related field. Progress toward or completion of the CFA, FRM, or PRM designation is preferred. Technical Skills Knowledge of portfolio risk concepts and measures, including market, liquidity, and concentration risk. Strong proficiency in analyzing and visualizing data using Power BI, Python, or related tools. Advanced SQL skills to query, prepare, and analyze data. Familiarity with Snowflake is a plus. Familiarity with AI tools and their application to data analysis and reporting. Soft Skills Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with close attention to detail. Ability to communicate clearly with technical and non-technical audiences. Strong sense of ownership and sound judgment. Ability to manage multiple priorities while focusing on high-impact work.

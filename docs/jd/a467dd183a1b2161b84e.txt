Business Planning & Risk Analyst (Remote)
First Citizens Bank
Location: Phoenix, Arizona
Link: https://jobs.firstcitizens.com/jobs/37085

Overview This is a remote role open for multiple markets across the United States. This position delivers strategic insight into business systems through complex analysis, reporting, and operational activities. Works directly with leadership, team leaders, internal business partners and stakeholders to business requirements and implements appropriate specifications, monitoring for compliance and effectiveness, as well as assist with resource and financial management that enables the business unit to increase and enhance production, while satisfying financial, regulatory, and compliance reporting requirements. Responsibilities & Qualifications Data Analysis - Sources, compiles, and interprets data. Performs data analysis accuracy and efficiencies, effectively communicates and provides analysis output. Identifying Requirements - Determines system requirements and communicates them through the use of specifications, reports, project plans, or other forms of documentation. Identifies issues that affect system processes and assists in correcting. Ensures compliance through testing and analysis. Reporting - Produces reports based on analysis, industry trends, and process capabilities. Identifies patterns, problems, and areas of improvement, and tracks effectiveness of implemented enhancements. Drives remediation plans, and other documentation in support of business needs. May develop process enhancements based on analysis results. System Support - Provides remediation support for systems by managing escalated inquiries, ensuring processes run smoothly, and working to continuously improve the end-user experience. Implements recommended modifications and enhancements. Serves as an analytics resource to wider organization or business units, offering guidance on systems and data output. Business Strategy - Supports current plans, processes, and requirements that identify factors negatively affecting the business unit. Aids in the delivery of strategies and solutions that address identified issues, coordinating business efforts to achieve success. Provides support in creating internal communications for the business. Project Management - Supports and Leads projects, serving as a resource to the business unit or organization. Utilizes expertise to provide guidance, feedback, and direction on complex matters. Aids in the communication of performance results and expected behaviors. Bachelor's Degree and 4 years of experience in Project Planning, and Financial Services industry OR High School Diploma or GED and 8 years of experience in Vulnerability Management, Project Planning, and Financial Services industry.

Build our future together: The Associate Director, GD Engagement leads the design and delivery of change communications and engagement strategies across GD transformation initiatives. This is a player-coach role responsible for both setting direction and executing high-quality work. The role partners closely with Change Management to ensure that change is clearly communicated, effectively experienced, and successfully adopted across leaders, managers, and teams. This role will focus on building and establishing a scalable engagement capability, demonstrating value through delivery on priority programs while creating the foundation for a more strategic, enterprise-level function over time.

When you join Prudential, you’ll unlock a motivating and impactful career – all while growing your skills and advancing your profession at one of the world’s leading financial services institutions! Prudential Retirement develops, markets, and services an array of fixed and variable retirement products designed to help investors accumulate wealth and generate guaranteed income that they cannot outlive. It is a leading provider of innovative retirement products. The Associate Actuary position on the Capital Management & Stress Testing team, within Retirement, will be a critical member of an agile team that supports the development, execution, and monitoring of various stress-testing analyses and deliverables designed to proactively manage risk in capital generation and deployment. The current employee work arrangement for this position is hybrid and requires your on-site presence on a reoccurring basis as determined by your business. Your manager will provide additional details relative to the specific number of days you are expected to be on-site. What you can expect: Complete stress-testing deliverables and analyses focused on impacts to Prudential’s solvency and liquidity positions. Perform replicating portfolio analysis of liability cash-flow profiles to inform asset management strategies. Proactively drive innovation and provide insights to enable business decision-making and deliver positive financial outcomes. Implement and maintain analytical tools or processes for understanding and explaining our business. Communicate key insights to Retirement and Corporate stakeholders. What you’ll need: Actuarial exam progress (ASA preferred but not required) with a degree in mathematics, actuarial science, finance, computer science, or other related quantitative fields. Min. 4 years of actuarial experience. Experience with Statutory reserve and risk-based capital requirements. Experience with Python and Excel/VBA. Strong quantitative and analytical skills; understands the “why” behind the work. Good oral and written communication skills; can effectively highlight key insights in a clear concise manner; can explain technical issues in understandable terms. Strong ownership of deliverables and independently see them to completion. Ability to effectively prioritizes multiple initiatives. It’d be a plus if you had: Actuarial experience with insurance products, variable and fixed annuities experience preferred. Experience with actuarial projection systems, SQL, and Power BI a plus.

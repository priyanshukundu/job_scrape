Introduction Established in 1912, Bank of China is one of the largest banks in the world, with over $3 trillion in assets and a footprint that spans more than 60 countries and regions. Our long-term outlook, institutional weight and global breadth provide our clients with a stable and reliable financial partner, whether in Corporate or Personal Banking or our Trade Services, Commodities, Financial Institutions and Global Markets lines of business. Overview This position is to ensure the timely and accurate operation and administration process of construction loan transactions and administration originated by the Front Business Line, review loan transaction requests and conditions effectiveness against current credit terms are performed and align with the Bank’s procedures, policy guidelines, standards, and regulatory requirements. Responsibilities Commercial Mortgage/ Construction Loan Transaction/Administration (80%) Review and verify loan requests are current to credit conditions, approval from Front Business Line on conditions effectiveness for advances, Process loan booking, rate changes, and closing fees, in the core banking system. Reconcile monthly fee amortization. Monitor the monthly collection of loan payments and associated escrow of tax and insurance payments when the Bank is the only lender/lead bank. Monitor and process Real Estate Tax Payment when the Bank is the only lender/lead bank. Process disbursement of flood search fee / R.E. tax service fee / appraisal fee when the Bank is the only lender/lead bank. Create and maintain loan files. Review end of day reconciliation to ensure all related transactions are processed. Monitor loan payment status and real estate tax payment. Assess and prepare monthly expired and expiring insurance policy items reported by DCT to Front Business Lines. Ensure flood and insurance policies are in current standing. Process balance confirmations per customer’s request. Risk Management/Compliance/Internal Audit (20%) Understand and comply with Bank’s procedures, policies, standards, and regulatory requirements. Assist to update procedure to reflect current workflow process and regulatory updates. Provide flood search information to Front Business Line prior to closing. Follow up for receipt of Flood Determination Form. Escrow the flood insurance premium if property is located in flood zone. Alert of any suspicious items and report to BSA Officer of the Front Business Line. Respond timely to Internal/External auditors’ requests. Assist to remediate any audit issue findings. Qualifications Bachelor's degree required 1+ year experience of banking and financing required Basic comprehension on mortgage terminologies, workflow processes, risk management and compliance guidelines is needed Understanding of construction lending structure, review budget reports, review flood & tax policies, reconciliation of payment and basic accounting principles required Pay Range Actual salary is commensurate with candidate’s relevant years of experience, skillset, education and other qualifications.

Job Summary The Sector Lead Equity Research, Director is a senior investment professional within Equity Investments, responsible for leading and directing work, and mentoring a team of research analysts focused on a broad industry sector, in addition to their responsibilities as a senior research analyst. This position reports to Thrivent’s Director of Research and plays a critical role in generating high-conviction insights and recommendations that inform portfolio decisions across all domestic equity portfolios and active strategies. The Sector Team Lead represents a senior leadership role within the Equity Research Department, although they have no direct reports. This role requires strategic leadership, hands-on analytical expertise, and strong interpersonal skills to cultivate a culture of accountability, collaboration, and continuous improvement. The Sector Team Lead will be accountable for a sound investment process and driving execution that results in above benchmark performance. Key ongoing responsibilities include personnel development, continuous process improvement, deepening sector research capabilities, enhancing communication, and ensuring alignment with the firm’s investment objectives. Success in the role is grounded in intellectual curiosity, integrity, humility, and a team-oriented mindset, with a genuine passion for investing and a commitment to continuous learning and development. Job Responsibilities and Duties Investment Process Leadership & Strategy Lead the communication, implementation, and ongoing refinement of a systematic investment process to identify risks and opportunities within assigned sector coverage. Develop and maintain a comprehensive sector universe, organizing tickers into custom subsectors where appropriate, and ranking companies based on valuation, operating performance, and market sentiment. Identify attractive investment opportunities, conduct rigorous research, and deliver clear, actionable recommendations. Drive continuous improvement of the equity investment process by sharing tools, insights, and process enhancements applicable across the research platform. Incorporate multiple investment styles and portfolio management approaches into sector analysis. Formulate and articulate a strategic view of the sector supported by data and facts, engaging analysts in the process to ensure alignment and depth. Monitor near-term and long-term sector developments, training analysts to identify winners and losers across market capitalizations and investment styles. Support analysts in developing industry outlooks that position portfolio managers ahead of market trends and in delivering well-supported recommendations backed by data and facts. Establish a sector level view of expectations, sentiment, and behavioral dynamics to guide conviction, risk management, and portfolio positioning. Lead analysts in recognizing narrative driven environments and regime shifts, challenging consensus, and mitigating behavioral biases in sector recommendations. Own sector‑wide valuation philosophy, uphold best practices, and audit modeling quality across coverage Team Coordination & Workflow Management Manage team workflows and assume accountability for overall work quality and performance. Oversee coverage assignments within the sector team, ensuring clarity of responsibilities and eliminating duplication of effort. Foster collaboration among team members to leverage collective knowledge and improve efficiency. Meet regularly with the Director of Equity Research to review team structure, assess analyst development needs, and identify opportunities for improvement. Communication & Collaboration Facilitate regular sector team meetings to discuss investment processes, research priorities, and emerging opportunities. Collaborate closely with analysts and portfolio managers across industries, market capitalizations, and investment styles. Participate in cross-industry research initiatives to enhance firm-wide insights. Serve as the primary liaison between analysts and portfolio management teams, ensuring timely communication of significant sector developments. Leadership Competencies Model Thrivent’s leadership competencies – Model the Way, Rally the Team, and Deliver Outcomes. ​ Support and/or develop an environment in which Thrivent employees and colleagues are focused on continuous improvement, exceptional employee engagement, and an unwavering commitment to our clients. Shape and/or support a culture that represents the Thrivent purpose, promise and values, ensuring that Thrivent’s trust and reputation remain strong with its clients. Job Qualifications Required: Experience : Minimum of 10 years of relevant equity investment professional experience with 3+ years leading or mentoring investment research professionals. Education : (i) Master's degree in finance, business, economics or related area, or (ii) Certified Financial Analyst (CFA) designation. Skills: Exceptional leadership and management skills, strong investment acumen, including analytical skills, excellent communication and presentation skills, and the ability to work collaboratively within and across teams. Extensive Sector Expertise: Demonstrated experience within the Software sector and comprehensive knowledge of the broader investment universe. Strategic Thinker: Ability to analyze growth catalysts, competitive positioning, and industry dynamics, including platform shifts and disruptive trends. Technical Proficiency: Deep understanding of the sector’s technical foundations and key operational drivers. Detail-Oriented: Ability to analyze complex financial data and make informed decisions. Proactive: Takes initiative in identifying and pursuing investment opportunities. Ethical: Adheres to high ethical standards and maintains confidentiality of sensitive information. Preferred: Master’s degree in finance, economics, business administration or a related field plus Chartered Financial Analyst (CFA) designation. Fifteen or more years of experience in equity research or a related financial role with 5+ years leading research professionals.

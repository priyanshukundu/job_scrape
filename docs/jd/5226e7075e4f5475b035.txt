Neuroscience Research Scientist
Guidehouse
Location: US - MD, Baltimore
Link: https://guidehouse.wd1.myworkdayjobs.com/External/job/US---MD-Baltimore/Neuroscience-Research-Scientist_46467

Job Family : Scientist Travel Required : None Clearance Required : Ability to Obtain Public Trust We are looking for a Neuroscience Research Scientist to support the Neural Circuits of Motivation and Addiction Unit within the National Institute on Drug Abuse (NIDA) Intramural Research Program. The role will support research experiments, laboratory operations, scientific instrumentation, data analysis, and research documentation. This is a full-time position located in Baltimore, Maryland. What You Will Do : Train laboratory personnel on the proper use of scientific instruments and equipment and associated procedures. Manage scientific equipment and resources, including reagents, stock solutions, frozen cell stocks, animal samples, acquisition, documentation, and storage. Plan, design, and conduct experiments and studies applying scientific theories and principles within the laboratory's overall research program. Operate, troubleshoot, calibrate, and maintain laboratory equipment and software, including HPLCs, microanalytical balances, centrifuges, spectrophotometers, vacuum apparatus, and analytical equipment. Evaluate new techniques, instruments, and software for potential incorporation into current standard operating procedures (SOPs). Support laboratory staff with research projects, experimental techniques, and interpretation of experiments performed by research scientists. Perform experimental work involving RNAscope, immunostaining, microscopy, histological analysis, rodent surgeries, and behavioral studies. Evaluate, analyze, and interpret research data and communicate scientific results and methods through reports, presentations, and manuscripts. Maintain detailed and current electronic laboratory notebook documenting experiments and results. Assist with maintaining adequate laboratory space and facilities for ongoing and projected research studies. Meet with laboratory members bi-weekly to present research and operational updates. Provide administrative updates and scientific experimental results within timelines established by the laboratory Principal Investigator. What You Will Need : Master's degree Minimum of ONE (1) year of experience in handling mice and other laboratory rodents. Experience in RNAscope, immunostaining, microscopy, histological analysis, rodent surgeries, and behavioral studies. Background in maintaining stock solutions and laboratory resources. Hands-on experience operating, maintaining, calibrating, and troubleshooting scientific laboratory equipment. Research background in evaluating, analyzing, and interpreting scientific data. Scientific recordkeeping experience, including maintaining detailed records and electronic laboratory notebooks. Proficiency with Microsoft PowerPoint, Excel, Word, and Outlook. Skilled in using MATLAB. Scientific communication experience, including preparing reports, presentations, and manuscripts. Must be able to obtain and maintain a Federal or DoD “public trust”; candidates must receive approved adjudication prior to onboarding with Guidehouse. Candidates with an active public trust or suitability are preferred. What Would Be Nice To Have : Histotechnologist (HT) certification preferred. Previous laboratory administration experience. Prior support of neuroscience, motivation, addiction, or behavioral research programs. The annual salary range for this position is $65,000.00-$108,000.00. Compensation decisions depend on a wide range of factors, including but not limited to skill sets, experience and training, security clearances, licensure and certifications, and other business and organizational needs.

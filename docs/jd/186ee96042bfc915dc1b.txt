State Street Investment Management Data, Analytics & AI Services (DAAIS) team is We are seeking an experienced Officer‑level Senior Data Engineer (Cloud & Analytics) to join our Boston office. The ideal candidate combines solid hands-on engineering fundamentals, system integration experience, and AI-enabled approach to day-to-day execution. The role requires strong ownership of technical components, independence in execution, and close collaboration with cross‑functional stakeholders while operating as a senior individual contributor.

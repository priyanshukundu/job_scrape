Job Description The North America Commercial Solutions organization is seeking a highly strategic, analytical and commercially minded Senior Director, Commercial Portfolio Growth & Optimization to lead one of the most significant growth opportunities within our existing commercial payments business. Across Visa's Small Business, Middle Market and Large Corporate portfolios, there is a significant opportunity to drive incremental payment volume by helping issuers and clients better understand, engage and optimize their existing customer portfolios. Multiple analytical approaches and data sets have identified meaningful opportunities within established portfolios where greater customer engagement and increased share of wallet could unlock incremental payment volume. The Senior Director will be responsible for validating and sizing the opportunity, identifying priority clients and portfolios, developing the portfolio growth and optimization strategy, and leading execution with key internal and external stakeholders. Importantly, this leader will be expected to be hands-on with data, leveraging existing analytics while also developing their own models and analyses to identify, quantify and prioritize opportunities. The role will translate insights into specific commercial actions, working closely with Visa account teams, product, analytics, consulting and other partners to develop and test strategies that help issuers capture more of their customers' commercial spend. This is a highly visible role with significant exposure across the North America organization. The successful candidate will combine strong analytical capabilities, commercial payments expertise, strategic thinking and an ability to move from data to insight to action, while operating effectively in an environment where experimentation, learning and iteration will be critical to success. Key Responsibilities Portfolio Growth Strategy & Opportunity Identification Lead the development of Visa's North America commercial portfolio growth and optimization strategy, initially focused on Small Business and expanding over time to Middle Market and Large Corporate. Assess and synthesize existing analytical models, client data and Visa insights to validate the size, location and drivers of the opportunity. Independently develop analytical models and conduct portfolio analyses to identify emerging opportunities, test hypotheses and uncover areas of potential incremental payment volume. Establish a clear prioritization framework to identify the clients, portfolios, customer segments and use cases with the greatest potential. Develop measurable objectives and success metrics to track portfolio performance and quantify incremental impact. Analytics, Modeling & Insights Work directly with large and complex data sets to identify portfolio trends, performance gaps and opportunities for growth. Build and run custom models, analyses and scenarios to identify and size opportunities that may not be captured through existing analytical approaches. Combine transaction data, client information, market insights and commercial judgment to form hypotheses and identify areas for deeper investigation. Partner with Visa analytics and data science teams where appropriate, while maintaining the ability to independently analyze data and develop recommendations. Translate complex analytical findings into clear commercial insights and actionable recommendations for Visa and client stakeholders. Establish measurement approaches to understand incremental lift and determine which strategies should be scaled, modified or discontinued. Commercial Strategy & Execution Translate portfolio insights into actionable growth strategies for Visa and its clients. Partner with account executives, client teams and issuers to co-create specific interventions designed to capture incremental payment volume from existing portfolios. Identify and test a range of potential growth opportunities. Areas to explore may include, but are not limited to: increasing customer engagement and card usage; retention and reactivation; category and share-of-wallet expansion; product upgrades or migration; early-tenure engagement and lifecycle management; cross-border usage; increasing acceptance or addressing spend leakage; and targeted client or cardholder activation strategies. Develop a disciplined test, learn and scale approach, forming hypotheses, measuring outcomes and rapidly adapting strategies based on results. Create a pipeline of successful approaches that can be replicated across additional issuers, portfolios and commercial segments. Cross-Functional & Client Leadership Identify and mobilize the partners required to capture the opportunity, building alignment across Product, Sales, Account Management, Client Services, Consulting, Analytics, Marketing and other relevant teams. Serve as a central leader connecting data, insights, Visa capabilities and client execution around portfolio growth. Build strong relationships with senior Visa and client stakeholders and influence across organizational boundaries. Create visibility into opportunities, progress, results, learnings and barriers for senior leadership. Bring a strong point of view while remaining comfortable challenging initial assumptions as new data and results emerge. Build & Scale the Capability Develop a repeatable portfolio growth and optimization methodology that can be deployed across Visa's priority commercial clients. Create analytical tools, playbooks and commercialization approaches that enable opportunities to be identified and activated more consistently. Capture learnings from client engagements and continuously refine the strategy based on results. Identify opportunities to automate or scale successful analytical approaches across a broader set of portfolios. Help establish portfolio growth and optimization as a sustainable driver of incremental commercial payment volume.

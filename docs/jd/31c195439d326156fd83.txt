Use Your Power for Purpose At Pfizer, our purpose is to deliver breakthroughs that change patients’ lives. The Manufacturing Sciences and Technology (MSAT) in Andover in-silico Modeling team is seeking a Senior Associate Scientist to develop and apply mechanistic, data-driven, and hybrid computational models that advance understanding and optimization of biopharmaceutical manufacturing processes with a particular focus on downstream unit operations. The successful candidate will work at the intersection of bioprocess science, mathematical modeling, machine learning, and scientific computing. The role will focus on translating biological and engineering problems into quantitative models that can improve process understanding, support root-cause analysis, enable prediction, and accelerate technical decision-making. This position is particularly well suited to a scientist with a strong quantitative background who is interested in combining first-principles/mechanistic models with modern machine-learning approaches for applications in bioprocessing. What You Will Achieve In this role, you will: Develop, implement, and evaluate mechanistic models of biopharmaceutical manufacturing processes, including downstream unit operations like chromatography and UFDF. Develop and apply hybrid modeling approaches that combine mechanistic process knowledge with machine learning (ML), deep learning (DL), and other data-driven methods. Apply mathematical and computational approaches to problems involving chromatography, filtration, cell culture, process kinetics, transport phenomena, and other bioprocess systems. Develop computational methods for parameter estimation, model fitting, sensitivity analysis, model evaluation, and prediction. Apply ML and DL approaches, where appropriate, to complement mechanistic models and improve predictive capability. Analyze complex experimental and manufacturing datasets to support process understanding and root-cause analysis. Develop scientific software in Python and related technologies to enable model development, simulation, data analysis, and visualization. Develop interactive applications and visualization capabilities that make sophisticated modeling approaches accessible to scientists and engineers. Integrate models into broader digital workflows, applications, and user interfaces in collaboration with software developers and other technical partners. Collaborate closely with process scientists, engineers, data scientists, and manufacturing stakeholders to identify high-value modeling opportunities and translate scientific needs into quantitative solutions. Clearly document model assumptions, governing equations, training methodologies, validation approaches, and limitations to enable reproducible scientific use. Communicate model results and technical concepts effectively to multidisciplinary audiences. Explore emerging computational techniques that can enhance MSAT modeling capabilities, including appropriate applications of machine learning, deep learning, generative AI, and scientific machine learning. Here Is What You Need (Minimum Requirements) Applicant must have a bachelor's degree with at least 2 years of experience; OR a master's degree with 0+ years of experience; OR an associate's degree with 6 years of experience; OR a high school diploma (or equivalent) and 8 years of relevant experience in Chemical Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Bioengineering, Computational Science, Applied Mathematics, Computer Science, Architecture/Design Technology, or another quantitatively intensive discipline, with relevant computational experience. Strong programming experience with Python or a comparable scientific-computing language. Experience developing mathematical, mechanistic, statistical, or ML/DL/Gen AI models. Understanding of numerical methods and mathematical concepts relevant to scientific modeling, such as differential equations, optimization, statistics, or numerical simulation. Experience developing mechanistic models of biological, chemical, physical, or engineering systems. Experience with hybrid or physics-informed machine-learning approaches that combine first-principles models with ML. Experience modeling chromatography, including mechanistic representations of separation behavior. Familiarity with parameter estimation, optimization, sensitivity analysis, uncertainty analysis, or model validation. Experience with machine-learning and deep-learning architectures applicable to scientific modeling, including sequence models, transformers, neural differential-equation approaches, or related methods. Experience using scientific-computing tools such as Python, MATLAB, SQL, C/C++, or comparable technologies. Experience developing scientific visualization tools or interactive applications using technologies such as Plotly/Dash, JavaScript/TypeScript, or similar platforms.

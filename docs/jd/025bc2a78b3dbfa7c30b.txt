JOB SUMMARY The Capital Markets Revenue Platform Developer (VP) is the senior technical owner of a business-critical revenue tracking application used across debt, equity-linked, private placement, and advisory businesses. The platform consolidates transaction data from internal and external sources, creates transaction records and related tranches, supports revenue workflow and reporting, and notifies stakeholders when new activity is created. This role owns the technical direction of the platform. The successful candidate will maintain and enhance the current Angular and Java/Oracle application, lead its modernization to a React-based, containerized architecture, and set engineering standards for the supporting team. This is a hands-on senior role that combines full-stack engineering, technical leadership, and direct partnership with Front Office, Finance, and Operations stakeholders. PLATFORM AND INTEGRATION LANDSCAPE * Application stack: Angular front end, Java services, and Oracle database, migrating to React and containerized deployment. * Upstream data sources: market-data and reference-data providers such as Bloomberg, the enterprise data warehouse (including FX rates), and market transaction data feeds such as IGM. * Business users: capital-markets analysts who enter transaction data and middle-office syndicate teams who review and approve it. * Downstream consumers: the general ledger (Oracle GL) for daily accruals and expenses, Hyperion for P&L reporting, the enterprise business-intelligence platform for analytics, and the client-data hub for client information and accrual exchange. * Reporting: SSRS-based user reporting, including transaction reports, rules reports, transaction summaries, and daily transaction listings. KEY RESPONSIBILITIES 1. Technical Leadership and Ownership * Own the technical roadmap, target architecture, and engineering standards for the revenue tracking platform. * Lead design reviews, set coding and testing standards, and mentor developers, analysts, and support staff on and offshore. * Translate business priorities into a sequenced delivery plan, and communicate scope, dependencies, risks, and status to senior business and technology stakeholders. * Act as the escalation point for significant production, data, and delivery issues affecting revenue reporting. * Represent the platform in architecture, change-advisory, audit, and risk governance forums. * Evaluate build-versus-buy options, vendor products, and platform services, and present recommendations with clear cost, risk, and resiliency analysis. 2. Application Development and Modernization * Design, build, test, and maintain user interfaces, services, APIs, database components, and integrations for the revenue tracking platform. * Enhance the existing Angular application and Java services while defining and executing a phased migration to React. * Modernize the application for containerized deployment, including Docker images, environment configuration, health checks, logging, and deployment automation. * Partner with architecture, infrastructure, and information security teams to establish a secure, supportable target-state platform. * Identify and remediate technical debt, obsolete dependencies, performance constraints, and operational risks without disrupting critical business processes. 3. Capital Markets Transaction and Revenue Workflows * Support workflows for debt, equity-linked, private placement, and advisory transactions. * Develop functionality that captures transaction data, creates transaction shells from inbound data, and manages one or more uniquely identified tranches per transaction. * Support enrichment and reconciliation of transaction data received from enterprise transaction-processing, market-data, and customer-relationship-management platforms. * Build and maintain the maker-checker workflow that allows analysts to enter transactions and middle-office syndicate teams to review, approve, or reject them with full attribution. * Implement and maintain the revenue allocation and fee rules engine, including tranche-level economics, FX conversion using rates sourced from the enterprise data warehouse, and daily accrual calculations. * Build features that allow authorized users to create and maintain additional tranches, correct exceptions, and track workflow status with a complete audit trail. * Implement stakeholder notifications for new transactions, workflow exceptions, and relevant status changes. * Partner with Front Office, Capital Markets, Finance, Operations, Risk, Compliance, and Technology stakeholders to translate business requirements into controlled technical solutions. 4. Data, Integration, and Reporting * Develop and maintain Java-based services and APIs that process inbound and outbound transaction data. * Design and optimize Oracle database schemas, SQL, stored procedures, data-access patterns, and reconciliation processes. * Build and support inbound integrations with market-data providers, the enterprise data warehouse, market transaction data feeds, and customer-relationship-management systems. * Build and support outbound integrations that deliver daily accruals and expenses to the general ledger, P&L data to the financial consolidation platform, datasets to the enterprise business-intelligence platform, and client information to the client-data hub. * Develop and maintain SSRS reports covering transactions, allocation rules, summaries, and daily transaction activity, and support their migration to strategic reporting tooling where appropriate. * Establish data validation, error handling, monitoring, and reconciliation controls to preserve completeness, accuracy, timeliness, and traceability. * Diagnose and remediate data-quality, interface, and batch-processing issues in partnership with business and technology teams. 5. Delivery, Quality, and Production Support * Participate in the full software development lifecycle, including requirements analysis, technical design, development, code review, testing, release planning, and production support. * Develop automated unit, integration, regression, and end-to-end tests for application, API, and data-processing components. * Maintain deployment documentation, support runbooks, data-flow diagrams, recovery procedures, and operational controls. * Support incident, problem, and change-management processes; perform root-cause analysis and implement durable corrective actions. * Work in an Agile delivery model using Jira or comparable tools to manage stories, dependencies, risks, and delivery commitments. 6. Security, Controls, and Resilience * Implement secure coding practices, role-based access control, audit logging, input validation, and appropriate data-protection controls. * Ensure application changes comply with established change-management, testing, approval, and evidence-retention requirements. * Contribute to resiliency planning, monitoring, alerting, backup, recovery, and disaster-recovery testing for critical services. * Collaborate with information security and risk partners to remediate vulnerabilities, audit findings, and control gaps. PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS * Experience supporting capital-markets, investment-banking, or securities-industry applications. * Knowledge of primary-market transaction workflows, including debt, equity-linked, private placement, advisory, underwriting, syndication, or revenue-credit processes. * Experience integrating with market-data platforms, enterprise transaction-processing applications, or customer-relationship-management systems. * Experience with general-ledger, accrual, FX revaluation, or P&L reporting interfaces, including Oracle GL and Hyperion or comparable platforms. * Experience developing SSRS, Power BI, or comparable operational and management reporting. * Experience migrating an Angular application to React or modernizing a monolithic application into containerized services. * Experience with Kubernetes or OpenShift, Helm, infrastructure-as-code, API gateways, application observability, and centralized logging. * Knowledge of Oracle performance tuning, data reconciliation, message-based integration, and batch-processing controls. * Familiarity with financial-services control requirements, including access governance, audit trails, change control, and data lineage.

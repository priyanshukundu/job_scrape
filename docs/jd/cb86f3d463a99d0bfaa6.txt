Overview This is a remote role that may be hired in several markets across the United States. Priority given to those candidates located in the area of Raleigh, North Carolina, Phoenix, Arizona and Dallas,Texas. We are seeking an experienced Senior Data Engineer to join our Data Platform Engineering Organization and help build scalable cloud-based data solutions that enable analytics and data-driven decision-making across the organization. The ideal candidate is a hands-on data engineer with expertise in cloud-based data platforms, data warehousing, ETL/ELT development, and modern data engineering practices. This individual will build and optimize scalable data pipelines, integrations, and data products utilizing AWS Services, Snowflake, dbt, Python, SQL, and related technologies. As part of our growing Cloud Data & AI Platform organization, you will collaborate with a talented team of engineers, product partners, and business stakeholders to deliver cloud-based data solutions that support critical business initiatives. This is an opportunity to work with modern technologies, solve complex data challenges, and contribute to enterprise-scale data platforms that enable data-driven decision-making across the bank. Data Governance and Quality Management - Establish and enforce data governance policies and best practices. Implement data quality frameworks and monitoring systems. Contribute to the development of best practices and standards. Collaborate with cross-functional teams to drive data literacy.

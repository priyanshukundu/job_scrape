What You’ll Do

 - Design, build, and operate the software that automates Lambda's network lifecycle, from data center build-out and device provisioning through day-2 operations

 - Build provisioning and zero-touch deployment systems that take network devices for new GPU clusters from racked to production-ready with minimal manual work

 - Develop pipelines that generate, validate, and deploy network configuration as code, with automated testing, staged rollouts, and safe rollback

 - Build capacity planning tools that combine inventory, topology, and utilization data to forecast port, bandwidth, optics, and IP address needs as Lambda's footprint grows

 - Keep intended and live network state in sync by integrating automation with Lambda's source-of-truth, inventory, and IPAM systems

 - Create APIs, services, and CLIs that network engineers and other internal teams use to request, change, and audit network resources

 - Instrument the systems you own with metrics, logging, and alerting, and use that data to improve their reliability and speed

 - Partner with Network Engineering, Data Center Operations, and platform teams to turn manual runbooks into reliable, self-service automation

 - Participate in the on-call rotation for the automation systems and tooling your team owns

You

 - Have 6+ years of professional software engineering experience building and operating production systems

 - Write production-quality code, with deep expertise in multiple languages

 - Have built automation or infrastructure tooling used by other engineers, such as provisioning systems, deployment pipelines, workflow engines, or internal APIs

 - Have a solid grasp of distributed systems design, including API design, data modeling, idempotency, and handling partial failures

 - Follow strong engineering practices: code review, automated testing, CI/CD, and git-based workflows

 - Are comfortable on the Linux command line and understand core networking concepts such as TCP/IP, DNS, DHCP, VLANs, and IP routing

 - Have led technical projects end to end, from design doc through production rollout, and mentored other engineers along the way

 - Communicate clearly with software engineers, network engineers, and operations teams, and can turn operational pain points into software requirements

Nice to Have

 - Experience in the networking domain, such as designing or operating data center networks (Clos/spine-leaf fabrics, BGP, EVPN/VXLAN, ECMP)

 - Experience automating network devices from vendors such as Arista, Juniper, Cisco, or NVIDIA (Cumulus/SONiC) through their APIs, NETCONF/gNMI, or Jinja templating

 - Familiarity with network automation tools and libraries such as Ansible, Nornir, Netmiko, NAPALM, or Terraform

 - Experience building on network source-of-truth, DCIM, or IPAM systems

 - Experience building capacity planning or forecasting tools, or working with large volumes of time-series telemetry

 - Familiarity with HPC/AI networking, such as InfiniBand or RoCEv2, at GPU cluster scale

 - Experience running services on Kubernetes

 - Understanding of data center power, space, and cooling constraints and how they shape build-out plans

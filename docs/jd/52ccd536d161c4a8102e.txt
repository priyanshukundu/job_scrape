Job Description The Manager, Embedded Software Development is accountable to successfully staff their team within the annual operating plan and onboard them to be effective contributors. The successful candidate provides technical leadership and vision to support active launch programs and is part of developing a future vision. This Manager fosters a culture of innovation and is a catalyst for a positive culture that focuses on sense of belonging. Lastly, you advocate for process transformation towards world-class development with focus on AI native workflows. You will provide technical leadership for motor-control, embedded software development, embedded platforming, toolkit development, and influences the business roadmap with their technical acumen. While doing so, the successful candidate fosters a culture of ownership, transparency, and innovation. You will collaborate globally to develop an embedded software repository than is leveraged across the full portfolio to reduce time-to-market, share technical debt, and offer a common user experience. Additionally, you will also ensure products are launched to realize the business vision at the expected time, scope, and quality. You will partner with Product and Project Management as well as their global engineering peers to foster a culture of innovation, ownership, and engagement across all levels to ensure our vision is being realized. You will also collaborate with Release Train Engineers to ensure embedded platform teams develop incremental value throughout program increments.

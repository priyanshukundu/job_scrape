Job Title Associate Director, Occupier Sustainability RFP & Sales Job Description Summary Job Description Job Description Summary The Associate Director (AD) is an experienced sales and proposal professional with 10+ years of experience in sales for energy management, facilities management, sustainability consulting, real estate services or professional services business development. This person will work in close partnership with Occupier Sustainability leadership and will be the primary driver of RFP and proposal support and sustainability sales across our Americas Occupier business, which includes sustainability work primarily within large facilities management contracts. This role manages the RFP process end-to-end, turns input from subject matter experts into a cohesive, compelling pitch, and sells our portfolio of sustainability services to existing and prospective Occupier accounts. The AD will identify and convert cross-sell opportunities within existing accounts, support sales pitches, and build repeatable tools and processes that improve win rates and accelerate growth for the sustainability business.

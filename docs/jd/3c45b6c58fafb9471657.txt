Identifying performance bottlenecks such as: High CPU usage in IIDR CDC capture; Log volume spikes; Locking/contention issues on source or target systems and subscription parallelism. Experience working with heterogeneous data environments (DB2, Oracle, Single Store, CockroachDB). Perform root cause analysis for replication failures. Subject Matter Expertise supporting near real time log based solutions, monitoring IBM IIDR health, latency, and overall pipeline performance.

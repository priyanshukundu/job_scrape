Senior Manager Product Specialists Liquidity-Escrow
First Citizens Bank
Location: New York, New York; Charlotte, North Carolina; San Francisco, California; Phoenix, Arizona
Link: https://jobs.firstcitizens.com/jobs/36286

Overview This is a hybrid role with the expectation that time working will regularly take place inside and outside of a company office. This position oversees a product specialist team, focusing on liquidity management solutions. Acts as subject matter expert in liquidity products, strong leadership, and the ability to develop product strategies from market insights. Responsible for improving FCB's liquidity offerings, fostering client growth, and contributing to the bank's success in the innovation economy. Responsibilities & Qualifications Duties & Responsibilities: Product Management and Development - Provide leadership oversight for projects. Coordinate the development and lead implementation for new complex liquidity products. Stakeholder Management and Collaboration - Identify and develop relationships with key leaders across partners (Sales, Treasury, Ops, Risk, etc...) to identify challenges and opportunities to improve product operating ratios. Represent the product team in cross-functional leadership meetings and provide leadership oversight for projects. Client Advisory and Engagement - Serve as the expert for set of owned liquidity products, and have intermediate knowledge of the bank's liquidity product set. Deliver high-level presentations to key clients and prospects. Provide guidance to product specialists, Sales and Relationship Management on complex client solutions. Engage with key clients to gather feedback and insights. Product Portfolio Performance Management - Oversee the performance of a set of products , making strategic decisions to optimize resource allocation, ROI, and market positioning. Provide regular reporting and insights to senior management. Review and provide feedback on financial analysis and scenarios . Product Portfolio Strategy and Execution - Develop and implement strategies for a portfolio of liquidity products. Drive product enhancements based on market trends and client needs. Collaborate with senior leadership on product roadmap and prioritization. Lead the strategic roadmap for a set of liquidity products. Product Set Risk Management - Review and correct process and standards for owned product set. Ensure team is actively participating in RCSA, TPRM, and Audits. Lead projects to mitigate known risk issues. Review risks to identify gaps and potential self-identified issues and mitigations. Strategic Leadership and Vision - Develop and communicate a comprehensive product portfolio strategy that aligns with overall business objectives. Guide the team in translating this vision into actionable plans and measurable outcomes. Team Management and Development - Manage and mentor a team of liquidity product specialists. Set performance goals, conduct regular evaluations, and set succession planning for team. Foster a culture of innovation and continuous improvement.

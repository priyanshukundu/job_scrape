EMPLOYER : Neuberger Berman Group LLC TITLE : Senior Portfolio Analyst LOCATION : New York, NY (A hybrid work schedule may be permitted within a commutable distance from the worksite, in accordance with company policies.) DUTIES : Build and maintain quantitative models that help to explain the performance and fundamentals of the portfolio versus the overall market or benchmarks. Research projects that would enhance the team’s knowledge about industries and company fundamentals. Aggregate/analyze holdings information as inputs into optimal portfolio construction and risk management. Automate internal processes (stock screens, marketing, administrative), perform performance analysis, and create proprietary analyses in support of marketing materials. Explore, build and maintain internal data warehouse and data pipeline for the team to conduct complex data analysis, fundamental analysis on stock and portfolio level, and speak to insights. Help with producing and publishing marketing materials for existing and prospective clients (quarterly reporting, market research, White Papers). Help streamline, automate the process of producing and publishing marketing materials from gathering, calculating and analyzing data. Participate in investment meetings with current holdings and prospects. Strengthen and improve existing processes around ESG information and broker use. REQTS : Must have a Master’s degree or foreign equivalent in Financial Engineering, Data Science, Computer Science, or a related field plus three (3) years of experience in the position offered, as a Portfolio Analyst or a related position. Must have three (3) years of experience with all of the following: Performing financial statement, economic, and in-depth market data analyses, and developing financial models and analytics to support Portfolio Managers; Performing and customizing portfolio performance contribution and attribution analysis utilizing Brinson-based methodologies to help identify drivers of portfolio performance; Conducting competitive analysis and portfolio valuation analysis for small cap equity portfolios, indexes, and peer groups using fundamental and consensus data from FactSet and Capital IQ, including evaluating data methodologies and coverage differences of vendor-provided datasets; Utilizing Python, SQL, and front-end dashboard tool (Python Dash) to develop interactive dashboards and centralized portfolio monitoring tools which support scalable investment analysis and workflow management; Developing, optimizing, and maintaining scalable data warehouse solutions and automated ETL pipelines to facilitate complex data analysis, including in Snowflake and Azure DevOps environments, and version control systems; Evaluating and integrating external investment and market datasets through API-driven workflows to support portfolio analytics and improve research workflows; Performing, customizing and enhancing multi-factor equity risk models, including Aladdin and Bloomberg MAC3, to evaluate portfolio risk exposures, identify sources of risk, and conduct return attribution analyses; and Developing and implementing customized equity-factor analytical model and backtesting methodologies to evaluate factor effectiveness and performance.

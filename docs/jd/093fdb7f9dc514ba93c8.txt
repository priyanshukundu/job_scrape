Title and Summary Senior Data Engineer Overview: Marketing Services Technology is seeking a Senior Data Engineer to build scalable data platforms, pipelines, and services that power marketing measurement, customer insights, campaign optimization, advanced analytics, and AI/ML capabilities. This role partners with Product Management, Engineering, Data Science, Analytics, and Business teams to deliver secure, reliable, and high-quality data solutions. The Senior Data Engineer will also contribute to cloud modernization, platform architecture, automation, and operational excellence across the Marketing Services data ecosystem. Role: • Design, build, deploy, and maintain scalable batch, streaming, and real-time data pipelines. • Develop reusable frameworks for data ingestion, transformation, orchestration, and delivery. • Build and support enterprise data lake, data warehouse, and lakehouse solutions. • Improve platform scalability, performance, reliability, resiliency, and operational efficiency. • Implement data quality, observability, governance, security, lineage, and monitoring capabilities. • Enable reporting, advanced analytics, experimentation, AI/ML, and customer insight use cases. • Support the migration of legacy platforms to modern cloud-native architectures. • Establish engineering standards across CI/CD, infrastructure as code, testing, automation, and production support. • Troubleshoot complex production issues and lead root-cause analysis to improve service reliability. • Partner with product, engineering, analytics, and business teams to translate requirements into scalable data solutions. • Mentor engineers and provide technical leadership across data initiatives. • Contribute to architectural decisions and technology roadmaps for Marketing Services data platforms. All About You: • Bachelor’s degree or equivalent experience in computer science, engineering, information systems, or a related technical field. • Strong experience designing scalable data platforms and distributed data-processing solutions. • Proficiency in SQL and at least one programming language such as Python, Java, or Scala. • Experience with technologies such as Spark, Kafka, Hadoop, or comparable large-scale data-processing frameworks. • Experience building data pipelines for analytics, reporting, machine learning, and operational data products. • Strong understanding of data modelling, ETL/ELT patterns, data architecture, and data lifecycle management. • Experience working with cloud platforms such as AWS, Azure, or GCP. • Knowledge of data governance, security, privacy, lineage, metadata management, and regulatory requirements. • Experience with Git, CI/CD, DevOps automation, infrastructure as code, testing, deployment, and observability. • Strong problem-solving skills with experience diagnosing complex issues in distributed environments. • Ability to balance technical quality with business priorities and customer outcomes. • Strong communication and collaboration skills across engineering, product, analytics, and business teams. Preferred Experience: • Experience leading technical initiatives, influencing architectural decisions, or mentoring engineers. • Experience modernizing legacy data platforms or supporting cloud migration programmes. • Exposure to marketing technology, campaign measurement, customer insights, or marketing analytics. • Advanced degree in a related technical discipline.

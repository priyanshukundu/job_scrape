Job Family : Finance Planning & Analysis Travel Required : Up to 25% Clearance Required : None What You Will Do: The Director of Financial Planning & Analysis will manage all financial planning including budgeting and forecasting for the business in addition to monthly reporting and analysis of the financial operations of the business. The individual will be responsible for the development and/or improvement of financial planning and reporting systems, will prepare financial and management reports, and present findings and specific recommendations to senior management. As a senior member of the Finance team, this position reports directly to a Finance Partner and regularly engages with Executive Leadership. We are seeking candidates with strong financial backgrounds in addition to very strong reporting tool systems experience. Experience in cost accounting for federal government contracts, and familiarity with government contract compliance is preferred. The Director will serve as a strategic financial advisor to business leaders, translating professional services operating drivers into actionable financial plans and recommendations. Partnering with Finance, Operations, Human Capital, and practice leadership, this leader will connect revenue opportunities, staffing capacity, utilization, pricing, and delivery costs to business performance, enabling informed decisions that support profitable growth and disciplined investment. Responsibilities: Oversee the month end financial reporting process including the monthly financial reporting package Lead annual budgeting, long-range planning, and quarterly forecasting for the business, using driver-based models that connect sales pipeline, contracted backlog, project schedules, billing rates, utilization, and staffing assumptions to revenue, costs, and profitability. Partner with the Project Finance Team to strengthen estimate-at-completion (EAC) and engagement forecasting processes, challenge delivery assumptions, and identify emerging cost overruns, scope changes, and margin risks. Enable strategic decision-making through financial modeling, scenario analysis, and business cases for hiring, growth initiatives, investments, and cost optimization; communicate trade-offs, risks, and recommended actions to senior leadership. Prepare Board and Banking packages as required. Assist the Finance and Accounting team in ad hoc reporting requests Manage the finance portion of the dashboards to ensure reporting accuracy Manage the financial planning and analysis migration and integration of acquisitions. Financial modeling, maintaining complex spreadsheets, and ease with multiple financial systems Working with equity partners on ad hoc analysis Serve as a trusted business partner to practice and operational leaders; lead financial performance discussions, explain variances to plan and forecast, and partner with accountable leaders on corrective actions and follow-through. Analyze profitability by engagement, client, practice, and service line, identifying the impact of pricing, rate realization, staffing mix, subcontractor costs, utilization, and delivery efficiency on margins. Partner with Human Capital and Operations on workforce and capacity planning, evaluating hiring, deployment, bench levels, and contractor usage against anticipated demand and financial targets. Collaborate with Pricing and business leadership to assess deal economics and financial assumptions, validate margin exception requests, and support approvals and escalation within delegated authority and firm policy. Partner with Accounting and Treasury to forecast cash flow and working capital needs, analyze billing and collection trends, and highlight financial risks requiring business action. Lead, coach, and develop the FP&A team, setting clear priorities and strengthening analytical rigor, business partnering, and the ability to communicate actionable financial insights. What You Will Need: Minimum of 12 Years of Experience Bachelor’s degree in accounting, finance or business Experience managing and supervising staff Experience in systems implementations Strong analytical mindset. Strong leadership skills. What Would Be Nice to Have: Experience leading and mentoring staff on all financial analysis functions Experience providing financial reporting guidance and training to accounting and finance staff on proper accounting controls. Proficiency at MS Office suite. software tools and highly skilled at Excel Expertise of Workday Financials (Costpoint and PeopleSoft Time & Expense) and Financial Reporting tools such as Cognos, Power BI, and TM1.

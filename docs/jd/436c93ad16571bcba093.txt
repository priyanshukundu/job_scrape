SMBC Group is a top-tier global financial group. Headquartered in Tokyo and with a 400-year history, SMBC Group offers a diverse range of financial services, including banking, leasing, securities, credit cards, and consumer finance. The Group has more than 130 offices and 80,000 employees worldwide in nearly 40 countries. Sumitomo Mitsui Financial Group, Inc. (SMFG) is the holding company of SMBC Group, which is one of the three largest banking groups in Japan. SMFG’s shares trade on the Tokyo, Nagoya, and New York (NYSE: SMFG) stock exchanges. In the Americas, SMBC Group has a presence in the US, Canada, Mexico, Brazil, Chile, Colombia, and Peru. Backed by the capital strength of SMBC Group and the value of its relationships in Asia, the Group offers a range of commercial and investment banking services to its corporate, institutional, and municipal clients. It connects a diverse client base to local markets and the organization’s extensive global network. The Group’s operating companies in the Americas include Sumitomo Mitsui Banking Corp. (SMBC), SMBC Nikko Securities America, Inc., SMBC Capital Markets, Inc., JRI America, Inc., SMBC Leasing and Finance, Inc., Banco Sumitomo Mitsui Brasileiro S.A., and Sumitomo Mitsui Finance and Leasing Co., Ltd. Role Description SMBC is seeking a self-motivated, detail-oriented professional for an Analyst position within the Controllers - Regulatory Reporting Analysis Group supporting international, cross-border and systemic risk regulatory reporting. The role will support the end-to-end production, review and timely submission of the FFIEC 019, FFIEC 009, Treasury International Capital (TIC) reports and FR Y-15. The Analyst will perform data preparation, reconciliations, variance analysis, validation and control activities, while working with business, finance, operations and technology partners to resolve reporting matters and enhance the reporting process. Role Objectives: Delivery • Support the preparation, review and timely submission of the FR 2420, FR 2004, FFIEC 019, FFIEC 009, Treasury International Capital (TIC) reports and FR Y-15 in accordance with applicable regulatory instructions and internal reporting standards. • Perform recurring production activities, including data sourcing, data validation, report population, edit checks and submission-readiness procedures. • Prepare and document period-over-period variance analysis, investigate unusual movements and coordinate the resolution of identified exceptions with relevant stakeholders. • Reconcile reported balances to the general ledger, supporting schedules, source systems and related regulatory reports. • Execute and evidence key controls, maintain supporting documentation and ensure workpapers provide a clear audit trail from source data through final reported amounts. • Assist with the review and interpretation of regulatory reporting instructions and ensure reporting changes are incorporated into production procedures and controls. • Track data quality, system and reporting issues through resolution, including root-cause identification, remediation support and documentation of final outcomes. • Maintain desktop procedures, process flows, data lineage and other governance documentation supporting the assigned reports. • Support responses to regulatory examinations, internal audit, external audit and other review requests by providing complete and accurate supporting documentation. • Participate in process improvement and automation initiatives, including Axiom enhancements, user acceptance testing, implementation validation and post-production monitoring. • Assist in defining and documenting business requirements for report enhancements, regulatory changes and control improvements. • Partner with senior team members and cross-functional stakeholders to meet reporting deadlines and escalate risks or delays promptly. Qualifications and Skills • Bachelor's degree in Accounting, Finance, Economics or a related field. • One to two years of relevant experience in financial or regulatory reporting, accounting, financial analysis or a related control function preferred. • Working knowledge of U.S. GAAP, financial statements and regulatory reporting concepts; familiarity with FFIEC 019, FFIEC 009, Treasury International Capital (TIC) reports or FR Y-15 is preferred. • Strong analytical skills, attention to detail and ability to investigate and explain data variances and reporting exceptions. • Proficiency in Microsoft Excel, including formulas, pivot tables, lookups and data reconciliation techniques. • Familiarity with regulatory reporting platforms such as AxiomSL preferred. • General familiarity with banking and financial products, including loans, deposits, securities, derivatives and secured financing transactions, as well as cross-border and country exposure concepts. • Strong organizational and communication skills, with the ability to manage recurring deadlines and maintain complete, accurate documentation. • Ability to work effectively in a team-oriented, deadline-driven and controlled production environment.

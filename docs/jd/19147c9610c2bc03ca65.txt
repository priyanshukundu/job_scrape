Position Location: Atlanta, GA HYBRID - work from the office a minimum of three days a week In this role you will own the data layer for the stablecoin platform. You will design the ledger schemas, event models, and reconciliation pipelines that feed ORMB and financial reporting. This role is deliberately framed as a data engineer rather than a traditional DBA: Western Union's platform teams handle database administration fundamentals (provisioning, backups, patching), while this role owns the stablecoin-specific data model and the pipelines that finance, compliance, and audit depend on. Responsibilities Design schemas for the stablecoin ledger, transaction events, and reconciliation data Build and operate data pipelines that feed ORMB, financial reporting, and audit trails Partner with the Principal Engineer on event model design and with the Blockchain Developer on on-chain event ingestion Implement data quality checks, lineage tracking, and reconciliation validation Enforce data governance: encryption at rest, retention policies, access controls, PII handling Partner with Finance, Compliance, and Audit stakeholders to validate that data models and reports meet their requirements Support archival, tiered storage, and cost-efficient data lifecycle management Required Skills 5+ years designing and operating production data systems Strong expertise with both relational (PostgreSQL, MySQL, or SQL Server) and NoSQL (DynamoDB, MongoDB) databases Data modeling for financial or transactional systems: ledgers, event schemas, reconciliation data, audit trails Experience building and operating ETL or ELT pipelines at production scale Query optimization and performance tuning Experience with data quality, lineage, and governance in regulated environments Preferred Skills Experience with streaming and event-driven architectures (Kinesis, Kafka, EventBridge) Change data capture (CDC) patterns for keeping analytical and operational stores in sync Experience building data models that support financial reporting, reconciliation, or audit requirements Infrastructure-as-code experience (Terraform or AWS CDK) for data infrastructure Exposure to on-chain indexing or blockchain data patterns AWS experience specifically (RDS, DynamoDB, S3, Redshift, Glue, Kinesis) Workplace Option HYBRID – Western Union values in-person collaboration, problem solving, and ideation whenever possible. We believe this fosters common ways of working and supports how we execute initiatives for our customers. The expectation is to work from the office a minimum of three days a week.

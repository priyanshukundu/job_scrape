We promote a digitally enabled work environment to continuously enhance the experience of our employees and customers. Overview This is a full-time career opportunity that can be remote. Must have the ability to be onsite at our headquarters location in Lancaster, PA on a quarterly basis. This role is the functional working manager responsible for their own work and the work of other data engineers. The role's responsibilities are to design, develop, test, deliver and maintain data engineering solutions that support Fulton's business strategies. Engages in the entire database development process, from inception through delivery. Leads and ensures consistency in the design and implementation of database development standards and procedures. Responsibilities Manage the design, development, testing, and implementation of database solutions related to optimal data pipeline architecture and infrastructure required for optimal extraction, transformation, and loading of data from a wide variety of data sources in accordance with established standards. Develop, implement, and continuously manage controls to ensure data integrity and regulatory compliance. Participate in peer reviews of solution designs and related code. Package and support deployment of releases. Lead efforts with business requestors and IT stakeholders to refine business requirements and ensure that sufficient detail is provided to guide design, development, and testing. Drive team innovation and improvement of areas such as development efficiencies, database component development and integration, database management and administration, and leading-edge industry concepts and developments. Develop and implement standards and changes to database administration and development processes. Develop and implement key performance indicators and service level agreements that serve to maximize value to the business. Maintain the integrity and performance of company databases and guarantee that data is stored securely and optimally. Oversee the production schedule and manage the remediation of job failures. Leverage technology to automate routine processes. Monitor key performance indicators and recovery time objectives to meet service level agreements and maximize value to the business. Provide high-level support or subject matter expertise to business users and internal IT stakeholders. Monitor and tune databases and direct the work of vendors where they are responsible for the Database Administrator function. Support enterprise-wide compliance with enterprise standards, processes, and policies. Create and monitor scorecards to ensure all data engineering staff are operating with excellence and address any performance issues as needed. Ensure consistency across various workstreams. Plan, assign, and evaluate work of staff, screen and select candidates, provide guidance and coaching, establish individual goals and objectives, monitor volume, workflow, and quality, manage performance to include conducting performance appraisals, make pay recommendations, partner with Human Resources to resolve employment related issues. Qualifications Education Bachelor's Degree or the equivalent experience. Specialty: Computer Science, Computer Information Science. (Required) Master's Degree or the equivalent experience. Specialty: Computer Science, Computer Information Science. (Preferred) Required Experience 7 or more years database administrator, database developer, data engineer experience.

Vice President – CIB / Front Office Quantitative Analyst (New York Equity Derivatives) Country: United States of America It Starts With You: Santander is a global leader and innovator in the financial services industry and is evolving from a high-impact brand into a technology-driven organization. The Difference You Make: We are seeking a talented Front Office Quantitative Analyst to join our New York Quant team supporting the USA Equity Derivatives business. This role sits at the intersection of quantitative research, technology, and trading, with responsibility for developing and maintaining pricing, risk management and market making models and tooling for Equity Derivatives across the US market. As part of a global Quant team, you will contribute to extend the Equity Derivatives quantitative pricing library and to enhance existing production systems. The role offers broad exposure across equity derivatives, including institutional and retail structured products, corporate equity derivatives, convertible bonds and listed U.S. equity options. Develop pricing and risk management models for the Convertible Bond and Equity Derivatives trading desks, covering structured notes, OTC vanilla and light exotic equity derivatives and Accelerated Share Repurchase (ASR) programs. Expand automated tools for calibration, market-data processing, model diagnostics and monitoring of volatility surfaces and forward curves across a large universe of U.S. equities. Maintain and extend existing C++ pricing libraries, while promoting best practices in software engineering, architecture, testing and code quality. Optimize numerical methods and improve the performance, robustness, scalability and reliability of pricing and risk calculations. Develop tools used directly by Sales and Trading in daily decision-making. Investigate pricing and risk anomalies, perform root-cause analysis, and deliver robust solutions within business timelines. Partner with Trading, Structuring and Sales teams to develop new pricing methodologies, improve risk analytics and support new product initiatives. Contribute to testing frameworks, model validation, production monitoring and quantitative governance standards. Document quantitative methodologies, model assumptions and system architecture for internal stakeholders. Collaborate closely with Risk and Technology teams across global locations. What You Bring: To succeed in this role, candidates must be able to perform the essential duties effectively. The requirements below outline the knowledge, skills and abilities needed. Education: A bachelor’s degree in Mathematics, Physics, Statistics, Engineering, Computer Science, Quantitative Finance, or a related quantitative discipline is required. A PhD would be preferred. Qualifications: Approximately 5–7 years of experience in Front Office Quantitative Analytics, Quantitative Development, Financial Engineering or related roles. Proven experience developing pricing and risk management models for equity derivatives. Experience supporting production derivatives trading environments and working directly with Front Office stakeholders. Strong programming skills in Python and modern C++. Experience designing APIs and integrating quantitative libraries into front-office applications. Familiarity with Linux, Git, CI/CD workflows and modern software development practices. Strong understanding of quantitative modeling techniques such as stochastic processes, numerical PDE methods and Monte Carlo simulation. Strong understanding of market data calibration methods. Strong understanding of the pricing and risk management of convertible bonds and structured equity products. Strong understanding of numerical optimization techniques and computational methods. Strong analytical and problem-solving abilities with a hands-on approach and an ownership mindset. Ability to perform effectively in a fast-paced Front Office trading environment. Excellent communication skills with the ability to explain complex quantitative concepts to technical and non-technical stakeholders. Collaborative approach with the ability to work effectively across Trading, Technology, Risk and Structuring teams. Demonstrated ability to balance quantitative rigor with pragmatic solutions that deliver business value. Certifications: Professional certifications such as CQF or FRM are a plus. It Would Be Nice For You To Have: Experience with performance optimization, parallel computing and scalable distributed systems. Experience with automated testing frameworks, model validation and production monitoring. Opportunity to work within a global team of quantitative professionals across multiple regions. Direct partnership with traders supporting one of the firm's core US businesses. Broad exposure to a diverse Equity Derivatives franchise, with direct impact on the models, analytics and infrastructure supporting Santander's Convertible Bond and Equity Derivatives trading businesses.

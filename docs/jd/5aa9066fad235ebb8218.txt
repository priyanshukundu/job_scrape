What You'll Contribute The Treasury Operations Manager supports the execution, control, and continued enhancement of Treasury activities, with a focus on derivatives and investment portfolio activity, Treasury technology implementation, reporting, risk management, and operational resiliency. The role will help strengthen and scale Treasury Operations by supporting new and evolving activities, improving processes and reporting, maintaining a strong control environment, and providing contingency coverage for critical cash desk functions. The Manager will partner closely with Treasury, Accounting, Risk, Compliance, Internal Audit, Technology, and external banking partners. What You'll Do Support derivatives and investment operations, including confirmations, settlements, collateral, reconciliations, reporting, and related controls. Play a key role in Treasury Management System implementation, including requirements gathering, process design, testing, data validation, implementation readiness, and post-go-live stabilization. Identify and implement opportunities to streamline and automate Treasury processes and management and compliance reporting. Support control testing, audits, Risk and Control Self-Assessment (RCSA) activities, issue remediation, and process documentation. Serve as a contingency resource for critical cash desk activities, including cash positioning, funding, payments, and liquidity monitoring. Maintain effective procedures, controls, and documentation to support a strong first line of defense. Partner across Treasury, Accounting, Risk, Compliance, Internal Audit, Technology, and external banking partners to support Treasury initiatives and resolve operational issues. What You Need to Succeed Demonstrate strong knowledge of Treasury Operations, cash management, derivatives, investments, payments, and liquidity management. Support derivatives and investment activity through accurate confirmation, settlement, reconciliation, collateral, reporting, and control processes. Contribute to Treasury Management System requirements, testing, data validation, implementation, and stabilization. Maintain a strong risk and controls mindset while supporting audits, control testing, RCSA activities, and issue remediation. Analyze financial and operational data to resolve issues, identify trends, and support decision-making. Identify opportunities to streamline and automate processes and reporting. Provide effective backup coverage for critical cash desk activities and promote cross-training to reduce key-person dependency. What you have Minimum: Bachelor's degree in Finance, Accounting, Economics, Business, or a related field. 5-10 years of Treasury, banking operations, cash management, or related financial services experience. 2+ years of people leadership, supervisory, or team lead experience. Experience with cash management, derivatives, investments, payments, or Treasury Operations. Experience supporting system implementations, testing, internal controls, audits, or risk management activities. Advanced proficiency in Microsoft Excel, Word, and PowerPoint. Experience with financial systems, databases, and reporting tools. Strong analytical, problem-solving, organizational, and communication skills. Preferred: MBA, Master's degree, or equivalent advanced education. Certified Treasury Professional (CTP), CFA, CPA, or comparable professional certification. Experience with Treasury Management Systems such as Summit, WallStreet, FIS Quantum, or similar platforms. Experience with process automation and reporting tools such as SQL, Power BI, or Alteryx. Experience supporting audits, regulatory examinations, SOX controls, or operational risk management programs. Treasury experience within a bank or regulated financial institution.

ABOUT FLUIDSTACK

We exist to make humanity more free. For most of human history, you farmed or you starved. Technology gave people more time for the things they wanted to do, instead of things they had to do. Powerful AI will be the biggest lever for human choice we've ever built - but only if models are aligned with what humanity actually wants. There are groups building AI who don't share these goals. Whoever deploys frontier compute infrastructure fastest will decide whether AI expands human freedom or shrinks it. 


We're singularly focused on delivering 10 to 100s of GWs of compute faster than anyone else, rethinking every layer of the stack. We acquire power, design and build data centers, and operate them - with teams spanning hardware and software. Speed and scale are our key differentiators. Come be a part of building civilization-scale infrastructure for AI.


We hire people who care deeply about this problem space. If that is you, please apply!




HOW WE OPERATE

 - Be a barrel. Full autonomy. Own things end to end, take on scope without being asked, no permission required to operate outside your core role.

ROLE SCOPE

 - Run our project financings from mandate to funding: bank selection, rating agency processes, the offering memorandum and disclosure, investor marketing, pricing, closing and the covenant and reporting obligations that follow, across several concurrent processes at all times.

 - Structure novel financings around the assets and contracts that make AI infrastructure bankable, including designing new lease, off-take, and power purchase structures to enable our business plan and project pipeline.

 - Finance accelerator purchases behind multi-year compute contracts, balancing bank delayed-draw term loans, private placements and bonds, and structuring the residual-value and hedging terms these markets require.

 - Create, close, and administer corporate-level facilities (revolvers, land loans, pre-construction loans, long-lead equipment facilities), keeping one view of leverage, liquidity and restricted-payment capacity across the parent, the project companies and our partnerships.

 - Support institutional equity and JV capitalization processes: operating models, the information memorandums, investor Q&A, and the coordination of placement consultants, agents, counsel and auditors.

 - Own the day-to-day relationships with the banks, private credit funds, insurance investors and rating agencies that fund the build, and the quarterly lender, bondholder and investor reporting that they require.

 - Build the capital markets team and function: hiring and mentoring junior team members and creating repeatable and scalable processes that let several transactions run in parallel as Fluidstack grows.

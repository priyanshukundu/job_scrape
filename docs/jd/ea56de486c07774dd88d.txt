Position Summary At BMS, Business Insights & Technology (BI&T) is critical to our vision of transforming patients’ lives through science. To accelerate our ability to serve patients around the world, we must unleash the power of technology. We are committed to being at the forefront of transforming the way medicine is made and delivered by harnessing the power of computer and data science, analytics, artificial intelligence, and other technologies to promote scientific discovery, faster decision making, and enhanced patient care. Transform your education and experience into fast-track career growth by joining the Technology Rotational Development Program, offering a high-performing culture that is made up of a powerfully diverse and inclusive workforce. The Technology Rotational Development Program’s (IT RDP) mission is to cultivate future leaders that accelerate BMS’s technology and AI transformation to ultimately serve our patients. We are looking for MBA/Master’s graduates with professional experience for our June 2027 cohort who have a passion for driving innovative technology solutions to accelerate our mission of helping patients prevail over serious diseases. The program offers you the flexibility to create your own path through rotational projects and stretch assignments aligned with your interests and the company’s most critical needs, allowing for a completely customized developmental experience. Upon completion of the program, associates will have the opportunity to continue in a full-time role that aligns with their career aspirations and passion for enhancing patient lives with digital solutions. IT RDP Is an Individualized Career Launch Pad that Offers 3 Key Areas of Opportunities: 1) Learn: Obtain Expertise in Your Desired Domains (Technical & Non-Technical) Real work from day one. Each rotation pairs you with an executive sponsor and a rotation sponsor, and you’ll have more mentors throughout the program. A seat at the leadership table. Regular meetings with technology leadership teams, including the Chief Digital & Technology Officer (CDTO), give you direct exposure to how BMS sets its technology strategy. An alumni network that opens doors. In more than 30 years, the program has built a strong network of alumni in leadership roles across BMS. Connections across BMS and beyond. You’ll meet peers in rotational programs in other BMS functions and grow your professional network outside the company. 3) Leadership Brand: Craft Your Leadership Trademark Learn directly from senior leaders. You’ll get visibility with senior leaders and mentorship from them, so you can see how they make decisions and lead. See the full picture. Monthly discovery sessions with BI&T leaders across the organization show you how each part of the business uses technology. This also helps you pick your next rotation and your long-term path. Growth built around you. A personalized development plan, internal courses, workshops, and free LinkedIn Learning help you shape the kind of leader you want to become.

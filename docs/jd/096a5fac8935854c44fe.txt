Your key responsibilities As a Senior in Capital Markets Technology group, your focus will be on solving technology problems of our clients in the Capital Markets Industry. Specifically, this role will require you to assist with design of technical solutions, performing replacements, upgrades migrations of systems as well as integrating systems to support pre and post-trade functions in Capital Markets organizations. Additionally, you may be involved in supporting client technology organizations with front-to-back SDLC processes such as QA (e.g., SIT, UAT, Regression, Parallel), DevOps process configuration and execution, CI/CD pipeline development and production implementation tasks. Moreover, as a member of EY team, you will converse with Capital Markets buy side and sell side technology, operations and business clients on traded products and the end-to-end trade lifecycle. Your ability to understand business problems and advise on proposed technology solutions, summarize, and document technical discussions, translate technical decisions into business English language and communicate with client’s business stakeholders will be critical to success. In this role you will be part of the engagement teams with diverse skills and backgrounds, fostering an innovative and inclusive team-oriented work environment and provide constructive on-the-job feedback/coaching to team members. You will be an active member providing counselling and mentoring to junior consultants within the organization.

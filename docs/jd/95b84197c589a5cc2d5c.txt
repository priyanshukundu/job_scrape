Description Summary: The Credit Card Risk Analyst is responsible for the development, implementation, and ongoing monitoring of quantitative credit strategies for Huntington’s consumer credit card acquisition programs. This role helps develop account approval strategies, initial credit line assignment, and risk-based pricing strategies. This position also monitors the credit risk profile and performance of newly acquired accounts to ensure alignment with established portfolio loss and risk-adjusted return objectives. Duties and Responsibilities: Develop, implement, and conduct ongoing oversight of consumer credit card new account origination strategies, including segment-level performance reporting and change management. Design, execute, and evaluate policy experiments and A/B tests, translating analytical findings into actionable credit policy and strategy enhancements. Provide timely visibility into credit risk performance and emerging portfolio trends. Partner cross-functionally with Credit Administration, Underwriting, Fraud, Model Risk Management, Product, and Legal/Compliance to advance business priorities and ensure alignment with risk and regulatory requirements. Performs other duties as assigned.

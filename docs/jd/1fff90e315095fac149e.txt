COMPANY OVERVIEW KKR is a leading global investment firm that offers alternative asset management as well as capital markets and insurance solutions. KKR aims to generate attractive investment returns by following a patient and disciplined investment approach, employing world-class people, and supporting growth in its portfolio companies and communities. KKR sponsors investment funds that invest in private equity, credit and real assets and has strategic partners that manage hedge funds. KKR’s insurance subsidiaries offer retirement, life and reinsurance products under the management of Global Atlantic Financial Group. References to KKR’s investments may include the activities of its sponsored funds and insurance subsidiaries. TEAM OVERVIEW KKR’s Asset Liability Portfolio Services (ALPS) is an investment operations and portfolio oversight function supporting KKR’s complex investment platform, particularly its credit investments and insurance-related portfolios. The ALPS team provides the operational infrastructure, portfolio oversight, data integrity and control environment necessary to support KKR’s growing and increasingly complex investment platform. Through operational excellence, technology innovation and strong business partnerships, ALPS enables investment teams and finance organizations to operate efficiently, scale effectively and make informed decisions. ALPS operates within the investment operations infrastructure supporting KKR’s investment activities. Its work connects investment teams, portfolio management, fund accounting, controllership and other operational groups. POSITION SUMMARY KKR is seeking a Senior Analyst to join its Asset Liability Portfolio Services (ALPS) team based in Boston, Massachusetts. This role is a key contributor to the firm’s investment operations function, supporting full lifecycle management, reconciliation, and control of all portfolio positions within Geneva. The Senior Analyst will play a pivotal role in supporting the Fund Accounting and Controllership teams during monthly and quarterly closing, ensuring the accuracy and integrity of financial reporting. An ideal candidate will bring strong operational expertise, analytical rigor, and a proactive mindset to help optimize investment processes across a complex portfolio. Prior experience with Private Credit, Middle Market Loans, or Structured Products is preferred. The role requires exceptional communication skills, attention to detail, and the ability to thrive in a dynamic, high-performance environment. RESPONSIBILITIES Support end-to-end investment lifecycle processing, including trade capture, reconciliation, and settlement. Maintain and resolve cash and position reconciliation exceptions daily. Provide fund accounting support for monthly and quarterly financial close processes. Manage and oversee offshore operational support teams, ensuring adherence to process controls and SLAs. Monitor and maintain position reconciliations, ensuring accuracy and timeliness across portfolios. Partner with internal functional teams to provide operational support around complexity and product onboarding. Contribute to process improvement initiatives and operational excellence across the broader Investment Operations group. Corporate Action oversight and processing. Comfort using AI-powered tools to improve productivity and quality of work Curiosity about and openness to adopting emerging AI technologies in day-to-day workflows Ability to critically evaluate AI-generated outputs for accuracy and relevance QUALIFICATIONS Bachelor’s degree required, concentration in Finance, Accounting, Economics, or related field preferred. 2-4+ years of experience in Investment Operations or a related function within asset management, fund administration, or investment banking. Familiarity with Geneva and/or Wall Street Office preferred. Strong analytical, quantitative, and problem-solving skills. Highly organized with the ability to prioritize, multi-task, and meet deadlines in a fast-paced environment. Exceptional communication and interpersonal skills with the ability to collaborate effectively across teams.

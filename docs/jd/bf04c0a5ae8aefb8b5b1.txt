Senior AI Risk and Governance Lead
Pacific Life

Job Description: As a Senior AI Risk & Governance Lead, you’ll play a key role in Pacific Life’s growth and long-term success by advancing governance and independent risk oversight for artificial intelligence (AI), including generative AI and emerging AI capabilities. You will serve as a subject matter expert and work across business, technology, risk, compliance, legal, privacy, cybersecurity, data governance, and third-party risk functions to establish and operationalize a consistent approach for identifying, assessing, monitoring, and reporting AI-related risks throughout the AI lifecycle. The role requires specialized expertise, independent judgment, and the ability to translate rapidly evolving AI technologies, risks, regulations, and industry practices into practical risk management requirements and solutions. How you will make an impact: • Serve as a subject matter expert for AI risk and governance, providing independent risk guidance, challenge, and thought leadership related to AI, generative AI, agentic AI, machine learning, and other emerging capabilities. • Develop, maintain, and mature components of Pacific Life’s AI risk governance framework, including supporting methodologies, standards, guidance, risk taxonomy, assessment criteria, risk appetite and tolerance considerations, key risk indicators, controls, and reporting practices. • Establish and apply risk-based intake, assessment, and tiering approaches that determine the appropriate level of governance and oversight for AI use cases throughout development, acquisition, implementation, material change, ongoing use, and retirement. • Evaluate higher-risk or novel AI use cases for applicable operational, technology, cybersecurity, privacy, data, third-party, legal, compliance, resiliency, consumer, and reputational risks, and provide practical, risk-based recommendations to stakeholders and governance bodies. • Define and maintain AI risk and control expectations in coordination with applicable risk and control functions, integrating AI-related requirements into existing frameworks and processes where appropriate. • Support governance and oversight of the enterprise AI use-case inventory, including expectations for completeness, classification, risk assessment, approvals, ongoing monitoring, material changes, exceptions, and periodic review. • Develop AI risk metrics and reporting for senior management and governance committees; identify cross-enterprise risk themes, control gaps, emerging exposures, and recommended management actions. • Maintain a strategy and roadmap for maturing AI risk governance capabilities, including methods, workflows, tooling, monitoring, reporting, training, and integration with enterprise and operational risk practices. • Monitor evolving AI laws, regulations, supervisory guidance, frameworks, and industry practices, including the NIST AI Risk Management Framework and ISO/IEC 42001, and translate relevant developments into practical enhancements to internal governance and controls. • Lead complex, cross-functional AI governance initiatives, working groups, and assessments; coordinate with Internal Audit, Compliance, Legal, Privacy, Cybersecurity, Technology Risk, Data Governance, Third-Party Risk Management, and business and technology teams to promote clear accountability and reduce duplicative assurance activity. • Act as a resource for colleagues and stakeholders with less AI risk experience, facilitating resolution of complex or novel issues and promoting awareness of responsible AI and AI risk management principles across the organization. The experience you will bring: • 8+ years of experience in operational risk management, enterprise risk management, technology risk, cybersecurity, compliance, data governance, AI governance, or a related discipline, preferably within financial services or another highly regulated industry. • Bachelor’s degree or equivalent experience in risk management, business, computer science, information systems, data science, law, or a related field. • Demonstrated experience applying governance, risk assessment, control, issue management, monitoring, and reporting methodologies to complex business or technology risks. • Working knowledge of AI, machine learning, generative AI, and emerging technology risks, including relevant considerations involving data, privacy, cybersecurity, third parties, resiliency, legal and regulatory compliance, and responsible use. • Ability to work independently with minimal guidance, exercise sound judgment in areas where precedent may be limited, and recommend practical approaches using established risk methodologies and industry practices. • Experience leading complex cross-functional initiatives or functional teams and influencing stakeholders across business, technology, risk, and control functions without direct authority. • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with a demonstrated focus on accuracy, quality, and continuous improvement. • Excellent written and verbal communication skills, including the ability to translate complex AI, technology, and risk concepts for business, risk, and executive audiences. What will make you stand out: • Hands-on experience designing, implementing, or overseeing an AI risk governance framework, AI use-case assessment process, AI inventory, or responsible AI program. • Knowledge of AI governance standards and frameworks such as the NIST AI Risk Management Framework, ISO/IEC 42001, and financial-services AI risk management guidance. • Experience integrating AI risk governance with operational risk, cybersecurity, privacy, data governance, third-party risk, compliance, and enterprise risk management processes. • Relevant certifications such as CRISC, CISA, CISM, CISSP, FRM, or an AI governance or responsible AI credential. • Experience with Archer Integrated Risk Management Platform or comparable governance, risk, and compliance technology.

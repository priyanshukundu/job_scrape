About the role Join Affirm as a Machine Learning Engineering Manager and lead our Commerce Optimization Machine Learning team. You will manage a team of ML engineers that builds and scales models, simulations, and optimization systems to improve how Affirm’s products are configured and offered. Your team will use novel ML techniques and rich data representations to model consumer behavior, optimize pricing and financing offers, and drive better outcomes for consumers, merchants, and Affirm. Under your guidance, the team will develop reusable user behavior models that power applications across Affirm, from personalized experiences and targeted incentives to consumer engagement and growth. In this role, you will help shape the future of machine learning at Affirm, partnering with engineering, product, and risk leaders to design, implement, and scale advanced ML solutions that drive critical capabilities across the company. Your leadership will include mentoring engineers, bringing clarity to complex, ambiguous problems, and contributing to a cohesive long-term ML strategy. What you’ll do * Set the technical strategy for your team, and help your engineers tie it together with critical, business-impacting projects in our underwriting system * Act as a force multiplier through your definition and advocacy of technical solutions and operational processes. * Collaborate across teams in the product development lifecycle by partnering with product management, design, analytics, and risk to ensure technical sustainability, risks and trade-offs are well understood and managed. * Develop talent by providing feedback and guidance, and leading by example. What we look for * Bachelor's in a technical field with 8+ years of industry experience, including 3+ years managing engineers. * A background in financial services, specifically in credit / lending. * Proficiency in machine learning with experience in areas including tree-based models, transformers, deep learning, and agentic ML. * Strong engineering skills and the ability to provide hands-on technical leadership while working with our code and architecture.

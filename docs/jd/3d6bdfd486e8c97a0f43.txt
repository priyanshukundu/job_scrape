About this role: Wells Fargo is seeking a Mid-Corporate Portfolio Manager, Senior Analyst in the Commercial Banking group. This role will work as a key member of deal teams that provide underwriting to prepare recommendations to extend credit and monitor existing credit relationships. This Senior Analyst will support our clients with revenues from $500M to $2B primarily focused on our C&I group. #CommercialBanking In this role, you will: Participate in the underwriting to support moderate to high complex Mid-Corporate Portfolio clients Assist with financial modeling and projections, credit analysis, and industry or business risk analysis, in support of loan structuring decision making activities for Mid-Corporate sized companies Support Senior Portfolio Managers in credit underwriting, which accounts for all risk factors and providing risk rating recommendations. Present recommendations for resolving complex situations and exercise independent judgement while developing expertise in the function, policies, procedures, and compliance requirements. General responsibilities include working with Mid Corporate Portfolio Management, relationship managers and credit approvers to originate, monitor and grow Mid-Corporate relationships. Specifically accountable for credit underwriting and origination, portfolio monitoring, renewals and annual reviews. Manage and promote the maintenance of a portfolio that adheres to all applicable credit policies and compliance requirements.

ROLE SUMMARY The Associate, AIA Strategy provides analytical and project support to the cross-portfolio strategic initiatives that shape how Pfizer's U.S. Commercial organization deploys AI. The role helps frame problems, structure analysis, build executive-ready materials, and keep initiatives moving, turning ambiguous cross-functional questions into clear, well-organized work that informs portfolio decisions. Reporting to the Senior Director, AI Strategy Lead, AIA, the Associate works closely with members of the Strategy & Portfolio team and partners across the broader AI Acceleration (AIA) organization. The role offers broad exposure to the portfolio and leadership team, with increasing ownership of workstreams and deliverables over time. WHY THIS ROLE MATTERS Cross-portfolio strategic initiatives are how AI Acceleration turns strategy into coordinated action across Product Management, Data, Tech, and Transformation. As the volume and ambition of that work grows, the Strategy & Portfolio team needs dedicated analytical capacity to keep it well-structured, rigorous, and moving. ROLE RESPONSIBILITIES 1. Strategic Initiative Support • Help frame problems and structure workstreams for cross-portfolio strategic initiatives. • Build and maintain project plans, timelines, and action trackers; keep initiatives on schedule. • Prepare agendas, pre-reads, and follow-ups for working sessions and leadership reviews. • Surface risks, dependencies, and open decisions early so they can be resolved. 2. Analysis and Synthesis • Gather, organize, and synthesize data, research, and stakeholder input into clear findings. • Build analyses and simple frameworks that inform portfolio prioritization and investment decisions. • Turn ambiguous or messy inputs into structured, decision-ready outputs. • Support value and ROI tracking by assembling and quality-checking inputs. 3. Executive-Ready Materials • Own specific deliverables end-to-end, including decks, briefs, and summaries, to a high standard. • Translate complex analysis into clear, concise materials for senior and executive audiences. • Maintain a consistent, on-brand standard across Strategy & Portfolio outputs. 4. Portfolio and Operating-Model Support • Help maintain the portfolio view, roadmap, and planning cadence, including trackers and operating rhythm. • Support preparation for portfolio council, planning, and leadership-review cycles. • Keep shared documentation, inputs, and records accurate and current. 5. Cross-Functional Coordination • Coordinate across the AI Acceleration leadership team — Product Management, Data, Tech, and Transformation — to gather inputs and move initiatives forward. • Build strong working relationships that help the team operate as the connective layer across the portfolio. • Communicate clearly and reliably with partners at all levels. 6. Learning and Growth • Take on stretch assignments that build strategic, analytical, and leadership skills. • Grow in scope, autonomy, and ownership over the first 12 to 18 months. SUCCESS MEASURES • Quality and relevance of horizon-scanning insights and strategic recommendations. • External technology opportunities converted into pilots, partnership recommendations, product concepts, or roadmap priorities. • Speed and rigor of opportunity evaluation and disposition. • Adoption of recommendations in U.S. Commercial planning and investment decisions. • Influence on future Commercial Engine and applied AI priorities. • Quality of external capability assessments and partnership recommendations advanced to the AI Partnerships & Ecosystem Lead. • Clarity and transparency of the innovation opportunity pipeline. QUALIFICATIONS Must-Have • Bachelor’s degree in computer science, engineering, artificial intelligence, data science, business, or a related discipline, or equivalent professional experience. • Internship or coursework in management consulting, corporate strategy, or product and portfolio analysis. • Interest in, or exposure to, AI and its commercial applications. • Familiarity with pharmaceuticals, healthcare, life sciences, or another regulated industry. • Comfort with data-analysis tools and modern productivity and AI tools. • Demonstrated leadership in academic, extracurricular, or early professional settings. Nice-to-Have • Prior Pfizer Futures 2026 internship experience strongly preferred. • Internship or coursework in management consulting, corporate strategy, commercial operations or product and portfolio analysis. • Interest in, or exposure to, AI and its commercial applications. • Familiarity with pharmaceuticals, healthcare, life sciences, or another regulated industry. • Comfort with data-analysis tools and modern productivity and AI tools. • Demonstrated leadership in academic, extracurricular, or early professional settings. TRAVEL AND WORK REQUIREMENTS • Limited travel anticipated based on business and team needs. • Ability to work across cross-functional and global teams. • Hybrid presence aligned with Pfizer workplace expectations.

Associate Director, Discovery Analytics

Job Description: Location: Bellevue, WA Hybrid: 3 days per week in office Our Opportunity Millions of pet parents turn to Chewy to find the right products for their pets. Our Discovery team brings together Product, Engineering, Data Science, and Analytics to improve how customers discover those products through search, personalized recommendations, and conversational experiences. We are seeking an Associate Director, Discovery Analytics to lead a team of BI engineers responsible for measurement, experimentation, and business insights across Discovery. You will own the analytics roadmap and its execution, develop your team, and translate complex findings into clear recommendations for product and business leaders. This role combines technical depth with business judgment to ensure that Discovery teams can make informed decisions and measure the results. What You'll Do Lead and develop a team of BI engineers. Set priorities, assign ownership, manage performance, and coach team members to strengthen their technical skills and business judgment. Own the Discovery analytics roadmap over a 12- to 18-month horizon. Translate business priorities into analytical initiatives, align resources and dependencies, and remain accountable for timely delivery. Define and govern Discovery metrics, from session-level engagement to customer-level outcomes. Align partner teams on definitions and analytical methods so performance is measured consistently. Manage the development of dashboards, reporting, and self-service analytics. Improve their usability and lead business reviews that explain performance, surface opportunities, and support decisions. Set measurement standards for Discovery experiments. Partner with Data Science and the experimentation platform team on A/B test design, long-running holdouts, and readouts that explain results and limitations. Partner with Product, Engineering, Data Science, and category teams to frame business questions, size opportunities, and develop business cases that inform the Discovery roadmap. Oversee scalable data models, pipelines, and a governed semantic layer that support reusable analysis and consistent reporting across Discovery. Implement data collection and quality standards, including validation, documentation, code review, and monitoring. Ensure the team applies these practices consistently. Introduce new analytics technologies and AI-assisted workflows that shorten time to insight and improve self-service access. Establish review practices that maintain the reliability of code, data, and findings. Communicate findings and recommendations to VP and senior leadership audiences. Represent Discovery Analytics in cross-functional planning and contribute to broader BI and experimentation initiatives. What You'll Need BA/BS in Computer Science, Engineering, Mathematics, Statistics, Economics, or a related quantitative field 10+ years of experience in analytics, BI engineering, or data science 2+ years managing analytics or BI teams through hiring, performance management, and career development. Expert SQL and deep experience with data modeling and ETL/ELT in cloud data warehouses such as Snowflake or Redshift, with the technical depth to review code and set engineering standards. Hands-on experience designing and interpreting A/B tests and holdouts, including evaluating conflicting metrics and effects that change over time. A track record of owning metrics for a product or business area, from definition through executive reporting, ideally in e-commerce or experiences driven by ranking algorithms. Experience developing BI roadmaps and self-service analytics products with tools such as Tableau. Demonstrated ability to influence Director, VP, and senior leadership audiences by framing business problems, explaining complex findings, and making clear recommendations. Ability to lead through ambiguity, clarify requirements, and coordinate work across teams when the business question, data, or ownership is not yet defined. Proficiency in Python or R. Bonus MS or PhD in a quantitative field. Experience measuring search relevance, ranking, or recommendation systems, including NDCG, interleaving, null-search rates, or reformulation rates. Background in causal inference for long-term outcomes such as customer lifetime value and incrementality. Experience building AI-powered analytics tools, such as text-to-SQL, Snowflake Cortex applications, MCP servers, or agent workflows. Familiarity with Airflow, dbt, and other AWS ecosystem tools. Experience with conversational analytics or natural language processing, including chatbots, virtual assistants, or conversation-level event modeling.

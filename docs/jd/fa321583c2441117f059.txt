Introduction to the Team Expedia Group Advertising helps Expedia Group and its partners connect with travelers and understand the impact of advertising across Expedia Group brands and partner channels. Within Emerging Business Technology, the Measurement, Attribution & Data Platform organization builds the capabilities that turn advertising interactions and outcomes into reliable measurements, attribution, and insights. As a Software Development Engineer II, you will contribute to the backend services and data platforms that support this work—from ingesting and processing advertising and conversion data to powering measurement and reporting experiences. The systems you build help advertisers and internal teams understand campaign performance and make informed decisions. You’ll work with engineers, product partners, and analytics stakeholders on distributed systems where data quality, timeliness, scale, and operational reliability matter. We’re looking for an engineer who takes ownership, delivers thoughtfully, and cares about building maintainable systems that work reliably in production. You’ll have opportunities to contribute across the development lifecycle, improve existing platforms, and help evolve the way Expedia Group measures advertising outcomes. In this role, you will: Lead software development engineering teams in delivering reliable, scalable products and services that address complex business and customer needs. Set technical direction across a domain or multiple services, guiding system design, low-level design, API design, data modeling, and implementation decisions. Build a high-performing engineering organization through coaching, talent development, effective planning, and clear accountability for technical outcomes. Drive operational excellence by establishing strong practices for reliability, quality, security, observability, incident response, and continuous improvement. Partner across product, design, data, and other technical disciplines to translate strategy into measurable delivery outcomes and make data-informed engineering decisions. Safely integrate and operate AI/ML-enabled solutions that improve outcomes, while developing familiarity with AI-driven systems, tools, and workflows and applying AI/ML concepts to real-world products. Minimum Qualifications: Bachelor’s Degree or Equivalent Level; Technical Degree Preferred. 8+ years of relevant professional experience and 3+ years of people management experience. Experience owning technical outcomes across a domain or multiple related services, with accountability for architecture, delivery, reliability, and operational performance. Strong knowledge of software engineering fundamentals, system design, low-level design, API design, data modeling, testing, deployment, and production operations. Demonstrated ability to set technical direction, lead through ambiguity, and develop engineers and engineering leaders. Preferred Qualifications: Experience leading complex, distributed systems and engineering organizations operating at significant scale. Track record of improving engineering effectiveness through automation, observability, reliability practices, and data-driven operational decision-making. Experience establishing architecture direction across multiple services or a broad technical domain while balancing customer value, technical quality, and delivery speed. Experience applying AI/ML concepts to production software, including evaluating, integrating, and safely operating AI/ML-enabled solutions. Demonstrated ability to use AI-driven development tools or workflows to improve engineering productivity, product quality, or operational outcomes.

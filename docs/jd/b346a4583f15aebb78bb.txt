Quality Control Automation Senior Associate
JPMorgan Chase
Location: Wilmington, DE, United States
Link: https://jpmc.fa.oraclecloud.com/hcmUI/CandidateExperience/en/sites/CX_1001/job/210808386

The Email Quality Control team provides independent pre-production validation of marketing email campaigns to ensure they are built accurately to approved requirements before release. The team executes defined quality reviews, identifies defects, validates remediation, and maintains evidence of results to support campaign quality and control adherence. As a Quality Control Automation Senior Associate on the Email Quality Control team, you will design, build, and enhance automation across the email build and test lifecycle—from requirements and template development through testing, defect remediation, approval, and release readiness. You will combine strong programming skills, reusable automation framework development, and practical knowledge of artificial intelligence (AI) to make email production and testing faster, more accurate, and more efficient. You will provide technical leadership and partner with email developers, testers, business stakeholders, and technology teams to strengthen email quality, reduce manual effort and rework, and improve delivery speed. Job Responsibilities: Lead the automation strategy and roadmap for email testing, prioritizing opportunities across requirements intake, template development, content assembly, validation, approval, and release readiness based on business risk and expected value. Design, develop, and maintain reusable automation frameworks to validate email templates, content, links, images, personalization, dynamic content, and rendering across relevant email clients and devices. Write, review, and troubleshoot automation code that supports reusable email components, build checks, test data preparation, regression testing, and actionable test reporting; establish standards for reliability and maintainability. Evaluate and apply approved AI tools to accelerate email build checks and test case creation, generate and refine automation code, identify content or rendering discrepancies, and assist with defect analysis. Validate AI-generated outputs against approved email requirements, content, and expected results, with human review and appropriate data privacy, security, and governance controls; retain required approvals before release. Improve email build and test efficiency by reducing repetitive manual checks, flaky tests, false positives, escaped defects, rework, and automation maintenance effort. Partner with email developers, testers, business stakeholders, and technology teams to define acceptance criteria, client and device coverage, personalization scenarios, defect resolution priorities, and documented evidence for approval and release readiness. Provide technical guidance and mentoring, document reusable email build and test patterns, and promote consistent automation practices across templates and campaigns. Track and communicate improvements in email build and test cycle time, automated coverage, first-pass approval rates, defect detection, test reliability, and manual effort. Required qualifications, capabilities and skills: Strong understanding of programming concepts and hands-on proficiency in one or more languages such as Python, Java, JavaScript, or TypeScript, together with working knowledge of HTML and CSS for email development. Demonstrated experience building and extending test automation frameworks for email production workflows, including reusable validations, test data management, reporting, and environment configuration. Hands-on experience with automation tools such as Playwright, Selenium, Cypress, pytest, or comparable technologies, and experience using email rendering and validation tools. Solid understanding of the email build and test lifecycle, including requirements, template and component development, content and link validation, accessibility checks, cross-client rendering, personalization and fallback testing, regression testing, defect remediation, approval, and release readiness. Working knowledge of AI and generative AI, including their limitations, and practical experience applying AI to improve email build and testing speed, accuracy, or efficiency. Ability to critically review AI-generated code and test cases against approved email specifications, verify correctness, and measure improvements against a baseline. Experience with version control, code reviews, automated build and test pipelines, API integration, and debugging email build or automation failures. Demonstrated ability to lead technical initiatives, coordinate work across teams, and communicate technical concepts clearly to business and technology audiences. Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with attention to quality, traceability, and responsible handling of test data. Preferred qualifications, capabilities and skills: Experience implementing AI-assisted email testing workflows, including prompt design, output validation, visual comparison, and repeatable evaluation. Experience with email production or deployment platforms, templating and dynamic content logic, and modernizing automation across multiple email templates or campaigns. Experience working in complex enterprise or regulated environments.

About the Role THE POSITION The Investment Implementation and Trading department is seeking to recruit a Risk Analyst to work on our IIT Risk team in Boston. The team’s role is to minimize risk by implementing best-in-class, first-line risk management practices throughout the order implementation and trade execution ecosystems. Risks include financial, operational, regulatory, compliance, reputational, and business. RESPONSIBILITIES The Implementation and Trading Risk Analyst’s primary responsibility is to identify, assess, monitor and mitigate risks across Investment Implementation and Trading by partnering with business teams to strengthen controls, enhance supervision and support effective risk-based decision-making. Identify and assess risks and potential control gaps and partner with business teams to design practical controls, strengthen processes and drive remediation. Analyze incidents and other risk data to identify root causes, recurring themes and emerging risks and develop actionable recommendations. Develop and monitor key risk indicators, supervisory reporting and other data to identify trends, concentrations and changes in the department's risk profile, escalating potential areas of concern and remediation. Support the Investment Implementation and Trading Leadership Team by monitoring adherence to policies, procedures and established controls and evaluating whether controls are operating effectively. Conduct focused risk assessments of workflows, new initiatives and areas of elevated risk, translating findings into clear recommendations for business leaders. Participate in the development of a new first line risk framework that will raise awareness with senior leaders re our risk profile, provide data to make risk based decisions, take on a variety of ad hoc projects that include establishing the approach to keep stakeholders up to date Assess risks associated with business change, including new products, processes, technologies and workflows, and partner with stakeholders to identify appropriate controls and mitigate risk prior to implementation.

Become a part of our caring community Launch your software engineering career with a team that values innovation, learning, and real-world impact. Whether you're a recent graduate or an early-career professional, this role offers the opportunity to build technical expertise, solve meaningful business challenges, and contribute to software solutions used by thousands of users. You'll work alongside experienced engineers, gain hands-on experience across the software development lifecycle, and develop the skills needed to grow your career in a collaborative and supportive environment. The Big Data Software Engineer is responsible for developing, testing, maintaining, and improving software solutions that support business objectives. This role ensures software quality through rigorous testing, debugging, issue resolution, and continuous enhancement of system performance and reliability. The ideal candidate understands how their work contributes to broader organizational goals, exercises sound judgment in problem-solving, and collaborates effectively across teams. Key Responsibilities Design, develop, test, and maintain high-quality software applications and systems. Establish and uphold software quality assurance standards, processes, and best practices. Identify, troubleshoot, and resolve complex software defects, bugs, and performance issues. Analyze technical problems and recommend corrective actions to improve software functionality and user experience. Collaborate with cross-functional teams, including product management, quality assurance, and business stakeholders, to deliver effective solutions. Translate business and operational requirements into technical designs and software features. Ensure software solutions align with departmental, segment, and organizational strategies and objectives. Monitor application performance and proactively identify opportunities for optimization and automation. Follow established development standards, processes, and governance requirements while contributing ideas for continuous improvement. Exercise independent judgment in completing assignments, navigating occasional ambiguity, and escalating issues when appropriate. Required Qualifications Bachelor’s Degree in Computer Science or related field. 1-2 years of experience in systems analysis or application programming development internships. Experience participating in continuous improvement efforts, with examples of measurable improvements to customer outcomes, quality, efficiency, or satisfaction metrics. Experience in Cloud-based data ELT/ETL or classroom training. Experience in PySpark or Python development. Experience in SQL writing and tuning. Preferred Qualifications Master’s Degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering or related field. Experience in Databricks development.

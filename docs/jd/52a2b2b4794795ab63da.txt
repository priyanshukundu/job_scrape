About this role Company: BlackRock Financial Management, Inc. Job Title: Associate, Capital Market Data Services Location: 100 Bellevue Parkway, Wilmington, DE 19809 Job Duties: Model new holdings for terms and conditions as well as time series data in the Aladdin Investment System for Blackrock and BRS clients. Partner closely with business analysts, developers, and technology teams on innovative initiatives, including the testing and validation of AI agents, to strengthen automation, improve data quality controls, and reduce operating risk. Partner with business analysts and developers in innovation to improve the Aladdin product, build process efficiency and scale, improve data quality, reduce operating risk, and navigate the changing market. Perform ad hoc data analysis for key projects, sales opportunities, and new client implementations. Resolve Security and Issuer modeling related issues for partner groups in the investment decision process, including trade confirmation, projected versus realized cash flows, risk analytics, compliance and regulatory adherence, collateral management, credit exposures and others. Support DIG’s broader mandate to enhance efficiency, improve data quality, and adapt to evolving market and product needs. Support the integrity, accuracy, and scalability of security, issuer, and credit reference data within the Aladdin Investment System. Use Bloomberg (BBG) terminals and interacting with banks and external counterparties to validate issuance details, market data, and ratings information to ensure accurate and timely data onboarding for BlackRock and BRS clients. This process requires a strong familiarity with financial documents and prospectuses, as well as the proper handling of this sensitive information. Executed end-to-end allocation & trade booking for primary market deals within CMDS, including order allocation across portfolios, trade execution in Aladdin, and post-booking validation. Regional Head, U.S. Capital Market Data Services (Publics) – Define and execute the strategic roadmap for the U.S. Publics function, leading a team of five (four direct reports), optimizing resource allocation, overseeing onboarding of new asset classes into CMDS, and partnering with cross-functional stakeholders to drive operational excellence, scalability, and business growth. Usage of AI tool, Docuflow to create New Issued Security Setups. This is a document scrubbing tool for asset creation it extracts and structures data from deal documents/prospectuses so CMDS can create and quality-check assets more efficiently. Qualifications: Bachelor's degree in Engineering, Technology, Computer Science, Finance, or a related field and 36 months of experience in the job offered or as an Associate, Analyst, or a related role. Must have three (3) years of experience with: SQL and Python for automation and process enhancement; and Workflow Automation including end to end automation, creation of tools to assist with process enhancement, and unit testing analysis. Must have two (2) years of experience with: Bloomberg terminal functions including Data validation, intra-bank communication, and Primary Market interactions; Fixed Income Data Modeling including modeling corporate, government, and municipal bonds, producing analytics, and troubleshooting data errors; Financial Payments experience including Cash reconciliation between firm and counterparties, payment validation, and escalation protocols for high value breaks; and Financial Legal Document Analysis including reviewing and extracting information from prospectuses, offering memorandums, term sheets, and credit agreement.

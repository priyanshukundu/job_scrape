Job Description Provide operational and/or financial risk management by leading the organization in critical risk control development and day-to-day risk oversight. Provide project support and lead process reviews identifying risk and developing solutions while maintaining regulatory and supervisory controls. Serve as escalation point of contact/ risk liaison by working closely with business leaders, staff, and external partners to ensure appropriate information exchange and to drive forward risk reducing recommendations and actions. The successful candidate will leverage Microsoft Power Platform tools, including Power Apps, Power Automate, and Power BI, to develop practical business solutions that streamline processes, automate manual activities, improve reporting, and enhance risk oversight. This individual will combine strong risk management and analytical skills with the ability to translate business needs into effective workflow, reporting, and automation solutions. Key Responsibilities Create and maintain reports, dashboards, workflows, and business tools that support risk management, governance, and operational activities. Develop and maintain Microsoft Power Apps, Power Automate, and Power BI to improve reporting, streamline manual processes, and enhance business oversight. Facilitate alignment, resolve escalated issues and provide a point of contact for business and external senior leaders. Identify and reduce risk through system and process validation, ensure system reporting and systems displays are accurate and compliant. Provide expertise to other areas for enhancement and implementation of Self –Monitoring plans. Ensure regulatory and supervisory controls are in place for products and processes by acting as a point of contact for internal, external business partners, such as Compliance, Legal, Products, and senior leaders. Direct and guide initiatives by validating applicable SEC or FINRA rules to business processes and enhancing processes or documentation as necessary. Review reports illustrating risk parameters and metrics associated with business lines. Ensure data sources have high integrity and appropriate risk measures are used. Mitigate risk by developing and/or monitoring operational metrics across business. Initiate and/or conduct Risk Assessments across business. Develop and enhance risk measurement policies, procedures and metrics. Enhance risk reporting with new ideas gathered from research, best practices, and knowledge. Lead operational and regulatory audits (i.e. NASD/SEC, FINRA, Sarbanes Oxley). Required Qualifications Experience creating, enhancing, and supporting Microsoft Power Apps and Power Automate solutions to automate workflows, manage business processes, and improve operational effectiveness. Education: Bachelors degree or equivalent. Experience: 3-5 years of relevant experience. Strong analytical, organizational, and time management skills. Strong ability to lead, partner, and influence across all leadership levels. Excellent written and verbal communication skills. Ability to drive results and meet deadlines to reduce risks. Must be able to work independently. Demonstrated people leadership experience, either through direct leadership or coaching/mentoring roles. Preferred Qualifications Experience in financial services, banking, broker-dealer, investment advisory, insurance, or other regulated environments. Knowledge of operational risk management, issue management, controls, audit, compliance, or governance practices. Experience improving business processes and reducing manual activities through workflow or reporting enhancements. Ability to translate business needs into practical and sustainable process improvements. Experience collaborating with business leaders, control partners, and support functions.

We are now looking for a Senior Research Scientist! NVIDIA is seeking a Senior Research Scientist to drive groundbreaking research at the intersection of AI, GPU computing, and Electronic Design Automation (EDA). AI and GPU acceleration are transforming the future of chip design, and this role offers the opportunity to shape that future. What you'll be doing: Define and conduct original research across EDA algorithms, VLSI design methodology, and advanced machine learning techniques. Innovate in EDA software and algorithms, with applications spanning supervised, unsupervised, and reinforcement learning, as well as GPU-accelerated optimization methods. Collaborate cross-functionally with circuit design, VLSI, and architecture teams, ensuring research translates into real-world product impact. Engage with the global research community by publishing in premier conferences, presenting at leading venues, and driving thought leadership. Foster collaboration with external researchers and diverse internal product teams to amplify research outcomes. What we need to see: Research Excellence: PhD in Computer Science, Electrical/Computer Engineering, or related field (or equivalent experience), with 2+ years of post-PhD research experience and a strong record of impactful publications in top EDA and AI/ML venues; proven ability to set research direction and translate ideas into tools or products. Domain & Technical Expertise: Deep knowledge in EDA/VLSI (e.g., synthesis, formal verification, timing, reliability, or physical design algorithms) combined with 5+ years applying AI techniques (supervised, unsupervised, RL) and GPU/accelerator-based computing to large-scale problems. Programming & Systems Skills: 5+ years of experience in software development; strong expertise in high-performance computing, parallelism, optimization, and large-scale software engineering. Leadership & Collaboration: experience in leading research projects or teams, with a track record of mentoring junior scientists, driving cross-functional initiatives with circuits/VLSI/architecture groups, and fostering collaborations with academia or industry. Communication & Impact: Excellent written and verbal communication skills, with demonstrated experience presenting at top conferences; track record of releasing high-impact open-source tools, datasets, or frameworks; ability to influence both technical and non-technical audiences.

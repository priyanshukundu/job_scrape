The role As a Compliance Associate Senior - Global Regulatory Reporting, you’ll prepare and deliver financial and regulatory reporting and support governance-related activities for Capital Group funds. You’ll analyze complex financial and regulatory information, reconcile data across reporting sources, investigate variances, and help ensure key deliverables are accurate, high quality, and on time. Working across teams, you’ll also contribute to process improvements that strengthen reporting and support Capital Group’s long-term approach to managing regulatory obligations. The team You’ll join Global Regulatory Reporting, a team responsible for fulfilling regulatory reporting obligations for Capital Group funds. The team prepares regulatory filings and reporting, supports materials for Board and Audit Committee meetings, and partners across the organization to analyze financial and regulatory information. Your work will help maintain accurate reporting, strong governance support, and dependable execution across recurring and ad hoc requirements. Your impact Perform financial and regulatory reporting activities of varying complexity to support accurate and timely deliverables. Prepare, analyze, and document transaction and reporting data so reporting requirements are supported by clear, reliable information. Reconcile accounts, source data, and internal reporting systems, investigating and resolving variances when outputs do not align. Calculate fees, expenses, and financial reporting metrics with accuracy and attention to reporting requirements. Prepare and support monthly, quarterly, annual, and ad hoc regulatory reporting requirements and related governance deliverables. Analyze fund financial statements and regulatory disclosures to identify trends, risks, inconsistencies, and reporting impacts. Process corrections identified through quality reviews and recommend improvements that strengthen reporting processes and controls. Support preparation of Board and Audit Committee materials and other governance-related reporting. What you’ll bring A strong accounting or financial reporting background, with experience working with complex financial information. Ability to analyze financial and regulatory information, identify trends and inconsistencies, and investigate reporting variances. Experience reconciling accounts, source data, and reporting outputs with strong attention to accuracy and detail. Ability to organize financial and regulatory data logically and document reporting information clearly. Ability to work independently while managing multiple recurring and ad hoc deliverables in a deadline-driven environment. Ability to partner effectively across teams to support reporting, governance, and continuous process improvement. What you’ll get When you go the distance for our clients, we go the distance for you.

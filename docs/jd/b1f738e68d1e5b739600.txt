Turner & Townsend are seeking an experienced and highly collaborative Associate Director, Accounting - Special Projects to lead strategic accounting and finance initiatives across our organization. This role is designed for a strong accounting leader who enjoys solving complex problems, improving processes, and leading initiatives that span Accounting, Finance, Operations, and Technology. Working within a dynamic, project-based professional services environment, the AD will take ownership of high-priority initiatives involving project accounting, revenue recognition, finance transformation, acquisitions and integrations, financial systems, internal controls, and process improvement. The Associate Director will play a critical role in translating complex accounting and operational challenges into practical, scalable solutions. This position will also provide leadership and strategic direction to the Finance Systems team, including oversight of Microsoft Dynamics 365 Finance, ensuring our systems and processes continue to evolve with the needs of the business. What You'll Do Lead Strategic Accounting & Finance Initiatives Lead complex, high-priority accounting and finance projects from assessment and solution design through implementation and stabilization. Serve as the accounting lead for strategic business initiatives, organizational changes, new business processes, and other non-routine activities. Evaluate the accounting, reporting, process, system, and internal control implications of proposed business changes. Develop practical, scalable solutions that balance accounting requirements, operational needs, strong controls, and business objectives. Bring structure and discipline to complex initiatives by establishing clear objectives, responsibilities, milestones, risks, and deliverables. Provide senior leadership with clear recommendations and visibility into key decisions, risks, and project progress. Drive Accounting Process Improvement & Transformation Identify opportunities to simplify, standardize, and automate Accounting and Finance processes. Lead initiatives designed to reduce manual processes, recurring adjustments, reconciliations, and workarounds. Develop future-state processes that improve the accuracy, timeliness, and consistency of financial information. Partner with process owners to establish clear responsibilities, handoffs, controls, and governance. Lead remediation of complex accounting and process issues, with an emphasis on identifying and resolving root causes. Ensure solutions are effectively transitioned into sustainable day-to-day processes. Support Acquisitions, Integrations & Business Change Lead Accounting and Finance workstreams associated with acquisitions, integrations, restructurings, and other significant business changes. Coordinate the integration of accounting processes, financial data, project structures, reporting requirements, and controls. Identify accounting and financial reporting risks associated with integrations and establish appropriate solutions and remediation plans. Support financial data conversion, reconciliation, process transition, and post-implementation stabilization. Partner with business leaders to transition acquired or legacy processes into standardized Finance processes. Provide Accounting & Controllership Leadership Provide accounting guidance for non-routine transactions, emerging business issues, and complex accounting matters. Partner with Controllership and Financial Reporting to ensure appropriate application of U.S. GAAP and company accounting policies. Assist in developing accounting methodologies, policies, procedures, and supporting documentation. Ensure changes resulting from strategic initiatives are appropriately incorporated into ongoing accounting and financial reporting processes. Embed appropriate internal controls into new and redesigned Finance processes. Support remediation of control deficiencies, reconciliation issues, and accounting process gaps. Provide Finance Systems Leadership Provide strategic leadership and direction to the Finance Systems team. Ensure financial system priorities are aligned with broader Accounting and Finance objectives. Provide Finance leadership for significant system implementations, integrations, enhancements, and process changes, including initiatives involving Microsoft Dynamics 365 Finance. Ensure accounting requirements and internal controls are appropriately incorporated into system solutions. Promote technology solutions that simplify Finance processes, reduce manual activity, and improve the quality and availability of financial information. Lead Across the Organization Build strong partnerships across Accounting, Finance, Operations, Project Management, IT, and other business functions. Translate complex accounting and financial concepts into practical business solutions. Facilitate decisions where accounting requirements, operational objectives, technology, and internal controls intersect. Effectively communicate complex issues, risks, alternatives, and recommendations to senior leadership. Lead and develop team members while fostering a culture of accountability, collaboration, continuous improvement, and strong financial stewardship. Bachelor's degree in Accounting, Finance, or a related field. Progressive leadership experience in accounting, controllership, finance transformation, or a related discipline. Strong knowledge of U.S. GAAP, financial reporting, accounting operations, and internal controls. Demonstrated experience leading complex, cross-functional Accounting and Finance initiatives. Demonstrated ability to identify complex problems, determine root causes, and implement sustainable solutions. Ability to work effectively across Accounting, Finance, Operations, and Technology teams. Strong project management, analytical, problem-solving, organizational, written, and verbal communication skills. Preferred Qualifications CPA or equivalent professional accounting qualification. Experience in a project-based professional services, engineering, consulting, or similar environment. Experience with ASC 606 revenue recognition in a project-based organization. Experience leading acquisition integrations, ERP implementations, finance transformations, or significant process-improvement initiatives. Experience with Microsoft Dynamics 365 Finance or another large-scale ERP environment. Experience working within a multi-entity or geographically dispersed organization. Experience with SOX, internal controls, and financial systems governance. *SOX control responsibilities may be part of this role, which are to be adhered to where applicable.

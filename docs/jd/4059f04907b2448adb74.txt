Job title : NHS Engagement and Access Associate – East of England/London Regions: East Midlands including Leicester, Northampton and East of England Contract type: Fixed-Term, 12-months About the job As the NHS Engagement and Access Manager (NEAM), you will be responsible for developing and implementing strategies to ensure the successful market access of healthcare products and services within the NHS at a Sub-National level. You will work closely with internal teams, external stakeholders, and Senior NHS decision-makers to navigate the complexities of the healthcare system and maximize product adoption. Main responsibilities: Knowledge of the commercial environment The NHS Engagement and Access Manager is predominantly a customer-facing role, with an emphasis upon the ability to interact, maintain and influence relationships in hybrid formats as required by the customer or healthcare organization. Develops and maintains productive and sustained relationships with Senior Decision makers within the NHS, who have the capability to influence, promote or inhibit Sanofi business at a subnational level (e.g. appropriate roles within ICBs, Subnational Networks, Regional NHSE ABPI & HINS). To be an expert in terms of the Specialist Care environment and the changing NHS by understanding national and subnational priorities and how Sanofi may support the NHS with implementation. A strong understanding of Integrated care & Specialist commissioning is a must. Works with all relevant functions within Sanofi in terms of head office and field based to help shape strategy in line with NHS priorities in order to accelerate product access and deliver on brand priorities at a National and subnational level. Analise and support market analysis to identify trends, opportunities, and challenges within the NHS landscape, in order to inform strategic decision-making and resource allocation. Develop and implement access plans tailored to specific NHS regions or specialties, ensuring alignment with local priorities and requirements. Policy and Market Access Advocacy: Monitor policy developments and advocate for policies that support improved market access within the NHS, engaging with relevant stakeholders and industry associations. Develop comprehensive market access strategies aligned with organizational goals and NHS priorities to facilitate the successful adoption of healthcare products and services. Monitor and evaluate the performance of market access strategies, using key performance indicators (KPIs) to track progress and identify areas for optimization. Lead on partnership opportunities and commercial connections The role will combine a regionally defined priority territory alongside a national flexible resource dependent on business demand and potential (determined by MA leadership) Develop and lead on Collaborative/Joint/Project in line with relevant Sanofi SOPs. Effective prioritization and application of bespoke value propositions based on the segmentation of key accounts. Provide expert assessment of our customers’ needs and based on in-depth analysis, develop and deliver effective solutions. Anticipates future NHS Service needs necessary for the Implementation of Sanofi Portfolio. Provide expert project management support to the NHS as well as effective project assessments, to deliver on agreed project outcomes / goals. To conduct relevant non-promotional activity in line with briefing documents i.e. Advanced Planning Notification, Strategic Account Mapping based on potential. Communicate an understanding of customers’ current and future needs as well as the health economic environment to colleagues across Sanofi (i.e. Value and Access, Heads of sales and Marketing, Franchise heads etc.) to ensure future business planning reflects current and future needs of the local/Regional/National healthcare environment as well as providing insights to support resource deployment to accelerate improvements and efficiency gains Responsible for upholding the integrity of Sanofi's vision and aligning this with the strategy of the NHS. About you Skills, Experience & Knowledge requirements: Educated to degree level or equivalent, preferably with a science background. Detailed understanding of ABPI Code of Practice and its practical application in a business environment. ABPI qualified. Detailed knowledge of the NHS, its agenda, the local resulting changes / needs and how these link to Company goals. Detailed knowledge of NHS Commissioning and finances. Demonstrable experience in delivering commercial and patient outcomes through partnership with the NHS. Demonstrated ability to influence relationships and leverage knowledge internally across a matrix structure, to optimize opportunities with customers and achieve shared goals. Proven commercial acumen, with clear understanding of the business market dynamics and the ability to accurately assess and convey market access intelligence to shape plans and targets. Detailed understanding of ABPI Code of Practice and its practical application in a business environment. Ability to assess business opportunities rapidly and accurately. Demonstrated flair and courage for proposing creative solutions and taking calculated risks. Demonstrated strength in negotiating with customers and associated stakeholders to drive mutually beneficial solutions. Experience of successfully generating sales opportunities and patient outcomes by developing partnerships with NHS organizations and (e.g. ICS's in England) Understand the current NHS structure, its financial and decision-making flows and provide evidence in ability to alter these.

About us Coalition is the world's first Active Insurance provider designed to help prevent digital risk before it strikes. Founded in 2017, Coalition combines comprehensive insurance coverage and innovative cybersecurity tools to help businesses manage and mitigate potential cyberattacks. Opportunities to make an impact with bold thinking are real—and happening daily at Coalition. About the role Coalition's Claims Operations team is the first step in every claim. Before a handler can work a file, we set it up, route it and make sure the insured and broker hear from us quickly. The Claims Operations Associate keeps that front door running across every line of business and every country we serve. You'll handle intake, claim setup and payments at high volume, and you'll help us test and adopt the automation that will change how this work gets done. You'll work closely with claims handlers and managers, brokers, policyholders and our vendor partners. This role reports to the Senior Manager, Claims Operations. Responsibilities Intake and claim setup Serve as a first point of contact by email and phone for brokers and policyholders reporting incidents, giving them confidence their claim is in good hands; Set up new claims across all lines of business and countries, ensuring data accuracy and routing each claim to the right handler; Send acknowledgement letters, claim summaries and notice letters, and keep claim records complete from day one; Answer and route claims hotline calls and triage the claims inbox; Payments and reporting Process and review claim payments with a focus on accuracy; Produce loss runs and respond to broker requests; Keep operational trackers current, including vendor progress and payment accuracy; Across the role Maintain key team records such as authority letters, licensing and quality review file pulls; Test new tools and automation before rollout, including AI, and give feedback that makes them better; Become a subject matter expert in our claims processes and systems; and Spot what slows the team down and recommend improvements as we grow. Skills and Qualifications 3+ years of experience in claims or insurance operations Strong working knowledge of Salesforce or a similar claims or CRM system Attention to detail and accuracy, especially when working at high volume; Professional communication via phone and email, including on urgent or stressful claims Self starter mentality who takes ownership of the work and follows it through without waiting to be asked; Highly organized, able to juggle a steady flow of daily new claims, payments and requests Comfortable with new tools and changing processes with a curiosity for how automation and AI can improve workflows Experience with cyber or specialty lines claims is a plus Compensation Our compensation reflects the cost of labor across several US geographic markets.

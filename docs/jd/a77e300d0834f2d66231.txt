Palantir Data Engineer The Opportunity: As a data scientist, you’re excited at the prospect of unlocking the secrets held by a data set, and you’re fascinated by the possibilities presented by IoT, machine learning, and artificial intelligence. In an increasingly connected world, massive amounts of structured and unstructured data open new opportunities. As a data scientist at Booz Allen, you can help turn these complex data sets into useful information to solve global challenges. Across private and public sectors, from fraud detection to cancer research to national intelligence, we need you to help find the answers in the data. On our team, you’ll use your analytical skills and data science knowledge to create real-world impact. You’ll work closely with your clients to understand their questions and needs and then dig into their data-rich environments to find the pieces of their information puzzle. You’ll develop algorithms and systems and use the right combination of tools and frameworks to turn sets of disparate data points into objective answers to help clients make informed decisions. Ultimately, you’ll provide a deep understanding of the data, what it all means, and how it can be used. In this role, you will work closely with technical leads, data engineers, business stakeholders, and client teams to design, develop, and maintain scalable data solutions within the Palantir Foundry platform. Working in an agile environment, you will help transform complex data into actionable insights that improve operational decision-making, program performance, and public health outcomes. The ideal candidate will apply strong data engineering and software development practices to build and optimize data pipelines, workflows, dashboards, and user-facing applications. This individual will support the ingestion, transformation, governance, and visualization of enterprise data while collaborating across technical and functional teams to deliver scalable, high-impact solutions. What You'll Work On: Design, develop, and maintain scalable data pipelines and workflows within Palantir Foundry using Python, PySpark, TypeScript, and related technologies. Import, integrate, clean, standardize, and manage data from multiple structured and unstructured data sources while resolving data quality issues. Build and maintain reusable code, transformation logic, Ontologies, and operational workflows using Foundry Code Repositories and version control best practices. Configure and optimize dashboards, Workshop applications, and visualizations to deliver actionable insights and improve business operations. Collaborate with cross-functional teams to translate business, functional, and technical requirements into scalable data solutions and platform capabilities. Participate in Agile delivery activities, including sprint planning, scrum ceremonies, Jira tracking, and collaboration with engineering and stakeholder teams to deliver high-quality solutions. Join us. The world can't wait. You Have: 2+ years of experience working in a professional technical environment 1+ years of experience with Palantir Foundry and its applications, including Code Repository, Ontology Manager, and Workshop Experience developing and maintaining data pipelines using Python and PySpark and applying software engineering and data engineering principles in enterprise environments Experience integrating, transforming, and managing large datasets from multiple data sources Experience translating business and customer requirements into scalable technical solutions Experience working in Agile delivery environments and tracking work using tools such as Jira Knowledge of fundamental programming principles, data structures, APIs, or web development concepts Ability to work take ownership and work independently to solve complex data challenges Ability to obtain and maintain a Public Trust or Suitability/Fitness determination based on client requirements Bachelor’s degree Nice If You Have: Experience leveraging Palantir Artificial Intelligence Platform (AIP) for large language model (LLM) integration and AI-enabled workflows Experience with JavaScript, TypeScript, HTML, and CSS Experience with statistical programming languages such as R Experience with version control systems, including Git Experience designing operational dashboards, workflow tools, or data-driven applications Experience supporting CDC or public health domain clients Experience with Databricks Knowledge of Agile and Scrum methodologies Possession of strong communication, analytical, and stakeholder engagement skills Palantir Foundry Certifications such as Data Engineer Certification Vetting : Applicants selected will be subject to a government investigation and may need to meet eligibility requirements of the U.S. government client.

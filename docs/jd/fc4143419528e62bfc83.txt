About the Role: We are hiring a Senior Software Engineer to join Global Network Experience (GNX) in Money Platform — the team that owns the decision intelligence behind every dollar Remitly moves. Our platform determines not just how money moves, but what's possible, what's required of a customer, and what outcome they should expect. You will work on a platform operating at real scale: 5,000+ corridors across 171 countries, 35,000+ routes, tens of thousands of receiving institutions, and a catalog serving hundreds of millions of requests a day. The complexity is shape as much as size: a route is a combination of country, currency, payment method, institution, partner and product line — only some combinations are valid, and we keep adding dimensions. The central engineering challenge is platformizing that complexity — democratizing the data, building autonomy into network management, optimizing business value with continuous monitoring and intelligence, and making each new corridor, partner, payment method or product line faster to launch and better-performing than the last. GNX's platform surface spans from defining the data model of the network to the intelligence that operates it: Modeling the network — the authoritative account of what the network can do: every partner, institution, destination and route we support, and the libraries everything else is built on. Deciding how money moves — what we ask and verify before a send, the route it takes, the limits that apply, when it will land across 171 countries of partner schedules and holidays, and what happens when a partner fails. Every transaction, continuously, and increasingly by model rather than rule. Controlling it safely — the platform operators run the network from, and the agent interfaces that let AI do the same under real change control. We are looking for range: depth in one of these areas, enough fluency in the others to make good calls at the seams, and the communication to represent your area to everyone who depends on it. You will take technical ownership of an area, become the person the company trusts on it, and then do it again somewhere else. The domains are interdependent — a change in how the catalog models an institution lands on routing, validation and estimates within the same quarter — so the engineers who do well here are the ones who can hold more than one of them in their head. The direction is autonomy — a network that notices a partner degrading, reroutes around it and corrects its own delivery promises without a human in the loop. You will also work closely with network operations, program management, analytics, the ML team, Treasury, integration squads and business product teams — the platform has a broad internal customer base, and part of the job is representing your area to it. This will be a hybrid role based in Seattle, WA and the role will require travel for up to 25% in a year to Remitly's engineering hubs in Poland, UK, Canada and/or our other offices in the US.

About Harry’s Harry’s is a men’s grooming brand that offers high quality shave, body, hair, and skin care products at an exceptional value. Launched in 2013, Harry’s redefined the shaving experience and helped pioneer the earliest era of direct to consumer in the process. Since then, the brand has become the #2 shave brand globally and expanded to offer products for every step in its customer’s routine. Harry’s is also on a mission to ensure guys have access to quality mental health care. To date, the brand has helped connect over 2 million men to quality mental health resources. Harry’s is part of Mammoth Brands, the modern CPG company behind category-leading brands Harry’s, Flamingo, Lume, Mando, and Coterie. Driven by a mission to “Create Things People Like More,” the company is creating a new model—and home—for brands, founders, and talent looking to solve unmet needs, improve peoples’ lives and ultimately change the status quo. Even as we grow, we take extra care to maintain the small, scrappy, entrepreneurial culture that helped to get us where we are today: a company that people like more, that better serves its customers, employees, and community. Mammoth Brands is committed to making a positive impact and donated over $20 million through our network of nonprofit partners to date. We build brands that move fast and think big — and we hire people who are curious about AI, willing to experiment with it in their day-to-day work, and excited to accelerate what's possible. We prioritize giving our team the tools, training, and space to build real fluency, so you grow alongside the technology and help shape how we use it. About the team The Harry’s technology team builds and runs the digital commerce experience for one of the key brands in the Mammoth portfolio. We own the headless Shopify storefront end to end — the React Router/Hydrogen applications our customers shop on, the subscription experience that keeps them coming back, and the infrastructure that serves it all. We are a small, high-leverage team operating in a long-tail SaaS ecosystem: rather than building everything ourselves, we integrate best-in-class platforms and write the code that makes them work as one coherent experience. That means the engineering here is genuinely full-stack and genuinely commercial — the work you ship shows up in conversion, retention, and revenue within days, not quarters. About the role We’re looking for a Senior Software Engineer to join the Harry’s storefront team. This is a hands-on individual contributor role for an engineer who has experience with Headless Shopify builds and consistently delivers high-quality, independent work, incorporates best practices, and is starting to step up as an emerging leader on a small team. You’ll build and operate features across our headless Shopify architecture — storefront performance, subscription flows, experimentation, and the integrations that tie them together. You’ll take well-scoped projects from requirements to production with little oversight, help teammates with their deliverables, and translate business needs into technical requirements. This role reports to an engineering manager and partners daily with Product, Growth, Retention, Creative, and Analytics. What you will accomplish Deliver projects autonomously. Own well-scoped storefront features and projects in familiar technology, delivering on time with work that requires little validation or oversight. Ship high-quality work. Deliver secure, testable, maintainable, low-defect, efficient code that incorporates best practices. Release with confidence. Collaborate within a small team to release changes to production without impacting performance or reliability. Practice operational excellence. Be fluent in the tools, techniques, and best practices the team uses for quality assurance and operational stability. Participate in On-Call rotations after your first 90 days. Document what you build. Create clear exemplars, frameworks, and technical documentation — design docs, runbooks, API documentation, code reviews, and incident write-ups. Debug beyond your own code. Identify and correct defects within the technologies you work in, from the React Router/Hydrogen application through third-party platform integrations. Course-correct quickly. Recognize when an initial approach isn’t working and pivot to a better solution. Map business needs to technology. Translate business requirements into technical requirements and recommendations, balancing short-term and long-term tradeoffs. Help the people around you. Assist teammates with their deliverables, share what you know, and grow into technical leadership on the team. This should describe you Required Solid professional experience building and operating production e-commerce applications, with a track record of delivering projects on time in a live, revenue-generating environment. Headless Shopify on React Router. Hands-on experience is a must — Storefront API, data loading and routing patterns, caching, and the realities of running a headless storefront in production. Shopify Hydrogen experience is important. Strong JavaScript/TypeScript, modern React, and front-end performance fundamentals (Core Web Vitals, rendering strategy, bundle discipline). Experience evaluating and integrating third-party SaaS platforms via APIs and webhooks — including the unglamorous parts: idempotency, retries, rate limits, data reconciliation, and graceful degradation when a vendor is down. Fluency in the practices that keep a storefront stable: testing, code review, observability, incident response, and releasing to production without impacting performance or reliability. The ability to communicate a clear technical point of view in a group, understand the views of others, and commit to the team’s decision. Preferred Custom Shopify Apps: Experience building and maintaining custom or private Shopify apps — Admin and Storefront GraphQL APIs, OAuth and webhook handling, app extensions, Shopify Functions and checkout extensibility — plus the operational side of keeping an app healthy as Shopify's API versions turn over. Third-party integrations for subscriptions — subscription lifecycle, billing cadence, and customer portal experiences. Cloudflare for headless app hosting — Workers, caching and cache invalidation, edge configuration, and WAF. Google Tag Manager and the broader client-side measurement stack, including consent and data-quality considerations. A/B Testing platforms, and comfort developing infrastructure and designing tests that produce trustworthy results. Experience in a DTC or subscription business, where there is a high bar for innovation and customer delight.Experience with AI tooling, AI native software development, and a habit of experimenting with it in your day-to-day engineering work.

About Us At ANZ, we're shaping a world where people and communities thrive, driven by a common goal: to improve the financial wellbeing and sustainability of our millions of customers. About the Role As the Associate, Integrity & Investigations, you'll play a key role in protecting ANZ by leading complex investigations into insider crime, including fraud, theft, serious misconduct, major reputational risk matters and matters involving the conduct of senior employees. You will manage investigations from end to end, ensuring they are conducted fairly, objectively and to a high standard, while providing valuable insights that help strengthen ANZ's risk and control environment. Banking is changing and we’re changing with it, giving our people great opportunities to try new things, learn and grow. Whatever your role at ANZ, you’ll be building your future, while helping to build ours. Role Type: Permanent, Full-time Role Location: ANZ House, Suva What will your day look like? Group Integrity are a team of investigators who are tasked with protecting the assets and reputation of ANZ and are based in Australia, India, New Zealand and Asia Pacific. Their primary focus is conducting investigations into allegations of internal fraud, bribery and corruption involving ANZ employees and contractors. Our business leaders (our customers) and their exceptional experience of our services is a core part of the role. As an Integrity Specialist, you will: • Lead and manage investigations into insider crime, including fraud, theft, serious misconduct and reputational risk matters. · Build and maintain strong relationships with key internal stakeholders, acting as a trusted advisor on investigation matters and internal fraud governance. · Conduct investigations efficiently, effectively and in accordance with procedural fairness requirements and ANZ Values. · Scope investigations appropriately, gather and assess evidence objectively, and develop balanced findings supported by high-quality analysis. · Prepare clear, comprehensive investigation reports and recommendations. · Support the broader Group Integrity team to deliver high-quality investigations that are procedurally and substantively fair. · Analyse and interpret case data to identify trends, risks and opportunities to strengthen controls and prevent future misconduct. · Use forensic analysis techniques, systems and data sources to investigate matters and support findings. · Provide insights and recommendations to stakeholders to support ongoing improvements to ANZ's risk and control environment. · Support the team in responding to emerging fraud and insider crime risks across the organisation. What will you bring? To grow and be successful in this role, you will ideally bring the following: • Experience conducting workplace investigations, including a strong understanding of procedural and substantive fairness requirements. · Experience investigating fraud, theft, insider crime or other complex workplace misconduct matters. · Strong report-writing skills, including the ability to produce high-quality investigation reports supported by evidence and sound analysis. · Experience performing forensic analysis, sourcing information from systems and interrogating data to support investigations. · Excellent verbal and written communication skills. · Strong planning, prioritisation and delivery capabilities. · Proven stakeholder management and influencing skills. · Ability to analyse complex information, exercise sound judgement and solve problems effectively. · Experience using data to generate meaningful insights and recommendations. · Knowledge of banking systems and the use of those systems to support investigations. · Understanding of risk frameworks, operational risk and compliance within a large corporate environment. · Demonstrated learning agility, adaptability and a continuous improvement mindset. · Legal qualifications, ACFE certification, investigations qualifications or workplace investigations training will be advantageous, although not essential. You’re not expected to have 100% of these skills. At ANZ a growth mindset is at the heart of our culture, so if you have most of these things in your toolbox, we’d love to hear from you.

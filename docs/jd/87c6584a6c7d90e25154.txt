Your Opportunity to Make an Impact As a Credit Risk Strategy Data Scientist at BILL, you’ll build cutting-edge solutions to replace outdated financial processes. Your expertise will help businesses gain control of their operations, giving them more time for what matters. What You'll Do Lead the way: Drive the development, maintenance, and refinement of risk strategy frameworks and complex rules to achieve ambitious business goals. Be a data detective: Use advanced analytics and data science techniques to solve complex business problems. This includes building sophisticated SQL/Python scripts, interpreting results, and influencing decision-making with your findings. Collaborate and create: Partner with cross-functional teams, including product, engineering, and operations, to identify new opportunities, establish business requirements, and guide the execution of strategies across the customer lifecycle. Build powerful tools: Develop flexible performance dashboards and monitoring tools using platforms like Tableau to provide clear, actionable insights to a variety of audiences. Stay ahead of the curve: Identify and execute new opportunities to optimize processes and proactively detect and respond to evolving risk trends. Mentor and grow: Help mentor and support junior team members to help them achieve their goals. What We're Looking For Proven experience: Minimum of 3+ years of experience in fraud or credit risk strategy within the fintech, e-commerce, or online payments industry. Technical expertise: Proficiency in SQL and Python, with a strong ability to work with large, complex datasets and leverage data science libraries. Analytical mindset: Demonstrated ability to use statistics and data science to solve complex, ambiguous problems and drive data-driven decisions. Exceptional communication: Ability to clearly and concisely communicate complex results to both technical experts and business leaders. Hands-on experience: Direct experience with developing and deploying data-driven and automated monitoring rules, and the ability to find and recommend enhancements for existing processes. Bonus Points Experience in experimental design and data/control governance. Experience with customer management or collections strategies for credit products like revolving credit cards or merchant cash advances.

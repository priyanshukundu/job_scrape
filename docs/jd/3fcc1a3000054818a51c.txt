About the Team eBay’s Risk Strategy, Operations & Policy Governance team drives strategic execution, operational excellence, and scalable governance across our global Risk organization. We engage with Risk Policy, Product, Engineering, Legal, Internal Audit, Risk Oversight, and business teams to strengthen risk management practices, enable cross-functional alignment, and deliver measurable outcomes! About the Role We are seeking an experienced Senior Manager to lead risk governance programs, strengthen accountability, and advance the maturity of how risk is managed within eBay! This role serves as a strategic thought partner to Risk leadership, driving cross-functional alignment, governance effectiveness, and operational improvements. The Senior Manager will lead all aspects of the risk documentation, including owning the Risk Policy Committee, policy recertification, audit and assurance engagements, ERM reporting, and M&A risk governance. The ideal candidate combines strong risk management expertise with critical thinking, operational rigor, and the ability to translate complex challenges into scalable solutions. Key Responsibilities Risk Governance & Assurance: Lead Risk engagements with Internal Audit, Second and Third Lines of Defense, coordinating assessments, issue tracking, remediation, and governance reporting. Risk Policy Committee (RPC): Lead governance forums, policy reviews, approvals, and decision tracking to ensure accountability and alignment across Risk leadership. Risk Register & Controls: Own and maintain the Risk Register, ensuring accurate documentation and traceability across risks, controls, policies, findings, and remediation activities. Policy Governance & Recertification: Responsible for the policy lifecycle management, periodic reviews, recertification, and documentation within governance platforms. Enterprise Risk Management: Serve as the primary Risk liaison to ERM, coordinating quarterly reporting, risk assessments, and governance updates. M&A Risk Governance: Develop and maintain a scalable Risk M&A playbook, defining engagement points, assessment requirements, ownership, and handoffs for pre- and post-acquisition activities. Governance Transformation & Automation: L ook for opportunities to modernize governance processes through AI, automation, workflow improvements, and technolo gy enablement. Strategic Partnership & Execution: Collaborate with Risk, Product, Engineering, Legal, Compliance, and business teams to clarify accountability, resolve gaps, and drive initiatives from strategy through implementation. Executive Communications: Develop concise, actionable reporting, presentations, and recommendations for senior leadership and governance forums. Qualifications Dedicated to working the Hybrid Model with 3+ days per week onsite in our Austin, TX office. Experience in payments, FinTech, financial services, or other regulated environments. Demonstrated experience designing, implementing, and improving risk governance frameworks and operating models. Strong understanding of risk and control frameworks, audit processes, policy governance, and organizational risk oversight. Experience managing Risk Registers, governance documentation, and GRC platforms. Strong business analysis capabilities, including requirements gathering, process mapping, BRDs, and workflow design. Experience maximising AI, automation, and technology to improve operational efficiency and governance effectiveness. Excellent executive communication, analytical, problem-solving, and stakeholder management skills. Proven ability to influence cross-functional teams, navigate ambiguity, and translate strategic priorities into actionable outcomes. Proficiency with enterprise tools such as Salesforce, Visio, Lucidchart, or comparable platforms. Ability to work onsite three days per week in eBay's Austin office. Ability and willingness to travel as business needs require.

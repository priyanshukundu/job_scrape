Job Title : Senior Software Engineer Reporting To: Manager, Engineering Work Schedule: Hybrid – Mineral Wells, TX Our Moog Military Aircraft Group is seeking a Senior Software Engineer to support the design, development, and integration of advanced avionics, radio, and flight control systems. In this role, you will develop innovation software solutions, establish technical standards, and lead the execution of large-scale engineering projects. Additionally, you’ll act as an advisor to management on advanced engineering applications. This position is based onsite at Moog Avionics in Mineral Wells, TX. Moog Avionics delivers customer-focused technologies and customized solutions for the Special Mission, Military & Defense, Military Trainer, Business Aviation, and General Aviation markets. Required Qualifications: Bachelor’s degree in Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Aerospace Engineering or other significant related engineering degrees. Minimum of five years’ technical engineering professional experience and expert in product requirements. Knowledge of Design and Development processes and complete understanding of an engineering discipline and industry practices and standards. Expert knowledge of relevant design tools; Altium, pads, SolidWorks, C, C#, Matlab or similar tools. Experience with DO-178B/C software development lifecycle. Must be eligible to work in the United States. Key Responsibilities: Contribute to the completion of specific programs and projects, focused on software for avionics and flight control systems, or Flight Control Law/Autopilot Development, Flight Test Engineering, Systems Engineering, Electromechanical, Electrical, Mechanical, or Computer Design. Develop software for advanced avionics and flight control systems. Develop system/software requirements. Develop new testing applications or modify existing tools to support integration activities. Perform software/system integration activities to verify proper functionality of new designs. Create technical documentation. Fabricate and debug prototype software or electrical, mechanical, and/or RF hardware. System and subsystem analysis and requirements specification. System and subsystem modeling and performance analysis. Certification project support: TSO and STC projects. Test and debug prototype electrical, mechanical, and/or RF hardware. Provide guidance, coaching, and training to lower-level engineers.

Manager, Data Science Your Role and Impact As the Manager, Data Science and Commercial Analytics, you will shape the technology and data science capabilities that enable Commercial Excellence globally. You will connect commercial strategy, analytics, data, applications, and technology delivery to create scalable solutions that improve how teams identify opportunities, generate leads, and make decisions. This is an opportunity to move commercial analytics from individually supported tools to a sustainable, globally managed capability. You will define technical standards, strengthen critical platforms, and build the playbooks, processes, and technical community needed to deliver reliable, high-impact commercial solutions. Key Responsibilities Lead the development and scaling of commercial lead-generation capabilities, including data validation, model development, testing, deployment, monitoring, and ongoing maintenance. Own the technical architecture for Commercial Analytics, including applications, data flows, integrations, dependencies, lifecycle management, and support models. Define and implement architecture principles, development standards, quality controls, and governance for commercial analytics solutions. Develop and maintain a technology roadmap that improves solution stability, scalability, modernization, documentation, testing, and deployment practices. Create technical playbooks and runbooks for solution design, coding, testing, deployment, incident response, knowledge transfer, and production support. Identify technical debt, continuity risks, platform dependencies, and modernization opportunities, and recommend investments that improve sustainability and delivery speed. Partner with Enterprise IT, Data and Analytics, digital teams, data scientists, product owners, and commercial stakeholders to translate business needs into scalable technical solutions. Mentor technical resources, build development pathways, strengthen internal technical capability, and ensure knowledge is transferred from contractors and subject-matter experts. Serve as the escalation point for significant technical issues and cross-platform dependencies, advising Commercial Excellence leadership on technology risks and emerging opportunities. Required Qualifications Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Data and Analytics, Information Systems, or a related field; equivalent relevant experience will also be considered. Eight or more years of experience in software development, analytics engineering, data science, solution architecture, application delivery, or a related technical discipline. Demonstrated experience designing, owning, or architecting enterprise analytics platforms, business applications, or data-driven solutions. Strong knowledge of data pipelines, analytics architecture, APIs, system integrations, application development, and production support. Experience translating business requirements into scalable technical solutions while establishing technical standards, governance, and delivery practices. Proven ability to work across global, cross-functional organizations and communicate effectively with technical teams, business stakeholders, and senior leaders. Preferred Qualifications Experience with Azure or Microsoft data and analytics technologies, including Power BI, Python, SQL, APIs, and cloud architecture. Experience developing or supporting predictive analytics, recommendation engines, lead-generation products, or other commercial data science solutions. Experience supporting CRM, sales, marketing, pricing, customer-data, or Commercial Excellence platforms. Experience with DevOps practices, including CI/CD, source control, automated testing, release management, monitoring, and production support. Experience leading technical communities of practice, mentoring technical talent, or managing knowledge-transfer programs.

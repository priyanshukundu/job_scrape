Job Description Lead Software Engineer -Agentic Cyber Operations & Platforms Help us build the next generation of AI-powered cybersecurity. What if you could use your software engineering skills to help transform how one of the world's leading insurance companies detects, investigates, and responds to cyber threats? At Allstate, we're building something new, and we're looking for engineers who want to be part of it. Our newly established Agentic Cyber Operations & Platforms (ACO&P) organization is bringing together software engineering, cloud technologies, security data, AI, and automation to create smarter, faster, and more connected cyber defense capabilities. We're looking for hands-on software engineers who enjoy building solutions, solving complex problems, and working with emerging technologies. What You'll Be Working On: We're growing two closely connected engineering teams . While both share a strong software development foundation, each focuses on a different part of our cybersecurity transformation. (1) Cyber Data & Intelligence: Build the Foundation Help us connect the dots across our cybersecurity ecosystem. This team is building the data intelligence and integration capabilities that bring together security information from different applications, cloud environments, and enterprise platforms. The team is also building scalable observability and telemetry capabilities that collect, process, enrich, and deliver data across applications, infrastructure, cloud environments, and security platforms. You'll have opportunities to: Design and develop software integrations, APIs, and data pipelines that connect multiple security and technology platforms. Design and evolve observability and telemetry pipelines that ingest data from multiple enterprise sources and enable monitoring, analytics, security intelligence, and automation use cases. Build solutions to collect, process, and deliver security telemetry and operational data. Develop automation that makes security information more accessible, reliable, and actionable. Apply software engineering and DevOps practices to build scalable, secure, and maintainable solutions. Help establish the foundation for future AI-powered security analytics and intelligent automation. Help build a unified data intelligence layer that makes security and operational telemetry accessible for advanced analytics, automation, and AI-enabled workflows. You might be a great fit if: You're a software engineer who enjoys backend development, APIs, systems integration, and working with data, with an interest in observability and modern cloud and platform engineering. Experience with cybersecurity, DevOps, or observability platforms is a plus. (2) Mission Control: Build the Experience Help us reimagine how cybersecurity teams manage and respond to incidents. This team is focused on bringing together incident management tools and capabilities into a more connected, streamlined experience for cyber defenders. You'll have opportunities to: Engineer and enhance applications that support cybersecurity incident management and response. Build integrations between enterprise applications and security platforms. Support and improve existing cloud-based applications while helping develop new in-house solutions. Take software solutions through the complete development lifecycle, from concept and proof of concept to production. Build and maintain CI/CD pipelines, automation, and cloud-based application infrastructure. Help create a unified platform that makes it easier for security teams to investigate, collaborate, and respond to threats. You might be a great fit if: You're a hands-on backend or full-stack software engineer with experience building applications, working with cloud technologies, and delivering production-ready solutions. Cybersecurity experience is a welcome bonus, not a prerequisite. Both teams offer opportunities to contribute to a growing engineering organization and help shape how we build and deliver modern cyber operations capabilities. What We're Looking For At the heart of this role is strong software engineering capability. We're interested in engineers who can build, integrate, automate, and deliver reliable technology solutions. The Essentials Software Engineering: Hands-on experience developing applications, backend services, integrations, or automation solutions using languages such as Python, Java, C#/.NET, JavaScript/TypeScript, or similar. Application Development & Integration : Experience building or supporting APIs, software integrations, data pipelines, or production applications. Cloud Technologies: Working knowledge of Azure, AWS, or GCP, along with an understanding of modern infrastructure and application environments. DevOps & CI/CD: Familiarity with source control, automated testing, deployment pipelines, and software delivery practices using tools such as Azure DevOps or GitHub Actions. AI-Assisted Development : Experience using AI coding assistants, such as GitHub Copilot, to support software development, with an understanding of code review, testing, and validation. Security Awareness: An understanding of secure software development principles, including application security, access controls, and protecting sensitive information. Collaboration: Experience working with cross-functional teams to translate technical requirements into practical engineering solutions. Experience That Would Be a Plus Depending on your background and interests, experience in any of the following areas could be valuable: Cybersecurity & Observability: Experience with observability, telemetry, log processing, security monitoring, or SIEM technologies such as Datadog, Cribl, OpenTelemetry, Azure Monitor, Microsoft Sentinel, Splunk, or similar platforms. Data Engineering & Integration: Working with telemetry and data pipelines, streaming technologies, Azure Data Explorer (ADX), Kusto Query Language (KQL), SQL/NoSQL databases, data lakes, Kafka, Databricks or similar technologies. Cloud & Platform Engineering: Infrastructure as Code, Terraform, Docker, Kubernetes, and cloud-native application development. Infrastructure & Observability: Familiarity with cloud infrastructure, networking, distributed systems, observability, telemetry, and modern application architectures. Full-Stack Development: Building REST APIs, microservices, or web applications using frameworks such as React or Angular. AI & Intelligent Automation: Exposure to AI/ML technologies, AI agents, agentic workflows, or automation solutions. Cybersecurity Concepts: Familiarity with vulnerability management, incident response, OWASP, MITRE ATT&CK, or zero-trust principles. These are preferred skills, not a checklist. We recognize that great engineers bring different technical strengths and experiences. You don't need to have worked with every technology listed to be considered. Why Join Us? Build something new: Join a growing organization where you'll help develop foundational platforms and capabilities rather than simply maintain existing systems. Engineering with real-world impact: Your work will help cybersecurity professionals access better information, make faster decisions, and respond more effectively to emerging threats. Explore AI and automation : Work in an environment where AI-assisted development and intelligent automation are part of our technology direction, with opportunities to expand your skills as these capabilities evolve. Bring your strengths and keep growing : Whether your background is in backend development, full-stack engineering, cloud platforms, or data integration, you'll have opportunities to apply your expertise while developing new capabilities. Collaborate across disciplines: Work alongside software engineers, cloud specialists, data professionals, and cybersecurity teams to solve meaningful technology challenges. Ready to Build What's Next? If you're a software engineer who loves solving problems, enjoys building technology from the ground up, and is curious about the intersection of software engineering, cybersecurity, and AI, this could be an exciting next step. You don't have to come from a traditional cybersecurity background. What matters most is your ability to engineer solutions, learn new technologies, and contribute to building something meaningful. Bring your engineering expertise. We'll bring the challenges, opportunities, and room to grow.

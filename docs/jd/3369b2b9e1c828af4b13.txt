US Corporates – Credit Analyst, Director – Project Finance (Industrial, Transportation and Project Finance Ratings Group) The Director position within the Industrials, Transportation, and Project Finance Ratings Group at Fitch Ratings is a pivotal role that involves leading the analysis of multi-sector transactions in project finance including transportation, social infrastructure and ports. This group is responsible for covering a diverse range of demand-risk and availability-based pay project finance credits based out of our Austin, New York, Chicago and Toronto office. Qualitative credentials will weigh meaningfully in the selection of the candidate. A healthy sense of intellectual curiosity is critical, along with a willingness and ability to attack and explain topics of analytical complexity. Fitch is a team-oriented work environment, so excellent communication skills are essential, as is a high productivity work-ethic and results oriented mindset. What We Offer: Hybrid Work Environment: 3 days a week in office required A Culture of Learning & Mobility: Dedicated trainings, leadership development and mentorship programs designed to ensure that your time at Fitch will be a continuous learning opportunity Investing in Your Future: Retirement planning and tuition reimbursement programs that empower you to achieve your short and long-term goals Promoting Health & Wellbeing: Comprehensive healthcare offerings that enable physical, mental, financial, social, and occupational wellbeing Supportive Parenting Policies: Family-friendly policies, including a generous global parental leave plan, designed to help you balance career and family life effectively Inclusive Work Environment: A collaborative workplace where all voices are valued, with Employee Resource Groups that unite and empower our colleagues around the globe Dedication to Giving Back: Paid volunteer days, matched funding for donations and ample opportunities to volunteer in your community We’ll Count on You To: Lead the analysis of transportation related transactions and assess key quantitative and qualitative factors influencing the credit quality of multi-sector Project Finance credits; Maintain comprehensive financial models; Lead meetings with Project Finance industry management teams, project sponsors, investment bankers, and financial advisors; Prepare presentations for internal credit rating committees and produce ratings and reports for general publication; Maintain a strong external network and profile to build Fitch's reputation for analytical excellence. Participate in the evaluation of other credits within Corporates, as well as other related credit groups. What You Need to Have: Bachelor's degree; CFA/CPA or CFA candidate preferred; 10+ years of relevant experience in capital markets and/or credit analysis role; Expertise in Excel and Word; Strong analytical, quantitative, and organizational skills; Excellent written and verbal communication skills; Ability to shift fluidly between multiple projects as priorities change; Ability to excel in a team-oriented environment. Relevant experience a plus, especially coverage or knowledge of diverse structures within multiple Project Finance sectors; What Would Make You Stand Out: Has experience in the Project Finance as well as Corporate industries; Experience focusing on credit on the buy-side, sell-side, a credit department at a bank, or another rating agency. Master’s degree in Finance, Business or Public Administration, or CFA.

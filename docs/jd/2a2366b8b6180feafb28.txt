About the role We are looking for a proactive and experienced individual to join as Affirm Bank Treasury Manager. As Affirm Bank is currently in its formation phase, we are actively building out our foundational team. Reporting to the Bank CFO, Bank Treasury is responsible for cash management and operations, funding and liquidity planning, and balance sheet risk management for Affirm Bank. As we continue to scale the Bank and build out the foundational team, the ideal candidate should be able to adapt and thrive in a fast-paced environment with an eagerness to learn and grow. What you’ll do Support all treasury-related functions with a focus on liquidity and funding risk management, cash management, and periodic funding planning Develop and execute liquidity risk stress testing models, defining stress scenarios, identifying risk exposures, and calibrating underlying model assumptions Develop and maintain the Contingency Funding Plan (CFP), lead CFP simulations and testing, and track action plan timelines for liquidity events Maintain and review Early Warning Indicators (EWIs) and existing risk limits, recommending updates and enhancements to balance sheet risk tolerances Support The Asset Liability Committee (ALCO) reporting and committee presentations under the Bank CFO, benchmarking liquidity and Interest Rate Risk (IRR) profiles against limits Support quarterly Interest Rate Risk (IRR) stress tests and semi-annual back-testing results reviews for ALCO Assist with annual policy and assumption reviews, policy exception documentation, and recordkeeping for Liquidity and IRR policies Guide the enhancement of balance sheet risk management reporting analytics and monitoring for senior leadership, regulators, and internal partners Work cross-functionally with Affirm Treasury, Capital Markets, Bank Engineering, Strategic Finance, and the Bank Controller / Record to Report teams What we look for 5+ years of treasury or related financial function experience within banking or financial services BA/BS required Certified Treasury Professional (CTP) preferred Broad range of Treasury specific experience with both market facing and risk management exposure at a financial institution and have familiarity with consumer deposits, working capital line, warehouse facilities and other typical financial funding instruments Possess strong analytical quantitative capabilities with statistical modeling knowledge and background Proactive self-starter who is eager to learn with positive attitude Base Pay Grade -

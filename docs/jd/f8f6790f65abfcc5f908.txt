As a Senior AI/ML Engineer in the Data Products & AI group, you own the full lifecycle of production AI/ML systems, from problem framing and model design through evaluation, deployment, monitoring, and continuous optimization. You’ll combine strong software engineering with hands-on expertise in modern AI, including foundation models, generative AI, and predictive modeling, to turn emerging AI capabilities into reliable, safe, and valuable product features. You’ll work at the intersection of research and production, partnering with engineers, product managers, and clinical, regulatory, and commercial stakeholders to ship AI systems at scale. In doing so, you’ll help position Dexcom as an industry leader in health AI and empower millions of customers to take control of their health. Where you come in: You architect end-to-end AI/ML product capabilities, from concept and experimental design through training, evaluation, and production deployment, writing reusable, production-quality code. You build and optimize AI/ML product features, whether powered by predictive models or generative AI such as large language models (LLMs), improving behavior through model selection, prompting, retrieval, feature engineering, fine-tuning, or retraining. You own evaluation and safety for your systems, defining metrics, benchmarks, and monitoring, identifying model and system risks, and driving mitigations and responsible-AI practices across the team. You lead technical strategy that balances model performance, latency, cost, safety, and regulatory requirements. You collaborate with engineers and architects to deploy and scale AI/ML systems in production, building and improving the training, serving, and CI/CD pipelines that keep them reliable, observable, and cost-effective. You coordinate with cross-functional teams, including stakeholders from R&D, Commercial, Product, Legal, and Regulatory, to ensure development aligns with company strategy and meets the highest safety standards. You display excellent written and verbal communication skills, with a proven ability to convey complex methodologies and results to technical and non-technical audiences. What makes you successful: You love being on the cutting edge of AI/ML, are eager to learn new technologies as they emerge, and share a passion for Dexcom’s mission and health-sensing applications. You have strong CS fundamentals and hands-on experience with Python and modern AI/ML libraries and frameworks. You have experience taking AI/ML systems all the way to production and operating them reliably at scale, including evaluation, deployment, monitoring, and MLOps practices. You have experience building scalable, cloud-based AI/ML solutions and are comfortable working with large datasets in distributed environments. You are familiar with relevant, modern software engineering and test tooling, and apply engineering rigor to AI/ML work. You bring a responsible-AI mindset, attentive to model safety, reliability, and risk in regulated, high-stakes settings. What you’ll get: A front row seat to life changing CGM technology.

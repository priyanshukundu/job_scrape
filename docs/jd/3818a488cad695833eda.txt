Manager : Jeffrey Sing ( jeffrey.sing@aig.com ) Financial Lines Join one of the world's leading Financial Lines insurers, helping businesses and financial institutions navigate an increasingly complex risk landscape. Our team provides innovative insurance solutions that protect organizations, their leaders, and professionals against a broad range of management, professional, cyber, and financial risks. As part of the Financial Lines team, you will gain exposure to a diverse portfolio of products, including: Directors & Officers (D&O) Liability - protecting company leaders against management liability claims, regulatory investigations, and defense costs. Professional Indemnity (PI) - covering financial losses arising from errors, omissions, and professional negligence. Cyber Insurance - supporting clients with cyber risk transfer, incident response, and data breach protection. Crime & Fidelity - protecting organizations against employee fraud, third-party dishonesty, and financial crime. Employment Practices Liability (EPL) - covering employment-related claims and investigations. Financial Institutions Solutions - tailored insurance programs for banks, asset managers, private equity firms, and other financial institutions. Working alongside experienced underwriters, brokers, and clients, you will learn how to assess complex risks, develop tailored insurance solutions, and help businesses operate with confidence in a rapidly changing world.

NBC News Digital is looking for an associate editor to join its platforms team, working nights and weekends. The editor will curate NBCNews.com’s owned and social platforms, including the homepage and app, write breaking news alerts for the app and email, and distribute stories across social accounts. The editor will curate content from all newsroom sections, including breaking news, politics, business, sports, and lifestyle. The ideal candidate will be a strong writer and editor with excellent news judgment and a deep understanding of digital platforms. The editor will be adept at packaging storylines with a wide variety of the best assets, including the video, graphics, artistry, text and beyond. Strong communication skills and the ability to work under tight deadlines are crucial. Given that this is a nights-and-weekends role, the editor must be a self-starter who is comfortable working independently and knows when to ask for support. This position is perfect for an ambitious journalist who wants to be at the center of breaking news at one of the best digital newsrooms in the industry. They will have an opportunity to curate platforms that reach millions of people and play a role in NBC News Digital’s curation of significant stories — including presidential campaigns, the Oscars, the Super Bowl and the buildup to the 2028 Olympics — and distinct and exclusive work. This is a full-time position based in L.A., Wednesday to Friday from 1 p.m. to 9 p.m. PT and Saturday and Sunday from 3 p.m. to 11 p.m. PT.

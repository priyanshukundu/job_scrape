Role Summary Reporting to the Executive Director, Engineering/Delivery and Cyber Fusion Center, the Principal Security Architect, Information Security, Risk & Compliance (SRC), provides enterprise-wide security architecture leadership and strategic direction in support of Gilead’s business and technology priorities. The role collaborates closely with cross-functional IT and business teams to guide security technology strategy, drive consistent application of security standards and technologies, and enable strong, seamless security across Gilead’s enterprise. ESSENTIAL JOB FUNCTIONS: Align security architecture with business and IT strategy, and advocate for security as an enabler of business goals • Analyze enterprise business context/needs (trends and business strategy) to derive security architecture • Research emerging security technologies and trends in support of security enhancement and development efforts • Analyze the current technology environment to detect critical deficiencies and recommend solutions • Develop and maintain IT Security Strategy, Standards, and Roadmap • Maintain 3-year Security Portfolio to deliver on IT Security Strategy • Create and deliver compelling presentations to articulate the security strategies, architecture, and roadmaps to various stakeholders in the Organization • Define the principles that guide security technology decisions and tradeoffs for the enterprise • Lead or consult on security architecture implementation activities for new/existing shared security solutions. Work closely with Infrastructure Engineering, Operations, and IT Security & Privacy team to define effective security technology architecture and roadmaps • Lead and facilitate the evaluation and selection of security technologies and product standards, and the design of standard configurations/implementation patterns for security solutions • Consult on solution architecture for projects to ensure compliance with the security technical architecture • Identify the organizational and financial impact of the security enforcement solutions Develop, document, and maintain security architecture artifacts covering design, analysis, and ongoing maintenance • Define and maintain the enterprise AI Security reference architecture, standards, and guardrails for the secure adoption of generative AI, LLMs, and AI agents across IT and the business • Partner with Architecture Review Board in architecture reviews and threat modeling for AI solutions, including RAG patterns, agentic workflows, copilots, and third-party AI services, addressing risks such as prompt injection, data leakage, model poisoning, and insecure plugin or tool access • Design identity, access, and data protection controls for AI systems, including least-privilege access for AI agents and service identities. • Assess AI vendors and embedded AI features in SaaS platforms for security, privacy, data residency, and model training/data retention risks • Partner with Security Operations to define logging, monitoring, and detection requirements for AI usage, model behavior, and AI-driven automation • Partner with Legal, Privacy, Compliance, and AI governance teams to translate Responsible AI policy into enforceable technical controls • Review and analyze existing security solutions for effectiveness and efficiency and develop strategies to improve and enhance the solutions. • Lead the development of architecture for new network / endpoint / IAM / platform security solutions and/or enhancements to such existing security solutions. • Facilitate conduct of security incident investigations, pen-tests, risk & vulnerability assessments, and recommend preventive measures to reduce risk. • Assist in formalizing and updating security policies, procedures and technical standards and auditing/monitoring compliance with those standards. Assist in developing responses to internal and external audit findings and regulatory inquiries.

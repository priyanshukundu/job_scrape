Management Level: 88 Work Shift: 1ST SHIFT (United States of America) Tyson Foods is seeking a Data Engineer to serve as senior technical authority for Inventory & Property Optimization (IPO), which unites Enterprise Asset Management, Enterprise Inventory Management, and MRO to strengthen working capital and free cash flow. This senior individual contributor role, scoped comparably to a Senior Manager, builds the applications, integrations, and AI capabilities that connect plant-floor activity to SAP S/4HANA and our Google Cloud data platform. The role reports to the Director of IPO with a dotted line to the Associate Director of Design and Development. Immediate Priorities • Transactional capture. Web and mobile apps posting to SAP through OData, BAPIs, and AIF, with named-user authorization and automated reconciliation. • Vision and document extraction. Multimodal LLM or OCR extraction from images and documents, with confidence thresholds and human review before writing to SAP. • API integration. REST and OAuth integrations with Autodesk Platform Services and Liquidity Services AssetZone on Cloud Run, Pub/Sub, and BigQuery. • Similarity and matching. Embedding-based retrieval and rule-scored matching of equipment specifications for form, fit, and function. • Enterprise AI and analytics. An LLM gateway such as LiteLLM, natural-language query over BigQuery, and Palantir Foundry inventory applications. Essential Duties and Responsibilities • Architect and build applications, integrations, and pipelines connecting maintenance, inventory, and asset activity to SAP S/4HANA, BigQuery, and Palantir Foundry. • Assess existing internally built applications that write to SAP, and remediate findings related to identity and credential use, authorization, and system-of-record integrity in partnership with IT Security, GRC Access Control, and Internal Audit. • Integrate engineering design, capital project, and surplus equipment data to identify idle or redeployable assets before new equipment is purchased. • Build analytics and AI-assisted solutions that identify slow-moving, obsolete, duplicate, and overstocked direct, indirect, and MRO inventory. • Build automated detection, using reconciliation rules, exception monitoring, and anomaly analysis, that expedites identification of non-conforming entries in direct, indirect, and equipment inventory that create financial risk or reduce operating income. • Embed SOX and GAAP requirements into application and integration design as built-in controls and audit-ready reporting, limiting financial exposure from deployment. • Define master data and classification standards for equipment, materials, and spare parts, and set architecture, integration, and AI guardrail standards. • Mentor Data Engineers I through III and translate technical and financial trade-offs into clear recommendations for leadership. Requirements Education: Bachelor’s degree in Information Systems, Computer Science, Engineering, Accounting, Finance, Supply Chain, or related field, or equivalent experience. SAP, Google Cloud, or Palantir certifications are a plus. Experience: 7+ years of total experience, primarily in asset-intensive manufacturing, including at least 3 years in an operations, engineering, or accounting role and at least 3 years in technical solutioning such as application development, data engineering, or ERP solution delivery. • Experience with the operational and accounting processes behind inventory and assets, including production planning, production inventory, operating standards, inventory valuation, CIP, capitalization, and settlement. • Delivery of production applications that write to an ERP system of record and of AI-enabled solutions used by business teams, plus a track record of mentoring engineers. Technical Skills • SAP S/4HANA. PM, MM with inventory and warehouse management, PP, PS, and FI-AA and CO settlement, with depth in at least two. • Enterprise applications. Coupa, Palantir Foundry and AIP, Power BI, Google Cloud and BigQuery, Microsoft 365, Collibra, and Autodesk, or comparable platforms. • Engineering and integration. SQL, Python, PostgreSQL, ETL/ELT and ingestion frameworks, REST APIs, OAuth, SAP OData and BAPIs, AWS (ECS/Fargate) or Google Cloud Run, and web, mobile, SAP Fiori/UI5, or Screen Personas development. • Artificial intelligence. LLM solutions on Amazon Bedrock, Google Vertex AI, and Palantir AIP behind a multi-model gateway such as LiteLLM; retrieval with embeddings and vector search, agent and tool-calling design, multimodal extraction, and audited, identity-propagated access, with judgment to run each workload on the platform closest to its governed data. Communication & Special Skills • Ability to explain technical decisions in financial and operational terms, and the credibility to recommend against a popular request when the evidence supports it. Preferred Qualifications • Protein, food, or regulated manufacturing experience, including SAP catch weight; enterprise AI guardrails, SOX-relevant system design, or SAP Business AI and Joule.

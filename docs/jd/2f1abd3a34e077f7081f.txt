The Impact You will have in this role : The Scrum Master Associate facilitates and coaches one or more Agile delivery teams within DTCC's technology organization, ensuring the effective adoption of Scrum and Agile practices. This role supports the delivery of data-focused initiatives and integrations across multiple systems and business processes, with an emphasis on collaboration, transparency, continuous improvement, and high-quality outcomes. The Scrum Master will partner closely with Product Owners, technology teams, and stakeholders supporting Asset Services. Your Primary Responsibilities : Facilitate Scrum ceremonies including sprint planning, daily standups, sprint reviews, retrospectives, and backlog refinement sessions. Coach teams on Scrum principles, Agile methodologies, and best practices to improve team effectiveness and delivery outcomes. Support teams deliver data-centric solutions, integrations, and process improvements across multiple applications and platforms. Identify, track, and remove impediments that impact team productivity and delivery timelines. Foster strong communication and collaboration among developers, business stakeholders, Product Owners, and dependent teams. Partner with Product Owners to manage, prioritize, and maintain product backlogs aligned with business objectives. Coordinate and manage dependencies across teams, applications, and enterprise processes. Drive continuous improvement through metrics, retrospectives, and feedback-driven action plans. Promote self-organization, accountability, and the development of high-performing Agile teams. **NOTE: The Primary Responsibilities of this role are not limited to the details above. ** Qualifications: Minimum of 4 years of related experience as a Scrum Master, Agile Coach, Agile Project Manager, or similar role supporting technology delivery teams.

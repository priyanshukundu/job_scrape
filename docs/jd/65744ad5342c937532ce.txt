Team Overview The Quant team builds the models, analytics, and data infrastructure that support the firm's investing businesses. The team works directly with portfolio managers and analysts across asset classes and is currently expanding its coverage of securitized products in partnership with the Securitized Products Investment team. Securitized Products operates within the larger framework of the firm's Credit team and invests across agency MBS, non-agency RMBS, CLOs, CMBS, and ABS. Job Overview We are looking for a quantitative strategist to help build models, analytics, and data infrastructure with focus on securitized products business. Working with a senior credit quantitative strategist, portfolio analysts, and portfolio managers, you will develop and maintain prepayment and credit analytics, automate deal and collateral data pipelines, and turn vendor platforms into reproducible infrastructure that the desk relies on daily. The business and its users are immediate: there is a substantial set of existing processes to develop, improve, and extend. The role spans agency and credit-sensitive securitized products. Major Responsibilities Develop and maintain prepayment, default, and severity analytics supporting valuation and relative value across agency and non-agency products Build pipelines for dealing, tranche, and collateral data from vendor platforms (Intex, Yield Book) and agency disclosure sources into a centralized, point-in-time data store Automate recurring desk workflows: surveillance of monthly remittance data, bid support analytics, and portfolio reporting Develop OAS and scenario analytics with clear attribution of results to prepayment, volatility, and curve assumptions, and validate vendor models and data against primary sources Partner with our portfolio managers and analysts to solve problems where AI and quant technology can enhance research, risk management, and decision making What We Value Above all, depth and self-sufficiency: production experience in prepayment or credit modeling within at least one securitized asset class, combined with strong hands-on engineering. Breadth across the rest of the securitized landscape is welcome.

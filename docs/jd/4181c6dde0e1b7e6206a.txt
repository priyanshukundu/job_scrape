We are looking for a Senior Financial Analyst to support NVIDIA's Operations and Manufacturing for business units within NVIDIA’s Graphics and Computing and Networking segments. As part of NVIDIA's Finance organization, the overarching responsibilities will be to build and deepen teamwork between Operations and Finance teams to strategically run analytics on production cost and to provide insights and actionable information for business decision. This is a role for an energetic and hard-working individual who thrives in a multifaceted and fast-paced environment and who has excellent critical thinking and communication skills. What you’ll be doing: Partner across Operations, Engineering and/or Business Units to analyze financial performance from the perspective of product costs, inventory, and supply management. Analyze and model accurate reporting and projection on COGS including period product costs, warranty, royalty, logistic expenses etc. Support monthly and quarterly financial close and related reporting processes. Continuously identity and drive process improvements and automation for better efficiency in reporting and analyzing financial information. Drive operational optimization projects to streamline cross-functional collaboration. What we need to see: BS/BA in Accounting or Finance (or equivalent experience), MBA or advanced degree in related fields a plus. 5+ years experience in Accounting and Finance within a global hardware technology company, with focus in manufacturing operations and/or business units. Experience with hardware industry warranty accounting is a plus. Understanding of manufacturing, supply chain, and operational flows. Advanced proficiency with Excel. Experience with SAP, Power BI, Tableau, or other finance applications or visualization tools. Detailed oriented with the highest regard for accuracy and completeness. Ability to multi-task, and work independently and in teams to identify and tackle problems enthusiastically and clearly identify key issues of importance for team resolution. Strong analytical skills in big data and processes. Ways to stand out from the crowd: Strong product cost and manufacturing knowledge with semiconductor industry. Advanced data analytics skills and experience with applications including SQL, Python, Alteryx, Tableau, and/or Power BI. Excellent communication and interpersonal skills, with proven track record of establishing relationships with cross functional business partners. Self-motivated and passionate in driving continuous improvement of systems and processes. Enjoy working in fast-paced environment with supreme skills of prioritization.

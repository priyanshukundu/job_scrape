Why Stifel Stifel strives for a culture that puts its clients and associates first: a culture where everyone belongs, everyone is welcome, and everyone contributes to the success of our clients, their careers, and the firm as a whole. Let’s talk about how you can find your place here at Stifel, where success meets success . What You'll Be Doing Join a team that keeps clients connected to their accounts. As an Operations Associate in Lost Security Holder, you will log and research large volumes of returned mail across the firm. You will use critical thinking to search databases, locate accounts, and resolve more complex situations, while building a strong understanding of department subject matter, including application support, training, and general operational questions. You will collaborate with team members, branch office personnel, and Stifel clients to deliver excellent service, following established procedures with the support of direct supervision. This is a great opportunity to build an operations foundation and develop research and problem-solving skills. What We're Looking For Sort large volumes of returned mail from various areas across the firm and log appropriately using internal systems. Identifies address mail issues and works to resolve including providing branches with the necessary documentation. Identifies potential problems or issues and escalates to department leadership if necessary. Performs searches for accounts with mail issues using various tools and identifies updated information by logging appropriately using internal systems. Makes updates to accounts and ensures all documentation is correct, which may include returning accounts to good status from abandonment. Completes various reports to ensure account coding, documents, and other account features are accurate for household mailing addresses. Work collaboratively within a team and with other department areas to perform assigned tasks. Provides the highest quality of customer service through written and verbal communication. Other department duties may be assigned, including hotline phone calls, department emails, document coding, and other account updates or projects. What You'll Bring Strong organizational and communication skills with the ability to follow up on open tasks. Ability to multitask, prioritize and manage time effectively. Basic knowledge of account types and account structures along with documentation needed beneficial. High-level customer service skills. Ability to learn Stifel's applications, industry rules/regulations, and Stifel policies that pertain to the department and Stifel's branches. Working knowledge of financial services applications, systems, terminology, and procedures. Education & Experience Minimum Required: High School Diploma, Bachelor's Degree preferred, or equivalent combination of experience and education. Minimum Required: 0-2 years of professional business-related experience. Systems & Technology Proficient in Microsoft Excel, Word, PowerPoint, and Outlook.

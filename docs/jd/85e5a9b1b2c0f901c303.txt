Position Location: Atlanta, GA Western Union is looking for a Senior Manager, Treasury Technology to provide technology leadership across a critical portfolio supporting Treasury operations, global liquidity management, bank connectivity, settlement processing, Treasury controls, and Treasury modernization. In this role, you will lead technology capabilities supporting more than $2 billion in daily liquidity and settlement activity and over 24,000 agent payments per month . You will be responsible for the strategy, delivery, and operational effectiveness of technology supporting Treasury and banking processes, while partnering closely with stakeholders across Treasury, Finance, Banking, Product, and Operations. This is an opportunity to lead a highly specialized technology domain while helping modernize the platforms, processes, and capabilities that underpin Western Union’s global financial operations. Applicants must be currently authorized to work in the United States on a full-time basis. Western Union will not sponsor applicants for work visas for this position. Role responsibilities Provide technology leadership across Western Union’s Treasury technology portfolio, including liquidity, settlement, banking connectivity, Treasury controls, and payment operations. Own technology strategy and roadmap development for Treasury platforms and capabilities, balancing modernization, business demand, regulatory commitments, operational stability, and technical sustainability. Lead technology delivery across strategic Treasury initiatives, including Ion Wallstreet, Kyriba, Stablecoin, SWIFT CAT-IM, and broader Treasury modernization activities. Oversee technology supporting SWIFT connectivity, bank access administration, banking partner integrations, Treasury compliance processes, settlement processing, and operational support . Partner with Treasury, Finance, Banking, Product, Operations, Risk, and Technology stakeholders to establish priorities and translate business needs into scalable technology solutions. Maintain strong operational oversight across business-critical Treasury platforms, with an emphasis on availability, resilience, controls, and production stability. Establish effective portfolio prioritization across production support, regulatory requirements, business initiatives, modernization, and technical debt. Drive simplification, automation, and optimization across Treasury platforms and supporting operational processes. Strengthen technology architecture and integration patterns as new banking relationships, Treasury capabilities, payment products, and platforms are introduced. Provide leadership for complex technology initiatives involving internal teams, banking partners, technology vendors, and other third parties. Develop organizational capability and resilience within a highly specialized technology domain, reducing concentration of knowledge and dependency on key individuals. Provide clear communication of portfolio health, risks, dependencies, priorities, and delivery progress to senior Technology and business leaders. Lead and develop a high-performing technology organization with strong accountability for delivery, operational excellence, and business partnership. Role requirements Master's degree with 8+ years of relevant experience, Bachelor's degree with 10+ years of relevant experience, or equivalent practical experience. 10+ years of experience supporting Treasury, banking, payments, financial services, or other mission-critical financial technology platforms, including leadership responsibilities. 8+ years of experience leading complex technology portfolios spanning both production operations and large-scale transformation initiatives. Strong understanding of Treasury technology domains, including liquidity management, cash management, settlement, bank connectivity, payment processing, and financial controls. 5+ years of experience working with banking connectivity and financial messaging technologies, preferably including SWIFT infrastructure and services. 5+ years of experience with Treasury Management Systems such as Wallstreet Suite, Kyriba, or comparable enterprise Treasury platforms. Demonstrated success leading complex integrations involving financial institutions, third-party technology providers, and enterprise platforms. Experience managing technology in highly regulated environments where resiliency, security, auditability, financial controls, and compliance are critical. Strong portfolio management and prioritization skills across competing operational, regulatory, technical, and strategic objectives. Proven ability to translate business strategy into technology roadmaps and communicate effectively with executive, business, and technical stakeholders. 5+ years of people leadership experience with a demonstrated ability to develop teams, establish accountability, and lead organizational and technology change. Preferred qualifications Experience within a global payments, banking, fintech, or financial services organization. Experience modernizing Treasury or financial technology platforms. Knowledge of Wallstreet Suite , Kyriba, SWIFT, bank connectivity, settlement platforms, digital assets, stablecoins, or blockchain-based financial infrastructure . Experience with technology supporting high-volume or high-value financial transactions. Experience leading strategic programs involving multiple banking, product, operational, and technology stakeholders. Familiarity with modern integration patterns, APIs, cloud platforms, automation, and observability within financial technology environments.

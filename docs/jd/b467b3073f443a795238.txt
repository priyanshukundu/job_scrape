Location: 4900 Tiedeman Road, Brooklyn Ohio Job Summary The Associate Central Supervision Principal serves as a supervisory principal for a group of Key Investment Services (KIS) registered representatives within a mid-sized, regional bank. Reporting to the Head of the Central Supervision Group (CSG), this role is responsible for overseeing bank brokerage, advisory, and insurance sales activities. Responsibilities include supervision of new account openings, suitability reviews, email and correspondence approvals, and ensuring compliance with firm policies and regulatory requirements. The role requires collaboration with senior supervision staff, compliance teams, and operational units to maintain a compliant and effective supervisory environment. Essential Functions Develop and maintain productive relationships with Sales Leaders and KIS registered representatives. Conduct supervisory principal reviews of new accounts, email communications, and customer correspondence. Review and respond to state registration and Blue Sky issues. Approve advertising materials, speaking engagements, seminars, and outside business activities. Conduct supervisory reviews of employee trading and periodic sales activity reports. Ensure electronic records of client files are maintained per firm and regulatory guidelines. Participate in training and education of KIS registered representatives. Assist with internal and external audit reviews and information requests. Identify sales activity trends and resolve concerns with registered representatives. Review annuity and insurance contracts, advisory program selections, and model changes. Performs other duties as assigned; duties, responsibilities and/or activities may change or new ones may be assigned at any time with or without notice Complies with all KeyBank policies and procedures, including without limitation, acting professionally at all times, conducting business ethically, avoiding conflicts of interest, and acting in the best interests of Key’s clients and Key. Strong ability to make informed decisions in a regulatory and supervisory context. Ability to work independently and manage multiple priorities effectively. Highly organized with strong attention to detail and accuracy. Demonstrated ability to prioritize tasks and meet deadlines. Ability to build and maintain effective relationships with teams and partners. Effectively resolve conflicts with internal and external partners or clients. Exhibits discretion and sensitivity in handling confidential information. Strong understanding of SEC and FINRA rules and regulations. Familiarity with investment products including annuities and insurance. Core Competencies All KeyBank employees are expected to demonstrate Key’s Values and abide by Key’s Code of Conduct.

Overview This is a remote role that may be hired in several markets across the United States. Priority given to those candidates located in the area of Raleigh, North Carolina, Phoenix, Arizona and Dallas,Texas . We are seeking a motivated Data Engineer to join our Data Platform Engineering Organization and help build scalable cloud-based data solutions that enable analytics and data-driven decision-making across the organization. The ideal candidate is a hands-on data engineer with experience in cloud-based data platforms, data warehousing, ETL/ELT development, and modern data engineering practices. This individual will contribute to the development and support of scalable data pipelines, integrations, and data products utilizing AWS Services, Snowflake, dbt, Python, SQL, and related technologies. As part of our growing Cloud Data & AI Platform organization, you will collaborate with a talented team of engineers, product partners, and business stakeholders to modernize enterprise data platforms and deliver cloud-based data solutions that support critical business initiatives. This is an opportunity to work with modern technologies, solve complex data challenges, and contribute to the bank's data and AI transformation journey. Responsibilities & Qualifications Duties and Responsibilities : The successful candidate will thrive in a collaborative environment, possess problem-solving skills, and be passionate about building reliable, scalable, and high-quality data solutions. Design, build, and support scalable data pipelines and cloud-based data solutions utilizing AWS Services, Snowflake, dbt, Python, SQL, and related technologies. Implement data warehouse solutions, dimensional data models, and ETL/ELT processes that support reporting, analytics, and business intelligence capabilities. Implement data quality validations, test cases, and controls to ensure accurate, consistent, and reliable data assets. Develop and maintain data tests, including dbt tests, to support data quality and reliability requirements. Support workflow orchestration, monitoring, troubleshooting, log analysis, and production operations using Airflow and cloud-native technologies. Collaborate with business users, product partners, analysts, and technology teams to deliver data solutions that support business objectives. Follow established engineering standards, development practices, documentation requirements, and testing procedures to support high-quality solution delivery. Participate in pipeline deployments, environment promotion activities, operational support, and performance optimization efforts using established CI/CD processes. Leverage AI-powered development and productivity tools to improve engineering efficiency, code quality, and delivery outcomes. Qualifications : Bachelor's Degree and 1 year experience OR High School Diploma or GED/Equivalent and 5 years' experience Preferred Area of Study : Computer Science, Information Systems, Data Science, or related field Preferred Area of Experience : Data engineering, database management, or related field. Experience with cloud-based data platforms and big data technologies Preferred Qualifications: Experience with enterprise ETL/ELT tools such as DataStage, SAP BODS, SSIS, Informatica, or equivalent technologies. Experience with Operational Data Stores (ODS), analytics platforms, and data products. Experience working with cloud-based and on-premises data environments. Exposure to Data Vault modeling and modern data warehousing methodologies. Understanding of data governance, security, compliance, and data quality management practices. Experience within Financial Services, Banking, or other highly regulated industries. Familiarity with Tableau, Power BI, or other analytics and visualization platforms. Experience using AI-powered development and productivity tools to improve engineering efficiency and delivery outcomes.

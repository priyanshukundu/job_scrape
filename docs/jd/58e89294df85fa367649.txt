Portfolio Claims Underwriter
Hiscox
Location: York; Glasgow; Colchester
Link: https://hiscox.wd3.myworkdayjobs.com/Hiscox_External_Site/job/York/Portfolio-Claims-Underwriter_R0020776

Job Type: Permanent Build a brilliant future with Hiscox What is the role? To deliver the Hiscox standard of claims service across a mixed portfolio of Bodily Injury and Medical Malpractice claims. To work closely with underwriters, ensuring they are aware of the latest trends and developments in their areas, and supporting them with product, sector and client development. To provide claims expertise and support across Claims and wider business projects, helping to ensure that business changes benefit our customers and the claims service provided to them. Key responsibilities Manage a portfolio of claims valued up to £100,000. Demonstrate customer focus throughout the claims lifecycle, with strong engagement with customers and brokers and a focus on achieving good customer outcomes. Investigate and manage claims from first notification through to conclusion, including pre-litigation, final hearing or settlement where appropriate. Review and confirm policy coverage positions with increasing confidence and the ability to reach decisions in-house. Set the claims strategy, handling matters in-house where possible and implementing prevention strategies to avoid formal claims being made against customers. Select, instruct and actively manage law firms and other experts where necessary, including proactive indemnity and cost management. Liaise and negotiate with clients, brokers, claimants and their representatives, including attending mediations, JSMs and court hearings. Proactively manage the claims portfolio in accordance with the CUG and relevant KPIs and SLAs. Identify fraudulent claims and opportunities for recovery, with clear evidence of strategic claims handling to maximise opportunities that benefit the customer or Hiscox. Identify trends and areas of concern, providing regular reports to key stakeholders through contributions to Trilogy Light, internal training sessions and claims managers. Develop and manage relationships with key internal stakeholders, including RUMs, RMs, Product and Sector Heads, Customer Relations, CWG and Conduct, while demonstrating commercial awareness. Develop and manage relationships with key external stakeholders, including insureds, brokers, law firms and other experts. Take referrals from DAs regarding coverage, strategy and reallocation to the internal claims team. Provide technical advice and guidance to insureds. Support the continuous improvement of the Claims operation in line with the current Claims strategy, identifying opportunities and helping to develop solutions. Embrace new technologies, including Copilot and other digital tools, as they become available. Participate in Group, UK or Claims-wide projects and change programmes that may affect the claims area and wider team, providing subject-matter expertise where required. Work towards relevant professional qualifications, such as CII, CILEx or an equivalent qualification. Development opportunities Formal development Hiscox mentoring scheme Career Gurus scheme Learning Hub and LinkedIn Learning Panel technical training Industry-specific and regulatory training Data, AI and new technology training Legal and CII training and qualifications Informal development Shadowing underwriters and claims underwriters Attending underwriter training sessions Attending monthly key claims meetings Active knowledge-sharing during team meetings On-the-job training and coaching Claims “interesting case” learning Experience required At least four years’ experience handling Bodily Injury and/or Medical Malpractice claims, either as an insurance claims handler or within a law firm. Insurance experience is advantageous but not essential. For candidates with insurance experience, policy coverage knowledge relating to Bodily Injury and/or Medical Malpractice would be relevant. Knowledge of pre-action protocols and civil litigation. Experience managing multiple stakeholders at different levels. Strong communication and interpersonal skills. Good organisational skills.

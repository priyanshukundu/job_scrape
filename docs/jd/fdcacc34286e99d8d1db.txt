Job Description As a Solution Engineer, you will own technical discovery, solution design, and client advisory across the entire project lifecycle in the Risk Decisioning space. You will lead the process of gathering, analyzing, and documenting complex client business and technical requirements, converting them into clear specifications for Equifax’s cloud-based integrations. In this dual pre-sales and post-sales role, you will build trusted relationships with clients from technical practitioners to C-suite executives by translating Equifax’s technology into measurable business value, ensuring feasibility prior to contract, and providing technical leadership during implementation. What you will do: Requirements Gathering & Documentation: Lead technical discovery sessions to elicit, analyze, and document client business needs, technical workflows, and data integration requirements. Architecture & Specification: Translate gathered requirements into detailed solution design documentation, API integration specifications, and technical scopes of work (SOWs) for Equifax’s Risk Decisioning platform. Pre-Sales Technical Advisory: Partner with the Sales & Technology Advisor teams during pre-sales discovery to validate technical feasibility, assist in technical solution presentations, and design customized proof-of-concept (POC) solutions where applicable. API Integration Consulting: Guide client engineering teams through post-sale integration with Equifax’s cloud-based RESTful APIs, addressing architecture and workflow questions. Delivery Team Alignment: Serve as the technical bridge between clients and internal Delivery resources, handing off structured requirements and translating high-level solution designs into actionable specifications for technical implementation. Product Advocacy & Collaboration: Collaborate with the internal product teams for requesting new platform features/enhancements, serving as an advocate for customers. Relationship & Quality Oversight: Maintain ongoing technical alignment with client stakeholders throughout project execution to safeguard quality, address changing requirements, and ensure high client satisfaction. Autonomy: Operates independently with minimal guidance, managing work plans for assignments of moderate complexity. Problem Solving: Applies fresh perspectives to existing client processes to solve complex data and technical challenges. Impact: Drives decisions within guidelines that directly affect client satisfaction, project deliverables, and operational execution.

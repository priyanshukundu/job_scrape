Job Description Program Management Analyst, Product Risk Programs & Executive Support The Program Management Analyst will support the execution and continued evolution of Visa’s A.360 and Product Risk Review programs. This role is approximately 75% Program Management and Business Operations and 25% Executive and Team Support. The ideal candidate is a highly organized, solutions-oriented professional who can manage multiple priorities, coordinate across teams, analyze data, improve processes, and develop clear communications for senior leaders. Prior risk management experience is not required. Key Responsibilities Program Management & Business Operations | 75% Coordinate program activities from intake through assessment, review, launch, and post-launch monitoring. Manage timelines, deliverables, dependencies, action items, and multiple concurrent priorities. Partner across Product, Technology, Legal, Compliance, Risk, and other teams to drive initiatives forward. Develop dashboards, reports, and executive updates using Power BI and other reporting tools. Analyze program data to identify trends, bottlenecks, and opportunities to improve efficiency and cycle times. Support process improvements, workflow enhancements, automation, and technology initiatives. Partner with Technology teams on tool enhancements, testing, implementation, and troubleshooting. Explore AI tools, including Microsoft Copilot and Claude, to improve reporting, workflow management, and operational efficiency. Maintain program documentation, procedures, training materials, and communications. Executive & Team Support | 25% Coordinate leadership meetings and working sessions. Prepare agendas, presentations, briefing materials, and executive-ready updates. Track decisions, action items, owners, and deadlines to ensure timely follow-through. Support scheduling, meeting preparation, team communications, and other administrative needs for program leadership.

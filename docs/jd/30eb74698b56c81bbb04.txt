About the Team: The Factory Platform team is building the digital backbone for all engineering, manufacturing, test, launch, and data review operations at Relativity. Each day we are getting to know our users in every department of the company, defining the core abstractions Relativity is using to scale our vehicle program. Like an operating system for manufacturing, our software enables users to understand, make decisions about, and command the factory. Every engineer on our team owns a core part of our system. As an engineer on our team, you will be responsible for understanding your software system and the domain area so you can make informed technical decisions about how to evolve the system over time. In an environment where everyone has access to AI, our team sees good software as a castle surrounded by a marketplace. In this metaphor, the castle represents a core database and APIs with all the domain objects and critical workflows relevant to the business. Our team builds that castle; we define the abstractions, own the database, and build the API. The village represents a large collection of user-created tools and dashboards that call our APIs. Our goal is to empower to company to build whatever they need on our platform. We are also building the biggest, best market tent in the marketplace, applying our craft and sense of good software to build something refined that is much higher wuality than what nontechnical users can vibecode in a day We believe in using AI to amplify an individual's own intellect and sense of design, as opposed to simply automating mediocre work. We also believe in using AI to bring order to chaotic systems, and to design software that is better and more robust than before. If you're fired up about building the digital foundation of humanity's future in space, we are the team for you. About the Role: As a Software Engineer I on the Factory Platform team, you will build applications that directly support design and manufacturing engineers across the company. You'll work on projects like automating the analysis of telemetry data streaming from test stands, building tools to streamline supply chain and production planning processes, and creating dashboards that give engineers real-time visibility into manufacturing operations. You will participate in the full software development lifecycle, from understanding user requirements to designing, implementing, testing, and deploying solutions. You'll work across the stack—building responsive front-ends, scalable back-ends, and data pipelines that connect systems together. You'll follow agile development practices, write quality code that is continuously integrated and deployed, and iterate based on feedback from the engineers and operators who use your tools every day. Product-oriented – obsessed with building a high-quality software product Curious about aerospace and manufacturing (prior experience not required!)

Senior Financial Analyst - JLL Location: Chicago, Illinois (Aon Center) What this job involves: Our Accelerate 2030 strategy needs sharp financial insight, and our Transformation Office is where that work happens. As our Senior Financial Analyst, you'll join the Transformation Office to build the analysis and metrics that show how we're progressing against our strategic priorities. You'll design financial reporting, maintain forecasting models, and partner with leaders across the business to identify risks, gaps, and opportunities. If you're a creative problem solver, a clear communicator, and organized enough to keep multiple projects moving at once, this role is for you. We're looking for someone comfortable with a high pace of change, who works independently, and who holds themselves to the highest standards of behavior, collaboration, and work ethic. What your day-to-day will look like: Design and deliver financial reporting and KPI scorecards that track progress on Accelerate 2030 initiatives, including baselines and financial targets Build and maintain financial models to support forecasting, budgeting, and scenario analysis Generate monthly and quarterly reports, including profit and loss analysis and variance commentary Prepare and develop the annual budget plan, partnering with leaders to research and correct budget discrepancies Identify tactical and strategic opportunities, gaps, and financial risks, and support cost-savings and process improvement initiatives across concurrent projects Organize and maintain data collection, reporting, and communication, supporting efforts to automate and simplify reporting processes while preserving data integrity Handle special projects and other Transformation Office priorities as required by your manager Required Qualifications: Bachelor's degree in Finance or Accounting 3 or more years of relevant experience in financial analysis, including financial modeling, forecasting, and budgeting Advanced Microsoft Excel skills Detailed financial expertise, including profit and loss analysis Solid organization, analytical, and coordination skills, with the ability to manage multiple projects at once Meticulous attention to detail and accuracy, with sound decision-making Ability to work in the office at least three days a week at the Aon Center in Chicago Preferred Qualifications: Proven initiative and the ability to work independently without constant oversight Polished oral and written communication skills; experience creating presentations is a plus Ability to collaborate across many levels and engage comfortably with senior leaders in meetings and calls Flexible and adaptable, comfortable with frequent change Outstanding customer service skills, with a partnership mindset toward internal stakeholders Exceptional interpersonal skills Compensation: Amount below reflects the base salary range.

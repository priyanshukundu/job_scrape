Analyst, Multi-housing Capital Markets Research – JLL What this job involves: The Analyst will join JLL's capital markets research team, partnering with multi-housing / Living sector business stakeholders to analyze U.S. capital markets liquidity and pricing trends, capital formation trends, and themes related to the multi-housing / Living sector and Living alternative sectors. The Analyst will report to the Director, Capital Markets Research, and will collaborate with various stakeholders across different lines and/or alternative property sectors. The Analyst will work in collaboration with a cross-functional team of property sector and business line researchers, each specializing in different commercial real estate property sectors and investment areas. This position will lead projects and initiatives that span U.S. multi-housing / Living capital markets and Living alternatives, including creating and updating capital markets research presentations, content and analysis on the overall investment market, liquidity and pricing trends, and economic themes. What your day-to-day will look like: Collaborate with the Director of Capital Markets Research and producers across JLL's multi-housing capital markets platform to develop bespoke research deliverables and analysis for the multi-housing market, with a focus on capital markets trends (identifying trends across top investors, debt markets, product subtypes, etc.) Create and maintain content for the market-leading Multi-Housing Capital Markets Overview, presented to clients and internal stakeholders Produce and maintain property sector talking points on transactions activity and pricing trends for the firm's senior executives Utilize and serve as a superuser of JLL's proprietary technology products to analyze bidding and pricing data, mine and maintain lists of underbidders, and develop unique content based on the data Serve as the go-to resource for ad hoc requests related to transactions, pitches, and research reports Maintain house view materials related to the U.S. multi-housing and Living sector, collaborating across global JLL Research teams Assemble data from, and develop mastery of, key research resources (such as Real Capital Analytics, RealPage, ApartmentIQ, CoStar, Green Street, NCREIF, Trepp, etc.) Required Qualifications: Bachelor's degree in business, economics, real estate, or a related field One to two years of experience in commercial real estate analysis, finance, or a related field, and/or relevant internship experience Preferred Qualifications: Working knowledge of commercial real estate dynamics Strong organizational skills, analytical skills, attention to detail, and problem-solving ability A focus on responsiveness and resourcefulness, with the ability to efficiently multi-task, prioritize, and manage work in a dynamic environment with senior stakeholders Strong command of Microsoft Office products (particularly Excel and PowerPoint) Location: New York, NY | Onsite JLL Research consists of 570+ full-time professionals globally, working as the largest research group in the real estate industry and among the best of any worldwide professional services firm.

To learn more about CIBC, please visit CIBC.com What You'll Be Doing The Treasury Management Implementation & Services Consultant partners with clients and internal stakeholders to deliver a seamless implementation and support experience across CIBC’s treasury management products and services. This role manages multiple client implementation projects across several banking groups, collaborates closely with Treasury Sales during client engagements, and serves as a knowledgeable resource on the bank’s treasury management capabilities and client-focused approach. The consultant is responsible for supporting the documentation, implementation, and delivery of a broad suite of treasury solutions, including remittance and deposit services, remote capture, internet banking, ACH origination, wires, positive pay, bill payment, sweeps, and account reconcilement.

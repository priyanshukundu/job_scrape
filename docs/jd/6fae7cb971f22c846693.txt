Role Overview The Enrollment Consultant serves as a strategic enrollment subject matter expert, partnering with Sales, Client Management, brokers, clients, and internal partners to develop enrollment strategies that support employee engagement, participation, and growth. The role combines enrollment expertise, market insight, client needs, and data to recommend the right enrollment approach for each case. Enrollment Consultants help shape enrollment strategy from initial planning through post-enrollment evaluation, identifying opportunities to improve outcomes and maximize the value of available enrollment capabilities. What You'll Do Develop enrollment strategies based on client objectives, employee demographics, product mix, enrollment environment, available capabilities, and desired outcomes. Serve as a strategic enrollment consultant to Sales and Client Management, providing recommendations on enrollment approaches, solutions, communications, and support. Participate in client and broker conversations as the enrollment subject matter expert, helping articulate enrollment capabilities and recommend strategies aligned with the client’s needs. Use enrollment data and results to drive recommendations by identifying trends, opportunities, and changes that could improve future enrollment performance. Partner with internal teams from enrollment strategy development through post-enrollment evaluation to ensure the strategy is understood, supported, and executed effectively. Evaluate enrollment outcomes to understand what worked, identify opportunities for improvement, and recommend adjustments for future enrollment strategies. Identify client and market needs through engagement with Sales, Client Management, brokers, clients, and other partners, bringing insights back to the organization to inform future enrollment capabilities. Evaluate existing and emerging enrollment solutions to identify where tools, technologies, communication approaches, and resources can create the greatest value. Support pilots and new enrollment approaches by identifying appropriate cases, contributing enrollment expertise, and evaluating results to help determine value and broader applicability. Partner with Marketing and other internal teams to help ensure enrollment communications and solutions remain relevant to evolving client and market needs. Build strong relationships across Sales, Client Management, Implementation, Marketing, and other partners, becoming a trusted resource for strategic enrollment consultation. What You'll Bring Bachelor’s degree strongly preferred. 5+ years of experience in enrollment and/or group insurance. Strong knowledge of enrollment practices, strategies, marketing approaches, and enrollment solutions. Deep knowledge of group employee benefits, including voluntary and contributory products. Experience with sales, implementation, and enrollment processes. Ability to translate client needs, market insights, and enrollment data into actionable strategies and recommendations. Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with the ability to evaluate enrollment results and identify opportunities for improvement. Strong consultative skills and the ability to influence decisions through expertise, insights, and recommendations. Ability to build and maintain effective relationships with internal and external partners. Strong verbal and written communication skills, including the ability to communicate enrollment strategy and recommendations to clients, brokers, Sales, and internal partners. Strong organizational and prioritization skills with the ability to manage multiple strategic enrollment engagements. Ability to travel within an assigned territory, including periodic overnight travel.

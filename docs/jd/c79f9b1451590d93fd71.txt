Meet the team: This role is an integral member of the Policy team, focused on building out policy content infrastructure to inform Company-wide goals, initiatives, and objectives in partnership with the Director of Policy. There will be strong cross-collaboration with Advocacy and Government Affairs to ensure policy position and initiative alignment. Come take on an exciting opportunity to dive into a range of critically important policy issues and empower people to take control of health. Where you come in: You will conduct foundational research on core issues used to develop policy materials, company policy positions, and support external policy initiatives. This role will also be responsible for administrating the internal policy database and infrastructure, to include internal dissemination efforts. You will help with policy pull-through efforts and partner with other team members in Advocacy and Government Affairs, to help shape their external engagement and champion the needs of people with diabetes, their caregivers, and the broader diabetes community. You will develop and contribute to high-level insights or trends on evolving policy areas within the health care landscape used to presentation to Department and Company leadership. You will develop subject-matter expertise, with guidance from the Director of Policy, on the evidence behind policy, to include understanding and leveraging Health Services Research and Health Economics and Outcomes Research fields to inform and shape policy. You will help manage the tracking of Annual Policy Priorities, and internal metrics and reporting used to quantify and measure Policy team goals and objectives. What makes you successful: Familiarity with and deep curiosity in understanding the core issues impacting medical device, pharmaceutical, and health care system businesses. Direct experience researching and analyzing public policy, in areas related to access and health delivery policy, science and regulatory policy, or fiscal and industrial policy. Direct experience conducting analyses and creating presentations and other materials that synthesize pertinent and actionable information across a range of stakeholder audiences. Bachelor's degree required, preferably in a relevant field such as Public Policy, Public Health, Health Services Research, Health Administration, Political Science, Communications, or Life Sciences. An exciting and innovative, industry-leading organization committed to our employees, customers, and the communities we serve.

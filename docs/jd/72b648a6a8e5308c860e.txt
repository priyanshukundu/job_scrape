Job Description What is the opportunity? As an Operations Associate II, you will play a critical role in ensuring the accuracy, compliance, and integrity of essential documents and forms within our organization. Your responsibilities will include meticulously reviewing a variety of materials to verify proper completion, confirm alignment with system data, and ensure adherence to established policies and procedures. This role demands strong attention to detail, exceptional critical thinking, and the ability to adapt to evolving requirements. By maintaining high standards of quality and efficiency, you will contribute directly to the smooth operation of our processes and the success of our team. Joining the established Document Processing team, you will have the opportunity to develop specialized expertise, enhance your analytical skills, and grow professionally in a dynamic and collaborative environment. This position is ideal for individuals who thrive in detail-oriented roles and are committed to delivering precise, reliable results. What will you do? Provide second to none customer service to our clients via email and phone interactions Process requests via Salesforce, Workflow and AOS Docupace Complete daily reports that support Document Processing Attention to Detail: Ensure all tasks, documents, and communications are executed with precision, minimizing errors and maintaining high-quality standards Review and validate documents for completeness, accuracy, and compliance. Compare submitted materials against system records to ensure consistency. Identify discrepancies, escalate issues, and support resolution efforts. Adhere to policies and procedures while maintaining confidentiality and data security. What do you need to succeed? Must-have 4-year degree from an accredited university (or equivalent) OR High school diploma (or equivalent) AND 3 years of job-related experience Demonstrated ability to learn new technology, critical thinking skills required to support escalated issue resolution and/or oversight requirements associated with exception-based complex processes Ability to handle multiple priorities in a fast paced and deadline driven environment with a high attention to detail and ability to maintain confidentiality Exceptional interpersonal, verbal and written communication skills, with an ability to work effectively with all levels of staff and management Proficient computer skills in Microsoft-based software products including Word, Excel, Outlook, and Access Nice-to-have Experience within the document review Previous experience in the securities, banking or finance industry preferred Ability to integrate Excel and Access databases into daily work Knowledge on Broadridge is preferred What’s in it for you?

The Device Risk product team is responsible for the continued The Device Risk Product team is responsible for the continued development, delivery, and support of TransUnion’s global Device Risk solution, the largest revenue contributor within the Global Fraud Solutions portfolio. The team manages the product roadmap and end-to-end product development lifecycle, partnering with Engineering, Data Science, Sales, Marketing, Customer Success, and Customer Engagement to deliver innovative, reliable fraud-prevention capabilities to approximately 500 customers worldwide. This role reports to the VP of Strategic Planning. This is a hybrid position and involves regular performance of job responsibilities virtually as well as in-person at an assigned TU office location for a minimum of two days a week. Role Overview and Core Responsibilities Own and prioritize the team backlog, ensuring epics, features, user stories, and acceptance criteria clearly reflect business requirements, customer needs, and expected outcomes. Lead daily Scrum meetings and other Agile ceremonies, working closely with Engineering and Product Management to plan sprints, clarify requirements, remove obstacles, and maintain delivery momentum. Translate product strategy and business requirements into actionable development work, clearly communicating the customer value and business rationale behind each initiative. Manage product updates across the full product development lifecycle, from ideation and sprint planning through testing, customer integration, production release, and post-release support. Review sprint demonstrations and completed work to confirm requirements and acceptance criteria have been met before approving releases. Partner with Engineering, Customer Success, Customer Engagement, and Product Management to triage customer-facing service disruptions, defects, and other time-sensitive issues. Balance planned roadmap commitments with urgent customer requests and unplanned work, clearly communicating priority changes and their business impact to technical and business stakeholders. Support development teams through internal governance and launch-readiness processes, including security, third-party risk management, pricing, billing, and release requirements. Coordinate across globally distributed teams in the United States, India, and Costa Rica to maintain alignment, continuity, and timely delivery. Serve as a product ambassador and primary point of contact for questions related to assigned Device Risk capabilities, including authentication, dashboards, and customer-configured rules. Required Knowledge and Experiences Bachelor’s degree in a related field or equivalent professional experience that demonstrates the technical, product, and business knowledge needed to perform the role. Seven or more years of relevant information technology, software product, or technical delivery experience, including experience working closely with development teams. Four or more years of experience in an Agile Scrum environment, with demonstrated responsibility for managing backlogs, refining user stories, defining acceptance criteria, and supporting sprint execution. Product ownership experience supporting an externally sold, customer-facing technology product through the full lifecycle, from ideation and development through release and ongoing support. Experience translating complex business and customer needs into clear technical requirements and driving cross-functional teams toward decisions and deliverable outcomes. Required Technical Skills Working knowledge of Agile and Scrum practices, including backlog management, sprint planning, refinement, daily stand-ups, sprint demonstrations, and release planning. Demonstrated ability to create and manage epics, features, user stories, acceptance criteria, and prioritized product backlogs. Strong technical aptitude and the ability to understand software architecture, product capabilities, dependencies, defects, and production-release requirements. Experience managing work across the Product Development Life Cycle, including development, testing, customer integration, deployment, and production support. Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to evaluate complex issues, clarify requirements, manage competing priorities, and determine appropriate courses of action independently.

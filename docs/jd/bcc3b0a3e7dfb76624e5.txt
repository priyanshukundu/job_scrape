Job Description: The Role As a Mainframe/Data Platform Production Support Engineer , you will provide Level 3 support for critical enterprise data platforms and applications that power Workplace Investing. You will troubleshoot and resolve complex production issues across Mainframe and distributed environments, with a strong focus on COBOL, JCL, DB2, Oracle, and SQL-based platforms . Working closely with development, infrastructure, and database teams, you will perform root cause analysis, support data integrity, and help ensure the reliability and stability of business-critical systems that are used across the organization. The Expertise and Skills You Bring Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or a related field, or equivalent experience. 5+ years of experience supporting enterprise applications, data platforms, or production environments. Experience supporting Mainframe environments, including COBOL, JCL, DB2, CICS, and VSAM . Strong experience with Oracle and SQL , including data analysis, query development, troubleshooting, stored procedures, and data validation. Experience supporting enterprise batch processing and scheduling tools, preferably Control-M . Experience supporting data platforms, database-driven applications, and backend services utilizing SQL and REST APIs. Familiarity with DevOps practices, CI/CD pipelines, and tools such as Git, Bitbucket, and Jenkins. Troubleshoot and resolve application defects through source code analysis. Read and debug COBOL application code to identify root causes and implement fixes. Participate in code reviews, releases, and application enhancements. Partner with development teams while maintaining ownership of application-level issues. The Team The Employer Journey Data Platform team supports the critical data services that power Workplace Investing.

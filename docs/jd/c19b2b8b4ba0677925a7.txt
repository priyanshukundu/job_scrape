Location Designation: Hybrid - 3 days per week Role Overview New York Life is seeking an AML Case Analyst to join its Anti-Money Laundering compliance team. This is a hands-on, case-level role focused on the review and disposition of AML alerts, investigation of potentially suspicious activity, and support of the SAR filing process across the Company’s insurance, annuity, broker-dealer, and advisory businesses. The AML Case Analyst will work closely with senior AML officers, contributing to the day-to-day execution of the AML program with particular emphasis on transaction monitoring, case documentation, and escalation of complex matters. This role is well-suited to a compliance professional with AML investigation experience in the broker-dealer or securities space who is looking to deepen their expertise in a dynamic, multi-entity program environment. Who We Are The Compliance department at New York Life serves as a strategic second-line-of-defense partner to the businesses it covers. The AML function supports a diverse set of business lines, including life, annuity and retail broker-dealer products, and investment advisory services. We are a team of practitioners engaged with substantive regulatory and financial crime questions daily, partnering with business units and other control functions to maintain a strong and well-documented compliance program. What You’ll Do Alert Review & Transaction Monitoring Review and disposition AML alerts, assess customer activity for indicators of money laundering, terrorist financing, structuring, or other suspicious behavior across business lines with a focus on retail broker-dealer activity. Research customer profiles, account and policy information, transaction histories, and relevant internal and external sources to understand activity and identify inconsistencies or red flags. Prepare accurate, well-organized case notes documenting research performed, relevant findings, and the factual basis for each proposed closure or escalation; obtain required review or approval. Case Investigation & SAR Support Conduct investigations into escalated cases involving potentially suspicious activity across business lines with a focus on retail broker-dealer activity. Organize transaction timelines, customer information, and supporting records; prepare clear factual case summaries for review by senior AML officers. Support the SAR filing process under the supervision of a senior AML officer, including drafting SAR narratives consistent with FinCEN guidance and enterprise documentation standards. Assist with no-SAR closure documentation, ensuring that decisions not to file are supported by a clear and defensible written rationale. OFAC & Sanctions Support Assist with review of OFAC screening alerts, including initial research on potential hits and preparation of disposition documentation for review by a senior AML officer. Support escalation and review of sanctions-related matters, including blocked or rejected transaction scenarios, under the oversight of the enterprise sanctions program. Documentation & Program Support Maintain accurate and complete case records in the AML case management system, ensuring all documentation meets program standards and is suitable for regulatory examination, and maintain documentation of AML policies and procedures. Assist with referrals, 314(a) searches, and other regulatory compliance activities as directed by senior AML officers. Contribute to training and program improvement efforts as requested, including sharing observations from casework that may be relevant to typology updates or procedural enhancements. What You’ll Bring Required 2+ years of AML compliance experience, with meaningful hands-on experience in alert review, case investigation, or SAR preparation. Working knowledge of BSA/AML regulatory requirements, including FinCEN SAR filing obligations, OFAC regulations, and USA PATRIOT Act Sections 314(a) and 314(b). Experience with OFAC screening platforms and sanctions alert review . Direct experience in a broker-dealer or securities AML environment, with working knowledge of FINRA AML rules, SEC requirements, and broker-dealer-specific financial crime typologies. Familiarity with transaction monitoring platforms and case management systems; ability to conduct thorough research using internal and external data sources. Strong verbal and written communication skills, with the ability to produce clear, accurate, and well-organized case documentation and SAR narratives. Attention to detail, sound escalation judgment, and commitment to documentation quality and regulatory accuracy. Bachelor’s degree. Series 7 license, or ability to obtain within 12 months Preferred Experience in a life insurance, annuity AML environment, or familiarity with insurance-specific financial crime typologies such as premium financing abuse, early surrender patterns, or third-party payors. CAMS certification (Certified Anti-Money Laundering Specialist) or active progress toward certification. Familiarity with Vulnerable Adult Financial Abuse (VAFA) indicators or elder financial exploitation red flags.

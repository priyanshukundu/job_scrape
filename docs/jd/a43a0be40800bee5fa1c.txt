Credit Risk Officer - Securitization
Natixis
Location: New York, United States
Link: https://recrutement.natixis.com/en/job/credit-risk-officer-securitization-2

Job Description The VP level Credit Officer will play a vital role in assessing and reviewing various proposals from the Global Structured Credit and Solutions (GSCS) group, with a focus on credit risk evaluation. The proposals may include CLO warehouse/lending facilities, capital call facilities, and ABS warehouse/lending facilities, among others. As a key member of the Natixis Credit Risk Financial Institutions Group (FIG), the Credit Officer will contribute to the growth and success of our structured credit initiatives. Essential duties and responsibilities Conduct in-depth credit analysis and risk assessments, primarily focusing on structured credit and securitized product proposals. Perform due diligence calls or visits when appropriate; represent the bank professionally to clients. Prepare comprehensive written risk opinions and analyses; effectively articulate compelling arguments in support of the risk opinion. Collaborate with bankers and originators to meet closing deadlines while maintaining high-quality credit analysis. Review deal documents and critically assess the Front Office’s analysis when necessary. Present findings to senior management and credit committees, providing clear and insightful recommendations. Monitor the existing GSCS portfolio and ensure covenant compliance, paying close attention to early warning indicators of credit risk deterioration. Stay informed on industry trends and macroeconomic issues that may impact the portfolio, recommending proactive changes or actions for specific clients or groups of clients as needed. Qualifications Bachelor's degree required; graduate degree preferred. A minimum of 6 years of risk management experience, specifically focused on the analysis of structured products and securitizations. Experience with various financial institutions and entities (e.g., banks, private equity funds, hedge funds, regulated funds, insurance companies, and broker/dealers) is a plus. Knowledge and understanding of internal credit rating models and methodologies, as well as credit risk monitoring approaches and systems. Strong verbal and written communication skills. Ability to think abstractly and project various "what-if" scenarios. MS Office (Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook) The salary range for this VP position will be between $160,000 - $200,000.

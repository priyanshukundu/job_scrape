Overview This is a hybrid role (remote Monday/Friday, in office Tuesday - Thursday). Raleigh, NC or Charlotte, NC. At First Citizens Bank, we believe meaningful careers are built through meaningful relationships. For more than 125 years, we've supported our clients, communities and colleagues through a culture rooted in connection, collaboration and long-term success. This role is part of the Legal Operations function within the Corporate Legal Department of a leading financial institution. The position serves as the department's primary resource for legal technology administration, analytics, process automation, and reporting. The individual will manage and optimize legal applications, support technology implementations, develop reporting and dashboards, and drive operational efficiencies across the legal department. The ideal candidate combines experience administering legal operations platforms such as CounselLink with strong technical capabilities in data analytics, business intelligence, reporting automation, and application support. This role will partner closely with Legal, IT, Finance, FP&A, Procurement, and outside counsel to enhance legal department technology and business processes. This position reports to the Head of Legal Operations and offers significant exposure to legal technology strategy, automation initiatives, and enterprise application management.

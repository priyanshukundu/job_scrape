Citi is hiring an Equity Research Business Analytics Product Manager to help shape how equity research is delivered, monitored, and continuously improved across the Americas business. In this role, you will build performance dashboards, analyze research output metrics, and coordinate oversight processes that keep one of the world's leading research franchises running smoothly. Your work will directly strengthen research quality and give analysts and managers the insights they need to perform at their best. Responsibilities Monitor equity research output quality and coordinate approval workflows to support day-to-day product oversight across sectors and regions. Coordinate the equity research oversight committee process, ensuring timely review of research initiations, ratings changes, and material updates alongside supervisory analysts, control teams, and the production group. Produce and analyze equity research performance metrics to help the management team identify opportunities across each research franchise. Build and maintain business analytics dashboards that translate key performance indicators into clear, actionable insights for managers and analysts. Identify and implement AI-driven automation to streamline recurring reporting workflows and promote broader AI adoption across the department. Establish project governance processes that surface dependencies, risks, and conflicts across programs. Keep stakeholders informed of program status and escalations through clear, structured communication. Maintain program documentation, meeting records, and organizational knowledge so the team has access to current information at all times. Required Qualifications & Skills 5 to 8 years of experience in financial services, research management, product management, or business analytics. Proven ability to manage multiple projects at once, meet deadlines, and deliver results within a large, complex organization. Analytical skill to interpret performance data, build dashboards, and produce metrics that support decision-making, including proficiency in SQL, Python, or Tableau. Working understanding of equity research processes and the key performance indicators used to measure research quality. Strong organizational skills with a detail-oriented approach to documentation, reporting, and process management. Clear, confident communicator capable of coordinating across teams, functions, and seniority levels, and resolving issues independently. Willingness to obtain and maintain FINRA licenses, including the SIE, Series 63, 86, 87, and 24, with a bachelor's degree or equivalent professional experience. Beneficial Skills & Qualifications Familiarity with equity research workflows, research product management, or capital markets operations. Experience applying AI or automation tools to improve reporting processes and operational efficiency. Exposure to working with oversight or governance committees in a regulated financial environment. Experience collaborating with cross-regional teams within a global financial institution. Background in forecasting, performance frameworks, or business intelligence reporting for research or financial data. What We Offer Joining the Americas Equity Research Management team gives you the chance to shape how research is produced and measured at one of the world's leading financial institutions, while building skills that span product, analytics, and operations. Grow your career locally while collaborating with colleagues across Citi's global network. Work in a hybrid model with three days in the office and two days working remotely. Build new skills through training programs, mentorship, and ongoing professional development. Access wellbeing support, including medical coverage, mental health resources, parental leave, and family support programs. Plan for your future with competitive retirement contributions and investment options.

The Elastic Global Services (EGS) team builds the highly available, scalable, multi-tenant Cloud Services platform that underpins Snowflake services. Our work includes autoscaling cloud virtual machines, managing compute-cluster topologies and lifecycles, and orchestrating workloads.

Our infrastructure runs on Amazon Web Services, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud. We are looking for a Senior Software Engineer to lead complex distributed systems projects and improve the reliability, performance, and efficiency of our platform as customer demand grows.

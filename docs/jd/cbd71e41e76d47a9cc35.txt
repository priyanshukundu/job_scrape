Corporate Financial & Regulatory Reporting Analyst, Specialist
Vanguard
Location: Malvern, PA
Link: https://vanguard.wd5.myworkdayjobs.com/vanguard_external/job/Malvern-PA/Corporate-Financial---Regulatory-Reporting-Analyst--Specialist_184028

The Global Transfer Pricing and Intercompany Accounting team sits at the intersection of Vanguard's strategy, operations, and legal entity financial reporting. As Vanguard expands globally, evolves its operating model, and launches new business initiatives, this team ensures those changes are accurately reflected in financial results and regulatory reporting. The team supports enterprise reporting, cost allocation, governance, and decision making across Vanguard's U.S. and international businesses. We partner closely with Accounting, Tax, FinTech, FP&A, Legal, and business leaders to deliver insights, strengthen governance, and enhance reporting capabilities. This is an opportunity to help shape a newly formed team with an important enterprise mandate. The role offers a unique perspective on Vanguard’s operations and the chance to tackle complex business challenges, improve processes, and strengthen controls. Responsibilities Own and enhance Vanguard's monthly allocation processes, ensuring accurate results, strong controls, and increased automation. Partner with Accounting, Tax, FP&A, FinTech, and business leaders to support strategic initiatives, including entering new markets, acquisitions, and operating model changes. Evaluate and implement allocation methodologies that accurately reflect evolving business activities, organizational structures, and global regulatory tax landscape. Support the development of processes and reporting capabilities that give leaders clear insight into projected capital requirements, dividend capacity, and opportunities to return value to shareholders. Partner with global reporting and tax teams to develop tools and dashboards that improve visibility into allocations and support executive and board reporting. Lead the annual department recertification process and oversee new department requests. Partner with FP&A and data governance teams to improve data quality and streamline incorporation within domestic and international allocation models. Drive continuous improvement initiatives focused on governance, controls, documentation, technology, and operating efficiency. Prepare audit support materials and respond to auditor inquiries related to transfer pricing and intercompany accounting processes. Maintain effective internal controls that support accurate financial and regulatory reporting. Validate intercompany balances and eliminations, reconcile balance sheet accounts, and oversee settlement activities. Build strong relationships across Finance and the broader enterprise to resolve issues, share knowledge, and support consistent execution. Contribute to special projects and broader finance transformation initiatives. Qualifications Minimum of five years of experience in accounting, FP&A, audit, reporting, controllership, transfer pricing operations, or a related finance function. Undergraduate degree in Accounting, Finance, or a related field, or an equivalent combination of training and experience. Experience with transfer pricing operations, cost allocations, or intercompany accounting is preferred. Experience with Oracle Cloud Analytics, Power BI, and AI technology is preferred. Experience with governance, controls, or financial process improvement is preferred. Demonstrated ability to partner effectively across multiple functions and influence outcomes in a complex environment. Strong analytical, problem solving, and communication skills.

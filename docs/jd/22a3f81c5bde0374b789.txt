This role provides advanced analytical support, serves as a subject matter expert (SME) across key market risk and ALM processes, and coaches junior analysts. The Senior Market Risk Analyst provides key insights into the drivers of market risk exposure, directly supporting risk oversight and balance sheet management strategies, and contributes to the continual improvement of the market risk management framework. Essential Functions Serves as the functional owner for key market risk analytical processes supporting core ALM models, leading areas of model development, assumption design, analytical workflows, and risk reporting. Independently applies advanced financial and quantitative modeling techniques to interpret and explain risk behaviors, providing insight into key risk drivers and emerging trends. Coordinates closely with Risk, Treasury, Finance, Risk Data Architecture, and other related teams to support ALM risk analysis initiatives, ensuring effective alignment across data sourcing, modeling, and reporting activities. Prepares and presents market risk reports, analyses and insights for committees, workgroups, and senior management, translating complex analytical results into clear, decision-relevant risk implications. Partners with the Risk Data Architecture team to ensure the integrity and appropriateness of data used in ALM models by applying expertise in key data elements to assess impacts on market risk measures and risk reporting. Drives continual improvement of market risk analytics by leading model enhancements, analytical tools, and documentation, ensuring transparency, consistency, and audit readiness. Provides subject matter expertise for complex ad‑hoc market risk analyses and coaches Market Risk Analysts through technical guidance, quality oversight, and knowledge transfer across ALM processes. CoBank is a cooperative bank serving vital industries across rural America. The bank provides loans, leases, export financing and other financial services to agribusinesses and rural power, water and communications providers in all 50 states. The bank also provides wholesale loans and other financial services to affiliated Farm Credit associations serving more than 76,000 farmers, ranchers and other rural borrowers in 23 states around the country. CoBank is a member of the Farm Credit System, a nationwide network of banks and retail lending associations chartered to support the borrowing needs of U.S. agriculture, rural infrastructure and rural communities. Headquartered outside Denver, Colorado, CoBank serves customers from regional banking centers across the U.S. and also maintains an international representative office in Singapore.

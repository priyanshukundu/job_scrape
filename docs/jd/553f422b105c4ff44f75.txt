POSITION SUMMARY The Automation Risk & Controls Senior Advisor position will serve as a senior individual contributor responsible for advancing a balanced operational risk, IT risk, and automation governance program across Provider Experience and Operations (PX&O), with particular emphasis on claims and provider operations. This individual will partner with business, technology, and development teams to embed practical and sustainable risk and control requirements across business processes, applications, automations, and emerging technologies. The role will provide subject matter expertise in automated application controls, data governance, key report validation, and full-lifecycle automation governance. The individual will establish control requirements, challenge proposed solutions, monitor and escalate risks, support remediation, and enable SOX and SOC audit readiness without serving as the independent audit function or as an automation developer. Candidates who reside within 50 miles of the following locations will be required to perform onsite 3 days per week: Bloomfield, CT; Chattanooga, TN; Denver, CO; St. Louis, MO; and Scottsdale, AZ. Role Objectives Control Governance Establish and maintain formal governance for operational risk, technology-enabled business processes, automated application controls, and business-owned and technology-developed automations. Define practical control requirements, assess risk across the technology and automation lifecycle, monitor control performance and exceptions, challenge solutions where requirements are not met, and track remediation through closure. Provide expert guidance on data integrity, key reports, access, change management, IT operations, interfaces, implementations, third-party technology risk, and emerging technologies. Collaboration Build and sustain strong partnerships across PX&O Operations, Technology, development teams, Audit, and other key business functions. Work with technical teams to understand solution architecture and automation workflows, communicate risk and control expectations in practical terms, and support the integration of requirements into design, testing, deployment, monitoring, and maintenance activities. Facilitate open communication, knowledge sharing, and collaborative problem-solving to address risks, resolve control issues, and support SOX and SOC audit readiness. Communication Translate complex operational, technology, automation, and audit concepts into clear and actionable guidance for diverse audiences. Provide concise updates to leadership on risk exposures, control performance, automation-governance activities, unmet requirements, issue escalation, and remediation progress. Communicate effectively with technology and development teams without requiring hands-on development or coding responsibilities. Key Responsibilities Provide subject matter expertise in operational risk, IT risk, ITGCs, automated controls, and automation governance across PX&O, with particular emphasis on claims and provider operations. Document and maintain business and technology process flows, risk assessments, control matrices, and governance standards for technology-enabled processes, applications, automations, system interfaces, and emerging technologies. Establish and advise on control requirements across major ITGC domains, including logical and privileged access, change management, IT operations, batch processing, interfaces, incident management, system development and implementation, and third-party technology risk. Provide full-lifecycle governance over business-owned and technology-developed automations, including initial risk assessment and tiering, control design, development and testing standards, deployment readiness, change management, ongoing monitoring, exception handling, periodic review, recertification, and business continuity or manual fallback planning. Partner with development and technology teams to understand solution designs, identify control implications, challenge proposed approaches, and confirm that risk and control requirements are appropriately incorporated into implementation plans. Assess and monitor risks related to data integrity, system access, technology changes, automated processing, interfaces, configurable business rules, and emerging technologies, and escalate material risks or unmet control requirements to leadership. Support internal and external audit activities, including SOX and SOC coordination, evidence readiness, issue evaluation, remediation design, and remediation tracking, while maintaining clear first-line accountability for control governance. Monitor control performance and automation-related exceptions, identify opportunities to strengthen preventive and detective controls, and work with accountable owners to resolve deficiencies in a practical and sustainable manner. Champion risk and control awareness by delivering training, guidance, and communications that translate technical and audit concepts into actionable expectations for business, Technology, and development stakeholders. Key Qualifications Bachelor's degree in Accounting, Business, Risk Management, Information Systems, or a related field preferred. CISA certification preferred. 5+ years of Big Four audit experience strongly preferred, or 8+ years of other relevant technology risk and controls experience. Experience in healthcare operations or payer-provider environments strongly preferred. Demonstrated experience across both IT audit and first-line technology risk or control-governance activities, with the ability to translate audit expectations into practical and sustainable controls. Deep knowledge of ITGCs, including logical and privileged access, change management, IT operations, system development and implementation, interfaces, incident management, and third-party technology risk. Experience governing automated application controls, key reports, data integrity controls, business-owned automations, technology-developed automations, and technology-enabled workflows. Ability to understand automation concepts, interfaces, APIs, configurable business rules, and emerging technologies sufficiently to identify risks, establish control requirements, and communicate effectively with development and technology teams. Hands-on development or coding experience is not required. Experience with full-lifecycle automation governance, including risk assessment and tiering, control design, testing and deployment expectations, change control, monitoring, exception management, recertification, and continuity planning. Experience managing SOX and SOC audit coordination and external auditor relationships. Deep knowledge of SOX, SOC 1, SOC 2, and technology risk management frameworks. Proven ability to establish control requirements, challenge proposed solutions, drive issue escalation, and track remediation across complex programs. Excellent communication, stakeholder management, and influencing skills in a senior individual contributor capacity.

About the Role Nasdaq Market Services is seeking an accomplished Senior Software Engineer with a focus on Cloud Development and DevOps to join our team. Nasdaq is continuously reshaping markets and adopting new technologies to build innovative solutions that rewrite tomorrow. As a Senior Software Engineer, you will join a high-performing, experienced team delivering critical data solutions for the U.S. financial markets, including sophisticated data analytics and ingestion pipelines for our internal clients. You will learn from domain experts and have the opportunity to take on increasing responsibility within the development team. We are looking for candidates with a genuine drive to deliver leading technology solutions to today's markets. Key Responsibilities You will focus on developing and implementing software solutions, partnering closely with your team and with Nasdaq teams across the globe. Develop, implement, test, and maintain software applications and cloud infrastructure. Participate in the full software development lifecycle, from planning and design through coding and testing. Partner with product managers and team members to translate business requirements into software solutions, and provide user support, training, and documentation. Serve as a technical expert on development projects and document software functionality. Contribute to design meetings, code reviews, and mentor less experienced team members. Adopt a “you build it, you run it” philosophy by participating in an engineering on-call rotation to monitor, troubleshoot, and remediate production issues for services the team own. Minimum Qualifications 8 years of experience as a Java/Kotlin software engineer, with strong proficiency in object-oriented design principles. Strong backend development background in Kotlin/Java with Spring Boot, including REST API design, relational data modeling, and database migration tooling. Familiarity with AWS, cloud platforms, and AI technologies. Familiarity with Infrastructure-as-Code (Terraform). Experience with containerization and orchestration (Docker, Kubernetes, ECS) and CI/CD pipelines (GitLab CI). Experience with analytics tooling, including Apache Spark, Databricks, Redshift/Athena, Apache Iceberg, and Parquet-based data lakes. Proficiency with Linux commands and utilities, including Bash scripting. Strong problem-solving and analytical skills, with the ability to troubleshoot and resolve issues. Excellent communication and collaboration skills. Degree in Computer Science, Information Systems, or a related field, or equivalent work experience. Preferred Qualifications Willingness to support production systems as needed. A self-starter who is resourceful with unfamiliar systems and works effectively independently. Passion for learning new technologies and keeping pace with industry trends. Creativity and innovation in delivering solutions that meet user needs and add business value. Attention to detail and a commitment to quality. Adaptability in a complex, fast-paced environment with changing priorities and deadlines. Curiosity about the financial industry and the markets, products, and services Nasdaq offers. This position will be located in Denver and offers the opportunity for a hybrid work environment at least 3 days a week in-office, subject to change, providing flexibility and accessibility for qualified candidates.

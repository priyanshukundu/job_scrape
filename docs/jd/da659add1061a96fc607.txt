Purpose Contributes to the overall success of GBM - Business Risk Management in US. ensuring specific individual goals, plans, initiatives are executed / delivered in support of the team’s business strategies and objectives. Ensures all activities conducted are in compliance with governing regulations, internal policies and procedures. What You'll Do Championing a customer focused culture to deepen client relationships and leverage broader Bank relationships, systems, and knowledge. Reviewing electronic and voice communications generated by Global Banking and Markets (“GBM”) and Group Treasury staff for compliance with regulatory requirements and/or internal policies and procedures. Performing and managing investigations of electronic and voice communications (e.g., discussing the facts and circumstances surrounding a particular communication with an employee), documents findings and, where required, escalates to relevant stakeholders which communications include potential violations of regulatory requirements and/or internal policies and procedures. Performing market conduct related transaction monitoring for GBM sales and trading employees and manages related investigations, documents findings, and, where required, escalates to relevant stakeholders which transactions may have violated regulatory requirements and/or internal policies and procedures. Being highly organized and diligent in tracking and documenting numerous parallel investigations at the same time. Communicating directly with the business regarding monitoring related investigations. Able to use judgment in managing highly sensitive information and/or situations. Assisting with creating and disseminating reporting relating to monitoring. Assisting with periodically reviewing and drafting Business Risk Management procedures. Responsible for participating in the periodic review of the parameters of communication and transaction-based reviews performed by Business Risk Management. Assisting with producing communication or transaction monitoring related evidence in connection with requests from external and internal auditors. Understanding how the Bank’s risk appetite and risk culture should be considered in day-to-day activities and decisions. Actively pursuing effective and efficient operations of his/her respective areas, while ensuring the adequacy, adherence to and effectiveness of day-to-day business controls to meet obligations with respect to operational risk, regulatory compliance risk, AML/ATF risk and conduct risk, including but not limited to responsibilities under the Operational Risk Management Framework, Regulatory Compliance Risk Management Framework, AML/ATF Global Handbook and the Guidelines for Business Conduct. Championing a high-performance environment and contributes to an inclusive work environment. What You'll Bring 3 to 5 years of experience in a front office supervision role or compliance function preferred Working knowledge of securities law, regulations, and trading rules Ability to conduct detailed investigations and write concise reports in a timely manner. Prior experience conducting communication surveillance would be highly desirable Thorough analytical skills required to work with the various programs and information to ensure completeness of a communication. JD preferred. Proficiency in Spanish is helpful Interested?

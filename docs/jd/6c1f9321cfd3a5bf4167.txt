Benefiting from EY’s full spectrum of services, we’ve reimagined strategic consulting to work in a world of increasing complexity. EY-Parthenon’s Strategy and Execution - Strategy - Industrials & Energy practice helps the C-suite, business unit leaders, boards, and investors answer their hardest growth and value questions — where to play, how to win, and how to build the organization to deliver it. Our mandate is to own the CEO agenda. We develop and then deliver value creation opportunities for our clients by providing in-depth market, competitor and customer insight — and incorporate financial, operational, and implementation perspectives across their entire organization. As part of the team, you’ll have exposure to challenging, C-suite problem statements, and deliver at the highest standards of quality and pace. You’ll be part of an intensive learning and development program on our common way of working, as well as a structured mentorship model. You’ll receive candid, real-time feedback. And through this work and coaching you’ll build a deep strategy toolkit, expand your abilities as an executive advisor, and advance quickly within our flat team. Your key responsibilities As an Associate with EY-Parthenon’s Strategy and Execution – Strategy – Industrials & Energy practice, you own the analysis. You’ll work under a Consultant on a defined part of the client’s problem, and we expect you to do more than execute the task in front of you — to see what comes next and bring it forward. More specifically, your responsibilities include: Own the research and analysis behind a defined piece of the work, applying our methods to the case with guidance from your Consultant and project leadership. Build the models. Construct standard financial models with guidance, and get to the point where you can pull apart a dataset and use it to test what the team believes is true. Use AI where it makes the work better or faster, apply business judgment to what comes out of it, and validate the outputs before they reach the team’s answer. Gather research from the right sources and bring back what matters — the fact, the implication, and where it fits in the team’s thinking — not just the material you found. Support primary research: help build the interview guide and survey questions, run fact-gathering conversations, and come out of every interview with the key takeaways, not a transcript. Develop fact-based implications from your analysis, and offer your point of view on that work rather than waiting to be asked. Build clear, well-structured client materials — oftentimes slides with charts, graphs, and narrative perspectives — that hold to our standards for writing and design, with guidance. Contribute to the team’s thinking — bring hypotheses to the discussion and be a real voice in how we frame the problem. Flag roadblocks early, and come with a proposed way through them. Communicate status, obstacles, and next steps clearly to your team, and volunteer to take work off others when the pressure is on. Team up with colleagues across EY, including our cross-border teams, on jointly delivered engagements. Contribute to building the team — recruiting, developing our thinking, or improving how we work. Skills and attributes for success Problem structuring. You can break a question down, form an early view on the answer, and contribute hypotheses to the team’s thinking. Insight generation. You move past summarizing what you found to saying what it means for the client. Written communication. You write with structure and concision, and what you produce is clean and easy to follow. Analytical judgment. You are strong in Excel and comfortable with data, and you are curious enough about a number to ask whether it makes sense. AI fluency. You use AI well in your own work, and you pressure test what it gives you before it goes to the team. Sector curiosity. You learn the fundamentals of a new business or sector quickly, and you are willing to go deep in Industrial Products. Teamwork. You keep the team informed, stay steady under pressure, and pitch in without being asked. Ownership of your own development. You know where you are strong and where you are not, you raise it, and you act on the feedback you receive. You ask questions when you are in doubt, to validate any assumptions and advance your understanding. To qualify for the role, you must have: A bachelor’s degree with strong academic performance and at least 1 year of related work experience or a graduate degree and some related work experience. Experience in consulting, banking, corporate strategy, market research or a comparable analytical role, with a track record of owning research and analysis through to a clear answer. Strong quantitative skills, including the ability to build and work through financial models. The ability to research industries, markets, competitors, and customers, and to draw out what matters. Proficiency in English, including the ability to communicate effectively in writing and verbally in a professional environment. The expectation for this role is in person teaming, at client site or in an EY office, a minimum of 60% of the time or on average 3 days a week over the course of an engagement, project or performance year. You must either reside in or be in a commutable distance to your office location for this position. The ability and willingness to travel and work in excess of standard hours when necessary. In certain circumstances, travel may be required beyond your work location based on client and project needs. Ideally, you will also have: Experience in industrial products or adjacent sectors — manufacturing, building products, distribution, transportation and logistics, or energy and chemicals. Practical experience applying AI tools to research, analysis, or synthesis in a professional setting. Knowledge of how to leverage firm-approved AI tools in a business setting, including Microsoft Copilot. Experience working with primary research — expert interviews, surveys, or customer conversations. What we look for: People who want to learn and get very good, very fast. We are looking for strong problem solvers with an entrepreneurial streak who want to build a durable strategy toolkit inside a team that will invest in them — and who hold themselves to a high standard when the work is hard.

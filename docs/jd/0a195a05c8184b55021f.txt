The Splunk Agent Resilience team is defining the future of AI resilience. Our team provides scalable, cost-effective evaluation and guardrails that ensure AI agents behave as intended, improving reliability and reducing risks. This unified approach empowers our customers to confidently deploy and run AI-powered applications with enhanced observability and control. As a Staff Software Engineer on the Data & Scalability Platform team, you will provide technical leadership for the architecture, scalability, and cost of the entire data plane: the stores that hold the data, the streaming and compute components that move and transform it, and tooling proving how all of it behaves under load. You will define the multi-quarter, technical direction for that data plane, establish the standards other teams build against, own the initiatives that keep it fast and correct as it grows by orders of magnitude, and influence the technical direction of the platform with engineering team leadership and product teams. Your Impact Define and drive the multi-quarter technical roadmap for the data platform — storage, streaming, compute, and the tooling that measures them. Lead the architecture and evolution of the data plane, including storage engine selection, sharding and tenancy models, and control/data plane boundaries. Establish platform-wide standards for schema design, migration safety, retention of data, and lead the review process that enforces them. Lead the capacity and performance model, and define the SLOs that set the definition of what correct and fast mean. Lead major scalability and performance initiatives spanning data stores, streaming pipelines, and distributed worker fleets. Drive cost efficiency, the cost per unit of telemetry, cost attribution, and quota and rate-limit design. Set the direction for multi-tenant isolation so that no tenant can degrade another. Design and build the internal frameworks and performance tooling — load testing, profiling, and benchmarking — that other engineers rely on to support design and capacity decisions. Establish observability standards for the platform so signals are correct, complete, and affordable. Lead the most production investigations and drive systemic remediation rather than point fixes. Make and document technology-selection and build-versus-buy decisions, with explicit cost, risk, and migration analysis. Partner with engineering leadership to influence platform direction and investment, and represent the data platform to the product teams building on it. Mentor engineers and raise the engineering bar through design reviews, technical standards, and documentation. Technical leadership experience, including experience setting technical direction, driving multi-quarter initiatives, and/or influencing decisions beyond your immediate scope. Backend software engineering experience with either Go, Java, Rust, C++, and/or Python. Experience operating and tuning both an OLTP database (e.g., PostgreSQL, MySQL) and an analytical, columnar, or time-series store (e.g., ClickHouse, Druid, BigQuery, Snowflake) at production grade at scale — schema design, indexing, query tuning, and migration safety. Deep production experience with streaming or queueing systems (e.g., Kafka, RabbitMQ, Pulsar, Kinesis) — partitioning, consumer-group semantics, delivery guarantees, and backlog and dead-letter handling. Experience with distributed task frameworks and worker orchestration (e.g., Celery, Temporal, Flink, custom consumer fleets) at production scale. Familiarity with streaming databases, stream processing frameworks, or distributed query engines (e.g., RisingWave, Flink, Spark Streaming, Trino/Presto) and with model-serving infrastructure.

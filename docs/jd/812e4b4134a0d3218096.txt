We are looking for an experienced Senior Software Engineer for the Embedded Platform team. This is an outstanding opportunity to accelerate the pace of Jetson, IGX and DGX Spark Product Software system development within NVIDIA. Using your expansive knowledge of embedded platforms, Linux Real Time kernel operating systems, and software architectures, you will work with a world-wide development team to address the unique challenges of delivering the world's most powerful platform for crafting solutions for AI at-the-edge. Have you worked on Linux-based embedded products through full product lifecycles and have a passion for hands-on building of innovative products? If so, this position is for you! What you'll be doing: Implement features that showcase the capabilities Jetson, IGX based robotics, drones or industrial automation platforms, from board bring-up through production release. Define, build and tune real-time Linux Kernel for deterministic workloads based on GPU and SoC dependent accelerators. Enable variuos IOs and validate the sensors interfaces robotics, drones or industrial automation depend on. Work with robotics partners and internal hardware, safety and middleware teams to turn customer use cases into platform features, reference configurations and tools. Engage in the full product lifecycle, from hardware bring-up to production deployment. What we need to see: Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer engineering or equivalent experience. 8+ years of shown experience in Linux and sophisticated embedded systems development with strong programming skills. Able to understand the robotics, drones or industrial automation platform hardware architecture and engage with hardware team. Hands-on experience shipping BSPs for robotics, drones or industrial automation platforms. Ability to navigate through linux kernel internals skills: device drivers, device tree, boot flow, debugging with tracing tools. Proven real-time Linux kernel development experience. Outstanding technical skills and the ability to work across complementary teams. Any RTOS experience such as FreeRTOS, Zephyr, etc. is a plus. Modeling various GPU, sensors and other accelerator engine workloads related to robotics, drones or industrial automation applications. Ways to stand out from the crowd An understanding of how various middleware frameworks like ROS2 behavior affects platform determinism. Built tools or frameworks that let customers configure and verify real-time behavior on deployed systems. Familiarity with embedded microprocessor architectures such as ARM, Linux kernel and various subsystems within. Experience with any developer kits for robotics, drones or industrial automation platforms.

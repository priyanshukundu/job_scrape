Our Opportunity Chewy is looking for a Principal Software Engineer to join our Fulfillment Technology & Science team. Our fulfillment systems power critical, high-volume operations across Chewy's warehouse network. You will set and execute the technical direction for the next evolution of our warehouse management system (WMS). You will design the architecture and bring it to life through production-quality code, reusable patterns, and technical foundations that engineering teams can rapidly build upon. You will advance the platform using modern cloud-first technologies while enabling more advanced analytics, optimization, machine learning, and AI-assisted decision-making. You will be both a technology visionary and a hands-on technical force multiplier. You will define a practical modernization strategy around existing custom and packaged solutions, remain directly engaged in implementation, and establish the architecture and standards needed to evolve the platform safely. You will create a flexible integration architecture that connects the WMS with intelligent decisioning systems, robotics, warehouse control systems, and other packaged platforms without compromising the reliability or continuity of warehouse operations. You will also design and build rich, intuitive mobile experiences for warehouse associates, ensuring workflows are fast, accessible, resilient, and effective in demanding operational environments. You will actively embrace and champion AI coding tools and AI-enabled software development frameworks to improve design quality, implementation speed, testing, documentation, and maintainability. This is a rare opportunity to shape a critical fulfillment platform, establish modern engineering practices, and build the technology foundation that will power the next generation of Chewy fulfillment. What You'll Do Own and execute the incremental modernization of Chewy's core fulfillment technology. Design the target architecture and prove it through production code, reference implementations, and reusable engineering patterns. Architect highly available, low-latency systems across cloud and edge environments, partnering with infrastructure and networking teams to maintain warehouse operations through failures and disruptions. Design and build rich mobile experiences for warehouse associates with a focus on usability, performance, accessibility, offline resilience, device manageability, and rapid task execution. Establish a robust workflow framework for fulfillment operations that can adapt to evolving business needs, including explicit state management, validation, exception handling, retries, recovery, observability, and consistent execution across channels and sites. Design extensible integration patterns across custom and packaged solutions, machine learning and optimization models, robotics, and warehouse control systems, with clear ownership, guardrails, fallback behaviors, and auditable data flows. Define patterns that capture the history of operational events, decisions, workflow transitions, and system changes so critical processes can be traced, explained, reconciled, and improved. Provide bold, decisive technical leadership across teams. Resolve ambiguity, challenge assumptions, lead critical design and code reviews, and mentor engineers to raise the technical bar. What You'll Need Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field. Master's degree preferred. 12+ years of software engineering experience designing and incrementally modernizing large-scale, business-critical systems. Demonstrated experience applying AI-assisted software development tools and workflows to accelerate the delivery of significant engineering initiatives. Expert hands-on programming ability in modern languages, with Java/Kotlin preferred and recent experience writing production code. Deep AWS expertise across cloud and edge architecture, compute, networking, security, observability, infrastructure automation, and production operations. Deep expertise designing and building high-throughput, low-latency, event-driven distributed systems using Kafka or comparable streaming technologies, including concurrency, idempotency, asynchronous processing, retry and replay patterns, and tradeoffs among availability, latency, correctness, and throughput. Deep understanding of distributed data storage technologies, including relational and NoSQL databases, key-value stores, caching layers, and object storage. Experience selecting and designing resilient solutions for high-volume transactional and event workloads using partitioning, replication, durability, and recovery patterns. Experience architecting modern web and mobile applications, including applications that operate reliably in demanding environments. Familiarity with technologies such as React, TypeScript, Node.js, and Android/Kotlin is preferred. Experience designing workflow-driven or stateful operational systems that are flexible, resilient, and observable. Experience integrating custom software, packaged platforms, data products, and decisioning models through well-defined APIs, events, and data contracts. Experience designing auditable systems, including access controls, change traceability, event history, reconciliation, and retention. Demonstrated ability to set technical direction, influence across teams, mentor engineers, and champion modern engineering practices, including AI-assisted and specification-driven development.

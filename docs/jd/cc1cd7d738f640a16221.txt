Job Title: Scientist I, Research Associate Job Description: The Role Entegris is currently seeking a Scientist I, Research Associate to join our Research & Development team. This position will be based in our Aurora, Illinois facility. In this role, you will provide laboratory support and assist in the development and commercialization of commercially viable products for chemical-mechanical planarization (CMP) applications at Entegris. This role will be responsible for performing wet lab experiments, lab-based polishing experiments, product reliability tests, formulation experiments, fundamental wet-chemistry or instrument studies, data collection/summarization/analysis, and maintenance of the laboratory. What You’ll Do With minimal guidance competently execute appropriate experiments in slurry and cleans formulations, measurement method development or process development. Competently execute the experiment plans set by scientists and engineers in the team. Able and willing to work in a laboratory environment at least 80% of the time. Accurately analyze experimental data by applying appropriate statistical tools; propose conclusions based on experience. With minimal guidance, summarize data into an impactful & readable format. Occasionally seeks new methods to improve data collection, analysis, and quality. Contributes to lab safety and operational efficiency, including leading safety committee teams, and conducting calibrations and internal preventative maintenance of laboratory equipment. What We Seek Minimum BA/BS in Chemistry, Chemical Engineering, Material Science, or related field required 1-3 years of experience with hands-on wet chemistry techniques and instrumentation for characterization Proven track record of safe laboratory practices and methodology Demonstrated ability to execute experiments and analyze basic data Hands-on experience in particle and surface science analysis techniques such as PSD, zeta potential, contact angle, and thin-film measurements. Self-motivated and driven to succeed; demonstration of flexibility and adaptability in a fast-paced work environment; Good oral and written communications skills Knowledge of MS Office Suite (Excel, Word, PowerPoint, Teams) and familiar with basic statistical analysis using Excel, Minitab, JMP, etc. Why Work at Entegris Lead.

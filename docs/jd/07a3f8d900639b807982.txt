Responsibilities: Primarily, candidate conducts research SAR and MTI processing and algorithm development Candidate provides technical analyses and support in developing and applying advanced methods, theories and research techniques on R&D through operational support projects. Support algorithm and analytical development using a variety of scientific programming languages (for example C, C++, Python, FORTRAN and MATLAB) to process synthetic aperture radar data (SAR) and Moving Target Indicator (MTI). Work independently and as a member of a project team to provide remote sensing and geospatial expertise in response to technical requests for information from DoD and Intelligence Community analysts.

Citizens Financial Group, Inc. (CFG) seeks a Sr. Data Scientist for its Boston, Massachusetts location. Responsibilities: • Analyze bank deposit data using quantitative techniques and analyze behavior of deposits products. • Design deposit models, including listing alternative models, conducting model selection, and generating statistical testing on assumptions to qualify selected models for their effectiveness and validity. • Establish quantitative and reasonable thresholds, metrics, and standards for models to evaluate their performance. • Monitors PPNR, CCAR, deposit model performance, and analyze back testing results, trends and changes. • Create reports to track performance, forecast future prices, and make business plans, strategies, and decisions. • Recalibrate, update, overlay, and redevelop models when performance deteriorates and no longer reflects underlying products behaviors. • Work with team to answer questions in FED and OCC annual examinations and provide quantitative supports. • Identify data quality issues and work with appropriate parties to resolve or remediate. • Maintain, review, and adhere to organization’s ALM policy and verify the integrity of the underlying data. • Lead peer bank quarter analysis and present financial conditions to treasury management after each quarter’s earning call. Qualifications: Requires a Master’s degree in Quantitative Finance or a related field and 2 years of experience in the position offered, Treasury Associate, or related position developing predictive models for financial services. In the alternative, employer will accept a Bachelor’s degree in the specified fields and 4 years of the specified experience. Must have at least 2 years of experience with the following: • Utilizing quantitative risk management systems for report production, including data input, reconciliation, model management, generating calculations, analyzing economic factors scenarios shocks, and summary reports for WALCO; • Managing treasury models including ALM, balance sheet, and liquidity models, designing statistical models, researching and building mathematical assumptions, considering alternative models, conducting model selection processes, performing data management with work automation in VBA and SQL, conducting quantitative model performance monitoring and proposing model overlay, recalibration, and redevelopment; • Utilizing relational databases, statistical analysis programming languages, and software, including SQL and VBA, to extract high volume and complex data from internal and external databases, including FRED and Bloomberg; • Developing predictive models by implementing data analysis and research, including time series analysis, regression analysis, categorical analysis, advanced econometric techniques related to panel data analysis, and data imputation; • Utilizing the underlying mathematical principles behind statistical methods to evaluate and interpret the results of predictive models in a reasonable manner; • Utilizing advanced statistical programming and visualization software, including VBA and quantitative risk management systems, to create complex reports and graphs; • Building back testing and benchmarking tools to monitor model performance in accordance with established ALM policy; • Implementing complex computational methods and models using 1VBA; and • Standardizing, harmonizing, cleaning, preparing, and using data for developing datasets, reporting and analysis. Remote work permitted 1 day per week. Worksite: 28 State Street, Boston, MA 02109 United States, 40 hrs/wk. Salary Range: $136,781- $165,000/year. EO employer.

Job Description Summary The Senior Data Engineer is a vital technical leader within the Enterprise Data Privacy & Protection organization, responsible for designing, implementing, and optimizing enterprise solutions supporting data discovery, data cataloging, data curation, privacy compliance, data protection, and automation initiatives across on-premises and cloud environments. This role requires deep expertise in privacy engineering, data protection technologies, data protection platforms, cloud and database technologies, and engineering automation. The ideal candidate possesses advanced problem-solving abilities, excellent communication skills, and the capability to lead technical initiatives that improve enterprise-wide data security, protection, and compliance. The role is expected to leverage emerging AI and Large Language Model (LLM) technologies to improve operational efficiency, privacy engineering productivity, data security, and data protection automation capabilities. Automate data management and operational processes through scripting, APIs, workflows, and engineering best practices. Troubleshoot and resolve complex technical issues while driving continuous improvement, scalability, and operational excellence. Partner with Technology, Cybersecurity, Risk, Governance, and Compliance teams to ensure regulatory and data protection requirements are met. Lead technical design discussions, mentor team members, and communicate complex solutions to both technical and business stakeholders.

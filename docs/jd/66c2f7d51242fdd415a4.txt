The role maintains robust data pipelines and infrastructure to ensure accurate and timely planning inputs and delivers automation solutions using Micron approved AI systems—including Microsoft 365 Copilot, Mia, MAX, Claude Code, and other sanctioned tools within the global Micron AI ecosystem. Leveraging AI enabled workflows, UiPath automations, and predictive validation logic, the engineer streamlines planning processes and reduces manual effort across a globally distributed network. Position requires: 1. Conducting production line analysis and forecasting to identify constraint points and implement actions to improve throughput, flow and line balance. 2. Leading bottleneck root-cause analyses by leveraging cycle time, equipment, area and capacity performance and highlight key constraint areas and risks. 3. Partnering with manufacturing shift, industrial engineering and operations improvement teams to track issues through closure, driving continuous improvements across manufacturing operations and supporting systems. 4. Manufacturing Science, including scheduling, inventory management, capacity planning and optimization modeling. 5. Semiconductor manufacturing process regarding capacity planning, automation, scheduling, cycle time, and manufacturing analytics and efficiency. 6. Statistical methods, data analysis, data modeling, ETL pipelines and problem solving. 7. Utilizing reports and AI tools including Tableau, Power BI, SQL, Python, R, Microsoft 365 Copilot, Mia, MAX, and Claude Code.

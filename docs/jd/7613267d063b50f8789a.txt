Job Description U.S. Bank is seeking the position of Lead Fenergo CLM Platform Software Engineer (multiple positions) in Charlotte, NC. Essential Responsibilities: Partner with product, compliance and architecture teams to define scalable onboarding architecture. Align Fenergo capabilities with enterprise cloud strategy. Lead design and configuration of Fenergo CLM (Client Life Cycle Management) solutions to support complex KYC, AML, and regulatory onboarding workflows across multiple business lines. Implement and customize business rules, workflows, and data models using Fenergo’s configuration-driven architecture. Develop and maintain RESTful APIs and system integrations between Fenergo and core banking, CRM, and third-party vendor systems. Manage environment promotions and configuration migrations across DEV, QA, UAT, and PROD using controlled deployment processes. Design reporting and dashboards using Fenergo data extracts and integrations, enabling visibility into onboarding KPIs and compliance metrics. Build and maintain micro services using Java, Spring Boot, C#, and .Net core, enabling high availability and seamless integration across distributed systems. Mentor junior engineers and drive adoption of best practices in micro services architecture and DevSecOps. Mentor engineering teams on Fenergo best practices, including configuration management, deployment strategies and environment setup . Basic Qualifications: Position requires a Bachelor's degree in Engineering, followed by 5 years in a Software Developer, or related, occupation. Required Skills/Experience: Must include 5 years of experience with each of the following: 1. Cloud Software Development, including AWS and Azure; 2. Build and maintain CLM Portal infrastructure on Azure Cloud platform using Kafka; 3. Lead Front End Development for CLM Portal using ReactJS and JavaScript; 4. Lead Backend Development for CLM Portal using Java, C#, Spring Boot, and .Net; 5. Define low level and high level design and architecture for CLM platform and its integration with third party systems; 6. Define technical roadmaps for the CLM platform; 7. Fenergo Rules Engine and Fenergo Journey Builder; and, 8 Terraform and CI/CD. Teams may work from home and the office. Base pay range may vary if an offer is made for work in a different location.

Job Title: Senior Software Engineer Location: OH - Columbus What you'll do: Digital Disbursements is a fintech company, led by a team with deep industry experience and a strong track record of value creation. We operate in the digital payments space and have a proven product-market fit. We specialize in helping bring B2C payments to the digital age and are poised for significant growth in the months ahead. Digital Disbursements was acquired by Western Alliance Bank (NYSE: WAL) in January 2022 and operates as a wholly owned subsidiary of WAL. Expanding Digital Disbursements engineering team to meet client, business, and WAB demands to provide a Payments-as-a-service platform and help our client integrate with our core services. Not hiring position will slow growth of cases, clients, payment fees, and deposits. Work with a very talented group of engineers to deliver solution for internal and external clients. Collaborates closely with peer developers, QA testers, and business partners to deliver high-quality solutions in a timely manner. Work through the entire software development life cycle building out a high availability multi-tiered platform accessed globally. Participate in production platform support. Contribute to peer code reviews. Proactively identify and fix issues improving scalability, resiliency, fault tolerance, and fraud monitoring. Respond to urgent bugs in a timely manner alongside engineers. Help foster good software engineering practices. What you'll need: Bachelor's degree in related field required. 7 + years of strong Object-Oriented Development experience. 3+ years of strong MySQL experience (designing and administrating). 4+ years of NodeJS experience. 4+ years of Strong ReactJS experience. 4+ years of Typescript experience. Strong HTML, CSS and JavaScript experience. Strong understanding of Amazon API-Gateway & Lambda. Solid experience building API frameworks (RESTful or GraphQL). Experience using Microsoft Team Foundation System (Azure DevOps) would be a plus. Experience with Amazon Web Services a plus. Experience with Amazon QuickSight a plus. Experience working with NoSQL DynamoDB a plus. Ability to work in a small, dynamic programming team. Able to work in a fast-paced environment. Advanced speaking and writing communication skills. May require up to 25% travel. Benefits you’ll love: We offer all the important things you'd want — like competitive salaries, an ownership stake in the company, medical and dental insurance, time off, a great 401k matching program, tuition assistance program, an employee volunteer program, and a wellness program. In addition, you’ll have the opportunity to bolster your business knowledge, learning the ins and outs of how successful companies operate and manage their finances, giving you invaluable hands-on experience to help grow your career! About the company: Digital Disbursements is a subsidiary of Western Alliance Bank, Member FDIC, which is a wholly owned subsidiary of Western Alliance Bancorporation. Serving clients nationwide, Western Alliance Bank includes six legacy bank brands — Alliance Association Bank, Alliance Bank of Arizona, Bank of Nevada, Bridge Bank, First Independent Bank and Torrey Pines Bank — that remain part of the company’s heritage.

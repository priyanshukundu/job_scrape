JOB SUMMARY: Reduces security risks associated with human behavior through targeted learning, practical interventions, and evidence-based evaluation. The role analyzes incident and behavioral patterns, designs activities appropriate to specific roles, and measures sustained improvement. Partners with business leaders, HR, Communications, and security specialists to strengthen secure decision-making and resilience to evolving social-engineering threats. POSITION RESPONSIBILITIES: Analyze human-risk patterns and higher-risk activities. Design role-specific interventions and reinforcement. Coordinate with business leaders, HR, and Communications. Translate simulation findings into behavioral follow-up. Measure sustained improvement beyond training completion. POSITON QUALIFICATIONS: Relevant degree or an equivalent combination of professional experience, education, and demonstrated expertise. 5+ years in relevant security learning, human-risk, or behavior-change work, including 3+ years measuring security-related outcomes. Social-engineering fluency and strong analytical and learning-design skills. PREFERRED REQUIREMENTS: Behavioral science, instructional design, or applied analytics CISSP or comparable security certification.lied analytics CISSP or comparable security certification.

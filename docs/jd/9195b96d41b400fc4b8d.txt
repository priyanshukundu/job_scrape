About the Role We’re looking for a Senior Applied Scientist, Property Analytics to join our growing team! As Senior Applied Scientist, Property Analytics, you’ll help us reinvent real estate lending by tackling complex research and machine learning problems related to automated valuation models (AVMs), asset valuation, and process optimization. You’ll work alongside engineering, product, and business leaders on a team of 360-degree thinkers who combine advanced analytics and machine learning skills with deep domain knowledge, using some of the most fascinating data sets in the world. You’ll lead technical direction, mentor team members, and partner closely with MLOps engineers, Product Managers, Credit Risk Management, and Capital Markets subject matter experts to drive strategic initiatives from initial research to production deployment.

Job Description As a Tech Lead Software Engineer, you are a hands-on technical leader with end-to-end ownership of web architecture, delivery outcomes, and production reliability. You set technical direction, make and communicate clear architectural tradeoffs, and lead through influence — aligning engineers, product, backend, platform, security, and partners around durable, scalable web solutions. You design, build, and operate customer-facing web platforms using Angular, TypeScript, NgRx, and Nx, applying modern front-end architecture, responsive design, accessibility, API integration, and secure development practices to improve reliability, usability, and developer velocity. You accelerate delivery through AI and agentic-first engineering practices, establishing safe, repeatable workflows that improve throughput and quality while maintaining strong validation, security, accessibility, and governance. You raise the engineering bar by mentoring others, setting clear guardrails, and continuously improving the systems and practices your teams rely on.

Is the opportunity to join a culture where “We Do the Right Thing,” and “We Courageously Shape Our Future Together” important to you? If so, Guardian is seeking a motivated individual to join our team as a Finance Lead for Underwriting, Group Benefits. As Finance Lead for Underwriting, Group Benefits, you will connect business execution with financial outcomes across Group Underwriting. This financial planning and analysis (FP&A) role combines annual planning, headcount analysis, and business-case development in a production-based insurance environment. Your insights will help leaders understand performance, assess trade-offs, and make informed decisions. You are You are a collaborative, detail-oriented finance partner who operates with urgency and communicates with clarity. You are a strategic thinker who challenges the status quo, takes intelligent risks, and thinks creatively to identify solutions and opportunities. You combine analytical problem-solving with sound business judgment, translating complex information into actionable insights that influence business decisions. You take ownership of outcomes, build trust across functions, and influence without formal authority. You will Act as a strategic consultant to the Underwriting business, helping it scale in alignment with strategic objectives. Combine strong business partnership with financial planning, modeling, and analysis to translate insights into actionable recommendations that influence business decisions and outcomes. 1) Monthly Performance Reporting · Monthly Performance Reporting: Support and shape reporting of actual monthly expenses and unit costs, providing insights into key performance drivers and variances to plan, including headcount, volumes, productivity, vacancies, and overtime. 2) Strategic Planning · Integrated Financial Planning: Serve as a strategic arm of the Underwriting business, connecting business-case and headcount models to the financial plan to challenge assumptions, provide actionable insights, and influence decisions that help scale the department in alignment with strategic objectives. · Headcount Planning & Productivity Insights: Partner on headcount planning and develop productivity insights and dashboards to anticipate staffing and capacity needs, guide resource decisions, and help Underwriting scale to achieve sales and renewal objectives across rate, persistency, and profitability · Business Cases & Financial Modeling: Use financial modeling and scenario analysis to evaluate business cases with cross-functional partners, challenge underlying assumptions and conventional thinking, explore creative solutions, and recommend actions aligned with strategic objectives. · Annual Financial Planning: Partner with Enterprise Expense Management to develop the annual financial plan. 3) Business Partnership & Enablement · Cross-Functional Alignment: Advise and influence Underwriting partners on planning assumptions, financial implications, and operational trade-offs, challenging perspectives and providing insights that help align decisions with business strategy. · Executive Insights & Decision Support: Translate complex financial and operational data into clear insights and compelling stories that enable informed, strategic business decisions. · Process Scalability & Efficiency: Apply advanced Excel, analytical skills, and responsible use of artificial intelligence (AI) to build scalable planning and reporting processes, streamline workflows, and reduce manual effort while maintaining data quality and human oversight. You have Required · Bachelor’s degree in Finance, Economics, or related field. · 5+ years of FP&A experience. · Experience partnering and consulting with stakeholders, building trust, and influencing without authority. · Strong analytical and financial modeling skills, advanced in Excel and PowerPoint. · Ability to synthesize complex data into clear, executive-level insights. · Strong communication and collaboration skills. · Experience developing annual financial plans, partnering on headcount and capacity planning, and building productivity dashboards. · Experience analyzing monthly expenses, unit costs, and variances to plan supported by drivers. · Experience developing business cases and scenario analyses to advise business partners on growth options and financial trade-offs. · Experience improving planning and reporting processes while maintaining data quality. · Experience making decisions with incomplete information, balancing risks and trade-offs, and adapting as new insights emerge. Preferred · Experience in insurance or financial services. · Familiarity with Oracle Planning / Hyperion / SmartView. · Experience with large, matrixed organizations. · Master of Business Administration (MBA) and/or Chartered Financial Analyst (CFA) designation. · Practical experience applying AI to streamline finance workflows, with data validation and human oversight. Reporting Relationships As our Finance Lead for Underwriting, you will report to the Head of Finance for Underwriting, who reports to the Head of FP&A for Group Benefits.

General Summary: We’re looking for a hands-on Senior MLOps Engineer who has experience building and operating machine learning systems in production. This is not primarily a data science/model-development role, and it is not a traditional DevOps position. We’re looking for someone who has personally worked across the production ML lifecycle, including CI/CD, model deployment and serving, monitoring, Kubernetes, cloud infrastructure, and Infrastructure as Code. You’ll help maintain and scale our existing ML platform while building new capabilities across MLOps, GenAI, and AgentOps. You’ll work closely with data scientists and engineers and serve as a senior technical contributor who can help establish standards and mentor other engineers. You may be a particularly strong fit if you are a software or DevOps engineer who moved into MLOps, or an ML engineer with strong production infrastructure experience. You are comfortable going beyond experimentation and can speak to ML systems you have personally deployed, monitored, troubleshot, and supported in production.

Your Role at Baxter The Quality Laboratory Associate II is responsible for performing routine and non-routine analytical, chemical, and microbiological testing of raw materials, in-process samples, utilities, environmental monitoring samples, and finished pharmaceutical products. The position supports product release, stability programs, investigations, process validation activities, and continuous quality monitoring while ensuring compliance with cGMP, regulatory requirements, and site procedures. The Associate II independently executes laboratory testing, evaluates data for accuracy and compliance, maintains laboratory instrumentation, and supports laboratory investigations. This role requires strong technical laboratory skills, attention to detail, and the ability to work effectively in a regulated pharmaceutical environment. What you'll be doing Analytical and Chemical Testing Perform routine and non-routine analytical testing using approved laboratory methods. Conduct chemical analysis of raw materials, intermediates, bulk product, and finished product samples. Perform testing using laboratory instrumentation including: High Performance Liquid Chromatography (HPLC) Ultra Performance Liquid Chromatography (UPLC) Gas Chromatography (GC) UV-Visible Spectroscopy Fourier Transform Infrared Spectroscopy (FTIR) pH meters Conductivity meters Osmometers Total Organic Carbon (TOC) analyzers Execute assay, identification, impurity, and physical characteristic testing. Perform method suitability and system suitability evaluations. Review analytical data for accuracy, completeness, and compliance. Microbiological Testing Perform microbiological testing of pharmaceutical products, utilities, and environmental monitoring samples. Conduct microbial identification and enumeration testing. Perform bioburden testing and microbial recovery studies. Execute environmental monitoring activities, including: Air sampling Surface sampling Personnel monitoring Support water system monitoring and utility testing programs. Perform endotoxin testing using approved methodologies. Conduct growth promotion and media qualification studies. Interpret microbiological results and escalate atypical findings. Laboratory Operations Prepare reagents, standards, media, and laboratory solutions. Maintain laboratory notebooks, worksheets, logbooks, and electronic records. Review laboratory documentation for completeness and compliance with Good Documentation Practices. Support sample receipt, storage, tracking, and disposal activities. Maintain laboratory inventory and consumable supplies. Ensure laboratory work areas remain clean, organized, and inspection ready. Equipment and Instrumentation Perform routine calibration, verification, and maintenance activities on laboratory equipment. Troubleshoot laboratory instrumentation and coordinate repairs when necessary. Assist in instrument qualification and validation activities. Support laboratory computer systems and data integrity requirements. Quality Systems Support Investigate out-of-specification (OOS), out-of-trend (OOT), and atypical results. Participate in deviation investigations, CAPAs, change controls, and laboratory investigations. Support method validation, method transfer, and verification studies. Assist with process validation, cleaning validation, and product stability programs. Participate in internal audits, regulatory inspections, and customer audits. Ensure compliance with cGMP, FDA, EMA, USP, and internal quality requirements. What you'll bring Required Bachelor's degree in Chemistry, Microbiology, Biology, Biochemistry, Pharmaceutical Sciences, or a related scientific discipline. Minimum 2 years of laboratory experience in a regulated pharmaceutical, biotechnology, medical device, or related industry. Preferred Advanced coursework in analytical chemistry, microbiology, or pharmaceutical sciences. Experience working within a cGMP laboratory environment. Experience with both chemical and microbiological testing. Experience supporting pharmaceutical manufacturing operations. Knowledge, Skills, and Abilities Knowledge of cGMP regulations and quality system requirements. Working knowledge of USP compendial testing requirements. Understanding of laboratory investigation processes. Familiarity with data integrity principles (ALCOA+). Knowledge of aseptic technique and contamination-control practices. Strong troubleshooting and problem-solving skills. Ability to interpret laboratory data and identify trends. Strong written and verbal communication skills. Ability to manage multiple priorities in a fast-paced environment. Proficiency with laboratory software systems and Microsoft Office applications. Physical Requirements Ability to stand and walk for extended periods. Ability to perform repetitive laboratory tasks requiring fine motor skills. Ability to lift up to 25 pounds. Ability to work while wearing laboratory PPE. Ability to distinguish colors and observe small details during testing and inspections. Preferred Competencies Analytical chemistry expertise. Microbiological laboratory techniques. Technical writing and documentation. Root cause investigation support. Statistical analysis and data trending. Continuous improvement mindset. Strong attention to detail and data accuracy. Key Performance Indicators On-time completion of laboratory testing. Right-first-time data generation. Documentation accuracy and compliance. Investigation support effectiveness. Laboratory audit readiness. Instrument uptime and maintenance compliance. Environmental monitoring and utility testing compliance. Adherence to safety, quality, and regulatory requirements.

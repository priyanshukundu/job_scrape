Meet The Team The Cisco Wireless Platform team builds the foundational embedded software that powers Cisco’s wireless products. We work closely with hardware, system architecture, test, and product teams to bring up new platforms, enable core subsystems, and deliver reliable, high-performance software at scale. Our work spans platform bring-up, low-level software development, system integration, and product readiness for enterprise wireless solutions. Your Impact As a Platform Engineer, you will contribute to the development of embedded platform software for Cisco wireless products. You will work on low-level software components, support hardware bring up and subsystem integration, and help debug and resolve system issues across the development lifecycle. We’re looking for engineers with strong embedded systems fundamentals, solid programming and debugging skills, and the ability to thrive in a highly collaborative environment. We also value candidates who are interested in modern AI-enabled engineering practices and how AI-assisted tools can improve engineering productivity, code quality, and execution speed. In This Role, You Will: • Contribute to the design, development, debugging, and validation of embedded software for wireless platform products. • Support the development of low-level platform software, including board support packages, platform services, boot-time initialization, and device-level components. • Assist with hardware bring-up and subsystem integration for new and existing platforms. • Investigate software and system issues in embedded Linux environments, working closely with senior engineers to drive resolution. Minimum Qualification: Meet one of the following (completed within the past 3 years or expected to be completed within the next 12 months): -A Master's degree with 3 years of work experience; OR -Recent graduate or in your final year of studies toward a PhD. Qualifying education should be in Computer Science, Electronics, Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, or a related technical field. Are proficient in C and C++, with familiarity in Python or shell scripting, and embedded Linux fundamentals. Have strong debugging, analytical, and problem-solving skills. Are interested in AI-enabled engineering practices, including the use of AI-assisted tools for software development, debugging, test automation, and productivity improvement. Preferred Qualification: • Exposure to board bring-up, device drivers, or BSP development through academic projects or internships. • Familiarity with interfaces such as UART, SPI, I2C, GPIO, PCIe, or Ethernet. • Exposure to networking fundamentals or wireless technologies.

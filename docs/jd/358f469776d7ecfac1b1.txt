Flexibility : Hybrid Work Model Your Future: Professional Development Reimbursement including access to SS&C University Work/Life Balance: Competitive holiday scheme Your Wellbeing: Competitive benefits designed to support the wellbeing of our staff Diversity & Inclusion: Committed to Welcoming, Celebrating and Thriving on Diversity Training: Hands-On, Team-Customised throughout your career What You Will Get To Do: About the role You will build the automations that take repetitive, high-volume work out of our BPO operations. Working closely with the Technical Lead, you will turn real operational processes into reliable software: AI agents, integrations and workflows that run in production, every day, for regulated financial services clients. You will spend time with the people who do the work today, understand the process in detail, then build, test, ship and support the automation that replaces or assists it. What you will do Build and maintain automations using LLMs, agent frameworks, APIs and workflow tooling. Integrate with internal platforms, databases, document stores and third-party systems. Write tests and evaluations for both code and AI behaviour, and add monitoring so issues are caught early. Map processes with operations teams, including edge cases, exceptions and controls. Handle documents and data at volume: extraction, validation, reconciliation and routing. Ship through CI/CD, support automations in production and fix issues quickly. Document what you build so it can be audited, supported and reused. Contribute to shared components and patterns across the programme. What You Will Bring: Solid software engineering skills with production experience in Python and/or TypeScript. Hands-on experience building with LLM APIs: prompting, tool calling, structured outputs; agentic workflows a strong plus. Experience with REST APIs, SQL and working with messy real-world data. Good engineering habits: version control, testing, code review, CI/CD. Comfort with cloud services and containers. Curiosity about how operations work, and the patience to get edge cases right. Clear communication with non-technical colleagues. Nice to have Experience with agent frameworks or protocols (e.g. Model Context Protocol, Claude Agent SDK, LangGraph). Background in RPA or workflow automation tools. Document processing, OCR or intelligent document understanding. Financial services, fund administration or other regulated operations experience. What success looks like in the first year Several automations you built are live and in daily use. You own them end to end: tests, monitoring, documentation and support. Operations teams see you as the go-to engineer for their processes.

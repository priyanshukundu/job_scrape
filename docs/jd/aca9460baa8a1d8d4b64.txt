The Impact you will have in this role: The product manager assists in business analysis and documenting requirements of moderately complex product management initiatives with a primary focus on enterprise client analytics, which support our ability to take a data driven approach to client engagement. The Product Manager requires keen business analysis and communication skills to draft and present business requirements to senior cross-leadership. The candidate must be proficient in depicting and describing business impact assessments between current-state and various future state business models. The position entails leading multiple internal stakeholder meetings and working cross functionally to collect, validate, and prioritize requirements. Your Primary Responsibilities: Work with the product management lead and business to deliver on the specific initiatives prioritized and budgeted for. Collaborate with product management peers globally to ensure best practices and core principals are understood, adopted and implemented in regional initiatives. Collaborate with client support teams and technology partners (both Application Development and Enterprise Infrastructure) on delivering new capabilities and enhancements. Collaborate with other client facing functions (sales, marketing, integration, etc.) to develop and implement strategies for building community on new product and service offerings. Manage achievement of project deliverables, including coordination of efforts across a variety of functional areas to ensure projects are completed on time and budget and within a quality framework. Monitor project budgets for specific initiatives / projects. Identifies potential risks to achieving project objectives and elevates to appropriate management. Document requirements and provide feedback on requirements documents that may impact their initiatives or product offerings.

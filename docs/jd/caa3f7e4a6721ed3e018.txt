Requisition #: 76921 The Role at a Glance The Security Risk Assessment Analyst conducts information security risk assessments on external parties to ensure that the risks associated with those relationships fall within Lincoln’s acceptable risk tolerances. The Analyst independently owns assessments for vendors and services, including complex vendors and high-risk engagements, determines their information security risk profiles, and works with vendors and internal stakeholders to drive risk mitigation and remediation. The Analyst guides stakeholders on the risks associated with assessment findings and on adherence to applicable procedures, regulations, and/or laws. The Analyst also informally mentors Sr. Associates and shares knowledge with the team. What you'll be doing Assessment and risk determination Independently conducts security risk assessments of external parties, including complex vendors and high-risk engagements, by reviewing questionnaires, audit reports, control documentation, and supporting evidence. Determines vendor risk profiles and evaluates control gaps using established methodologies, Lincoln policies, industry standards, business context, data sensitivity, and access. Applies critical thinking and independent judgment to analyze information from multiple sources and develop clear findings, recommendations, and reporting. Integrates business, technical, and risk information to provide actionable guidance. Performs vendor risk management activities, leads portions of projects, and coordinates activities across stakeholders. Mitigation, remediation, and escalation Recommends mitigation strategies to reduce identified risk and clearly communicates expectations to vendors and internal stakeholders. Drives remediation by setting expectations, tracking progress, and following up until findings are closed, accepted, or escalated. Independently resolves moderately complex issues and promptly escalates matters outside the role’s scope with clear recommendations. Respectfully challenges risks, exceptions, and concerns using a fact-based, solution-oriented approach. Communication and stakeholder engagement Communicates security and risk concepts clearly and persuasively, adapting messages, reports, and presentations for technical, business, and leadership audiences. Builds trusted relationships and influences stakeholders regarding risk implications, assessment outcomes, and remediation strategies. Partners across business and risk functions to support assessments, remediation, and risk management efforts. Reports engagement status to Information Security management, project managers, and stakeholders. AI and emerging risk Independently evaluates third-party AI solutions, including generative AI, using established policies, standards, and industry practices. Advises stakeholders on AI risk and maintains knowledge of emerging AI technologies and security risks. Metrics, reporting, and documentation Maintains complete, accurate, and timely assessment documentation and communications in Lincoln’s GRC platform. Analyzes assessment and remediation data to identify trends, reporting opportunities, and data-quality improvements. Owns reporting for assigned vendors and workload, developing metrics, dashboards, reports, and presentations that communicate risk trends, remediation progress, and program performance. Standards, improvement, and team contribution Contributes to information security standards, policies, procedures, and related documentation. Leads process improvements that enhance program efficiency, consistency, and quality. Informally mentors Sr. Associates and shares expertise across the team. Maintains knowledge of security issues, threats, technologies, and industry best practices and applies expertise to complex situations. Contributes to departmental and enterprise initiatives, change efforts, and team objectives. What we’re looking for Must Haves Bachelor’s degree in Information Systems, Information Security, Information Risk Management, or related field, or 4+ years of equivalent work experience in lieu of a degree. 3–5 years of experience in IT Security or Information Risk Management directly aligned with the responsibilities of this role. 1+ year of experience or demonstrated working knowledge of AI technologies, including generative AI, as applied to security or third-party risk. Ability to read, analyze, and interpret technical procedures, regulations, policies, audit reports, proposals, and standard operating procedures. Strong written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to tailor information for technical, business, and leadership audiences. Strong critical thinking and analytical skills, including synthesizing information from multiple sources into clear conclusions. Strong organizational and project management skills, with the ability to prioritize and manage multiple projects, meet deadlines, and maintain attention to detail in a time-sensitive environment. Strong interpersonal and collaboration skills, including the ability to build consensus and influence outcomes without direct authority. Sound judgment and discretion when handling confidential information. Proficiency with Microsoft Office Suite, including Word, Excel, PowerPoint, and Outlook. Ability to successfully complete regulatory and job training requirements. Nice to Have CompTIA Security+ certification or higher preferred.

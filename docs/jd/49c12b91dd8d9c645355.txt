Portfolio Planning & Governance Consultant
MassMutual

The Opportunity The Portfolio Planning & Governance consultant is a seasoned individual contributor who aligns priorities, strengthens governance, and drives disciplined portfolio execution across Operations. You will provide portfolio insights, analysis, and data-driven recommendations that help senior leaders make informed decisions and ensure initiatives deliver measurable business value. The Impact In this role you will be accountable for enabling disciplined portfolio management by managing project intake, supporting prioritization, maintaining visibility into resources and capacity, and strengthening governance across the portfolio. You will translate project and portfolio data into actionable insights, identify risks and dependencies, and support leaders in making informed decisions that align work to business priorities and operational capacity. You will handle broad-scope portfolio work independently, anticipate shifts in priorities and capacity, and work with guidance on the most complex matters. Key Responsibilities but not limited to: Manage project intake, demand review, and readiness validation within established governance standards Support portfolio prioritization based on value, risk, capacity, and strategic alignment; prepare analysis and options for leadership review Maintain dashboards and visual reporting on portfolio health, resources, milestones, and risks Analyze capacity, dependencies, and delivery constraints across the portfolio, anticipating emerging constraints Facilitate governance forums, decision-making routines, and action tracking Identify and escalate risks, issues, resource gaps, and prioritization conflicts Translate portfolio data into executive-ready insights and recommendations Drive standardization, transparency, and continuous improvement in portfolio practices Partner with stakeholders to sequence initiatives for successful delivery Guide resolution of portfolio conflicts/delivery issues, escalating the most complex, while identifying opportunities to improve execution/business outcomes Minimum Requirements Bachelor's degree 5+ years of related experience in portfolio management, business operations, strategic planning, project delivery, or related disciplines. 3+ years of demonstrated expertise in portfolio governance, prioritization, resource planning, risk management, and executive reporting. 3+ years of demonstrated ability to inform leader decisions through strategic analysis, structured recommendations, and data-driven insights. 3+ years of experience leading cross-functional initiatives and driving execution within complex, matrixed environments. Ideal Qualifications Experience supporting portfolio management, governance, strategic planning, or PMO functions. Experience within financial services, insurance, operations, or large matrixed organizations. Experience presenting recommendations and portfolio insights to senior leaders. PMP, CAPM, PMI-PBA, Lean Six Sigma, Agile, or related certifications. Advanced proficiency with portfolio reporting, data visualization, and portfolio management tools. Demonstrated ability to influence without direct authority and drive cross-functional alignment. Experience using generative AI or other tools to improve execution, personalization, and efficiency. Track record of independently resolving portfolio issues and finding ways to improve operations.

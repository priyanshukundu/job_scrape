Job Description: The Role As a Business Analyst – Skills and Learning Platforms , you will play a key role in advancing Fidelity's digital learning and skills ecosystem. Working within Enterprise HR Platforms, you will help shape the technology experiences that enable associates to build skills, access learning opportunities, and support their professional growth across the organization. In this role, you will partner with business stakeholders, product teams, technology teams, vendors, and HR leaders to support and continuously improve Fidelity's Skills and Learning platforms. You will contribute to platform enhancements, production support, business process improvements, and strategic initiatives that modernize how Fidelity develops workforce capabilities. You will serve as a critical link between business needs and technology solutions, helping ensure our platforms deliver a seamless, scalable, and engaging experience for associates. This role offers the opportunity to work with modern cloud-based HR technologies while gaining exposure to enterprise-wide initiatives focused on learning, skills, talent development, and workforce transformation.

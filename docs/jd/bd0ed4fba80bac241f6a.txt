Job Description Application Deadline: December 1, 2026 What is the opportunity? As an Associate Relationship Manager, you will partner with Relationship Managers to deliver exceptional client experience, strengthen client relationships and support portfolio growth. Working within a collaborative, results-oriented team, you will identify client needs, recommend tailored banking solutions, support credit and portfolio activities, and leverage One RBC partnerships to deliver value to our clients. What will you do? Deliver an exceptional client experience through proactive engagement, seamless onboarding, timely resolution of client concerns and consistent follow-through on client needs. Partner with Relationship Managers to execute client contact strategies and discovery, support pre-contact planning for client meetings and document Client Relationship Plans to strengthen and deepen relationships. Prepare credit applications including financial analysis, borrower risk ratings, and collateral assessments, ensuring accuracy, completeness and timely submission of documentation. Collaborate with Relationship Managers to monitor portfolio quality, including lending excesses, delinquencies, expired credits and out-of-orders, ensuring adherence to established risk management benchmarks. Collaborate with Relationship Managers to coordinate and validate post-approval credit documentation, ensuring compliance with approval conditions to facilitate timely loan drawdowns and disbursements. Proactively identify business opportunities through client interactions, connecting clients with appropriate One RBC partners. Maintain accurate client information and documentation in accordance with established due diligence and regulatory requirements. Contribute to operational soundness and resilience by adhering to established policies and controls, exercising sound judgment and identifying and escalating potential risks. Educate clients on RBC’s digital solutions, highlighting their benefits in improving cash flow management, operational efficiency, and simplifying everyday banking. What do you need to succeed? Must-have First degree in Banking, Business Administration, Finance or a related field or an equivalent qualification Minimum of three (3) years’ banking experience Strong organizational and time management skills, with the ability to manage competing priorities Strong verbal and written communication skills Excellent attention to detail and accuracy Good working knowledge of commercial banking products, services and credit guidelines Proficiency in Microsoft Office and business communication technologies Strong financial analysis, risk assessment, and problem-solving skills Strong analytical capability, critical thinking and sound business acumen Team player with a growth mindset, dedicated to continuous learning, and a commitment to delivering exceptional client experience. Nice-to-have Good negotiation skills Awareness of emerging financial regulations, market trends, and technologies impacting commercial lending undefined What’s in it for you? We care about each other, reaching our potential, making a difference to our communities, and achieving success that is mutual.

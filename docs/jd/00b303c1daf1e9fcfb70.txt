Senior Software Engineer – Data and Platform Tools – Bloomberg Connects - 45014 - Bloomberg Senior Software Engineer – Data and Platform Tools – Bloomberg Connects Location New York Business Area Engineering and CTO Ref # 10054281 Description & Requirements Bloomberg Connects is a rapidly growing, free platform that helps museums, galleries, and cultural spaces make their institutions, stories and dynamic content more engaging to audiences around the world. Launched in 2019, Connects features multimedia guides with unique content exclusive to each of our over 1,500 cultural institution partners across 33 countries. As part of Bloomberg's culture of giving back, Connects is a joint effort between Bloomberg and Bloomberg Philanthropies in support of their work to ensure better, longer lives for the greatest number of people by focusing on five key areas: the arts, education, the environment, government innovation, and public health. We believe that technology opens doors, and we created Connects as a part of our mission to make art and culture accessible to all—in-person, at-home, and anywhere. We are a team of designers, researchers, marketers, editors, engineers, and leaders working together to enhance our partner's capacity to reach audiences around the world and improve their visitor experience. Built through empathy and research, qualitative and quantitative data, and experimentation and iteration, our products seek to delight and empower. The Data and Platform Tools team builds and runs the services behind the platform's analytics, search, speech, computer vision, generative AI, and agentic systems. We are looking for a senior engineer to own the infrastructure those services are built on. What's in it for you? Own systems end to end: Design, build and operate the services behind Connects' analytics, search, speech, computer vision, and AI features, from architecture through to what runs in production. Work on AI systems in production: Build and run the infrastructure behind our LLM-based and agentic features, from deployment through evaluation. Work the way we're building toward: Use AI-assisted and agentic engineering practices in your own daily work, and help build the internal platform and tooling other disciplines rely on to do the same. Shape how we build: Establish the infrastructure, deployment and delivery patterns our services are built on. Shape global cultural engagement: Directly impact how millions access and experience art and culture through a rapidly expanding platform. Contribute to a philanthropic mission: Align your work with a meaningful initiative supporting arts and cultural accessibility.

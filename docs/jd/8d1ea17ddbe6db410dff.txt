General Summary: Unum is hiring a hands-on Software Engineer II to join an Agile team supporting applications used for insurance quoting and proposal development. This role will focus primarily on enhancing existing applications, developing features for new products, supporting releases, and helping maintain reliable production systems. The ideal candidate has strong professional experience with C#, .NET/.NET Framework, and SQL and can deliver moderately complex work with minimal guidance. This is a good opportunity for an engineer who enjoys solving business problems, working across established applications, and contributing to both back-end services and front-end functionality. Job Specifications: Approximately 4+ years of professional software development experience, or equivalent demonstrated experience. A bachelor’s degree in computer science or a related field is preferred, but equivalent professional experience will also be considered. Strong hands-on development experience with C# and .NET or .NET Framework. Strong experience working with SQL and Microsoft SQL Server. Experience designing, developing, testing, and maintaining production software. Understanding of object-oriented design and software development practices. Experience working in an Agile development environment. Ability to independently analyze requirements, estimate work, solve technical problems, and deliver features with minimal guidance. Experience using source control and participating in code reviews. Familiarity with software deployment and established CI/CD processes. Commitment to software quality through unit testing, integration testing, or automated testing. Strong communication and collaboration skills.

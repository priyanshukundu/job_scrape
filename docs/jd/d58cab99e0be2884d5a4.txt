Job Description Summary The Acoustic Software Engineer will join the Systems Engineering team at GE HealthCare Austria. The team is responsible for optimizing image quality, developing acoustic simulation systems, conducting measurements in a certified laboratory, integrating ultrasound probes into our systems, and developing future platforms for our high-end medical ultrasound products. In this role, you will be part of a global Acoustic Power Control (APC) development team. You will help bring together two existing software implementations for acoustic measurement and APC into a shared C/C++ architecture. Working with colleagues across three development sites, you will establish automated testing and a coordinated approach to integrating future improvements into the shared software. Accountable for the quality of own work. Subject to direct operations supervision/prescribed work instructions/systems checking. Executes within a well-defined operations framework. There is generally a step by step sequence of standard operational tasks which need to be followed to achieve an end result. Job Description Responsibilities Analyze and compare the existing software implementations, including their common functionality and site-specific requirements. Refactor and integrate the implementations into a shared, maintainable software architecture that supports all development sites. Develop and maintain automated tests to detect unintended effects of software changes and support reliable future development. Collaborate with teams across three sites to define a structured approach for proposing, reviewing, and integrating software changes. Develop and implement improvements to the acoustic measurement software and APC. Preparation of technical verification evidence for quality assurance and audits. Document the software architecture, interfaces, and development processes. Required Qualifications Bachelor’s or master’s degree, or equivalent qualification, in Software Engineering, Mathematics, Electrical Engineering, Medical Engineering, or a related field. Good programming skills in C/C++ and MATLAB. Strong analytical and problem-solving skills. Good written and spoken English communication skills. Ability to work collaboratively in an international, cross-site team. German language skills are an advantage.

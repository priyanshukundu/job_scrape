About This Role This position is for an established Materials Planning and SIOP professional who participates in the supply and demand planning process and is responsible for making minor changes or enhancements in systems and processes to solve problems and improve effectiveness of work area. May work with internal team to develop financial forecasts. Key Responsibilities Work directly with Vendor contacts to ensure material is planned, transported, and delivered to meet schedule requirements. Utilize material planning systems and tools to plan requirements, communicate schedule, generate reports, and maintain data. Monitor forecasts to balance material availability, inventory, and cost. Lead problem resolution discussions with Suppliers, Purchasing, Receiving, Quality, Production. Scheduling and Operations to resolve and improve supply chain issues. Develop improvement plans where gaps in supplier performance are recognized. Participate in Continuous Improvement and Process Excellence culture to build a more robust Inbound Supply Chain. Identify Cost Savings opportunities in support of company targets. Lead Load and Capacity assessments at high-risk vendors to validate their ability to deliver. Evaluates supplier lead times and creates or maintain purchase orders respective of supplier lead times and constraints. Cycle count production material on the manufacturing operations floor. Periodically covering Material Handling Supervisor responsibilities.

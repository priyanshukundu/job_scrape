Job Description Summary Sensitive client circumstances. Complex investigations. Decisions that protect clients. The Advisor, Supervision – Senior & At-Risk Clients (SARC) helps protect Raymond James clients by investigating concerns involving potential financial exploitation of senior and vulnerable adults and possible diminished capacity when an authorized party is not available to make financial decisions. This role requires careful fact-finding, sound judgment, and a thoughtful approach to situations that may be sensitive or difficult to resolve. You will manage your own caseload, partner with financial advisors and regional management, and work closely with Fraud, New Accounts, Legal, and Anti-Money Laundering to identify concerns, determine appropriate next steps, and resolve matters. The work requires both independence and collaboration—and can make a meaningful difference for clients. You may also be cross-trained to support Dispute Resolution (DR) and Money Movement Supervision and may transition temporarily or permanently among these functions based on business needs. The work can be complex—but it makes a meaningful difference. Job Description What You Will Do · Investigate concerns involving suspected financial exploitation of senior or vulnerable adults and possible diminished capacity. · Assess facts, research account activity, prioritize investigations, and make well-supported supervisory decisions. · Maintain and manage an individual caseload, following matters through to appropriate resolution and documentation. · Report suspected elder financial exploitation to appropriate state agencies when required. · Monitor relevant regulations and legislation affecting senior and vulnerable investors and apply requirements to assigned matters. · Use Passport and other internal systems to research, evaluate, and document investigations. · Work with Fraud, New Accounts, Legal, Anti-Money Laundering, and other partners; refer or escalate concerns when appropriate. · Communicate with regional management, branch managers, and financial advisors about supervisory concerns and work collaboratively to resolve client-account issues or conflicts. · Build constructive relationships with financial advisors and internal partners while maintaining appropriate supervisory standards. · Share knowledge and resources with newer associates and support their development when asked. · Perform other duties and responsibilities as assigned. Experience and Education · Experience: A minimum of two (2) years of experience in Supervision and/or the financial services industry. · Education: A bachelor’s degree in a related discipline, or an equivalent combination of education, experience, and training approved by Human Resources. · Industry knowledge: Familiarity with securities-industry investment concepts, practices, and procedures, and the rules and laws affecting senior and vulnerable investors. What Will Help You Succeed · Strong critical thinking and decision-making skills, including the ability to interpret regulatory rules and compliance policies. · Effective time management and the ability to prioritize and manage multiple investigations independently. · Careful attention to detail and risk without unnecessarily disrupting workflow. · Clear, professional written and verbal communication with financial advisors, branch managers, regional leaders, and internal partners. · A calm, service-oriented approach to sensitive client matters and difficult conversations. · Working knowledge of Raymond James structure, policies, and systems, with proficiency in the software tools used for research, reporting, and communication. · The ability to establish clear priorities, build positive working relationships, and know when to seek additional input or escalate. Licenses and Certifications · No securities licenses are required for this position. · Appropriate securities licenses for the assigned functional area are preferred, or may be obtained within an established timeframe. How You Will Work Follow the facts: Investigate carefully, connect relevant information, and reach conclusions supported by the evidence. Protect vulnerable clients: Recognize potential exploitation or diminished capacity and respond with appropriate urgency and care. Own your caseload: Manage competing priorities, follow through on investigations, and document actions and decisions. Communicate with clarity: Explain supervisory concerns and next steps professionally, including when conversations are sensitive. Partner effectively: Work constructively across SARC, Fraud, Legal, Anti-Money Laundering, New Accounts, and field leadership. Keep learning: Stay current on regulatory requirements, firm policies, and investigative tools. Are You Ready to Make an Impact? If you are curious, detail-oriented, and committed to protecting clients through thoughtful investigation and sound judgment, we would like to hear from you. The work can be challenging—but it matters.

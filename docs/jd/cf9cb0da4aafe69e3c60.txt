Hi, we're Oscar. We're hiring a Senior Data Scientist, Lucie to join our Data team. Oscar is the first health insurance company built around a full stack technology platform and a relentless focus on serving our members. We started Oscar in 2012 to create the kind of health insurance company we would want for ourselves—one that behaves like a doctor in the family. About the role: We are a Data Science team at Oscar dedicated to improving the member experience by bringing data and analytics into the products and operations members rely on. We build data products and deliver insights that make complex health insurance interactions simpler and more effective for members. As a Senior Data Scientist, you will work closely with Product, Engineering, and business stakeholders to understand and improve the member experience. You will develop ML models and data products that members interact with, build the data pipelines that support them, and lead product analytics and experimentation. You will evaluate how members use our digital tools and services, including AI experiences, and apply methods ranging from statistical analysis to LLMs to characterize their needs and pain points. Your findings will guide product and operational improvements. You will report into the Senior Manager, Data Science. Work Location: This position is based in our New York City office, requiring a hybrid work schedule with 3 days of in-office work per week. Thursdays are a required in-office day for team meetings and events, while your other two office days are flexible to suit your schedule. #LI-Hybrid Pay Transparency: The base pay for this role is: $163,944 - $215,176 per year. You are also eligible for employee benefits, participation in Oscar's unlimited vacation program, company equity grants, and annual performance bonuses.

Positioned in the first line of defense, this role leverages advanced technical expertise in Information Technology (IT), Cybersecurity, and Information Risk Management to strengthen risk and compliance programs within a technology-focused business operation. This role will partner with business units, Compliance, Risk Management, Audit, and Regulators to identify, assess, and mitigate risks related to the adoption of AI capabilities across the company. Responsible for supporting business and technology leader adherence to the established risk framework and ongoing supervision of business controls and the new enhanced AI risk management frameworks developed by NIST and Cyber Risk Institute for the financial sector and the new OCC Super Intelligence supervisory guidance. The implementation of AI capabilities requires an enhancement to our existing risk and control environment across programs to include risk and control self-assessments, identification and evaluation of control effectiveness, identifying control failures, facilitating risk and compliance remediation, internal and external audits and regulatory exams, and monitoring of the first line of defense to minimize risk exposures and strengthen the overall control environment leveraging industry frameworks. This role will partner across business and technology to manage risk assessment data and uses critical thinking to identify key data-driven insights tied to first line of defense, specifically focusing on the identification of trends and patterns related to IT, Cyber, and Data risks. What you'll do: Directs staff to oversee AI specific Risk Assessment and Management to include but not limited to: Conducting regular risk assessments of IT systems, applications, infrastructure, and data, identifying vulnerabilities and recommending appropriate mitigation strategies. This includes evaluating risks related to cybersecurity, data management, system availability, architectural health and application failures. Advises senior management and influences process change related to AI Risk. Builds and manages a high performing team of AI risk and internal control technology professionals through recruitment, training, coaching, performance management and related managerial activities. Manages consulting relationships with internal and external clients regarding budget and implementation deliverables and risk mitigation plans as appropriate. Partners with key stakeholders in the business and oversees the identification, assessment and documentation of risks and controls, including risks associated with new or modified products, services, distribution channels, regulations and/or third-party operations. Reviews, communicates and recommends the development of AI Risk governance policies and procedures in partnership with senior leaders of technology organizations to ensure appropriateness and adequacy versus industry best practices and regulatory requirements. Provides counsel to technology business unit managers on AI risk management issues and supports the evaluation of new product strategies on their impact to USAA's risk profile. Educates risk owners on AI risk/control best practices and may work with other lines of defense in the development and implementation of controls. Manages processes for collecting accurate and complete internal loss data from business areas. Directs analysis and data mining initiatives that identify, validate, and manage emerging risk exposures, as necessary. Assists in the early identification of risk trends by establishing and monitoring key performance and risk indicators. Oversees the preparation of key reports and communications and may present to governance committees, senior leaders, regulatory bodies and the USAA Board of Directors. Stays informed of changes to the regulatory environment to ensure proper compliance with processes and requirements are followed. Ensures risks associated with AI enabled business activities are effectively identified, measured, monitored, and controlled in accordance with risk and compliance policies and procedures. What you have: Bachelor's degree in a related field; OR 4 years of relevant education and/or experience. 8 years of experience supporting risk-related, compliance related, or business control design activities; OR 8 years of experience in a relevant quantitative discipline; OR Advanced degree or designation in a risk management, technology, or quantitative discipline, and 6 years of experience supporting risk-related, and/or compliance-related, or business control design activities; OR PhD in a risk management, technology, or quantitative discipline, and 4 years of experience supporting risk-related, and/or compliance-related, or business control design activities. 3 years of direct team lead or people management experience. 3-5 years of direct experience in technical roles in IT, Information Security, or Information Governance (e.g., systems administration, IT operations, application development, infrastructure support, cloud engineering, information security engineering, IT architecture, information governance). Familiarity with key technology and data processes such as change management, access management, SDLC, patching, information governance, sensitive data management and incident management. Strong understanding of IT controls and risk management principles, ideally within a financial services or regulated industry context. Note: This role requires deep technical expertise in IT, Information Security, and Information Management to support technology and data-centric functions. What sets you apart: Master's degree in Cybersecurity, Computer Science, Data Science, Artificial Intelligence, Information Systems, Risk Management, or a related field. Leadership experience at the intersection of Technology Risk, Data Governance, Cybersecurity, Artificial Intelligence, and Operational Risk Management. Experience establishing and leading AI Governance, Responsible AI, and Data Risk Management programs within highly regulated environments.

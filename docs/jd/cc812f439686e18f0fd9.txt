As a Capital Modeling Actuary on Marsh Re's Global Analytics team, you'll serve as an actuarial partner to our brokers and their clients, producing a probabilistic view of their risk profile and evaluating capital strategies, including reinsurance programs that protect their earnings and capital. You'll work largely independently, owning analyses from setup through client presentation, and you'll lead the day-to-day work on projects, coordinating with modeling and broking colleagues. Your work combines four perspectives: Marsh Re's independent view of risk, the regulatory and rating agency view (NAIC, AM Best, S&P), reinsurers' view of risk, and the client's own view. Your job is to turn those into recommendations that are technically sound and commercially useful. Roughly 60% of your time will be technical analysis and modeling, and 40% will be client- and broker-facing: presenting results, participating in client discussions, and helping shape client strategy. You'll work across all P&C lines, depending on client needs. We're also modernizing how we work. You'll help us use Databricks, Python, and AI tools to make our analyses faster, more consistent, and easier to scale. What to expect: Build a holistic view of client risk. Using MetaRisk and custom-built tools, model prospective underwriting risk, property catastrophe risk, asset risk, reserve development risk, and reinsurer credit risk. You'll work with concepts such as dependency and correlation structures, economic scenario generators, large loss modeling, and financial statement projection. Assess the financial impact of reinsurance. Show clients how different structures affect earnings volatility, capital position, and rating agency metrics, and make sure the recommended program fits their risk appetite. Advise brokers and clients. Translate analysis into clear recommendations, anticipate client needs, and help brokers position analytics-led solutions that strengthen client relationships and support new business. While you won't carry a sales target, you'll be a visible part of how we win and retain clients. Modernize our workflow. Use AI and automation for repeatable tasks such as analysis setup, sensitivity testing, QA checks, and first-draft documentation, in line with Actuarial Standards of Practice and our data governance policies. What you need to have : ACAS or FCAS with 5+ years of P&C actuarial experience. Bachelor's degree in Actuarial Science, Mathematics, Statistics, Economics, or a related field. Experience in pricing, reserving, or capital modeling. We'll train strong pricing and reserving actuaries on our capital modeling framework and MetaRisk. Working proficiency in Python or R, and SQL, or a willingness to build these skills quickly. Experience with Databricks is a plus. The ability to explain technical results to non-technical audiences, both in writing (client reports, executive summaries) and in person (broker meetings, client presentations). Sound judgment in making decisions that keep projects moving without constant direction. What makes you stand out: Capital modeling experience Experience with MetaRisk or a similar capital modeling platform, such as ReMetrica or Igloo Familiarity with economic scenario generators or asset modeling A track record of using automation or AI to improve the speed or consistency of analysis Experience presenting to and influencing senior management Breadth across multiple lines of business or roles (e.g., primary carrier, reinsurer, consulting) Why join our team : We help you be your best through professional development opportunities, interesting work and supportive leaders.

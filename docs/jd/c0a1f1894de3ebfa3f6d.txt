About this role BlackRock Global Markets and Index Investments (BGM) brings together BlackRock’s Index Investments, Markets, Liquidity & Financing, and Platform & Strategy businesses. Together, we provide clients with superior market access and index investment outcomes. The BlackRock Global Markets – ETF Markets team works across primary and secondary markets to protect, grow, and steward ETF trading for the benefit of clients. We are seeking an experienced, commercially driven Vice President to join our U.S. ETF Liquidity Solutions team within ETF Markets . This highly visible, client-facing role will be instrumental in expanding BlackRock's presence across institutional and wealth management trading channels, deepening strategic client relationships, and accelerating ETF adoption through differentiated execution advisory and liquidity solutions. The successful candidate will combine deep ETF market expertise with a commercial mindset to deliver innovative trading strategies, improve client execution outcomes, and identify new opportunities for business growth. Working closely with distribution, product, and ETF Markets teams, the role will help clients navigate complex trading and portfolio implementation challenges while translating market intelligence and trading expertise into actionable commercial opportunities, product insights, and strategic business decisions. Key Responsibilities ETF Execution Advisory & Client Trading Solutions Deliver strategic ETF execution advisory to institutional and wealth clients to improve execution quality, reduce transaction costs, and optimize liquidity access across primary and secondary markets. Develop and implement tailored trading strategies for complex portfolio transitions, model rebalances, and large-scale transactions to minimize market impact, manage execution risk, and improve implementation efficiency. Leverage expertise in ETF market structure and cross-asset instruments, including cash securities, futures, swaps, and other Delta One products, to deliver cost-effective portfolio implementation, hedging, and risk management solutions. Commercialization & Strategic Client Engagement Partner with institutional and wealth distribution teams to originate ETF trading opportunities, expand strategic client relationships, and convert execution expertise into incremental ETF adoption and asset growth. Collaborate with sales, product, and portfolio management teams to overcome trading barriers, accelerate product adoption, and unlock commercial opportunities across new and existing ETFs. Strengthen BlackRock's ETF market positioning and client engagement through strategic meetings, industry forums, and thought leadership that differentiates our ETF liquidity and execution capabilities. Market Intelligence & ETF Ecosystem Development Deepen relationships with authorized participants, market makers, and dealer trading desks to enhance ETF liquidity, expand execution capacity, and improve trading outcomes for clients. Translate ETF trading activity, liquidity trends, investor flows, and competitive intelligence into actionable insights that inform execution strategies, identify commercial opportunities, and support business growth. Partner across ETF Markets and broader BlackRock teams to advance market structure initiatives, leverage emerging trading technologies, and deliver scalable improvements in ETF liquidity and execution capabilities. Qualifications 5+ years of experience in ETF capital markets, trading, Delta One, derivatives, or institutional client coverage, with strong knowledge of cross-asset markets and portfolio implementation. Deep understanding of ETF market structure, including primary and secondary market mechanics, creation/redemption processes, liquidity dynamics, market making, and execution strategies. Strong knowledge of Delta One instruments, including ETFs, futures, swaps, and cash securities, with the ability to evaluate relative value, financing, liquidity, and transaction costs to optimize portfolio implementation. Strong analytical skills and understanding of equity and/or fixed income markets, with the ability to assess trading dynamics, identify execution opportunities, and translate complex market data into actionable client recommendations. Demonstrated commercial mindset with the ability to identify client opportunities, influence trading decisions, and drive adoption of ETF solutions. Excellent communication and relationship management skills, with the ability to engage senior clients, trading counterparties, and cross-functional teams to advance commercial and strategic priorities. Established relationships with dealer trading desks, ETF liquidity providers, Delta One counterparties, and institutional investors preferred.

Job Description As a valued colleague on our team, you will contribute to designing, producing, testing, or implementing routine or foundational software, technology, or processes, as well as contribute to creating and maintaining IT architecture, large scale data stores, and cloud-based systems. THE IMPACT YOU WILL MAKE The Software Engineer - Associate (AWS) role will offer you the opportunity to make an impact, while working alongside people who care so that you can deliver on the following responsibilities: Gather and document information about the needs of the customer and create preliminary solution frameworks. Under the guidance of management, design and develop software solutions to meet needs. Apply fundamental knowledge of process-driven approach in designing solutions. Collaborate with senior staff to implement new software technology. Maintain existing software using standard methods, practices, and precedents. THE EXPERIENCE YOU BRING TO THE TEAM Desired Experience: Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Information Technology, or a related technical field, or equivalent professional experience. Experience designing, developing, testing, and supporting enterprise software applications. Programming experience with Java and/or Python. Experience working with AWS or comparable cloud technologies. Familiarity with generative AI or AI-assisted development tools such as Microsoft Copilot. Understanding of APIs, databases, application integration, and modern software engineering practices. Experience supporting applications in a production environment. Analytical and problem-solving abilities. Ability to operate effectively in an Agile software development environment. Shows curiosity and adaptability in learning and responsibly applying new technologies, including artificial intelligence, to reimagine how we work.

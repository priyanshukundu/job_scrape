Senior Business Analyst
Aristocrat
Location: Las Vegas, NV, US
Link: https://aristocrat.wd3.myworkdayjobs.com/AristocratExternalCareersSite/job/Las-Vegas-NV-US/Senior-Business-Analyst_R0023087

At Aristocrat, our mission to “bring happiness to life through the power of play” guides all our work. As a Senior Business Analyst in Information Technology, you will play a key role in our fast-growing business where technology meets creativity. This role is vital for improving our IT platforms that manage product approvals and licenses. You will ensure a smooth experience for our collaborators. Join our outstanding team and help us keep innovating and delivering world-class gaming experiences. This is your chance to join a driven team dedicated to excellence! What You'll Do Collaborate with collaborators across various departments to gather and prioritize business requirements. Collaborate with the Application Owner to prioritize user stories, bugs, and improvements based on business value, technical feasibility, and strategic objectives. Build and maintain a product backlog together with the Application Owner and business partners, ensuring it accurately reflects current business priorities. Serve as a Subject Matter Expert for compliance processes, providing clear and concise user stories, acceptance criteria, and mentorship throughout the development lifecycle. Plan and coordinate product releases with project managers, developers, and QA teams. Provide input on test strategies to support UAT for planned improvements. Work with business collaborators to complete UAT in a timely manner, supporting production releases. Manage and respond to queries from within compliance and from other users interacting with compliance systems. Identify and resolve test problems with engineering, compliance, and regulatory agencies. Assist in implementing process measurement by comparing data from operational systems to processes in Signavio. Support release management, including risk assessment and prioritization. Interact with various collaborators across the business, attend meetings, present ideas, and help develop solutions. What We're Looking For Passionate, self-motivated, and enthusiastic individuals continuously seeking ways to achieve excellence. 5+ years of hands-on experience in finding solutions to process/technical issues. Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, a related field, or equivalent experience. Business Analysis certification such as IIBA or Certified Analytics Professional (CAP) is preferred. Experience with software development lifecycles and multiple releases of enterprise-class software products. Ability to analyze information and use logic to perform tasks efficiently within deadlines. Strong problem-solving skills to address work-related issues and problems. Excellent organizational skills. Proven experience working within cross-functional business teams and software delivery teams. Effective communication skills with cross-functional teams as well as technical and business engagement managers. Ability to prepare, present, and clearly convey solutions to both technical and business audiences. High level of personal and professional accountability with the ability to enlist the cooperation of peer groups involved in common business goals and objectives. Ability to meet changing demands and adapt to frequently changing priorities. Excellent oral and written communication skills along with excellent customer service skills.

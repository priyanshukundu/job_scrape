Projects start with a trader’s or analyst’s question, not with a model. You will work alongside traders and analysts from the first conversation through to production and beyond. You frame the problem with them, review results with them as the work progresses, and stay with the tool after it ships. You own both the science and its path to production. The work is fast and phased, and it is judged on one thing: whether the desk uses it to make better decisions. What You Will Do Work side by side with traders and analysts to turn market questions into scoped projects, each with a clear commercial hypothesis and a defined go/no-go point. Review progress with the desk throughout each project, not just at handoff. Show intermediate results early, test them against the desk’s market knowledge, and change course when the signal or the priority shifts. Build LLM, NLP and multimodal pipelines that extract structured signals from unstructured sources such as outage reports, news, filings, and web content. Design and run quick experiments to test feasibility across modelling approaches, and stop weak ideas early. Build models that predict outage impacts, classify disruption patterns, and detect anomalies and regime changes in fundamental data. Take the models that prove out into production. That means packaged, tested, deployed through CI/CD, with monitoring and drift detection built in from day one. Deliver insights where traders already work: Teams, alerts, and the Vision Excel and Python tooling. Standalone dashboards are not the default. Source data through the Vision platform. When you need a new source, work with the Vision team rather than building side pipelines, and follow the existing web-scraping architecture. Evaluate commercial AI platforms and tools against real desk problems, and give a clear recommendation. Explain results, uncertainty, and limits in plain terms to traders, analysts, and senior stakeholders.

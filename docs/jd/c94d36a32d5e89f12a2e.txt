About the Role We're looking for a Business Analyst to define and deliver requirements for DriveWealth's options project: the order, trading, margin, and post-trade capabilities that let partners offer options to their customers through our API and FIX interfaces. You'll turn brokerage, clearing, and regulatory needs into clear specs that engineering, QA, compliance, and operations can build and test against. You'll own requirements for a defined workstream end to end with limited oversight. You'll spot gaps early, whether a change in clearing requirements or a missing case in exercise, assignment, and expiration handling, and work closely with product, engineering, compliance, and operations. Margin and buying-power rules for options accounts are a key part of this work. What You’ll Do Elicit, document and prioritize business and functional requirements for options trading: order entry and validation, strategies (single-leg and multi-leg), exercise and assignment,

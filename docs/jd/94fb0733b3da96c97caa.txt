Capital Markets Product Specialist – East (Vice President) 
CAIS
Location: New York, New York
Link: https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/cais/jobs/8939073002

CAIS is the pioneer in democratizing access to and education about alternative investments for independent financial advisors, empowering them to engage and transact with leading asset managers at scale through a wide variety of alternative investment products and technology solutions. CAIS provides access to strategies including hedge funds, private equity, private credit, real estate, digital assets, and structured notes. CAIS serves over 2,500 wealth management firms that support more than 65,000 financial advisors who oversee approximately $8.5 trillion in end-client assets. CAIS is seeking a driven sales professional to join its Capital Markets team. This high-impact role sits at the intersection of relationship management and product distribution, supporting RIAs, Independent Broker-Dealers, and Bank & Trust clients across an assigned region. The right candidate brings 7-10 years of financial services experience, and a strong foundation in structured products and defined outcome investments. This role owns key advisor and RIA relationships, drives distribution strategy, and helps develop the Capital Markets team. Candidates may be based within the Mid-Atlantic territory or in New York, NY. Responsibilities Own and grow relationships with RIAs, broker-dealers, and enterprise wealth platforms within an assigned region Lead structured note distribution strategy, including product positioning and pipeline development Generate leads, originate calls, and drive in-person meetings with prospects and existing clients Distribute CAIS' suite of structured notes, defined outcome ETFs, and alternative investment products Partner with issuers and asset managers on new product launches and distribution sequencing Represent CAIS externally at industry events and client meetings Develop deep fluency across all CAIS products, platforms, and workflows Qualifications 7-10 years of experience in financial services, ideally in product distribution or a client-facing sales role Bachelor's degree in finance, economics, business, or a related field Strong background in structured products, equity derivatives, fixed income, or defined outcome strategies Experience wholesaling or distributing structured notes, ETFs, mutual funds, or similar investment products Excellent written and verbal communication skills; able to distill complex products for advisor audiences High integrity, strong attention to detail, and the ability to manage competing priorities in a fast-moving environment Proficiency in Microsoft Office, particularly Excel; Salesforce experience preferred Series 7 and 63 required Compensation CAIS is consistently recognized as a Best Place to Work, and our culture is at the heart of our success.

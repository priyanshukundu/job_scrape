Innovate Remotely This position is fully remote, allowing you to enjoy the flexibility of working from home while collaborating with skilled team members and contributing to groundbreaking solutions. Your Impact The Associate Manager, Media Planning & Strategy is an important member of the Lowe's Media Network (LMN) Media Planning team. This role will be responsible for the development of omnichannel vendor media plans aligned to client KPIs and category-relevant performance recommendations. The Associate Manager, Media Planning & Strategy will partner closely with the Manager, Media Planning & Strategy in addition to Client Services, Ad Operations, and Insights in producing high-quality programming that meets client objectives and advances retail media investments based on performance. What You Will Do Supports assigned accounts with the development of full-funnel media recommendations, including high-level annual strategy as well as individual campaign planning. Determines recommended targeting, channels, KPIs, and budgets for all vendor media initiatives and builds media plans in internal system. Develops audience recommendations to target relevant consumers based on campaign objectives. Leverages media metrics (iROAS, ROAS, CTR, CVR, etc.) to rationalize and explain strategic recommendations based on performance. Cross-functionally collaborates with Client Services, Ops, Commercialization, & Insights on the execution of harmonized programming. Prepares & presents plans to vendor partners, with support where necessary from Media, Sales, and Insights leadership. Maintains a strong understanding of the retail media ecosystem, including onsite & offsite display, video, audio, social, & search. Minimum Qualifications Bachelor’s degree Marketing, Finance, Analytics or equivalent years of experience in lieu of education requirement, if applicable 5 Years Experience in Marketing 3 Years Experience in retail media, digital marketing, performance marketing, or marketing strategy, marketing analytics or related field 3 Years Experience in full funnel Media Strategy and/or Planning in CPG or specialty commerce with exposure to broader marketing strategy Experience presenting to or working with CPG, D2C, or specialty commerce brands Preferred Skills/Education 1 Year Digital Marketing Analytics 1 Year Campaign Management & Media Operations IAB Digital Media Buying and Planning Certification Google Analytics Certification Pay Range: $86,000.00 - $143,600.00 annually Starting rate of pay may vary based on factors including, but not limited to, position offered, location, education, training, and/or experience.

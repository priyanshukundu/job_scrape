Underwriter or Underwriting Specialist - Middle Market

Location: Los Angeles, CA, US

Zurich is looking for an Underwriter or Underwriting Specialist – Middle Market to join our team in Los Angeles! While this position will be based out of our Los Angeles office, you will have the flexibility to work on a hybrid basis. You will partner with your manager to set a flexible work schedule that supports you, our customers, and our brokers and agents. This role will require you to be visible in the marketplace to meet with our agents and brokers and be in the office/market three days per week. As a member of this team, you will manage a portfolio of complex new and renewal business, while serving as a trusted advisor to brokers and customers throughout the Los Angeles region. You'll have the opportunity to leverage your underwriting expertise, market knowledge, and relationship-building skills to deliver profitable growth and exceptional customer experiences At Zurich, Middle Markets is a key business segment focused on serving the evolving needs of businesses across a wide range of industries. We are committed to attracting customer-focused professionals who bring a sales mindset, strong technical underwriting expertise, and a solution-oriented approach to risk management. This is an exciting opportunity to join a growing and highly visible team within Zurich Middle Market! As a member of this team, you will: Serve as a customer and industry advocate by understanding client risks and delivering tailored insurance solutions that address their evolving business needs. Evaluate exposures, assess risk, and develop pricing strategies using a disciplined underwriting approach. Utilize risk insights, data, analytics, and modeling tools to support informed underwriting decisions at the point of sale. Identify coverage gaps and align Zurich's products, capabilities, and value proposition to deliver contract certainty and comprehensive protection for customers. Demonstrate strong analytical thinking and provide innovative risk management guidance to brokers and clients. Underwrite, analyze, and generate profitable new and renewal business opportunities. Maintain a strong market-facing presence and contribute to production goals within Zurich's growing Middle Markets business. Apply underwriting guidelines, insurance regulations, rating methodologies, and company policies to ensure sound underwriting decisions. Develop and strengthen relationships with agency and broker partners to drive sustainable growth. Operate within established authority levels while managing highly complex assignments and customer needs. This role will be filled at either the Underwriter or Underwriting Specialist level. The hiring manager will determine the appropriate level based upon the selected applicant's experience and skill set relative to the qualifications listed for this position.

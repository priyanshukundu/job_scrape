Job Description: Job Title Municipal Capital Markets Affordable Housing Risk Officer Corporate Title Analyst Location New York Overview The Commercial Real Estate (CRE) credit team is tasked with managing the real estate credit risk for Deutsche Bank’s Commercial Real Estate Group. The US Public Finance & Solutions Affordable Housing Risk Officer is responsible for handling approvals for loan and municipal bond financing of Affordable Housing related transactions. Additional tasks include setting and monitoring counterparty credit limits, reviewing due diligence and underwriting, and interfacing with senior management, asset management, and business personnel. You will be expected to review, challenge, analyze, suggest loan terms, and recommend approval or decline transactions. The Risk Officer will act as an intermediary between the Business units, department heads and credit committees, assist in structuring transactions to achieve optimal risk/return outcomes, and monitor portfolio and financial/market developments within commercial real estate and related sectors.

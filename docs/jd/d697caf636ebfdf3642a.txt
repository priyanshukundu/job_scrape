Data Scientist Lead - Vice President
JPMorgan Chase
Location: Jersey City, NJ, United States
Link: https://jpmc.fa.oraclecloud.com/hcmUI/CandidateExperience/en/sites/CX_1001/job/210812720

Shape how engineering teams use data and AI to build, deliver and improve software. In this hands-on technical leadership role, you’ll build production-ready solutions, influence technical direction and help a talented analytics team grow. Job summary As a Technical Lead, Engineering Analytics and AI within our PXT Analytics team, you’ll set the technical direction for analytics and AI solutions that improve engineering productivity. You’ll build GenAI and agentic tools, develop graph-based approaches to understanding engineering work, and measure the impact of developer tools and programs. You’ll work with partners across Technology, Product and Finance, and share your work with senior leaders. Job responsibilities Set technical standards for coding, model validation and AI tool use; lead code and design reviews, mentor data scientists, and participate in hiring. Define how the team measures adoption, engagement and value delivery across developer tools and platforms. Design and build GenAI and agentic solutions, including MCP servers, productivity tools, natural-language insight assistants and agents embedded in developer platforms. Partner with the firm’s graph team to extend its graph product, build GraphRAG solutions, and apply graph analytics and embeddings to engineering collaboration and dependency networks. Analyze CI/CD, developer activity, product telemetry and GenAI adoption data; build models and run experiments to assess effects on engineering output and delivery speed. Put machine learning models into production and maintain scalable analytics pipelines using platforms such as Databricks and MLflow. Encourage effective use of agentic coding tools, including GitHub Copilot and Claude Code, by sharing practices and building reusable AI-assisted workflows. Build web applications and dashboards to track measures such as DORA, cycle time and adoption funnels; partner with Technology, Product and Finance leaders and present work to senior leadership. Required qualifications, capabilities, and skills Bachelor’s degree in Data Science, Statistics, Computer Science or a related field. At least seven years of experience in data science or machine learning engineering, preferably in financial services or technology. At least two years of hands-on experience building with large language models, including retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), agents and prompt engineering. Daily experience using agentic coding tools such as GitHub Copilot or Claude Code, and the ability to coach others in their use. Proficiency in Python, SQL, machine learning and statistical modeling, with experience using Snowflake, Databricks or similar platforms. Experience setting technical standards, leading code and design reviews, and mentoring data scientists or engineers. Product analytics experience and the ability to define metrics for ambiguous problems without an established measurement approach. Ability to explain technical work to technical and non-technical audiences, including senior leaders. Preferred qualifications, capabilities, and skills Master’s degree or PhD in Mathematics, Data Science, Computer Science or a related field. Experience with graph databases such as Neo4j, Amazon Neptune or TigerGraph, including graph embeddings and GraphRAG. Experience developing MCP servers or other integrations for AI agents. Experience putting machine learning models into production using Databricks/MLflow, AWS SageMaker or similar, and working with dbt or Airflow. Knowledge of experimentation and causal inference methods, such as A/B testing, difference-in-differences or synthetic control. Web application development experience with Flask or FastAPI and React, and knowledge of DevOps metrics, the software development lifecycle, internal tool adoption and developer experience.

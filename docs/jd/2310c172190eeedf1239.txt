Join JPMorganChase's Financial Controller function, where you'll play a critical role in safeguarding the accuracy and integrity of the firm's financial records across complex capital markets operations. This is an opportunity to partner with front office, risk, and treasury teams while building deep expertise in general ledger controls and financial reporting. If you thrive in fast-paced environments and bring a sharp analytical mindset with a passion for controls, this role is for you. As a Financial Controller Associate within the Financial Controller function, you will be responsible for balance sheet reconciliation and substantiation, reporting controls, and the month-end close process and coordination. You will ensure adherence to firmwide General Ledger Reconciliation & Substantiation (GLRS) standards while analyzing and resolving material variances between the general ledger and upstream systems. You will also partner across product-aligned businesses, treasury, and corporate finance groups to identify control issues and drive efficiency improvements.

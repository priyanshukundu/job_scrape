What you’ll do The Senior Developer is responsible for the timely development, enhancement, and ongoing support of the Bank's Summit Core Banking application within the Treasury and Asset Liability Management platform. The role uses Bank-standard technologies, methods, and toolsets, including continuous integration/deployment (CI/CD) tools and practices, to deliver reliable and well-controlled solutions. The Senior Developer must bring expert-level functional knowledge of core banking, treasury services, capital markets, and financial assets, including bonds, loans, and derivatives (options and swaps). This includes understanding how these instruments are priced and how to support their sub-ledger accounting.

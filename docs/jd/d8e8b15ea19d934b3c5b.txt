Associate Director, AI Engineering
Regeneron
Location: Uxbridge1
Link: https://regeneron.wd1.myworkdayjobs.com/Careers/job/Uxbridge1/Associate-Director--AI-Engineering_R53159

Build our future together: At Regeneron, we use science and innovation to develop life-changing medicines for people with serious diseases. We are seeking a Associate Director AI Engineering to join our Global Development function, supporting our digital transformation initiatives. In this role, you will define and scale AI engineering capabilities, develop agentic AI solutions, and advance automation across Global Development while collaborating with enterprise IT, data teams, business stakeholders, and vendor partners. This position offers the opportunity to contribute to a fast-growing, science-driven organization making a meaningful difference to patients worldwide. When & where: Uxbridge, UK (3 days per week on site) Travel up to 20% Discover your role: Define AI architectures, reference patterns, and engineering standards. Design and deliver AI/ML and agentic AI solutions for Global Development. Partner with data enablement, Global Development IT, and enterprise IT teams. Translate business needs into scalable technical solutions and priorities. Evaluate vendor solutions against technical, quality, and validation standards. Establish validation frameworks and monitor production AI performance. Mentor engineers through technical guidance, design reviews, and code reviews. You champion practical AI adoption and continuous improvement. This role requires: PhD with 6+ years' experience or MS with 8+ years' relevant experience. Advanced degree in computer science, statistics, biomedical informatics, or a related field preferred. 5+ years leading and delivering AI/ML solutions within life sciences or healthcare and deep expertise in generative AI, machine learning, natural language processing, and statistical modeling. Hands-on experience building and deploying agentic AI, including multi-agent orchestration, retrieval-augmented generation, tool integration, and guardrails along with strong programming expertise, including Python, data pipelines, automation, and software development. Expertise with database platforms and cloud computing infrastructure as well as experience with MLOps/LLMOps, CI/CD, automated evaluation, and production monitoring. Experience managing and analyzing large-scale clinical datasets, setting technical direction and influencing cross-functional teams and vendor partners. Experience managing multiple projects simultaneously. Experience with software factory approaches, AI coding agents, automated testing, and human review preferred. Strong communication and collaboration skills across technical and non-technical audiences, including global teams. Does this sound like you?

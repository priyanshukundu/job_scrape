Wealth Management Operational Risk Wealth Management Operational Risk sits within the 1st line of defense and is responsible for overseeing the operational risk and control framework across the Wealth Management business. This team partners with key stakeholders throughout the organization to execute independent risk monitoring functions, support process and control improvement, provide tactical and strategic reporting, execute incident management protocols, facilitate issue and action plan management and coordinate new operational risk programs in partnership with the business as well as 2nd Line of Defense business partners. WM Incident Management – Assistant Vice President The Operational Risk Incident Management team is responsible for supporting the identification, assessment, escalation, documentation, remediation, and closure of operational risk incidents across Wealth Management. The role partners closely with business areas, Operations, Technology, Finance, Legal, Compliance, and Risk teams to ensure incidents are appropriately managed, reported, and governed throughout their lifecycle. Specific duties will include: Coordinating the end-to-end operational risk incident lifecycle, from identification through closure and post-incident review Assessing incident reportability, ownership, impact, root cause, and escalation requirements Partnering with business stakeholders to create and maintain accurate incident records within RECAp, including impact assessments, root cause analysis, and remediation plans Facilitating timely escalation of significant incidents to senior management and key stakeholders Monitoring incident remediation activities, workflow SLAs, signoffs, and closure milestones Supporting lessons learned exercises, post-incident reviews, deep dives, and broader risk assessments to drive sustainable control improvements Producing management reporting and metrics related to incident trends, timeliness, root causes, remediation activities, and emerging risks Serving as a trusted advisor to business partners on operational risk requirements, incident management standards, documentation quality, and reporting expectations Required Qualifications: A successful candidate will be able to support a variety of operational risk management initiatives and must be able to manage changing priorities, make sound decisions, complete their assignments and proactively communicate where there are potential risks to the Firm. Attention to detail and accuracy is critical, given the executive audience for much of the team’s work. A candidate needs to be collaborative and able to build effective working relationships with all levels across the organization.

Role Overview: The Learning & Development team within Technology, Data, AI, Ventures is looking for a creative individual to develop and support learning and connection across our AI, Data, and Technology teams—and across the broader enterprise. This role helps our technologists build the skills they need today by delivering learning opportunities and driving awareness and participation, so teams can work more productively and innovate faster. The ideal candidate is interested in building a career within technology who works well at the intersection of education, community building, and program management and is highly organized, collaborative, and creative. What You’ll Do: You’ll be part of the Learning & Development team, helping us build and deliver learning and engagement programs that help employees learn from each other, share new ideas, and connect across the company. This is a great opportunity to grow your skills in program design and employee engagement while making a real difference. Key responsibilities: Shape program content in partnership with engineers, data scientists, and AI experts, and bring your own ideas for new initiatives. Support and build training, technical communities, and events that bring employees together. Run programs that build skills in cloud, engineering, AI, and data from the first planning meeting to the final follow-up. Coordinate training for high focus initiatives including agentic AI. Track engagement, gather feedback and continually improve and evolve programs as needs arise. What You’ll Bring: You’ll bring hands-on experience coordinating programs, events, or engagement initiatives, and you know how to keep multiple projects moving while paying attention to the details. You’re comfortable with collaboration and content tools like Teams, SharePoint, and PowerPoint, and you can turn a rough idea into a polished, engaging learning experience. You enjoy delving into data from participants to see what’s working and make programs better. You also have a real interest in AI, data, and technology. You’re curious about how these tools are changing the way our teams work and learn, and you enjoy finding ways they can help people do their jobs better. Beyond your program development and management skills, you communicate clearly and can explain ideas to both technical and non-technical audiences. You work well with partners across departments, take initiative when something needs doing, and stay steady when priorities shift or details are still taking shape. Required Skills Experience: 2+ years of experience in program coordination, learning, employee engagement, planning, or change management. Project Management: Ability to manage multiple projects at once while keeping work accurate and high quality. Change Management: Experience building and sustaining engagement in a community, program, or initiative, using feedback and participation data to keep it relevant. Communication: Clear written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to explain ideas to both technical and non-technical audiences. Collaboration: Enjoys working with colleagues in L&D, SMEs across TDAV and partners across other departments Initiative: Self-motivated and willing to jump in and help wherever needed. Creativity: Ability to help design engaging and fresh learning experiences. Adaptability: Comfortable when priorities shift or details continue to evolve. Technical Knowledge: Proficient with collaboration and content tools (e.g., Teams, SharePoint, PowerPoint). Education: Bachelor’s degree with tech related coursework. Preferred Skills Data-driven mindset, with experience using metrics and participation data to measure program success. Experience with design, content creation, or digital storytelling. Demonstrated interest in AI, data, or technology (for example, coursework, personal projects, or self-directed learning). Exposure to working in a large, technology-focused organization. Success Profile You’ll thrive in this role if you are: Curious and growth-minded — you seek to learn new technologies and find better ways to enable others.

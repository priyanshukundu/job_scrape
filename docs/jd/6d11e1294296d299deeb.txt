Job Description: Job Title Treasury Markets Investments Pool Trading Specialist Corporate Title Assistant Vice President Location New York, NY Overview Treasury Markets Investments (TMI) is the market-facing arm of Treasury, responsible for liquidity and risk management. In this role, you will manage both linear and non-linear hedges across multiple entities and help manage the US central pool of cash, deploying funds to business-line assets and Treasury investments. TMI also coordinates with Capital and Liquidity Management to meet the Firm’s liquidity and regulatory requirements.

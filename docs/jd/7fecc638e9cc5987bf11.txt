Responsibilities: Lead and actively participate in the design and development of high-volume, low-latency applications for mission-critical systems, with a focus on availability and performance using C#, AngularJS, jQuery, and Microsoft SQL Server 2022. Collaborate with Product Owners and Product Managers to translate business challenges into technical solutions and support sprint planning and product demonstrations. Conduct technical assessments of risks and issues and document recommendations to support decision-making. Coordinate solution design with Architecture, Data, Infrastructure, and Business teams to support prioritized business outcomes and speed to market. Coach and mentor engineers during development and production implementation, including the creation and maintenance of technical documentation aligned with SDLC guidelines. Facilitate regular communication among technical and non-technical stakeholders to support products, maintain alignment, and communicate technical decisions and impacts. What Are We Looking For?

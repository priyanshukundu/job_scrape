Overview: The Principal Software Engineer provides senior technical leadership within M&T Bank’s Origination Technology team, supporting critical banking capabilities across multiple channels and products. This individual shapes how complex solutions are designed, modernized, and delivered, ensuring they remain reliable, secure, and aligned with enterprise architecture, in partnership with engineering, architecture, and business teams. Primary Responsibilities: Lead complex technical implementations across multiple applications, platforms, and engineering teams, from design through production delivery, guiding engineering decisions and resolving cross-system integration challenges and dependencies. Partner with enterprise and solution architects to translate architectural direction into practical implementation approaches; identify design gaps, risks, and tradeoffs. Lead adoption of new technologies and incremental modernization of legacy platforms that preserve interoperability and business continuity; contribute to technical roadmaps addressing strategic needs, engineering capabilities, and technical debt. Promote engineering standards for code quality, automated testing, deployment, security, observability, performance, and resiliency, using delivery and operational metrics to improve engineering practices and system reliability. Provide technical guidance on production issues, root-cause analysis, and sustainable remediation. Mentor engineers and share technical knowledge and effective implementation practices across teams. Lead technical discussions with business, product, engineering, architecture, and senior technology stakeholders; communicate risks and recommendations. Understand and adhere to M&T Bank risk and regulatory standards, policies, and controls; identify issues requiring escalation and support timely remediation of audit findings. Promote an environment that supports a culture of belonging and reflects the M&T Bank brand. Complete other related duties as assigned. Scope of Responsibilities : Senior individual contributor with the engineering depth to engage directly in code, design, and implementation decisions; day-to-day coding is not necessarily the primary focus. Partners with Architecture and engineering teams rather than owning enterprise architecture approval. Supervisory/Managerial Responsibilities : No supervisory responsibilities. Education and Experience Required: Associate’s degree and a minimum of 9 years’ systems analysis and/or application development experience; or Bachelor’s degree and a minimum of 7 years’ systems analysis and/or application development experience; or, in lieu of a degree, a combined minimum of 11 years’ education and/or relevant work experience, including a minimum of 7 years’ systems analysis and/or application development experience. Extensive hands-on software engineering experience, with demonstrated success leading complex technical implementations across multiple applications, platforms, and teams in partnership with enterprise and solution architects. Demonstrated experience delivering significant change, including legacy modernization, in highly available, business-critical environments where reliability, resiliency, performance, security, and operational continuity are essential. Strong knowledge of software development practices (application design, code quality, automated testing, integration, deployment, and production support) and of integrating interconnected systems, including APIs, distributed systems, and systems of record. Ability to resolve complex technical problems, influence decisions across teams, and communicate effectively with technical and nontechnical stakeholders. Education and Experience Preferred: Experience delivering technology within banking or financial services, particularly consumer or business banking. Experience with deposit and lending origination capabilities, such as account opening, credit decisioning, underwriting, application processing, fulfillment, and booking. Experience supporting high availability digital/self-service and colleague-assisted channels, including online, branch, and contact-center experiences. Experience with event-driven integration, microservices, or cloud-based technologies. Experience leading multi-team transformation initiatives, contributing to long-term modernization roadmaps, and presenting technical recommendations to senior leaders. Experience delivering change in highly regulated environments with strong risk, security, and control requirements.

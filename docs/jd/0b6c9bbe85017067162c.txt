Overview The Mastercard Authentication Program delivers authentication solutions that enable secure digital commerce globally. Smart Interface is Mastercard’s EMV 3-D Secure 3DS Server platform and a critical component of the EMVCo 3DS ecosystem, enabling secure, scalable and standards-compliant communication across merchants, gateways, acquirers, Directory Servers and issuer Access Control Servers. We are seeking a highly experienced Lead Software Engineer (Architect) to provide technical leadership and help define and drive the long-term architecture and technology strategy of the Smart Interface platform. The successful candidate will combine deep software engineering and architectural expertise, EMV 3DS domain knowledge, and hands-on technical leadership to drive the design, development, and evolution of Mastercard’s next-generation authentication platform. Role Software Engineering & Technical Leadership • Lead the design, development and evolution of Smart Interface. • Translate business, product and technical requirements into scalable, maintainable and secure solutions. • Provide hands-on leadership across design, development, testing, deployment, production readiness and support. • Own and evolve platform architecture and contribute to technical roadmaps aligned with business and product strategy. • Guide engineers through complex technical decisions and lead code and design reviews. • Design highly available, low-latency transaction systems and cloud-native distributed applications using microservices, APIs and event-driven architectures. • Define integration and API standards and lead modernization initiatives that improve scalability, performance and maintainability. • Apply software design, architecture, Domain-Driven Design and data-structure principles to distributed systems. • Develop reusable components, frameworks and engineering patterns. • Apply secure coding, encryption, authentication, TLS/mTLS, certificate management and secrets management practices. • Design for resilience using fault isolation, retry strategies, disaster recovery and multi-region deployment. • Promote unit, functional and integration testing, performance engineering, observability and production diagnostics. • Troubleshoot complex issues, perform root-cause analysis and address technical debt and engineering risks. EMV 3DS Domain Leadership • Serve as a technical lead for Mastercard’s 3DS Server platform. • Lead development of EMV 3DS capabilities supporting current and future EMVCo standards. • Drive adoption of emerging authentication technologies and ensure interoperability across merchants, gateways, acquirers, Directory Servers and ACS providers. • Partner with product, engineering, architecture and standards teams to evolve authentication capabilities. Leadership & Influence • Mentor engineers and technical leads in software design, development and engineering practices. • Communicate technical decisions, trade-offs and recommendations to engineering, product and business stakeholders. • Influence technical direction without direct authority and foster collaboration and continuous improvement.

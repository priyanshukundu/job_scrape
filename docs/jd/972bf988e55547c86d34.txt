Job Summary As a Senior Business Analyst supporting Energy Efficiency Strategy, Innovation, and Engineering, you’ll tackle complex business challenges that directly influence how customers access, experience, and benefit from energy efficiency programs. From evaluating emerging technologies and identifying new market opportunities to streamline program operations and shaping go-to-market strategies, your analysis will help increase participation, improve customer experience, and deliver greater value to our customers and energy efficiency portfolio. This position serves as a hands-on business analyst and data engineer. The role translates business needs into reliable data integrations, Power BI reporting, and workflow solutions that support sales, marketing, and operations. Responsibilities include developing and maintaining data models and reporting solutions, improving data quality, automating recurring processes, troubleshooting existing tools, and documenting solutions to support continuity and adoption. The position partners with business stakeholders and enterprise technology teams to deliver practical, maintainable solutions within established governance standards.

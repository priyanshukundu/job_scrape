Try new things, learn new skills and discover what you excel at—all from Day One. Job Description U.S. Bank is seeking a .NET Application Developer to support applications and technology solutions. This role partners with business and technology teams to design, develop, test, maintain, and enhance finance applications while ensuring high performance, reliability, and scalability. The ideal candidate has strong experience with .NET, C#, SQL Server , application architecture, and the full software development lifecycle. Responsibilities Develop, test, and maintain .NET/C# applications . Collaborate with stakeholders to gather requirements and define technical solutions. Perform change impact analysis and implement application enhancements. Create and execute unit and system test cases to ensure quality deliverables. Troubleshoot and resolve application, database, and production issues. Develop and maintain technical documentation and support procedures. Participate in code reviews and application design discussions. Support the full Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC) from design through deployment. Basic Qualifications - Bachelor’s degree, or equivalent work experience - Three to five years of relevant experience Preferred Skills/Experience Hands on enterprise business application development experience . Strong proficiency in C#, .NET Framework/.NET Core, and ASP.NET . Experience with Microsoft SQL Server , SQL development, and database design. Knowledge of application architecture and object-oriented programming principles. Experience with application testing, debugging, and production support . Understanding of data modeling, information management, and system integration . Familiarity with change management, source control, and SDLC methodologies . Strong analytical, problem-solving, communication, and collaboration skills. Nice to Have Skills Cloud experience in AWS. CI/CD experience Location expectations This role requires working from a U.S. Bank location three (3) or more days per week.

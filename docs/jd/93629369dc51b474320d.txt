What You'll Do In this role, you will play a key part in advancing business priorities by delivering high-impact work across your area of expertise. We are seeking an experienced and highly motivated Data Scientist III to join our Business Intelligence team. This role will apply advanced analytics, statistical modeling, machine learning, and optimization techniques to solve complex business and supply chain problems and improve data-driven decision-making. The successful candidate will work closely with business and technical stakeholders to translate ambiguous business challenges into analytical frameworks, develop models and decision-support tools, and communicate findings in a way that drives action. The position will also play an important role in the development and analysis of key performance indicators (KPIs), helping the organization better understand the drivers of operational and customer-facing performance. This role is well suited for someone who combines strong statistical and technical capabilities with business curiosity and practical problem-solving skills. The Data Scientist III will have opportunities to work across the analytical lifecycle, from exploratory analysis and experimentation through modeling, optimization, deployment, and ongoing performance monitoring. Key Responsibilities: Advanced Analytics & Data Science · Develop statistical and machine learning models to address complex business and supply chain challenges. · Apply techniques such as regression, classification, forecasting, simulation, clustering, and other statistical or machine learning methodologies as appropriate to the business problem. · Design analytical approaches for problems involving uncertainty, complex relationships, and large or heterogeneous datasets. · Evaluate model performance, assumptions, limitations, and business applicability to ensure analytical solutions are both technically sound and practically useful. · Perform exploratory and diagnostic analyses to identify trends, relationships, risks, and opportunities within business and operational data. Optimization & Decision Science · Develop optimization models and decision-support engines that translate business objectives, constraints, and analytical insights into recommended actions. · Apply approaches such as mathematical optimization, simulation, statistical modeling, and algorithmic decision-making to supply chain and operational problems. · Partner with business stakeholders to define objective functions, constraints, trade-offs, and measures of success. · Evaluate alternative strategies through scenario analysis and quantify their expected operational or business impact. KPI Development & Performance Analytics · Design, develop, and analyze KPIs and performance measurement frameworks. · Partner with stakeholders to translate strategic and operational objectives into measurable indicators. · Conduct deeper analysis beyond KPI reporting to identify underlying drivers of performance and opportunities for improvement. · Develop analytical methods that improve the organization’s ability to interpret performance and make informed decisions. Analytical Solution Development & Deployment · Develop reusable analytical pipelines, models, and tools primarily using Python and/or R. · Work with technical partners to deploy analytical models and decision-support solutions into scalable business processes and applications. · Develop appropriate validation and monitoring approaches to ensure deployed models and analytical solutions continue to perform as intended. · Identify opportunities to automate recurring analytical workflows and improve the scalability of the team’s solutions. · Leverage modern development practices, including coding agents and AI-assisted development tools where appropriate, to accelerate analytical development while maintaining solution quality and reliability. Preferred Qualifications: · Degree in Data Science, Statistics, Mathematics, Economics, Supply Chain Management, Computer Science, Engineering, Operations Research, or a related quantitative field. · Minimum of five (5) years of relevant experience with a Bachelor’s degree, three (3) years of relevant experience with a Master’s degree, or no (0) years of relevant experience with a Doctorate. · Strong proficiency in Python, R, or another programming language commonly used for statistical analysis and data science. · Strong proficiency in SQL for querying, transforming, and analyzing large datasets. · Demonstrated experience applying statistical modeling, machine learning, optimization, simulation, or other advanced analytical methodologies to real-world problems. · Strong foundation in statistical reasoning, including the ability to appropriately select, evaluate, and interpret quantitative methods. · Demonstrated ability to structure ambiguous business problems and translate them into rigorous analytical approaches. · Strong written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to explain complex analytical concepts and recommendations to non-technical audiences. Ability to independently manage complex analytical projects while collaborating effectively across functional and technical tea.

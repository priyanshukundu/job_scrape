Job Description The team: Our Adversarial Validation team is part of Allstate’s broader Exposure Management organization, focused on identifying, testing, and validating security vulnerabilities across enterprise applications and technology environments. Our team is evolving beyond traditional penetration testing to take a more proactive approach to security. In addition to supporting critical application and compliance testing, we investigate newly disclosed vulnerabilities to understand their exploitability and potential business impact. The team brings together penetration testing professionals with complementary technical expertise who collaborate closely with Exposure Intelligence, Exposure Response, and Threat Intelligence teams. Overall Work Scope: Lead Consultant, Penetration Testing - Adversarial Validation As a Lead Consultant within our Adversarial Validation team, you'll combine advanced penetration testing with real-time security exposure validation. You'll work closely with security engineers, exposure management teams, and technology stakeholders, providing both hands-on technical expertise and clear guidance to leadership. This is an opportunity to influence how we approach offensive security while continuing to develop your skills across emerging technologies. What You'll Do Lead complex, hands-on penetration testing across web applications, APIs, networks, and enterprise technologies. Investigate emerging vulnerabilities, analyze publicly available exploit code, and validate whether identified exposures can be exploited in our environment. Assess attack paths, security controls, and potential business impact to help prioritize remediation. Translate technical findings into clear, actionable risk insights for engineers, business stakeholders, and executive leadership. Collaborate with exposure intelligence, exposure response, threat intelligence, and technology teams to investigate and address security risks. Leverage penetration testing tools and automation capabilities to improve testing efficiency and validate security exposures. Support security assessments of critical applications and environments, including those subject to regulatory and compliance requirements. Contribute to testing methodologies, knowledge sharing, and continuous improvement across the team. What You Bring 5+ years of hands-on experience in penetration testing, offensive security, red teaming, or related adversarial security testing. Strong practical experience conducting penetration tests, particularly across web applications, APIs, and network environments. Experience investigating vulnerabilities, a nalyzing proof-of-concept exploit code, and validating real-world exploitability. Ability to assess attack paths, evaluate security controls, and determine the technical and business impact of security exposures. Strong communication skills, i ncluding the ability to explain complex technical findings and security risks to both technical and executive audiences. Working knowledge of penetration testing methodologies, common vulnerabilities, and security frameworks such as OWASP and PTES. Working knowledge of security tools such as Burp Suite Professional, Postman, or comparable testing technologies. Familiarity with PCI DSS or similar security compliance requirements and their implications for risk prioritization. Nice to Have Experience assessing cloud environments, including AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud. Exposure to penetration testing of AI-enabled applications, generative AI, or large language model technologies. Experience with mobile applications, SaaS platforms, or PaaS environments. Experience leveraging automated security testing or adversarial validation tools. Scripting experience using Python, PowerShell, Bash, or similar languages. Experience with Active Directory, identity security, advanced network testing, or adversary simulation. Offensive security certifications such as OSCP, OSWE, OSEP, GPEN, or equivalent demonstrated capabilities.

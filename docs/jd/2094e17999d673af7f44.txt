The Fixed Income Client Portfolio Management team has deep expertise in fixed income markets and fixed income strategies at State Street Investment Management. We collaborate across the organization to solve our clients’ challenges and enable them to achieve their investment objectives. We are experts in index, active, and systematic fixed income, as well as cash and currency strategies. Portfolio Specialists are proactive, curious, and highly organized. Specific areas of focus include: Work closely with portfolio management teams and others on the Client PM team to serve as internal and external subject matter experts for Fixed Income Partner with our distribution colleagues to develop new business opportunities and to support the existing institutional client base Own complex projects and tasks, driving them to successful conclusion Share knowledge with our clients by developing timely and high-quality thought leadership and investment content. Candidate Qualifications Successful candidates will have a BA/BS or similar degree and will have some combination of the following: Experience: Extensive knowledge of and intellectual curiosity in Fixed Income 5-10 years of investment experience Experience working with institutional client relationships Masters degree or CFA is preferred, or progress towards Technical Skills: Excellent quantitative and analytical skills are required, including the ability to analyze, evaluate, and present financial, economic, and competitive data High degree of comfort working with and appropriately leveraging AI Working knowledge of Bloomberg, eVestment, Morningstar Proficient in macro economics, capital markets and the competitive landscape Fundamental Skills: Very comfortable working in a collaborative culture across a range of fixed income strategies Highly motivated and able to multitask Attention to detail and high degree of accuracy Able to deliver on tight deadlines A solutions mindset through creative thinking and process improvements Strong written and verbal communication skills with the ability to develop and present client-facing material In a fast-paced, team-oriented environment a sense of humor is always a plus!

Job Summary: Venmo is hiring Staff Data Scientists across Core Experiences, Consumer Financial Services (CFS), and Commerce. Core Experiences includes payments, requests, the social feed, notifications, search, and network growth. CFS includes products such as the Venmo debit card and funds-in. Commerce focuses on business payments and helping merchants grow. As a senior data science leader, you’ll help shape Venmo’s growth strategy by finding and sizing opportunities and deciding what to measure, test, and build. You’ll partner with business, product, engineering, risk, and other teams, and develop scalable approaches to experimentation and measurement, including causal inference, social and network effects, forecasting, and engagement. You’ll translate complex data into clear insights that guide product, design, and investment decisions for a leading payments app. Job Description: Essential Responsibilities: Lead and manage data science projects, ensuring timely delivery and alignment with business goals. Develop and maintain data models, algorithms, and reporting systems to support data analysis and decision-making. Analyze complex datasets to identify trends, patterns, and insights that drive strategic initiatives. Collaborate with cross-functional teams to understand data needs and provide actionable insights. Ensure data quality and integrity through regular audits and validation processes. Mentor and guide junior data scientists, fostering a culture of continuous learning and improvement. Minimum Qualifications: 5+ years relevant experience and a Bachelor’s degree OR Any equivalent combination of education and experience. Additional Responsibilities & Preferred Qualifications : Essential Responsibilities: Lead data science for your area, setting the analytical strategy and ensuring timely delivery aligned with product and business goals. Design and analyze complex experiments, including tests where one person's experience affects their friends, tests in a two-sided network, and tests with small samples or long-term outcomes, and define the guardrails that protect payments and trust. Define the metrics, funnels, and models that follow people and businesses from first use to lasting engagement, including activation, retention, and healthy engagement for a payments app. Measure how new features and broad changes affect people, separating real behavior change from novelty and clicks. Uncover what drives user and business behavior across Venmo, such as how the social feed, notifications, search, payments, financial products, and merchant experiences help people connect, pay, and manage money. Build modeling frameworks, leveraging machine learning, statistics, and optimization, to solve propensity modeling problems and other related product problems. Apply classic machine learning algorithms, such as regression, tree-based models (random forests, gradient boosting) and clustering, to propensity, segmentation, churn and forecasting problems. Develop forecasts and growth models with Finance and Product, and recommend where to invest next. Collaborate with Product, Design, Engineering, and Finance to turn complex analysis into clear recommendations, with an honest view of confidence and risk. Build and own AI-native data science workflows: agents, skills and context layers for instrumentation, analysis, experiment readouts and data quality checks, with clear boundaries between trusted automation and expert judgment. Measure the quality and impact of AI and agentic product experiences, combining product metrics with model-quality and evaluation signals. Help lead how AI makes data science faster at Venmo, from instrumentation to analysis and data quality checks. Ensure data quality and integrity by validating instrumentation and experiment results before decisions are made. Mentor and guide data scientists, and set the bar for analytical rigor across the team. Minimum Qualifications: 5 years of relevant experience and a Bachelor’s degree OR Any equivalent combination of education and experience. 5 years of experience in experimentation, causal inference, and statistical methods 5 years of experience using SQL and at least one scripting language: Python or R, for processing large, complex, and messy datasets. 3 years of experience designing and reading out A/B Testing or hypothesis testing to inform product decisions and new experience launches 3 years of experience partnering with leadership engineers, product managers, and data engineers to understand business needs and how these can be addressed through analytics 3 years of experience translating complex problems into simpler terms, and effectively influencing both peers and senior leadership (3 years). Additional Responsibilities & Preferred Qualifications: Bachelor’s degree with at least 8 years of professional experience, or a master’s degree with at least 6 years of experience. Advanced proficiency in SQL and strong Python or R for large-scale transaction and event data, forecasting, experimentation, and causal inference. Deep experience in end-to-end product experimentation, including test design, power analysis, small-sample and long-horizon tests, and decision-ready readouts. Experience with network effects, social products, marketplaces, two-sided platforms, recommendations, or notification and engagement systems. Experience with consumer financial products or analytics for payments, cards, deposits, or credit, or with merchant and small business analytics. Experience building forecasts, growth models, or predictive models (including machine learning) that a business used to plan or make decisions. Experience with agentic analytics and AI-enabled data science workflows, including leveraging AI and LLM-based tools and agents to speed up analysis, instrumentation, experimentation, and data quality checks. Highly skilled with agentic coding tools and workflows (for example, Claude Code or Cursor) to speed up analysis and production code. Ability to translate complex analyses into clear insights and recommendations for technical and non-technical stakeholders. Strong communication and collaboration skills, with experience influencing senior leaders on investment and roadmap decisions and partnering across Product, Design, Finance, Engineering, and Analytics. Ability to independently lead complex analytical projects, identify measurement gaps, and recommend improvements to product and business strategy, while managing multiple priorities in a fast-paced environment. Collaborative team player who builds trust across functions, shares knowledge openly, and puts team and Venmo outcomes ahead of individual credit. Strong ownership and commitment: follows through on commitments, raises risks early, and stays accountable for results from first analysis through business impact. Consistent record of delivering high-quality work on time, including through ambiguity and shifting priorities, and of making clear trade-offs to protect what matters most.

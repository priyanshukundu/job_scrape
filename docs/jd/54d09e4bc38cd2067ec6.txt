The application window is expected to close on: 12/30/2026 Meet the Team The AI Platforms and Enablement team is the engine behind Cisco’s internal AI transformation. Our mission is to build and scale CIRCUIT, our universal enterprise AI platform designed to collapse information silos and amplify productivity through intelligent, agentic experiences. Your Impact As a Software Engineer, you will be a core contributor to our engineering efforts, focusing on building high-performance data pipelines, search infrastructure, and agentic components that power our AI capabilities. You will be the search infrastructure and data engineering specialist within the CIRCUIT ecosystem, from data ingestion and indexing to the implementation of generative AI workflows. You will build and optimize robust data ingestion pipelines using Dataflow (Apache Beam), ensuring enterprise data flows seamlessly into our search ecosystem. You will implement and tune Elasticsearch configurations, develop re-ranking algorithms, and improve relevancy measurements to ensure high-quality outputs. Additionally, you will build and refine AI Agents, deploy and manage services on GCP using Kubernetes, and work directly with LLMs to implement RAG pipelines. You will write clean, maintainable Python code, participate in rigorous code reviews, and collaborate in Agile ceremonies to ensure cross-functional success. Minimum Qualifications Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field, or equivalent practical experience. 5+ years of experience in professional software engineering, with a focus on backend, data, or search systems. 2+ years of hands-on experience designing and operating Elasticsearch in a production environment. Demonstrated experience delivering production-grade software in a cloud environment using Kubernetes. Proven proficiency in Python and building robust data pipelines using Dataflow (Apache Beam). Preferred Qualifications Experience designing and implementing hybrid search architectures, combining semantic (vector-based) and keyword (BM25) results. Exposure to MCP (Model Context Protocol) and experience connecting LLMs to external data sources. Experience using AI-assisted development tools such as Cursor, GitHub Copilot, or Claude to accelerate development workflows. Strong analytical ability to understand and measure relevancy, providing quantitative improvements to search quality.

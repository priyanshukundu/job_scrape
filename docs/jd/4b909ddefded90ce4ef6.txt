Power Platform Business Analyst & Developer The Opportunity: As a data scientist, you’re excited at the prospect of unlocking the secrets held by data. In an increasingly connected world, structured and unstructured data open new opportunities. As a data scientist at Booz Allen, you can help turn these complex data sets into useful information to solve global challenges. We are seeking an experienced Power Platform Business Analyst & Developer to support the design, development, and delivery of business process solutions, such as tool, apps, and dashboards, for our Federal clients. The work to date has included creating and enforcing permissions within the app based on user roles, utilizing components and themes identified in the formulas, running workflows to send email notifications, standardizing data, and creating finalized pdf documents based on requests built in PowerApps using SharePoint lists as a back end. The ideal candidate combines strong business analysis and stakeholder engagement skills with hands-on Power Platform development experience. This individual will work directly with customers to gather requirements, document business processes, design solutions, develop applications and automations, and support deployment and adoption efforts. Work with us as we use data science for good. Join us. The world can’t wait. You Have: 5+ years of experience developing solutions using Power BI, Power Apps, including Canvas Apps, Power Automate, including Cloud Flows, and SharePoint Online Experience gathering, analyzing, and documenting business and functional requirements Experience developing data-driven business applications Experience working within Agile delivery environments Knowledge of data relationships, normalization, and application data architecture Ability to obtain and maintain a Public Trust or Suitability/Fitness determination based on client requirements Bachelor’s degree Nice If You Have: Experience creating UAT plans, user guides, training materials, and solution documentation Experience supporting low-code/no-code solution delivery initiatives Experience with data migration, reporting, analytics, and dashboard solutions Possession of strong client-facing communication, facilitation, and collaboration skills Vetting: Applicants selected will be subject to a government investigation and may need to meet eligibility requirements of the U.S. government client.

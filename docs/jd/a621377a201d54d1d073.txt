Job Description Summary The Manager, Treasury & Capital Markets will support the development and execution of the company's global treasury strategy, with a primary focus on capital markets, credit ratings, and liquidity management with exposure to pension and cash management. This individual will play a key role in evaluating financing alternatives, supporting relationships with credit rating agencies and capital markets participants, assessing liquidity and capital structure considerations, and supporting the company's global cash and funding strategy. The role will partner closely with Corporate FP&A, Controllership, Tax, Investor Relations, Pension and Benefits, and regional treasury teams to deliver sound financial analysis, effective risk management, and disciplined execution of treasury priorities. The successful candidate will combine strong financial and analytical capabilities with sound business judgment, excellent communication skills, and the ability to operate effectively in a complex, global corporate environment. Job Description Key Responsibilities Support the development and execution of the company's capital markets strategy through analyzing capital structure alternatives, funding requirements, debt capacity, maturity profiles, and the implications of financing decisions on the company's cost of capital and financial flexibility. Prepare financial models, market analyses, and recommendations to support capital allocation, financing, ratings considerations and capital structure decisions. Support debt investor and rating agency materials, internal financing presentations and capital markets reporting. Support the company's credit ratings strategy and ongoing engagement with major rating agencies. Develop and maintain financial analyses, credit metrics, forecasts, and rating sensitivities to assess credit profile trends and potential ratings implications. Partner with FP&A and Controllership teams to understand forecasts, assess downside scenarios, and evaluate implications for credit ratings and financial flexibility. Support the development and ongoing assessment of the company's global liquidity strategy, including liquidity targets, funding requirements, and contingency funding considerations. Analyze cash flow forecasts, liquidity positions, sources and uses of funds, and potential funding gaps under base-case and downside scenarios. Evaluate the appropriate balance among cash, committed credit facilities, commercial paper, debt maturities, and other available sources of liquidity. Develop scenario analyses and sensitivity assessments to support liquidity planning, capital structure decisions, and risk management. Support the financial analysis of the company's pension obligations, funded status, and associated cash funding requirements. Incorporate pension-related considerations into broader capital structure, liquidity, and credit ratings assessments. Support global cash management activities, including cash positioning, forecasting, repatriation considerations, and the efficient deployment of available cash. Support the development of treasury reporting, key performance indicators, and management dashboards to improve transparency and decision-making. Prepare high-quality financial analyses, presentations, and recommendations for Treasury Senior Leadership Translate complex financial and market information into clear, concise insights that support strategic decisions. Collaborate across Treasury, Corporate FP&A, Controllership, Tax, Legal, and Investor Relations. Drive analytical projects and process improvements, ensuring deliverables are accurate, timely, and aligned with business priorities.

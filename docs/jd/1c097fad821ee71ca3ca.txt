Meet the Team The Enterprise Integrated Systems team develops the next generation of platform capabilities that integrate Splunk across Cisco's enterprise software portfolio. Our mission is to enable enterprise customers to seamlessly deploy, operate, and scale Splunk Core, Enterprise Security (ES), and IT Service Intelligence (ITSI) across on-premises and air-gapped environments. We build high-performance, reliable, and reusable platform software that simplifies deployment, lifecycle management, and operations while improving customer experience. Our engineers solve complex distributed systems challenges, influence architecture across multiple Cisco organizations, and partner closely with Product Management to deliver scalable solutions used by enterprise customers worldwide. If you're passionate about platform engineering, distributed systems, Kubernetes, performance optimization, and building software that operates at enterprise scale, you'll find exciting technical problems and significant ownership on this team. Your Impact Lead the architecture, design, and implementation of complex platform capabilities spanning multiple products and engineering teams. Drive scalable, reusable, and maintainable software designs that improve product quality, reliability, and customer experience. Partner with Product Managers, Architects, and Development teams to shape technical roadmaps and influence product direction. Own critical subsystems throughout their lifecycle—from design through production support and continuous improvement. Drive engineering excellence through design reviews, code reviews, automated testing, observability, CI/CD, and performance engineering. Evaluate emerging technologies and recommend architectural improvements that enhance scalability, security, and developer productivity.

Position Overview We are seeking a highly motivated scientist for a postdoctoral fellowship position within the Translational Imaging group at our company's Research Laboratories in South San Francisco, California. The successful candidate will contribute to the development and application of advanced magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) and magnetic resonance spectroscopy (MRS) approaches to investigate skeletal muscle physiology, muscle metabolism, and muscle health in metabolic disease. The fellowship will focus on quantitative imaging biomarker development and translational imaging technologies that enable noninvasive assessment of muscle composition, function, and energetics in preclinical models. The position offers a unique opportunity to combine MRI physics, imaging technology development, and muscle physiology research within a multidisciplinary drug discovery environment. Responsibilities Develop and implement advanced MRI/MRS methodologies for preclinical research. Design, optimize, and validate imaging acquisition and analysis workflows. Contribute to MRI pulse sequence development and programming. Analyze and interpret complex imaging datasets using quantitative and computational approaches. Collaborate with multidisciplinary teams across imaging, biology, pharmacology, and data science. Present research findings at scientific conferences and publish results in peer-reviewed journals. Drive innovative research projects while developing scientific independence and technical leadership. Education Minimum Requirement Must currently hold a PhD OR Receive a Ph.D. no later than spring 2027 Ph.D. in Physics, Medical Physics, Biomedical Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Imaging Science, Biophysics, Applied Mathematics, or a related quantitative discipline.

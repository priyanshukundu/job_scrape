What You'll Contribute The Associate, Technology Audit will be responsible for assisting with technology-focused audits, evaluating IT controls, and assessing the overall effectiveness of IT processes. What You'll Do Collaborate with the audit team to plan and execute technology-focused audits, including IT general controls, application controls, cybersecurity, and other relevant areas. Assist in identifying potential risks and control deficiencies within IT processes and systems. Participate in assessing IT governance frameworks and evaluating the effectiveness of internal controls. Conduct audit fieldwork by gathering and analyzing data, documenting processes, and performing control testing. Assist in evaluating the efficiency and effectiveness of technology controls, identifying control gaps, and making recommendations for improvement. Collaborate with cross-functional teams to promote effective risk management practices and control enhancements. What you have Minimum: Indicate minimum education, skills and experience required. Solid understanding of technology systems, IT processes, and control frameworks (e.g., COBIT, NIST Cybersecurity Framework). Familiarity with auditing IT general controls, application controls, and cybersecurity controls. Knowledge of industry regulations and standards (e.g., SOX, GDPR, ISO 27001) is desirable. Proficient in using audit tools and data analytics to support audit procedures. Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to identify control deficiencies and propose practical solutions. Excellent written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to clearly document and present audit findings. Detail-oriented mindset with the ability to work accurately and efficiently.

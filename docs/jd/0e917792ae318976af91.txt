Job Description: We are seeking a Systems / Software Engineer who wants an opportunity to learn software/hardware security, keys management, and Post Quantum Computing. Responsibilities: Participate in design and development of key signing infrastructure enhancements. Participate in design and development of product security features using TPM 2.0 security chip. Participate in design and development of secure boot flow with Hardware Root of Trust. Education and Experience Required: BS or MS degree in Computer Engineering, Computer Science, or related degree or equivalent experience. Knowledge and Skills: Domain knowledge in software and security engineering, including software signing Familiarity with open-source usage; must understand the concept of code provenance and how to maintain it over time Strong knowledge of Linux and FreeBSD kernel. Familiarity with different CPU architectures and knowledge on Hardware Root of Trust (HRoT) is a plus. Familiarity with TPM 2.0 and devID technology as specified by Trust Computing Group (TCG) Familiarity with Post Quantum computing technology and different signature algorithms such as ML-DSA is a plus Coding proficiency in the C/C++, Python, RUST programming language with ability to learn others, as necessary.

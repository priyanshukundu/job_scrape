Title: Associate Lead Analyst 1 Location: 7 World Trade Center, 250 Greenwich Street, New York, NY 10007 Duties: Analyze the credit aspects of structured finance transactions, including analysis of the underlying assets, transaction structure, legal documents, and deal-level financial modeling to formulate and substantiate independent credit opinions to assign ratings. Monitor financial industry conditions and trends, present transactions, and their analysis to rating committees, and identify and write accurate and relevant credit research. Contribute to various special research projects, develop in-depth knowledge of the asset class, and pursue thought leadership initiatives to enhance our analytics, outreach, and research impact. Gain exposure to ESG credit analytics and assist with process enhancement initiatives, including digital enablement projects where data analytics and general artificial intelligence (GenAI) are applied to deliver innovative solutions. Develop working knowledge of Moody’s methodologies to apply to various analyses, demonstrate the ability to understand and identify key factors that affect credit ratings. Communicate credit knowledge and rationale and develop and maintain an understanding of financial industry trends and issues. Requirements: Requires a Master’s degree or foreign equivalent in Finance, Quantitative Finance, Financial Engineering, or a related quantitative field plus two (2) years of experience as an analyst or in a related position performing quantitative analysis in a financial services firm. Must have experience with the following: conducting quantitative financial modeling and statistical analysis; analyzing credit impact of macro-economic events; presenting credit analyses; analyzing deal documents and authoring credit opinions, issuer analyses, or internal research memos; applying knowledge of capital markets and structures; applying data analytics and GenAI for insightful problem-solving; and reviewing legal documents, including indentures or loan documents. Up to 5% domestic and international travel required. Destination and frequency impossible to predict.

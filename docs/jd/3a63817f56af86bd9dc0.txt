The Principal Program Quality Planning & Risk Lead is responsible for driving quality risk identification, mitigation, and prevention across the PW1500G and PW1900G product lines. This role serves as the focal point for program quality risk management, product assurance, Critical Parts governance, and customer quality engagement to improve product reliability, manufacturing robustness, customer satisfaction, and operational performance. As a senior individual contributor, this position influences product, manufacturing, and program decisions through proactive risk management, defect prevention, and quality leadership. What You Will Do: Program Quality Risk Management. Lead evaluation of the PW1500G/PW1900G quality signature; help to define and evolve KPIs and data analysis needs within and across programs. Establish and maintain the 24K Quality Risk Register, ensuring that critical product, manufacturing, supplier, and delivery risks are identified, assessed, escalated, and mitigated. Drive cross-functional alignment on and execution of preventive actions that reduce risk and improve quality performance; communicate, drive visibility to Integrated Product Management Team (IPMT). Establish robust read-across processes for risk identification and mitigation/retirement. Represent Program Quality on Product Management Teams (PMTs); facilitate / drive alignment across Engineering, Industrial Operations, and Programs to ensure consistent application of Quality requirements and adherence to regulator, customer, and Pratt & Whitney QMS requirements​. Partner with APQP team to identify, assess, and monitor Critical Parts (i.e. Path to Zero Defects) to improve product safety, reliability, manufacturability, and fleet performance; influence and drive mitigation of risks with Component(?) Integrated Product Team (CIPT). Product Recall. Ensure Root Cause / Corrective Actions (RCCAs) effectively address root causes, prevent recurrence, and deliver sustained improvement. Monitor risk trends and escalate systemic issues and barriers to resolution. Serve as the PQM Problem Resolution Team (PRT) delegate for PW1500G/PW1900G. Represent the Program Quality within applicable Quality Systems Councils and cross-functional reviews. Qualifications You Must Have: Bachelor's degree in Engineering, Manufacturing, Quality, or related technical discipline and minimum 10 years prior relevant experience, or An Advanced Degree in a related field and minimum 7 years experience. Experience leading cross-functional risk management and quality improvement initiatives. Demonstrated knowledge of RCCA methodologies and quality systems. Qualifications We Prefer: Aerospace or highly regulated manufacturing experience. Experience with APQP, PPAP, Critical Parts management, and Path-to-Zero Defects initiatives. Knowledge of PFMEA, MSA, Control Plans, and risk management methodologies. ASQ CQE, CQM, or equivalent certification preferred. Learn More & Apply Now!

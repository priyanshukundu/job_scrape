About the team The Agentic AI team at Zillow is at the forefront of transforming the real estate industry by helping millions of people use AI assistants to find their next home. Within this organization, the Agentic Solutions team develops AI-powered experiences that help people navigate their real estate journey, understand their options, make informed decisions and take action. Our applied scientists work closely with engineering, product and design to turn advances in AI into reliable features that deliver measurable customer value. Our work sits at the intersection of large language models and traditional machine learning, such as ranking and personalization, and we are a small team where every scientist has real ownership and influence over what we build. About the role As a Senior Applied Scientist on the Agentic AI team, you will tackle complex, ambiguous problems from scientific framing and modeling through evaluation, experimentation and production delivery, and help shape which problems we solve and how we measure success.

About this role: Wells Fargo is seeking a Business Execution Consultant to support implementation needs within Fraud & Claims Management Business Assurance Quality Controls across Claims and Detection Operations. In this role, you will: Execute and implement changes supporting the Quality Assurance / Quality Control (QA/QC) Program, including partnering with subject matter experts to provide recommendations on internal survey questions, assisting Program resources with documenting desktop procedures, and developing business training and communications. Support components of the control lifecycle from inception through implementation. With guidance from senior management, research and provide responses to moderately complex audit and testing inquiries for U.S.-owned controls, including supporting screenshots, transactional research, desktop procedures, and other relevant documentation. Review business changes to identify potential impacts to the QC control inventory. Identify and, where appropriate, implement process improvements that strengthen the QC control and process environment. Leverage reporting and source materials to develop presentations and other business documents that effectively communicate key business announcements and changes.

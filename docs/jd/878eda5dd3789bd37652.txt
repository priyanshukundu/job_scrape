Role Overview As a Production Control Operations Analyst, you will help ensure the stability, availability, and reliability of New York Life's production technology environment by supporting 24x7x365 production operations across mainframe and distributed platforms. You will monitor critical production workloads, manage production incidents, support batch processing and scheduling activities, and coordinate production changes to help maintain uninterrupted business operations. Working closely with application teams, infrastructure partners, vendors, and business stakeholders, you will provide operational support for production scheduling, file transmissions, mainframe operations, incident management, and business resumption activities. This role requires strong operational discipline, technical troubleshooting skills, and a commitment to delivering reliable technology services in a highly regulated environment. What You'll Do Monitor and support the enterprise production environment across mainframe and distributed platforms, ensuring successful batch processing, online processing, file transmissions, and adherence to established service level objectives. Manage production scheduling and change activities by creating and maintaining workloads within enterprise scheduling platforms, supporting production migrations, coordinating implementation activities, and ensuring operational readiness for production changes. Respond to production incidents by performing root cause analysis, restoring service, coordinating with application teams, vendors, and technology partners, and following established incident management and escalation procedures. Support mainframe operational activities, including system startup and shutdown procedures, IPL support, infrastructure maintenance, printer management, capacity activities, and production environment administration. Maintain operational documentation, production reporting, and management metrics while participating in business continuity, disaster recovery, and operational readiness activities that strengthen the resilience of the production environment. AI-Enabled Operations & Automation Use enterprise-approved Claude, OpenAI/ChatGPT, and Microsoft Copilot capabilities to support event correlation, incident triage, ticket enrichment, knowledge retrieval, shift handoffs, runbook development, reporting, and operational documentation. Build and maintain reusable AI skills, agents, and workflow automations that reduce repetitive operational tasks, improve process consistency, and accelerate response and recovery activities. Leverage Model Context Protocol (MCP) and approved API integrations to connect AI assistants with monitoring, scheduling, ServiceNow, PagerDuty, CMDB, and approved knowledge sources while maintaining security, human review, and auditability. Capacity, Health & System Utilization Conduct recurring health checks and capacity reviews across mainframe and distributed production environments, enterprise schedulers, batch queues, storage, compute, and network dependencies. Analyze workload volumes, processing windows, queue depth, utilization, and trend data to identify bottlenecks, underutilized resources, and emerging capacity risk. Coordinate tuning, workload balancing, scheduling changes, right-sizing, or capacity escalation to improve system utilization, operational headroom, and service resilience. What You'll Bring Required Skills Bachelor's degree or equivalent combination of education and relevant technology operations experience. Experience supporting enterprise production control, technology operations, or mainframe operations within a 24x7 production environment. Working knowledge of production batch processing, enterprise scheduling, file transfer operations, production monitoring, and incident management processes. Experience with enterprise scheduling and operational tools such as ESP, Stonebranch Scheduler, ServiceNow, PagerDuty, or comparable technologies. Familiarity with IBM mainframe technologies, including JES2, TSO, JCL, HMC, IPL activities, and production job management. Strong analytical, troubleshooting, and problem-solving skills with the ability to effectively prioritize work in a fast-paced production environment. Excellent written and verbal communication skills with the ability to collaborate across technology teams, vendors, and business partners. Demonstrated commitment to operational excellence, process discipline, and continuous improvement. AI Skillsets of the Future - Required Today Demonstrated hands-on experience using enterprise-approved Claude, OpenAI/ChatGPT, and Microsoft Copilot in production operations or a comparable technology environment. Experience building AI skills, agents, prompt assets, or workflow automations that improve operational support, knowledge access, and process execution. Familiarity with MCP, REST APIs, enterprise integration patterns, access controls, data protection, testing, and human-in-the-loop review. Experience interpreting health, capacity, and utilization metrics and converting findings into practical optimization actions. Preferred Skills Experience supporting IDMS region migrations, ISPW production migrations, or other mainframe deployment technologies. Familiarity with Secure Transport, Rumba, PuTTY, Telnet, Topaz, Omegaview, NetView, SAVRS, SSM, or similar enterprise operations tools. Experience supporting technology operations within the insurance, financial services, or another highly regulated industry. Knowledge of business continuity, disaster recovery, and production recovery processes. Success Looks Like Production workloads consistently execute successfully while meeting established service level objectives. Production incidents are resolved efficiently through proactive monitoring, timely escalation, and effective collaboration with technology and business partners. Production scheduling, change management, and deployment activities are completed accurately with minimal operational risk. Operational reporting, documentation, and production metrics provide timely visibility into production health and support informed decision-making. Business continuity and operational readiness activities strengthen the resilience, stability, and reliability of New York Life's production environment. AI-enabled workflows reduce manual ticket handling, triage, shift handoff, reporting, and documentation while improving accuracy and response speed. Health and capacity assessments improve system utilization, processing headroom, and production stability.

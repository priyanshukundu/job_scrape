Job Description The Team: The Mechatronic Software team develops foundational embedded software that helps vehicle controllers communicate and work together across GM’s software-defined vehicle platforms. The team focuses on core software and networking capabilities—including LIN and CAN communications, Ethernet switching, and in-vehicle I/O—supporting systems such as central compute, aggregators, and extenders. Working closely with software, systems, and hardware partners, the team builds robust, reusable software foundations that enable reliable integration and evolving vehicle features. The Role: We are looking for a Software Developer/Engineer with a strong passion for developing the software tools and the engineering process to enable a global software organization. The job will require to develop in house tooling to generate GM defined basic infrastructure software for controller builds based on SVD (Software Defined Vehicles) architecture. The main responsibility will include development, integration and testing of the tool generated basic software on a comm stack for end-to-end readiness, and product line software release for a controller . What You'll Do (Responsibilities): Software developer and integrator of tooling to generate to basic infrastructure software for GM product line software tools (C and Java). Working in agile team with Continuous Integration/Continuous Integration methodology. Gathering and understanding the requirements to generate tooling adhering to serial data communication protocol based on AutoSAR Standards. Test , Debug and validate generated artifacts on the standard platforms. Adapting to the already existed code framework and build new capabilities to extend functionality and enhance performance. Active participation as an SME to identity, design, and implement workflow modifications with multiple business teams and application extensions. Whiteboard and brainstorming session with internal tools team members. Your Skills & Abilities (Required Qualifications): Bachelor’s degree in engineering: Computer Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Computer Science, or relevant discipline. 2+ years of professional experience in Automotive embedded software development. Knowledge of the AUTOSAR Standards and serial data communication protocols. Demonstrated proficiency with programming language: Java and C. People Skills : High level of interpersonal skills to work effectively with others. Good business sense, ability to communicate, and a desire to understand customer needs. Ability to thrive in a dynamic, high-change environment with an initiative demonstrated by reaching beyond assigned work to improve output. What Will Give You A Competitive Edge (Preferred Qualifications): Master’s degree in Engineering or Computer Science. 4+ years of experience in a related field. Understanding of Automotive Embedded Systems. Working experience with COMM Stack. Experience with Agile Product Development.

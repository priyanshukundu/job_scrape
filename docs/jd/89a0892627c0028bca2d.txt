Job Description Key Responsibilities Provide QC and analytical technical expertise for assigned established programs through product and analytical sub-teams, PDM teams, PQTs, and other cross-functional forums, and execute assigned QC deliverables, including testing, stability, regulatory submissions, and commercial lifecycle activities. Provide oversight and management of contract testing laboratory activities by coordinating GMP sample shipments and testing and apply QC analytical knowledge to evaluate testing issues, recommend solutions, and support resolution, escalating complex issues and decisions to the appropriate technical lead. Support contract laboratory method lifecycle management and influence external QC testing in alignment with Gilead's GMP policies, including onboarding, readiness, method transfer and validation, sample and data transfers, change management, closeout, and offboarding; provide QC and analytical input on outsourcing decisions and changes to laboratory scope, testing location, methods, specifications, equipment, or sample management, and support continuity and risk-mitigation plans. Coordinate day-to-day outsourced testing with contract laboratories, including scheduling, capacity and demand planning, deliverable tracking, governance meetings, and performance metrics, and promptly escalate risks to product supply or project timelines. Monitor contract laboratory performance and GMP compliance by reviewing quality events, investigation and CAPA status, testing trends, recurring issues, key performance indicators (KPIs), and service and quality metrics; partner with Quality Assurance and other stakeholders to drive follow-up actions. Collaborate with Quality Control, Quality Assurance, Manufacturing, Regulatory, Supply Chain, Procurement, and other technical functions to define laboratory requirements, clarify roles and responsibilities, support technical and quality agreement content, complete assigned deliverables, communicate progress and dependencies, and maintain communication with external laboratories. Provide analytical input to laboratory investigations, deviations, and change controls, working with Quality, technical functions, and laboratories to assess product and method impacts and support resolution, and provide input to assigned GMP documentation, including protocols, reports, SOPs, deviations, change controls, CAPAs, and closeout documents. Partner with Finance and Procurement to plan and track budgets for outsourced analytical activities and manage requisitions and purchase orders for contract laboratory services. Provide oversight of contract management for contract laboratories, working with Legal to support contract and agreement negotiation, review, execution, amendments, and renewals. Preferred Qualifications: Experience with Quality Control sub-functions and strong knowledge of industry best practices and trends. Experience with, or ability to quickly learn, US and international GMP principles, practices, and standards. Ability to apply quality principles and theories to develop and refine complex concepts, techniques, standards, processes, and applications, and devise creative solutions to complex problems. Sound judgment and the ability to act independently where no precedent exists, while knowing when to seek assistance or escalate. Proven ability to work with senior management to develop and secure support for Company-wide, long-term solutions. Experience coordinating outsourced analytical testing across Quality, technical, supply, procurement, and business functions, including managing laboratory deliverables, risks, and dependencies. Demonstrated ability to influence processes and outcomes across functions. Collaborative team player who adapts quickly and performs well in a fast-paced, dynamic environment. Knowledge and experience in quality control. Stay ahead of shifting trends in industry. Must think critically and creatively and be able to work independently to determine appropriate resources for resolution of problems. Strong organizational and planning skills. Shows excellent verbal and written communication skills and collaborative interpersonal skills. Basic Requirements: BS degree in Chemistry, Biochemistry or related field with 6+ years' experience OR MS degree with 4+ years' experience.

Neuroscience Data Scientist
Guidehouse
Location: US - MD, Bethesda
Link: https://guidehouse.wd1.myworkdayjobs.com/External/job/US---MD-Bethesda/Neuroscience-Data-Scientist_46996

Job Family : Scientist Travel Required : None Clearance Required : Ability to Obtain Public Trust What You Will Do: We are looking for a Neuroscience Data Scientist to support the National Institute on Aging (NIA) with research focused on Alzheimer’s disease and related dementias. The Neuroscience Data Scientist will support experimental research, data science and statistical analysis, manuscript development, and presentation of scientific findings. This is a full-time, telework-eligible position located in Bethesda, MD. Provide scientific and technical expertise, mentorship, and training to visiting fellows, postdoctoral researchers, technicians, and junior-level scientists, including training on data science analysis methods and techniques. Perform data science, bioinformatics, and statistical analyses using R and Python; collaborate with research scientists to analyze and interpret experimental results, oversee research data quality, and assist with data analysis monthly. Support laboratory staff in preparing scientific documents and presentations; write and edit technical reports, abstracts, manuscripts, posters, and papers for peer-reviewed publication. Plan, design, and conduct experiments and studies supporting Alzheimer’s disease and related dementia research; complete experiments monthly. Develop new research protocols monthly; support assay design, method development, validation of assay results, and evaluation of alternative experimental approaches. Evaluate techniques, instruments, and software for potential incorporation into laboratory procedures. Propose and investigate new research projects based on scientific novelty and relevance to human health, and review scientific literature to identify relevant techniques and approaches. Prepare and deliver PowerPoint presentations, posters, and scientific findings for internal meetings, national conferences, and scientific symposia; meet with laboratory members biweekly to present research updates. Maintain detailed and current electronic laboratory notebooks documenting experiments. Maintain, calibrate, and troubleshoot laboratory instruments and equipment and assist with training staff on proper equipment use. Manage scientific equipment and laboratory resources, including reagents, frozen cell stocks, and animal samples. Coordinate with IT staff regarding scientific software and computer-related issues. What You Will Need: Master’s degree in Biology, Neuroscience, or a closely related scientific field. Minimum of ONE (1) year of experience supporting scientific research, preferably involving Alzheimer’s disease, dementia, neuroscience, or related biomedical research. Experience with data science, data analysis, and statistical analysis, including use of R and Python for scientific or statistical analysis and bioinformatics analyses in a biomedical or neuroscience research environment. Experience writing scientific manuscripts, technical reports, abstracts, or other research publications, including preparing manuscripts for peer-reviewed scientific journals. Scientific communication experience, including conveying complex data through presentations, posters, and/or manuscripts and presenting research at regional or national scientific meetings. Proficiency with Microsoft PowerPoint, Excel, and Word. Ability to work effectively with multidisciplinary scientific staff, including postdoctoral researchers, biological scientists, technicians, and IT personnel. Ability to work safely under Biosafety Level 2 (BSL-2) practices with human blood or other body fluids and non-human primate tissues, and to use required personal protective equipment, including gloves and a laboratory coat. A background in developing or modifying scientific protocols and assays. Mentoring or training experience with junior scientists, fellows, postdoctoral researchers, or laboratory technicians. Research experience managing data quality and maintaining electronic laboratory notebooks. Troubleshooting experience with scientific instruments, research software, and laboratory equipment. Must be able to obtain and maintain a Federal or DoD “public trust”; candidates must receive approved adjudication prior to onboarding with Guidehouse. Candidates with an active public trust or suitability are preferred. What Would Be Nice To Have: Previous research experience in Alzheimer’s disease and related dementias.

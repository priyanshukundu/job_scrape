Business Analyst, Finance AI & Digital Solutions Position Summary The Business Analyst, Finance AI & Digital Solutions serves as a trusted partner connecting Finance stakeholders with data, analytics, AI, digital, and technology teams. This role develops a strong understanding of business processes, stakeholder needs, project objectives, and desired outcomes and translates that understanding into clear business, data, and technology requirements. The Business Analyst leads discovery to understand how the business operates, the decisions stakeholders need to make, the business rules that guide those decisions, and the data and capabilities required to achieve the desired outcome. Success in this role requires curiosity, strong business and data literacy, and the ability to build trusted relationships. The Business Analyst connects business knowledge, processes, data, and technology to help teams develop scalable and sustainable solutions. The role identifies business problems and processes where AI and agentic capabilities can create value and defines the business requirements and roadmap to advance opportunities. This role is critical to advancing a data- and AI-driven Finance organization by identifying business problems where data, automation, and AI can create value, translating those opportunities into clear requirements, and driving alignment from discovery through validation and adoption. Responsibilities Lead discovery through stakeholder interviews, workshops, process walkthroughs, and analysis to define business problems, current-state processes, desired outcomes, and success criteria. Understand stakeholder decisions, information needs, pain points, business rules, controls, exceptions, and dependencies. Apply process mapping, root-cause analysis, RACI, and similar methods to structure complex business problems and clarify processes, ownership, and requirements. Translate business needs into clear business, functional, data, and non-functional requirements, including user stories and measurable acceptance criteria. Define and document business rules, calculations, decision criteria, data requirements, controls, exceptions, and validation requirements. Identify business problems and processes that are strong candidates for analytics, automation, AI, or agentic capabilities based on business value, process requirements, data, risk, and level of human judgment. For agentic use cases, define agent boundaries, human-in-the-loop requirements, decision authority, escalation paths, exception handling, and stop conditions. Partner with business, Data Engineering, AI, and technology teams to identify authoritative data sources and translate business needs into actionable requirements. Maintain requirements traceability from the original business need through implementation, validation, and acceptance. Develop business scenarios and support user acceptance testing (UAT) to validate requirements and intended business outcomes. Facilitate alignment and decisions across business and technical stakeholders when requirements, processes, or business-rule interpretations differ. Capture business rules, processes, decisions, and project knowledge in structured, reusable formats that support future analytics, automation, and AI capabilities. Qualifications Bachelor’s degree in information systems, business, or a related field.

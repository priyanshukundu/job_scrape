Job Description Summary As a member of this world class team, the Lead Controls Engineer will lead and execute the protection and control aspects of Flexible AC (Alternating Current) Transmission System (FACTS) projects. In this role, you will be responsible for the design, testing, start-up and commissioning of the protection and control aspects of Fixed Series Capacitors(FSC) and Synchronous Condenser (SC) solutions within GE Vernova’s Flexible AC Transmission Systems(FACTS) portfolio. Job Description Position Overview: We are seeking a highly skilled and motivated Lead Controls Software Engineer to join our GSI FACTS Controls Software team in Saratoga Springs, NY. In this role, you will lead the design and implementation of advanced control systems for Synchronous Condensers, which provide inertia, steady state and dynamic support to the power system. The Lead Controls Engineer will bring expertise in power system controls, strong experience with simulation and real-time testing in both the lab and field, the ability to lead and mentor a team of engineers, and interface with customers and cross-functional stakeholders. Responsibilities: Lead the design, development, and implementation of control systems for FACTS devices, including power generation, transmission, and distribution. Participate in the testing and validation of control systems, ensuring compliance with industry standards and regulations. Collaborate with cross-functional teams, including electrical engineers, software developers, and project managers, to ensure the successful integration of control systems. Prepare technical documentation, including design specifications, test plans, and user manuals. Conduct system modeling, simulation, and analysis to optimize control strategies and improve system performance. Develop and maintain coding libraries for both system software and HMI. Provide technical guidance and mentorship to junior engineers and support their professional development. Troubleshoot and resolve complex technical issues related to control systems. Stay current with the latest advancements in control systems engineering and power systems technology. Support business development activities by providing technical expertise and contributing to proposal development. Participate in Lean training and lead Lean initiatives Lead problem solving efforts for field issues, using tools such as Root Cause Analysis or Problem Solving Reports. Required Qualifications: Bachelor’s degree in electrical engineering, control systems engineering, or a related field. Minimum of 4 years of experience in control systems engineering, with a focus on power transmission systems. Eligibility Requirements: Must be able to work in the FACTS office 4 days per week located in Saratoga Springs, NY. No fully remote options available for this role. Ability and willingness to travel 20% of the time, including international, and must comply with all relevant company travel and tax policies. Desired Characteristics: Strong knowledge of control theory, power system dynamics, and real-time control systems. Proficient with GE Vernova’s MarkVIe product family including Toolbox ST and Cimplicity software (SCADA) Familiar with GE Vernova Excitation systems, such as EX2100E Expert in PLC programming Experience with industrial/utility-grade protection relays and protective relaying coordination Working knowledge of grid interconnection requirements (NERC/FERC) Excellent problem-solving skills and the ability to think analytically and strategically. Strong communication and interpersonal skills, with the ability to work effectively in a team environment. Proven track record of successfully leading and managing engineering projects. Ability to adapt to changing priorities and work in a fast-paced, dynamic environment.

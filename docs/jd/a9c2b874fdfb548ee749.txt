Frontier AI Research Engineer The Opportunity: Frontier Research operates at the edge of what’s possible to convert breakthrough capabilities into real-world impacts. Frontier Research functions as both a strategic radar and an execution engine, turning frontier AI capabilities into differentiated IP, new product directions, and transformational business opportunities. The Lead Engineer is a senior technical builder who drives the architectural rigor and engineering execution behind Frontier Research prototypes and integrations. This role is responsible for designing, building, and validating advanced AI systems across the full stack. The Lead Engineer translates frontier concepts into working systems, ensures technical excellence across prototypes, and serves as the lab’s senior-most hands-on engineering authority. If you are passionate about building distributed AI systems, architecting agentic applications, and transforming cutting-edge research into production-grade capabilities, this role is for you. What You’ll Work On: Evaluate emerging advancements across hyper scalers, leading AI labs, academia, and venture-backed companies. Conduct hands-on technical assessments, benchmarks, and proof-of-concepts to validate applicability and performance. Own the technical delivery of end-to-end AI systems spanning infrastructure, frontier LLMs, agentic and multi-agent applications and physical AI pipelines. Deliver working systems not slideware. Work with business units, to support hands-on integration of emerging AI technologies in complex customer environments such as cloud, hybrid, edge, or disconnected. Ensure production rigor without sacrificing innovation speed. Provide day-to-day technical leadership to engineers and researchers. Conduct design reviews, guide architectural decisions, and raise the overall technical bar of the Applied Research team. Convert prototype learnings into reusable frameworks, reference architectures, accelerators, and technical artifacts that scale across programs and business units. Join us. The world can’t wait. You Have: Experience in deep technical engineering Experience designing and building advanced AI systems, including managing them Experience developing AI prototypes, products, or high‑visibility technical demonstrations Experience leading technical exchanges and evaluations with commercial partners and executive stakeholders Experience with translating research into scalable, revenue‑generating solutions Experience guiding R&D efforts from concept through implementation Ability to create and drive technical roadmaps aligned with organizational goals Ability to display high creativity, intellectual curiosity, and enthusiasm for showcasing innovative AI capabilities Bachelor’s degree Nice If You Have: Experience scaling AI systems from prototype to enterprise-grade product environments Experience in a leadership role of multidisciplinary engineering or research teams delivering high‑impact technical outcomes Experience building or advising AI‑driven businesses, including monetization strategies and go‑to‑market planning Experience with driving innovation programs, incubators, or R&D portfolios Experience with cloud-native AI infrastructure, MLOps, or large-scale model deployment Experience as a thought leader in AI, including speaking at technical keynotes, conferences, or public forums Ability to engage and influence senior executives, commercial partners, and external stakeholders Ability to display a record of published research, patents, or conference presentations in AI, ML, or emerging technologies Ability to maintain a strong network within the AI research or engineering community to support recruiting and partnership development Master's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, AI, or a related technical field preferred; Doctorate degree in Computer Science, Engineering, AI, or a related technical field a plus Compensation At Booz Allen, we celebrate your contributions, provide you with opportunities and choices, and support your total well-being.

Summary of the Job: The Senior Developer, Technology is responsible for collaborating with an agile team to deliver secure, high-quality solutions that create value for our customers and the business. This role partners with product ownership and the business to define tasks and provide estimates for User Stories. The Senior Developer brings strong expertise in modern micro front-end development with React.js, and is eager to grow into back-end development with Java Spring Boot and AWS-native services. Developers are responsible for attending and participating in all Scrum ceremonies. About This Role: As a Senior Developer, you will be a hands-on, full-stack contributor with a primary focus on building modern, modular user experiences using React.js and micro front-end architecture. You will work alongside Lead Developers and Enterprise Architecture, mentor developers earlier in their careers, and if needed expand your skills in back-end development using Java Spring Boot and AWS-native services. We are looking for someone who is curious, collaborative, and excited to broaden their technical range across the full stack.

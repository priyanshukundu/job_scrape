As an Associate Retirement Consultant, you will play a hybrid role supporting both Operations and Consulting functions within our Retirement Services line of business. Reporting to one of the Managing Consultants on the Retirement Services Operations team, you will support 4 to 5 Consultants in delivering a variety of services to their clients. In this role, you will have the opportunity to attend client meetings and contribute to a range of activities, including meeting note-taking, monthly and quarterly reporting, due diligence research, benchmarking, and preparing requests for proposals (RFPs). You may also present data and respond directly to client inquiries, as needed. This position will provide a strong foundation toward understanding all aspects of the retirement business, readying you to grow within the business and Marsh Agency. Our future colleague.

This role is four days onsite at our Seneca One Buffalo, NY location, with the flexibility to work from home one day per week Position Summary: We are seeking a highly skilled Lead Software Engineer to help drive the development of a new Tech 360 Platform. Tech 360 is an enterprise technology platform that provides CIOs and technology leaders with a single, trusted view of performance, risk, cost, delivery, talent, and business value across the organization. It enables data-driven decision making by consolidating fragmented reporting into a unified platform that improves transparency, accountability, and strategic execution. This role will provide technical leadership in designing, developing and delivering scalable solutions for technology reporting.

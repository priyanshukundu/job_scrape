Citibank, N.A. seeks a Model Validation 2nd LOD Sr. Analyst for its Tampa, Florida location. Duties: Conduct independent validation of quantitative risk models used in large financial institutions for regulatory and risk management purposes. Review model methodology, assumptions, data inputs, implementation, and performance monitoring to ensure accuracy, robustness, and compliance with applicable regulatory requirements for models used in credit risk, liquidity risk, operational risk, interest rate risk, and structured finance. Perform quantitative testing, sensitivity analysis, back testing, benchmarking, and stress scenario analysis to assess model performance under different economic conditions. Evaluate data quality, data transformations, and programming implementation to ensure consistency between model documentation and system execution. Prepare comprehensive technical validation reports that clearly document findings, limitations, and recommendations. Collaborate with model developers, risk management teams, technology teams, and business stakeholders to resolve validation findings and improve model controls. Review model changes, assessing model limitations, and ensuring that model governance standards are properly followed throughout the model life cycle. Require independent oversight responsibilities, advanced statistical expertise, and the ability to critically challenge complex quantitative models used for regulatory capital and enterprise risk management at large financial institutions. Responsible for model risk management, which primarily involves overseeing the development, implementation, validation, governance, and monitoring of models used for various purpose, such as credit risk, operational risk, and liquidity risk. Generate statistical models to improve methods of obtaining and evaluating quantitative and qualitative data and identify relationships and trends in data and factors affecting research results. Validate assumptions, escalate identified risks, and sensitive areas in methodology and process. Automate data extraction and data preprocessing tasks, perform ad hoc data analyses, design and maintain complex data manipulation processes, and provide documentation and presentations. A telecommuting/hybrid work schedule may be permitted within a commutable distance from the worksite, in accordance with Citi policies and protocols. Requirements: Requires a Master’s degree, or foreign equivalent in Statistics, Physics, Mathematics, Financial Engineering, Quantitative Finance, Engineering or related field and 2 years of experience as a Model Validator, Model/Analysis Validation Officer or related position extracting, transforming and analyzing large datasets.. Alternatively, employer will accept a Bachelor’s degree or foreign equivalent in the stated fields and 5 years of progressively responsible, post-baccalaureate experience as stated above. 2 years of experience must include: Apply exploratory data analysis, statistical and machine learning modeling techniques, sensitivity analysis, stress testing, benchmarking, and back testing to independently identify, analyze, and measure the risk within financial models. Perform quantitative testing and replicate model outputs to assess accuracy and implementation integrity using SQL and statistical software - R, Python, and MATLAB; Evaluate models used for Credit Risk, Market Risk, Liquidity Risk, Operational Risk, Securitization, Pension Risk, IRRBB and capital adequacy in accordance with regulatory guidance - SR 11-7, Basel frameworks, SR15-18 and Regulation YY. Prepare detailed validation reports documenting methodology review, quantitative findings, model limitations, and remediation recommendations for senior risk management review; and Communicate with stakeholders regarding validation findings and results.

TransUnion's Job Applicant Privacy Notice Personal Information We Collect Your Privacy Choices Team Overview This role reports directly to Senior Manager, Data Science & Analytics within the AI R&D organization. This team is responsible for evaluating, developing and advancing next-generation AI capabilities that can create strategic advantage for TransUnion and its customers by engaging with TransUnion’s internal clients across a broad array of credit, fraud, insurance and marketing business applications. You’ll join a team that encourages curiosity, innovation, and scientific exploration with access to large-scale consumer, marketing, fraud, and risk datasets for applied AI research and opportunities to shape the future direction of AI-powered products and solutions across TransUnion's businesses. The team has access to Modern cloud-based computing environments and state-of-the-art AI infrastructure for experimentation and rapid prototyping. This is a hybrid position and involves regular performance of job responsibilities virtually as well as in-person at an assigned TU office location for a minimum of two days a week. Role Overview and Core Responsibilities Design and develop AI-powered automation solutions for enterprise workflows and business processes. Design knowledge management, semantic search, and retrieval systems. Drive innovation in areas such as: neuro-symbolic AI, reasoning systems, knowledge-enhanced machine learning, agentic AI, causal AI, explainable AI, human-AI collaboration. Design, build, and evaluate proof-of-concepts that demonstrate the value of emerging AI technologies within TransUnion's products and services. Create repeatable frameworks for assessing AI capabilities, limitations, risks, and business impact. Collaborate with engineering teams to transition successful research concepts into production-ready solutions. Represent TransUnion through publications and patents.

We're looking for a Principal Financial Analyst, FP&A to lead day-to-day financial modeling and portfolio analysis for our FP&A team, reporting to the Senior Director, FP&A. The impact you'll make: As a Principal Financial Analyst for FP&A, you'll build forecasts and provide the analysis that shows credit, growth, and strategy leaders how the business is performing. You'll improve the portfolio forecasting methods already in place and work on the monthly allowance for credit losses estimate, so the numbers leaders plan with and the numbers accounting records tell one consistent story about expected losses. You'll spend your first few months learning how our financial model works and building relationships across credit, growth, accounting, and capital markets. By month six, you'll contribute to the quarterly forecast and the monthly reserve calculation. By your first year, you'll manage the quarterly forecast process and run the model for baseline and stress scenarios on your own. As Principal Financial Analyst, FP&A you will: Build annual operating budgets with business leaders and run quarterly rolling forecasts that meet internal audit standards Create and maintain driver-based financial models in Workday Adaptive and Excel, and develop new techniques that improve forecast accuracy and automate repetitive work Run deep-dive variance analyses on portfolio data to find root causes and recommend ways to improve profitability Work with credit, growth, and strategy leaders to show the financial impact of their decisions, using ROI, NPV, and payback analysis Run the monthly allowance for credit losses calculation, the estimate of expected portfolio losses Turn financial results into clear narratives and presentations for the CFO, executive leaders, and the board, and support the monthly financial review packages You'll thrive in this role if: You roll up your sleeves and like building analyses and reports from the ground up You're curious about what portfolio data says about performance and profitability You balance big-picture priorities with hands-on execution Minimum qualifications: Bachelor's degree in Finance, Economics, Computer Science, Information Systems, or a related quantitative field 3+ years of experience in FP&A, strategic analytics, business analytics, or investment banking. Prior experience in the credit card or financial services industry Familiarity with and experience modeling GAAP financial statements Ability to use data as an investigative tool to drive efficiencies and analyze performance variance Project management skills to plan, coordinate, and run complex financial projects across multiple priorities Communication and interpersonal skills, with a record of building relationships with both technical and non-technical teams Ability to travel 2-3 times per year for high quality in person collaboration Preferred qualifications: Experience with EPM tools such as Workday Adaptive, OneStream, Oracle, or SAP Proficiency in Excel, SQL, or Python, with a mindset for automation and continuous improvement Working knowledge of how credit loss reserves are estimated Compensation: Annual full-time starting base salary range: $118,000-$135,000 This role is eligible for additional compensation in the forms of participation in our annual incentive and equity programs. About Mission Lane: Founded in December 2018, Mission Lane is a purpose-driven fintech company based in the U.S., with headquarters in Richmond, Virginia. It all started with a realization: nearly fifty percent of the adult population in the U.S. doesn’t have access to a clear line of credit. Most traditional credit card companies either overlook or overcharge this group because they have less-than-perfect credit scores or no scores at all. We decided this just wouldn’t do. In partnership with our sponsor banks, we offer credit cards under the Mission Lane brand name, with better, clearer terms, and a more refined customer experience than the alternatives available to people working hard to improve their credit. To date, over four million consumers have chosen Mission Lane, earning high customer ratings on Credit Karma for its market segment and industry leading Net Promoter scores. Mission Lane has cumulatively raised over $600 million of equity from leading investors, including Invus Opportunities, QED Investors, LL Funds, funds affiliated with Oaktree Capital Management, and other leading investors.

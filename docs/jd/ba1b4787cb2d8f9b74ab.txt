Location: 127 Public Square, Cleveland Ohio AI Strategy Leader, Real Estate Capital About the Role KeyBank Real Estate Capital (REC) is seeking an AI Strategy Leader to lead the development and delivery of AI-enabled capabilities across the business. This role combines strategy, hands-on solution development, and delivery leadership. The AI Strategy Leader will work directly with REC leaders, frontline teams, and subject matter experts to identify high-value opportunities, determine where AI can meaningfully improve how work gets done, and turn those opportunities into practical solutions. This is a hands-on leadership role. The successful candidate will be expected to personally prototype, test, and validate AI solutions using tools such as Microsoft Copilot Studio, Power Automate, and Google Gemini, while also leading successful solutions through implementation, adoption, and scale with technology and other enterprise partners. The role owns REC’s AI opportunity portfolio, with success measured by delivered capabilities, adoption, and measurable improvements in productivity, capacity, speed, quality, and user experience. Key Responsibilities AI Strategy & Opportunity Development Develop and maintain a prioritized portfolio of REC AI opportunities aligned with business strategy and enterprise technology direction. Work directly with business leaders and frontline subject matter experts to understand workflows, pain points, and opportunities for improvement. Identify where AI, automation, process redesign, or existing technology can reduce manual effort, improve turnaround time, increase capacity, or improve quality and consistency. Evaluate and prioritize opportunities based on business value, feasibility, scalability, risk, and time to value. Maintain awareness of enterprise AI capabilities and strategic platforms and make informed build, integrate, wait, or stop recommendations. Hands-On AI Development & Validation Personally build and refine AI prototypes and early-stage solutions using Microsoft Copilot Studio, Power Automate, Google Gemini, and other approved enterprise tools. Develop prompts, agents, workflows, business rules, and structured outputs that translate business requirements into working AI capabilities. Test solutions against representative business scenarios, including exceptions, missing information, conflicting evidence, and other edge cases. Evaluate outputs for accuracy, consistency, grounding, and appropriate handling of uncertainty, and establish clear validation and acceptance criteria. Determine where AI reasoning is appropriate versus deterministic processing, traditional automation, or human review. Use testing results to determine whether solutions should advance, be redesigned, or be discontinued. Delivery & Business Outcomes Own prioritized AI initiatives from validated concept through implementation, adoption, and measurable value realization. Establish clear deliverables, owners, milestones, dependencies, success measures, and target dates. Drive incremental delivery, favoring usable releases and rapid learning over unnecessarily large or complex solutions. Coordinate the business, technology, data, risk, and other partners needed to transition successful solutions into scalable, supportable production capabilities. Identify and resolve or escalate barriers that threaten delivery. Track and communicate portfolio progress, risks, adoption, and realized business outcomes, including improvements in manual effort, turnaround time, capacity, quality, rework, cost, employee experience, and client experience. Governance, Scale & Leadership Ensure AI solutions appropriately address information security, privacy, regulatory, model risk, data, and responsible AI requirements. Partner early with Technology, Architecture, Data, Risk, Compliance, Information Security, and enterprise AI teams to establish viable paths from prototype to production. Define appropriate human review, validation, monitoring, and controls based on each solution’s business use and risk. Develop reusable AI patterns, components, and delivery approaches that accelerate future solutions across REC. Monitor emerging AI capabilities and commercial real estate applications and translate relevant developments into practical opportunities for REC. Provide direction and mentorship to AI process analysts and other resources supporting discovery, development, testing, and implementation. Required Qualifications Bachelor’s degree or equivalent combination of education and relevant experience. Demonstrated experience leading AI, technology-enabled transformation, product management, process improvement, or comparable initiatives from business problem through implementation and adoption. Hands-on experience developing AI solutions or prototypes using Microsoft Copilot Studio, Google Gemini, or comparable enterprise AI platforms. Experience designing prompts, agents, workflows, automation, structured outputs, and AI validation approaches. Ability to understand complex business processes and translate them into practical technology-enabled solutions. Strong understanding of the appropriate roles of generative AI, deterministic automation, data, and human judgment. Strong analytical, prioritization, problem-solving, and business-outcome measurement skills. Strong written, verbal, and executive communication skills, with demonstrated ability to influence and drive delivery across business and technical teams without direct authority. Preferred Qualifications Experience in commercial real estate lending, underwriting, portfolio management, servicing, or commercial banking. Experience delivering AI or automation solutions in a regulated financial-services environment. Experience with Microsoft Power Automate, intelligent document processing, knowledge retrieval, APIs, data integration, or agentic workflows. Working knowledge of data structures, SQL, Python, or comparable technical capabilities. Experience developing business cases and measuring productivity, capacity, cycle-time, quality, or financial outcomes.

Agentic Coding Enablement (ACE), part of the Process Intelligence practice in Performance Consulting (PC), helps consulting teams across PC work faster and sharper by applying agentic coding tools (AI tools such as Claude Code or GitHub Copilot) to real engagement problems. As a Senior Process Improvement Associate on ACE, you lead the hands-on work on engagements and help the rest of PC learn to do it too. You are an excellent problem-solver and a credible consultant who is learning and applying agentic coding to make the work better: understanding how a process really works, testing ideas quickly by turning them into working prototypes, and communicating what you find so clients can act on it. You decide where a quick build will sharpen the work, and where it will not. People come to this work from many backgrounds: process improvement, data science, information systems, analytics, engineering, consulting, and more. What we ask for most is how you solve problems, and that you are learning and applying agentic coding tools to do it. The work centers on fit-for-purpose builds, such as working prototypes, discovery tools, and internal tools, that help us learn and help the business understand its own requirements and needs sooner, not on building and maintaining production systems or handing off finished solutions. You will also help colleagues across PC build their own skills, through direct partnership and by sharing what works. If you have taught yourself to solve real problems with AI tools, at work or on your own time, and you can showcase what you made and what you learned, we encourage you to apply.

About the Role Happen Bank is looking for a Lead Software Engineer to help design and build the tools and systems that make operations work faster, more reliable, and easier to scale, with deep backend ownership and enough front-end fluency to work across the stack when needed. In this role, you'll partner closely with Operations and Underwriting teams to understand how their work happens, then use that knowledge to design and deliver systems that simplify workflows and improve efficiency. You'll have real influence over the technical direction of the systems these teams rely on every day.

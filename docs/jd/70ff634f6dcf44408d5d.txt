We are seeking an Operations Research Analyst to analyze complex operational data and develop actionable insights that support customer decision-making. This position combines business intelligence and data analytics with elements of data science, operations research, and business analysis for a Department of Homeland Security training organization. The successful candidate will analyze complex training scheduling and resource-allocation challenges involving facilities, instructors, housing, students, and other constrained resources. The role will focus on identifying root causes, evaluating alternatives, developing analytical prototypes and decision-support capabilities, and translating complex data into practical business recommendations. Primary Responsibilities: Perform exploratory, quantitative, and root-cause analysis using advanced SQL, scripting, data visualization, and analytical techniques to investigate complex operational questions. Analyze scheduling, resource-capacity, business-rule, and constraint interactions; develop and test hypotheses; and distinguish underlying causes from symptoms, correlations, or data-quality issues. Develop analytical models, queries, prototypes, visualizations, dashboards, and decision-support tools that enable users to evaluate scenarios, alternatives, tradeoffs, and operational impacts. Apply techniques such as optimization, constraint analysis, simulation, sensitivity analysis, and resource-capacity modeling as appropriate. Translate analytical findings into practical recommendations for technical and non-technical stakeholders and document data sources, assumptions, methodologies, validation activities, and results. Collaborate with software developers, analysts, functional subject matter experts, and customer stakeholders to translate analytical findings into operational or system improvements. Basic Qualifications: U.S. Citizen capable of obtaining Public Trust clearance. Bachelor's degree from verifiable accredited institution in Data Science, Statistics, Operations Research, Computer Science, Mathematics, Industrial or Systems Engineering, Economics , or a related quantitative discipline, with 6+ years professional experience. Advanced SQL expertise and demonstrated ability to independently investigate complex relational data models, construct sophisticated analytical queries, validate results, and analyze large datasets. Strong scripting and analytical skills using Python, R, or comparable technologies , including experience rapidly prototyping analytical solutions. Demonstrated experience solving ambiguous, data-intensive problems involving multiple interacting constraints, dependencies, resources, or business rules. Experience with scheduling, optimization, operations research, simulation, constraint analysis, or resource-capacity planning and related quantitative techniques. Strong analytical judgment, data-validation skills, and the ability to translate complex quantitative findings into understandable business recommendations. Strong written and verbal communication skills and demonstrated ability to work with technical teams, subject matter experts, and customer stakeholders. Preferred Qualifications: Experience with enterprise business systems, business intelligence and visualization platforms, complex scheduling or resource-allocation environments, Agile software development, and Government or other large operational organizations is preferred.

Back to search results Position Summary We are seeking a hands-on Software Engineer to help build and deliver new capabilities for Data Collection, platform, across tax, immigration, and other service lines. Working within the technical direction set by the team's principal engineers and architects, the role turns requirements and designs into maintainable, well-tested software, builds prototypes to validate ideas, and partners with product and engineering to deliver features that span the platform. Work you’ll do As a Manager, Software Engineer focused on Data Collection, you will support how client information is gathered and validated for tax, immigration, and related services. AboutMe+ is a configurable, questionnaire-driven platform built on Angular and .NET running on AWS, with a single JSON-based Individual Data Store (IDS) as the source of truth and rules-driven conditional content across service types. The team works across product, architecture, engineering, and business groups to evolve a platform used by clients and practitioners worldwide. Build and deliver new capabilities across the Angular front end and .NET APIs, implementing designs into production-quality, maintainable code. Build working prototypes and proofs of concept to test ideas, assess feasibility, and inform implementation decisions. Implement and consume APIs and integrations for workflows that span multiple systems and service lines, following established contracts and data patterns. Build and evaluate AI-assisted features and engineering workflows, with testing, human review, privacy safeguards, and governance. Write and review code, and uphold standards for automated testing, maintainability, and release quality. Extend configuration-driven content and rules-based workflows (conditional logic, helper keys, answer-key handling) to make data collection more reusable and adaptable. Work with product and business stakeholders to clarify requirements, assess technical feasibility, and raise dependencies early. Contribute to design reviews, documentation, and knowledge sharing, and help mentor less-experienced engineers. A successful candidate would possess these skills: Ability to work independently and collaborate as part of a team Effective written and verbal communication skills Meticulous attention to detail and quality of work product Ability to build and sustain professional relationships Ability to lead projects or workstreams Ability to manage and prioritize multiple tasks in a fast-paced and dynamic environment Strong interpersonal skills and professional demeanor Ability to meet deadlines Ability to mentor and provide clear guidance to others The team At Deloitte Tax LLP, our Global Employer Services (GES) Technology Group consultants help multinational clients develop programs, processes and digital offerings to manage a global workforce and the compliance obligations arising from global mobility, business travel and remote working. People within our technology group come from a diverse background — they partner with our go-to-market teams and global clients to solve challenging problems and design experiences and products that anticipate what our Deloitte clients will want and solutions that keep them compliant with global and local regulations.

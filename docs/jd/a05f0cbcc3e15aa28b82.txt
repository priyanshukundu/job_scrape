Job Title Software Development Engineering, Advisor About your role: As a Software Development Engineering, Advisor, you will join a vibrant team focused on creating innovative solutions that streamline financial transactions and ensure security. This role will involve designing, developing, and maintaining applications that meet the dynamic needs of our clients. At Fiserv, we are dedicated to transforming financial services technology to benefit our clients. You will lead the design, development, and implementation of software applications to support Fiserv's business initiatives and work closely with cross-functional teams to gather requirements and translate them into technical specifications. In addition, you will perform coding, testing, and debugging of applications to ensure optimal performance and reliability along with mentor junior developers and provide technical guidance to ensure best practices. What you’ll do: Lead end-to-end design, development, and maintenance of mainframe applications (e.g., COBOL, JCL, DB2, CICS, VSAM). Guide modernization efforts, including integration with distributed systems or cloud platforms. Collaborate with enterprise architects to design scalable, secure, and maintainable solutions. Oversee project timelines, resource allocation, and deliverables for mainframe-related initiatives. Coordinate with cross-functional teams (QA, DevOps, Business Analysts) to ensure smooth delivery. Ensure system reliability, performance, and uptime through proactive monitoring and tuning. Evaluate legacy systems and propose enhancements or replacements aligned with business goals. Lead incident management and root cause analysis for production issues and provide on call support. Implement security best practices for data handling, access control, and audit logging. Ensure adherence to regulatory requirements (e.g., SOX, HIPAA) and internal policies. Implement security best practices for data handling, access control, and audit logging. Experience you’ll need to have: 4-7 years of experience in application development. 5+ years of experience in COBOL, JCL, DB2, CICS & VSAM. 5+ years of experience maintaining high volume batch workloads and supporting OLTP. 3+ years of payments experiences. Bachelor's degree in computer science, Information Technology, Information Systems, or a related field (or foreign equivalent degree) or equivalent work experience. Experience that would be great to have: Experience in the financial services industry. Important information about this role: This role is on-site Monday through Friday.

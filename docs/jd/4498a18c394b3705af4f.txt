Non-Financial Risk & Controls Engineering Product Management Non-Financial Risk & Controls Engineering is responsible for providing the Chief Risk Officer, Chief Compliance Officer, senior management, regulators, and other stakeholders with high quality non-financial risk information required to keep the firm’s activities within the defined risk appetite and comply with regulatory requirements. Our user base spans thousands of users globally. We partner with Risk, Compliance and other leaders across divisions, to fully understand the products, business strategies, and regulatory expectations. This knowledge enables us to build long-lasting software solutions, and to innovate with a purpose. The Non-Financial Risk & Controls Engineering Product Management team is responsible for guiding products through all stages of their lifecycle, including research, design, scoping, delivery, promotion and post-launch monitoring. As a member of our team, you will partner globally across multiple divisions and engineering teams to create end-to-end solutions. You will be able to innovate and incubate new ideas, and you will learn and share knowledge across the organization. You will have an opportunity to work on a broad range of projects. Your Role As an Associate in the Legal Product team within Non-Financial Risk & Controls Engineering Product Management, you will support product management and project execution across multiple products within the Legal domain. You will work with product managers, Legal stakeholders, Engineering team members, and business users to deliver technology-enabled solutions that improve the way risk and compliance information is captured, assessed, monitored, and used across the firm. This role will support the full product lifecycle, including requirements gathering, user research, functional design, delivery coordination, testing, launch readiness, issue resolution, and post-launch monitoring. The role requires strong organization, analytical thinking, communication skills, and interest in building scalable technology solutions for risk management. Job Responsibilities As an Associate, you will: Drive requirements through release activities to deliver product enhancements. Partner with product managers, business stakeholders, and engineering teams to understand business problems and translate them into clear functional requirements. Support current-state assessments, user research, requirements gathering, process mapping, and documentation of business needs. Help rationalize user requirements across products, processes, and stakeholder groups to support scalable and consistent solutions. Draft user stories, functional requirements, acceptance criteria, test scenarios, release notes, user guides, and training materials. Support backlog management, issue triage, prioritization discussions, and delivery planning. Coordinate day-to-day project activities, including status updates, timelines, dependencies, action items, and delivery risks. Participate in functional design discussions and help challenge requirements to ensure solutions are practical, usable, and aligned to product strategy. Partner with Engineering teams to clarify requirements, support sprint execution, and help resolve blockers. Support user acceptance testing, defect validation, business sign-off, and production readiness activities. Interact with business users to resolve product questions, application issues, and enhancement requests. Monitor product adoption and user feedback after launch to identify opportunities for improvement. Qualifications Undergraduate degree in Business, Finance, Technology, Risk Management, or a related field. 4+ years of experience in product management, project management, risk management, or transformation. Strong understanding of project and product management basics, including requirements gathering, testing, delivery planning, and go-live preparation. Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to break down complex processes and identify practical solutions. Strong written and verbal communication skills. Ability to simplify technical or process-heavy content for non-technical stakeholders. Strong organizational skills and ability to manage multiple assignments concurrently. Ability to work effectively with business users, product managers, engineers, and functional stakeholders. Comfortable operating in a dynamic environment with changing priorities. Preferred Skills Experience writing user stories, functional requirements, test scripts, release notes, or product documentation. Experience supporting UAT, defect tracking, issue triage, or implementation readiness. Experience working with engineering teams or agile delivery models. Interest in AI, automation, data integration, and workflow simplification.

About The Team The CoverMyMeds (CMM) FP&A organization serves as the central hub for financial planning, performance management, and strategic decision support across the business. The Sr. Financial Analyst, Consolidations FP&A will play a critical role in bringing together financial results, forecasts, plans, risks, opportunities, and business insights into a clear, executive-ready financial story that enables informed decision-making and drives business performance. What You'll Do Lead key components of the consolidation of financial results, forecasts, annual plans, risks and opportunities, and multi-year outlooks. Proactively coordinate Consolidations deliverables across Forecast, Plan, R&O, and executive reporting processes, driving timelines, dependencies, accountability, and follow-through across FP&A teams. Ensure accuracy, consistency, and financial integrity across consolidated reporting, including validation of key financial outputs and alignment across reporting materials. Partner with FP&A leaders to align key assumptions, financial narratives, risks and opportunities, and recommendations across the organization. Develop executive-level financial reporting, presentations, and insights for the CFO, Finance leadership, business leaders, and Corporate FP&A. Translate consolidated financial performance and outlook into a clear executive narrative, highlighting key drivers, risks, opportunities, and decisions required. Drive automation, standardization, and process improvements that enhance reporting quality, strengthen controls, and increase analytical capacity Skills You’ll Need Bachelor's degree in Finance, Accounting, Economics, or a related field. 5+ years of progressive experience in Finance, FP&A, Accounting, or a related discipline. Strong knowledge of P&L, balance sheet, cash flow, and financial reporting principles. Experience in executive-level financial storytelling, reporting, and presentation development. Exceptional analytical, problem-solving, and business partnering skills. Ability to influence senior stakeholders and lead through cross-functional collaboration. Strong attention to detail while maintaining a strategic, enterprise-wide perspective. Demonstrated ability to proactively manage complex, cross-functional financial processes with multiple stakeholders, dependencies, and deadlines. Preferred Skills MBA, CPA, CMA, or other relevant advanced degree or professional certification. Experience within healthcare, healthcare technology, pharmaceutical services, or other complex, matrixed organizations. Columbus Ohio _ Highly Preferred Proven track record improving financial planning and reporting processes through automation and standardization.

Job Title: Data Services Software Engineer Location: TX - Dallas/Irving What you'll do: Come join the AmeriHome's team, a Western Alliance Bank company, as a Data Services Software Engineer of the Data Services team. We’re building a cutting-edge platform designed to streamline and enhance business workflows in the mortgage lending space. As part of a dynamic and growing team, you'll help shape the software that drives our evolving business needs. As a Data Services Software Engineer, you'll work in a mid-level role with proficiency in one or more key products and who is responsible for managing and supporting the organization's IT infrastructure or applications. You'll identify development and support needs as well as taking on design responsibilities supporting project tasks. As an IT Staff Engineer 1, you'll facilitate dialogue and activities, while also ensuring team collaboration. Participate in the architectural design, development, and implementation of robust and scalable software applications using .NET and C#. Provide application support for AmeriHome Technology platforms, including production issue triage, root-cause analysis, release validation, and resolution coordination. Design transaction specific application performance monitoring metrics that can be captured and passed to business partners. In Addition, will layout reference architecture and visual diagrams that capture work. Support production releases, vulnerability remediation, modernization efforts, Azure Key Vault migration, and application configuration improvements. Partner with development, DevOps, infrastructure, networking, database, security, and business teams to resolve cross-platform issues and improve application stability. Ensure compliance with IT policies, procedures, and industry standards, including reviewing and refining IT control enhancements. Collaborate closely with product managers, architects, and other stakeholders to translate business requirements into technical solutions. Conduct code reviews, provide constructive feedback, and ensure adherence to coding standards. Contribute to the continuous improvement of our software development processes and tools. Leverage GitHub Copilot and other generative AI tools throughout the software development lifecycle to accelerate coding, debugging, refactoring, testing, documentation, and code reviews. What you'll need: 5+ years of experience in designing and building highly available and scalable applications with C# in .NET, ASP.NET, ASP.NET Core or MVC. Bachelor's degree in related field required. Intermediate to advanced knowledge of general Financial Services or Banking preferred. Advanced knowledge of applicable regulatory and legal compliance obligations, rules and regulations, industry standards and practices. Familiarity with cloud platforms (e.g., Azure, and AWS). Advanced proven experience in leading cross-functional teams and managing multiple projects simultaneously. Additional experience with pipeline development and leveraging Azure DevOps while working on multiple projects simultaneously. Intermediate to advanced ability to see the big picture and align projects with organizational goals. Capable of leading and motivating cross-functional teams. Expertise in resolving conflicts and addressing challenges as well as skilled at identifying and mitigating risks at the project level. Proficient in governance patterns tied to intake, technical reviews and architectural compliance. Demonstrated ability to use AI assistants effectively for code generation, troubleshooting, refactoring, unit testing, technical documentation, and code review. Advanced speaking and writing communication skills. May require up to 10% travel. Benefits you’ll love: We offer all the important things you'd want — like competitive salaries, an ownership stake in the company, medical and dental insurance, time off, a great 401k matching program, tuition assistance program, an employee volunteer program, and a wellness program. In addition, you’ll have the opportunity to bolster your business knowledge, learning the ins and outs of how successful companies operate and manage their finances, giving you invaluable hands-on experience to help grow your career!

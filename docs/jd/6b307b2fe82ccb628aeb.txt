About This Role As Associate Director, Study Start-Up Oversight Lead, you will lead Study Start-Up and site activation across assigned global clinical studies. You will line manage and coach Senior Manager Study Start-Up Oversight Leads while directly leading studies of your own. Your goal is to deliver timely, compliant and consistent site activation across your studies and those led by your team. You will also look across the function to spot risks, capability gaps and inefficiencies, and lead improvements to SSU processes, tools and ways of working. The value you bring is faster, more predictable study activation and a stronger, more standardized Study Start-Up organization. You will partner closely with Clinical Trial Leads, Regional SSU Leads and SSU Specialists, and act as the key Study Start-Up point of contact for study teams. What You’ll Do Line manage, coach and develop Senior Manager Study Start-Up Oversight Leads Lead and oversee Study Start-Up and site activation across assigned global studies Build and maintain critical paths and planned activation dates (PADs) Oversee ICF, ethics committee/IRB and EU CTR/CTIS readiness Keep SSU trackers, dashboards and Veeva systems accurate and inspection-ready Identify, escalate and mitigate Study Start-Up risks and delays Provide clear visibility of study status and activation progress to stakeholders Analyze SSU metrics and KPIs to drive continuous improvement Champion standardized SSU processes, tools and templates Represent SSU with designated vendors, such as translation and Investigator Site File printing partners Who You Are You are a seasoned Study Start-Up leader who thrives on keeping complex global studies on track. You coach people to do their best work, influence without authority, and bring a problem-solving mindset to every bottleneck. Required Skills Bachelors degree in a scientific discipline (advanced degree preferred) 10+ years of clinical research experience, including 5+ years of global Study Start-Up experience within a sponsor, CRO, or related pharma/biopharma environment 5+ years of people management experience including coaching, developing, and providing performance and delivery oversight to employees or functional teams. Proven delivery of global site activation, including critical-path planning and risk mitigation Strong knowledge of ICH-GCP, IRB/EC submissions and EU CTR/CTIS Experience with CTMS and eTMF systems Ability to influence and lead in a matrix environment Strong project management, communication and stakeholder management skills Willingness to travel occasionally - less than 20% (site visits, conferences, etc) Preferred Skills Experience with Veeva and Veeva Site Connect Experience with Microsoft Project This position is available to be based remotely for the right candidate.

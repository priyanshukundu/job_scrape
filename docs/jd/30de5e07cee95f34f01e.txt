Morgan Stanley is seeking a Vice President Business Analyst to join Information Controls Technology. The role will support technology platforms and initiatives associated with electronic communications surveillance and information controls. The successful candidate will partner closely with Compliance, Operations, Product Owners, Technology teams and other stakeholders to translate complex business, regulatory and control requirements into clear technology requirements and executable delivery plans. The role requires strong business analysis and technology delivery experience, a good understanding of data and system workflows, and the ability to operate effectively across complex surveillance and control processes. Responsibilities Partner with Compliance, business and technology stakeholders to understand surveillance, regulatory and operational requirements and translate them into detailed functional and technical requirements. Serve as a subject matter expert for electronic communications surveillance workflows, including alert generation, review, disposition, escalation and evidence of supervisory processes. Analyze existing business processes, controls and system capabilities to identify gaps, inefficiencies and opportunities for automation or simplification. Define requirements for enhancements to surveillance applications, workflows, data integrations and reporting capabilities. Develop user stories, functional specifications, acceptance criteria, process flows and other artifacts required by engineering teams. Work closely with developers, architects, QA and Product Owners throughout the software development lifecycle to clarify requirements and ensure solutions meet intended business and control outcomes. Perform detailed data analysis to investigate issues, validate requirements and support technology and business decision-making. Coordinate user acceptance testing, including development of test scenarios, expected results, defect investigation and business sign-off. Support production releases and post-release validation, working with technology and business stakeholders to investigate and resolve issues. Partner with application and data teams to understand upstream and downstream dependencies across communications ingestion, surveillance processing, workflow and reporting. Support prioritization and management of the product backlog, balancing regulatory commitments, production issues, control requirements and strategic enhancements. Maintain clear documentation of requirements, decisions, workflows and system behavior to support governance, auditability and ongoing application support. Communicate status, risks, dependencies and decisions effectively to senior business and technology stakeholders. Required Qualifications Bachelor's degree or equivalent experience. Significant experience as a Business Analyst, Technology Business Analyst, Product Analyst or similar role within a complex technology environment. Demonstrated experience translating business and functional requirements into technology solutions. Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, including the ability to investigate complex workflows, systems and data issues. Experience working with software engineering teams throughout the software development lifecycle. Experience developing business requirements, functional specifications, user stories and acceptance criteria. Strong understanding of data flows, system integrations and relational data concepts. Experience with Agile software delivery practices and tools such as JIRA. Excellent written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to work effectively with both technical and non-technical stakeholders. Ability to manage multiple priorities and operate effectively within a highly controlled environment. Preferred Qualifications Experience within financial services, ideally supporting Compliance, Risk, Surveillance or other control functions. Experience with Smarsh Enterprise Conduct (EC) or comparable electronic communications surveillance platforms is highly desirable. Knowledge of electronic communications surveillance and supervisory review processes. Experience with communications platforms, surveillance applications or large-scale data-processing environments. Working knowledge of SQL and the ability to perform independent data analysis. Experience with reporting and data analysis tools. Understanding of technology controls, regulatory requirements and audit or evidence-management processes. Experience working with globally distributed technology and business teams.

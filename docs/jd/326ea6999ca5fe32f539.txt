The Team: Upstart's Home Lending teams are building digital lending experiences that help homeowners access their home equity. Our engineers work across borrower and partner experiences, lending decisioning, and the platform workflows that bring an application from start to finish. As a Senior Software Engineer, you’ll partner closely with product, design, and engineering teammates to solve meaningful customer problems and ship improvements across a growing product. It’s an opportunity to take ownership of complex, real-world systems while helping make the borrowing experience simpler for customers.

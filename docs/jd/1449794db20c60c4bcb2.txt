The Opportunity We are seeking a dedicated Product Development Director – UW Capabilities and Tools - Auto. This role is remote eligible in the continental U.S. with occasional business travel. However, individuals residing within a 60-mile radius of a USAA office will be expected to work on-site four days per week. What you'll do: The Product Development Director -UW Capabilities and Tools - Auto will be accountable for developing new products and overseeing and managing the launch and maturation of new products into target strategic markets. You will act as a cross functional expert providing expertise and support for product line initiatives related to product development, compliance, operational and process improvements, claims management, vendor management, product documentation, fulfillment, and/or system releases. Responsible for development of digital first products supporting the needs and risks of USAA Members. Leads development and launch of new innovative product offerings as well as auxiliary/enhanced features/services for existing products. Responsible for maintaining thorough level of knowledge of business and underwriting performance for products in scope (e.g. trends, results, etc.) Responsible for post-launch monitoring, maintenance and underwriting of 'new in market' products based on product performance, member needs and competitive changes in the market including but not limited to product rules, rates, and forms. Develops recommendations to launch new products and the evaluation and decision to buy/build or ally for individual product(s). Generates creative product and coverage solutions for member needs to ensure a dynamic product development pipeline exists. Conducts complex analysis of internal and external data and member information to support formulation of product strategy and enhance competitive intelligence. Develops and communicates the new product value proposition for individual products. Ensures sales support is delivered to all distribution channels (e.g., com, mobile, contact center) so that the new product is properly positioned to members. Leads the analysis and evaluation of new product performance metrics to make changes to offerings, if necessary, prior to full launch. Coordinates with relevant control partners (to include Legal, Compliance, and Risk Management) to ensure product offerings are designed and implemented in compliance with all applicable laws and regulations. Ensures risks associated with business activities are effectively identified, measured, monitored, and controlled in accordance with risk and compliance policies and procedures. What you have: Bachelor's degree in Business, Marketing, Supply Chain, or Statistics; OR 4 years of relevant education and/or experience. 8 years relevant experience in Actuary, Underwriting, Marketing, Operations, Financial Services or relevant military experience. Extensive experience in developing, deploying and supporting product and/or integrated suite of products. Experience working through agile product development process, managing multiple products simultaneously. Knowledge and application of Product Development Life Cycle (PDLC). Creative problem solver with hands on experience in problem framing, qualitative & quantitative research, running product experiments, hypothesis testing and prototyping to launch products at the intersection of Human, Technology and Business dimensions. Navigates through ambiguous contexts with confidence. Ability to break large complex initiatives into smaller manageable features and capabilities and deliver them early and often. What sets you apart: Deep knowledge of personal lines property products (e.g. HO, Fire, Renters, AU, UMB) from both a product management and product development perspective. Tech-savvy leader with a proven ability to implement UW solutions across legacy and modernized platforms (e.g. Guidewire, Earnix). Experience implementing advanced geospatial data, aerial imagery, and 3P data solutions. Extensive experience designing and embedding complex predictive models, machine learning algorithms, and AI solutions into core UW decision engines. Exceptional skill in leading a team of SMEs to translate intricate UW business problems into buildable functional requirements for IT. Master level ability to define clean MVP parameters vs full scale builds, maximizing speed to market for new technical capabilities. Experience working in an agile development environment with the ability to coach a team of Product Owners through feature development and establish benefits realization plans. Experience working across functional area verticals to build and implement solutions with the ability to recognize and activate natural and needed stakeholder engagement points. US military experience through military service or a military spouse/domestic partner. USAA does not provide visa sponsorship for this role.

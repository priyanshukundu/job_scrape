Job Description As a Staff Data Engineer, you will be a hands-on technical leader and expert problem-solver responsible for designing, implementing, and improving large-scale data systems, pipelines, and services. You will spend a significant portion of your time coding, debugging, testing, and optimizing data workloads using modern engineering tools, distributed processing technologies, cloud services, and automation frameworks. You will partner with product managers, designers, data scientists, quality assurance, operations, compliance, and other engineering teams to translate business requirements into reliable technical solutions. You will own data products end to end, strengthen engineering standards, and mentor engineers while meeting security, privacy, governance, and regulatory requirements. All roles require digital fluency, including the ability to work with emerging technologies such as Generative AI tools (e.g. ChatGPT, Microsoft Copilot) to support everyday work. Key Responsibilities: Partner with stakeholders to translate business requirements into scalable data architectures and technical designs. Develop, debug, and optimize distributed data workloads using Apache Spark, Scala, Hadoop, Hive, and Kafka. Build and operate high-volume batch and streaming pipelines, including orchestration, incremental processing, historical backfills, and data quality controls. Deliver curated datasets, reusable data products, and secure API-based data services for enterprise consumption. Own pipelines end to end, including deployment, monitoring, troubleshooting, documentation, and technical debt reduction. Write maintainable code and enforce code reviews, automated testing, CI/CD, and secure coding standards. Use AI-assisted development tools and coding agents to accelerate delivery while validating generated code for quality and security. Improve performance, reliability, and cost efficiency through observability, automation, and operational metrics. Ensure compliance with data governance, security, privacy, and retention requirements; support security remediation where applicable. Mentor engineers and lead technical problem-solving and cross-functional delivery. Visa requires at least 3 days in office, expectations of these days will be confirmed by your Hiring Manager.

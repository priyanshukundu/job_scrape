Sr Crop Data Scientist Analyst
Zurich Insurance
Location: Anoka, MN, US
Link: https://www.careers.zurich.com/job/Anoka-Sr-Crop-Data-Scientist-Analyst-MN/1371349057/

RCIS (a division of Zurich North America) is currently hiring a Sr Data Scientist Analyst, and this person will report to the Head of Underwriting, Crop Analytics. This role will work in a hybrid schedule from Schaumburg, IL or Anoka, MN OR remote for the right person. Some of the key accountabilities of this role include: Building and/or enhancing models for crop insurance business Identify and integrate external data sources needed for predictive analytics Analyzing and reporting on crop products requiring modeling with respect to RMA pricing, yield, and performance expectations Refreshing profitability models at the county and crop level to determine our strategic appetite Monitor and streamline premium estimation models Leading the maintenance and enhancements of fund designation models Scenario modeling on various RMA products to determine impact on profitability Manage Review Project – looking at historical data and state mix, county plan level, and industry data Participation in NCIS organization committees and new RMA product development and reviews Basic Qualifications: Bachelors Degree in Computer Science or Engineering or Mathematics/Statistics or Technology and 8 or more years of experience in the Predictive Analytics area AND Experience applying data transformation techniques such as exact and probabilistic matching methods; fuzzy matching, text mining, and data reduction Experience with programming and database mining tools such as Hadoop, Hive, Pig, Java, SQL, and Python Preferred Qualifications: Possesses strong crop insurance knowledge and technical expertise Understanding how MPCI (multi-peril crop insurance) products work and how they are priced Experience with extracting data from relational databases Advanced knowledge of statistical techniques and their application to business decisions Ability to develop actionable solutions to business challenges Advanced analytical and problem-solving skills Strong verbal and communication skills Your pay at Zurich is based on your role, location, skills, and experience.

Bristol Myers Squibb is seeking a motivated and detail-oriented (Senior) Associate Scientist to join our Analytical Sciences team. In this role, you will support routine and non-routine analytical testing activities critical to the advancement of our RNA focused drug discovery and development pipeline. The ideal candidate possesses hands-on laboratory experience, a strong scientific foundation, and a commitment to data quality and compliance.

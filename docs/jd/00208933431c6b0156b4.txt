Become a part of our caring community We are looking for a motivated and detail-oriented Data Engineer II to join our Home Health Data Engineering team. In this role, you will contribute to the design, development, and maintenance of cloud-based data solutions that support business intelligence, analytics, and operational reporting initiatives. You will work closely with senior engineers, architects, analysts, and business stakeholders to build reliable and scalable data pipelines while developing your expertise in modern cloud data platforms. This position is ideal for an engineer who is passionate about data, eager to learn emerging technologies, and interested in growing their skills in Snowflake, Azure, and modern data engineering practices. As a Data Engineer, you will help design, build, and maintain modern cloud-based data solutions that support enterprise analytics and business intelligence. Working closely with architects, analysts, application teams, and business stakeholders, you will develop scalable data pipelines and deliver reliable, high-quality data solutions that drive business outcomes. Key Responsibilities Develop, maintain, and optimize ETL/ELT pipelines that support enterprise data integration and analytics initiatives. Design and support Snowflake-based data warehouse solutions, including data ingestion, transformation, validation, and performance optimization. Build and maintain cloud-native data solutions using Azure services, including Azure Databricks, Azure Storage, and Azure DevOps. Create and support scalable data integration processes that ensure data quality, consistency, and availability. Develop and maintain API and system integrations, including MuleSoft and other integration platforms. Troubleshoot and resolve data pipeline and integration issues while collaborating with senior engineers and cross-functional teams. Implement and support data governance, security, and compliance standards across enterprise data platforms. Participate in code reviews, testing activities, documentation, and continuous improvement initiatives. Support cloud migration and modernization efforts by moving data workloads from legacy systems to cloud platforms. Use your skills to make an impact Required Qualifications Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Systems, Engineering, or a related field (or equivalent experience). 2-5 years of experience as a Data Engineer, Data Integration Engineer, or in a related data-focused role. Hands-on experience with Snowflake for data warehousing, data transformation, and data pipeline development. 2+ years of experience building and supporting ETL/ELT pipelines in cloud-based environments. 2+ years of experience working with Azure data technologies, including Azure Databricks, Azure Storage, and Azure DevOps. Strong proficiency in SQL and experience using Python to develop and support scalable data solutions. Experience integrating data across systems using MuleSoft, APIs, or other enterprise integration platforms. Working knowledge of data modeling, database design, and cloud data architecture best practices. Experience using Git and CI/CD tools within a software development environment. Strong analytical, troubleshooting, and problem-solving skills with the ability to support production data environments. Excellent communication skills and the ability to collaborate effectively with cross-functional teams. Preferred Qualifications Experience working in healthcare, regulated, or highly governed environments. Exposure to Terraform or other Infrastructure-as-Code (IaC) tools. Experience supporting cloud-based analytics, reporting, or enterprise data platforms. Familiarity with Agile development methodologies. Experience with cloud migration or modernization initiatives. Demonstrated commitment to continuous learning and professional growth within data engineering and cloud technologies.

Role Purpose: The Manager of Corporate FP&A is a critical role on the Corporate FP&A team. Reporting to the Senior Director of FP&A, this role provides the unique opportunity to analyze multiple business units (Streaming, Media, Destinations & Experiences), manage the strategic planning and estimate cycles, and create presentations to senior NBCU and Comcast management. Responsibilities : Assist in managing the P&L forecasting process for all quarterly estimates, yearly budgets, and five-year strategic plans Communicate regularly with finance leaders at each of NBCUniversal's business units to monitor performance metrics and synthesize trends Perform P&L analysis for all forecasting cycles, including understanding each division's variances (e.g. budget/prior year) and related risks and opportunities to the current forecast Assist in the creation of presentations summarizing NBCU's performance for Senior Management, Board of Directors, Investor Relations, etc. Manage and maintain monthly pacing/reporting system Significant involvement in projects to improve/upgrade total company planning processes Help execute on ad hoc requests as assigned by NBCU's head of FP&A and/or CFO Identify opportunities and implement process improvements for automation of planning processes, trend analysis, and reporting actuals, including the use of AI tool Basic Requirements: Bachelor’s Degree in Finance, Accounting or related field 3+ years relevant accounting/finance experience Solid Computer / Analysis Skills, particularly in Excel and PowerPoint Desired Characteristics: Previous experience in the media and entertainment industry highly preferred SAP BW/BPC/SAC or Anaplan experience Interpersonal skills with the proven ability to work and communicate effectively with all levels of the organization Demonstrated ability to synthesize and encapsulate complex data and information in order to build financial models and presentations to senior leadership Ability to multi-task and prioritize work assignments and work well under pressure Must be accurate, highly organized with great attention to detail Proactive approach to problem solving including analytical and information system skills Excellent work ethic and follow through Hybrid: This position currently has a hybrid schedule, which requires contributing from the office a minimum of four days per week.

Position Title Senior Risk Governance & Policy Program Analyst Location Hicksville, NY 11801 Job Summary The Senior Risk Governance & Policy Program Analyst is responsible for the day-to-day execution of governance, policy program and regulatory support functions, under the direction of the Chief of Staff to the CRO. The role assist in the end-to-end policy program activites for the Risk Management Division, supports Bank-wide policy governance, and provides a second line of defense (2LoD) governance perspective across Risk Division policies, issues, controls, remediation activities, training and operational resiliency, giving the CRO an integrated and independent view of the risks in the Risk Management Division. The role applies strong technical skills, including visual tools, workflow automation and low-code solutions (e.g., Microsoft Power Apps), to modernize how governance and policy activities are managed, tracked and reported. The candidate will support regulatory remediation activites, and serve as a key support to the Chief of Staff to the CRO on strategic priorities and special projects. The role builds relationships with first line and second line colleagues across the Bank, provides analytical expertise, communicates compelling and persuasive analysis and effective challenge on matters of heightened focus or escalated risk, works independently on assigned projects and delivers high quality, sustainable solutions. Job Responsibilities: JOB RESPONSIBILITIES Assist in the end-to-end Bank Policy Program, including the policy inventory, standardized templates, annual review cycle, approval routing, version control, monitoring and reporting, exception tracking and attestations, ensuring alignment with the regulatory expectations. Partners with policy owners across the first and second lines to draft, challenge, simplify and clarify policy and procedure requirements; ensures consistency of approach, monitors adherence, and reports program status, overdue items and exceptions to CRO leadership and relevant committees. Supports Bank-wide policy governance by contributing to enterprise policy standards, templates and guidance. Provides a 2LoD governance view across the Risk Division, monitoring and reporting on policy compliance, issues, controls, remediation activities, training completion and operational resiliency, and providing effective challenge to Risk functions where gaps, delays or inconsistencies are identified. Tracks the status, aging and quality of closure of Risk Division issues and remediation action plans (including regulatory, audit and self-identified findings), and escalates items at risk of missing commitments to the Chief of Staff and CRO leadership. Monitors the Risk Division control environment and governance of related standards, including the alignment of policies to key controls, control gaps and results of testing and monitoring, and consolidates the information into integrated management reporting. Oversees governance of Risk Division training and resiliency requirements, including tracking completion of required training, alignment of training content to policy updates, and readiness of resiliency and business continuity activities. Designs, builds and maintains workflow automation and low-code solutions (e.g., Microsoft Power Apps, Power Automate, SharePoint) to digitize policy inventories, approvals, attestations, issue and action tracking and governance processes, partnering with Technology as needed. Develops visual management reporting and dashboards (e.g.,Power BI, database querying) that give CRO leadership clear insight into policy program status, issues, controls, remediation, training and resiliency across functional areas within the Risk organization. Supports Regulatory Remediation activities by tracking commitments, milestones and supporting evidence, and provides independent analysis of progress on top Bank priorities within the Risk Management Division. Collaborates with the Chief of Staff to the CRO on a wide range of projects, processes and assignments that provide support to, and critical awareness of, second line risk functions across the Risk organization, including special projects for the CRO. Assists with preparation, development and progress monitoring of the annual strategic goals and objectives set by the Office of the CRO. Identifies emerging risks and gaps in risk reporting, and works with relevant stakeholders to implement improvements to the monitoring activities performed by the Office of the CRO. Conducts market, company, industry and peer research on designated topics and communicates actionable insights pertinent to the Risk organization and the Bank at large. May be asked to lead or support change management activities for Risk Division, integration or policy program initiatives as business needs require.

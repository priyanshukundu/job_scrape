NVIDIA is looking for a creative software engineer to join the team. The incredible growth of AI factories represents the greatest change the world of networking has seen. Spectrum-X is NVIDIA's end-to-end Ethernet Networking solution designed to meet this new challenge with customers including the biggest and fastest-moving organizations in the world. We need a software engineer with strong customer-facing technical skills to join our Spectrum-X engineering team and help make this technology a success. What you'll be doing: Working in a team of US-based software engineers developing NVIDIA's networking products. Debugging and diagnosing complex networking problems, and crafting the tools/processes to do it better. Engaging directly with NVIDIA customers to ensure we are aligned on their networking requirements and solve their problems. Working with a worldwide team of engineers, architects, product managers, and field personnel. What we need to see: B.A./B.Sc. in Computer Science or equivalent experience. 5+ overall years of experience developing embedded networking software (e.g. switches, routers, NICs) Strong programming skills in C and Python on Linux. Leadership in AI-native software development. Outstanding analytical and problem-solving skills, with a keen attention to detail, particularly in identifying and resolving networking issues. Good English communication and interpersonal skills. A track-record of autonomous and successful delivery under challenging conditions. Ways to stand out from the crowd: Experience of data center network engineering spanning servers, NICs, cabling and switches. Strong understanding of ethernet networking, from switch pipelines (e.g. ACLs, forwarding rules) through networking protocols (like TCP/IP and BGP/EVPN-VxLAN) to network operating systems and their automation. Experience of NVIDIA networking technology such as Bluefield/ConnectX NICs, Spectrum switches, and the Cumulus Linux and SONiC operating systems.

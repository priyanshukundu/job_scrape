Treasury Operations is responsible for forecasting funding requirements to support the Global Clearing business and the Global Treasury function. The team ensures all Clearing House margin obligations are met through the effective deployment of cash and non-cash collateral, while balancing time-sensitive requests from Client Service and Global Treasury. As a Margin & Collateral Analyst within Treasury Operations, you will support daily funding projections and collateral movements to ensure margin requirements are met efficiently and in line with established controls. The role requires regular interaction with local and global stakeholders, including Client Service, Global Treasury, and Operations partners, as well as frequent collaboration with Technology and Project Delivery teams to support process enhancements and strategic initiatives.

Job Profile Summary The Business Control function is the Bank's 1LOD (first line of defense) and is responsible for facilitating identification, assessment, monitoring, mitigation and reporting of operational risk for the entirety of the firm. This function works directly with all business units and functions to develop a strong risk management environment. Responsibilities This Business Control Associate works collaboratively to support effective risk management activities of Texas Capital across a broad range of frameworks. Key responsibilities include supporting the Business Control Director to: Ensure the business/function identifies, owns and manages risks within its processes and products. Design and implement preventative and detective controls tailored to business’/functions’ activities Maintain evidence of effective control execution; Identify gaps and drive remediation plans Facilitate and coordinate the Risk & Control Self-Assessment (RCSA ) process for the business/function Challenge control effectiveness ratings and ensure risks are appropriately scored Keep risk and control inventories current, complete, accurate and reflective of actual processes Facilitate development of KRIs and KCIs for ongoing monitoring Provide risk and control reporting to senior management and governance committees, including Quarterly Business Reviews (QBR) Monitor adherence to policies/standards/procedures, limits, and tolerance thresholds Assess operational risks and controls for new products, processes, or system changes; Ensure operational risks are documented and controls are embedded before go-live Validate processes, procedures and Business Continuity plans are properly updated as appropriate Proactively prepare for/support regulatory, compliance and audit reviews with evidence of control effectiveness Act as the “front door” for 2LOD inquiries ensuring consistent responses Track and oversee issue management program for the business, reporting Quality of Risk Management (QRM) to measure effectiveness Thematically monitor complaints and risk events for business impacts Manage business continuity program to ensure operational resilience Qualifications Bachelor’s degree (preferred in Finance, Accounting, Business or Computer Science); or equivalent combination of education, training, experience Minimum 2 years of operational risk management experience in financial services or a related field Good understanding of financial institution operational processes/risks/controls. Specific experience/knowledge of Operations processes, risks & controls is preferred.

What Financial Planning & Analysis contributes to Cardinal Health Finance oversees the accounting, tax, financial plans and policies of the organization, establishes and maintains fiscal controls, prepares and interprets financial reports, oversees financial systems and safeguards the organization's assets. Financial Planning & Analysis provides strategic partnership and objective guidance to business units. It leads cross functional efforts to enable forward looking, actionable insights with a focus on driving strategic value that influences key business decisions. Job Summary The Director, Financial Planning and Analysis collaborates with key members of Cardinal Health’s executive leadership team to develop financial strategies and drive analytics to enable business decision making. This job drives the long-term planning, forecasting, and budgeting processes. In addition, the Director, Financial Planning and Analysis develops detailed reports and analysis on the performance of the businesses. This job provides leadership in the development of the financial strategy, produces logical scenario analyses and insights, accurate forecasts, and make sound recommendations to support key business decisions. Responsibilities Leads the segment budget, forecasting and reporting processes for P&L, balance sheet and capital. Develops calendars and timelines, standard templates, and communications to and hold all business units accountable. Acts as a liaison between the segment and enterprise FP&A, investor relations, SEC Reporting, Assistant Secretary (for Board reporting), and Strategy. Supports the strategic and financial planning process for a specific business unit and segment level financial schedules. Manages product type profitability model process and reporting. Provides support in the budget planning process including analyzing trends and providing tactical recommendations. Generates reports to communicate FP&A activities to the segment CEO, CFO, and business unit President.

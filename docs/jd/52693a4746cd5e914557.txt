Job Family : Technology Consulting Travel Required : Up to 10% Clearance Required : Ability to Obtain Secret What You Will Do : Guidehouse is seeking a Senior Data Engineer – Subject Matter Expert to design, build, and sustain enterprise data pipelines within the War Data Platform. You will work under senior technical leads to produce traceable, secure datasets in support of an integrated Revenue Cycle Management system. You will develop ingestion, transformation, and orchestration components, implement data quality controls, and mentor junior engineers while collaborating with architecture, interface, and BI teams. In this role, you will be responsible for: Data Pipelines & Lifecycle: Design, build, test, and maintain batch, incremental, and streaming ETL/ELT pipelines from ingestion and cleaning through transformation and delivery. Data Modeling & Quality: Develop source-to-target mappings, data models, data lineage, reconciliation, and quality controls—including traceable, auditable datasets for financial reporting and FIAR. Integration & AI Readiness: Standardize data access, support API/integration feeds, optimize database schemas, and prepare mission-ready datasets for BI tools, analytics, and AI/ML workflows. Security & Compliance: Implement security controls, governance, and compliance standards suited to defense data environments. Develop and optimize SQL, Python, and ETL/ELT processes for large financial datasets. What You Will Need : Must be able to OBTAIN and MAINTAIN a Federal or DoD "SECRET" security clearance; candidates must obtain approved adjudication of clearance prior to onboarding with Guidehouse. Candidates with an ACTIVE "SECRET" or higher-level clearance are preferred. Bachelor's degree FIFTEEN (15) or more years of data engineering experience including ETL/ELT pipelines. Experience must include: SQL and Python proficiency Databricks proficiency Data modeling, orchestration, performance tuning, and data lineage What Would be Nice to Have: MHS experience, preferably with War Data Platform The annual salary range for this position is $149,000.00-$248,000.00.

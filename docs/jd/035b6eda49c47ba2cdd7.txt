What You'll Contribute The Senior Manager – Enterprise Risk reports to the Head of Enterprise Risk Management. The Senior Manager will be responsible for supporting Risk Management’s oversight and effective challenge of Market and Liquidity Risk as well as Capital and implications of these risks from the Allowance for Credit Losses. In addition, the Senior Manager will provide effective challenge and oversight of Strategic Partnership relationships (private credit firms) from the perspective of Market, Liquidity, and Capital Risk. This includes the ongoing maintenance and / or enhancement of these programs. The Senior Manager may take on additional responsibilities at the direction of the Head of Enterprise Risk Management. What You'll Do Support the Head of Enterprise Risk Management for ensuring the effectiveness of the Market and Liquidity Risk Management Program, including: Conduct periodic targeted reviews of Treasury activities related to the above Provide oversight and effective challenge of capital management and capital stress testing Provide oversight and effective challenge of liquidity stress testing and market risk management by Treasury Collaborate with key stakeholders to establish risk tolerances and develop risk mitigation strategies and action plans. Provide guidance and support to business units and departments in identifying and managing risks specific to their areas of operation. Maintain strong relationships with internal stakeholders, including senior management, business unit leaders, and functional teams, to drive risk awareness and accountability. Support the Head of Enterprise Risk Management in the effective challenge and oversight of the Allowance for Credit Losses process, including the impact on Liquidity, Market, and Capital Risk. This includes maintaining strong relationships with stakeholders. Support the Head of Enterprise Risk Management in the oversight and effective challenge of Strategic Partnerships from the perspective of Market, Liquidity, and Capital Risk. This includes maintaining strong relationships with stakeholders. Provide ad hoc research and project support.

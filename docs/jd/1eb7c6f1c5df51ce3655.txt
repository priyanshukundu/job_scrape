Model Risk Oversight provides independent oversight of quantitative models used to support financial risk management, forecasting, and regulatory reporting. As a Senior Manager, Risk Analytics/Modeling, you will lead complex model validations and help ensure models are conceptually sound, appropriately implemented, and effectively governed. In this role, you will apply advanced quantitative judgment to challenge model methodologies, assumptions, data, and results, with particular focus on margin, deposits, and other balance sheet products. You will communicate validation conclusions through clear written reports and discussions with model owners, senior leaders, and other stakeholders. Working across business and risk functions, you will identify material model risks, develop practical recommendations, and help strengthen model risk management practices. You will also provide technical guidance and mentorship to quantitative professionals while balancing multiple validation priorities. What you have Required Qualifications Advanced degree in a quantitative discipline such as Statistics, Economics, Finance, Mathematics, Physics, Engineering, or a related field. 5+ years of professional experience in model development, model validation, or related quantitative modeling practices. Prior experience modeling margin, deposits, and other balance sheet products. Advanced proficiency with one or more quantitative analytics tools such as Python, SQL, R, MATLAB, or VBA. Strong quantitative and Critical Thinking skills with the ability to independently evaluate model methodology, assumptions, data, implementation, and results and exercise sound judgment when identifying model risk. Strong written and verbal Communication skills with the ability to clearly present complex quantitative findings and recommendations to technical and nontechnical stakeholders. Demonstrated Collaboration skills and the ability to work effectively across model owners, business partners, risk functions, and other stakeholders. Preferred Qualifications PhD in a quantitative discipline. Experience within banking, brokerage, or financial services, particularly involving Asset Liability Management, deposit runoff or balance behavior models, financial forecasting, interest rate risk, or related balance sheet modeling. Knowledge of model governance practices and regulatory expectations applicable to large U.S. financial institutions. Experience leading, developing, or mentoring quantitative professionals and providing effective review and challenge of complex analytical work. Experience with IBM Planning Analytics or a comparable financial planning and analysis platform. Adaptability and demonstrated ability to manage competing priorities, solve complex or ambiguous problems, and adjust analytical approaches as business and risk needs evolve.

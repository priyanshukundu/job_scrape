About the team The Finance Controls team helps management establish and maintain effective internal controls over financial reporting. We partner closely with control owners, Finance and Accounting leaders, Internal Audit, external auditors, Technology and Security teams, Legal, Compliance, and other cross-functional partners to strengthen the control environment and support reliable financial reporting. As an Associate Manager, SOX & Internal Controls, you'll own a defined set of business processes within Coinbase's SOX and SOC 1 programs from end to end. Our Internal Controls team keeps Coinbase audit-ready by designing, documenting, and monitoring the controls that safeguard financial reporting across a fast-changing crypto platform. You'll bring a technology lens to controls work, mapping data flows from source systems to financial statements, evaluating automated control evidence, and driving remediation when gaps arise. What you'll do: Own assigned SOX business processes end to end, including process narratives, risk-and-control matrices, key systems, automated controls, and business process controls, keeping each area audit-ready through continuous documentation and design updates. Partner with Technology, Engineering, Security Compliance, and Finance Analytics to scope IT general controls and IT application controls, map data flows, and evaluate the reliability of system-generated evidence. Lead day-to-day SOX and SOC 1 execution for your area, including risk assessment, scoping, walkthroughs, testing coordination, deficiency evaluation, and remediation tracking with control owners. Coordinate audit readiness and information requests with Internal Audit and external auditors, ensuring support is timely, complete, and well organized. Advise control owners on control design, evidence quality, and sustainable remediation while helping improve repeatable standards, playbooks, and evidence workflows across the function. Required Skills and Experience: 6+ years in SOX, IT audit, internal controls, controllership, or a closely related controls function, with Big Four or public accounting experience preferred. Demonstrated ownership of a business process or controls workstream end to end, from documentation through testing and remediation, in a heavily automated, high-change environment. Working knowledge of ITGCs (access, change management, operations), IT application controls, and system-generated reports, with the ability to trace how a system supports a financial process. Hands-on experience with data validation across systems using SQL, spreadsheets, or analytics tools to test control logic and verify system output. CPA, CISA, CIA, or comparable certification preferred; Bachelor's degree in Accounting, Finance, Information Systems, or a related field (or equivalent practical experience). Utilizes generative AI responsibly, maintaining human oversight to deliver business-ready outputs and drive measurable improvements in workflow efficiency, cost, and quality.

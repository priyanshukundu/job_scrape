Business Group: Blackstone Technology & Innovations Business Group Overview: Blackstone Technology & Innovations (BXTI) is the technology team at the core of each of Blackstone’s businesses and new growth initiatives. Serving both internal and external clients, we work to build the next generation of systems that manage risk, create efficiency and improve transparency within the firm and across our broad community of investors and portfolio companies. Job Title: Blackstone Technology & Innovations - Data Engineering & Visualization - Associate Job Responsibilities: Produce and maintain standard analytical reports plus data visualizations using approved analytics platforms and designated data sources to support business and investment reporting needs. Implement and refine documented business requirements into analytics deliverables and data outputs, escalating questions or issues as needed. Manage and execute assigned tasks across multiple projects while meeting established timelines and priorities. Refine and drive adoption of data visualization and data governance best practices throughout the firm. Develop proof‑of‑concept (POC) engagements by developing limited‑scope analytical prototypes using approved technologies. Maintain documentation and data permissions related to analytics processes, data definitions, and reporting outputs. Assist with data ingestion and preparation processes using existing ETL pipelines and data integration tools. Perform data validation and quality checks to ensure accuracy, consistency, and completeness of analytics outputs. Perform other duties as needed. Qualifications: Education: Bachelor’s degree (or foreign equivalent) in Data Science, Data Analytics, or a related field required. Experience: Minimum of 2 years of experience in job offered or related occupations required. Experience: Minimum of 2 years of experience using Tableau and Sigma Computing to build internal metadata usage dashboards, and administer software license allocations. Minimum of 2 years of experience using Python and SQL to query, transform, and analyze large datasets within Snowflake to support internal reporting and analytics need. Minimum of 2 years of experience using Microsoft Excel and Python to cross-validate data between source systems for accuracy and consistency. Minimum of 2 years of experience gathering and documenting business requirements using JIRA and Confluence to understand existing processes and support the design and delivery of data-focused solutions. Minimum of 2 years of experience using Benevity and Sigma Computing to track and report on volunteer and donation activities. Minimum of 1 year of experience using Python and REST API endpoints to migrate Microsoft Excel reports into Tableau and Sigma Computing dashboards. Minimum of 1 year of experience using REST APIs, Fivetran, and Prefect to extract, ingest, and standardize data to create and maintain golden‑copy analytics datasets serving as an individual source of truth across multiple source systems. Minimum of 6 months of experience using Sigma Computing to develop financial scenario models for internal analysis. Minimum of 6 months of experience using Oracle expense data and Sigma Computing to analyze, monitor, and report on enterprise expenses. Experience can be concurrent.

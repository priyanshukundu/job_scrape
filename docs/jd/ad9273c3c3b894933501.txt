Markets In-Business Risk and Trading Development (IBR&TD) is a Front Office 1st Line of Defense team within Citi’s Markets division. IBR&TD is responsible for identifying potential, emerging and material market risks, partnering closely with the 2nd Line of Defense Risk Management to monitor, manage and analyze financial risk exposure and recommend actions that mitigate risks across asset classes globally, including Rates, FX, Spread Products, Commodities and Equities. Also, understand, anticipate, and proactively manage stress testing results and capital resources. In addition, IBR&TD is responsible for New Activities and Trading Development, 1st line Model/Valuation Risk, Volcker Rule Office, Daily Flash P&L Reporting, Markets Recovery and Resolution Planning and Intra-day Counterparty Credit monitoring. Responsibilities Build data analytics tools that identify, quantify and analyze key market risks across Citi's trading inventory. Strengthen risk-weighted assets monitoring and analytics, pinpointing key drivers, emerging concentrations and opportunities to manage risk across trading portfolios. Support the Fundamental Review of the Trading Book program, including internal model implementation, standardized approach readiness, data analytics and model performance analysis. Apply knowledge of risk appetite, limits and capital frameworks to optimize and allocate risk effectively across the business. Track the performance of products across global markets to form a forward-looking view of material, concentration and emerging risks. Translate complex risk, profit and loss and market data into actionable insights for first-line market risk decisions. Partner with technology, quant and asset-class risk teams to develop advanced analytics tools and risk monitoring frameworks. Respond to regulatory inquiries and prepare deliverables related to market risk capital requirements. Required Qualifications & Skills Demonstrate strong proficiency in Python and SQL to build and maintain market risk analytics tools. Use Tableau, Power BI or similar dashboard tools to visualize risk, profit and loss and market data. Apply financial acumen and profit and loss management experience to inform risk decisions. Translate complex risk and market data into clear, actionable insights for technical and non-technical audiences. Build strong working relationships with colleagues, stakeholders and senior leaders across a large organization. Communicate complex topics clearly and adapt quickly to new information in a fast-moving environment. Hold a bachelor's degree, ideally with a concentration in engineering, statistics or finance Beneficial Skills & Qualifications Hold a master's degree What We Offer At Citi, you will build market risk expertise at the heart of a global markets business, working alongside risk, trading, quant and technology teams to shape how the firm manages financial risk. Work a hybrid schedule with 3 days in the office and 2 days working remotely from our New York office. Build market risk and data analytics expertise through direct exposure to Citi's global trading businesses. Collaborate with risk, trading, quant and technology teams across global markets. Access ongoing learning and development opportunities that support long-term career growth. Benefit from wellbeing, financial wellbeing and family support programs designed to support work-life balance.

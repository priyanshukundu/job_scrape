Job Location IVORYDALE INNOVATION CENTER Job Description Research and Development (R&D) at Procter & Gamble, the largest consumer packaged goods company in the world, includes a diverse group of roles that contribute to the innovation and development of our products. It encompasses roles in product research, formulation, testing, and scientific analysis. You will find variety and excitement starting Day 1. This is an Associate Researcher role within our Researcher Track career path. Researcher Track employees are motivated by hands-on experimentation and applying foundational science through defined or standardized experimentation, data generation, detailed observations, and documentation of results. The exact day-to-day work depends on the position. The Researcher Track is part of P&G's Business, Technical and Administrative (BTA) level roles and provides a technical, non-management career path. As employees grow in the track, they deepen their technical mastery through increasing application of scientific, experimental, and procedural skills. The Associate Researcher is an entry-level role for individuals who are competent in basic science and math and are inspired to apply those skills in a laboratory, pilot plant, or technical development environment. At P&G, a wide variety of R&D work is available to Associate Researchers, in the following major areas: • Products Research -Deeply understand our consumers and their needs to inform product design parameters for new or improved products. • Product Formulation & Design - Translates consumer needs into finished products that delight consumers using the best available technologies/chemicals/materials. • Analytical - Use lab equipment to solve tough technical problems via chemical, physical, mechanical, imaging, and other measurement capabilities. • Packaging & Device Development - Creates and improves packages and devices that delight consumers and retailers. • Process Development & Pilot Plant Operations - Translates ideas, concepts, and technologies into commercially feasible chemical and mechanical processes and scales them up from lab to pilot plant to manufacturing. About the Role At P&G, our Strategic Innovation & Technology organization builds the pipeline of next-generation technologies behind consumer-winning products. We drive business growth by developing breakthrough, sustainable chemical and materials technologies that redefine categories. We are looking for a motivated, technically curious Associate Researcher to join our R&D team. This role is primarily lab-based, working hands-on with new raw materials and laundry formulations. You will plan and run experiments, analyze data, and share what you learn, while building your understanding of the chemistry behind consumer products. You will work independently and bring new ideas to improve how laundry is done. What You Will Do Formulate and test: Prepare new laundry formulations incorporating novel raw materials, and evaluate their performance, such as stain removal, whiteness, and fabric care, from small-scale lab tests up to larger-scale evaluations. Run experiments end to end: Set up and define experiments, procure materials, run the experiment, and summarize the outcome and recommendations. Collect and analyze data: Run performance tests and use technical instrumentation to gather data. Analyze results in Excel, draw conclusions, and share them with the team. Support analytical work: Learn and apply analytical methods to better understand material properties and how they relate to performance. Explore new ideas: Contribute to the development of new approaches to laundry that deliver a superior cleaning experience for consumers. Collaborate: Work with a diverse, global, cross-functional team of scientists, engineers, and technicians, clearly sharing observations, data, and recommendations. Use digital systems: Record data, track materials, and manage information in digital tools. Full training will be provided. Job Qualifications Education: Associate degree in a scientific or related field or a High School diploma with relevant experience and interests. Candidates with 2-year or 4-year degrees in non-engineering disciplines are welcome to apply. Scientific method: Proficiency in designing and running experiments: setting up and defining experiments, procuring materials, executing the work, and summarizing outcomes. Data management: Strong computer skills, including data management and analysis in Excel, and strong math skills. Laboratory skills: Comfort working primarily in a lab, following formulation instructions to prepare samples, and spotting potential issues such as stability, pH, color, or scent. Attention to detail: Accurate, organized record keeping of all experimental work. Teamwork and communication: Ability to work independently and collaboratively, and to share results clearly.

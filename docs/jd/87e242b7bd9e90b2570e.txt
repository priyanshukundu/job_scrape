We are global private markets specialists delivering tailored investment solutions, advisory services, and impactful, data driven insights to the world’s investors. Leveraging the power of our platform and our peerless intelligence across sectors, strategies, and geographies, we help identify the advantages and the answers our clients need to succeed. Position Overview: We are seeking an experienced Director, Portfolio Manager to join our Private Debt team. This individual will lead a small team that will play a critical role in managing and optimizing the performance of our co-investment portfolio and co-investment partner relationships. The ideal candidate will have a strong foundation in investment analysis with a proven ability to transition into portfolio management, focusing on oversight and strategic decision-making rather than direct investing. Restructuring experience is required. Key Responsibilities: Co-Investment Portfolio Monitoring & Analysis: Oversee and analyze the performance of the private debt co-investment portfolio. Identify trends, risks, and opportunities to optimize portfolio outcomes. Report to the Risk Committee on a quarterly basis. Co-Investment Partner Monitoring & Analysis: Oversee, track and analyze key performance metrics of co-investment partners. Data Management & Reporting: Collaborate with outsourced data input providers to ensure accuracy and timeliness of portfolio data. Prepare detailed performance reports and insights for senior leadership and stakeholders. Help internal stakeholders respond to investor and leverage provider data requests. Management of Underperforming Positions & Amendments: Support the analysis of underperforming portfolio positions including restructurings. Conduct thorough analysis on proposed amendments. Support negotiations and documentation related to portfolio adjustments. Qualifications & Experience: Education: Bachelor’s degree in Finance, Economics, or related field; MBA or CFA preferred. Experience: 8+ years of experience in private debt. Prior exposure to investment analysis with a strong interest or proven transition into portfolio management. Experience with corporate restructurings. Skills: Strong analytical and quantitative skills with attention to detail. Excellent communication and stakeholder management abilities. Ability to work independently and collaboratively in a fast-paced environment. Ability to lead small teams also across offices. Ideal Candidate Profile: A professional with investment and restructuring experience who is eager to focus on portfolio oversight and strategic management. Comfortable working with complex data sets and outsourced service providers. Highly organized, proactive, and capable of influencing decisions at senior levels.

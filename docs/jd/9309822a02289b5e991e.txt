Responsibilities The Business Analyst I is an early-career member of the IT Wealth Technology team who applies foundational business analysis skills to understand problems, clarify needs, evaluate options, and help move business-sponsored opportunities toward practical solutions. The role is designed for someone with curiosity, strong problem-solving skills, and an interest in how AI, data, digital capabilities, automation, and other technologies can improve Wealth experiences and outcomes. The analyst will work across a flexible range of assignments while developing Wealth knowledge, business judgment, technology fluency, and the ability to support work from discovery through an appropriate delivery handoff. Salary Range The salary range for this position is $63,600/yr - $124,300/yr plus bonus. The base salary indicated for this position reflects the compensation range applicable to all levels of the role across the United States. Actual salary offers within this range may vary based on a number of factors, including the specific responsibilities of the position, the candidate’s relevant skills and professional experience, educational qualifications, and geographic location. Key Accountabilities Partner with Wealth business sponsors, product partners, and technology teams to define the problem, users, desired outcome, success measures, constraints, information needs, risks, and open questions. Gather and maintain fit-for-purpose requirements, user stories, scenarios, acceptance criteria, findings, and decisions needed for the current stage of work. Analyze solution options across process, data, reporting, digital experience, automation, AI, and other technology approaches. Develop fit-for-purpose prototypes and bounded experiments for business, departmental, or enterprise opportunities using approved tools and environments; gather feedback, validate assumptions, and recommend whether to proceed, refine, transition, or stop. Use approved tools, including Microsoft Copilot, Amazon Quick, Claude or Claude Code, Databricks and Genie, Amazon Bedrock, Jira, Confluence, SQL, Python, APIs, and data visualization capabilities, as appropriate to the assignment and role access. Use these tools to accelerate research, analysis, prototyping, requirements drafting, testing, findings, and documentation while remaining accountable for quality. Communicate business value, findings, limitations, risks, dependencies, and recommended next steps in language business and technology audiences can understand. Help users learn and apply approved AI capabilities while reinforcing human review and responsible-use expectations. Develop working knowledge of Wealth products, platforms, data, digital experiences, operations, and regulatory considerations. Technology and Responsible AI Expectations Use only approved tools, data sources, environments, and access patterns. Validate AI-assisted work for accuracy, completeness, relevance, sourcing, business usefulness, and appropriate human review. Follow applicable security, privacy, data handling, model risk, compliance, records, and regulatory expectations; escalate concerns promptly. Recognize role boundaries: the analyst supports discovery, analysis, testing, validation, documentation, enablement, and handoff, but does not independently approve production architecture, data usage, controls, or deployment. Qualifications and Education Requirements Bachelor's degree in Business, Information Systems, Computer Science, Data Analytics, Engineering, or a related field, or equivalent practical experience. Entry-level to approximately two years of relevant internship, academic, capstone, project, business analysis, technology, data, process improvement, or AI experience. Strong analytical, research, problem-solving, writing, organizational, and communication skills. Ability to translate a business problem into clear analysis, requirements, scenarios, findings, and recommendations with guidance. Curiosity, technical aptitude, sound judgment, and comfort learning new tools in an evolving environment. Foundational familiarity with AI, data, analytics, digital workflows, automation, or software development through coursework, internships, certifications, or projects. Ability to travel and stay overnight as needed. Preferred Experience Internship, coursework, capstone, or project experience involving business analysis, AI, data analytics, product, technology, financial services, or Wealth Management. Exposure to generative AI, enterprise search, data platforms, prompt and context design, APIs, SQL, Python, or data visualization. Examples of currently relevant enterprise capabilities include Microsoft Copilot, Amazon Quick, Claude or Claude Code, Databricks and Genie, Amazon Bedrock, Jira, and Confluence; direct experience with every tool is not required. Familiarity with responsible AI principles, including human oversight, privacy, security, testing, documentation, and escalation. Experience presenting findings or project work to business and technical audiences. Key Measures of Success Frames business problems clearly and keeps work grounded in measurable outcomes and accountable ownership. Produces concise, useful analysis and documentation appropriate to the decision, risk, and next delivery step. Uses evidence and stakeholder feedback to recommend whether work should proceed, be refined, transition, or stop. Provides receiving teams with enough business context, requirements, risks, dependencies, and open decisions to continue the work. Demonstrates measurable growth in Wealth knowledge, business analysis, AI fluency, responsible technology practices, and communication.

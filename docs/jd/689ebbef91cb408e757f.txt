PS Regional Ops Director Business: Portfolio Services | Reports to: Global Portfolio Services COO What this job involves You'll be the in-region owner of Portfolio Services operations, and the single accountable leader for day-to-day performance. You'll exercise resource management and commercial management accountability day in and day out, and own delivery of the target operating model and cost-to-serve savings, from design into steady state. You'll apply the rules set by the PS Global COO, who ensures alignment across regions, and work with regional leaders so the region moves from local optimization to a shared operating model. What your day-to-day will look like: Sell : Go/No-Go and pursuit support. Apply the CRM Go/No-Go process to regional pursuits, and confirm that proposed staffing, pricing, and offshore mix are delivered at target margin before the business commits. Deliver : AI and offshore leverage. Deliver the adoption targets for AI tooling, automated templates, and offshore delivery. Challenge local teams that bypass them, and track adoption by team and project. Onboarding and mobilization. Make sure new accounts and engagements are staffed and mobilized on time, including technology onboarding, using standard templates, tools, and offshore hubs. Operate : Resource and commercial accountability. Hold daily accountability for resource management and commercial management in your region: staffing, utilization, burn-rate, margin, and billing discipline. Operating model and cost to serve. Own delivery of the target operating model and cost-to-serve savings in your region, from design into steady-state performance. Capacity planning. Run weekly capacity-planning sessions that move under-utilized staff across borders and route suitable delivery work to offshore hubs. Burn-rate and utilization control. Apply the burn-rate and utilization rules set for each business line, act on triggers and warnings, and prevent unbilled scope creep. Governance. Lead all governance and IMS activities, and apply MIS, CRM, and financial tracking standards consistently across the region. Client and network oversight. Provide CRM oversight for critical accounts and oversee the best-in-class network. Platform and cross-region alignment. Align regional operations with the JBS platform, and work with the PS Global Ops Director and other Regional Ops Directors for consistent delivery. Leadership and delegation. Act as the Head of Portfolio Services' delegate in key meetings where needed, sponsor regional initiatives, and build the next generation of Portfolio Services leaders. Regional reporting. Give the Global PS COO, regional leaders, and the PS Global Ops Director clear, timely views of utilization, margin, and delivery cost. What we're looking for Bachelor's degree in business, finance, or a related field. Experience in operations, resource management, or delivery leadership in professional services or portfolio services. Process-oriented, analytical, and commercially minded. Able to balance delivery standards with financial discipline, and willing to challenge local teams where necessary. Experience running resourcing or capacity processes across teams or borders, and leading offshore or shared-service teams. Credibility with senior management teams. They will regard you as a peer and a high performer. Why this role matters Regions have optimized locally for years, with country-based resource pools that lead to unbilled over-servicing and uneven benches. You'll be the single accountable owner who translates the common operating model into steady-state performance in your region, and who keeps resource and commercial discipline in place every day. Your work lifts utilization and gross margin in your region, builds the leadership pipeline behind you, and sets the model other regions will follow. Accelerate 2030. This role advances Win with the JLL Brand, Elevate Our People Advantage, Outpace through Data & AI, and Unlock Platform Excellence. PS AMER Ops Director Business: Portfolio Services (AMER regional; OP and Lease Administration global) | Reports to: Global PS COO What this job involves You'll lead Portfolio Services operations in the Americas with a larger remit than any other region. You'll support operations for Consulting and Energy Advisory in AMER, and OP and Lease Administration globally. You'll exercise resource management and commercial management accountability day in and day out, apply the rules set by the PS Global Ops Director, and drive one operating model across the regional and global business lines you lead. What your day-to-day will look like: Sell : Go/No-Go and pursuit support. Apply the CRM Go/No-Go process to pursuits in Consulting and Energy Advisory in AMER and in OP and Lease Administration globally, and confirm that proposed staffing, pricing, and offshore mix are delivered at target margin before the business commits. Deliver : AI and offshore leverage. Deliver the adoption targets for AI tooling, automated templates, and offshore delivery across your business lines. Challenge teams that bypass them. Onboarding and mobilization. Make sure new accounts and engagements across your business lines are staffed and mobilized on time, including technology onboarding, using standard templates, tools, and offshore hubs. Operate : Resource and commercial accountability. Hold daily accountability for resource management and commercial management across Consulting and Energy Advisory in AMER: staffing, utilization, burn-rate, margin, and billing discipline. Global OP and Lease Administration. Own operations for OP and Lease Administration globally. Apply the same resource and commercial discipline, and align both with the common operating model. Operating model and cost to serve. Own delivery of the target operating model and cost-to-serve savings across your remit, from design into steady-state performance. Capacity planning. Run weekly capacity-planning sessions that move under-utilized staff across borders and route suitable delivery work to offshore hubs. Governance. Lead governance and IMS activities, and apply MIS, CRM, and financial tracking standards consistently across your remit. Cross-regional alignment. Work with the PS Global Ops Director, other Regional Ops Directors, and the TM Ops Lead for consistent delivery. Align with the JBS platform. Leadership. Lead operations leaders across regions and business lines, sponsor regional and global initiatives, and build the next generation of Portfolio Services leaders. Reporting. Give the Global PS COO, regional leaders, and the PS Global Ops Director clear, timely views of utilization, margin, and delivery cost across your remit. What we're looking for Bachelor's degree in business, finance, or a related field. 10+ years in operations leadership within professional services or real estate services, including multi-business-line scope. Process-oriented, analytical, and commercially minded. Experience leading leaders across regions and business lines, with both regional and global accountability. Able to balance delivery standards with strict financial discipline, and willing to challenge local teams where necessary. Credibility with senior leaders across the business. They will regard you as a peer and a high performer. Why this role matters This is the largest operations remit in Portfolio Services outside the global role. It covers a full region and two global business lines, and it holds resource and commercial discipline together across all of them. Your results show up in utilization, margin, and cost to serve across AMER, OP, and Lease Administration. Accelerate 2030.

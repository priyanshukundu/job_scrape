Hi, we're Oscar. We're hiring a Staff Data Engineer to join our Data team. Oscar is the first health insurance company built around a full stack technology platform and a relentless focus on serving our members. We started Oscar in 2012 to create the kind of health insurance company we would want for ourselves—one that behaves like a doctor in the family. About the role: In this role, you will build the trusted member-context and event-data foundation that powers personalization, predictive models, and LLM-enabled experiences. This role will help build Oscar Health’s personalization platform. We combine longitudinal member context with real-time behavioral signals to forecast what is likely to happen for a member and determine the most useful next action for the member to engage across web and app, our member facing health AI agent (Oswell), tools used by Oscar teammates during member conversations, and intentional outbound engagement. The work includes predictive modeling; creating a catalogue of action and journey options to engage members with; recommendation and ranking systems; experimentation and measurement; and practical use of AI and LLMs to interpret context, support recommendations, and generate tailored experiences. We are looking for people who have built and operated personalization, recommender, ranking, predictive-modeling, search, decisioning, or applied-AI systems at meaningful scale. This is a unique opportunity to build an advanced platform leveraging the latest capabilities at an AI-forward company that owns its technology stack, with tangible benefits for members’ health and lives. You will report into the Senior Director, Data Science. Work Location: This position is based in our New York City office, requiring a hybrid work schedule with 3 days of in-office work per week.

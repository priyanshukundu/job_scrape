The VOD Engineering team is looking for a Software Engineer to join our engineering team responsible for building and supporting business critical video workflows, systems, and video-related cloud solutions. What the VOD Workflows team does The Video on Demand (VOD) Workflows team is responsible for the automated media pipeline delivering VOD content to NBCUniversal’s streaming platforms Troubleshoots issues arising in the VOD workflow and during VOD Playback. Configures encoding of video, audio, and subtitles for delivery across various platforms/devices. Research new technologies and innovations in the stream media industry for use in our system Collaborates with Operations, Player, and Client teams to drive innovation and improvements in video delivery. Role Responsibilities Design, build, deploy, and support an automated, highly resilient VOD workflow system that delivers content to NBCU streaming platforms, with attention to scalability, reliability, and maintainability. Partner with Product Owners to turn requirements and models into specifications, technical designs, and testable implementation plans for premium audio and video experiences. Design software and system interfaces, including APIs and data contracts, that support a large agile program and rapid development cycles. Design and evolve agentic workflows, including tool integration, orchestration, guardrails, observability, and human-in-the-loop controls, that improve media processing and engineering operations. Manage and maintain cloud-based content delivery and services, primarily on AWS. Work with vendors and the Media Engineering team to define clear requirements, deploy media-processing changes, and identify and resolve defects. Work independently and collaboratively, with transparency, autonomy, and a commitment to quality in assignments of every size. Continuously learn, improve, and optimize processes and ways of working, and help foster an inclusive team culture in which engineers from diverse backgrounds can grow. Bachelor’s degree or equivalent practical experience in Computer Science, Information Technology, or a related field 3–5 years of software development experience with streaming media, VOD or other media-processing workflows, or comparable ETL/workflow systems Proficiency in Go; Python experience is preferred Experience with web technologies, digital video workflows and operations, and video technology development Applied experience designing and building cloud-based systems, particularly on AWS Strong software design skills, including system decomposition, API and data-contract design, scalability, reliability, and maintainability Experience with specification-driven development, translating requirements and technical designs into testable implementation plans Hands-on experience building AI agents and agentic workflows, including tool integration, orchestration, guardrails, observability, and human-in-the-loop controls, is highly preferred.

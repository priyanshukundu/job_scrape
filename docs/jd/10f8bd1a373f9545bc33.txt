As a Portfolio Manager, AVP you will support the underwriting, credit monitoring, and portfolio management of Commercial Real Estate and/or Commercial, Corporate, or Specialized Banking relationships. This role is responsible for evaluating credit requests, managing portfolio risk, strengthening client relationships, and identifying opportunities to expand banking solutions that support client and business growth. Key Accountabilities Analyze financial information and credit requests to assess risk, evaluate loan opportunities, and recommend appropriate credit structures. Manage portfolio administration activities to ensure compliance with established credit policies, business strategies, and regulatory requirements. Monitor borrower performance, loan repayment activity, and financial condition, taking appropriate action to manage risk and support portfolio quality. Partner with Relationship Managers to support client relationships, coordinate renewals, and serve as a liaison between clients and internal business partners. Conduct due diligence and credit analysis for new and existing client relationships, including preparation of supporting credit documentation. Present loan recommendations and participate in the credit approval process, providing insight on credit quality and structure. Identify and refer opportunities for additional banking products and services to support client needs and deepen relationships. Represent the bank in community and professional organizations to enhance relationships and maintain a positive market presence. Education & Experience Required Bachelor’s degree in Business, Finance, Accounting, or an equivalent combination of education and experience. 2+ years of experience in Commercial Banking, Corporate Banking, Commercial Real Estate Banking, Specialized Banking, and/or Credit Approval. Preferred 2+ years of experience handling complex credit relationships. Sales experience. Formal credit training. Chartered Financial Analyst (CFA) designation. Access to reliable transportation and the ability to lawfully drive, if applicable. You'll gain exposure to complex commercial banking relationships and a broad range of banking products and services in a collaborative, relationship-focused environment.

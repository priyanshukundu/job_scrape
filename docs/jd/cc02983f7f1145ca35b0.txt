Benefiting from EY’s full spectrum of services, we’ve reimagined strategic consulting to work in a world of increasing complexity. EY-Parthenon’s Strategy and Execution – Strategy – Industrials & Energy practice helps the C-suite, business unit leaders, boards, and investors answer their hardest growth and value questions — where to play, how to win, and how to build the organization to deliver it. Our mandate is to own the CEO agenda. We develop and then deliver value creation opportunities for our clients by providing in-depth market, competitor and customer insight — and incorporate financial, operational, and implementation perspectives across their entire organization. As part of the team, you’ll have exposure to challenging, C-suite problem statements, and deliver at the highest standards of quality and pace. You’ll be part of an intensive learning and development program on our common way of working, as well as a structured mentorship model. You’ll receive candid, real-time feedback. And through this work and coaching you’ll build a deep strategy toolkit, expand your abilities as an executive advisor, and advance quickly within our flat team. Your key responsibilities As a Senior Associate/Consultant with EY-Parthenon’s Strategy and Execution – Strategy – Industrials & Energy practice, you own a workstream — a discrete piece of the project answer. You’ll work closely with your project leadership on the recommendations to our clients, and you’ll guide the analysts working alongside you. More specifically, your responsibilities include: Own the delivery of a workstream end-to-end — frame the key questions, propose the plan and approach, adapt our frameworks to the specifics of the case, and drive it to client-ready insights with limited guidance. Lead hypothesis generation and problem structuring for your workstream across a variety of business issues. Build the analysis. Independently construct standard models, including valuation and DCF work, and build more tailored models (e.g., market sizing, growth modeling) for other business questions with guidance. Use AI where it makes the work better or faster, apply business judgment to what comes out of it, and validate the outputs before they reach the team’s answer. Develop meaningful insights from your research and analysis and put those findings into the client’s context. Create the initial storyline for your findings with clear horizontal and vertical logic, and produce error-free, client-ready deliverables and communications. Lead primary research — run expert interviews with consistency to compare findings across conversations and the instinct to pursue an unexpected thread, and write surveys that achieve the research objective. Triangulate secondary research, understand the limitations of your sources, and propose workarounds when the data does not cooperate. Develop and communicate the day-to-day project plan for your workstream, and flag obstacles early. Coach the analysts on your workstream — identify their strengths and development needs, and give them real feedback. Team up with colleagues across EY, including our cross-border teams, on jointly delivered engagements. Support proposal development and contribute to practice development efforts such as recruiting, intellectual capital, or tools and methods. Skills and attributes for success Problem structuring. You can take a defined business issue, generate the hypotheses, and build a path to the answer for your workstream. Insight generation. You go beyond summarizing the analysis to saying what it means, and you can shape that into a storyline. Written communication. You write with structure and concision, and you can build a storyline that holds together logically. Analytical judgment. You are fluent in Excel and modeling, you understand the three financial statements (income statement, balance sheet, cash flow statement), and you can spot when an output does not look right. AI fluency. You know where AI helps and where it misleads, you use it well in your own work, and you hold a consistently high standard and burden of proof when pressure testing AI outputs. Sector curiosity. You learn the fundamentals of a new business or sector quickly, and you are willing to go deep in Industrial Products. Teamwork. You keep the team informed, stay steady under pressure, and make the people around you better. Entrepreneurial streak. You hustle, and are energized by innovating new tools and methodologies as well as contributing to practice development. To qualify for the role, you must have: A bachelor’s degree with strong academic performance and at least 2 years of related work experience; or a graduate degree and approximately 18 months of related work experience. Management consulting or comparable analytical experience, with a track record of owning a workstream through to a client-ready answer. Strong quantitative skills, including the ability to build and interrogate financial statements. The ability to research industries, markets, competitors, and customers, and to draw out what matters. Experience conducting primary research, including leading interviews with senior business audiences. Proficiency in English, including the ability to communicate effectively in writing and verbally in a professional environment. The expectation for this role is in person teaming, at client site or in an EY office, a minimum of 60% of the time or on average 3 days a week over the course of an engagement, project or performance year. You must either reside in or be in a commutable distance to your office location for this position. The ability and willingness to travel and work in excess of standard hours when necessary. In certain circumstances, travel may be required beyond your work location based on client and project needs. Ideally, you will also have: Experience in industrial products or adjacent sectors — manufacturing, building products, distribution, transportation and logistics, or energy and chemicals. Practical experience applying AI tools to research, analysis, or synthesis in a professional setting. Knowledge of how to leverage firm-approved AI tools in a business setting, including Microsoft Copilot. Experience coaching or mentoring junior teammates. What we look for: People who can own a piece of the answer and run with it. We are looking for strong problem solvers with an entrepreneurial streak who want to build a durable strategy toolkit inside a team that will invest in them — and who hold themselves to a high standard when the work is hard.

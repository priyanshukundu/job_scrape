First Hawaiian Bank is currently seeking a Model Risk Team Lead to join our Analytics Risk Management Department in Downtown Honolulu. The Model Risk Management Team Lead is responsible for the strategic leadership, independent challenge, and oversight of First Hawaiian Bank’s enterprise‑wide Model Risk Management (MRM) program. This role partners with executive leadership, business units, model owners, internal audit, regulators, and other risk functions to ensure models are developed, implemented, monitored, and governed in a safe, effective, and regulatory‑compliant manner, while advancing the Bank’s adoption of advanced analytics, machine learning, and emerging AI technologies. Work Schedule: Monday-Friday 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM (hours may vary) Compensation: The salary range for this role is $130,000 - $165,000/yr; commensurate with experience. Set strategic direction and continuously enhance the enterprise Model Risk Management program, including policies, standards, and governance frameworks. Provide independent challenge and expert guidance on model design, methodology, assumptions, controls, and performance to senior leadership and risk committees. Oversee independent validation, performance monitoring, and risk‑based remediation across all model types and risk domains. Ensure comprehensive model inventory management, classification, risk‑rating, and tracking throughout the model lifecycle. Represent the Model Risk Management function in regulatory examinations, internal audits, and external reviews. Lead, coach, and develop a team of model risk professionals, managing resources, priorities, and vendor relationships. Drive organizational change initiatives, training, and awareness programs to embed robust model governance enterprise‑wide. Define the long‑term vision and roadmap for integrating advanced analytics, machine learning, generative and agentic AI governance into the model risk framework.

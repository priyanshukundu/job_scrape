About the team The Agentic AI team at Zillow is at the forefront of transforming the real estate industry by helping millions of people use AI assistants to find their next home. We aim to redefine the home shopping and transaction experience by providing an always-on assistant that integrates personalized user insights with Zillow's knowledge. Within this organization, our team owns how the assistant remembers and understands each user across a home search that can last for months: agent memory, context engineering, and the evaluation of agents running in production. We are a senior, fast-moving team of machine learning engineers who do the science and own taking it to production. About the role As a Principal Machine Learning Engineer on the Agentic AI team, you will partner closely with Engineering, Science, Product, and Platform leaders to advance Zillow’s agentic capabilities. You will: Shape the architecture for agent memory, how Zillow’s assistant reasons about, writes, retrieves, updates, and retires memories across experiences. Drive context engineering for production agents by assembling, prioritizing, and compressing context from conversation history, user memory, tools, and skills so every response is relevant, fast, and cost-efficient Collaborate across federated projects to build and orchestrate agents end to end, from prompt and context design through deployment and production operations Design and develop offline and online evaluation frameworks for memory and context quality, using them to guide product and technical decisions Stay at the forefront of emerging agentic paradigms, including memory architectures, long-context techniques, and self-evolving agentic systems, and translate them into tangible product innovation Lead complex, multi-team, cross-functional initiatives; align with peer technical leaders; and communicate technical direction clearly across Engineering, partner teams, and leadership This role has been categorized as a Remote position. “Remote” employees do not have a permanent corporate office workplace and, instead, work from a physical location of their choice, which must be identified to the Company.

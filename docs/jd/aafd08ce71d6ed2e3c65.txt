For our Associate Director, Individual Wealth position, we are seeking an experienced Financial Advisor to serve and grow a premier book of approximately $120 million in assets under management (AUM), and work collaboratively with internal specialists across investment management, estate planning, tax, insurance, and workplace financial benefits to deliver integrated, client-centered solutions. This role requires strong technical expertise, sound judgment, exceptional communication skills, and a demonstrated ability to deepen existing relationships and develop new business. Key responsibilities for this position include: • Portfolio Management and Wealth Advisory: Deliver comprehensive investment and wealth management advice aligned with clients' objectives, risk tolerance, liquidity needs, tax considerations, and long-term financial goals. • Financial Planning: Develop and implement integrated financial plans addressing cash flow, retirement, education funding, estate planning, charitable giving, business ownership, intergenerational wealth, and liquidity events. • Client Communication and Education: Explain complex market dynamics, economic trends, investment strategies, and planning recommendations in clear, accessible language. Proactively communicate during periods of market volatility or significant change. • Relationship Management: Build and maintain trusted relationships with high-net-worth individuals, families, business owners, and executives. Serve as a primary point of contact and strategic advisor on clients' broader financial needs. • Workplace Financial Benefits Advisory: Advise clients and their employees on the financial planning implications of workplace compensation and benefit programs, including equity compensation, deferred compensation, tax strategies, and insurance coverage. See the dedicated section below. • Integrated Solutions and Collaboration: Partner with colleagues across investment management, estate planning, tax advisory, insurance, and other service lines to identify client needs and coordinate appropriate solutions. Make introductions and facilitate collaboration in a manner that prioritizes client outcomes rather than product-based selling. • Business Development and AUM Growth: Deliver exceptional service to existing clients while identifying opportunities to deepen relationships, and attract additional assets. • Investment and Planning Implementation: Translate recommendations into clear action plans, coordinate implementation with internal partners and third-party providers, and monitor progress against agreed objectives. • Compliance and Documentation: Maintain accurate and complete client records; document recommendations, client meetings, suitability considerations, and follow-up actions; and ensure all activities comply with applicable regulatory, fiduciary, and firm requirements. • Team Contribution and Thought Leadership: Participate in client presentations, educational events, business development initiatives, and internal knowledge-sharing activities.

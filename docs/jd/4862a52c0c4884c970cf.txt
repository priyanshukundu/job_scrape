Our vision is to transform how the world uses information to enrich life for all . Micron Technology is a world leader in innovating memory and storage solutions that accelerate the transformation of information into intelligence, inspiring the world to learn, communicate and advance faster than ever. Micron’s US Site Procurement Operations is seeking a high-performing Capital Supplier Manager to lead capital procurement and spares management activities across Boise factories. This position is critical to supporting industry-leading Technology Development and the ID1 Greenfield High-Volume Manufacturing (HVM) startup! The role offers responsibility in factory startup readiness, supplier engagement, inventory and spares strategy, operational expense & budget management, and digital transformation initiatives. The Capital Supplier Manager will work closely with cross-functional teams and play a key role in capital procurement, supplier management, and process optimization. Responsibilities Own one or more factory areas for capital equipment, spare parts, and services procurement. Lead capital equipment scheduling, delivery, and purchase order management with suppliers. Manage supplier contract negotiations and ongoing contract administration. Build and maintain strong supplier relationships; resolve delivery and supply continuity issues. Drive automation and digital transformation of procurement processes and metrics. Track and report performance using key process indicators; lead continuous improvement initiatives. Analyze and resolve forecast disconnects to meet demand volume and timing. Identify and execute operational expense reduction opportunities. Ensure compliance with company policies and departmental procedures. Represent Procurement with a customer-centric approach to issue resolution. Lead supplier QBR documentation and meetings, ensuring alignment with Fab and TD teams. Embrace and integrate AI-enabled tools and ways of working, where applicable, to enhance productivity, streamline processes, and support Micron’s broader AI transformation. Minimum Requirements Bachelor’s or master’s degree in supply chain, Industrial Engineering, Operations, Engineering, Business, or related field. 0 - 2 years of total experience in procurement, supply chain, engineering or operations (including internships, co-ops, or full-time roles). Strong preference for candidates who have completed an internship or co-op at Micron in procurement, operations, or engineering. Demonstrated ability to increase efficiency through the use of AI and automation tools Advanced proficiency in Excel, PowerPoint, and Microsoft tools; experience with ERP systems (SAP/Oracle) preferred. Proven track record in supplier negotiation, contract management, and project leadership (internship/co-op experience may qualify). Preferred Requirements: Strong analytical, organizational, and communication skills. Ability to work independently and collaboratively in a fast-paced environment. Demonstrated interest in digital transformation, AI, and automation in procurement. Willingness to work extended hours as needed based on business needs Demonstrated experience applying AI tools and capabilities to enhance productivity, automate workflows, and improve business outcomes.

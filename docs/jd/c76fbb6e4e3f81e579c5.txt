Join our Advanced Processing Architectures organization as a Principal Embedded Platform Software Engineer, where you will influence the design, development, and integration of the foundational software that enables secure, deterministic, and high‑performance operation of next‑generation avionics processing platforms. In this role, you will define technical direction across programs, shape platform architecture, and guide teams developing critical early‑boot, hardware‑bring‑up, HAL, diagnostic, and platform‑security components that support advanced SoC and ASIC‑based systems. You will collaborate across systems, hardware, software, and verification disciplines to ensure cohesive platform behavior, introduce innovative solutions to complex hardware/software challenges, and drive engineering excellence through technical mentorship and cross‑team leadership. This role provides an opportunity to influence program‑level strategy, strengthen development workflows, and elevate the technical capabilities of the broader platform engineering community.

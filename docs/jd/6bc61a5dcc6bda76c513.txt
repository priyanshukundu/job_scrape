Transportation Data Scientist (Mid-Level)
Leidos
Location: McLean, VA
Link: https://leidos.wd5.myworkdayjobs.com/External/job/McLean-VA/Transportation-Data-Scientist--Mid-Level-_R-00197165

Are you interested in improving and shaping the transportation industry alongside a group of intelligent and motivated people? Leidos operates the Federal Highway Administration's (FHWA) Saxton Transportation Operations Laboratory (STOL), a USDOT research lab focused on improving transportation operations, safety, mobility, and environmental impacts. STOL supports the advancement and deployment of emerging technologies, including vehicle automation and communication. Leidos is seeking a mid-level Transportation Data Scientist to support FHWA-funded projects at the intersection of AI, data science, and transportation. You will build, deploy, and evaluate AI/ML models for applications such as vehicle load classification using weigh-in-motion (WIM) data and imagery, crash prediction for traffic management centers (TMCs), and data ecosystems for trustworthy AI. We are looking for a hands-on applied data scientist who has taken models from raw data all the way to deployment, has real experience with generative AI, and wants to apply AI to state and federal transportation problems. Transportation experience is a plus but not a requirement. We will help you learn the domain. Because STOL is a research lab, the work also includes literature reviews, testing new methods, and contributing to technical reports. A research background is not required, but you should enjoy that part of the job. Location: This role is based at the customer site in McLean, VA with a current hybrid plan of four days onsite. Candidate MUST: Have lived in the United States for the three most recent consecutive years and be eligible for a Public Trust clearance. https://careers.leidos.com/search/jobs?q=stol&ns_job_category=stol-jobs Primary Responsibilities Build ETL pipelines that clean, normalize, enrich, and fuse multi-source data (e.g., traffic logs, imagery, weather, and permitting records), and address quality issues such as inconsistency, sparsity, and bias. Design, develop, and deploy AI/ML models for transportation applications, including generative AI and LLM-based solutions. Package and deploy models into working environments, and support MLOps practices such as versioning, monitoring, and retraining. Evaluate model performance under varied conditions, such as differing data quality, and recommend ways to improve robustness, scalability, and trustworthiness in real-world transportation settings. Conduct targeted literature and data reviews on AI methods, datasets, and technologies relevant to freight analytics, traffic safety, and operations (e.g., sensor fusion, computer vision, and multimodal AI). Support stakeholder outreach, including peer exchanges, workshops, and technical briefings with state DOTs, MPOs, enforcement agencies, and vendors. Work with cross-functional teams to keep projects aligned with USDOT goals, including risk management, quality assurance, and compliance with federal standards. Contribute to monthly progress reports, risk mitigation, and iterative model refinement based on federal feedback. Required Qualifications Education and experience (one of the following) Bachelor’s degree in computer science, Data Science, Artificial Intelligence, Transportation Engineering, or a related field, plus 4+ years of relevant professional experience. Master's degree in one of the fields above, plus 2+ years of relevant professional experience. Relevant professional experience means non-academic, hands-on work in data science or AI/ML. Graduate research and coursework do not count toward this minimum. Hands-on experience in “at least two of the three” core areas Data preparation and ETL: You have built pipelines that ingest and integrate imperfect, real-world data from multiple sources, including multimodal data such as imagery, sensor data, or time series. Model deployment: You have taken at least one model beyond a notebook and into a deployed or production setting, and you are familiar with MLOps tools and practices (e.g., Docker, MLflow, CI/CD, or cloud ML services). Generative AI: You have personally built solutions using LLMs or other generative models (e.g., retrieval-augmented generation, fine-tuning, prompt design, or evaluating generative outputs). Also required Strong Python skills and working knowledge of common ML and data libraries (e.g., PyTorch or TensorFlow, Scikit-learn, Pandas, NumPy). Solid understanding of model evaluation (e.g., precision, recall, F1, AUC), and the ability to explain why a model works, where it falls short, and how you troubleshoot it. Clear communication skills, including technical writing and presenting AI concepts to non-technical audiences. Ability to work in a fast-paced, research-oriented environment, with travel up to 20% for stakeholder meetings, site visits, or conference support. Ability to obtain and maintain a Public Trust clearance (requires three years of immediate residency in the US). Legal authorization to work in the United States. Preferred Qualifications Ph.D. in a related field. Experience working with state DOTs or federal transportation agencies (e.g., FHWA, USDOT) on AI initiatives, including prototyping AI applications in ITS. Computer vision or deep learning experience, especially with imagery or video. Familiarity with transportation data sources (e.g., HSIS, SHRP2, NGSIM) and standards (e.g., SAE J2735 for V2X). Experience with synthetic data generation (including GANs) or physics-informed ML. Knowledge of federal AI governance, risk management, and equity considerations in transportation. Experience leading AI tasks in multi-agency initiatives or contributing to communities of practice (CoPs). Publications or presentations at AI or transportation conferences (e.g., TRB, ITS America).

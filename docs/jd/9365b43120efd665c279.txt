Job Description Provides financial leadership and decision support for assigned business, technology, or capital portfolios. Partners with business and finance leaders to deliver planning, forecasting, performance reporting, and investment analysis. Works independently on complex assignments, recommends actions based on financial insights, and supports broader financial strategy and resource allocation decisions. Key Responsibilities Financial Partnership & Decision Support Partner with business and finance leaders to provide financial insights that support operational, investment, and resource allocation decisions. Provide recommendations based on analysis of financial results, trends, risks, and opportunities. Translate complex financial and operational data into clear, actionable information for management audiences. Budgeting, Forecasting & Performance Management Manage budgeting and forecasting activities for assigned portfolios, including operating and capital expenditures. Perform monthly and quarterly variance analysis, identify key drivers, and recommend appropriate follow-up actions. Maintain forecast accuracy and financial integrity by validating assumptions, reviewing source data, and improving analytical methods. Support month-end close activities, including general ledger review, reconciliations, accruals, and explanations of actual results versus forecast. Capital & Investment Management Provide financial support for development, technology, and capital programs from business case development through ongoing reporting and performance tracking. Prepare investment proposals, funding requests, and approval materials for management and governance review. Support quarterly and annual capital reporting, highlighting performance trends, risks, and opportunities. Apply established financial controls, standards, and governance requirements for capital and expense management. Analytics & Financial Modeli ng Develop and maintain financial models, forecasting tools, and analytical frameworks used in planning and decision-making. Conduct ad hoc financial analyses, benchmarking, and internal consulting for assigned business partners. Identify and resolve data issues within financial reporting systems to improve the accuracy, consistency, and reliability of reporting. Cross-Functional Collaboration & Process Improvement Collaborate with technology, development, accounting, FP&A, and other finance teams to align financial plans with operational execution. Influence cross-functional partners through strong business acumen, sound analysis, and clear communication. Identify and support process improvements that enhance financial transparency, reporting effectiveness, controls, and analytics capabilities. Team Contribution & Developmen t Provide guidance and subject-matter support to analysts and peers on financial analysis, modeling, and reporting practices. Review financial analyses and management presentations for accuracy, clarity, and completeness. Contribute to the development and adoption of finance tools, templates, and best practices. Basic Qualifications Bachelor’s degree in Finance, Accounting, Economics, or a related field, or equivalent experience. Typically 5 or more years of progressive experience in financial analysis, FP&A, accounting, or capital program finance. Preferred Skills / Experience Strong knowledge of financial analysis, forecasting, planning, and capital investment management. Advanced Excel and financial modeling skills. Experience supporting management and senior leadership. Ability to interpret complex data, develop recommendations, and influence business decisions. Experience with financial reporting systems, data mining, and visualization tools. Demonstrated business partnership, communication, and informal leadership capabilities. Master’s degree, CPA, CMA, or comparable professional credential preferred. *This role requires working from a U.S. Bank location three (3) or more days per week.

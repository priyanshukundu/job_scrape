Role Overview We're looking for a Software Engineer to independently drive the design and delivery of features across our customer-facing GTM applications, services, and APIs. This is a hands-on engineering role — you'll own medium-to-high complexity features end-to-end, from design through production, and act as a go-to subject matter expert for at least one of our core domains, such as commercial workflow automation, product discovery, or platform integrations. You'll partner closely with your Product Manager and Scrum Master to keep the team's plans realistic, support and coach less experienced engineers, and help raise the bar on quality across your team. Key Responsibilities Independently drive your own designs and take on features of increasing complexity across commercial workflow automation, product discovery, platform integrations, and customer-facing experiences. Deliver high-quality features end-to-end as a developer, reviewer, and tester — from design through execution. Solve complex implementation problems, make sound design decisions, and act as the subject matter expert for at least one of the team's domains. Understand, investigate, and resolve complex production bugs; use and improve the team's tooling and instrumentation to debug issues and ship fixes. Write design specs and run research spikes as needed; help assess and evaluate the feasibility of feature requests and issues. Act as a key technical partner to your Product Manager, Lead Engineers, and Scrum Master — help slice stories and epics, define tasks, and surface dependencies and risks so the team's forecasts stay realistic. Understand how your work fits into the bigger picture for customers, and ensure your features are properly monitored and measured so Product Management and your team understand how customers experience them in the real world. Apply modern engineering best practices to your own designs; proactively seek and integrate feedback from peers and senior engineers, and give clear, constructive feedback in reviews. Regularly assess code quality in your area and bring concrete improvement suggestions to planning; help ensure your team's code and designs meet quality standards. Take ownership of your features in production — monitor them proactively and drive improvements to monitoring and service-ownership practices. Provide technical coaching to AMTS and MTS engineers through pair programming and code reviews; help delegate work in ways that grow more junior teammates. Communicate clearly with internal partners and stakeholders — document features and processes for peers and Support, partner with Technical Writers on customer-facing docs, and be comfortable presenting your team's work to your peer group or org. Qualifications A related technical degree required. 5+ years of experience in software engineering, with a track record of independently designing and delivering features in distributed systems. Strong grasp of engineering fundamentals — fault tolerance, concurrency, API design, data modeling, maintainability, security, scalability, testability. Hands-on experience across the full SDLC (hybrid engineering), owning features from design through production support. Experience contributing to high-scale services in Salesforce Core platform and/or distributed service environments. Strong written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to explain technical trade-offs clearly to both technical and non-technical stakeholders. Demonstrated ability to mentor and support less experienced engineers. Passion for learning and staying current with industry trends and technologies. Preferred Qualifications Experience with e-commerce, checkout, order, quoting, payment, tax, or invoicing systems and related technologies. Experience designing APIs that serve multiple consumers, including Connect APIs, with financial-grade data accuracy. Familiarity with Lightning Web Components (LWC) and the Salesforce Core platform services and integrations behind them. Experience participating in post-incident reviews and driving process improvements for critical systems. Skill translating feature requirements into clear, actionable technical designs.

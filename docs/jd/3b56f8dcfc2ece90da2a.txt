Job Title Portfolio Administration Associate Manager Job Description Summary The Portfolio Administration Associate Manager is responsible for overseeing the daily operations and performance of a team of Portfolio Administration Analysts supporting assigned real estate portfolios. This role provides leadership, coaching, and operational oversight to ensure timely and accurate processing of landlord communications, invoice validations, dispute management activities, and lease administration deliverables. The Associate Manager serves as the primary point of support and escalation for analysts, ensuring adherence to established procedures, service level agreements (SLAs), audit controls, and client expectations. This position partners closely with Relationship Management, Process Management, Finance, and client stakeholders to drive operational excellence, maintain data integrity, and support continuous process improvement. Job Description Essential Functions and Responsibilities Team Leadership & Performance Management Oversee and support Portfolio Administration Analysts within assigned regions, ensuring effective execution of responsibilities and consistent service delivery. Monitor daily workloads and queue management to ensure timely processing of landlord communications, invoice validations, reconciliation reviews, and dispute resolutions. Provide coaching, guidance, and ongoing performance feedback to team members. Conduct regular quality reviews and identify opportunities to improve accuracy, consistency, and productivity. Ensure compliance with established procedures, internal controls, client requirements, and SLAs. Support performance management activities, including goal setting, development planning, and corrective action when necessary. Coordinate onboarding and training for new analysts and provide ongoing support related to process, system, and policy updates. Promote a collaborative and accountable team environment focused on customer service and operational excellence. Operations & Portfolio Administration Serve as the primary escalation point for complex landlord issues, billing discrepancies, reconciliation disputes, and lease administration concerns. Monitor invoice processing, payment support activities, landlord correspondence, and dispute resolution efforts to ensure timely completion. Review aging reports and operational metrics to proactively address workflow bottlenecks, service risks, and backlog concerns. Oversee application cleanup activities and ensure data integrity within lease administration and portfolio management systems. Validate adherence to audit requirements and documentation standards across all portfolio administration activities. Partner with internal stakeholders to resolve portfolio issues and support business continuity. Act as operational backup for analysts during planned and unplanned absences. Support the resolution of escalated landlord inquiries and sensitive client matters. Collaboration & Continuous Improvement Partner with the Relationship Management Lead, Process Manager, and cross-functional stakeholders on regional and enterprise initiatives. Identify opportunities for process improvement, standardization, automation, and operational efficiency. Assist in developing and maintaining standard operating procedures, training materials, and process documentation. Monitor SLA performance, operational trends, and key performance indicators and communicate findings to leadership. Escalate operational risks, recurring issues, and systemic concerns appropriately and timely. Support implementation of process changes, system enhancements, and client-driven initiatives. Required Qualifications High school diploma or equivalent required; associate or bachelor’s degree preferred. Minimum three years of experience in lease administration, portfolio administration, real estate operations, financial operations, contract administration, or a related field. Previous experience leading, training, mentoring, or coordinating the work of others. Strong understanding of workflow management, service delivery standards, and operational controls. Experience managing competing priorities in a fast-paced, deadline-driven environment. Strong analytical, problem-solving, and decision-making skills. Proficiency with Microsoft Outlook, Excel, Teams, SharePoint, and portfolio management systems. Strong verbal and written communication skills with the ability to effectively interact with clients, landlords, and internal stakeholders. Preferred Qualifications Experience supporting commercial real estate portfolios and lease administration functions. Experience managing landlord communications, invoice validation, reconciliation reviews, and dispute resolution activities. Familiarity with lease administration systems, workflow tools, and reporting platforms. Experience monitoring SLA performance, workload balancing, and operational reporting. Strong client-service orientation and experience supporting an onsite client environment. Experience leading process improvement initiatives and driving operational efficiencies.

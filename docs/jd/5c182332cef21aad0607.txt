Salary Range $142,900.00 - $235,800.00 Target Openings 1 What Is the Opportunity? As a model monitoring lead at Travelers, you will drive the engineering behind how Business Insurance monitors the models that support our pricing decisions, working alongside data science and actuarial teams who own the methodology. You will build much of it yourself, including automated data pipelines and data quality checks that help us find issues earlier and with less manual effort. This role brings execution and strategy together. We are looking for someone to not only build out capabilities, but also shape the strategy that makes model monitoring consistent, automated and built in from day one, on data our partners can rely on. With no direct reports your impact comes from technical credibility, clear communication and strong relationships. The ideal candidate pairs deep data engineering skills with the ability to guide decisions across teams, explain tradeoffs in plain terms and follow through on the work. Your contributions will help the people who use our models act with confidence on what those models are telling them. What Will You Do? Drive the engineering approach for model monitoring, setting direction on architecture and consistent design patterns, while data science and actuarial teams remain accountable for methodology Create the strategy and build the framework that engineering teams adopt in their model implementations, so monitoring is in place from day one when a model goes live Catalog current monitoring processes and data quality checks, then agree on a prioritized roadmap with data science and actuarial partners that targets the most manual processes and the biggest gaps Lead development of analytic data products, including pilots and proof of concepts. Execute data strategies with an understanding of enterprise architecture, consumption patterns, platforms and application infrastructure. Manage the operationalizing and automating of all capabilities to ensure secure, supported and scalable solutions. Present analysis and recommendations to help influence management decisions. Guide and coach team members to accelerate career development. Establish team processes and procedures. Maintain relationships with partner teams to ensure needs are met and impacted areas plan work accordingly. Perform other duties as assigned. What Will Our Ideal Candidate Have? Bachelor’s Degree in STEM related field or equivalent. Five or more years of related experience. Two or more years of management experience.

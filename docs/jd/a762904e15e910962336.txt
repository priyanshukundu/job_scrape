Data Integrity Specialist, Funding/Onboarding
Valley National Bank
Location: Wayne, NJ, USA | St Petersburg, FL, USA
Link: https://myjobs.adp.com/vnbcareers/cx/job-details?reqId=5001238498106

Responsibilities include but are not limited to: Monitor the disbursement of loan funds through wire transfer, internal transfers verifying for accuracy and appropriate approval. Communicate with internal and external parties, such as lending specialists, lending Officer, title companies, or outside legal counsel. Review commercial loan closing documents to ensure completeness, accuracy, and compliance with company policies and procedures. Interpret complex loan documents, including credit agreements, closing statements, to properly input all loan terms and collateral and loan covenants. Accurately book new, renewed, and modified commercial loans into the core banking system. Ensure all necessary data fields are populated correctly to maintain data integrity across core loan banking systems. Perform quality control checks on loans booked by reviewing data integrity for accuracy. Address any data integrity errors timely. Partner with Lending Teams, other Loan Servicing teams to resolve issues and ensure alignment with procedures. Comply with all bank policies, internal procedures, and applicable regulatory requirements and support all audit and quality control reviews.

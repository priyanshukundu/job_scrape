The Impact you will have in this role: The FP&A Director provides financial leadership and strategic decision support across DTCC’s multiple business lines. Serving as a trusted advisor to business General Managers and senior leadership teams, the Director is accountable for comprehensive financial management of the businesses, including P&L performance, budgeting and forecasting, pricing and fee analysis, investment evaluation, expense management, and balance sheet and cash flow oversight. The Director will partner closely with stakeholders to translate business strategy, market dynamics, and operating trends into forward-looking financial insights and actionable recommendations. The role requires a strong understanding of U.S. capital markets and multiple asset classes, as well as the ability to assess the financial implications of macroeconomic, geopolitical, regulatory, competitive, and industry developments. Your Primary Responsibilities: Business Partnership & Financial Leadership Serve as the primary finance partner for multiple business lines, including leading the annual budget, long-range forecast, monthly forecast, and monthly performance management processes across multiple stakeholders. Support executive management with forward-looking insights on cost trends, capacity, and funding requirements. Provide objective financial counsel, challenge assumptions, and translate business strategies into clear financial implications, trade-offs, risks, and opportunities. Partner with the Enterprise Transformation (ET) team to support investment prioritization and funding decisions across each business line. Develop financial reporting and recommendations for business leaders, the Chief Financial Office, executive management, and the Board. Strategic P&L Management Own P&L management, including revenue, operating expenses, allocations, profitability, and key performance drivers. Evaluate and optimize fee structures and pricing strategies using client, product, volume, competitive, and profitability analysis. Develop and assess business cases for new products, services, investments, and strategic initiatives, including returns, sensitivities, risks, and value realization measures. Forecast revenue and transaction volumes by linking historical trends and business drivers with macroeconomic, market, geopolitical, regulatory, and competitive factors. Monitor actual results against plan and forecast; identify underlying drivers, emerging trends, risks, and opportunities, and recommend corrective actions. Establish meaningful financial and operating metrics to assess product, client, and business performance. Expense, Resource & Allocation Management Drive disciplined expense management, workforce planning, and resource prioritization aligned with strategic and financial objectives. Identify and evaluate cost optimization, productivity, process improvement, and investment reallocation opportunities while considering business and control requirements. Collaborate with shared service functions to understand support costs and how they are allocated to the businesses; increase transparency into cost drivers and consumption to help identify and influence expense reduction opportunities. Balance Sheet, Cash Flow & Financial Governance Project balance sheet and cash flow impacts of new debt issuances, including liquidity and capital implications, and support related capital raise decisions. Partner with Accounting, Treasury, Tax, Risk, and other control functions to ensure financial plans and decisions reflect applicable accounting, regulatory, liquidity, and capital considerations. Maintain strong financial governance, accurate management reporting, and effective controls across planning, forecasting, and performance management processes.

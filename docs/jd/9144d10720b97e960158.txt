Job Description: At Remitly, we believe everyone deserves the freedom to access, move, and manage their money wherever life takes them. Since 2011, we’ve tirelessly delivered on our promise to customers sending money globally, providing secure, simple, and reliable ways to manage their money, ensuring true peace of mind. Whether it’s supporting loved ones back home, growing a business across continents, or pursuing new opportunities abroad, we’re not just here to move money— we’re here to move our global customers forward. We’re looking for builders, reimaginers, and global thinkers who want to work at the intersection of technology, trust, and transformation. If that’s you and you’re ready to do the most meaningful work of your career — we invite you to join over 3,000 passionate Remitlians worldwide who are united by our vision to lift lives, one cross-border story at a time. About the Role Remitly Business is building a two-sided platform that helps businesses and individuals in emerging markets move money across borders. On the send side, we let businesses pay vendors, suppliers, and contractors abroad. On the receive side, we let businesses and sellers get paid in USD, hold balances in stable currency, and withdraw across Remitly's 170+ country disbursement network. As a Senior Full Stack Software Engineer on Remitly Business, you will build product and platform across both sides of this marketplace — from customer-facing onboarding and payment experiences to the services, APIs, and money-movement flows beneath them. You will be a key technical voice on a senior, cross-functional team, owning features end to end and raising the quality bar for those around you. You will work in a hybrid capacity from our Seattle office. You Will Design and ship full stack features end to end — from React frontends to backend services, APIs, and data — breaking complex work into parallel workstreams and driving it from design through launch. Use modern AI-assisted and spec-driven development to move faster, while owning the architecture, correctness, and quality that tooling cannot guarantee on its own. Set the technical standard with systems that are well-tested, resilient, extensible, and scalable, and that make it easy for others to contribute. Build for money movement at scale, delivering idempotent, observable flows with correct ledgering, reconciliation, and compliance gating. Uphold our Foundational Excellence standards for security, reliability, and observability, resolving risks before they become incidents. Develop and deliver short- and long-term roadmaps, making tradeoffs across priorities, user experience, and a sustainable technical foundation. Collaborate with partners across Product, Design, Analytics, Compliance, Infrastructure, and Operations to turn customer needs into scalable solutions. You Have A degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field, or equivalent practical experience. 4+ years of engineering experience building backend services in a collaborative, cross-functional team environment. Experience as a full stack engineer with React/TypeScript and Go. Experience designing secure, scalable, performant software on cloud infrastructure (e.g., AWS). Experience working on large features in partnership with Product Management and other stakeholders. Comfort with, or strong enthusiasm for, AI-assisted development tools as a force multiplier. Clear, objective written and in-person communication, including the ability to articulate technical trade-offs to technical and non-technical stakeholders.

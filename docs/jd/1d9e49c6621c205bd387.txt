Job Description As a Senior Software Engineer in our camera systems group, you will play a crucial role in designing, developing, and optimizing modern C++ code for embedded systems. You will work on advanced algorithms and embedding AI models directly on our camera hardware, enabling real-time analysis of video feeds for critical safety and security applications. This position requires a deep understanding of modern C++, strong software engineering principles, and experience with camera technologies. Responsibilities: Design, develop, and test high-performance modern C++ code for implementing computer vision algorithms and deploying AI models on embedded camera platforms. Optimize code and GPU accelerate for performance within the resource constraints of embedded systems. Develop unit, integration, and performance tests for quality. Participate in code reviews and debugging, and contribute to documentation and best practices Contribute to new camera product development and features Qualifications: Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field. Masters or Ph.D. is a plus. 3+ years of experience in C++ software development, with emphasis on product delivery and embedded performance optimization. Experience working with camera systems, including image processing, video encoding/decoding, and camera SDKs/APIs. Experience deploying AI models on embedded platforms is highly desired. Proficiency in Python for scripting and tooling is a plus. Strong understanding of software engineering principles, including design patterns, testing methodologies, version control and CI/CD Experience with Linux/embedded Linux development environments. Excellent problem-solving and debugging skills. Strong communication and collaboration skills.

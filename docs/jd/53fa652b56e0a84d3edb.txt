What you will be doing PRINCIPAL DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES: 1. Independently assesses and manages complex patient accommodation and hospitality needs, exercising discretion to evaluate available options, determine appropriate service solutions, and resolve competing priorities related to scheduling, lodging, transportation, private room placement, amenity services, and specialized requests. 2. Serves as a primary advisor and liaison for assigned high-profile patients, board members, and other designated individuals, providing informed recommendations to patients, families, physicians, and internal stakeholders and independently addressing sensitive or complex situations within established authority. 3. Investigates complex patient concerns, evaluates potential resolutions, and independently determines appropriate next steps in collaboration with relevant hospital departments. Exercises judgment in resolving issues, implementing appropriate solutions, and escalating matters that fall outside delegated authority. 4. Evaluates patient feedback, service data, recurring needs, and operational challenges to identify trends and opportunities for improvement. Develops recommendations to enhance departmental workflows, service standards, and the overall patient experience. 5. Independently assesses patient needs throughout the HSS experience and determines appropriate navigation, accommodation, and support strategies across consultations, appointments, surgical days, admissions, and other key touchpoints, adapting plans as patient circumstances and priorities evolve. 6. Develops and maintains cross-functional relationships with physicians, clinical teams, administrative departments, and other stakeholders to resolve complex service issues, evaluate options, and facilitate coordinated solutions for patients and families. 7. Evaluates patient service information and maintains accurate documentation to assess service effectiveness, identify recurring operational issues, and provide insights and recommendations that inform departmental planning and process improvement. 8. Exercises independent judgment in prioritizing patient requests, assessing urgency, determining appropriate responses, and managing competing needs, including time-sensitive situations and complex requests received outside standard business hours. 9. Contributes to the development, refinement, and implementation of departmental service standards, workflows, and patient experience initiatives by identifying operational needs and recommending improvements in collaboration with Ambassador Services leadership. 10. Maintains accessibility to assigned patients and departmental leadership as required by the needs of the role, including early mornings, evenings, weekends, and after-hours situations. 11. Performs other duties as assigned in support of Ambassador Services and the broader HSS patient experience. 12. Supports operational coverage for The Guest Suites, HSS’s nine-room on-campus hotel, by assisting with guest inquiries, accommodations, service requests, and coordination with relevant departments to support a seamless guest experience and continuity of daily operations. Exercises judgment in addressing guest concerns and resolving service issues, escalating complex matters as appropriate.

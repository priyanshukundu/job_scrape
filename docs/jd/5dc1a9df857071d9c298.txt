Job title : Director Portfolio Sales Project & Integration Lead Location: Remote, US About the Job You will enable the Sanofi Vaccine Portfolio Sales organization to deliver on enterprise strategy through clear prioritization, cross-functional alignment, and disciplined execution. Acting as an extension of the Head of Sales, US Vaccines, you will connect Sales Strategy and Marketing to ensure Portfolio teams are focused on the highest-impact initiatives. You will serve as the central point of coordination for newly aligned and strategic projects as assigned, ensuring they are effectively translated, prioritized, and activated within the Portfolio Sales organization. You will help set the strategic roadmap for the team transformation and oversee prioritizing topics for leadership, assume responsibility on behalf of leadership for advancing key initiatives, and prioritize establishing strong internal cross-functional partnerships while ensuring the voice of the customer is disseminated across the organization. Join the team protecting half a billion lives every year with next-gen science, mRNA innovation, and AI driven breakthroughs. In Vaccines, you’ll help advance prevention on a global scale - and shape the future of immunization. About Sanofi We're an R&D-driven, AI-powered biopharma company committed to improving people's lives and delivering compelling growth. Our deep understanding of the immune system — and innovative pipeline — enables us to invent medicines and vaccines that treat and protect millions of people around the world. Together, we chase the miracles of science to improve people's lives.

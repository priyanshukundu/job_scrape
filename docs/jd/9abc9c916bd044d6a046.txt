About This Job The Associate Relationship Manager – Innovation Banking supports the acquisition, development, and management of relationships with innovative and technology-focused companies. Working alongside senior Relationship Managers and cross-functional partners, this role helps identify client needs, support new business opportunities, coordinate onboarding, and deliver banking solutions that drive relationship growth and an exceptional client experience. This position offers the opportunity to develop expertise in Innovation Banking while building foundational skills in relationship management, business development, commercial banking, and financial solutions.

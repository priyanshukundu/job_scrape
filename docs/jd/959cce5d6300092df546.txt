We’re actively seeking a talented Data Engineering Leader to join our Workforce Benefits Team in Newport Beach, CA. Reporting to the Head of Data Engineering, the Data Engineering Leader owns the strategy, lifecycle, delivery, and operational performance of Workforce Benefits Division (WBD) data products. The role leads a team of data engineers and analysts to build secure, scalable data pipelines, infrastructure, models, marts, and real-time data solutions that support reporting, analytics, operations, and business decision-making. This leader translates stakeholder needs and data strategy into prioritized agile backlogs, provides technical direction, and partners across business, technology, architecture, governance, and vendor teams to deliver trusted data products aligned with business goals. How you’ll help move us forward: Own the vision, roadmap, lifecycle, and delivery of WBD data products; partner with business and technology stakeholders to define outcomes, prioritize demand, and align data investments with business goals. Translate stakeholder requirements into delivery plans and sequenced product backlogs; lead Agile execution, capacity planning, metrics, and continuous improvement. Provide technical leadership for the architecture, design, implementation, and optimization of secure, scalable data pipelines, integrated and published data marts, and real-time data solutions. Ensure data quality, security, privacy, and governance standards are embedded throughout delivery, including controls for protected health information and HIPAA-regulated data. Lead day-to-day execution and operational support, including release readiness, quality assurance, production support, root-cause resolution, enhancements, and delivery against scope, schedule, and budget. Build and lead a high-performing team through recruiting, coaching, development, and performance management for employees. Establish and monitor KPIs for data-product delivery, quality, and business value; use results to manage priorities, risks, resources, and service improvements. Manage and forecast department, project, software, contractor, and vendor expenses Oversee vendor service agreements, staff augmentation, and technology contracts as assigned. Serve as a trusted partner and subject matter expert across WBD functions, analytics and reporting, data architecture, data governance, and corporate technology teams. Provide technical support outside standard business hours when required for planned releases or critical production issues, and travel up to 25% for planning, leadership, and technology meetings The experience you bring: Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or a related field; master's degree preferred. 10+ years of experience delivering enterprise data products, data warehouses, data integrations, and enterprise data architecture. 5+ years leading a data engineering team at the Director level or equivalent, including people leadership, resource planning, and budget accountability. 5+ years of hands-on experience with data warehouses, advanced analytics, and cloud-based data engineering. Demonstrated experience with agile product ownership, backlog management, technical requirements, delivery metrics, and cross-functional stakeholder leadership. Preferred Qualifications Proficiency in SQL and Python and experience with semantic layers, ontology, and reference data management. Experience with the latest data management and orchestration technologies including Snowflake and dbt. Experience with RESTful APIs, event-driven integration, and microservices administration platforms.

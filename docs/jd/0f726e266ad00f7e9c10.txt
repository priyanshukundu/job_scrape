ROLE OVERVIEW The Equities Alternative Data team is responsible for sourcing, integrating, curating, and delivering differentiated alternative datasets that support BAM’s Equities Long/Short investment teams. We build and operate the data products, platforms, and workflows that enable portfolio managers, analysts, and quantitative researchers to efficiently discover, evaluate, back-test, and consume high-quality alternative data signals. We are looking for an experienced, pragmatic, and eager data engineer to join the team. This individual will help design and scale the data infrastructure underpinning our alternative-data offering—from vendor onboarding and ingestion through normalization, entity mapping, quality controls, historical backfills, and researcher-facing delivery.

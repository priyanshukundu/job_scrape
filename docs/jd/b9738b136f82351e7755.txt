Help us transform our workforce of the future, today. We are seeking Senior CIAM Software Engineer in Salt Lake City to join our CIAM team to help support and enhance our Customer Identity and Access Management platform. CIAM is a critical system that provides identity and access management services for customer access to important business applications. The Engineering and Core Services organization is at the heart of innovation at Zions Bancorporation. We continually modernize on many of Zions’ key banking applications and its banking core. In addition to application development, we manage the key platforms, services, and processes which enable modernized engineering and IT operations and delivery through DevOps and lean principles. Ensuring scalable, quality, innovative solutions for our teams and our customers is integral to what we do. Come join us in transforming engineering and banking software! This Software Engineer will be responsible for maintenance as well as development of new capabilities for managing customer identities and providing security and enhanced experience. The Software Engineer will: Provide support and maintenance of various systems. Be responsible for technical analysis, application development, application configuration, incident response and resolution, troubleshooting, testing, source code management and executing and managing change processes. Work as a member of Agile team, develop software solutions by studying requirements analysis and information needs; conferring with stakeholders and IT Management; studying systems flow, data usage, and work processes; perform software design using software development fundamentals and processes, debug, test, and deploy software solutions. Review, analyze, evaluate business processes, procedures, systems and make recommendations for improvements and changes. Manage source code version control system artifacts. Work with Business Analysts to design technical requirements and perform application changes. Conduct unit testing, provide input for feasibility, and prepare systems documentation. Work On-Call Rotation and After-Hours that is required to support responsibilities. Perform other duties as assigned. Required Qualifications 6+ years of software development experience. Strong knowledge of Java, Spring Boot, REST APIs, and SQL. Experience with Ping Identity, ForgeRock, Okta, or similar IAM platforms. Understanding of OAuth 2.0, OIDC, SAML, MFA, and SSO. Experience with cloud technologies, Docker, Kubernetes, or application servers such as Tomcat. Familiarity with secure application development and software development lifecycle practices. Strong problem-solving, communication, and teamwork skills. Bachelor’s degree in computer science, Information Systems, or a related field, or equivalent practical experience. Preferred Qualifications Experience with Generative AI, LLMs, prompt engineering, and AI-assisted development. Familiarity with AI integration frameworks. Experience with centralized entitlements, CIAM, RBAC, and ABAC. Knowledge of CI/CD tools, automation, and cloud-native development. Experience in financial services or commercial banking applications Please consider applying even if you feel you don’t meet 100% of the requirements A combination of education and experience may meet requirements.

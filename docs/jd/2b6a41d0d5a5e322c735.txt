Treasury Management Risk and Compliance Manager
United Community Bank
Location: Greenville, SC, US
Link: https://careers-ucbi.icims.com/jobs/3007/treasury-management-risk-and-compliance-manager/job

Overview The Treasury Management Risk & Compliance Manager is responsible for leading and overseeing Treasury Management risk, fraud prevention, compliance, audit readiness, and regulatory adherence across United Community’s Treasury & Payments organization. This role serves as the Bank’s subject matter expert for NACHA Operating Rules, ACH risk management, Treasury Management controls, and fraud mitigation while providing leadership to the Treasury Management Analyst Team and Treasury Management Fraud Team. The successful candidate will ensure all Treasury Management products, services, operational processes, payment systems, and third-party providers comply with applicable regulatory requirements, NACHA Operating Rules, Bank policies, and industry best practices. This individual will partner closely with Treasury Sales, Product Management, Operations, Information Security, Enterprise Risk Management, Third-Party Risk Management, Compliance, Audit, and Technology teams to maintain a strong control environment while supporting the continued growth of the Treasury & Payments business. What You’ll Do Lead the Treasury Management risk framework, including risk identification, assessment, monitoring, mitigation, and governance across all products, services, and payment channels. Serve as the Bank's subject matter expert for NACHA Operating Rules, ACH compliance, and Treasury Management risk controls. Ensure compliance with applicable regulations, NACHA requirements, Bank policies, and industry best practices through ongoing monitoring, reviews, and training. Oversee Treasury Management audits, regulatory examinations, compliance reviews, and remediation efforts to maintain a strong control environment. Develop, maintain, and test Treasury Management policies, procedures, controls, and governance documentation. Lead fraud risk management activities, including oversight of ACH, wire, digital banking, remote deposit capture, and Treasury Management fraud monitoring programs. Develop and implement fraud mitigation strategies, customer education initiatives, and internal training programs to reduce fraud losses and exposure. Serve as the primary liaison for Enterprise Risk Management (ERM) and Third-Party Risk Management (TPRM), ensuring Treasury risks are properly documented, assessed, reported, and escalated. Oversee risk assessments, due diligence, ongoing monitoring, and compliance reviews for Treasury Management vendors and payment-related service providers. Partner with Compliance, Internal Audit, Information Security, Technology, Operations, and business line leaders to address risk, compliance, fraud, and operational challenges. Lead, coach, and develop Treasury Management Analysts and Fraud Team members while fostering a culture of accountability, compliance, and continuous improvement. Review new Treasury Management products, services, and system enhancements to ensure risk, compliance, security, and regulatory requirements are addressed prior to implementation. Support Treasury Sales, Product, Implementations, Client Service, and Operations teams by providing risk guidance, complex customer assessments, and subject matter expertise. Monitor industry trends, emerging threats, regulatory changes, and vendor performance to proactively strengthen Treasury Management controls and operational effectiveness. Act as a trusted advisor to Treasury & Payments leadership, providing strategic guidance on risk, compliance, fraud prevention, audit management, and governance initiatives. Requirements For Success Education: Bachelor’s degree in finance, Business Administration, Accounting, Risk Management, or related field. Experience: Minimum 10 years of Treasury Management, ACH Operations, Payments Risk, Compliance, Treasury Operations, or related banking experience. Minimum 5 years of leadership experience managing teams and complex operational functions. Extensive experience with ACH Origination, Wire Transfers, Positive Pay, Remote Deposit Capture, Integrated Payables, Commercial Payments, Fraud Prevention Solutions, Commercial Digital Banking Platforms, Treasury Management Risk Controls, and Regulatory Compliance Requirements. Required Skills: Expert-level knowledge of NACHA Operating Rules and ACH Network requirements. Advanced knowledge of Treasury Management risk management and compliance practices. Strong understanding of commercial payments, fraud mitigation, banking regulations, and operational controls. Experience managing internal audits, regulatory examinations, and compliance reviews. Knowledge of Enterprise Risk Management and Third-Party Risk Management frameworks. Strong leadership, communication, stakeholder management, and relationship-building skills. Exceptional analytical, problem-solving, and decision-making capabilities. Ability to influence cross-functional stakeholders and senior leadership. Experience implementing controls within Treasury Management and payment systems. Strong project management and organizational skills. Preferred Skills: Accredited Payments Risk Professional (APRP) certification. Certified Treasury Professional (CTP) certification. Certified Fraud Examiner (CFE) certification. Certified Regulatory Compliance Manager (CRCM) certification. Experience leading large-scale Treasury Management risk, compliance, and fraud initiatives. Experience supporting payment technology enhancements and digital transformation initiatives.

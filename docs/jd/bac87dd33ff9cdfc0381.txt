About this role: Wells Fargo is seeking a Fraud & Claims Operations Associate in Restraint Quality Control as part of Fraud and Claims Management Operations. This team checks the quality of fraud-related restraint work to make sure the right decisions are being made, procedures are being followed, and potential risks or errors are caught and corrected. This position plays an important role in maintaining the quality and accuracy of fraud and claims operations through detailed case reviews, quality scoring, and identification of errors, exceptions, and potential risk. The Associate will use fraud detection and claims knowledge to provide guidance on complex cases, resolve scoring disputes, support team calibrations, and escalate issues requiring additional review. The role will also contribute to coaching, training, and process improvements designed to strengthen quality and consistency across the operation.

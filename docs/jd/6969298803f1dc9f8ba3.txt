Financial Analyst I
Intercontinental Exchange
Location: New York, New York
Link: https://careers.ice.com/jobs/14196

As a Financial Analyst, you will perform financial analysis work and develop a deep understanding of our revenue and expense drivers. The role will support the annual budget process and generate a monthly forecast. This individual will create key performance indicators to measure progress and update monthly reporting as required. Additionally, you will perform ad hoc projects and be a key liaison for business leaders. This position is based in New York and reports to the Sr. Manager of Finance as part of our Fixed Income and Data Services Finance team. Responsibilities Lead monthly financial forecast process for key business units or departments Develop key growth drivers and facilitate deeper understanding of impacts to revenue Create and perform key performance indicators Communicate financial analysis to our financial team and management Create and maintain management reporting packages Support monthly forecasting and annual budget process Prepare ad hoc projects as necessary Maintain Hyperion Financial System which supports Budget and Forecast Knowledge and Experience Bachelor's Degree in Finance or closely related discipline 0-1 years’ experience in financial analysis Must possess strong analytical and financial analysis skills Must have excellent written and verbal communication skills Must be detail-oriented, well-organized and self-motivated Must work well in a fast-paced, deadline driven environment Excellent Microsoft Office skills (Excel, Word, PowerPoint) Knowledge of Oracle Hyperion products is preferred New York Base Salary Range The expected base salary for this role, if located in New York, is between $67,000 - 77,300 USD.

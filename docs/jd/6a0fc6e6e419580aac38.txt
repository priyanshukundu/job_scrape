Summary: The Quality Control (QC) Associate is responsible for performing routine testing and data review of stability and batch release samples in a cGMP-compliant environment. This position requires expertise in a variety of assays, including ELISA, and qPCR analysis. In addition to testing, the QC Associate participates in activities that support laboratory operations, such as maintaining housekeeping standards, acknowledging alarms, preparing reagents, managing inventory, ordering materials and reagents, picking up and discarding samples, and executing work orders. Key Responsibilities Conduct routine assays such as ELISA, and qPCR analysis. Support laboratory operations by performing housekeeping, reagents preparation, inventory management, material and reagent ordering, sample pickup and disposal, and executing work orders. Collaborate with the team leader to coordinate and schedule assignments. Analyze laboratory data to identify and respond to emerging trends, ensuring issues are proactively addressed and product quality remains within established acceptance criteria. Record performance metrics and analyze trending data. Update QC documents for managerial review and approval as needed. Contribute to and lead simple projects and participate in complex projects within the team. Train colleagues in task execution and testing procedures. Support deviation and laboratory investigations through data gathering and interviews; may lead no-impact deviations and lab investigations. Identify and escalate challenges and barriers to execution, suggest solutions, and participate in remediation activities. Identify and escalate method and instrument issues, and support solutions. Engage in Operational Excellence (OpEx) and Continuous Improvement (CI) initiatives within the team. Collaborate positively and supportively within the team environment. Complete all required tasks compliantly and safely, consistently modeling BMS values: Integrity, Accountability, Passion, Urgency, Innovation, and Inclusion. Perform other assigned tasks as necessary. Qualifications Bachelor of Science (BS) degree, preferably in Bioanalytical, Molecular Biology, or a related field. A minimum of 2 years of relevant experience in a Biologics QC laboratory or a related GMP-regulated testing environment. Proficiency in analytical test methods such as ELISA, and qPCR analysis is highly preferred. Familiarity with laboratory software applications (e.g., LIMS and LES) is preferred. High attention to detail and strong organizational skills. Demonstrated ability to work independently and as part of a team, completing assignments within defined time constraints. Ability to interpret and apply cGMPs, USP standards, regulatory requirements, and industry best practices. Strong problem-solving and analytical thinking skills. Experience with visual management scheduling tools, 5S, and operational excellence initiatives is highly preferred. Work Environment QC Associates work collaboratively within teams and engage regularly with other teams throughout the facility to exchange information regarding completed, in-process, and planned testing. Daily contact with supervisory staff is expected for work assignments, coaching, and general management discussions. Instructions for routine work are generally provided, while specific guidance is given for new assignments. Occasional interaction with other line management staff may occur for project-specific responsibilities. Overtime may be required on weekdays, weekends, and holidays, as well as during adverse weather conditions, to support manufacturing facilities.

Aon is looking for a Private Risk Claims Advocacy Executive. This is a virtual position, eastern time zone preferred. As part of an industry-leading team you will drive results for our clients by delivering innovative and effective claims solutions across all personal lines of business. The Private Risk Claims Advocacy Executive will strengthen relationships with existing clients and prospects through proactive advocacy for the best possible claim outcome, helping to ensure that our clients’ interests are protected/represented. APRM is dedicated to protecting our clients on a personal level with thoughtful risk management and custom-tailored personal insurance products and services. APRM partners with highly rated personal insurance providers who possess superior financial ratings, offer the broadest coverage available in the marketplace and specialize in resolving claims in an expeditious and empathetic manner. Our role is to design bespoke solutions that protect all that our clients and their families have achieved. Aon is in the business of better decisions At Aon, we shape decisions for the better to protect and enrich the lives of people around the world. As an organization, we are united through trust as one inclusive team, and we are passionate about helping our colleagues and clients succeed.

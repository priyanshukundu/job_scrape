What Financial Planning & Analysis contributes to Cardinal Health Finance oversees the accounting, tax, financial plans and policies of the organization, establishes and maintains fiscal controls, prepares and interprets financial reports, oversees financial systems and safeguards the organization's assets. Financial Planning & Analysis provides strategic partnership and objective guidance to business units. It leads cross functional efforts to enable forward looking, actionable insights with a focus on driving strategic value that influences key business decisions. Job Summary As part of the Global Operations & Supply Chain finance team, the Senior Analyst, Financial Planning and Analysis will be responsible for the support of the GOSC organization, reporting to the FP&A Manager for GOSC Admin. This role’s primary responsibilities will include month end close activities, reporting, and support to drive accuracy and understanding business unit results. Responsibilities Support month-end close activities, including posting of journal entries, data generation and analysis, reporting, and reconciliation activity. Support system and reporting maintenance, with a focus on continuous process and reporting improvement. Providing clear, concise and accurate analysis of variances to plan/forecast. Being a business partner for the GOSC Admin team for capital, budgeting and forecasting. Support various month and quarter end activities related to SEC reporting and other controls. Manage multiple deliverables and deadlines concurrently. Act as a key utility player across GOSC Admin finance, working on ad hoc requests and initiatives as required. Qualifications Bachelors degree in business related field, preferred, or equivalent work experience preferred. 2+ years' experience in related field preferred. Experience in manufacturing/procurement preferred. Experience with budgeting/forecasting highly preferred. Strong knowledge of Excel (pivot tables, v-lookup, multiple embedded formulas) required. Highly effective written and verbal communication skills. Solid understanding of Generally Accepted Accounting Principles. Adaptability and flexibility in balancing multiple projects and changing priorities. Experience with SAP and HFM preferred. Anaplan experience a plus.

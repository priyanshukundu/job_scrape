Our Impact Wealth Management Across Wealth Management, Goldman Sachs helps empower clients and customers around the world to reach their financial goals. Our advisor-led wealth management businesses provide financial planning, investment management, banking, and comprehensive advice to a wide range of clients, including ultra-high net worth and high net worth individuals, family offices, foundations and endowments, and corporations and their employees. Private Wealth Management Goldman Sachs Private Wealth Management (PWM) specializes in creating comprehensive wealth management plans for high net worth individuals and families, as well as select institutions. PWM teams work directly with clients to deliver customized strategies drawing on Goldman Sachs' investment capabilities and global reach. Within PWM, custody capabilities are foundational to the client and advisor experience. The Product team partners across the organization to enhance the platforms, processes, and services through which clients hold, fund, transfer, and manage their assets. Your Impact As a Product Manager within Private Wealth Management, you will help shape and deliver the custody capabilities that support our clients and advisors across the wealth management lifecycle. You will focus on building and enhancing solutions across asset custody, cash and payments, account servicing, asset movement, and related operational workflows. You will work closely with Private Wealth Advisors and partners across Product, Operations, Engineering, Legal, Compliance, Risk, and other teams to identify client and advisor needs, define product requirements, improve existing capabilities, and deliver new solutions. We are looking for someone with a strong understanding of custody and/or payments products and workflows who combines domain expertise with a product-management mindset. You should be comfortable understanding complex financial processes, translating business and client needs into actionable requirements, managing multiple stakeholders, and owning initiatives from concept through implementation.

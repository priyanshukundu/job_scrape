Job Description Step into a pivotal role as the Senior Android Software Engineer to enhance mission-critical communication solutions. Here, you'll have the opportunity not only to apply and elevate your technical skills but also to guide a team of dedicated professionals, including product owners, technical leads, architects, and project delivery teams. As a Senior Engineer, you'll steer the development and ongoing enhancement of our Android applications for two-way radios. Collaborate with cross-functional teams to envision, design, and launch innovative features that are vital for users in high-stakes situations. Your leadership will ensure that our solutions are not only robust and reliable but also at the cutting edge of communication technology. Responsibilities: Lead/Manage all aspects of the product development process including requirement definition, design, implementation and testing of ASTRO radios. Able to manage engineering and also project the software for the products. Collaborate with different stakeholders from SW development, System Test, PDM, SCM, Quality and Hardware. Responsible for timeline alignment and resources management. Inter-group and intra-group teamwork in a multi-site world-wide development environment. Responsible for meeting project's deliverables and timeline requirements in a highly dynamic environment. Manage team's performance and quality metrics.

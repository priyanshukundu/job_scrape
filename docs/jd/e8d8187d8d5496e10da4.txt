Job Description: As the GTM Business Analyst, you help business leaders and teams turn questions, needs, and opportunities into clear requirements and useful analysis. You spend time with users to understand how work is performed today, what decisions they are trying to make, and where better information or simpler processes could improve the outcome. Reporting into the Business Intelligence function, you work across go-to-market, sales, client, service line, and firm leadership teams. You connect business users with reporting, data, and technology resources, making sure the problem is well understood before a solution is designed and that the final result works for the people who will use it. This role combines business analysis, requirements management, user research, and hands-on analytical work. You may support a new dashboard, investigate a performance question, improve an existing process, or help define a larger reporting initiative. In each case, you bring structure to the request, test assumptions with data, and communicate the answer in clear business terms. Essential Functions & Responsibilities: Meet with business users and leaders to understand their goals, decisions, pain points, current processes, and information needs. Turn business needs into clear problem statements, requirements, user stories, process flows, measures, and acceptance criteria. Ask focused questions, challenge assumptions constructively, and distinguish the core business need from a requested feature or report. Analyze business, sales, pipeline, client, and performance data to identify trends, explain drivers, test hypotheses, and answer ad hoc questions. Create clear summaries, visualizations, and recommendations that explain what the analysis means and why it matters. Work with reporting, data, CRM, and technology teams to clarify requirements, answer questions, and keep delivery aligned with the user's need. Support testing and user acceptance by developing test cases, validating results, documenting issues, and confirming that agreed requirements were met. Map current and future processes, identify gaps or unnecessary manual work, and recommend practical improvements. Help prioritize requests by clarifying expected value, urgency, effort, dependencies, and the users affected. Maintain a clear record of requirements, decisions, definitions, open questions, and changes throughout the work. Support adoption by preparing user guidance, facilitating feedback sessions, and helping business teams understand new reports or processes. Build reusable requirement templates, definitions, and analytical approaches that improve consistency across the Business Intelligence function. Other duties as assigned You Have the Following Technical Skills and Qualifications: Bachelor's degree in Business, Analytics, Finance, Information Systems, or a related field 3-5 years of experience in business analysis, analytics, business intelligence, sales operations, consulting, or a related role Demonstrated ability to gather requirements, understand users and processes, and translate business needs into clear specifications Strong analytical skills, including the ability to work with imperfect data, test hypotheses, identify drivers, and form practical conclusions Working proficiency with Excel and data visualization tools such as Power BI; experience with SQL or similar data-query tools is preferred Ability to explain analysis and technical topics in plain language to both business and technical audiences Strong facilitation, listening, writing, and stakeholder-management skills, with a practical and curious approach to problem solving Experience supporting testing, process mapping, or user acceptance is preferred Knowledge of pipeline, sales performance, CRM, client, or professional services data is preferred Ability to manage multiple requests, organize details, and work effectively in a matrixed environment The base salary range for this position is between $76,000 and $114,000.

OUR MISSION & IMPACT In today's digital landscape, network downtime is simply not an option. Our team owns the critical Image Install component of Cisco IOS-XE, the operating system running across Cisco's industry-leading enterprise switching, routing, and wireless portfolios. We architect and deliver the software lifecycle management mechanisms that allow millions of enterprise devices to upgrade, provision, and recover seamlessly. From automated zero-touch provisioning to robust in-service software upgrades (ISSU), our code ensures mission-critical networks remain secure, resilient, and always online.

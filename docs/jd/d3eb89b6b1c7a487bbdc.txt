General Summary: This position is for an experienced Senior Data Engineer who will serve as a hands-on technical lead for data engineering across assigned Value Streams, providing technical leadership across at least two Value Streams, translating enterprise architecture standards, patterns, and governance guardrails into detailed, production-ready designs that engineering teams can successfully implement. This is a 100% hands-on technical role. The Senior Data Engineer will actively design solutions, solve complex engineering challenges, make key technical decisions, review code and implementations, and guide engineers through delivery. The role will lead by doing, not simply coordinate the work. The Senior Data Engineer will lead the design and delivery of complex data pipelines, ETL/ELT processes, data warehouses, data marts, data models, and integration solutions using technologies including Snowflake, SQL, Python, and modern ETL/ELT tooling. This individual will partner closely with architects, product owners, engineers, analysts, and business stakeholders to deliver scalable, reliable, secure, and maintainable data solutions. They will also play an important role in Value Stream transformation by establishing engineering best practices, leading design and code reviews, mentoring engineers, and driving consistency and technical excellence across teams. Job Specifications: Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Information Systems, or a related technical field preferred. 6+ years of experience in Data Engineering, Software Engineering, Data Integration, or related technical disciplines. Demonstrated hands-on experience designing and building complex data pipelines, ETL/ELT solutions, data warehouses, data marts, and data models in large-scale enterprise environments. Strong hands-on expertise with Snowflake, SQL, Python, and modern ETL/ELT technologies. Experience working with cloud data platforms and integrating data through APIs, batch, near-real-time, and real-time integration patterns. Deep knowledge of data modeling, data integration patterns, solution design, and modern data engineering practices. Demonstrated ability to translate enterprise architecture patterns, standards, and guardrails into detailed, scalable, production-ready technical designs. Proven experience serving as a hands-on technical lead, including making design decisions, conducting design and code reviews, solving complex engineering problems, and guiding engineers through implementation. Strong understanding of DevOps and CI/CD practices, Git, automated testing, automation, deployment, and operational excellence. Experience delivering solutions within complex enterprise data environments involving multiple systems, platforms, and data sources. Familiarity with legacy data platforms such as Teradata and DB2 is beneficial, particularly within modernization or transformation environments. Demonstrated ability to mentor engineers and influence technical direction while remaining actively involved in engineering delivery. Strong problem-solving, communication, and stakeholder management skills. Strong business acumen with the ability to balance technical quality, delivery objectives, and business outcomes. Principal Duties and Responsibilities: Serve as the hands-on technical lead for data engineering across assigned Value Streams, providing technical direction across at least two Value Streams. Lead the design and implementation of complex data engineering solutions, including data pipelines, ETL/ELT processes, data warehouses, data marts, data models, and enterprise data assets. Translate enterprise architecture standards, patterns, and governance guardrails into detailed, scalable, and implementable technical designs. Lead complex integrations across cloud, on-premises, internal, and external data sources, including batch, near-real-time, real-time, and API-based integrations. Partner with architects, product owners, analysts, engineers, and business stakeholders to understand requirements and determine appropriate technical solutions. Establish and promote engineering best practices for data modeling, integration, automation, testing, performance optimization, deployment, and operational support. Guide engineers through implementation and provide hands-on technical coaching and mentoring to strengthen engineering capabilities across teams. Support Value Stream transformation through the adoption of modern data engineering patterns, technologies, and development practices.

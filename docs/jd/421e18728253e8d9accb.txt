Role Description The Enterprise Risk Management is seeking a Vice President to join the enterprise stress testing team at SMBC Americas Division. The incumbent will be engaged in the development, governance, and execution of enterprise stress tests and the build-out and ongoing management of a CCAR program from a Risk perspective. The role ensures that stress testing methodologies, assumptions, and governance processes support effective capital adequacy assessment, risk appetite and strategic decision-making, in alignment with U.S. regulatory expectations and SMBC Group standards. The role will report to the Enterprise Stress Testing Lead. Role Responsibilities Design the governance, and execution of enterprise-wide stress testing covering credit, market, liquidity, operational, and non-financial risks. Partner with multiple stakeholders to build and implement a CCAR Risk program, including stress testing governance, documentation, challenge processes – working actively with Risk Stripes, Finance and Front Office to deploy the program across the businesses. Establish and maintain stress testing policies, standards, and procedures aligned with SMBC Group frameworks and U.S. regulatory guidance. Provide independent second-line challenge of stress testing assumptions, methodologies, overlays, and results produced by first-line and Finance partners. Aggregate enterprise stress testing outputs to form an independent Risk view for senior management and Board oversight. Ensure effective integration of stress testing results into capital planning, risk appetite, recovery and resolution planning, and strategic decision-making. Coordinate closely with Finance, Treasury, Model Risk Management, Credit Risk, Market Risk, Liquidity Risk, and Regulatory Relations to ensure end-to-end process integrity. Drive ongoing enhancement of stress testing capabilities in response to regulatory developments, internal reviews, and evolving risk exposures. Qualifications and Skills Bachelor’s degree in finance/economics/business/STEM or related disciplines 7+ years of experience working in stress testing / CCAR initiatives in large and complex banking organization, ideally in a Category 3 bank or GSIB Deep expertise in enterprise stress testing, CCAR and capital adequacy frameworks for large banking organizations; Strong understanding of U.S. regulatory expectations, including Federal Reserve CCAR and capital planning guidance (SR 12-7/ 15-19), and related supervisory stress testing standards. Broad knowledge of financial risk types and how stress scenarios transmit across trading and lending portfolios and balance sheets. Ongoing awareness of regulatory priorities, model risk considerations, and industry stress testing practices. Experience interacting with and managing regulatory expectations with respect to CCAR submissions; experience closing regulatory findings around CCAR is highly desirable Exceptional ability to communicate complex stress testing outcomes and limitations to senior management, Boards, and regulators.

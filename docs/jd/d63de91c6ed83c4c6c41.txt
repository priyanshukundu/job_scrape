Work you’ll do Lead the communications strategy for CEB priorities, ensuring messages are clear, consistent, and aligned to business objectives Shape executive-ready narratives and storytelling efforts that elevate CEB’s impact across internal and external audiences Drive brand awareness and engagement through high-visibility campaigns, major moments, events, and other strategic initiatives Partner across Brand, Marketing, Executive Communications, PR, Social, and Risk/Crisis/OGC teams to deliver coordinated communications and marketing Guide development of toolkits, presentations, newsletters, speaking materials, and other content that bring key CEB themes to life Bring structure and momentum to a fast-moving environment by managing priorities, coordinating contributors, and ensuring follow-through on critical workstreams The successful candidate would possess these skills Ability to work independently and collaborate as part of a team Effective written and verbal communication skills Meticulous attention to detail and quality of work product Ability to build and sustain professional relationships Ability to lead projects or workstreams Ability to manage and prioritize multiple tasks in a fast-paced and dynamic environment Strong interpersonal skills and professional demeanor Ability to meet deadlines Ability to mentor and provide clear guidance to others The team The CEB Strategy & Integration team helps foster the CEB mission to foster an environment where people thrive and sustainable growth follows, supported by an inclusive culture, meaningful and accessible experiences, and everyday choices that strengthen well-being. The team helps advance this mission by supporting CEB leader priorities, coordinating key forums and processes, managing engagement tools, and driving initiatives that strengthen team effectiveness, culture, and development. The team also plays an important role in helping make CEB widely known and clearly understood across the firm. Through thoughtful communications, shared stories, and connected experiences, the team helps build awareness of CEB’s purpose, deepen belonging, and strengthen how leaders and professionals engage with the firm’s culture of belonging and leading talent experience.

Goldman Sachs Private Bank Goldman Sachs Private Bank provides competitive borrowing and cash management solutions from Goldman Sachs Bank USA that are strategically integrated with our client’s wealth management goals. Our experienced team partners with PWAs to advise clients on new loans, refinancing and restructuring of existing loans along with overall liability management. Our lenders and mortgage specialists provide a wide range of creative borrowing solutions against asset classes ranging from marketable securities and alternative investments, residential and commercial real estate to more tailored solutions against private assets such as aviation, fine art or vineyards. All team members are employees of Goldman Sachs Bank, USA. Your Impact The SBL and Margin Risk and Portfolio Management Associate plays a critical role in supporting the Private Bank’s credit delivery model. Working closely with Senior Underwriters, Originators, Credit Executives, Strats, Engineering and Risk partners, the Associate is responsible for designing risk methodology for lending values and initial margins, the management of the SBL and Margin portfolio and preparing underwriting analyses of portfolios of ultra‑high‑net‑worth individuals, families, and family offices. This role blends quantitative financial analysis, credit judgment, and cross‑functional collaboration. Key Responsibilities Quantitative analysis and portfolio management Prepare detailed analysis of financial collateral incl. Equities, Fixed Income, Structured Notes. Funds, Commodities, and exposure analysis including derivatives exposure. Support ongoing portfolio monitoring, including tracking of market risk factors, scenario stress analysis, and margin call oversight. Methodology Partner with Strats, Engineering and Business Stakeholders to develop loan-to-value methodology for financial collateral and initial margin methodology for derivatives. Provide theoretical and empirical analysis to support model development and ensure proper governance throughout the model development process. Risk & Governance Identify data gaps or potential risk issues and escalate appropriately. Qualifications Required 1-3 years of experience in VaR modeling for financial collateral and derivatives. Strong mathematical and statistics skills. Preferred Experience supporting UHNW or institutional‑level borrowers. Familiarity with SBL and Margin lending CFA, FRM or similar qualifications Success Factors Strong analytical rigor and excellent logical reasoning Ability to collaborate effectively with partners within the Private Bank and more broadly across Goldman Sachs. Ownership mindset and accountability for quality and accuracy. Professional communication and client‑focused execution.

About the Job Seeking a Software Engineer I to develop, support, secure, and modernize enterprise applications, automation solutions, and custom legacy tools. This role works within an Agile support team to deliver reliable solutions, improve application performance and user experience, and resolve issues through hands-on engineering and cross-functional partnership. What You'll Do Develop, test, deploy, secure, and maintain custom enterprise applications and services to deliver reliable business capabilities. Support and enhance existing and legacy applications while contributing to modernization initiatives that improve maintainability, security, and performance. Monitor application health and drive performance improvements to increase stability, availability, and operational visibility. Troubleshoot and resolve complex application issues with limited direction, partnering across teams to restore service and prevent recurrence. Partner with stakeholders and product teams to understand business requirements and user feedback, then translate them into effective technical solutions. Apply object-oriented programming principles and approved development practices to produce clear, reusable, and supportable code. Use Service Management tools and best practices to support incident, problem, and change management processes. Document solutions, testing results, deployment procedures, support requirements, and changes to enable sustainable operations. Participate in Agile ceremonies, software delivery activities, code reviews, source control, and continuous improvement efforts. Collaborate through clear written and verbal communication to maintain alignment, surface risks, and support continued progress. Develops a robust understanding of digital products that you and your team support. Builds, enhances, and supports those digital products with the guidance of team. Participates in code evaluations. What You'll Bring to the Table Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Information Systems, Software Engineering, or a related field, or equivalent practical experience. 0–3 years of software development experience. Knowledge of object-oriented programming principles and modern software development practices. Experience with C#, Java, C++, or another modern programming language; C# and .NET experience is preferred. Familiarity with Visual Studio 2019 or newer, SQL, and Git-based source control. Ability to resolve complex issues using structured testing, debugging, and problem-solving techniques. Familiarity with Kubernetes and modern application deployment practices. Experience with Angular, React, AngularJS, or Node.js. Knowledge of Agile software processes, change management, and application support practices. Classroom programming experience. Demonstrate a continuous learning mindset. Able to dissect problems and recommend solutions. Basic technical skills. Able to handle straight-forward tasks. Skills You Have Analytical Thinking - Formulates strategies to be used to convey complex information about services, products, systems, or processes to targeted audiences; communicates and liaises between technical and non-technical audiences. Application Security - Introduces security standards during application development and implements security procedures to protect applications in production. Utilizes knowledge to implement authentication for applications that contain sensitive data. Ensures that no PII/PCI information is logged. Conducts PenTest for external facing apps. Customer Centricity - Applies a customer first mindset to design and continuously improve solutions, systems, processes, and services that support enterprise strategy, impact critical business outcomes, and drive organizational success. Learning Agility & Critical Thinking - Pursues learning and obtains knowledge continuously in relevant fields, methods, or technologies in current and future practices; continuously utilizes critical thinking to identify opportunities, execute solutions, and measure impact to constantly improve existing practices and processes based on feedback, lessons learned, and market trends. Release Management - Utilizes knowledge of leveraging CI/CD tools to automate the build and deployment of applications. Software Development - Creates and maintains applications, frameworks, and other software components via designing, programming, testing, and bug fixing; writes and maintains source code for all processes in the software development life cycle and facilitates the automation and orchestration of disparate systems. Leverage TDD (Test Driven Development) and BDD (Behavior Driven Development) methodologies to ensure unit, integration, and end-to-end testing with appropriate level of coverage is met. Software Problem Solving - Conducts in-depth analysis of technology needs, issues, and roadblocks within assigned scope, applies experience and expertise to apply best practices, explore the “art of possible”, and deliver blueprints for the most effective and efficient solutions. Detects and removes existing and potential errors in software code or a system that can cause it to behave unexpectedly or operate incorrectly.

The Role The Senior Supply Chain Systems Engineer, Global Converse is a Boston-based employee role responsible for designing, developing, integrating, and supporting technology solutions across Order Management, Warehouse Management, Fulfillment, and Logistics ecosystems. This role blends hands-on Java engineering, supply chain domain expertise, systems integration, and technical delivery leadership. The engineer will partner with business, operations, digital, enterprise technology, and external partners to translate complex requirements into secure, scalable, resilient solutions. The role will support strategic transformation initiatives and critical operational platforms, including Converse’s AI-native order management capabilities, Manhattan WMS, SAP AFS/ERP, Multi-Channel Fulfillment, and related supply chain integrations and automation.

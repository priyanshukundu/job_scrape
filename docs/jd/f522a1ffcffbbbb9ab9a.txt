Title and Summary Senior Software Engineer Overview At Mastercard Commercial Solutions, we empower businesses to succeed through innovative, trusted payment solutions that deliver seamless, digital-first experiences around the world. We are building a global business-to-business (B2B) technology platform that helps businesses of all sizes streamline how they manage payments when buying or selling products and services. The Mastercard Commercial Solutions team is looking for a Senior Software Engineer to advance our customer experience strategy through innovation and problem-solving. The ideal candidate is passionate about the customer journey and is highly motivated, intellectually curious, and analytical. Role You will work at our Mastercard technology location in O’Fallon, which provides a collaborative, state-of-the-art environment where technology teams can thrive within our Agile engineering culture. You will play a key role in transforming the B2B space by helping standardize, automate, and optimize digital payment efficiency for buyers and sellers. • Own software delivery activities at the application or software-component level, including design, development, testing, deployment, support, documentation, and configuration. • Develop full-stack solutions, including front-end applications, REST APIs, and backend services. • Translate business and technical requirements into scalable, secure, and maintainable software designs, defining appropriate components and system interactions. • Participate in Scrum ceremonies and contribute to continuous improvement using Agile methodologies. • Drive code, design, and process trade-off discussions within the team. • Promote the adoption of engineering principles, technology standards, and best practices. • Review code, testing, and automation produced by team members to ensure quality, security, standards compliance, and maintainability. • Develop a strong understanding of end-to-end technical architecture and system dependencies. • Evaluate application architecture and design decisions with consideration for scalability, performance, reliability, and long-term maintainability. • Mentor and guide new and less-experienced team members. • Develop and maintain unit, integration, functional, and regression tests. • Analyze and optimize application performance through testing, measurement, troubleshooting, and tuning. • Design and maintain applications for operational readiness by incorporating reliability, observability, monitoring, and effective production-support practices. • Troubleshoot production incidents and implement corrective and preventive actions. • Share and seek knowledge within the Guild and program to promote the reuse of patterns, libraries, and engineering practices.

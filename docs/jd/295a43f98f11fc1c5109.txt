What You'll Be Doing The Business Process Integration Business Analyst II works in an agile environment to turn business needs into clear, testable work. This position analyzes business processes and data, writes user stories and acceptance criteria, helps maintain and prioritize the product backlog, and supports delivery through sprint testing, demonstrations and releases. The analyst acts as the liaison between business owners, technical analysts, QA, IT and application vendors. What We're Looking For • Elicit requirements through stakeholder interviews, design sessions, and workflow and data analysis, challenging assumptions so solutions fit the underlying need.• Write clear user stories with acceptance criteria, and verify requirements for completeness, consistency and feasibility.• Maintain and help prioritize the backlog in Jira, keeping upcoming work refined and ready for sprint planning.• Participate in backlog refinement, sprint planning, stand-ups, sprint reviews and retrospectives, and report progress, risks and issues for assigned work.• Work with technical analysts, developers, QA, IT and application vendors to clarify stories, weigh tradeoffs between usability and performance, and resolve questions during the sprint.• Review test cases against acceptance criteria, and perform and facilitate sprint, regression and user acceptance testing with business partners.• Present sprint demonstrations of new functionality to business stakeholders and gather feedback.• Support release readiness by confirming release content, helping verify deployments, and communicating changes to stakeholders and support teams.• Use approved AI tools to speed analysis and drafting, reviewing all output for accuracy and following firm policies on data handling. What You'll Bring • Self-directed, requiring minimal oversight, with the ability to mentor less experienced staff.• Working knowledge of agile and iterative delivery, including user stories, acceptance criteria and backlog management.• Knowledge of the software development lifecycle, including requirements analysis, design, testing (integration, user and regression) and implementation.• Strong analytical, organizational and problem-solving skills, with the ability to weigh alternative solutions.• Excellent written and verbal communication, including facilitating sessions and presenting to business audiences.• Ability to recognize needs outside one's own specialty and apply appropriate business and technology expertise.• Experience with process improvement efforts desired. Education & Experience • Minimum required: Bachelor's degree in Business or a related field.• Minimum required: 2 to 4 years of related experience.• Preferred: experience in financial services or operations; experience with workflow or configurable business applications. Systems & Technology • Working knowledge of Jira and Microsoft applications (Outlook, Excel, Word, PowerPoint, Visio and SharePoint).• Basic SQL knowledge and query skills.

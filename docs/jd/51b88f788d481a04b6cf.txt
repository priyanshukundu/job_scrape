Job Description Summary GE HealthCare's MIM team is seeking a highly skilled and experienced Staff Software Engineer to join our internal CORE development team. You will be instrumental in designing, developing, and deploying changes for our internal licensing, QMS, and engineering management tool. This role requires a strong technical background, leadership potential, and a passion for working on a tool where your colleagues are your customers. CORE is a web application written primarily in Ruby on Rails and Javascript. This is a full-stack development position. Job Description Responsibilities Lead the design, development, testing, and deployment of complex software systems and applications within a regulated healthcare environment. Mentor junior and mid-level engineers, providing technical guidance and fostering a culture of excellence and continuous improvement. Drive technical innovation, evaluating new technologies and methodologies to improve software quality, performance, and scalability. Participate in code reviews, ensuring adherence to coding standards, best practices, and security guidelines. Troubleshoot and resolve complex software defects, performance issues, and system failures. Contribute to the evolution of the software development lifecycle, including CI/CD pipelines, automated testing, and release management. Document software designs, technical specifications, and operational procedures. Qualifications Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related technical field. 8+ years of progressive experience in software development, with a significant focus on complex, scalable systems. Proven expertise in one or more programming languages (strongly preferred Ruby on Rails and Javascript) Strong understanding of software architecture principles, design patterns, and distributed systems. Experience with cloud platforms (e.g., AWS, Azure, Google Cloud) and containerization technologies (e.g., Docker, Kubernetes). Demonstrated ability to lead technical initiatives and mentor other engineers. Excellent problem-solving skills and a meticulous attention to detail. Strong communication and interpersonal skills, with the ability to articulate complex technical concepts to diverse audiences. Experience working in a regulated industry (e.g., healthcare, medical devices) is a significant plus. We will not sponsor individuals for employment visas, now or in the future, for this job opening.

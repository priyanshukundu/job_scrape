The Product Pricing & Economics – Senior Analyst is responsible for advancing our understanding of fund economics and pricing as strategic levers to drive competitive advantage and optimize client value. The Senior Analyst will conduct competitive intelligence, collaborate with product teams to align pricing with strategy, and partner with finance to inform enterprise capital allocation. With over $10T invested in our funds, the role offers high impact on our company and clients and significant exposure to senior leadership. Ideal candidates are strong problem solvers who thrive in ambiguity, can manage diverse stakeholders, and translate complex analysis into clear insights. Responsibilities: · Support product pricing research and analysis on new and existing funds to ensure the competitive health of our product lineup and to support strategic pricing and capital allocation decisions. · Synthesize analysis, insights, and thought leadership into compelling storylines and actionable strategies. Prepare and deliver presentation materials to senior leaders and business partners. · Maintain broad knowledge and awareness of the investment industry, competitor products, and Vanguard's fund line-up. · Build financial models to evaluate pricing strategies and their impact on revenue, capital allocation, and enterprise financial health; deliver insights that optimize shareholder value and inform long-term strategic planning. · Collaborate with business partners (e.g., Product Strategy, Marketing, Business Units) to determine how new and existing fund pricing can drive enterprise strategic goals. Qualifications : · Minimum of five years' related work experience, with at least two years' financial services industry experience. · Undergraduate degree or equivalent combination of training and experience. Economics or Finance degree preferred. · MBA or other graduate degree preferred. · CFA or other financial designation preferred. Preferred skills: · Excellent written and verbal communication skills, including the ability to synthesize complex information into concise, decision-oriented recommendations. · Strong analytical skills and experience using qualitative and quantitative information to generate business insights. · High attention to detail and demonstrated commitment to quality, accuracy, and well-supported decision-making. · Strong collaboration and relationship-management skills, with confidence engaging cross-functional partners and senior stakeholders. · Ability to manage multiple priorities, work through ambiguity, and identify pragmatic solutions to complex problems.

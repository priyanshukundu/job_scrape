Help businesses access the financing they need while guiding consistent execution across a regional portfolio management team. In this role, you will provide day-to-day leadership to Regional Portfolio Managers, help navigate complex transactions and portfolio matters, and maintain ownership of an assigned portfolio. You will partner closely with bankers, sales leadership, credit, and operations to keep priorities aligned, risks visible, and business moving forward. Job summary As a Team Lead Portfolio Manager in Commercial Lending, you oversee the day-to-day work of regional Portfolio Managers while directly managing an assigned commercial lending portfolio. You provide guidance on new financing opportunities, pricing analysis, and ongoing portfolio management, helping Portfolio Managers navigate complex matters and maintain consistent, timely execution. You partner closely with Commercial Solutions bankers and sales leadership and serve as a key point of coordination and escalation across credit, operations, and client service teams. You balance regional oversight with hands-on portfolio ownership, keeping deliverables on track, risks visible, and client needs at the forefront. Job responsibilities Manage regional Portfolio Managers, aligning priorities for timely, consistent execution of new business, pricing, annual reviews, and portfolio management. Develop and coach Portfolio Managers in supporting bankers and their aligned regions; share technical knowledge and best practices to strengthen analysis, onboarding, portfolio maintenance, and execution. Drive regional alignment and help shape strategic initiatives that strengthen credit origination and advance portfolio management capabilities. Lead the team through policy and procedure changes, identifying opportunities to improve efficiency while maintaining strong controls. Manage an assigned portfolio, driving disciplined credit execution, proactive risk management, and consistent portfolio practices. Partner with bankers to structure and execute new financing opportunities, providing cash flow and risk grade analysis, pricing support, and credit submission collaboration. Lead client onboarding with Credit, Operations, Treasury, Know Your Customer, Environmental, and Appraisal teams; guide Portfolio Managers through complex onboarding matters. Monitor portfolio performance, identify and address emerging risks, and ensure timely completion of portfolio maintenance and client documentation requirements. Required qualifications, capabilities, and skills At least 10 years of related experience in commercial lending, sales, portfolio management, commercial credit, or underwriting support. Experience directly managing teams or leading large initiatives, influencing others, aligning priorities, and driving execution. Commercial lending experience in cash flow and financial statement analysis, new financing requests, pricing, annual reviews, and portfolio oversight. Strong quantitative skills and sound judgment when addressing issues and escalations. Ability to work independently and collaboratively, balance portfolio ownership with regional oversight, and manage competing priorities. Strong follow-through, attention to detail, and adherence to established procedures and controls. Strong communication, client management, and cross-functional coordination skills, including the ability to explain complex matters clearly to Portfolio Managers, bankers, clients, and senior stakeholders. Bachelor’s degree in business, accounting, finance, economics, mathematics, management, or a related field. Proficiency with AI, Microsoft Word, Excel, and PowerPoint. Preferred qualifications, capabilities, and skills Master of Business Administration degree. Prior team lead experience in commercial lending or portfolio management.

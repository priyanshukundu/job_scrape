Understand data science opportunities, based on analytical knowledge, needed to deliver innovative solutions to uncover drivers of gaps in understanding of patient unmet needs and HCP prescribing patterns to improve strategic opportunities. Act as a data science expert, by learning and contributing to GSK’s data science community to grow their data skills, build best practices, and grow data science literacy within the organization Earn the trust of stakeholder partners to drive data-driven decisions through active engagement, advanced analytics expertise, demonstrated learning, and business knowledge. Establish and define project plans, proactively seek inputs needed to complete results according to plan and deliver milestones according to plan. Collaborate on cross-matrix teams and demonstrate subject matter expertise with other teams/functions to drive e]iciencies and seamless decision making. Use storytelling, powerful visualizations to demonstrate power of utilizing data science with business partners and analytics team members which enable fact-based recommendations that inspire usage for data driven decisions Why You?

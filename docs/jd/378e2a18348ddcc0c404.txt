We believe the greatest impact comes from combining technological capabilities with human expertise, judgment, and accountability Group Details Tradeweb is seeking an experienced sales professional to join our Buyside Sales team and grow our Institutional business across fixed income rates products, with a strong focus on mortgage-backed securities (MBS). In this role, you’ll own client relationships, develop new business, and help institutions expand their use of electronic trading. You’ll also bring client feedback and market insight to Product, Technology, and Operations, helping shape platform capabilities and trading workflows. Job Responsibilities Manage and grow institutional client relationships , including key MBS accounts, by understanding client needs, identifying revenue opportunities, and expanding market share. Win new buy-side business by leading prospecting, proactive outreach, and the sales process from initial engagement through securing new clients. Demonstrate Tradeweb’s value through client presentations, platform demonstrations, and sales materials tailored to clients’ trading needs. Guide clients through onboarding and integration , partnering with internal teams to resolve setup issues and ensure clients are ready to trade. Increase platform adoption by training clients on platform capabilities, execution strategies, and best practices that improve trading efficiency. Represent client needs internally , working with Product, Technology, and Operations to translate feedback into platform and workflow enhancements. Monitor rates and mortgage market developments and competitor activity to provide relevant client insights, anticipate needs, and inform product strategy. Qualifications Bachelor’s degree in Finance, Economics, or a related field, or relevant work experience required. 10+ years of experience in fixed income rates sales or institutional client coverage, particularly in MBS, strongly preferred. Series 7 and Series 63 licenses required. Strong client relationship, presentation, and platform demonstration skills, with excellent written and verbal communication. Familiarity with Tradeweb or similar electronic trading platforms preferred. Operations experience at a buy-side or sell-side institution is a plus.

The application window is expected to close on: 11/27/2026 Meet the team Join our Cryptographic Services Applications team, a group of security engineers who make sure Cisco products are secured from manufacturing through the field. We work cross-functionally with internal and external partners, including Trust Anchor Module groups and Supply Chain, to build and operate the cryptographic identity and provisioning capabilities that protect our products. Our work depends on deep cryptography expertise, close partnership, and shared accountability for security outcomes. We value mentorship and continuous improvement. Your impact As a Software Engineer in Cryptographic Services, you will write the code that secures our products from manufacturing through the field. You will design, implement, and test software that handles cryptographic operations, key and certificate management, and secure provisioning. You will take features from technical design through delivery with minimal guidance, writing clean, well-tested code. You will work hands-on with Trust Anchor Module groups, Supply Chain, and external manufacturing partners to integrate and validate security capabilities in the manufacturing flow. You will troubleshoot complex issues in production, perform code reviews, and share your knowledge with peers to strengthen our software development lifecycle. Minimum qualifications Bachelor’s degree with 5 years of related experience, Master’s degree with 3 years, or PhD with 1 year. Strong software development skills, including writing clean, functional code and unit tests with minimal guidance. Deep, hands-on experience implementing cryptography in software, including public key infrastructure, key and certificate management, digital signatures, and secure key storage. Experience applying secure coding principles and threat models to software features. Demonstrated ability to debug, test, and troubleshoot complex software issues throughout development and in production. Working knowledge of the SDLC and agile engineering practices. Preferred qualifications Hands-on experience with hardware roots of trust or trust anchor modules, such as TPMs, secure elements, or HSMs. Experience building or integrating software for secure manufacturing, including device identity, key injection, and secure provisioning. Experience working with cross-functional teams, including Supply Chain and external partners. Experience creating technical design documentation and performing code reviews.

JPMorgan Chase
Location: OH, United States

The Operations Risk and Control Associate will be responsible for self-audits of critical functions performed by Annuity, Life and Education Operations. This role will entail a good deal of attention to detail, independent judgment, and the ability to work with executive managers in the firm. This person will need the ability to 1) partner in business analysis, audit and process design sessions 2) assist with root cause analysis and 3) participate in or lead small projects. Responsibilities include: • Perform process control reviews to identify areas of improvement/validate the control environment (process maps, procedures, controls) • Ensure Risk & Control Self-Assessment findings are evaluated for root cause analysis and Action Plans are opened and completed, where necessary • Participate in shaping and improving processes across the platform to mitigate risk, improve efficiency and enhance controls • Identify, recommend, review and implement process changes to improve services and systems • Participate in product, platform, and workflow testing for technology releases

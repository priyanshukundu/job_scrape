Associate Manager, Service Delivery Management – Application Managed Services (AMS) Build our future together: The Associate Manager, Service Delivery Management will work collaboratively with the Manager, Service Delivery Management and platform team members within Application Managed Services (AMS) to support day-to-day service delivery for a defined subset of applications within a platform team, as part of Regeneron's Enterprise Managed Services (EMS) organization. This role supports and may oversee contractor or MSP resources delivering operational services for those applications. Where: Based at our Warren, NJ/Sleepy Hollow, NY location Where: 4 days onsite per week required Discover your role: Defining and managing project timelines within support and application monitoring services for your assigned applications, and assisting with system upgrades and installations within that scope Developing and maintaining standard processes for engagement, knowledge transition, and operational support; helping plan day-to-day work for yourself and any contractor resources you support Monitoring operational stability and performance for your assigned applications; supporting incident and change management activities and escalating issues to your supervisor as appropriate Supporting Computer System Validation (CSV) activities within your area for any GxP-validated applications you help support, in compliance with regulatory standards Contributing to continuous improvement and transformation efforts within your team, including automation and process standardization initiatives Tracking vendor and Managed Service Provider SLA adherence for your assigned applications, and flagging performance concerns to your manager Assisting with near-term technology evaluations for your area, and helping document recommendations for adoption Supporting or overseeing a small number of contractor or MSP resources, helping coordinate day-to-day task assignment Assisting with root cause analysis for service issues through discussions with users and review of incident data. This role requires: Bachelor’s degree in a relevant field. 6-8+ years of experience delivering IT services to business or consumers, including some experience supporting outsourced managed services or third-party delivery teams. Working knowledge of ITSM/ITIL, Agile and DevOps concepts, and CI/CD practices. Experience supporting vendor or Managed Service Provider relationships, including SLA tracking and issue documentation. Previous experience in the biotechnology and/or pharmaceutical industry, along with exposure to your assigned platform's core technologies (e.g., Veeva, Salesforce, Adobe, Power Platform, Atlassian, or GxP/Scientific systems), is preferred. Previous exposure to GxP-validated applications and computer system validation (CSV) concepts is preferred. Does this sound like you?

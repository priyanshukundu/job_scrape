Job Description About Aberdeen Aberdeen Group plc is one of Europe’s largest investment companies, and we are built on a long-standing culture of caring about the future and making a positive impact. Together we invest for a better future. We do it to make a difference to the lives of our clients and customers, our employees, society, and our shareholders. Our business is structured around three distinct areas focused on our clients' changing needs: Aberdeen Investments, Aberdeen Adviser, and interactive investor. As part of Aberdeen Adviser, this role helps deliver the insight, governance and operational excellence that enable advisory businesses to thrive and supports our commitment to Invest in your tomorrow. As a Portfolio Governance Lead, you will help turn this purpose into action by ensuring change investment is governed effectively and delivers meaningful outcomes for colleagues, advisers and clients. About the Department The Adviser business supports financial advisers across the UK with the technology, expertise and services they need to thrive. The Portfolio Governance team provides the structure, insight and oversight that enables effective portfolio delivery, helping teams make informed decisions, prioritise work and manage change consistently. Working collaboratively across Product, Technology, Finance and Risk functions, the team helps enable our clients to be better investors. About the Role As a Portfolio Governance Lead based in Edinburgh, you will establish and operate the governance, reporting and portfolio control environment that supports effective delivery and decision-making. You will lead portfolio governance, demand management, prioritisation, reporting and tooling strategies while providing clear oversight of risks, dependencies and performance. This is an influential role with significant exposure to senior stakeholders and the opportunity to shape portfolio management practices across Adviser. Through your work, you will help colleagues invest in their tomorrow by enabling successful delivery of strategic change initiatives. Key Responsibilities Establish and operate portfolio governance forums, standards and controls that support consistent and compliant delivery. Lead portfolio intake, demand management, prioritisation and roadmap planning to ensure work is aligned to strategic outcomes. Provide oversight of portfolio performance, risks, issues and cross-portfolio dependencies, recommending interventions where required. Own PMO tooling, portfolio data standards and reporting automation to improve efficiency and data quality. Deliver executive-level reporting, forecasting and decision support that translates complex information into clear actions. Work closely with the Resourcing & Value Lead to ensure capacity, capability and benefits insight informs portfolio decisions. About the Candidate - Essential skills Proven experience leading PMO, portfolio governance, planning and reporting within a complex change environment. Strong understanding of portfolio controls, governance frameworks, prioritisation and delivery assurance. Experience translating complex delivery information into actionable recommendations and executive insight. Knowledge of PMO tooling, reporting automation and data quality management across platforms such as Jira, ServiceNow, SharePoint or Power BI. Experience working across agile, waterfall or hybrid delivery environments. Desirable; Excellent analytical, communication and stakeholder management skills.

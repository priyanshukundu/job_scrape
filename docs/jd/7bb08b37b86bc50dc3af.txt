What our team members are saying: Video Clip 1 Video Clip 2 Video Clip 3 Learn more about us at cathaybank.com GENERAL SUMMARY The financial planning and analysis team is seeking a senior FP&A Data & Analytics Analyst to support the FP&A team. This role sits at the intersection of IT and FP&A, offering exposure to both the bank’s data infrastructure and its financial planning process. The role reports administratively to Information Technology, with day-to-day oversight and direction from the Head of FP&A, who establishes the position’s primary business priorities and deliverables. IT provides administrative management as well as technical oversight and guidance around the bank’s data infrastructure, tools and governance. This role offers an excellent opportunity to develop deep expertise in the bank’s data infrastructure, budgeting and planning process as well as frontline sales performance measurement. This position is a liaison between IT and the Financial Planning & Analysis team and is central to providing data analytics to drive the bank’s financial planning, budgeting and business analyses. The Senior Analyst will be a dedicated data expert resource to the FP&A team to support the team’s budgeting and planning system implementation, perform data analytics to help drive business decision making and support FP&A team’s profitability reporting needs. This role blends technical skills with business understanding and offers opportunities to contribute to automation and analytics enhancements within FP&A. It also provides a path to grow toward FP&A, data engineering or analytics leadership roles. Strong ability to understand critical business processes and to analyze complex problems is essential for the successful execution of value-added analytics. The Senior Analyst must have a strong commitment to quality and be skilled in operating and promoting an environment of teamwork. The Senior Analyst must be systematic and meticulous with details to effectively process large amounts of data into meaningful information. ESSENTIAL FUNCTIONS Integrate, reconcile and validate data from multiple systems to create coherent datasets for analytics; Investigate data discrepancies and resolve root causes Manage data integration workflows, including writing and optimizing SQL queries and leveraging Snowflake for data extraction and transformation Drive the design and fine-tuning of ETL (SSIS) processes to fetch and integrate data from diverse sources. Assist in creating the “single-source-of truth” loan and deposit data for FP&A; create and maintain data tables to build and scale the team’s database Build and maintain visualizations and dashboards in Power BI or similar BI tools Support the team’s Empyrean system implementation efforts and act as the system administrator once system goes live Create reports and forecasting models using advanced Excel tools including complex formulas, macros for data analysis and reporting Support FP&A’s annual budget & goal setting process, produce routine performance reports and scorecards as well as ad hoc analyses for frontline teams Analysis of operational and financial data to identify trends and drivers; summarize conclusion and implications of analysis QUALIFICATIONS Education: Bachelor’s degree in Information Systems, Finance, Accounting, Technology, or a related discipline preferred. Experience: 3-5 years of experience as a Business Analyst, Data Analyst, or in a similar position, preferably in financial institutions. A strong background in the following: Mastery in Structured Query Language, especially within the Microsoft SQL Server framework. Reporting/Business Intelligence tools such as Business Objects, SSRS, QlikView. Data marts and data warehouse. Good understanding of SQL, relational databases, mathematical formula, and data integration fundamentals. Demonstrable strengths in data-driven reporting and effective interpreting based on the discovery and findings. Experience in corporate FP&A or accounting preferred Skills/Ability: Strong written and verbal communication skills. Able to effectively communicate with peers and managers. Self-motivated. Must be able to prioritize, multi-task, and meet agreed deadlines. Able to work both independently and in a team. OTHER DETAILS Pay Range: $117,000 to $143,000/year Pay determined based on job-related knowledge, skills, experience, and location. This position may be eligible for a discretionary bonus.

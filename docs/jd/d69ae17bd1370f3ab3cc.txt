Lead Java Software Engineer - Vice President
Citi
Location: Tampa Florida United States
Link: https://citi.wd5.myworkdayjobs.com/2/job/Tampa-Florida-United-States/Lead-Java-Software-Engineer---Vice-President_261008691

As a Lead Java Software Engineer, you will design and deliver complex application systems that power Citi's technology platforms, working within a global Technology team based in Tampa, FL. You will lead the full software development lifecycle, from architecture through delivery, while mentoring developers and shaping engineering standards. This role offers the opportunity to influence how large-scale, mission-critical applications are built and scaled across the organization. Responsibilities Lead the design and development of Java-based application systems, ensuring solutions align with Citi's overall technology architecture. Partner with cross-functional management teams to integrate new products and deliver system enhancements that improve business processes. Resolve complex, high-impact technical issues by evaluating business and system processes against industry standards. Define coding, testing, debugging, and implementation standards that raise the quality and reliability of applications. Analyze complex technical challenges and develop practical solutions that address root causes rather than symptoms. Mentor and coach mid-level developers and analysts, guiding their technical growth and allocating project work. Apply sound judgment to day-to-day technical decisions, helping safeguard Citi and its clients by following relevant laws, regulations, and policies. Required Qualifications & Skills 8+ years of experience as a Software Engineer or Developer using Java, with a clear command of data structures and object-oriented design principles. Background in cloud-native development and container orchestration tools such as Docker, Kubernetes, OpenShift, or serverless platforms. Hands-on expertise in service-oriented and microservices architectures, including REST and GraphQL implementations. Practical experience with frameworks such as Spring Boot, Quarkus, Micronaut, or Vert.x, combined with software engineering best practices including unit testing, automation, design patterns, and peer review. Familiarity with front-end technologies such as Angular, JavaScript, or TypeScript, and CI/CD pipelines such as Tekton, Harness, CircleCI, or Jenkins. Experience working within agile delivery frameworks such as Scrum or Kanban, alongside exposure to database technologies including RDBMS, NoSQL, Oracle, MySQL, or MongoDB, and event-driven architectures such as Kafka or IBM MQ. Bachelor's degree in engineering, computer science, or equivalent practical experience. Beneficial Skills & Qualifications Experience architecting horizontally scalable, highly available, and low-latency applications across cloud and on-premise environments such as OpenShift or AWS. Familiarity with API management, infrastructure-as-code tools such as Terraform or CloudFormation, and job scheduling tools such as Autosys. Knowledge of security, observability, and monitoring tools such as Grafana, Prometheus, Splunk, ELK, or CloudWatch. Experience mentoring and providing technical leadership for teams of five or more developers. Background in the finance industry with exposure to compliance or risk management practices, and a master's degree in a related field. Education: Bachelor’s degree/University degree or equivalent experience What We Offer Citi invests in your growth as a technologist, giving you the tools, mentorship, and global scale to advance your career while building solutions that matter. Work in a hybrid model with 3 days in the office and 2 days working remotely each week. Access ongoing learning and development programs that strengthen your technical and leadership skills. Collaborate with engineering teams across the globe on high-impact, large-scale technology platforms. Benefit from competitive compensation and resources that support your financial wellbeing. Gain mentorship opportunities and the chance to shape engineering standards across the organization.

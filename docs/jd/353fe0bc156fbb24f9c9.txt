Here, you will find more than just a job—you will find purpose and pride. Your Role at Baxter This is where your work saves lives. At Baxter, every breakthrough has the potential to improve and save lives. As a Research Scientist I , you'll help advance next-generation biologic therapies by developing cutting-edge analytical methods, solving complex scientific challenges, and generating the data that drives innovation from early development through commercialization. Working alongside a collaborative team of scientists and engineers, you'll play a key role in bringing new healthcare solutions to patients worldwide. What You'll Be Doing Develop, optimize, and validate advanced analytical methods for biologic products, including LC-MS, bioassays, chromatography, capillary electrophoresis, and circular dichroism . Lead and execute scientific studies, analyze results, and communicate findings through protocols, reports, and presentations. Investigate and solve complex technical challenges, including product characterization and impurity identification. Generate high-quality analytical data to support product development, stability programs, and regulatory submissions. Collaborate across R&D, Quality, and Manufacturing to accelerate innovation and improve patient outcomes. Mentor team members, share technical expertise, and contribute to a culture of scientific excellence. What You'll Bri ng Bachelor's degree with 5+ years, Master's degree with 3+ years, or PhD in Biochemistry, Chemistry, Pharmaceutical Sciences, or a related field . Hands-on analytical development or characterization experience with biologic or biosimilar products Strong hands-on expertise with modern analytical instrumentation in a regulated environment. A passion for scientific problem-solving, innovation, and continuous learning. Preferred Qualifications Experience in a GMP-regulated pharmaceutical laboratory. Exposure to small molecule analytical techniques. Experience with Empower or comparable chromatography data systems. Why Baxter? Join a team where scientific curiosity, innovation, and purpose come together. Your work won't just advance products, it will help deliver life-sustaining therapies to patients around the world.

JOB DESCRIPTION: Main Purpose of Role The Senior Financial Analyst, FP&A serves as a key partner for the Cancer Diagnostics divisional headquarters, providing financial planning, analysis, reporting, and decision support to drive business performance and achievement of strategic objectives. This role is responsible for analyzing the division's P&L, balance sheet, and cash flow forecasting and analysis processes, ensuring accurate financial visibility, identifying risks and opportunities, and delivering actionable insights to leadership. The position also helps the coordination of budgeting, forecasting, and strategic planning processes across the business units by developing timelines, managing deliverables, ensuring timely and accurate submissions. This position is based in our Madison, WI office location. Main Responsibilities Manage and coordinate planning calendars, timelines, and deliverables across multiple business units to ensure successful completion of forecast, budget, and reporting cycles. Assist with the divisional budgeting, forecasting, and strategic planning processes, delivering accurate and timely financial projections. Analyze and report divisional financial performance, including P&L, balance sheet, cash flow, and key operational metrics. Partner with business and functional leaders to identify trends, risks, opportunities, and actions to improve financial performance and achieve business objectives. Prepare monthly and quarterly financial review materials, providing clear insights and recommendations to divisional leadership. Assist in develop financial models, scenario analyses, and business cases to support strategic initiatives, investments, and decision-making. Monitor working capital, cash flow, and balance sheet performance, recommending actions to optimize cash generation and capital utilization. Consolidate and validate financial submissions from business units, ensuring consistency, accuracy, and compliance with corporate requirements. Drive continuous improvement of financial planning, forecasting, reporting, and analytical processes. Support leadership with ad hoc financial analysis, special projects, and executive presentations as needed. Serve as a trusted financial partner, translating complex financial data into actionable business insights.

About TPG TPG is a leading global alternative asset management firm, founded in San Francisco in 1992, with investment and operational teams around the world. TPG invests across a broadly diversified set of strategies, including private equity, impact, credit, real estate, and market solutions, and our unique strategy is driven by collaboration, innovation, and inclusion. Our teams combine deep product and sector experience with broad capabilities and expertise to develop differentiated insights and add value for our fund investors, portfolio companies, management teams, and communities. TPG’s success depends on our people, and we build and sustain our world-class team by creating an inclusive, supportive culture within the firm that seeks excellence and encourages humility and transparency. The quality of our investments and our ability to build great companies depend on the originality of our insights. Reaching our firm’s full potential means supporting every team member to bring the fullness of their unique perspective to their work and to our community. We are committed to a diverse, equitable, and inclusive workplace to foster diversity of thought and reflect the breadth of our limited partners and portfolio companies. Description of Position To support the growth and success of TPG’s fundraising efforts in the Private Wealth channel, the team seeks to recruit several RIA Internal Sales team members in New York. Candidates will support the TPG wealth platform in establishing and maintaining relationships with RIAs in a particular region. Principal Responsibilities Partner with regional RIA sales leaders to contribute to the team’s regional business plan centered around a consultative, education-led, service-oriented approach to advisor engagement Uphold and enhance the reputation of TPG in the wealth management marketplace Maintain a high level of contact with intermediary clients and prospects acting as the subject matter expert across TPG’s diverse set of alternative investment offerings Cultivate relationships with RIAs through high-quality interactions including proactive and reactive calls, emails and follow-ups as well as limited in-person territory meetings Demonstrated ability to understand and articulate TPG’s products Take a consultative approach in profiling RIAs in order to understand their business, clients and investment challenges to better match TPG solutions to their needs Utilize the CRM system to log all client / prospect related activity on a daily basis Requirements 2-4 years of professional experience, preferably from a asset management internal sales desk or wealth management firm 7 & 63 licensed Ability to build new relationships and establish trust quickly and effectively Highly motivated, proactive and a sincere team player Results oriented, strong organizational and ability to multi-task Would contribute positively towards the development of TPG’s team, talent, and reputation in the private wealth marketplace Exceptional presence and interpersonal and communication skills, including the ability to articulate TPG’s strategy, investments and investment performance, as well as current and future market opportunities Extensive experience working with Microsoft Suite (Outlook, PowerPoint, Word, Excel) It is expected that the annual base salary for this role will range from $120,000 – $150,000.

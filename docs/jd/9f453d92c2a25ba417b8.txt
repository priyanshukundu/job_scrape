Role Overview This is a hands-on Lead Engineer role focused on building backend and full-stack capabilities for Mastercard's next-generation Decision Management Platform, the real-time system that helps score and approve billions of payment transactions. You will own technical areas end to end, write code, shape architecture, mentor engineers, and turn ambiguous goals into reliable services, APIs, workflow tools, and user-facing experiences. Key Responsibilities • Own meaningful backend and full-stack capabilities from problem definition through design, implementation, testing, deployment, and production support. • Design and build scalable services, APIs, workflow tooling, frontend experiences, and integrations. • Set technical direction, make pragmatic architecture decisions, and align engineers around maintainable solutions. • Use design reviews, code reviews, and hands-on coaching to raise engineering quality and reduce complexity. • Write code, build prototypes, and use AI coding tools to improve speed, quality, and team effectiveness. • Work closely with product, engineering, and senior stakeholders to turn ambiguous goals into working software and measurable outcomes. What We're Looking For • You lead through delivery. You have shipped meaningful backend, full-stack, or product-facing capabilities in complex production systems. •You think end to end. You connect user experience, APIs, backend design, data flows, operations, and business outcomes. •You influence by doing the work. You guide technical direction through judgment, clear communication, and hands-on credibility. •You care about quality. Testing, observability, security, clean interfaces, and maintainability are part of how you build. •You are adaptable.

Senior Associate Scientist, Cardio/Renal
Regeneron

Build our future together: At Regeneron, we use science and innovation to develop life-changing medicines for people with serious diseases. We are seeking an Associate Scientist/Senior Associate Scientist to support preclinical cardiovascular, renal, and metabolic disease research programs. In this role, you will contribute to the planning, execution, and analysis of in vivo studies and laboratory-based assays, working closely with scientists and cross-functional teams to generate high-quality data supporting therapeutic research and development. This position offers the opportunity to contribute to a fast-growing, science-driven organization making a meaningful difference to patients worldwide. When & where: Location: Tarrytown, NY Work model/flexibility: Onsite Discover your role: Conduct and support rodent in vivo studies, including animal handling, dosing, sample collection, necropsy, and tissue harvesting Perform laboratory assays, including ELISA and other biomarker analyses Process and organize biological samples for downstream analyses Maintain accurate experimental records, databases, and study documentation Analyze, interpret, and summarize experimental data Collaborate with scientists, research associates, and core facilities to support study objectives and timelines Assist in study planning, protocol execution, troubleshooting, and implementation of new experimental techniques Present experimental findings and contribute to study reports, presentations, and team discussions This role requires : M.S. in Biology, Biomedical Sciences, Pharmacology, Physiology, or a related field and 3+ years ; or B.S. with 4+ years relevant industry experience Hands-on experience conducting rodent in vivo studies Experience with ELISA and other laboratory-based assays Strong organizational, analytical, and communication skills Preferred: experience in cardiovascular, renal, metabolic, or translational disease research Preferred: experience with tissue collection, sample processing, and biomarker analysis Preferred: familiarity with experimental design and data interpretation in a preclinical research setting Does this sound like you?

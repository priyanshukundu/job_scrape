Job Overview: The Associate Analyst role within the Custody department will be responsible for providing problem-solving, reporting, project and training support to the Custody team, dealing primarily with physical security management. This will require the Associate Analyst to have a foundational level of knowledge of all team processes, as well as the ability to assist management and senior colleagues in feedback and implementation of updated processes. Support Management on project-based work, processing mergers and acquisitions from the Custody team, and participating in general business growth in the securities space for LPL. Responsibilities: · Communicate complex issues both in writing and verbally to management and internal/external vendors and customers. · Knowledge in balancing, reconciling and researching breaks related to DTC and our Foreign Custodian. · Act as a subject matter expert for all functions in Custody, assisting junior staff where help and guidance is needed. · Performs Key and Non-Key Controls in a tightly controlled environment relating to the management of physical securities for the firm. · Generating reports and tracking trend numbers. · Train junior staff on all Custody functions. · Work with management and analysts to test and implement updated processes. · Actively provide support to core processing on all functions when staffing needs arise, including fielding subject-matter specific calls from CXOne. · Provide high-level primary assistance in the maintenance of Policies and Procedures. What are we looking for? We’re looking for strong collaborators who deliver exceptional client experiences and thrive in fast-paced, team-oriented environments. Our ideal candidates pursue greatness , act with integrity , and are driven to help our clients succeed .

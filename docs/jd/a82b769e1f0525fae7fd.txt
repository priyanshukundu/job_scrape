A rapid research and development group within Analog Devices, Inc. is seeking a collaborative, self-driven Software Engineer to develop next-generation medical devices, diagnostic platforms, and clinical laboratory systems. The ideal candidate has hands-on experience developing software for regulated medical devices and diagnostic instruments that integrate with enterprise healthcare environments, including Laboratory Information Systems (LIS/LIMS), Electronic Health Records (EHR/EMR), PACS/VNA imaging systems, and clinical decision-support workflows. This individual will partner closely with biomedical engineers, clinicians, scientists, and healthcare stakeholders to translate innovative concepts into production-ready medical technologies. Successful candidates will have experience delivering software that supports clinical workflows, laboratory operations, diagnostic testing, healthcare interoperability, and regulatory compliance. Experience bringing connected medical devices from prototype through verification, validation, regulatory submission support, and commercial deployment is highly valued. Key Responsibilities Design, develop, and maintain software for regulated medical devices, in-vitro diagnostic (IVD) systems, laboratory instrumentation, and connected healthcare platforms. Develop and support integrations between medical devices and healthcare enterprise systems including LIS/LIMS, EHR/EMR, PACS/VNA, middleware, and laboratory automation platforms. Implement healthcare interoperability standards including HL7 v2.x, FHIR, DICOM, ASTM, and IHE profiles to enable bidirectional clinical data exchange. Build software supporting core clinical and laboratory workflows such as patient demographics, order management, specimen tracking, instrument connectivity, result reporting, quality control, and clinical documentation. Collaborate with clinicians, laboratory directors, pathologists, biomedical engineers, and healthcare IT teams to define requirements and ensure successful deployment within hospital and laboratory environments. Contribute to system architecture across embedded software, instrument control, cloud infrastructure, middleware, and clinical-facing applications. Develop secure APIs, cloud services, and enterprise integration solutions that support healthcare and diagnostic workflows. Implement and optimize software for real-time control systems, sensor integration, data acquisition, signal processing, analytics, and monitoring applications. Support software lifecycle activities including requirements management, risk analysis, design reviews, verification, validation, traceability, and change control within regulated environments.

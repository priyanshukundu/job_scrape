The Role The Operations Data Science team is looking for a Data Scientist to drive data-informed decision-making across our Member Experience organization. This role will partner closely with Operations, Contact Center, Product, and Engineering teams to identify opportunities to improve the end-to-end member experience, optimize operational processes, and help the organization achieve its goal of delivering an exceptional member experience (NPS 70). You will leverage experimentation, statistical analysis, and data-driven insights to understand the drivers of member satisfaction and identify opportunities to improve the member journey across various products, frontline agent tools and workflows, and manual review processes. The ideal candidate combines strong technical and analytical skills with business acumen and the ability to translate complex analyses into actionable recommendations that drive meaningful operational and member experience improvements. What you’ll do: Define and measure operational and member experience KPIs, including NPS, CSAT, resolution rates, and member effort. Design and analyze A/B tests and quasi-experiments to measure the impact of operational improvements and agent tooling. Conduct root cause and deep-dive analyses to identify member friction, operational inefficiencies, and opportunities for improvement. Partner with Operations Product and Teammate Experience teams to optimize agent workflows, internal tooling, and back-office processes. Translate insights into actionable recommendations that inform strategy, prioritize investments, and improve member outcomes. Build measurement frameworks, dashboards, and reporting to drive performance monitoring and data-driven decisions. Leverage AI tools to accelerate analyses, enhance problem-solving, and continuously explore emerging capabilities and applications. Evaluate IVR and conversational AI experiences, developing measurement frameworks to assess effectiveness and member outcomes. What you’ll need: Bachelor's degree in Statistics, Mathematics, Economics, Engineering, or another quantitative field; Master's degree preferred. 4+ years of relevant experience in Data Science or Analytics. Strong SQL and Python/R skills, with proficiency in data visualization tools such as Tableau. Demonstrated experience designing and evaluating A/B tests, quasi-experiments, and other causal inference methodologies. Strong foundation in statistics, analytical problem-solving, KPI development, and root cause analysis. Experience with operational, customer experience, or contact center analytics, including funnel analysis, cohort analysis, segmentation, and performance measurement. Ability to connect operational metrics to customer or member outcomes and translate findings into actionable business recommendations. Strong business acumen, communication, and stakeholder management skills, with the ability to independently drive ambiguous analytical projects from problem definition through execution and impact measurement. Nice to have: Experience in fintech, financial services, or other regulated industries. Experience partnering with Operations, Contact Center, Customer Support, or Member Experience teams. Familiarity with operational metrics such as first contact resolution, contact rates, average handle time, escalation rates, manual review rates, and processing turnaround times. Exposure to conversational AI, IVR systems, chatbot analytics, or AI evaluation methodologies. Experience with dbt, Airflow, and Snowflake.

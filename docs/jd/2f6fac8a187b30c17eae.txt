Performance Consulting (PC) is JPMorganChase's internal management consulting practice, a strategy and transformation team that partners with the firm's businesses to solve their hardest operational and process challenges. Agentic Coding Enablement (ACE), part of PC's Process Intelligence practice, helps consulting teams across PC work faster and sharper by applying agentic coding tools (AI tools such as Claude Code or GitHub Copilot) to real engagement problems. As a Process Improvement Associate on ACE, you are first and foremost a problem-solver. You will work alongside engagement teams to understand how a process really works, test ideas by turning them into quick working prototypes, and help communicate what you learn clearly enough for clients to act on. You will be learning and applying agentic coding as you go. We are looking for strong problem-solving first, and real curiosity about these tools. People come to this work from many backgrounds: data science, information systems, process improvement, analytics, engineering, consulting, and more. What we ask for most is how you think about problems, and that you are learning and applying agentic coding tools to solve them. The work centers on fit-for-purpose builds, such as working prototypes, discovery tools, and internal tools, that help us learn faster and help the business get clear on what it needs, not on building and maintaining production systems or handing off finished applications. If you have taught yourself to use AI tools to solve a problem, at work, in school, or on your own time, and you can showcase what you made and what you learned from it, we encourage you to apply.

Sr Associate Scientist, Drug Product Development and Technology - Analytical Coordination and Execution (ACE)
Regeneron
Location: TARRYTOWN
Link: https://regeneron.wd1.myworkdayjobs.com/Careers/job/TARRYTOWN/Sr-Assoc-Scientist_R52885-1

At Regeneron, we use science and innovation to develop life-changing medicines for people with serious diseases. We are seeking a Sr. Associate Scientist to join our Drug Product Development and Technology (DPDT) team, supporting major drug product development activities within the Analytical Coordination and Execution (ACE) team and the Compatibility/In-Use Stability (CIS) team, as the teams work to support all stages of clinical and commercial drug product development. In this role, you will apply biochemical and biophysical analytical methods to characterize biotherapeutics and support routine testing for the ACE team, working closely with program leads to coordinate analytical activities, evaluate data, and communicate results in support of product development. You will also support the CIS team in characterizing the physical and chemical properties of protein therapeutics and assessing their compatibility with drug delivery systems. The role spans diverse therapeutic modalities, including monoclonal and bispecific antibodies, recombinant proteins, antibody conjugates, and AAV-based therapeutics, with the goal of enabling robust clinical administration and commercial use. This position offers the opportunity to contribute to a fast-growing, science-driven organization making a meaningful difference to patients worldwide. When & where: Shift Requirements: Full-time Location: On-site in Tarrytown, NY What you’ll do: Perform routine sample analysis to support formulation and product development activities for both the ACE and CIS teams Serve as a key point of contact for 20+ program leads, coordinating weekly sample submissions, analytical testing activities, priorities, and timely delivery of analyzed results Partner with the manager to review analytical data and ensure the accuracy, integrity, and quality of results communicated to program teams Manage instrument scheduling for ACE activities and coordinate with teams across DPDT to resolve scheduling conflicts and accommodate program-specific testing needs Support DPDT general laboratory operations to maintain a safe, organized, and efficient working environment Join the Analytical Procedure (AP) team to maintain instruments, train new hires, and serve as a Subject Matter Expert (SME) Prepare and present annual updates to senior management summarizing ACE team activities, accomplishments, workload, and operational needs The environment and physical rhythms of this role: This is a laboratory environment requiring the ability to multi-task, working both independently and within a collaborative environment. Detail-oriented, self-motivated teamwork, with good oral and written communication skills, is required. Let’s find out if we’re a fit: Must-haves: B.S. or M.S. in Chemistry, Biochemistry, Biophysics, Pharmaceutical Sciences, or a related field, with at least 3 years of relevant industry experience Experience with biochemical and biophysical techniques for protein characterization such as particle size (DLS, HIAC, MFI), chromatography (SEC, IEX, RP), gel electrophoresis (CE-SDS, MCE, icIEF), and spectroscopy (UV-Vis) Strong interpersonal and communication skills with the ability to collaborate effectively with 20+ program leads Proficient with common laboratory software (Empower, ELN, etc.) Thrive today. Grow tomorrow.

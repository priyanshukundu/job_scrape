Medicinal Chemistry Senior Principal Research Scientist
Vertex Pharmaceuticals

Job Description Vertex is seeking a Senior Principal Research Scientist, Medicinal Chemistry to provide scientific leadership in the design and optimization of small-molecule therapeutics across discovery programs. Leading from the lab, this role will play a critical part in advancing high-quality drug candidates from early discovery through candidate selection, while partnering across multidisciplinary teams to solve complex scientific challenges. The ideal candidate is an accomplished medicinal chemist with deep expertise in small-molecule drug discovery, a strong record of scientific innovation, and demonstrated success in a fast-paced and matrixed research environment. This is an on-site role with approximately 75% of time being spent in the lab. Key Responsibilities Lead medicinal chemistry strategy for small-molecule discovery programs aligned with portfolio and project objectives. Design, prioritize, and guide synthesis of novel compounds to optimize potency, selectivity, pharmacokinetics, and developability. Interpret and integrate SAR, structural biology, DMPK, pharmacology, and safety data to drive compound design and progression. Partner closely with biology, DMPK, toxicology, and pharmaceutical sciences to advance program goals. Provide technical and strategic leadership to internal and external chemistry resources. Provide scientific leadership, mentorship, and development support to chemistry team members. Communicate program strategy, data, risks, and recommendations to senior leadership and governance teams. Support intellectual property strategy in partnership with internal stakeholders. Knowledge and Skills: Medicinal chemistry Organic synthesis Small-molecule drug discovery Lead optimization SAR analysis Compound design Candidate selection DMPK/ADME collaboration Drug property optimization Patent support Education and Experience: Ph.D. in Chemistry or related scientific discipline with 6+ years of relevant postdoctoral industry experience; Master’s degree with 9+ years of relevant industry experience; or Bachelor’s degree with 11+ years of relevant industry experience. Significant experience in medicinal chemistry and small-molecule drug discovery. Demonstrated success advancing discovery programs through candidate selection. Experience leading cross-functional and multidisciplinary teams including in matrixed settings.

Job Description Summary Essential Functions / Responsibilities: Work closely with a team of 8-15 people who are responsible for enhancing, evolving, and supporting large highly-available Electricity Market Management System (MMS). The team includes power systems, database, software, and optimization engineers. As a staff Database developer, contribute and lead advanced development in MMS Application software design, development, testing, integration and tuning to meet project and/or product commitments. Projects’ main deliverable is an overall customized system that’s based on GE Vernova's next generation Market System, a cloud-based solution that hosts multi-state wholesale energy markets. End user customers are Electric Utilities, Independent System Operators (ISOs) and Transmission System Operators (TSOs). These markets are key to decarbonizing the power system by ensuring reliability while allowing increasing penetration of Solar, Wind and Storage technologies. Collaborate with system engineers, frontend developers and software developers to implement solutions that are aligned with and extend shared platforms and solutions. Convert design documents into working applications. Apply principles of SDLC and methodologies like Lean/Agile/XP, CI, Software and Product Security, Scalability, Documentation Practices, refactoring, and Testing Techniques. Write code that meets standards and delivers desired functionality using the technology selected for the project. Build features such as web services and Queries on existing tables. Understand performance parameters and assess application performance. Job Description Qualifications / Requirements: Bachelor’s degree or equivalent in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, Information Technology, or related. Eight (8) years of experience in Oracle development within an on-premises or cloud-based enterprise environment. Work experience to include: Eight (8) years of experience with Oracle 12c or higher; Eight (8) years of experience programming with PL/SQL and supporting advanced PL/SQL features to include: advanced queues, triggers, types, functions, reference cursors, pipeline functions, subquery factoring clauses, and built-in Oracle packages (e.g., DBMS_SCHEDULER); Eight (8) years of experience with ANSI, and Oracle join syntax; Eight (8) years of experience diagnosing and remediating SQL performance issues; Eight (8) years of experience translating requirements into functional designs and implementing code solutions from functional designs; and Five (5) years of experience with GIT, TFS, or Toad for Oracle.

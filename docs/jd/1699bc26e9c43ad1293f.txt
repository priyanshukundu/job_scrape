Senior Software Engineer, Android Platform / AOSP
Qualcomm
Location: San Diego, California, United States of America
Link: https://careers.qualcomm.com/careers/job/446722040795

Company: Qualcomm Technologies, Inc. Job Area: Engineering Group, Engineering Group > Software Engineering General Summary: We are looking for a hands-on Senior Software Engineer with strong experience in Android platform software and AOSP to develop, integrate, debug, and optimize Android-based software solutions on Qualcomm Snapdragon platforms. The ideal candidate will have experience working across the Android software stack, including Android Framework, system services, native services, Binder IPC, HAL, AIDL/HIDL, Linux kernel/BSP interfaces, and platform integration. This role requires strong system-level debugging and problem-solving skills, with the ability to analyze complex issues spanning Android, Linux, middleware, device drivers, and hardware. The engineer will work closely with Qualcomm platform, BSP, multimedia, graphics, camera, connectivity, and customer engineering teams to enable and optimize Android solutions for production programs. Responsibilities Design, develop, integrate, and maintain Android/AOSP platform software on Qualcomm Snapdragon platforms. Develop and customize Android Framework components, system services, native services, and platform-level functionality. Integrate hardware functionality through HAL, AIDL/HIDL, JNI, Binder IPC, and native C/C++ components. Support Android platform bring-up, feature enablement, integration, and debugging on new Qualcomm SoCs and reference platforms. Debug complex system-level issues across Android Framework, native services, Linux kernel, BSP, device drivers, middleware, and hardware interfaces. Perform root-cause analysis of issues involving boot, stability, performance, memory, process lifecycle, IPC, networking, graphics/display, multimedia, camera, storage, and other Android subsystems. Analyze and optimize boot time, memory utilization, CPU utilization, system responsiveness, stability, and overall platform performance. Investigate and resolve Android compatibility and platform validation issues, including CTS/VTS failures where applicable. Develop test tools, scripts, automation, and diagnostics to improve software quality and development efficiency. Participate in software design and code reviews and contribute to architecture and technical decisions. Work closely with cross-functional Qualcomm engineering teams to resolve issues spanning hardware and software boundaries. Support customer and product teams in reproducing, debugging, and resolving complex platform issues. Drive assigned features and technical issues independently from requirements through implementation, integration, validation, and release. Mentor junior engineers and contribute to engineering best practices, code quality, and continuous process improvement. Minimum Qualifications: • Bachelor's degree in Engineering, Information Systems, Computer Science, or related field and 2+ years of Software Engineering or related work experience. OR Master's degree in Engineering, Information Systems, Computer Science, or related field and 1+ year of Software Engineering or related work experience. OR PhD in Engineering, Information Systems, Computer Science, or related field. • 2+ years of academic or work experience with Programming Language such as C, C++, Java, Python, etc. Preferred Qualifications 5+ years of software development experience, with significant hands-on experience in Android/Linux platform software. Strong programming skills in C/C++ ; experience with Kotlin/Java and Rust is a plus. Strong understanding of Android architecture and AOSP. Hands-on experience with Android Framework, system/native services, Binder IPC, HAL, AIDL/HIDL, JNI, Linux processes/threads/memory management, Android/Linux boot sequence, and the Android build system. Experience developing and debugging software in an embedded Linux/Android environment. Strong debugging and root-cause-analysis skills using tools such as ADB, logcat, dumpsys, tombstones, debugger traces, kernel logs, Perfetto, or similar tools. Strong understanding of software design, multithreading, concurrency, data structures, and the software development lifecycle. Ability to independently own complex technical problems and work effectively across multiple engineering teams. Experience with Qualcomm Snapdragon platforms, Android BSPs, or similar embedded SoC platforms. Experience with Android platform bring-up or porting to new hardware. Strong knowledge of Linux and familiarity with QNX, including system-level development, integration, and debugging, is a strong plus. Understanding of Linux kernel and device-driver architecture. Experience in one or more Android subsystems such as graphics/display, camera, audio, multimedia, connectivity, power/performance, or vehicle integration. Experience with Android Automotive OS or embedded Android products. Familiarity with Android CTS/VTS and platform certification/validation. Experience profiling and optimizing CPU, memory, startup/boot time, latency, and system performance. Experience with CI/CD, automated testing, Git, Gerrit, and large-scale software-development environments. Experience debugging complex HW/SW interactions on ARM-based SoCs. Strong written and verbal communication skills and the ability to work with distributed cross-functional engineering teams. Automotive experience is a strong plus Experience with Android Automotive OS (AAOS) and automotive infotainment/ADAS compute platforms. Understanding of Android vehicle architecture, including Vehicle HAL (VHAL) and integration with vehicle services. Experience with automotive interfaces and middleware such as CAN, Ethernet, SOME/IP, or DDS. Familiarity with automotive software-development processes, including ASPICE, functional safety, security, and production/SOP requirements. Experience working with automotive OEMs, Tier-1 suppliers, or production automotive software Qualcomm is an equal opportunity employer.

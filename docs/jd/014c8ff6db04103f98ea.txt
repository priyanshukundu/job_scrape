Role Overview We are seeking a Surveillance Data Scientist to help build and scale surveillance capabilities across private asset-backed finance. Sitting at the intersection of investments, data and technology, the role will develop analytical tools and data infrastructure that enable Portfolio Managers, Asset Management and Risk to monitor performance, compare realized outcomes with underwriting assumptions and identify emerging risks and opportunities. Responsibilities Design and build analytical tools for private asset-backed investments, including loan-level performance aggregators, return calculators, benchmarking tools and exception identification. Build and automate data flows with Technology to support accurate exposure, collateral, cash-flow and performance monitoring. Develop consistent surveillance across standardized asset types such as consumer loans, student loans and auto finance, and extend capabilities over time to mortgage whole loans and other private asset classes. Reconcile realized performance against underwriting assumptions, identify material variances and help translate findings into actionable insights for Portfolio Managers, Asset Management and Risk. Partner with public- and private-side Technology teams to develop reusable tools and APIs for investment and analytical platforms. Work directly with Portfolio Managers, Asset Management, Risk and quantitative modelers to identify gaps in investment performance analysis, assumptions and models. Produce clear surveillance reporting and, over time, targeted sector research to identify performance trends and relative-value opportunities. Requirements Master's degree in Financial Engineering, quantitative finance, statistics, applied mathematics, computer science or a related quantitative discipline. Strong quantitative skills and a solid understanding of structured credit and asset-backed investments; experience with private credit or asset-backed finance is strongly preferred. Hands-on working knowledge of Python and SQL, with the ability to query data, develop analytical tools and automate repeatable processes. Experience working with loan-level, collateral, cash-flow or investment-performance data and translating analysis into practical investment insights. Ability to work independently in an evolving environment, shape solutions from ambiguous requirements and partner effectively across investment, risk and technology teams.

Commercial Real Estate Finance Group (CREFG) Asset Originations The Commercial Real Estate Finance Group (CREFG) Asset Originations team is responsible for the underwriting, structuring, and closing of commercial real estate loans across our CMBS and balance sheet lending platforms. Our team works directly with originators to evaluate opportunities, structure transactions, assess credit risk, and lead deals from initial underwriting through closing. Role Overview (Analyst) We are seeking Analysts to underwrite, structure, and execute commercial real estate debt transactions across the full US lending platform. This individual will work on live deals from day one spanning fixed and floating rate CMBS deals to balance sheet and bespoke lending opportunities. Working across all property types and regions, this individual will combine hands-on credit analysis with direct exposure to originators, capital markets, and internal credit committees as transactions move from initial bid through closing and distribution.

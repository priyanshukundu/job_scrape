Citibank, N.A. seeks a Risk Reporting 2LOD Lead Analyst for its Irving, Texas location. DUTIES: Develop Operational Risk loss forecasts under base and stress scenarios for Enterprise-wide regulatory exercises. Execute loss forecasting methodologies using internal loss data, external events, emerging risks, and macroeconomic assumptions. Generate forward-looking loss projections across risk types and business segments to support capital adequacy and stress testing requirements. Partner with Model Risk Management, Finance, Regulatory Affairs, and Enterprise Stress Testing teams to ensure forecasts meet governance and regulatory standards. Prepare and maintain methodology documentation, including assumptions, limitations, and model overlays for review and approval. Review and challenge forecasting inputs to ensure consistency, transparency, and conservatism aligned with supervisory expectations. Respond to regulatory, audit, and internal review inquiries by explaining forecast drivers, assumptions, and changes across cycles. Conduct benchmarking to evaluate forecast performance and support continuous model refinement. Summarize forecast results for internal stakeholders, highlighting key loss drivers, emerging risk themes, and stress vulnerabilities. A telecommuting/hybrid work schedule may be permitted within a commutable distance from the worksite in accordance with Citi policies and protocols REQUIREMENTS: Bachelor’s degree, or foreign equivalent, in Business Analytics, Computer Science, or a related field, and six (6) years of experience in the job offered or in a related occupation performing data analysis activities. Six (6) years of experience must include: Working with large datasets and extrapolating insights, interpreting complex financial models, and using data visualization and advanced analytics skills to evaluate the impact of different economic scenarios; Validating and documenting processes, risks and controls including methodologies in line with policies and guidelines applicable to identify and mitigate risks; Utilizing Microsoft Office including Excel and Data Visualization tools for generating metrics and data visualization, PowerPoint for presentation decks, and Word for writing and editing procedural and technical documentation; and Performing deep-dive data analyses and end-to-end data flow tracing of the reporting process for both regulatory reports and internal management reports and identifying the root causes for data concerns and data quality issues. In the alternative, the employer will accept Master’s degree, or foreign equivalent, and four (4) years of experience. 40 hrs./wk. Applicants submit resumes at https://jobs.citi.com/ .

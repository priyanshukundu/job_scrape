Instructional Design Associate, Global Financial Crimes Training Team
JPMorgan Chase
Location: Jersey City, NJ, United States
Link: https://jpmc.fa.oraclecloud.com/hcmUI/CandidateExperience/en/sites/CX_1001/job/210808487

The Instructional Design Associate will support the Global Financial Crimes Training Team by designing, developing, and implementing engaging learning solutions that advance business objectives and meet regulatory requirements. Working closely with subject matter experts, Compliance teams, business leaders, and external vendors, this role will translate complex content into clear, effective training for employees at all levels. As an Instructional Design Associate within the Global Financial Crimes Training Team, you will contribute to learning programs from development through implementation, helping ensure they are relevant, accessible, and aligned with business and regulatory needs. Success will require strong collaboration, attention to detail, and the ability to work with diverse stakeholders to create learning experiences that support employee understanding and application. Job Responsibilities Design, develop, and implement learning solutions using instructional design principles and adult learning theory to support Global Financial Crimes Function. Create interactive and innovative instructional materials, including learning objectives, storyboards, assessments, and compliance training content in various modalities (e.g., videos, microlearning, interactive PDFs, e-learning). Ensure all e-learning courses and training materials meet Section 508 accessibility standards and comply with industry regulations and company policies. Utilize authoring tools (e.g., Articulate Storyline 360) and Learning Management Systems (LMS) to design and deliver instructional materials. Apply instructional design models (e.g., ADDIE, SAM) to create engaging and effective learning experiences. Manage individual workload throughout the training development process, including meeting project deadlines, staying within budget, communicating and documenting project status, and obtaining SME approvals. Partner with Content Experience Team (CXP) or external vendors to design high-quality compliance training, including e-learning courses. Liaise with internal stakeholders to understand key objectives and requirements for the development of compliance training solutions. Evaluate the effectiveness of training programs through feedback, assessments, and performance metrics. Continuously update and improve learning content based on feedback and changes in regulations or business needs. Required Qualifications, Skills, and Capabilities Bachelor’s degree (or equivalent) in Instructional Design, Education, Human Resources, or a related field. 3+ years of experience in learning and development, including instructional design, technical writing, and adult learning theory, preferably within the financial services industry. Experience applying adult learning methodology in a corporate training setting. Proven experience with e-learning development tools (Articulate Storyline 360) and LMS platforms. Experience managing relationships with external vendors. Working knowledge of project management disciplines to govern training design and development projects. Excellent organizational, interpersonal, and communication skills (written and oral). Ability to effectively interact with stakeholders and clearly articulate key messages and strategies. Creative and strategic thinker with a strong control mindset. Proficiency in developing courses and training videos to meet Section 508 accessibility compliance. Intermediate to advanced knowledge of Microsoft Office applications (PowerPoint, Word). Preferred Qualifications, Skills, and Capabilities Master's Degree in a related field Knowledge of Learning Management Systems. Knowledge of graphic design principles. Knowledge of JavaScript. Proficiency in using Artificial Intelligence (AI) tools to enhance instructional design, such as: Microsoft Copilot LLM Suite or similar AI platforms Custom AI assistants

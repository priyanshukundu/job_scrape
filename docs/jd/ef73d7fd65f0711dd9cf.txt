Job Description: HPE is seeking a Digital Architect, Supplier Onboarding to lead the end-to-end business architecture for Supplier Onboarding within HPE Networking’s Lead-to-Cash (L2C) transformation. Serve as the L2C horizontal architecture lead for Supplier Onboarding, connecting requirements across Plan (planning, inventory), Source, Make, Deliver (fulfillment & logistics) & Spares. Partner with supplier workstream leads, business, operations & IT teams to translate business needs into scalable process, data, system & operating-model designs. Align future-state processes & digital solutions, identify cross-functional dependencies & opportunities for standardization, & support our ambition to build the best networking business on the planet. Provide cross-workstream business architecture leadership for Supplier Onboarding, connecting supplier-specific requirements to the end-to-end Lead-to-Cash design. Partner with Supply Chain stakeholders & IT teams to analyze business needs, develop future-state designs & requirements, evaluate options & bring architecture recommendations to the appropriate decision owners. LOCATION: Candidates must reside & be able to work a hybrid schedule in one of the following HPE offices: Houston, TX; Sunnyvale, CA; Roseville, CA; Westford, MA RESPONSIBILITIES Supplier Onboarding Architecture Leadership: Lead development of future-state onboarding processes, capabilities, policies, controls & business architecture across in-scope supplier workstreams. Connect supplier onboarding designs to Plan, Source, Make, Deliver, Spares & Enablement, partnering with IT architects on the supporting digital solution designs. Supplier Requirements & Capability Analysis: Partner with Supply Chain stakeholders, supplier workstream leads & subject matter experts to define process, capability, data, integration, system & operating-model requirements. Distinguish common onboarding requirements from supplier-specific variations, identify gaps & exceptions, & determine where existing capability patterns can be adopted, extended or newly defined. Epics, User Stories & Requirements Documentation: Partner with IT, business workstream leads & subject matter experts to translate desired capabilities & future-state processes into well-defined epics, features & user stories. Document & clarify requirements, acceptance criteria, dependencies & business outcomes. Maintain traceability through design, build, testing & deployment. Onboarding Blueprint & Design Artifacts: Develop & maintain the business architecture content of the Supplier Onboarding Blueprint. This includes the common onboarding Playbook, reusable Capability Patterns & design inputs to supplier-specific workplans (Supplier Workplans) & supplier-facing information packs (Supplier Packs). Maintain supporting process flows, capability models, business rules, functional requirements, design inventories & architecture artifacts in partnership with workstream leads & the program management office (PMO). End-to-End Impact Assessment: Evaluate upstream & downstream impacts of design decisions across product & master data, planning, sourcing, manufacturing, inventory, order fulfillment, transportation, logistics, spares & finance. System, Data & Integration Alignment: Partner with IT architects & delivery teams to align supplier onboarding requirements & future-state processes with enterprise platforms & data models, including enterprise resource planning (ERP), planning, product lifecycle management/product data management (PLM/PDM), order management, warehouse management, transportation & integration capabilities. Define business requirements for supplier connectivity, data exchanges & process handoffs, including B2B connectivity where applicable. Assess the business implications of proposed message types & integration approaches, escalating unresolved architecture dependencies before supplier commitments are finalized. Design Workshop Leadership: Facilitate or participate in cross-functional Supplier Onboarding architecture & design workshops to resolve process handoffs, clarify requirements & develop recommendations for business & IT decision owners. Document decisions, dependencies, assumptions, risks & open questions, coordinate design follow-up actions & complete assigned actions. Horizontal & Cross-Domain Alignment: Coordinate business design alignment across supplier workstreams & other Lead-to-Cash architecture teams. Identify integration points, handoffs, design dependencies & opportunities for standardization & harmonization; reconcile conflicting requirements & raise unresolved cross-workstream decisions with the appropriate business & IT owners. Decision Traceability: Maintain architecture repositories, decision logs & design documentation, ensuring requirements remain traceable through future-state design & implementation. Architecture Recommendations: Analyze business needs, evaluate solution options & develop fact-based recommendations to inform architecture & business decisions. Delivery & Supplier Readiness Support: Partner with program, business & IT teams through design, build, testing, deployment readiness & transition to operations. Review design-related acceptance criteria, exceptions & readiness evidence with Testing & MOC & Readiness leads. Help ensure delivered solutions align with approved business requirements, architecture & intended Supply Chain outcomes, and that supplier-facing requirements reflect the agreed design baseline. KNOWLEDGE & SKILLS Strong understanding of end-to-end Supply Chain processes across Plan, Source, Make, Deliver & Spares including demand & supply planning, inventory, manufacturing, fulfillment, logistics, asset management & repair. Working knowledge of supplier & partner onboarding, including capability assessment, process & data requirements, EDI/B2B integration, supplier master data, testing & operational readiness. Understand how supplier onboarding decisions affect inventory management, inventory optimization, supply/demand matching & planning and execution concepts such as sales & operations planning (S&OP), material requirements planning (MRP) & available-to-promise (ATP). Ability to apply business architecture, process design & digital transformation principles to define reusable supplier onboarding patterns, evaluate design trade-offs & guide cross-workstream alignment. Strong analytical & systems-thinking capabilities, with the ability to identify dependencies across business processes, data, systems & geographies. Ability to translate complex Supply Chain business needs into clear functional & technology requirements. Ability to develop process maps, capability models, business rules, functional requirements & solution-design documentation. Experience or familiarity with applying AI & automation in Supply Chain, including defining AI-enabled use cases, data requirements, controls & success measures, is preferred. Strong cross-functional communication, workshop facilitation & stakeholder management skills, with the ability to work independently as part of a large global transformation team. REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS 6+ years of relevant experience involving Supply Chain, with experience in at least one of the following disciplines: business analysis, solution design, business architecture, process design, digital transformation or enterprise systems implementation. Experience in one or more functions within the end-to-end Supply Chain, including planning, sourcing, manufacturing, inventory, fulfillment, logistics, spares or partner operations. Experience supporting large-scale enterprise transformation, systems implementation or operating-model transformation initiatives. Experience translating business requirements & future-state processes into functional requirements, epics, user stories & acceptance criteria. Experience using tools such as Jira is preferred. Demonstrated experience leading cross-functional requirements or design alignment, partnering with business stakeholders, IT architects & technology delivery teams throughout design, build, testing & deployment. Application familiarity is beneficial, not mandatory; experience with every listed platform is not required. Relevant areas include planning & execution (Oracle Supply Chain Planning / Oracle SCP & SAP S/4HANA); supplier networks, sourcing & collaboration (SAP Ariba / SAP Business Network, Coupa / Coupa CSO & Z-Flow or comparable supplier collaboration workflow platforms); integration (webMethods / Seeburger); product & supplier data (PLM/PDM platforms such as Agile / Oracle PLM & supplier master / eMDM / MDG); & data & analytics (Snowflake / EDP / Databricks). Advanced hands-on SAP experience supporting Supply Chain processes is required; SAP S/4HANA experience is preferred. Experience with supplier or partner onboarding, B2B connectivity, or multi-party Supply Chain integration is preferred. Bachelor’s degree in Supply Chain, Information Systems, Engineering or a related field, or equivalent relevant experience; advanced degree preferred.

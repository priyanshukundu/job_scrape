Job Description Summary: The secondary market is a rapidly growing segment of the global private investment universe and has become a widely used tool by both limited partners and general partners for portfolio management. The CA Secondaries Team, comprising 8 investment professionals across Boston, San Francisco, and London, manages all aspects of the investment process for LP-led, GP-led, and direct secondary transactions on behalf of Cambridge Associates’ client base. Working closely with the broader CA Private Investments Research Team, the Secondaries Team sources, evaluates, and executes secondary transactions globally across all private market asset classes, including private equity buyouts, growth equity, venture capital, private credit, and private real assets. We are looking for a highly motivated and capable individual to join the Secondaries Team in Boston as an Investment Analyst/Associate. The ideal candidate is eager to build a long-term career in private markets secondary investing with our team and contribute to the growth of our platform. The Analyst/Associate will receive in-depth training and ongoing mentorship from senior investment professionals while contributing to all stages of the investment process, from sourcing and underwriting through execution. This role is open at the Analyst or Associate level; title and responsibilities will be calibrated to each candidate’s experience, and the team welcomes applicants at either level. Job Description: Job Responsibilities: The Analyst/Associate will work alongside senior deal team members to evaluate secondary transactions. Responsibilities include: Conducting due diligence on private investment funds and underlying portfolio companies, researching relevant industries, and building financial models to assess valuations, cash flows, and expected returns. Screening investment opportunities and supporting deal flow management. Preparing for and participating in meetings with private equity fund executives, portfolio company executives, and other senior investment professionals, both internal and external to CA. Preparing written research and analysis to inform investment decisions by the CA Secondaries Investment Committee, including concise opportunity summaries and in-depth due diligence reports. Additionally, the Analyst/Associate will serve as a resource to teams across CA on secondaries-related matters and collaborate with senior professionals to support the team’s engagement with existing and prospective clients. The Analyst/Associate will also contribute to projects and analyses beyond individual transactions to deepen the team’s market knowledge and support the continued development of the CA Secondaries platform. Qualifications: Bachelor’s degree with a record of achievement (any major; preference for finance, accounting, economics, or a related field). 0-2 years of relevant investment or transaction experience. Demonstrated interest in private equity, including secondary investments and/or direct investments. Strong analytical and quantitative skills; familiarity with financial statements and financial analysis. Proficiency in Excel and experience building financial models. Ability to thrive in a fast-paced, entrepreneurial environment and work effectively both independently and as part of a team. Strong written and verbal communication skills, including the ability to present investment analysis in clear, concise writing and communicate effectively with internal and external stakeholders. Strong work ethic and attention to detail; a self-starter who takes initiative and ownership of their work. Willingness and ability to travel domestically and internationally. Must be eligible to work without restriction in the US.

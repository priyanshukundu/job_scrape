Job Description As a valued colleague on our team, you will collaborate with the team to build, develop, and design functionality for products, as well as participate in the implementation, enhancement, and maintenance of existing products. THE IMPACT YOU WILL MAKE The SF Risk - Product Management – Senior Associate role will offer you the flexibility to make each day your own, while working alongside people who care so that you can deliver on the following responsibilities: Participate in activities that support strategies for new and existing products. Assist the team with the implementation and rollout of new products and product enhancements. Identify opportunities for product improvements and provide recommendations and feedback to senior team members. Collaborate with cross-functional partners on the ongoing maintenance and management of the product lifecycle. Assist the team in ensuring product strategy and priorities align with business objectives. Support product demand, release, and backlog activities, including translating business and design requirements into actionable product work. Contribute to prototypes, wireframes, reporting, and data analysis that inform product decisions and improve the user experience. Please note: This position based in Reston, Virginia.

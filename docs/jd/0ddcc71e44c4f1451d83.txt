GENERAL FUNCTION: The Data Engineer I supports the design and development of data platforms, tools, and solutions that enable the Bank to manage, secure, and extract value from its data. This role builds foundational skills while contributing to well-defined solutions for collecting, storing, processing, and serving data across traditional and modern architectures. Engineers work with guidance in on-premises and cloud-based environments to support reliable, secure, and well-governed data capabilities. The role includes learning to use AI-assisted engineering capabilities to improve productivity, code quality, testing, and documentation, while contributing to trusted data assets that support analytics, artificial intelligence, and machine learning use cases. Data Engineers I grow their individual abilities through continuous learning, collaboration, and successful delivery of assigned work. Responsible and accountable for risk by openly exchanging ideas and opinions, elevating concerns, and personally following policies and procedures as defined. Accountable for always doing the right thing for customers and colleagues while ensuring that actions and behaviors drive a positive customer experience. While operating within the Bank's risk appetite, achieves results by consistently identifying, assessing, managing, monitoring, and reporting risks of all types. ESSENTIAL DUTIES & RESPONSIBILITIES: - Participate in the design, development, and support of data solutions, APIs, tools, and processes that enable delivery of business capabilities across traditional and modern data platforms. - Work with engineers, IT application teams, Enterprise Architecture, infrastructure, information security, and LOB stakeholders to understand defined business and technical requirements and contribute to data-driven solutions for the Bank. - Apply foundational technical knowledge to investigate problems related to system and application design, performance, integration, and security, seeking guidance as needed. - Learn about current trends and technologies related to the banking industry, data engineering and architecture, data security, AI-readiness, and related topics. - Assist with building and maintaining CI/CD pipelines and self-service deployment tools. - Assist with evaluating data and software products and documenting findings as needed. - Support assigned data solutions throughout their lifecycle, including development, testing, deployment, monitoring, operational support, modernization, and retirement. - Provide support and troubleshooting for data pipelines and platforms, escalating complex or critical production issues as appropriate. - Participate in internal projects, including legacy system modernization, data pipeline modernization, monitoring, and documentation improvements. - Contribute to the optimization and automation of data pipelines for efficiency, scalability, observability, and performance. - Develop and maintain automated testing, validation, reconciliation, and data quality controls with guidance to support reliable and trusted data solutions. - Learn to use approved AI-assisted engineering capabilities to improve productivity, code quality, testing, documentation, and problem solving. - Follow established data product expectations for quality, discoverability, documentation, lineage, and governance within assigned work. - Build relationships, learn from other engineers, share knowledge, and contribute to the overall effectiveness and growth of the team. - Manage and prioritize assigned work with guidance in a dynamic environment. - Deliver assigned work in Agile teams through close collaboration with engineers, product owners, and stakeholders. SUPERVISORY RESPONSIBILITIES : None. MINIMUM KNOWLEDGE, SKILLS AND ABILITIES REQUIRED: - Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Systems, or related field, or equivalent combination of education and experience. - Strong verbal and written communication skills, with the ability to tailor messaging to technical and non-technical audiences including business stakeholders, IT sponsors, and leadership. - Foundational knowledge of SQL, including joins, data transformations, and query troubleshooting. - Familiarity with relational databases, SQL-based data platforms, and common data processing patterns.

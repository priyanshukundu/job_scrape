Our vision is to transform how the world uses information to enrich life for all . Micron Technology is a world leader in innovating memory and storage solutions that accelerate the transformation of information into intelligence, inspiring the world to learn, communicate and advance faster than ever. As a Software Development Engineer at Micron you will configure, develop, test, maintain and support Micron and third party applications. You will create and analyze software specifications, and research, design, document, modify, test, and support software throughout the development life cycle. You can demonstrate the functional and technical skills necessary to switch between development technologies and merge them to deliver the best solution. You have a proven ability to create quality software and participate in quality improvement efforts. You will provide solutions that create business value while at the same time minimizing negative impact through defects, downtime, or performance degradation. Responsibilities: Coordinate tool automation and host enhancements by partnering with automation developers and domain experts to deliver projects on schedule Gather, prioritize, and document requirements for new automation systems and enhancements across 300mm fabs globally Test and support manufacturing and automation software releases, including upgrades and resiliency improvements Fix and resolve automation and software issues in a daily support role, including 24x7 on-call rotation Maintain user documentation and provide regular project status reporting to management Minimum Qualifications: Bachelor’s degree or Master’s degree or equivalent experience in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field with 5+ years of enterprise software development experience. Hands-on experience with object-oriented programming, software development lifecycle (SDLC) practices, and Agile/Scrum methodologies. Experience using Artificial Intelligence (AI) tools and technologies. Software testing experience within manufacturing or automation environments. Strong analytical, written, and verbal communication skills, with the ability to manage multiple priorities effectively. Preferred Qualifications: Experience supporting or integrating automation systems in a semiconductor or manufacturing environment Knowledge of software architecture, system integration, and automation tool frameworks Experience crafting user documentation and training materials for software releases Proven ability to solve complex software and automation issues across multiple systems Experience supporting global or multi-site operations with standardized reporting and metrics As a world leader in the semiconductor industry, Micron is dedicated to your personal wellbeing and professional growth.

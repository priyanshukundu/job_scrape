Job Description Summary The Risk Director plays a critical leadership role in driving the commercial risk strategy and governance for the Vernova Wind business within the North America region. Acting as the right hand to the Global Executive Commercial Risk Leader, you will provide assertive, day-to-day risk governance, deal underwriting, and commercial guidance for regional deal pipelines. You will ensure that regional commercial transactions adhere to GE Vernova risk processes, balancing growth and profitability with rigorous risk mitigation. Recognized as a regional subject matter expert, you will empower commercial and sales teams to navigate complex customer negotiations, drive process simplification, and foster a culture of continuous improvement and disciplined underwriting Job Description Essential Behaviours The Risk Director will: Create the agenda: Proactively identify and mitigate regional risk trends before they impact deal execution or project delivery. Make the complex simple: Translate intricate contractual, technical, and financial risks into clear, actionable recommendations for regional and global leadership. Bring a point of view: Apply strong commercial judgment to add value where it matters most during deal structuring and negotiations. Drive organizational change: Champion digital tools, standardized processes, and continuous improvement initiatives across the regional commercial team. Raise the quality of governance: Ensure consistent application of delegation of authority (R-table) and risk review frameworks locally. Exercise courage and judgment: Challenge assumptions in deal reviews and act as a trusted advisor to Regional Commercial Directors. Roles and Responsibilities 1. Deal Underwriting & Risk Governance Provide contracting and risk underwriting advice for a broad range of product/service offerings (New Units, Services, Re-powering, Aftermarket) and execution scopes (Equipment, Logistics, Installation, TSAs, FSAs). Lead regional deal and risk review meetings, validate risk/reward balances (terms & conditions, technical risks, margin, and security of payment), and contribute to approval escalation meetings. Review and approve regional risk modules, maintaining oversight of deal approval status to ensure smooth velocity through the pipeline. Ensure rigorous compliance with the global risk review and delegation of authority process (R-table) 2. Strategic Commercial Partnership Partner closely with Regional Commercial Directors and sales teams to proactively address issues, structure winning proposals, and maximize margin while minimizing risk exposure. Identify high-impact regional risk trends and feed insights into the global analytics and process improvement frameworks. Collaborate with key functional stakeholders, such as Legal, Finance, Supply Chain and Projects in the proactive identification and mitigation of risks. Support lean negotiations on terms and conditions without compromising the desired risk profile. 3. Process Improvement & Regional Leadership Lead regional initiatives to drive rigor, standardization, and simplification within the risk management and sales/commercial processes. Collaborate with Global Projects, Services, and Insurance Risk Management teams to implement "lessons learned" and mitigate contractual injury/damage or insurance risk exposures. Support New Country Entry assessments within the region, ensuring legal, tax, product/grid compliance, and delivery readiness are thoroughly vetted. Be a champion of digitization and contribute to the delivery of improvements in deal review, deal flow, Salesforce system and AI enabled contract review, among other initiatives. Required Qualifications Bachelor's degree from an accredited university. Minimum of 15 years of experience in commercial, sales operations, or risk management within the energy sector, including capital equipment or large projects. Minimum of 15 years of experience with large energy project contracting, including Turbine Supply Agreements, Services, and/or Aftermarket agreements. Minimum of 15 years of experience in risk underwriting and negotiating complex commercial terms and conditions. Minimum of 15 years of experience working in a matrixed, cross-functional international environment. Desired Qualifications MBA, Master’s Degree, or Juris Doctor (JD) from an accredited university. Direct commercial operations or risk leadership experience specific to the wind energy or heavy equipment sector. Demonstrated track record in driving continuous improvement initiatives or digital transformation within commercial operations. Familiarity with regional energy markets, project financing, and energy construction/logistics within the assigned geography. Strong commercial acumen and deep contractual/legal understanding of large energy project contracting. Strong oral, written, and presentation skills, with the ability to communicate effectively in a fast-paced, dynamic commercial environment. Strong interpersonal and influencing skills, including the ability to constructively challenge senior stakeholders. Demonstrated ability to navigate complex commercial risks and contractual negotiations.

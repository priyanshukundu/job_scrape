Key responsibilities Are you a sharp evaluator of risk who enjoys investigating complex business processes and products? Do you want to strengthen critical controls and help UBS manage current and emerging risks? Are you keen to use data and analytics to make audit work more effective? We are looking for an Audit Expert to: • lead or contribute to reviews and audits of specific business areas, providing practical, innovative and value-added solutions to identified issues • evaluate the design and operating effectiveness of market risk controls and recommend improvements where needed • assess whether controls are working effectively, identify current and emerging risks and escalate significant concerns appropriately • deliver high-quality audit work and documentation in line with internal standards and relevant divisional, functional, regional and legal entity coverage requirements • develop well-supported issues and audit reports in line with internal reporting and issue disposition standards • identify and implement data analytics opportunities to strengthen audit processes, risk assessment and risk monitoring across Group Risk Control, while building effective relationships with audit colleagues and stakeholders at all levels The team In our agile operating model, audit crews are aligned to business and functional areas, products and services, and consist of several autonomous pods. This role is based in New York and focuses primarily on audits of the Global Market Risk Control function within Group Risk Control. You’ll also periodically support audits across other divisions and functions, including our Investment Bank. Your skills and experience • bachelor’s degree, with a master’s degree considered a plus • ideally 3+ years of experience as an auditor, audit expert or risk management professional • experience in a quantitative area such as market risk, counterparty credit risk or intraday risk • previous experience in financial services, investment banking or a Big Four firm • strong analytical skills and a quantitative approach to problem-solving • ability to work independently, quickly understand complex processes, challenge the status quo and identify risks and process weaknesses • willingness to stay current with industry and regulatory developments, with strong written and verbal communication skills and the ability to communicate effectively with stakeholders • curiosity about how AI can improve the way we build, deliver and optimize workflows, with sound judgment in validating outputs and aligning their use with policies, risk standards and ethical principles *LI-UBS *UBS-MOGUL About us UBS is a leading and truly global wealth manager and the leading universal bank in Switzerland.

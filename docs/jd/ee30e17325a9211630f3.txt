Essential Responsibilities: The Senior Pega Software Engineer will demonstrate deep expertise in enterprise application engineering on Pega Platform (Infinity) in Pega Cloud, including case management, integration architecture, performance optimization, observability, production support, and rational database design; lead and mentor engineers, provide technical direction, and partner with business and technology stakeholders to deliver scalable, secure, and maintainable solutions that align with organizational goals; analyze business requirements, define technical designs, and implement complex applications using Pega best practices and guardrails; build and maintain case types, flows, data pages, data transforms, activities, decision logic, validations, data models grounded in sound relational database design, and user interface components (including Sections and Harness rules), as well as develop and support integrations with internal and external systems using REST, SOAP, HTTP, SQL, and event-driven patterns with Kafka; own end-to-end delivery responsibilities including coding, unit and integration testing, debugging, defect resolution, and post-production support; prepare and maintain technical design documents, code review artifacts, deployment checklists, implementation/validation plans, and backout plans for controlled code migrations across environments; and communicate technical concepts clearly to both technical and non-technical audiences, demonstrate strong ownership, and deliver production-ready, testable solutions in a regulated enterprise environment. Position may allow working from home within commuting distance of worksite location. Multiple Positions. Required Skills/Experience: Requires at least a Bachelor’s degree (or foreign equivalent) in any Engineering or Computer Science, plus 5 years of progressive, post-baccalaureate experience as a Software Engineer, Consultant, or related. Must possess 5 years of experience with each of the following: troubleshooting and operational analysis using Pega Predictive Diagnostic Cloud (PDC) and enterprise monitoring/logging platforms including Splunk; providing architectural guidance and oversight to delivery teams; reviewing peer code for quality, maintainability, and compliance with Pega guardrails; performing log analysis, alert triage, root cause analysis, and performance tuning; driving refactoring and technical debt reduction to improve reliability and long-term maintainability; resolving production incidents, documenting recurring issues, and creating backlog items for permanent fixes; collaborating with the Pega support team to diagnose platform issues and coordinate hotfix implementation where required; supporting integration reliability and data consistency across Pega and external systems; advising on project effort, design tradeoffs, and implementation risk; and utilizing the following tools and technologies: Pega Platform (Infinity) on Pega Cloud; Kafka; REST/SOAP/HTTP integrations; PostgreSQL and SQL; Pega data and case design (flows, activities, data pages, data transforms, decisioning); Pega Tracer, Clipboard, and Expression Builder; PDC; Splunk; Artifactory; and enterprise application servers/platform environments.

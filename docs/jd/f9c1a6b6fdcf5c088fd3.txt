Job Summary: The AVP, Client Executive, Private Equity serves as a strategic advisor and primary relationship leader for a portfolio of middle-market private equity clients, delivering comprehensive risk management and insurance solutions that support their evolving business objectives. This highly visible role is responsible for building and maintaining trusted relationships with private equity sponsors, portfolio company executives, and key stakeholders while ensuring a seamless, coordinated client experience across NFP’s full suite of capabilities. Working collaboratively with producers, industry specialists, and internal service teams, the Client Executive drives client retention, relationship expansion, and organic revenue growth through proactive engagement, strategic account planning, and the identification of new business opportunities. The position requires an entrepreneurial approach, strong commercial lines competence, and the ability to navigate complex client organizations, anticipate emerging needs, and deliver significant value throughout the private equity investment lifecycle, including acquisitions, portfolio management, and exits. This is a full-time position from our Charlotte, NC office. We offer the flexibility of a hybrid schedule. Essential Duties and Responsibilities: Responsible for leading the overall Private Equity relationship with assigned Mid-Market core clients, including developing and engaging multiple relationships throughout and within the client organization. Establish oneself as client’s primary point of contact and communication conduit for NFP; keep client producer informed at all times. Liaise with private equity industry practice and due diligence team to ensure deliverables during transaction times across all NFP business functions. Cultivate internal and external relationships to establish effective open collaboration, client advocacy, and trust. Develop and obtain a broad knowledge of internal operations to efficiently and effectively solve the needs of the audience and the circumstances at hand. Maintain accurate records of client (or prospect) contacts and their products applying account management software or other technology. Creating and maintaining a robust understanding and tracking system of key client personnel and their products is essential. Drive revenue growth on existing and new clients through development of a strategic consultative sales plan in conjunction with producer, including identifying and driving cross-sell opportunities when appropriate. Establish oneself as a self-starter leader with client account teams and take ownership of the success of client and colleague relationships. Contribute to client team’s comprehensive service strategy and establish quality standards to improve client’s experience. Demonstrate a broad understanding of insurance products and coverages, their applicability, relevant industry trends, and basic program design. Understanding basic insurance program structure and content is crucial. Develop expertise and remain current in assigned client industry and/or market area, NFP’s resources and capabilities, and engage in structured continuing education Produce an annual or semi-annual stewardship report that highlights NFP’s efforts for the client over the previous twelve months. Establish, complete and be accountable for implementation of the formal NFP strategic relationship management cadence. Seeks annual client feedback/grading on NFP’s performance. Travel to visit clients and engage in client development activities and entertainment. Knowledge, Skills, and/or Abilities: 10+ years' experience in relationship management with face-to-face private equity (or similar) client experience. Exceptional interpersonal skills capable of interacting with private equity and C-suite clients to cultivate strong relationships through effective communication and trust. Elite project management, communication, and presentation skills. Manages time effectively through excellent organization and time-management skills. Dedication to working in collaborative, team-oriented environment with a commitment to operating with respect and integrity. Proficient in Microsoft Office products (Outlook, Word, Excel, PowerPoint), Copilot or other AI tools. Property & Casualty producer license and Benefits License (or ability to get licensed quickly). Education and/or Experience: Property & Casualty Broker’s License required upon hire; CPCU or similar designation preferred. Bachelor’s or Master’s degree preferred; High School Diploma or equivalent required. 10+ years of industry experience with a strong background in private equity, commercial insurance- middle markets is ideal. Prior retail brokerage experience required. Advanced MS Office skills, including Excel, Word, PowerPoint.

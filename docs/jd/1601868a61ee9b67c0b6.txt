The Opportunity We are seeking a curious, detail-oriented Treasury Analyst to join our Capital Markets team. You will support Adobe's debt and capital structure activity, cash and interest forecasting, and corporate finance work, partnering with Treasury leadership, FP&A, Accounting, Tax and Legal, as well as banks and rating agencies. What you'll Do · Debt and Capital Structure: Support debt issuance and refinancing, the commercial paper program and the revolving credit facility, including pricing analysis, bank group materials, documentation tracking and closing checklists. · Swaps and Interest Rate Risk: Prepare interest rate swap analysis to support asset liability management strategy. Support competitive bidding and execution of interest rate swaps, and partner with Accounting on post-trade documentation, controls and reporting. · Forecasting: Help prepare the corporate cash flow forecast and the interest income and expense (OIOE) modeling, including weekly reviews, intra-quarter updates and annual plan scenarios. Prepare variance analysis versus the plan. Analyze short‑term and long‑term cash forecasts to support investment and capital structure planning. · Reporting and Presentations: Prepare clear materials for Treasury leadership, including Board, rating agency and investor-facing slides. · Accounting and Audit Support: Support quarter-end reviews, auditor requests (PBCs), and 10-Q and 10-K Treasury disclosures in partnership with Accounting. · Cross-Functional & Strategic Support: Partner with Tax, Legal, Accounting to support intercompany corporate finance (e.g. dividends). Support ad-hoc strategic analyses for Treasury leadership, including capital return programs and stress/scenario analysis. Aide in tracking and managing purchase orders tied to Treasury budgets, banking technology, and vendor services. · Process and Automation: Build and maintain models, checklists and templates; use data analytics, AI and automation to make recurring work faster and more reliable. What you need to succeed · Bachelor's degree in finance, economics, accounting or a related field. · 1-3 years of experience in Treasury, corporate finance, banking or a related field, or relevant internships. · Intellectual curiosity and a sincere interest in the capital markets. · Strong Excel and financial modeling skills, with high attention to detail. · Clear written and verbal communication; able to explain numbers simply. · Genuine team player who can manage multiple priorities in a fast-paced environment. · CFA progress is a plus. · D ata analytics, Python, AI tools and/or process automation experience are a plus.

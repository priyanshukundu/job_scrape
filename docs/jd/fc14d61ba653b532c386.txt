Shape SaaS Growth and AI Strategy as Lead Business Partner, Product Finance (AECO) Ready to operate at the intersection of high-growth SaaS and global infrastructure as the lead finance partner for a multi-product portfolio? Trimble is seeking a Lead Business Partner, Product Finance to anchor our AECO team, collaborating directly with product and engineering leaders to translate roadmap, headcount, and AI investment decisions into a defensible financial plan that drives sustainable, long-term growth. About Us: Trimble is an industrial technology company transforming the way the world works by delivering solutions that enable our customers to thrive. We create technologies that connect the digital and physical worlds, helping our customers increase productivity, quality, safety, and sustainability. From purpose-built products to enterprise-level solutions, our technology empowers professionals in construction, geospatial, government, transportation, and more. AECO: Trimble is empowering stakeholders through planning, design, construction and the entire asset lifecycle. The company’s innovative and connected ecosystem of solutions improves coordination and collaboration between teams, phases and processes. Automating work and transforming workflows, Trimble is enabling teams to deliver with confidence at every turn. For more information, visit: construction.trimble.com. What Makes This Role Great: As the lead senior individual contributor on the AECO Product Finance team, you will serve as the financial anchor for product General Managers, engineering leadership, and pricing partners. You will drive the financial strategy behind AECO's $1.64 billion ARR portfolio, setting the standard for Product P&L, Bookings, NPI, and ROI frameworks across the function. With high visibility to segment leadership, you will leverage modern AI tools and data-driven workflows to act as a force multiplier for how AECO allocates capital. Key Exciting Responsibilities: Direct capital allocation across the multi-product portfolio and measure return on R&D so leadership sees where every dollar and headcount investment delivers the greatest impact. Build New Product Introduction (NPI) financial models that integrate AI consumption costs alongside revenue, margin, and payback to ensure launch decisions scale profitably. Own contribution margin and R&D AI spend analysis while partnering with Hosted Operations to identify efficiencies and expand gross margin. Collaborate with GTM and Customer Finance to pressure-test reported ARR bridges, cost-to-serve economics, and retention metrics (GRR and NRR) for a defensible product view. Synthesize Product P&Ls and contribute to Quarterly Business Reviews to deliver a unified, consistent financial narrative across every portfolio. Essential Skills & Experience: 7+ years of progressive finance experience, including 3+ years in SaaS or recurring revenue, with direct business partnering to product or engineering leadership. Working command of contracted and reported ARR bridges, gross and net revenue retention, and SaaS bookings mechanics. Strong grasp of contribution margin, gross margin, cost to serve, and recurring revenue unit economics. Advanced Excel financial modeling skills, Domo dashboard authoring, experience with an EPM platform (such as Pigment, Anaplan, or Adaptive), and daily proficiency using modern AI tools for analysis and modeling. Proven judgment and communication skills to defend forecasts under pressure, translate margin trade-offs for non-finance partners, and guide decisions autonomously. Bonus Points For: Experience with hosted, cloud, or consumption-based pricing and cost models, including AI or compute usage costs. Independent querying and scripting skills using SQL, Python, or GitHub. Track record of leading NPI or new product launch financial frameworks. Background in construction technology, AEC software, or related vertical SaaS industries.

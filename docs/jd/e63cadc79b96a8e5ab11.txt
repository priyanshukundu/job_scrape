Research Portfolio Analyst
Guidehouse
Location: US - MD, Bethesda
Link: https://guidehouse.wd1.myworkdayjobs.com/External/job/US---MD-Bethesda/Research-Portfolio-Analyst_46995

Job Family : Research Analyst Travel Required : None Clearance Required : Ability to Obtain Public Trust What You Will Do: We are looking for an experienced Research Portfolio Analyst to support the National Institute on Minority Health and Health Disparities (NIMHD) by providing scientific, analytical, programmatic, and coordination support to Federal Program Officers overseeing diverse research portfolios. This is a full-time position located in Bethesda, MD. Compile, clean, organize, and analyze scientific and program data from Government-approved sources; check data quality, identify limitations, and prepare authorized extracts, tables, charts, graphs, dashboards, inventories, and factual summaries. Participate in scientific and program meetings; document discussions, Government decisions, action items, and follow-up needs in draft summaries and briefings. Plan and coordinate scientific meetings, workshops, conferences, symposia, lectures, and special projects, including agendas, participant materials, logistics, presentations, and follow-up. Support project management and cross-divisional coordination for large or complex scientific programs; maintain documentation, communications, schedules, trackers, and action logs; track activities, milestones, deadlines, dependencies, and deliverables; and provide consolidated status updates and reminders. Search PubMed, Web of Science, and other scientific sources; interpret, analyze, and summarize literature, reports, and background materials in literature reviews, environmental scans, annotated references, source summaries, and scientific memoranda. Develop scientific and program presentations, including slides, tables, graphs, charts, abstracts, posters, handouts, and briefing materials. Coordinate scheduling, information requests, meeting logistics, and follow-up communications with internal and external stakeholders as authorized by the Government. Prepare progress, monthly status, and technical reports; portfolio summaries; spreadsheets; correspondence; and other scientific and program documentation. Analyze scientific programs, research portfolios, and grantee information by grant mechanism or scientific topic to identify factual trends, gaps, recurring issues, data limitations, and operational matters for Federal staff consideration. Assist with management and administrative analyses of procedures, policies, processes, and systems to support operational efficiency. Maintain Government-approved program data, inventories, databases, logs, SharePoint repositories, and other information resources. Ensure scientific judgments, grants stewardship, policy interpretations, funding decisions, approvals, and inherently governmental functions remain with authorized Federal personnel. What You Will Need: A Master’s degree in a relevant scientific, health, behavioral, social science, public health, data science, interdisciplinary, or related field. Minimum of TWO (2) years of experience supporting NIH biomedical, behavioral, social, public health, data science, or other scientific research programs. Knowledge of NIH grants, funding mechanisms, and Federal extramural research programs. Experience analyzing scientific and Federal research portfolios, including grantee progress and scientific program reviews. Experience collecting, cleaning, organizing, analyzing, and presenting scientific or programmatic data, including dashboards, visualizations, and portfolio inventories. Strong technical and scientific writing and verbal communication skills, including preparing reports, summaries, executive-level briefings, agendas, and other professional documents and conveying scientific and technical information clearly using plain language when appropriate. Experience supporting and coordinating scientific meetings, workshops, conferences, lectures, and stakeholder events. Strong project management and organizational skills, including maintaining schedules, trackers, milestones, deadlines, and action items. Ability to work effectively with Federal staff and internal and external scientific stakeholders. Familiarity with minority health, health disparities, public health, biomedical, behavioral, or social science research portfolios. Experience conducting scientific literature research using PubMed and Web of Science and using bibliographic or reference-management software. Proficiency with Microsoft Office Suite, Adobe Acrobat, SharePoint, and collaborative productivity tools; advanced Excel skills for data analysis, organization, visualization, and reporting; and experience developing or maintaining SharePoint sites, research databases, program trackers, or data analytics tools. Must be able to obtain and maintain a Federal or DoD “public trust”; candidates must receive approved adjudication prior to onboarding with Guidehouse. Candidates with an active public trust or suitability are preferred. What Would Be Nice To Have: Prior experience supporting NIMHD or another NIH Institute or Center preferred. The annual salary range for this position is $74,000.00-$124,000.00. Compensation decisions depend on a wide range of factors, including but not limited to skill sets, experience and training, security clearances, licensure and certifications, and other business and organizational needs.

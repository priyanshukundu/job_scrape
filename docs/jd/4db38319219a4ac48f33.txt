The Team Upstart’s Machine Learning team applies machine learning and statistical methods to improve how we evaluate risk and make lending decisions. The team combines scientific research, experimentation, and engineering to develop models that support Upstart’s mission of expanding access to affordable credit. As an Applied Scientist at Upstart, you will develop and evaluate machine learning models that address meaningful business problems. You will collaborate with scientists, engineers, and cross-functional partners to translate complex data into modeling improvements and measurable outcomes. How you’ll make an impact Develop, train, and evaluate machine learning models to improve predictive performance and support lending decisions. Analyze large datasets to identify patterns, develop features, and inform modeling approaches. Design and execute experiments to assess model performance, validate hypotheses, and identify opportunities for improvement. Collaborate with scientists and engineers to implement, monitor, and refine models in production environments. Document methodologies, communicate findings, and contribute to reproducible scientific workflows. Minimum Qualifications Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Mathematics, Statistics, or a related field, or equivalent practical experience. 0–2 years of experience in machine learning, applied statistics, or a related quantitative field. Experience developing or evaluating machine learning models using Python. Experience applying statistical methods, including hypothesis testing, regression, or predictive modeling, to quantitative datasets. Preferred Qualifications Advanced degree, ideally a PhD, in mathematics, applied mathematics, statistics, physics, econometrics, operations research, computer science, or a related quantitative field. Knowledge of supervised learning techniques, model evaluation, and feature engineering. Familiarity with machine learning frameworks such as PyTorch, TensorFlow, or scikit-learn. Experience working with large-scale datasets using SQL or distributed data processing tools. Ability to communicate modeling approaches and experimental results to technical and nontechnical audiences.

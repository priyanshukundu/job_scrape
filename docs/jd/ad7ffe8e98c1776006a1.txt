Join us and transform the lives of patients while transforming your career. Associate Director – Data and Visualization What you will do Let’s do this. Let’s change the world. Amgen is seeking an experienced and accomplished Associate Director to join our drug discovery efforts in the Computational Chemistry team. The Associate Director, Data & Visualization will play a key role in determining the strategy and implementing solutions for data pipelines and analysis to empower decision making on small molecule pipeline projects. This role demands a strong blend of business acumen, technical expertise, and strategic thinking to align data capabilities with the company's mission and growth. The Associate Director will also function as a representative for data initiatives across Research, influencing technology investment and enterprise direction. This position is a senior scientific role with significant leadership responsibilities and opportunities. In this vital role, you will: Own the continued development of software packages for the creation and maintenance of custom small molecule data pipelines. Support data integration with platforms for analytics and visualization to empower decision making on drug discovery projects. Collaborate with teams across Research to implement and adopt solutions for the management of scientific data. Champion innovation and continuous evolution of Amgen’s data and analytics landscape through the adoption of new technologies and industry best practices. Partner closely with Enterprise Architecture, Data Governance, Data Engineering, Analytics, AI/ML, and IT Security teams to operationalize data solutions. Directly supervise and lead computational scientists and data engineers. Communicate data strategy and team deliverables to executive leadership and other non-technical stakeholders. What we expect of you We are all different, yet we all use our unique contributions to serve patients. The experienced professional we seek is a leader with these qualifications. Basic Qualifications: Doctorate degree and 3 years of experience in Data Science, Data Engineering, or related experience; OR Master’s degree and 5 years of experience in Data Science, Data Engineering, or related experience; OR Bachelor’s degree and 7 years of experience in Data Science, Data Engineering, or related experience; OR Associate’s degree and 12 years of experience in Data Science, Data Engineering, or related experience, OR High school diploma / GED and 14 years of experience in Data Science, Data Engineering, or related experience Preferred Qualifications: Academic or professional background in chemistry, biology, biochemistry, biophysics, computational biology, bioinformatics, or a related life-science discipline. Expertise in cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, or GCP), modern data technologies (data lakes, APIs, ETL/ELT frameworks), and programming languages (e.g., Python). Experience with standard cheminformatics tools and software packages/ platforms to analyze molecular structures and SAR data (e.g., rdkit, Schrodinger’s LiveDesign, etc). Experience building dashboards and visualizations for small molecule data (e.g., using Spotfire). Experience designing and evaluating agentic AI architectures, including tool use, orchestration, planning, memory, guardrails, human oversight, and production monitoring. Experience building and leading high-performing technical teams, developing professionals, and informing resource allocation. Familiarity with DevOps, CI/CD pipelines, and Infrastructure as Code (Terraform, CloudFormation). The preferred location for this position is at our Thousand Oaks (CA); but consideration will be given to remote candidates as well.

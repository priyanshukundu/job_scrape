Team & Role Overview About the Role: We are seeking a detail-oriented Portfolio & Trading Analyst to join the investment team and support portfolio management, trading, and day-to-day portfolio operations for Brad Markets, Franklin Templeton Fixed Income team in Boston. The analyst is responsible for ensuring investment decisions are accurately implemented across portfolios, keeping portfolio data clean and reliable, and resolving issues quickly when they arise. Working closely with portfolio managers, research analysts, and traders, the analyst will help book and allocate trades, manage investable cash and client flows, and monitor exposures and performance. The portfolios make extensive use of derivatives and rely on large datasets, so comfort with numbers, data, and tools is essential; however, this is not a quantitative research or financial modeling role. We place a premium on self-starters who thrive in an environment that may lack explicit direction. Proposing practical solutions to complex problems is a must; seeing those solutions through to completion, while working across a geographically dispersed organization, is what will truly set you apart.

About Akuna: Akuna Capital is an innovative trading firm with a strong focus on collaboration, cutting-edge technology, data driven solutions, and automation. We specialize in providing liquidity as an options market-maker – meaning we are committed to providing competitive quotes that we are willing to both buy and sell. To do this successfully, we design and implement our own low latency technologies, trading strategies, and mathematical models. Our Founding Partners first conceptualized Akuna in their hometown of Sydney. They opened the firm’s first office in 2011 in the heart of the derivatives industry and the options capital of the world – Chicago. Today, Akuna is proud to operate from additional offices in Sydney, Shanghai, London, and Singapore. What you’ll do as a Software Engineer – Python, Post Trade at Akuna: Akuna Capital is looking for an experienced hands-on engineer to join our growing development team and enhance our global Post Trade system. The Post Trade development team designs and operates the global Middle Office, Compliance, Risk, and Trade Enrichment systems. The team maintains the systems used to reconcile trades, satisfy regulations, manage risk, and monitor trading performance across the firm. The ideal candidate will work closely with our trading and quant research teams and build tools and automated data flows that drive trader insights and ensure the smooth running of our global trading operations. In this role, you will: Join one of our Post Trade teams as a Software Engineer (Compliance, Middle Office, Risk, or Trade Enrichment) Design and implement features for your team predominantly using Python and PostgreSQL Design applications in a distributed system to implement functionality and improve performance Implement trading and risk methodologies to monitor performance and manage risk Develop software components that perform intraday trade reconciliation, manage positions, and calculate P&L Write clean and maintainable code to build large distributed systems using microservices Collaborate with multiple teams to understand business problems, prioritize work, and devise technical solutions Qualities that make great candidates: 2+ years working with Python in a professional environment Experience working with relational databases and SQL Highly collaborative in nature, with excellent written and verbal communication skills Familiarity with the trading domain and finance is strongly preferred, but not required Ability to gather requirements and generate design documents that implement solutions Experience with web development using React/Redux is a plus Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Math or equivalent The ability to react quickly and accurately to rapidly changing market conditions, including the ability to quickly and accurately respond and/or solve math and coding problems are essential functions of the role In addition to technical skillsets, Akuna values the unique perspectives people can bring to the table to collaboratively solve complex problems and drive Akuna forward.

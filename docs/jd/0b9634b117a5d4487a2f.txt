Assistant Vice President, Capital Solutions
Antares Capital
Location: New York

The Assistant Vice President, Capital Solutions is a key position within Antares’ Capital Solutions team, responsible for managing the structuring and placement of Antares’ CLOs, hybrid financing solutions, CLO-related fund vehicles, insurance solutions and other asset-based financings. The role reports to a Managing Director on the Capital Solutions team. The role acts as a conduit between internal constituencies seeking optimal funding solutions and external financial intermediaries including CLO arrangers, CLO investors, lending banks and non-bank lenders to execute financing transactions from start to finish. Antares uses a number of financing technologies and while this role will be CLO-focused, the ideal candidate will also have experience in one or more of bank ABL lending, rated feeders and CLO tranche trading. Responsibilities Responsible for the negotiation and execution of CLO transactions with arrangers (Private Credit and BSL CLOs, warehouses and hybrid financings). Lead cash flow modeling, portfolio construction and arbitrage analysis to size CLO liability structures and optimize equity returns across BSL and Private Credit deals. Directly manage the CLO execution process – direct arrangers in CLO structuring, work with counsel to draft offering documents, review CLO document terms and run the closing processes. Monitor post-closing compliance and surveillance – including OC/IC and collateral quality tests – and coordinate trustee and investor reporting for CLOs and other asset-based financing transactions where applicable. On an ongoing basis, respond to investor/lender inquiries relating to Antares CLOs and financing facilities. Collaborate with internal partner functions as necessary to provide accurate reporting information. Assist internal marketing and product groups with marketing materials and transaction collateral as needed. Prepare analysis and presentations in support of ad hoc requests from team members. Manage rating agency relationships and processes end-to-end, including portfolio presentation, model runs and negotiation of structural terms and documentation. Oversee CLO warehouse facilities across their lifecycle – ramp-up, reinvestment and take-out – coordinating with lending banks and internal portfolio managers on eligibility criteria and portfolio construction. Qualifications Bachelor’s degree, preferably in a quantitative field 5 to 7 years of experience in CLO structuring at a sell-side institution. Direct experience with running rating agency models, bank structuring models and third-party structuring tools Working knowledge of CLO risk retention rules and applicable securities law and regulatory requirements. Strong proficiency in Intex and Excel is essential. Programming experience is a plus. Solid understanding of rating agency methodologies relating to CLOs. Direct experience managing rating agency relationships and processes for CLO transactions. Knowledge of insurance capital requirements would be a plus. Excellent verbal, written, and interpersonal communication skills. Experience and relationships with CLO tranche investors. Strong work ethic; comfortable working in a fast-paced environment with hard deadlines for projects.

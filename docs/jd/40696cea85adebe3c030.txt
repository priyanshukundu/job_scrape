NBCUniversal is seeking a Senior Software Engineer with strong expertise in SAP SuccessFactors Core, SAP HCM, ABAP, and SAP BTP Integration Suite (CPI). This is a key role in NBCUniversal's HR technology transformation and is responsible for technical solution design, technical specifications, development leadership, governance, and quality assurance across enterprise HR technology initiatives The successful candidate will serve as the technical lead for SuccessFactors and SAP integrations, partnering with Product Managers, Functional Consultants, Architects, and business stakeholders to translate business requirements into detailed technical designs. The role will lead ABAP and BTP development teams, review completed builds against approved specifications, validate technical unit test evidence, and coordinate Center of Excellence (COE) review and approval. Responsibilities Lead the technical design and delivery for SAP SuccessFactors Talent modules. Translate business requirements into solution architecture, technical designs, integrations, and RICEF specifications. Lead the design, development, and support of integrations using SAP BTP Integration Suite (CPI), APIs, and middleware technologies. Lead SAP BTP, ABAP, and integration development teams, including vendors and offshore resources. Facilitate architecture reviews, governance approvals, and design checkpoints. Coordinate testing, deployments, cutover, production support, and incident resolution. Mentor engineers and drive SAP SuccessFactors and SAP BTP best practices. Basic Requirements Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Information Systems, Engineering, or a related field with 5+ years of relevant experience; or a Master’s degree with 3+ years of relevant experience; or an equivalent combination of education and experience. Experience developing technical designs, integration specifications, and RICEF specifications. Strong technical and functional expertise in SAP SuccessFactors Talent Management, including Recruiting, Onboarding, Learning, Compensation, and Performance & Goals. Hands-on experience designing, developing, and supporting integrations using SAP BTP Integration Suite (CPI), including API, file-based, and middleware integrations leveraging OData, Compound Employee API, REST, SOAP, XML, JSON, and SFTP. Experience developing and extending cloud applications using SAP Cloud Application Programming Model (CAP). Experience conducting technical reviews and ensuring adherence to enterprise architecture, governance, and development standards. Strong analytical, problem-solving, communication, and stakeholder management skills. Desired Characteristics SAP Integration Suite (CPI) certification preferred. Certification in two or more SAP SuccessFactors Talent modules, including Recruiting, Onboarding, Learning, Compensation, or Performance & Goals. Experience with SAP Business Technology Platform (BTP), including Cloud Foundry, API Management, Cloud Connector, Event Mesh, Build, Work Zone, Cloud ALM, SAP Joule, and ABAP Cloud. Experience with SAP Analytics Cloud, SAP Datasphere, SAP Signavio, and SAP Fieldglass. 2+ years of experience integrating SAP SuccessFactors with SAP HCM, Employee Central Payroll (ECP), SAP S/4HANA, SmartRecruiters, Onboarding, and other third-party applications. Preferred to have experience with at least one SmartRecruiters implementation. Experience supporting large-scale HR transformation and SAP SuccessFactors implementation programs. Demonstrated ability to influence cross-functional teams and drive technical decisions in complex enterprise environments Media and entertainment industry experience is desired.

Job Description Summary The Senior Associate, Corporate Development leads financial evaluation and execution support for acquisitions, divestitures, reinsurance transactions, strategic investments, joint ventures, and partnership opportunities. This role develops complex financial and capital analyses, leads cross-functional due diligence workstreams, evaluates transaction risks, presents recommendations to senior leadership and governance forums, and helps manage opportunities through successful execution. Job Description Key Responsibilities Lead financial evaluation and execution support for acquisitions, divestitures, reinsurance transactions, strategic investments, joint ventures, and partnership opportunities. Develop and maintain complex financial models, valuation analyses, scenario assessments, transaction return analyses, and capital impact evaluations. Perform strategic market assessments, competitive analyses, and industry research to identify emerging opportunities, risks, and market trends. Lead due diligence workstreams and coordinate activities across actuarial, finance, legal, compliance, tax, treasury, investments, risk, technology, operations, and human resources. Evaluate financial, operational, regulatory, and strategic risks associated with potential transactions and business opportunities. Prepare and present analyses, recommendations, and transaction materials to senior leadership, investment committees, and other governance forums. Support development of business cases and strategic recommendations related to organic growth initiatives, partnerships, and capital deployment alternatives. Manage transaction timelines, key milestones, deliverables, and issue resolution activities to support successful execution. Partner with internal stakeholders and external advisors, including investment bankers, consultants, accountants, and legal counsel, throughout transaction and strategic evaluation processes. Provide guidance, knowledge sharing, and informal mentorship to less experienced team members supporting corporate development initiatives.

THE ROLE: Title: Applied AI – Vice President Location: New York, NY Division: Engineering – Applied AI As a Senior Applied AI Research Engineer, you will conduct original research at the frontier of deep learning, time-series modeling, and quantitative finance. You will formulate important research questions, develop novel predictive and representation-learning methods, and evaluate their statistical and economic relevance using large-scale financial datasets. You will own research to apply problems end to end: framing the question, curating and engineering the data, designing the model architecture, running large-scale training experiments, building the evaluation harness, and partnering closely with trading desks and engineers to ensure that research is grounded in real market problems and can inform impactful quantitative applications.

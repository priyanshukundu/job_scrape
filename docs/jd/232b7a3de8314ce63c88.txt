About the Role: As a Senior Robotic Software Engineer, Grinding, you will develop the algorithms and software that drive robotic grinding and surface finishing on our cooperative multi-robot platform. You will build the path planning, force control, and material removal logic that turns as-built WAAM and machined parts into finished components, and you will integrate off-the-shelf (COTS) tool changers, compliance devices, sensors, and scanners into the STARR platform and its robotic control system through their SDKs and APIs. This role will focus on adaptive, closed-loop grinding that removes the right material, avoids over-grinding, and runs reliably in a real factory environment. What you will do: Work with a dedicated team of inventive engineers building a cohesive, next-generation robotics platform representing the future of autonomous manufacturing Grind Path Planning: Develop toolpath generation and path planning algorithms for grinding, blending, and finishing of complex, variable as-built geometry Material Removal and Over-Grind Prevention: Implement material removal models, coverage and overlap management, and dwell control to hit dimensional targets without over-grinding Force-Adaptive Control: Integrate closed-loop force/torque control and real-time robot interfaces to adapt grinding behavior to part geometry and process feedback COTS SDK and Device Integration: Wrap and integrate vendor SDKs and APIs for tool changers, compliance devices, force/torque sensors, and 3D scanners into the STARR platform and robotic control system with clean, maintainable abstractions Consumable and Tool Management: Develop software for consumable wear detection, life tracking, and orchestration of automated tool and consumable changes Process Data and Traceability: Capture and expose grinding process data (force, spindle load, removal, consumable state) to enable traceability, debugging, and continuous improvement Productionization : Help transform prototype grinding software into production-ready software by improving testability, observability, and runtime robustness Integration and Testing: Collaborate with cross-functional teams including hardware engineers and system integrators to ensure seamless integration of grinding software with the centralized codebase. Conduct thorough testing and validation to guarantee system functionality and reliability Safety: Cultivate safety-critical software practices and support the development of systems that behave

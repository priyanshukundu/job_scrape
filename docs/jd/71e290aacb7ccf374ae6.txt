BUILDING THE FUTURE OF OPEN FINANCE


Payward - the parent company behind Kraken, NinjaTrader, Breakout,  xStocks, Payward Services and CF Benchmarks - has spent the last 15 years building one of the most modern and globally accessible financial infrastructure platforms in the industry, built to advance an open, global financial system.




THE TEAM

Payward Services is an enterprise infrastructure platform that enables companies to seamlessly integrate trading, payments, funding, and digital asset services through a single unified system. It provides coordinated liquidity, custody, compliance, risk management, and settlement capabilities, reducing operational complexity for fintechs, banks, brokerages, and payment providers.

We are hiring for a Capital Formation lead who will be responsible for  LP Relationship Management and Fundraising efforts  within our asset management business. You will originate and convert new Limited Partners relationships (LP) and grow existing LP relationships. You will own a large prospect book end to end, take opportunities through to serious evaluation on your own, and bring in senior partners to close when needed. You will ideally arrive with an existing book of relationships in at least one LP segment and start closing immediately.




THE OPPORTUNITY

 - Build and cultivate relationships with existing and new LPs. You are the primary point of contact for your LPs, and you drive re-ups, referrals, co-investment and cross-strategy engagement.

 - Own, pioritize and develop new LP relationships at an exceptional level, and run the book independently.

 - Guide investors through the full journey: first meetings and qualification, diligence, negotiation, investment committee approval and signed subscriptions. You convert prospects into serious evaluations yourself.

 - Partner with the deal team on transactions. You bring LPs into transactions and work with the deal team and senior partners to bring them to close.

 - Shape the fundraising plan for your segments, with clear goals, probability-weighted forecasts and conversion metrics.

 - Synthesize qualitative and quantitative inputs, including performance, research and market data, into clear, persuasive stories for sophisticated investors.

 - Position the firm across investor channels (consultant, institutional, family office and private wealth), and bring leadership market intelligence on allocation trends, investor sentiment and competitive fundraising dynamics.

 - Lead investor communications and experiences, LP meetings, quarterly and annual reporting, conferences and relationship-building events.

 - Oversee external materials, including pitch books, fact sheets, DDQs, data-room content and customized investor requests, ensuring accuracy and consistency.

 - Take on broader investor relations and capital formation work at the director level beyond your own book, as the business needs.

 - Work across the firm. Partner with senior partners and fund leadership, investment and portfolio teams, legal, compliance, investor operations, marketing and fund finance.

 - Close deals from day one. You turn your existing network into commitments quickly, not after a ramp period.




WHAT YOU BRING

 - Experience: 5 to 8 years in investor relations, fundraising, capital formation or institutional sales, ideally in alternative investments, at an asset manager, alternatives firm or fund platform.

 - Ability to talk fluently about venture capital, SPVs, private credit as asset classes and how deals are structured, economics etc. 

 - Education: Bachelor's degree in finance, economics, business or a related field, or equivalent experience. An MBA or CFA is a plus.

 - An established, proven book of LP relationships in at least one segment (for example pensions, endowments, family offices, funds of funds or wealth platforms), and a track record of closing significant capital commitments you can speak to in detail.

 - Experience taking investors through diligence, negotiation and subscription, with working knowledge of fund structures, fee terms and side letters.

 - Experience managing LP relationships after the close, including reporting, communication and re-ups.

 - Ability to run independently as an individual contributor, with the operational discipline to manage a large pipeline largely on your own.

 - Strong analytical skills and the ability to turn complex, incomplete data into clear written and verbal narratives.

 - Executive presence and credibility with CIOs, consultants, senior partners and firm leadership.

 - Judgment on when to escalate and how to use senior partners' and the deal team's time well, with a collaborative, team-first approach.

 


NICE TO HAVES

 - Experience with digital assets, crypto-native funds or both traditional and digital allocators.

 - Relationships across more than one LP segment.

 - Experience organizing LP events and community initiatives.

 - Experience with CRM and investor-portal tooling (for example Salesforce or Dynamo).

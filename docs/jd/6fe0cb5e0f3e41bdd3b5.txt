Title and Summary Senior Software Engineer | Data & AI Platform Overview: Data Foundations, part of Business & Market Insights, solves shared data problems for Mastercard's analytics products: secure customer data exchange, consistent business definitions and metrics, reference datasets, data quality, and AI-assisted customer data onboarding. Engineering and product set direction together, and we own outcomes, not just delivery. Where enterprise platforms fit, we reuse and shape them. Where there is a shared gap, we solve it once. We are looking for a Senior Software Engineer to be one of the first engineers in a new US-based team. You will start by making business definitions, metrics, and dataset relationships reusable across products, delivering key components independently and helping set the team's engineering habits. Early on, you will prototype answers to questions such as how shared metrics are versioned and where data access is enforced, then ship the first shared component with the product team that will use it. About the Role: • Design, build, test, and release shared services, libraries, and pipelines that enable and support other engineering teams. • Partner with product managers to understand business problems, challenge assumptions, and simplify solutions whenever possible. • Create clear and concise design proposals that outline trade-offs, recommendations, and implementation approaches. • Evaluate enterprise and vendor technologies through proofs of concept to inform build-versus-buy decisions. • Develop reusable, versioned interfaces with comprehensive documentation and automated deployment and testing capabilities. • Build secure, scalable, and observable components while supporting incident response, troubleshooting, and root cause analysis. • Participate in code and design reviews, mentor engineers, and contribute to the team's technology strategy and roadmap. All About You: • Strong expertise in software design, testing, secure coding practices, CI/CD, and operating production services. • Advanced SQL skills and hands-on development experience with Python or other modern programming languages such as Java, C#/.NET, Go, or TypeScript. • Experience working with modern cloud and data platforms such as Databricks, Snowflake, AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud. • Proven ability to design APIs, reusable libraries, and platform capabilities that are consumed by other engineering teams. • Strong understanding of data modeling, including entity relationships, aggregation challenges, data contracts, and version control of shared metrics. • Product-minded approach to engineering with strong technical communication and documentation skills. • Ability to understand end-user needs and build solutions that deliver measurable business value. Preferred Qualifications: • Experience working with semantic layers, metric stores, dbt, data catalogs, or business glossary platforms. • Experience building tools, frameworks, or supporting context for AI agents and large language model (LLM) applications. • Knowledge of data governance, access controls, row-level security, and handling sensitive or regulated data. • Experience using AI-powered engineering tools to improve productivity, with sound judgment regarding accuracy, risk, and appropriate use cases. • Demonstrated curiosity, continuous learning mindset, and passion for driving innovation through technology.

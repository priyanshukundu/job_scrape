Position Overview: Are you looking for a career that enables you to bring your technology and operations expertise to influence and develop tools / solutions that will improve an organizations Identity Governance capability? Freddie Mac is seeking a creative hands-on Senior Software Engineer to be a member of the Identity and Access Management (IAM) team within the Enterprise Business Technology Operation (BTO) organization. You will be working to build innovative solutions for improving our operational efficiency through automation, increasing user satisfaction with improved user experiences and helping to modernize our technology stack by leveraging SailPoint/AWS Cloud native technologies. Our Impact: The Enterprise BTO organization enables business operational excellence through the flexible use of technology. The department provides IT strategic services (software development, enhancements, and production support) to support the needs of the Enterprise groups - External Relations and Corporate Communications, Finance, Human Resources, Internal Audit, Legal, Operational Resilience, and Regulatory Affairs. This role will be focused within IAM and often have intersections with the other above listed business lines. Delivery of IAM solutions to enable self-service capabilities Delivery of integrations between IAM systems and other systems Collaboration with other technology teams to advance our technical capabilities, automate and optimize processes to meet business challenges. Your Impact: Be an active member of multiple high-performing teams. Provide technical leadership, contributions and identify trends, patterns, and insights that can advise business decisions and strategies. Support engineering domain to craft, develop, and implement solutions that meet business needs. Ensure solutions align with business objectives and deliver measurable outcomes. Stay up to date with industry/enterprise standards, latest technologies and general principles, and techniques. This is an individual contribution role. Qualifications: Bachelor’s degree or equivalent experience; advanced studies or an advanced degree preferred. At least 5–7+ years of relevant full-stack software development experience, with proficiency in at least one programming language, such as Node.js, Python, or Java, and front-end technologies such as ReactJS and TypeScript . Experience in the IAM field , including technologies such as SailPoint, Okta, CyberArk, and Auth0, is preferred. Hands-on experience implementing RESTful APIs on AWS infrastructure, as well as working with CI/CD pipelines, GitHub Actions/GitLab CI, and Infrastructure as Code (IaC). Preferred to have knowledge of core Identity and Access Management (IAM) and Identity Governance and Administration (IGA) concepts, including Joiner-Mover-Leaver (JML) lifecycle management, access certifications and reviews, Segregation of Duties (SoD) policies, and integrations with various platforms and cloud environments. Experience with cloud platforms such as AWS and containerized environments using technologies such as Docker and Kubernetes. Ability to design, architect, and implement scalable, reliable, and maintainable applications. Ability to collaborate with cross-functional teams, including QA, DevOps, Security, Governance, Operations, and Product, to deliver high-quality software. Ability to troubleshoot and resolve complex performance and scalability issues and perform root cause analysis in fast-paced environments. Keys to Success in this Role: Ability to work with multiple technologies and pioneer new solutions. Strong verbal and written communication skills. Communicate effectively within a matrixed organization. Motivated to learn new technologies and identify process improvements and efficiencies.

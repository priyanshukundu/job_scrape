About the team Our security team supports the unwritten fourth tenet of Slack’s mission: make people’s working lives more secure. We are passionate about protecting our infrastructure, operations, and most importantly, our customers’ data. We take a thoughtful approach to security and strive to provide a low friction, high-impact security model across everything we do. Slack Security Operations (SecOps) is a small, engineering-led team covering the US and ANZ. We run detection and response for Slack's production infrastructure, with on-call around the clock. Unlike a traditional CSIRT, the team is made up of software engineers who specialize in security . We also build and run core infrastructure and identity services deeply embedded in how Slack works: including network encryption, identity tools, secrets management, logging pipelines and alerting. We work in a lean, high-ownership, startup-style way, with heavy cross-functional collaboration. Slack's incident response is open and culture-driven: engineers across the company join in to solve problems together, and SecOps is at the center of it for anything security-related. We're also leaning hard into AI agents for continuous monitoring, triage and response. About the role We are looking for a staff level blue team software engineer to help drive our threat detection and response efforts and to collaborate on our development efforts. An interest in development, operations, curiosity, and a deep desire to learn are key to a successful member of the team. As a Staff Security Engineer, you'll be a senior technical leader for the team. You'll play an active role in setting direction across detection and response, security infrastructure, and the secure adoption of AI agents. You'll still be hands-on: writing code (Go/Python), leading incidents, and owning hard problems from start to finish. Your impact will be measured by how much safer, more resilient and more scalable Slack becomes. This posting is to grow our US-based, widely distributed SecOps contingent, which works closely with our additional Melbourne & Auckland team. We’re eager to communicate and collaborate often (over Slack, of course!). You’ll join the rest of the team in our on-call rotation, and we also strongly look for you to grow your engineering participation at Slack more broadly! Slack has a positive, diverse, and supportive culture—we look for people who are curious, inventive, and work to be a little better every single day. In our work together we aim to be smart, humble, hardworking, and above all, collaborative. If this sounds like a good fit for you, why not say “hello?” What You Will Actually Be Doing: Design, build, and operate security tools and infrastructure protecting Slack’s production environments at scale. Develop reliable, auditable, and automated monitoring and threat-hunting capabilities for continuous detection.

The Opportunity Our location in Lake Bluff, Illinois has an exciting opportunity for a Senior Finance Manager supporting Business & Technology Services (BTS) and the Office of the CIO . This strategic finance leadership role serves as a key business partner to technology leaders, providing financial planning, forecasting, investment analysis, and decision support while leading a high-performing team and driving financial excellence across a complex global organization. What You'll Work On Serve as a strategic finance partner to leaders across the Corporate BTS organization, including Infrastructure, Data, AI, and Automation teams, influencing business decisions and driving financial performance. Lead forecasting, budgeting, long-range planning, and financial analysis activities to support organizational priorities and investment strategies. Oversee monthly close activities, financial reporting, and variance analysis in partnership with shared services teams, ensuring accurate and timely financial results. Develop executive-level financial presentations and recommendations for the Chief Information Officer (CIO) and senior leadership team. Proactively identify financial risks and opportunities and develop action plans to optimize business outcomes. Partner with business leaders to drive cost reduction, productivity, and cost optimization initiatives. Lead financial review and analysis of technology investments, capital expenditures, and major projects, including Requests for Capital Expenditure (RCEs). Provide financial support for vendor and contract negotiations through business case development and scenario modeling. Own balance sheet, cash flow, and capital forecasting processes, continuously improving forecasting accuracy and visibility. Drive process simplification, automation, and continuous improvement initiatives across finance operations. Lead, coach, and develop a team of finance professionals, fostering a culture of collaboration, accountability, and continuous learning. Required Qualifications Bachelor's degree in Finance or Accounting Minimum of 8 years of progressive experience in financial planning & analysis (FP&A), finance, and/or accounting. Previous people leadership, supervisory, or management experience. Strong financial modeling, budgeting, forecasting, and analytical skills. Advanced proficiency in Microsoft Excel and experience working with enterprise financial systems. Experience using SAP, Hyperion Essbase, or similar financial planning and reporting tools. Excellent communication and presentation skills with the ability to interact effectively with senior and executive leadership. Demonstrated ability to synthesize complex financial and technical information into clear business recommendations. Proven ability to manage multiple priorities and deliver results in a fast-paced, deadline-driven environment. Preferred Qualifications CPA, CMA, and/or MBA. Experience supporting Information Technology, Digital Transformation, Business Technology Services, or related corporate functions. Experience partnering directly with executive leaders, including CIO-level stakeholders. Demonstrated success leading cost optimization, productivity, or transformation initiatives. Experience evaluating large-scale technology investments and capital projects. Track record of driving process improvement, financial automation, and operational efficiencies. Experience developing and mentoring finance talent in a complex global organization.

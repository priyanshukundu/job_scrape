Job Description Application Deadline: November 20, 2026 What is the opportunity? In this hybrid role, you will provide business and financial advice to a team-managed portfolio of high value small business clients who need your help as they start, manage and grow their enterprises. You contribute to sound operational management and resilience by exercising due diligence, maintaining accurate client information, adhering to applicable risk and regulatory requirements - proactively identifying and escalating risks in accordance with established processes. You partner and support the Relationship Manager in the completion of effective client discovery, annual credit reviews and Client Relationship Plans, strengthening portfolio quality. You leverage the full ecosystem, strategically bringing the right partner at the right time to deliver integrated solutions that meet clients’ financial and business needs. You have a posture of continuous learning, developing your knowledge and capability to enable you to provide solid solutions to our clients. Your attention to detail and curious nature will ensure outstanding client experience for every client. What will you do? Responsible for retention, growth and oversight of a team managed portfolio of small business clients through disciplined execution of practice management routines. Proactively contact and engage business clients in the branch or business center by providing everyday financial solutions during the account opening process, for credit-worthy clients Collaborate with Relationship Manager to monitor portfolio quality, including excesses, delinquencies, expired credits and out-of-orders, ensuring adherence to established risk management benchmarks. Ask questions to uncover additional financial needs that you can help clients with, now or in the future, either by fulfilling or referring them to an RBC partner. Educate and sign up clients for our digital/self-serve options like our ATMs and online banking application and proactively advise on Merchant and Cash Management solutions. Manage risks and exercise due diligence by adhering to compliance routines, processes, and controls to protect client and shareholder interests As a member of the business banking team, from time to time you may be required to assist clients that are generally served by the Commercial Banking Associate or Relationship Manager. What do you need to succeed? Must-have Diploma or University degree in Business, Finance, Banking, or equivalent qualifications (consideration will be given to individuals pursuing a degree or long tenure with RBC) Demonstrated passion for putting clients first, as well as success in a client-facing, target-driven environment Minimum 2 years banking experience, preferably business banking or in branch with personal lending Caring, curious, energetic with a desire to help clients and to make a difference within their communities Drive and self-motivation, as well as excellent communication skills, time management and emotional intelligence Digital literacy across a broad range of devices (i.e., smartphones, tablets, laptops, etc.) Analytical assessment capabilities Proficient computer skills using standard software – Microsoft office suite Nice-to-have Successful completion of Moody’s Analytics Small Business Lending Credit skills and knowledge, including an understanding of risk assessments, financial analyses, credit structuring/solutions Track record in building rapport and maintaining client relationships within the financial, service, hospitality or retail industry What’s in it for you? We thrive on the challenge to be our best, progressive thinking to keep growing, and working together to deliver trusted advice to help our clients thrive and communities prosper. We care about each other, reaching our potential, making a difference to our communities, and achieving success that is mutual.

Senior Associate, Sales Reporting and Analytics - Chase Media Solutions
JPMorgan Chase
Location: New York, NY, United States

Join a rapidly scaling business that delivers measurable outcomes for clients and meaningful experiences for customers across digital and owned channels. You will help Chase Media Solutions evaluate opportunities to improve revenue growth and commercial performance. As a Senior Associate, Sales Reporting and Analytics in Chase Media Solutions, you develop reporting, investigate performance trends, support campaign budget management, and communicate insights across revenue, pipeline, campaign performance, and sales productivity. You partner with Sales, Finance, Campaign Operations, Product, and Marketing to clarify metric definitions, address data-quality issues, and make reporting more useful to business stakeholders. Job responsibilities Build, maintain, and improve dashboards and recurring reports covering revenue, pipeline, campaign performance, and sales productivity. Analyze trends, conversion rates, and forecast variances to identify risks, opportunities, and potential drivers. Deliver ad hoc analyses that answer business questions and inform sales planning and prioritization, including market opportunity, segmentation, and sales productivity analyses as business needs evolve. Monitor campaign performance and delivery on a weekly basis. Support post-campaign analysis and maintain historical performance benchmarks to inform future campaign planning. Present findings through clear visualizations, concise written takeaways, and actionable recommendations. Validate reporting outputs, investigate discrepancies, and work with data owners to resolve quality issues. Document metric definitions, reporting logic, data sources, and refresh processes. Partner with Sales, Finance, Product, and Marketing to align reporting with business questions and agreed-upon definitions. Improve reporting efficiency through repeatable processes and appropriate automation. Gather user feedback and refine dashboards to improve usability, accuracy, and adoption. Required qualifications, capabilities, and skills 3+ years of experience in reporting, business analytics, sales analytics, or a comparable function, including experience using Tableau and Microsoft Excel to develop reports, dashboards, or business analyses. Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, including the ability to investigate unexpected results. Ability to explain data findings clearly to technical and non-technical stakeholders. Experience working with large datasets, defining metrics, and validating data accuracy. Strong attention to detail, documentation habits, and ability to manage competing priorities. Collaborative approach and ability to connect analysis to business decisions. Preferred qualifications, capabilities, and skills Working knowledge of SQL or a similar query language. Experience with Salesforce data and reporting. Familiarity with revenue, pipeline, forecasting, or campaign-performance metrics. Experience improving dashboard performance or automating recurring reporting. Exposure to market sizing, segmentation, sales performance or go-to-market analysis.

The ideal candidate combines strong software engineering expertise with financial crimes regulatory knowledge and experience leveraging automation, machine learning, and AI to improve detection effectiveness and operational efficiency. Key Responsibilities Design, develop, and maintain scalable financial crimes applications, monitoring integrations, and investigative tools. Build and enhance a centralized, end-to-end case management platform supporting alert management, investigations, escalations, documentation, and reporting. Integrate internal data and third-party monitoring solutions to create unified investigative workflows. Develop automated processes, data pipelines, and AI/ML-enabled capabilities to improve alert prioritization, risk scoring, and investigative efficiency. Design configurable solutions supporting multiple jurisdictions, regulatory requirements, data privacy, and access controls. Translate regulatory and operational requirements into technical solutions in partnership with Global Financial Crimes, Product, Engineering, and Data teams. Own ongoing platform maintenance, production monitoring, troubleshooting, system enhancements, and operational reliability. Implement appropriate audit trails, data integrity controls, regulatory recordkeeping, and reporting capabilities.

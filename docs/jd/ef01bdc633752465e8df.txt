ViiV Healthcare is a specialist pharmaceutical company 100% dedicated to developing medicines to treat and prevent HIV. Born out of a partnership between GSK and Pfizer in 2009, with Shionogi joining in 2012, our ambition is to end the HIV epidemic. As the pioneers in HIV innovation, we constantly look to push the boundaries of science to develop medicines that can change lives and give people more choice. Beyond our medicines, we are proud of our deep connection with the HIV community and work with partners to advance research and development, address HIV-related stigma, increase access to our medicines and provide funding and support to local community organisations to support their efforts in the HIV response. We are ViiV Healthcare. Here until HIV and AIDS are not. As an Associate Director, Field Reimbursement for the Alabama and Florida Panhandle region. In this role, you will lead efforts to overcome patient access challenges by working closely with healthcare providers, internal teams, and external partners. You will provide education and support to help healthcare offices navigate reimbursement and coverage complexities so patients receive the care they need. We seek a collaborative, adaptable, and motivated individual ready to grow their career while making a real difference. This role offers a meaningful opportunity to contribute to GSK’s mission of uniting science, technology, and talent to get ahead of disease together. Responsibilities This role will provide YOU the opportunity to lead key activities to progress YOUR career. These responsibilities include some of the following: - Lead education initiatives that empower healthcare providers and office staff to independently manage reimbursement and access challenges. - Identify barriers to patient access and develop clear, actionable plans to resolve them. - Collaborate closely with cross-functional internal teams such as sales and reimbursement support to align access strategies. - Serve as a subject matter expert on payer coverage, reimbursement policies, and benefit programs including Medicare and commercial insurance. - Support resolution of complex reimbursement issues and escalate as needed to ensure patients can access therapies without delay. - Track, analyze, and communicate access metrics to inform continuous improvement efforts. Why You? Basic Qualification We are seeking professionals with the following required skills and qualifications to help us achieve our goals: - Bachelor’s degree & 5 plus years of experience in pharmaceutical or healthcare reimbursement, patient support programs, payer marketing, or reimbursement hub services. - Experience in reimbursement billing codes, including J codes and Q codes. - Experience navigating payer policies across Medicare (Parts A, B, C, D), Medicaid, and commercial insurance programs. - Experience educating healthcare providers and internal partners - Ability to travel up to 75% within the field territory. Preferred Qualification If you have the following characteristics, it would be a plus: - Advanced degree such as MBA, MPH, or related field. - Experience with specialty injected or infused products, especially in buy-and-bill settings. - Familiarity with specialty pharmacy and reimbursement hub services. - Proven ability to simplify complex reimbursement systems and communicate clearly across diverse audiences. - Strong relationship management and problem-solving skills. - Demonstrated leadership or coaching experience with a focus on process improvement and team collaboration. #LI-ViiV #LI-GSK #LI-Remote Skills Data to Insights, Digital Fluency, Pricing Management, Pricing Policy, Pricing Strategies, Scientific Literacy, Stakeholder Management Please visit GSK US Benefits Summary to learn more about the comprehensive benefits program ViiV offers US employees.

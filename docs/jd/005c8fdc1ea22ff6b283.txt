Estate Settlement Associate Our Estate Administration team, consisting of nine professionals based in New York City, is hiring a motivated Estate Settlement Associate to join the team. The Associate will partner with Estate Settlement Officers in the administration of complex, high-net-worth and ultra-high-net-worth estates and terminating revocable trusts. The role provides meaningful exposure to fiduciary administration, estate taxation, trust and estate planning concepts, client relationship management, and the administration of unique and illiquid assets. The Associate will interact directly with internal and external clients, co-fiduciaries, beneficiaries, attorneys, accountants, tax professionals, valuation specialists, and other advisors while supporting all phases of the estate settlement process. Key Responsibilities: Works with Estate Settlement Officers, Relationship Managers, Portfolio Managers, Trust Officers, and Tax professionals on large and complex estate settlements, including administration of accounts. Prepares estate administration summaries and PowerPoint presentations for client meetings; prepares cash flow analyses; communicates with co-fiduciaries, beneficiaries, and outside advisors, including attorneys, accountants, and tax preparers. Assists in the review of fiduciary accountings, estate tax returns, gift tax returns, fiduciary income tax returns, and related filings. New Account Openings: Analyzes governing documents and account information to support the KYC (Know Your Client) process in accordance with internal account opening procedures. Collaborates with Estate Settlement Officers to review fiduciary appointments, dispositive provisions, beneficiary structures, tax situs, GST status, fee arrangements, and applicable state law requirements. Coordinates preparation of account opening documents and onboarding materials. Provides support to Estate Settlement Officers in processing distributions, wire transfers, bill payments, and other actions related to the estate administration process. Assumes responsibility for collecting assets from outside institutions and maintaining related records. Assists with administration of unique and illiquid assets, including real estate, closely held business interests, private investments, collectibles, and other specialized assets through ultimate sale or distribution to beneficiaries. Coordinates with attorneys, accountants, valuation professionals, and real estate professionals regarding valuation, insurance, maintenance, transfer, and disposition of estate assets. Assists Estate Settlement Officers with the development, research, and drafting of client publications, educational materials, thought leadership pieces, and external trust and estate publications. Actively participates in meetings with clients, including beneficiaries and co-fiduciaries, as well as prospective clients. Develops and maintains an understanding of trust and estate documents, probate administration, fiduciary accounting, and estate, gift, fiduciary income, and generation-skipping transfer tax concepts to support the tax-efficient administration of estates and trusts. Administrative duties may include: Organizing files and maintaining electronic records utilizing internal document management systems. Preparing reports generated from internal accounting, trust, and portfolio systems. Completing recorded callbacks with co-fiduciaries, beneficiaries, and other interested parties. Preparing correspondence for internal and external distribution to clients, beneficiaries, and professional advisors. Assisting with preparation of client meeting materials, presentations, and internal reports. Ideal Qualifications: Bachelor’s Degree 2+ years of experience in the financial services industry or legal industry in a client-facing environment preferred – ideally in a trusts and estates department of a law firm or a trust company.

Director I, Advanced Analytics
Liberty Mutual
Location: Boston, Massachusetts, United States
Link: https://searchjobs.libertymutualgroup.com/careers/job/618521008062

Description The Director of Analytics leads the LMRe Management Information (MI), Analytics and AI capability, ensuring LMRe leaders have trusted, timely and actionable insight for strategic and operational decision-making, and evolving the function from a reporting service into an AI-enabled insight partner. Acting as the primary interface between the Data & Analytics team and the business, the role owns the MI portfolio, leads the delivery of analytics and AI-enabled solutions and drives responsible AI adoption across LMRe. The role is both hands-on and oversight: delivering analysis directly where it adds most value while directing and quality-assuring the work of the team. Responsibilities Leadership & Strategy • Lead and develop the MI, Analytics and AI team, including offshore team members • Define and deliver the analytics and AI strategy, aligned to the Data Office roadmap • Set priorities, delivery plans and performance objectives, and build a culture focused on business value and continuous improvement Business Partnering & Insight • Act as the senior analytics partner to LMRe leadership across Underwriting, Claims, Operations and Strategy • Translate business goals into measurable insight requirements, ensuring analytics influences decisions rather than simply producing reports • Challenge assumptions and provide evidence-based recommendations Management Information & Analytics • Own the LMRe MI portfolio, ensuring Power BI reporting is trusted, governed, standardised and fit for purpose • Oversee report lifecycle management, rationalisation and quality controls • Promote self-service through governed datasets and semantic models • Lead the delivery of advanced analysis supporting underwriting, portfolio management, claims and operational decisions • Work with the Data & Analytics Enablement, Data Engineering and Actuarial teams to maximise value from enterprise data assets and resolve data quality issues • Champion data storytelling and clear communication of insight AI Adoption & Governance • Own the rolling 12–18-month AI adoption strategy for LMRe, partnering with Global Risk Solutions (GRS) leadership, Technology and the AI Champions network • Embed AI into reporting, analysis and high-value workflows such as underwriting research, claims summarisation and regulatory monitoring • Drive AI literacy and adoption across LMRe through the Super User network, targeted support for lower-adopting functions and a shared prompt library • Own responsible-AI guardrails, controls and compliance in partnership with Legal and Risk, and represent LMRe in enterprise responsible-AI forums • Adherence to all relevant regulatory requirements forms part of your role, and the demonstration of behaviours as set out in the Conduct RisK Policy, as they relate to your role, is core to LSM's commitment to our Liberty Values, placing customers at the centre of our business and behaving with integrity. LSM expects you to understand your responsibilities relating to all regulatory and internal requirements, and to proactively demonstrate compliance with these requirements and behaviours at all times. These requirements include the Senior Managers and Certification Regime Conduct Rules ,Solvency II, fair treatment of customers, Financial Crime, Data Protection, Conflicts of Interest, and Whistleblowing. Preferred Skills Relevant education and professional experience • Proven leadership of analytics, BI or MI teams • Strong stakeholder management and executive engagement skills • Hands-on proficiency in SQL, Python and Power BI on modern cloud data platforms, with the ability to oversee the work of others • Demonstrable experience influencing business decisions through data and analytics • Experience adopting AI, GenAI or analytics automation in a business setting Reinsurance, insurance or financial services experience • Snowflake experience • Experience leading AI adoption or change programmes, and of leading offshore or distributed teams Qualifications Master's degree in Mathematics, Economics, Statistics or other quantitative field; Ph.D. helpful given quantitative work they are leading. Minimum 8, typically 10, years' experience; must have exceptional planning, analytical, decision-making and project management skills. Expert knowledge of data sources, tools and business drivers. Knowledge of statistical techniques required. Industry knowledge critical.

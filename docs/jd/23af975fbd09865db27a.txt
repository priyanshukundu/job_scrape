The application window is expected to close on: 11/29/2026 Meet the Team The Cisco 8000 Layer 3 Forwarding team develops the IOS XR software that powers packet forwarding on Cisco Silicon One, Cisco’s family of high-performance networking ASICs. As part of Cisco’s Distributed Systems Engineering (DSE) organization, we build scalable, high-performance routing platforms for service providers and hyperscale networks, supporting the growing demands of cloud infrastructure and AI/ML workloads. Our work connects routing decisions to hardware forwarding behavior—from programming routes and next hops to optimizing forwarding scale, performance, and reliability. We collaborate closely with routing control plane teams to translate routing information into hardware forwarding entries, and with Cisco’s Silicon One ASIC and SDK engineering teams to program the network processors using their software development kit (SDK). These partnerships put our team at the intersection of routing software and networking hardware. You will contribute across the full development lifecycle, from design and implementation to validation and production troubleshooting. Join a team where your work directly shapes how some of the world’s largest networks move traffic.

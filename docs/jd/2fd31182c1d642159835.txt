Are you a seasoned Life & Health actuary who thrives at the intersection of technical depth, strategic thinking and real-world impact? If so, this could be the role you've been waiting for. About the Role This is a high-impact senior role within Swiss Re's L&H Portfolio Performance & Steering (PP&S) Americas team sitting within the L&H Chief Underwriting Office. You will provide independent oversight of L&H portfolios across the Americas, assessing performance, risk and strategic fit across in-force and new business. You will translate portfolio insights into recommendations that shape business strategy and portfolio steering. Key Responsibilities Provide independent technical challenges and advice across In-force and New Business activities, including material or complex transactions, management action proposals and selected New Business quotes, developing clear recommendations to support CUO and senior governance decisions Design and embed effective governance frameworks, clarifying decision rights, escalation pathways, standards and controls to enable consistent, risk-informed business decisions Develop integrated product-line views across performance, growth, profitability, concentration, risk and strategic fit to inform portfolio steering and future business decisions Review reserve assumptions, providing an independent portfolio perspective to support Chief Underwriting Office review and decision-making Lead and shape Group-wide and regional strategic initiatives, working across functions and geographies to deliver key L&H PPP priorities Partner with Market Units to continuously improve the effectiveness of Smart Circles feedback loops, ensuring that emerging risks are appropriately identified, understood and addressed About the Team The Portfolio Performance & Steering (PP&S) Americas team sits within the Pricing & Portfolio Performance (PPP) function, at the heart of Swiss Re's L&H Chief Underwriting Office. This is a team that takes pride in connecting the dots — bridging deep technical rigor with strategic business impact across markets and functions. As part of the broader global L&H Portfolio, Pricing & Performance (PPP) community, the team is a trusted partner in enabling sustainable business performance. From pricing governance and underwriting standards to portfolio steering and business oversight, the team drives consistent practices and informed decision-making that supports the long-term success of Swiss Re's Life & Health business. It's a collaborative, intellectually curious group that values diverse perspectives, constructive challenges and a shared commitment to making the world more resilient. About You You are a credible, commercially minded L&H actuary who moves confidently from technical detail to portfolio-wide implications and from insight to action. You create structure in ambiguous situations, build trust with senior stakeholders and see complex work through to a timely, high-quality conclusion. You connect transaction-level considerations to broader portfolio strategy, performance and business outcomes. We are looking for candidates who meet these requirements: Fellow of the Society of Actuaries (FSA) with 10+ years of actuarial experience in Life & Health insurance or reinsurance Broad L&H re/insurance experience spanning both in-force and new business activities Proven ability to lead complex, cross-functional initiatives and drive outcomes without direct authority Strong grounding in actuarial assumption setting and experience analysis, with the ability to critically assess emerging experience, challenge underlying assumptions and identify implications for portfolio performance and future business decisions Strong understanding of L&H portfolio economics and financial performance, with demonstrated ability to identify underlying value drivers, explain deviations from expectations and connect financial outcomes to portfolio and business decisions These are additional nice to haves: Significant global L&H re/insurance experience across multiple regions, markets and product lines Proven ability to influence executive leadership and senior governance bodies through clear, compelling narratives grounded in complex, multi-source evidence. Comfort working across time zones within a global matrix organization IFRS 17 expertise Our company operates under a hybrid work model, with the expectation that employees will work from the office three days per week every week .

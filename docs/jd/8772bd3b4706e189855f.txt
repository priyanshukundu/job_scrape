Visa will accept applications for this role until at least 12-04-2026 Job Description As the Head of Acceptance Risk & Security Solutions Management in North America, you will lead the development, commercialization, and growth of Visa’s acceptance-side risk, fraud prevention, cybersecurity, threat intelligence, authentication, and identity solutions. Your mandate is to help merchants, acquirers, payment facilitators (PayFacs), and other acceptance ecosystem partners address their most pressing risk and security challenges through differentiated, commercially compelling solutions leveraging Visa’s data, network, technology, and partner capabilities. You will define the North America acceptance risk and security solutions strategy, identifying the distinct needs of different client segments, developing tailored value propositions, and driving solution adoption and revenue growth. A critical part of the role is connecting capabilities across Visa’s risk and security portfolio to address complex client challenges, improve fraud and cyber risk outcomes, optimize authentication performance, and enhance client profitability. Success in this role requires broad and substantive expertise across multiple risk and security disciplines, including payment fraud, cybersecurity, threat intelligence, digital identity, and authentication. You must understand how these disciplines intersect, how their applications differ across client segments, and how to translate technical capabilities into practical, scalable, and commercially differentiated solutions. You will work closely with North America sales and market teams to identify growth opportunities, shape complex client engagements, develop segment-specific solutions, and accelerate commercialization. You will also partner with global product and technology teams to communicate market requirements, influence solution development priorities, and identify opportunities to expand Visa’s acceptance risk and security portfolio through internal capabilities, partnerships, and acquisitions. This is a market-facing, commercially accountable solutions leadership role, requiring the ability to combine deep domain expertise, strategic client engagement, solution development, and commercial execution. Success will be measured by revenue growth, client adoption, penetration across priority segments, and Visa’s market leadership in acceptance risk and security solutions.

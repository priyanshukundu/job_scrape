Purpose The Corporate Banking Associate supports senior team members in the origination, development, execution, and ongoing management of relationships with existing and prospective clients across the Automotive, Transportation & Logistics sectors. Clients include automotive manufacturers, suppliers, dealerships and other companies across the broader automotive ecosystem, as well as trucking and freight carriers, railroads, third-party logistics providers, transportation equipment manufacturers, and equipment leasing and fleet management companies. The Associate will be support the full lifecycle of client coverage and transaction execution, including credit analysis, financial modeling, preparation of credit presentations, marketing and pitch materials, due diligence, documentation, portfolio monitoring, and analysis of relevant company and industry trends. The role requires strong analytical capabilities, attention to detail, commercial judgement, and the ability to manage multiple workstreams in a fast-paced, transaction-oriented environment. What You’ll Do • Champion a client-centric culture by combining financial expertise and sector knowledge to deliver value-added solutions to clients. • Evaluate and analyze the financial needs and credit profiles of corporate clients, including preparing detailed financial models, forecasts, and downside scenarios. • Assess lending and other product opportunities, prepare marketing materials and presentations, and support client pitches and transaction execution. • Perform financial and written analysis of companies and industries, including historical and projected financial analysis, valuation and comparable company analysis, pro forma capitalization and liquidity analysis, and assessment of creditworthiness. • Monitor Automotive industry trends and developments, including vehicle demand, production volumes, supply chains, electrification, capital investment, tariffs, regulatory developments and competitive dynamics affecting clients across the automotive ecosystem. • Monitor Transportation and Logistics industry trends and developments, including freight volumes and rates, fuel costs, fleet utilization and replacement cycles, equipment leasing, trade flows and tariffs, and other factors affecting carriers, logistics providers and equipment manufacturers. • Prepare clear, concise, and well supported credit presentations and recommendations for review by senior team members and Global Risk Management and liaise directly with Global Risk Management throughout the credit approval process. • Assist in the ongoing management of the portfolio, including coordinating account management activities and handling client requests for waivers, amendments, and restructurings of credit facilities. • Support the negotiation of loan terms and documentation with borrowers, legal counsel, and other financial institutions through transaction closing. • Develop an understanding of each client’s strategy, capital structure, and priorities to help identify opportunities to deepen the relationship and expand the Bank’s share of wallet. • Work closely with the Bank’s specialized product groups, including Global Transaction Banking, Debt and Equity Capital Markets, Investment Banking, International Banking, and Treasury Services and other relevant parties to develop solutions tailored to our clients’ needs. • Understand how the Bank’s risk appetite and risk culture should be considered in day-to-day activities and decisions, and escalating issues appropriately. • Ensure the adequacy, adherence to and effectiveness of day-to-day business controls to meet obligations relating to operational risk, regulatory compliance risk, AML/ATF risk and conduct risk, including responsibilities under the Operational Risk Management Framework, Regulatory Compliance Risk Management Framework, AML/ATF Global Handbook and Guidelines for Business Conduct. • Provide guidance and mentorship to more junior bankers, by reviewing their work, sharing industry and credit knowledge, and supporting their development. • Champion a high-performance environment and contribute to an inclusive work environment, through collaboration, accountability, and active support of team priorities. What You’ll Bring • Bachelor’s degree in Business, Finance, Economics, Accounting or a related field. • At least two years of experience in Corporate Banking, Investment Banking, Commercial Lending, Leveraged Finance, Credit or a related financial services environment. • Strong financial modeling, accounting and credit analysis skills, including the ability to analyze cash flow, liquidity, leverage, capital structure, and debt capacity. • Experience covering the automotive, transportation or logistics sectors, or related industrial sectors, is strongly preferred but not required. • Knowledge of automotive manufacturers, suppliers and the broader automotive value chain, as well as transportation and logistics business models (e.g., trucking, rail, and equipment leasing), is an asset. • Experience analyzing corporate financial statements, capital structures, liquidity and creditworthiness, with the ability to identify key risks and develop appropriate mitigants. • Proficiency in Microsoft Excel, PowerPoint, Word and working knowledge of SharePoint, as well as financial platforms such as FactSet, Capital IQ and Bloomberg. • Strong written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to communicate complex financial and credit matters clearly and concisely to internal stakeholders and clients. • Strong attention to detail, organizational skills to manage multiple priorities, and commitment to producing accurate, high-quality work within established timelines. • Self-motivated, proactive, and driven team-player attitude with the ability to thrive in an entrepreneurial role, taking initiative with drive and motivation in a lean, matrixed team.

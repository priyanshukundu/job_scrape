Regular or Temporary: Regular Language Fluency: English (Required) Work Shift: 1st shift (United States of America) Please review the following job description: Independently designs, develops, tests, and maintains complex software solutions that meet business and user needs. Emphasizes delivering scalable, reliable, and high-quality applications by applying advanced best practices in coding, architecture, and software lifecycle management, while collaborating with cross-functional teams to drive technical initiatives and mentor junior engineers. ESSENTIAL DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES Following is a summary of the essential functions for this job. Other duties may be performed, both major and minor, which are not mentioned below. Specific activities may change from time to time. 1. Independently designs, develops, tests, and maintains complex software components and services addressing business and user requirements. 2. Implements advanced features and enhancements using established coding standards, architectural patterns, and development best practices. 3. Leads code reviews and provides constructive feedback to ensure code quality, maintainability, and performance. 4. Collaborates with cross-functional technical teams to plan and execute technical initiatives, ensuring timely delivery of solutions. 5. Troubleshoots, debugs, and resolves complex software defects and production issues, applying root-cause analysis and corrective actions. 6. Participates actively in the full software development lifecycle, including requirements analysis, design discussions, development, testing, deployment, and support. 7. Applies secure coding practices, testing strategies, and documentation standards to ensure software quality and compliance with organizational guidelines. 8. Mentors and supports junior engineers to foster technical growth and adherence to best practices. 9. Utilizes and contributes to existing frameworks, tools, and technologies to enhance application development across relevant technology domains.

The Opportunity The Senior Risk Analyst serves as a key Second Line of Defense (SLOD) resource within the Property & Casualty (P&C) Risk portfolio, with primary responsibility critical risk reporting and analytics. This individual contributor independently owns and enhances critical reporting products, including recurring risk reporting, Information Stewardship activities, data controls, documentation, and reporting automation. This analyst can source, validate, analyze, and translate data for SLOD oversight, while maintaining and modernizing critical risk reporting products. Working closely with risk, compliance, audit, and business partners, the Senior Risk Analyst independently sources, reconciles, validates, and analyzes enterprise data to translate complex information into clear risk insights and actionable recommendations. This role will investigate trends, anomalies, concentrations, and data-quality concerns while improving the scalability and reliability of reporting through automation and repeatable processes. The ideal candidate brings strong risk management experience, hands-on data fluency, intellectual curiosity, and technical aptitude, with the ability to challenge results that do not make sense and clearly communicate the risk implications to both technical and executive audiences. We offer a flexible work environment that requires an individual to be in the office 4 days per week. This position can be based in one of the following locations: San Antonio, TX, Plano, TX, Phoenix, AZ, or, Charlotte, NC Relocation assistance is not available for this position. What you'll do: Partners with key stakeholders in the business to identify, assess, aggregate and document risks and controls, including risks associated with new or modified products, services, distribution channels, regulations and third-party operations using advanced knowledge. Communicates results of risk assessments to governance committees, business process owners and various levels of leadership. Enhances and contributes to strategies, tools, and methodologies to measure, monitor, and report risks. Applies advanced knowledge to utilize or produce analytical material for discussions with cross functional teams to understand business objectives and influence solution strategies. Serves as a key contributor in cross-functional teams to identify, assess, aggregate and mitigate current and emerging risk events. Own the critical reporting portfolio, including Information Stewardship, recurring reporting, Tableau automation, and associated controls and documentation. Increase SLOD's data independence, particularly for Compliance Monitoring & Testing and other Risk Programs use cases. Improve reliability and scalability by reducing manual manipulation and strengthening repeatable data sourcing, reconciliation, and reporting processes. Produce analysis, identifying trends, anomalies, concentrations, drivers, and emerging questions. What you have: Bachelor's degree in Risk Management, Business, Finance, or related field; OR 4 years of relevant education and/or experience. Experiences that will support your success: 6 years of risk management, regulatory or operations experience in a relevant functional area to include banking, insurance, financial services, audit, project management, public accounting or other related operational area. Or advanced degree/designation in Risk Management, Business, Finance, or other discipline relevant to risk management and 4 years of work experience in a discipline relevant to risk management. Risk management experience in a complex institution and/or highly matrixed environment. Demonstrated experience in conflict resolution management and ability to effectively challenge at all levels of management and influence business outcomes. Ability to effectively work with both internal and external partners in a highly collaborative environment. Demonstrated critical thinking and knowledge of data analysis tools and techniques and decision-making abilities, to include demonstrated ability to effectively make data-driven decisions. Proactively identifies potential concerns and follows-up to resolve issues. Advanced knowledge of Microsoft Office products including Excel and PowerPoint. What sets you apart: Experience applying analytics in a risk, compliance, audit, P&C insurance, or similarly complex environment. 3+ years of experience in risk management, compliance, audit, Issue Management, or a related risk oversight function, preferably within financial services or P&C insurance. Experience sourcing, manipulating, reconciling, and validating large or complex datasets, including identifying data-quality issues, understanding data lineage, and challenging questionable results. Hands-on experience with analytical and reporting tools such as SQL, Tableau, Power BI, advanced Excel/Power Query, Python/R, Alteryx, Snowflake, or comparable technologies, with SQL strongly preferred. Demonstrated experience developing, maintaining, or automating risk reporting and analytics, including identifying trends, anomalies, concentrations, key drivers, and emerging risk themes. Strong communication and analytical storytelling skills, with demonstrated ability to translate complex data from analysis to insight, articulate the associated risk implications, and present actionable conclusions to senior or executive-level stakeholders.

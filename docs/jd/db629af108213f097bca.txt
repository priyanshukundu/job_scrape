The Opportunity As a dedicated Business Strategy and Planning Director , you’ll provide strategic guidance to executive leadership in identifying, developing, and communicating business strategies across functional areas. Leveraging deep knowledge of USAA, industry trends, business development, and financial planning, you’ll lead analyses of business needs and market conditions to support business and enterprise strategy and planning. You’ll also conduct strategic reviews to evaluate progress, identify opportunities, and drive awareness and achievement of key strategic objectives. We offer a flexible work environment that requires an individual to be in the office 4 days per week. This position can be based in one of the following locations: San Antonio, TX or Charlotte, NC. Relocation assistance is not available for this position. What you'll do: Oversight of Transformation Portfolio Deliver strategic automation and AI pilots (e.g. Digital Sourcing, DD AI, Digital Negotiation, etc.) Drives the development and execution of functional strategy, including near- and long-term roadmaps and complex cross-functional initiatives, partnering with senior leaders to translate strategy into execution. Partners across BPOs, STAR, GSP, and Change Management to integrate change strategies, project plans, and documentation that support successful implementation and adoption of complex initiatives. Evaluates operational effectiveness, identifies gaps, and establishes best practices and management routines across GSP to drive continuous improvement. Facilitates routines across the GSP organization driving visibility (e.g. GSP Alignment, Transformation Steering Committee, project/program routines, etc.) Drives the alignment of business process and frameworks to enterprise strategy, process and framework. Leads the design, governance, and execution of strategic and operational planning, partnering with executive leadership and cross-functional teams to establish frameworks, goals, and key results. Guides team members as they perform research and analysis. Ensures risks associated with business activities are effectively identified, measured, monitored, and controlled in accordance with risk and compliance policies and procedures. What you have: Bachelor's degree; OR 4 years of relevant education and/or experience. 8 years of directly related business planning consultation experience to include developing business strategy for short-term and/or long-term goals. Experience influencing and managing communication to senior executives, internal colleagues through concise, official and regular updates on status, progress, strategies and roadmaps. Extensive experience in Project Management. Strong business acumen in financial services or related industry, and a proven ability to balance strategic thinking with practical implementation skills. Experience working with clients/customers to realize business issues and develop a strategy for the business's direction from the gathered insights. What sets you apart: US military experience gained through military service or gained as a military spouse / domestic partner.

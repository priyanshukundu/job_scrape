Back to search results Position Summary M&A Human Capital Consultant Our Deloitte Strategy & Transactions team helps guide clients through their most critical moments and transformational initiatives. We offer comprehensive end-to-end support and advisory services, covering areas such as valuation modeling, cost optimization, restructuring, business design and transformation, infrastructure and real estate, mergers and acquisitions (M&A), and sustainability. The team works closely with clients at every stage of their journey, helping them navigate challenges, avoid financial pitfalls, and implement practical solutions before, during, and after major transformational projects or transactions creating lasting impact. Shape the people agenda behind transformative deals—helping clients navigate the talent, culture, and organizational changes that make M&A work. As a consultant, you’ll turn complex challenges into practical solutions and grow alongside a collaborative team at the center of high-impact transactions. Recruiting for this role ends on 11/24/2026 Work You’ll Do As an M&A Human Capital Consultant on the Integration & Divestiture team, you will: Conduct Human Capital due diligence across compensation, benefits, human resources (HR) compliance, labor relations, HR communications, operations and technology, talent, and culture; identify transaction risks and opportunities. Support organizational change management programs, including impact assessments, stakeholder and employee engagement, leadership enablement, and transaction communications. Assess legacy cultures, define future-state culture objectives, and develop plans to support cultural alignment. Support integration of human resources functions, including HR systems, service delivery, total rewards, payroll, leadership development, and talent strategy. Support organizational design, workforce transition, and workforce optimization initiatives for combined, separated, or transformed organizations. A successful candidate would possess these skills: Ability to work independently and collaborate as part of a team Effective written and verbal communication skills Meticulous attention to detail and quality of work product Ability to build and sustain professional relationships Ability to lead projects or workstreams Ability to manage and prioritize multiple tasks in a fast-paced and dynamic environment Strong interpersonal skills and professional demeanor Ability to meet deadlines Ability to provide clear guidance to others The Team M&A advises corporate and PE clients across inorganic growth/divestiture scenarios through M&A strategy, diligence, integration/separation planning and execution, financial and modeling services, and transaction financing. Within Deloitte’s M&A offering, the Integration & Divestiture - Human Capital team helps clients address the people-related dimensions of transactions across the deal lifecycle. The team supports organizations through diligence, pre-close planning, Day 1 readiness, integration, and separation by focusing on areas such as organization design, workforce transition, employee communications, change management, and human resources operating model decisions. Working alongside cross-functional deal teams, the practice helps clients manage workforce risks, support business continuity, and execute human capital priorities that enable transaction value. Qualifications Required: Experience supporting and/or delivering at least 1 M&A Human Capital project(s), including restructuring, enterprise cost reduction, due diligence, assessment of transaction risks, target screening, integration planning and execution, and/or post-merger integration. 1+ years of experience in the one of the following areas: HR Strategy, HR Employee Experience, HR Operating Models, HR Mergers & Acquisition (M&A), HR Outsourcing Advisory, and HR Technology demonstrating knowledge of HR functions & processes. 1+ years of experience supporting Human Resources, consulting, or corporate communications projects. 1+ years of experience supporting workshops, large meetings, or presentations for senior leaders. Bachelor’s degree and 2+ years of consulting or industry experience in change management, communications development, organizational design and development, HR strategy or operations, or talent solutions; or a Master of Business Administration (MBA) and 1+ year of work experience in these areas. Ability to travel up to 50%, on average, based on the work you do and the clients and industries/sectors you serve. Limited immigration sponsorship may be available. Preferred: MBA or graduate degree in HR or Organizational Behavior 2+ years of experience with Human Capital M&A, restructuring, or enterprise cost reduction, including due diligence, transaction-risk assessment, target screening, integration planning and execution, or post-merger integration. Experience executing project plans with defined milestones and deadlines and collaborating with cross-functional stakeholders. Experience developing presentations in Microsoft PowerPoint, analyzing data using Microsoft Excel, Tableau, or Structured Query Language (SQL), preparing written reports, and formulating recommendations based on analysis.

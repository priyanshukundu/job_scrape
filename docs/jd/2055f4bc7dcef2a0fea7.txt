Job title: Data Scientist Target Identification Location: Waltham, MA. About the job As Data Scientist – Bioinformatics within our team, Computational Target Identification, you’ll turn complex biological questions into the computational tools that shape the future of vaccine discovery. Ready to get started? In Vaccines, you'll help advance prevention on a global scale - and shape the future of immunization. Join the engine of Sanofi's mission — where deep immunoscience meets bold, AI-powered research. Our team, Computational Target Identification, is part of the broader global Data and Computational Science (DCS) organization. Our team's primary objective is to leverage cutting-edge computational approaches to uncover and prioritize vaccine targets, directly contributing to the advancement of Sanofi's drug discovery pipeline. In this role, you'll act as the critical link between experimental biologists and computational teams, applying your expertise in bioinformatics and data science applied to microbiology to guide critical scientific decisions. You'll independently build the analyses, databases, and dashboards that keep vaccine projects moving forward with confidence. Join the engine of Sanofi's mission — where deep immunoscience meets bold, AI-powered research. In R&D, you'll drive breakthroughs that could turn the impossible into possible for millions. About Sanofi We’re an R&D-driven, AI-powered biopharma company committed to improving people’s lives and delivering compelling growth. Our deep understanding of the immune system – and innovative pipeline – enables us to invent medicines and vaccines that treat and protect millions of people around the world. Together, we chase the miracles of science to improve people’s lives. Main responsibilities Investigate bioinformatics and data science approaches to assess and prioritize vaccine targets and antigen selection that meet the evolving analytical needs of vaccine projects. Design and apply bioinformatics and data science methods — including bacterial comparative genomics, phylogeny, functional prediction, omics data analysis — to establish robust evidence of association and causality between infections and diseases. Develop and maintain bioinformatics tools, pipelines, and databases that support antigen target identification and selection. Interpret and communicate scientific findings clearly to both computational and experimental colleagues, contributing to publications, reports, and internal presentations. Coordinate cross-functional project activities with Vaccines R&D data and digital teams as well as broader Sanofi stakeholders, defining action plans and ensuring steady project progression. About you Basic Qualifications Education & Experience Bachelor's degree in Bioinformatics, Computational Biology or a related field with 3+ years of bioinformatics research experience in academic and/or industry settings. OR Master's degree in Bioinformatics, Computational Biology or a related field with 2+ years of bioinformatics research experience in academic and/or industry setting. Preferred Qualifications Hands-on experience in omics and large-scale biological data analysis. Comparative genomics (pangenome), phylogeny, functional prediction, NGS data analysis (genomics, transcriptomics), data visualization, data engineering.

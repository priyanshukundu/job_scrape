Professional Readiness & Experience Program The Professional Readiness & Experience Program (PREP) is a two-year rotational program designed to develop the next generation of business and functional leaders. The program attracts, develops, and retains exceptional early-career talent by providing: Diverse business experiences Opportunities to build leadership skills and business acumen Exposure to leaders and executives across HPE A strong network of peers and professionals Structured mentoring, feedback, and professional development Opportunities to expand skills, perspectives, and career possibilities Participants complete four six-month rotations. Three rotations are within Office of the COO teams, and one rotation is within another HPE organization. This structure provides broad business exposure while allowing participants to develop specialized capabilities and identify areas of interest for future career growth. The program is designed for energetic, highly motivated professionals who demonstrate leadership potential, strategic thinking, adaptability, and a commitment to continuous learning. Role Overview As a Strategy & Operations Associate in the Office of the COO PREP Program, you will contribute to strategic initiatives, business insights, organizational transformation, and cross-functional programs. You will work with teams across HPE to support business priorities, analyze information, manage projects, develop insights, and help drive positive change. Through rotational assignments, you will gain practical experience and develop the business, analytical, communication, and leadership skills needed for a successful career at HPE.

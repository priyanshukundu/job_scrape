Job Posting Title: Product Design (Accessories) Intern, Spring 2027 Req ID: 10160436 Job Description: About the Role & Program Disney Consumer Products (DCP) brings the magic of Disney’s beloved stories, franchises, and characters to families and fans around the world. From toys to apparel, books to games, our global organization has a profound impact on The Walt Disney Company and the global markets in which we operate! With a portfolio of renowned franchises including Disney, Pixar, Marvel, 20th Century Studios, National Geographic, and Star Wars, we are a worldwide industry and category leader delivering innovative products that entertain and inspire. As an Product Design Intern, you will support the Accessories team with project development, assist with managing a small single category as needed, and help support the Online Product Approvals (OPA) system. You will have the ability to work with various accessories categories including: bags, backpacks, footwear, headwear, hosiery, jewelry, timing, and vision teams to ensure the highest quality, consumer-focused, trend-right product lines across licensing. If selected, you will report to the Senior Manager, Product Design. This internship is a full-time, in -person, six-month commitment in Glendale, California, from January 11th 2027 through June 4th 2027. What You Will Do: Develop and design product mock-ups, gather images and layout creative presentations, and trend point of view boards Collaborate with Sr. Managers and Design managers to design, approve, and review products and packaging while collaborating on feedback Create templates for design point of view boards for creative rollouts Pull cross-category product trends for Accessories Assist in pulling art direction and assets for collaborations Monitor competitors, product innovation, and market dynamics.

Sr. Regulatory Data Scientist (Multiple Openings)
CME Group
Location: Chicago - 20 S. Wacker
Link: https://cmegroup.wd1.myworkdayjobs.com/cme_careers/job/Chicago---20-S-Wacker/Sr-Regulatory-Data-Scientist--Multiple-Openings-_34976

Analyze order, trade, and position data from Market Regulation’s data warehouses and develop working solutions to augment the department’s analytical capabilities. Support vendor applications and proprietary tools used by department staff to ensure data is complete and accurate. Develop regulatory data analysis reports and analytical solutions based on staff requests. Work closely with business users to document business requirements and data analysis specifications. Perform functional testing of regulatory applications to ensure the implementation of defectfree applications and algorithms which adhere to the defined requirements. Create and implement new data visualizations using third party visualization tools. Develop creative analytical methods and processes to mine the regulatory data warehouses and enhance order with trading pattern identification algorithms. Telecommuting permitted on a hybrid schedule as determined by the employer. MINIMUM REQUIREMENTS: Master’s degree in Mathematics, Informational Technology, Engineering (any), or a related field and 36 months of experience as a Data Scientist or related occupation. Must have three (3) years of experience with/in: Data science, data analytics, or a related field; Agile, CRISP-DM, and/or traditional software development methodologies such as Waterfall; Programming in relation to SQL and relational database management systems; At least one of the following: Python, R, VBA, Perl, SAS, or Mathematica; Microsoft Office, with expertise in Microsoft Excel, specifically to manipulate large datasets; Data visualization tools including either Tableau or D3; and Machine learning and statistical analysis SALARY: $118,664 - $190,300 per Year.

Job Description The Senior Manager, Data Engineering, leads teams in designing, building, and overseeing the deployment and operation of technology architecture, solutions, and software to capture, manage, store, and utilize structured and unstructured data from internal and external sources. This role is responsible for establishing processes, automations, and big data systems based on business and technical requirements, ensuring data quality, troubleshooting user issues in large distributed systems, and creating machine learning pipelines. The Senior Manager acts as a leader for engineering teams, removing blockers, enabling productivity, and fostering an inclusive, high-performance culture. The role also involves coaching and developing engineers to adapt to evolving tools, frameworks, and AI-driven workflows, and partnering closely with product managers, QA, risk, compliance, and security functions to deliver safe, transparent, and customer-centric solutions. All roles require digital fluency, including the ability to work with emerging technologies such as Generative AI tools (e.g. ChatGPT, Microsoft Copilot) to support everyday work. Key Responsibilities: Provide hands-on technical leadership and mentoring to a team of 8-10 engineers, delivering robust, scalable solutions on time Serve as technical lead for some of the products in the organization — driving planning, architecture, hands-on development, and technical direction Own customer engagement for assigned products: manage customer expectations, drive adoption, and act as the primary technical point of contact Lead the design and implementation of secure, reliable, and performant systems that meet the platform's functional needs Build and promote an agile, high-quality engineering culture across design, development, and production deployment Partner with geographically distributed teams to resolve technical challenges and improve processes Drive knowledge sharing with Operations teams during and after deployment Champion innovation by evaluating and adopting technologies and practices — including GenAI — that improve productivity and quality Visa requires at least 3 days in office, expectations of these days will be confirmed by your Hiring Manager.

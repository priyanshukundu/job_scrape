Validate, maintain, and document software solutions to ensure reliability and accuracy. Solve complex mathematical and geometric challenges related to semiconductor verification. Required Qualifications Master’s degree in Computer Science or Computer Engineering & 10+ years work experience Strong proficiency in C and C++ programming languages. Solid problem-solving skills and the ability to tackle technical challenges. Effective written and verbal communication skills for collaboration across teams. Desired Skills Exceptional math background, including the ability to address complex mathematical and geometric problems. Expert in Physical Verification domain or Computational Geometry Experience in developing software for engineering or scientific applications. Ability to quickly learn new technologies and adapt to project requirements.

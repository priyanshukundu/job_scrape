Your Impact In this role, you will bridge design engineering with world-class supply chain operations to accelerate time-to-market, ensure product compliance, and drive supply chain excellence through our PLM platform. You will drive innovation by exploring AI-driven capabilities, establish standards for Teamcenter configuration, and ensure scalable, maintainable solutions aligned with long-term PLM strategy. Your leadership will guide functional teams, manage cross-track dependencies, and optimize workflows using emerging technologies such as AI. Key Responsibilities Architect scalable BMIDE data models, dynamic LOVs, naming rules, revision/versioning rules, and Deep Copy Rules (DCR), adhering strictly to out-of-the-box design principles. Explore and implement emerging capabilities such as AI-driven automated part classification, geometric deduplication, and digital thread traceability from requirements to manufacturing execution. Partner with business leadership to challenge, rationalize, and finalize business requirements, ensuring alignment with strategic goals. Establish principles for Teamcenter standardization, fit-to-standard approaches, configuration versus customization, and solution reuse. Evaluate sophisticated requirements to determine appropriate Teamcenter solutions, considering cross-module and cross-system impacts. Review and approve IT solutions, ensuring alignment across multiple project tracks and providing direction to functional leads and development teams on complex decisions. Identify cross-track dependencies, functional gaps, and risks, driving timely resolution to maintain project momentum.

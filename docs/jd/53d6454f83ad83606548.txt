Software Engineering Director We are hiring a Software Engineering Director to lead, grow, and inspire multiple full stack engineering teams that design, build, and ship production-grade systems end-to-end, from data modeling and backend APIs through user interfaces and cloud deployment. You will own the technical vision, delivery outcomes, and engineering culture for your organization, and partner closely with Product, Design, ML/Data Science, Security, Risk, and business leaders to turn strategy into reliable, observable, and scalable platforms. You will also guide the responsible adoption of AI-powered agentic systems across the product development lifecycle (PDLC). You stay close enough to the craft across backend, frontend, cloud infrastructure, quality automation, data modeling, and data engineering to set the bar for technical excellence, while leading through people, partnerships, and results. Key Responsibilities Leadership & Organizational Ownership Lead, mentor, and develop engineering managers, senior engineers, and teams; build a high-performing, inclusive culture grounded in accountability, psychological safety, and continuous learning. Own end-to-end delivery outcomes for your portfolio: roadmap, quality, reliability, security, cost, and customer impact, from idea through production and ongoing operation. Hire, onboard, retain, and grow talent; define career paths, set clear expectations, conduct performance reviews, and plan succession. Set engineering strategy, standards, and operating rhythms (planning, design reviews, postmortems, quarterly objectives) that scale across teams. Build the case for investment: manage budget, headcount, vendor relationships, and capacity planning, and make clear trade-offs between speed, cost, risk, and quality. Model ownership and accountability: lead through ambiguity, make timely decisions, and take responsibility for outcomes, including incidents and misses. Collaboration, Partnership & Stakeholder Management Partner with Product, Design, ML/Data Science, Security, Compliance, Risk, and business leaders from conception through production to translate strategy into deployable, well-tested solutions. Build trusted relationships with senior stakeholders and executives; communicate roadmaps, progress, risks, and trade-offs clearly, candidly, and in business terms. Align priorities and dependencies across teams and organizations; resolve conflicts and remove blockers to keep delivery predictable. Represent engineering in executive, architecture, and governance forums; influence enterprise technology direction and shared platform investments. Collaborate with internal audit, regulators, and risk partners to ensure systems meet control, compliance, and regulatory expectations. Foster a culture of shared ownership between engineering, product, operations, and support, with clear accountability and fast feedback loops. Full Stack Architecture & Technical Direction Set technical direction and guide architecture across the full stack: database schema and backend service design, frontend interfaces, and cloud deployment. Guide teams in designing scalable, distributed systems using microservices, event-driven architecture, and domain-driven design principles. Lead architectural decisions that balance performance, reliability, maintainability, and cost across all layers; sponsor design reviews and technical documentation. Champion secure-by-default applications following Zero Trust principles: verify explicitly, enforce least privilege, and assume breach across every layer from UI to data. Drive reuse and consistency through shared platforms, component libraries, design systems, and paved-road tooling. Manage technical debt deliberately, balancing modernization and new feature delivery. Engineering Practices Across the Stack Backend & APIs: Guide teams building scalable services in Java (Spring Boot), Python, or TypeScript/Node.js using REST, GraphQL, gRPC, streaming/WebSocket protocols, and event-driven, asynchronous architectures. Frontend: Set standards for responsive, accessible web applications using React JS and/or Angular with TypeScript, including AI-native experiences such as streaming chat, agent activity timelines, and approval workflows. Data: Oversee batch and streaming pipelines (Spark, Kafka, Airflow, dbt, Snowflake, Databricks, BigQuery or equivalent), data quality, lineage, and privacy and compliance controls (GDPR, SOC 2). Cloud & DevOps: Direct AWS infrastructure, infrastructure as code (Terraform or CloudFormation), CI/CD, Kubernetes/serverless runtimes, security and compliance as code, observability, and cost management. Quality: Establish automated testing across all layers (unit, integration, end-to-end, and performance) and a culture of code and design review. Reliability: Set SLOs and error budgets; lead on-call practices, incident response, and blameless postmortems; ensure disaster recovery, auto-scaling, and capacity planning are designed in from the start. AI & Agentic Systems Leadership Set strategy and guardrails for AI-powered agentic systems where intelligent agents plan, act, and integrate into user workflows, including LLM APIs, agentic frameworks (LangGraph, LlamaIndex, OpenAI and Anthropic SDKs, Model Context Protocol or equivalent), and RAG pipelines. Ensure responsible and safe AI: human-in-the-loop approvals, evaluation harnesses, behavior validation, cost controls, and monitoring for anomalies and drift. Partner with ML, data science, security, and risk teams on model governance, data usage, and the management of cost, latency, failure, and user trust in production AI systems. Enable teams to use AI-assisted development responsibly to improve productivity and quality across the PDLC. Delivery, Quality & Operational Excellence Drive predictable delivery using Agile methods and metrics (e.g., DORA, cycle time, quality, and reliability measures) and continuously improve how teams work. Be accountable for production health of owned services, including on-call readiness, incident leadership, and follow-through on postmortem actions. Build observability and cost-management practices: centralized logging, distributed tracing, metrics, alerting, and dashboards for incident investigation and executive reporting. Preferred Domain Expertise / Experience Payments: Working knowledge of payment rails (ACH, wire, card, real-time payments), reconciliation and settlement flows, and relevant ISO standards such as ISO 20022 and ISO 8583. Lending: Experience with loan origination and servicing workflows, underwriting and decisioning, and regulatory reporting. Banking: Familiarity with core banking operations, account and customer data models, and regulatory and compliance requirements. Experience operating in a regulated environment with strong risk, audit, and compliance expectations.

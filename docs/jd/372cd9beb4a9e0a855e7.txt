Senior Software Engineer, Enablement team (Shipyard)
NAIC
Location: Kansas City

Job Description: The Information Technology division of the National Association of Insurance Commissioners (NAIC) has an exciting opportunity for a Sr. Software Engineer I. This position develops, tests, and maintains scalable applications supporting business needs. Translates requirements into technical solutions, resolves application issues, and delivers quality code and documentation. This is a full-time remote position. Residency within a 100-mile radius of the Kansas City office is required. Responsibilities Include: Evaluate business and technical requirements, recommend application designs, and estimate development effort. Develop, unit-test, and optimize scalable applications, delivering quality code within established timelines. Troubleshoot data and software issues, maintain technical documentation, and follow NAIC/NIPR version-control standards. Collaborate across the organization, provide status reports, participate in team meetings, and present project updates and technical topics. Provide excellent customer service, follow through on customer needs, and identify opportunities to improve satisfaction. Mentor less experienced staff, strengthen teamwork, and improve project delivery while supporting organizational goals and methodologies. Research emerging technologies, present recommendations, and support assigned division-wide technical initiatives. Management Responsibilities: This position does not have direct reports. Minimum Education and/or Experience Required: Bachelor's degree (B.A. or B.S.) in Computer Science or related field and twelve years related work experience or equivalent combination of education and experience. Must have experience with the entire development life cycle: analysis, design, construction, and implementation. Preferred Experience: Experience with Kubernetes, observability tooling Background in developer experience, platform engineering Familiarity with security and compliance practices integrated into developer workflows Experience using AI-assisted development tools and evaluating their impact on productivity Systems & Technology Requirements: Microsoft 365, Jira, Slack, WebEx, SQL, no SQL(Mongo), Java, JavaScript, Angular, SOA and Web Services and AI and cloud tools. Travel: This position does not require overnight business travel. Employees are responsible for their personal transportation to/from the home and office, including events, meetings, and training required by the NAIC. Compensation: $107,200- $130,000, commensurate with education and experience. Sponsorship: Applicants must be authorized to work for any employer in the U.S. We are unable to sponsor or take over sponsorship of an employment Visa at this time.

Morgan Stanley Outsourced Chief Investment Office, or OCIO, and Morgan Stanley Family Office, or MSFO, partner with Morgan Stanley Financial Advisors across all channels to deliver portfolio management solutions to clients of Morgan Stanley Wealth Management. The Institutional Portfolio Solutions, or IPS, team is uniquely situated between two fast-growing and strategically important client channels within Morgan Stanley Wealth Management: OCIO and MSFO. These programs partner with Financial Advisors to offer discretionary and non-discretionary portfolio management services to institutional and private clients, including defined benefit plans, endowments, foundations, ultra-high-net-worth families, family offices, and other sophisticated investors. The IPS team provides customized portfolio solutions across alternative and traditional investments and is responsible for making recommendations on portfolio construction, asset allocation, and manager selection across a broad spectrum of asset classes. This Vice President role is a newly created position focused primarily on traditional investments (public equity and fixed income) , including liquid alternative offerings where relevant. The role is uniquely situated between OCIO/MSFO and the Global Investment Manager Analysis, or GIMA, team, Wealth Management’s due diligence group. The role will focus specifically on the initial sourcing, diligence, and ongoing monitoring of institutional-quality public equity and fixed income offerings for OCIO and MSFO clients. These offerings may include custom accounts, separately managed accounts, mutual funds, UCITS, collective investment trusts (CITs), exchange-traded funds, and limited partnerships. The successful candidate must have significant experience in both: Sourcing, evaluating, and conducting due diligence on traditional managers across public equity, fixed income, and liquid alternative strategies, including institutional-quality, hard-to-access offerings; and Constructing customized public equity and fixed income portfolios for institutional, family office, ultra-high-net-worth, or other sophisticated client portfolios. The role requires the ability to identify compelling traditional investment opportunities, evaluate managers through an institutional-quality due diligence process, and translate those views into client-specific portfolio recommendations across asset allocation, manager selection, vehicle selection, sizing, risk management, and implementation sequencing. Candidates with experience only in manager research or only in asset allocation / portfolio construction are unlikely to be a fit for this role.

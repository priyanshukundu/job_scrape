As Senior Data Scientist, you’ll develop and apply modern machine learning and artificial intelligence across a range of products and business problems. You’ll combine strong computer science foundations, rigorous experimental judgment, and the ability to turn advances in AI into reliable systems with measurable business impact. The work spans document intelligence, multimodal systems that combine text, images, and structured data, and the adaptation of foundation models using proprietary datasets. These models support complex workflows in regulated financial services, where accuracy, explainability, data governance, reproducibility, and careful evaluation are essential. You’ll work across the full machine learning lifecycle and turn successful approaches into reusable AI capabilities, patterns, and components that support multiple teams rather than building a one-off solution for every problem.

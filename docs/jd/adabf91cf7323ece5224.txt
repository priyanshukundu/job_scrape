The Opportunity At Ally, we believe AI has the potential to fundamentally transform how technology is built, operated, and scaled. As AI capabilities become embedded across software delivery, platforms, engineering tools, cloud services, and third-party technologies, managing AI risk must become part of how technology is designed, not an activity performed after the fact. The Technology AI Risk team helps enable the safe, responsible, and scalable adoption of AI across Technology. We work at the intersection of engineering, architecture, cybersecurity, technology risk, and AI governance to identify emerging risks, establish practical guardrails, and embed risk management into the technology lifecycle. This role is ideal for someone who enjoys solving complex problems, influencing technical decisions, and helping define how AI risk management evolves in a rapidly changing environment. Beyond administering governance processes, you will help shape the operating model, guide how AI capabilities are designed and controlled, and ensure AI capabilities can scale safely across the enterprise. The Work Itself 1. Assess AI Technology Risk Assess the risk of all AI use cases sponsored by Technology and complete the associated deliverables outlined in the enterprise AI governance framework. Results will require an understanding of how capabilities will be built, deployed, and operated; identify material risks and dependencies; and determine whether appropriate safeguards exist to support responsible use. 2. Build & Maintain Reusable Controls and Standards Help establish and evolve AI risk guardrails, control requirements, engineering patterns, and implementation standards. Determine when existing enterprise controls are sufficient, where they should be adapted, and where new approaches are needed to address emerging AI risks. 3. Cross Functional Engagement with Risk Partner and Technology Functio ns Integrate AI controls and model evidence into the software development lifecycle to ensure consistency in control adherence and reduce cycle time. Engage Engineering, Architecture, Cybersecurity, and platform teams to translate risk requirements into practical engineering solutions. 4. Enable Responsible Adoption of AI Engage with teams early to understand proposed AI capabilities and provide actionable guidance on what must be true for those capabilities to proceed safely. Help teams navigate requirements, leverage existing controls, and solve issues in ways that reduce risk without creating unnecessary friction. 5. Mature Technology AI Risk Operating Model & Governance Contribute to the continued development of Technology's AI risk management approach, including risk-tiering methodologies, governance pathways, assessment frameworks, monitoring practices, reporting, and decision-making criteria. Identify opportunities to simplify, automate, and scale governance as AI adoption expands. 6. Support the Rapid Evolution of A I Maintain awareness of new regulatory guidelines for AI and ensure they are incorporated into Ally’s program where appropriate. Stay current on world, national, and financial services events related to AI to effectively lead discussions around emerging risk and provide insights/recommendations on how to protect the organization. Effectively operate in an environment where runbooks or prior knowledge may not exist and apply strong leadership behaviors to navigate the situation. 7. What Success Looks Like Success in this role means helping Ally move from evaluating AI risk one implementation at a time toward a scalable operating model where appropriate risk management is built into platforms, engineering practices, and technology processes. You will help establish practical guardrails, identify emerging risks early, and enable teams to adopt AI capabilities safely, responsibly, and at scale while maintaining the speed of innovation.

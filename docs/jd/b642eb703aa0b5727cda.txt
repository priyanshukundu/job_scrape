Your opportunity We are seeking a Digital Delivery Specialist to help transform and modernize our digital ecosystem through the application of AI, automation, and innovative web technologies. This role will serve as a bridge between digital delivery, marketing technology, content operations, and business stakeholders to identify opportunities to simplify processes, accelerate content delivery, improve website performance, and expand the use of AI across our web and content platforms. The successful candidate will challenge existing ways of working, identify opportunities for automation and integration, and help define the next generation of digital experiences. This role is ideal for someone who is equally comfortable discussing AI agents, APIs, content workflows, and website architecture as they are collaborating with marketers, content owners, and technology teams. Key Responsibilities AI & Digital Innovation Identify and implement AI-powered solutions across the website and digital experience ecosystem. Evaluate emerging technologies, AI tools, and automation opportunities that improve efficiency, scalability, and user experience. Develop use cases for AI-driven content creation, optimization, metadata generation, tagging, content governance, personalization, and workflow automation. Partner with business and technology teams to pilot and scale AI initiatives that drive measurable value. Stay current on advances in generative AI, AI agents, large language models (LLMs), answer engines, and digital experience technologies. Website Modernization Assess current website workflows and identify opportunities to modernize content publishing, page creation, governance, and maintenance models. Recommend and implement new approaches to creating, managing, and supporting digital experiences. Help drive adoption of AI-ready website practices, structured content, metadata standards, and scalable content architectures. Support initiatives to improve website flexibility, speed-to-market, and operational efficiency. Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) Partner with content, SEO, and digital teams to improve visibility across AI-powered search and answer engines. Identify opportunities to enhance structured data, schema, FAQs, content organization, and machine-readable content. Measure and report on AI visibility and AEO performance. Evaluate tools and technologies that support AI search optimization and content discoverability. Integration & Automation Identify opportunities to expand integrations across marketing technology, CMS, DAM, analytics, CRM, compliance, and content platforms. Work with technical teams to design and implement automated workflows that reduce manual effort and improve content delivery. Support API strategy, integration opportunities, and automation initiatives that connect systems across the digital ecosystem. Help establish scalable processes that enable content and data to flow seamlessly across platforms. Digital Experience & Process Improvement Analyze current-state workflows and recommend improvements to increase efficiency and reduce operational complexity. Collaborate with stakeholders to identify pain points and prioritize modernization opportunities. Support experimentation and proof-of-concept initiatives that demonstrate new capabilities. Develop business cases, recommendations, and roadmaps for future digital investments.

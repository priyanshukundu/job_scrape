Staff Software Engineer - Device Fingerprinting
Plaid
Location: San Francisco HQ
Link: https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/plaid/8315280a-2498-4b41-9b98-621a61c1f4dc

We believe that the way people interact with their finances will drastically improve in the next few years. We’re dedicated to empowering this transformation by building the tools and experiences that thousands of developers use to create their own products. Plaid powers the tools millions of people rely on to live a healthier financial life. We work with thousands of companies like Venmo, SoFi, several of the Fortune 500, and many of the largest banks to make it easy for people to connect their financial accounts to the apps and services they want to use. Plaid’s network covers 12,000 financial institutions across the US, Canada, UK and Europe. Founded in 2013, the company is headquartered in San Francisco with offices in New York, Seattle, Washington D.C., Raleigh, London, and Amsterdam.

Plaid’s fraud products use financial identity, device, and behavior data to assess risk. You will combine new device intelligence with that data to improve fraud detection.

You will be the hands-on lead of the device intelligence area. You will propose its technical direction and implement the systems, from collection in our SDKs to use in fraud products.

Your scope will cover device and network risk, device fingerprinting, and agent detection across web and mobile. You will bring these capabilities currently provided by vendors in house.

You will study what browsers and mobile devices reveal, how attackers alter that evidence, and which observations remain useful. You will turn those findings into production defenses.

You will work across SDK, Product, Data Science, and Protect teams to connect device observations and history with fraud features.

Responsibilities: 

 - Reverse engineer tools that spoof device attributes, hide instrumentation, emulate devices, or conceal automation.

 - Collect device, network, and interaction signals within platform and client performance limits.

 - Build server analysis and matching that preserve useful device history as software, storage, and networks change.

 - Develop agent detection. Test it against legitimate traffic and evasive automation.

 - Measure device matching and detection quality. Establish whether new signals improve fraud detection.

Qualifications:

 - Deep expertise in browser internals, mobile systems, or offensive security, with research or engineering work on device behavior.

 - Code, experiments, or production work that demonstrate device evasion and implemented defenses.

 - A record of developing research prototypes into reliable production systems.

 - Ability to set technical direction and personally implement across unfamiliar systems.

 - Sound experimental judgment, including how to measure errors when labels are incomplete.

 - Preferred: Experience with fraud prevention, device fingerprinting, AI agent detection, or both browser and mobile platforms.

Blackstone is the world’s largest alternative asset manager. Blackstone seeks to deliver compelling returns for institutional and individual investors by strengthening the companies in which the firm invests. Blackstone’s over $1.3 trillion in assets under management include global investment strategies focused on real estate, private equity, credit, infrastructure, life sciences, growth equity, secondaries and hedge funds. Further information is available at www.blackstone.com . Follow @blackstone on LinkedIn , X (Twitter) , and Instagram . Business Group: Blackstone Multi-Asset Investing Business Group Overview: Blackstone Multi-Asset Investing (BXMA) manages $101 billion across a diversified set of businesses. We strive to generate attractive risk-adjusted returns for our clients across market cycles. Our strategies include Absolute Return, Multi-Strategy, Total Portfolio Management, and Public Real Assets. Job Title: Blackstone Multi-Asset Investing - Lead Data Engineer-Product Management Job Responsibilities: Support BXMA Product Management, the team responsible for the development, governance, and continuous enhancement of all client- and prospect-facing investment materials — including marketing presentations, portfolio and performance reviews, recurring and customized reporting, due diligence questionnaires, and responses to analytical and product-related inquiries. Within Product Management, specifically support our Data Team to own and develop our data infrastructure, which is the foundation of all the content we create, whether it’s qualitative or quantitative. Understand our business requirements, from how our products work, what clients need, and what we as a business want to communicate to clients, and design, build, and maintain complex data processes starting from raw data tables and ending with client-friendly visuals and reports. Responsible for overseeing Data Team, partnering with developers to build production-ready code to automate reports. Dissect and diagram complex Excel-based processes, develop new data models and data workflow designs from scratch, while using best practices and tools (SQL, Python, Git). Oversee and train junior resources on the team, locally and across Blackstone global offices. Supervise (3) Software Developers professionals. Perform other duties as needed. Qualifications Education: Master’s degree (or foreign equivalent) in Finance, Computer Science, Mathematics, or a related quantitative field required. Certification: CFA Level 1 or equivalent expertise in applied financial math required. Experience: Minimum of 3 years of experience in job offered or related occupations required. Minimum of 3 years of experience in financial data analytics, ideally focused on liquid multi-asset strategies. Minimum of 3 years of experience working with databases, data structures, and data analysis tools. Minimum of 3 years of experience with SQL and Python; with proven strong Excel skills. Minimum of 3 years of experience implementing data validation, reconciliation, and exception-handling processes to ensure accuracy of client-facing financial and performance reporting. Minimum of 3 years of experience designing relational data models and end-to‑end data workflows, transforming raw source data into governed, client‑ready analytical datasets. Minimum of 3 years of experience understanding of portfolio analytics, including performance measurement, exposure analysis, liquidity metrics, and asset‑class-specific conventions. Minimum of 3 years of experience working in a client‑facing environment with time-sensitive data deliverables. Minimum of 3 years of experience utilizing data to solve real-world business problems.

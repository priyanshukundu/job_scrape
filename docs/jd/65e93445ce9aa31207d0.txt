Job Description: About the Role At Broadcom, we believe enterprise software should be powerful, intuitive, and seamlessly integrated. We are seeking a Senior Software Engineer who operates at the intersection of systems engineering and modern software design. In this role, you will help build a unified ecosystem of tools, common libraries, and automation frameworks that empower our Mainframe product teams to deliver high-quality, accessible, and consistent solutions. Join our Mainframe Division to leverage your dual expertise in z/OS environments and modern software practices to drive the future of enterprise software modernization. Key Responsibilities Platform & Tooling Development: Design, develop, and maintain high-impact software, common libraries, and developer tools used across all Broadcom Mainframe product teams. Mainframe Modernization: Integrate modern CI/CD pipelines, automation scripts, and AI-assisted workflows into traditional z/OS development environments. Cross-Functional Collaboration: Partner with Product Management, User Experience, and Engineering leadership to define technical requirements, contribute to strategic roadmaps, and build scalable solutions. Quality & Reliability: Analyze, troubleshoot, and resolve complex issues across both development and production environments, ensuring high enterprise-grade performance. Technical Leadership: Support agile practices, participate in iteration demos, mentor team members, and promote modern software engineering principles within the division. Technical Qualifications & Experience Mainframe & Systems Expertise: Hands-on experience with IBM Mainframe z/OS environments, system components, and product maintenance. Proven experience performing software installations and maintenance using SMP/e and z/OSMF . Modern Software Engineering & DevOps: Strong proficiency in programming languages such as Java , Python , or Groovy . solid experience building and maintaining CI/CD pipelines using Git , Jenkins , and related tools. Hands-on experience with automation tools (e.g., Ansible ) to build self-service workflows and streamline routine administrative tasks. Familiarity with AI-assisted development tools to accelerate scripting, pipeline creation, and troubleshooting. Preferred / Bonus Qualifications: Experience designing and integrating RESTful Web Services and Cloud platform interfaces. Solid understanding of software system design concepts for enterprise-grade solutions. Leadership & Soft Skills Problem-Solving: Ability to translate complex business and technical requirements into structured, executable solutions. Communication: Clear, effective communication skills with the ability to create knowledge-transfer documentation, present solution demos, and collaborate across distributed teams. Adaptability: High self-motivation and an eagerness to learn, adopt, and apply emerging technologies to enterprise business needs. Education & Experience Requirements Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, or a related technical field with 5+ years of professional software development experience; OR Master’s degree in a related field with 3+ years of professional software development experience. Location & Equal Opportunity Must be located in the US to support multiple US business hours, production environments, and R&D lab locations (e.g., Pittsburgh, PA, Plano, TX, or Lisle, IL).

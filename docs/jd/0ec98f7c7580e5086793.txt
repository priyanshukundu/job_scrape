To learn more about CIBC, please visit CIBC.com What You’ll Be Doing As the CIBC Core Growth Team’s dedicated Client Portfolio Manager (CPM) in Boston, you will be a vital bridge between our investment team and internal sales and advisor teams, third party intermediary clients, Institutional clients, internal product, legal, middle office, performance, and compliance teams. You will be acting as a team ambassador representing the Core Growth Team’s specific investment strategies. You will play a critical role in fostering client relationships and supporting business development activities, such as increasing the team’s strategy adoption across all distribution channels, managing our RFP and RFI activity, monitoring track record composites, generating marketing materials, such as the team’s strategy presentation decks and quarterly marketing presentations geared to specific clients. This role also will have you on a high volume of virtual calls and in person meetings with prospects and existing clients to give portfolio updates on your own and or also with members of the investment team. Significant travel in the US and Canada is expected in the early years of this position. You will raise awareness and educate advisors, business development officers, Institutional gate keepers, retail advisors, Institutional clients, consultants, and potential prospects about the team’s investment process, investment strategies and specific stock holdings. In this role, you will also be directly embedded and involved in our team’s investment research meetings, new stock pitches in our Seven Step Investment Process, and various debates that the team has on investment related matters. While not conducting portfolio management decisions or covering specific companies, you will be very involved in the discussions that the team has on various topics, and you are expected to be an active participant in our research meetings, which will in turn make you an expert in our team’s Seven Step Investment Process. To successfully perform the work, you will be on-site in Boston when not traveling and the team is in the office five days a week. Over time, the role will involve substantial travel, and it is our expectation that the CPM will be on the road often promoting the team’s investment strategies on a regular basis. This role will report directly to Sandy Sanders, the team lead for the CIBC Core Growth Team in Boston, and also will be dedicated to this investment team specifically, while also collaborating with other CPMs and senior leadership across the firm. How You’ll Succeed CPM Strategy : Increase adoption of the team’s investment strategies. Contribute to and implement distribution strategy for the team’s equity investment strategies, deliver consistent execution of distribution strategy, and contribute to vision for the team. Investment Capabilities : Contribute to best-in-class capability development/evolution and represent investment team in front of advisors, clients, prospects, consultants, and other intermediaries. Translate the team’s investment strategies and market trends into clear, actionable information. Articulate our investment method, portfolio positioning, strategies, outlook, as well as market insights of our portfolio. Client & Advisor Feedback Integration: Actively solicit and gather feedback from clients and advisors regarding team’s equity investment strategies and solutions. Collaborate with Core Growth Team to integrate this feedback into capability enhancements and new products, ensuring that client needs are met effectively. Thought Leadership : Generate thought leadership ideas to fill the content platform for the Core Growth Team’s strategies and leverage this information to generate client discussions. Education: Collaborate with various parts of the organization (investment communications, sales teams, marketing, compliance, operations, etc.) to raise awareness and increase the team’s investment strategies adoption. Content Ownership : Contribute and deliver the narrative for all client-facing materials and collateral. Industry Leadership : Deliver sales, servicing, and thought leadership presentations to all client channels, building a reputation as a subject matter expert alongside portfolio managers. Attend conferences/industry/panel events as a subject matter expert. Reporting & Compliance: Partner with internal teams to design and automate reporting, bridging front-office insights with back-office data, ensuring all actions comply with regulatory standards.

Are you looking to join an organization that is growing and dynamic? What about a high-energy, collaborative environment that rewards hard work? J.S. Held is a global consulting firm that combines technical, scientific, financial, and strategic expertise to advise clients seeking to realize value and mitigate risk. Our professionals serve as trusted advisors to organizations facing high stakes matters demanding urgent attention, staunch integrity, proven experience, clear-cut analysis, and an understanding of both tangible and intangible assets. The firm provides a comprehensive suite of services, products, and data that enable clients to navigate complex, contentious, and often catastrophic situations. We are seeking a Treasury Manager to join our Strategic Advisory team, combining hands-on treasury execution with trusted-advisor service for dozens of clients and engagements across a wide range of industries. The role will manage hundreds of millions of dollars across hundreds of bank accounts at multiple financial institutions, with responsibility for treasury transactions, processes, cash management, and liquidity oversight. Reporting to the Controller and Senior Managing Director levels, this is a hybrid in-office position based at our Solana Beach location in North County San Diego. This is an opportunity for an entrepreneurial, highly driven professional with a well-rounded skill set to join a fast-paced, nationwide practice and a dynamic, collaborative, and growing team. Job Responsibilities Serve as a consulting Treasurer and trusted advisor to clients, executives, attorneys, bankers, and other stakeholders across multiple industries. Coordinate treasury onboarding and wind-down with accounting and project teams. At inception, establish bank access and cash-management workflows; during delivery, provide transaction records and reconciliation support; at completion, coordinate authorized final disbursements, account closure, and records handoff. Manage relationships with banks and other financial institutions across client engagements, coordinating account services, bank documentation, service and fee reviews, and resolution of access, funding, and transaction issues; recommend banking arrangements appropriate to each client’s operating needs and approved engagement scope. Manage client cash, liquidity, financial risk, and forecasting within approved engagement scopes and client authorizations. Monitor daily cash positions, distinguish available cash from restricted funds, forecast near-term funding needs, coordinate authorized funding transfers, and promptly escalate liquidity shortfalls and material financial exposures. Prepare and deliver accurate treasury management reporting, including cash positions, liquidity forecasts, cash-flow variances, account balances, transaction exceptions, and other analyses tailored to project and client needs. Partner with accounting teams to provide treasury schedules and supporting information for accurate consolidated financial statements and broader management reporting. Oversee treasury delivery across multiple concurrent client engagements, establishing engagement-specific workflows, priorities, deliverables, and communication routines; maintain clear boundaries between client funds, records, and authorizations, and escalate resource constraints or treasury risks to the Controller and Senior Managing Director levels. Maintain strong treasury controls and data integrity, including segregation of duties, independent payment approvals, verification of new or changed beneficiary instructions, appropriate banking access and transaction limits, and complete audit trails. Protect confidential client and banking information, monitor transaction exceptions, and escalate suspected fraud or control failures promptly. Partner with accounting teams to research and resolve complex accounting matters in accordance with U.S. GAAP and applicable requirements. Manage treasury transactions, including wire transfers, ACH payments, receipts, and authorized transfers between accounts. Coordinate payment preparation, required approvals, release, settlement confirmation, and exception resolution within bank cutoff times and established authorization limits. Administer account opening, maintenance, and closure, authorized signers, banking access, and required bank compliance documentation, as well as other client financial resources within the approved engagement scope. Assess, design, document, and improve treasury processes, payment workflows, banking administration, reporting, and internal controls so they can scale across clients, accounts, and financial institutions. Partner with accounting leaders on related accounting processes, department structure, and reporting workflows to establish clear ownership and effective treasury-accounting handoffs. Lead change effectively, communicate clear expectations, and balance competing priorities in a fast-paced consulting environment. Collaborate with Technology Services, Human Resources, Marketing, and other corporate functions to support client engagements and business objectives. Support treasury needs for real estate holding and operating companies, including active commercial, residential, and mixed-use portfolios, when included in the assigned engagement scope. Bachelor’s degree in accounting, finance, or a related field is required; a master’s degree in accounting is helpful but not required. 10+ years of progressive treasury, cash-management, banking-operations, or closely related finance experience, including at least five years leading treasury, accounting, or finance teams. Demonstrated hands-on responsibility for high-value cash balances, large multi-account banking structures, payment execution, and operational controls across multiple entities or client engagements. Strong technical knowledge of cash management, liquidity forecasting, payment processes, bank account administration, and treasury controls, together with working knowledge of U.S. GAAP and treasury-related accounting. Demonstrated commitment to financial accuracy, completeness, and timely reporting, with the ability to identify and resolve transaction and reconciliation issues in partnership with accounting teams. Experience managing, coaching, and developing teams while promoting accountability, collaboration, and a positive attitude. Consultative mindset and experience building trusted-advisor relationships with executives, business decision-makers, clients, and external stakeholders. Excellent communication, negotiation, analytical, and problem-solving skills, with sound professional judgment. Ability to manage multiple engagements, evolving responsibilities, and changing scopes of work in a fast-paced environment. Advanced Microsoft Excel skills and practical experience using online banking platforms, payment workflows, access entitlements, and financial systems. Ability to organize and validate large volumes of account and transaction data, use treasury reporting tools effectively, and learn new financial systems. Collaborative, service-oriented approach and a genuine desire to contribute to a mission-driven team. Preferred Qualifications Certified Treasury Professional (CTP) preferred; CPA and/or MBA also valued. Experience with Microsoft Dynamics 365, NetSuite, QuickBooks, Yardi or comparable enterprise financial systems. Experience in a professional services firm. Experience supporting real estate holding companies, operating companies, or active real estate portfolios. Warmth, authenticity, curiosity, and genuine concern for colleagues and clients. Physical and Mental Job Qualifications Ability to perform mathematical computations and analyze financial information for extended periods. Ability to review detailed transactions and supporting documentation with sustained accuracy and attention to detail. Ability to work effectively in a professional office environment and adapt to changing engagement demands. Availability for occasional evening or weekend work based on client or project needs.

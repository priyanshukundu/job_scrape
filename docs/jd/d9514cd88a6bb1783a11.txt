Job Title : Data Scientist / Biostatistician II Location: Framingham, MA Grade: L2-1 Hiring Manager: Mike Springer About The Job: Join Sanofi's Data Science and Digital Transformation (DSD) team as a Data Scientist supporting our rare disease biologics portfolio—one of the most impactful and rapidly growing areas of our global Manufacturing Science, Analytics and Technology (MSAT) organization. This position is based at the Sanofi Massachusetts Bio campus in Framingham, MA, and reports to the Head of DSD Rare Disease. You will serve as a technical authority providing advanced statistics and data science support across the Recombinant Mammalian Drug Substance platform , which is responsible for lifecycle management of mammalian cell culture-based processes across Sanofi's global biologics manufacturing network. We deliver innovative, robust, and cost-effective next generation processes, enable launch of new products, and provide commercial manufacturing/Quality Control support. The DSD Recombinant team acts as Data Stewards in Technical Product Teams and is pioneering the future of biomanufacturing through digital labs, advanced analytics-based process understanding and control, and deployment of cutting-edge ML/AI tools (Dataiku, SIMCA, SeeQ). In this role, you will provide day-to-day advanced statistics and data science support across Cell Culture, Purification, and Analytical functions. Typical activities include process/method optimization and improvement, process investigations, comparability studies, process monitoring, method transfer/validation, small-scale model qualification, and process characterization.

Fund Administration & Accounting Position Summary The Fund Administration & Accounting team member will provide oversight of fund accounting, financial reporting, valuation, and third-party service providers for registered investment companies. The role requires strong technical accounting knowledge, sound judgment, and the ability to evaluate complex investments, including private equity and other Level 3 securities. Key Responsibilities Oversee fund accounting and administration activities, including NAV calculations, financial reporting, expense accruals, portfolio transactions, and related controls. Monitor and evaluate third-party service providers, ensuring compliance with established procedures, service standards, and contractual requirements. Review complex accounting matters, including corporate actions, restructurings, private investments, and other nonstandard transactions, applying relevant U.S. GAAP and regulatory requirements. Identify, investigate, and resolve significant accounting, valuation, NAV, and operational issues in coordination with internal teams and external service providers. Support valuation oversight for private equity, private credit, and other complex or illiquid investments, including evaluating valuation methodologies, assumptions, financial information, and market developments. Review and challenge third-party valuation specialists and pricing vendors, including the reasonableness of their methodologies, inputs, and conclusions. Support the Fund's Rule 2a-5 valuation framework, including valuation governance, testing, documentation, and preparation of materials for management and Fund Board committees. Partner with Portfolio Management, Operations, Legal, Compliance, Tax, and internal and external auditors to support effective fund governance and risk management. Qualifications Required Bachelor's degree in Accounting, Finance, Economics, or a related field. Approximately 5 years+ of relevant experience in fund accounting, administration, valuation, investment accounting, or public accounting, preferably involving registered investment companies. Strong understanding of mutual fund accounting, NAV calculations, financial reporting, and applicable U.S. GAAP, including ASC 946 and ASC 820. Experience overseeing third-party fund administrators, accountants, custodians, or pricing and valuation service providers. Familiarity with private equity, Level 3 valuation methodologies, and SEC Rule 2a-5 requirements. Strong analytical, problem-solving, and communication skills, including the ability to challenge conclusions and work effectively with internal and external stakeholders. Preferred CPA, CFA, or other relevant professional designation. Experience with private equity, private credit, venture capital, or other alternative investments within registered funds. Experience working with third-party valuation specialists and presenting complex accounting or valuation matters to senior management or Fund Boards.

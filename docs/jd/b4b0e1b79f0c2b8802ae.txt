Corporate Functions Lead Software Engineer – VP III Who We Are Looking For We are seeking a highly experienced, hands-on technology leader to serve as the Corporate Functions Lead Software Engineer – VP III , responsible for designing, building, and modernizing strategic technology solutions across Human Resources, Legal, Audit, Compliance, Risk, Realty, and other Corporate Functions domains. This role is intended for a senior technologist who remains deeply involved in software engineering while providing technical leadership across multiple initiatives and engineering teams. The successful candidate will serve as a trusted technical leader responsible for shaping engineering standards, guiding solution design, mentoring developers, and driving delivery of enterprise-scale applications, integrations, and data platforms. The ideal candidate combines deep expertise in Java engineering, enterprise application development, integration architecture, cloud technologies, and Databricks-based data platforms with the ability to influence technology strategy and execution across a complex global environment. Unlike a traditional engineering manager, this role is expected to remain highly technical and hands-on, participating directly in architecture reviews, software development, design decisions, code reviews, performance optimization, and technical problem solving. Why This Role Matters Corporate Functions is accelerating its modernization agenda through automation, cloud adoption, advanced analytics, AI enablement, and contemporary application development. This role will: Lead the engineering modernization of critical Corporate Functions platforms. Drive adoption of modern software engineering practices and architectures. Deliver scalable enterprise applications and API ecosystems. Enable analytics, AI, and automation initiatives through modern data platforms. Establish reusable technology capabilities that can be leveraged across multiple business domains. Improve speed-to-market, operational resilience, and engineering effectiveness. This is a highly visible technical leadership role with significant influence across business and technology organizations. What You Will Be Responsible For 1. Technical Leadership & Engineering Strategy Serve as a senior engineering leader across Corporate Functions Technology. Define technical roadmaps and engineering approaches for strategic initiatives. Cloud & Modern Engineering Drive adoption of cloud-native engineering practices.

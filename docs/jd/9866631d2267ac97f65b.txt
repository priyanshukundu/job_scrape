Start your Voyage – Apply Now Profile Summary: The Voya Investment Management Equity Team is seeking a Quantitative Research Analyst / Developer for the Investment Analytics group. The group manages an analytical platform delivering actionable insights to the fundamental equity investment team. The group constantly develops new tools that leverage quantitative techniques and traditional/alternative/proprietary data with the objective of improving the fundamental investment process. As a Quant Analyst/Developer, you will help to design, build, and maintain a next generation analytics platform including the underlying infrastructure. Profile Description: Enhance investment process with particular focus on portfolio construction, performance attribution, risk decomposition, and stock-selection models. Design and build analytical tools used to analyze investment strategies with a focus on functionality and user experience Support and enhance our investment platform through design, testing, and implementation of modular components in the existing code base Knowledge & Experience: Bachelor’s degree in a quantitative discipline with a strong academic background in a technically oriented field (Computer Science, Engineering, or related discipline) Master’s degree in a scientific field a plus. Experience with Axioma/Barra (optimization and risk models) and FactSet/Bloomberg (portfolio analytics) Experience working on quantitative research problems on either the buy or sell side Experience working with PMs and strong communication skills Experience in general purpose programming languages (Python, R, Java, C++, or similar) Basic web development experience, including functional knowledge of HTML and CSS Awareness of OO design principles and patterns, data structure, and algorithms Basic knowledge of version control tools (Git), relational databases (MSSQL), and cloud platforms (Azure or similar) Must be a team player, have excellent problem-solving skills, keen attention to detail, and ability to work in a fast-paced environment Must be passionate about technology, mathematics, and their intersection #LI-MG1 Compensation Pay Disclosure: Voya is committed to pay that’s fair and equitable, which means comparable pay for comparable roles and responsibilities.

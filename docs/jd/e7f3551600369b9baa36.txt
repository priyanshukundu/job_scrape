About the Role At eBay, we focus on advancing innovation and encouraging a diverse atmosphere where everyone can bring their outstanding identities to work. As the AI/ML Data Science, Manager 2, you will lead a team of expert engineers and data scientists located in Austin. You will design world-class AI solutions to guarantee the seamless functioning of our platform. Your role will be vital in upholding the integrity and trust of our marketplace for millions of users around the world. Primary Job Responsibilities Team Leadership & People Management : Guide, hire, and support a group of skilled AI/ML engineers. Promote career development, carry out performance evaluations, and encourage an environment of technical excellence and inclusivity. Roadmap & Strategy Execution : Collaborate with Product Managers, Legal counsel, and senior architects to define and complete the technical vision and roadmap for AI-driven compliance solutions. Delivery of Production AI Systems : Coordinate the development and deployment of machine learning models and systems, ensuring they meet the highest standards of quality and scalability. Governance & Auditability : Maintain adherence to internal AI safety guidelines and regulatory requirements, upholding rigorous standards for explainability and model risk management. Operational Excellence : Establish and maintain SLAs/SLIs for AI services, manage operational on-call rotations, and proactively address technical debt. Cross-Functional Influence : Act as the primary liaison between engineering and non-technical collaborators, ensuring alignment on technical trade-offs and business priorities. Resource & Program Allocation : Manage team capacity, budgets, and delivery timelines across multiple initiatives, ensuring successful implementation of AI solutions. Required Skills and Experience Management Experience : 4+ years of experience leading engineering teams passionate about machine learning or data-intensive platforms. Technical Background : 6+ years of experience in software engineering and applied AI/ML, with proficiency in machine learning systems and data architectures. GenAI & Applied ML Expertise : Strong grounding in modern AI paradigms, enabling effective technical review and mentorship. Collaborator Management : Proven ability to align cross-functional priorities and manage relationships with non-engineering collaborators. Production Delivery : Demonstrated success in shipping and maintaining high-availability, large-scale distributed ML systems. Talent Development : Experience in hiring, mentoring, and retaining top technical talent. Education : Bachelor’s degree or Master’s degree or equivalent experience in Computer Science, Engineering, Mathematics, or a related field. Nice to Have Experience in applying AI/ML in regulated domains such as compliance, fraud detection, or fintech. Experience with international AI governance structures and Model Risk Management approaches. Experience managing GPU infrastructure, inference serving costs, and LLM provider contracts and budgets.

Job Description As a Sr Quant Analytics Associate, you will be a key member within the Consumer and Community Banking Data & Analytics organization. You will work closely with AML/KYC stakeholders to deliver insights in terms of descriptive, diagnostic, predictive, and prescriptive analytics with a focus on Controls, Risk, and Regulatory areas. The successful candidate will bring strong SQL expertise, practical ETL experience, comfort with Python, curiosity about AI-assisted code creation, and a strong ability to independently investigate ambiguous data problems. This role is ideal for someone who wants to combine hands-on data engineering, analytics, controls awareness, and business problem solving to help modernize AML/KYC decision support. Job Responsibilities Understand and translate business needs into data models supporting long-term solutions. Develop AI-ready metadata, business definitions, lineage documentation, data dictionaries, and semantic layers that enable AI agents, self-service analytics, and natural-language querying. Contribute to innovation and creativity within the team by actively proposing new ideas and approaches to improve processes and support effective solutions. Partner closely and collaborate with Product teams, Data Management teams, Strategy teams, Technology teams, and broader D&A organization resources across multiple functional units to understand business requirements and objectives. Use Python/SQL/Alteryx as the primary tools for data extraction, transformation, profiling, validation, and performance optimization across large and complex datasets. Prepare clear documentation and executive-ready presentations summarizing data findings, development progress, data quality risks, and recommended next steps. Provide ongoing production support for data assets, including troubleshooting data issues, answering stakeholder questions, managing enhancements, and communicating impacts of source-system changes. Ensure analytics, data transformation, and AI-enablement activities comply with applicable controls, model/data governance expectations, privacy requirements, and regulatory standards. Create data quality checks, reconciliation routines, exception monitoring, and validation frameworks to ensure reports are accurate, complete, timely, and fit for purpose. Required Qualifications, Skills, and Capabilities: 5+ years of related experience. Bachelor’s degree in a quantitative or related field required; Master’s degree preferred. Proficiency in SQL, Python, Alteryx, and Tableau. Familiarity with cloud-based (AWS, Snowflake, Databricks) data solutions and tools. Basic skills with JIRA and Confluence. Intermediate knowledge of machine learning and Copilot or Claude Code. Knowledge of how to integrate with APIs preferred. Excellent strategic thinking and problem-solving abilities. Strong project management skills with the ability to manage multiple projects simultaneously. Experience delivering analytics solutions following an Agile (Scrum or Kanban) methodology. Familiarity with data mining, statistical modeling, machine learning, and other advanced analytics methods. Robust data management skills with a focus on ensuring data quality and organization. Demonstrated ability to independently make the decisions and judgments necessary to deliver quality analytics solutions. Effective communication skills, both written and verbal. Strong soft skills, including adaptability, emotional intelligence, and conflict resolution. Demonstrated ability to analyze and solve problems, innovate, and think creatively.

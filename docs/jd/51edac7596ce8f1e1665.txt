Duties: Responsible for supporting the design, development and maintenance of new and existing automation frameworks, as well as continued evaluation of test cases for inclusion in test regression, tests coverage and automation of newly implemented functionality, and development of tools to enable acceptance test efficiencies. Work closely with Software Developers, Project Management and Customer Support to form and follow quality procedures, standards and specifications ensuring they are met and deliver the highest quality projects in a timely and cost-effective manner. Assist in the testing of Delivery Controls. Examine evidence of delivery controls and reports result. Identify areas of improvements in capturing and reporting of control data. Build and deploy automation tools using Python and Shell scripting to streamline workflows, reduce manual effort and improve consistency across testing and translation processes. Perform unit testing, defect diagnosis, and end-to-end validation across integrated enterprise systems such as SAP and Clarity PPM. Develop and execute automated test plans and unit test cases in Java and SAP ABAP within Agile sprint cycles, and leverage JUnit and TestNG alongside JIRA for task tracking and cross-functional collaboration. Collaborate with other groups to review control data and identify improvements in the IT delivery process and controls. Contribute to the creation of dashboards and reports demonstrating control adherence and opportunities for improvement. Up to 40% telecommuting permitted. Founded in 1973, OCC is dedicated to promoting stability and market integrity by delivering clearing and settlement services for options, futures and securities lending transactions. As a Systemically Important Financial Market Utility (SIFMU), OCC operates under the jurisdiction of the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC), the U.S. Commodity Futures Trading Commission (CFTC), and the Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System. OCC has more than 100 clearing members and provides central counterparty (CCP) clearing and settlement services to 19 exchanges and trading platforms.

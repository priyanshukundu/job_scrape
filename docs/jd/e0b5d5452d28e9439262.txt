The Software Engineer (.NET/Azure) is a hands-on, mid-level contributor responsible for designing, building, and modernizing high-volume, high-transaction applications across the Experian Employer Services (EES) organization. You will write production code, participate in code reviews, evaluate SAST findings, and work closely with product, quality engineering, security, and operations partners to deliver secure, reliable software to our clients and partners. AI and automation are the main focus of our team. EES is adopting an AI-Driven Development Lifecycle (AI DLC) and spec-driven agentic development, in which teams define intent, constraints, acceptance criteria, and validation before AI agents build approved work. As an engineer in this model you will work from versioned specifications, direct AI coding agents to implement approved work, and oversee the review, testing, and security validation of what they produce. You will have already worked this way hands-on; this is a requirement of the role, not a learning goal. Humans remain accountable for technical decisions, data protection, and production outcomes.

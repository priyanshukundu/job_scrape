Senior Research Scientist Job Description Role Summary : This position is responsible for leading original applied research in a field of specialization relevant to R&CO’s work for professional publication, either generally or internally refereed. About the RCO Research Team : The R&CO Research team shares expertise and conducts research designed to build knowledge, inform Bank leadership, and enable others to build evidence-based strategies that support the economic well-being and mobility of low- and moderate-income communities and families in New England. The department’s focal areas are: Promoting the prosperity of smaller cities and rural areas, financial opportunity including understanding wealth, and employment outcomes; and we also do work to monitor community economic conditions. Our work contributes to the Fed’s larger goal of building an economy that works for everyone. The Senior Research Scientist’s work should aim to inform policy and/or the work of organizations by providing background on the topic, empirical analysis, and explanation of how the findings may inform issues related to the economic well-being of low-and moderate-income communities. This position is responsible for communicating clearly and formally to the public, including key stakeholders, research communities, the media, and leadership across the Federal Reserve System on economic and community development topics. The Senior Research Scientist is also responsible for providing direction to less experienced social scientists and other staff members. Responsibilities: Leading and contributing to applied research using empirical methods and analysis that aim to contribute to and build upon subject matter expertise. (50%) Conducting analyses in a manner expected to meet academic peer review standards. Writing for department and external publication. Selecting topics relevant to improving conditions of low- and moderate-income populations--informed by research literature, department leadership, RCO engagement teams and community stakeholders. Perform other duties as assigned including descriptive data analyses across a range of relevant topics. Communicating research (25%) Working with Corporate Communications to expand reach and impact. Providing written communication and oral presentations internally to the President, the Board of Directors, Senior Management, internal staff and colleagues at other Reserve Banks as needed as well as externally to government, academic and other communities. Attending and presenting research at meetings, conferences and workshops held by the Bank and academic, business, and international organizations to hear, discuss and present current research. Speaking to non-professional audiences on areas of professional expertise and economic developments and answering questions from the media and public. Organizing workshops, seminars, or conferences as requested. Team contributions (25%) May manage the Visiting Fellows Program, consisting of up to three external researchers per year who work with Fed research partners to publish briefs. Evaluating others' work and advising colleagues on their research, publications, and policy analysis. Assist in staff recruitment. May work with senior management, the Board of Governors. May provide briefings and presentations to support the Bank President’s policy obligations. May supervise Policy Analysts. Contribute to the broader RCO department and collaborate with colleagues engaged in activities across the region. Other Job expectations Keeping abreast of theoretical and empirical developments, and of recent policy-relevant economic and financial trends. Contributing to advances in social scientific theory, evidence, and research methods. Using independent judgment to apply such advances to research and policy/practice. Qualifications include : Knowledge and experience normally acquired through, or equivalent to, the completion of a Ph.D. degree in relevant social science field and a minimum of 3-5 years of job-related experience. Excellent quantitative analysis skills and advanced expertise in a statistical programming language (e.g. R, Stata, SAS) Experience leading or co-leading large research projects, demonstrated research capability and publishing expertise. Experience in a specialized discipline relevant to R&CO’s work. Ability to function on an independent basis on analytical projects, and superb written and communication skills.

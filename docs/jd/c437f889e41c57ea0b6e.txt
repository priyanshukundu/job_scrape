Job Family : Strategy & Transformation Consulting Travel Required : Up to 50% Clearance Required : None What You Will Do: The Patient Services team provides strategy, program design, implementation, technology enablement, and monitoring services to pharmaceutical, biotechnology, and medical device companies. We support clients across the product lifecycle to determine the right suite of Patient Support Programs (PSPs), translate strategy into executable operating models and technology-enabled capabilities, and bring programs to life through strong partnerships with manufacturers, internal functions, and third-party vendors. We also help clients establish practical reporting and performance management approaches that turn operational, CRM, and program data into actionable insights while ensuring programs achieve their strategic intent. Associate Directors are responsible for managing engagements from cultivation through completion. In this role, you will lead designated project teams and support the day-to-day management of client relationships. You will develop and manage work plans, staffing, timelines, budgets, risks, and deliverables across multiple workstreams or projects. Your work products will be high quality, analytically sound, and client ready. In this visible role, you will lead team and client meetings; guide, manage, and coach consultants; and provide constructive feedback on project performance and career development. You will collaborate with Directors and Partners, keeping them informed of project progress, risks, decisions, and opportunities. You will also contribute to sales initiatives, proposal development, thought leadership, and broader practice development. Travel is project dependent and averages up to 30 percent. Guidehouse encourages long-term career development. Associate Directors follow a clearly defined career path. As you strengthen your sales, client leadership, project management, functional expertise, and ability to lead multiple workstreams while meeting business development goals, you will progress to the next level, Director. Specific responsibilities will include: Defining patient treatment and support journeys, including engagement points, access barriers, unmet needs, and opportunities to improve the patient and caregiver experience. Conducting secondary market research on competitor and analog programs, emerging Patient Services models, technology capabilities, and vendor solutions. Primary research with patients, caregivers, healthcare professionals, field teams, program operators, and other stakeholders to identify needs and validate potential support offerings. Developing Patient Services program blueprints and detailed designs, including the desired customer experience, service mix, eligibility and business rules, channel strategy, staffing, operating model, and level of support. Supporting requirements definition, vendor selection, process design, implementation, and operational build-out for in-house and outsourced hubs and related services, including access, affordability, adherence, and treatment management support. Translating Patient Services business needs into CRM and technology requirements, including case management workflows, user stories, data capture, integrations, role-based access, testing, deployment readiness, and adoption considerations. Assessing and improving the use of CRM, workflow, telephony, digital engagement, data, and automation capabilities to support case managers, field reimbursement teams, contact centers, and cross-functional stakeholders. Defining functional and operational reporting requirements, KPI frameworks, dashboard specifications, data definitions, and governance processes that support frontline management, program leadership, and executive decision-making. Analyzing operational, CRM, vendor, and patient journey data to identify performance drivers, service gaps, root causes, and targeted interventions; translating findings into actionable recommendations. Defining methodologies to measure and benchmark Patient Services program effectiveness, including access, speed to therapy, affordability, engagement, adherence, quality, productivity, and patient experience measures. Leading implementation planning, readiness, testing, training, launch, hypercare, and continuous improvement activities across business, operational, vendor, data, and technology workstreams. Engaging executive-level audiences to communicate complex findings, facilitate decisions, and deliver practical recommendations tied to patient, operational, and business outcomes. Identifying business development opportunities and supporting follow-through through proposal development, solution design, scoping, pricing inputs, and client presentations. Developing, coaching, and managing junior staff while fostering collaboration, accountability, and high-quality delivery. What You Will Need: Bachelor's degree in science, biomedical engineering, healthcare, business, technology, analytics, or a related field; advanced degree preferred. (SIX) 6+ years of relevant life sciences industry and/or consulting experience, including pharmaceutical, biotechnology, specialty pharma, medical device, patient services, market access, commercial operations, or health technology work. Demonstrated subject matter expertise in Patient Services programs, including strategy, program design, implementation, operations, performance monitoring, or continuous improvement. Working knowledge of CRM-enabled Patient Services operations, technology delivery, data flows, and functional reporting needs; experience translating business and user needs into clear requirements and implementation priorities. (THREE) 3+ years leading project engagements, including planning, scoping, budget development, resource allocation, risk management, and delivery of multiple or complex workstreams within scope, timeline, and budget. (THREE) 3+ years formally managing team performance to achieve project goals and exceed client expectations. Demonstrated success supervising, coaching, and mentoring junior staff, including ownership of feedback and career development. Ability to communicate effectively with business, operations, technology, analytics, vendors, and senior client stakeholders and to translate across functional perspectives. Willingness and ability to travel to client sites across the United States, up to 30 percent of the time. While this role is available remotely, preference will be candidates within 50 miles of a Guidehouse office. What Would Be Nice To Have: Excellent written, oral, facilitation, and executive presentation skills. Expertise in Patient Services CRM or case management platforms, such as Salesforce Health Cloud, Microsoft Dynamics 365, Veeva, or comparable solutions, and with adjacent hub, contact center, workflow, data, or reporting technologies. Experience in developing process maps, business and functional requirements, user stories, test scenarios, training materials, KPI definitions, dashboard requirements, or data governance artifacts. Ability to partner with technology, data, analytics, operations, market access, field reimbursement, legal, compliance, privacy, and vendor teams to deliver Patient Services capabilities. Track record of managing team performance, building inclusive teams, and maintaining high standards for client service and delivery quality. Exceptional client management experience, including expanding relationships and identifying follow-on opportunities within an organization. Success in collaborating with senior leadership partners to pursue add-on, cross-functional, and cross-practice opportunities at external clients. Collaborative skillset, pragmatic, and comfortable operating in complex environments with multiple stakeholders and evolving priorities.

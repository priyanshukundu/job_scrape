About this role Your team BlackRock’s Global Corporate Communications team is part of Corporate Affairs and leads internal and external communications that advance the firm’s purpose of helping more and more people experience financial well-being. The team develops communications and stakeholder engagement strategies that support growth, strengthen BlackRock’s reputation and brand, and contribute to its culture. Your role and impact BlackRock Global Corporate Communications is seeking an Associate, based in New York, to support strategic communications for the firm’s global public markets investment platform. The role supports public markets products and capabilities across active, exchange-traded funds (ETFs), index and cash, including the iShares business, as well as macro and market thought leadership. You will work closely with the Global Public Markets Communications team and business leaders to execute proactive, multi-channel communications campaigns that support commercial outcomes. Your responsibilities In every role at BlackRock, you'll be expected to apply sound judgement and critical thinking to solve complex problems, adapt as the business evolves, and combine the curiosity to explore new approaches and technologies with the rigor to challenge the results. The scope of this role also includes the following responsibilities: Support the execution of proactive, multi-channel communications campaigns that advance the objectives of our public markets investment platform in the U.S. across active, ETFs, index and cash, including our iShares business. Coordinate media interviews and speaking engagements with trained spokespeople across the firm, and support the development of key messages and narratives to brief them in advance. Partner with product, investment and client teams to promote key products and capabilities across our public markets platform. Support communications plans for investor thought leadership, such as the Global Equity Outlook, tailoring messaging for institutional, wealth and consumer audiences. Coordinate the distribution of written communications, including press releases, bylined articles and reports related to the business. Develop and maintain strong relationships across earned media, including leading business and financial media, trade and specialist press, and emerging and digital-first outlets. Collaborate with the global team of Analysts and Associates responsible for monitoring media coverage and industry and geopolitical developments. Your experience 3–5+ years of communications experience in financial services. Understanding of the asset management industry and capital markets, including active and index investment products. Experience engaging with senior executives and navigating complex, global organizations. Strong media relations, verbal and written communication skills, with the ability to translate complex topics into simple, compelling narratives. Strong project management and attention to detail, with the ability to prioritize multiple assignments and adapt in a fast-paced environment.

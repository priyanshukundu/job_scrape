Job Description We are seeking a highly motivated individual to join our team as Associate Director, Quality to be the Compliance and Technical Lead for the external global manufacturing network of Contract Manufacturing Organizations (CMO’s). This role will report to the Director, External Quality. Key responsibilities include overseeing all Quality aspects associated with external manufacturing, testing and release operations while acting as a point of contact for compliance and technical issues. Responsibilities: Serves as the Quality lead for specific CMO’s. Provides compliance and technical leadership/support for OOS investigations associated with manufacturing, process improvement and laboratory issues or failures. Develops/recommends corrective actions. Accountable for resolution of major/critical manufacturing or process improvement or testing issues or Quality Management System (QMS) trends at CMO’s. Escalate issues as needed/required. Provides leadership regarding compliance and technical issues, including resolution for operational review meetings and in support of supplier performance scorecards. Manages tech transfers and phase appropriate qualification/validation of methods and processes into and external of CMO’s. Ensures all processes and methods are validated/qualified, robust prior to transfer. Reviews proposed changes to systems, procedures, methods, and submissions to regulatory agencies as appropriate. Generates CoAs (Certificate of Analysis) for in-process material (e.g. viral vector), reviews batch release package and performs final product for product disposition. Collaborates and maintains routine interactions with Global External Supplier, External Supply Chain, Global Manufacturing Sciences and Technology, Process Development, Analytical Development, and Regulatory. Supports identification, selection, and qualification and on/off-boarding of CMO’s. Write or support review of quality agreements to ensure scope of work, compliance and testing requirements are defined accurately, including metrics, quality deliverables. Supports data gathering, analysis and compilation for Annual Product Reviews (APR) and Annual Vector Reviews (AVR) where applicable. Lead escalation for chronic supplier performance problems and determine when additional controls, suspension, or disqualification are appropriate. Up to 25% travel (both domestic and international) Basic Qualifications: Advanced scientific degree (i.e. MD, PharmD, PhD) and 2+ years’ experience of Compliance and Quality Assurance/Quality Control within Biopharmaceutical or Pharmaceutical manufacturing, OR Master’s Degree and 8+ years’ experience of Compliance and Quality Assurance/Quality Control within Biopharmaceutical or Pharmaceutical manufacturing, OR Bachelor’s Degree and 10+ years’ experience of Compliance and Quality Assurance/Quality Control within Biopharmaceutical or Pharmaceutical manufacturing, OR High School Degree and 14+ years’ experience of Compliance and Quality Assurance/Quality Control within Biopharmaceutical or Pharmaceutical manufacturing Preferred Qualifications: Advanced science degree.

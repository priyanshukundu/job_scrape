Our team is the Compute Service team. We build and operate the systems that schedule and run warehouse-backed work beyond a user’s interactive query: asynchronous and scheduled compute, elastic capacity for parts of live queries, and the scheduling control plane those workloads run on. We balance usability, performance, cost, and reliability for the teams and customers that depend on this compute.



We are actively hiring talented distributed systems developers. This role is an opportunity to shape the architecture and operation of core services that the rest of Snowflake runs on.


AS A SENIOR DISTRIBUTED SYSTEMS ENGINEER AT SNOWFLAKE YOU WILL:

 - Solve real business needs at large scale by applying software engineering and analytical problem solving.

 - Design and implement scalable distributed systems for scheduling, queueing, dispatch, and completion of warehouse-backed work.

 - Analyze fault tolerance, high availability, performance, and scale challenges, and solve them.

 - Build admission control, isolation, and capacity management so shared compute stays fair and predictable.

 - Lead cross-functional initiatives, and collaborate with engineers, product managers, and technical program managers across teams.

 - Judge trade-offs among consistency, durability, latency, and cost, and build systems that meet the demands of rapidly growing services.

 - Ensure operational readiness of the services, and meet commitments to customers on availability and performance.

 - A track record of identifying problems and implementing creative solutions.

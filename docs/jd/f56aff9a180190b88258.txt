Copywriter-Senior Associate

Bring your writing expertise to the J.P. Morgan Commercial & Investment Bank Integrated Marketing team, where you’ll turn complex business and product information into clear, compelling content that connects with clients. You’ll create and shape content across web, video, social, paid media, client stories and product insight articles, bringing strong writing, storytelling and editorial judgment to every assignment. As a Copywriter-Senior Associate on the Commercial & Investment Bank Integrated Marketing team, you will bring a strong foundation in writing, editing and storytelling to turn complex business strategy, product information and subject-matter expertise into clear, compelling, channel-ready content. You will work independently and with cross-functional partners to develop and refine copy, manage assigned projects and uphold brand, legal and compliance standards. You will also use approved generative AI tools to support research, ideation, outlining, drafting and versioning, applying sound editorial judgment and maintaining clear human ownership of the final work. By testing useful applications and sharing effective practices, you will help the team improve speed, flexibility and consistency without compromising quality, accuracy or brand standards. Job Responsibilities Partner with the broader Commercial & Investment Bank team to understand business strategy, audience needs and project goals, then shape them into clear messaging, smart structure and channel-ready copy. Write, edit and proofread content across web pages, social, paid media, video, client stories and product insight articles. Interview subject-matter experts and clients to translate complex financial concepts into benefit-driven copy that supports informed decisions without overpromising. Collaborate with content strategists, designers, animators and channel partners to refine briefs and develop outlines and first drafts, then revise quickly and thoughtfully based on feedback. Use approved generative AI tools to support research, ideation, outlining, drafting, versioning, summarization and optimization while critically reviewing outputs for accuracy, sourcing, originality, brand voice and appropriate handling of firm and client information Serve as an editorial last set of eyes on select deliverables by proofreading, copyediting and strengthening clarity and consistency while preserving the brand voice. Build and apply repeatable, documented AI-enabled workflows that support the team’s responsible use of approved tools. Share tested workflows, templates and examples, including prompting patterns, review checklists and evaluation criteria, and document recommended approaches by channel and use case. Build knowledge of emerging discovery practices, including answer engine optimization and generative engine optimization, and apply relevant practices to digital content when appropriate for the channel and audience. Own assigned projects end to end, manage priorities and contribute to realistic timelines in a fast-paced, cross-functional environment while adhering to brand, legal, compliance, privacy, information security and records management requirements. Required Qualifications, Capabilities and Skills: Bachelor’s degree in marketing, communications, finance or related field, or equivalent professional experience. Five or more years of copywriting, editing or content experience in a highly professional corporate or financial services environment. Portfolio demonstrating strong writing and editing fundamentals, including structure, clarity, editorial judgment and line-level craft, and the ability to communicate client benefits clearly. Demonstrated ability to build a narrative from a brief, outline quickly, interview subject-matter experts and deliver clean copy with minimal editorial rework. Superior command of English, with strong writing, editing, verbal and presentation skills. Demonstrated experience using approved generative AI or AI-enabled writing tools as part of a professional content workflow, including versioning, quality review and structured evaluation of outputs. Ability to write effective prompts, apply repeatable AI-enabled workflows and edit AI-assisted material into accurate, original, brand-appropriate copy with clear human accountability. Sound judgment regarding confidential information, source validation, human review and the appropriate use of AI in a controlled environment. Strong collaboration approach with content strategists, designers, digital, search engine optimization and channel teams. Ability to manage multiple assignments, adapt as priorities shift and work effectively both independently and as part of a team. Preferred Qualifications, Capabilities and Skills Commercial and investment banking experience. Strong understanding of search engine optimization best practices and familiarity with answer engine optimization and generative engine optimization. Highly organized, detail-oriented and proactive approach, with strong analytical and problem-solving skills.

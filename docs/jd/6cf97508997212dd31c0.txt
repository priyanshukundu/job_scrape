What you'll do: As a Capital Markets Quantitative Analyst, you will support the quantitative models, analytical tools, and daily processes used by the Bank's Capital Markets trading desks. You will perform analysis related to the Bank's assets, liabilities, capital, liquidity, and hedging activities while contributing to investment, funding, and risk-management decisions. You will work closely with teams across the organization, including Risk, Finance, Accounting, Sales, Balance Sheet Management, and Information Technology. This role provides the opportunity to combine quantitative analysis, programming, fixed-income knowledge, and business partnership in support of the Bank's balance sheet and broader mission. How you'll make an impact: Directly influence the Bank’s trading and investment strategies by developing quantitative analysis, models, and market insights that support pricing, risk management, portfolio positioning, and execution decisions. Develop a deep understanding of the Bank’s balance sheet and financial strategy, gaining visibility into how funding, liquidity, interest-rate risk, capital markets activity, and portfolio decisions interact across the institution. Serve as a quantitative link between market data and business decisions, translating complex market movements and analytical results into actionable insights for traders, portfolio managers, and senior decision-makers. Build meaningful exposure to leading Wall Street firms and market participants, interacting with dealers, banks, and other counterparties to evaluate market conditions, products, pricing, and emerging opportunities. What you can expect: Design, develop, maintain, and run quantitative tools that support daily Capital Markets processes. Maintain and improve models used by the Bank's trading desks. Assist in developing strategies related to liquidity, funding, hedging, asset acquisition, and balance-sheet management. Monitor risk metrics and prepare reporting to support compliance with management and regulatory guidelines. Participate in the daily pricing process for the Mortgage Partnership Finance cash window. Perform pre-trade analysis and research on mortgage-backed securities, collateralized mortgage obligations, and other fixed-income investments. Prepare model documentation, analytical reports, presentations, and completed trade files. Analyze and interpret financial and market data to support investment and rsk-management decisions. Collaborate with internal partners, including Risk, Accounting, Finance, Sales, Balance Sheet Management, and Information Technology. Communicate with external partners, including brokers, dealers, vendors, and other counterparties. Learn related Capital Markets functions and provide coverage across the team as needed. Perform other duties as assigned.

Job Description: Job Title Software Engineer Corporate Title: Assistant Vice President Location: New York, NY Overview As a Software Engineer in the Client Onboarding Transformation team, you will build and scale the digital platform that simplifies how Corporate Bank clients are onboarded. You will deliver modern, low-friction capabilities within a global engineering organization of more than 150 colleagues, partnering across technology, business, operations, product, and architecture. Your work will reduce manual effort and complexity by advancing automation, unified user experience, workflow orchestration, strategic APIs, and responsible AI solutions. You will help shape an engineering platform that improves both client and colleague experiences across products and regions.

We're seeking someone to join our team as an Americas Workforce Screening Risk & Investigations Director to serve as a senior escalation point in our Investigative Review & Escalation (IRET) team. Your focus will be on background screening results for third-party contingent worker candidates that pose potential reputational and/or operational risk to the Firm. This role will lead the review and adjudication of escalated third-party contingent worker cases and help stakeholders reach decisions that are timely, consistent and defensible. You'll partner closely with Legal and Compliance, Employment Law, Human Resources, Global Supplier Services and business unit stakeholders. In the Corporate Services division, we empower our businesses by creating collaborative workplace solutions and commercial services that enhance the employee and client experience, while optimizing the value of our sourcing and third-party lifecycle to enable the Firm to do-and-win business. What you'll do in the role: Act as the primary escalation point for adverse or incomplete background screening results for third-party contingent worker candidates, including criminal history, misrepresentation, fraud, identity concerns and other reputational risks. Present risk assessments and recommendations to hiring managers, business unit leaders, Legal and Compliance, Employment Law and Human Resources, and help stakeholders reach a timely, documented engagement decision. Apply the Firm's adjudication standards and risk-based framework so third-party contingent worker decisions are consistent across businesses and suppliers, and document any exception or override. Where appropriate, interact with third-party contingent worker candidates undergoing pre-engagement screening, in accordance with the Firm's regulatory and other operational requirements. Prepare review documentation, including interview notes, summaries of findings, risk rationale and final decisions, to support audit and regulatory review. Produce ad hoc, weekly, monthly and quarterly metrics on escalation volumes, cycle times, outcomes and trends, and flag emerging risks to leadership. Identify recurring issues in escalations and recommend changes to policy, procedures or supplier requirements. Interact with counterparts within Firm stakeholder groups, participating in working groups and ad hoc meetings. What you'll bring to the role: Bachelor's degree required. Strong understanding of third-party contingent workforce risk management and third-party due diligence. Experience managing complex stakeholder relationships across business functions and jurisdictions. Proven ability to lead stakeholders to a decision, including when interests compete, timelines are tight or the facts are ambiguous. Ability to assess and communicate risk in a clear, concise and defensible way. Excellent analytical, problem-solving and decision-making skills. Advanced interviewing and conflict resolution skills, with tact, patience and respect. Knowledge of applicable U.S. and Americas screening, privacy and employment laws, including FCRA and fair chance/ban-the-box requirements. Mastery of public record databases and proficiency in Microsoft Office. Working knowledge of AI-enabled tools and automation, and the ability to use them responsibly to improve the speed and quality of screening reviews, while keeping human judgment in final risk decisions. A high degree of integrity, discretion and sound judgment when handling sensitive and confidential information. Capable of multi-tasking, managing time effectively, and adapting to a dynamic organizational environment. Strong executive communication and influencing skills. At least 8 years of experience in workforce screening, investigations, employment law/employee relations, compliance, risk management or a related field.

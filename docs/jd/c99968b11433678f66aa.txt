The Model Risk Oversight team independently evaluates quantitative models used to support market risk, financial forecasting, and regulatory reporting. As a Senior Specialist, Risk Analytics/Modeling, you will contribute to model validations by analyzing data, assumptions, methodologies, and results and helping determine whether models perform as intended and appropriately capture risk. You will apply quantitative analysis, critical thinking, and programming skills to test models, investigate results, and clearly document conclusions. Working collaboratively with experienced model validators, model owners, and other partners, you will develop an understanding of Schwab’s model environment while strengthening your ability to independently conduct validations. Your work will help provide effective challenge and support sound model risk management across the firm. What you have Required Qualifications Advanced degree in a quantitative discipline such as Finance, Financial Engineering, Mathematics, Statistics, Economics, Physics, Engineering, Data Science, Computer Science, or a related field. Experience gained through professional work, internships, academic research, coursework, or projects involving quantitative finance, financial modeling, model development, model validation, quantitative research, data analysis, or independent model testing. Programming and analytical experience using Python and SQL to analyze data, evaluate quantitative results, and support model testing. Knowledge of statistics, econometrics, and quantitative modeling concepts, with the Critical Thinking skills to evaluate model assumptions, methodologies, data, and results. Strong Communication skills, including the ability to clearly explain quantitative concepts and document analytical conclusions in writing. Strong Collaboration skills and the ability to work effectively with model validators, model owners, and other stakeholders. Preferred Qualifications Knowledge of finance or macroeconomics and exposure to interest rates, fixed-income products, yield curves, cash flow modeling, valuation, sensitivity analysis, or stress analysis. Experience or exposure to quantitative models used in market risk, interest rate risk, macroeconomic forecasting, net interest income forecasting, or related financial applications. Experience with R, MATLAB, SAS, or comparable quantitative programming tools. Academic research, thesis, dissertation, or comparable technical writing experience demonstrating the ability to communicate quantitative analysis and conclusions. Adaptability and demonstrated ability to learn new quantitative methods, programming languages, tools, or financial concepts as responsibilities evolve.

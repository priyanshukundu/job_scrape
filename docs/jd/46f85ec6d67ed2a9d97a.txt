The team works on, but is not limited to, testing customer use cases, integration testing, scalability testing, stress and longevity testing. Collaborate with Product Managers and development teams to represent QA in requirements and design discussions. Drive innovation by applying AI-driven ideas and contributing to feature development with a Test Driven Development approach. Automation/Development experience.

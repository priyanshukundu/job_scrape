The Impact You will have in this role : As a Cloud Development Engineering Senior Associate, you will play a key role in ensuring the reliability, stability, and operational excellence of DTCC's production AWS environments. You will help drive automation, strengthen observability, improve incident response, and enhance platform resiliency across cloud and hybrid infrastructure. Through infrastructure-as-code, AI-enabled solutions, and continuous operational improvement, you will help deliver secure, scalable, and highly available technology services that support DTCC's critical business functions. Your Primary Responsibilities : Maintain the alert inventory across CloudWatch, Grafana, and Datadog, ensuring every alert has a documented runbook and defined firing condition. Lead incident troubleshooting and resolution across cloud and hybrid environments. Review Terraform plans, reconcile infrastructure drift, and validate service health following production deployments. Ensure manual changes made during incidents are codified back into the owning repository within established SLAs. Build pipeline gates, policy-as-code controls, and drift detection capabilities to automate routine operational changes. Certify services as operationally ready by executing runbooks and documenting gaps or areas for improvement. Drive problem records to verified closure, eliminating recurring issues related to databases, connectivity, certificate expiration, and capacity management. Identify and escalate security findings to consuming teams before they become operational or security exposures. Develop AI-based solutions for Cloud Operations using agentic coding tools such as Amazon Kiro. Participating in industry training, professional certifications, cross-training opportunities, and GameDay exercises to strengthen operational readiness and technical expertise. **NOTE: The Primary Responsibilities of this role are not limited to the details above. ** Qualifications: Minimum of 6 years of related experience Bachelor's degree preferred or equivalent professional experience Talents Needed for Success: Strong experience supporting AWS production environments, including EC2, Auto Scaling Groups, EKS, S3, RDS, VPC, IAM, and multi-account AWS Organizations. Demonstrated ability to diagnose and resolve complex infrastructure and networking issues, including VPC routing, security groups, network ACLs, DNS resolution, hybrid connectivity, federated access, and credential-related failures. Hands-on experience with Infrastructure as Code (IaC) using Terraform and Terraform Enterprise, including reviewing plan outputs, managing drift remediation, and supporting CI/CD pipelines. Proficiency with Git-based source control practices, including pull request reviews, branch strategies, and artifact promotion across environments. Strong Linux administration skills with experience in process management, system performance, disk and memory management, and networking; Windows Server administration experience is a plus. Experience with configuration management and automation tools such as Chef or Ansible, including golden image management and instance bootstrap methodologies. Proficiency in scripting and automation using Python, Bash, YAML, and JSON, with working knowledge of AI-enabled development tools such as Amazon Kiro, Claude Code, or similar agentic coding platforms. Experience implementing and supporting enterprise observability solutions, including CloudWatch, Datadog, Dynatrace, Grafana, Prometheus, PagerDuty, and ServiceNow. Strong analytical, troubleshooting, and problem-solving skills with a focus on operational excellence and continuous improvement.

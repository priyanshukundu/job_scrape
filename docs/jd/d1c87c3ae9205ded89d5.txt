We're looking for a Principal Governance, Risk and Compliance Analyst to own the bridge between security compliance and engineering work, reporting to the Senior Security and Governance Compliance Manager. The impact you'll make: A Mission Lane cardholder opens the app on a Tuesday night to check her balance and schedule a payment before rent is due. She never sees the safeguards behind that screen, and that's by design. As Principal Security GRC Analyst, you make sure those safeguards are operating correctly and efficiently. You'll take requirements from PCI DSS, SOC 2, SOX, and NIST and turn them into controls that run continuously across GCP, Kubernetes, and deployment pipelines. Evidence comes from the systems themselves, and engineers get guardrails that fit how they already ship. Our GRC practice is still taking shape as we scale, and as a Principal you help define what good looks like: the standards, the workflows in our GRC platform, and the security habits engineering teams carry day to day. Our core tech stack includes: AWS/GCP, Kubernetes, and automated deployment pipelines. About Mission Lane: Founded in December 2018, Mission Lane is a purpose-driven fintech company based in the U.S., with headquarters in Richmond, Virginia. It all started with a realization: nearly fifty percent of the adult population in the U.S. doesn’t have access to a clear line of credit. Most traditional credit card companies either overlook or overcharge this group because they have less-than-perfect credit scores or no scores at all. We decided this just wouldn’t do. In partnership with our sponsor banks, we offer credit cards under the Mission Lane brand name, with better, clearer terms, and a more refined customer experience than the alternatives available to people working hard to improve their credit. To date, over four million consumers have chosen Mission Lane, earning high customer ratings on Credit Karma for its market segment and industry leading Net Promoter scores. Mission Lane has cumulatively raised over $600 million of equity from leading investors, including Invus Opportunities, QED Investors, LL Funds, funds affiliated with Oaktree Capital Management, and other leading investors.

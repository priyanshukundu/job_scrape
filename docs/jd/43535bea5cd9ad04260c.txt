Organizational Readiness Business Analyst II/III
First Citizens Bank
Location: Raleigh, North Carolina
Link: https://jobs.firstcitizens.com/jobs/36821

Overview The Organizational Readiness Business Analyst is responsible for ensuring business partners, frontline teams, and support organizations are aware, educated, and prepared to successfully support changes across Enterprise Card Services products, platforms, processes, and marketing initiatives. This role serves as a strategic liaison between Product, Technology, Operations, Marketing, Compliance, Risk, and Customer Support teams to assess impacts, drive organizational readiness activities, coordinate stakeholder preparation, and ensure successful implementation of business initiatives. The ideal candidate combines strong business analysis, change management, business readiness, communication, and problem-solving skills with the ability to influence stakeholders, manage competing priorities, and execute complex initiatives from planning through implementation. Responsibilities & Qualifications Duties & Responsibilities: Organizational Readiness Strategy & Planning - Lead organizational readiness efforts for product launches, enhancements, and business initiatives by developing and executing readiness plans tailored to project scope and complexity. Identify risks, dependencies, and stakeholder impacts while providing strategic recommendations to support successful implementation. Business Impact Analysis - Assess the impact of changes on customers, employees, processes, systems, and support teams. Partner with stakeholders to identify requirements, readiness needs, and solutions that minimize operational disruption. Stakeholder Preparation & Enablement - Prepare stakeholders for upcoming changes through targeted communications, training, support materials, and rollout planning. Facilitate alignment and consensus across business partners to ensure successful adoption. Communications & Customer Readiness - Develop and coordinate internal and customer-facing communications that support product launches, process changes, and business initiatives. Ensure messaging is clear, timely, accurate, and aligned with regulatory and business requirements. Issue Management & Production Support - Support the triage, investigation, and remediation of implementation and post-launch issues, including customer communications, disclosures, and operational processes. Coordinate with business and support teams to drive resolution and communicate impacts. Documentation & Knowledge Management - Maintain accurate and current product, operational, and customer-facing documentation, including procedures, job aids, manuals, bulletins, and knowledge resources. Ensure required updates are completed and available prior to implementatio Develop and enhance processes that support compliance and audit readiness. Qualifications: Minimum Required Education and Experience: Bachelor's Degree and 3 years' experience OR High School Diploma or GED/Equivalent and 7 years' experience in System Support or Analytics Preferred Qualifications: Bachelor's degree in Business Administration, Communications, Organizational Development, Project Management, Finance, or a related field, or equivalent experience. 2+ years of experience in business analysis, organizational readiness, change management, product implementation, operations, project management, or a related discipline. Proficiency with Microsoft Office Suite, Teams, SharePoint, and collaboration tools. Strong critical thinking, active listening, and analytical skills, with the ability to synthesize information from stakeholder discussions, identify risks and opportunities, connect related business impacts, and develop effective solutions. Exceptional attention to detail and accuracy, a proven ability to insure communications, disclosures, and customer-facing materials are complete and free of errors or inconsistencies. Positive, adaptable, and collaborative mindset with the ability to navigate ambiguity, shifting priorities, and challenges while maintaining strong partnerships and driving results. Excellent written, verbal, presentation, and facilitation skills. Experience developing business communications, training materials, procedures, and support documentation. Strong relationship management and stakeholder engagement skills. Experience supporting consumer and business banking, credit cards, payments, or financial services products. Understanding of change management and organizational readiness methodologies. Knowledge of customer communication and product launch processes.

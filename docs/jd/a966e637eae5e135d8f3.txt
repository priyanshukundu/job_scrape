Join Mizuho as an IT Risk and Control Manager! The ideal candidate serves as the dedicated data and technology risk and control professional supporting IT Data and the Enterprise Data Management Office. The role acts as a trusted advisor to leadership, helping the organization identify, understand, manage, and communicate data and technology risk. The ideal candidate promotes adoption of the Technology Control Framework and its associated risk management activities - risk assessments, issue management, metrics and risk reporting, independent audits, and regulatory requests. Key Responsibilities Risk and Control Advisory Advise technology leadership on technology risk, control requirements, and risk reduction priorities in the data domain focused on topics such as data classification, handling, quality, lineage, retention, privacy, and records management. Translate control requirements into practical expectations for teams responsible for executing and implementing technology (e.g., development teams). Act as a subject-matter expert for relevant policies, standards, and procedures. Support maintenance of the control library, including relevant control descriptions and control attributes. Facilitate required technology risk assessments (e.g., risk and control self-assessments) and control testing activities. Coordinate regulatory, external audit, and internal audit activities such as walkthroughs, evidence collection, and risk treatment discussions. Risk Treatment Promote a culture of risk management by partnering with technology teams to self-identify technology issues. Evaluate potential control gaps and coordinate root causes analysis, escalation, risk treatment, and remediation activities. Support the selection and documentation of appropriate risk-treatment methods and remediation timelines, while considering the risk severity and existing commitments. Govern issue remediation commitments and ensure on-time delivery of action plan commitments. Ensure issue remediation is approximately evidenced and documented, uplifting control procedures when applicable. Metrics and Risk Reporting Monitor key risk and performance indicators against established thresholds and tolerances. Coordinate the establishment or enhancement of measures needed to monitor technology risks. Provide risk commentary, context, and escalation for material trends or persistent threshold breaches.

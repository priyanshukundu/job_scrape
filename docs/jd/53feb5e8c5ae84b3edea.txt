Job Description The Senior Data Engineer is responsible for the design, development, and delivery of scalable data pipelines and data solutions supporting enterprise analytics and data platform initiatives. This role is a highly hands-on individual contributor focused on building reliable, efficient, and maintainable data pipelines across modern data engineering ecosystems. The position operates across cloud platforms, data integration frameworks, and transformation tools to deliver high-quality, production-grade data solutions. The Senior Data Engineer collaborates closely with Data Engineering, Data Platform Engineering, Architecture, and Analytics teams to implement best practices, optimize performance, and ensure consistent delivery of data solutions. Team Culture Join a team where leadership is genuinely invested in your growth—because your potential is our priority. How You'll Spend Your Time You will design and maintain scalable data pipelines and products. You will build data ingestion, transformation, and integration workflows. You will develop scalable data models aligned with business requirements. You will optimize data pipelines for performance, scalability, and cost. You will ensure data quality through testing, validation, and monitoring. You will automate pipeline execution, testing, and deployment processes. You will troubleshoot data issues and mentor junior engineers. Education & Experience Requirements This level of knowledge is normally acquired through completion of a Bachelor’s Degree in a data-centric field (Computer Science, Economics, Information Systems, Data Analytics, etc.) and 8–12 years’ experience with a demonstrated track record of designing and delivering scalable data pipelines, implementing modern data engineering practices, and supporting production-grade data solutions, or equivalent combination of education and experience. Experience designing, building, and supporting scalable data pipelines and integration solutions. Strong knowledge of ETL/ELT processes, data transformation, and modern data engineering practices. Experience with cloud-based data platforms (AWS, Azure, GCP) and distributed data processing technologies. Proficiency with modern data platforms such as Snowflake, Databricks, or similar solutions. Advanced SQL and Python skills for data development, automation, and performance optimization. Knowledge of data modeling, database design, data quality, and monitoring best practices. Experience with CI/CD, version control, and automated testing for data engineering workflows. Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with the ability to troubleshoot complex data issues. Ability to independently deliver high-quality data solutions in a fast-paced environment. Effective collaboration and communication skills, with the ability to translate technical concepts for business and technical stakeholders. Demonstrated ownership, accountability, and commitment to delivering reliable, high-quality data products.

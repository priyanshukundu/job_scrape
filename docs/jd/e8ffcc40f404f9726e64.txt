Job Description: At Regions, the Software Engineer oversees application development, modification, and maintenance of applications. This role monitors system applications, ensures systems run smoothly, and creates reports and other documentation outlining findings/solutions. This position sits within Regions' Technology department. Primary Responsibilities Assists with the development and installation of new hardware and software Installs and configures systems and other software Tests installed software for malfunction detection Identifies and resolves business systems issues Assists with the design and deployment of new applications and enhancements to existing applications, software, and operation systems Measures effectiveness and efficiency of existing systems Monitors and tests system performance Performs routine systems duties Performs daily actions focused on administering defined processes Ensures compliance with risk management programs, rules and regulations, and cybersecurity practices; identifies opportunities for and supports process improvements; applies disciplined change management practices May have work thoroughly reviewed by more senior associates This position is exempt from timekeeping requirements under the Fair Labor Standards Act and is not eligible for overtime pay. Requirements Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Computer Engineering or a related field Skills and Competencies Ability to collaborate with programmers, developers, and other technology professionals to achieve a common objective Ability to conduct system analysis to detect issues with performance Ability to interpret and ensure compliance with applicable rules, regulations, and industry guidance Ability to manage multiple projects simultaneously Knowledge of software testing techniques, code optimization and software debugging Strong communication, analytical and problem-solving skills Additional job detail and preferences for this specific opportunity Experience developing applications using C# and .NET. Experience SQL skills, including query development and troubleshooting with relational databases such as PostgreSQL, SQL Server, or Oracle. Source control using Git. Understanding of SDLC, Agile practices, CI/CD concepts and automated deployment processes. Strong problem-solving and troubleshooting skills. Experience using AI assisted development tools such as GitHub Copilot or similar technologies. Experience with Python development. Experience with ReactJS. Familiarity with Docker and containerized applications. Experience developing and consuming RESTful APIs. Exposure to messaging technologies such as Kafka. Understanding of asynchronous or event driven applications. Exposure to Kubernetes and container orchestration platforms. Experience with AWS cloud services. Familiarity with common software design and integration concepts. Experience developing and maintaining automated tests to support software quality and reliable application delivery.

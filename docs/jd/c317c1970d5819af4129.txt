HOW YOU'LL MAKE IMPACT

Growth Engineering at Airwallex is building an AI-native platform that powers customer acquisition, activation, conversion, retention, and expansion. We're developing scalable infrastructure, intelligent decision systems, and automation capabilities that connect product, sales, and marketing to drive measurable business outcomes.

As a Senior Frontend Engineer, you'll design, build, and own high-performance web experiences and frontend platforms that power Growth at scale. You'll work on everything from customer-facing acquisition and conversion journeys to AI-powered internal tools, combining frontend engineering excellence with rapid experimentation and measurable business impact.

 - Build scalable frontend experiences, including high-performance websites, landing pages, growth funnels, campaign management platforms, and personalized customer journeys using React and TypeScript.

 - Develop AI-native user experiences, building intuitive interfaces and workflows that help teams leverage AI-powered insights, automation, and intelligent decision-making.

 - Own end-to-end technical delivery, independently designing, implementing, testing, deploying, and optimizing complex frontend applications and components.

 - Drive performance and experimentation, improving web performance, SEO, accessibility, and conversion through experimentation, feature flagging, and data-driven iteration.

 - Solve ambiguous business problems, partnering with Product, Design, Marketing, Data Science, and Backend Engineering to translate requirements into intuitive, scalable solutions.

 - Elevate frontend engineering quality through thoughtful architecture, reusable component systems, code reviews, knowledge sharing, and mentoring other engineers.



US: You'll work from our Seattle office three days a week.

LEARN MORE ABOUT YOUR TEAM

Growth at Airwallex is building a unified, AI-native growth system across product, sales, and marketing. Rather than applying AI to isolated tasks, we use customer context, machine learning, automation, and continuous learning to transform how Airwallex acquires, activates, converts, retains, and expands customers.

You’ll build agentic AI platforms, experimentation frameworks, and intelligent decision systems that turn signals—such as company profile, product usage, lifecycle stage, behavior, and intent—into personalized journeys, next-best actions, and measurable incremental value.

The work spans both human + AI and autonomous operating models: AI-native tools that increase the leverage of growth teams, alongside agentic workflows that can research opportunities, launch and optimize campaigns, orchestrate customer journeys, and measure outcomes within clear strategies and guardrails.

The challenge is to combine consumer-technology speed with the precision, trust, and regulatory complexity of global B2B finance. If you enjoy owning ambiguous problems end to end, prototyping quickly, measuring real business impact, and using every outcome to improve the next decision, you’ll help define how an AI-native global financial platform grows.

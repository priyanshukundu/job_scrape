We are seeking a highly motivated and technically skilled Sr Software Engineer to join our Sales & Marketing team. In this role, you will design, develop, support, and modernize software solutions that enable critical Sales and Marketing business processes. A primary focus of this position will be reviewing, refactoring, and enhancing existing C#/.NET code to improve maintainability, performance, scalability, and long-term supportability while reducing technical debt. This role will also be responsible for developing and supporting ETL processes, large-scale data movement initiatives, system integrations, and automation workflows utilizing SQL Server, Jenkins, and modern source control and CI/CD practices. The engineer will play a key role in application modernization efforts, including repository cleanup and migration to GitHub, while helping establish development standards and best practices across the team. Success in this position requires strong technical expertise, a problem-solving mindset, and close collaboration with Product Owners, business stakeholders, and development team to deliver reliable, scalable, and business-focused solutions. The individual will also participate in application support and on-call rotations to help ensure the stability and availability of critical Sales & Marketing systems.

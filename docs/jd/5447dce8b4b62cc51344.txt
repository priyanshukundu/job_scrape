Associate Scientist, Immunology & Inflammation
Regeneron
Location: TARRYTOWN
Link: https://regeneron.wd1.myworkdayjobs.com/Careers/job/TARRYTOWN/Associate-Scientist--Immunology---Inflammation_R52714-1

The role At Regeneron, we use science and innovation to develop life-changing medicines for people with serious diseases. We seek an Associate Scientist for our Immunology and Inflammation group to support therapeutics development for severe immune and inflammatory skin diseases. You will conduct hands-on research in cell biology, immune-focused assays, and disease-relevant experimental models, collaborating across multiple preclinical programs. Contribute to a fast-growing, science-driven organization making a meaningful difference to patients worldwide. When & where Location: Tarrytown, NY Work model/flexibility : Onsite Discover your role: Handle and care for mice, including injections, colony management, and blood/tissue sample collection. Perform tissue dissection and cell isolation from skin and other tissues for downstream analysis. Maintain, expand, and/or treat cells in vitro, using established cell lines and primary human and mouse cells. Perform ex vivo immune profiling using flow cytometry, ELISA, and MSD/Luminex to characterize pharmacodynamic responses and mechanism of action. Analyze data with programs such as FlowJo, Excel, and Prism, producing high-quality datasets and internal reports to support program advancement. Accurately document experimental procedures and results with attention to detail, organization, rigor, and reproducibility. Support daily laboratory operations, including equipment maintenance and ordering supplies and reagents. Adapt to new techniques, shifting tasks, and multiple workflows in a fast-paced, innovative, team-driven environment. This role requires: B.S. in a relevant scientific field with 0–2 years of life-sciences experience; practical laboratory experience is highly valued. Strong organization, attention to detail, scientific rigor and creative problem-solving. Open, frequent communication with your manager and team. Preferred qualifications: Practical familiarity with mouse handling and basic procedures, including injections, colony management, and blood collection. A background in mammalian cell culture using cell lines and primary cells. Experience with immunoassays such as ELISA, MSD, or Luminex is desirable but not required. Tissue dissection, cell isolation, or flow cytometry experience is a plus. Proficiency in basic computer applications and biological data collection and analysis. Does this sound like you?

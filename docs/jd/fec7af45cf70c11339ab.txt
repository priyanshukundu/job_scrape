About The Opportunity As a Senior Procurement Associate for Wonder, you will work collaboratively & cross functionally with the merchant team, forecasting team, warehouse operations & accounts payable. You will own supplier performance and inventory health for a portfolio of raw materials, ensuring they arrive at Wonder production & distribution facilities on time, in specification and at the right cost. You will also serve as a go-to resource and mentor for associates on the team. The Impact You Will Make Own relationships with key supplier partners via e-mail, virtual meetings & direct calls, and lead escalations on shorts, out-of-stocks, substitutions and delivery issues. Sustain proper inventory levels by evaluating/submitting purchase orders & ensuring they are delivered on time; proactively resolve gaps before they reach production. Analyze demand forecasts, inventory positions and vendor fill rates to anticipate shortages, and communicate forecasting changes to supplier partners. Build and maintain reporting on supplier performance (fill rate, on-time delivery, quality, pricing) and drive corrective actions & credits. Monitor shelf life, expiry and minimum shelf life (MSL) on receipt and in inventory; partner with warehouse operations on lot verification, cycle counts and FEFO practices. Evaluate waste and purge reporting at production & distribution facilities, and recommend actions to reduce waste and COGS exposure. Address & rectify quality concerns of raw materials raised by culinary & operational teams. Work cross-functionally to meet launch dates of new raw materials, including sourcing alternates and backup SKUs. Support cost analysis, price and variance reviews, and vendor risk assessments. Train, mentor and back up Procurement Associates; document and improve procurement processes and tools.

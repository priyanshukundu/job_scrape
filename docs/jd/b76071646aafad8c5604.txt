Job Description We aspire to be the premier research-intensive biopharmaceutical company. We're at the forefront of research to deliver innovative health solutions that advance the prevention and treatment of diseases in people and animals. As the Associate Director, Instrumentation and Controls (I&C), you will play a pivotal role in ensuring the successful execution of multiple capital projects related to Instrumentation and Control systems, particularly within the Human Health and our Company Research Laboratories facilities. The Associate Director, Instrumentation and Controls (I&C) plays a pivotal role in ensuring the successful execution of multiple capital projects related to Instrumentation and Control systems, particularly within the Human Health and our Company Research Laboratories facilities. This position supports U.S. projects and travel as required to vendor sites to support testing of equipment. The incumbent acts as a technical subject matter expert, fostering collaboration among various stakeholders, including business leadership, Global Engineering Solutions (GES), Digital Shop Floor Platform teams, and external contractors. Responsibilities Partner closely with local business leaders and project teams to define the scope of instrumentation and control system hardware necessary for capital projects. Critically review and approve electrical and loop drawings for OEM process and utility equipment, including custom equipment skids and stick-built instrumentation designed by Engineering Contractors. Review and approve Instrumentation data sheets and Control System panel drawings. Provide guidance and education to local project teams on automation standards, including technology selection, system configuration, and validation practices. Maintain a close working relationship with GES engineers, Engineering Contractors, skid suppliers, and instrumentation vendors to ensure alignment on project goals and adherence to engineering standards. Actively participate in Factory Acceptance Tests (FATs) at skid suppliers and OEMs,and conduct periodic Quality Assurance (QA) checks during the panel construction phase. Ensure that field grounding checks of DeltaV control panels are conducted prior to the power-up phase. Assist with loop checks and verify drawings during Automation IQ testing as required for certain projects. Engage with project stakeholders at all levels, demonstrating active participation in project team meetings. Ensure that all projects comply with our Company’s engineering standards and guidelines, including industry best practices and regulatory compliance. Qualifications Required Bachelor of Science (BS) in Engineering, Computer Science, or other technical degree Minimum seven (7) years of experience in Instrumentation and Controls (I&C) Ability to travel up to 25%; local and overnights required Technical expertise on DCS and PLC hardware and HMI hardware. Technical expertise in all types of instrumentation used in the pharmaceutical industry. Familiar with system networking. Strong vendor management skills. Excellent problem-solving and troubleshooting skills. Excellent written and oral communication skills. Ability to handle multiple projects simultaneously. Preferred DeltaV hardware expertise. Understanding of programming of PLCs and HMIs. Excellent understanding of cGMP's, SDLC (System Development Life Cycle) methodologies, and Lean/Six Sigma principles. Proven meeting facilitation, project management, and change management skills. Strong presentation skills.

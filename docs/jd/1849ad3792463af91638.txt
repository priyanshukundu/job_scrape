We are looking for a Principal Applied Scientist to join our growing Personalization, Search & Recs team. This Principal will be a key contributor to thought leadership, development and optimization of Expedia’s Search & Personalization AI models including natural language search, multi-modal search, recommendations, and generative AI–based traveler experiences. The ideal candidate for this position is a true deep learning, ML expert who can develop effective AI solutions for search and recommendations. Innovation and developing cutting-edge technology as well as implementing industry-leading solutions are key responsibilities of this role. This is a rare opportunity to build foundational systems in a high-impact strategic area, backed by Expedia’s AI-first vision.

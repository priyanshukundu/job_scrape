Senior Lead Software Engineer The Senior Lead Software Engineer is a hands-on player-coach who partners with business users, product leaders, and technology teams to solve high-value Order Management problems through secure, scalable software. You will work across discovery, solution design, hands-on development, deployment, and adoption while leading and developing a small pod of engineers. This role is ideal for a pragmatic technical leader who can translate ambiguous needs into production-ready applications, integrations, APIs, and data solutions; measure business impact; and create reusable engineering patterns for the broader Order Management and enterprise application landscape. Qualified candidates must be legally authorized to be employed in the United States. The company does not intend to provide sponsorship for employment visa status (e.g., H-1B, TN, etc.) for this employment position.

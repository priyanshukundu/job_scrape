About This Job As a member of the Axos Commercial Banking team, the Sr. Relationship Onboarding Associate will manage the end-to-end onboarding process for new and complex Commercial Banking clients. You will play a vital role to ensure a seamless client account onboarding experience. You will also work closely with clients to capture Treasury Management setup requirements. You will collaborate closely with internal stakeholders including Relationship Managers, Treasury Management Sales Officers, Operations and Compliance to facilitate a smooth transition for clients while adhering to defined service level agreements and ensuring an exceptional client experience. Responsibilities: Manage the end-to-end onboarding process for new Commercial Banking clients, including account setup, documentation and due diligence collection, and capture of Treasury Management services and Online Banking setup requirements Work with clients directly and collaborate with Relationship Managers and Treasury Management Sales Officers to gather client requirements and ensure a thorough understanding of client needs and expectations Conduct due diligence on clients to ensure compliance with regulatory guidelines and internal policies while working closely with Compliance and BSA officers to ensure adherence to anti-money laundering (AML), know-your-customer (KYC), and other regulatory requirements Serve as a primary point of contact for clients during the onboarding process, addressing any inquiries or concerns promptly and professionally Coordinate with internal Operations teams to ensure the timely and accurate setup of accounts, products, and Treasury Management services while ensuring compliance with internal policies and procedures related to client account and Treasury Management onboarding Qualifications: Bachelor’s degree in Business, Finance,

Job Description: We are looking for a skilled Jenkins Developer with strong DevOps experience to implement, maintain, and optimize CI/CD pipelines and automation frameworks. In a typical day as a Systems/ Software Engineer, you would... Design, develop, configure, and maintain Jenkins CI/CD pipelines for application build, testing, deployment, and release automation. Create and maintain Jenkinsfile / Pipeline-as-Code implementations using Declarative or Scripted Pipelines. Troubleshoot and resolve CI/CD pipeline failures, build issues, deployment problems, and performance bottlenecks. Monitor build and deployment pipelines and improve reliability, scalability, and security. Participate in incident resolution, root-cause analysis, and continuous improvement initiatives. What you need to bring: Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Information Systems, or equivalent. Typically 4-6 years experience. Strong hands-on experience with Jenkins and Jenkins Pipeline . Good understanding of CI/CD concepts and DevOps practices . Experience with Groovy for Jenkins Pipeline scripting. Good understanding of CI/CD concepts and DevOps practices . Strong troubleshooting and problem-solving skills. Good communication and collaboration skills. Strong focus on automation, reliability, security, and continuous improvement. Ability to manage multiple priorities in a fast-paced environment.

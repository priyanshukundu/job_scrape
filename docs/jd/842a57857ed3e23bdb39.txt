The Trust & Safety (T&S) Fraud Risk Management organization protects Merchant Services (MS) from fraud and compliance-related losses by providing detection tooling and operating strategy. The Fraud Strategy team serves as the end-to-end operating partner for the MS business: managing fraud rules and loss prevention with business growth, and owning the full fraud lifecycle — onboarding strategy, monitoring, loss analysis, controls, and product launch governance — while embedding fraud considerations early in product design and execution. As a Trust & Safety -Fraud Strategy Analyst-Senior Associate within the Fraud Strategy team, you will play a critical role in identifying and mitigating fraud risks, leveraging your analytical expertise to promote impactful strategies and deliver end-to-end solutions . Job Responsibilities Develop and maintain analytics to provide management with comprehensive insight into emerging fraud trends and account quality. Analyze key performance metrics and profitability drivers to deliver insights across the full account lifecycle. Gain a deep understanding of operational processes (e.g., manual underwriting, portfolio management, collections) to inform performance drivers. Conduct ad hoc analytics and participate in strategic projects representing the Embedded Finance and Solution organization. Collaborate with cross-functional teams to support solution development and implementation. Present findings and recommendations to stakeholders to inform business decisions. Advanced proficiency in SAS, SQL, and spreadsheet tools. Ability to query large datasets and transform raw data into actionable management information.

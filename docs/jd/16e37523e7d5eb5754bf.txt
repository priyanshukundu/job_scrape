Summary: The Associate Director, Technical Product Manager – Medical owns technical product management and IT portfolio delivery for Medical Affairs' customer engagement capabilities, including Medical Customer Interaction Capture & Analysis, Congress Preparation & Operation, and Medical Digital Engagement. This role sets the roadmap, owns the budget and financial outcomes, and leads delivery teams to build and continuously improve these capabilities in partnership with Medical Excellence, Therapeutic Area, Commercialization BI&T, and Content Capabilities teams. This is a hands-on technology leadership role for someone who can move comfortably between senior Medical leaders and engineering teams, combining strong technical judgement with the ability to turn complex business problems into scalable products. The role is also expected to champion an AI-first engineering mindset — embedding AI coding assistants, LLM-augmented development workflows, and automated testing into daily practice — and to build strong partnerships with Medical leadership to drive adoption of new digital capabilities. Key Responsibilities: Technical Product & Engineering Leadership: • Owns technical product direction from problem discovery through architecture, engineering, launch, and continuous improvement. • Partners deeply with engineers, architects, and data teams to shape solution architecture, APIs, integrations, data models, and non-functional requirements. • Challenges technical approaches and makes pragmatic trade-offs across user value, architecture, speed, quality, cost, and scalability. • Rapidly prototypes and tests emerging technologies against real Medical business problems before committing to scale. • Makes informed build, buy, and partner decisions, maximizing reuse of enterprise platforms while avoiding unnecessary complexity. • Uses product telemetry and business outcomes to continuously improve capabilities after launch. AI, Data & Innovation : • Champions an AI-first engineering mindset — embedding AI coding assistants, LLM-augmented development workflows, and automated testing into day-to-day engineering practice to accelerate delivery and raise code quality. • Experiments hands-on with AI/LLM technologies, applying them to real product and engineering problems, and brings the technical judgement to evaluate feasibility, value, risk, and scalability before scaling an approach. • Brings an external view by actively maintaining awareness of external drivers and relevant technology trends in Medical Affairs, bringing new ideas into the portfolio. • Establishes and maintains data products, with an intimate understanding of the data (quality, location, and value) that informs and is generated by business processes. Business Partnership & Product Strategy : • Leads cross-functional teams to generate and execute technology roadmaps. • Leads the prioritization and development of strong, clear business cases for digital capability investments, defining and achieving business outcomes for the highest-value capabilities at the lowest cost. • Collaborates with business leaders within the supported business segment to establish and execute a governance process to prioritize IT initiatives and enhancements. • Builds strong, trusted partnerships with senior Medical Affairs leaders, serving as a recognized thought partner who plans and delivers digital capabilities and owns the BI&T value proposition in the relationship. • Drives business change management planning, execution, and communication to ensure digital capability investments achieve maximum value. Portfolio & Value Management: • Manages the IT portfolio for assigned business functions, including portfolio creation, IT budget forecast, and financial outcome accountability. • Creates and owns appropriate business-aligned implementation plans and roadmaps. • Manages technology-based projects including portfolio analysis, budgeting and planning, tracking and resolving issues, risks and dependencies across projects, programs and functions, executive communications, and resource management. • Prioritizes the portfolio appropriately and is accountable for overall portfolio execution, even when not always directly responsible for delivery. • Maintains IT scorecards for reporting to senior leaders, measuring IT effectiveness as a valued business partner and conveying strategies, initiatives, and key projects to increase value. Matrix Leadership: • Demonstrates matrix leadership by leveraging other functions in the delivery of services to Medical Affairs, such as Core Services, Commercialization BI&T, Content Capabilities, and Data & Analytics. • Creates empowered product teams in which business, product, and engineering jointly discover problems, test solutions, and own outcomes rather than operating through traditional requirements hand-offs. • Builds empowered, trusting partnerships with delivery team members at all levels, including application owners, Cybersecurity, and ITQA. • Shows courage, tenacity, and respect to raise difficult issues and remove roadblocks to value delivery. Qualifications and Experience: • Bachelor's degree required. • 5+ years of experience in technical product management, IT portfolio management, or related technology leadership roles — gained in any industry. • Prior experience as a software engineer, solutions architect, or technical lead, or close hands-on partnership in that capacity, sufficient to engage credibly in technical design discussions and challenge engineering decisions. • Working knowledge of API design and event-driven integration patterns, modern cloud platforms (e.g., AWS, Azure), and enterprise data architecture (e.g., data warehouses, data lakes, platforms such as Databricks). • Familiarity with AI/LLM architecture patterns (e.g., retrieval-augmented generation, agentic workflows, model evaluation) sufficient to assess technical feasibility, risk, and scalability. • Comfortable prototyping directly — in code, notebooks, or low-code tools — rather than only directing others to build. • Demonstrated experience taking technology products from problem discovery and experimentation through engineering, production deployment, adoption, and measurable business outcomes. • Ability to rapidly understand complex business domains and translate them into technology, data, and product opportunities.

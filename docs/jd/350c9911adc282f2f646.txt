In this role, you will have the opportunity to work with our machine learning systems that impacts our ads delivery system, including candidate retrieval, user engagement modeling, and ad experience on Etsy.com and the Etsy app. In addition to expanding our machine learning systems, you will work closely with other machine learning engineers and our product analytics partners to help drive product and customer impact. This is a full time role reporting to the Engineering Manager. What’s this team like at Etsy? Etsy is seeking a Senior Engineer for our Ads Quality team, one of the machine learning engineering teams for Etsy Ads, our onsite ad platform. Etsy Ads is used by our smallest and largest sellers to grow their businesses and it is one of the major revenue streams for Etsy. The Ads Quality team focuses on determining which listings a buyer should be shown using machine learning techniques in order to maximize relevance, seller return, and Etsy revenue. This includes building machine learning systems designed to retrieve the most relevant ads for our buyers across a range of advertising surfaces throughout the buyer's journey. What does the day-to-day look like? Work cross functionally with product engineering teams and communicate your ideas and model capabilities to product managers Apply the latest advances in machine learning to improve the relevance and increase buyer ad engagement Prototype, optimize, and productionize large-scale ML models to improve our ads funnel Conduct A/B experiments to validate the efficiency of ML models and pipelines Work closely with product managers, ML engineers, and analytics partners to measure and improve our ads product Of course, this is just a sample of the kinds of work this role will require! You should assume that your role will encompass other tasks, too, and that your job duties and responsibilities may change from time to time at Etsy's discretion, or otherwise applicable with local law Qualities that will help you thrive in this role are: Must-Haves Solid engineering & coding skills and proficiency in at least one programming language of Python, Scala, Java or C/C++. Experience with writing scalable and performant data pipelines on distributed systems Experience with the ML lifecycle: training, deployment, monitoring, debugging and iterating on production machine learning systems Nice-to-Haves Familiarity with vector databases, retrieval systems, and ANN tuning in production environments, with Vespa experience preferred. Hand-on experience in machine learning frameworks & technologies such as Tensorflow, Pytorch.

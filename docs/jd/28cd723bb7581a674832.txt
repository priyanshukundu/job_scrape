Pharmacy Risk & Underwriting Advisor – Hybrid Role Summary: The Pharmacy Risk & Underwriting Advisor is responsible for developing pricing strategies and managing financial risk across a complex pharmacy book of business. This role partners closely with Sales and other business teams to develop competitive pricing, support customer negotiations, and drive profitable growth and retention. Using pharmacy data, financial modeling, and market insights, you will assess risk, develop account-level pricing strategies, and provide recommendations that influence underwriting, product positioning, and broader business strategy. Responsibilities: Manage an assigned pharmacy book of business with accountability for growth, retention, and financial performance. Assess account-level risk and develop competitive, financially sound pricing strategies. Develop rates and partner with Sales on complex customer pricing and negotiations. Analyze pharmacy data, financial performance, market trends, and competitive dynamics to support underwriting decisions. Develop and monitor account strategies that support profitable growth and retention. Provide recommendations on pricing, product positioning, and underwriting practices. Partner across Sales, Medical Underwriting, Product, Finance, and other key stakeholders. Serve as a pharmacy underwriting subject matter expert. Mentor new underwriters in organization Qualifications: 5+ years of pharmacy underwriting, PBM pricing, healthcare underwriting, or related experience. Experience developing pricing strategies and evaluating financial risk. Strong analytical skills and experience working with large datasets and financial models. Experience using reporting or analytical tools to support pricing and underwriting decisions. Strong knowledge of pharmacy market dynamics, competitive conditions, and pricing drivers. Strong communication skills with demonstrated ability to influence and partner across functions. Bachelor's degree or equivalent relevant work experience.

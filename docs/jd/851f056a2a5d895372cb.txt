Key responsibilities We are looking for an experienced risk professional to join the US First Line of Defense team covering UGM Cross Asset Solutions. The role combines counterparty underwriting, portfolio risk management and business enablement across OTC and listed derivatives. You will assess individual counterparties and transactions while developing a holistic view of risk across products, portfolios and legal entities. • Lead first-line counterparty risk reviews and make risk recommendations, including appropriate mitigation, escalation or challenge. • Partner with the business and control functions to enable complex client activity within the firm's risk appetite. • Monitor portfolios for concentrations, stressed exposures and emerging risks across products and entities. • Assess exposure, stress, margin and collateral risk, including risks that may develop intraday or are not fully captured by traditional metrics. • Help coordinate the first-line response to material counterparty or margin events. • Identify gaps in existing risk frameworks and turn lessons from individual events into scalable controls and monitoring. • Contribute to strategic initiatives across exposure aggregation, limits, models and infrastructure, including identifying opportunities to improve capital and RWA efficiency while maintaining appropriate risk discipline. The team You will work closely with Sales, Trading, Credit Risk Control, Legal, Quantitative Risk Management and Operations to identify emerging risks, manage complex or stressed situations and develop scalable risk solutions. Your skills and experience • Experience in counterparty credit risk, derivatives risk, prime brokerage, financing risk, market risk or a related discipline. • Strong understanding of derivatives and key counterparty risk concepts including exposure, collateral, margin, stress and concentration. • Sound risk judgment and the ability to make decisions with imperfect information. • Ability to move between detailed counterparty analysis and broader portfolio and cross-product risk. • Intellectual curiosity and a willingness to learn, challenge existing approaches and develop expertise across new products and risk topics. • A hands-on, problem-solving mindset, with the willingness to develop new tools and analytics where existing capabilities do not adequately capture the risk • Strong communication and collaboration skills, including the confidence to constructively challenge front-office and senior stakeholders. • You’re curious to explore how AI can improve how we build, deliver, and optimize workflows. You do this with sound judgment – validating outputs and aligning with policies, risk standards, and ethical use. The scope and level of responsibility will be calibrated to experience. More senior candidates will be expected to independently lead complex risk situations, influence senior stakeholders and drive enhancements to first-line risk frameworks and controls.

Job Description: The Opportunity: The Associate Director, Data Strategy & Measurement is a highly collaborative, business-facing leader responsible for helping business partners leverage reporting, measurement, data, and technology solutions to solve complex business challenges and drive informed decision-making. As a member of the Measurement & Reporting team, this role serves as a trusted consultant to Finance, Operations, Distribution, and senior business leaders, partnering to identify opportunities, define business needs, and develop innovative, scalable solutions. The successful candidate combines strong business acumen, consulting skills, and technical knowledge to deliver practical solutions that create business value and improve business outcomes. Working closely with business stakeholders and Technology partners, the Associate Director helps shape data strategy, influence priorities, improve reporting capabilities, and drive continuous improvement across the organization. This role requires an individual who can effectively bridge business and technical teams, bringing both strategic thinking and hands-on problem solving to complex initiatives. What you will do: Business Consulting & Strategic Partnership Serve as a trusted advisor to business leaders, helping identify opportunities to leverage reporting, measurement, data, and technology to improve business performance and decision-making. Partner with stakeholders to understand business challenges, uncover root causes, and develop creative, scalable solutions. Facilitate discovery sessions, requirements gathering, and solution design efforts across multiple business functions. Translate complex business needs into actionable reporting, measurement, automation, and technology solutions. Partner with business leaders to identify opportunities where reporting, technology, automation, and process improvements can simplify work, improve decision-making, and enhance business outcomes. Influence prioritization and investment decisions by helping partners evaluate opportunities, trade-offs, risks, and expected outcomes. Promote a consultative approach that balances business value, technical feasibility, and long-term sustainability. Proactively identify opportunities to enhance reporting, improve processes, and deliver greater value to business partners. Measurement & Reporting Leadership Lead the development and enhancement of reporting, dashboards, measurement frameworks, and business performance solutions. Partner with leaders to define meaningful metrics, KPIs, and measurement approaches that support business objectives. Ensure business leaders have access to reliable, timely, and actionable information. Drive continuous improvement of reporting processes, measurement methodologies, and business insights. Identify opportunities to automate manual reporting activities and improve efficiency. Support executive reporting, strategic initiatives, and complex business problem solving as needed. Provide thought leadership on reporting, measurement, and business performance best practices. Data Strategy & Technology Partnership Partner closely with Technology teams to drive data strategy, reporting modernization, and enterprise data initiatives. Serve as a key business representative on reporting, data, and technology projects. Collaborate on the design and evolution of data platforms, reporting environments, and self-service capabilities. Help prioritize and advocate for business reporting and measurement needs within technology roadmaps. Leverage technical expertise to evaluate business challenges and recommend practical solutions that align business needs with enterprise technology capabilities. Partner effectively with Technology teams while serving as a translator between technical and business stakeholders. Drive User Acceptance Testing (UAT) and support implementation activities to ensure solutions meet business requirements. Promote scalable and sustainable solutions that align with enterprise technology and data strategies. Leadership & Continuous Improvement Serve as a subject matter expert for reporting, measurement, business process improvement, and data strategy initiatives. Build strong partnerships across Finance, Operations, Distribution, Technology, and other business functions. Mentor team members on business problem solving, measurement practices, and solution development. Communicate complex business and technical concepts effectively to both technical and non-technical audiences. Lead and influence cross-functional initiatives that improve business outcomes, reporting capabilities, and organizational effectiveness. Stay current on emerging technologies, industry trends, and best practices to bring innovative ideas and solutions to the organization.

Location: 127 Public Square, Cleveland Ohio Job Summary Supports the organization’s Enterprise Risk Management (ERM) Framework and provides program oversight to ensure risks are identified, assessed, monitored, and managed in a consistent and effective manner across the organization. This role partners closely with First Line of Defense (1LOD) Business Risk Teams and Second Line of Defense (2LOD) Risk Management Subject Matter Experts (SMEs) to provide guidance, analysis, and oversight related to risk management activities and helps drive the continued maturity and effectiveness of the bank’s risk management program. This role leads risk management projects and provides subject matter expertise, oversees risk monitoring and reporting, mentors junior analysts, and ensures compliance with internal control standards. The ideal candidate is a thoughtful, analytical professional who can evaluate complex information, apply sound judgment, identify areas of risk or opportunity, and communicate clearly with a wide range of stakeholders. This individual should be comfortable managing multiple priorities, offering thought leadership, and balancing attention to detail with a broader understanding of business and enterprise risk. Essential Functions Provide oversight and ongoing enhancement of the Enterprise Risk Management (ERM) program, specifically the Risk and Control Self-Assessment (RCSA) process, including methodologies, requirements, and tools Maintain expert-level ERM program knowledge and provide guidance and subject matter support to business units and risk partners on requirements and expectations including consultation and guidance on complex, unique, or non-standard risk management situations, applying enterprise risk management requirements and professional judgement to determine approaches and solutions Review and analyze risks, controls, findings, metrics, risk profiles, and other risk management information to evaluate completeness, accuracy and adequacy, consistency, and quality Conduct quality reviews and provide constructive feedback to improve the quality and effectiveness, and follow through with 1LOD and 2LOD partners to ensure identified exceptions are appropriately addressed and resolved Apply critical thinking and professional judgment to identify potential risks, gaps, trends, inconsistencies, and opportunities for improvement Support risk governance activities, including preparation of materials, analysis, documentation, and follow-up on action items and decisions Support the development, maintenance, and implementation of risk management procedures, guidance, standards, and tools Maintain administrative-level access within the Archer GRC to facilitate record updates, monitor data and content quality, identify areas requiring updates to support the integrity and accuracy of risk management information Develop and generate reports from Archer GRC to support risk management activities, data analysis, quality reviews, monitoring, and management reporting. Assist with regulatory, audit, and internal risk management requests and examinations as needed Analyze risk information and prepare clear, concise materials and reporting for management and other stakeholders Identify opportunities to improve risk management processes through automation, technology, and process enhancements Support the implementation of new or enhanced risk management requirements and help assess the impact of changes across the organization Build and maintain effective working relationships with 1LOD Business Risk Teams, 2LOD SMEs, peers, other risk partners, and other stakeholders across the organization including senior leadership Perform other duties as assigned and participate in projects and initiatives assigned;

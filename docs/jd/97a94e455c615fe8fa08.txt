About Vercel: Vercel is the agentic infrastructure company, freeing people and agents to ship what's next. For more than a decade we've helped builders move from idea to production with speed, security, and exceptional developer experience. Now we're scaling our products for both agents and people to ship and run software, built in the open and trusted by OpenAI, PayPal, Ramp, Supreme, and millions of developers worldwide. About the Role: Vercel started as the home for frontends. The Languages and Runtimes team is making it the home for the rest of the application: APIs, background workers, AI agents, and the services that connect them. This past year we took Python on Vercel to general availability, launched Vercel Services so frontends and backends deploy together as one project, and, alongside our Compute team, helped bring always-on background workers to the platform. Our work spans the path from source code to a running process: tooling that understands an application’s structure, packages its dependencies, and runs it on Vercel, plus language runtimes, SDKs, private service-to-service communication, and production observability. Much of it is making existing applications run efficiently without asking developers to rework them, whether that means taming large dependency sets, cutting cold starts, precompiling Python bytecode at build time, or serving a framework’s static assets straight from the CDN. Next, we’re bringing first-class support to Go and Rust, from builds and packaging to runtime integration and SDKs, and improving observability so developers can see how their services connect and where requests spend their time. You’ll work across these layers, following problems from a developer’s application down to how it builds and executes. What You Will Do: Build and maintain backend services and infrastructure using Python and Rust. Improve serverless Python execution, including dependency management, runtime behavior, startup performance, and resource efficiency. Design distributed systems that support reliable deployment and execution of customer applications and AI workloads. Develop cloud infrastructure and deployment automation using Infrastructure as Code. Investigate performance bottlenecks and production issues across services, runtimes, and infrastructure. Partner with engineering and product teams to turn developer needs into reliable platform capabilities. Own the services you ship, including monitoring, incident response, and participation in an on-call rotation. About You: Experience shipping and operating backend software in production, with strong Python skills and practical experience with Rust. Hands-on experience with cloud infrastructure, such as AWS, and an understanding of compute, networking, storage, and service deployment. Familiarity with distributed systems, concurrency, asynchronous execution, and the tradeoffs involved in building reliable services.

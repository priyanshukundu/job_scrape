Job Description The AI Research Center advances Gilead's mission by developing and applying Artificial Intelligence technologies that accelerate scientific discovery, drug development, and enterprise innovation. As part of Clinical Data Science, the AI Research Center serves as a center of excellence for responsible AI adoption across Research, Development, Clinical Operations, Regulatory, Safety, and enterprise business functions. This role sits within AIx Operations and works closely with ARC product, translational AI, governance, and delivery teams, along with partners across the Development organization. The role will be part of a team that manages contractors and technical delivery partners while remaining hands-on with the technical work. The role helps guide AI researchers, data scientists, machine learning engineers, and contractors responsible for designing, developing, implementing, evaluating, and scaling AI-driven solutions. It requires the ability to move comfortably between product thinking, architecture, code, experimentation, contractor oversight, and production delivery. The role brings a strong product mindset to AI innovation. Success is measured not only by technical innovation, but by trusted AI capabilities that are adopted by scientists and business partners and generate measurable impact. This role is located in Foster City, CA.

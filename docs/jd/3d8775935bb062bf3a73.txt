The Opportunity : As IT Business Analyst - IT Renewals , you will join us in St Paul, MN location as part of the Business & Technology Services (BTS) organization—where you will have the chance to join a fast-paced environment managing hardware and software renewals for the 6 Tower Leads in BTS IS . You will also manage the intake of the budget transfer process coordinating with the Finance leads and Finance management. In addition, this role will drive the tracking and monitoring of KPIs. You will help define and deliver solutions that will shape the future of our organization. What You’ll Work On Renewal Management: · Collaborate with 6 BTS IS Tower Leads and additional business owners to manage $200MM+ contracts and renewals · Assist in the governance of the contract renewal management system (Poseidon) · Partner with Procurement to ensure vendors are providing quotes with the appropriate information, business requirements are met, and contract terms meet the business needs · Maintain vendor relationships · Update process documentation Shopping Carts: · Create all renewal shopping cart requests · Ensure the shopping cart requests are entered correctly · Escalate shopping cart approvals when needed Financial Management: · Align with Finance on each LBE for all BTS IS cost centers · Attend all Business Owner/Finance meetings Minimum Requirements & Qualifications: · Bachelor’s degree or equivalent practical experience in Information Technology, Computer Science, Business, or related discipline. · 2+ years of IT Business Systems Analysis experience handling IT renewal processes. · Proven ability to translate user needs into clear system configurations and enhancement requirements. · Strong capability to convert complex business requirements into actionable solutions. · Proficiency with Microsoft 365 tools, including Excel (advanced functions preferred), PowerPoint, SharePoint, OneDrive, and Teams. · Handson experiences working in ServiceNow, Power BI, Poseidon, Power Apps , etc. · Expertise in business process analysis including understanding of renewal change management processes. · Demonstrated ability to influence and collaborate across cross-functional, matrixed teams and external vendors. · Experience working in geographically distributed or global organization.

Travelers is seeking a Software Engineer II to join our organization as we grow and transform our Technology landscape. Individual will complete advanced end to end engineering tasks for specific system assignments including designing, developing, analyzing, configuring, testing, debugging, troubleshooting, documenting, health monitoring/alerting, and implementing based on user or system specifications, consulting with users to determine hardware, software or system functional specifications, managing interaction between the systems and other technical support areas and defining technical requirements and coordinating team resources to solve problems. What Will You Do? This engineer will contribute to the BSI Cyber Scan platform — a multi-codebase system spanning Elixir services, Python components, JavaScript/React front-end, PostgreSQL databases, and AWS Lambda/Step functions. The platform is central to Travelers' cyber underwriting capability and feeds multiple stakeholder groups: Data Science, Risk Services, Data Platform, Underwriting, Pricing, and RQI Platforms. ​ Per the job requisition, this role is responsible for: Performing analysis, design, development, and configuration functions and defining technical requirements for assignments of intermediate complexity. Lead projects and work with the business. Participating with the team to perform analysis, assessment, and resolution for defects and incidents of intermediate complexity, escalating appropriately. Working within team guidelines to independently tackle well-scoped problems. Seeking opportunities to expand technical knowledge and capabilities.

Your Opportunity At Schwab, you’re empowered to make an impact on your career. Here, innovative thought meets creative problem solving, helping us “challenge the status quo” and transform the finance industry together. Corporate Financial Planning & Analysis provides forecasting and decision support to stakeholders across Schwab, including the Chief Financial Officer, Board of Directors, Investor Relations, and senior business leaders. Within Corporate FP&A, the Revenue Forecasting team develops an enterprise view of revenue and net income across a complex business model influenced by client activity, market conditions, interest rates, asset levels, and other dynamic economic factors. As a Senior Specialist, Finance, you will help develop Schwab’s consolidated net income forecast, analyze revenue components and key business drivers, maintain and enhance financial models, and translate detailed data into insights senior leaders can use to make informed decisions. Your work will contribute directly to understanding Schwab’s future financial picture and identifying the themes, risks, and opportunities shaping performance. This role offers broad exposure across Schwab and the opportunity to deepen your knowledge of financial services, enterprise forecasting, financial modeling, and executive decision support. You will join a collaborative team that values curiosity, thoughtful analysis, continuous improvement, automation, and clear communication. What you’ll do Collaborate with business partners and team members to forecast interest rates, client assets, balances, revenue, and consolidated net income. Develop a strong understanding of Schwab’s business model, revenue streams, and the market, client, and economic factors that influence financial performance. Perform detailed financial and data analysis to identify trends, risks, opportunities, anomalies, and key forecast drivers. Assess business inputs and assumptions used in financial models, challenge inconsistencies, and communicate the potential impact of changes. Prepare recurring and ad hoc analyses of revenue trends, drivers, and performance metrics to support senior management decisions. Maintain, enhance, and document financial forecasting models to improve accuracy, usability, transparency, and scalability. Identify opportunities to automate reporting and modeling processes, strengthen controls, and create efficiencies for the team. Partner across Finance and the business to gather inputs, reconcile results, explain variances, and develop a consolidated view of expected performance. Ensure revenue models and supporting documentation align with applicable Model Risk Oversight standards and governance expectations. Communicate complex financial information through clear analysis, presentation materials, and concise conclusions tailored to the audience. What you have To ensure that we fulfill our promise of “challenging the status quo,” this role has specific qualifications that successful candidates should have. Required qualifications Bachelor’s degree in finance, economics, accounting, business analytics, or a related field, or equivalent relevant professional experience. 2+ years of experience in financial planning and analysis, corporate finance, forecasting, financial modeling, management reporting, accounting, treasury, investment analysis, or a related finance function. Hands-on experience analyzing financial statements, business performance, revenue or expense drivers, and large or complex data sets. Experience building, maintaining, or evaluating financial models, forecasts, budgets, or scenario analyses using clearly documented assumptions. Strong proficiency in Microsoft Excel, including formulas, pivot tables, lookups, data analysis, and financial-modeling techniques, plus proficiency in Microsoft PowerPoint. Working knowledge of finance and accounting principles and the ability to connect operating or market drivers to financial results. Ability to identify key themes and trends, investigate variances, assess the reasonableness of inputs, and convert analysis into clear, decision-useful insights. Strong written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to explain complex financial information to Finance, business, and senior-leadership audiences. Demonstrated ability to build productive relationships, manage competing priorities, exercise sound business judgment, and deliver accurate work in a fast-paced, deadline-driven environment. A proactive, collaborative approach with strong attention to detail and a commitment to continuous improvement. Preferred qualifications Experience in financial services, banking, brokerage, asset management, wealth management, or another business with market-sensitive revenue streams. Understanding of how financial services firms generate revenue, including the effects of interest rates, client cash, asset levels, trading activity, and market performance. Experience developing consolidated forecasts, revenue forecasts, net income forecasts, or executive-level financial reporting. Experience with IBM Planning Analytics or TM1, Oracle Hyperion, Anaplan, Workday Adaptive Planning, or a comparable database, multidimensional planning, or OLAP tool. Experience improving or automating forecasting, reporting, data-validation, or financial-modeling processes. Familiarity with model governance, model documentation, validation, or Model Risk Oversight practices.

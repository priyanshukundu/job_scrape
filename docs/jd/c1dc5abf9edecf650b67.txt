The Role As a Compliance Associate - Fund Financial Reporting, you’ll prepare and review financial statements, shareholder reports, and related regulatory reporting deliverables for Capital Group funds. You’ll coordinate fund audits, review supporting accounting records, and help resolve reporting questions. You’ll also contribute to process improvements that strengthen financial reporting and support scalable growth over the long term. The Team You’ll join the Fund Financial Reporting team, which produces accurate and timely quarterly, semi-annual, and annual financial reports for Capital Group funds. The team works closely with stakeholders to coordinate fund audits and establish accounting policies, ensuring consistency with U.S. Generally Accepted Accounting Principles (U.S. GAAP) and SEC requirements. The team also responds to ad hoc inquiries and oversees offshore resources and vendors. Your Impact Prepare and review mutual fund financial statements, shareholder reports, and related regulatory reporting deliverables to support accurate, timely reporting. Prepare and review workpapers and schedules that support financial statements and fund audits. Review accounting transactions, general ledger accounts, and reconciliations for consistency with U.S. GAAP. Respond to auditor requests and inquiries, coordinating with internal stakeholders to provide complete and accurate information. Coordinate reporting activities with offshore resources and vendors to support consistent delivery. Identify and contribute to process improvements that strengthen financial reporting and support scalable growth. What You’ll Bring Knowledge of U.S. GAAP, financial statement presentation, and financial data reporting. Advanced proficiency with Microsoft Excel and proficiency with Microsoft Office applications. Excellent attention to detail, accuracy, and aptitude for working with numbers. Strong time-management skills and the ability to manage multiple priorities and deadlines. Strong written and verbal communication skills. Ability to collaborate effectively in a team environment. Ability to work independently and take ownership of assigned deliverables. Preferred A bachelor’s degree in Accounting, Finance, or a related field; a CPA is a plus. One to two years of accounting, financial reporting, or audit experience. Knowledge of U.S. GAAP and SEC accounting and reporting requirements applicable to mutual funds and investment companies is a plus. Experience in the mutual fund, ETF, or broader investment management industry, including accounting, financial reporting, or audit functions is a plus.

Title and Summary Software Engineer II The Core Payments department is hiring a highly motivated Software Engineer II with a passion for Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Generative AI technologies. This role is responsible for designing, developing, testing, deploying, and maintaining scalable software solutions that leverage AI capabilities to solve business problems, improve operational efficiency, and create innovative customer experience. The ideal candidate combines strong software engineering fundamentals, system design expertise, and experience building enterprise-grade applications and service with proficient familiarity with AI/ML technologies, cloud platforms, and modern development practices to build production-ready AI-enabled applications. Performance & Reliability Engineering • Monitor and optimize application performance, scalability, and resource utilization. • Identify performance bottlenecks and implement improvements to enhance system efficiency. • Design applications for resiliency, fault tolerance, and high availability. • Support performance testing, capacity planning, and operational readiness activities. Operability & Support • Implement logging, monitoring, alerting, and observability capabilities to support operational excellence. • Troubleshoot and resolve software defects, production issues, and system incidents. • Support deployment automation, release management, and operational support activities. • Contribute to system reliability, maintainability, and continuous service improvement initiatives.

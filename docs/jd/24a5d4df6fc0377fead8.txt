This team collaborates closely with our perception, safety, and systems engineering team to guide the end-to-end functionality of these products. The successful candidate will Initially focus on longitudinal safety systems, which includes software that activates the brakes to avoid collisions (Automatic Emergency Braking), In this capacity, said candidate will help extend the current system into the next generation of products, ensuring that existing systems continue to exceed industry standards.

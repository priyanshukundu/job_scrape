Job Summary: As a Capital Markets Senior Manager based in New York or London, you will work across the full lifecycle of PayPal’s structured credit transactions: from deal inception to structuring and execution and post trade portfolio management. Externally, you will be a key interface for investors, banks, advisers, and rating agencies on structure, pricing, collateral performance and post-closing management. Internally, you will integrate data, market intelligence and analytics to shape the strategy of the Capital Markets function to enable PayPal’s credit businesses to fulfil their economic ambitions. This is a high profile role, so stakeholder management will be key to your success. Job Description: Essential Responsibilities: Develop and articulate clear objectives and implement strategies that deliver results and meet Financial goals Influence decision-making at the senior leadership level Perform the most highly complex credit and lending assessments using knowledge and extensive business expertise Develop and deliver innovative strategies that benefit customers and/or clients for financial success Lead major business projects which impact a business unit or entire function Design, evaluate, and select financial objectives based on thorough understanding of business needs and industry trends Lead others to solve issues of diverse scope Minimum Qualifications: 8+ years relevant experience and a Bachelor’s degree OR Any equivalent combination of education and experience. Additional Responsibilities & Preferred Qualifications : Responsibilities • Deal Structuring & Analysis – Develop and evaluate structuring alternatives for new issuances, extensions and upsizes (e.g. ABS, warehouse, forward-flow and other structures); model collateral performance, advance rates, triggers, and credit enhancement against capital, liquidity and return objectives; and advise leadership on structure, pricing, and investor appetite. • Transaction Execution & Governance – lead due-diligence workstreams, contribute to negotiations and present transactions to internal committees to obtain stakeholder buy-in and ultimately approval. • Post-Execution Portfolio Management – Own weekly/monthly performance reporting, trigger monitoring and incident resolution to preserve balance-sheet resilience; serve as a primary contact for investors, banks and rating agencies on collateral performance and post-closing management. • Global Focus – PayPal’s assets span the world with deals outside of the US as well. This role will require managing multiple time zones and counterparties based outside of the US to achieve successful executions. • Market Intelligence & Valuation – Monitor primary/secondary markets, agency reports and sell-side color to value portfolios and inform pricing, structure and timing of future transactions. • Portfolio Strategy – Support the strategy for PayPal’s on-balance sheet credit assets by using unit economics, asset yields, and customer lifetime value calculations to perform portfolio valuation analysis and inform funding and deal execution decisions. Experience & Skills • Experience of structuring and executing asset-backed finance/ABS transactions • Managing and monitoring ongoing success of outstanding transactions and actively working through issues that might arise. • Ability to build, interpret, and sensitize financial and cash flow models, and to assess structural features such as triggers, waterfalls and credit enhancement. • Ability to interpret transaction legal documents and work effectively with counsel. • Comfort translating financial analysis into narrative for consumption by senior management. • Strong stakeholder-management skills spanning investors, banks, rating agencies, advisers and internal stakeholders. • Proven ability to work with large data sets of loan products using Excel, Python, SQL or other programming languages. • High self-direction and ability to take ownership and work autonomously. • Some experience of US GAAP and/or IFRS three-statement analysis.

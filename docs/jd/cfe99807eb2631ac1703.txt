1. Lead cost management activities on assigned client projects, ensuring delivery of cost planning, estimating, cost control, and reporting requirements. 2. Prepare detailed cost plans, early-stage estimates, and cost models using CostX, and BIM-based quantity extraction and T&T Specific integrated Hive Commission Delivery. 3. Apply the RIBA Plan of Work Stages to structure cost deliverables, design reviews, and commercial reporting throughout the project lifecycle. 4. Manage change control and change management, including assessment, negotiation, documentation, and financial impact analysis of variations. 5. Produce comprehensive cost reports, financial dashboards, and monthly updates using Excel and PowerPoint to support client decision-making. 6. Conduct cost data collection, analysis, and benchmarking to support forecasting, value engineering, and commercial recommendations. 7. Utilize standard methods of measurement (POMI, CESMM, NRM, etc.) to prepare accurate Bills of Quantities and structured cost breakdowns. 8. Support development of procurement strategies and manage tendering processes, including bid evaluations, commercial comparisons, and contractor clarifications. 9. Review and participate with design teams and general contractors in the development and validation of cost estimates. 10. Reconcile design and scope changes during planning phases and assist contractors in maintaining accurate and reliable cost data. 11. Review contractor payment applications, verify progress, and issue payment certificates and recommendations with proper justification and substantiation. 12. Draft and review contract agreements, ensuring clarity on rights, obligations, liabilities, and commercial risk allocation. 13. Ensure compliance with internal quality standards and support participation in ISO audits and quality assurance reviews. 14. Maintain accurate project records, ensuring key cost information, lessons learned, and benchmarking data are captured and uploaded to internal systems. 15. Provide guidance to junior team members, support performance reviews, and promote diversity and inclusion within the project team. RELATED OCCUPATION: Sr. Cost Manager or any other job title performing all listed job duties.

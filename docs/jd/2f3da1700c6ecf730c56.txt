MongoDB’s Application Modernization Platform (AMP) helps organizations modernize legacy applications and move their workloads and associated data from relational databases to MongoDB. Our customers need a dependable way to bring analysis, transformation, testing, and migration capabilities into their modernization workflows. The Agentic Integrations team builds the interfaces that let AMP capabilities be discovered, invoked, and orchestrated from AI agents and development environments. We expose product services through consistent, well-specified tool and API surfaces, keep authentication and execution context correct across every boundary, and make each integration observable and safe to operate. Our work is what makes AMP usable from the assistants and coding agents our customers already run. We are looking for a Software Engineer 3 to build and operate these integrations. You will design tool and API surfaces for AMP services, implement them end to end, and work with product and platform engineers so that they behave predictably when a model calls them. Your impact will be measured by how reliably agents and developers can invoke AMP capabilities, and by how much integration work your components remove for other teams. We are looking to speak to candidates who are based in New York City for our hybrid working model. contracts for each integration, including validation, error semantics, and behavior when a call is malformed or only partially succeeds Propagate authentication, authorization, and tenant context across integration boundaries so that agent-invoked calls carry no more privilege than the user they act for Build the instrumentation needed to observe agent-driven traffic, including structured What success looks like In your first month Build a working understanding of AMP’s architecture, the team’s integration patterns, and the development and testing workflow. Ship small, well-scoped integration fixes and contribute effectively to code reviews. In your first three months Own delivery of an integration for one AMP capability, from interface definition through production rollout. Establish the tests and telemetry that let the team detect and diagnose failures in that integration. In your first six months Take regular ownership of code reviews and participate meaningfully in design reviews for agent-facing interfaces. Independently drive a scoped technical project from investigation through implementation and validation, coordinating with the partner team that consumes it. In your first twelve months Lead the development of a substantial integration or reusable component that other teams adopt. Help mentor new engineers and contribute to the team’s integration standards and technical direction.

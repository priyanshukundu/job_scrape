D.A. Davidson & Co. - the Strength of Advice
Our firm delivers a breadth of quality financial solutions to individual and institutional investors, companies, and government entities. D.A. Davidson Companies provides straightforward, personalized advice, nationally recognized research, and capital market investment banking expertise to clients who need help achieving their strategic financial goals.


D.A. Davidson & Co. is accepting applicants for Experienced Analyst positions in the Investment Banking Diversified Industrials Group. The Analyst will play a critical role working with senior bankers on strategic assignments, assisting clients with mergers and acquisitions, public offerings, private placements and other advisory services. 
 
The Analyst program includes training with Wall Street Prep in the first year, with mentorship from senior bankers and ongoing training to advance their skills throughout the three-year program. After three years, Analysts will have the opportunity to be promoted to an Associate. 
 Responsibilities: Create pitch books, market updates and other client materials. Identify market trends and conduct analyses used in client communications. Communicate with investment banking, sales, trading and research to discuss industry trends and investor feedback. Assist senior bankers in the origination, structuring and execution of capital markets transactions. Perform ad hoc shareholder and trading analyses. Leverage new and existing technologies to re-engineer and streamline internal business processes. Qualifications: Bachelor’s degree with outstanding academic achievement. Currently live in or ability to relocate to New York City metro area. Experience in finance, accounting or consulting, in investment banking, capital markets or trading. Ability to work in a fast paced environment while maintaining accuracy and accountability. Strong quantitative and analytical background with creative problem solving abilities. Excellent verbal and written communications skills, strong work ethic and team oriented. Self-motivated, with the flexibility to work long hours, nights, and weekends, as needed. Excellent knowledge of Microsoft Office (Excel, PowerPoint and Word) is required. A strong interest in the equity capital markets. Energetic with a proactive mentality and desire to participate in helping grow the business.

The Universal Pictures International Finance team plays a key role in supporting the UPI Senior Management Team. It oversees a complex, multinational network of owned operating companies, licensees and joint ventures. The team will partner closely with other HQ functions, all UPI operating companies and corporate groups based in London, Amsterdam and New York. Key Responsibilities: Work closely with our owned operating company finance teams on accounting related queries and drive process improvements and standardization across all territories Support monthly and quarterly closing processes, including journal entry preparation and ownership for areas such as print cost, cash true-up, accruals, etc. Execute reporting processes such as preparing account reconciliations, consolidating and reviewing AR aging and other data requests submitted to Film HQ Controllership Prepare quarterly balance sheet review package and presentation to UPI CFO and Film HQ Controllership team Investigate and facilitate Participation, Comcast internal audit and statutory audit queries Monitor compliance and effectiveness of accounting policies and procedures Support master data management, management of distribution deals and provide ad hoc analysis to non-finance departments as required. Basic Requirements: 2+ years of professional experience in accounting, finance, or related field Bachelor’s Degree in Accounting, Finance or related field such as Economics Desired Characteristics: Excellent Excel and financial analytical skills are essential Knowledge of Business Warehouse / SAP is a significant advantage Strong analytics, problem-solving skills, attention to detail and high standards in quality and accurate reporting Highly organized with the ability to effectively manage multiple competing priorities Demonstrated strong communication skills and a pro-active approach to building working relationships in a matrix environment Media/Entertainment distribution experience is desired Hybrid: This position currently has a hybrid schedule, which requires contributing from the office a minimum of four days per week.

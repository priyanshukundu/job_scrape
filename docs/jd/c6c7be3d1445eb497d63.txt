Work Flexibility: Onsite We are seeking a Staff Embedded Software Engineer to lead the design, development, and documentation of Linux-based embedded software for medical devices. In this role, you will be responsible for delivering high-quality, reliable, and compliant software solutions while adhering to internal software development lifecycle processes and FDA software development guidance. This role requires strong expertise in embedded systems, excellent problem-solving skills, and the ability to influence technical direction while delivering solutions that enhance product quality, reliability, and operational efficiency. What You Will Do Design and develop embedded software and supporting documentation for medical devices in accordance with internal software development lifecycle processes and FDA software development guidance. Collaborate with cross-functional teams, including R&D, Systems Engineering, Quality, Manufacturing, Regulatory Affairs, and Advanced Operations, to ensure effective communication and successful product development. Support legacy products through root-cause investigation, defect resolution, and continuous improvement initiatives. Partner with multidisciplinary teams to plan and execute software verification and validation activities. Conduct advanced problem solving, identify potential solutions, and evaluate them against system and sub-system requirements. Follow and mentor others on R&D procedures like design controls and risk management, per the Quality Management System. What You Will Need (Required Qualifications) Bachelor's degree in Software Engineering, Computer Science or related engineering discipline 4+ years of professional software development experience Experience in C/C++ software development for embedded systems. Experience with embedded Linux build systems such as Yocto. Preferred Qualifications (Strongly Desired) Experience developing embedded software using C/C++ on Linux-based embedded platforms. Experience driving adoption of software lifecycle management and requirements traceability tools and processes. Understanding of embedded communication protocols, including CAN, SPI, I2C, UART, USB, Ethernet, or similar technologies. Experience with HMI/UI frameworks such as Crank Storyboard. Knowledge of static and dynamic analysis tools, memory management, code coverage, and software quality analysis techniques. Knowledge in creating estimates for code implementation time/resource for assigned tasks and projects. Familiarity with regulatory and compliance standards applied to the SDLC (Software Development Life Cycle). Experience using source control systems such as GitHub, Perforce or similar, including branching, merging, code reviews, and release management workflows. Knowledge in build systems and automation tools such as Jenkins, CMake, or equivalent CI/CD frameworks.

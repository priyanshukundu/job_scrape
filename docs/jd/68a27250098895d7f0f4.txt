This leader craves working hands-on in system design and development and leads by example to make sure time sensitive projects get done on time and to specifications. This leader will be a strong advocate for the adoption of generative AI and modern engineering practices to improve developer efficiency, accelerate delivery and raise the overall quality of software development. You will be occasionally requested to commute to our nearest office for in person engagement activities such as team meetings, training and culture events. Essential Responsibilities: Manage a large team of web application developers with direct accountability for performance, engagement, hiring and career development. Set technical direction and execution standards for scalable, secure and maintainable web applications Drive the migration of enterprise workloads from on premise to AWS, ensuring scalability, resiliency, performance, security and operational readiness. Partner with architects, product managers, reliability engineers, quality engineers and security team to deliver robust software applications. Ensure engineering decisions align with non-functional requirements including availability, latency, throughput, fault tolerance and recoverability. Evaluate, build and introduce generative AI tools and workflows to improve engineering efficiency, such as code generation, test creation, documentation, analysis and support automation. Measure and improve team productivity, delivery predictability, code health and platform stability. Provide strong technical direction while balancing delivery and organizational leadership. Create a culture of engineering excellence, ownership and continuous improvement. Build trust across teams through clear communication, technical credibility and execution discipline Ensure compliance with architecture, security and technology standards set by Synchrony. Assist in crafting and maintaining the future state technology architectural strategy, along with our application modernization and migration roadmap. Passionate about the latest technologies, open-source projects and languages Provide 24x7 on-call support periodically throughout the year Identify ways to increase efficiency and improve system performance Work with third party vendors to develop software and/or integrate their software into the company's products Coach and mentor tech leads and dev leads to enhance their technical expertise and leadership abilities. Perform other duties and/or special projects as assigned Qualifications/Requirements: Bachelor’s degree and a minimum of 10 years of experience with software engineering. A minimum of 8 years in engineering banking systems A minimum of 5 years of people management experience Strong hands-on experience with ReactJS, Typescript, JavaScript and enterprise application development Desired Characteristics: Experience in Digital Service Engineering environment Experience designing and operating scalable applications in AWS Experience leading cloud migration or application modernization initiatives Knowledge of distributed systems, service-oriented architecture and cloud native design patterns. Experience with CI/CD, test automation, monitoring and production support. Proven ability to influence technical direction across multiple teams and stakeholders Experience with AWS services like ECS, EKS, Lambda, RDS, S3 and step functions. Excellence in technical communication with technical and non-technical peers Background mentoring other managers or technical leads Strong communication and stakeholder management skills Desire to work in a dynamic, fast paced environment Ability to analyze, use structured problem solving and available tools to troubleshoot systems, identify root cause, action plans, impact and resolution options.

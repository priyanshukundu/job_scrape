[Agentic AI] Senior Data Scientist – Edge AI Platforms & Systems Analytics Description - Senior Data Scientist – Edge AI Platforms & Systems Analytics Job Summary The Commercial Systems Software Engineering organization is developing next-generation AI software platforms that enable intelligent, secure, and scalable experiences across client and edge computing environments. We are seeking an experienced Senior Data Scientist to lead the development of the data and analytics foundation supporting strategic AI software initiatives. This role will be responsible for integrating data from a wide range of software, hardware, AI, telemetry, performance, and business systems, enabling data-driven decision making across engineering, product, and program management organizations. The successful candidate will combine expertise in data engineering, machine learning, analytics, experimentation, and statistical modeling to deliver insights that improve platform performance, software quality, customer experiences, and business outcomes. This role will work closely with software architects, engineering teams, technical program managers, and leadership teams to establish scalable data pipelines, develop predictive models, and create actionable insights that guide technology and investment decisions. Responsibilities Design and implement scalable data architectures that support multiple software platform initiatives. Integrate data across software telemetry, hardware platforms, AI workloads, validation systems, engineering tools, benchmarking environments, and business systems. Develop and maintain reliable data pipelines, data models, transformation processes, and reporting frameworks. Define data governance, quality, lineage, security, and lifecycle management standards. Build machine learning models that identify performance trends, anomalies, optimization opportunities, and operational risks. Apply advanced statistical methods to understand workload behavior, system utilization, software effectiveness, and customer usage patterns. Develop workload characterization models and comparative analyses across different platform configurations and operating conditions. Build predictive models for performance, reliability, capacity planning, and system behavior. Design and execute experiments that validate hypotheses and measure the effect of software or platform changes. Develop executive dashboards, scorecards, and visualizations that provide meaningful visibility into platform health and key business metrics. Partner with engineering teams to translate data-driven insights into product and platform improvements. Present analytical findings and recommendations to technical teams, business leaders, and executive stakeholders. Drive data literacy and analytics best practices across the organization. Mentor engineers and analysts on machine learning, experimentation methodologies, and data science practices.

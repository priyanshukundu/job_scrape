New York Life

Job Requisition ID: 95462 Location Designation: Fully Remote Role Overview Our Agency distribution system is a cornerstone of New York Life, built on trusted relationships between our agents and the clients and communities they serve. The Life Product team plays an important role in helping agents deliver meaningful financial solutions by providing the product expertise, sales consultation, and technical support they need to grow their life insurance business. The Life Product Consultant (LPC) is responsible for driving life insurance sales results across an assigned territory and helping individual General Offices achieve and exceed their life sales objectives. As a trusted resource to agents and field management, the LPC provides consultative sales support, product expertise, case development and design guidance, and technical knowledge to help agents identify opportunities and effectively position life insurance solutions with clients. This is a field-facing role that combines relationship management, life insurance expertise, sales consultation, and territory execution. The LPC works closely with agents and General Office leadership to strengthen life insurance capabilities, introduce product enhancements, support individual cases, and drive sales opportunities across the territory. Location & Territory: The ideal candidate will be located within the West Central Zone. This position primarily supports General Offices across Arkansas, Dallas-Fort Worth, Louisiana, New Orleans, Shreveport, and West Texas and requires significant travel throughout the assigned territory. What You'll Do Drive Territory Sales: Take ownership of life insurance sales objectives across the assigned territory, partnering with General Offices and agents to help achieve and exceed established sales goals. Build Strategic Relationships: Develop and maintain strong, trusted relationships with agents and field management, serving as a knowledgeable resource for life insurance products, sales concepts, and business opportunities. Provide Consultative Sales Support: Work directly with agents to understand client needs, identify opportunities, and provide product and sales guidance that helps move life insurance opportunities forward. Develop and Design Cases: Provide case development and design support, helping agents evaluate client needs and structure appropriate life insurance solutions. Deliver Product Expertise : Develop and maintain a strong command of New York Life's life insurance products and sales processes, translating product and technical information into practical guidance agents can effectively use with clients. Introduce Product Solutions: Educate agents and field management on new products and product enhancements, with particular emphasis on individual and business needs, market opportunities, and relevant tax considerations associated with life insurance products. Present Sales Solutions: Deliver compelling sales concepts, product presentations, and educational sessions to agents and clients, adapting the approach for individual meetings as well as larger group presentations. Engage in the Field: Provide hands-on sales support through face-to-face consultations, presentations, case discussions, and regular engagement with General Offices and agents throughout the assigned territory. Partner with Management: Proactively collaborate with field management to understand local business needs, identify life insurance opportunities, and support sales activity within individual General Offices. What You'll Bring Life Insurance Expertise : Approximately five years of wholesaling experience in life insurance, with broad and comprehensive knowledge of the life insurance business, products, sales process, and dynamics of selling life insurance solutions. Consultative Sales Skills: Demonstrated ability to provide sales guidance, communicate product concepts, and support agents throughout the life insurance sales process. Relationship Management : Proven ability to build and maintain strong business relationships with agents, field management, clients, and other key stakeholders. Communication & Presentation Skills: Excellent verbal and written communication skills, including the ability to explain product and technical concepts clearly and present effectively in both individual and large-group settings. Independent Drive: A motivated, energetic, and results-oriented approach, with the ability to manage territory responsibilities and drive objectives with a high degree of autonomy. Education: Bachelor's degree preferred or an equivalent combination of education and relevant professional experience. Professional Designations : Industry-recognized designations such as CLU, ChFC, or CFP are highly desirable. Licensing & Registrations : Life insurance license and Series 6 or Series 7 and Series 63 registrations. Advanced Markets Knowledge: Understanding of Advanced Markets concepts and their application to life insurance opportunities is considered a plus. Travel Flexibility: Ability and willingness to travel frequently throughout the assigned territory, typically approximately 50% and potentially up to 100% based on business needs. Pay Transparency Salary Range: Overtime eligible: Exempt Discretionary bonus eligible: No Sales bonus eligible: Yes Actual base salary will be determined based on several factors but not limited to individual’s experience, skills, qualifications, and job location. Additionally, employees are eligible for an annual discretionary bonus. In addition to base salary, employees may also be eligible to participate in an incentive program. Please note : This role requires FINRA licensed and/or FINRA Associated Person pre-hire fingerprinting.

A day in the life of a Client Benefits Enrollment Technology Associate involves supporting the implementation, renewal, and ongoing administration of employee benefits enrollment platforms. The associate serves as a resource for client administrators, helping them navigate the system, resolve enrollment or demographic data issues, and complete administration tasks accurately. They work closely with clients, carriers, vendors, payroll providers, HRIS teams, and broker account teams to coordinate file feeds and ensure employee and enrollment data transfers correctly between systems. The role also includes manipulating and analyzing data, preparing reports, researching issues, and communicating findings and recommended solutions to clients and account team members. The associate collaborates with the Benefits Technology team on daily operations and special projects while building strong relationships across internal departments and external partners. They help ensure systems remain effective throughout implementation, renewal, and ongoing service, provide training and guidance to benefit administration teams, and support client administrators with system-related questions. Because the role involves confidential employee and client information, the associate must exercise sound judgment and maintain appropriate data security. Success requires strong analytical and problem-solving skills, proficiency with Microsoft Office, the ability to manage multiple priorities in a fast-paced environment, and flexibility to support client needs, meetings, and special assignments.

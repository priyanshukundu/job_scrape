Regular or Temporary: Regular Language Fluency: English (Required) Work Shift: 1st shift (United States of America) Please review the following job description: JOB SUMMARY The AI Risk Specialist provides independent second line of defense (2LOD) oversight of AI risk, with a particular focus on Generative AI, Agentic AI, and emerging AI technologies. The role requires deep technical expertise to understand AI architectures, capabilities and behaviors; translate technical developments into business and risk implications; identify emerging risks; and strengthen independent assessment, challenge, monitoring, and AI risk strategy. ** This position can be remote for the right candidate** Essential Duties and Responsibilities Following is a summary of the essential functions for this job. Other duties may be performed, both major and minor, which are not mentioned below. Specific activities may change from time to time. 1. Provide independent risk oversight by applying deep technical expertise in Generative AI, Agentic AI, foundational models, and emerging AI technologies to assess AI systems and use cases, including architecture, model capabilities, autonomy, tool and system access, data usage, human oversight, external connectivity, and potential consequences. 2. Provide independent assessment, review, and challenge of the maturity, adequacy, and execution of 1LOD AI risk management across the AI lifecycle, including risk identification and assessment, risk tiering, testing, controls, monitoring and risk detection, human oversight, and escalation. 3. Independently identify and assess emerging AI technologies, behaviors, failure modes, and risk patterns to inform AI risk strategy, oversight priorities, governance expectations, and the evolution of the AI Risk Framework and AI Risk Program. 4. Translate complex AI architectures, capabilities, behaviors, limitations, and emerging developments into clear business and risk implications that strengthen independent challenge and support informed decision-making by senior management and governance bodies. 5. Collaborate closely with business, technology, control functions, and other risk SMEs to bring together diverse perspectives, strengthen the organization's understanding and management of AI risk, and advance effective risk management while maintaining appropriate 2LOD independence. Qualifications Required Qualifications: The requirements listed below are representative of the knowledge, skill and/or ability required. Reasonable accommodations may be made to enable individuals with disabilities to perform the essential functions. 1. Bachelor's Degree or an equivalent combination of education and experience. 2. Five+ years of banking, technology, operations or risk management experience. 3. Experience with AI lifecycle governance and risk assessment methodologies. 4. Knowledge of AI regulatory frameworks and compliance standards. 5. Strong business acumen / knowledge, management experience, problem solving, critical thinking, influencing and decision-making skills. 6. Experience operating independently and navigating ambiguity to deliver value. 7. Excellent interpersonal and communication skills demonstrating the ability to establish credibility with all levels of management effectively. 8. Demonstrated ability to organize and manage complex initiatives and deliver high-quality, executive level work products. Preferred Qualifications: 1..Advanced degree in Computer Science, Data Science, Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Engineering, Risk Management, or a related field. 2. Deep technical knowledge of Generative AI, Agentic AI, foundational models, machine learning, and emerging AI technologies, including their architectures, capabilities, limitations, behaviors, and potential failure modes. 3. Experience providing independent risk oversight and challenge of AI, technology, model, data, or other complex technology risks within financial services or another highly regulated environment. 4. Knowledge of established AI and technology risk management frameworks and standards, such as the NIST AI Risk Management Framework, CRI FS AI RMF, OCC SI Guidance, and other relevant regulatory or industry standards. 5. Experience with risk measurement and monitoring approaches, including development of Key Risk Indicators, thresholds, escalation criteria, and risk reporting and analytics. 6. Experience collaborating across business, technology, risk, and control functions on complex AI or technology risk matters. 7. Relevant professional certifications in AI, technology risk, information security, model risk, or risk management (e.g., CRISC, CISM), or demonstrated equivalent expertise.

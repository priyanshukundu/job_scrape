The Difference You Make: Assist senior team members in the origination, structuring and execution of leveraged finance transactions for non-investment grade corporate and sponsor clients. Perform credit and capital structure analysis to evaluate financing alternatives across bank, institutional loan, private credit and high yield bond markets, incorporating comparable transactions, ratings considerations and market data Contribute to the preparation of client pitch books and other marketing materials to support origination and financing discussions. Assist in evaluating underwriting and syndication risk, including proposed terms, pricing flex, investor appetite and potential balance-sheet exposure. Support credit and underwriting approval processes and the preparation and review of transaction documentation. Support live transaction execution, including preparing launch materials, tracking investor feedback and orders, maintaining book updates, and assisting with pricing, allocation and closing. Coordinate transaction workstreams with industry coverage, origination, sales and trading, credit, legal and operations teams to ensure accuracy and timely execution. Develop relationships with salespeople, traders and institutional investors to support investor engagement and transaction distribution. Monitor leveraged finance market conditions, maintain leveraged loan, high yield and private credit issuance databases, and prepare weekly market updates for internal colleagues and clients.

BIN Sponsorship Credit Risk Manager

Description Summary: The BIN Sponsorship Credit Risk Manager is responsible for evaluating and monitoring credit, settlement, and liquidity risk associated with sponsored issuing and acquiring programs. This role adapts departmental plans into team priorities, oversees due diligence and ongoing risk-management activities, and ensures decisions are consistent with Huntington’s risk appetite, card-network requirements, and established policies. This role partners with program managers, payment processors, Product, Credit, Compliance, Operations, and other risk functions to resolve issues that may affect sponsored programs or the broader department. Duties and Responsibilities: Manage the performance and results of the BIN sponsorship credit risk team, establishing priorities, allocating work, developing colleagues, and monitoring the quality and timeliness of risk decisions. Oversee initial due diligence and onboarding of fintech program managers, payment processors, Payment Service Providers, Independent Sales Organizations, marketplaces, and other sponsored partners by evaluating financial condition, capital and liquidity, ownership, operating models, and risk-management capabilities. Direct credit and risk assessments across issuing models, including expense cards, neobanks, and virtual wallets, and acquiring models, including payment facilitators, processors, marketplaces, and merchant-acquiring programs. Oversee the calculation and monitoring of settlement and liquidity exposure arising from authorization, clearing, funding, chargebacks, returns, and other payment activity, escalating material exposure or deteriorating conditions in accordance with established authority. Manage annual reviews, portfolio renewals, continuous monitoring, velocity and exposure-limit tracking, and risk-based on-site or remote assessments of sponsored programs. Apply and maintain credit-line and collateral practices involving settlement limits, transaction thresholds, rolling reserves, letters of credit, guarantees, and other forms of credit protection. Ensure team activities incorporate applicable Visa, Mastercard, and American Express operating requirements, including network thresholds, reporting obligations, chargeback structures, and potential assessments or fines. Operate within the Three Lines of Defense framework by coordinating with first- and second-line partners, resolving technical and operational issues, implementing corrective actions, and influencing internal or external stakeholders when risk conditions require a change in approach. Performs other duties as assigned. Basic Qualifications: Bachelor’s Degree 7 years of relevant experience in commercial credit, payment risk, BIN sponsorship, card issuing, merchant acquiring, fintech risk management, or a related financial services discipline. undefined Preferred Qualifications: Experience evaluating financial condition, creditworthiness, settlement exposure, liquidity, collateral, or reserves for commercial clients, fintech companies, payment processors, or sponsored programs. Working knowledge of both issuing and acquiring payment models and the risks created throughout the authorization, clearing, settlement, chargeback, and funding processes. Knowledge of Visa, Mastercard, American Express, or comparable card-network operating requirements. Experience working within a first- or second-line risk function and coordinating with business, Compliance, Credit, Operations, Legal, Audit, or other control partners. Demonstrated ability to establish team priorities, address resource and operational challenges, resolve complex issues, and influence stakeholders. Experience managing credit and settlement risk for BIN sponsorship, Banking-as-a-Service, embedded finance, payment facilitation, or fintech partnership programs. Experience assessing Payment Service Providers, Independent Sales Organizations, marketplaces, neobanks, virtual wallets, commercial card programs, or merchant-acquiring relationships. Experience developing or administering credit limits, velocity controls, rolling reserves, letters of credit, guarantees, or collateral requirements. Experience conducting on-site risk assessments, annual program reviews, control testing, or issue-remediation activities. Knowledge of the Three Lines of Defense framework, card-network reporting requirements, chargeback exposure, systemic risk events, and network compliance programs.

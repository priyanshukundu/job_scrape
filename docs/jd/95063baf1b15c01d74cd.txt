Job Description Summary The Software Engineer & Feature Readiness Coordinator is a hybrid position with an expected allocation of approximately 50% software development and 50% feature readiness facilitation. The position develops, debugs, tests, integrates, and maintains product software while also enabling feature owners to complete and commit applicable readiness-for-verification (RFV) checklist items with clear ownership, evidence, and timing. The readiness responsibility does not replace the feature owner or functional subject-matter experts; it establishes execution discipline across Software, Hardware, Systems, V&V, Clinical, Quality, Regulatory, Documentation, DevOps, and Program teams. The allocation may be adjusted temporarily based on program milestones, integration needs, and verification-readiness risk while maintaining meaningful contribution to both responsibilities. tex Job Description Software Development Responsibilities — approximately 50% Duties include (but are not limited to): · Working closely with senior engineers and architects to understand requirements and software development activities. · Implementation of software features based on design specifications and guidance · Contributing to development and maintenance of application software components. · Participating in testing and debugging software to ensure quality and reliability. · Collaborating with global teams to support consistent and reusable software development practices. · Troubleshooting and resolving software defects and performance issues. · Contributing to continuous improvement of development processes and software quality Feature Readiness Facilitation Responsibilities — approximately 50% Duties include (but are not limited to): Own the operating cadence for the RFV checklist across assigned features, including kickoff, periodic reviews, closure reviews, and readiness reporting. Facilitate each feature owner’s commitment to applicable checklist items by confirming accountable owner, due date, completion criteria, planned deliverable, and evidence location. Convert unclear checklist entries into actionable follow-ups by coordinating with the appropriate functional lead while keeping accountability with the designated owner. Maintain an accurate status view for Completed, In Progress, Not Started, Blocked, and Not Applicable items; challenge unsupported N/A decisions and missing evidence. Send reminders, document decisions, track dependencies, and escalate overdue or high-risk items through the agreed program governance path. Prepare cross-functional readiness reviews and ensure decisions, actions, owners, and target dates are captured and followed through closure. Partner with Systems, V&V, Clinical, Quality, Regulatory, Documentation, DevOps, Program teams to coordinate required deliverables and evidence. Provide concise readiness summaries to engineering and program leadership, highlighting open decisions, blockers, aging actions, and risks to feature check-in or verification entry. Identify recurring process gaps and propose improvements to checklist wording, completion criteria, templates, evidence storage, and dashboard visibility.

ABOUT RAMP

Ramp is building the smart infrastructure for finance teams, embedded in the transaction flow of every dollar a business spends. We automate how over $275B in annualized spend flows in and out of 85,000+ companies: authorizing payments, flagging risk, categorizing spend, and closing books.

The problems are high-stakes, data-dense, and unforgiving.

We hire people with high agency and high urgency. We look for slope over intercept. We care less about where you trained and more about what you’ve built. At Ramp, everyone is a builder who owns problems end to end and makes consequential decisions that shape the outcome.

The median Ramp customer saves 7.5% and grows revenue 16% in their first year – far in excess of businesses operating without Ramp. We believe every ambitious company deserves the same.

If you want to build systems that directly shape how companies move and manage billions, Ramp is the place to do it.




ABOUT THE ROLE 

Ramp is building a more intelligent, scalable go-to-market engine. As an Associate on the GTM Operations team, you’ll combine analytical rigor with hands-on process design, systems thinking, and AI-enabled automation to help our GTM teams execute more effectively.

You’ll work directly with GTM leaders and cross-functional partners to identify performance gaps, turn insights into operating improvements, and build repeatable workflows that scale. This is a high-velocity individual contributor role with broad ownership: you’ll define problems, build solutions, and measure their impact.

Your primary focus will be defined by the team you support, with shared standards and close partnership across Strategic Finance, Growth Marketing, Sales, Customer Success, GTM Systems, and Data.




WHAT YOU'LL DO

 - Lead cross-functional projects beyond these core operating areas, taking ambiguous problems through implementation, stakeholder adoption, and impact measurement.

 - Diagnose pipeline, conversion, and rep-execution gaps; partner with leaders to prioritize interventions and measure their impact.

 - Partner with GTM leaders to improve segmentation, account books, and territory design, aligning coverage with customer needs and team capacity.

 - Strengthen attribution definitions, logic, and data quality so leaders can reliably connect GTM activities to outcomes.

 - Partner with Product and Growth to enhance sales tools that improve rep efficiency and coverage.




WHAT YOU NEED

 - 2-4 years of relevant experience in GTM Strategy and Operations, Management Consulting, Investment Banking, or similar analytical role, preferably in a high-growth technology environment

 - Demonstrated ability to turn insights into scalable processes, systems, and automation, owning execution from design through rollout and delivering measurable gains in adoption, productivity, and GTM performance.

 - Exceptional verbal and written communication skills; ability to explain analytical findings, recommend a course of action, and align stakeholders.

 - The ability to prioritize high-leverage work, manage multiple projects, and follow through from a loosely defined problem to a reliable operating solution.




NICE-TO-HAVES

 - Ability to partner across Sales, Finance, Product, and post-sale teams to support multiple GTM motions in a multiproduct business, translating shared priorities into clear decisions, coordinated execution, and measurable results.

 - Familiarity with product-led, usage-based, or consumption-driven GTM motions.

 - Demonstrated ability to drive adoption of new tools and processes through documentation, enablement, and change management.

 - Hands-on experience with Salesforce and a BI platform such as Looker, including familiarity with CRM data structures, funnel reporting, and data-quality controls.

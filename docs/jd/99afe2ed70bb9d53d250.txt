Embedded Solutions is a new and growing business within Great American Insurance Group focused on bringing insurance products to customers through nontraditional purchasing channels. The team develops solutions that allow insurance and related protections to be incorporated into everyday transactions and experiences, including areas such as renters insurance, travel and event protection, vendor liability, guarantees, and other emerging risks. As the business grows, the team is building new products, partnerships, and capabilities designed to create meaningful long-term growth for the organization. Embedded Solutions is hiring an Associate Product Analyst! This role supports the development, implementation, and ongoing management of insurance products within the growing Embedded Solutions business. This is an early-career opportunity for someone who is curious, analytical, detail-oriented, and interested in learning how insurance products move from an idea to the marketplace. The position will initially support products such as renters’ insurance, with opportunities to gain exposure to additional products as the business expands. The role works closely with internal partners to support regulatory filings, product changes, market analysis, and new product development. Great American’s culture is built on connection, shared learning, and strong relationships. To support this, employees in this role are expected to be onsite four days a week, with the flexibility to work one day remotely. Core in‑office days are Monday–Thursday. Essential Job Functions & Responsibilities Support the development, implementation, and ongoing maintenance of new and existing insurance products. Prepare and coordinate regulatory product filings and assist with responses to state insurance departments. Maintain product documentation and implement updates as products, requirements, and business needs evolve. Collaborate with underwriting, actuarial, claims, and other internal partners to support product design, pricing, policy language, and implementation. Conduct market and competitive research to identify industry trends, opportunities, and potential product enhancements. Analyze market information, customer feedback, and other data to support product recommendations and decision-making. Assist with creating and updating product materials, underwriting guidelines, manuals, procedures, and related documentation. Support product strategies and roadmaps that align with business objectives and growth opportunities. Partner with marketing, business development, and insurance operations teams to support product launches and ongoing product management. Build knowledge of insurance products, regulatory requirements, emerging risks, and the embedded insurance marketplace. Contribute to a collaborative, entrepreneurial team environment as the Embedded Solutions business develops and expands. Job Qualifications Bachelor’s degree in business, finance, economics, risk management, liberal arts, or a related field, or equivalent experience. Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with an interest in using data and research to inform decisions. Strong attention to detail with the ability to organize information and manage multiple priorities. Effective written and verbal communication skills with the ability to build productive working relationships. Proficiency with Microsoft Office tools and an interest in using artificial intelligence and emerging technology to improve productivity and problem-solving. Interest in learning insurance products, regulatory requirements, and product development; prior property and casualty insurance experience is helpful but not required.

Job Description The future of money is being built right now. At FIS, we help the world’s businesses, banks and communities move, manage and grow money with confidence. Join a team that values fresh thinking, works together to solve complex challenges, and creates technology that powers everyday life. About the role As a Data Engineer, you will build and support the data pipelines that turn information from many sources into trusted, well-governed data in a regulated financial services environment. Your key responsibilities Design, develop, test and document scalable data pipelines and integration processes using extract, transform and load (ETL), extract, load and transform (ELT), incremental and change data capture (CDC) patterns. Collect, transform and organize data from multiple sources for reporting, analytics, AI and business applications. Support production data pipelines, including monitoring, troubleshooting and resolving incidents, and improve their performance and reliability. Apply data quality, validation, reconciliation, lineage, security and governance standards throughout the data lifecycle. Partner with engineers, analysts and business stakeholders to understand data needs and deliver effective solutions. Participate in design and code reviews. Identify opportunities to automate manual processes and improve data engineering practices. What you bring Required qualifications Experience building, supporting and troubleshooting data pipelines or data integration solutions. Working knowledge of SQL and a programming language commonly used for data engineering, such as Python, Java or Scala. Experience working with relational databases, data warehouses or other large-scale data platforms. Understanding of data modeling, data quality and ETL or ELT processes. Strong communication and collaboration skills, with the ability to work across technical and business teams. Preferred qualifications Experience with cloud data platforms such as Microsoft Azure, Amazon Web Services or Google Cloud. Experience with data pipeline and lakehouse technologies such as Azure Data Factory, Databricks, Apache Spark, Delta Lake or Snowflake. Experience with TypeScript or Node.js for building data integrations, APIs or event-driven services. Experience preparing data for machine learning or generative AI use cases. Experience with workflow orchestration, version control and continuous integration practices. Familiarity with data catalog, metadata management or data observability tools. Experience supporting data solutions in financial services or another regulated industry. The impact you’ll make Your work will give FIS teams reliable data they can trust for analytics, AI and business decisions, reduce manual effort and help deliver better outcomes for customers.

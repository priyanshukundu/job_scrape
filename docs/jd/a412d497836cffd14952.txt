Meet the Team Cisco ThousandEyes is a Digital Experience Assurance platform that empowers organizations to deliver flawless digital experiences across every network – even the ones they don’t own. Powered by AI and an unmatched set of cloud, internet and enterprise network telemetry data, ThousandEyes enables IT teams to proactively detect, diagnose, and remediate issues – before they impact end-user experiences. ThousandEyes is deeply integrated across the entire Cisco technology portfolio and beyond, helping customers deploy at scale while also delivering AI-powered assurance insights within Cisco’s leading Networking, Security, Collaboration, and Observability portfolios. The Account Management team builds the foundational services that manage customer identities, access, entitlements, and contracts across Cisco ThousandEyes. Our work supports secure, reliable customer and internal experiences while providing a platform that other engineering teams can build on. We are a collaborative, technically ambitious team that values ownership, pragmatic delivery, and proactive improvements to architecture, reliability, and developer productivity. Your Impact As a Lead Software Engineer, you will set the technical direction for the Account Management Platform and guide architecture across secure, scalable microservices, high-throughput APIs, and customer-facing web applications. Identify gaps in architecture and operations, then initiate and lead improvements that strengthen reliability, security, scalability, cost efficiency, and developer productivity. Lead high-impact initiatives such as web application migrations, API and workflow modernization, and asynchronous service decoupling. Partner with engineering, product, security, customer success, and other stakeholders to align priorities, communicate complex decisions, and deliver results across organizational boundaries. Mentor software engineers and raise engineering standards so the team can deliver dependable platform capabilities and sustain long-term technical progress.

Job Description Responsible for developing, managing, and retaining full-service relationships with Ascent private banking clients. As the primary daily contact for these clients, this person provides advice, counsel, and high-level customer service to meet the financial needs of clients. Supports standards for managing net portfolio in a manner that ensures compliance with banking regulations, policies and procedures, credit quality, and credit policy requirements. Shared responsibility with, and support of, Ascent Senior Private Banker for soliciting, developing, managing, and retaining client relationships for Wealth Management. Shared responsibility with, and support of, the entire Ascent team, ensuring a consistent and recognized Ascent client experience. While this job does have banking focus, the successful applicant must possess knowledge of investments, trusts, and finance to provide support to those specialty areas. This position requires National Mortgage Licensing System (NMLS) registration under the terms of the S.A.F.E. Act of 2008 and Regulation Z. You will be subject to the required registration process, which includes a criminal background and credit check. Failure to meet or maintain any of the NMLS registration requirements, including maintaining a satisfactory criminal and credit record, may result in a rescission of your offer or termination of employment. This is a hybrid role that will work in the office an average of three days per week.

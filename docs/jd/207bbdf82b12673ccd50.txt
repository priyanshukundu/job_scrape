Meet the Team Isovalent, now part of Cisco, is the company founded by the creators of Cilium and eBPF. Cisco Secure Workload is Cisco's flagship product for workload protection and micro-segmentation, and part of Cisco's Hybrid Mesh Firewall solution. Together, Isovalent and Secure Workload are building the next generation of products for securing workloads and AI infrastructure. Our team turns security intent into enforcement. We take the policy a customer writes and make sure it is applied correctly on every enforcement point, across data centers, public clouds, and Kubernetes. We build with a focus on simplification and secure coding, and we treat quality as an engineering discipline. We welcome candidates from all backgrounds and are committed to an inclusive team where everyone can do their best work. Your Impact In this role, you will provide technical and architectural contributions to our backend services. You will build the software that decides which computers in a company's data center or cloud are allowed to talk to each other, making that rule stick on every endpoint. You will be responsible for: Collecting and processing telemetry at scale from diverse data sources. Reacting to environment changes to render policies efficiently for large numbers of enforcement points. Implementing high-performance API endpoints with minimal latency for large data volumes. Creating technical design documentation, contributing to user documentation, and providing mentorship to peers and junior team members. Debugging and addressing production issues, while contributing new ideas for product innovation and process improvement. Minimum Qualifications Bachelor's degree with 7 years of related experience, Master's with 4 years, PhD with 1 year, or an equivalent combination of education and experience. Experience building software systems in either Go and/or Java. Experience programming against cloud APIs for AWS, Azure, or GCP. Experience with large-scale distributed architecture. Agile experience. Preferred Qualifications Proficiency in computer science fundamentals: algorithms, systems, databases, performance engineering, and security architecture. Experience with large-scale event processing architectures. Schema/data modeling and database design. Systems programming and performance engineering. Experience with Kubernetes and Linux.

About Fitch Group: As a leading, global financial information services provider, Fitch Group delivers vital credit and risk insights, robust data, and dynamic tools to champion more efficient, transparent financial markets. How You’ll Make an Impact: Work as part of an agile team in design, development, and deployment of enterprise-scale web applications. Build an internal portal application using React, micro-frontend, micro-services, and event-driven architecture. Collaborate with DevOps and other squads on the CI/CD pipeline. Provide support on production issues and mentor the team on React and micro-frontend developments. You May be a Good Fit if: You hold a Bachelor’s degree in computer science, Software Engineering, or a related field. You have 5+ years of experience in software engineering. You possess in-depth knowledge of JavaScript, React, Nodejs and Python, C#, .NET You have experience with Webpack, Module Federation, and micro-frontend development. You have experience with relational database technologies such as SQLServer, PostgreSQL. What Would Make You Stand Out: Familiarity with AWS services such as EC2, S3, RDS, EKS, or Elasticsearch. Familiarity with Azure services such as Blob storage, Azure Functions. Experience with Artificial Intelligence (AI) technologies and frameworks.

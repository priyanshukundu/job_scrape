We are looking for a Senior Financial Analyst to join our FP&A team. Reporting to the Finance Manager, FP&A, you’ll primarily own financial tracking and analysis for our hardware programs and R&D functions, working closely with Program Managers and R&D leadership. You’ll build and maintain program-level P&Ls and investment frameworks that inform strategic priorities, track budget vs. actuals, and surface cost variances in real time. You’ll also manage headcount forecasts and spend, support month-end close in partnership with Accounting, and contribute to key deliverables, such as Monthly Financial Results presentations and Board materials. What You Will Do: Help evolve tracking of program-level financials, partnering with Program Managers to track budget vs. actuals on active programs and surfacing cost variances in real time Build and maintain program-level P&Ls and investment frameworks to evaluate whether hardware/R&D programs clear investment hurdles and inform go/no-go decisions Own monthly financial file updates for each assigned R&D function, identifying and explaining cost variances Review and approve purchase orders for assigned functions, ensuring spend is coded correctly and aligns with approved budgets Partner with business leaders to manage the headcount forecast for owned R&D functions, tracking departmental headcount changes and ensuring managers are hiring according to the approved plan Support the month-end close process, partnering closely with Accounting on accuracy of program and functional financials, and provide variance commentary vs. plan/forecast Support the preparation of key monthly and quarterly deliverables, including Monthly Financial Results presentations, Board meeting materials, and function and program-specific reviews Identify opportunities to streamline and automate program and R&D financial reporting processes (e.g., templates, dashboards), enhancing efficiency and accuracy across monthly and quarterly deliverables Drive best practices in reporting and data utilization across the Program Management and R&D finance workflows. Prepare other ad hoc analysis and modeling as needed This is a remote role open to candidates across the US, with a slight preference for those based in the Pacific time zone. What you'll bring 4-6 years of relevant finance experience, experience partnering with Product facing teams is a plus Strong proficiency in Excel/Google Sheets, working with planning tools (e.g., Anaplan, Pigment) preferred Established experience in FP&A or a similar analytical finance role, strong Accounting foundation, and a familiarity with annual planning, forecasting, and reporting Exceptional attention to detail, ensuring accuracy and precision of financial data and resolving discrepancies effectively Ability to work cross-functionally and experience communicating with or presenting to people at all levels of the organization with varying levels of financial literacy, including Program Managers and R&D leadership A self-starter attitude, with a demonstrated ability to operate well with ambiguity and build things from scratch Ability to learn and adapt quickly to new systems and tools High integrity, intellectual curiosity, self awareness, and a self starter attitude Nice to have: Experience in an opex-focused FP&A role Big 4 experience (e.g., Deloitte, EY, PwC, KPMG) Familiarity with hardware build process, especially through a consumer health/wearables or tech lens Benefits At Ōura, we care about you and your well-being.

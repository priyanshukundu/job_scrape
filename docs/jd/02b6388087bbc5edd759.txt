Senior Technical Product Leader, Insurance Risk
Clearwater Analytics
Location: Office - New York
Link: https://clearwateranalytics.wd1.myworkdayjobs.com/Clearwater_Analytics_Careers/job/Office---New-York/Senior-Technical-Product-Leader--Insurance-Risk_R12537-1

As Senior Technical Product Leader for Insurance Risk, you will lead the product managers and engineering teams who build it. You own the roadmap delivery and contribute the commercial outcomes. You will work with the market facing teams on one hand, engineering, data science, and architecture leaders on the other. This role suits someone who can speak credibly about enterprise risk frameworks and make sound calls about calculation engines, data platforms, and AI-enabled workflows. Key Responsibilities: Portfolio vision and strategy Deliver on the multi-year product strategy and roadmap for the insurance risk portfolio, aligned with company goals and market direction. Deliver requirements for regulatory and market change (Solvency II, IFRS 17, NAIC RBC and ORSA, NAIC climate disclosures, DORA, NYDFS, and equivalent regimes) and convert it into product investments. Product leadership Lead, coach, and grow a team of product managers and technical product managers; set clear goals, standards for discovery and delivery, and a strong product culture. Establish consistent planning, prioritization, and decision-making practices across the risk portfolio. Partner with design, engineering, data science, and QA leaders to balance speed, quality, and technical debt. Technical depth Make informed trade-offs on architecture for large-scale simulation, scenario and stochastic modeling, and near-real-time risk aggregation. Define data strategy for ingesting and reconciling policy, claims, exposure, asset, market, and ledger data, including APIs, data lakes, and standard schemas. Ensure model governance, lineage, explainability, auditability, and reproducibility are built into the product, with model risk management expectations in mind (SR 11-7, ORSA, and similar). Guide responsible use of AI and ML in risk workflows, including validation, bias and drift monitoring, and human oversight. SaaS and go-to-market Own the SaaS lifecycle for the portfolio: tenant management, release cadence, SLAs, uptime, observability, and cost to serve. Work with Security and Compliance on SOC 2, ISO 27001, GDPR, data residency, and carrier vendor-risk requirements. Partner with Sales, Marketing, Customer Success, and Implementation on positioning, RFPs, launches, onboarding, and expansion. Communicate roadmap, trade-offs, and results to the executive team and board-level stakeholders. Required Qualifications: 8+ years in product management or product leadership, including 3+ years leading product managers, in enterprise B2B SaaS. Significant experience in insurance, reinsurance, actuarial, or financial risk technology (enterprise risk management, catastrophe or exposure modeling, capital and solvency, market and credit risk, underwriting risk, or regulatory reporting). Strong command of enterprise risk concepts in insurance: risk appetite and limits, economic and regulatory capital, stress and scenario testing, risk aggregation, and emerging risks including climate and cyber. Working knowledge of insurance regulation and accounting frameworks (such as Solvency II, IFRS 17, NAIC RBC, ORSA, and US statutory and GAAP). Solid technical fluency in cloud architecture (AWS, Azure, or GCP), APIs, data engineering, and compute-intensive or distributed workloads. Track record of shipping complex, regulated-industry products and showing measurable commercial results. Ability to influence senior stakeholders, including C-suite buyers and internal executives, and to lead through others. Excellent written and verbal communication and strong analytical judgment. Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Mathematics, Actuarial Science, Finance, or a related field, or equivalent experience. Preferred Qualifications: Advanced degree (MBA, MS in Financial Engineering, or similar) or professional credentials (ASA, FSA, ACAS, FCAS, FRM, PRM, CFA, CERA, or progress toward them). Experience with catastrophe or climate risk analytics, economic scenario generators, or stochastic and Monte Carlo simulation. Hands-on exposure to the insurance risk and actuarial software landscape (for example Moody's, Verisk, Milliman, WTW, SAS, Guidewire ecosystem, or in-house carrier systems). Experience applying AI and ML, including generative AI, to risk, claims, or underwriting workflows in a regulated environment. Experience with data platforms and tools (Snowflake, Databricks, Python, SQL, BI tools). Experience moving customers from on-premise or legacy risk systems to cloud and SaaS delivery. Experience with usage-based pricing, platform and partner ecosystems, or marketplace models.

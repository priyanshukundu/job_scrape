Job Summary Bring your expertise to JPMorgan Chase. As part of Risk Management and Compliance, you are at the center of keeping JPMorgan Chase strong and resilient. You help the firm grow its business in a responsible way by anticipating new and emerging risks and using your expert judgement to solve real-world challenges that impact our company, customers and communities. Our culture in Risk Management and Compliance is all about challenging the status quo and striving to be best in class. As a Senior AI Enablement Associate in Risk Management & Compliance (RM&C), you will help advance the AI enablement strategy through strong portfolio governance, reporting, and execution. You will manage recurring portfolio reporting, maintain consistent and reliable information, analyze portfolio trends and risks, and translate findings into clear dashboards, executive materials, and decision-ready insights. You will work across lines of business, technology, data science, finance, and control teams to improve reporting processes, support governance forums, and strengthen leadership visibility into AI activity, adoption, value, and portfolio performance. This role offers you the opportunity to collaborate with diverse teams, drive meaningful changes, and contribute to the responsible growth and innovation of our firm. Job Responsibilities Own recurring AI portfolio reporting, including use case inventory, financial benefits, expenses, adoption, and key business outcomes. Maintain reporting logic, business rules, and supporting documentation to allow consistent reconciliation across source systems and reporting outputs, investigate discrepancies, and resolve data quality issues with accountable partners. Analyze portfolio trends, risks, gaps, and opportunities, including lifecycle progression, benefit realization, and potential reuse or overlap across use cases. Develop dashboards, scorecards, executive presentations, and written updates that clearly communicate portfolio performance. Support governance forums and leadership reviews through agendas, materials, meeting inputs, action tracking, and timely escalation of reporting or data issues. Improve reporting processes through standardization, automation, documented controls, and practical use of AI and analytics tools.

Job Description What is the opportunity? We're recruiting a Portfolio Manager or Associate Portfolio Manager to join our growing team in Newcastle. This is an exciting opportunity created to support our expansion and build for the future. In this role, you'll manage investment portfolios for high-net-worth individuals, families, and institutional clients. You'll combine investment strategy, client relationship management, and performance analysis to create customised wealth solutions that align with each client's unique objectives. This is a chance to make a real impact on clients' financial wellbeing while developing your career with a leading financial services organisation. This is a permanent, full-time role with 4 days in the office. What will you do? Analyse client aims and needs to construct tailored investment solutions and portfolios that support advisor communications Produce high-quality proposals and analytical information to enable effective client advice and relationship management Deliver ongoing portfolio monitoring and review, making recommendations to ensure continued suitability of investments Review and adapt client portfolios in response to market forces or changing circumstances, leveraging research outputs and collaboration with senior colleagues Build strong rapport with clients and industry professionals; demonstrate effective communication and relationship skills Continuously expand market and portfolio management knowledge across diverse client scenarios and specialisms (e.g., Charities, Trusts) Embody RBC Brewin Dolphin values through working practices, challenge existing processes to improve client experience, and act with integrity to achieve fair outcomes; contribute to business development activities and support team growth while establishing positive, collaborative relationships with colleagues What do you need to succeed? Must-have Broad understanding of portfolio management, with previous experience as a Portfolio Manager or in a Portfolio Management support role under supervision of Portfolio Managers or Wealth Managers Has attained, or studying towards relevant industry qualifications e.g. CISI Level 7 Wealth Management Masters or CFA Adept at data analysis with strong proficiency in Excel and analytical tools If Certified Person - maintain SPS in line with professional body requirements Real team player, comfortable working as part of a team to deliver client outcomes What is in it for you?

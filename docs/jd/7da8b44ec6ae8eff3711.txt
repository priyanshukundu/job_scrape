About ChaseNet ChaseNet is JPMorganChase's proprietary closed-loop payments network that connects Chase-issued cardholders directly with merchants acquired by Chase Merchant Services. By routing eligible transactions through a single Chase-owned rail rather than the traditional open-loop network, ChaseNet simplifies the economics of card acceptance, enables customized pricing constructs, and delivers richer transaction data to both merchants and the firm. ChaseNet supports some of the largest merchants in the United States and plays a central role in Chase's merchant services growth strategy. As a ChaseNet Data Scientist, Associate in the ChaseNet Analytics team, you will work with large and complex payments datasets to generate insights that inform pricing strategy, merchant profitability, portfolio performance, and strategic decision-making.

Job Description Purpose of the role Portfolio Managers within Investing & Lending manage loan exposures in their respective sectors, participate in underwriting diligence processes and act as gatekeepers and voting members within the firm’s capital commitment process. Accountabilities Will manage all new lending and existing exposures in a certain sector(s) Will perform bottoms up fundamental credit analysis and due diligence to aid in structuring/pricing as appropriate all new held exposures for Investment Banking Role includes liaising with the origination deal team, risk, and the sponsor/company to develop a view on credit and structure Analyze the business cases for lending proposals based on the creditworthiness of borrowers and counterparties, in line with the bank’s strategy Evaluate and vote on new capital commitments, amendment requests, and restructuring proposals The role will also include in-depth monitoring of the portfolio’s existing exposures Expected to provide in depth quarterly reviews of “at-risk” names in the sector and report on key sectoral trends Through discussions with traders, manage risk by selling loans and/or developing structured trades Director Expectations To manage a business function, providing significant input to function wide strategic initiatives. Contribute to and influence policy and procedures for the function and plan, manage and consult on multiple complex and critical strategic projects, which may be business wide.. They manage the direction of a large team or sub-function, leading other people managers and embedding a performance culture aligned to the values of the business. Or for an individual contributor, they lead organisation wide projects and act as deep technical expert and thought leader, identifying new ways of working and collaborating cross functionally. They will train, guide and coach less experienced specialists and provide information affecting long term profits, organisational risks and strategic decisions.. Provide expert advice to senior functional management and committees to influence decisions made outside of own function, offering significant input to function wide strategic initiatives. Manage, coordinate and enable resourcing, budgeting and policy creation for a significant sub-function. Escalates breaches of policies / procedure appropriately. Foster and guide compliance, ensure regulations are observed that relevant processes in place to facilitate adherence. Focus on the external environment, regulators, or advocacy groups to both monitor and influence on behalf of Barclays, when appropriate. Demonstrate extensive knowledge of how the function integrates with the business division / Group to achieve the overall business objectives. Maintain broad and comprehensive knowledge of industry theories and practices within own discipline alongside up-to-date relevant sector / functional knowledge, and insight into external market developments / initiatives. Use interpretative thinking and advanced analytical skills to solve problems and design solutions in often complex/ sensitive situations. Exercise management authority to make significant decisions and certain strategic decisions or recommendations within own area. Negotiate with and influence stakeholders at a senior level both internally and externally. Act as principal contact point for key clients and counterparts in other functions/ businesses divisions. Mandated as a spokesperson for the function and business division. All Senior Leaders are expected to demonstrate a clear set of leadership behaviours to create an environment for colleagues to thrive and deliver to a consistently excellent standard. The four LEAD behaviours are: L – Listen and be authentic, E – Energise and inspire, A – Align across the enterprise, D – Develop others. All colleagues will be expected to demonstrate the Barclays Values of Respect, Integrity, Service, Excellence and Stewardship – our moral compass, helping us do what we believe is right. They will also be expected to demonstrate the Barclays Mindset – to Empower, Challenge and Drive – the operating manual for how we behave. Who we’re looking for: We are open to candidates at different stages of their leadership journey. We are particularly interested in candidates who can demonstrate strong credit and portfolio management capabilities, sound commercial judgement, and the potential to grow into broader leadership responsibilities.

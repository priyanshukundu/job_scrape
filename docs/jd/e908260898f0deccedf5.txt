Job Classification: Finance & Accounting - Finance & Accounting Prudential is seeking a customer-focused Billing Case Worker to join our Group Insurance Billing organization. This role is ideal for a motivated professional who can manage complex premium billing and accounting activities while delivering a responsive, industry-leading client experience. Supporting Premier and National Accounts, the Billing Case Worker serves as an end-to-end owner of the billing experience. You will help ensure premium accuracy, timely cash application, effective receivables management, prompt inquiry resolution, and strong financial and customer outcomes. The team You will join the Group Insurance Billing team within Service Operations, who support approximately 3,000 active customers and nearly $6 billion in premium. The team plays a critical role in premium administration, financial accuracy, accounting health, and the overall customer experience. The role You will manage an assigned book of business across the billing lifecycle, including premium billing, cash application, account reconciliation, inquiry resolution, and performance objectives related to accounts receivable, suspense, and quality. Success requires financial acumen, disciplined follow-through, sound judgment, clear communication, and a strong customer and control mindset. Key responsibilities Own day-to-day billing relationships for assigned complex clients, overseeing premium activity, outstanding balances, account status, and timely follow-up. Process and validate accounting transactions, including distributions, disbursements, vouchers, and debit and credit memos, in accordance with established controls. Reconcile account activity, identify discrepancies, and resolve complex billing, cash application, receivables, and suspense issues in partnership with internal and external stakeholders. Although most of the work is through email communications, you will be required to also lead professional on-camera discussions with clients and brokers, explaining account activity, clarifying actions, and driving issues to resolution. Monitor recurring payment issues and account trends, document insights, and contribute to root cause analysis, control enhancements, and process improvement. Analyze year-end statements to support ERISA-related reporting and customer needs. Guide new and existing customers on Prudential’s billing practices, payment expectations, and available service channels. Meet service, quality, and account management expectations by following established procedures, maintaining accurate documentation, and applying industry-aligned billing practices. Qualifications 2–3 years of experience in accounting, premium billing, accounts receivable, cash application, or a related financial operations discipline; general ledger and Group Insurance knowledge are preferred. High school diploma or equivalent required; bachelor’s degree preferred. Ability to manage competing priorities, service commitments, and detailed financial work in a fast-paced, evolving environment. Strong written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to explain complex billing matters clearly to clients, brokers, distribution partners, and internal stakeholders. Strong attention to detail, analytical thinking, and the ability to make progress through ambiguity. Proficiency in Microsoft Office, particularly Excel, with the ability to pass a skills-based Excel assessment. Ability to investigate and resolve questions from brokers, clients, and distribution partners with minimal guidance. Strong time management, organization, relationship management, and follow-through.

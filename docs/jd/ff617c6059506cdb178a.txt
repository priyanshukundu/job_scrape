Job Description The role of Associate Director, Global Manufacturing Communications (U.S. Southern Site Cluster) is a unique opportunity to join our Company, a global healthcare leader, as a key member of the Global Corporate Affairs and Manufacturing teams. The role will support the creation and implementation of multi-media internal and external communications across site, product, supply, crisis and enterprise priorities. The role requires a 360-degree communicator who can connect manufacturing operations, supply chain management and reputation considerations to clear stakeholder messaging, site-level communications strategies, plans, and pull-through execution for our manufacturing sites in Elkton (VA), Durham (NC), and Wilson (NC), comprising approximately 2,500 employees. The candidate selected for this role will report to our Manufacturing Division Site Communications Director, U.S., and work in a fast-paced and ever-changing environment. This hybrid role requires onsite presence 2-3 days a week, with up to 50% travel between sites. Primary Responsibilities: Integrated communications: Develop and deliver an integrated communications and editorial strategy that supports divisional, site and regional priorities and connects site performance to the enterprise narrative to reach both wired and non-wired employees. Show how manufacturing performance, supply continuity, product milestones and operational readiness affect patients, customers, employees and our Company’s reputation, and measure reach and engagement with key audiences. Site storytelling and transformation: Identify, develop and support unique storylines, change management initiatives and workforce engagement opportunities that showcase site achievements and supports organizational transformation. Leadership counsel and preparation: Advise site leadership on communications and develop supporting strategies, programs and materials. Prepare site leaders for message delivery, media engagement and speaking opportunities (events, town halls, local announcements, and community, government and industry engagements), partnering with policy and media teams as needed. Campaigns, social media and AI integration: Lead multi-channel campaigns across site employee channels, reaching both wired and non-wired colleagues. Integrate approved AI tools into content development, insight generation and workflows, maintaining human oversight, accuracy, confidentiality and required approvals. Content and review management: Manage a high volume of materials (briefings, talking points, site newsletters, digital screens, core response documents) through appropriate review processes, building collaborative stakeholder relationships. Risk and crisis communications: Reduce risk exposure by educating employees on policies, escalating reputational risks and partnering with divisional communications to assess and prioritize actions. Anticipate issues, build scenario-based response plans, and develop core messages and leader talking points for rapid response to quality, safety, supply, business continuity, activism, media or other reputationally sensitive matters. Required Qualifications, Experience and Skills: Bachelor’s degree in communications, journalism, life sciences, or a related field, with 8 or more years of communications experience. Proven strategic, 360-degree communicator with experience developing and executing internal and external communications plans spanning employee engagement, executive communications, media preparedness, product and supply communications, change management and stakeholder issues, including site-level organizational shifts. Experience supporting a site launch or operational milestone is highly desirable. Deep understanding of the biopharmaceutical industry and manufacturing landscape, with sharp business acumen and insight into how product strategy, supply reliability, quality expectations, regulatory requirements and external stakeholder perceptions shape communications strategy. Strong executive presence and the ability to influence and advise senior leaders, providing long-term strategic counsel, handling crisis communications and delivering short-term tactics that reduce organizational risk exposure. Outstanding verbal and written skills, including translating scientific, business or technical information into compelling communications, with proficiency in social and digital channels, familiarity with approved AI tools and awareness of communications trends. Excellent project management skills and keen attention to detail, including moving communications through review under tight deadlines and working with public relations agencies and project budgets. Proven ability to balance multiple stakeholders and competing priorities across sites, prioritize high-impact work, and establish and maintain clear boundaries and realistic expectations. Demonstrated issues management experience, including identifying emerging issues, assessing reputational risk and coordinating timely, aligned responses with site leadership and cross-functional partners. Exceptional interpersonal and people skills, with the ability to quickly build trusted relationships with site leaders, colleagues and partners across multiple sites. Collaborative team player who works independently with minimal supervision and within a highly matrixed organization, and thrives in a fast-paced, high-visibility role. Preferred Experience and Skills: Experience supporting R&D, manufacturing or complex operational communications, including directly advising site leadership. Experience at a global, publicly traded pharmaceutical, healthcare or biotechnology company, with an understanding of how manufacturing operates across a global enterprise network. Creative and passionate professional with experience developing and implementing communications programs for both wired and non-wired workforces, including labor relations communications. Proven media engagement experience, including building ongoing relationships with local trade and news media, and experience owning communications budgets and leading agency relationships independently.

The role leads design of data modeling, physical schema properties, triggers, and stored procedures and contributes developing architecture and design of data management solutions, and implements database structures to support efficient and scalable software applications. The role identifies, diagnoses, and recommends solutions independently to software development issues, demonstrates advanced project management skills, contributing to company's and software development department's performance and goals.

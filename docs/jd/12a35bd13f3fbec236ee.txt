Job Description Summary Join the team developing the industry leading next generation of CT scanners. You will bring innovative CT image reconstruction algorithms from research into production, using modern GPU and multicore computing technologies to optimize image quality and performance. You will also develop software platform components that enable algorithm and application development across the image reconstruction subsystem. Job Description Every CT image a radiologist interprets passes through our software. We transform raw detector measurements into clinically meaningful images that help physicians diagnose disease and make critical patient care decisions. Our work sits at the intersection of software engineering, applied mathematics, high-performance computing, and medical-device development. We don't expect expertise in all these areas on day one. We are looking for engineers who are curious, learn quickly, take ownership, and enjoy solving difficult multidisciplinary problems. In this role you will: Take reconstruction algorithms from research prototypes to production software running on high-performance GPU and multicore CPU platforms. Design and deliver reliable, maintainable, and well-tested software for a medical-device environment. Investigate image-quality and performance problems across algorithm, software, hardware, and system boundaries. Develop and improve software platforms supporting CT reconstruction algorithms and applications. Collaborate across engineering disciplines, take ownership of complex problems, and drive solutions through completion. Required Qualifications: Bachelor's degree in computer science, engineering, a related STEM field, or equivalent. A minimum of 3 years of professional software development experience. Strong analytical, mathematical, and problem-solving skills. Strong C++ development skills in Linux environments, with experience using Git and CI/CD. Experience with, or demonstrated ability to quickly learn, high-performance and GPU-accelerated computing. Ability to independently learn complex systems, exercise sound technical judgment, and deliver solutions to challenging problems. Desired Characteristics We are seeking engineers who demonstrate exceptional potential through curiosity, ownership, learning agility, and technical excellence. Strong intellectual curiosity and a desire to understand systems deeply, not just use them. Demonstrated ability to learn new technologies, tools, and domains quickly and independently. Self-motivated and resourceful in solving difficult, ambiguous problems with limited direction. Takes ownership of problems, drives solutions to completion, and continuously seeks better approaches. Comfortable contributing across the software development lifecycle, from requirements and design through implementation, testing, optimization, and support. Strong systems thinking, with the ability to understand complex interactions across software, algorithms, and hardware. Actively seeks feedback, learns from mistakes, and continuously improves. Collaborative team player who strengthens the team through knowledge sharing, constructive discussion, and technical leadership. Demonstrates sound technical judgment, balancing innovation, quality, and delivery. What Success Looks Like Become proficient in the team's C++, Linux, CI/CD, and GPU-accelerated development environment. Build enough CT reconstruction and system knowledge to contribute to technical design decisions. Deliver maintainable, well-tested software that meets medical-device quality expectations. Deliver features, algorithm improvements, or performance optimizations that become part of released CT products.

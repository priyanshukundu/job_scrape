Overview This is a hybrid role with the expectation that time working will regularly take place inside and outside of a company office. The Escrow Sales Professional is responsible for originating, developing, and managing corporate escrow opportunities across a broad range of transaction types, including mergers and acquisitions, asset purchases, debt issuances, capital markets transactions, infrastructure projects, and other complex commercial arrangements. The role focuses exclusively on corporate and institutional escrow solutions and partners closely with attorneys, investment bankers, private equity firms, corporate treasury teams, and financial sponsors, to deliver customized escrow structures that mitigate risk and facilitate transaction execution. The successful candidate will be responsible for driving new business revenue, managing a pipeline of opportunities, and serving as a trusted advisor on escrow products and transaction-related cash management solutions. Responsibilities & Qualifications Business Development & Sales • Develop and execute a strategic sales plan to grow corporate escrow revenues and market share. • Originate new escrow opportunities from corporations, law firms, private equity sponsors, investment banks, financial institutions, and other transaction intermediaries. • Build and maintain a robust pipeline of new corporate escrow mandates. • Identify opportunities to expand existing client relationships through additional escrow and related treasury solutions. • Conduct client meetings, presentations, and educational sessions regarding escrow structures, capabilities, and risk mitigation benefits. • Negotiate escrow fee arrangements and oversee pricing discussions. Relationship Management • Establish and maintain strong relationships with key decision-makers, including: o Corporate treasury and finance executives o General counsel and legal departments o External law firms o Investment bankers o Private equity firms o Financial sponsors and lenders • Serve as the primary point of contact during business development and transaction structuring discussions. • Maintain regular communication with existing clients to identify future transaction opportunities. Transaction Structuring & Advisory • Consult with clients and advisors on escrow structures designed to support complex corporate transactions. • Review transaction requirements and collaborate with legal counsel and internal escrow to ensure escrow agreements align with operational capabilities and risk standards. • Provide guidance on escrow mechanics, funding requirements, disbursement processes, and contingency provisions. • Coordinate with Escrow Team to facilitate successful transaction onboarding and execution.[CD1.1] Risk and Compliance Management • Ensure all transactions comply with applicable regulatory, risk, and internal policy requirements. • Partner with KYC, AML, compliance, and legal teams throughout client onboarding and transaction review processes as needed. • Maintain accurate documentation and records related to escrow transactions and sales activities. • Adhere to established sales practices, governance standards, and operational controls. Market Leadership • Monitor industry trends, transaction activity, and competitive developments within the corporate escrow market. • Represent the organization at industry conferences, legal forums, financial sponsor events, and transaction-focused networking opportunities. • Contribute to product development initiatives by providing market feedback and identifying emerging client needs.

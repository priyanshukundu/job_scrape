Role Overview The Capital Markets Senior Associate supports the company's funding strategy and helps ensure it has the capital, liquidity, and financing flexibility to execute its business plan. You will build and maintain the financial models that drive funding decisions, run the structuring and execution of debt facilities, and turn complex analysis into clear materials for executive leadership, the board, and investors. This is a high-visibility role for someone who combines strong modeling skills with sound judgment and clear communication. Key Responsibilities Financial Modeling and Analysis Develop and maintain complex financial models to forecast funding capacity, liquidity, capital adequacy, covenant compliance, and cost of funds Evaluate how key business decisions, such as growth targets, product changes, credit policy shifts, and pricing, affect funding capacity, funding costs, capital, liquidity, and covenant headroom Conduct capital markets modeling using tools such as Excel, SQL, or Python Analyze collateral performance, advance rates, concentration limits, and borrowing base availability Support asset-liability management, including interest rate hedging analysis and ongoing monitoring of hedge programs Capital Markets Execution Runs the structuring, execution, and management of debt facilities, including securitizations, warehouse facilities, forward-flow arrangements, unsecured debt, and new financing transactions Coordinate with legal counsel, rating agencies, banks, trustees, investors, and other external parties throughout the transaction lifecycle Prepare term sheet comparisons, pricing analyses, and structural alternatives to inform decision-making Benchmark peer transactions, pricing, and performance, and recommend improvements to funding facilities Understand credit agreement terms and verify their accuracy Executive Communication and Reporting Synthesize complex analysis into clear, compelling presentations and reports for executive leadership and the board Prepare materials for corporate board meetings, including funding updates, liquidity and capital positions, and transaction recommendations Develop investor and lender presentations that clearly communicate the company's financial position, funding strategy, and credit story Present findings and recommendations to senior stakeholders and respond to follow-up questions Cross-Functional Collaboration Partner with Finance, Accounting, Credit Risk, Legal, and Business teams to align funding strategy with business plans and forecasts Ensure data integrity and consistency between internal financial reporting and external lender and investor reporting Contribute to process improvements and automation of recurring

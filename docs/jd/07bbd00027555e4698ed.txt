Blackstone Credit & Insurance (“BXCI”) - Liquid Credit Strategies (“LCS”) Research, Credit Analyst, Principal Employer: Blackstone Business Unit: Blackstone Credit & Insurance Blackstone Credit & Insurance (“BXCI”) is one of the world’s leading credit investors. Our investments span the credit markets, including private investment grade, asset based lending, public investment grade and high yield, sustainable resources, infrastructure debt, collateralized loan obligations, direct lending and opportunistic credit. We seek to generate attractive risk-adjusted returns for institutional and individual investors by offering companies capital needed to strengthen and grow their businesses. BXCI is also a leading provider of investment management services for insurers, helping those companies better deliver for policyholders through our world-class capabilities in investment grade private credit. The Liquid Credit Strategies Team (“LCS”) manages $127B in AUM across diversified portfolios of fixed income investments, primarily consisting of senior secured floating rate bank loans, high yield and investment grade corporate bonds, and debt and equity tranches of Collateralized Loan Obligations ("CLOs"). The group manages and sub-advises a wide array of funds and accounts in the US and Europe, including CLOs, separately managed accounts, commingled funds, and registered funds. Job Description: The LCS credit analyst is an integral part of the investment team. The credit analyst will conduct fundamental credit analysis to identify attractive investment ideas and proactively communicate recommendations to Portfolio Managers. The credit analyst will have the opportunity to work with a wide range of industries and investment types. As a result of the breadth of the experience, the credit analyst will develop a unique understanding of companies and capital structures. Responsibilities include: • Evaluate new investments in the Primary and Secondary Markets performing thorough analysis of the Borrowers’ business, industry drivers, inherent credit risks, and valuation/recovery prospects. • Formulate a clear and concise credit view in order to persuasively communicate recommendations to the Investment Committee (including CLOs, SMAs, BDCs, and Closed End Funds) both verbally and in writing. • Diligently monitor companies in the assigned industry segments including recent news, quarterly/annual earnings announcements, ratings changes, and legal requests. • Update financial models for the assigned portfolio companies on a quarterly basis. Qualifications: Blackstone seeks to hire individuals who are highly motivated, intelligent, and have demonstrated excellence in prior endeavors as well as: • 6+ years of relevant work experience, ideally with a corporate credit, public or private equity asset manager, sell-side corporate credit or equity investment research, or an investment bank in an advisory, capital markets or restructuring group.

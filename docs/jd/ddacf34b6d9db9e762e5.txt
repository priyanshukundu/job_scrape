If you're eager to gain hands-on experience in a company that values fresh perspectives and continuous improvement, we'd love to hear from you. www.berkleyheathcare.com Responsibilities The AVP, Underwriter is responsible for the production, underwriting and profitable growth of Berkley Healthcare Financial Lines’ Large and Complex Enterprise (LCE) portfolio, including Directors and Officers Liability, Employment Practices Liability, Fiduciary Liability, and Fidelity/Crime coverages for large (>$1B revenue) and complex private and not-for-profit healthcare provider organizations. This position is responsible for handling new and renewal business, including primary and multi-layer excess placements, throughout the underwriting process from submission review through policy issuance. The role develops and maintains strong broker relationships, applies advanced underwriting judgment and technical expertise, and collaborates across functional areas to expand market penetration, enhance brand recognition, and achieve profitable growth objectives. Key Functions will include but not be limited to: · Determine risk acceptability in accordance with underwriting risk appetite and guidelines · Prepare thorough underwriting analyses with detailed exposure analysis and independent underwriter assessment · Make pricing, coverage, terms and conditions determinations and recommendations within defined limits/authority and underwriting guidelines and authority parameters · Develop, grow and maintain a profitable, diversified book of business aligned with company goals · Ensure production and loss ratio goals are achieved for assigned book by reviewing, analyzing and evaluating information and taking appropriate action as necessary · Develop positive agency relationships within assigned territory while maintaining a good rapport through responsive levels of service · Travel nationally up to 20% of time · Coordinate marketing activities including travel in coordination with BHC colleagues including the BHC Distribution Lead · Participate in group or one-on-one insured meetings (in-person and virtual) and attend industry conferences and events · Foster growth of less experienced staff in a positive and growth-oriented environment · Actively self-educate as to line of business, industry and insurance trends including exposures, claims, legal, legislative, healthcare, current events, self-improvement and innovation · Contribute to innovation initiatives that improve underwriting, distribution, operational effectiveness, and the overall customer experience Qualifications Education BA/BS/BBA degree from an accredited university or college Qualifications Minimum 5 years of recent experience in financial lines underwriting , with a proven track record of success. Specialized experience in healthcare financial lines underwriting is highly preferred. Comprehensive knowledge of financial lines products , including coverages, policy forms, and industry standards.

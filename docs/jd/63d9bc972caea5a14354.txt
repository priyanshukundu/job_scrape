The Impact You Will Have As a Lead Data Engineer, you will serve as a senior technical leader within the Data Engineering organization, helping define the architecture, engineering standards, and best practices that support Walker & Dunlop’s enterprise data platform. This role focuses on technical leadership and platform modernization, partnering closely with Engineering leadership and distributed engineering teams to build scalable, reliable, and well-governed data systems. You will play a key role in shaping the future state of the data platform by driving architectural consistency, improving engineering practices, and supporting the adoption of modern data engineering capabilities across the organization. Primary Responsibilities Define and maintain architectural standards for the enterprise data platform, including data modeling, pipeline design, orchestration, and observability practices. Lead the Technical Design Document (TDD) process, including authoring, reviewing, and approving technical designs for complex initiatives. Conduct design reviews and production readiness reviews across Data Engineering teams. Guide the evolution of the core data platform stack, including Redshift, Airflow, Kafka, SQL Server, and Aurora PostgreSQL. Establish and promote engineering standards related to code quality, testing, CI/CD, documentation, and release management. Identify and help address technical debt, platform fragmentation, scalability concerns, and reliability improvements. Partner with Engineering Managers and teams on technical planning, solution design, and implementation approaches. Mentor and coach engineers on architecture, engineering best practices, and technical leadership skills. S erve as a technical point of contact for cross-functional stakeholders on platform capabilities, integrations, and data architecture patterns. Support evaluation and adoption of new tools, frameworks, and technologies related to data engineering, transformation, quality, and governance. Perform other duties as assigned Education and Experience Bachelor’s degree preferred , preferably in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field, or equivalent practical experience. 8+ years of experience in data engineering, including experience serving as a technical lead or senior individual contributor across multiple teams or initiatives. Strong experience building and supporting cloud-native data platforms within AWS environments. Experience with modern data engineering tools and technologies including Airflow, Kafka, Redshift, PostgreSQL, or similar platforms. Experience implementing CI/CD practices, automated testing, and production support processes for data pipelines and platforms. Familiarity with enterprise data warehouse technologies and data transformation frameworks preferred. Commercial real estate, financial services, or regulated industry experience preferred. Ability to lead technical initiatives and influence engineering standards across distributed teams.

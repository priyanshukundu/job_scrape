Job Description U.S. Bank is seeking a highly effective and dynamic Payment Card Industry (PCI) Risk Analyst to lead Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard compliance activities in partnership with business line partners. This position is responsible for understanding PCI business processes, communicating PCI requirements, validating compliance, and coordinating activities necessary to maintain ongoing PCI compliance. Success will be measured through a thorough understanding of PCI business processes within Consumer and Business Banking and effective execution and oversight of activities required to maintain PCI compliance. The Payment Card Industry (PCI) Risk Analyst in Consumer and Business Banking (CBB) will be responsible for business line adherence to enterprise policies, including Information Security and Data Protection and Privacy Policies. Position Highlights Act as a business line subject matter expert for PCI Data Security Standard requirements and escalate matters of risk or non-compliance to enterprise partners Participate in risk management initiatives related to Data Protection and Information Security Work with business line partners to maintain continuous compliance with PCI requirements, including identification and management of PCI processes, people, and technology Define and document the scope of the cardholder data environment (CDE), systems connected to the CDE, and business processes within each business unit Coordinate PCI compliance activities, including documentation, evidence collection, stakeholder follow-up, and remediation tracking Identify compliance gaps, determine necessary next steps, and work with accountable business and technology partners to drive actions through resolution Maintain documentation and reporting necessary to demonstrate ongoing PCI compliance Basic Qualifications

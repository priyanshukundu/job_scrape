Introduction to the team Our Technology Team partners with teams across Expedia Group to create innovative products, services, and tools to deliver high-quality experiences for travelers, partners, and our employees. A singular technology platform powered by data and machine learning provides secure, differentiated, and personalized experiences that drive loyalty and traveler satisfaction. The Pricing applied science team has critical impacts on the business health. The team is building a scalable and robust system to enable pricing as a lever to optimize business performance. The system should be steered based on desired outputs, such as a given tradeoff between volume and profit. Our work directly decides prices across different lines of business, influencing multi-billion dollar revenue. As a Senior Manager of Applied Science, you will lead a team and collaborate with a strong team of strategists, engineers, product managers, operations and analytics. You will be deeply involved in the technical details of building applied science solutions to optimize business performance. As a leader in Applied Science, you will set best practices, architect solutions, design processes and manage a highly skilled team that continues to grow. You will also contribute to an area of active research: causal inference using machine learning methods, stochastic optimization, ML-based demand estimation, experiment design and analysis. In this role, you will: Manage a team of applied scientists to deliver scalable and robust machine learning and data science solutions to pricing and monetization with engineering rigor. Architect solutions, review models and provide necessary technical guidance. Dissect business problems into digestible solutions. Follow company strategic directions and align with stakeholders on goals and roadmaps. Set the team vision, design roadmap and manage the execution to deliver outstanding results. Hire, mentor and grow the team. Collaborate with other applied science, analytics and data engineering teams to build a great data culture in EG.

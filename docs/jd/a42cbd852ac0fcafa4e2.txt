Corporate Location Management Associate
JPMorgan Chase
Location: New York, NY, United States
Link: https://jpmc.fa.oraclecloud.com/hcmUI/CandidateExperience/en/sites/CX_1001/job/210810880

JPMorganChase's Corporate Center is a vibrant, large-scale workplace where thoughtful strategy and genuine care for people come together. This role offers meaningful exposure to senior leadership and the opportunity to contribute directly to programs that shape how employees connect, engage, and experience the workplace every day. As a Corporate Location Management Associate at JPMorganChase, you will own and lead a portfolio of site-specific initiatives that enhance the Chicago Corporate Center employee experience. You will partner with local leadership, HR Market Leads, and cross-functional teams to execute workplace programs, deliver employee engagement activities, and translate data into decisions that matter. Your work will be visible, your contributions will be direct, and your ability to drive projects from planning to completion will make a meaningful difference to thousands of employees on campus. Job responsibilities Lead assigned Corporate Location Management projects from planning through execution, developing structured project plans, setting milestones, tracking deliverables, and escalating risks to ensure timely and effective completion. Partner with local leadership and HR Market Leads to support the rollout of site-specific programs and initiatives aligned to Chicago Corporate Center priorities. Build and maintain reporting frameworks to measure engagement, participation, and the success of key location initiatives, translating data into clear, actionable insights for leadership. Analyze employee metrics, demographics, and survey feedback — including Employee Opinion Survey results — to identify trends, surface opportunities, and develop informed recommendations that shape site strategy. Support the development of executive presentations and the coordination of senior leader market visits, managing planning, logistics, and materials preparation to ensure polished and effective delivery. Contribute content for Chicago Corporate Center communication channels, ensuring messages are clear, consistent, timely, and aligned to location and business priorities. Plan and manage logistics for both virtual and in-person employee events and activities, ensuring inclusive participation and strong attendee engagement. Support local Employee Groups — including Business Resource Groups, Affinity Groups, and Volunteer Chapters — to facilitate events, communications, and community engagement opportunities across the site. Partner with on-site teams to coordinate workplace infrastructure improvements, managing timelines, stakeholder inputs, and communications tied to workplace enhancements. Required qualifications, capabilities and skills 3+ years experience in Project Management. Bachelor's degree or equivalent relevant experience. Demonstrated project management experience, with the ability to manage multiple concurrent priorities, build structured project plans, and drive delivery to completion. Strong written and verbal communication skills, with demonstrated comfort supporting senior stakeholders and contributing to executive-level materials. Analytical capability with experience building and maintaining reporting, metrics, and performance dashboards. Strong organizational skills and attention to detail, with the ability to maintain accuracy while managing competing priorities in a fast-paced environment. Proficiency in Microsoft Office Suite, including Excel and Microsoft Teams. Experience planning and coordinating both virtual and in-person events. Preferred qualifications, capabilities and skills Experience working within a large, complex corporate or financial services environment. Proficiency in PitchPro or a comparable presentation tool used for executive-level deliverables. Familiarity with employee engagement measurement tools, including survey platforms or workforce analytics applications. Experience supporting Employee Groups, Business Resource Groups, or community engagement programs in a corporate setting.

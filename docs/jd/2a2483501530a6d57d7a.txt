Team & Role Overview About the Team and Department Franklin Equity (FE) is an investment group within Franklin Templeton managing more than $200 billion globally, offering in-depth expertise in managing global, U.S., sector and thematic equity strategies across the style and market-capitalization spectrum. Rooted in Silicon Valley and invested worldwide, Franklin Equity draws on perspectives across public and private markets, from growth to value. We seek to understand how innovation and transformational change are reshaping companies and industries, and where long-term value may emerge next. Our diverse range of investment products, including mutual funds, ETFs, SMAs, and CITs, are designed to cater to the future-focused investor. About the Role We are looking for a Product Specialist to join our team in San Mateo, CA . Working closely with Franklin Equity’s investment team , the Product Specialist will serve as a product expert and product champion for several equity strategies, largely focused on high-growth themes including technology and biotechnology. The Product Specialist is responsible for the promotion, defense, and competitive positioning of the product line, and is actively involved in global distribution initiatives as well as institutional and retail client service. Key success metrics include demonstrating ownership, strategic direction-setting, and prioritization for the assigned investment strategies. How You Will Add Value Serve as the primary subject matter expert (SME) for the investment-related aspects of several equity investment strategies to sales, client service, consultant relations, marketing, other business partners and external clients. Support asset growth and retention through the development of strategic messaging for use with a wide range of internal (sales, client service, marketing, and consultant relations) and external (potential and existing clients, consultants and third parties) audiences. Effectively and efficiently answer client servicing questions, articulate investment team views, and respond to performance and other investment-related inquiries on assigned investment strategies. Perform portfolio and market analysis to enhance investment narrative and strategic messaging in support of business development and client servicing efforts. Liaise with internal marketing teams to communicate updates and develop new content for standard and ad hoc client presentation materials. Partner with FT’s product strategy teams to reposition, extend and innovate the FE product line including product rationalization, launching new strategies, and executing product line extensions. This includes development of investment guidelines and pricing frameworks. Develop investment-related insight for use in investment communications including RFPs, DDQs, pre-sale and post-sale marketing collateral, and corporate communications. Partner with investment staff and writers to develop thought leadership and white papers for a range of client groups. Assess new product and business opportunities (customized separate accounts, sub-advised products, and new funds). Update and deliver monthly and quarterly performance data to internal business partners. Support broader platform or corporate initiatives and ad hoc projects.

Position Summary The Senior Associate, IT Security is a hands-on security operations practitioner embedded within Fortitude Group Services' Security Operations function. This role is responsible for day-to-day monitoring, investigation, and response to security events across the enterprise, with primary focus on security operations center (SOC) activities, vulnerability management operations, phishing and email security analysis, and audit evidence support. The Senior Associate owns the operational execution of recurring security workflows, serves as a technical contributor during incident response, and partners with the Managed Security Service Provider (MSSP) on alert triage and escalation quality. The role is a trusted operational resource across the Security team and reports to the AVP Security Architect, with success defined by consistent operational delivery, measurable improvement in alert handling and vulnerability remediation workflows, and quality of audit and regulatory evidence produced from day-to-day work. assist with containment, evidence collection, timeline construction, and documentation under the direction of security leadership Analyze logs, telemetry, and security tool outputs from EDR, SIEM, CNAPP, email security, and web security platforms to identify threats and anomalies Monitor queue health, service levels, and escalation quality from the MSSP; flag gaps and recommend tuning or process improvements Maintain operational playbooks and runbooks for recurring incident types; keep documentation current as tooling and processes evolve Produce after-action notes and operational metrics from incident handling to support continuous improvement and executive reporting Vulnerability & Attack Surface Management Own the day-to-day operation of the vulnerability management workflow including scan review, finding triage, remediation tracking, SLA reporting, and risk acceptance coordination Prioritize exposures based on exploitability (CVSS, EPSS), CISA Known Exploited Vulnerabilities (KEV), business impact, and regulatory significance Coordinate directly with infrastructure, cloud, and application teams on remediation workflows;

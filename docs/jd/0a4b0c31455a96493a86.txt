Mobile Architecture & Technical Strategy Define and execute mobile technology strategy aligned with retail business objectives and senior leadership priorities. Own end-to-end solution architecture across mobile and backend systems, balancing stability, scalability, security, and cost. Simplify complex technical challenges into scalable solutions that evolve with business needs. 2. Mobile Engineering & Development Provide technical leadership across Android, iOS, and cross-platform mobile development. Architect shared business logic using Kotlin Multiplatform (KMP) and dynamic experiences using Server-Driven UI (SDUI). Drive native development using Android (Kotlin, Jetpack Compose) and iOS (Swift, UIKit, SwiftUI). 3. Backend Integration & Quality Integrate mobile applications with Spring Boot, Java 21, REST APIs, microservices, and core services including SearchNav, ProductGateway, and TypeAhead. Drive high-quality design, automated unit and integration testing using JUnit 5, XCTest, MockK, Espresso, and XCUITest. Champion modern software engineering methodologies and secure development practices, including SAST and DAST. 4. Mobile Delivery, Performance & Reliability Drive mobile delivery using Fastlane, TestFlight, Firebase App Distribution, and automated deployment pipelines. Monitor performance using GCP Cloud Monitoring, Prometheus, and distributed tracing across mobile-to-backend journeys. Lead troubleshooting, root cause analysis, and resolution of complex production issues and critical outages. 5. Technical Leadership & Collaboration Proactively identify customer, engineering, and business challenges and drive clarity, convergence, and closure. Partner with product owners, engineers, and stakeholders to ensure successful deployment and adoption of mobile solutions. Evaluate technical tradeoffs and drive engineering decisions that deliver scalable, reliable, and high-performing mobile experiences.

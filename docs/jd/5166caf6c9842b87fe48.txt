Bring your expertise to JPMorgan Chase. As part of Risk Management and Compliance, you are at the center of keeping JPMorgan Chase strong and resilient. You help the firm grow its business in a responsible way by anticipating new and emerging risks, and using your expert judgement to solve real-world challenges that impact our company, customers and communities. Our culture in Risk Management and Compliance is all about thinking outside the box, challenging the status quo and striving to be best-in-class. As a Compliance Risk Management Senior Officer within the Compliance, Conduct and Operational Risk (CCOR) organization, you will be partnering with Line of Business (LOB) and global/regional Compliance teams; including Internal Audit, Operational Risk and other Control functions. In this role, you will be required to have the necessary knowledge and experience in Compliance, as well as familiarity with regulatory and/or audit best practices. Additionally, you will be required to use one or more High Security Access (HSA) systems. As a user of these systems, you will be subject to enhanced screening which includes both criminal and credit background checks, and/or other enhanced screening at the time of accepting the position and on an annual basis thereafter. You will need to successfully complete the enhanced screening process prior to commencing employment or assignment. High attention to detail, flexibility, patience, good analytical as well as interpersonal skills, and the ability to work independently will be required of you in this role. Job responsibilities Perform on-site compliance examinations of broker-dealer and investment advisory business to determine adherence to JPMorgan Securities policies and procedures. Develop scope, process flows, design evaluation, testing evaluations to ensure risk controls are implemented, functioning properly, and are being executed properly. Execute examinations consisting of trading analysis, on-site inspections, and document reviews. Evaluate findings and formulating conclusions in written reports on compliance assessments, required schedules, worksheets and narrative materials. Required qualifications, capabilities and skills Undergraduate degree or equivalent experience. 3+ years' experience in financial services, compliance or regulatory role required. Ability to travel (domestically) 35% of the time. Valid driver's license required. Familiarity with trading analysis and document review procedures. Experience collaborating with cross-functional teams. Knowledge of regulatory and audit best practices in financial services. Demonstrated ability to challenge the status quo and drive process improvements. Strong written communication skills for reporting and documentation. Preferred qualifications, capabilities and skills FINRA Series 7, 66, and 24 licenses (or equivalents) not required, however, preferred. Experience with High Security Access systems and enhanced screening processes. Prior broker/dealer experience with compliance or audit is desirable.

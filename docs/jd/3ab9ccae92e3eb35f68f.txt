Join us and transform the lives of patients while transforming your career. Strategic Sourcing Senior Manager - Capital Construction What you will do Let’s do this. Let’s change the world. In this vital role you will report to the strategic sourcing team in the Construction, Equipment, Facilities and Utilities vertical within the Global Procurement Organization (GPO). The Senior Manager will develop and manage the category’s sourcing strategy and supplier management processes in collaboration with key stakeholders across the Operations organization, developing long-range sourcing plans and forecasts to support innovative value creation strategies for capital projects across the USA and elsewhere as project organization needs require. This role will lead the definition and execution of strategies that shape how Amgen sources and contracts for capital construction projects. It will focus on identifying productivity opportunities and supporting speed to market, quality, compliance, and effective project delivery. The Senior Manager will partner with stakeholders earlier in the capital project lifecycle to help define sourcing needs, assess delivery and contracting approaches, and integrate procurement considerations into project planning and execution. Responsibilities: Lead the global capital sourcing strategy and portfolio roadmap. Build and manage key stakeholder and supplier relationships. Serve as a trusted advisor to Operations Engineering teams. Provide market intelligence and sourcing expertise. Develop business cases and procurement recommendations. Engage early in project planning to optimize sourcing and contracting outcomes. Advise on construction delivery and contracting models (CMAR, EPCM, Design-Build, A&E, etc.). Lead sourcing, supplier selection, negotiations, and contract strategy. Leverage market data and benchmarking to improve cost, quality, schedule, and risk outcomes. Act as the primary procurement lead and category ambassador. Identify, evaluate, and onboard qualified suppliers. Resolve supplier performance issues and drive continuous improvement. Develop and support Supplier Relationship Management (SRM) programs. Enable collaboration across regions, functions, and categories. Adapt sourcing strategies to evolving business and market conditions. Mitigate supply, contractual, and regulatory risks in partnership with Legal, Compliance, and other functions. Translate business requirements into sourcing strategies, bid packages, and commercial terms. Support construction contracts and commercial frameworks, including pricing, scope, risk, and performance management. Promote compliant and consistent sourcing practices. Monitor and communicate sourcing performance, risks, and opportunities. Develop long-term sourcing strategies aligned with capital investment plans. Lead stakeholder engagement across global and cross-functional teams. Build and execute a pipeline of value-generating sourcing initiatives. Maintain industry expertise and identify best practices and innovations. Develop sourcing strategies based on project needs, supplier capacity, market conditions, and regulatory requirements. Assess construction market trends, supplier capabilities, and pricing dynamics. Advance procurement transformation through earlier stakeholder engagement. Drive process improvements using portfolio insights, market intelligence, and cross-project learnings.

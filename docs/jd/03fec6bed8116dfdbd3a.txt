About the Role At eBay, we strive to build world-class systems that ensure our global marketplace operates flawlessly and in strict compliance with evolving regulations. Our mission is to transform complex legal and regulatory requirements into scalable, reliable, and auditable engineering solutions. These solutions protect our platform's integrity and maintain trust for millions of buyers and sellers worldwide. We are seeking a Staff AI/ML Engineer - MTS1 to contribute to core initiatives within the Compliance Engineering organization. This role focuses on crafting, implementing, and operating production-grade AI systems for compliance use cases, including classification, policy enforcement, monitoring, and auditability. You will work hands-on with Generative AI, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), prompt and context engineering, and agentic AI systems to build reliable, explainable, and regulation-ready solutions. This is an applied systems role passionate about execution, correctness, and deploying robust AI components in high-risk production environments. Primary Job Responsibilities As a Staff AI/ML Engineer - MTS1 in Compliance Engineering, your responsibilities will include: Compose, build, and deliver scalable ML and AI services supporting compliance classification, policy enforcement, automated auditing, and continuous monitoring. Implement end-to-end AI pipelines, including data preprocessing, feature engineering, model integration, deployment, and real-time/batch inference. Build and improve Generative AI and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) pipelines over regulatory and policy corpora, incorporating hybrid retrieval, query rewriting, and grounding mechanisms. Implement structured timely workflows and context engineering techniques, such as tool calling, schema validation, and constrained decoding. Build and maintain agentic workflows for compliance tasks, including tool-integrated agents connecting to internal services and multi-step reasoning pipelines. Implement evaluation frameworks, factuality checks, hallucination-detection mechanisms, and regression test suites to ensure system reliability and compliance adherence. Actively monitor production models and services, establishing key performance metrics, debugging operational issues, and resolving pipeline failures. Translate technical specifications and business/compliance requirements into clean, well-tested, and maintainable production code. Collaborate cross-functionally with Product, Policy, Legal, and Platform Engineering teams to ship compliant, production-grade solutions. Participate in code reviews, contribute to technical documentation, and help uphold engineering guidelines across the team. Required Skills and Experience Education: BS or MS or equivalent experience in Computer Science, Statistics, Mathematics, or a related field. Experience: 6+ years of software engineering and machine learning experience with strong fundamentals in algorithms, data structures, and system development. Production Delivery: Proven track record of implementing, testing, deploying, and maintaining production-grade ML/AI services and data pipelines. GenAI & NLP: Hands-on experience developing with LLMs, RAG architectures, timely engineering, context management, and vector databases (e.g., FAISS, Milvus, Pinecone, OpenSearch vector). Agentic Workflows: Practical experience implementing agentic components, function calling, tool use, or multi-step execution flows. Engineering Rigor: Strong background in writing unit/integration tests, implementing evaluation and monitoring frameworks, and debugging distributed services. Data & Distributed Systems: Experience with large-scale data processing engines (e.g., Apache Spark, Kafka, or modern streaming/batch systems). Programming: High proficiency in Python (or an equivalent language) for building production services, data pipelines, and ML systems. Execution & Collaboration: Ability to take ownership of features, work independently within agile sprints, and communicate clearly with engineering and non-engineering partners. Nice to Have Prior experience applying ML/AI to compliance, trust & safety, fraud detection, risk, or policy enforcement. Hands-on experience fine-tuning LLMs (e.g., LoRA, PEFT) or optimizing inference runtimes (e.g., vLLM, TensorRT-LLM, batching/caching techniques). Familiarity with AI governance concepts, model risk management (MRM), or safety guardrails (e.g., NeMo Guardrails, Llama Guard). Experience working with high-volume e-commerce, fintech, or regulated cloud architectures.

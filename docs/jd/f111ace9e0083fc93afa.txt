Position Summary The Associate Director of Global Medical Neuroscience, Specialty Neurology will partner with the broader medical team to support the development, implementation, and execution of disease area and asset-specific medical strategies for BMS's Neuroimmune, Neuromuscular, and Motor neuron portfolio, with primary focus on Multiple Sclerosis and Spasticity. This role will partner with cross-functional Medical team members to deliver key medical activities, align Specialty Neurology strategy, and foster cross-functional collaboration with key stakeholders including US and WW Commercial, Research and Development, Global Drug Development, HEOR, Regulatory Affairs, and other global medical stakeholders. This position reports to the Senior Director, Global Medical Neuroscience, Medical Product Lead, Specialty Neurology. Key Responsibilities Medical Strategy & Planning Participate in Medical Matrix Team activities and meetings, contributing to the development, planning, and execution of a comprehensive Medical Plan aligned with Neuroimmune, Neuromuscular, and Motor neuron disease area and portfolio strategies, under the guidance of senior medical leadership. Maintain a deep medical knowledge of the Multiple Sclerosis, Spasticity, and broader Neuroimmune, Neuromuscular, and Motor neuron treatment landscape, including competitors' plans, and translate insights into action. Support the monitoring of emerging science, competitive intelligence, and regulatory trends relevant to the Specialty Neurology space and help communicate findings to the broader team. Contribute to asset development activities and lifecycle management efforts for Neuroimmune, Neuromuscular, and Motor neuron assets as directed. Cross-Functional & Stakeholder Collaboration Represent Medical Strategy in cross-functional matrix teams, contributing to the Brand planning process and collaborating with marketing, sales, value and access, legal, regulatory, and development teams. Effectively partner with stakeholders on medical activities including labeling, safety surveillance, scientific advisory boards, congress support, competitive intelligence, real-world data generation, and publications. Collaborate with Commercialization & Access organizations to integrate medical perspectives into the commercialization process and ensure alignment between commercial and medical plans. Coordinate with Marketing and other matrix partners to communicate results from clinical trials, treatment guidelines, and other clinical data internally and externally. Act as a core member of the brand team, collaborating with key stakeholders to craft plans that address market needs. External Engagement & Scientific Support Support Thought Leader identification, mapping, profiling, and engagement in an evolving environment with changing needs. Engage with high-impact external thought leaders in Neurology and Specialty Neurology (Neuroimmune, Neuromuscular, and Motor neuron) to identify and translate key insights. Establish and maintain interactions with external medical and scientific experts, clinical researchers, and academic centers to assess unmet medical needs and help develop appropriate medical strategies. Support and contribute to BMS-sponsored clinical research activities, including non-registrational data generation and real-world evidence initiatives. Represent BMS at medical congresses, advisory boards, and scientific forums in support of the Specialty Neurology medical strategy. People & Organizational Contribution Serve as an internal disease expert, providing critical input into the development of effective communication tools and programs for the Neuroimmune, Neuromuscular, and Motor neuron franchise. Contribute to a collaborative, inclusive, and high-performing team culture within the Specialty Neurology medical team. Model BMS Values and uphold the highest standards of scientific integrity in all activities. Performs other responsibilities as assigned by leadership. Operational & Compliance Excellence Complete all assigned SOPs and training within designated timeframes and adhere to job-specific SOPs and work instructions. Industry Knowledge: Strong understanding of the biopharma drug development and commercialization process. Strategic Thinking: Demonstrated strength in understanding overarching company goals, performing strategic assessments, and developing clearly framed choices and recommendations.

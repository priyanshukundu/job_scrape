Your Opportunity Janus Henderson Investors US, LLC seeks an Associate Director, Senior Corporate Accountant in Denver, CO to prepare journal entries, financial analyses and financial statements including supporting documentation, income statements, balance sheets, monthly account reconciliations and fluctuation explanations related to Compensation across various countries under both United States Generally Accepted Accounting Principles and local statutory requirements. Make recommendations related to the accounting of reserves, assets, and expenditures. File and maintain records of Corporate Accounting data. Support Finance transformation through participating in current state and future state assessment activities. Create current state assessment documents with all the gaps including process, technology, controls and change documented. Develop future state process maps and document key design decisions. Collaborate with process owners to identify and resolve all emerging issues on Finance transformation programs. Prepare, analyze, post, reconcile and report on data related to long-term incentive plans across multiple countries and currencies under both United States Generally Accepted Accounting Principles and International Financial Reporting Standards. Analyze, post, reconcile and report on data prepared by payroll across multiple countries and currencies under both United States Generally Accepted Accounting Principles and International Financial Reporting Standards. Prepare and reconcile funding for monthly payrolls across multiple countries and currencies. Remote work permitted 2 days a week. Requirements: Bachelor’s degree in accounting or a related field, plus 2 years of experience in Long-Term Incentive Accounting, Generally Accepted Accounting Principles (GAAP) and Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) systems, specifically SAP. Advanced knowledge of International Financial Reporting Standards (IFRS) also required and can be gained through either employment or education. Salary: $90,000 - $110,000 per year.

When an earthquake strikes, fires can do as much damage as the shaking itself, and wildfires are reshaping how the world thinks about risk. Verisk Catastrophe and Risk Solutions builds the catastrophe models that help insurers, reinsurers, and governments understand these threats and make confident decisions before and after disaster strikes. As a Research Engineer on the Fire Team within Earthquake and Fire Engineering, you will help shape the science behind our post-earthquake fire and wildfire models. You will combine fire-model research, algorithm development, and simulation implementation with data analysis, probabilistic risk assessment, and model validation across earthquake shaking, fire following earthquake, and wildfire. You will take research from initial concept through execution, run large-scale simulations, and test model performance against historical events and experimental observations. Working alongside senior engineers and researchers, you will turn scientific findings into models that drive insurance portfolio loss estimation, and you will share your results with colleagues and clients. Your work will have real-world impact. What You'll Do Establish research frameworks and execute projects from initial concept through completion. Collect and analyze post-event surveys, historical records, and related data on fire ignition, spread, and suppression across residential, commercial, and industrial properties and infrastructure. Develop and improve models of fire ignition, spread, and suppression for fire following earthquake and wildfire applications. Execute large-scale simulations in cloud environments and manage computational resources efficiently. Apply statistical and machine learning methods to analyze large datasets and simulation outputs. Perform deterministic and probabilistic risk assessments for earthquake shaking, fire following earthquake, and wildfire. Validate model results against historical events and experimental observations using statistical and machine learning methods. Collaborate with senior engineers and researchers to review findings and refine the models. Communicate findings to technical and nontechnical audiences, including clients, colleagues, and government officials; provide technical support; and contribute to internal and external technical publications.

Summary We are seeking qualified candidates in Corporate Treasury to coordinate the Bank's capital planning framework and enterprise-wide capital stress testing processes. The candidate will also support ongoing liquidity, funding and capital management activities. This position may include analysis of commodities-related liquidity exposures. This role partners closely with Corporate Finance, Risk Management (2LoD), Investor Relations & Corporate Development, Internal Audit (3LoD), and senior leadership to ensure the institution maintains strong capital adequacy and liquidity under both baseline and stressed conditions while supporting balance sheet growth, strategic objectives, and shareholder value creation. The position leads a vital role to develop capital and liquidity forecasts, evaluate strategic capital and funding actions, and assess the impact of emerging risks that support management and Board-level decisions. Capital Planning & Management Lead Corporate Treasury's role in the capital planning process, including development of multi-year capital forecasts under baseline and stress scenarios. Maintain the Texas Capital's Capital Management Framework and monitor capital adequacy metrics, ensuring alignment with strategic objectives, risk appetite, and regulatory expectations. Evaluate the capital impact of business growth initiatives, balance sheet strategies, and new product offerings. Develop recommendations regarding dividends and other capital actions. Perform ongoing assessment of capital requirements relative to regulatory minimums, internal targets, and peer institutions. Enterprise Capital Stress Testing Coordinate the bank's annual capital stress testing program. Design and execute enterprise-wide capital stress testing methodologies, assumptions, and scenarios. Review and challenge model outputs and assumptions to ensure results remain reasonable, supportable, and actionable. Evaluate capital resilience under severe but plausible stress scenarios and identify management actions where necessary. Present stress testing results and capital adequacy assessments to capital stress testing working groups and ALCO sub-committee. Liquidity & Funding Management Support Support the development and execution of the Bank's liquidity risk management and funding strategies Assist in the management and monitoring of funding sources, including deposits, secured funding, Federal Home Loan Bank facilities, Federal Reserve facilities, repurchase agreements, and capital market funding alternatives. Assess and monitor liquidity risks arising from commodities hedging activities and derivative portfolios using an internally developed framework. Regulatory & Governance Coordinate documentation, governance, controls, and validation activities related to capital planning, liquidity management, and stress testing. Ensure strong governance and adherence to regulatory expectations for capital stress testing, liquidity stress testing, and forecasting frameworks. Work with Model Risk Management (MRM) to ensure models are compliant with MRM policy. Develop and maintain model documentation, procedures and performance monitoring framework in line with MRM policy. Support regulatory examinations, internal audits, and independent reviews related to Treasury activities. Qualifications Bachelor’s degree in finance, Accounting, Economics, Mathematics, Statistics, or related field. Master's Degree, MBA preferred. Professional Certifications: CFA, FRM, or CPA preferred. 8-10+ years of experience in Corporate Treasury, Capital Management, Liquidity Risk Management, Funding Management, Capital Stress Testing, Financial Planning & Analysis, Asset-Liability Management, or Regulatory Capital within the banking industry. Strong knowledge of regulatory capital frameworks, capital planning, liquidity risk management practices, and stress testing methodologies. Experience developing capital forecasts, liquidity forecasts, and stress testing methodologies for large regional or national banking organizations. Working knowledge of capital adequacy metrics, liquidity risk metrics, wholesale funding strategies, and contingency funding practices. Knowledge of commodity hedging preferred but not required. Proven experience presenting analytical results and recommendations to senior management committees. Strong analytical, problem-solving, and quantitative skills with the ability to translate complex concepts into actionable business insights. Advanced proficiency in Excel, PowerPoint, and financial modeling. Programming experience (Python, SQL, or similar analytical tools) preferred.

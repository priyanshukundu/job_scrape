The High Risk Unit Analyst is responsible for conducting enhanced due diligence (EDD) reviews and investigations of high-risk customer relationships to ensure compliance with Bank Secrecy Act (BSA), Anti-Money Laundering (AML), USA PATRIOT Act, OFAC, and internal policies and procedures. This role performs detailed reviews of transaction activity, customer profiles, complex corporate ownership structures, and unusual account activity to identify potential money laundering, fraud, sanctions, or other financial crime risks. Responsibilities include conducting high-risk customer reviews, evaluating and documenting investigative findings, recommending customer risk mitigation strategies including account closures and SAR filings, maintaining regulatory documentation, and supporting internal audits, regulatory examinations, special projects, and law enforcement requests. The analyst must be able to independently manage a high-volume workload, produce high-quality investigative casework, and communicate effectively with account officers and business partners. Qualifications Bachelor's Degree required; CAMS certification preferred. Minimum 5 years of BSA/AML compliance experience with an emphasis on high-risk customer reviews, investigations, enhanced due diligence, and transaction analysis. Strong working knowledge of BSA/AML regulations, USA PATRIOT Act, OFAC requirements, SAR investigations, and financial crime typologies. Experience with international banking, complex ownership structures, and BSA/AML monitoring systems preferred. Excellent analytical, investigative, organizational, and written communication skills. Ability to manage multiple priorities in a fast-paced regulatory environment while maintaining a high level of accuracy and attention to detail.

What you’ll do Responsibilities Deliver cutting-edge infrastructure and product platforms that scale to users’ needs, with a focus on reliability and efficiency. Drive the execution of key reporting features for the company, oversee the entire development lifecycle from planning to delivery while maintaining high standards of quality and timely completion. Build APIs that back reporting experiences, and provide a seamless integration between frontend components and backing data stores. Design, develop, and own data pipelines, models, and products that power different teams across the company. Interface with product teams at the company to understand the reporting needs within their products. Communicate with company users to understand how they utilize reporting.

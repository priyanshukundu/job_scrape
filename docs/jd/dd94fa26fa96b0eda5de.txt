Charles Schwab Trust Bank provides trust, custody, trading, and technical services supporting retirement plan sponsors, advisors, and clients. As an Associate on the Cash Operations Distribution Team, you will process retirement distributions and disbursements, enter transactions and file loads, validate and balance files, and support checks, tax forms, wire and ACH activity, returned items, and mail operations. You will maintain complete, audit-ready records, protect Non-Public Information, and provide accurate production data and timely updates that support a consistent Client Experience. You will demonstrate Accountability by owning work through resolution, meeting service-level commitments and control requirements, and escalating risks or exceptions promptly. Continuous Learning will help you build proficiency across systems and processes, support cross-training, and adapt as business needs shift. Through Process Improvement, you will identify issues, manual pain points, and control gaps while sharing ideas that strengthen accuracy, efficiency, and team performance. What You Will Do Process retirement distributions and disbursements in accordance with documented procedures, client directives, service-level expectations, and required controls. Enter transactions and complete system file loads, validating information for completeness and accuracy before progressing work. Print checks and tax forms and maintain complete, traceable, audit-ready supporting documentation. Generate, balance, validate, and transmit files; research and escalate out-of-balance conditions and processing exceptions. Support wire funding, ACH or wire reversals, recalls, stop payments, returned items, and rejected transactions within established authority levels. Coordinate outgoing and incoming mail activities, including checks, tax forms, overnight deliveries, returned items, and rejected mail. Monitor shared mailboxes, respond to routine inquiries, route requests appropriately, and maintain ownership through resolution. Protect Non-Public Information, physical checks, tax documents, and other sensitive materials. Support audit and control requests with complete, accurate, and timely documentation and evidence. Record production metrics, communicate risks before deadlines are missed, and identify practical Process Improvement opportunities. Build proficiency across assigned processes and systems, contribute to cross-training, and provide backup support as business needs shift.

Early Careers: Property Risk Specialist The Property Risk Control team provides technical consulting services to help clients identify, evaluate, and mitigate property-related risks. As an Entry-Level Property Risk Control Consultant, you will work closely with senior consultants and brokers to analyze risk exposures, assess facility operations, and provide data-driven consulting that enhances client property risk quality and reduce loss potential. This role offers a unique opportunity to combine technical expertise, analytical thinking, and client interaction while developing a strong foundation in property risk consulting and engineering! What You Will Do Participate in property risk assessments for client facilities, focusing on fire protection, equipment reliability, and overall loss prevention practices. Analyze engineering data, inspection reports, and risk metrics to identify exposures and recommend mitigation strategies. Prepare and deliver clear, professional reports and presentations that communicate technical findings to both technical and non-technical audiences. Collaborate with cross-functional teams including broking, claims, and analytics to support client risk management goals. Organize and maintain accurate documentation of site visits, client communications, and technical evaluations. Participate in ongoing training and mentorship to build expertise in property risk evaluation and fire protection systems and natural hazards. Skills/Experience That Will Lead to Success Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with an ability to interpret and research complex technical data. Effective communication and presentation skills; comfortable explaining technical findings to varied audiences. Thorough with excellent organizational and time management abilities. Proficiency in Microsoft Office Suite (Excel, PowerPoint, Word); familiarity with data visualization or analysis tools a plus. Ability to work independently and collaboratively within a team environment. Education Applicants for this role are on track to graduate between Spring 2026 – Spring 2027 and have a GPA of 3.0 or above. Bachelor’s degree or equivalent experience in Engineering (Fire Protection, Mechanical, Electrical, Civil, Chemical, or related), other technical field required.

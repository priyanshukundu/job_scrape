Team and Role Summary The Wealth Management Non-Financial / Operational Risk Governance and Analytics team supports first-line Wealth Management Operational Risk through analytics, executive and committee reporting, framework governance, and decision support. The Assistant Vice President will combine risk-management judgment, hands-on analytics, and practical AI skills to strengthen governance and turn complex data into clear, actionable insights. The role spans data and risk governance, regulatory reporting, and Operational Mapping, while partnering with colleagues responsible for issues, incidents, risks, and controls. The successful candidate will improve recurring analysis and reporting, develop reusable AI-enabled workflows, and share knowledge to build the team’s analytical and applied-AI capabilities. Responsibilities Deliver accurate, decision-useful dashboards, executive and committee materials, and regulatory reporting; translate risk data into clear insights on trends, concentrations, recurring themes, anomalies, and emerging areas requiring management attention. Validate the accuracy, completeness, traceability, and fitness for purpose of data, analytical findings, and AI-assisted outputs through reconciliation, quality controls, and investigation of unexpected results. Identify, prioritize, and implement practical AI use cases that improve risk analysis, data-quality checks, thematic reviews, and reporting efficiency across the team and broader Wealth Management Operational Risk organization. Build repeatable analytical workflows and visualizations using tools such as SQL, Snowflake, Dataiku, and Tableau; apply approved AI capabilities, including Microsoft 365 Copilot and Snowflake Cortex, with appropriate testing, documentation, and human review. Develop reusable analytical and AI-enabled approaches, document methods and limitations, and share knowledge with colleagues to build team capability and reduce reliance on individual specialists. Partner with colleagues responsible for issues, incidents, risks, and controls to define business needs, connect insights across risk disciplines, and deliver analytical solutions that support risk-management decisions and framework governance. Support Operational Mapping analytics by evaluating connections across processes, risks, controls, systems, vendors, services, and other operational dependencies. Required Qualifications Bachelor’s degree in finance, risk management, business, technology, analytics, or a related field. At least five years of relevant experience in Non-Financial / Operational Risk, risk or control management, financial services, analytics, reporting, or technology, with demonstrated ability to connect analytical work to business and risk-management needs. Hands-on experience developing analytical workflows, dashboards, or data-driven solutions, together with practical experience applying AI tools to business problems and evaluating their outputs. Working knowledge of SQL or a comparable data-querying language, with the ability to extract, reconcile, and analyze data from multiple sources. Understanding of risk-management concepts, including issues, incidents, risks, controls, key risk indicators, or Operational Mapping, and the ability to apply sound judgment when interpreting results. Experience translating complex analysis into clear reporting, presentations, and actionable insights for senior management or governance forums. Strong attention to detail and a disciplined approach to validating data, analytical findings, AI-assisted outputs, and reporting conclusions. Strong communication, collaboration, and problem-solving skills; ability to independently manage multiple deliverables, learn new tools, and share practical knowledge with colleagues. Preferred Qualifications Experience with one or more of the following: Dataiku, Tableau, Snowflake, Microsoft 365 Copilot, or Snowflake Cortex; proficiency in every platform is not required. Experience improving recurring risk or governance processes through automation or AI, including documenting workflows and supporting adoption by colleagues.

The Datastores team owns Coinbase's production online databases and caches: Aurora, RDS Postgres, MongoDB Atlas, DynamoDB, ElastiCache (Valkey, Redis), and ClickHouse. We build the paved paths, tooling, and client libraries that let hundreds of engineering teams run reliable, secure, cost-efficient data layers serving millions of requests per second. We are hiring a Staff Software Engineer to help evolve our storage platform: consolidating how databases are provisioned and operated, building zonal resilience and disaster recovery into every production datastore, securing access to data, and giving teams well-paved ways to use it. What you'll do Shape the architecture and multi-year roadmap of Coinbase's storage platform across relational, document, key-value, and caching datastores. Lead platform initiatives from design through production, such as consolidating databases onto shared, proxy-fronted clusters and building client libraries that make applications resilient to failover. Drive resilience and disaster recovery for online datastores: zonal failover, backups and replication, restore tooling, and regular recovery validation. Harden the security and compliance posture of our datastores: encryption and key management, least-privilege access, audit logging, and the controls and evidence that regulators and auditors expect. Design for reliability, performance, and cost efficiency; set and defend SLOs, lead capacity planning, and eliminate recurring classes of incidents. Partner with senior engineers and product-group leadership on database technology choices, and work directly with AWS, MongoDB, and other vendors. Mentor engineers across the team, raise the bar on design reviews and operational excellence, and participate in the on-call rotation. Required Skills and Experience 8+ years of industry experience building and operating large-scale, highly available distributed systems in production. Deep hands-on experience operating online databases at scale in production (e.g., PostgreSQL/Aurora, MongoDB, DynamoDB, Redis/Valkey, Memcached), including replication, failover, performance tuning, and version upgrades. Solid understanding of distributed database architecture, consistency models, and the trade-offs between datastore technologies. Experience securing data infrastructure in a regulated environment: encryption at rest and in transit, key management, access control, and audit requirements. Strong backend development skills in Go, Java, or Python, with a track record of building database tooling, client libraries, or automation. Experience leading cross-team technical initiatives, writing clear design documents, and influencing without direct authority.

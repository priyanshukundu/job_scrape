Business Banking Deposit - Product, Senior Associate
JPMorgan Chase

Join the Business Banking Deposit Product team as a Senior Product Associate and help turn small-business needs into better banking experiences. From uncovering opportunities and shaping the roadmap to supporting launches and improving products over time, you’ll work with customer insights, market perspectives, and performance data to guide thoughtful product decisions. Partnering with the Senior Product Manager and teams across the business, you’ll help deliver reliable, scalable deposit products that create value for customers and support business goals. As a Senior Product Associate on the Business Banking Deposit Product team, you will help manage deposit products throughout their life cycle—from opportunity identification and discovery through launch and ongoing improvement. You will champion the needs of small-business customers and help develop product experiences that deliver customer value and support business objectives. Working with the Senior Product Manager and cross-functional partners, you will use customer feedback, research, market insights, and product performance data to inform recommendations and priorities. You will also consider how product changes affect the broader customer experience and contribute to delivering reliable, scalable deposit products. Job responsibilities Partner with the Senior Product Manager to identify product opportunities based on customer needs, user research, and market insights. Gather and synthesize customer research, journey maps, market analysis, and product data to inform the product roadmap and recommendations. Assess upstream and downstream impacts of proposed features on the end-to-end product and customer experience. Track and evaluate product performance, including delivery against time, cost, and quality targets. Collaborate with cross-functional partners to align on priorities, dependencies, and delivery timelines. Identify opportunities to improve existing products using customer feedback, data, and ongoing performance analysis. Support preparation of product updates, recommendations, and decision materials for stakeholders. Contribute to launch readiness and post-launch reviews by monitoring outcomes and surfacing follow-up opportunities. Required qualifications, capabilities, and skills At least 3 years of experience in a collaborative business role and/or product owner role. Ability to prioritize work and perform effectively in a fast-paced environment. Strong collaboration and communication skills. Ability to analyze information, identify key themes, and communicate findings. Strong organizational skills and attention to detail when managing competing priorities. Preferred qualifications, capabilities, and skills Basic knowledge of the small-business landscape, frontline branch sales, or service-support practices. Familiarity with banking products and services. Strong presentation skills. Ability to translate customer insights and market data into actionable product recommendations. Experience supporting product initiatives from discovery through launch and post-launch evaluation. Experience using reporting, spreadsheet, or data-visualization tools to assess product performance.

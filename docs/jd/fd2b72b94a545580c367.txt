Supervision of five credit analysts. Position based in New York. Key responsibilities: For assigned files for which RPC is responsible for the primary credit analysis, preparation of the first level credit application and analysis based on requests received from the Front Office including: Presentation of sector trends and drivers, and counterparty market position. Peer comparison. Detailed presentation of financial statements and financial projections when applicable. Review of legal protections including credit and trading agreements, guarantees and financial covenants when applicable. Prepare and/or review Internal ratings. Submission of files to NY Credit Committee via email or in person. Processing of Crew (credit process) and Dafne (internal ratings). For assigned files for which the Front Office prepares the first level credit application, preparation of the second level RPC opinion, including independent analysis of the borrower, transaction structure, term sheet, financial models, etc. Review and verify Internal ratings (Dafne). Run the credit process (Crew). Presentation of the RPC Opinion to RPC management and to the NYCC. Frequent interaction with the various Front Office teams to gather additional information concerning the client, discuss credit risk issues and mitigants, and obtain relationship profitability information and commercial support/strategy. Active coordination with RPC Paris to answer any inquiries for additional information/explanations, follow-up on rating validations, and communicate credit issues and trend as appropriate. Active monitoring of the sectors and portfolios covered, and communication to senior management locally and in Paris when appropriate. Participation to Early Warning and SAR meetings when necessary. Contribute to portfolio reviews, preparation of documents for US Risk Committee, audit assessments, loan reviews, or special projects when needed. Data Quality: verify accuracy of, and correct if necessary, data in risk systems (Crew/Eurecca/RICOS/Daphne etc.). May also be asked to analyze counterparties in different sectors depending on workloads.

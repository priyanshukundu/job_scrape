Bring your expertise to JPMorgan Chase. As part of Risk Management and Compliance, you are at the centre of keeping JPMorgan Chase strong and resilient. You help the firm grow its business in a responsible way by anticipating new and emerging risks, and using your expert judgment to solve real-world challenges that impact our company, customers and communities. Our culture in Risk Management and Compliance is all about thinking outside the box, challenging the status quo and striving to be best-in-class. As a Data Owner Senior Associate within Risk Management and Compliance, you will play an important role in executing data strategy and managing data risk across business processes and data domains. Working with business, Technology, data provider and consumer stakeholders, you will help ensure data supports business operations, regulatory obligations, strategic objectives, risk management and analytics. You will help ensure data is understood, fit for purpose and effectively governed throughout its lifecycle. You will translate business, regulatory and data management requirements into practical data requirements and controls, support the identification and remediation of data issues, and contribute to strategic change and data initiatives by embedding appropriate data quality, ownership, protection, metadata, lineage and lifecycle requirements. Job Responsibilities Develop and execute operational procedures supporting delivery and management of data across business operations, strategic initiatives and analytics. Define and document business data requirements, including sourcing, quality, metadata, lineage, classification and fitness for purpose, working with business, Technology, data providers and consumers. Support implementation of data governance, data products, data publishing standards and risk management processes, ensuring requirements are effectively embedded within Risk Management and Compliance. Execute processes to identify, monitor and mitigate data risks throughout the data lifecycle, including data quality, protection, access, retention, storage and appropriate use. Partner with Technology and business stakeholders to ensure data sourcing, content and quality meet established business, regulatory and data management requirements. Support strategic change and data initiatives by helping incorporate data requirements into solution design, data products and analytics, reducing reliance on downstream remediation. Investigate data quality and control issues, identify root causes and work with business and Technology stakeholders to recommend and support sustainable remediation. Leverage subject-matter knowledge and internal partnerships to define and maintain metadata and data classifications, supporting consistent understanding and appropriate use of data. Communicate data risks, issues, dependencies and requirements effectively across technical and non-technical stakeholder groups.

Use Your Power for Purpose At Pfizer, our purpose is to deliver breakthroughs that transform patients' lives. Central to this mission is our Research and Development team, which strives to convert advanced science and cutting-edge technologies into impactful therapies and vaccines. Whether you are engaged in discovery sciences, ensuring drug safety and efficacy, or supporting clinical trials, your role is crucial. You will leverage innovative design and process development capabilities to expedite the delivery of top tier medicines to patients globally. By integrating advanced scientific knowledge with practical applications, we aim to bring the most effective treatments to those in need, fulfilling our commitment to improving health outcomes worldwide. What Will Be Achieved The Associate Scientist in the Clinical Immunology & Diagnostics, Robotics & Automation group supports Pfizer's vaccine research portfolio through the execution of automated sample preparation workflows. The role utilizes advanced laboratory automation systems to generate high-quality assay-ready materials, while ensuring high standards of compliance and operational excellence. How It Will Be Achieved Operate, monitor, troubleshoot, and maintain laboratory automation systems to ensure reliable, compliant, and efficient laboratory operations. Follow approved procedures, safety requirements, and quality standards while seeking guidance as appropriate to ensure successful execution of assigned responsibilities. Execute assigned work and responsibilities to meet schedule demands, project milestones, and operational deadlines while maintaining a high standard of quality. Support the management of laboratory materials, reagents, controls, reference standards, and consumables required for automated workflows. Collaborate effectively with scientific, operational, and quality partners to support laboratory objectives and project deliverables. Maintain current departmental, quality and safety training requirements. Minimum Qualifications: Bachelor’s degree in Biology, Chemistry, Biochemistry, Biotechnology, Engineering, or a related scientific field. Experience working in a laboratory, pharmaceutical, biotechnology, or academic environment. Strong troubleshooting, analytical, and problem-solving skills. Excellent attention to detail and organizational skills. Ability to manage multiple priorities and meet deadlines in a fast-paced environment. Verbal and written communication skills with the ability to collaborate effectively across cross-functional teams. Proficiency with Microsoft Office and electronic laboratory systems. Experience using common AI tools, including generative technologies such as ChatGPT or Microsoft Copilot, to support problem solving and enhance productivity. Demonstrated curiosity for exploring how these tools can improve outcomes and understanding of responsible AI practices, including risk management and ethical use. Preferred Qualifications: Experience with laboratory automation, robotic liquid handling platforms (e.g., Hamilton, Tecan, Beckman Coulter), and high-throughput sample preparation workflows. Knowledge of regulated laboratory practices, data integrity principles, and quality systems (e.g., GxP). Familiarity with common AI-enabled tools to support problem solving and enhance productivity. PHYSICAL/MENTAL REQUIREMENTS This position requires significant time in a laboratory environment and the ability to wear required personal protective equipment (PPE). Ability to lift up to 50 pounds and perform activities such as standing, walking, bending, reaching, and sitting for extended periods while working at laboratory workstations, automation platforms, and computer workstations. Ability to analyze data and apply critical thinking and problem-solving skills to support laboratory operations and process improvements. NON-STANDARD WORK SCHEDULE, TRAVEL OR ENVIRONMENT REQUIREMENTS This position involves a non-standard work schedule, working Sunday through Thursday. A scheduled workday would consist of a total of 8.5 hours, between 10:00 a.m. and 9:00 p.m., according to business need. This schedule will be evaluated on a monthly basis.

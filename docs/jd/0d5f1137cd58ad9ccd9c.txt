Your opportunity: Make your mark in Risk Engineering AIG Risk Engineering is a global team of over 160 professionals supporting underwriters, brokers and clients across Commercial Property, Energy, Casualty and Marine product lines. Operating in more than 200 countries, the team helps identify, quantify and manage risk to support informed business and underwriting decisions. This involves partnering directly with clients through on-site assessments, while collaborating with underwriters and brokers to provide risk insights, navigate complex exposures and support effective risk management strategies. How you will create an impact Join a dynamic career where no two days are the same, with opportunities for site visits, client engagement and travel across a range of industries. Working closely with underwriters and clients, you will develop expertise in natural catastrophe exposures, manufacturing processes and commercial risks, while helping assess exposures, identify vulnerabilities and support risk mitigation strategies. This role offers valuable industry exposure and the opportunity to build strong stakeholder relationships while developing the skills to help clients enhance their business resilience. What you can expect Assess property risks including fire protection systems, electrical infrastructure, flammable liquids and gases, storage arrangements, production processes, and operational hazards. Assess natural catastrophe exposures including flood, bushfire, windstorm, earthquake, and hail. Identify vulnerabilities that could contribute to major property damage and business interruption losses. Apply AIG risk assessment methodologies to evaluate risk quality and recommend improvements. Review third-party engineering and risk reports to support underwriting assessments and account risk selection. Develop fire, flood, and other loss estimates to support underwriting decisions and portfolio management. Document survey results in a data-driven report writing application Work closely with underwriters to communicate key risk strengths, weaknesses, exposures, and practical risk mitigation opportunities. Build technical expertise through training, mentoring, client engagement, and exposure to diverse industries, operations, and risk management practices. Dutch-speaking communication with local colleagues, brokers, customers. English-speaking trainings and internal meetings. What you’ll need to succeed: Bachelor's or master's degree, e.g. in Business Administration, Science, Technology, Engineering or Mathematics or a comparable course of study graduated in 2025 or 2026. A demonstrated strong educational achievement and a genuine interest in engineering, risk assessment and client service. Initial work experience through internships is an advantage, ideally in the technical sector. A curious nature with strong communication skills (verbal and written) and the ability to engage effectively with a range of stakeholders. Confident and capable of operating effectively under pressure while maintaining a high standard of professional service. Ability to build, develop, and maintain positive relationships with internal stakeholders, broker partners, and clients. High level of initiative and ability to prioritize tasks in an agile environment along with excellent time management and organizational ability. Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with the ability to assess risk, evaluate information, and support sound decision-making. Physical ability to conduct multi-day surveys, which may include climbing ladders and extended periods on your feet. Readiness to be on a steep learning curve and agile comprehension skills. Very good Dutch and English language skills. MS Office skills.

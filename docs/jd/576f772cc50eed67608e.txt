DAVE VS. GOLIATH. WE’RE DAVE.

Dave is a financial app on a mission to build products that level the financial playing field. It is redefining the financial landscape by leveraging technology to create an affordable, transparent, and user-centric access to liquidity for millions of Americans. As a leading innovator in the U.S. financial services sector, Dave’s digital financial platform offers products designed to meet the credit needs of those underserved by traditional financial institutions. Dave’s offerings include its flagship ExtraCash product, providing members up to $500 within minutes. The company is on track to launch several new product offerings in 2026, including a Buy Now Pay Later (BNPL) option.



Dave is focused on serving Americans who are financially vulnerable or living paycheck to paycheck. Dave is leading the charge in creating a new era of credit products that prioritizes speed, affordability, and accessibility, making it the go-to financial partner for those who need it most.



We're looking for a Senior Manager, Data Engineering to lead the team building and operating Dave's data platform. You'll shape the technical direction, develop talented engineers, and make trusted, accessible data a foundation for better decisions and member experiences.




THE OPPORTUNITY

You'll lead a multidisciplinary team of data engineers, analytics engineers, and database administrators responsible for the systems that power analytics, financial reporting, and AI initiatives. You'll own the platform roadmap, guide architectural decisions, and build a team that delivers reliable data products at scale. Partnering with our Director, Data Platform & AI Acceleration and leaders across Product, Finance, Science, and Analytics, you'll translate business priorities into sustainable engineering solutions. This is a people-first leadership role where your technical judgment matters most in complex decisions and high-impact challenges.




WHAT YOU'LL BUILD & OWN

 - Develop a strong team: Hire, coach, and support engineers through clear expectations, thoughtful feedback, career development, and opportunities to grow into technical leadership.

 - Shape our data platform: Own the architecture and roadmap for cloud data warehouses, data pipelines, streaming systems, and data modeling, balancing scalability, reliability, and cost.

 - Build trust in our data: Establish measurable service level objectives (SLOs) and standards for data quality, lineage, observability, security, governance, and disaster recovery.

 - Enable AI innovation: Partner with AI and data science teams to deliver governed, accessible datasets and infrastructure supporting machine learning, vector stores, and large language model applications.

 - Connect engineering to business outcomes: Work across Product, Finance, Science, Analytics, and Compliance to prioritize investments, navigate trade-offs, and communicate decisions clearly.

 - Improve how we operate: Guide critical design reviews and complex incident resolution while reducing operational work, managing cloud costs, and creating sustainable on-call practices.


THE IMPACT

Your team will make critical data more reliable, consistent, and accessible across Dave. That foundation helps teams make better decisions, develop new products, support responsible AI adoption, and ultimately improve financial outcomes for our members.




WHAT WE'RE LOOKING FOR

 - 8+ years of experience in data engineering, architecture, or platform operations, including designing and operating production data platforms.

 - 4+ years of direct people management experience, including hiring, performance management, coaching, and developing technical leaders.

 - Experience leading multidisciplinary engineering teams and translating business priorities into technical roadmaps.

 - Strong proficiency in SQL, Python, PySpark, and at least one major cloud platform (Amazon Web Services, Google Cloud, or Microsoft Azure).

 - Deep knowledge of data modeling, cloud warehouses or lakehouses, data pipelines, streaming architectures, and data platform reliability.

 - Experience implementing data governance, security, quality standards, monitoring, and service level objectives, including support for AI-ready data.

 - Demonstrated ability to manage infrastructure costs, improve operational efficiency, and communicate technical trade-offs to senior stakeholders.

 - Bachelor's degree required. [Confirm any required field of study with the hiring manager.]

 - Hands-on experience with Snowflake, dbt, Airflow, or Kafka.

 - Experience modernizing legacy data platforms or supporting machine learning and generative AI data infrastructure.

To ensure uncompromising accuracy and timeliness in the delivery of firmwide metrics and reporting that support Goldman Sachs’ decision making process, our data platform is continuously growing and evolving. The Core Data Architecture Engineering combines the principles of Computer Science and Data Engineering to process large volume of data from multiple sources across the firm and ensure its integrity and availability for enrichment into a consumable data model. The Core Data Architecture Engineering has an opportunity for an Associate level Software Engineer to work across a broad range of applications and extremely diverse set of technologies to keep the platform operating at peak efficiency. As an Engineer in the organization, you will work with a global team of talented engineers to drive the build & adoption of common tools, platforms, and applications. The team builds solutions that are offered as a software product or as a hosted service. We are a dynamic team of talented developers and architects who partner with business areas and other technology teams to deliver high profile projects using a raft of technologies that are fit for purpose (Java, Python, Cloud computing, HDFS, Spark, S3, ReactJS, Sybase IQ, Snowflake among many others). A glimpse of the interesting problems that we engineer solutions for, include acquiring high quality data, storing it, enriching it, ensuring integrity, transforming it into a consumer model for use by diverse consumer types, including the new generation of AI use cases and MCP tools. WHAT WE LOOK FOR How You Will Fulfill Your Potential This role is for a Senior Developer in large projects across a global team of data platform engineers You will apply your prior experience and expertise at a large bank or financial institution, and ramp up very quickly to work with the global team Performance tune applications to improve memory and CPU utilization. You will facilitate robust utilization of the firm’s Data Platform capabilities by the different functional domains as a SME Utilize web development technologies to facilitate application development for front end UI used for firm wide usecases Develop software for calculations using databases like Snowflake, Sybase IQ and distributed HDFS systems. Interact with business and engineering users to help resolve issues with applications. Design and support batch and streaming processes for data validation and enrichment and further distribute data to other systems. Oversee junior technical team members in all aspects of Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC) including design, code review and production migrations. Skills And Experience We Are Looking For Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Mathematics, Electrical Engineering or related technical discipline 3-5 years’ experience working in a large investment or retail bank, or a comparable financial institution. Experience in software development, including a clear understanding of data structures, algorithms, software design and core programming concepts Comfortable multi-tasking, managing multiple stakeholders and working as part of a team Comfortable with working with multiple programming languages, including learning proprietary software coding when required Technologies: Scala, Java, Python, Spark, Linux and shell scripting, TDD (JUnit), build tools (Maven/Gradle/Ant), AWS deployment stack Experience with one or more major relational / object databases.

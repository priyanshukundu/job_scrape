portfolio manager
Standard Chartered
Location: Amritsar, IN
Link: https://jobs.standardchartered.com/job/Amritsar-portfolio-manager/1369600857/

Job Summary • Manage and grow a portfolio of priority banking clients by delivering personalised relationship management, financial needs assessment and service excellence. • Drive sustainable revenue growth through deepening, cross-sell and acquisition while ensuring fair outcomes, conduct discipline and adherence to risk and compliance standards. • Act as a trusted point of contact for clients, coordinating with product, service, operations and branch teams to deliver seamless client solutions. Key Responsibilities Client Relationship Management and Portfolio Growth • Build, manage and deepen relationships with priority clients through regular engagement, needs-based conversations and high-quality service delivery. • Deliver agreed business outcomes across deposits, investments, wealth, lending and other eligible banking solutions in line with client suitability and Bank standards. • Identify opportunities for acquisition, portfolio activation and cross-sell while maintaining strong client retention and satisfaction. • Work closely with product specialists, service teams, branch operations and other internal stakeholders to resolve client requirements promptly and effectively. Risk, Conduct and Operational Discipline • Ensure all client conversations, product recommendations and account activities are conducted in line with applicable policies, regulatory requirements and conduct standards. • Maintain accurate documentation, timely follow-ups and strong oversight of KYC, client due diligence, transaction monitoring and operational risk requirements. • Escalate service, compliance, fraud, complaint or risk issues promptly and support timely resolution to protect clients and the Bank. Skills and Experience Must-have Skills • Relationship Management • Client Needs Analysis • Risk and Compliance Management Other Skills • Sales Planning and Pipeline Management • Stakeholder Collaboration • Digital Banking and CRM Tools • Communication and Client Service Motivations • Energised by building trusted long-term client relationships and creating positive client experiences. • Motivated by achieving business goals through ethical, needs-based banking conversations and disciplined portfolio management. • Driven by collaboration, problem-solving and delivering timely solutions in a regulated banking environment. Qualifications • Experience in retail banking, priority banking, wealth relationship management or branch banking with direct client portfolio responsibility. • Proven ability to acquire, deepen and retain client relationships while meeting sales, service, risk and compliance expectations. • Working knowledge of deposits, investments, lending, digital banking and customer due diligence processes. • Graduate degree or equivalent professional qualification preferred. • Any market-mandated banking, investment, insurance or regulatory certifications must be completed before performing regulated activities, where applicable. • English and local market language proficiency required for client engagement and documentation.

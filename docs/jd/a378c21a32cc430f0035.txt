Regular or Temporary: Regular Language Fluency: English (Required) Work Shift: 1st shift (United States of America) Please review the following job description: Independently designs, develops, tests, and maintains complex software solutions that meet business and user needs. Emphasizes delivering scalable, reliable, and high-quality applications by applying advanced best practices in coding, architecture, and software lifecycle management, while collaborating with cross-functional teams to drive technical initiatives and mentor junior engineers. ESSENTIAL DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES Following is a summary of the essential functions for this job. Other duties may be performed, both major and minor, which are not mentioned below. Specific activities may change from time to time. . 1. Independently designs, deploys, and maintains cloud infrastructure and platform services that support business and technology objectives. 2. Develops and implements infrastructure automation solutions using Infrastructure as Code (IaC), configuration management, and orchestration tools to improve efficiency, consistency, and scalability. 3. Builds, supports, and enhances CI/CD pipelines to enable automated software delivery, infrastructure deployment, and operational excellence. 4. Collaborates with software engineering, architecture, security, and operations teams to design and implement secure, scalable, and highly available cloud solutions. 5. Monitors platform performance, availability, and operational health through observability, logging, alerting, and performance management practices. 6. Troubleshoots and resolves complex infrastructure, application, and production issues by performing root-cause analysis and implementing preventive measures. 7. Implements cloud security best practices, vulnerability remediation activities, and compliance controls to maintain a secure technology environment. 8. Participates actively in cloud migration, platform modernization, resiliency, disaster recovery, and operational readiness initiatives. 9. Develops technical documentation, operational procedures, and reusable engineering patterns to support platform consistency and knowledge sharing. 10. Mentors and supports engineers in cloud technologies, automation practices, DevOps methodologies, and engineering best practices. 11. Evaluates emerging cloud technologies, tools, and engineering practices to drive continuous improvement and platform innovation. Qualifications Required Qualifications The requirements listed below are representative of the knowledge, skill and/or ability required. Reasonable accommodations may be made to enable individuals with disabilities to perform the essential functions. 1. Bachelor’s degree in computer science, Software Engineering, or related field with 5 years of relevant experience or equivalent education and training. 2. In-depth knowledge of information systems and ability to identify, apply, and implement IT best practices. Preferred Qualifications Experience designing, deploying, and supporting cloud-native solutions on platforms such as AWS, Azure, or comparable public/private cloud environments. Experience with Infrastructure as Code (IaC), automation, and configuration management tools such as Terraform, CloudFormation, Ansible, or similar frameworks. Experience building and supporting CI/CD pipelines using tools such as GitLab CI/CD, Azure DevOps, Jenkins, GitHub Actions, or comparable software delivery platforms. Experience with containerization and orchestration technologies such as Docker, Kubernetes, Amazon ECS, Amazon EKS, or equivalent platforms. Experience implementing monitoring, observability, logging, and synthetic monitoring solutions using tools such as Dynatrace, CloudWatch, Splunk, ELK, Grafana, Prometheus, or similar platforms. Understanding of cloud security, networking, identity management, secrets management, and resiliency concepts. Cloud platform certifications such as AWS Certified Solutions Architect, AWS Certified Developer, Kubernetes (CKA/CKAD), Terraform Associate, or equivalent industry certifications. Previous experience in the banking, financial services, or other highly regulated industries. Experience working in Agile development environments.

Product Strategist, FAS Product & Portfolio Strategy At Vanguard, our core purpose is to take a stand for all investors and give them the best chance for investment success. Financial Advisor Services (FAS) delivers investment products and insights that help advisors navigate increasingly complex markets and build better outcomes for their clients. We are seeking a Product Strategist to provide product expertise to crew and clients and to collaborate with business and investment teams to ensure the health and commercial success of the products. This role plays a critical part in scaling and commercializing our product lineup; activating the sales and marketing organizations behind strategic product priorities. Additionally, it will focus on bringing the voice of the client back to the enterprise, shaping the roadmap and delivering recommendations to leadership and governance forums. Core Responsibilities Serve as an investment product expert. Provide investment product expertise and strategic guidance to internal partners, relationship management teams, and Sales. Participate in meetings with clients and intermediaries to explain investment strategies, portfolio applications, product positioning, and relevant market developments. Contribute thought leadership. Develop informed perspectives on investment products, markets, industry trends, and competitive developments that advance product discussions and strengthen the firm’s product strategy. Shape product positioning and go-to-market strategy. Partner with Sales, Marketing, and other stakeholders to develop differentiated product positioning, technical content, and go-to-market strategies. Support new product launches and significant changes to existing products. Lead product strategy and analysis. Lead complex product-related analyses that inform product launches, enhancements, positioning, and broader product-line strategy. Apply a strong understanding of investment products, markets, and client needs to identify implications and recommend a clear path forward. Evaluate product opportunities and trade-offs. Assess product opportunities across market, client, competitive, investment, commercial, and operational dimensions. Frame key trade-offs, risks, and dependencies to support informed decision-making. Develop actionable recommendations. Translate complex quantitative and qualitative analysis into clear, decision-ready insights and recommendations for senior management, governance bodies, and other internal decision-making forums. Partner across the organization. Build and maintain strong relationships with senior stakeholders and subject-matter experts across Product, Investments, Sales, Marketing, Compliance, Operations, and other relevant functions. Lead or coordinate cross-functional teams when required to advance product priorities. Bring the client and marketplace perspective into product decisions. Synthesize feedback from clients, advisors, Sales, and relationship management teams with market, competitive, and product-performance insights to assess product health, identify unmet needs, and recommend ongoing improvements. Deliver effective product communication. Create and present clear, compelling written and verbal communications for senior leaders, internal partners, clients, and industry audiences. Ensure that product-related messaging is accurate, consistent, and aligned across internal and external channels. Build organizational capability. Facilitate product training and internal communications related to investment strategies, product launches, enhancements, and positioning. Provide guidance, feedback, and subject-matter support to analysts and other junior team members. Participates in special projects and performs other duties as assigned. Qualifications Eight years related work experience, including five year's of experience in product management and investment analysis/strategy. Supervisory experience preferred. Undergraduate degree or equivalent combination of training and experience. Graduate degree preferred. CFA preferred. This job requires a regulatory license and/or registration (e.g. FINRA, state, SFC). These will be determined by Compliance based on role-specific duties.​ Special Factors Sponsorship Vanguard is not offering visa sponsorship for this position.

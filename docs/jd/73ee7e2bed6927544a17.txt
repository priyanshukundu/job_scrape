Become a part of our caring community The Senior Software Engineer designs, develops, and enhances scalable software solutions that support critical business objectives. This role combines strong technical expertise with cloud engineering capabilities to deliver secure, high-performing, and reliable applications. The Senior Software Engineer collaborates with cross-functional teams to solve complex technical challenges, drive innovation, and influence technology decisions that improve customer and business outcomes. Key Responsibilities Design, develop, test, deploy, and maintain software applications using modern engineering practices and technologies. Partner with product managers, architects, and business stakeholders to translate business requirements into scalable technical solutions. Lead the design and implementation of cloud-native applications, services, and infrastructure. Develop and maintain application architectures that prioritize performance, security, reliability, and maintainability. Evaluate, recommend, and implement cloud technologies, tools, and services to support business and technology objectives. Collaborate with engineering teams to establish technical standards, best practices, and reusable design patterns. Drive continuous improvement through automation, code quality initiatives, and DevOps practices. Troubleshoot and resolve complex application, integration, and infrastructure issues. Monitor application performance and implement enhancements to improve scalability, efficiency, and user experience. Conduct code reviews and provide technical mentorship to engineers across the team. Contribute to technical roadmaps, architecture discussions, and strategic technology initiatives. Ensure solutions comply with security, regulatory, and organizational standards. Required Qualifications Bachelor’s Degree in Computer Science or related field. Minimum of 5 years of experience designing, developing, and testing of software applications and/or infrastructure. Minimum of 3 years of hands-on Python development experience in Databricks. Minimum of 3 years of hands-on experience designing, developing, and optimizing solutions using Databricks. Minimum of 3 years of experience with relational and NoSQL databases, including SQL Server and MongoDB. Minimum of 3 years of experience with Azure DevOps, Git, CI/CD, TDD, and automated build and deployment processes. Minimum of 3 years of experience with cloud platforms such as Azure, GCP, or AWS. Minimum of 5 years of experience with SDLC, Agile methodologies such as Scrum or Kanban, SOA, object-oriented principles, and continuous improvement practices. Preferred Qualifications Master’s Degree in Business Administration, Computer Science, Software Engineering, Information Systems or related fields. Demonstrated ability to collaborate with product managers, architects, engineers, and business stakeholders to deliver complex technology solutions. Ability to communicate complex technical concepts clearly to both technical and nontechnical audiences. Relevant professional certification, such as Databricks Certified Data Engineer Associate, Microsoft Certified: Azure Developer Associate, or an equivalent cloud certification.

We are looking for a Senior Machine Learning Engineer to join our new User Simulation and Advanced Synthetic Data Generation team. You will own the systems our research runs on: the architecture, scale, and releases of our open-source framework. You'll also build the environments where simulated users meet models and agents. User simulation is quickly becoming core infrastructure for evaluating and training AI models and agents. They are judged by how they work with users, people or other agents, over many turns: holding a conversation, using tools, or collaborating on code in a real repository. Real data is thinnest here: slow and costly to collect, skewed toward existing users, and missing the long tail of languages, domains, and safety scenarios. We build grounded synthetic users and simulators, then use both to find capability gaps and close them with targeted training data and RL environments. A lot of our work ships as open source, including NeMo User Sim, Nemotron-Personas, and the data and environments we generate. We build our own systems on these same tools. We publish and present at top conferences, and we organize UserSim @ NeurIPS 2026, the first NeurIPS workshop on user simulation. The team serves three groups: Internal partners , including Nemotron and other model, agent, and product teams who evaluate and train their models with our simulators. Open-source users and contributors who build on the framework for their own models, agents, and languages. The research community , through work that pushes the state of the art and is published at top venues. The mission is the boss. We organize around the problem, not titles or ownership. Researchers write production code, engineers shape research, and everyone works directly with partner teams. We share credit, review each other's work candidly, and pick up whatever moves the mission forward. What you'll be doing: Own the architecture of NeMo User Sim: the conversation engine, configuration-driven pipelines, and extension API. Scale simulation to tens of thousands of concurrent multi-turn episodes against hosted and self-served models (e.g., vLLM, NIM), sharded across Slurm or Kubernetes. Runs must survive rate limits and partial failures, resume cleanly, and reproduce down to the row. Build and run environments for agents. These include stateful simulated tool services, and sandboxed coding environments where simulated users work with coding agents on real repositories. Verifiers and hidden tests grade the results. Connect simulated users to NeMo Gym and RL training, and keep the loop fast and cheap. Turn research prototypes into reliable, tested features together with research scientists. Run the open-source project: versioned releases, CI/CD, dependency and license hygiene, issue triage, and contributor reviews. Build the pipelines behind our open data releases, onboard internal partner teams, and present the framework at conferences. What we need to see: BS, MS, or PhD in Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, or a related field (or equivalent experience). 6+ years building production ML systems, data pipelines, or developer libraries. Expert Python skills, with a track record of typed, tested libraries that other teams depend on. Experience running LLM workloads at scale: serving (e.g., vLLM, TensorRT-LLM, NIM), concurrency, and cost. Experience with LLM evaluation, training-data pipelines, or agents that use tools. Strong software design judgment on interfaces, versioning, and backward compatibility, with the mission ahead of ownership and credit. Ways to stand out from the crowd: Maintaining a widely used open-source ML library. RL training infrastructure or environments, such as NeMo Gym or NeMo RL. Infrastructure for coding agents: container sandboxes and SWE-bench-style harnesses. Inference optimization: distillation, quantization, or speculative decoding. Experience with NeMo Data Designer, Ray, or Spark and experience with multilingual text processing, such as language identification, scripts, and localization.

What You’ll Do: Formulation Preparation: Independently or work with internal scientists to prepare solid dosage formulations (blends, tablets, capsules, etc.) in development and pilot plant labs. Characterization: Execute comprehensive material characterization for drug substances, excipients, drug product intermediates, and final solid dosage formulations to assess material attributes and formulation performance. Evaluate powder flow, bulk/tapped density, particle size distribution, powder compaction properties, tablet hardness/tensile strength, solid fraction, friability, disintegration time, solubility measurement, etc. Data Analysis & Experimental Design: Review and analyze experimental data, identify key trends and takeaways, and propose hypothesis-driven follow-up experiments. Ensure documentation of all research work in electronic lab notebook. Follow departmental level documentation guidelines. Comply with all EHS and safety requirements. Ensure completion of all required trainings. Technical Communication: Present research findings and deliver technical recommendations to cross-functional teams within the Synthetic Molecule Pharmaceutics group.

VP, Structured Products Capital One Financial Corporation (www.capitalone.com) is a financial holding company whose subsidiaries, which includes Capital One, N.A., and Capital One Bank (USA), N.A., had $476 billion in deposits and $669 billion in total assets as of December 31, 2025. Capital One is a top 10 U.S. bank, with the strength and stability to meet the needs of our business lines. The commercial banking group services a $89 billion loan portfolio and over 6,100 clients. We’ve had strong growth at the commercial bank in a broad array of products and services over the past few years, including but not limited to financing (commercial lending, loan syndications), capital markets (interest rate derivatives, commodity derivatives, foreign exchange, investment banking (including structured products, debt capital markets, & equity capital markets), institutional sales & trading, equity research and treasury management. This role will report to the Head of Structured Products within Capital One’s Capital Markets Financing Group (CM) and will be a member of the Structured Products team driving new business opportunities. The Structured Products Group works with clients across the bank, primarily the Financial Institutions Group (FIG), to structure, arrange and distribute ABS and CLO transactions to institutional investors. The FIG client set operates across a broad range of asset classes in the consumer & commercial sectors which includes ABS, MM CLO’s & CRE CLO’s. Responsibilities: Provide support to senior capital markets bankers to facilitate the team winning left lead and joint lead ABS/CLO mandates and managing financing facilities across a variety of consumer and commercial asset classes Provide supporting role in the execution & closing of ABS and/or CLO transactions and financing transactions Managing and leading deal process Discussions with Rating Agencies, Accounting Firms, Trustees and Legal Counsel Negotiation of key deal terms Reviewing and commenting on legal documentation Running investor calls and meetings Assist deal sponsorship internally Preparing key transaction materials Liaise with internal and external groups for deal execution Assist in preparation of pitch materials for client discussions for securitization opportunities Provide R&D efforts as the group looks to expand into new products and asset classes within structured products Coordinate ABS and/or CLO activities with internal sales team to ensure proper messaging to investors Lead due diligence sessions for clients Engage in special projects to support new business generation Successful relationship and interactions with partner groups such as Operations, Credit, Counterparty, Market Risk, Technology, Compliance, Internal Audit & Legal Basic Qualifications: Bachelor’s Degree or military experience Preferred Qualifications: At least 5 years of structured product or banking experience At least 5 years of experience with cash flow modeling of ABS and CLO Strong quantitative skills At this time, Capital One will not sponsor a new applicant for employment authorization for this position.

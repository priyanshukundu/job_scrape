Senior Software Engineer, Networking Software Systems
Qualcomm
Location: Santa Clara, California, United States of America

Company: Qualcomm Atheros, Inc. Job Area: Engineering Group, Engineering Group > Software Engineering General Summary: As a leading technology innovator, Qualcomm pushes the boundaries of what's possible to enable next-generation experiences and drives digital transformation to help create a smarter, connected future for all. As a Qualcomm Senior Software Engineer, you will design, develop, create, modify, and validate embedded and cloud edge software, applications, and/or specialized utility programs that launch cutting-edge, world class products that meet and exceed customer needs. Qualcomm Software Engineers collaborate with systems, hardware, architecture, test engineers, and other teams to design system-level software solutions and obtain information on performance requirements and interfaces. General Summary: Work as part of a networking and wireless engineering team on tasks that consistently push the boundaries of performance, intelligence, and scalability. You will be working on advanced networking protocols, WiFi wireless systems, AI/ML-based traffic optimization, Linux-based data path solutions, and other features across our wireless access point and connectivity platforms. You will tackle challenges related to secure and high-performance wireless connectivity, intelligent network management, and the integration of AI/ML into real-world networking systems. If designing the intelligent, high-performance networks of the future excites you, then we share a common goal. Minimum Qualifications: • Bachelor's degree in Engineering, Information Systems, Computer Science, or related field and 2+ years of Software Engineering or related work experience. OR Master's degree in Engineering, Information Systems, Computer Science, or related field and 1+ year of Software Engineering or related work experience. OR PhD in Engineering, Information Systems, Computer Science, or related field. • 2+ years of academic or work experience with Programming Language such as C, C++, Java, Python, etc. Requirements: 3-5 years of experience in software feature development with a focus on networking, wireless technologies using C and C++programming languages. Understanding of Linux kernel internals and hands-on experience with low-level kernel programming, including writing and modifying network device drivers. Solid knowledge of core networking protocols including TCP/UDP/IPv4/IPv6/ARP/DHCP, and familiarity with the Linux network stack. Experience with WiFi/wireless LAN protocols and Ethernet device drivers is highly desirable. Hands-on experience developing and integrating AI/ML models and algorithms for networking applications is a strong plus. Knowledge of L2/L3/L4 packet forwarding schemes such as bridging/routing/OpenVSwitch/DPDK/VPP would help you stand out from others. Qualcomm is an equal opportunity employer.

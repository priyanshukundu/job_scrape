Press space or enter keys to toggle section visibility As an Associate, Fund Management Secondaries, you will be an important part of the finance team responsible for financial control, financial fund reporting, investor reporting and treasury management for all secondaries funds . You will oversee the preparation and review of fund financial statements in accordance with US GAAP, assess complex accounting and reporting matters arising from secondary investment transactions, and work closely with fund administrators, offshore teams and external auditors to support consistent accounting treatment and timely financial reporting. You will provide accounting and transaction support, drive management and corporate reporting, as well as provide thoughtful input to resolve technical accounting issues. You will be part of a growing team, working closely with colleagues, ensuring the team and business are set up for long term success. What you offer Press space or enter keys to toggle section visibility Self-motivated with the ability to work autonomously through complex structures in a fast-paced, collaborative environment and accustomed to working with a team, both in-person and virtually. Bachelor’s degree or equivalent and a CPA/CA designation, plus 5+ years of related work experience across public accounting and private sector accounting. Experience in private equity fund accounting or fund finance, with secondaries experience [strongly preferred], including oversight of fund administrators and complex fund structures. Highly motivated, able to work independently, and consistently apply diligence and care. Proactive in managing priorities and fostering a culture of improvement and strong controls. Demonstrable experience working on automation and / or digitalization efforts. Strong technical knowledge of US GAAP as applied to investment companies, including investment accounting, fair value measurement and financial statement disclosures, with demonstrated experience preparing and reviewing financial statements.

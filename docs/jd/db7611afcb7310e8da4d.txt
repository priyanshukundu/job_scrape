Job Description: Principal Security Software Engineer - SD-WAN Location (4 days a week): Sunnyvale, CA As a Principal Software Engineer, you will serve as the technical leader and chief architect for our next-generation Secure SD-WAN solutions. In this highly influential role, you will define the technical vision, drive architecture and design decisions, and lead the development of highly secure, scalable, and high-performance networking products. You will tackle complex engineering challenges across secure connectivity, advanced cryptography, VPN technologies, and large-scale packet processing. Working closely with engineering leadership, product management, and external customers, you will translate complex business and technical requirements into innovative solutions that shape the future of our Secure SD-WAN portfolio. This role combines strategic technical leadership with hands-on engineering. You will guide and mentor senior engineers, review code, establish architectural best practices, and drive critical engineering initiatives while remaining actively involved in software development. Key Responsibilities Technical Leadership & Architecture: Own and drive the architecture, design, and technical strategy for next-generation Secure SD-WAN products, including enterprise site-to-site and remote access VPN solutions. Secure Networking Innovation: Lead the development of advanced secure connectivity solutions leveraging IPSec, IKEv2, VPN technologies, and modern security protocols. Performance & Optimization: Drive high-performance packet processing and networking optimization using technologies such as DPDK, VPP, eBPF, and hardware cryptographic accelerators, including Intel QAT and AES-NI. Software Engineering Excellence: Provide hands-on technical leadership through code reviews, architectural assessments, design reviews, and implementation of critical software components. Technical Mentorship: Serve as a technical authority and trusted advisor to engineering teams, mentoring senior engineers and establishing best practices for software quality, security, scalability, and maintainability. Cross-Functional Collaboration: Partner with engineering leadership, VPs, product management, hardware, and QA teams to define technical direction and translate business requirements into robust engineering solutions. Security Architecture & Innovation: Research, evaluate, and integrate emerging security technologies, including quantum-safe cryptography, to strengthen product security and prepare for evolving cybersecurity threats. Vulnerability Management: Proactively analyze Common Vulnerabilities and Exposures (CVEs), assess security risks, and partner with product development teams to prioritize and implement effective remediation strategies. Security Testing & Compliance: Collaborate with external penetration testing firms, oversee security assessments, and drive vulnerability remediation throughout the secure software development lifecycle. Customer Engagement & Escalations: Work directly with external customers and internal stakeholders to address complex networking and security escalations, conduct threat analysis, and drive technical resolutions that maintain customer confidence. Technical Problem Solving: Lead the investigation and resolution of complex system-level, networking, performance, and security issues across enterprise-scale environments. Basic Qualifications 10+ years of software engineering experience, with a strong emphasis on secure networking, embedded systems, and Linux kernel development. Deep expertise in IPSec/VPN technologies, including IKEv2 protocols, tunnel modes, and advanced troubleshooting. Experience developing solutions using open-source networking and security tools such as StrongSwan, Libreswan, OpenVPN, or WireGuard. Extensive experience with hardware offloading for cryptographic operations, including Intel QAT and AES-NI. Advanced proficiency in C/C++ and multithreaded, system-level programming. Proven track record of architecting, leading, and delivering large-scale, secure networking products. Excellent communication, collaboration, and technical leadership skills. Preferred Qualifications Strong experience with networking frameworks such as DPDK, VPP, and eBPF. Knowledge of security protocols, including IPSec, TLS, and SSL. Experience with cryptographic libraries, algorithms, and accelerators, including OpenSSL, AES, RSA, ECC, Intel QAT, and AES-NI. Experience with FIPS 140-2/3 validation and Common Criteria certification for security products.

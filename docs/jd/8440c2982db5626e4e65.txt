Senior Software Engineer, Atlas Clusters Platform
MongoDB
Location: New York City
Link: https://www.mongodb.com/careers/job/?gh_jid=8406252

MongoDB is seeking a Senior Software Engineer to join the Atlas Clusters Platform team in NYC. The team is responsible for building MongoDB Atlas, our database-as-a-service offering and fastest growing product. Atlas allows users to deploy fault-tolerant, secure, globally distributed MongoDB clusters in just minutes. The team develops software to interface with the three major cloud providers (AWS, Azure, and GCP) in order to bring security, durability, availability, and performance to all MongoDB customers. We are looking to speak to candidates who are based in the NYC area and would like a hybrid or in-office working model.

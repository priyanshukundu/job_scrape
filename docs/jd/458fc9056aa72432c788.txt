We’re looking for a Senior Risk Analyst to help ensure our policies, procedures, and controls keep pace with evolving regulatory requirements. In this role, you’ll partner with business owners across Octane to identify gaps, drive improvements, and lead compliance initiatives that support our continued growth. Responsibilities: Evaluate and understand current policies & procedures and ensure they align with regulatory requirements. Evaluate and understand current controls and where gaps exist. Support and align regulatory requirements within Octane Business processes. Oversee all Committee coordination and documentation. Communicate with other team members and matrix partners to solve problems or recommend improvements. Follow-up with appropriate Business owners when issues are identified and follow up is required. Manage and maintain self-Identified issues. Lead compliance initiatives as required. Requirements: Bachelor’s degree 3+ years of experience in a financial services industry Preferred Skills: Experience with GRC software, specifically SAI360, preferred. Demonstrated knowledge of compliance requirements and regulatory expectations. Demonstrated knowledge of process QA assessment. Demonstrated understanding of Risk policy, policy change approval and implementation processes. Detail oriented, organized, energetic and action oriented. Strong leadership, collaboration and communication skills. Conflict resolution/management skills. Comfortable working in a dynamic and evolving environment. Strong change management and project management skills. Outstanding analytical and problem solving skills.

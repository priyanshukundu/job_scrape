Role Overview Electronic Execution Services (EES), a core business within Global Equities, delivers Scotiabank's electronic trading offering to institutional clients across Canada, the United States, and international markets. EES combines advanced trading technology, quantitative analytics, market structure expertise, and high-touch client service to provide innovative execution solutions across a broad range of equity trading workflows. As electronic trading continues to transform capital markets, EES is at the forefront of developing and operating sophisticated execution technologies, including algorithmic trading strategies, smart order routing, direct market access (DMA), FIX connectivity, trading analytics, and client-facing execution services. The team works closely with institutional clients, sales, trading, technology, compliance, operations, and external market participants to deliver best-in-class electronic trading capabilities. This role offers a unique opportunity to work at the intersection of trading, technology, market structure, and client engagement. Successful candidates will play a key role in supporting and enhancing Scotiabank's electronic trading platform while contributing to strategic initiatives that directly influence client growth, trading performance, and business expansion What You’ll Do Client Coverage & Electronic Trading Support • Provide exceptional service and support to institutional clients utilizing Scotiabank's electronic trading platform. • Partner with Sales Trading, Cash Trading, Prime Services, and Client Coverage teams to deliver seamless electronic trading solutions. • Monitor client trading activity and proactively identify opportunities to improve execution outcomes and client experience. • Develop a strong understanding of client workflows and collaborate with internal teams to deliver tailored solutions. Product Development & Business Strategy • Contribute to the design, enhancement, and rollout of electronic trading products, algorithms, routing strategies, and client-facing technology solutions. • Assist in evaluating new business opportunities, market initiatives, exchange offerings, and execution services to drive business growth. • Work closely with technology teams to prioritize product enhancements and ensure successful delivery of strategic initiatives. • Participate in the evaluation and integration of new trading venues, data products, and third-party technologies. Trading Technology & Automation • Leverage programming, data analytics, and automation skills to improve operational efficiency, monitoring, reporting, and client service workflows. • Develop tools, dashboards, and analytic solutions that support trading operations, client coverage, and business decision-making. • Assist in identifying, troubleshooting, and resolving issues across FIX connectivity, order routing, execution platforms, and related trading infrastructure. • Support ongoing enhancements to the firm's electronic trading ecosystem, including algorithmic trading and smart order routing technologies. Market Structure & Regulatory Expertise • Maintain current knowledge of Canadian, U.S., and global equity market structure developments, trading regulations, and industry trends. • Analyze the impact of regulatory and industry changes on electronic trading products, client workflows, and business strategy. • Support internal and external discussions relating to market structure initiatives, venue changes, and evolving execution practices. Operational Risk & Platform Oversight • Support the daily operation and oversight of electronic trading systems and client connectivity infrastructure. • Monitor electronic trading workflows and assist in maintaining a controlled, scalable, and resilient trading environment. • Partner with Compliance, Risk Management, Operations, and Technology teams to ensure adherence to regulatory requirements and internal governance standards. • Contribute to business continuity, operational resilience, and risk management initiatives across the EES platform. What Makes This Role Exciting • Direct exposure to institutional equity trading and electronic markets. • Opportunity to work on cutting-edge trading technology and execution products. • Meaningful ownership of projects with visibility across trading, technology, sales, and senior management. • Involvement in market structure developments and industry initiatives shaping the future of electronic trading. • Ability to influence client outcomes, business growth, and platform strategy. • Dynamic environment combining trading, technology, data analytics, and client interaction.

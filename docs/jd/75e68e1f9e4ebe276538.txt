Join us and build for travelers everywhere. Senior Data Scientist, EG Advertising The Product Team creates high-quality end-to-end experiences for travelers, partners, and Expedia Group. Our focus on customer-centric innovation enables us to develop products that build loyalty and repeat business. We're looking for a senior-level Data Scientist to provide deep analytics and decision support to the EG Advertising division to drive growth and achieve results for our travelers and partners. In this role, you will partner closely with teams across Expedia Group and contribute to many aspects of product development, including auction optimisation, bidding mechanisms, measurement and incrementality. In this role, you will: Apply advanced statistical, analytical, and machine learning techniques to solve complex business and product problems, translating findings into measurable outcomes. Design, build, and validate data models, experiments, and analytical solutions that inform product strategy, customer experiences, and operational decisions. Partner with product, engineering, and business stakeholders to frame ambiguous problems, define success measures, and communicate actionable insights. Develop scalable data science solutions across relevant domains, demonstrating technical fungibility and applying expertise to a range of products, services, and business challenges. Safely integrate and operate AI/ML-enabled solutions that improve outcomes, while evaluating model performance, limitations, risks, and ongoing effectiveness. Influence technical direction through rigorous analysis, data-driven decision making, and clear communication of complex concepts to both technical and non-technical audiences. Lead and mentor data scientists and engineers, fostering technical excellence and a culture of continuous learning. Co‑own with Product and business leaders the metric frameworks and decision workflows for your area Minimum Qualifications: Bachelor’s degree in data science, computer science, statistics, mathematics, engineering, economics, or a related quantitative field. 5+ years of professional experience applying data science, statistical analysis, experimentation, and machine learning methods to real-world products or business problems. Experience owning data science workstreams or solutions that span a product area, service, or multiple related business domains. Strong programming and analytical skills, including experience with data modeling, statistical inference, experimentation, machine learning, and communicating insights through effective visualizations or other decision-support tools. Familiarity with AI-driven systems, tools, or workflows and applying AI/ML concepts to real world products. You have demonstrated ability to mentor junior data scientists, balance speed, risk, and analytical rigor You have experience with advanced visualization techniques / best practices, and building scalable dashboards Preferred Qualifications: Master’s or doctoral degree in a quantitative discipline, or equivalent depth of applied data science experience. Experience operating data science or machine learning solutions at scale, including monitoring performance, improving reliability, and translating model outputs into measurable product or business outcomes. Demonstrated ability to shape analytical or machine learning architecture within a product, domain, or multi-service scope and to influence technical decisions across partner teams. Strong operational and analytical discipline, including experiment design, metric development, model evaluation, and using data to guide prioritization under ambiguity. Hands-on experience applying modern AI/ML approaches to production products, with the judgment to select appropriate methods, assess tradeoffs, and safely improve outcomes.

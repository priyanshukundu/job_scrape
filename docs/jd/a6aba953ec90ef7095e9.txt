With a career at The Home Depot, you can be yourself and also be part of something bigger. Position Purpose: The Salesforce Engineering team is responsible for scaling Home Depot's Pro CRM and enterprise Salesforce ecosystem, and for expanding our use of AI-driven development to accelerate delivery and modernize how we build, test, and migrate Salesforce solutions. The Staff Software Engineer is the technical anchor for the team, setting architecture direction for Salesforce development, integration, and release patterns, and partnering closely with the Engineering Manager, Product Manager, and Principal Engineer. This person owns hard technical decisions on complex Salesforce initiatives, drives disciplined adherence to PDLC/SDLC methodology, and balances delivery speed with long-term platform scalability, security, and maintainability. In this role you will: Lead end-to-end Salesforce solution design and development – Apex, Lightning Web Components (LWC), Flow, and declarative configuration – across Sales Cloud, Service Cloud, and custom Salesforce applications. Architect and build integrations between Salesforce and enterprise systems using REST/SOAP APIs, Platform Events, Bulk API, and middleware. Own the technical governance of code and configuration migration and release management across sandboxes and production orgs, ensuring adherence to PDLC/SDLC methodology, version control, and CI/CD best practices. Partner with Salesforce admins on user maintenance, licensing, and the security model – profiles, permission sets, and sharing rules – as well as ongoing platform administration. Bring AI into the daily engineering workflow, from AI-assisted development and code review to building AI-powered features directly into Salesforce experiences. Set the technical bar: lead architecture reviews, mentor engineers at every level, and raise the standard for code quality across the team. Partner directly with Product, IT leadership, and cross-functional engineering teams to turn ambiguous business requirements into scalable, shippable Salesforce solutions. Key Responsibilities: 50% Delivery and Execution – Develops, tests, deploys, and maintains Salesforce solutions with a clear understanding of the business value they provide; takes a broad, enterprise view when approaching integration and platform challenges; builds automated test suites to enable rapid, reliable deployment to production; takes on complex technical challenges with urgency and ownership, consistently delivering results even under tight timelines. 10% Learns and Grows – Actively pursues growth in Salesforce, AI, and cloud technologies through formal and informal channels; learns through both successful and failed experiments when tackling new integration or migration challenges. 20% Plans and Aligns – Creates new and better ways for the organization to scale its Salesforce platform; communicates clearly across technical and non-technical audiences; partners with the Product team to ensure user stories are developer-ready, well-scoped, and testable; collaborates within agile/PDLC processes; adapts approach to shifting business priorities. 20% Supports and Enables – Fields questions from Product, IT, and business teams on Salesforce capabilities; mentors engineers on Salesforce development standards and AI-assisted engineering practices; identifies platform gaps and proposes improvements to development and release processes.

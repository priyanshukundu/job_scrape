Job Description Technical Product Managers at U.S. Bank oversee the strategic product management of assigned technical product lines, including ServiceNow Integrated Risk Management (IRM) products and capabilities, in support of Enterprise goals, technology strategy, risk management objectives, and business needs. They engage key stakeholders to define, document, and prioritize features; ensure delivery of scalable and sustainable capabilities; and collaborate with engineering, architecture, business, risk, and agile teams. As a ServiceNow IRM Technical Product Manager, you will: Align the strategic vision for ServiceNow IRM with cross-functional stakeholders to shape outcome-based product roadmaps, product risk, investment priorities, and success measures. Drive ServiceNow IRM product development and enhancement through ownership of the backlog, prioritizing IRM capabilities, workflows, integrations, automation, AI opportunities, and technical debt to accelerate business outcomes and speed to market. Partner with Enterprise Risk, Technology, and other stakeholders to define and deliver product performance goals, including IRM adoption, user experience, platform effectiveness, scalability, and business outcomes. Own engagement for the assigned ServiceNow IRM portfolio across the SDLC, including product adoption, engineering collaboration, release planning, upgrades, and lifecycle management. Partner with engineering, architecture, security, risk, and business teams to promote ServiceNow IRM standards, reuse, configuration-first design, and sustainable solutions while effectively governing customization and technical complexity. Develop, analyze, monitor, and report ServiceNow IRM product and performance metrics to identify trends, adoption opportunities, operational improvements, and areas for continuous improvement. Leverage OKRs (Objectives and Key Results) to prioritize the ServiceNow IRM roadmap and connect technology investments to measurable Enterprise risk and business outcomes. Drive the evolution of ServiceNow IRM capabilities, including workflow automation, AI/GenAI enablement, integrations, data strategy, and governance, while balancing business demand, platform health, risk, and long-term maintainability. Basic Qualifications Technical Product Management experience Preferred Skills/Experience 5+ years of relevant experience in Technical Product Management, technology delivery, risk management, or related fields. Proven experience gathering and translating business requirements into actionable user stories, managing dependencies, and navigating roadblocks to drive successful delivery. Demonstrated ability to partner with cross-functional stakeholders to align technical solutions with business objectives. Understanding risk management processes, frameworks, and controls. Experience with ServiceNow, particularly Integrated Risk Management (IRM) capabilities. Familiarity with Archer governance, risk, and compliance (GRC) solutions. Familiarity with artificial intelligence (AI) technologies and their potential applications in process optimization, automation, and operational efficiency. Expertise on the critical interdependencies among product model system elements that help and hinder performance; ability to plan and design solutions that synchronize resources to achieve business results using business metrics, key performance indicators design systems, service blueprints, and reusability Technical know-how understands the product technical architecture and understands the economics of technical investments Demonstrates deep understanding of target customer through internal/external research (e.g., creation of personas, story maps, product chartering/mapping experience) Strong collaboration with software engineers to execute product vision and roadmap Preferred skills: product strategy, vision and planning, product discovery, product development, agile ways of working, enterprise technical alignment and performance measurement and optimization Location expectations This role requires working from a U.S. Bank location three (3) or more days per week.

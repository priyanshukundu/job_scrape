Associate Product Manager, Closings We are Lennar Lennar is one of the nation's leading homebuilders, dedicated to making an impact and creating an extraordinary experience for their Homeowners, Communities, and Associates by building quality homes and providing exceptional customer service, giving back to the communities in which we work and live in, and fostering a culture of opportunity and growth for our Associates throughout their career. Lennar has been recognized as a Fortune 500® company and consistently ranked among the top homebuilders in the United States. Join a Company that Empowers you to Build your Future As Product Manager for Road to Close, you will own developing and refining the technology supporting the closing experience for internal homebuyer concierges using Salesforce, and our homebuyers using the Lennar account on both mobile and web. The goal is to create a better closing experience for our customer, make our closing associates more efficient, standardize a complex operational process, and improve buyer communications. You will make sure every feature is fully designed, tested, and shipped with a consistent experience for both audiences, tailored to how each uses it. This is a hands-on role where you will drive the full agile process, from discovery and design scope through stories, sprints, QA, and release, and own each feature to completion. You will work closely with Design, Engineering, QA, Data, Operations, and the users. Your Responsibilities on the Team Own the Road to Close experience across Salesforce and the Lennar Account (app and Lennar.com), driving core features, task logic, notifications, and adoption. Own features end to end, from problem definition and design scope through development, testing, release, and post-launch measurement, until they are complete and performing. Partner with Design to define user flows, UX/UI requirements, and work through the easy paths and edge cases make a feature production-ready. Partner with Engineering to write clear user stories and acceptance criteria, groom and prioritize the backlog, plan sprints, and triage and prioritize bugs. Coordinate QA and user acceptance testing across devices, operating systems, and users and own release readiness. Lead customer research, including user interviews, usability testing, and surveys, to understand homebuyer and homeowner needs and validate designs before and after launch. Collect, synthesize, and act on user and customer feedback from dedicated sessions, service tickets, and customer care teams, turning insights into roadmap priorities. Define and maintain the Road to Close metrics dashboard, including adoption, task completion, logins, and data quality, and use it to drive weekly decisions with business and sales leaders. Partner with mortgage and title teams to consolidate tools into the Lennar Account, making closing easier for customers Plan and support phased rollouts across divisions, including training materials, release notes, field shadowing, and adoption tracking, working with division leaders and concierge teams. Communicate status, risks, and trade-offs clearly to stakeholders and leadership, applying a lean, test-and-learn approach to inform product direction quickly. Requirements Bachelor's degree required. 1-3 years of Product Management experience, with hands-on ownership of a consumer-facing product. Strong working knowledge of agile delivery, including writing user stories and acceptance criteria, backlog grooming, sprint planning, and bug triage. Detail-oriented approach to edge cases, testing, and quality, with a drive to own work through to completion. Experience mapping, standardizing, and improving complex operational workflows, and comfort with ambiguity as the product expands to new divisions. Excellent communication skills, with the ability to align cross-functional teams and explain trade-offs clearly. Analytical mindset with experience using product analytics and experimentation to prioritize and evaluate product decisions. Familiarity with CRM platforms such as Salesforce is a plus. Experience in homebuilding, real estate, mortgage, or other complex consumer transactions is a plus. Humble, hungry to make an impact, and proactive in taking initiative. Willingness to travel 20% for field visits and user shadowing. What is Product at Lennar like? We believe in the lean startup philosophy. It is important that we test and learn quickly to inform our product strategy and direction. You will report directly to our Director of Product and will work closely with other product managers to develop a vision for your specific product area.

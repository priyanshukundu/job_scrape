Position Summary The AVP, Underwriting, will be responsible for the technical underwriting of property reinsurance treaties. Successful candidates will have strong property treaty analytical skills, with an established ability to assess risk into clear recommendations, supported by accurate interpretation of modeling and pricing methodologies. This individual will work closely with the broader underwriting team to deliver coordinated support to the organization’s Latin American and Caribbean clients. The AVP will support marketing and travel engaging with broker and clients and contribute to thought leadership, representing RenaissanceRe’s sector views and underwriting approach. KEY RESPONSIBILITIES: Analyze, model and make risk decisions on assumed property treaty submissions Support underwriting decisions for the assumed portfolio; analyzing and presenting submission information Use third party vendor models as well as proprietary tools and systems to effectively evaluate, compare, and report on reinsurance transactions; interpret and challenge the modelled results and include relevant non-modelled data in the assessment of risk. Make informed underwriting decisions in alignment with RenaissanceRe’s risk appetite and tolerance levels Protect and grow strong broker and client relationships, traveling to maintain strategic engagement, leading effective meetings, and communicating our underwriting requirements and philosophies Support enhancements and development of internal risk and underwriting systems including exposure management, underwriting and portfolio management and reporting tools. Support ad hoc projects such as model research, benchmarking studies and portfolio analysis. Candidate Qualifications: 4+ years of experience working in technical underwriting or broking roles at a sophisticated re/insurer or reinsurance broker, with deep knowledge of insurance and reinsurance markets Experience focused on property pro rata and excess of loss treaties in Latin America and Caribbean. International markets experience a plus. Strong understanding of technical concepts, knowledge of relevant vendor catastrophe models (e.g. RMS, AIR, ERN), and ability to critically evaluate complex model results. A reputation for strong analytical, quantitative, and problem-solving skills, with the ability to synthesize complex information into clear recommendations. Excellent mathematical, statistical, and problem-solving skills Fluent in Spanish and English with strong written and verbal communication skills; ability to communicate effectively across all levels and engage in strategic negotiations. Portuguese a plus.

Start your Voyage – Apply Now Get to Know the Opportunity: This role is part of the Voya Investment Management Entry Level Analyst Program, beginning in July 2027. The program is designed to develop the careers of entry-level employees and provides a unique opportunity to begin an investment career while gaining hands-on experience, on-the-job development, and formal training. This entry-level role will support the investment team across portfolio analysis, equity research, and investment communications while gaining exposure to the full equity research lifecycle.The Equity Research Associate will work alongside senior investment professionals to conduct company and industry research, analyze portfolio and market data, maintain financial models, and support the development of investment and product content. The role will also partner with portfolio managers, client portfolio managers, marketing, product, distribution, and other internal teams to help communicate Voya’s equity strategies and investment insights to internal and external audiences. This role is based in our New York office and follows an Office Centric approach requiring on average 3-4 days onsite each week. The Contributions You’ll Make: Work alongside Senior Analysts and other investment professionals to conduct company and industry research supporting the Fundamental Equity investment team. Analyze and synthesize relevant information from industry conferences, company earnings calls, sell-side research reports, portfolio data, and broader market data. Create, maintain, and update financial models to support company and investment analysis. Gather and analyze portfolio and market data to help develop client commentaries, presentation materials, and other investment, marketing, and communication pieces. Partner with marketing, product, and distribution teams to develop and maintain high-quality content for internal and external audiences that clearly communicates the investment team’s structure, philosophy, process, strategy, and performance. Help ensure the quality and integrity of client-related investment communications, portfolio data, and marketing materials. Partner with sales and distribution teams to respond to client and prospective client inquiries and provide specialized investment input for requests for proposals. Support client-facing team members by producing portfolio-specific reports and completing ad hoc analytical projects. Build strong working relationships across the Fundamental Equity investment team and with internal partners throughout Voya Investment Management. Perform other related duties as assigned. Minimum Knowledge & Experience: Applicants should be graduating with an undergraduate degree in December 2026 or spring/summer 2027. Pursuing a bachelor's degree with accounting coursework High school diploma or GED. Passion for finance with a demonstrated interest in investing and the stock market. Strong analytical skills and attention to detail, including the ability to analyze, organize, and visualize data to help explain the performance of Voya’s equity strategies. Excellent verbal and written communication skills. Strong interpersonal skills with the ability to collaborate effectively with individuals across the organization. Self-starter with the ability to work both independently and as part of a team. Strong organizational and time-management skills with the ability to manage multiple priorities and meet deadlines in a fast-paced environment. Exceptional Microsoft Excel and PowerPoint skills. Preferred Knowledge & Experience: Relevant internship experience in Investment Management or a related Finance field. Prior experience with financial modeling and advanced proficiency in Excel. Bloomberg experience. Strong and demonstrated fluency with varied AI tools. How This Role Aligns to Our Core Four: Voya’s Core Four guide how we work and the outcomes we deliver, and this role brings them to life through disciplined investment research, thoughtful analysis, strong collaboration, and high-quality communication. Drive Higher Performance Through Our People: Collaborate closely with Senior Analysts, portfolio managers, client portfolio managers, marketing, product, sales, and distribution partners to share insights and deliver high-quality investment research and communications.

Your Role As a Senior Financial Analyst, you will be a key member of Aptiv’s Corporate Finance organization, supporting Corporate Staff reporting and serving as a liaison between the Financial Planning & Analysis and Accounting teams. This highly visible role requires a strong understanding of the Income Statement, Balance Sheet and Cash Flow Statement and how they interact. You will partner with regional and functional leaders, Business Unit Finance teams and senior leadership to provide financial insights and support informed business decisions. Roles and Responsibilities Support the annual budget, business plan and monthly forecasting processes. Consolidate and report Corporate Staff P&L and cash flow results. Partner with Accounting throughout the close process and analyze actual results and journal entries against expectations. Provide guidance to regional and functional leaders on actual and forecasted financial performance. Partner with Business Unit Finance teams to analyze cost allocations and segment financial results. Analyze historical and forecasted spending and headcount trends. Prepare foreign exchange sensitivity analyses for international entities. Standardize and automate reporting processes to improve accuracy and workflow efficiency. Support Tax with legal entity reporting. Prepare ad hoc financial reporting and analysis for Finance and senior leadership. Your Background Bachelor’s degree in Accounting, Finance or a related field. Approximately five years of experience in Accounting, Financial Planning & Analysis or a related finance function. Strong understanding of the Income Statement, Balance Sheet and Cash Flow Statement. Strong analytical, financial modeling and problem-solving capabilities. Advanced Microsoft Excel skills, including financial modeling and consolidations. Experience with budgeting, forecasting, variance analysis and management reporting. Experience analyzing foreign exchange impacts and preparing sensitivity analyses. Strong written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to collaborate across global teams and communicate with senior leadership. Experience working in a global manufacturing environment is preferred. Experience with SAP and Hyperion or similar financial planning and reporting systems is preferred.

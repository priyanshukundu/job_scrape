Role Overview: The Operations Data Engineering & Analytics role is responsible for delivering data, analytics and automated solutions that enable better business decisions and improve operational performance. This position will partner with business teams and leaders to understand needs, identify opportunities and translate strategic priorities into practical, data-driven solutions while providing subject matter expertise and recommendations that inform the broader data and analytics strategy. A successful candidate will have the ability to combine strong business analytics expertise with hands-on technical capabilities, including developing solutions using advanced automation, scripting and data analytics tools and will work in partnership with technology and analytics teams to effectively leverage, develop and support a modern cloud-based data environment. Primary Responsibilities Develop and enhance dashboards, data models, scorecards, metrics, self-service analytics capabilities and reporting to support operational and strategic business decisions. Partner with business leaders and stakeholders to understand priorities and objectives; identify opportunities to improve processes, increase productivity, reduce operational risk and translate business challenges into practical data and analytic solutions. Identify and automate manual, repetitive and/or inefficient processes using technologies such as Power Automate, Power BI, Power Query, PowerShell, Python, SQL and VBA Editor. Partner with Technology and Data teams to develop and support a modern cloud-based data environment; ensure solutions are supported by reliable, accessible and appropriately governed data. Identify data quality and accessibility issues and contribute to data definitions, ownership, stewardship and governance practices that improve trust in business data. Provide business, analytical and technical insights that inform the broader data and analytics strategy and roadmap. Solid knowledge and understanding of AI concepts and technologies including their potential application, limitations and implications for analytics and business processes. Translate complex data and analytical findings into clear recommendations for presentation to stakeholders, business leaders and executives. Work effectively across business, technology and analytics teams to deliver solutions and influence outcomes. Challenge inefficient or outdated approaches to reporting, analysis and business process and introduce more effective ways of working. Measure and communicate the business value of implemented solutions while ensuring that solutions are appropriately documented, maintainable and sustainable. Bachelor’s degree or equivalent experience in a quantitative field (e.g., Mathematics, Actuarial, Data Science, Business Analytics or a related discipline). 10+ years of progressive experience in business analytics, data analytics, business intelligence, automation, data strategy, or related disciplines. Insurance experience and/or Management Consulting experience a plus Demonstrates understanding of business objectives and focuses analytics and solutions on meaningful business outcomes. Able to investigate complex questions/issues, identify patterns and translate data into actionable insights. Hand on experience developing analytical and automated solutions leveraging Python, VBA Editor, PowerShell, SQL and/or similar technologies. Advanced experience in data modeling, dashboard development, visualization tools and self-service analytics. Experienced in the development and maintenance of cloud-based data environments such as Snowflake and Synapse. Solid understanding of AI tools and technology and can recognize where they may create value. Demonstrated ability to influence stakeholders, contribute to strategic direction and collaborate effectively across business, technology and data teams.

Overview The Sponsor Finance group at East West Bank provides credit and banking solutions that support the activities of middle market private equity sponsors and their portfolio companies, as well as privately held businesses. We support our clients through complex transaction scenarios including change of control, add-on acquisitions, corporate divestitures, refinancings, and recapitalizations. The team is industry agnostic and provides senior secured financings that include cash flow revolver, cash flow term loan, asset-focused, unitranche, and senior/sub structures. The Sponsor Finance team is responsible for all phases of the credit process, including sourcing, structuring, underwriting, documenting, closing, and ongoing credit monitoring. We are seeking an analyst that will be an integral part of the Sponsor Finance team supporting our underwriting, portfolio management, origination, and customer service activities, with opportunities to expand responsibilities and professional skills, based on performance. As an East West Bank employee, you will be part of a growing and stable organization that provides career path development opportunities while serving a growing and profitable market. Responsibilities Perform ongoing due diligence, financial statement spreading, analysis, and monitoring of existing portfolio borrowers. Analyze potential new borrower relationships, completing or assisting with diligence, financial modeling, financial statement review, industry analysis, customer interface. Assist in preparation of credit previews and formal committee and approval documents. Work with other bank departments on loan closing and monitoring. Assist with daily operations, including loan advances, deposits, treasury management and other customer service needs.

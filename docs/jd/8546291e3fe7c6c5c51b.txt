Meet the team: We are seeking an experienced Lead, R&D Strategic Finance and FinOps for a newly created individual contributor role supporting R&D and Product Development. This role combines strategic finance, engineering economics, and cloud FinOps to bring greater financial insight to technical and product decisions. The successful candidate will turn complex information into clear recommendations, challenge assumptions, and help high impact teams make sound business and investment decisions. Where you come in: Cloud FinOps Ownership: You partner with R&D Data Platform Engineering to oversee and analyze spend and token usage across cloud infrastructure (Google Cloud, AWS, Microsoft Azure), AI, and SaaS to manage budgets, forecasts, long-term models, consumption analysis, cost allocation, tagging, usage optimization, committed use discounts, reporting, and financial governance. Research and Development Decision Support: You evaluate product, software, hardware, infrastructure, and manufacturing alternatives through business cases, cost models, scenario analysis, sensitivity analysis, and short- and long-term cost assessments, using ROI, NPV, IRR, and TCO. Product and Manufacturing Economics : You model the financial effects of design, process, automation, scale, yield, capacity, make-versus-buy, and technology-selection decisions, linking early product development engineering choices to downstream high-volume manufacturing economics, including COGS, standard cost, bill of materials (BOM), and unit economics. Program Investment Partnership: You collaborate with Engineering Program and Project Management teams to support program financial views, prioritization, resource allocation, investment decisions, risk identification, and repeatable frameworks for evaluating alternatives earlier in development. Cross-Functional Collaboration: You establish strong relationships with supporting corporate functions (Legal, Sourcing, HR) in conjunction with business stakeholders for governance and management of resources and vendors. What makes you successful: You bring strong financial analysis, modeling, forecasting, and business case development skills to the table. You have demonstrated experience managing or analyzing Google Cloud Platform costs from a financial or FinOps perspective. You possess experience providing financial and business case creation for product engineering, IT, product development, or R&D organizations. You understand and have reported on both software and hardware economics. Your communication and stakeholder management skills enable you to effectively engage technical and operational leaders at multiple levels. You communicate complex financial concepts simply and effectively to non-financial audiences. You understand technical discussions and translate them into financial implications and storytelling without needing to be the technical expert. You have strong problem-solving skills with the ability to ask the right business and technical questions to team members. You are comfortable operating independently and developing analyses without a predefined template or model to follow. You have a bachelor's degree in finance, accounting, economics, business, engineering, computer science, information technology, software or cloud engineering, or a related field. You have hands on experience with SQL, BigQuery, and data visualization tools such as Power BI, Tableau, or Looker, plus ERP systems such as SAP or Oracle.

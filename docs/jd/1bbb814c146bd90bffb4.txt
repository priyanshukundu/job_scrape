THE OPPORTUNITY At Element Fleet Management, we're looking for a Billing Business Analyst who can turn complex data into meaningful insights that improve billing accuracy, operational efficiency, and the client experience. In this role, you'll identify trends, investigate billing variances, develop reporting solutions, and partner with teams across the business to solve problems before they become client concerns. Your analytical expertise will help us strengthen our processes and deliver a more proactive, transparent billing experience. WHAT YOU WILL OWN Billing Analytics: Build and maintain dashboards, reports, and analytical tools to identify billing trends, variances, disputes, and improvement opportunities. Proactive Client Insights: Analyze billing activity, identify emerging risks, and support client-facing teams with clear explanations and recommendations. Root-Cause Analysis: Investigate recurring billing issues, data quality concerns, and process gaps to recommend sustainable solutions. Performance Reporting: Develop KPIs, operational scorecards, and leadership-ready reporting to measure service performance and identify opportunities for improvement. Cross-Functional Collaboration: Partner with Billing Operations, Finance, IT/Data, Client Success, and other stakeholders to resolve issues and improve reporting capabilities. Continuous Improvement: Identify opportunities to automate repetitive analysis, standardize reporting, and improve operational efficiency. YOU SHOULD CONSIDER THIS ROLE IF: You enjoy working with data and translating complex information into practical business recommendations. You're naturally curious, detail-oriented, and motivated to understand why problems occur. You thrive in collaborative environments and can communicate analytical findings to different audiences. You enjoy improving processes and finding smarter, more efficient ways of working. You're comfortable balancing multiple priorities while maintaining accuracy and delivering meaningful results. YOU SHOULD BE ABLE TO TELL US About your experience in business analysis, data analysis, billing operations, finance operations, client service operations, or a related field. How you've used Excel, dashboards, reporting tools, databases, or operational systems to analyze data and identify trends. Examples of your analytical, problem-solving, and root-cause analysis capabilities. How you've translated business questions into data requirements, insights, and actionable recommendations. How you've communicated complex findings and collaborated with stakeholders across different teams. Experience in billing, invoicing, finance shared services, fleet, leasing, or client service operations would be valuable. Familiarity with tools such as Power BI, Tableau, SQL, Salesforce, Xcelerate, Mainframe, Alteryx, or Excel Power Query is also an asset, as is experience supporting automation or process improvement initiatives. WHY ELEMENT At Element Fleet Management, your work has a direct impact on how we serve our clients and improve the way we operate. You'll have the opportunity to collaborate across teams, influence meaningful improvements, and help build scalable reporting and analytical solutions. We value curiosity, collaboration, and people who turn ideas into action.

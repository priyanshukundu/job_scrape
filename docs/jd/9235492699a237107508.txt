As a Senior Data Engineer within Schwab Technology Services Insights, you will lead the design and delivery of scalable enterprise data warehouse and data integration solutions that support analytics, reporting, and informed decision-making. You will combine hands-on engineering expertise with technical leadership to develop reliable data pipelines, dimensional and relational data models, and data governance controls that strengthen data quality, lineage, security, and auditability. Working across business, architecture, product, governance, quality assurance, and engineering teams, you will translate complex requirements into supportable technical solutions and guide work from design through production. You will also establish engineering standards, lead design and code reviews, resolve production issues, coach engineers, and help manage delivery and technology risks. Your problem-solving, collaboration, and adaptability will support continuous improvement and Schwab’s ongoing modernization of its data platforms. What you have Required qualifications 8+ years of experience in data engineering, enterprise data integration, or data warehousing, including technical leadership responsibilities. Advanced hands-on experience with Informatica, including IDMC, IICS, or substantial PowerCenter experience. Advanced SQL experience supporting complex data transformations, source analysis, reconciliation, performance optimization, troubleshooting, and production validation. Experience designing conceptual, logical, and physical data models, including fact and dimension structures, slowly changing dimensions, and historical processing patterns. Experience establishing data governance controls for data quality, metadata, lineage, ownership, access, privacy, retention, auditability, and regulatory compliance. Experience translating business requirements into technical designs, source-to-target mappings, transformation rules, data models, and testing strategies. Experience designing supportable batch data solutions with monitoring, restartability, exception handling, failure recovery, and audit controls. Demonstrated technical leadership, problem-solving, and communication skills across business, engineering, governance, architecture, and distributed teams.

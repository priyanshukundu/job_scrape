Role: Associate, Software Engineer – Mutual Funds Trading Engineering Division: Asset & Wealth Management Function: Engineering Role Overview The Mutual Funds Trading Engineering team is seeking an Associate Software Engineer to help build and modernize the technology supporting and scaling the end to end Mutual Funds trading and settlement business for Wealth Management. The role will focus on developing scalable components and contributing to the strategic uplifts, the Wealth Management platform supporting Mutual Fund order entry, routing, contract and settlement processing, and trade booking across advisory, brokerage and managed client businesses. The successful candidate will work closely with global Mutual Fund Operations, Business users, Product, Compliance & Risk and cross-engineering teams to deliver platform enhancements, new business capabilities and improvements to the long-term architecture. Mutual Funds Trading Engineering has a significant roadmap covering new fund capabilities, remediation of critical technology risks and modernization of the end-to-end Mutual Funds trading platform. The role will contribute directly to improving the platform's scalability and long-term engineering architecture while enabling new business requirements. Key Responsibilities Design, develop and maintain software supporting Mutual Funds trading and settlement workflows. Build scalable components as part of the strategic modernization of the legacy Mutual Funds trading platform. Translate business requirements into robust technical solutions. Enhance and optimize existing application components and code. Deliver new business and product requirements, including capabilities supporting new fund rollouts. Address Technology Risks and operational resilience requirements. Investigate and resolve end user queries and implement sustainable fixes. Partner closely with global Mutual Fund Operations, business users and other Engineering teams. Required Skills / Experience Strong software engineering and programming fundamentals in full-stack development for 3-5 years ( UX design & developments skills are additional edge) Hands-on experience developing applications using Java, relation or NoSQL databases and RESTful/micro-services Strong problem-solving and analytical skills. Good understanding of algorithms, data structures and software design principles. Experience working collaboratively across Engineering and business/operations teams. Ability to work effectively on both strategic platform development and BAU production requirements. Preferred Qualifications (Optional) Experience with trading, financial-services or transaction-processing platforms. Exposure to Mutual Funds, securities trading, order management, settlement or related workflows. Experience modernizing or uplifting legacy technology platforms. Familiarity with technology-risk remediation and large-scale production systems.

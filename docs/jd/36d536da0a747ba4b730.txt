Introduction to Team Our Technology Team partners with teams across Expedia Group to create innovative products, services, and tools to deliver high-quality experiences for travelers, partners, and our employees. A singular technology platform powered by data and machine learning provides secure, differentiated, and personalized experiences that drive loyalty and traveler satisfaction. This Software Development Engineer II role is part of the Service Data team which sits within our Software Engineering & Architecture division. The team builds reliable, scalable services and data products that unify Traveler, Partner, Booking, and Service attributes and metrics, providing governed, low-latency access for servicing, analytics, and decision-making. In this role, you will implement and enhance business-critical services across the software development lifecycle, helping deliver simple, reliable solutions that meet customer and business needs. In this role, you will: Design, develop, test, deploy, and maintain batch and real-time ETL pipelines that produce reliable domain datasets for partners and decision-makers. Build scalable backend services, APIs, data models, and frontend applications that deliver actionable insights to stakeholders. Apply software design principles, algorithms, data structures, design patterns, and distributed-systems practices to develop fault-tolerant, maintainable solutions. Lead small, well-defined projects while contributing to complex, cross-team initiatives and aligning technical solutions with business needs. Identify and resolve inefficiencies across systems and development processes through testing, monitoring, observability, documentation, and effective debugging. Collaborate through peer reviews, knowledge sharing, stakeholder engagement, and experimentation while safely integrating and operating AI/ML-enabled solutions that improve outcomes. Minimum Qualifications: Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science or a related technical field; or equivalent related professional experience. 2+ years of relevant professional experience. Experience developing software in a team environment using Agile practices, version control, CI/CD, and AI-enabled tools or workflows. Experience delivering solutions within a defined service or project scope, including system design, low-level design, API design, data modeling, testing, deployment, and ongoing maintenance. Proficiency in at least one of Python, Scala, Kotlin, or Java, with familiarity in other languages; experience with distributed systems, relational and NoSQL databases, Apache Spark, Confluent Kafka, the Spring Framework, and common API integration patterns. Preferred Qualifications: Experience designing and operating scalable batch and real-time data platforms using stateful stream-processing technologies such as Kafka Streams, Java KafkaConsumer/KafkaProducer, or Faust. Experience delivering reliable AWS solutions with fault tolerance, observability, automated testing, and production monitoring. Experience contributing across the technical stack, including backend and frontend frameworks such as Node.js and React.js, and adapting across related engineering domains. Demonstrated ability to use data-driven analysis to identify system or process inefficiencies and improve software delivery, maintainability, and operational performance. Experience applying AI/ML concepts and AI-enabled engineering workflows to real-world data products, including safely integrating AI/ML solutions that improve engineering outcomes.

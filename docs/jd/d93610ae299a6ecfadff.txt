Job Description Summary About the Role As a Senior Software Engineer, you'll help shape the next generation of MRI technology used by clinicians and patients around the world. This is an opportunity to work on complex software systems that directly power medical imaging equipment, solving challenging problems at the intersection of software, hardware, and healthcare. You'll design, develop, test, and support software components for GE HealthCare MR scanners, collaborating closely with software engineers, systems engineers, product leaders, and domain experts. Your work will contribute to reliable, high-quality solutions that help improve patient outcomes and advance healthcare innovation. This role is ideal for experienced software engineers who enjoy solving complex technical challenges, working in Linux environments, and developing software that interfaces with physical devices in highly regulated industries. This position is based in Waukesha, WI and requires regular onsite collaboration with engineering teams. Candidates should be comfortable working closely with cross-functional teams in an in-person environment. Job Description What You'll Do Design, develop, test, and maintain software components and features for GE HealthCare MR scanners. Collaborate closely with representative users, feature leads, systems engineers, and subject matter experts to define and implement innovative solutions. Develop software within Agile development practices and a continuous integration environment. Apply modern software engineering principles including software architecture, design, testing, security, scalability, and maintainability. Contribute to the development and support of distributed and microservices-based applications. Investigate and resolve complex technical issues across software and system environments. Support continuous improvement initiatives across development, sustaining, and support organizations. Partner with domain experts to learn and apply complex healthcare and imaging system knowledge. Write clear, maintainable, well-tested code that meets quality and performance standards. Share knowledge, mentor team members, and contribute to a collaborative engineering culture. What Will Help You Succeed Curiosity and a passion for learning new technologies and approaches. Strong problem-solving and troubleshooting skills. Ability to break down complex technical challenges and develop practical solutions. Strong communication and collaboration skills in cross-functional environments. Ability to balance multiple priorities and deliver on commitments. Ownership mindset with a focus on quality, accountability, and continuous improvement.

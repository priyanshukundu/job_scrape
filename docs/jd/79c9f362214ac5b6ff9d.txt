Senior Software Engineer - Analytics Platform - 21026 - Bloomberg Senior Software Engineer - Analytics Platform Location New York Business Area Engineering and CTO Ref # 10052820 Description & Requirements The Analytics Platform department builds the distributed infrastructure that powers Bloomberg's analytics ecosystem. Our systems process more than 700 billion requests every day, providing the foundation that enables Bloomberg applications, the Terminal, Enterprise products, and analytics services to access structured data and real-time content reliably at global scale. As part of the team, you'll help modernize one of Bloomberg's most critical platforms by evolving a decades-old monolithic architecture into a cloud-ready, service-oriented ecosystem. You'll work on large-scale distributed systems spanning high-performance gateways, workload management, query execution, developer platforms, APIs, authorization, and core backend services. The focus is on solving challenging problems in scalability, low latency, reliability, resource management, and operational excellence while building reusable platforms that enable engineering teams across Bloomberg. We're looking for engineers who enjoy designing robust distributed systems, optimizing performance at massive scale, and building infrastructure that other engineers depend on. If you're excited by high-throughput systems, complex architectural challenges, and writing software with company-wide impact, you'll find opportunities to work across multiple layers of Bloomberg's analytics platform and help shape its next generation.

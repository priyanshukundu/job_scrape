As a Markets New Activity Risk Lead Analyst, you will play a central role in assessing the risks and controls behind every new product, transaction, and strategic initiative launched across Citi's Markets business. Sitting within the first line of defense risk function, you will review New Activity Committee submissions, challenge control owners, and confirm that new business is supported by sound operational processes before it goes live. Your assessments will directly shape how Citi manages emerging risk across its Markets Operations functions. Responsibilities Review and assess New Activity Committee submissions for new Markets products, transactions, and strategic investments, identifying which Markets Operations functions and support areas are affected. Challenge the risks and controls embedded in supporting processes, flagging where further follow-up or escalation is required. Coordinate with operational, technology, and functional teams to confirm new business is supported by adequate controls before it goes live. Track communications and outcomes across all New Activity Committee assessments, maintaining accurate and current documentation. Produce qualitative and quantitative reporting on new activity review activity for Operations Control management. Present assessment findings and escalate unresolved risks ahead of New Activity Committee meetings. Partner with Audit, Compliance, and Risk colleagues to resolve findings and support control framework enhancements. Mentor junior analysts and share risk assessment expertise as part of the team's day-to-day delivery. Required Qualifications & Skills 5 or more years of experience in a financial institution, internal audit, or risk management role, with a focus on identifying and implementing operational controls. Working knowledge of Markets operational functions, processes, and products. Ability to assess and challenge risks and controls across multiple business and support functions. Strong analytical skills, combining quantitative and qualitative judgment to evaluate complex issues. Proven organizational skills, with the ability to manage multiple assessments and adapt to shifting priorities. Clear verbal and written communication skills, with experience presenting findings to stakeholders and management. Proficiency in Microsoft Excel and PowerPoint for reporting and presentations. Beneficial Skills & Qualifications Familiarity with SharePoint for managing documentation and tracking workflows. A Master's degree in a related field such as finance, business, or risk management. What We Offer Working on Citi's Markets Operations risk team gives you a front-row seat to how new products and strategies come to life, backed by the development support and benefits to match your growth as a risk professional. A hybrid working arrangement, with 3 days in the office and 2 days working remotely each week. Exposure to global collaboration across Citi's Markets businesses and control functions. Structured learning and development programs to build your risk, controls, and leadership expertise. Wellbeing support and resources to help you manage work-life balance. Financial wellbeing programs and family support benefits.

About the team and the role Goldin, an eBay company, is seeking a diligent Accounting Associate to support its growing accounting operations. This role will focus on partnership accounting, consignor advances, account reconciliations, and month-end close activities. The ideal candidate will possess a solid accounting background, strong analytical abilities, and the capacity to handle detailed financial records in a fast-paced environment. This role will collaborate closely with the Accounting department to guarantee accurate financial reporting, maintain supporting schedules, and enhance accounting procedures. What you will accomplish Partnership Accounting Maintain accounting schedules for strategic partnership agreements, including sponsorships, minimum guarantees, inventory commitments, and revenue-sharing arrangements. Track partnership payments, contractual obligations, recoupments, and outstanding balances. Support journal entries, accruals, and reconciliations linked to partnership activities. Coordinate with internal teams to obtain supporting documentation and resolve discrepancies. Consignor Advances Track and reconcile consignor advances, including new advances, repayments, and recoupments. Maintain accurate advance schedules and monitor outstanding balances. Reconcile advance activity against consignor transactions, auction proceeds, and accounting records. Identify discrepancies and assist with resolving unapplied or outstanding balances. Prepare monthly advance rollforwards and supporting reconciliations. Assist with monitoring aging advances and outstanding commitments. Month-End Closing and Accounting Prepare journal entries, accruals, and account reconciliations as part of the monthly and quarterly closing activities. Reconcile general ledger accounts and investigate discrepancies. Maintain supporting schedules and documentation for financial statements. Support balance sheet reconciliations, account analysis, and variance investigations. Support audit requests and internal control requirements. Ensure assigned accounting tasks are completed accurately and within established deadlines. Process Improvements and Systems Find opportunities to improve manual tracking, reconciliations, and accounting workflows. Assist with accounting system enhancements and the transition to SAP S/4HANA. Support data validation, testing, and reconciliation between accounting systems. Maintain accurate accounting records and standardized documentation. What you will bring Required Bachelor's degree in Accounting, Finance, or a related field, or equivalent professional experience. 1–3 years of accounting or relevant financial operations experience. Knowledge of general ledger accounting, journal entries, and account reconciliations. Proficiency in Microsoft Excel, including pivot tables and lookup functions. Strong attention to detail, organizational skills, and ability to manage time effectively. Ability to investigate discrepancies and work with large volumes of financial data. Strong communication skills and willingness to collaborate across teams. Preferred Experience handling month-end close and balance sheet reconciliations. Experience using QuickBooks, SAP, or other accounting systems. Familiarity with advances, contractual commitments, or payment reconciliations. Experience in e-commerce, marketplaces, or transaction-intensive environments. Exposure to accounting system implementations or process improvements.

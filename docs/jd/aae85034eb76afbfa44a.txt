Research Associate Fiduciary Trust International's Research team is hiring a Research Associate to join the team in New York City (One Madison Avenue). The research team works with ultra-high net worth and institutional clients to create custom multi-asset class investment portfolios and bring them a full suite of investment advisory services, including guidance on sustainable investing. As part of the Research team the candidate will support due diligence, sourcing, and monitoring efforts while gaining exposure to the full investment selection and portfolio management process. Main ongoing responsibilities will include creating quarterly updates of investments, supporting the due diligence of investment funds, and sustainable investing research. The Research Associate will work across asset classes, gaining exposure to a variety of traditional and sustainable investing strategies. In addition to general research tasks, the Research Associate will also have routine responsibilities that are data-management focused. The ideal candidate has a desire to explore the investment management business, including the practice of sustainable investing; has a strong initial understanding of the markets; is highly organized; and, is detail-oriented. The candidate can be a recent graduate with a BA or BS degree or a professional with 1 to 2 years’ experience. The candidate may have previous investment experience through their current role or past internships, but it is not a requirement. What are the ongoing responsibilities of a Research Associate?

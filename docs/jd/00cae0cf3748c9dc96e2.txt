Job Description: Job Description - Intro Broadcom’s Mainframe Software Division (MSD) is looking for a Software Engineer with a talent for developing software and contributing to a culture dedicated to quality, security, and a smooth user experience. This role focuses on our non-mainframe applications, with opportunities to expand your experience into mainframe software. This role is in the Mainframe DevOps value stream, as a software engineer for one of our flagship products, Gen (formerly known as COOL:Gen or CA Gen). Gen is a low-code, model-driven development environment for enterprise applications. Gen enables global enterprises to generate and deploy applications in a single, integrated development environment for multiple platforms. We value a lean and flat organization, with team members who work in-office, for a more collaborative culture that focuses on execution and delivering value. #BroadcomSoftware The Opportunity As a Software Engineer (ICB 3), you will focus on the development, quality, and security of Gen. This is a hands-on technical role: you will write code, contribute to code reviews, and help maintain technical standards and testing strategies. Working independently and collaboratively, you will address problems of diverse scope and partner with cross-functional team members and customers. While your primary focus will be on our distributed (non-mainframe) applications, you will also have the chance to engage with our mainframe software, offering a unique opportunity to broaden your technical expertise across the enterprise stack.

What You'll Be Doing & Why It Matters Slack is hiring an Associate User Researcher to help make people's working lives simpler, more pleasant and more productive. You'll join the Research & Analytics team, which improves the product experience and keeps Slack customer-centered through practical, impactful research on how people use Slack and what they need. Our work covers every stage of the product cycle, from formative to evaluative. You'll help expand Slack's established Rolling Research program, our rapid research program for fast customer evidence at the pace product teams ship. It replaces guesswork with validated insight, protects revenue-critical roadmap decisions, and raises launch confidence and adoption. You'll lead rapid, primarily evaluative studies across product areas. You'll partner with Product, Design, Engineering and fellow researchers to turn time-sensitive questions into focused studies with clear recommendations. Your work makes customer evidence useful before decisions are locked in. Slack has a positive, diverse and supportive culture. We look for people who are curious, inventive and work to be a little better every day. We aim to be smart, humble, hard working and collaborative. Core Responsibilities & Scope Gather and prioritize key research questions from product, design, engineering and marketing partners Triage incoming requests from PDE partners, then prioritize them and route segment-specific questions to the right audiences Plan, run and synthesize short-cycle studies across multiple product areas, using a diverse set of methods throughout the product cycle Write discussion guides Analyze results and turn them into clear, compelling narratives that inspire action, delivered fast in formats product teams can act on in the moment Engage with cross-functional product teams as they build, consulting on execution and identifying new opportunities Partner with fellow Researchers and Data Scientists to keep a consistent, evolving view of the user experience Partner with Research Operations and recruiting to manage screener profiles, panel health and participant quality Improve the program's methods, templates and tooling, including AI-assisted analysis and synthesis Keep studies consistent and compliant (consent, privacy, data handling) Act as an internal evangelist for our customers, our users and research as a discipline Minimum Requirements Current or prior Salesforce intern Undergraduate or graduate degree in Psychology, Human Computer Interaction or a related field, or similar work experience Understanding of, and ability to implement, basic qualitative research design best practices Ability to conduct qualitative or quantitative data analysis Strong collaboration and communication skills Preferred Qualifications Strong storytelling skills and an ability to frame insights into compelling narratives Familiarity with developing or using frameworks to align cross-functional teams Willingness to roll up your sleeves on a wide variety of research tasks, including recruiting participants when needed Unleash Your Potential When you join Salesforce, you’ll be limitless in all areas of your life.

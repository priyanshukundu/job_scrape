Software Engineer - IE08DE We’re determined to make a difference and are proud to be an insurance company that goes well beyond coverages and policies. Working here means having every opportunity to achieve your goals – and to help others accomplish theirs, too. Join our team as we help shape the future. We are seeking a Full Stack Software Engineer to join our Data Team within HIMCO IT. This role is critical in partnering with technology and investment professionals to deliver innovative solutions that meet dynamic information and automation needs. You will build systems & supply data that empower Portfolio Managers, Traders, and Analysts to make informed investment and financial decisions. As a Software Engineer, you will play a key role in designing and building HIMCO’s next-generation enterprise data platform, a strategic initiative that will provide a secure, scalable, and AI-ready foundation for data delivery, analytics, reporting, and business operations across the firm. In this role, you will: Design, develop, and enhance a modern enterprise data platform that enables scalable, reliable, and secure access to data across HIMCO Build and support data ingestion, transformation, orchestration, and delivery solutions that power critical business processes and decision-making Develop APIs, services, web applications, and automation solutions that integrate seamlessly with the data platform ecosystem Collaborate with business partners, data engineers, architects, and application development teams to translate requirements into scalable technical solutions Integrate internal and third-party data sources while ensuring data quality, governance, and operational excellence Apply software engineering best practices, including object-oriented design, automated testing, CI/CD, cloud technologies, and AI-assisted development tools Contribute to architecture decisions and help shape the future direction of HIMCO's data and technology capabilities This is an exciting opportunity for an engineer who is passionate about building data-intensive systems, solving complex business challenges, and creating technology platforms that have a broad impact across the organization. You'll work on high-visibility initiatives, leverage modern technologies, and help establish the foundation for HIMCO's future data strategy.

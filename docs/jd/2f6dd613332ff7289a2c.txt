As a Brand Partnership Associate - esports, Gaming & Internet Culture, you'll own the coordination and operational execution across Coinbase’s gaming and crypto portfolio, including partnerships with Riot Games, Polytopia, and gaming and crypto-native creators, managing timelines, partner deliverables, and activation logistics. What you’ll do: Develop and maintain relationships with key gaming partners and support the Sr. Manager and cross-functional teams on brand initiatives leveraging the gaming and creator portfolio. Own asset inventories, activation trackers, and timeline management for Riot Games, Polytopia, and gaming and crypto-native creator partnerships, and maintain a tracker for emerging pilot opportunities. Lead day-to-day project management for gaming activations, including weekly status updates, meeting notes, creative approvals, partner follow-ups, and coordination with external partners. Support GTM strategy and briefing/ideation for marquee moments like Worlds and Champs, and build mid-season and end-of-season recaps. Surface and track emerging gaming, esports, and internet culture experiments and unofficial partnership opportunities for senior leadership. Drive budget tracker upkeep, purchase order processing, and invoice reconciliation across the sports portfolio. Required Skills and Experience: 2+ years of experience in esports, gaming marketing, and/or creator/influencer marketing partnerships. Demonstrated ability to manage multiple concurrent project timelines, deliverables, and external partner relationships simultaneously. Hands-on experience coordinating with agencies and creators, including managing briefs, content approvals, deliverables, and posting schedules. Proven success working cross-functionally with creative, social, communications, and analytics teams to deliver activations on time. Familiarity with the gaming, esports, or internet culture ecosystem, including publishers, tournament organizers, streaming platforms, and creator networks. A pulse on emerging gaming, internet and crypto culture trends, with a track record of identifying moments early and connecting them to brand opportunities. Agency-side experience in gaming partnerships is a plus. Utilizes generative AI responsibly, maintaining human oversight to deliver business-ready outputs and drive measurable improvements in workflow efficiency, cost, and quality.

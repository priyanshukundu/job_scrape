YOUR IMPACT As a Vice President and Technology Risk Engineer for **Global Banking & Markets (GBM)**, you will lead the Dallas Technology Risk team and partner technically with GBM application owners to remediate security vulnerabilities. The central Technology Risk team identifies vulnerabilities and defines risk programs. Our team works alongside application owners and their engineers to resolve those issues quickly, durably, and at scale. This is a hands-on, engineering-focused role. We are looking for a software engineer by background who has built modern applications at scale. You should be comfortable working in application owners' code, architecture, and platforms as a technical peer who helps solve problems and delivers lasting fixes. You will also lead an AI- and technology-first approach to how the team works. That means using AI to speed up remediation and automating our reporting and remediation workflows so the team moves off email and spreadsheets. You will advise GBM engineering and business leadership on the division's risk profile, and you will build, develop, and lead a high-performing team in Dallas. HOW YOU WILL FULFILL YOUR POTENTIAL Technical Partnership with Application Owners - Partner technically with GBM application owners to remediate security vulnerabilities and meet risk program requirements from the central Technology Risk team, and drive them to closure. - Work with application teams on root causes and practical fixes through code and architecture reviews, design input, and improvements to CI/CD, cloud, and infrastructure configuration. - Turn recurring vulnerabilities into systemic, reusable solutions, such as shared libraries, guardrails, and automated controls, so the same problems do not return. - Help application owners prioritize remediation by business risk and engineering effort, and build plans that fit how their teams deliver. - Build credibility with application owners and engineers as a peer who understands their systems, constraints, and trade-offs. AI-Enabled Remediation - Design, build, and champion AI-enabled tools that help application teams remediate faster. Examples include LLM-assisted fix recommendations, contextual remediation guidance, and agentic workflows that draft remediation changes for engineer review. - Work with platform, engineering, and AI/ML teams to embed these tools in developer workflows such as IDEs, pull requests, ticketing, and pipelines. - Ensure AI-enabled solutions are developed and used responsibly and in line with firm policy and model risk management expectations. Automation of Reporting and Remediation Workflow - Replace manual, email- and spreadsheet-driven program management with automated, data-driven workflows for tracking, routing, follow-up, and escalation. - Build or adopt tools that give real-time visibility into remediation status, ownership, and risk posture for application owners and leadership. - Use AI to reduce administrative overhead, for example by generating status summaries, drafting stakeholder updates, and flagging at-risk items automatically. - Define and track metrics such as time-to-remediate, backlog burn-down, and recurrence rates, and use them to show impact and guide priorities. People Leadership - Lead, coach, and grow the Dallas Technology Risk team. Set clear goals, give regular feedback, and build an inclusive, high-accountability culture. - Strengthen the team's engineering, automation, and AI capabilities through hiring, development, and hands-on leadership. - Act as the senior Technology Risk presence in Dallas, connecting the local team with global Technology Risk, GBM engineering, and peers across Technology Risk. - Manage team capacity and priorities across workstreams so the team focuses on the highest-impact risk. Risk Advisory and Governance - Advise GBM business unit and engineering leadership on the division's technology risk profile and remediation progress. - Partner with central Technology Risk, risk program owners, and peers in other divisions to manage risk consistently and transparently, and provide credible challenge where needed. - Support issue and incident management, escalations, audit and regulatory engagements, and third-party risk activities as they relate to remediation. - Report remediation progress and risk posture to senior management clearly, with data and a point of view.

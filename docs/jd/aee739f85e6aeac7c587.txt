Job Description Summary The Nuclear Capital Expenditure Leader is responsible for driving the successful planning, governance, and execution of Nuclear’s capital expenditure (CapEx) portfolio, representing more than $700M of investment over the next five years across the Installed Base and Advanced Nuclear businesses. This role will lead the cross-functional operating rhythm required to prioritize investments, establish standard work, monitor execution, and ensure capital is deployed effectively in support of Nuclear’s growth and operational objectives. The CapEx portfolio includes investments to expand manufacturing and sourcing capabilities, scale and optimize the existing footprint, introduce new technologies and processes, and ensure the facilities and infrastructure required to deliver the highest levels of safety, quality, delivery, and cost competitiveness. Success in this role requires strong partnership across Product Management, Engineering, Sourcing, Manufacturing, Facilities, Project Management, and Finance, with the ability to translate strategic priorities into executable investment plans and drive accountability across the organization. Job Description Essential Responsibilities As the Nuclear Capital Expenditure Leader, you will: Lead Nuclear’s overall CapEx planning and execution process , ensuring investments are aligned with business priorities, financial commitments, and operational needs. Establish and continuously improve standard work, governance, operating rhythms, and reporting for the Nuclear CapEx portfolio, providing clear visibility to investment status, risks, milestones, and outcomes. Lead the long-term and annual CapEx planning cycles , including investment prioritization, budget preparation, appropriation approvals, and portfolio reviews. Partner across Product Management, Engineering, Sourcing, Manufacturing, Facilities, Project Management, and Finance to industrialize new capabilities on time, meeting safety, quality, delivery, and cost objectives. Drive execution of investments supporting manufacturing capacity expansion, new product introduction, advanced manufacturing, infrastructure, and footprint optimization . Develop and maintain analytical models and portfolio management tools to support investment prioritization, scenario analysis, capacity decisions, and effective capital allocation. Integrate applicable GE Vernova tollgate and investment governance processes into CapEx execution, ensuring appropriate rigor in business cases, approvals, execution, and benefits realization. Support critical work transfers, footprint optimization, and product/component placement decisions , ensuring capital requirements and execution plans are fully integrated. Monitor portfolio execution, proactively identifying schedule, cost, resource, and implementation risks , and drive cross-functional actions to close gaps. Provide clear and concise CapEx portfolio updates to Nuclear leadership, highlighting performance, risks, decisions required, and corrective actions. Apply Lean principles and problem-solving methodologies to improve CapEx planning, execution, governance, and investment effectiveness.

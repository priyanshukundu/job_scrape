Who You Are: Compensation costs are a key driver of profitability for the data center business given revenues are contracted for 15-year periods and operating costs are passed through to tenants. This role will own the allocation, analysis and reporting of that information across data halls, between construction and operations, recoveries, etc. You are a detail-oriented CPA with a genuine appetite for analysis. You will build and run the allocation models, reconcile them monthly, explain what is driving the numbers, and prepare the support that our accountants rely on to book entries. Over time you will take ownership of share-based compensation calculations and the related disclosures for our financial statements, proxy statements and other filings. You will be trusted with highly confidential employee compensation information which you will handle with absolute discretion. You will regularly work directly with the Data Center Head of Finance and the Co-Heads of the Data Center business, presenting your analyses and fielding their questions. This role reports to the Head of FP&A – Data Centers, with a dotted-line relationship to the Controller. The ideal candidate is a self-starter with proven analytical skills and the judgment to know when a number needs a second look.

TransUnion's Job Applicant Privacy Notice Personal Information We Collect Your Privacy Choices Team Overview Senior Analysts, Data Science & Analytics for White Plains, NY location. Responsible for handling key Commerce Signals Data Science deliverables to clients including measurement studies. Utilize statistical and machine learning techniques to create high performing models that comply with regulatory and privacy requirements and address business objectives and client needs. Test new statistical analysis methods, software, and data sources for continual improvement of quantitative solutions. Process, cleanse, and verify the integrity of data used for analysis. Perform ad-hoc analysis and present results clearly. Create clear and easy to understand documents for models. Collaborate with Lead Data Scientists to develop technical/business approaches and new or enhanced technical tools. Technical Environment: Python, SQL (Postgres & MySQL), Shell, AWS, SageMaker, Git, VS Code, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Scikit-Learn, PyTorch, Keras, TensorFlow, Spark, PySpark. Job Requirement: Bachelor’s degree in Data Science, Business Analytics, Computer Science, Computer Applications, Computer Information Systems, or a related field plus 2 years of experience with Data Analytics required. Required skills: Python, SQL (Postgres & MySQL), Shell, AWS, SageMaker, Git, VS Code, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Scikit-Learn, PyTorch, Keras, TensorFlow, Spark, PySpark. Some telecommuting permitted.

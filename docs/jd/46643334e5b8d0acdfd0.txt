Job Description General Summary: The Associate Director, Manufacturing Technical Services – Finished Goods Core Portfolio Operations, reports to the Director, Manufacturing Operations & Technical Services – Finished Goods and is accountable for the technical execution of small molecule and biologic packaging activities at external manufacturing partners. This role provides technical stewardship across the finished goods CMO network, ensuring manufacturing compliance, operational excellence, and a reliable supply of product. The Associate Director serves as the technical lead for packaging execution, supporting consistent performance across programs and CDMOs. In this capacity, the Associate Director drives rapid issue resolution by partnering cross-functionally and with external manufacturers to assess technical challenges, clarify ownership, and lead quality event resolution that may affect operations. The role also contributes technical expertise to business continuity planning, CMO selection, governance forums, and long-term network strategy. Key Duties and Responsibilities: Provide technical leadership for finished goods (FG) manufacturing activities at external partners, serving as the senior technical decision-maker during active operations to ensure compliant, consistent, on-time execution and operational readiness. Oversee triage, resolution, and documentation of execution-related deviations. Support business impact analysis and ensure rapid containment to protect product quality and patient supply. On site presence might be needed ad-hoc Triage each deviation and define ownership. Escalate urgencies and assign issues resolution requiring significant process changes to MSAT, ensuring clear technical framing and efficient handoff Lead cross-functional coordination across MOps, MSAT, Quality, and manufacturing teams, both internal and external. Escalate and frame complex issues as needed Feed internal operations governance process (Tiers Model) with accurate and timely updates on deviations statuses and reporting Lead and enable production runs readiness, including process updates and documentation alignment. Own process operability and execution robustness at the packaging site, including batch record clarity and usability Partner closely with MOps Managers, who own the business relationship with CMOs, by providing technical expertise, risk assessment, and continuous improvement strategies Partner closely with MSAT Engineering and Packaging teams to stay informed on validation plans, launch readiness, equipment changes, process improvements, CPV outputs, and longer-term technical strategy that may affect commercial execution. Represent Technical Services in CMO governance forums, to communicate technical risks/opportunities and recommendations, both externally (JMC, BRM, JSC…) and internally (AT, PT, CAB…) Serve as the technical subject matter expert in support of Quality during audits and inspections related to escalated technical events or issues with potential health authority impact Support evaluation and selection of new CMO partners through plant visits, technical due diligence reviews, audits, and capability assessments, while advising on operational fit, technical capability, and long-term network strategy Strengthen the Technical Services function, including defining the operating model, capabilities framework, training curricula, ways of working, etc. Knowledge and Skills: Demonstrated experience managing complex deviations in commercial GMP operations, including rapid escalation, cross-functional coordination, and business continuity support Previous people management experience preferred, and working across matrixed organizations, and influencing internal and external stakeholders without direct authority Experience supporting or assessing external manufacturing networks, including troubleshooting site visits, technical due diligence, audits, and partner selection activities Deep technical knowledge of commercial finished good manufacturing and packaging operations in a cGMP-regulated pharmaceutical or biotechnology environment Strong experience with cGMP live production support, including batch execution, troubleshooting , deviation and investigation practices, change control management Strong problem-solving and decision-making skills, with the ability to rapidly assess risk and define practical recovery actions Proven ability to influence cross-functional teams and external partners, driving alignment. Without direct reporting authority, in high pressure circumstances Working knowledge of process validation, equipment qualification, launch readiness, and the interface between CPV and ongoing commercial execution Excellent communication skills across all levels, with the ability to drive clear decisions under time-sensitive conditions Ability to provide on-site technical support at CMOs during business-critical events when required Ability to travel domestically and internationally, as needed, to support CMO initiatives, on-site meetings, audits, and partner assessments Education and Experience: Bachelor's Degree in Packaging, Chemical, Mechanical, Material Engineering, or a relevant Life Sciences discipline Typically requires 8+ years of progressive involvement in pharmaceutical manufacturing, with demonstrated experience supporting or leading finished good manufacturing/packaging operations at a site or external partner At Vertex, we embrace continuous learning and encourage employees to stay informed on advances in AI, technology, automation, and industry trends.

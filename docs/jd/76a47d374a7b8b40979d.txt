Job Title .NET Software Development Engineer What does a successful .NET Software Development Engineer do at Fiserv? A successful .NET Software Development Engineer at Fiserv designs, develops, tests, and maintains enterprise applications that are secure, scalable, and reliable. This role works across Agile teams to deliver cloud-native, AI-enabled software solutions that support business growth and operational excellence. Success in this position also means strengthening application performance, supporting production stability, and promoting engineering best practices across the team. What you will do? Design, develop, test, and maintain enterprise-grade applications using C#, .NET 8/.NET Core, ASP.NET Core, RESTful APIs, and microservices architecture. Build reusable, scalable, and maintainable software components while participating in architecture discussions, design sessions, code reviews, troubleshooting, and root cause analysis. Develop and deploy cloud-native applications on Microsoft Azure, build containerized solutions with Docker and Kubernetes, implement scalable deployment patterns, and manage releases through Azure DevOps CI/CD pipelines. Integrate AI services into enterprise applications, including Azure OpenAI solutions, Retrieval Augmented Generation (RAG) architectures, AI-powered chatbots and copilots, and semantic search and vector database capabilities, while supporting responsible AI, governance, security, and compliance requirements. Partner with Product Owners, Architects, QA Engineers, DevOps teams, and Business Analysts, contribute to Agile ceremonies, mentor junior developers, support vulnerability remediation and security reviews, and help drive production support and operational excellence initiatives. What you must have? 8+ years of professional software development experience. 8+ years of experience with C# and .NET technologies. 5+ years of experience with Azure cloud services. Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Information Technology, or a related field, or an equivalent combination of education and experience. What would be nice to have? 2+ years of experience with AI tools such as Devin and Claude. 1+ year of experience in financial services, payments, or fintech environments. 1+ year of experience with automated testing frameworks, API development and integration, and secure coding practices.

About the Team:

Snowflake's mission is to mobilize the world's data by building the greatest data and applications platform. Not just of the cloud era, but in the history of computing. To support this mission, the Storage team focuses on building the world's best storage platform. That means enhancing value-added services such as disaster recovery and AI integrations, and providing enterprise-grade, cloud-native storage for containerized workloads, especially in the fast-growing AI space.

We are seeking an outstanding Staff Software Engineer with a passion for large scale databases and distributed systems to help drive our continued growth. In this role, you'll connect customer needs with the business and technology, and act as a technical leader who helps the team carry out its vision.

We are at an inflection point as we continue to scale the footprint and expand the TAM of Snowflake through new and Neo Clouds onboarding, changes in the workloads, and need to simplify in order to enable Snowflake customers to unlock their full data potential.

AS A STAFF SOFTWARE ENGINEER, YOU WILL:

 - Translate business and customer needs into technical designs that deliver new capabilities to existing and new Snowflake customers

 - Drive innovation in how table data is stored, accessed, and processed in the Snowflake cloud

 - Architect, design, and implement new systems and solutions that advance the Snowflake Data Infrastructure platform

 - Collaborate closely with product managers, architects, other engineering teams, and cross-functional partners to design and build new technologies that go directly into our customers' hands

 - Provide technical leadership for your team, setting direction on design, code quality, and operational excellence

 - Mentor and coach engineers on your team and across partner teams

AN IDEAL CANDIDATE WILL HAVE MOST OF THE FOLLOWING QUALIFICATIONS:

 - 10+ years of industry experience designing, building, and supporting large-scale, durable distributed systems in production

 - An excellent team player who is committed to helping everyone around them reach their potential and grow

 - Alignment with the Snowflake values

 - A proven track record of leading and delivering large, complex projects across multiple teams in the storage space (object storage, data warehouses, SQL/NoSQL databases, distributed file systems, etc.)

 - Strong development skills and AI-forward mindset with a critical eye towards code maintainability

 - Experience with public cloud (AWS, Azure, GCP)

 - Demonstrated ability to build alignment across teams and disciplines to bring complex technical solutions to life

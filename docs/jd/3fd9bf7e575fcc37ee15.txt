Visa will accept applications for this role until at least 11-30-2026 Job Description Overview: Visa Corporate IT has embarked on a major transformation journey to become an engineering-first organization where reliability, automation, customer experience, and end-user productivity are central to how we operate. As part of this transformation, Corporate Reliability Engineering (CRE) is focused on shifting from reactive support to proactive, predictive, and preventative engineering practices that reduce operational toil, improve service reliability, and enable scalable support across Visa's global workforce. The Agentic Developer will support the design, development, testing, and maintenance of agentic automation solutions that reduce manual effort and accelerate problem resolution across Corporate IT services. Agentic workflows combine AI-assisted reasoning with approved tools and defined decision paths to complete bounded operational tasks. Working under senior CRE engineer guidance, this role will help convert recurring Incident and Request (INC/REQ) handling into reliable, auditable automation, supporting the CRE team's engineering-first operations. This is an Individual Contributor I4 / entry-level position for an early-career developer with foundational software engineering and automation skills. The successful candidate will develop hands-on experience with GenAI, agentic workflows, and reliability engineering through well-defined assignments, code review, and mentoring. Scope and independence will increase as technical proficiency and operational judgment develop.

Job Description About Aberdeen Aberdeen Group plc is one of Europe’s largest investment companies, and we are built on a long-standing culture of caring about the future and making a positive impact. Together we invest for a better future. We do it to make a difference to the lives of our clients and customers, our employees, society, and our shareholders. Our business is structured around three distinct areas focused on our clients' changing needs: Aberdeen Investments, Aberdeen Adviser, and interactive investor. As a Risk Analyst, you’ll play an important role in supporting this purpose while investing in your tomorrow through exposure to complex private markets investments and a highly collaborative global environment. About the Department The Investment Risk Governance division provides independent oversight of investment risk across Aberdeen’s investment capabilities. Within the Private Markets Investment Risk Governance team, you will work across Infrastructure and Real Estate funds and mandates, helping ensure risk is understood, monitored and managed in line with client objectives, regulatory requirements and internal standards. The team partners closely with investment, operations, compliance and governance colleagues, enabling our clients to be better investors through robust risk oversight and informed decision-making. About the Role This is an excellent opportunity for an early-career risk professional to build specialist expertise in private markets risk management. Supporting the Senior Investment Risk Manager, you will contribute to the ongoing development of Aberdeen’s risk management framework while analysing Infrastructure and Real Estate investments, conducting stress testing, reviewing valuations and supporting regulatory reporting. You will gain exposure to senior stakeholders, investment committees and fund governance processes, making a meaningful impact on how risk is identified, assessed and communicated. In return, you will have the opportunity to develop your technical, analytical and stakeholder management skills in a business that encourages continuous learning and growth. Key Responsibilities Support the Senior Investment Risk Manager in maintaining and enhancing the Risk Management Process for Infrastructure and Real Estate funds. Perform quantitative and qualitative risk analysis, including stress testing, review of valuations, financial statements, asset management reports and investment due diligence materials. Produce risk reporting for boards and governance committees, helping communicate key risks, trends and insights to stakeholders. Partner with investment, operations, compliance and project teams to gather, analyse and interpret risk-related information. Support regulatory reporting requirements, client due diligence reviews, risk-related RFP responses and internal risk framework improvements. Build effective relationships across the business and contribute to risk projects driven by regulatory change and evolving industry practice. About the Candidate Experience within the funds industry, ideally with exposure to Real Estate, Infrastructure or other private market asset classes. Strong understanding of valuation methodologies; experience in valuation, investment risk or a related discipline would be advantageous. Excellent analytical skills, attention to detail and the ability to interpret complex information and draw balanced conclusions. Good working knowledge of Microsoft Office, particularly Microsoft Excel. Collaborative approach with the ability to work effectively in a multicultural and international environment. Ability to manage priorities, meet deadlines and work independently while remaining adaptable to change.

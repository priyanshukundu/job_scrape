Research Triangle Park | Where People + Ideas Converge (rtp.org) The Director, Access Strategy, Portfolio and Pipeline Innovation will serve as the US Market Access strategic lead for selected in-line portfolio initiatives, including loss-of-exclusivity and other lifecycle transitions, and for selected pre-launch pipeline assets. The role will translate anticipated changes in the US access, reimbursement, pricing, and policy environment into clear implications and recommendations for product development, evidence generation, investment, and commercialization decisions. Working closely with cross-functional partners, the Director will ensure that US payer, purchaser, channel, and patient-access requirements are incorporated early and consistently across the product lifecycle. This position requires enterprise-level influence, strong strategic judgment, and the ability to lead through ambiguity. The Director will connect external market signals with portfolio choices, identify potential access risks and opportunities, and drive cross-functional alignment on strategies that support sustainable patient access and long-term portfolio value. The Director will own Access Strategy & Marketing recommendations for assigned lifecycle and pipeline initiatives. Brand-aligned Access Strategy Directors will retain ownership of in-line brand access strategy and execution, while Portfolio Access & Optimization will remain a critical partner in portfolio analytics, optimization, and cross-portfolio planning. Responsibilities will be aligned through initiative-specific charters and joint work plans. Reporting to the Senior Director, Access Strategy & Marketing, the Director, Access Strategy – Lifecycle & Pipeline will lead the development of integrated US market access strategies for priority lifecycle, portfolio, policy, and pipeline initiatives across ViiV Healthcare’s HIV treatment and prevention portfolio. Responsibilities This role will provide YOU the opportunity to lead key activities to progress YOUR career. These responsibilities include some of the following: Portfolio and lifecycle access strategy Lead US market access strategy for assigned portfolio and lifecycle priorities, including loss-of-exclusivity, indication expansion, formulation or dosing evolution, and other events that could materially affect access, reimbursement, or portfolio value. Lead the development of the US access strategy for products facing loss-of-exclusivity, integrating payer behavior, channel dynamics, competitor evolution, and implications for the broader ViiV portfolio. Ensure lifecycle strategies consider the interdependencies among oral treatment, long-acting treatment, prevention, emerging competitors, and future ViiV assets. Partner with brand-aligned Access Strategy Directors to ensure that portfolio-level recommendations translate into coordinated product strategies and execution. Pipeline and commercialization strategy Serve as the US Market Access lead for assigned early- and late-stage pipeline assets, embedding payer, purchaser, provider, channel, and patient-access insights into development and commercialization strategies. Define the access ambition, critical success factors, and minimum and aspirational access requirements for pipeline assets in partnership with Global Market Access and US cross-functional stakeholders. Shape target product profiles, value propositions, evidence plans, pricing hypotheses, channel strategies, and launch assumptions from a US access perspective. Translate emerging long-acting treatment and next-generation PrEP concepts into actionable access hypotheses, including benefit design, site-of-care, acquisition, reimbursement, distribution, and patient-start considerations. Policy and external-environment strategy Establish a structured process to monitor, assess, and communicate external developments that could affect ViiV’s portfolio, including government pricing policy, Medicaid eligibility and financing changes, PBM and payer-model evolution, reimbursement reform, and channel consolidation. Translate external policy developments into product-level and portfolio-level implications, including potential effects on access, evidence needs, channel economics, and patient affordability. Develop scenario analyses and strategic recommendations for high-impact policy risks in partnership with cross-functional subject-matter experts in Government Relations. Provide senior leaders with concise, decision-oriented assessments of major external access developments rather than descriptive policy updates alone. Why You?

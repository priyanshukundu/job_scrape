About the Team Product Management owns the core sales and marketing collateral for our investment platforms and is responsible for delivering high-quality, timely content and reporting, along with managing various product initiatives to support business development across retail, institutional and retirement channels. The Product Management team acts as a liaison between the client portfolio managers (CPMs) & investment teams with other cross-functional business areas on product-specific matters, ensuring alignment of the investment team needs with commercial priorities across the business. About the Role The Product Manager is responsible for supporting a set of investment strategies and will maintain critical connectivity with CPMs and portfolio teams, enabling alignment on product positioning, messaging, internal sales needs, portfolio data, and other potential issues to ensure strategic priorities are met. The Product Manager is expected to have a deep subject matter expertise of their product platforms, which includes an intimate knowledge of each strategy’s investment philosophy, process, positioning, and performance drivers. The person in this role also must understand fixed income asset classes and markets, maintain awareness of competitive landscape and trends, and ensure they can clearly articulate how our products differentiate from peers across relevant distribution channels. This position is a multi-faceted, dynamic, and challenging role that demands highly developed skills in analytics, content management, communications, relationship building and business acumen. Key Responsibilities of the Role: Product & Investment Expertise Own the month- and quarter-end product collateral update process, including production and maintenance of quarterly presentations, factsheets, commentaries, infographics, annual reports, risk and performance attribution data reporting, and portfolio team information. Coordinate cross-functional stakeholders within Invesco (including legal, compliance, risk, technology, sales/marketing, and other departments) to ensure clear, compliant product messaging, with timely review, approval and delivery of content for client and prospects aligned with firm standards. Support regulatory and governance deliverables, including preparation of fund board materials, prospectus/SAI review support, and contributions to other reporting. Serve as a key point of contact for marketing and RFP teams, maintaining accurate product data, managing consultant databases, and supporting standard RFP/DDQ content and positioning. Maintain quality control for product-related websites and internally posted content to ensure information remains current, accurate, and aligned with approved messaging and disclosures. Support product change management by coordinating stakeholders and ensuring timely updates across collateral systems, and client-facing communications. Business & Distribution/Marketing Support Partner closely with the CPMs and investment teams to help drive collaboration across product, investments, distribution, marketing, and other cross-functional stakeholders. Actively engage with the CPMs/investment teams to maintain deep subject-matter expertise about our strategies, portfolios, and investment results. Provide key support in creating and reviewing marketing materials. Support CPMs in regular communications, analysis and insights to our sales team for use with clients; Respond to ad hoc internal and external sales team and client requests. Monitor industry and product trends: compile, analyze, and interpret data to inform product strategy and prioritization. Conduct competitive and peer analysis using Morningstar and other related resources to help support CPMs with product positioning and go-to-market workflow. Requirements of the Role Undergraduate degree required, preferably in business or related discipline. Minimum five (5) years of related work experience in financial services, preferably asset management. Series 7 and 63 highly preferred but not required. Experience across fixed income asset classes and an overall understanding of financial markets, investment concepts and processes. Experience in Global/Emerging Market Debt and/or Municipal Bonds is a plus. Strong technical skills, including proficiency with Microsoft Office, Morningstar, eVestment, Bloomberg, and other software products; Experience with AI tools is also preferred. Strong analytic skills with a focus on data and content management processes, as well as research, writing, and marketing. Passion for the investment industry and capital markets is a must. Entrepreneurial spirit, with a focus on continuous improvement, willingness to take risks, embrace and lead change, determination to find creative solutions to problems. Strong work ethic and drive to succeed. Highly developed communication skills, both written and verbal. Detail and deadline oriented. Ability to demonstrate sound judgment.

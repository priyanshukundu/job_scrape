About the Job: Associate – Investment Strategy NM’s Investment Strategy Department (ISD) focuses on multi-asset class portfolio management for NM’s $300 billion General Account and related portfolios. ISD is responsible for tactical and strategic asset allocation, public equity portfolio management, external manager oversight, impact investing portfolio management, and managed investments technology and data strategy. What You'll Do: · Key member of the ISD external manager oversight team supporting due diligence activities, including manager searches, oversight and reporting. · Responsible for the quantitative and qualitative analysis and reporting processes, and performance analysis and reporting processes for ISD’s manager oversight and supervision function. Develops and implements performance analysis and reporting systems and processes for the externally managed portfolios. Tracks and analyzes investment performance for the externally managed portfolios, including attribution studies, risk/style monitoring, and benchmarking against relevant indices. Has a strong understanding of each portfolios’ characteristics, strategies, positioning and risk parameters. These activities require familiarity with the investment management industry. · Collects and analyzes investment performance data for use in reports for ISD’s senior management and relevant internal and external parties. Verifies accuracy of externally managed portfolio and benchmark performance results and monitors performance trends. · Helps develop and execute policies and procedures that mitigate various types of risks relating to the external managers and portfolios, including investment mandate risk particularly as it relates to adherence to investment objectives, guidelines, and operational considerations. · Analyzes investment opportunities for multi-asset class portfolios including asset allocation studies, macroeconomic research and more. Strong knowledge of macroeconomics and financial markets with the expectation of contributing to the formulation of tactical allocation strategies. · Partnering with Directors and others in Investment Strategy Department to come up w/ creative solutions for the Investment function’s requests and maintain strong working relationships across internal Investments function. · Involved in various other investment research projects and assignments. What Experience You'll Bring: · Bachelor's Degree in Business required, MBA, CFA designation preferred. · 5-10 years of work experience in the financial services industry on top of education. · Experience in manager oversight activities at investment management organization. · Strong communications skills to coordinate efforts internally and inform stakeholders. · Brings excellent analytical and problem solving skills. · Expert Excel skills required, familiarity with Power BI dashboards. · Intermediate skills and working knowledge of Bloomberg and FactSet. Familiarity with Aladdin, Morningstar Direct a plus. Skills: Investment Research: Performs financial analysis, monitors market trends and economic indicators, studies company financial statements and reports, and uses various research tools to evaluate potential investments and make investment recommendations based on research findings. Communicates investment research findings effectively to others, such as clients or portfolio managers Economic Acumen: Applies knowledge of economic theories, models, and principles to make informed decisions related to business, finance, and policy. Interprets and applies economic data and indicators to make sound financial decisions and develops effective strategies based on a thorough understanding of economic conditions and trends. Financial Market Monitoring: Consistently tracks and analyzes financial market data and news, evaluates trends and events that may impact investment portfolios or financial decisions, and makes informed decisions based on this information. Stays up to date on economic indicators, market news, and global events, and understands how they relate to investment opportunities and risks. Advanced Analytics: Analyzes data using advanced quantitative methods and models to analyze complex data sets and extract valuable insights that can inform business decisions. Risk Evaluation: Assesses the significance of identified risks, makes judgments about the significance of the risk in relation to the organization's objectives, and assesses whether the risk is acceptable. Considers factors such as the organization's risk appetite, risk tolerance, and risk management resources to help decision-makers determine which risks require further action and which can be accepted or tolerated.

Job Description About the role As a Principal Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning Engineer, you will set technical direction for generative artificial intelligence and agent-based solutions used across the organization. You will solve complex engineering problems, shape architecture and standards, and influence delivery across teams. As a senior individual contributor, you will remain hands-on while guiding others through technical leadership and deep expertise. Your key responsibilities Define architecture and engineering standards for generative artificial intelligence applications and agent-based systems. Lead the technical design of multi-agent, human-in-the-loop and autonomous workflows for complex business tasks. Build reusable patterns for retrieval, context management, memory, tool use, orchestration and evaluation. Resolve complex challenges involving scale, latency, reliability, grounding, safety and cost. Guide engineers through design reviews, code reviews, testing strategies and production readiness. Influence technical roadmaps and align product, data, platform, security and risk stakeholders. Evaluate emerging models, frameworks and methods and recommend practical adoption paths. What you bring Required qualifications Deep experience designing and delivering production artificial intelligence, generative artificial intelligence or agent-based systems. A track record of setting technical direction and influencing engineering work across multiple teams. Advanced experience with large language models, prompting, structured outputs, model routing, context management and memory management. Strong Python skills and experience with machine learning frameworks such as scikit-learn, TensorFlow, PyTorch, Keras or Pandas. Advanced experience with retrieval-augmented generation, vector databases, hybrid retrieval patterns and evaluation methods. Experience designing RESTful services with Flask or FastAPI and deploying solutions on AWS, Google Cloud Platform or Microsoft Azure. Preferred qualifications Experience with large language model fine-tuning or parameter-efficient methods such as LoRA or QLoRA. Familiarity with orchestration frameworks and tools such as LangChain, LlamaIndex, AutoGen, OpenAI Assistants API or Model Context Protocol. Experience defining standards for machine learning operations, monitoring, observability, security, compliance or data governance. The impact you’ll make The Principal Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning Engineer will shape how the team designs and delivers scalable artificial intelligence solutions. Your technical leadership will raise engineering standards, reduce delivery risk and help teams across FIS apply emerging technology responsibly to high-value opportunities.

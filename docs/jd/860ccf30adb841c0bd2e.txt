About this role: Wells Fargo is seeking an Associate Fraud & Claims Operations Manager in First Party Fraud, providing leadership and operational support for Consumer and Small Business Banking Operations. This role will manage a team responsible for researching and resolving complex account activity, fraud and claims transactions, policy violations, and consumer identification concerns within the contact center. Learn more about the career areas and lines of business at wellsfargojobs.com . The Associate Manager will oversee daily operations and team performance, provide coaching and development, support escalated fraud and claims matters, and ensure work is completed in accordance with established procedures, controls, and risk requirements. This position will also partner with cross-functional teams to identify fraud trends, address operational and control gaps, improve processes, and support strategies designed to mitigate and recover financial losses.

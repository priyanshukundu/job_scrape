About the team The Agentic Experience team sits at the forefront of Zillow's transformation toward an agentic product experience. We are the stewards of the agentic UX framework, the foundation that allows Zillow's agentic AI capabilities to come to life across its surfaces, and we operate at the intersection of product and platform: shipping meaningful agentic experiences today while building the durable infrastructure that enables teams across Zillow to build more, faster, tomorrow. We built and launched the user experience for Zillow AI Mode, now available to every signed-in Zillow user across web, iOS, and Android, and we own and evolve that core experience every day. We also stay at the forefront of agentic UX: exploring what comes after the conversational interface, including generative, context-adaptive interfaces that personalize to each person's goals and stage of their real estate journey, and bringing the most promising of those ideas into a product used by millions. About the role We are looking for a Senior Software Development Engineer, iOS, to help build and evolve Zillow AI Mode in the Zillow iOS app. You will be a core contributor to the mobile experience of AI Mode, a multimodal, context-aware agentic experience that is increasingly woven into the rest of the app. Beyond the conversational experience itself, you will help shape the interfaces by which other parts of the Zillow iOS app integrate with AI Mode, so they can hand context to the agent and surface its capabilities in place. And as the team pushes into generative UX, you will have the opportunity to contribute to how we bring frontier interaction patterns into a production app used by millions. This is a hands-on engineering role on a team that is central to Zillow's AI strategy. Much of what comes next is still being defined, and you will have a real voice in defining it. You Will Get To Build and ship the iOS experience for Zillow AI Mode (conversational, multimodal, and context-aware) in Swift and SwiftUI, with a high bar for craft, performance, and reliability in a consumer app at scale. Design and evolve the integration interfaces that let other surfaces in the Zillow iOS app connect to AI Mode: passing context to the agent and bringing agentic capabilities into the flows where users already are. Contribute to the team's generative UX frontier, helping prototype, evaluate, and productionize AI-composed, hyper-personalized interfaces on iOS. Partner closely with your web and Android counterparts to keep the AI Mode experience consistent across platforms: aligning on experience decisions, evolving the shared schema and protocol, and learning from how each platform solves the same problems, all while building natively on iOS. Collaborate with product managers, designers, applied scientists, and ML engineers to turn new agentic skills and model capabilities into shippable, measurable user experiences, and support rapid experimentation with real users. Champion operational excellence for the iOS experience, from instrumentation and observability to testing strategy and safe, incremental rollout, so we can move fast without compromising quality or user trust. Who you are A builder at heart who takes pride in the craft of mobile engineering and has the judgment to balance speed with rigor. A champion for user experience quality. You care how things look, feel, and perform, and you hold a high bar for what great mobile work is. A collaborative engineer who works well across platforms and functions (product, design, AI/ML, and peer engineering teams) and drives outcomes through influence rather than authority. A pragmatic technologist who is comfortable in ambiguity, breaks down complex problems, and surfaces risks and trade-offs early. A clear communicator who documents designs and decisions well and can explain technical trade-offs to non-technical partners. A practitioner of AI-native development. You actively use AI coding tools and assistants in your own workflow and treat them as a genuine force multiplier throughout the software development lifecycle. Qualifications Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, or equivalent industry experience. 5+ years of software engineering experience, the majority of it building native iOS applications in Swift. Deep proficiency in SwiftUI and UIKit, Swift concurrency, and modern iOS architecture patterns, with experience evolving a large, long-lived codebase. Demonstrated experience shipping consumer-facing iOS products to a large user base, with an appreciation for performance, reliability, accessibility, and user experience quality. Strong understanding of modern engineering practices: CI/CD, automated testing, observability, and incremental rollout. Desired Skills Experience building AI-powered or agentic applications, including familiarity with LLMs, streaming responses, tool/function calling, and multimodal input/output (text, voice, images). Experience with server-driven or dynamically composed UI, or other approaches to rendering generative, context-adaptive interfaces on mobile. Experience on a cross-platform team, aligning experience decisions with web and Android counterparts and learning from how other platforms solve similar problems. Comfort with rapid prototyping and iterative product development in a fast-moving, research-adjacent environment.

Job Description Summary The Staff Software Engineer will serve as a technical leader for test automation and Continuous Testing across CT software products. This role is responsible for defining the technical vision, architecture, and roadmap for scalable automation frameworks and platforms that enable reliable, repeatable, and efficient software verification. The individual will lead the design and evolution of automation solutions using technologies such as Squish, Selenium, BDD frameworks, and CI/CD platforms. The role requires deep technical expertise, hands on software development capabilities, and the ability to guide globally distributed engineering teams toward common automation standards, framework guidelines, and engineering practices. The Staff Software Engineer will work across product, software, verification, systems, architecture, and DevOps teams to improve automation coverage, test reliability, execution efficiency, and Continuous Testing adoption. The individual will influence technical decisions across multiple teams and product lines while ensuring that automation platforms remain scalable, maintainable, reusable, and aligned with product and business roadmaps.

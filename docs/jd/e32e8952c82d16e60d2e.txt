Senior Software Engineer - RDF Infrastructure - 44991 - Bloomberg Senior Software Engineer - RDF Infrastructure Location New York Business Area Engineering and CTO Ref # 10054258 Description & Requirements Our group The BQL and Semantic Platform group builds the core query and semantic infrastructure used to access, analyze, model, and organize Bloomberg data. The group develops the BQL engine , which powers Bloomberg Query Language and provides a common way to query and analyze data across Bloomberg. We also build the semantic platform used to define, manage, and materialize the semantic artifacts that describe Bloomberg data and the relationships between it. Together, these capabilities provide the foundation for making Bloomberg data consistently queryable and semantically connected across products, applications, and emerging AI-driven use cases. Our team As part of Bloomberg’s investment in Semantic Technologies, we are building and operating Bloomberg’s RDF triplestore infrastructure. Our team develops the database itself, working across the internals of graph storage and query processing rather than simply deploying or operating an existing database product. Our work spans physical storage and indexing, SPARQL query planning and execution, transaction and isolation semantics, statistics and cardinality estimation, inference, replication, scalability, and operational reliability. The platform stores semantic models and large RDF datasets used by engineering teams across Bloomberg. We are responsible for making billions of RDF statements efficiently queryable while providing the performance, correctness, availability, and reliability expected of enterprise-grade database infrastructure.

Job Description Analyzes credit data to estimate degree of risk involved in extending credit or lending money to firms or individuals and supporting the Credit Analysis Department in any and all daily activities by performing the following duties. Duties and Responsibilities include the following. Develops and administers pre-credit analysis function and loan reviews/annual reviews on borrowers and guarantors on credit relationships of $2,500,000 and greater. Performs cash flows, financial credit analysis, collateral valuations, and global cash flows, etc. for commercial borrowers and guarantors on credit relationships of $2,500,000 and greater.

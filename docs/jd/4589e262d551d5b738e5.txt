Introduction Established in 1912, Bank of China is one of the largest banks in the world, with over $3 trillion in assets and a footprint that spans more than 60 countries and regions. Our long-term outlook, institutional weight and global breadth provide our clients with a stable and reliable financial partner, whether in Corporate or Personal Banking or our Trade Services, Commodities, Financial Institutions and Global Markets lines of business. Overview This position handles general administrative duties for the Branch to ensure effective control of office supplies and stationeries inventory; reserves supply of bank checks and passbooks. Performs daily ticket scanning, outgoing and incoming check clearing and daily works in the department. Assists to perform the physical security administration. Responsibilities General Administration Duties (40%) Manage files and provide administrative support for Branch Management. Comply with administrative procedures in good administration system and suggest solution to Branch Management. Safeguard duplicate keys for teller’s box and other locks. Scan daily tickets. Keep and record all expenses for the Branch. Serve as backup for general administration duties in Administration Section. Assist to maintain a clean and sanitary office/work environment and monitor employees work discipline to meet with the bank’s policy including their work attitude to clients and their compliance with dress codes during business hours. Act as back up for handle and review requests in Expense Payment system. Comply with the Bank’s policies and procedure. Strictly comply with BOC Global Service Standard and BOCNY performance evaluation. Understand, comply with and monitor the activities, if necessary, of all applicable laws and regulations regarding Anti-Money Laundering, Bank Secrecy Act, and suspicious activity reporting. Inventory Control (25%) Place orders and distributes office supplies to all departments. Keep inventory of office supplies and stationeries at adequate level. Assist to keep and update the log for fixed assets and office supplies of the Branch. Maintain and update inventory records of stock room. Take control of reserve supply of bank checks and CD/savings passbooks. Mail Room (15%) Manage daily operation of Mail Room. Manage and handle outgoing and incoming mails to ensure timely and correct delivery. Check Clearing (15%) Input the account entry related to check clearing. Verify customer signature, endorsement and date for incoming checks. Manage Check scanning outgoing checks and verify balance of outgoing checks processed. Perform control of canceled and returned check. Physical Security (5%) Manage periodical check of security equipment such as emergency light, hold-up button to ensure the adequate conditions in the absence of AVP of Administration Section. Provide instruction for the problem encountered if AVP of Administration Section and the Branch Management are not available. Maintain fire safety equipment properly. Act as Back-up to Check of CCTV monitor. Act as Back-up to prepare and arrange training related to physical security for the Branch’s staffs. Qualifications Bachelor's Degree required Minimum 1 year of Business administration/banking industry experience Demonstrate Administrative work knowledge, check clearing process knowledge, physical security knowledge, record management knowledge, audit knowledge, and account knowledge Demonstrate Risk Governance Framework knowledge, regulatory and compliance knowledge, financial/banking industry knowledge, due diligence process knowledge, business line and process flow knowledge, and product knowledge Pay Range Actual salary is commensurate with candidate’s relevant years of experience, skillset, education and other qualifications.

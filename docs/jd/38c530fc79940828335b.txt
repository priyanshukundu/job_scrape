Job Summary Are you a meticulous and detail-oriented data professional? We're seeking a skilled and efficient Data Engineer IV to join our Enterprise Data and Analytics team! You'll play a critical role in ensuring the accuracy, efficiency, and reliability of our enterprise data processes and systems built on Azure Databricks. Key Responsibilities: Monitor and maintain data pipelines and workflows built on Azure Databricks. Troubleshoot and resolve data pipeline issues to ensure accurate and consistent data flows. Collaborate with data engineers and our business partners to understand data requirements and system behavior. Document and update data processes and procedures for clarity and consistency. Perform regular system backups and disaster recovery drills to ensure data security and availability. Provide technical support to stakeholders regarding data access and usage. Skills and Background You’ll Need EDUCATION: A High School Diploma or GED is required. A Bachelor’s degree is preferred. EXPERIENCE: Typically requires a minimum of 8 years of related experience. Hands-on, technical experience with Azure Databricks. Strong understanding of Azure data ecosystem and incident management techniques. Experience working with financial services data and systems is highly preferred. Excellent attention to detail and problem-solving skills. A proactive and adaptable attitude with a willingness to learn new technologies.

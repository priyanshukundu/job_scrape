NVIDIA played a key role in launching the modern AI era with GPU-accelerated deep learning. Today, generative AI opens outstanding opportunities—helping people share ideas, gain knowledge, and solve problems once out of reach. Making that promise trustworthy is one of the major scientific challenges today. As a Deep Learning Scientist in AI Safety, you will advance research in model alignment, create methods to reduce harmful behaviors, and transform new insights into practical protections for large language models. Join us to develop how advanced AI interacts with people and help extend its benefits worldwide. NVIDIA is recruiting Senior Deep Learning Scientists to push forward our work in multimodal AI safety, covering Product Security, Content Safety, Bias, and Human-AI Interactions. You will bring core expertise in deep learning to reinforce models for safe reasoning, planning, and acting across different modalities. This opportunity enables you to contribute essential algorithmic advances for foundational models processing diverse data formats. Your initiatives will reach scale via Nemotron Omni platforms, guardrails, and partnerships outside the organization. You will focus on high-impact, prominent safety challenges of large language models and multimodal AI products that improve user experiences for millions. If you are imaginative and driven to tackle real-world agentic AI safety issues, join our team. What you will be doing: Apply fundamental and applied safety research to develop, train, fine-tune, and deploy large language architectures supporting multimodal agentic reasoning systems and guardrails encompassing text, audio, image, video, and tool usage. Advance post-training and alignment methods including instruction tuning, preference optimization, and RLHF/RLVR/MOPD to improve safety of multimodal agents for complex use cases. Research and advance the latest trends in AI safety as applied to pre-/mid-/post-training, guardrails, and steering techniques. Lead the collection, development, and benchmarking of multimodal safety datasets, ensuring high-quality evaluation of model accuracy, safety, and task completion success. What we need to see: Master’s degree (or equivalent experience) or PhD in Computer Science, AI, or Applied Math with 8+ years of relevant work experience. Excellent programming skills in Python with strong fundamentals in scalable model development and deep learning frameworks like PyTorch. Strong knowledge of ML/DL techniques and modern foundation model architectures, including Transformers and mixture-of-experts models. Hands-on experience in post-training multimodal models for multimodal reasoning. Proven ability to manage model development life cycles, including dataset versioning, experiment tracking, and evaluation pipelines. Responsible AI Expertise: 1+ years of experience in content safety, ML fairness, AI model security, human-AI interaction, or related areas. Ways to stand out from the crowd: Strong record of publications in top-tier AI and machine learning venues such as NeurIPS, ICML, ICLR, FAccT, or CVPR. Experience applying deep reinforcement learning techniques to train multimodal agents in complex simulation. Safety and Compliance: Proven experience addressing safety and legal and regulatory compliance in multimodal, multilingual, or multi-turn systems. Multi-turn Safety Evaluation: dynamic and multi-turn alignment benchmarks. Experience in at least one of the following risk areas: CBRNE, persuasion and manipulation (influence operations, disinformation, and the erosion of democratic values through AI-generated content), discrimination at scale enabled by AI systems, mental health (sycophancy and manipulation), LLM security (backdoors, data poisoning, latent malicious behavior, structural model vulnerabilities), frontier risks (model deception, manipulation, and loss-of-control scenarios), agentic safety (tool-calling, orchestration, and execution), multi-turn.

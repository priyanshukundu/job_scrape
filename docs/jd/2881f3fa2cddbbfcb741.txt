Data Science Engineer We are seeking a Data Science Engineer to join our global Operations Research team, where innovation knows no borders . This team applies advanced statistical analysis, data modeling, and predictive analytics to solve complex strategic and operational challenges in the airline industry . It helps airlines optimize revenue performance, advance retailing and distribution through Offer/Order Management and Continuous Dynamic Pricing, and elevate the traveler experience . With an inclusive culture and flexible work environment, we work together with boldness, curiosity and commitment so we can all win together . As a Data Science Engineer, you will translate complex business questions into robust statistical models, define critical KPIs, and communicate empirical results to internal and external decision makers . You will source data across diverse platforms, build high-quality data manipulation scripts, and apply machine learning algorithms to drive predictive insights and product development cycles . This role requires expert analytical capability, proactive collaboration with software development teams, and the drive to deliver measurable business outcomes . What you'll do Partner with airline subject matter experts to turn operational and consumer data into actionable predictions that optimize customer behavior and business performance . Develop, test, and calibrate custom data models, forecasting techniques, and machine learning algorithms, performing proof-of-concept studies and proving value through benchmark demonstrations . Sourced data from multiple data sources and write high-quality data manipulation scripts in R, Python, Perl, and bash . Collaborate with software engineers to write, implement, and test production-quality C++ and Python code for forecasting and data analytics tools . Establish monitoring processes and tools to continuously evaluate data accuracy, assess model performance, and resolve software improvements or customer complaints . What you'll bring Required qualifications Advanced degree in Statistics, Operations Research, Computer Science, Mathematics, Machine Learning, or a related quantitative discipline . Advanced programming skills in Python and modern C++, alongside proficiency in R, SQL, PySpark, Perl, bash, Java, or JavaScript . Proven ability to apply advanced statistical concepts (regression, distributions, hypothesis testing) and machine learning techniques (clustering, decision trees, neural networks) to real-world problems . Experience deploying statistical and machine learning models in cloud environments using services like Amazon SageMaker and Amazon Forecast . Strong communication skills with demonstrated experience presenting graphical analytics to clients, sales, and software engineering teams . Preferred qualifications Industry experience or domain knowledge in airline, hospitality, or retailing decision support systems . Hands-on experience developing customer choice models, price elasticity estimations, and market potential estimations .

What You'll Be Doing The Treasury Management Analytics Analyst supports the transformation of Treasury Management data into actionable insights and reporting solutions that enhance decision-making, product performance, and operational efficiency. This role assists in developing dashboards, reports, and data visualizations while building foundational analytical and technical skills. The position works under guidance to support reporting, automation, and process improvement initiatives, partnering with stakeholders to deliver accurate and meaningful insights across Treasury Operations. What We're Looking For Support the development and maintenance of dashboards, reports, and analytics solutions. Analyze datasets to identify basic trends and data insights. Assist in delivering accurate and timely reporting outputs. Help translate data into clear summaries and insights for stakeholders. Support automation efforts for reporting processes and workflows. Identify opportunities for process improvements and assist with implementation. Contribute to improving reporting efficiency through standardized tools and practices. Gather reporting requirements from business partners with guidance. Assist in maintaining standardized metrics, KPIs, and data definitions. Support stakeholder communication by providing data and reporting outputs. Perform data validation checks to ensure accuracy and consistency. Support data governance and quality control activities. Participate in User Acceptance Testing (UAT) and issue tracking. Maintain documentation for reporting processes and datasets. What You'll Bring Strong analytical thinking and problem-solving skills. Ability to translate data into clear, actionable insights and business narratives. Demonstrated ability to create visually effective dashboards and reports. High attention to detail and commitment to data accuracy. Effective communication skills with the ability to present insights to diverse audiences. Self-motivated with a continuous improvement mindset. Education & Experience Minimum Required: Bachelor's degree in Finance or Business, or equivalent experience. Minimum Required: 0+ years of experience. Preferred: 1+ years of experience in analytics, reporting, or Treasury/operations. Licenses & Credentials Minimum Required: None. Systems & Technology Minimum Required: Proficiency in Microsoft Office (Excel, PowerPoint, Word, Outlook), including Excel data analysis (formulas, pivot tables), basic SQL or data querying knowledge, familiarity with BI tools (e.g., Power BI, Tableau). Preferred: Exposure to data platforms (e.g., Snowflake, PostgreSQL), exposure to automation tools (e.g., Make, Zapier) or scripting (Python, R), familiarity with APIs and data integration, exposure to treasury or banking platforms.

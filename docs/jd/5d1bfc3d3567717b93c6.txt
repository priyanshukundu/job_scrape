Senior Associate, Product Management - Profiles & Favorites | Capital One Travel About Capital One Travel & The Role Capital One Travel is reimagining how travelers discover, plan, and book their journeys. As a Senior Associate Product Manager (SA, PdM) on the Consumer Web team, you will own the end-to-end product strategy, discovery, and execution for two foundational pillars of the digital travel journey: Favorites & Trip Planning and Customer Profiles & Personalization . Travel planning is rarely linear. Travelers often research destinations, compare properties, and assemble itineraries across multiple sessions before booking. In this role, you will build intuitive, high-utility features that help travelers organize their ideas, save preferences, and receive tailored, context-aware recommendations, turning passive browsing into booked trips. What You’ll Do Own the Roadmap: Build, prioritize, and drive the end-to-end product roadmap for Trip Planning features (Favorites, saved searches, collaboration) and Customer Profiles/Personalization. Day-to-Day Product Execution: Partner closely with Design as well as Backend and Frontend engineers every day. Lead standard agile ceremonies, including daily stand-ups, backlog grooming, sprint planning, capacity forecasting, and sprint retrospectives. Design & Discovery Partnership: Collaborate deeply with Product Design and UX Research to run user testing App Partnership & Cross-Channel Delivery (COTA): Partner closely with the Capital One Travel on App (COTA) team to build, align, and ship Trip Planning/Favorites and Customer Profile experiences natively on the mobile app, ensuring seamless parity and a synchronized multi-platform traveler journey. Data-Informed Experimentation: Formulate hypotheses, design rigorous A/B and multivariate tests, monitor user behavior, and translate test results into iterative product enhancements. Balance Strategy and Execution: Maintain a clear long-term vision for personalized travel planning and Customer Profiles while consistently shipping near-term wins that move KPIs. Voice of Customer (VOC) Advocacy: Continuously pressure-test product hypotheses using both quantitative behavioral telemetry and qualitative customer feedback. What We Look For Ownership Mindset: Highly proactive and accountable; comfortable navigating ambiguity and taking end-to-end ownership across a domain. Customer Obsession & Design Sensibility: Deep empathy for the traveler journey, advocating relentlessly for clean user experiences, strong visual craft, and customer utility. Analytical Rigor: Grounded in data; adept at defining meaningful success metrics, and validating decisions with VOC data. Clear Communication: Able to articulate technical constraints, design trade-offs, and business value concisely to leadership, designers, engineers, and cross-functional peers. Results-Driven Focus: Relentlessly focused on tangible customer impact and measurable business outcomes. Basic Qualifications: Bachelor’s degree or military experience At least 1 year of product management experience or at least 1 year of experience in product design, agile delivery, business analysis, data science, or software engineering At least 1 year of product management experience building, testing, and shipping customer-facing digital consumer products Preferred Qualifications: Bachelor’s degree in computer science or engineering 1+ years translating business strategy or analysis into consumer facing digital products 1+ years of experience working on core product platforms powering digital experiences 1+ years working with cross functional teams as a product owner in an agile development process 1+ years of experience in travel tech, hospitality, e-commerce, or two-sided marketplace platforms At this time, Capital One will not sponsor a new applicant for employment authorization for this position.

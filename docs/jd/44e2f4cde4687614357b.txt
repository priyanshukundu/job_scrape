Job Description Role Summary: The Associate Director, Independent Medical Education, (AD IME), position resides in the Value & Implementation (V&I) organization, which includes Global Medical and Scientific Affairs as well as Outcomes Research. This position sits within the V&I Global Medical & Value Capabilities (V&I GMVC) Global Medical Proficiency and External Affairs (GMPEA) organization and is primarily responsible for planning, coordinating and reviewing IME grant proposals. This position is responsible for IME strategy including IME grant-proposal assessment, conducting educational-needs assessments to identify and address gaps in health-care professional (HCP) education, cross-functional collaboration, budget management, and compliance oversight. Responsibilities and Primary Activities: Develops and executes IME strategy by identifying practice gaps and supporting high-impact education designed to improve clinical practice and patient care. Supports innovative, independently created medical education that is aligned with our therapeutic area (TA) priorities to advance HCP knowledge, skills, competence, and performance. Conducts educational-needs assessments to identify educational gaps of HCP learners ensuring grant support is addressing evidence-based needs, working with regional company representatives to ensure global understanding of educational needs. Partners cross-functionally and within GMPEA to understand educational needs from stakeholders including Global Professional Society Affairs (GPSA) and the Healthcare Quality Improvement Center of Excellence (HQI CoE). Maintains knowledge of trends in IME and continuing professional development (CPD) along with emerging technological approaches using adult-learning principles. Establishes and facilitates Grant Review Committee (GRC) meetings across areas of responsibility to gain insights on medical and scientific alignment of IME grant proposals to areas of educational need (AEN) and TA priorities. Liaises with global and U.S. V&I stakeholders to compliantly share outcomes of completed IME initiatives and TA educational-impact summaries. Ensures compliance with existing internal and external guidelines as they pertain to continuing medical education, as well as the internal standards of our company based on its interpretation of the applicable laws and regulations. Assesses, negotiates and monitors operating budget for approved IME programs throughout the lifecycle of a grant. Maintains appropriate processes and financial controls to ensure completion of programs within established budgets. Attends, monitors and assesses supported IME programs. Required Qualifications, Skills and Experience Minimum: BA/BS degree. Minimum of 5 years of relevant experience across the pharmaceutical industry and professional medical organizations, with at least one year of pharmaceutical industry experience required. High level of business acumen and influence, as well as communication, planning, collaborative, interpersonal, and organizational skills. Experience applying adult-learning principles and assessing IME against the seven levels of Moore’s Outcomes of Knowledge and Practice Gaps. Preferred: MPH, PhD, or credentialed health-care professional degree (PharmD, NP, PA). Previous external-facing position. Experience in one or more TAs with company-supported IME.

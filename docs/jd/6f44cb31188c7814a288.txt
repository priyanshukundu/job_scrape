Description About the Team Corporate Strategy & Research (CS&R) group is a ~40-person internal strategy consulting team within Liberty Mutual Insurance, a Fortune 100 company with 45,000 employees in 900 offices worldwide. Established in 1972, CS&R has an outstanding track-record of developing future leaders for Liberty Mutual such as business unit President, Chief Marketing Officer, Senior Vice-President of Product Imagination, Transformation Office Strategy Director. The Details As a Senior Associate Consultant, you will work with a small project team to advise Liberty Mutual’s senior executives in making strategic decisions. Projects are typically in the areas of innovation, strategy development, forward-looking thought leadership, or business transformation. You will engage in a targeted training program and you will partner with an experienced Manager Director who will support your development, provide feedback and perspective, and serve as a mentor. Through your project work, you will develop key thought-partner relationships with leaders and executives across the organization. Responsibilities: Responsibilities will be commensurate with your skills, abilities, and development needs. Each team member interfaces directly with the team lead as well as members of the client organization. All members of the team are involved in the analysis and development of reports and presentations for clients and other stakeholders. While no two projects are the same, most have similar team structures and will typically last 2-4 months. Understanding the strategic issues faced by your client and the role of your team in addressing those issues Designing workplans, conducting targeted research, collecting data from various internal and external sources (e.g. executive interviews and workshops, customer surveys) Performing high-impact analysis and evaluating alternative solutions Crafting storylines that clearly articulate findings, insights and recommendations Communicating with senior leadership to move the organization to action Contributing to CS&R initiatives such as recruiting, talent development, thought leadership or alumni engagement initiatives such as recruiting, talent development, thought leadership or alumni engagement You will support projects by designing and executing analysis and evaluating the impact of alternate management decisions on company business and financial performance by: Sharing your insights to build cohesive, compelling, logical arguments and assist your team in creating and communicating presentation material that influences senior management. Designing data collection strategies to yield high-quality, reliable data, utilizing your understanding of the strengths and weaknesses of various analysis tools and techniques. Performing the analysis and the cross-functional integration required to support business decisions. Along with up-front and on-going training you will partner with a Career Coach - a senior consultant - who is there to help you with your development, provide feedback, and serve as a mentor. As you work on projects you will develop key thought-partner relationships with company managers and executives throughout the organization. These relationships will serve as platforms for strategic advice and your future career development. Qualifications The group is looking for intellectually curious, creative, and driven individuals with an interest in developing the management consulting toolkit. Bachelor’s degree with a demonstrated record of academic success 1 - 3 years professional experience preferred. Top-tier strategy consulting experience preferred Strong quantitative, analytical and problem-solving skills Strong written communication, oral communication, and interpersonal skills Ability to influence others using supporting analysis Strong time-management skills Ability to adapt to a changing environment and client needs Ability to work independently and as a member of a team Able to travel, up to 10% (including international, as needed) What really gets our attention: You are motivated by high impact, high visibility work You are comfortable operating in an ambiguous environment You have interest in financial services and insurance and demonstrate strong leadership potential Required steps: All candidates must apply to the job posting on the Liberty Mutual Careers website, with their cover letter and resume.

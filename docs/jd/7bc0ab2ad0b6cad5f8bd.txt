The Opportunity Abbott Rapid Diagnostics is seeking a highly motivated Research Associate II – Oligonucleotide Chemistry to join our Research & Development team in San Diego, California. In this role, you will contribute to the development of next-generation molecular diagnostic products by supporting the synthesis, purification, characterization, and evaluation of oligonucleotides used in diagnostic applications. You will work in a collaborative, fast-paced environment alongside scientists and engineers to deliver high-quality oligonucleotide reagents critical to assay and product development. What You'll Work On Synthesize DNA and RNA oligonucleotides using automated solid-phase oligonucleotide synthesizers. Perform oligonucleotide cleavage, deprotection, desalting, and purification using established laboratory procedures. Purify oligonucleotides using reverse-phase HPLC, ion-exchange chromatography, and cartridge-based purification platforms. Characterize oligonucleotides using analytical techniques including HPLC, UV-Vis spectroscopy, LC-MS, and related methods. Maintain detailed and accurate laboratory records, including synthesis documentation, analytical results, yields, and quality-control data. Collaborate with assay development, product development, and cross-functional teams to provide oligonucleotide reagents and technical support. Execute laboratory experiments, prepare samples, and assist in the evaluation of oligonucleotide performance in diagnostic applications. Operate, maintain, and troubleshoot laboratory instrumentation and ensure compliance with laboratory safety and quality requirements. Required Qualifications Bachelor's degree in Chemistry, Biochemistry, Organic Chemistry, Molecular Biology, or a related scientific discipline. 1-4 years of industry or academic laboratory experience in oligonucleotide chemistry, nucleic acid technologies, or a related field. Hands-on experience with oligonucleotide synthesis and post-synthesis processing techniques. Familiarity with analytical characterization methods, including HPLC, UV-Vis spectroscopy, and mass spectrometry. Strong organizational and documentation skills with the ability to maintain accurate laboratory records. Demonstrated attention to detail and commitment to producing high-quality, reproducible data. Strong communication, collaboration, and problem-solving skills. Ability to work effectively both independently and within multidisciplinary teams. Preferred Qualifications Experience operating automated oligonucleotide synthesizers such as MerMade, Dr. Oligo, or equivalent platforms. Knowledge of solid-phase oligonucleotide synthesis and common probe and primer modifications. Hands-on experience with fluorescently labeled oligonucleotides, quenchers, and other diagnostic probe chemistries. Experience with post-synthetic oligonucleotide conjugation and modification techniques. Familiarity with oligonucleotide purification using reverse-phase and ion-exchange chromatography methods. Experience using chromatography systems such as Agilent or ÄKTA platforms. Experience with LC-MS analysis and interpretation for oligonucleotide characterization. Understanding of solid-phase organic synthesis principles and their application to nucleic acid chemistry. Experience supporting molecular diagnostic, qPCR, or nucleic acid assay development programs.

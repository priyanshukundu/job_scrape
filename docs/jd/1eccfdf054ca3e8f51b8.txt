Role overview We’re looking for a .Net Software Engineer to help design, build, and support our core platform. Our technology stack includes C#, .NET, PostgreSQL, AWS, Blazor, and MudBlazor, with services running across Lambda, ECS, and EC2. We build distributed systems that communicate through APIs and events, and we continue to improve our engineering practices, tooling, and platform capabilities. We take an AI-first approach to software development, using approved AI tools to support design, coding, testing, refactoring, documentation, and problem-solving. You’ll be encouraged to use these tools thoughtfully and responsibly as part of your day-to-day work. This role suits someone who enjoys hands-on development, collaborative problem-solving, and delivering reliable software in a practical, iterative environment.

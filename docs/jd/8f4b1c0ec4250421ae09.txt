Job Title: Scientist I, Research Associate Job Description: The Role Entegris is currently seeking a Scientist I, Research Associate to join our Research & Development ICPS NA team. This position will be based in our Aurora, Illinois facility. In this role, you will provide laboratory support and assist in the development and commercialization of commercially viable products for chemical-mechanical planarization (CMP) applications at Entegris. This role will be responsible for independently performing wet lab experiments, product reliability tests, formulation experiments, fundamental wet-chemistry or instrument studies, data collection/summarization/analysis, and maintenance of the laboratory. What You’ll Do Participate in lab test method development and run laboratory tests for characterizing CMP slurries and abrasives Plan and execute experiments to understand slurry/abrasive behavior such as; colloidal stability, shelf stability, and abrasive properties Perform preliminary interpretation of data in order to recognize and correct test abnormalities, and present results to the team Prepare slurry samples and coordinate sample submission to quality lab and NPIE team Uphold and follow strict laboratory safety requirements and policies Maintain and control inventory of chemicals used for experimental products Collaborate with colleagues in the maintenance and upkeep of laboratory tools and instruments What We Seek BA/BS in Chemistry, Biochemistry, Material Science, or Engineering Technology. Recent college grads are welcome to apply! Hands-on experience with wet chemistry techniques and instrumentation used in chemistry laboratories.

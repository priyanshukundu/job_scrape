Strategic Intelligence: Software & AI Research Analyst (Denver)
D. E. Shaw
Location: Denver
Link: https://www.deshaw.com/careers/strategic-intelligence-software-ai-research-analyst-denver-5647

The D. E. Shaw group seeks a highly skilled research analyst to join its rapidly expanding Strategic Intelligence team in the firm’s new Denver office, with a dedicated focus on software, artificial intelligence, and related technology sectors. This team enhances the firm’s long-cycle, thematic industry coverage and delivers in-depth trend analysis to support investment decision-making. As part of a collaborative and innovative group, this role will help develop unique insights into the evolution of software markets, AI adoption, cloud infrastructure, enterprise technology, developer ecosystems, data platforms, cybersecurity, and other high-impact areas of technological change. Conduct research on software and AI markets, including enterprise software, generative AI, cloud computing, semiconductors and AI infrastructure, cybersecurity, data analytics, automation, and vertical software. Attend industry conferences, expert consultations, product briefings, and technology events to gather targeted insights from executives, customers, developers, consultants, researchers, and other key stakeholders. Track AI adoption trends across industries, including use cases, spending patterns, implementation challenges, productivity impacts, and competitive positioning. Analyze software business models, customer demand, pricing trends, usage metrics, product roadmaps, go-to-market strategies, and competitive landscapes. Produce clear, concise written summaries and analytical memos that synthesize findings and highlight investment-relevant implications. Monitor market developments, regulatory changes, product launches, earnings commentary, technology shifts, and other signals relevant to software and AI companies. Support strategic research initiatives tied to long-term technology themes, including the commercialization of AI, cloud migration, automation, cybersecurity resilience, and the evolution of enterprise software. Collaborate closely with investment analysts to help frame research questions, identify information gaps, and develop differentiated perspectives on companies and sectors. A bachelor’s degree or equivalent with at least two years of relevant experience; experience in technology research, software industry analysis, investment analysis, consulting, market intelligence, data analysis, or a related field is preferred. Deep interest in software, artificial intelligence, and broader technology trends, with the ability to evaluate both technical developments and business implications. A demonstrated record of academic and professional achievement, along with excellent verbal and written communication skills. Keen analytical skills, intellectual curiosity, and the ability to synthesize complex technical and market information into clear, actionable insights. Familiarity with software business models, enterprise technology purchasing, AI applications, cloud infrastructure, or technology sector competitive dynamics is a plus. Initiative, adaptability, and a collaborative and thoughtful approach to problem-solving. The expected annual base salary for this position is $120,000 to $145,000. Our compensation and benefits package includes variable compensation in the form of a year-end bonus, guaranteed in the first year of hire, and benefits including medical and prescription drug coverage, 401(k) contribution matching, wellness reimbursement, family building benefits, and a charitable gift match program.

Requisition #: 76894 The Role at a Glance We are excited to bring on highly motivated Supplemental Health Appeals Associate to our team within Workplace Solutions. As a Supplemental Health (SH) Appeals Associate you will be responsible for being a product subject matter expert to provide full, fair appeal reviews for multiple voluntary product lines. You will perform and complete full, fair reviews and adjudicating appeal decisions on Accident, Critical Illness, and Hospital Indemnity claims for our customers. You will analyze and manage a caseload of SH appeals claims. You will review, investigate, and make appeal determinations regarding coverage, compensability, and appropriateness of claims. You will provide technical information and detailed guidance to Claims management & teams regarding products, claims processes, regulations, and applicable systems for their assigned claims area. If you enjoy working in a fast-paced environment, then please read on! What you'll be doing You will be communicating with claimants, employers as needed to ensure complete submission of necessary information for appeal review. You will be leveraging knowledge of coverage, certificate provisions, eligibility & benefits as well as other appropriate resources (e.g., other internal teams, practices/procedures, clinical etc.) to include as part of your appeal review. You will be applying core knowledge of Supplemental Health/voluntary insurance products, claims processes and certificates to render appeal decisions You will be identifying, interpreting, and resolving gaps in financial calculations when applicable, share technical knowledge and approach on financial calculations with claims. You will be ensuring appeals processing is consistent with applicable policies, procedures, and department guidelines. You will be researching questions and escalations as needed for complete appeal review; liaises with various stakeholders from across the business (Enrollments, Legal, Compliance, Account Management, etc.) to obtain needed information to clarify and address issues. What we’re looking for Must-haves: 2+ Years of experience in Supplemental Health Claims: Accident, Critical Illness, Hospital Indemnity. (Required).

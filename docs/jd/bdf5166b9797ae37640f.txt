Job Description Risk Data & AI designs, builds, and operates governed data products, analytics solutions, and AI-enabled capabilities that support risk and compliance decision-making. The team applies modern data, software, and AI engineering practices to deliver scalable data pipelines, trusted analytical products, intelligent automation, and responsible AI solutions that enable TORC to identify emerging risks, generate actionable insights, and make timely, evidence-based decisions. We are looking for a Software Engineer with a passion for AI product development to join the team. Reporting to the Managing Engineer, Risk Data & AI, you will design, build, and run full-stack, AI-powered products that help TORC turn risk data into insight and action. This is a hands-on role where you will take ideas from concept to production, integrating Generative AI into enterprise-grade solutions that are scalable, secure, and responsibly implemented. Key Responsibilities Design and develop full-stack applications using Java Spring Boot/Python, and modern front-end frameworks (React, Angular, or Vue.js). Integrate Generative AI models and APIs into enterprise-grade solutions. Build and maintain APIs, microservices, and data pipelines to support AI-driv en features. Implement CI/CD pipelines, containerization (Docker), and orchestration (Kubernetes). Deploy and manage applications on cloud platforms, preferably Microsoft Azure. Collaborate with cross-functional teams, including Digital Product Managers and risk stakeholders, to deliver high-quality, AI-powered software solutions. Required Skills 3+ years of full-stack development experience with Java Spring Boot and/or Python. Proven experience with Generative AI frameworks (e.g., OpenAI, Hugging Face, LangChain) and APIs. Strong understanding of CI/CD pipelines, DevOps practices, and infrastructure as code. Hands-on experience with Microsoft Azure cloud services. Proficiency in front-end development using JavaScript, TypeScript, and modern frameworks. Familiarity with containerization (Docker) and orchestration (Kubernetes). Experience in RAG technology Excellent problem-solving skills and a passion for innovation. Desirable Skills Experience with vector databases, prompt engineering and fine-tuning LLMs. Exposure to multi-modal AI systems and agentic AI frameworks. Background in MLOps or AI model deployment on cloud platforms. #LI-SM1 Skills API Development, CI/CD, Full Stack Development, JavaScript, Microsoft Azure, Python (Programming Language), Retrieval-Augmented Generation, Software Development, Spring Boot Compensation Compensation offered for this role is 90,700.00 - 153,925.00 annually and is based on experience and qualifications.

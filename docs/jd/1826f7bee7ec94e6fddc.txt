About Clear Street: Clear Street is modernizing the brokerage ecosystem. Founded in 2018, Clear Street is a diversified financial services firm replacing the legacy infrastructure used across capital markets. We started from scratch by building a completely cloud-native clearing and custody system designed for today’s complex, global market. Our platform is fully integrated with central clearing houses and exchanges to support billions in trading volume per day. We’ve agonized about our data model abstractions, created horizontal scalability, and crafted thoughtful APIs. All so we can provide a best-in-class experience for our clients. By combining highly-skilled product and engineering talent with seasoned finance professionals, we’re building the essentials to compete in today’s fast-paced markets. The Team: You will work on a team that will own risk across Clear Street’s business lines, playing a crucial role in the success of our growing business. As a Prime Brokerage and Clearing Risk Manager specializing in options, you will contribute to creating, evolving and executing the risk frameworks and best practices that support our futures and options clearing as well as our prime brokerage clients. This is an impactful position that provides the opportunity to expand both your quantitative and client-facing skill sets. You will cover US and international listed options markets, across multi-asset portfolios. Responsibilities: Serve as a primary point of contact for clients, including options market makers, hedge funds and proprietary trading firms, on portfolio onboarding, risk reviews and ongoing risk management topics. Monitor intraday and end-of-day risk exposures and margin requirements across client options portfolios, including exposure to delta, gamma, vega and volatility and underlying price shocks. Track US and international options markets through OMS, clearing and risk systems, and escalate material issues such as concentrated positions, expiration and assignment risk, and large moves in volatility to senior management as needed. Develop and maintain robust margin, risk and stress-testing methodologies, including options-based portfolio margin (such as TIMS, SPAN and OCC STANS), customer margin under Reg T, and clearing margin. Monitor and manage clearing exposures, including OCC and clearinghouse margin and clearing fund requirements, exercise and assignment, and settlement risk. Generate and automate ad-hoc reporting to highlight key risk factors, scenario results and portfolio exposures. Communicate critical risk observations to senior risk management and external clients. Support ongoing initiatives to develop and enhance the firm’s risk, margin and clearing platforms. Collaborate with internal stakeholders, including technology, operations and product teams, to improve risk management infrastructure and margin capabilities. Requirements: At least five (5) years of professional experience in prime brokerage risk, clearing risk, options trading risk or related roles, either on the sell-side or buy-side, with direct exposure to listed options and equity derivatives. A minimum of five (5) years of overall professional experience in financial services; prior experience as a trader or a prime brokerage / prime services risk manager highly preferred. Bachelor’s degree in Finance, Economics, Mathematics or Financial Engineering required; Master’s degree preferred. Strong understanding of options pricing and risk measures (the Greeks, implied volatility, skew and term structure), complex multi-leg strategies, and equity and index products. Proficiency in Python and SQL preferred. Familiarity with industry-standard tools such as Bloomberg, ICE and FactSet, and with options risk and clearing platforms. Working knowledge of the regulatory frameworks, capital and margin rules for options and clearing, including Reg T, portfolio margin, SEC Rule 15c3-1 and 15c3-3, and OCC clearing member requirements. Strong communication skills, with the ability to engage confidently with clients and regulators. Proactive, collaborative and a strong team player with a high sense of ownership. Demonstrated project management experience and the ability to work closely with engineering and cross-functional teams. Analytical and detail-oriented, with strong quantitative aptitude.

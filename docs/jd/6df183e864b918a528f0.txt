This role serves as a key finance business partner, providing financial planning, forecasting, reporting, and analytical support to commercial and functional leaders. The position plays a critical role in the monthly close, Last Best Estimate (LBE), annual Plan, and strategic decision-making processes by delivering timely insights that drive business performance. The successful candidate will be a highly analytical, results-oriented finance professional with strong business acumen, communication skills, and the ability to influence decisions through insightful financial analysis. This individual will thrive in a collaborative environment and be comfortable managing multiple priorities while working independently with minimal supervision. What You’ll Work On Core Job Responsibilities: Partner with the Corporate Financial Services (CFS) team to support month-end and quarter-end close activities, ensuring accurate and timely financial reporting. Lead and support the forecasting, annual Plan, and LBE processes for the U.S. Commercial business. Develop financial forecasts and perform variance analysis to identify key business drivers, risks, and opportunities. Present financial results, forecasts, and key business insights to finance and commercial leadership, highlighting risks, opportunities, and recommendations. Partner with commercial and functional leaders to provide financial insights, identify risks and opportunities, and support data-driven decision-making. Monitor spending against budget and provide recommendations to improve financial performance and resource allocation. Evaluate business initiatives, investments, and growth opportunities through financial modeling and scenario analysis, providing actionable recommendations. Ensure compliance with Abbott, Division, and Corporate financial policies, procedures, and internal controls. Drive continuous improvement in financial processes, reporting, and forecasting methodologies. Lead or contribute to special projects and strategic initiatives as assigned. Required Qualifications Bachelor’s degree in Accounting, Finance, Business Administration, or a related field. 3+ years of experience in financial planning and analysis, forecasting, budgeting, and month-end close activities. Advanced proficiency in Microsoft Excel and PowerPoint. Strong analytical, financial modeling, and problem-solving skills. Demonstrated ability to communicate financial concepts and insights to business partners and leadership teams. Preferred Qualifications Excellent written and verbal communication abilities. Strong analytical, critical thinking, and data interpretation skills. Highly organized with strong interpersonal and follow-up skills. Exceptional attention to detail and commitment to accuracy. Proven ability to manage multiple priorities and meet deadlines in a fast-paced environment. Experience with SAP, TM1, Power BI, or similar financial reporting and planning systems.

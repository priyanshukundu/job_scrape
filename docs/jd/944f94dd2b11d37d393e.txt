Associate Scientist, Flow Cytometry
Regeneron
Location: TARRYTOWN
Link: https://regeneron.wd1.myworkdayjobs.com/Careers/job/TARRYTOWN/Associate-Scientist--Flow-Cytometry_R52942

Build our future together: At Regeneron, we use science and innovation to develop life-changing medicines for people with serious diseases. We are seeking an Associate Scientist in Flow Cytometry to join our Protein Expression Sciences team, supporting a busy departmental core facility in an on-site capacity. In this role, you will operate and maintain high-speed cell sorters and analyzers while collaborating with scientific staff to develop flow cytometry protocols and deliver high-quality data. This position offers the opportunity to contribute to a fast-growing, science-driven organization making a meaningful difference to patients worldwide. When & where: Location: Tarrytown, NY Work model/flexibility: On-site Discover your role: Operate multi-laser, high-speed spectral cell sorters and analyzers independently Maintain, align, calibrate, and troubleshoot instruments to meet specific experimental requirements Perform cell sorting and data acquisition for scientific staff Provide consultation, protocol design, and interpretation of flow cytometry experiments Analyze data using a variety of flow cytometry software Train users to operate analyzers and support instrument qualification Partner with field engineers and technical support, staying current on the latest technologies Write standard operating procedures (SOPs) as needed This role requires: Bachelor’s degree and 1-2 years of relevant experience Exposure to a multi-laser, high-speed cell sorter and analyzer Preferred: Knowledge of flow cytometry principles and hands-on flow cytometry technical experience Preferred: A desire to grow your flow cytometry skillset Does this sound like you?

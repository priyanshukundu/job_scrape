About the team and the role: We are looking for a talented mid-level engineer to join the Search Backend team and help us push the boundaries of index distribution. You'll be at the heart of building and operating the distribution pipelines that deliver search indexes to thousands of Search nodes, serving as the critical backbone that keeps eBay's search engine fast, fresh, and reliable. This is a high-stakes, high-impact role where your work is the foundation of a superior Search experience for hundreds of millions of eBay users every day. Responsibilities: Design and build core components of eBay's search index distribution infrastructure including real-time streaming pipelines that move data with precision and speed across thousands of nodes. Architect highly scalable, fault-tolerant pipelines that power both real-time and batch index distribution at global scale. Build intelligent systems for auto-managing index distribution clusters — think self-healing infrastructure with proactive health monitoring, smart alerting, and automated remediation. Take full ownership of distributed pipeline components from initial design all the way to production, driving quality and reliability at every step. Write clean, well-documented code that is maintainable, extensible, and ready to scale and built for the long haul. Work closely with Search Backend, Infrastructure, and partner teams to ensure indexes are fresh, consistent, and delivered on time, every time. Dive deep into complex distributed system issues debugging and troubleshooting pipeline failures, index delivery anomalies, and performance bottlenecks across a large-scale, multi-pool infrastructure.

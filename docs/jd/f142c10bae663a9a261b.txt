A role in Enterprise Risk Management (ERM) means helping protect Target by identifying, assessing and managing a portfolio of the most significant strategic, financial, operational, compliance, reputational and event-based risks the company faces. The Critical Risk Activation function serves as the enterprise’s mechanism for mobilizing a unified, decisive and well-governed response to critical events, strengthening Target’s resilience and safeguarding its brand, guests, team members and shareholders. As Principal, Critical Risk Activation, you will lead and orchestrate Target’s response to critical events across all risk domains, including operational reliability, cybersecurity, reputational risk and corporate security. Operating with composure, decisiveness and sound judgment in high-consequence environments, you will convene the right stakeholders and subject matter experts, establish clarity of decision making and accountability, and drive an integrated, enterprise-wide response. In parallel, you will define and advance the enterprise strategy for Critical Risk Activation, building a sustainable and scalable capability within the broader ERM ecosystem. As a principal-level individual contributor, you will advise senior and executive leaders, influence enterprise decision-making, and drive alignment and outcomes across multiple pyramids and stakeholder groups. As Principal, Critical Risk Activation, you will: Serve as the enterprise lead for the activation, coordination and governance of Target’s response to critical events spanning reliability, cybersecurity, reputational and corporate security risk domains, from initial escalation through stabilization and recovery. Rapidly assess emerging events, determine when enterprise activation is required , and convene the appropriate executive, functional and technical stakeholders to enable coordinated, timely and effective decision-making. Establish and maintain clarity of roles, decision rights, priorities and action ownership throughout active events, resolving competing priorities and driving disciplined execution across the enterprise. Provide senior and executive leaders with timely, concise and decision-ready guidance , including situational assessments, scenario analyses, risk trade-offs and recommended courses of action. Define and execute a multi-year strategy to establish, mature and scale the Critical Risk Activation capability as an integral part of the broader ERM ecosystem, including its scope, operating model, governance and maturity roadmap. Design, implement and continuously strengthen the enterprise activation framework , including escalation criteria, response protocols, playbooks, communication cadences and governance mechanisms, aligned with ERM priorities and established risk management practices. Partner with the Portfolio Management team to identify, prioritize and assess the enterprise’s highest-priority activation risks , developing mitigation and preparedness strategies that reduce exposure and strengthen enterprise resilience. Lead an enterprise program of readiness exercises, tabletop simulations and scenario planning with senior leaders to validate capabilities, identify interdependencies and strengthen cross-functional coordination in partnership with the governance team. Lead structured post-event reviews to capture lessons learned, establish accountability for remediation and embed improvements across processes, controls and governance. Build trusted partnerships across Reliability, Cybersecurity, Corporate Security, Corporate Affairs and other key enterprise teams to establish Critical Risk Activation as a trusted partner in enterprise response and preparedness. Integrate AI and emerging capabilities into Critical Risk Activation practices to accelerate situational assessment, strengthen scenario planning and support faster, more informed decision-making throughout an activation.

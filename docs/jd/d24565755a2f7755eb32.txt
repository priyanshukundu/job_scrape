Position Overview The Associate Director IT Projects, Enterprise Service Management (ESM), is responsible for leading strategic enterprise service management transformation projects across IQVIA, with an initial focus on modernizing, standardizing, and consolidating service management capabilities on the ServiceNow platform. This role provides overall project leadership, governance, planning, execution oversight, stakeholder management, risk management, budget oversight, and delivery coordination across multiple business and technology workstreams. The Associate Director IT Projects will work closely with business leaders, product owners, architects, development teams, implementation partners, and operational stakeholders to ensure successful delivery of Enterprise Service Management capabilities that support IQVIA business functions, technology teams, and internal service organizations. Essential Functions Lead project management for Enterprise Service Management transformation initiatives and strategic platform projects. Project manage the modernization, standardization, and consolidation of enterprise service management capabilities on ServiceNow. Manage project governance, steering committees, status reporting, and executive communications. Develop and manage integrated project plans, roadmaps, milestones, dependencies, and deliverables across multiple workstreams. Coordinate activities between business stakeholders, technology teams, vendors, and implementation partners. Manage project financials, budgets, forecasts, resource plans, and vendor engagements. Identify, assess, and manage project risks, issues, assumptions, and dependencies. Drive decision-making processes and facilitate resolution of business and technical challenges. Ensure alignment between business objectives, service delivery goals, and enterprise platform strategies. Oversee requirements, solution design, testing, deployment, and transition activities across the project lifecycle. Lead organizational change management, business readiness, communication, and adoption activities. Monitor project performance and delivery metrics, ensuring commitments are achieved on time and within budget. Provide leadership, direction, and coordination across project managers, business analysts, architects, and technical teams. Support continuous improvement initiatives and drive adoption of ServiceNow and enterprise service management best practices. Qualifications Bachelor's Degree in Business Administration, Computer Science, Information Systems, Engineering, or equivalent experience required. 10+ years of project management, transformation delivery, or related technology leadership experience. 5+ years managing large-scale enterprise technology projects. Experience leading ServiceNow, ITSM, ESM, platform consolidation, or enterprise service management initiatives. Demonstrated success managing complex, cross-functional transformation projects involving multiple stakeholders and vendors. Strong understanding of software delivery methodologies including Agile, Scrum, Waterfall, and Hybrid delivery models. Experience managing budgets, forecasts, resource plans, contracts, and vendor relationships. Excellent executive communication, stakeholder management, negotiation, and leadership skills. Ability to manage multiple concurrent projects and priorities in a global environment. Preferred Qualifications PMP, PgMP, PMI-ACP, Certified Scrum Master, or equivalent certification. Lean Six Sigma certification required or equivalent process improvement experience. Knowledge of ITIL and Service Management principles. Experience leading global projects across multiple regions and business functions. Familiarity with enterprise transformation, organizational change management, and business process reengineering. Experience with ServiceNow Service Management platform preferred.

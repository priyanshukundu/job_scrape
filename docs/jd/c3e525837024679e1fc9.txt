The Senior Manager, Treasury Accounting will report directly to the Director, Treasury Accounting and will play a critical role in the US GAAP recording, reporting, modeling and analysis of stock-based compensation US Fringe and basic and diluted shares. The individual will be a key liaison and support to Equity Operations, Treasury Capital Market Operations, Benefits and the US Corp Staff FP&A area with significant exposure to senior management. The Senior Manager position is a highly visible position and requires a proactive self-starter with the ability to effectively interact, communicate, and present results, ideas and concepts clearly with multiple departments and to many levels within the organization. The position will have substantial exposure to all areas of Treasury accounting and provides an opportunity to enhance US GAAP reporting, analysis and budgeting skills, strengthen verbal and written communication skills and to expand business partnering (internal/external) and leadership skills in a global environment. Major Responsibilities and Accountabilities : Total company Stock Based Compensation expense recording and reporting for BMS employees and Board of Directors. Create Stock Compensation budget and projection models, develop Stock Compensation Statement of Cash Flows impacts and analyze Stock Compensation components as well as actual and budget variances. Prepare management reports, fluctuation analyses and other deliverables as part of the quarterly accounting close. Present detail results to Management. Responsible for BMY stock accounting including dividends, common stock, treasury stock and additional paid in capital. Prepare Basic and Diluted share calculations and analysis, including budget and periodic projection updates, in close coordination with Treasury Operations. Ensure that all Stock Based Compensation and Share disclosures in the Company Security and Exchange Commission (SEC) quarterly 10Q and annual 10K filings are timely, complete and accurate. Leads financial reporting, forecasting, analysis, and management insights for actual, budget and projected US employee fringe benefit costs. The role partners with FP&A, Benefits, Payroll, HR, Hyderabad teams to deliver timely and accurate reporting of Fringe costs, identify key financial drivers, and provide actionable insights to leadership. Proactive interaction with cross-functional team partners, including Equity Operations, Treasury Operations, Investor Relations, Consolidated Financial Reporting, Global Benefits, Tax, US Corp Staff and Corporate Financial Planning to accomplish monthly, quarterly and annual actual and budget duties as listed above. Manage third party service providers, including stock plan administrators, actuaries and external auditors. Key contact for external auditors and internal Business Controls Functions function during quarterly reviews and annual audits. Serve as an internal subject matter expert in Stock Based Compensation and EPS Share calculations as well as BMS US Fringe and fringe components. Cross train with other Treasury Accounting areas (hedging, derivatives, cash, investments, 11K Filings, debt, pensions). Qualifications Minimum Requirements Minimum education of a bachelor’s degree or advanced degree in accounting is required. A CPA license is required. Minimum of five (5) years of accounting experience, preferably with a Big Four public accounting firm or a large multinational pharmaceutical company. Strong US GAAP technical accounting, analytical and problem-solving skills. Ability to work independently and be self-motivated as well as to communicate and be a collaborative team player. Must be highly organized, accurate and detail-oriented, ability to manage numerous projects or deliverables simultaneously in a dynamic, fast-paced, deadline-driven environment. Advanced use of AI, excel and data base management skills.

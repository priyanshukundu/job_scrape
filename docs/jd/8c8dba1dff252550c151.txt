JOB DESCRIPTION: 1.Conceptualize, design, program, test, implement and deploy software applications using the appropriate documentation, processes, and quality standards. 2. Provide business and project expertise when collaborating with clients, IT resources, vendors, consultants, and other stakeholders to identify the user requirements, assess technologies, recommend, and implement technical solutions. 3. Apply knowledge of application architecture principles. 4. Perform formal code reviews to ensure specifications and standards are met. 5. Mentor members of development team promoting good development principles and provide expertise in complex problem solving. 6. Oversee and provide technical direction to multiple Sprint teams. 7. Apply defined code management, build, and deployment standards and practices. 8. Apply expert knowledge of Agile principles and lead and improve SCRUM ceremonies. 9. Ensure accuracy of coding, create technical documentation; utilize software configuration, version, and quality management processes. 10. Work with project participants to maximize productivity, effectiveness, and timely completion. 11. Independently apply problem solving skills to investigate and research complex system problems to establish root cause and resolution. 12. Share information with team; recognize confidential information and handle with discretion. 13. Participate in special projects as assigned. MINIMUM EDUCATION/EXPERIENCE REQUIREMENTS: Bachelor of Science degree in Computer Science, or a directly related field of study, plus the following experience: (1) 5 years of experience in a structured environment working on systems, applications, programming, development, testing, implementation, and deployment. (2) 5 years of experience in C#, .NET Framework 4.5+, and T-SQL. (3) 3 years of experience in Microsoft Azure. (4) 3 years of experience in modern UI development Angular JS and Angular V10 or above. (5) 3 years of experience .NET Core, JavaScript, REST API, and Entity Framework. Experience may be gained concurrently. Employer will accept any suitable combination of education, training or experience.

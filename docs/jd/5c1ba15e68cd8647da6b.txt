Become a part of our caring community CenterWell Pharmacy is seeking a strategic and results-driven Associate Director, Technology Solutions to lead the delivery and modernization of technology capabilities that support our pharmacy business. This role will partner closely with Product, Pharmacy Operations, Clinical, Finance, Compliance, and Technology leaders to drive innovation, improve operational performance, and deliver exceptional experiences for patients, providers, and associates. As a key technology leader, you will be responsible for shaping technology strategy, leading large-scale transformation initiatives, managing high-performing teams, and ensuring the successful delivery of critical pharmacy technology solutions. This position requires a strong combination of business acumen, technical leadership, operational excellence, and stakeholder management skills. As the Associate Director, Technology Solutions at CenterWell Pharmacy, you will play a critical role in shaping and executing technology strategies that drive business growth, operational efficiency, and pharmacy modernization. This position serves as a trusted business partner and technology leader, responsible for delivering scalable solutions, supporting enterprise transformation initiatives, and fostering innovation across pharmacy technology platforms. Key Responsibilities: Strategy & Transformation Develop and execute technology roadmaps that align with CenterWell Pharmacy's business objectives and long-term strategic vision. Lead pharmacy modernization initiatives, including cloud transformation, digital enablement, automation, AI, and platform modernization efforts. Evaluate emerging technologies and industry trends to identify opportunities that improve business outcomes and create competitive advantage. Drive innovation and continuous improvement across pharmacy technology platforms and services. Business Leadership & Strategic Partnership Partner with business, product, and technology leaders to translate strategic priorities into actionable technology solutions. Serve as a trusted advisor to senior leadership, providing recommendations on technology investments, operational improvements, and transformation strategies. Build strong relationships across Pharmacy Operations, Clinical Services, Finance, Compliance, and Enterprise Technology teams. Support business case development, roadmap planning, and prioritization activities. Technology Delivery & Operations Lead large-scale, cross-functional programs and strategic initiatives from planning through execution. Establish governance models, performance metrics, and reporting mechanisms to measure technology effectiveness and value realization. Ensure operational stability, scalability, and performance of pharmacy systems and technology platforms. Drive process improvements and operational excellence initiatives that improve productivity, quality, and service delivery. Support ongoing Run, Maintenance, and Prioritization activities to ensure business continuity and operational effectiveness. Architecture & Modernization Collaborate with architecture and engineering teams to design scalable, secure, and resilient technology solutions. Support cloud adoption and modernization efforts leveraging platforms such as Google Cloud Platform (GCP), Microsoft Azure, and Amazon Web Services (AWS). Champion Agile, DevOps, Automation, and Site Reliability Engineering (SRE) best practices. Promote technology standards, governance frameworks, and architectural consistency across solutions. Vendor & Financial Management Lead vendor evaluations, contract negotiations, and ongoing vendor performance management activities. Manage technology budgets, forecasting, and investment planning to maximize business value. Ensure strategic alignment and accountability across vendor partnerships and delivery teams. Risk, Security & Compliance Ensure compliance with healthcare and pharmacy regulatory requirements, including HIPAA, PCI, and internal governance standards. Partner with cybersecurity, risk, audit, and compliance teams to mitigate technology risks and maintain strong controls. Support disaster recovery, business continuity, and resiliency planning for critical pharmacy systems. People Leadership Lead, coach, and develop managers, engineers, architects, analysts, and technology professionals. Foster a culture of accountability, innovation, collaboration, and continuous learning. Support career development, succession planning, talent retention, and organizational growth. Lead teams across onshore, offshore, and vendor delivery models. Use your skills to make an impact Required Qualifications Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, Engineering, or a related field. 10+ years of experience in pharmacy technology, healthcare technology, or a similarly regulated industry. 5+ years of leadership experience managing technology teams and people leaders. Experience leading enterprise-scale technology initiatives, transformation programs, and modernization efforts. Strong understanding of Agile, Scrum, DevOps, Lean, and product-focused delivery methodologies. Experience with healthcare regulatory and compliance requirements, including HIPAA and PCI. Experience implementing and supporting cloud-based solutions using Azure, AWS, and/or Google Cloud Platform (GCP). Demonstrated ability to manage technology budgets, forecasts, vendor contracts, and resource planning. Strong analytical, problem-solving, and decision-making capabilities. Excellent communication, presentation, and stakeholder management skills. Proven ability to lead complex cross-functional initiatives and influence executive stakeholders. Preferred Qualifications Master's degree in Business Administration, Computer Science, Information Systems, or a related discipline. Experience supporting Pharmacy Benefit Management (PBM), specialty pharmacy, mail-order pharmacy, or pharmacy fulfillment operations. Experience leading large-scale cloud migration and modernization programs. Experience implementing AI, automation, data analytics, and digital transformation solutions. Industry certifications such as PMP, SAFe, AWS, Azure, GCP, TOGAF, or ITIL. Demonstrated success leading geographically distributed teams across onshore, offshore, and vendor-supported delivery models.

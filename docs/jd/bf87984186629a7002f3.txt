Third-Party Risk Management Analyst
Stifel
Location: Saint Louis, MO, US

What You'll Be Doing Under the direction of Third-Party Risk Management (TPRM) leadership, the Third-Party Risk Management Analyst supports the TPRM Program and the Enterprise Risk Management framework. The position assists with third-party intake and onboarding, inherent risk assessments, due diligence, ongoing monitoring, issue tracking, reporting, and offboarding. The Analyst coordinates with business owners, third parties, and internal subject matter experts to help identify, document, escalate, and monitor third-party risk in accordance with established policies, procedures, and risk standards. What We're Looking For • Support third-party intake, onboarding, risk assessments, due diligence, ongoing monitoring, issue management, and offboarding activities.• Assist with Inherhent Risk Questionnaires (IRQs), Due Diligence Questionnaires (DDQs), and other risk-based reviews, including reviewing supporting documentation and coordinating follow-up with business owners, third parties, and internal subject matter experts.• Maintain accurate TPRM records and track assessments, identified risks, remediation activities, approvals, and outstanding actions.• Identify and document potential third-party risks, control gaps, and exceptions, escalating concerns in accordance with established policies and procedures.• Support TPRM reporting, metrics, audits, regulatory examinations, committee materials, outside due diligence requests, and other program initiatives.• Perform data-quality reviews and reconciliations, support process improvements, maintain awareness of applicable TPRM requirements, and protect confidential information. Key Working Relationships• Business Owners, and other business stakeholders responsible for third-party relationships.• Risk, control, and oversight partners, including other Enterprise Risk Management stakeholders, Information Security, Privacy, Information Technology, Compliance, Legal, Procurement, and Internal Audit.• Third parties, vendors, and service providers supporting due diligence, monitoring, remediation, incidents, and other TPRM lifecycle activities. What You'll Bring • Strong analytical, organizational, problem-solving, and time-management skills. • Strong attention to detail and ability to manage multiple assignments, priorities, and deadlines. • Effective written and verbal communication skills, including documentation, follow-up, and reporting. • Ability to work collaboratively with business partners, third parties, and Stifel Associates at various levels of the organization. • Ability to follow established policies and procedures, exercise sound judgment, and appropriately escalate questions or concerns. • Ability to handle confidential and sensitive information with discretion. • Interest in developing knowledge of third-party risk management, operational risk, compliance, audit, procurement, and financial services. Education & Experience • Minimum Required: Bachelor's degree in Finance, Accounting, Business Administration, Risk Management, Economics, Information Systems, or a related discipline; equivalent education and relevant experience may be considered.• Minimum Required: No experience required, internship or 1+ year of experience preferred. • Preferred: Experience or exposure to third-party risk, vendor management, operational risk, compliance, audit, procurement, information security, business continuity, or financial services. Licenses & Credentials • Progress toward a relevant certification, such as the Certified Third Party Risk Professional (CTPRP), is a plus. Systems & Technology • Proficiency with Microsoft Excel, Word, PowerPoint, Outlook, and Teams. • Ability to learn and effectively use third-party risk management, workflow, reporting, and document-management systems. • Experience with OneTrust or a similar governance, risk, and compliance platform is a plus.

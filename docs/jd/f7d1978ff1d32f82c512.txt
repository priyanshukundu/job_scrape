SMBC Group is a top-tier global financial group. Headquartered in Tokyo and with a 400-year history, SMBC Group offers a diverse range of financial services, including banking, leasing, securities, credit cards, and consumer finance. The Group has more than 130 offices and 80,000 employees worldwide in nearly 40 countries. Sumitomo Mitsui Financial Group, Inc. (SMFG) is the holding company of SMBC Group, which is one of the three largest banking groups in Japan. SMFG’s shares trade on the Tokyo, Nagoya, and New York (NYSE: SMFG) stock exchanges. In the Americas, SMBC Group has a presence in the US, Canada, Mexico, Brazil, Chile, Colombia, and Peru. Backed by the capital strength of SMBC Group and the value of its relationships in Asia, the Group offers a range of commercial and investment banking services to its corporate, institutional, and municipal clients. It connects a diverse client base to local markets and the organization’s extensive global network. The Group’s operating companies in the Americas include Sumitomo Mitsui Banking Corp. (SMBC), SMBC Nikko Securities America, Inc., SMBC Capital Markets, Inc., JRI America, Inc., SMBC Leasing and Finance, Inc., Banco Sumitomo Mitsui Brasileiro S.A., and Sumitomo Mitsui Finance and Leasing Co., Ltd. Role Description SMBC is seeking a highly analytical, detail-oriented and control-focused professional for an Associate position within the Controllers - Regulatory Reporting Analysis Group. The Associate will be responsible for the preparation, review and timely submission of Bureau of Economic Analysis (BEA) reporting and regulatory Structure reporting, including the FR Y-10, FR Y-6 and FR Y-7. The position will apply sound judgment to reporting matters, coordinate across business and functional stakeholders, and support a strong control and governance environment for the assigned reporting portfolio. Responsibilities • Own the end-to-end preparation, review and timely submission of assigned BEA reports and Structure reports, including the FR Y-10, FR Y-6 and FR Y-7, in accordance with applicable instructions, internal standards and filing deadlines. • Manage the full reporting production cycle, including data sourcing, report population, validation, analytical review, edit checks, certification support, submission readiness and post-filing documentation. • Perform and evidence detailed review of filings, reconciliations, adjustments, supporting schedules and workpapers, ensuring a complete and auditable trail from source data through final reported amounts. • Challenge the completeness, accuracy and reasonableness of reported information, investigate unusual movements or changes, and develop clear, supportable explanations for management and regulatory review. • Maintain accountability for the design, execution and effectiveness of key controls within the assigned reporting scope, including remediation of control gaps, documentation deficiencies and repeat exceptions. • Interpret new and revised BEA and Structure reporting requirements, assess impacts to data, systems, processes and controls, and implement required changes in a controlled and timely manner. • Monitor changes to legal entities, organizational structure, ownership, investments and other reportable attributes, and coordinate with relevant stakeholders to ensure reporting records remain complete and current. • Partner with Legal, Tax, Finance, Treasury, Corporate Secretary, Data Governance, Operations and Technology stakeholders to obtain, validate and maintain information required for BEA and Structure reporting. • Provide clear and timely updates to Regulatory Reporting leadership regarding filing status, key changes, emerging risks, open issues, control concerns and remediation progress. • Coordinate responses to regulatory inquiries, internal audit, external audit and independent review requests, ensuring responses and supporting evidence are accurate, complete and delivered timely. • Lead process improvement, standardization and automation initiatives, including business requirements, user acceptance testing, implementation validation and post-production monitoring. • Maintain and enhance desktop procedures, process flows, data lineage, report inventories, control documentation, reporting calendars and other governance materials. • Serve as a subject-matter resource for assigned BEA and Structure reports, escalate complex matters appropriately, and represent the reporting function in relevant stakeholder discussions. Qualifications and Skills • Bachelor's degree in Accounting, Finance, Economics, Business Administration or a related field; an advanced degree or professional certification is a plus. • Three to five years of relevant experience in regulatory reporting, financial reporting, legal-entity reporting, accounting or a related control function. • Experience with BEA reporting and regulatory Structure reporting, including the FR Y-10, FR Y-6 or FR Y-7, as well as legal-entity data or organizational ownership reporting, is highly preferred. • Knowledge of U.S. GAAP, financial statements, legal-entity structures and regulatory reporting concepts. • Demonstrated ability to manage recurring deliverables, exercise sound judgment and support the resolution of data, control or reporting issues. • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with experience reviewing reconciliations, variance analysis, controls and reporting workpapers. • Strong stakeholder-management and communication skills, including the ability to explain reporting matters clearly to management, auditors and cross-functional partners. • Advanced proficiency in Microsoft Excel and familiarity with data analysis • Ability to prioritize multiple deadlines, work independently, escalate risks promptly and maintain a strong control-focused production environment.

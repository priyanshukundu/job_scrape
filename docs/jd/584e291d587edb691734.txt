InsuraMatch is an independent agency company, owned by Travelers Insurance, that uses an innovative online platform to help consumers compare offerings from more than 40 carriers across the United States. With a focus on personal insurance, InsuraMatch offers coverage for auto, home, boat, motorcycle, renters, umbrella, and flood, among others. InsuraMatch operates independently and manages all carrier partnerships. InsuraMatch is seeking a versatile Senior Associate Software Engineer to support a variety of applications, systems, and platforms that provide acquisition targeting, retention modeling projects, predictive analytics, call routing, data architecture, and in-depth data analyses. In this role, you will have a direct impact on the growth of this business and work closely with the team and leadership to drive profit and customer growth. What Will You Do? Under general supervision, this role will actively participate as a member of the InsuraMatch team in collaborating, conceiving and developing scalable, performant, secure solutions. Perform basic analysis, development, testing, debugging, configuration, incident investigation, documentation, health monitoring/alerting, and implementing based on and related to user or system design specifications. Support and contribute to the improvement of the development process Provide tier one support for cloud and physical networks, servers, operating systems, and processes Worth within the guidelines set by the team to monitor, manage, and administer Serverless, Linux, and Window computer platforms Support critical aspects of our technology operations in areas including Production, deployment process automation, monitoring, and security Ensure alignment between security architecture frameworks, standards, and overall IT cloud strategy Create and maintain build scripts, templates and other artifacts for the Infrastructure stack Support the migration of applications to the cloud infrastructure and assist in the analysis, tuning, and re-architecture when necessary to ensure adherence to business requirements Seek opportunities to expand technical knowledge and capabilities.

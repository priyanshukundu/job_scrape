Wells Fargo is seeking a Lead Software Engineer in Technology as part of Cybersecurity Governance. Learn more about the career areas and lines of business at wellsfargojobs.com. Wells Fargo is seeking a highly skilled, hands-on Lead Software Engineer to design and build modern, scalable applications supporting strategic Cybersecurity capabilities. This role will lead the development of a new full-stack platform focused on modernizing legacy application capabilities, establishing reusable integration and workflow services, and enabling the secure adoption of Artificial Intelligence and Generative AI use cases. The Lead Software Engineer will provide technical leadership across application architecture, microservices, API development, data integration, user experience, DevOps, and AI integration. The individual will partner with engineering, architecture, cybersecurity, and product teams to move new capabilities from prototype through secure, production-ready implementation. In this role, you will Lead complex technology initiatives with broad impact and act as a key technical contributor across the design, development, and implementation lifecycle. Lead the design, and hands-on development of modern, enterprise-grade full-stack applications supporting Cybersecurity capabilities. Design and implement scalable Java/Spring Boot microservices, REST APIs, Python services, and modern user interfaces. Define reusable API and integration patterns that enable applications, data services, and enterprise platforms to securely exchange information and capabilities. Lead modernization of legacy application capabilities by translating complex workflows and business processes into configurable, reusable platform services. Design and develop an AI integration layer that enables enterprise applications and workflows to securely consume reusable AI and Generative AI capabilities. Integrate AI and Generative AI capabilities into production applications while addressing model limitations, security, resiliency, privacy, and operational considerations. Translate AI prototypes and experiments into scalable, secure, supportable production capabilities. Develop and integrate data-processing capabilities, including APIs, data services, ETL processes, and relational or non-relational data stores. Establish technical standards and engineering practices for application design, code quality, automated testing, security, DevOps, observability, resiliency, and production readiness. Lead technical and code reviews, troubleshoot complex engineering challenges, and provide technical direction and mentorship to other engineers. Partner with product owners, architects, cybersecurity, AI governance, and platform engineering teams to translate emerging requirements into long-term technical solutions. Demonstrate proficiency in using AI‑assisted development and analysis tools (e.g., GitHub Copilot and approved code‑centric agents) Leverage AI to accelerate system design, coding, testing, analysis, and troubleshooting Apply strong technical judgment when validating and integrating AI‑assisted outputs into solutions Understand and account for model limitations, security risks, and operational considerations Apply AI responsibly in development and production environments Ensure AI usage aligns with security, compliance, privacy, and ethical standards Required Qualifications 5+ years of Software Engineering experience, or equivalent demonstrated through one or a combination of the following: work experience, training, military experience, education. 5+ years of experience designing, developing, and delivering enterprise full-stack applications using microservices and API-based architectures. Desired Qualifications Strong experience designing and building enterprise applications across the full technology stack. Hands-on development experience with Java, Spring Boot, microservices, REST APIs, and Python. Experience developing modern web-based user interfaces using React, Angular, or comparable JavaScript/TypeScript frameworks. Experience implementing Generative AI or AI-enabled capabilities within enterprise applications, including moving AI use cases from prototype into production. Experience integrating applications with large language models, AI services, or reusable AI platforms. Experience designing reusable application, API, and integration architectures supporting multiple use cases or consuming applications. Experience with GraphQL and API-first application design. Experience developing ETL or data-integration solutions leveraging relational and non-relational databases. Experience with Docker, Kubernetes, OpenShift, CI/CD, and automated DevOps practices. Experience designing highly scalable, resilient, secure, and observable distributed applications. Understanding of secure AI development considerations including model limitations, data security, access controls, privacy, logging, and responsible AI use. Experience modernizing legacy applications or migrating legacy business processes onto modern application platforms. Demonstrated ability to provide technical direction, conduct design and code reviews, mentor engineers, and resolve highly complex production issues.

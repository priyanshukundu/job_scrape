What you’ll do As the Screening & Transaction Monitoring Associate, you will review and escalate potential sanctions matches and politically exposed persons (PEPs) hits across our customers, payments, and vendors. You will review and assess screening data to make sound match decisions and assess the sanctions risk each alert poses. Clear communication is essential for documenting match dispositions, writing escalation memos, and discussing potential sanctions exposure with senior management. Experience working with external financial partners, auditors, and regulators is valuable. Adaptability is crucial, as sanctions programs, designations, and geopolitical risk can change overnight, as is keeping a positive, collaborative mindset while managing a high volume of alerts. Where you’ll work This role will be based in our Seattle office. We are a hybrid environment that combines the energy and connections of being in the office with the benefits and flexibility of working from home. We currently require a minimum of three coordinated days in the office per week, Monday, Wednesday and Thursday. As a perk, we also have up to four weeks per year of fully remote work!

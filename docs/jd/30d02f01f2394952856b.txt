COMPANY OVERVIEW KKR is a leading global investment firm that offers alternative asset management as well as capital markets and insurance solutions. KKR aims to generate attractive investment returns by following a patient and disciplined investment approach, employing world-class people, and supporting growth in its portfolio companies and communities. KKR sponsors investment funds that invest in private equity, credit and real assets and has strategic partners that manage hedge funds. KKR’s insurance subsidiaries offer retirement, life and reinsurance products under the management of Global Atlantic Financial Group. References to KKR’s investments may include the activities of its sponsored funds and insurance subsidiaries. TEAM OVERVIEW The GMAA team is seeking an Analyst / Associate to join the team on a full-time basis in the New York office. The group reports to Henry McVey (KKR Balance Sheet CIO) and is responsible for: Developing investment-oriented portfolio recommendations for KKR’s Private Markets funds ($100bn+ AUM), supported by quantitative analysis; and Providing asset allocation recommendations for various KKR Balance Sheet and client portfolios. POSITION SUMMARY The new hire will focus primarily on supporting Portfolio Construction initiatives across KKR’s Private Markets portfolios. Key responsibilities may include: Support the development of Portfolio Construction recommendations for Private Equity and Real Assets businesses across the Americas, Europe, and Asia. Conduct pro forma analysis to assess the impact of pipeline investments on existing portfolios for Investment Committee discussions. Manage and enhance Portfolio Construction models across KKR’s various Private Equity and Real Assets funds. Perform ad hoc and recurring quantitative analyses requested by Portfolio Managers. Build and maintain models to identify and assess factor exposures across various KKR portfolios and strategies. Review, document, and enhance the team’s analytics and reporting code base in Python / R. Work closely with dedicated engineering and data teams to automate and enhance proprietary Portfolio Construction models. RELEVANT SKILLS AND EXPERIENCE Strong structured problem-solving skills and willingness to work through complex quantitative and numerical problems. The role will involve working with and improving proprietary models, so intellectual curiosity and the ability to think creatively are important. Strong quantitative research skills, ideally developed through coursework in quantitative finance, portfolio theory, statistics, econometrics, or related fields. Excellent communication skills. The role will involve working closely with senior management at KKR and externally with KKR’s LPs, making the ability to synthesize complex information and communicate key takeaways clearly particularly important. Strong programming skills in a structured language such as Python, with the ability to develop scripts and work with SQL. An understanding of fundamental analysis to complement quantitative skills, such as experience through internships or prior roles in Investment Banking or Capital Markets, is helpful but not required. Strong work ethic and willingness to take ownership of responsibilities.

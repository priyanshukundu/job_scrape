Ownership of the product line includes financial management, sales development, and product lifecycle execution. Development of product reference materials for clients, product sales materials, marketing materials, and pitchbooks. Evaluate the competitive environment for the assigned product set, including competitive offerings, pricing, marketing vs. Simmons bank, and leverage this information to ensure our products maintain competitive in the marketplace Reporting of the product portfolio, client metrics on utilization, financial impacts, and opportunities in the product set Owning risk management for the assigned product line, inclusive of 1st line of defense risk management, identifying KRI’s / KPI’s, and implementing processes to mitigate risk Manage vendor relationships, as applicable, for the assigned product line Ensures all departmental documents and activities are performed in compliance with applicable laws, regulations, policies and procedures as applicable to this position, including completion of required compliance training.

Job Description Summary Under limited direction, uses extensive knowledge and skills obtained through education and experience to support all components of the Enterprise Risk Management (ERM) program, with primary responsibility for risk governance and enterprise risk reporting activities. This role partners across the organization to enhance and maintain an effective risk governance framework, ensuring committees operate consistently, effectively, and in alignment with enterprise-wide standards. The position leads governance and reporting activities supporting the Enterprise Risk Management Committee (ERMC) and Board Risk Committees, including committee structure development, governance process enhancement, agenda planning, meeting coordination, reporting material preparation, and continuous improvement efforts. The role serves as a key contributor to executive and Board-level risk reporting by gathering, reviewing, challenging, quality-assuring, and synthesizing information from multiple stakeholders into cohesive and executive-ready materials. The successful candidate will possess exceptional communication and presentation development skills, with the ability to transform complex risk and business concepts into clear, concise, and visually compelling presentations utilizing high-quality graphics, data visualization, and executive storytelling techniques. Provides comprehensive solutions to escalated complex problems. Results are evaluated at agreed upon milestones for effectiveness in achieving specified results. Extensive contact with executive management and is required to lead others in identifying, researching, analyzing and resolving complex issues.

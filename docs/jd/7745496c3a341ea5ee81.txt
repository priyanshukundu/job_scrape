Position Summary As a Senior Specialist in the Cross Study Operations and Specimen Management (CSO & SM) Product Line within Global Development Operations Business Insights & Technology (GDO BI&T), you will reimagine how AI enabled technology solutions will streamline study design and orchestration. This role sits at the front end of the clinical trial lifecycle, where protocol concepts become executable plans. You’ll embed with the teams doing that work, uncover the friction, and use AI tools to rapidly prototype working solutions. This is a hands-on role. You’ll partner with Product Managers to take the strongest prototypes from concept through production and lifecycle support, working alongside clinical scientists, global development operations, clinical design and delivery center, and R&D BI&T teams. We’re looking for someone eager to make AI their default way of working — not as a novelty, but as the way they investigate a problem, test an idea, and build the solution. You’ll thrive here if you: Build with AI habitually — prototyping, analyzing data, generating code, and stress-testing your own assumptions Spot problems worth solving before anyone files a ticket, and can tell the difference between a real pain point and a stated preference Are drawn to complex, multi-variable problems — study design and orchestration rarely have one right answer, and the interesting work is in the tradeoffs Are committed to a career in technology and energized by applying it to improve patient health Collaborate easily across teams, bring bold ideas, and are willing to challenge the status quo (including your own first draft) Communicate clearly to both engineers and clinical stakeholders Key Responsibilities Serve as the technical business analyst on study design and orchestration initiatives within the CSO & SM Product Line, owning the path from problem identification through delivery Embed with study design, clinical operations, and trial planning stakeholders to surface pain points — then make the case for which are worth building Partner with Product Managers and business stakeholders to solve these problems with an AI-first approach, evaluating where AI genuinely outperforms conventional solutions and where it doesn’t Rapidly prototype solutions using AI tools (e.g., Claude, Claude Code, Copilot) to deliver working artifacts in front of users early — sharpening requirements through demonstration rather than debate Design, test, and refine prompts, agents, and automations that become durable parts of the product, including the guardrails and human checkpoints that make them trustworthy in a regulated setting Create IT documentation artifacts in support of agile technology implementations, including but not limited to

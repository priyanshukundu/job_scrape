Description Solaria Labs is Liberty Mutual’s innovation team. We partner with business leaders across the enterprise to incubate new products, services and experiences. We prove ideas out quickly and transition successful solutions into the business. The Applied Science team leads Solaria’s AI experimentation and delivery. We apply frontier AI to real business problems, including agentic workflows, enterprise knowledge systems, GenAI evaluation, and predictive modeling. What sets this role apart: • Breadth : You’ll work across commercial and retail insurance and enterprise functions such as Finance, Talent, and Investments. • Visibility : You’ll work at the intersection of senior business and technology leaders. • Research : Our world-class partnership with MIT keeps our work at the forefront AI. • Lasting impact : You’ll build reusable capabilities, such as evaluation frameworks, tools and knowledge layers, that extend beyond any single project.

Key responsibilities Do you have solid knowledge of financial markets and an interest in fixed income research? Are you able to turn detailed credit analysis into clear investment recommendations? We’re looking for a Fixed Income Research Analyst to: • conduct detailed fundamental credit analysis of approximately 50–60 US financial issuers, primarily investment grade • monitor and research relevant economic, industry and company data, including company filings, transcripts and quarterly results • make investment recommendations and provide credit updates to global portfolio management teams, as well as to external clients when required • monitor existing portfolio holdings and proactively generate trade ideas for portfolio implementation • work with analysts and portfolio managers across the globe to collaborate on investment ideas, including relative value opportunities across the capital structure • use artificial intelligence tools to enhance and help evolve the research process • build relationships across the fixed income platform and UBS Asset Management, as well as with sell-side analysts and company management The team You’ll be working in the Fixed Income Research Team in New York or Chicago with a seasoned group of analysts. Our team is responsible for identifying well researched, original and actionable investment ideas. As a credit analyst, you will play a vital role in further enhancing our fixed income research capabilities. Your skills and experience • solid knowledge of and interest in financial markets • bachelor’s degree, Chartered Financial Analyst qualification or progress towards it, is a plus • ideally experience at an asset management firm or investment bank, preferably within fixed income, or equivalent experience • excellent analytical, verbal and written communication skills • able to take initiative, learn quickly and work independently • willingness to use artificial intelligence tools to support research activities • able to manage multiple tasks, prioritize effectively and meet deadlines • knowledge of and programming experience with statistical software applications, such as R or Python, is a plus About us UBS is a leading and truly global wealth manager and the leading universal bank in Switzerland. We also provide diversified asset management solutions and focused investment banking capabilities. Headquartered in Zurich, Switzerland, UBS is present in more than 50 markets around the globe.

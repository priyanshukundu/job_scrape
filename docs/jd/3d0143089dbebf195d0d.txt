Ankura is a team of excellence founded on innovation and growth. Operations and Workplace Services Overview: The Operations & Workplace Services (OWS) Team provides operations, business services and workplace services to senior professionals and employees supporting the daily management of the business and local office. Working within a global team, OWS team creates a sense of community while driving operational excellence, providing office support, administering processes and systems, supporting projects, and assisting in the overall efforts to ensure efficient service delivery for the firm and its clients. Status: Exempt Location: Fairfield, Connecticut (4 days in the office, 1 day remote). Occasional travel to other Americas offices. Role Overview: This Operations Associate serves in two capacities: Regional administrative support (about 75% of time): Provides operations administrative support for all offices across North and South America, covering people and workplace administration, vendors and invoices, real estate and space records, compliance, and process documentation. Fairfield site leadership (about 25% of time): Serves as the Operations and Workplace Services (OWS) site point of contact (POC) for the Fairfield office. The role focuses on operations, facilities and workplace administration, with an emphasis on supporting offices, processes and vendors across the Americas. Responsibilities: Regional administrative support, all North and South America sites (75%) Vendors, invoices and finance support Create purchase orders (POs) and process invoices for all sites and subtenants, and submit new vendor requisitions. Onboard new vendors, including set-up in accounts payable, and keep vendor certificates of insurance (COIs) current. Arrange security badging and building access for vendors. Process supplier invoices and subtenant invoices. Chase unpaid invoices (accounts payable and accounts receivable). Maintain budget trackers. Assist in developing capital (CAPEX) and operating (OPEX) budgets. Assist accounts payable with updating 1,000+ supplier contacts. Assist Procurement with corporate mobility billing reports. Assist expense auditor with confidential expense-report tasks. Manage contracts and invoices for print and mail, shipping, document shredding, off-site records storage and similar service vendors. Manage FedEx and UPS accounts. Support relationships with office supplies vendors. People and workplace administration Run time-entry compliance reports and send missing-time reminders. Run onboarding and offboarding processes and keep the documentation current. Onboard new hires, including collecting W-9 forms and complete I-9 employment eligibility verifications. Assist with employee orientation and with laptop, cell phone and other equipment set-up for new hires. Offboard leavers and prepare People Office documentation. Send and track new hire surveys. Order business cards for the firm, including new hire, title change and reorder requests. Process office visit and badge access requests. Manage requests in OWS ticket portal. Maintain the team paid time off (PTO) calendar. Prepare monthly and quarterly presentations. Assist with setting up recurring meetings, such as monthly team meetings. Assist with preparing presentations and standardized forms, such as capital requests. Real estate, space and compliance Maintain corporate real estate (CRE) lease and transaction records and rent schedules. Support CRE construction projects, including leading project meetings, tracking meeting minutes, and managing action items through completion. Support move, add and change (MAC) requests and neighborhood space planning. Support workplace health and safety (WHS) compliance and programs, including first aid kits and automated external defibrillators (AEDs) Records and process Maintain and update site location information, including location guides, for all offices. Maintain and update master documents, contracts and vendor lists. Maintain insurance certificates and records. Prepare DocuSign electronic signature requests for agreements, contracts and other documents, and track them through completion. Organize operations file documentation. Maintain the operations intranet site and shared drives, keeping content current, organized and easy to find. Manage SharePoint files for all sites, including preventative maintenance reports, construction drawings, permits, inspections and close-out documentation. Maintain agreements and contracts. Write and update standard operating procedures (SOPs) and playbooks. Fairfield site point of contact (25%) Serve as the primary contact and escalation point for Fairfield employees, business leaders and office leadership. Work with the local property management team for the Fairfield site, including building access, maintenance requests, building services and notices. Oversee office readiness, walkthroughs, quality checks and on-site vendors. Uphold office standards. Manage access control for the Fairfield office, including badges, keys and visitor access. Order snacks and beverages and stock the pantry and kitchen and order and stock office supplies. Act as the health and safety, security and emergency point of contact, including first aid kits and AEDs. Host local in-office gatherings and staff events, including planning, setup, catering and vendor coordination. Manage the mailroom, shipping, supplies, and meeting and event logistics.

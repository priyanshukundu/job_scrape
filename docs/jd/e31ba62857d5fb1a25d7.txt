Bring your expertise to JPMorgan Chase. As part of Risk Management and Compliance, you are at the center of keeping JPMorgan Chase strong and resilient. You help the firm grow its business in a responsible way by anticipating new and emerging risks, and using your expert judgment to solve real-world challenges that impact our company, customers and communities. Our culture in Risk Management and Compliance is all about thinking outside the box, challenging the status quo and striving to be best-in-class. As a Compliance Risk Management Lead Vice President with Compliance, Conduct and Operational Risk (CCOR) organization, you will provide regulatory advice and compliance oversight for an assigned portfolio of registered funds, investment products, or related compliance responsibilities. You will independently manage a defined area of coverage, partner with business, Legal, Operations, Governance, and other control functions, and contribute to the continued development of a scalable and risk-based Funds Compliance program. Additionally, you will combine regulatory and product knowledge with sound judgment, disciplined execution, and the ability to manage matters through resolution. This is a substantive compliance role requiring the ability to provide practical advice, review and challenge information, identify themes and emerging risks, and communicate effectively with senior and cross-functional stakeholders. Job Responsibilities Provide regulatory and compliance advice for assigned funds, products, asset classes, or distribution and sub-advisory activities. Interpret applicable requirements under the Investment Company Act, Investment Advisers Act, SEC rules and exemptive relief, fund policies and disclosures, and, where relevant, OCC fiduciary standards. Support new products, fund launches and changes, investment-guideline matters, disclosure questions, regulatory implementation, and business initiatives. Own assigned monitoring, advisory, governance, or program workstreams from planning through documented completion. Review and challenge information from portfolio management, operations, fund administrators, guideline monitoring, surveillance, Legal, and other control or service providers. Identify issues early, establish the relevant facts, assess regulatory and investor impact, distinguish isolated exceptions from systemic concerns, and escalate appropriately. Manage issues through resolution, maintaining visibility into ownership, remediation, evidence, and closure. Connect product advice, monitoring results, findings, and regulatory developments to risk assessment, annual planning, governance, and reporting. Prepare or contribute to clear, decision-useful materials for the Funds CCO, fund Boards, committees, and senior management. Provide credible challenge while maintaining constructive and effective relationships with business and control partners; maintain procedures, calendars, trackers, and evidence necessary to support transparent, repeatable, and resilient execution. Guide and review the work of supporting team members and contribute to knowledge sharing and capability development. Required qualifications, capabilities and skills Relevant experience in compliance, legal, risk, audit, fund operations, or a related regulatory discipline. Demonstrated ability to interpret regulatory, policy, or contractual requirements and apply them to actual business, product, investment, or operational activity. Experience independently owning complex deliverables or workstreams through resolution. Strong written and verbal communication, including the ability to articulate a clear compliance position and recommendation. Sound judgment, effective escalation, and the ability to operate in situations involving incomplete information or competing considerations. Demonstrated ability to work across Compliance, Legal, business, operations, governance, and other control functions. Strong organizational and documentation discipline. Preferred qualifications, capabilities and skills Experience with registered investment companies, collective investment trusts, investment advisers, or related asset-management products and services. Experience with fund governance, Board reporting, compliance monitoring, disclosure, investment restrictions, service-provider oversight, regulatory implementation, or examination support. Experience with open-end funds and ETFs; interval, tender-offer, or other registered closed-end funds; private-market strategies; CITs; or sub-advisory relationships. Project, matrix, coaching, or informal leadership experience. Experience improving compliance processes, controls, workflows, evidence, or technology enablement.

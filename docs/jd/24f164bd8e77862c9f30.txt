To make electronics, you need chips, wafers, transistors, reticles, and... To make these, you must see, test and manufacture them at scale—faster and better than ever before. That's where KLA comes in. Whether you're early in your career or an experienced professional, you'll solve complex challenges, work alongside brilliant minds and help shape the future of technology. Group/Division The Semiconductor Products and Customers (Semi PC) business unit designs, builds and sells KLA’s product portfolio to help chip manufacturers meet high-quality standards for technologies like AI, data centers, automotive and electronic devices. Our inspection, metrology, and analytics systems detect and monitor defects across logic and memory chips, specialty semiconductors, wafers, reticles, advanced packaging, process equipment and materials. We also develop technologies for specialty processes, IC substrates, and PCB manufacturing, including etch and deposition, component inspection, and imaging and analytics. The e-Beam division plays a critical role in KLA’s inspection ecosystem, with tools that deliver high-resolution, timely insights into defects and critical locations on the wafer. Used alongside KLA’s patterned and bare wafer inspectors, e-Beam systems help our customers quickly identify and understand yield-impacting issues—enabling faster, more effective actions to correct the manufacturing process. What You'll Do In this role, you will play a key part in advancing business priorities by delivering high-impact work across your area of expertise. KLA is seeking an experienced Software Engineer to help build the next generation of AI-driven analytics and automation solutions for advanced semiconductor manufacturing systems. In this role, you will develop intelligent software platforms that combine large-scale equipment data, hardware telemetry, machine diagnostics, and Agentic AI technologies to improve equipment performance, reliability, and productivity. Working at the intersection of software, AI, big data, and complex hardware systems, you will build scalable applications that enable semiconductor engineers to diagnose issues, predict failures, and interact with sophisticated electron-beam inspection and metrology tools using natural language. This is a hands-on engineering role with global impact. The solutions you develop will be deployed within semiconductor manufacturing facilities worldwide, supporting some of the most advanced hardware systems used in semiconductor production. Responsibilities Design and develop production-quality software solutions supporting AI-driven equipment diagnostics, monitoring, and predictive maintenance. Build and enhance Agentic AI frameworks that integrate large language models (LLMs) with engineering and equipment data systems. Develop software that interfaces with complex hardware platforms, equipment telemetry streams, machine control systems, and manufacturing data sources. Integrate AI technologies with scalable data platforms, including streaming architectures, NoSQL databases, vector databases, and knowledge management systems. Build secure APIs, services, and orchestration layers that support intelligent workflows and automated decision-making. Develop high-performance data pipelines that transform equipment and operational data into actionable insights. Create intuitive user experiences and modern web applications that simplify troubleshooting and equipment diagnostics. Collaborate across software, systems, hardware, data science, and product engineering teams to deliver end-to-end solutions. Deploy and support solutions operating in highly secure, customer-controlled semiconductor manufacturing environments. Drive software design, code quality, testing, and continuous improvement initiatives across the platform.

Coinbase Credit Risk is hiring a Credit Risk Manager to support our institutional financing and lending business. Reporting to the Director, Credit Risk — Institutional Financing & Funds, you will underwrite fund counterparties and help design and operate the risk frameworks behind cross margin, portfolio margin, exchange lending, and extended settlement products. Our product surface is expanding fast and you will have a direct hand in how it is risk-managed. What you’ll do: Underwrite institutional counterparties, with a focus on hedge funds and asset managers; recommend internal credit ratings and risk-based limits, and approve analyst-prepared credit reviews. Build and maintain margin, collateral, and liquidation parameters across cross margin, portfolio margin, exchange lending, trade finance, and OTC extended settlement products. Partner with Product and Engineering during new-product design to surface structural risks early and translate framework requirements into system specifications. Deliver requirements and test cases to Engineering to productionize counterparty data, ratings, limits, exposure monitoring, and automated controls on the credit risk platform. Review risk-relevant terms in master loan agreements, security interest and pledge structures, derivatives documentation, and prime brokerage agreements with Legal, escalating material deviations. Run stress testing and potential exposure analysis; monitor single-name, sector, and collateral concentrations and surface emerging risks to the Director and senior leadership. Lead deep dives on internal and external credit events and present findings and recommendations to senior management. Required Skills and Experience: 8+ years in credit risk, counterparty risk, or securities financing at a global financial institution, with hands-on experience in secured lending, prime brokerage, and/or derivatives. Demonstrated experience underwriting funds and corporate counterparties — financial analysis, rating recommendations, limit setting, and ongoing surveillance. Working knowledge of collateral mechanics, LTV structuring, margin methodology, potential future exposure, and wrong-way risk, applied to live products. Experience contributing to risk frameworks for new products and working with Engineering or quant teams to implement risk controls. Familiarity with industry documentation (MLAs, PBAs, ISDA/CSA) and comfort reviewing risk terms alongside Legal. Strong written and verbal communication; able to build well-reasoned recommendations and influence stakeholders across Product, Legal, and Finance. Strong interest in crypto and digital asset markets. Utilizes generative AI responsibly, maintaining human oversight to deliver business-ready outputs and drive measurable improvements in workflow efficiency, cost, and quality. Demonstrates the ability to responsibly use generative AI tools and copilots (e.g., LibreChat, Gemini, Glean) in daily workflows, continuously learn as tools evolve, and apply human‑in‑the‑loop practices to deliver business‑ready outputs and drive measurable improvements in efficiency, cost, and quality. #LI-Remote Position ID: P79006 Pay Transparency Notice: Base salary varies by location (see range below).

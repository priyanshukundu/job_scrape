Job Summary: The Entertainment Finance Group (EF) structures and underwrites loan facilities for companies across the entertainment industry. As an entry-level corporate finance professional, the Credit Analyst will join the Portfolio Management Team supporting the EF lending business. Key responsibilities include performing financial and credit analysis, supporting underwriting and ongoing portfolio management, assessing credit risk, and assigning appropriate internal and regulatory ratings in accordance with established policies and guidelines. The role requires the ability to work both independently and collaboratively in a team environment, take ownership of analytical assignments and deliverables, proactively identify emerging risks, and escalate material credit concerns as appropriate in support sound risk management and risk-adjusted returns. Responsibilities: Support Senior Portfolio Managers in ongoing management and monitoring of the Entertainment Finance portfolio. Perform due diligence and periodic evaluations of loan exposures, proactively identifying key risks, trends, and potential rating implications. Prepare detailed credit analyses and credit applications in accordance with policy, including assessments of corporate, regulatory and industry risk, historical and projected operating performance, and capital structures. Develop projections using management forecasts and prepare base and downside cases, as appropriate, to assess the strength and sustainability of the primary source of repayment. Coordinate and communicate account information across various constituencies throughout the organization. Develop expertise in the sectors in which EF clients operate and monitor relevant industry, market and company developments using internal and external sources. Monitor the timely receipt of client reporting and covenant-compliance materials, and support facility-closing due diligence, including documentation review. Attend meetings with Relationship Managers, clients and other bank stakeholders, as appropriate. Participate in special projects and other assignments, as needed. Advanced Credit / Underwriting Solutions : Work in a collaborative deal-team environment with Relationship Management and Product Partners, supporting Senior Portfolio Managers in developing and analyzing financing structures and strategies for M&A and corporate recapitalization opportunities. Prescreen proposed transactions to identify material structural issues and credit risks while balancing institutional policy requirements with business development objectives. Support structured credit transactions, including financial solution opportunities, securitizations, specialized leasing transactions, and up-tier proposals. Maintain knowledge of the industries represented in the portfolio, including relevant technical considerations, regulatory developments and specialized credit products. Participate in client and bank meetings, as appropriate. Policy and Deal Advocacy : Assist Senior Portfolio Managers in shaping and influencing policy development through participation in committees, project teams and transaction discussions. Support Senior Portfolio Managers in communicating and facilitating transaction approvals with Credit Division, including final hold level strategies that align risk appetite, risk strategy and policy. Evaluate and recommend appropriate transaction ratings and exposure levels at both the individual transaction and group levels. Ensure compliance with applicable policy and regulatory requirements in coordination with second and third line of defense functions, legal counsel and external regulators. Skills & Experience: Minimum of one year of experience at a financial institution or accounting firm. Completion of a formal credit-training program is preferred - direct entertainment lending experience is a plus. Experience analyzing corporate credit and financing transactions and preparing credit applications, particularly for asset based and cash flow lending structures. Experience assessing both internal risk and regulatory ratings. Excellent written, verbal and interpersonal communication skills, with the ability to work independently, manage multiple priorities and perform effectively under pressure. Strong financial-modeling, accounting and analytical skills. Proficiency in Microsoft Word, Excel, and PowerPoint. Bachelor's degree in Business, Accounting, Finance, Economics or a related field; progress toward the CFA designation is a plus.

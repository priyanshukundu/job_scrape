Overview This is remote role that may be hired in NC, VA, MD, or DC. The SBA Analyst performs data analysis to identify factors that affect business profitability, growth, and efficiency. Assesses current practices, collects information and industry trends, and creates specifications to support business operations. Assists in determining improvements or solutions to address business issues. Prepares technical reports by examining and summarizing data. Performs quality control reviews of SBA loan files to ensure compliance with SBA SOP requirements, internal procedures, regulatory guidelines, and documentation standards. Identifies exceptions, operational risks, and process improvement opportunities to support portfolio quality and compliance objectives. *This role may be hired as a Business Analyst I/II depending on experience level of the candidate. Responsibilities & Qualifications Duties & Responsibilities: Data Analysis - Sources, compiles, and interprets data. Analyzes data for accuracy and efficiencies, effectively communicates analysis output. Operational Support - Provides support for business unit by responding to inquiries, identifying issues, and working to improve standard practices. Assists in implementing recommended modifications and enhancements. Identifying Objectives - Determines overall business needs through analysis and reporting. May identify strategies to achieve company and department goals, and assist in creating specifications, project plans, or other forms of documentation to communicate business initiatives to wider work group. Reporting - Produces reports based on data analysis, industry trends, and process capabilities. Conveys patterns, problems, and areas of improvement. Conducts quality control reviews of SBA loan files and supporting documentation to verify compliance with SBA SOPs, loan authorizations, internal policies, and regulatory requirements. Identifies, tracks, and reports documentation deficiencies, compliance exceptions, and operational risks. Analyzes trends and supports corrective action plans, process improvements, audit responses, and quality assurance initiatives. Position Specific Skills: Product Knowledge Operational Excellence Risk Mitigation Critical Thinking Qualifications: Minimum Required Education and Experience: Bachelor's Degree OR High School Diploma or GED/Equivalent and 4 years' experience in Analytics, or Operations, or Support Preferred Qualifications: Experience in SBA Lending, loan documentation review, and quality control reviews Knowledge of SBA SOP Compliance Advanced Excel Ideal candidate has a strong attention to detail, communication skills, and is a team player Additional Information If hired in DC, MD, or VA the hourly pay for this position is generally between $25.89 and $34.52 per hour.

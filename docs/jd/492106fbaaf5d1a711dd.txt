The team engages with business and support (Risk, Compliance, Legal) partners across Fidelity in a variety of ways, including issue identification and escalation, risk and compliance reporting, program monitoring, and supporting new programs to further protect Fidelity and our customers. To support the Issue Management Program, the Risk Analyst will analyze and interpret data sets to identify, assess, and monitor issues across the organization; support the end-to-end administration of the Issue Management Program, including issue intake, validation, tracking, remediation, and closure activities; and assist in the ongoing enhancement and automation of program processes and reporting to improve efficiency, transparency, and risk oversight. This role will partner with stakeholders across business units to ensure issues are documented, prioritized, and remediated in accordance with established governance standards while contributing to continuous program improvement and regulatory readiness. The role requires analytical and critical thinking skills, along with an ability to work effectively within a team, think conceptually, and interact with others using interpersonal skills.

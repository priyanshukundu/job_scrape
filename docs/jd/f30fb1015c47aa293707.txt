Job Description Simmons Bank invites you to collaborate in an agile team of peers developing and supporting on-premises & cloud-based analytics platform integrating data from a broad number of systems to enable next-gen analytical products. The Data Engineer I is responsible for designing, developing and delivering effective data solutions for different business units. This position requires advanced level knowledge of Microsoft O365 Power Platform and cloud native services for building data engineering pipelines and data products in alignment with architecture and best practices. They will collaborate with cross-functional teams to develop and manage scalable and reliable data engineering solutions. Essential Duties and Responsibilities Assist in building and maintaining ETL (extract, transform, load) pipelines. Write basic scripts to automate data collection and preprocessing tasks. Integrate data from various sources into data warehouses. Ensure data quality and consistency across different datasets. Perform data cleansing and validation tasks. Monitor data quality and report issues to senior team members. Work under the guidance of senior data engineers and data scientists. Assist in documenting processes and maintaining technical documentation. Performs other duties and responsibilities as assigned.

P-211 At Databricks, we are passionate about enabling data teams to solve the world's toughest problems — from making the next mode of transportation a reality to accelerating the development of medical breakthroughs. We do this by building and running the world's best data and AI infrastructure platform so our customers can use deep data insights to improve their business. Founded by engineers — and customer obsessed — we leap at every opportunity to tackle technical challenges, from designing next-gen UI/UX for interfacing with data to scaling our services and infrastructure across millions of virtual machines. And we're only getting started. The Threat and Abuse Defense team protects Databricks, its customers, and its employees from attackers and misuse of the product. Our mission is to deter, detect, and disrupt threats and abuse before they cause business-impacting incidents. Engineers on this team build and operate a real-time, highly-scalable decision platform that includes inline enforcement at critical chokepoints, shared infrastructure for feature stores and entity graphs, machine learning model scoring, and an agent framework for triaging and investigating threats. We measure our impact through quantifiable metrics and leverage frontier AI to ensure the trust and safety of the Databricks platform. The Impact You Will Have Set the technical direction and own the architecture of the real-time decision platform, driving improvements in latency, availability, and safe deployment of detection rules and enforcement actions. Design and build inline enforcement integrations with product and internal systems, partnering with teams across the organization to ensure safe adoption. Build shared data foundations including online and offline feature stores, entity graphs, and streaming pipelines that reduce detection latency and enable real-time threat response. Lead the technical response to security and abuse incidents, conducting root cause analysis and implementing systemic improvements to prevent recurrence. Drive cross-team technical initiatives from design through production, influencing roadmaps across Security, Platform, and Product organizations. Mentor engineers, review technical designs, and raise the engineering standard across the team and broader organization. What We Look For 8+ years of software engineering experience. Experience architecting and operating large-scale distributed systems with strong requirements for reliability, performance, and security. Experience building real-time decision systems, streaming data pipelines, or high-throughput services with strict latency requirements. Experience with security engineering, threat detection, abuse prevention, or related domains. Ability to lead complex technical projects involving multiple teams and system boundaries, from conception through production delivery. Strong technical judgment and communication skills, with the ability to influence technical direction and drive alignment across organizations. Experience with machine learning systems, feature engineering, or model deployment in production environments. B.S., M.S., or Ph.D. in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field, or equivalent practical experience.

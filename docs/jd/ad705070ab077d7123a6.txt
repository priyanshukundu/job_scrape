Job Summary: This job identifies issues and recommends best practices for internal control processes, leads projects to enhance internal control effectiveness, analyzes business trends, develops strategies for process improvements, and ensures compliance with laws and regulations. Job Description: Essential Responsibilities: Identify issues and recommend best practices for internal control processes. Lead functional projects and programs to enhance internal control effectiveness. Partner with teams to analyze business trends and their impact on internal controls. Develop and implement strategies for process improvements within the internal controls function. Ensure compliance with laws and regulations through effective internal control measures. Minimum Qualifications: 5+ years relevant experience and a Bachelor’s degree OR Any equivalent combination of education and experience. Additional Responsibilities & Preferred Qualifications : Job Description: We are seeking an experienced risk leader to lead and own Payments Risk Assessments. The ideal candidate brings expertise in global payment rails, risk assessment, control frameworks, and regulatory compliance, paired with a proven track record of leading high-performing teams and managing complex, cross-functional initiatives. Key Responsibilities: Lead gap assessments across global payment schemes (Card Schemes, NACHA, Real-Time Payments, and others) whenever scheme rules change or new schemes are onboarded, keeping our compliance posture strong as we expand into new capabilities such as self-processing and self-acquiring globally. Partner with Product Managers to identify net-new and evolving risks associated with product launches and changes, facilitating risk disposition decisions including mitigation or acceptance. Oversee escalation and resolution of payment-related risk and compliance issues, coordinating cross-functional communication to minimize exposure and maintain regulatory compliance. Define, monitor, and report on key risk and compliance metrics, providing regular updates to senior leadership on the health of the global payments risk environment compliance metrics. Stay current on global payment services directives and regulatory developments and proactively assess their impact on payment products and operations. Develop and maintain control mapping frameworks, ensuring controls are appropriately designed and operating effectively across payment products. Drive a culture of risk awareness, continuous improvement, and accountability across the team and with cross-functional stakeholders. Apply critical, data-driven thinking to assess risk criticality and exposure, justifying risk decisions with quantitative and qualitative analysis. Provide coaching, guidance, and professional development to team members on risk management best practices, issue management discipline, and compliance requirements. Qualifications: Minimum of 8 years of experience in risk management, compliance, or a related role within payments or financial services industry, with significant exposure to payment processing and/or acquiring. Knowledge of global payment rails and schemes, including card networks, ACH networks, instant payment rails, and emerging open banking infrastructure. Strong understanding of global payment services frameworks governing payments and relevant network compliance requirements. Demonstrated ability to perform risk assessments, control mapping, and gap analyses in a fast-paced product environment. Experience with issue management frameworks — including categorization, prioritization, escalation, and resolution tracking across complex payment ecosystems. Excellent analytical and problem-solving skills, with a track record of using data to drive risk decisions and quantify exposure. Strong communication and stakeholder management skills, with the ability to translate complex risk topics for both technical and executive audiences. Ability to operate effectively in ambiguous, rapidly evolving environments and manage multiple competing priorities. Passion for learning and using AI tools such as ChatGPT, Claude to find more efficient ways of operating.

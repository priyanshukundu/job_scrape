Job Summary: The Portfolio & Service Delivery Lead, Offensive Security (Vice President) owns the strategy, road mapping, service delivery and operations, and financial and workforce planning for MUFG's Offensive Security product portfolio supporting the Global Red Team Center of Excellence. Working alongside technical Offensive Security leadership, this role turns cybersecurity priorities into scalable, measurable services. It also supports governance and executive reporting, drives adoption, and partners with stakeholders across Head Office (HO), the Americas, EMEA, and APAC. Key Responsibilities: Product Portfolio Strategy & Delivery Develop the multi-year strategy and Mid-Term Business Plan (MTBP) for the Offensive Security product portfolio, and translate it into annual plans aligned with cybersecurity priorities, regulatory requirements, and the evolving threat landscape Own product road mapping across the portfolio, including capability priorities, maturity targets, and sequencing of new services Lead service management across red team, penetration testing, adversary simulation, attack surface management, bug bounty, and vulnerability disclosure Manage demand, prioritization, capacity, and dependencies, and deliver initiatives through go-live in Agile and waterfall environments Lead onboarding of MUFG entities to Offensive Security services, including onboarding waves, prerequisites, and onboarding and run-cost estimates Drive adoption of AI and automation to expand testing coverage and scale Operations Management Support the definition of service descriptions, scope, and service levels, and oversee day-to-day business-as-usual (BAU) operations across the product portfolio, ensuring services run consistently and meet agreed service levels Establish and maintain operational governance, including operating procedures, service standards, controls, and a regular operating cadence Transition new capabilities from project delivery into BAU, monitor operational performance, resolve issues, and drive continuous improvement in efficiency and service quality Financial & Workforce Planning Own budgeting, monthly forecasting, staffing, contracts, and business cases Governance & Partnership Establish KPIs, dashboards, and executive reporting across delivery, operations, financials, workforce, and risk, and prepare HO, senior leadership, and partner-bank materials positioning Offensive Security services Manage risks, issues, and dependencies, and lead steering committees and status reviews Support and coordinate

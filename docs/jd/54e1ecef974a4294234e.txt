Job Description Senior Technology Risk Analyst Corporate Functions Risk is seeking a Technology Risk Analyst to join our team. This role will focus on the identification, vetting, and analysis of technology risks and audit-related concerns across the Technology organization. This role serves as an independent challenge function, helping stakeholders assess potential issues, review supporting evidence, and develop clear risk statements, ownership assignments, and remediation strategies. Daily activities include potential issue triage, detailed review and challenge of potential issue documentation, facilitation of collaborative vetting discussions, executive reporting, and presentation of risk analysis to senior leadership. This opportunity provides exposure across all three lines of defense and requires strong partnerships with business lines, internal audit, and risk management teams. Success in this role will be predicated on the ability to evaluate complex risk scenarios, influence stakeholders, drive consistency, and deliver timely, well-supported results in a dynamic environment. Position Highlights Performing analysis, vetting, and validation of Technology, Information Security Services (ISS) and AI, Data and Innovation (AiDI) risks Facilitating collaborative discussions with internal audit, risk management, and business line stakeholders to evaluate and disposition potential issues Reviewing and challenging risk documentation for completeness, accuracy, risk alignment, and adherence to issue management standards Supporting audit engagements through consultation, issue evaluation, status reporting, documentation review, and stakeholder coordination Analyzing themes and trends across audit potential issues, Technology, ISS and AiDI risks, and control deficiencies to identify broader risk implications and thematic issues Developing communications, presentations, and reporting materials that translate complex technical and risk concepts into actionable insights Identifying opportunities to improve issue management, audit support, and risk governance processes

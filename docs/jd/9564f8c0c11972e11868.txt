Senior Investment Associate Fiduciary Trust International is hiring a Sr. Investment Associate to join the Coral Gables, FL office. The Senior Investment Associate supports Portfolio Managers in delivering investment management solutions and maintaining high-quality client service. This role contributes to portfolio oversight, investment implementation, client reporting, and fiduciary review processes while working closely with investment professionals and internal partners.

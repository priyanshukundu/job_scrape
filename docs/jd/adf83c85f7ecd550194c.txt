Position Summary We are seeking a highly motivated Associate Director, AI/ML & Digital Health Analytics to join the Digital Sciences, Imaging & Translational AI (DITA) team within AI/ML Quantitative and Digital Sciences (AQDS). The primary focus of this role is the development and application of modern AI/ML technologies, including Generative AI, large language models (LLMs), and agentic AI workflows, to address complex scientific and analytical challenges across pharmaceutical R&D. The successful candidate will design, prototype, evaluate, and help operationalize AI-enabled solutions that enhance scientific analysis, automate quantitative workflows, and accelerate data-driven decision-making. The candidate will play a key role in establishing rigorous evaluation frameworks, benchmarking strategies, and statistical methodologies for assessing Generative AI and agentic systems. The candidate will work across multimodal biomedical and healthcare data , including omics, medical imaging, digital health technologies (DHTs), and real-world data (RWD), and collaborate with multidisciplinary teams to translate emerging AI capabilities into scientifically rigorous and impactful solutions. The role is ideal for an individual who combines strong AI/ML and computational capabilities with a solid foundation in statistics and quantitative sciences , and who enjoys working at the intersection of emerging technology, data science, and drug development. Key Responsibilities AI/ML, Generative AI & Agentic AI: Lead the development and application of AI/ML solutions to address scientific, analytical, and operational challenges across pharmaceutical R&D. Design, prototype, and evaluate Generative AI and agentic AI workflows that automate or augment statistical analysis, data science, programming, scientific reasoning, and other knowledge-intensive workflows. Evaluate AI/ML solutions using rigorous, fit-for-purpose frameworks addressing factors such as accuracy, reproducibility, robustness, reliability, and human oversight. Collaborate with statisticians, data scientists, clinicians, scientists, engineers, and technology partners to translate scientific problems into practical AI/ML solutions. Quantitative Science & Advanced Analytics: Serve as a statistical SME within multidisciplinary project teams, providing guidance on digital biomarker (imaging and wearables) study design, statistical methodology, analysis, interpretation, and evidence generation. Apply AI/ML, statistical, and data-science methods to a broad range of multimodal biomedical data, including: omics, imaging, DHT, clinical and real-world data. Translate complex analytical findings into clear scientific insights and recommendations for cross-functional partners. Basic Qualifications PhD with 2 years of relevant professional experience in Data science, Computer Science, Engineering, Biostatistics, Machine Learning, or another quantitative STEM discipline; or Master's degree with at least 5 years of relevant professional experience in Computer Science, Data Science, Biostatistics, Machine Learning, or a related quantitative field. Strong hands-on experience in AI/ML, data science, or advanced quantitative analytics. Experience developing or applying Generative AI, LLMs, agentic AI, or other modern AI/ML approaches. Strong programming skills in R and/or Python. Solid understanding of statistical concepts, experimental/study design, data analysis, and quantitative interpretation. Demonstrated ability to work effectively in multidisciplinary and multifunctional teams. Excellent written, verbal, and scientific communication skills, with the ability to communicate complex AI/ML and quantitative concepts to both technical and non-technical audiences. Candidate demonstrates a breadth of diverse leadership experiences and capabilities including: the ability to influence and collaborate with peers, develop and coach others, oversee and guide the work of other colleagues to achieve meaningful outcomes and create business impact. Preferred Qualifications Hands-on experience with PyTorch, TensorFlow , or comparable machine-learning/deep-learning frameworks. Experience working with open-source/open-weight LLMs and modern LLM development ecosystems. Experience with including one or more of medical imaging data, digital health technologies such as wearable sensors, omics, and real-world data. Experience in foundation models and multimodal AI. Understanding of clinical development and the use of quantitative evidence in drug development. Key Capabilities for Success AI/ML innovation and execution Strong hands-on ability to develop and evaluate modern AI/ML solutions, particularly Generative AI, LLMs, and agentic workflows, while translating rapidly evolving technologies into practical scientific applications. Quantitative and statistical rigor Ability to apply sound statistical thinking to complex biomedical data and provide statistical leadership for digital health and wearable studies. Scientific problem solving Ability to start with a scientific or drug-development problem rather than a particular technology and determine when AI/ML, statistics, or a combination of approaches provides the appropriate solution. Communication and influence Ability to communicate complex technical concepts clearly and serve as a trusted quantitative and AI/ML partner across multidisciplinary teams. PHYSICAL/MENTAL REQUIREMENTS office job involving use of computer, ability to type, communicate and work on teams.

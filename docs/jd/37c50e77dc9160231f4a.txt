About the Job The Associate Director, Transportation Management – CSA is responsible for the strategic and operational leadership of transportation across Carrier's Climate Solutions Americas (CSA) distribution network. Reporting to Matthew Deen, this role ensures reliable, cost-effective freight execution while improving transportation productivity, provider capacity, service performance, and customer delivery outcomes across North and South America. This enterprise leader directs transportation planning, international transportation, transportation coordination, track-and-trace capabilities, and provider performance through designated functional leaders. Steering initiatives across Full Truckload (FTL), Less-Than-Truckload (LTL), parcel, international, and cross-border movements, the Associate Director partners with SIOP, Manufacturing, Distribution Center (DC) Operations, Procurement, and Finance to optimize freight spend, trailer utilization, standardized reporting, and long-term network capability. Hybrid 3 days in office at the Atlanta Digital Hub, 2 days remote per week Key Responsibilities Transportation Operating Model & Multi-Mode Execution: Lead the end-to-end transportation operating model across Full Truckload (FTL), Less-Than-Truckload (LTL), parcel, international, and cross-border movements. Transportation Strategy & Customer Commitments: Establish transportation strategies that balance cost, service, capacity, and customer delivery commitments across Carrier's Americas distribution network. Financial Ownership & Cost-to-Serve: Own transportation financial performance, including freight spend, Finance-validated productivity savings, budget adherence, freight cost per shipment/unit/pound, and cost-to-serve. Network Optimization & Trailer Utilization: Lead transportation planning, route optimization, dynamic load consolidation, LTL-to-FTL conversion, and trailer utilization initiatives. SIOP & Cross-Functional Capacity Planning: Establish network capacity plans aligned with Sales, Inventory, and Operations Planning (SIOP), manufacturing plants, distribution centers, and business-unit demand. Operational Accountability & Exception Governance: Drive operational accountability for delivery-ticket execution, shipment aging, provider pickup compliance, and rapid resolution of transportation exceptions. Procurement & Carrier Commercial Partnerships: Partner with Procurement on transportation sourcing, provider contracts, quarterly provider performance reviews, commercial performance, and freight optimization. Standardized Reporting & Governance Cadence: Establish standardized transportation reporting, monthly financial and service performance reviews, escalation protocols, and operating procedures (SOPs). Continuous Improvement & Digital Automation: Lead continuous improvement across transportation processes, Transportation Management Systems (TMS), analytics, automation, and execution. Organizational & Leadership Development: Develop designated functional transportation leaders, establish succession plans, and build a high-performing, accountable transportation organization. Regulatory, Safety & Cross-Border Compliance: Maintain strict compliance with transportation regulations, internal controls, safety standards, and applicable international/cross-border requirements. Minimum Qualifications Bachelor’s degree in Supply Chain Management, Logistics, Transportation, Engineering, or Business. 10+ years of progressive transportation or supply chain management experience. 5+ years of direct people-leadership experience managing transportation leaders, planners, or logistics operations teams. 5+ years of experience managing complex, multi-site transportation operations and large freight budgets across FTL, LTL, parcel, and international/cross-border modes. 3+ years of hands-on experience with Enterprise Transportation Management Systems (TMS) (such as Blue Yonder or SAP) and delivering measurable freight cost reductions while protecting on-time customer service. Preferred Qualifications MBA or advanced degree in Supply Chain Management, Logistics, or Operations. Experience in manufacturing, HVAC, industrial distribution, or similarly complex multi-site supply chains. Hands-on experience with Blue Yonder, SAP, transportation analytics, and network optimization tools. Lean Six Sigma (Green Belt or Black Belt) or APICS CSCP/CLTD continuous improvement certification. Enterprise leadership presence with strong financial judgment, clear ownership, cross-functional influence, and disciplined execution.

Group/Division KLA is known for its high-performing business model. Our Finance organization, comprised of Operations and Corporate Finance teams, delivers expert guidance to develop and execute sound business practices. Operations Finance teams use analytics, modeling and data tracking to shape business decisions. Corporate Finance's spectrum of services includes internal and external financial reporting, accounting, taxes, industry analysis, benchmarking, internal audits, and merger and acquisition support. What You'll Do In this role, you will play a key part in advancing business priorities by delivering high-impact work across your area of expertise. We are looking for a hard-working individual with a positive attitude to contribute to a great Finance team. Our charter is to understand and influence the business in making the right trade-offs and proactively find efficiencies. The ideal candidate will work closely with multiple levels of a dynamic business and has the opportunity to drive division-wide projects and initiatives in the areas of reporting, planning and analysis for KLA. Duties may include but are not limited to: Extract data sets, perform analyses, build actionable reports and financial models Initiate and maintain coverage of relevant business metrics on regular basis to help identify and analyze critical issues, risks and opportunities Revenue and expense analysis and forecasting Partner with business teams for systems and business processes improvements Perform various ad hoc analysis including scenario planning Support period close processes, preparation of financial packages and reporting Qualifications Ideal candidate should have a Bachelor's degree in Finance, Accounting, Economics, or related field. Independent and critical thinker, fast learner and excellent eye for detail. Must be hardworking, thrives in a fast-paced environment, and willingly contributes to a team. Excellent communication skills and ability to build strong working relationships. Has a strong sense of ownership of their responsibilities and is self-motivated.

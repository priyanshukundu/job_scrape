The Securitized Products Trading Floor Support Lead is responsible for the production support function for the Securitized Products Trading and Financing businesses. This role ensures the stability, availability, and performance of critical front-office trading platforms supporting loans, mortgages, structured products, and securitized asset trading activities. Acting as the primary technology liaison between Trading, Sales, Operations, Risk, and Development teams, you will drive incident resolution, operational excellence, risk mitigation, and platform enhancements in a fast-paced trading floor environment. This role requires experience supporting both front-office trading applications and backend systems, with the ability to demonstrate by example during high-severity incidents and business-critical events. To be successful in this role, you should have experience with: Fixed income and securitized products markets (Loans, RMBS, CMBS, ABS, whole loan trading) Supporting front-office trading systems and electronic trading platforms Deep understanding of trade lifecycle, pricing, risk, settlements, regulatory reporting, Implement and maintain monitoring, alerting, and observability solutions Solid experience with Linux/Unix/Windows systems and systems engineering fundamentals Experience with client-server model architecture and scalability knowledge monitoring high traffic by distributing load across multiple backend servers Some other highly valued skills may include: Good evaluative, problem-solving, and communication abilities Experience working in a high-pressure trading floor environment Excellent incident management and good stakeholder communication You may be assessed on the key critical skills relevant for success in role, such as risk and controls, change and transformation, business acumen, strategic thinking, digital and technology, as well as job-specific technical skills.

Press space or enter keys to toggle section visibility As part of the Infrastructure Projects and Capital team, you will work across investment and advisory workstreams, supporting the development, procurement and financing of infrastructure projects, particularly US P3s across transportation, social infrastructure and other core infrastructure sectors, with additional exposure to energy and digital infrastructure. You will support project financings from bid development and financing strategy through due diligence, documentation and financial close. You will build and analyze project cash flow models, assess construction and operating risks, and evaluate financing structures and investment returns under different scenarios. You will help prepare materials and coordinate with sponsors, public-sector counterparties, lenders, legal advisers and technical advisers throughout the transaction process.

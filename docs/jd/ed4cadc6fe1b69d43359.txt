Job Description This role uses data, statistical analysis, machine learning, and predictive modeling to solve complex business problems and improve data-driven decision-making. The Senior Data Scientist designs, develops, and implements analytical and machine learning solutions, builds tools that support business decisions, and identifies and integrates data that improves modeling outcomes. Working collaboratively with business partners and technical teams, this role translates business challenges into analytical solutions and helps integrate those solutions into business processes. The role provides opportunities to lead analytical projects, influence the direction of modeling work, explore new data and modeling approaches, and share technical knowledge with others. Key Responsibilities: Design, develop, evaluate, and implement statistical, machine learning, and predictive modeling solutions that address business needs and improve data-driven decisions. Work with data to identify patterns, develop features, test hypotheses, and build reliable analytical and modeling solutions. Collaborate with business partners and technical teams to understand business challenges, translate them into analytical problems, and identify appropriate modeling approaches. Develop analytical tools and solutions that use machine learning, predictive modeling, statistical methods, and data to support business decision-making. Evaluate modeling techniques, assumptions, performance, and results, and clearly communicate findings and recommendations to technical and non-technical stakeholders. Identify and evaluate new data sources, programming languages, tools, techniques, and analytical approaches that can improve modeling capabilities, efficiency, or business outcomes. Partner with stakeholders to incorporate modeling solutions into business processes and evaluate their effectiveness. Plan and manage moderately complex to complex machine learning, predictive modeling, and analytical projects, including defining tasks and delivering results within established timelines. Identify opportunities to use new data, research, modeling techniques, tools, or analytical approaches to solve evolving business problems. Communicate analytical findings and recommendations to stakeholders and leadership to support business decisions and help shape the direction of analytical work. Share technical knowledge and best practices with team members and contribute to the development of others. Experience: 3 or more years of relevant experience preferred. Master’s degree or equivalent work experience. Experience developing and evaluating statistical, machine learning, or predictive models using real-world data. Experience using programming and analytical tools to prepare and analyze data, develop models, evaluate results, and solve business problems. Experience translating business problems into analytical or modeling solutions. Experience communicating analytical findings and recommendations to technical and non-technical audiences. Supervisory Responsibilities: This role does not have direct supervisory responsibilities. #LI-YS1 Skills Artificial Intelligence Markup Language, Business Case Analyses, Data Analytics, Google Services, Microsoft Azure, Predictive Modeling, Python (Programming Language), Waterfall Model Compensation Compensation range for this position is $100,000 - $177,150 per year, based on experience and qualifications.

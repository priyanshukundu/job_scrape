About the Growth Division and the GTM Office Growth is FactSet’s newly formed commercial engine, sitting between Revenue Client Success (RCS) and Product. The division exists to turn great products into sustainable long-term commercial impact by transforming product capabilities into adoption and commercial impact. We do this through firm-type, persona-based expertise and storytelling; client insight; product marketing; competitive positioning; sales enablement; commercial excellence and transformation initiatives; and pricing and packaging. Within Growth is the Go-to-Market (GTM) Office . The GTM office owns FactSet’s multi-year ASV growth strategy and is organized around our key growth initiatives and client segments including: AI & Data, Capital Markets, Corporates, Institutional Buy-Side, Quant & Hedge Funds, and Wealth. Each segment is led by a GTM leader and supported by two shared functions – Product Marketing & Sales Enablement and GTM Operations. Your Team's Impact The Associate Director GTM Wealth is responsible for developing and executing go-to-market strategies that drive client adoption, commercial growth, and market success for FactSet's Wealth Solutions. Reporting to the Senior Director, GTM, this role serves as the bridge between Product, Marketing, Sales, and Customer Success to translate business strategy into actionable commercialization plans and measurable outcomes. You will partner with dedicated Product Marketing and Programs and Sales Enablement leaders, while working closely with centralized Pricing leadership to drive execution against Wealth segment strategy. You will help define market positioning, support product launches, develop sales motions, synthesize customer and market insights, and drive cross-functional execution. The role combines strategic thinking, program management, commercial acumen, and stakeholder leadership to support revenue growth and customer adoption.

As a member of the Global Video Engineering – Live team, you will play a key role in delivering the quality and reliability of the live encoding and packaging space, ensuring consistency and quality against KPI. You will build solutions, test integrations, document workflows, assist in deployments, and monitor performance, working with the technical operations group and other technical teams to ensure consistent 24x7 monitoring. You will work closely with key vendors, developing solutions side by side, and you will embrace diversity as part of an inclusive and flexible workplace. Primarily you will: Design, develop, and maintain high-performance software systems and features, primarily using Go, to support large-scale video streaming platforms such as Peacock and NOW/WOW. Build and maintain live and linear video streaming pipelines using in-house and third-party tools, focusing on video encoding, packaging, and content delivery. Support live events and linear channels, helping ensure reliable and high-quality streaming experiences. Collaborate with Engineering Managers and Principal Engineers to develop solutions that improve video delivery and enhance audio and video quality. Work closely with Site Reliability Engineering (SRE) teams to build reliable, scalable, and resilient services across multiple regions. Monitor system performance using metrics, logs, and traces to identify issues, troubleshoot problems, and improve service reliability. Manage and maintain cloud-based services and content delivery systems. Continuously improve code quality, system performance, and development processes. What’s in it for you?

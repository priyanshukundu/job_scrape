Join Mizuho as a Lead Data Engineer! We are seeking an experienced Lead Data Engineer with 7+ years of experience designing, building, and delivering scalable enterprise data solutions. The ideal candidate will be a strong hands-on engineer with deep expertise in Snowflake, Python, and Airflow , along with experience working in Azure or a comparable cloud platform . The role will be responsible for developing robust data pipelines and data platforms while contributing to technical architecture, engineering standards, performance, data quality, and production reliability. Experience with modern data platforms such as Databricks and Spark, as well as data governance and enterprise architecture principles, is highly desirable. Key Responsibilities Design, develop, and maintain scalable data pipelines and ETL/ELT frameworks supporting enterprise data and analytics workloads. Build and optimize data solutions using Snowflake , including data modeling, transformation, performance optimization, and workload management. Develop reusable and production-grade data engineering components using Python . Design and orchestrate complex workflows using Apache Airflow or similar orchestration platforms. Develop and support cloud-based data solutions using Microsoft Azure or comparable cloud technologies. Partner with business, analytics, architecture, governance, and technology teams to translate requirements into scalable technical solutions. Define technical designs, integration patterns, engineering standards, and best practices for enterprise data solutions. Implement appropriate data quality, monitoring, lineage, security, and operational controls across data pipelines. Troubleshoot production issues and drive improvements around performance, resiliency, scalability, and maintainability. Conduct design and code reviews and provide technical guidance to data engineering teams. Contribute to modernization of the data platform and adoption of modern engineering practices and technologies. Working experience in an Agile environment Required Qualifications 7+ years of professional experience in data engineering, data platforms, or related technology roles. Strong hands-on expertise with Snowflake & Star Schema Architecture . Advanced programming experience with Python & SQL . Strong experience with Apache Airflow or comparable workflow orchestration technologies. Hands-on experience with Microsoft Azure or another major cloud platform such as AWS or GCP. Strong experience designing and developing ETL/ELT pipelines , data integration solutions, and enterprise data processing workflows. Strong knowledge of SQL, data modeling, data warehousing, and performance optimization . Experience designing solutions for scalability, reliability, maintainability, and production support. Strong problem-solving, communication, and stakeholder-management skills. Ability to operate effectively in a complex enterprise environment and work across engineering, architecture, governance, and business teams. Preferred Qualifications Experience with Databricks and Apache Spark . Experience with Azure Data Factory (ADF) and other Azure data services. Knowledge of data governance, metadata management, lineage, data quality, and access-control frameworks . Experience with enterprise data architecture and solution design . Understanding of modern architectural approaches such as lakehouse, medallion architecture, and cloud-native data platforms . Experience establishing engineering standards, reusable frameworks, and design patterns. Financial services or other highly regulated industry experience is a plus. Ideal Candidate The successful candidate combines strong hands-on data engineering capabilities with technical leadership . They should be comfortable moving between architecture and design discussions, hands-on engineering, technical problem-solving, and collaboration with business and technology stakeholders. The role is well suited for someone who can take ownership of complex data solutions from design through production while helping raise overall engineering standards across the team.

About the Role As a Senior Software Development Engineer, you will be a founding engineer on an API-First platform for managing ADI's product specifications, building a single trusted source for product specification data. This role sits at the intersections of software, hardware, design, and marketing. The data you will model describes integrated circuits, including parametric and non-parametric data. You will need to understand what that data means to the engineers who write it and the customers who design with it. Working closely with the hiring manager, you will deepen your knowledge of the product specification domain, help shape the architecture, and build and ship usable software in early, incremental releases. You will also own the migration of existing specification data into the new platform, making sure it arrives complete, accurate, and traceable. This is a hands-on, high-visibility role working with cross-functional teams across the business. As the platform and team expand, this role will grow into the platform's technical lead. As a core technical member of this system-driven software product development team, you will collaborate with experienced colleagues representing a well-rounded team, e.g., product management, project management, software engineering, software QA, business analysis, user experience, etc. Our goal is to set new standards of software product development excellence together. Key Responsibilities Build the platform Design, build, test, and deploy the services and APIs of an API-first specification platform, from data model through to downstream consumers. Implement a structured data model for product specifications, aligned with industry standards and supporting traceability from requirements to specification to verification. Build stable, versioned, well-documented APIs that let teams across ADI read and use specification content. Integrate with existing systems and tooling, such as Jama requirements management, document control, and data sheet generation. Work directly with design, applications, and test engineers to understand how specifications are defined, measured, and guaranteed, and reflect that faithfully in the data model, including parameters, conditions, units, MIN/TYP/MAX limits, and test coverage. Own the data migration Own the migration of large volumes of specification data from existing systems into the new platform, including source analysis, data mapping, transformation, validation, reconciliation, and cutover planning. Reconcile specification content during migration: identify values that are missing, inconsistent, or stated without their conditions, and work with engineering owners to resolve them. Keep the migration transparent: define measurable completeness and accuracy criteria, report progress and data quality to stakeholders, and ensure every migrated record can be traced back to its source. Deliver production-quality software Take end-to-end ownership of what you build, from design through production operation, including fixing what breaks. Deliver reliable, secure, observable, and maintainable software in incremental releases, starting with an early usable release and evolving it toward a production platform. Apply and help establish industry-standard product development practices: backlog management, design and code reviews, automated testing, CI/CD, and release management.

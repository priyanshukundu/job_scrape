Job Description: About the Role HPE Networking is seeking an experienced and passionate technical leader to manage our Associate Sales Engineer (ASE) Program , a structured early-career development initiative designed to build the next generation of networking and security Sales Engineers. This unique role combines Sales Engineering leadership, technical mentorship, curriculum ownership, and program management. The ideal candidate brings a strong background in enterprise networking and technical presales, along with demonstrated leadership, a passion for developing talent, and the ability to translate technical expertise into meaningful learning experiences. As HPE Networking brings together the strengths of Juniper Networks and HPE Aruba Networking , we are entering an exciting era of AI-native networking, intelligent automation, and cloud-managed infrastructure. This transformation creates an opportunity to develop future Sales Engineers with the skills to navigate emerging technologies and help customers realize their business value. The successful candidate will lead the ASE Program from onboarding through graduation, developing participants' technical capabilities, customer engagement skills, business acumen, and professional confidence while ensuring consistent execution of the program's curriculum and development milestones. About the Associate Sales Engineer Program The Associate Sales Engineer Program is HPE Networking's structured early-career development program , designed to accelerate the transition from emerging technical talent to confident, customer-facing Sales Engineers. Participants develop foundational and advanced networking knowledge, technical selling capabilities, customer engagement skills, and business acumen through a combination of structured learning, hands-on labs, mentorship, and field experience. The program emphasizes modern networking technologies, including AI-native networking, wired and wireless infrastructure, routing, switching, network security, SD-WAN, SASE, cloud-managed networking, and automation. Participants gain exposure to the combined HPE Networking portfolio, including Juniper Mist AI, Marvis AI, HPE Aruba Networking Central, and related networking and security solutions . Our goal extends beyond technical training. We aim to develop curious, adaptable, technically credible professionals who can connect technology to business outcomes and become trusted advisors to customers and partners.

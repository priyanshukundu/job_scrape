About This Job The Senior Business Analyst supports the Business Management team by leading process improvement initiatives, coordinating special projects, and contributing to Chief of Staff and business management support for the Securities business unit. This is a newly approved position established to increase capacity within the Business Management function, with a focus on project coordination, portfolio visibility, and strengthening the team's operating model. Responsibilities Business Process Improvement: Lead in-depth analysis of business processes, identify opportunities for improvement, and drive implementation of recommended solutions. Special Projects: Coordinate and support execution of strategic and special projects on behalf of Business Management leadership. Business Management Support: Support the Chief of Staff and broader business management priorities for the Securities business unit as the organization evolves. Project Portfolio Management: Maintain visibility across the team’s portfolio of initiatives, track progress and key milestones, identify risks or dependencies, and support prioritization. Leadership Reporting & Operating Cadence: Own preparation of executive reporting, meeting materials, and recurring business reviews that support the leadership team’s operating rhythm and decision-making. Cross-Functional Execution: Partner with stakeholders across the business to gather requirements, align priorities, and support successful implementation of process and system changes. Team Leadership: Provide guidance and mentorship to Business Analysts supporting the team. Documentation & Best Practices: Maintain business management documentation, templates, processes, and resources while helping establish consistent best practices across initiatives. Qualifications Bachelor’s degree or equivalent combination of education and relevant professional experience. Typically 3–5 years of relevant experience in business analysis, project coordination, business management, or a related function. Strong understanding of business analytics and the ability to translate data and business performance into strategic insights and recommendations. Advanced analytical thinking and decision-making skills , with the ability to evaluate complex situations and independently reach sound conclusions. Demonstrated ability to collaborate cross-functionally and influence outcomes without direct authority . Foundational project management skills , including planning, coordination, prioritization, tracking, and execution of multiple initiatives. Strong written and verbal communication skills, including the ability to develop clear, executive-ready presentations, reporting, and meeting content . Results-oriented and proactive, with strong judgment, integrity, accountability, and follow-through. Advanced proficiency in Microsoft Excel and PowerPoint , with strong overall proficiency across the Microsoft Office Suite. Experience with process mapping, project management, and data analysis tools preferred.

Job Duties: Associate, Data Analytics & Reporting with Goldman Sachs Services LLC in Dallas, Texas. Provide end-to-end data analytics and reporting support to the Firm’s global headcount and talent processes, including project planning, requirement gathering, report build, data governance, and quality control. Leverage advanced knowledge of overall suite of Business Intelligence (BI) tools including SQL, Tableau, Alteryx, and SAP Business Objects. Understand and analyze internal client needs to build and enhance reports, identify areas for automation and process improvement, and recommend technology solutions. Build complex data workflows to eliminate complicated manual processes in headcount and talent organization using Alteryx. Design and build ETL processes, integrating data from sources such as Relational (IQ) or No-SQL Databases, analytical databases like Snowflake or other cloud platforms. Generate analysis and visualization using Tableau to assist with real time reporting and analysis and manage Tableau dashboard’s health, maintenance and expansion. Liaise between business stakeholders and product & technology teams to provide operational efficiency through data source design to facilitate report automation. Participate in design and User Acceptance Testing of new technology driven product. Job Requirements: Master’s degree (U.S. or foreign equivalent) in Computer Science, Business Analytics, Data Architecture/Management, Information Technology/Science, or related field with one (1) year of experience in the job offered or in a related role OR Bachelor’s degree (U.S. or foreign equivalent) in Computer Science, Business Analytics, Data Architecture/Management, Information Technology/Science, or related field with three (3) years of experience in the job offered or in a related role. Prior work experience must include one (1) year (with a Master’s degree) OR three (3) years (with a Bachelor’s degree) with: using advanced visualization tools, including Tableau or Power BI, to effectively provide insights from very large datasets; building automated logic-based workflows in Alteryx or similar ETL (Extract, Transform, Load) tools to transform and integrate data; using advanced Excel tools, including complex formulas, pivot tables, macros, and advanced modeling techniques; working on advanced research design, analytics, or decision science, utilizing statistical techniques, data mining or statistical modeling skills; mining through large unstructured datasets, analyzing them and presenting results that deliver clear insights or story; and using relational database systems to write and modify SQL queries and extract existing data to calculate and format into ad-hoc metrics. ©The Goldman Sachs Group, Inc., 2026.

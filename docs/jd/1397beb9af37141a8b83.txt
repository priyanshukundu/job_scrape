At Bristol Myers Squibb, patients are at the center of everything we do, and the systems that protect their data have to be as dependable as the medicines we deliver. Identity and Access Management sits within Cybersecurity, part of Business Insights & Technology, and is responsible for ensuring that every identity at BMS — employee, partner, workload, and AI agent — is authenticated, authorized, and governed. This role leads the engineering and operation of the platforms that make that possible: enterprise directory services (PingDS, Active Directory, Microsoft Entra ID, certificate services) and the identity infrastructure that now underpins agentic AI and machine identity across the enterprise. It works in close partnership with the teams that own single sign-on and federation. The Associate Director leads through Senior Managers and is accountable for platform availability, security posture, modernization, and delivery of the IAM platform roadmap. Success is measured by what the teams deliver, by the reliability of services that nothing else at BMS can function without, and by the pace at which the platform absorbs new identity patterns rather than blocking them. Key responsibilities Platform ownership and reliability. Accountable for the availability, performance, and security of enterprise directory and identity services operating 24x7 at global scale. Own the operational model end to end — patching, vulnerability remediation, upgrades, disaster recovery, and incident response — and ensure the teams responsible for these platforms are reachable and engaged when other teams depend on them. Identity data quality and lifecycle. Own the accuracy, consistency, and completeness of identity data across the directory environment. Ensure identity records flow correctly from authoritative sources through the synchronization and provisioning layer into Active Directory, Entra ID, and PingDS, that attributes stay synchronized across platforms, and that joiner, mover, and leaver events originating in identity governance are provisioned into the directory platforms through SCIM promptly and completely. Establish the monitoring, reconciliation, and remediation practices that keep orphaned, stale, and duplicate identities out of the environment, and define the data standards that downstream applications and governance processes depend on. Directory services modernization. Drive the continued modernization of enterprise directory services, including keeping domain controllers and forest functional levels on supported, current versions, secure LDAP adoption, and progressive reduction of technical debt. Own the PingDS platform — availability, replication health, version upgrades, and its evolution from a legacy LDAP endpoint into a governed, API-accessible identity data service. Position directory services for post-quantum readiness. AI agent and machine identity. Own the identity lifecycle for non-human identities: service accounts, workload identities, and AI agents. Extend and operate the enterprise framework for agentic AI identity — registration, authorization patterns including on-behalf-of and delegated authority, credential management, and the governance and revocation controls that make autonomous agent access auditable and reversible. Automation and AI-assisted engineering. Make AI-assisted engineering standard practice across the teams. Establish the patterns, tooling, and review standards that let engineers build and operate at a higher rate, and reduce dependence on manual operational work and external development capacity. Partnership and delivery. Work across Cybersecurity, Infrastructure, Application Development, HR, Procurement, Legal, and external partners to deliver identity capability the business can consume. Because these platforms sit underneath nearly every other service at BMS, this role is expected to be a dependable partner — reachable and engaged during incidents, transparent and early when commitments are at risk, and fluent enough in emerging identity patterns, particularly agentic AI, to enable the business quickly and safely rather than defaulting to no. Represent IAM platforms in audit, regulatory, and risk conversations. People leadership. Lead through Senior Managers: set direction, build technical and leadership depth, develop succession for critical platform roles, and sustain a team culture where engineers are responsive to colleagues, own outcomes beyond their own platform boundary, treat a degraded login experience with the same seriousness as a missed delivery date, and are supported in sustainable 24x7 operating models. Cross-platform collaboration. Partner closely with the SSO and Federation Services team, which owns the authentication platforms — PingAM, PingGateway and PingOne Protect. Directory services and authentication are deeply interdependent, and this role is expected to understand those platforms well enough to make sound joint architecture decisions, resolve shared incidents, and represent the combined IAM platform position to stakeholders and in audit and risk forums. Qualifications: Required: Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Information Systems, or equivalent experience. Ten or more years in identity and access management or enterprise infrastructure, including five or more years leading technical teams, with demonstrated experience leading through other managers. Deep hands-on experience with PingDS (ForgeRock Directory Services) in a production enterprise environment — including deployment, replication topology, tuning, upgrade, backup and recovery — is required, as PingDS is the enterprise directory platform at BMS. Equally strong hands-on background in Active Directory and Microsoft Entra ID at global scale. Deep working knowledge of modern authentication standards — OAuth 2.0, OIDC, SAML, SCIM, token exchange and delegation patterns — and practical experience with enterprise SSO and federation platforms sufficient to lead cross-functional work with the teams that own them. Strong hands-on technical foundation across both Linux and Windows Server, practical programming and scripting ability, and solid command of networking and the HTTP stack — enough to build, troubleshoot, and deploy solutions directly rather than only directing others to do so. Proven record operating business-critical platforms at global scale with high availability requirements. Demonstrated ownership of platform modernization and legacy retirement. Strong record of cross-functional partnership and of clear, timely communication under incident conditions. Preferred: Experience with PingAM and Ping Advanced Identity Cloud, including identity synchronization and provisioning through remote connector infrastructure. Experience integrating directory platforms with an enterprise identity governance system such as SailPoint, including SCIM-based provisioning. Experience with PingGateway, PingOne Protect, CyberArk, or comparable platforms. Experience designing identity and authorization models for AI agents, workloads, or other non-human identities. Public cloud and infrastructure-as-code experience, including AWS. Experience in a regulated industry; pharmaceutical or life sciences preferred.

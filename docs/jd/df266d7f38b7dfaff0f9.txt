Job Description The Process Central Services Associate performs moderately complex medical claim related activities for non-technical claims in accordance with established quality and timeliness standards on specific losses. As directed by management, the individual handles one or a combination of claim files. The individual is also expected to deliver compassionate service that is fast, fair, and easy, to ensure customer retention. Key Responsibilities Assists adjusters with more complex medical claim handling, analyzes documentation, and settles basic, straightforward, and routine claims quickly and efficiently. Contacts or receives contact from customers and other claim-related third parties to obtain and/or provide necessary file information in accordance with quality and process standards. Makes and maintains a connection with customers by understanding and meeting their needs. Generates and sends appropriate forms to insureds and/or claimants for completion. Completes necessary forms, logs documents into the system, and routes them to the appropriate parties. May investigate prior losses and other information on file and order reports as needed at the direction of adjusters and management. Retrieves or sends supporting documentation to vendors or other parties as directed. Handles claim files, including locating/requesting files, filing documents, reconstructing missing files, correcting misfiled documents, and sending documentation to other offices or parties. Receives, screens, and routes incoming calls and other correspondence. Performs clerical and administrative duties, including data entry, filing documents, email, and calendar management. Participates in or assists with training new or less experienced employees and demonstrates work processes. Preferred Education 4-year Bachelor’s Degree. In lieu of the above education requirements, an equivalent combination of education and experience may be considered. 0-2 years’ experience. Supervisory Responsibilities This job does not have supervisory duties. This position is not available for California, Alaska, Hawaii, Washington, or Puerto Rico residents. Skills Business Communications, Claims Processing, Communication, Detail-Oriented, Insurance Claims, Microsoft Excel, Microsoft Office, Organizing, Written Communication Compensation Compensation range for this position is $17.98/hour - $27.79/hour, based on experience and qualifications.

Start your Voyage – Apply Now Voya Financial is seeking a Director of Data Engineering to provide hands-on technical leadership for multiple data-engineering teams supporting our Wealth business, including retirement and retail wealth capabilities. This leader will combine people leadership with deep technical judgment, helping teams make sound architecture, solution-design, and data-engineering decisions while delivering reliable end-to-end data pipelines. This is an opportunity for an experienced data-engineering leader who still enjoys working in the technical details. You will guide engineering teams and technical leads, review designs and code, establish strong engineering practices, and collaborate with fellow data leaders on standards and initiatives that span multiple business domains. Please note: This position allows remote work from home. East Coast location or Eastern Time working-hour alignment is strongly preferred based on the team’s collaboration model. The Contributions You’ll Make: Lead four data-engineering teams supporting the Wealth domain, providing leadership to technical leads and a distributed engineering organization. Provide hands-on technical leadership across architecture, solution design, implementation, and engineering quality. Review technical designs and code, challenge engineering decisions constructively, and help teams implement scalable, maintainable solutions. Guide the development and operation of end-to-end data pipelines, with a strong focus on Databricks and modern data-engineering practices. Establish and reinforce engineering standards for data quality, error handling, monitoring, and reliable pipeline development. Partner with peer data-engineering directors to establish consistent standards, practices, and procedures across data domains. Lead and coordinate initiatives that span engineering teams and business domains. Partner with internal customers, product teams, technology partners, and other stakeholders to translate business needs into effective data solutions. Support critical production incidents when leadership and technical expertise are needed, including problem assessment, root-cause analysis, and resolution planning. Develop technical talent through coaching, mentoring, feedback, and thoughtful leadership. Advance modern engineering practices, including appropriate use of automation and AI-assisted development capabilities. Minimum Knowledge & Experience: Bachelor’s or master’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Information Technology, or a related field of study. Significant professional experience in data engineering, including progressively responsible technical and leadership experience. Demonstrated experience leading multiple data-engineering teams and/or technical leads. Strong knowledge of SQL, relational database concepts, and data architecture. Experience designing, developing, and operating modern end-to-end data pipelines. Experience with Databricks or a comparable modern data-processing platform, with the ability to transfer knowledge between technologies. Sufficient programming experience to understand and review Python or comparable data-engineering code. Strong understanding of data quality, error handling, observability, and engineering practices that support reliable pipelines. Experience contributing to technical architecture and solution-design decisions. Experience providing technical leadership during significant production incidents. Demonstrated ability to communicate and collaborate across engineering teams and technical and business stakeholders. Preferred Knowledge & Experience: Direct experience with Databricks and Spark. Experience with API and integration concepts within broader enterprise data architectures. Experience leading globally distributed or offshore engineering teams. Experience establishing engineering standards across multiple teams or domains. Experience with cloud-based data platforms such as Azure, AWS, or Google Cloud Platform. Familiarity with automated data-quality frameworks and mature CI/CD practices. Experience within financial services, retirement, wealth management, or investment-related data environments.

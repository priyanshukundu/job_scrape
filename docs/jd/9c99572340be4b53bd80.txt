Build our future together: At Regeneron, we use science and innovation to develop life-changing medicines for people with serious diseases. We are looking to fill a Data Scientist position to support the Process Controls and Validation group. These teams support the Manufacturing and Regulatory Sciences departments by developing and maintaining robust, data driven control programs and performing validation activities. This position will be responsible for completing Data Scientist and Data Engineering tasks around the development and maintenance of validated tools for automated report generating, data analysis, and key performance indicator (KPI) tracking. In this fast-paced environment, Data Scientists have an opportunity to work on numerous aspects of the process and business. When & where: Location: East Greenbush, NY, United States Shift: Monday- Friday 8-4:30pm Discover your role: Gather requirements from users, evaluate existing systems, and provide guidance on the utilization of existing and potential new systems Interface and collaborate with various departments across the business (Manufacturing, Quality Assurance, IT, etc.) to support efficient review and transfer of data and analysis in an automated way. Analyze data sources, collate data, and prepare source data in support of testing and validation tools supporting GMP manufacturing. Develop and maintain new tools for tracking, analyzing, and reporting data within automated dashboards or reports. Present on project status and team metrics during cross functional meetings. Generate design, requirements, and testing documentation in support of validation activities. Automate routine tasks and data solutions to continuously improve processes. This role requires: BS/BA in science, engineering, information technology management, computer science or a related field Bachelor’s degree in mathematics, statistics, with computer science highly preferred Associate Data Scientist: Degree with 2+ years’ experience Data Scientist: Degree with 5+ years' experience Level will be determined based on qualifications relevant to the role Experience with software development lifecycle and coding is strongly preferred Experience working with Python is preferred Report automation and software validation experience AI-assisted coding experience is a plus Biopharmaceutical current good manufacturing practice (cGMP) experience is an advantage Does this sound like you?

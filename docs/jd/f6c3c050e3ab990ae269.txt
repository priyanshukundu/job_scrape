Job Description: Senior Data Scientist The Role At Fidelity's AI Solutions Engineering and Applications (AISEA) organization, you will be experimenting with the frontier technology and also developing and deploying AI solutions that solve real business problems at enterprise scale and create lasting value for Fidelity associates and customers. We partner closely with business and product teams to identify opportunities, define AI strategies, and deliver innovative solutions powered by machine learning, Generative AI, and intelligent automation. Our work spans document intelligence, knowledge discovery, workflow automation, and emerging agentic AI applications that transform how work gets done across the enterprise. AISEA is seeking a Senior Manager, Data Science with experience in Large Language Models (LLMs), Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), agentic AI systems, document intelligence, and related AI technologies. The ideal candidate combines strong technical depth with practical experience delivering AI solutions that drive measurable business impact. The ideal candidate combines strong data science and AI fundamentals with product sense, broader curiosity, detail-oriented and a track record of delivering measurable business impact via innovation. The Expertise and Skills You Bring PhD in Computer Science, Machine Learning, Artificial Intelligence, Statistics, Applied Math, Operations Research, Physics, or related quantitative discipline with 3 plus years of relevant professional experience; OR Master's Degree in a related field with 6 plus years of relevant professional experience. Strong technical foundation in machine learning and statistical modeling, with deeper experience in one or more applied AI areas such as LLM. natural language processing, large language models, deep learning, knowledge graphs, or related methods. Strong Python and SQL programming skills with demonstrated proficiency in data extraction, data engineering, exploratory analysis, feature engineering, data modeling, pipeline automation, and model evaluation. Experience or working knowledge in one or more applied AI areas such as: Modern AI/ML frameworks including PyTorch, TensorFlow, JAX, LangChain, LlamaIndex, or equivalent technologies Information Retrieval and Semantic Search, question answering and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), chatbot evaluation Generative AI and Large Language Models (LLMs) AI Agents and agent orchestration frameworks, Knowledge Graphs Document Intelligence and Intelligent Automation Exposure to intelligent document processing use cases, which may include document classification, OCR, key-value extraction, signature or seal detection, annotation strategy and dataset creation, and evaluation of extraction quality. Experience designing or contributing to AI/ML evaluation and monitoring frameworks, including benchmark datasets, labeled and synthetic test data, model and prompt comparison, precision/recall analysis, error analysis, latency assessment, cost-quality tradeoff analysis, and production monitoring with tools such as Fiddler, LangSmith and so on. Experience deploying AI and machine learning solutions with commercial and open-source foundation models into production environments. Experience with cloud computing platforms and AI development environments, preferably AWS and related AI services. Solid verbal communication, presentation, and technical writing skills with an ability to explain complex data science, statistics, and computer science concepts clearly to nontechnical audiences. The Value You Deliver Design, develop, and deploy AI solutions that deliver measurable business outcomes across Fidelity. Lead data science activities across multiple projects involving complex business and technical challenges. Partner with product, technology, and business stakeholders to identify and prioritize high-value AI opportunities. Develop and evaluate LLM, RAG, and agentic AI solutions that improve knowledge access, automate workflows, and enhance user experiences. Drive innovation in document intelligence and information extraction using modern AI approaches. Establish best practices for experimentation, model evaluation, prompt optimization, and AI solution development. Contribute to the technical direction and adoption of emerging AI technologies across the organization. Present technical recommendations, project results, and business impact to senior technical and non-technical audiences. Mentor junior data scientists and contribute to a culture of technical excellence, innovation, and continuous learning.

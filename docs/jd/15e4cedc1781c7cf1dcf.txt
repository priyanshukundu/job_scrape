Business Analyst — Town Capital and Delivery Governance
Citizens Financial Group
Location: United States
Link: https://hcgn.fa.us2.oraclecloud.com/hcmUI/CandidateExperience/en/sites/CX_1/job/50104

Advance Wealth Platform Transformation through disciplined capital management, resource alignment, and actionable delivery insights. The Business Analyst supports Town operations by executing capital planning, forecasting, and reporting and aligning delivery pod resources with approved budgets. Partnering with Experience Owners, Neighborhood leads, technology, and finance, this role translates complex financial and operational data into decision-ready insights, enables informed investment and resourcing decisions, and drives issues and actions to resolution. Key Responsibilities Coordinate daily with Experience Owners and Neighborhood leads to align delivery pod resources with approved budgets, identify staffing and funding discrepancies, and escalate gaps for resolution. Maintain and reconcile resource records daily in the Agile Resource Data Mart (TargetProcess) application and underlying tracking systems, updating assignments to reflect resource allocation changes. Execute capital forecasting and reporting each month by established submission deadlines; consolidate inputs, validate assumptions, analyze variances against budget and prior forecasts, and present funding risks and required decisions. Lead monthly Neighborhood capital readouts; prepare agendas and supporting materials before each session, document decisions and action owners, and track actions, dependencies, and escalations through the next monthly review. Validate delivery updates and prepare Strategic Delivery Reporting for each required reporting cycle by established submission deadlines, clearly communicating progress, risks, issues, dependencies, and financial implications. Review reporting routines and reconciliation activities during each monthly reporting cycle to identify improvements in accuracy and efficiency; recommend changes with defined owners and target dates while maintaining required controls. Required Qualifications Strong analytical and Microsoft Excel skills, with the ability to validate data and produce clear management reports and presentations. Knowledge of Agile delivery structures and resource pod alignment, including the relationship between staffing plans, funding, and delivery commitments. Demonstrated business analysis experience supporting technology delivery, capital management, or resource planning, with the ability to independently manage complex, recurring deliverables. Strong understanding of budgeting, capital allocation, forecasting, and variance analysis, with the ability to explain financial implications to business and delivery stakeholders. Experience reconciling resource and financial data across multiple systems, investigating discrepancies, and coordinating corrective actions. Experience supporting governance, change control, approval workflows, and documentation requirements in a controlled environment. Excellent facilitation, written communication, and stakeholder coordination skills, including the ability to influence without direct authority and escalate issues with clear supporting analysis. Strong organizational skills and attention to detail, with the ability to meet monthly reporting deadlines, maintain reliable records, and manage competing priorities. Preferred Qualifications Hands-on experience with Oracle, TargetProcess, Jira, and Confluence. Experience in financial services, with fluency in Wealth Management preferred. Experience in an enterprise delivery model or technology management office. Strong data-analysis and Excel skills. Familiarity with project governance (including Town management, Strategic Delivery Reporting, QBR routines, and ECF Governance Exception and Change Request processes).

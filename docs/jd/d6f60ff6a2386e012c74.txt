Associate Director, Real-World Data and Analytics
Regeneron
Location: SLEEPY HOLLOW
Link: https://regeneron.wd1.myworkdayjobs.com/Careers/job/SLEEPY-HOLLOW/Associate-Director--Real-World-Data-and-Analytics_R51892

Build our future together: At Regeneron, we use science and innovation to develop life-changing medicines for people with serious diseases. We are seeking an Associate Director, Real-World Data and Analytics (RWDnA), to join our RWDnA team and help generate real-world evidence (RWE) that informs decisions across our portfolio. In this role, you will turn real-world data into data-driven insights, working with limited guidance to develop and implement analyses that address key research questions while collaborating with project teams, senior RWDnA colleagues, and external analytics partners. This position offers the opportunity to contribute to a fast-growing, science-driven organization making a meaningful difference to patients worldwide. Where & when : Location: Sleepy Hollow, NY Work model: On-site 4 days/week Discover your role : • Deliver data-driven insights by analyzing real-world data to answer research questions • Contribute to the development of RWE queries, protocols, and statistical analysis plans • Execute hands-on programming and quality control of analytic code on internal RWD assets • Evaluate and recommend appropriate RWD sources for specific research questions • Communicate findings clearly to project teams and cross-functional partners • Support abstracts, posters, manuscripts, and internal presentations • Coordinate work with external analytics vendors and research partners • Work independently, balance multiple priorities, and deliver high-quality results in a fast-paced environment This role requires : • Doctoral degree in a quantitative discipline applying data science to healthcare data with 4+ years of hands-on programming experience, or a Master's degree in a related field with 6+ years • Hands-on experience with RWD sources such as administrative claims, registries, and electronic healthcare records • Advanced programming skills in SAS, R, and/or Python • Proficiency in survival analysis, propensity score methods, and generalized linear models • Excellent written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to collaborate in cross-functional teams • Preferred: 3+ years of life science company or healthcare consulting experience; machine learning experience Does this sound like you?

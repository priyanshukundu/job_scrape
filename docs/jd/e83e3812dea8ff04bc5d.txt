Who we are looking for We’re looking for a Business Analyst to join the GCI CAO, Governance & Insights team and help transform how senior leaders access insights and make decisions. You’ll work at the intersection of business, data, technology, automation, and AI, translating complex business needs into reporting and analytics solutions that improve visibility and support informed decision-making. A core focus will be advancing the Executive Hub, our centralized executive reporting and analytics capability. You’ll partner directly with senior stakeholders and cross-functional teams to shape requirements, enhance reporting, and turn data into meaningful insights. You’ll also support broader GCI CAO strategic initiatives and help identify opportunities to simplify processes and enhance insight delivery through automation and AI.

Department Overview Motorola Solutions Global Education is responsible for creating innovative training strategies, and the design, development, and delivery of training content across the MSI solutions/product portfolio. Our training services address the educational needs of our Motorola Solutions customers, partners and employees, enabling each group to be more efficient, effective, and ensure established business and operational outcomes are met. Job Description We are seeking an organized, detail oriented Training Portfolio Manager to support the execution and operational rollout of our product training initiatives. Reporting directly to the Director of Global Training Content, you will play a key operational role in connecting content creators, product teams, and our diverse audiences including internal and external stakeholders. Responsibilities Prioritize projects based on business impact, product release schedules, and cross-functional demand. Own the intake workflows, prioritization frameworks, and resource-allocation models to evaluate and schedule incoming training requests. Maintain portfolio-level visibility for stakeholders, tracking launch health, resource constraints, and overall delivery timelines. Partner with internal leaders within Global Education and other leaders within the organization to align training priorities with corporate strategy. Directly lead and manage a team of project managers. Ability to pull, aggregate, and analyze data across multiple data sources. Track end-to-end launch health and post-release learner engagement metrics across both internal and external tracks. Specific Knowledge and Skills Bachelor’s degree. 5+ years of experience in training, project management, program management, or portfolio management. 1+ years of experience in a formal supervisory, team lead, or people management role. Proficiency with Google Sheets and experience with pulling reports from an LMS or analytic dashboards, preference given to experience with Tableau or Cornerstone. Proven experience managing a multi-project roadmap using Jira or Smartsheet. Strong written and verbal communication skills with cross-functional stakeholders.

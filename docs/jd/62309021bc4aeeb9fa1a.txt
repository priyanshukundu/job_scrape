Job Description Multifamily/Workforce Housing Acquisitions (Equity Fund Investments) What is the Opportunity? RBC Capital Markets – Community Investments (www.rbccm.com/Community Investments or “RBCCI”), part of the Royal Bank of Canada (RBC) firm, is a leading national syndicator of Federal and State Low Income Housing Tax Credits (LIHTC), Historic Rehabilitation Tax Credits (HRTC), New Markets Tax Credits (NMTC), Renewable Energy Tax Credits (RETC) and Workforce Housing Equity Investments (WFH). By delivering on these products, RBCCI executes on the bank’s stated Purpose of “helping clients thrive and communities prosper”. RBCCI is expanding its housing platform to capture the growing institutional investor interest in WFH equity investments in the US multifamily real estate sector. WFH investments for RBCCI include both new development and acquisition/rehab multifamily housing assets (financed without tax credits) with the majority of any project’s units restricted to incomes between 80% and 120% of Area Median Income (AMI) with rents affordable to those tenants and the balance of the units unrestricted targeting up to achievable market rents. The Vice President, Workforce Housing Investments will be responsible for the sourcing, origination and execution of equity investments for the WFH platform sufficient to satisfy the needs of the group’s institutional Limited Partners. This individual will curate and manage developer/owner and broker relationships new to RBCCI to develop and maintain a pipeline of investment opportunities consistent with the WFH platform’s objectives. Additionally, the individual will be responsible for maintaining deep knowledge of their assigned markets and submarkets, for assistance with underwriting and structuring of new investments and for contributing their knowledge to the acquisition and investment goals of the WFH strategy. Furthermore, as a significant component of this role, the VP, WFH Investments will be responsible for oversight of the financial modeling and execution of investment structures from initial sourcing through the Letter of Intent process prior to transitioning the investment to the underwriting and closing team.

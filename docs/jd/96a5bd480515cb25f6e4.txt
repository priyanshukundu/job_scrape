Job Summary: PayPal Marketing Technology team is dedicated to creating a best-in-class platform. We are looking for highly talented, enthusiastic, and motivated engineers to join our team. As a Staff Software Engineer on our Marketing Technology Platform, you will own the architecture and delivery of complex, high-scale services built on Java/Spring, Node.js, React, and Python, evaluate and introduce the right technologies, and drive the hardest technical challenges independently. You will remain hands-on in design, coding, security, testing, and production releases, while leading and overseeing the work of a team of engineers. You will partner with engineers, architects, product owners, and program managers to deliver features that impact millions of PayPal users globally.

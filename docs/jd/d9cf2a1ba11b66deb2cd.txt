BANC OF CALIFORNIA AND YOUR CAREER Banc of California, Inc. (NYSE: BANC) is a bank holding company with over $34 billion in assets and the parent company of Banc of California. Banc of California is one of the nation’s premier relationship-based business banks, providing banking and treasury management services to small, middle market, and venture backed businesses. As the largest independent bank headquartered in California, the bank offers a broad range of loan and deposit products and services through a network of full-service branches and regional offices, as well as through digital and nationwide capabilities. The bank also provides full-service payment processing solutions to its clients and serves the Community Association Management industry nationwide through its technology forward platform, SmartStreet™. Banc of California is committed to supporting its local communities through the Banc of California Charitable Foundation and by partnering with organizations that promote financial literacy, job training, small business support, affordable housing, and more. At Banc of California, our success is powered by our people and a shared commitment to delivering meaningful results. We foster an environment where entrepreneurial thinking is encouraged, and accountability and operational excellence are expected. Our team members are empowered to take ownership, make informed decisions, and make a meaningful impact as the bank continues to grow and evolve. We are dedicated to supporting your growth and wellbeing through comprehensive benefits, robust development opportunities, and inclusive programs that enable you to perform at your best. Together we win! THE OPPORTUNITY Responsible for assisting Commercial Banking Managers with the management of the Commercial Bank portfolio. Provides accurate, timely, and comprehensive information in response to client inquiries and requests, supporting the retention of profitable client relationships. Works with internal operational business units to facilitate timely financial reporting, accurate documentation, and resolution of payment delinquencies. Participates in financial analysis and credit presentations to Credit Administration. Performs all duties in accordance with the Company’s policies and procedures, all U.S. state and federal laws and regulations, wherein the Company operates. HOW YOU’LL MAKE A DIFFERENCE Apply existing guidelines and procedures when proposing solutions within the department. Use sound judgment and experience to work through potentially complex problems based on precedent, examples, reasonableness, or a combination of these. Maintain up-to-date knowledge of banking services and implementation processes. Assist with loan portfolio maintenance, including tickler system management, past-due collections, financial reporting collection, organization and analysis, and covenant compliance testing. Perform credit and financial statement analysis, including spreading, projection modeling, and underwriting. Prepare concept memos for Credit Administration approval and letters of intent for borrower execution. Interface with prospective clients, as directed, to collect necessary financial information and formation documents. Review client information for completeness and accuracy and follow up until all required information is collected. Review potentially complex legal entity documentation and facilitate due diligence and Bank Secrecy Act documentation. Work in tandem with Relationship Managers to review and participate in the negotiation of potentially complex legal documentation with outside counsel and specialists within the Bank. Work with loan coordinators and the loan closing team to facilitate a seamless loan closing experience for clients. Partner with deposit and treasury management operations teams to support a positive client experience. Work with the Appraisal Department to order appraisals, environmental reports, and preliminary title reports. Provide effective customer service and assist in resolving problems within assigned authority. Escalate customer calls and problem accounts as appropriate. If applicable, primarily support Commercial Real Estate Relationship Managers with the management of the Commercial Real Estate Bank portfolio. Follow all established policies and procedures. Perform other duties and projects as assigned. WHAT YOU’LL BRING Bachelor’s degree, relevant work experience, or an equivalent combination of education and training. Three to five years of experience in the banking and/or financial services industry required. Demonstrated knowledge of, adherence to, monitoring of, and responsibility for compliance with applicable state and federal regulations and laws, including Regulation Z, Regulation B, the Fair Housing Act, the Home Mortgage Disclosure Act, the Real Estate Settlement Procedures Act, the Fair Credit Reporting Act, the Bank Secrecy Act in conjunction with the USA PATRIOT Act, Anti-Money Laundering requirements, the Customer Identification Program, the Right to Financial Privacy Act, and the Community Reinvestment Act. Intermediate knowledge of banking products and services, including Bank Secrecy Act compliance. Effective organizational and time-management skills. Intermediate proficiency in computer and personal computer operations, including Microsoft Word, Excel, PowerPoint, and Outlook. Intermediate typing skills sufficient to meet the production needs of the position. Strong financial mathematics skills, including financial ratios, interest, percentages, and account balancing. Ability to add, subtract, multiply, and divide using whole numbers, common fractions, and decimals; identify routine mathematical errors; calculate rates, ratios, and percentages; and draft and interpret graphs. Exceptional oral, written, and interpersonal communication skills, with the ability to carry out instructions, interpret documents, understand procedures, prepare reports and correspondence, and communicate clearly with customers and employees. Ability to work with general supervision while performing assigned duties. High school diploma or equivalent required.

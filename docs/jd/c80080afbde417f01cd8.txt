Job Description Summary The Lead Business Analyst – IT Actuarial leads the translation of actuarial business needs into technology solutions that deliver measurable business value. Working across Life, Health, Retirement, and Annuity products, this role connects actuarial experts, business stakeholders, and IT teams to modernize platforms, data, models, and processes. The role leverages AI, automation, and advanced analytics where they can meaningfully improve efficiency, insights, decision-making, and business outcomes. Job Description Our actuarial and finance systems generate the figures the company reports and support the calculations underlying the products we offer. This team is integrating AI and automation into these processes to address actuarial questions that were previously too time-consuming or labor-intensive to pursue, while also proactively maintaining system health without requiring continuous manual monitoring. This makes the position a distinctive analyst role. Much of what you will define has not yet been built, and in some cases, established methods for specifying it do not yet exist. AI systems can produce outputs that appear credible but are incorrect, so requirements based on the assumption of a single definitive answer may not be effective in practice. You will serve as the bridge between actuarial teams and technology, understanding both the business context and the technology's practical limitations, and providing each side with clear, candid guidance about the other. Responsibilities · Lead with an AI-first perspective to identify opportunities where artificial intelligence, automation, and advanced analytics can improve actuarial systems and processes. · Partner with actuarial business teams to gather, analyze, and document requirements, with a focus on AI-enabled use cases such as predictive insights, automation, deep research and decision support. · Translate complex actuarial concepts into clear technical and AI-ready specifications for IT, data, and AI engineering teams. · Collaborate with AI, data engineering, and platform teams to support AI-driven enhancements, data integrations, and modernization initiatives. · Define and document requirements for actuarial data usage in AI/ML workflows, including data quality, lineage, and governance considerations. · Work closely with QA and validation teams to define test scenarios, acceptance criteria and evaluation measures for AI outputs, and validate system results against actuarial expectations — including deciding what "good enough" means where an AI output has no single correct answer. · Serve as a strategic bridge between business outcomes and AI-enabled technical execution, ensuring clarity and alignment throughout delivery. · Research, evaluate, and propose AI approaches for actuarial modeling, risk assessment, process optimization, and analytics. · Gather and document requirements for operational intelligence and L2/L3 support automation — application and asset discovery, incident and batch job correlation, infrastructure health, and application cost visibility. · Write and maintain epics, stories, acceptance criteria and traceability in Jira so that delivery, quality and audit evidence all draw on the same requirements. · Support change management, documentation, and stakeholder communication related to AI adoption within actuarial functions. · Promote responsible AI usage, explainability, and transparency in actuarial systems and analytics. Qualifications · Bachelor's degree in Business, Information Systems, Mathematics, Actuarial Science, Computer Science, or a related field. · Proven experience as a Business Analyst in an IT, actuarial, data, or analytics-focused environment. · Strong understanding of Life, Health, Retirement and Annuity insurance products and actuarial processes. · Demonstrated ability to translate business and actuarial needs into AI-aware functional and technical requirements. · Familiarity with data-driven systems, data integrations, and analytics platforms. · Working knowledge of data analysis tools such as SQL, Excel, and BI platforms. · Strong analytical thinking, problem-solving, and documentation skills. · Excellent communication skills in English, with the ability to engage effectively with actuarial, technical, and business stakeholders across a distributed US and European team. Preferred Qualifications · Hands-on experience or strong conceptual understanding of AI and machine learning, including predictive modeling, NLP, or decision intelligence. · Experience supporting or partnering on AI-enabled initiatives within actuarial, finance, or insurance domains. · Technical background in software development, data engineering, or analytics. · Exposure to actuarial or financial data platforms and large-scale data environments. · Experience with automation, data modernization, or digital transformation initiatives. · Familiarity with Agile or hybrid delivery methodologies. · Working familiarity with how agentic AI systems are built and constrained — tools and integrations, retrieval and grounding, memory, human approval steps, and evaluation — and with what that changes about writing requirements for them. · Experience in insurance or financial services organizations.

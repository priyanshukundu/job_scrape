What You'll Do: The position sits at the center of the Software Engineering organization, serving as connective tissue between engineering teams and the rest of the enterprise. This individual works closely with the Director, Software Engineering Delivery & Execution, and directly with Executive Directors, managing work intake and cross-functional coordination, executive reporting, and governance tracking. This individual will be responsible for initiatives requiring coordination across Software Engineering teams related to technology implementations, organizational transformation, and executive communications.

Associate Compliance Officer

At Aristocrat, we are committed to bringing happiness to life through the power of play! As an Associate Compliance Officer, you will join a dynamic team dedicated to ensuring our products meet the highest regulatory standards. This is an outstanding opportunity to embark on a career in product compliance, where you will support the approval process for our innovative technologies and platforms. With a strong focus on collaboration and quality, you will play a meaningful role in maintaining our reputation for excellence. What You'll Do Collect, check, and maintain approval letters, certification evidence, submission records, and correspondence in approved systems. Assemble regulatory and independent testing laboratory submission packages using approved templates, checklists, and mentorship. Update assigned trackers with confirmed status, dates, outstanding inputs, and next actions using source records. Perform trained, controlled system and record updates using approved evidence and prescribed validation steps. Locate specified regulations, technical standards, and approval conditions using designated sources, and document sources and dates. Request missing inputs, communicate confirmed internal status, and detail unresolved questions. Perform prescribed quality checks, submit work for review, and address feedback promptly. Complete assigned training, apply feedback, and build product and jurisdiction knowledge through progressively broader tasks. What We're Looking For No prior relevant experience required, typically suited to candidates with 0+ months of relevant experience. Ability to follow documented procedures, coordinate detailed information, compare records against source evidence, and identify discrepancies. Clear written and verbal communication, including the ability to ask questions and convey factual status. Ability to use standard office applications and learn controlled business systems, document repositories, and tracking tools. Willingness to ask questions, recognize uncertainty, adhere to directions, and seek mentorship promptly. Ability to manage assigned deadlines and priorities, work collaboratively, receive feedback, and apply learning consistently. Ability to handle confidential information, follow access and record controls, and acquire and maintain occupational or regulatory registrations where applicable. Exposure through coursework, training, internships, or employment to compliance, regulated products, quality assurance, technical documentation, or evidence-based research is a plus. Familiarity with product certification, regulatory submissions, approval records, or gaming-product terminology is advantageous.

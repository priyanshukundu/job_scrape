Key Responsibilities: The CAO group is responsible for supporting the senior management of the Risk department in managing the department. This role will report to the Department COO locally, and the Global CAO (currently based in Japan). The role is part of small team, with support from office managers/administration personnel. The junior CAO will support the department with: Departmental Financial Management: Departmental budgeting, forecasting, budget to actuals analysis and management reporting Management accounting, primarily business and project cost allocations Oversight / review of financial accounting and associated controls, including accruals, expenses, T&E Coordination of personnel related matters with senior leaders across the department and Corporate HR Headcount management - hire approvals, headcount tracking and reporting Development of departmental wide training programs Advancement, performance management and compensation Diversity and Inclusion initiatives Intern and graduate hire programs Consultants and temporary staff contracting and management Communications: Responsible for all departmental communications (townhalls, intranet, notices, newsletters, etc) Coordinate departmental management meetings (set agenda, materials etc) Prepare presentations and reports for senior management and boards Coordination of departmental events General: Coordinate with Sourcing and Legal departments as required Business continuity plan ownership and testing Office management and general administration There will also be growth opportunities to engage in other areas as responsibility for the COO. Required Qualifications: Qualifications: Required: bachelors in relevant subject or suitable qualifying experience Knowledge: Proficient with Microsoft suite, notably excel and powerpoint Preferred: Experience / Understanding of Risk Management departmental responsibilities Preferred: Working knowledge of financial and management accounting Preferred: Work experience in financial services, ideally in a similar role Associate requirements: At least three years of work experience in financial services, ideally in a similar role Skills: Analytical Excellent written and verbal communication skills Ability to multi-task, prioritize multiple projects, and manage strict deadlines Good stakeholder management skills - ability to develop and maintain strong and mutually supportive working relationships both inside and outside of immediate team and the Risk Management department.

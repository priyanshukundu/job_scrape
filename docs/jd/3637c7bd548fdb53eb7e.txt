Infrastructure is our product, and we operate it at a massive scale to power the most demanding workloads. About the role As a Junior Software Engineer (IC1/IC2) on the Inference team, you’ll own a single production service end-to-end shipping features, maintaining dashboards/alarms, and improving operational excellence. You’ll collaborate with peers across runtime, scheduling, and networking to move key SLIs. What you’ll do Own one model-serving service end-to-end: design, implementation, tests, deployment, and ops. Add and tune observability (dashboards, alerts, traces); automate runbook steps to reduce toil. Improve latency/throughput via micro-batching, concurrency tuning, and caching. Participate in on-call; lead post-incident action items for your service. Review peers’ PRs and contribute design feedback in team reviews. What we’re looking for 0–3 years of software engineering experience (or equivalent) building cloud/distributed services in production. Proficiency in Python or Go; familiarity with gRPC/HTTP APIs and containerized deployments. Working knowledge of Kubernetes (or Slurm/Ray) and CI/CD pipelines. Experience with metrics/alerting (Prometheus/Grafana/OpenTelemetry) and structured logging. Basic understanding of GPU inference concepts (KV caching, mixed precision, streaming tokens). Nice to have Experience with inference frameworks (Triton, vLLM, TensorRT-LLM, Ray Serve, TorchServe). Hands-on performance profiling/benchmarking (e.g., wrk, vegeta, perf, Nsight). Contributions to reliability practices: SLI/SLOs or postmortem improvements. Why CoreWeave Help shape an industry-defining inference platform that enables teams to deploy generative AI and real-time applications at scale. If optimizing tail latency and delivering reliable model serving excites you, this is the place to build.

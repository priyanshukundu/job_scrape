Job Description Visa's Data Platforms organization is responsible for the foundational data ecosystem that powers some of the company's most critical business capabilities. Our teams manage the large-scale platforms that support authorization, clearing, settlement, and other core payment data domains, while enabling the next generation of data products, analytics, and business insights across Visa. As a Senior Data Engineer (Sr. Consultant internal title at Visa), you will help design and build platforms that process and manage data at petabyte scale , ensuring it is accessible, reliable, secure, and optimized for a wide range of internal and customer-facing applications. You will work with distributed data systems, modern data engineering technologies, and cloud-scale architectures to solve highly complex data challenges that directly support Visa's global operations. This role sits within a cross-functional Agile team responsible for developing scalable data solutions, modernizing data delivery capabilities, and advancing Visa's Master Data Management and Data Platform initiatives. You will play a key role in driving technical excellence, influencing platform architecture, mentoring engineers, and delivering solutions that enable teams across Visa to discover, access, and leverage data more effectively. All roles require digital fluency, including the ability to leverage emerging technologies such as Generative AI tools (e.g., Microsoft Copilot, ChatGPT, and LLM-powered engineering solutions) to improve productivity, innovation, and software delivery practices. Key Responsibilities Partner with product managers, architects, and business stakeholders to define requirements and translate business needs into scalable data platform solutions. Design, develop, and enhance distributed data processing systems that support Visa's critical payment, settlement, authorization, and analytics workloads. Build and maintain high-performance, reliable data pipelines that process and deliver large volumes of data across multiple platforms, products, and business domains. Contribute to the evolution of Visa's data platform architecture, data delivery capabilities, and Master Data Management solutions. Lead technical design discussions, architecture reviews, and engineering decision-making for complex initiatives. Develop clean, scalable, maintainable, and reusable code that adheres to engineering best practices and platform standards. Drive improvements in system reliability, observability, automation, performance, and operational excellence. Lead code reviews and mentor fellow engineers, fostering a culture of technical excellence and continuous learning. Troubleshoot and resolve complex production issues while identifying opportunities to automate manual processes and reduce operational risk. Develop comprehensive testing strategies and quality assurance practices to ensure robust and reliable data solutions. Collaborate closely with engineers, data scientists, architects, product managers, and platform teams to deliver business-critical capabilities. Identify, analyze, and proactively address data quality, performance, scalability, governance, and security challenges. Contribute to project planning, estimation, and delivery while balancing business priorities, technical debt, and long-term platform health.

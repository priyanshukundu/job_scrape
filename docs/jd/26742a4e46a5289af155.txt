Conducts financial research and analysis using established methods and procedures to support accurate financial data for use in determining, tracking, and realizing overall bank financial objectives. Supports the monthly analysis and annual budgeting process. Essential Functions Prepares complex financial reports and analysis which support financial reporting activities, including segment analysis, budget development, and profitability related trend analysis. Supports the Lead Analyst in the monthly review and analysis process to ensure accuracy of reported information and timely resolution of budget to actual variances. Supports the Monthly Adjusted Data process by validating loan and leasing data from source systems to ensure completeness, accuracy, and reliability for internal and external commitment exposure reporting. Performs pre-month and month-end reconciliations, researches and resolves data discrepancies across systems, and communicates regularly with loan operations and leasing teams to address potential downstream reporting issues and meet established reporting deadlines. Supports the preparation and maintenance of information used by divisions in evaluating the state of the business. Proactively identifies key metrics and indicators of business trends. Develops ad-hoc reporting/analysis as needed based upon division and senior management needs. Provides technical research and analysis to provide definitive financial data and advice for use in determining, tracking and realizing overall bank financial objectives. Provides management with timely and accurate profitability accounting reports and project reports, applying allocation methodologies as appropriate. Performs standard monthly business processes including maintenance of desk procedures. Maintains knowledge of industry conditions and trends that could impact the bank. Participates in special projects, and performs other duties as assigned. CoBank is a cooperative bank serving vital industries across rural America. The bank provides loans, leases, export financing and other financial services to agribusinesses and rural power, water and communications providers in all 50 states. The bank also provides wholesale loans and other financial services to affiliated Farm Credit associations serving more than 76,000 farmers, ranchers and other rural borrowers in 23 states around the country. CoBank is a member of the Farm Credit System, a nationwide network of banks and retail lending associations chartered to support the borrowing needs of U.S. agriculture, rural infrastructure and rural communities. Headquartered outside Denver, Colorado, CoBank serves customers from regional banking centers across the U.S. and also maintains an international representative office in Singapore.

The application window is expected to close on: 12/30/2026 Meet the Team We are a collaborative group of data scientists, engineers, and AI specialists dedicated to designing, building, and deploying cutting-edge machine learning solutions. Our focus spans from traditional predictive modeling to advanced Generative AI and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) applications. We partner closely with product managers and enterprise architects to transform complex business challenges into scalable, production-grade AI solutions that drive measurable impact. Your Impact As a Senior Data Scientist, you will build end-to-end AI and ML solutions, taking ownership from data preparation and model development through deployment, monitoring, and continuous improvement. You will design and operationalize machine learning models using supervised, unsupervised, deep learning, and NLP techniques. Additionally, you will engineer scalable ML pipelines, implement robust MLOps practices for model lifecycle management, and develop enterprise-grade Generative AI and RAG applications. Your work will directly influence our AI strategy, ensuring models are accurate, reliable, and seamlessly integrated into production environments. Minimum Qualifications Bachelor’s or master’s degree in Computer Science, Data Science, Statistics, Mathematics, Engineering, or a related discipline. 10+ years of professional experience in data science, machine learning, or applied AI, with extensive hands-on experience building, validating, and deploying machine learning models in production. Advanced programming skills in Python and SQL, with practical experience using ML frameworks such as scikit-learn, PyTorch, TensorFlow, XGBoost, or LightGBM. 6+ years of proven experience developing automated, scalable ML training and inference pipelines using tools like Airflow, Kubeflow, MLflow, or Databricks. 2+ yars of experience hands-on experience developing Generative AI and RAG solutions, including working with Large Language Models (LLMs), embeddings, vector databases, and frameworks like LangChain. Preferred Qualifications Strong practical experience with MLOps, model lifecycle management, CI/CD, model monitoring, and automated retraining using Git, Docker, and Kubernetes. Experience developing REST APIs and model-serving applications using FastAPI or equivalent frameworks. Experience deploying high-volume, business-critical ML and GenAI workloads on cloud platforms such as Azure, AWS, or Google Cloud. Knowledge of advanced GenAI techniques, including agentic AI, tool calling, multi-agent orchestration, and contextual retrieval. Experience implementing responsible AI practices, model governance, explainability, fairness, privacy, and security controls throughout the ML lifecycle. Why Cisco?

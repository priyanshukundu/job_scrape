What You'll Do In this role, you will play a key part in advancing business priorities by delivering high-impact work across your area of expertise. Calibration is fundamental to the accuracy, stability and productivity of semiconductor inspection and metrology systems. KLA is seeking a Senior Applied Scientist to rethink how electron-optical systems are calibrated and recalibrated—using machine learning, mathematical and statistical modeling, physics-informed AI and large-scale engineering data. You will develop next-generation calibration solutions that reduce calibration time, improve accuracy and repeatability, compensate for drift, strengthen tool-to-tool matching, and enable more intelligent diagnostics and recovery. This role combines advanced modeling with real experimental systems: you will develop algorithms, validate them against hardware data and work with engineering teams to integrate successful solutions into products. The underlying mission and scope come directly from the team’s need to modernize calibration, diagnostics and optimization across electron sources, electron-optical columns, steering systems, detectors, alignment, focus, high-voltage, and vacuum-related performance. What You Will Do Redesign calibration and recalibration workflows to improve speed, accuracy, repeatability, robustness, and system productivity. Develop and validate calibration algorithms for electron sources, electron-optical columns, deflectors and beam-steering systems, detectors, alignment and focus functions, and performance compensation associated with high-voltage and vacuum behavior. Apply machine learning, statistical modeling and physics-informed AI to predict calibration outcomes, model system behavior, and improve calibration decisions. Build methods to detect drift, anomalies and performance excursions using engineering, operational and fleet-level data. Optimize calibration sequences to reduce time, eliminate unnecessary steps, and balance accuracy, throughput, and system risk. Develop adaptive and self-correcting approaches that respond to changing system conditions and support more autonomous calibration behavior. Create intelligent diagnostic and recovery methods that accelerate root-cause identification and restore system performance after calibration or hardware excursions. Build and combine physics-based and data-driven models, including correlation models, response models, sensitivity studies, uncertainty estimates and error-propagation analyses. Analyze large, heterogeneous engineering datasets to identify performance patterns, tool-to-tool differences, failure modes, and opportunities for improvement. Design and execute experiments to evaluate calibration repeatability, reproducibility, robustness, and generalization across operating conditions. Correlate model predictions with experimental measurements and use discrepancies to refine models, algorithms, and calibration strategies. Partner with electron optics, systems, software, algorithm, manufacturing, and service teams to translate system requirements into deployable calibration and monitoring solutions. Support the full path from concept through algorithm implementation, experimental validation, product qualification, and production deployment. Continuously evaluate advances in scientific machine learning, optimization, anomaly detection, surrogate modeling, uncertainty quantification, and intelligent control.

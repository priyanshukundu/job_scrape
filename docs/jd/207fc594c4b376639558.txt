About the Role Carta is building the end-to-end ERP platform for private markets, and our relationships with PE and private credit firms are at the center of that strategy. This is a new role, created to deepen those relationships through a differentiated value-add: access to senior finance talent. This person is part talent connector, part relationship builder, and part BD engine. You'll come with a rolodex of emerging CFOs, Fund Controllers, and senior Fund Accountants in the PE/private credit space and you'll use that network to make Carta an indispensable partner to our clients. Think of how a16z built their talent marketplace: not as a recruiting firm, but as a strategic resource that makes their portfolio companies better and stickier. This role is as much BD as recruiting. You're not just filling open reqs, you're using talent intelligence as a lever to deepen client trust, open new conversations, and create loyalty that translates to retained and expanded business. What You'll Do Client Talent Partnerships Serve as a trusted talent resource for Carta's PE and private credit fund clients, specifically focused on senior finance roles: emerging CFOs, Fund Controllers, and senior Fund Accountants Proactively build and maintain a curated bench of high-quality finance talent operating in the PE/private credit ecosystem Match talent to clients opportunistically, not as a placement agency, but as a value-add that makes Carta indispensable Business Development Use talent intelligence as a relationship lever: knowing that Client A needs X talent, you introduce the right person, generating loyalty and new business conversations Partner closely with the PE sales team to activate talent relationships as part of broader account strategy Identify and attend senior finance events: CFO symposiums, private markets conferences, fund accounting forums, representing Carta and building pipeline of both talent and client relationships Talent Pipeline & Network Recruit and maintain relationships with passive senior finance talent who may not be actively looking but are open to new opportunities, at funds, fund admins, and portfolio companies Create a feedback loop where talent who moves from Carta's fund admin team to client firms is embraced, not lost, modeling the consulting firm approach of welcoming alumni movement as a long-term relationship Map talent across key targets: other fund admins and PE firms we're focused on Cross-Functional Collaboration Partner with Carta's Talent Acquisition team to avoid conflicts between client-facing talent activity and Carta's own hiring needs;

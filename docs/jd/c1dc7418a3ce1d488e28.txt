Start your Voyage – Apply Now Get to Know the Opportunity: This role is part of the Voya Investment Management Entry Level Analyst Program beginning in July 2027. The program is designed to help entry-level employees launch their investment careers through hands-on experience, on-the-job development, and training. The Analyst will join the Leveraged Credit/Multi-Sector Below Investment Grade team and support Portfolio Management through credit underwriting, trading activities, portfolio analytics, and reporting. This role is in our Atlanta office, with expected onsite presence on average up to 4 days per week to support team and business priorities. The Contributions You’ll Make: Support credit analysts with credit underwriting activities, with the opportunity to progress toward independently underwriting credits. Coordinate with Portfolio Managers, Traders, and Analysts on action items and trades across investment mandates and assist with order management. Support Portfolio Managers and Traders with order management and trade processing to help ensure accounts are invested at appropriate targets and reflect desired positioning across sectors and issuers. Assist with developing automated processes to improve the efficiency of order management, trade processing, and related activities. Communicate with sell-side counterparties regarding trading activities. Track bond new issues, including documenting ongoing decisions and communicating with underwriters. Assist with developing and maintaining portfolio and analyst dashboards across High Yield mandates to support portfolio construction and risk management. Support Analysts with sector review reporting and data needs. Partner with the Middle Office on settlements and cash management matters, the DIG group on data-related issues, particularly security and portfolio analytics data, and Compliance as appropriate. Prepare weekly, monthly, and quarterly reporting for internal executive committees, Compliance, counterparties, and client presentations. Provide ad hoc reporting and other support to Portfolio Managers, Traders, and Analysts as needed. Minimum Knowledge & Experience: Applicants should be graduating with an undergraduate degree in December 2026 or spring/summer 2027. Bachelor's degree in business, finance, or a related field. High school dipolma or GED. Strong intellectual curiosity and research orientation. Comfort working with and analyzing financial statements / financial information. Strong Excel skills. Comfort with technology, systems, and analytical tools. Familiarity or experience with AI-enabled tools. Fundamental understanding of accounting principles. Strong analytical and data skills. Excellent communication skills. Ability to work effectively as a team player with a positive attitude and eagerness to learn in a supporting role. Passion for building a career in financial services and investments. Preferred Knowledge & Experience: Willingness to pursue the Chartered Financial Analyst (CFA) designation. Exposure to Bloomberg is beneficial. How This Role Aligns to Our Core Four: Voya’s Core Four are at the heart of how we work, and this role brings them to life through thoughtful investment analysis, collaboration, disciplined execution, and continuous improvement. Instill Customer Obsession: Support portfolio management, analysis, and reporting that contribute to thoughtful investment decisions and effective management of client portfolios. Transform with Automation/AI: Help develop automated processes and portfolio and analyst dashboards that improve efficiency and support stronger analysis and decision-making. Drive Higher Performance Through Our People: Collaborate closely with Portfolio Managers, Traders, Analysts, Middle Office, DIG, Compliance, and other partners to execute investment activities and support shared priorities.

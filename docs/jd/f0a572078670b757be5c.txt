Grow Your Skills, Grow Your Potential Responsibilities Are you a software engineer who leads by example, inspires others, and enjoys helping teams succeed? State Farm's Billing and Payments Area is seeking two Lead Software Engineers to support our State Farm Payment Plan (SFPP) team. This opportunity is ideal for engineers who combine strong technical expertise with the ability to build trust, mentor others, influence decisions, and foster a positive team environment. You'll help support and modernize business-critical payment systems, including exploring how Artificial Intelligence (AI) can accelerate the migration of mainframe capabilities to modern web technologies. Strong web engineering skills are essential, while mainframe experience is a plus. Key Responsibilities Leadership & Team Development Lead by example, demonstrating accountability, integrity, professionalism, and a collaborative mindset. Mentor and coach engineers, encouraging professional growth, knowledge sharing, and continuous improvement. Build trust, foster an inclusive team environment, and navigate challenges or conflicts constructively. Influence decisions, encourage innovative thinking, and help teammates achieve shared goals. Collaboration & Business Impact Partner with engineers, business stakeholders, and technology leaders to deliver reliable, customer-focused solutions. Communicate technical concepts clearly, balance competing priorities, and help guide team direction. Champion modernization, operational excellence, and improvements to the customer and user experience. Technical Delivery & Innovation Design, develop, test, deploy, and support full-stack web applications, APIs, and integrations with mainframe systems. Develop solutions using Java, Spring Boot, JavaScript, and modern frontend technologies. Support modernization initiatives involving cloud platforms, containers, and AI-assisted development tools. Troubleshoot complex issues while promoting security, reliability, automation, and engineering best practices.

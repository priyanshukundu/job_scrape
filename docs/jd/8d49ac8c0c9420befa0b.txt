UAS Ground Control Systems Software Engineer The Opportunity: Build the bridge between end users and autonomous systems through the development and implementation of Command and Control (C2) applications and mission software for autonomous Uncrewed Aerial Systems (UAS). Research, development, integrate, and deployment software for the Ground Control Station (GCS) across desktop and mobile platforms. Collaborate with autonomy developers and to provide the human-computer interface across a range of DoW missions and objectives. Apply advanced engineering skills, extensive technical expertise, and full industry knowledge. Develop innovative solutions to complex problems. Work without considerable direction, and mentor and supervise team members. Join us. The world can't wait. You Have: 3+ years of experience as a software developer working on Linux-based embedded or edge systems 3+ years of experience with extensible software development, including plugins, extensions, and modular design patterns 1+ years of experience developing applications using QGroundControl Experience with software package management and configuration management tools, such as Conan and Nix Knowledge of the UAS domain including mission planning or vehicle management Ability to obtain a Secret clearance Bachelor’s degree in a Software Engineering, Computer Science, Computer Engineering, Electrical Engineering, or Robotics field Nice If You Have: Experience with Tactical Assault Kit (TAK) software development Experience designing and developing user-interface workflows Experience developing software following DevOps principles, including Agile, CI/CD, test-driven development, and using automation tools, including pipelines and runners Master’s degree in a Software Engineering, Computer Engineering, Electrical Engineering, or Robotics field preferred; Doctorate degree in a Software Engineering, Computer Engineering, Electrical Engineering, or Robotics field a plus Clearance: Applicants selected will be subject to a security investigation and may need to meet eligibility requirements for access to classified information.

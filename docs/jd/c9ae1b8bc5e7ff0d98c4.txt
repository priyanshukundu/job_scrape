To make electronics, you need chips, wafers, transistors, reticles, and... To make these, you must see, test and manufacture them at scale—faster and better than ever before. Group/Division The Semiconductor Products and Customers (Semi PC) business unit designs, builds and sells KLA’s product portfolio to help chip manufacturers meet high-quality standards for technologies like AI, data centers, automotive and electronic devices. Our inspection, metrology, and analytics systems detect and monitor defects across logic and memory chips, specialty semiconductors, wafers, reticles, advanced packaging, process equipment and materials. We also develop technologies for specialty processes, IC substrates, and PCB manufacturing, including etch and deposition, component inspection, and imaging and analytics. The Measurement, Analytics and Control (MACH) division is KLA’s software powerhouse, responsible for developing high-performance software platforms that support semiconductor manufacturing. MACH delivers cutting-edge technologies in patterning analytics, modeling, and data science—empowering chipmakers to ramp next-gen devices faster and more efficiently. Our cross-functional team of research scientists, software engineers, apps engineers and process engineers are solving the industry’s most complex technical problems. What You'll Do In this role, you will play a key part in advancing business priorities by delivering high-impact work across your area of expertise. Position Overview KLA is seeking a highly motivated Software AI Engineer to develop next-generation artificial intelligence and machine learning solutions supporting semiconductor process control, inspection, and metrology systems. The successful candidate will work at the intersection of AI, computer vision, algorithm development, and high-performance software engineering to deliver scalable solutions that drive product innovation and customer value. Key Responsibilities Design, develop, and deploy AI/ML algorithms for semiconductor inspection, metrology, and process control applications. Build and optimize machine learning models using large-scale engineering and manufacturing datasets. Develop software systems supporting model training, validation, deployment, and inference workflows. Collaborate with algorithm engineers, software engineers, application engineers, and product teams to integrate AI capabilities into production systems. Evaluate emerging AI technologies and develop proof-of-concept solutions for new product capabilities. Improve performance and scalability through optimization techniques including parallel computing, GPU acceleration, and efficient software architectures. Contribute to cloud-enabled and edge-computing AI solutions supporting semiconductor equipment platforms. Drive technical innovation while maintaining software quality, reliability, and maintainability. Required Qualifications MS in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Applied Mathematics, Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, or a related field. Strong programming experience in Python and C++. Experience developing and deploying machine learning or deep learning solutions. Solid understanding of data structures, algorithms, software engineering principles, and object-oriented design. Experience working in Linux development environments. Strong analytical and problem-solving skills. Preferred Qualifications Experience with computer vision, image processing, computational imaging, or machine vision systems. Experience with deep learning frameworks such as TensorFlow or PyTorch. GPU programming experience using CUDA or other acceleration technologies. Experience with semiconductor manufacturing, metrology, inspection, or process control applications. Exposure to MLOps, cloud computing, distributed systems, or large-scale data platforms. Experience productizing AI solutions in a commercial software environment.

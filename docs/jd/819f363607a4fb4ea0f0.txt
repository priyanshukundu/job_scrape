Job Summary We are seeking a highly motivated individual to join the Financial Planning & Analysis (FP&A) team as a Manager, Financial Planning & Analysis. In this role, you will own the financial planning, forecasting, and reporting for the Derivatives category, the largest category at Cboe and a high-visibility area of the firm. You will be responsible for the full Derivatives P&L, including revenue and expenses for our Futures and Options segments, partnering closely with business leaders to help them understand financial performance month to month and quarter to quarter. You will report to the Senior Director, Financial Planning & Analysis. As a leader of the team, you will supervise one Senior Analyst and work closely with finance and business teams across the organization. Key Responsibilities Own the Derivatives category P&L, covering revenue and expenses for both Futures and Options. Lead the annual budget and quarterly forecast process for Derivatives, partnering with business leaders across functions to forecast revenue and operating expenses Research and explain variances between forecast and actuals, as well as between current and prior forecast versions, and communicate the key drivers to leadership Provide strategic decision support for Derivatives growth projects, including revenue forecasting, pricing analysis, and KPI data integrity and reporting Analyze current and future operational performance trends and prepare quarterly business review presentations for senior management Prepare monthly volume and revenue per contract (RPC) files that are included in Cboe’s monthly volume release Assist with the firm wide expenses categories, partnering with department leaders across the firm Assist with preparation of subsidiary reports and the financial dashboards Assist with the forecast of non-operating income and the guidance associated with it Prepare board materials and support the monthly close process Lead, develop, and mentor a Senior Analyst, providing continuous guidance, support, and performance feedback Serve as a subject matter expert in the finance systems and drive process improvements across the FP&A team Lead and execute ad-hoc projects Required Qualifications Bachelor’s degree in Finance or Accounting; MBA a plus Five to 10 years of relevant experience in financial analysis, FP&A, or accounting, including prior management experience Experience in the derivatives / exchange industry highly preferred.

Food Safety, Quality, & Regulatory (FSQR) means supporting processes that help Target succeed by consistently delivering safe, high quality and compliant food and beverages. As an Associate FSQR (Food Safety, Quality and Regulatory) Scientist you will ensure that Target Owned Brand Food labeling meets relevant regulations and internal Target standards. You will have accountability for specific products and/or categories and will contribute to projects to advance labeling processes and systems, and labeling and claims guidance. This role as an Associate Scientist will be responsible for interpreting compliance & labeling regulations. The role will rely on mentorship from Sr/Lead/Principal FSQR Scientists in order grow their knowledge in these regulations across food & beverage products. Ideal candidates will have a general understanding of food labeling requirements and some experience with food regulatory compliance and labeling. Review and approve product specification and labeling information (nutrition facts, ingredient declarations, allergens, and claims) and packaging proofs/key-lines for assigned products or categories. Communicate labeling requirements and necessary changes to internal colleagues and external vendor partners. Review and approve product digital copy and marketing promotions. Collaborate with senior FSQR scientists/ colleagues, cross-functional teams, or vendors to resolve labeling questions or discrepancies. Complete competitive assessments, regulatory or nutrition reviews, or other assignments to support project strategies and labeling and regulatory claims guidance. Contribute to the improvement of internal FSQR programs, processes, and systems through project participation. Develop and maintain regulatory, labeling, nutrition, and marketplace knowledge for assigned product areas. Maintain Target labeling guides and related documents. Develop relationships with internal and external partners through active engagement and effective communication to share knowledge and gather perspectives. Deal constructively with challenges, accept change and constructive feedback, and seek opportunities to acquire new knowledge and skills. Stay focused on have a growth mindset. Use Best Methods, resources and available data to help identify process issues and support remedial action needed.

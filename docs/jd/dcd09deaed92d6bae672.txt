Lead Software Engineer, Plan Intelligence We are Lennar Lennar is one of the nation's leading homebuilders, dedicated to making an impact and creating an extraordinary experience for their Homeowners, Communities, and Associates by building quality homes and providing exceptional customer service, giving back to the communities in which we work and live in, and fostering a culture of opportunity and growth for our Associates throughout their career. Lennar has been recognized as a Fortune 500® company and consistently ranked among the top homebuilders in the United States. A Career that Empowers You to Build Your Future We are seeking a talented Lead Software Engineer to join our team. This role involves designing, developing, testing, and deploying software applications. You will collaborate with cross-functional teams to understand business requirements and translate them into technical solutions. Your Responsibilities on the Team Collaborate with project stakeholders (product owners, UX researchers, data team, QA team, project managers, cross team leads and designers) to understand functional and non-functional requirements. Promote a collaborative team environment and work closely with colleagues to achieve business objectives. Responsible for planning work estimation and forecasting. Adhere to architecture, design, implementation, security standards and best practices. Investigate and propose solutions to development and design problems. Conduct analysis to determine integration needs, and design and plan integrations. Prepare technical documentation, including software design evaluation plans and test results. Write high-quality code that satisfies customer needs and strives for simplicity, clarity, and testability. Implement unit and integration tests and conduct functional and performance testing where appropriate. Participate in code reviews or use collaborative programming techniques to promote high quality business outcomes. Develop reusable software building blocks to enable faster delivery. Improve performance of existing software by diagnosing and resolving critical issues. Establish development practices and processes. Manage and mentor software engineers Lead the design and delivery of Plan Intelligence, an internal analytics platform for plan portfolio analysis, market insights, consumer segments, and EI packages used by division and product teams. Own features end to end across a server-rendered React and TypeScript front end and a Python FastAPI service backed by PostgreSQL on AWS. Partner with data engineering on the pipelines that feed plan, geospatial, and margin data into the platform, and help define the contracts between them. Build and tune interactive mapping, charting, and export experiences over large plan and community datasets. Extend LLM-assisted features built on AWS Bedrock, keeping output quality, latency, and cost within agreed limits. Support the platform across development, UAT, and production environments on AWS, using application monitoring and product analytics to find and resolve issues.

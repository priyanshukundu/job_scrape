Job Title: Senior Incentive Business Analyst Location: CityScape What you'll do: As a Senior Incentives Business Analyst you'll participate in administering the Incentive Compensation Plans for the Bank, including design and modeling, eligibility management, payment calculations, governance, providing process/system mapping and design focusing on quality improvement, data integrity and transparency, process improvement, integration of new systems with legacies, and long-term projects. Other duties include championing and deploying business and process improvement methodologies and toolsets across the organization for all Incentive Compensation Plans, building capability through knowledge sharing and coaching, building the Business Process Improvement Team’s credibility by engaging effectively with stakeholders, deploying appropriate methodologies, and toolsets and create and maintaining a reputation as the internal consultant of choice by senior management for any problem solving or design initiatives. Responsible for supporting leadership in the monitoring of eligibility of plan participations and ensure compliance with both plan provisions and government requirements. Provide guidance and counsel to employees, HRIS representatives on the requirements and provisions of programs and assist with the resolution of potential problems including hand off to vendors. Monitor the administration of incentives compliance to support the Western Alliance incentive plans. Utilize workflow and process mapping to understand the organization and its operations. Create and update project work plans, completing project activities and communicating progress/outstanding issues and risks, and coordinate and serve as a Subject Matter expert. Document and monitor business processes, business rules, metrics and standard operating procedures as a result of improvement initiatives. Identify improvement opportunities and help manage improvement requests from the business leaders. Present findings and recommendations to sponsors and key stakeholders. Coach and support project team members in use of tools and methodologies. Facilitate and provide specialist process improvement advice to business units to identify and resolve specific issues that are critically impacting business outcomes and require urgent resolution. Research current trends and regulatory/legislated requirements. Compile, analyze, and report data including employee, total rewards strategic planning and evaluation, best practices, and benchmark surveys. What you'll need: 5+ years of related experience; 3+ years of experience in Incentive Compensation or similar field. Bachelor's degree in related field required. Intermediate knowledge of applicable regulatory and legal compliance obligations, rules and regulations, industry standards and practices. Prior experience with Workday strongly preferred. Strong interpersonal and partnering skills to facilitate effective working relationships. Ability to take initiative, drive project work and complete work within established deadlines. Proficient with Microsoft Office products, with advanced working fluency in Excel, SQL, VBA, or Visio. Previous Varicent incentive compensation platform experience strongly preferred. Advanced speaking and writing communication skills. Certified Compensation Professional or World at Work Coursework preferred. Benefits you’ll love: We offer all the important things you'd want — like competitive salaries, an ownership stake in the company, medical and dental insurance, time off, a great 401k matching program, tuition assistance program, an employee volunteer program, and a wellness program. In addition, you’ll have the opportunity to bolster your business knowledge, learning the ins and outs of how successful companies operate and manage their finances, giving you invaluable hands-on experience to help grow your career!

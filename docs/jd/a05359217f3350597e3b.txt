Job Description: The Special Assets Group (SAG) manages the Bank's criticized, classified, and distressed commercial credit portfolio. SAG's primary objective is to maximize recoveries, minimize loss severity, and execute effective resolution strategies that protect the Bank's interests while preserving long-term enterprise value where appropriate. The SAG Sr. Credit Analyst serves as a key analytical partner to SAG Credit Officers in the management of assigned Global Corporate & Investment Banking / Global Markets credit relationships. The analyst performs comprehensive financial, credit, and portfolio analysis to assess borrower performance, identify emerging risks, develop recommendations, and inform the development and execution of resolution strategies. Responsibilities: Perform complex financial, credit, collateral, and risk analyses for an assigned portfolio of criticized, classified, and distressed commercial credit relationships. Evaluate borrower financial performance, liquidity, cash flow, collateral coverage, repayment capacity, and industry trends to assess credit quality and identify emerging risks. Analyze and recommend internal risk ratings consistent with applicable credit policies, standards, and procedures. Prepare and present detailed borrower reviews, covenant analyses, portfolio assessments, and credit memos to support decision-making. Develop recommendations to support credit resolution strategies, restructuring alternatives, repayment plans, and recovery initiatives. Partner with SAG Portfolio Officers to evaluate strategic options and support the execution of resolution plans. Participate in client meetings, portfolio reviews, lender discussions, and strategy sessions to assess risk and support resolution strategies. Serve as a key liaison with Credit Risk, Legal, Operations, and other business partners to support portfolio management and credit resolution efforts Support special projects, process improvement initiatives, regulatory requests, and business priorities that enhance SAG performance and effectiveness. Provide guidance and informal mentorship to junior analysts as appropriate. Required Qualifications: 3+ years of experience in commercial banking, credit underwriting, portfolio management, workout, restructuring, or a related discipline. Strong knowledge of commercial lending, financial statement analysis, credit risk assessment, and commercial credit structures. Experience analyzing commercial borrowers and preparing credit approval, underwriting, or portfolio management materials. Strong analytical and critical thinking skills with the ability to identify key risks, assess alternatives, and develop recommendations. Demonstrated ability to work independently and exercise sound judgment in support of credit and portfolio management activities. Desired Qualifications: Experience supporting or participating in corporate workouts, restructurings, or special situations, including amendments, forbearance, covenant resets, and recovery strategies for criticized or distressed credits. Exposure to syndicated and agented credit transactions, including coordination with multiple lenders, agents, and advisors, and familiarity with bank group dynamics and decision processes. Advanced proficiency in Microsoft Excel, including financial modeling, scenario analysis, and cash flow forecasting. Understanding of commercial banking regulations, credit policies, risk frameworks, and governance requirements. Strong relationship management skills with the ability to influence and collaborate across multiple business functions.

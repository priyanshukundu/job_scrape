About Goldman Sachs Goldman Sachs Asset Management is one of the world’s leading asset managers with over $3 trillion in assets under supervision. As the primary investment area within Goldman Sachs, we provide investment and advisory services for pension plans, sovereign wealth funds, insurance companies, endowments, foundations, financial advisors, and individuals. About ACF The Alternatives Capital Formation (ACF) business manages capital raising, client servicing and strategy for alternative investments including private equity, private credit, real estate, infrastructure, growth equity, sustainability, secondaries, GP stakes, and hedge funds / liquid alternatives. Within ACF, team members work within investment product verticals and collaborate across strategies to identify portfolio-wide solutions that best meet the needs of clients. They also partner with other teams within ACF, and across the Client Solutions Group (CSG), Private Wealth Management (PWM), and investing teams. Professional development initiatives provide opportunities to enhance expertise across alternative investment products, and to sharpen market knowledge from industry experts investing across the Firm. This specific ACF role sits within the External Investment Group (“XIG”) on the Alternative Insights and Strategy team. About the Team The Alternative Insights and Strategy team brings together proprietary data, external data and insights from investment teams across the firm. It translates that information into clear views, portfolio allocation frameworks and client-ready content for alternative investments. The team works closely with investment teams, portfolio managers, strategists and client-facing professionals across the firm. Role Description This Vice President will drive the market analysis and synthesis process across assigned private market verticals, bringing together independent analysis, quantitative research, market data and perspectives from investment teams across the firm. The strategist will distill these inputs into clear conclusions and partner closely with portfolio managers, CIOs and other investment professionals to test and refine those conclusions, incorporate different perspectives and help build consensus around the team's views. The role requires strong investment judgment, intellectual curiosity and exceptional interpersonal skills, including the credibility to work effectively with senior investment leaders and navigate differing perspectives. The strategist will also work with colleagues to identify broader themes across alternative investments and respond to market developments, investment questions and content needs.

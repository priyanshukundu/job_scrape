Job Description Summary Responsible for the design, development, verification, and maintenance of software solutions that enable safe and effective operation of Computed Tomography (CT) imaging systems. Collaborate closely with Systems Engineering, Clinical Applications, Product Owners, Software Architects, Verification & Validation teams, and global development teams to translate customer, clinical, and workflow requirements into high quality software solutions. Design, develop, debug, test, and sustain software components across Scanner Desktop, operator workflow, system integration, and related platform services. Participate in architecture discussions, feature design, requirements analysis, impact assessments, code reviews, and technical problem solving. Investigate customer reported issues and drive root cause analysis and corrective actions to improve product quality and reliability. Contribute throughout the software development lifecycle, including requirements decomposition, design documentation, implementation, automated testing, verification, release readiness, and post release support. Ensure compliance with medical device software development processes and standards, including traceability, risk management, software FMEA, cybersecurity, and quality system requirements. Drive engineering excellence through Agile and Lean practices, continuous integration, test automation, code quality improvements, and effective use of modern software development tools and AI enabled productivity solutions. Mentor junior engineers and actively contribute to a collaborative, innovative, and customer focused engineering culture.

Job Description: We are seeking a Senior Software Systems Engineer to join our team in designing, developing, troubleshooting, and enhancing software for networking products in Campus & Branch Business Unit. This role focuses on networking systems software development and collaborative problem-solving and is ideal for candidates with strong C/C++ programming skills and experience working on software systems and networking applications. The candidate will work on developing new platforms and solutions collaborating with product management and architects. Responsibilities: Design and implement software enhancements, updates, and programming changes for subsystems such as operating systems, networking protocols in Junos & Junos Evolved software. Analyze design requirements and carry out coding, testing, and integration activities per project guidelines. Develop and execute portions of testing plans and documentation; identify and debug code issues and recommend improvements. Collaborate with internal and external teams to deliver high-quality, reliable, and cost-effective software solutions. Design, develop and maintain software for forwarding ASICs Communicate progress, design status, and technical issues with project stakeholders. Requirements: Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, or related field. 6-10 years of experience in software engineering. Proficiency in C, C++ programming and software systems design tools. Strong analytical, problem-solving, and communication skills. Knowledge of software systems testing methodologies, including test plan execution, and debugging. Experience in designing software systems for with different forward ASICs Working knowledge of L2, L3 networking protocols Willingness to explore & contribute to various software components to build next generation networking solutions Desired Skills: Embedded Operating Systems & low-level Software Development Forwarding ASIC programming for L2/L3/L4 Networking Protocols Strong networking fundamentals What We Can Offer You: Health & Wellbeing We strive to provide our team members and their loved ones with a comprehensive suite of benefits that supports their physical, financial and emotional wellbeing.

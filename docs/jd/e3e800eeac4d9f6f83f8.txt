Monitor SLA performance with banking partners and flag deficiencies. Assist with daily cash, intraday liquidity, settlement flows, and margin/collateral activities. Working knowledge of bank account documentation, signature authority, and KYC/AML requirements. Strong attention to detail with proven record‑keeping discipline. Ability to interact effectively with external banks and internal stakeholders. Excellent organizational skills and ability to manage multiple priorities. Clear written and verbal communication skills. Bachelor’s degree in Finance, Accounting, Business, or related field (preferred).

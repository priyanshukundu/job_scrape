Collateral Review Officer
FHLB Topeka
Location: Topeka, Kansas
Link: https://fhlbtopeka.wd1.myworkdayjobs.com/fhlbt/job/Topeka-Kansas/Collateral-Review-Officer_JR100402

Job Description This position supports the Credit function by leading collateral reviews and verifications for borrowing members and housing associates to ensure the accuracy, reliability, and eligibility of pledged loan collateral. This role analyzes collateral data and loan files, prepares management reporting, provides member support and education, and contributes to policy and process enhancements. The position also monitors regulatory, legal, and market developments and recommends updates to collateral and credit practices as appropriate. In This Role, You Will Lead on-site and off-site collateral verification reviews to validate the accuracy, reliability, and eligibility of pledged loan collateral and loan-level data. Analyze loan files and collateral delivered to the Bank to ensure data integrity and compliance with collateral requirements. Serve as a resource to members by providing guidance, education, and support related to collateral practices and requirements. Assist in developing and enhancing policies, procedures, and processes related to loan deliveries and collateral verification activities. Support regulatory and examination remediation efforts, as needed. Monitor industry, regulatory, and market developments and recommend improvements to credit and collateral practices. Qualifications Bachelor’s degree in business/finance or related field and/or equivalent work experience. Three to five years of similar or related experience with two to three years of loan underwriting experience. Lending or examination experience is preferred. Broad knowledge of the banking and thrift industry. Thorough knowledge of lending markets (residential, commercial and agricultural). Effective verbal and written communication skills. Knowledge and proficient use of MS Office applications including Word, Access and Excel. Ability to travel independently. Travel is 60% to 70%.

About this role: Wells Fargo is seeking a Business Execution Associate in Merchant Services. As a Business Execution Associate within Merchant Services, you will support the planning and execution of key business programs, initiatives, and operational priorities that help drive efficiency, performance, and effective controls. You will partner with leaders and cross-functional teams to evaluate processes, support policy and technology changes, identify opportunities for improvement, and help ensure initiatives are executed effectively. Learn more about career areas and lines of business at wellsfargojobs.com In this role, you will: Support the planning, execution, and ongoing monitoring of Merchant Services programs, initiatives, and business operations. Review key performance and control indicators to help identify trends, risks, and opportunities for improvement. Evaluate policy and process changes, technology enhancements, and operational improvements to increase efficiency and effectiveness. Identify opportunities to streamline processes, improve business experience, and generate potential cost savings. Exercise independent judgment while applying established policies, procedures, and business practices. Collaborate with mid-level leaders and cross-functional partners to advance business priorities and resolve operational needs. Provide guidance and work direction to less experienced Strategy and Execution team members. Ensure quality, accuracy, and efficiency in the execution and ongoing management of assigned controls. Independently review, analyze, and disposition new and existing errors, ensuring issues are appropriately documented and resolved. Manage the error backlog, continuously monitoring outstanding items and maintaining accurate status updates through resolution. Identify trends, recurring issues, and opportunities to improve processes, strengthen controls, and increase operational efficiency. Partner with Data Analytics and cross-functional teams to investigate complex errors, validate data, and support timely resolution. Identify and escalate data anomalies, control concerns, and potential operational risks, including submitting incident tickets when appropriate. Maintain clear and accurate documentation of error status, findings, remediation activities, and resolution. Develop and expand knowledge of Hogan, Merchant Services systems, data, and business processes to strengthen analysis and decision-making. Proactively seek opportunities to simplify processes, reduce errors, and improve the overall effectiveness of the control environment. Required Qualifications: 6+ months of Business Execution, Implementation, or Strategic Planning experience, or equivalent demonstrated through one or a combination of the following: work experience, training, military experience, education Desired Qualifications: Knowledge of BSA/AML, KYC, CDD, EDD, CIP, or related risk, compliance, and regulatory disciplines. Experience partnering with Data Analytics, Risk, Operations, Technology, or other cross-functional teams to resolve complex business issues. Knowledge of or the ability to quickly develop knowledge of Hogan and Merchant Services systems, processes, and data. Experience executing, monitoring, and reporting on operational controls, with a strong focus on quality, accuracy, and timely resolution. Demonstrated ability to identify, assess, and communicate risk clearly and decisively, translating complex issues into actionable business insights. Experience reviewing and resolving operational, control, or data-related errors, including managing outstanding issues through resolution. Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with the ability to identify data anomalies, investigate root causes, and escalate issues appropriately. Proven ability to identify process improvement, automation, and efficiency opportunities that strengthen execution and the overall control environment. Ability to manage multiple priorities and deliver results in a fast-paced, dynamic, and complex environment. Strong verbal, written, and interpersonal communication skills, with the ability to build effective partnerships across teams and levels. Ability to work independently and proactively, exercise sound judgment, and make informed decisions with a high degree of ownership. Exceptional attention to detail, organization, and accuracy, particularly when working with business-critical data and controls.

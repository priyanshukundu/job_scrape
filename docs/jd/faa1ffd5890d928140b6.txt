The Opportunity Sitting at the heart of Ally’s Treasury Organization, the Funding Desk’s key responsibilities are to manage enterprise liquidity, generate short-term cash flow forecasts and monitor encumbrance of assets. The Funding Desk Team offers an opportunity to work in a fast-paced environment where learning and growth are a key focus. The Funding Desk works in partnership with Liquidity Risk Group and other teams to establish a robust funding plan to manage short-term liquidity. This high visibility function has daily interactions with the funding markets and internally across most Treasury functions and business lines. Additionally, the team is responsible to managing relationships with brokers and key banking partners providing access to many of the largest financial firms on Wall Street, the research they provide, and industry events. This role reports to the office fully on-site (5 days a week) #LI-onsite The position will report to the Director of the Funding Desk and be part of a dynamic Treasury Funding Desk and will be responsible for managing daily enterprise liquidity through understanding of daily cash flows and execution of market-based funding products. This role will have high exposure to wholesale funding markets, and other functional areas across Treasury organization and internal business lines. The Work Itself Understand major cash inflows and outflows and monitor appropriate cash balance throughout the day to ensure end-of-day cash balances meet all internal targets and guidelines. Assist in preparation of a daily short-term liquidity forecast. Evaluate various funding sources including FHLB advances, Repo, Brokered CD, Retail Term Notes and Federal liquidity programs to manage daily enterprise liquidity. Interact and develop relationships with broker dealers across various funding products and markets. Partner with Liquidity Risk Group, Balance Sheet Management, Capital Markets Team, and other business lines to assist in developing funding plans to meet liquidity needs. Gain understanding of short-term interest rates markets, monetary policy and other drivers impacting short term funding markets. Learn Ally Liquidity position and related risks, support management of Liquidity Risk. Develop and maintain understanding of the company’s business, balance sheet, liquidity position and liquidity risk metrics. Develop understanding of Ally's lines of businesses and funding programs. Perform annual requirements related to maintenance and access to various funding programs. Manage various daily, monthly, and quarter end reporting requirements. Perform data analysis on various funding metrics and assist with ad hoc analysis. The Qualifications We’re Looking For Finance, Accounting or Economics four-year undergraduate degree, or equivalent.

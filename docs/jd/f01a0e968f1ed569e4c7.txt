The Liquidity Product Specialist III - Deposit Sweep Products is a mid-level product management role responsible for the strategy, performance and day-to-day management of the commercial bank's automated sweep products within Deposit Solutions. The portfolio includes zero balance accounts (ZBA), Insured Cash Sweep (ICS), Business Interest Sweep (BIS), Target Allocation Sweep (TAS ) and Loan Sweep. This position requires solid experience with commercial deposit and liquidity products, an understanding of how sweep mechanics drive balance growth/ retention and client value, and the analytical skills to size and evaluate product opportunities. The Product Specialist will play a key role in growing and modernizing the bank's sweep offering and supporting clients' liquidity management needs.

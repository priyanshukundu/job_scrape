And because we’re only as good as our people, we make sure our teams have the support and opportunities they need to thrive. WHAT YOU'LL DO: As a Senior Software Engineer at FACTS SIS, you will be a key component of the application development process. You will work closely with Product, Engineering and other internal teams to better understand customer needs in order to create new products and product features that will enhance and evolve FACTS SIS solutions. You will also mentor less experienced engineers and give guidance on adherence to best practices and development/quality standards. This will include engaging with resources that are located across the globe. While we work in a .NET stack with Angular, we value engineering fundamentals, passion and the ability to learn over specific tech experience. This position offers a hybrid work option. Nelnet values flexibility and understands the importance of work-life integration. Our hybrid work environment allows associates within 30 miles of an office to work remotely for part of the week, while also fostering collaboration and team connection through in-office presence. Please note that we are unable to provide visa sponsorship for this position. To be considered, candidates must already be authorized to work in the United States without the need for current or future sponsorship. Compensation range for this role is $120,000-$150,000 annually depending on experienc e. JOB RESPONSIBILITIES: Researches, designs, implements and manages software applications. Partners with Product and Engineering team to define requirements. Write, test, and implement efficient code. Review and provide feedback on pull requests. Use AI coding tools as part of your daily workflow. Stay positive and solution-oriented with team members and stakeholders. EDUCATION: Bachelor’s degree in a related field or equivalent professional experience. EXPERIENCE: 5+ years of hands-on experience developing enterprise web applications using technologies such as C#, ASP.NET Core, Angular, TypeScript, SQL Server, NoSQL databases, JSON, and RESTful APIs. Familiarity with Kubernetes and Azure Cloud Services preferred. Experience with AI-assisted development tools and a forward-thinking approach to incorporating AI into daily development workflows to improve productivity, quality, and efficiency. COMPETENCIES – SKILLS/KNOWLEDGE/ABILITIES: Strong technical competency, with a logical, analytical, and creative approach to problem-solving. Ability to communicate clearly with clients, colleagues, and management on complex issues. Comfortable working independently and as part of a team, managing multiple priorities.

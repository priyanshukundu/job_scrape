MongoDB Atlas, our database-as-a-service offering, lets customers deploy fault-tolerant, globally distributed clusters in minutes. The Cloud Payments team is part of Cloud Core Engineering, which builds the systems behind Atlas and our other cloud products. Our team is the last stage of the revenue pipeline for Atlas and our other cloud products. Once usage has been metered, we create the payments, charge customers, issue invoices, and make sure customers know what they owe and pay it. Our data also feeds MongoDB's financial reporting, including quarterly earnings. The team also owns integrations with external payment providers and the major cloud marketplaces, tax computation on cloud usage, and sanctions screening, which stops collection and blocks new cluster provisioning for sanctioned parties. The difficulty is business complexity: customers can pay in many different ways, we support all of them, and even small changes touch several areas at once. We need services that integrate with multiple third parties, stay highly available, and recover from failures on their own. This role can be based remotely in the United States. What You'll Do Take end-to-end ownership of problems: work out the solution, talk to the people and teams involved, and bring back a recommendation Design and build backend services for payment creation, charging, and invoicing, and the integrations around them Look at requirements from the customer's perspective, and ask critical questions when they don't make sense Work with Product and neighboring engineering teams, and collaborate with teammates on design, code review, testing, and troubleshooting Take part in the team's one-week HELP on-call rotation. Business hours can be busy when several tickets and urgent requests land together, but overnight pages are rare What We Look For 5+ years of professional backend software engineering experience building and running production services Strong object-oriented programming skills.

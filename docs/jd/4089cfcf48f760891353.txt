Job Title: Associate Location: 725 Ponce De Leon Avenue NE Atlanta, GA 30306 Job Duties: Design, develop, and maintain highly scalable, highly available, and fault tolerant software systems that support mission critical financial and transaction driven workflows, including components related to ETF systems and primary market operations, within a large, distributed platform. Perform work across the full software development lifecycle, including requirements analysis, system and API design, implementation, testing, deployment, and comprehensive production support of ETF applications. Develop and maintain backend services, APIs, and integration components. Build and operationalize AI workflows that automate SDLC processes accelerate QA execution, and improve team’s performance while meeting enterprise security, compliance, and governance standards. Contribute to the end-to-end lifecycle of ETF order processing by designing and supporting systems that integrate portfolio manager and trader workflows using Aladdin applications. Enable seamless order initiation, execution, fulfillment, and post-trade operations across the platform, leveraging hands-on experience executing orders to support timely processing and operational completion. Ensure system stability, performance, and operational reliability through rigorous peer code reviews, thorough automated testing, proactive risk identification, and continuous monitoring. Provide advanced production support, including complex incident investigation, root cause analysis, and post incident remediation, for distributed systems operating in a regulated financial environment. Partner with stakeholders to assess trade-offs, negotiate priorities, and drive adoption of scalable and maintainable technology solutions. Apply Agile and iterative development methodologies to deliver incremental enhancements. Drive continuous improvement in development practices through automation, tooling, and selective adoption of emerging technologies. Analyze the ongoing operation and evolution of large-scale financial systems to inform the design and development of next-generation platform components. Qualifications: Bachelor's degree or foreign equivalent in Computer Science or related field and 24 months of experience in job offered or closely related role. Position requires two years of experience in the following: Backend Engineering in Financial systems; Use Java, Spring Boot, Kafka and Cassandra to develop backend services; Leveraging Artificial Intelligence to create engineering workflows; Software development lifecycle; RESTful, and gRPC APIs; Data Modeling and SQL; Unit and integration tests using Junit; Agile delivery in enterprise settings; ETF workflows; and Enterprise Security, Data Handling, and Government workflows.

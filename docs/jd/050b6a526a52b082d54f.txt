The Associate Director, Digital Analytics & Data Integration is the global owner of how we measure digital performance, connect data across our digital ecosystem, and ensure customer and marketing data is used responsibly. In this role, you will set the digital measurement strategy, lead the evolution and governance of our analytics platforms, and establish the data and reporting standards that create a consistent view of digital performance across the organization. You will connect digital activity to business outcomes, including customer experience, adoption, and revenue, while enabling teams to access trusted insights more independently. You will also help establish how we use AI within digital analytics, building the roadmap for connecting analytics platforms and enterprise AI capabilities to create more scalable, self-service access to digital data and insights. This is a highly cross-functional leadership role that partners with Marketing, Commercial Operations, regional teams, IT, Security, Legal, and other functions to establish global standards, drive adoption, and continually evolve our digital analytics capabilities. Key Responsibilities Digital measurement, insights & reporting Define and maintain the global KPI and measurement framework for digital programs, aligning stakeholders on the metrics that matter most. Own the digital analytics roadmap and establish standards for measurement across platforms, regions, and functions. Translate digital performance into actionable insights tied to business outcomes such as customer experience, adoption, and revenue. Develop enterprise dashboards and standardized reporting that provide leadership and teams with a consistent view of digital performance. Identify gaps in measurement, reporting, and data collection and lead efforts to resolve them. AI & self-service analytics Define and lead the roadmap for incorporating AI into our digital analytics practice, prioritizing high-value use cases and scaling capabilities over time. Partner with IT, Security, Legal, and other teams to connect digital analytics data with approved enterprise AI capabilities and establish appropriate governance. Develop self-service analytics capabilities that enable teams to access trusted digital data and answer common business questions independently. Establish standards for AI-enabled analytics, including metric definitions, data quality, access, privacy, and responsible use. Drive adoption of AI-enabled analytics through education, enablement, and ongoing evolution of the capability. Analytics platforms & governance Serve as the global business owner for digital analytics and behavior analytics capabilities, including platforms such as Adobe Analytics, Google Analytics, ContentSquare, and associated tag management tools. Define the operating model, implementation standards, governance, and data-layer requirements that enable consistent measurement across our digital ecosystem. Evaluate emerging analytics capabilities and platforms and recommend where to adopt, consolidate, or evolve our technology landscape. Partner across technical and business teams to maintain data quality, platform reliability, and consistent measurement as technologies evolve. Data integration & governance Define how digital analytics data connects with our broader ecosystem, including data platforms, CRM, eCommerce, personalization, and customer experience technologies. Partner with technical teams to improve data flows, reduce manual workarounds, and increase consistency across systems. Establish standards for data quality, documentation, ownership, and monitoring across critical digital data flows. Partner with Legal, Security, and technology teams to ensure customer and marketing data practices meet applicable privacy, consent, and security requirements. Global adoption & enablement Drive adoption of digital analytics capabilities and standards across global Marketing, regional teams, Commercial Operations, and other key functions. Develop scalable training, playbooks, and onboarding that enable teams to use analytics tools and data effectively. Partner with regional and functional leaders to embed common measurement and data standards into how teams operate. Monitor adoption and data quality, identify areas of inconsistency, and lead improvements across the organization. Required Qualifications Typically requires a Bachelor’s degree and a minimum of 15 years of related experience Significant experience with enterprise digital analytics platforms such as Adobe Analytics or Google Analytics, including tag management and data-layer concepts. Experience integrating digital analytics data with enterprise systems such as data warehouses, CRM, eCommerce, or personalization platforms. Experience developing dashboards, reporting frameworks, and insights for senior business audiences. Experience using AI or emerging technologies to improve analytics, data access, or business decision-making. Working knowledge of privacy, consent, and data governance requirements, including GDPR and CCPA. Demonstrated ability to establish strategy, governance, standards, and roadmaps across complex organizations. Experience driving adoption of tools, processes, or standards across multiple regions or business functions. Strong ability to translate complex technical concepts and data into clear business recommendations. Proven ability to influence cross-functional stakeholders and operate independently in a global environment. Preferred Qualifications Experience developing AI-enabled analytics or self-service data capabilities. Experience with both Adobe and Google analytics ecosystems. Experience with data visualization platforms such as Tableau and cloud data platforms such as Snowflake. Experience with personalization, experience optimization, or behavior analytics platforms such as Adobe Target, Adobe Experience Manager, or ContentSquare. Experience with Salesforce and eCommerce data ecosystems. Experience evaluating, implementing, or consolidating enterprise analytics platforms. Experience working in a global, regulated, or B2B organization.

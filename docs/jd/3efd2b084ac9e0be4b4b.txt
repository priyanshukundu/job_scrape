Responsibilities We are seeking a Data Enginee r to simplify and modernize our on-premises SQL Server data environment and support the transition to a corporate-managed Databricks platform. This role will redesign legacy ETL processes, build reliable ODS and DWH pipelines, and embed operational controls, data quality and governance into the engineering lifecycle. Candidate must sit in the Jacksonville, FL office. Key Functions/Duties of Position: · Assess, document and rationalize existing SQL Server ETL pipelines, databases and data silos. · Refactor redundant or fragile SQL processes into standardized, maintainable ETL/ELT pipelines. · Design and develop ingestion, transformation and reconciliation pipelines for governed ODS and DWH environments. · Implement logging, monitoring, error handling, auditability, lineage and repeatable deployment practices. · Develop Databricks notebooks and pipelines using SQL and, as appropriate, Python or PySpark. · Partner with corporate platform teams to align solutions with enterprise architecture, security and engineering standards. · Support source-to-target mapping, testing, production support and migration validation. Perform other duties and/or projects as assigned Qualifications Education Requirement Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Information Systems, Data Analytics, Data Engineering, or a related field Qualifications: Include necessary skills/experience and core competencies required · Strong SQL and Microsoft SQL Server development experience. · Hands-on experience designing, building and supporting ETL/ELT pipelines. · Knowledge of relational databases, data warehousing and incremental data processing. · Experience troubleshooting complex data issues and reconciling source-to-target results. · Experience with source control and disciplined software development practices. · Strong analytical, documentation and communication skills. Preferred Qualifications · Databricks, Python, PySpark or Apache Spark experience. · Experience migrating legacy ETL workloads to a cloud data platform. · Dimensional modeling and data quality automation experience. · Experience in a SOX-controlled or regulated environment. · Property and casualty insurance industry knowledge, including policy, billing, claims or underwriting data, is a strong plus. · Experience working with Guidewire applications or data, including Guidewire ClaimCenter (GWCC), Guidewire BillingCenter (GWBilling) or related policy systems. · Experience engineering feature-ready datasets or pipelines for predictive analytics and machine learning. · Familiarity with model lifecycle concepts, including experimentation, deployment, monitoring and reproducibility. · Exposure to generative AI, large language models (LLMs), retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), vector search or AI-assisted data engineering.

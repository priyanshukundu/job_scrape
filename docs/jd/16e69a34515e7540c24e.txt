Become a part of our caring community The Associate Director, Software Engineering leads software engineering teams responsible for the development, delivery, support, and modernization of enterprise applications, data platforms, and API-based integration solutions. This leader is accountable for ensuring technology investments align with business objectives while maintaining operational excellence, scalability, security, and reliability. The role is responsible for managing engineering resources, technology and application lifecycles, and delivery execution across a portfolio of applications, enterprise data stores, databases, and API services. The Associate Director partners closely with business leaders, product teams, architects, and enterprise stakeholders to define priorities, drive strategic initiatives, and deliver measurable business value. This position owns the technology roadmap for assigned assets, including applications, enterprise databases, data platforms, and APIs. The leader is responsible for driving modernization efforts, ensuring alignment with enterprise architecture standards, and communicating strategy, progress, risks, and outcomes to business and technology stakeholders. Key Responsibilities Lead and develop high-performing software engineering teams responsible for enterprise applications, databases, data platforms, and API services. Own the technology roadmap for assigned applications, enterprise data stores, databases, APIs, and integration platforms. Drive the delivery of technology solutions that support strategic business initiatives and enterprise objectives. Manage resource planning, staffing decisions, skill development, and budget responsibilities across multiple engineering teams. Oversee application lifecycle management, including development, maintenance, modernization, support, and retirement activities. Lead technology lifecycle management efforts, ensuring platforms, frameworks, databases, and tools remain current, secure, and supportable. Establish engineering best practices across software development, data engineering, API development, DevOps, and operational support. Ensure the scalability, availability, performance, security, and reliability of enterprise applications, databases, and API ecosystems. Partner with enterprise architects and business stakeholders to define data, integration, and application strategies. Govern API and data management standards, ensuring consistency, reusability, and alignment with enterprise architecture principles. Communicate technology strategy, delivery status, risks, dependencies, and investment recommendations to senior leadership. Drive continuous improvement through automation, modernization, cloud adoption, and engineering excellence initiatives. Drive the adoption and effective utilization of AI-powered engineering tools and platforms to improve software development productivity, code quality, testing efficiency, operational support, and overall delivery outcomes.

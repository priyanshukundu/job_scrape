Job Description Provides technical direction, guidance and support for architectural development that encompasses multiple technology disciplines, platforms and applications. Facilitates creation of architectural blueprints as documentation of design and systems integration. Ensures projects are designed within approved enterprise architectural standards and are implemented successfully. Coordinates communication to management and teams regarding project development, strategy and goals. May function as a Project Leader. Provides direction and mentoring for less experienced staff. Key Responsibilities Design and implement scalable Databricks Lakehouse architectures supporting enterprise data processing, analytics, and high-volume workloads. Develop data solutions using Databricks, Python, PySpark, Databricks SQL, and Delta Lake . Modernize and migrate complex Oracle SQL and PL/SQL workloads to Databricks and cloud-based data platforms. Design and implement high-volume ETL/ELT pipelines , data ingestion, transformation, orchestration, and integration solutions. Optimize Databricks performance , including SQL queries, data layouts, compute utilization, jobs, and distributed data processing. Integrate Databricks with Oracle databases, AWS S3, cloud object storage, enterprise applications, and analytics platforms . Establish reusable data architecture, engineering, CI/CD, security, and platform standards using Databricks best practices. Partner with cloud, infrastructure, database, security, and application teams to troubleshoot issues and deliver scalable, secure, and supportable data solutions . Basic Qualifications - Bachelor's degree, or equivalent experience - Seven to nine years of experience in a specialized area of technology architectural design process, or - Ten or more years of experience across technology disciplines Skills & Qualifications Strong hands-on experience with Databricks, Databricks Lakehouse, Delta Lake, and distributed data processing . Advanced development skills in Python, PySpark, Databricks SQL, and SQL performance tuning . Strong experience with Oracle databases, SQL, and PL/SQL , including stored procedures, packages, and complex data workloads. Proven experience with Oracle-to-Databricks, legacy-to-cloud, or cloud data platform migrations . Experience designing high-volume ETL/ELT pipelines, data integration, data ingestion, and transformation solutions . Experience with Databricks Workflows/Lakeflow Jobs, Unity Catalog, and AWS S3/cloud object storage . Strong knowledge of data architecture, distributed computing, performance optimization, CI/CD, and source control . Strong troubleshooting, problem-solving, and cross-functional collaboration skills; experience with financial or transactional data preferred.

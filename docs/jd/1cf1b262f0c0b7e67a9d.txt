Key responsibilities Are you interested in the forces shaping the global semiconductor industry? Do you enjoy connecting industry, company and market developments to financial performance and investment implications? Do you enjoy helping to answer investor questions such as: When semiconductor manufacturers increase capital spending, which equipment suppliers are positioned to benefit, and when could those orders translate into revenue? When chip demand exceeds expectations, which companies have the capacity to respond, and where could supply constraints limit growth? When manufacturing indicators improve, what might that mean for automotive production and analog semiconductor demand? You will: • build and maintain Excel-based financial and valuation models for semiconductor and equipment companies. • translate earnings, supply chain data, technology shifts, competitive developments, and regulatory changes into earnings estimates and stock views. • write earnings previews and recaps, coverage initiations, and thematic reports. Build exhibits that support the investment case. • discuss research with institutional clients, company management, and industry contacts in line with research and compliance standards. • collaborate across the bank, especially with colleagues covering semiconductors in Asia. • contribute to a fast-paced research environment where business and market developments may occasionally require work outside standard business hours, including occasional weekend work Your Career Comeback We are open to applications from career returners. Find out more about our program on ubs.com/careercomeback . The team Join the US Semiconductors & Semiconductor Capital Equipment equity research team, ranked No. 1 by Extel in 2026. Based in New York, NY, or San Francisco, CA, you’ll partner with the Lead Analyst to cover publicly traded chip designers, integrated device manufacturers, foundries, and equipment suppliers. Your skills and experience • two + years of relevant experience. Semiconductor equity research or investment banking at a bulge-bracket bank is preferred. We also welcome buy-side experience, corporate finance or development at a semiconductor company, and Big Four audit experience with semiconductor clients. • ability to build and audit three-statement models, identify key forecast drivers, and explain where estimates differ from consensus. • strong written, verbal, and presentation skills. • bachelor’s degree in finance, accounting, economics, engineering, or a related field. • MBA, CPA or CFA charter, or progress toward the CFA charter, would be an advantage but is not required. Elijah Watt Sells Award recipients are encouraged to apply. • you’re curious to explore how AI can improve how we build, deliver, and optimize workflows.

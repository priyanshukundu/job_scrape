As an AVP, P&C Risk Management, you’ll lead the development and execution of the P&C risk management strategy for Nationwide. Your deep insurance risk expertise and strong business acumen will guide executives in identifying, assessing and managing risks across underwriting, pricing, claims, reserving, reinsurance and catastrophe areas. You’ll oversee governance, frameworks and reporting that align the P&C risk profile with Nationwide’s risk appetite, while developing a high-performing team and fostering cross-functional collaboration to achieve sustainable business outcomes. Job Description Develop and maintain the P&C risk management framework, policies, standards and guidance in alignment with the Enterprise Risk Management framework, Nationwide’s risk appetite, business strategy and regulatory expectations. Establish clear governance, decision rights, committees, escalation protocols and follow-through mechanisms. Partner with Corporate ERM to establish appropriate risk management and risk limit frameworks across property and casualty lines of business including catastrophe risk, cyber risk, casualty risk, and others as appropriate. Lead discussion with P&C Risk Committee for approval or updates to risk limits and risk guidelines. Lead the identification, assessment, prioritization, aggregation and monitoring of material P&C risks. Maintain the risk taxonomy, risk appetite statements, limits, tolerances, triggers, key risk indicators and control expectations across underwriting, pricing, product, claims, reserving, reinsurance, catastrophe, climate, operational, strategic, third-party, conduct, data, technology and emerging risks. Coordinate scenario analysis, stress testing and other forward-looking assessments, including catastrophe, climate, underwriting cycle, reserve, reinsurance and concentration scenarios. Monitor regulatory expectations, rating agency perspectives, market conditions, legal and economic trends and other emerging threats affecting the P&C risk profile. Establish consistent processes for recording, evaluating, escalating, remediating and closing P&C risk issues, events, findings and action plans. Lead or support targeted risk reviews, strategic initiatives, remediation programs and special projects that strengthen risk management capabilities. Partner with business, finance, and actuarial leaders to ensure appropriate risk governance across P&C including the maintenance of the appropriate governance committee structures, charters, calendars, and meeting content are in place. Owns the operating model and executive support for the P&C Risk Committee and P&C Pricing Risk Committees, including agendas, materials, escalations, decisions, and follow-through. Develops and maintains a pricing risk infrastructure and dashboards to provide transparency of latest pricing indications with clear lineage to underlying assumptions and support. Provides regular reporting of indications and key assumptions to Pricing Risk Committee. Provide risk reporting for P&C risks for internal management, regulatory requirements, and rating agencies. Maintain risk reporting dashboards and ensure that data is timely and accurate and that the dimension of the reporting supports the business appropriately. Represent P&C risk management in enterprise and appropriate external forums as requested. Develop and maintain P&C risk capacity guidelines to support the business by translating enterprise limits to business unit and geographic capacity guidelines. Partner with finance and business leaders to assess plans and strategies to ensure alignment with risk appetites and guidelines. Identify opportunities to shape the P&C risk portfolio to improve profitability and stability through appropriate risk diversification. Develop and maintain unconstrained property technical price models in partnership with ECRM and actuarial and ensure that reporting of actual versus technical pricing is reported timely and accurately to business and finance partners. Partner with the pricing and modeling teams on the development of property technical pricing across the P&C portfolio, ensuring pricing models meet enterprise standards and reflect appropriate catastrophe risk standards including custom adjustments and risk margins. Partner with business-unit actuaries to apply risk-based pricing metrics, including AAL, custom adjustments, risk margins, and relevant geographic or business segmentation. Responsible for delivering timely and accurate inputs to finance and actuarial teams for development of cat and associated risk margin and ceded loss components for the operating and strategic plan. Maintain clear documentation on underlying assumptions. Lead and develop a high-performing team, providing guidance, mentorship, and performance management. Foster a culture of collaboration, continuous improvement, and accountability. May perform other responsibilities as assigned. Reporting Relationships: Reports to VP, Chief Actuary. Manages a team of 4+ associates Typical Skills and Experiences: Education: Undergraduate degree in risk management, actuarial science, insurance, finance, accounting, business, economics, or a related discipline preferred. Advanced degree a plus. License/Certification/Designation: Risk or Actuarial designation (ACAS or FCAS) strongly preferred. Experience: 10+ years of related experience, including significant experience in P&C insurance, risk management, underwriting, actuarial, claims, reinsurance, finance, compliance, audit, or a related discipline. Demonstrated experience leading enterprise or business-unit risk frameworks, governance, risk assessments, reporting, and remediation. Experience leading people and working cross-functionally with senior executives and multiple levels of the organization required. Knowledge, Abilities and Skills: Demonstrated focus on building and developing a team of strong performers who can work effectively as individuals, and as a cohesive team for the good of the larger organization. Demonstrated ability to develop strong relationships at high levels across the organization and deliver difficult messages to functional leaders. Experience with strategic planning, continuous improvement, and operational leadership experience. Must possess strong communication skills, both verbal and written, and ability to make difficult decisions and facilitate actively in a team setting. Proven leadership skills as well as problem solving skills. Other criteria, including leadership skills, competencies and experiences may take precedence.

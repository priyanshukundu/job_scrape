Job Description What is the opportunity? Analyst, Central Funding Group Sales About the team: Integral to supporting CFG (Central Funding Group) is the ability to effectively communicate and sell our products on an ongoing basis to clients and their support personnel. Central Funding plays an integral role in delivering best-in-class solutions to RBC’s clients, and introducing them to the larger firm. What will you do? •Service CFG clients/counterparties through taking orders and answering daily inquiries regarding portfolios, pending trades, loans due diligence requests, website reporting, and deal amendments; •Ongoing assessment of portfolio composition to ensure that client/counterparty portfolios are in compliance with CFGs position concentration policies and risk limits; •Facilitate communication between all internal groups of CFG, particularly due diligence, documentation and trading book running; •Coordinate and review Audit and other requests from clients/ counterparties; •Work on projects related to client/counterparty servicing that arise from time to time. •Proactively identify operational risks and control deficiencies in the business •Ensure that you understand and comply with the relevant policies and procedures that apply to you •Escalate operational risk loss events, control deficiencies and other risks that you identify to your line manager and the relevant risk and control functions •Work with our AI partners to automate processes, reporting, and other tasks What do you need to succeed? B.S./ B.A. in marking, finance, economics (preferred) Entry level or internship experience within financial services FINRA registration: Series 7 and 63 Strong mathematical skills and technical acumen Strong communication skills and ability to navigate complex interactions with teammates and stakeholders Experience with Excel, Programming, AI What’s in it for you?

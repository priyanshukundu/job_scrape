ROLE SUMMARY The Senior Associate, macOS End Point Engineering & Support is a hands-on engineering role responsible for L3 operational support of Apple macOS endpoints across the enterprise. This role maintains a reliable global Mac fleet, supporting colleague productivity worldwide. The Senior Associate will develop automation, manage platform tooling, partner with global engineering and support teams to deliver a best-in-class Apple experience. ROLE RESPONSIBILITIES Provide L3 escalation support and root-cause analysis for complex macOS issues. Engineer, deploy, and maintain macOS endpoint configurations, security baselines, and policies using Jamf Pro and Apple Business Manager. Build and maintain application packages, patch workflows, and macOS upgrade paths for the global Mac fleet. Develop scripts and automation (e.g., Bash, Python) to streamline provisioning, remediation, and compliance reporting. Collaborate with cybersecurity teams to implement and validate secure configurations, certificate management, and identity integration. Maintain technical documentation, standards, and knowledge transfer materials for engineering and support teams. BASIC QUALIFICATIONS Applicant must have a bachelor's degree with at least 2 years of experience; OR a master's degree with more than 0+ years of experience; OR an associate's degree with 6 years of experience; OR a high school diploma (or equivalent) and 8 years of relevant experience 2+ years of experience in endpoint engineering or enterprise IT, with hands-on macOS experience. Expertise in Apple device management tools (e.g., Jamf Pro, Intune, Apple Business Manager) and macOS security configuration. Proficiency in scripting for automation (e.g., Bash, Python, AppleScript). Working knowledge of identity, certificate, and network services integration for Apple platforms. PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS Apple, MSFT or Jamf certifications (e.g., Jamf Certified Admin/Expert, Apple Certified Support Professional). Experience in global enterprise environments and regulated industries. Familiarity with Zero Trust security models and digital employee experience (DEX) tooling. PHYSICAL/MENTAL REQUIREMENTS Ability to analyze technical problems and develop innovative solutions. Occasional travel may be required to support global teams and initiatives. NON-STANDARD WORK SCHEDULE, TRAVEL OR ENVIRONMENT REQUIREMENTS Flexibility to work outside standard hours to support global operations and critical incidents. Occasional travel to company sites or vendor locations as needed.

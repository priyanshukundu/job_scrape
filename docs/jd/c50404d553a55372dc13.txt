Who we are looking for: This is a leadership position in Chief Data and Artificial Intelligence Office (CDAIO) team. Our primary objective is to lead & drive transformational AI efforts across STT’s processes and functions; partner with business and technology teams to design and implement transformational AI across people, process and technology. As a member of the CDAIO Team, the incumbent will partner with technology teams, third parties, and with stakeholders across different business teams, corporate functions & regions, to identify risk areas & work with business units and various other stakeholders to design and implement Artificial Intelligence controls and requirements, using world class tools and techniques. What you will be responsible for: As a VP - AI Governance you will: Enterprise Data & AI Strategy : Translate strategy into clear product-aligned roadmaps accounting for risk and controls that support design of an end-state that relies on the latest AI offerings Develop an in-depth understanding of business processes, functions, teams, and incumbent technology Product & Portfolio Management and Execution Tracking : Drive the AI risk and control strategy, to include all aspects of automation, AI and ML across STT’s footprint Conduct core project management activities including facilitating meetings, interviewing stakeholders, hosting workshops, documenting findings, and creating stakeholder presentations Executive & Client (Internal & External) Communication : Liaise with cross-functional teams to proactively support the CDAIO strategy and address key questions Create materials required to support future state socialization and execution (eg: Strategy presentations, detailed Requirement & process flow diagrams, job aids, POC’s etc.) Portfolio Governance : Ensure our delivery team has the highest likelihood of success, and all risks are managed by demonstrating consistent and proactive ownership of assigned responsibilities for standard work products, version control, model lifecycle, and Quality Control Team Collaboration : Engage with cross-functional colleagues to capture and express AI risk and control execution and requirements & design in business services by contributing to a culture of precision and clarity, strong execution discipline, and high-quality output. Skills: These skills will help you succeed in this role: Experience with risk and controls, strategy, product management, or portfolio support Strong background in data platforms and data strategy, as well as AI/ML and Generative AI Being comfortable with ambiguity and the ability to work well with teams, particularly ones that span different reporting lines, cut across multiple functions and geographies Strong analytical skills, critical thinking, problem solving, and decision making Education & Preferred Qualifications: AI Capability & Strategy experience Experience supporting large-scale implementation programs in consulting or financial services industry Business Process Re-Engineering – Workflow, AI & Process Automation experience (from current state thru target state and implantation). 12+ years of experience Knowledge of AI / automation technologies and/or a master’s degree will be an added advantage Ability to build stakeholder networks, effective collaboration and partnership capabilities and ability to influence at all levels of the organization Very strong organizational skills, ability to meet deadlines and proven experience in working with large cross functional teams will be a plus Salary Range: $120,000 - $217,500 Annual The range quoted above applies to the role in the primary location specified.

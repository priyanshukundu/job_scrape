Staff Software Engineer - LV Battery
General Motors
Location: Milford, Michigan, United States of America
Link: https://generalmotors.wd5.myworkdayjobs.com/Careers_GM/job/Milford-Michigan-United-States-of-America/Staff-Software-Engineer---LV-Battery_JR-202621085

Job Description Work Classification: Hybrid: The successful candidate is expected to report to Milford, MI three times per week, at minimum, or as dictated by the business. The Role: We are seeking a highly skilled and motivated Staff Software Engineer to lead the development of innovative battery management software solutions for low-voltage (LV) systems. This role demands a strong technical leader who can drive strategic initiatives, foster cross-functional collaboration, and champion best practices in software engineering within a dynamic automotive environment. What You'll Do : Lead the architectural design and development of complex software for LV battery sensing systems, ensuring scalability, reliability, and performance. Drive continuous improvement initiatives in software development processes, tooling, and methodologies, incorporating best practices such as SAFe. Mentor and provide technical guidance to junior engineers, fostering a culture of continuous learning and growth. Collaborate extensively with cross-functional teams, including hardware engineers, quality assurance, and product management, to define requirements and deliver integrated solutions. Act as a subject matter expert in LV battery software, contributing to strategic technical roadmaps and decision-making. Identify and resolve complex technical problems through effective group problem-solving techniques and innovative software coding solutions. Contribute to the development and implementation of enterprise data architecture strategies relevant to battery system data. Ensure adherence to industry standards, safety regulations, and internal quality processes throughout the software development lifecycle. Your Skills & Abilities (Required Qualifications): Bachelor's in Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, or a related technical field. 7+ years of progressive experience in software development, with a significant focus on embedded systems or automotive applications. Proven expertise in software coding, with strong proficiency in C or other relevant programming languages. Demonstrated experience in leading technical projects and initiatives, exhibiting strong strategic leadership. Prior experience with developing and leading battery management sensing software and expertise with different sensing AFEs. Extensive knowledge of Scaled Agile Framework (SAFe) principles and practices. Strong understanding of enterprise data architecture concepts and their application in complex systems. Exceptional problem-solving skills and the ability to facilitate effective group problem-solving sessions. Excellent communication and interpersonal skills, with a proven ability to lead and collaborate effectively across diverse teams (Cross-Functional Leadership). A strong commitment to continuous learning and staying abreast of emerging technologies and industry trends. What Will Give You A Competitive Edge (Preferred Skills): Master's degree in Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, or a related technical field. 10+ years of progressive experience in software development, with a significant focus on embedded systems or automotive applications. Prior low voltage battery or Tier One experience launching programs. Ability to use AI tools to accelerate product development.

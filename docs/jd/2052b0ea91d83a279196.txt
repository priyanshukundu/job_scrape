The Identity & Access Management (IAM) team enables customers to confidently bring their most sensitive data and workloads to Snowflake. We build the authentication and authorization capabilities that help customers secure their Snowflake accounts, with a growing focus on AI security, including access control for Snowflake Intelligence, MCP servers and clients, agent identity, and administrative guardrails for agent workflows.

Our systems are foundational to customer trust, security, reliability, and performance. The team is highly collaborative, with opportunities to learn from and partner with experienced engineers across the organization.




AS A SOFTWARE ENGINEER - IDENTITY & ACCESS MANAGEMENT, YOU WILL:

 - Design and implement secure identity and access management capabilities for Snowflake’s cloud services.

 - Build features that support authentication and authorization for human users, service identities, agents, and external applications.

 - Contribute to AI security capabilities that enable controlled, auditable, and appropriately restricted agent workflows.

 - Develop authorization frameworks that provide fine-grained access control across large numbers of users, objects, and services.

 - Design and implement integrations with identity providers and industry-standard authentication protocols.

 - Build reliable solutions for secure data sharing, application access, and external service integrations.

 - Collaborate with engineers, product managers, security partners, and other teams to deliver high-quality projects.

 - Participate in design and code reviews, contribute to technical decisions, and help improve engineering practices.

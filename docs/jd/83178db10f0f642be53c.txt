Claims Risk Management Teammate Shift Day shift: 9:00 AM – 5:30 PM MST , including one weekend day About the Team Claims Risk Management is a critical function within the GCX organization, specializing in managing high value claims from start to finish. We identify complex fraud trends, conduct deep-dive case reviews, partner with high-value accounts, and stay ahead of emerging threats across the platform. About the Role As a Claims Risk Management teammate, you'll conduct in-depth account and claim reviews for high-value, risky, or potentially fraudulent claims. This is a cross-functional, high-visibility role. You'll collaborate with departments across the business, engage directly with customers and leadership, and operate in an environment where expectations around quality, efficiency, and impact are real and evolving.

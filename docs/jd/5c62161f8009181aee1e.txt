Position Summary Join our Translational Research department at our Seattle site for a full-time, hands-on laboratory co-op supporting clinical flow cytometry. You will work alongside experienced flow cytometry scientists to help develop, qualify, and maintain the flow cytometry assays used to study patient samples in clinical research, and to support quality control, analysis, and interpretation of the resulting data. This co-op offers translational, clinical, and industry experience at the interface of immunology, oncology, and biomarker science. Key Responsibilities Generate data to support the development of clinical flow cytometry assays, including assay design, optimization, and qualification. Use analytics tools to review the quality of flow cytometry data ahead of downstream analysis, and support data analysis and interpretation. Support critical reagent management, including assay controls and antibodies, ensuring reagents are properly handled, stored, and tracked. Perform routine maintenance on flow cytometry instruments, troubleshoot issues, and communicate with vendor support as needed. Document experiments accurately in an electronic lab notebook (ELN) and laboratory data systems, in line with good laboratory practice. Set and manage priorities in a collaborative, matrixed team environment. Present work and results to the team and perform other tasks to support team and program objectives. Qualifications & Experience Currently enrolled in a degree program in Immunology, Oncology, Biology, or a related scientific field, with a co-op term of Jan 11 – Jun 18, 2027 and the ability to work full-time for the duration. Prior hands-on wet-lab experience through coursework, an academic lab, or a previous internship or co-op. Working knowledge of flow cytometry (sample preparation, acquisition, and analysis). Familiarity with flow cytometry analysis software (for example FlowJo) and with electronic lab notebooks or laboratory data systems. Strong attention to detail and organizational skills. Clear communication, and the ability to work in a fast-paced, matrixed team and meet deadlines. Must be able to work in Seattle on a hybrid schedule for the full co-op term. All candidates must be legally authorized to work in the United States at the time of hire and for the duration of their employment. Please note that visa sponsorship, including for student or temporary work authorization programs, is not available for this role. Candidates must have work authorization that does not require sponsorship now or in the future. Preferred Experience designing, troubleshooting, or validating multiparameter flow cytometry assays. Experience with more than one flow cytometry platform, including spectral flow cytometry. Experience with flow data analysis and visualization tools beyond standard gating software. Experience in a CRO, clinical, or regulated laboratory setting.

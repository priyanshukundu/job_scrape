Associate Manager, Embedded Software Engineering
Stryker
Location: Portage, Michigan
Link: https://stryker.wd1.myworkdayjobs.com/StrykerCareers/job/Portage-Michigan/Associate-Manager--Embedded-Software-Engineering_R576802

Work Flexibility: Hybrid or Onsite The Associate Manager, Embedded Software Engineering is a technical and people leadership role supporting the development and delivery of software for regulated medical devices. In this role, you will lead and develop a team of software engineers while providing technical guidance across the design, development, integration, and verification of high-quality embedded and application software. You will partner closely with systems, hardware, quality, test, regulatory, clinical, and project management teams to ensure software solutions meet product requirements, architectural direction, risk controls, and applicable regulatory requirements. This role is ideal for an experienced software engineer ready to combine strong technical judgment with people leadership while building a collaborative, accountable, and high-performing engineering team. What You Will Do Lead, coach, and develop a team of software engineers, providing clear expectations, actionable feedback, technical mentorship, and opportunities for professional growth. Guide the planning, design, development, integration, and verification of embedded and/or application software for regulated medical devices. Provide technical direction through software architecture and design reviews, risk assessments, technical problem solving, and resolution of software defects and integration challenges. Ensure the team follows established software development lifecycle processes, design controls, development standards, secure software practices, and applicable regulatory requirements. Partner with cross-functional engineering, quality, regulatory, clinical, and project teams to translate product needs into executable software plans and deliver high-quality solutions. Lead software projects and initiatives, balancing technical priorities, staffing, schedules, budgets, and project goals to support successful execution. Review and approve technical documentation and software deliverables while promoting effective verification, test automation, continuous integration, and software quality practices. Build an inclusive, accountable, and collaborative team culture while attracting, developing, engaging, and retaining strong engineering talent. What You Need (Required Qualifications) Bachelor’s degree or higher in Computer Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related engineering field. 8+ years of relevant embedded software engineering experience. Experience developing embedded and/or application software for medical devices, regulated products, or other safety-critical systems. Strong understanding of the software development lifecycle, including software design, implementation, integration, verification, and defect resolution. Experience contributing to software architecture, design reviews, technical decision-making, and engineering risk assessments. Experience working collaboratively across engineering and cross-functional teams to deliver complex technical projects. Preferred Qualifications Previous experience leading, mentoring, or providing technical direction to embedded software engineers; direct people management experience is preferred but not required. Experience developing software within a regulated medical device environment and working with design controls and software documentation requirements. Knowledge of medical device software standards and practices, including IEC 62304, software risk management principles aligned with ISO 14971, and secure software development practices. Experience with software verification and validation, automated testing frameworks, and continuous integration practices. Familiarity with embedded software architecture, software design patterns, hardware/software integration, and complex electromechanical systems. Demonstrated ability to coach others, provide actionable feedback, and support individual and team development.

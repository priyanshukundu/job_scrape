The Opportunity The Software Engineer II is a hands-on builder within the product-led Software Engineering team in Abbott’s cancer diagnostics division, focused on Digital Engineering and Integrations. This team owns the customer-facing portals that serve patients, providers, and payers, along with the integrations that power them. You will design, build, and run secure, reliable, full-stack services on AWS, working in a squad that owns its product end to end. You will write high-quality code, strengthen how we build, and help the engineers around you grow. We are looking for someone who builds with the full lifecycle in mind: thoughtful design, solid testing, and real responsibility for how services run once they are live. The ideal candidate is a strong collaborator, shares what they know and learn generously, and is able to work towards solid solutions. What You’ll Work On Build RESTful and GraphQL APIs, services, and event-driven integrations using Java, Spring Boot, and messaging platforms such as Kafka, with attention to versioning, error handling, and idempotency. Contribute to front-end work, building responsive, accessible user interfaces using Angular, TypeScript, RxJS, HTML, and CSS. Treat performance, availability, observability, security, and compliance requirements as first-class parts of every design, not afterthoughts. Write and automate unit and integration tests that give the team confidence to ship often. Deploy and operate services on AWS using containers and infrastructure as code, supported by GitLab CI/CD pipelines. Contribute to design discussions and code reviews and help raise the quality bar across the squad. Work within their team in an Agile environment to deliver features that serve our portal users. Required Qualifications Bachelor’s degree in computer science, information technology, or a related field, or equivalent experience, plus 5+ years of related software engineering experience. Demonstrated experience building and supporting distributed microservices in production using Java or a comparable object-oriented language, a framework such as Spring Boot, and RESTful or GraphQL APIs, including event-driven integrations with messaging platforms such as Kafka. Experience building and running services on a major cloud platform such as AWS or Azure. Experience with SQL and relational database skills. Experience writing automated unit and integration tests, and working with Git, Docker, and CI/CD pipelines. Experience troubleshooting issues across application services, APIs, databases, and cloud infrastructure. Strong written, verbal, and listening communication skills, with the ability to collaborate within their team and independently own technical deliverables. Preferred Qualifications Experience building user interfaces with Angular, TypeScript, and RxJS, with a solid grounding in HTML, CSS, and accessibility basics, and with state management (NgRx) or micro-frontend approaches. Experience with AI-assisted software engineering tools such as GitHub Copilot, Claude Code, or equivalent solutions. Experience with AWS services such as EKS/ECS, S3, RDS, Lambda, and IAM. Experience with Terraform or Kubernetes. Knowledge of NoSQL databases. Knowledge of securing APIs using Spring Security and OAuth 2/OIDC. Willingness to work in a regulated industry (such as healthcare or life sciences).

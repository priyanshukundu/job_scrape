Director, Fraud Risk Management
Valley National Bank
Location: Morristown, NJ, USA
Link: https://myjobs.adp.com/vnbcareers/cx/job-details?reqId=5001240228306

Responsibilities include but are not limited to: Develop and execute Valley's enterprise fraud risk strategy, framework, policies, standards, and risk appetite. Maintain an enterprise-wide view of fraud risk across products, channels, customers, digital platforms, and third parties. Lead fraud detection, prevention, monitoring, and control strategies, including rules, models, and risk-based decisioning. Partner across Technology, Operations, Payments, Digital, Information Security, Data, and business teams to strengthen fraud capabilities. Oversee fraud technology solutions, vendor relationships, and strategic technology roadmaps. Develop and govern customer authentication and digital identity strategies that balance security, fraud prevention, and customer experience. Define fraud risk requirements for new products, payment capabilities, fintech partnerships, acquisitions, and key initiatives. Leverage data and analytics to identify emerging threats, control gaps, and opportunities to improve detection effectiveness. Establish fraud risk metrics, reporting, and governance for senior management, committees, regulators, and auditors. Serve as the Bank's senior fraud risk expert, providing strategic guidance and integrating fraud events, losses, and investigative insights into risk management.

Regular or Temporary: Regular Language Fluency: English (Required) Work Shift: 1st shift (United States of America) Please review the following job description: The Mortgage Banker is responsible for managing bank-generated client relationships and delivering customized financing solutions for commercial real estate debt transactions. Serving as a trusted advisor, the Mortgage Banker develops a deep understanding of each client's objectives and provides strategic guidance that extends beyond the economics of an individual transaction, aligning Truist's capabilities with the client's long-term goals and interests. In this role, the Mortgage Banker evaluates each financing opportunity and structures the most appropriate capital solution by leveraging a broad range of lending sources, including Fannie Mae, Freddie Mac, FHA/HUD, life insurance companies, debt funds, banks, and other institutional capital providers. ESSENTIAL DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES Following is a summary of the essential functions for this job. Other duties may be performed, both major and minor, which are not mentioned below. Specific activities may change from time to time. 1. Work on all aspects of loan executions both agency and non-agency. 2. Partner with Truist Relationship Managers to “pitch” various execution options, identifying the best execution outcomes for various products. 3. For Agency execution: Partner with Agency Underwriters to underwrite and size the transaction. Manage the process from start to finish. 4. For Non-Agency execution: Mortgage Banker will work closely with Capital Markets team to determine the best source of capital. 5. Work with support staff, underwriting and credit in the preparation of the preliminary and final loan submissions. Ability to organize, direct and motivate others. 8. Ability to meet deadlines. 9. Capability to simultaneously handle a variety of assignments. 10. Ability to travel, including overnight. Preferred Qualifications: 1. Master of Business Administration (MBA) or Master’s degree in Finance or Real Estate. 2. Proficiency in Salesforce, CoStar, Trepp and Argus. 3. Deep understanding of Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac lending programs.

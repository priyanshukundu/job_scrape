Job Profile Summary: The AP Associate Accountant will work under close to general supervision to review, code, and process PO and Non-PO invoices through the invoice workflow, ensure accurate general ledger coding, capture available vendor discounts, support and monitor automation processes, and provide customer service to operations, vendors, and internal stakeholders as needed. Job Description: • Review, code, and process PO and Non-PO invoices accurately and timely through the invoice workflow • Apply correct general ledger, cost center, WBS/AFE, and tax coding to invoices • Route invoices for approval and follow up on exceptions, holds, and disputes to keep the invoice workflow moving • Identify and capture early payment discounts by prioritizing discount-eligible invoices and meeting payment terms • Perform invoice matching (2-way/3-way) and resolve price, quantity, and receipt discrepancies • Identify potential duplicate invoices prior to processing • Maintain accurate records and ensure compliance with accounting policies, procedures, and internal controls • Provide responsive, professional customer service to vendors, field operations, and internal stakeholders on invoice status and coding questions • Monitor daily automation processes and escalate exceptions or failures • Support and help improve invoice automation tools and workflow rules • Bring an automation mindset by identifying manual, repetitive tasks that can be streamlined or automated • Support Open Invoice workflow activities, including vendor and user setup requests • Assist with daily/monthly reporting on invoice workflow data, aging, and discount capture • Collaborate with team members to identify process improvement opportunities and implement best practices • Work effectively in a fast-paced, high-volume environment while maintaining a high level of accuracy • Support operations and accounting teams as needed Education: • Bachelor's Degree in Accounting, Finance or any other related discipline and/or commensurate work experience.

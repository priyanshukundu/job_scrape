About this Team: The FieldX team owns the end-to-end development, operation, and support of Target’s retail merchandising experimentation platform. This includes the frontend experience, backend services, analytics and metrics pipelines, AI-powered insights and recommendations, documentation, and user support. The team enables merchandisers to design and run store experiments, measure results, and make data-driven rollout decisions. Bring your skills, experience, and talents to help turn ambitious ideas into impactful solutions.

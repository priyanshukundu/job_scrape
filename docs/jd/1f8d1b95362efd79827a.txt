OPERATIONS Operations is a dynamic, multi-faceted area that partners with teams across the firm to support banking, securities and asset management activities. Operations also plays an important role in managing risk, strengthening controls and ensuring transactions are processed accurately and efficiently. YOUR IMPACT We are looking for an Associate to join our FX Operations team supporting the confirmation and post-trade lifecycle of Foreign Exchange transactions. In this role, you will help ensure FX trades are accurately captured, matched and confirmed with counterparties. You will investigate trade discrepancies, resolve exceptions and work closely with Trading, Sales, Middle Office and other internal teams to reduce operational and settlement risk. This is a good opportunity for someone with experience in FX, trade support, confirmations or post-trade operations who enjoys problem solving and working in a fast-paced environment. OUR IMPACT The FX Operations team supports the day-to-day processing and control of Foreign Exchange transactions. The team works closely with Trading, Sales, Middle Office, Technology, Legal and Risk to ensure trades are confirmed accurately and on time. The team also helps identify and resolve trade breaks, improve processes and maintain a strong operational control environment.

Contact Center Associate Director
Guidehouse
Location: US - TX, San Antonio

Job Family : Business Operations Travel Required : Up to 10% Clearance Required : Ability to Obtain Public Trust What You Will Do : As a Contact Center Associate Director, you will perform as the Deputy to the Project Director. You will play a key role in delivery to our client while simultaneously providing hands-on leadership to our team of contact center professionals. Role and responsibilities include, however are not limited to: • Translates operational goals into contact center work plans, addressing contact volume, staffing, scheduling, workflows, and technology needs to support consistent customer service. • Manages day-to-day contact center delivery through operations managers and supervisors, coordinating teams and workstreams to meet contractual requirements and service-level agreements. • Maintains working relationships with client operational leaders, addresses service concerns, and escalates issues requiring Director-level guidance or decisions. • Reports contact center results, staffing needs, risks, and corrective actions to clients and internal leadership, using measures such as service level, average handle time, first-contact resolution, quality scores, and customer satisfaction. • Coordinates workforce management, quality assurance, training, and team development; monitors queue coverage, schedule adherence, and productivity; and implements process improvements within approved staffing and budget parameters while maintaining compliance and service quality. What You Will Need : Experience managing contact center operations, including staffing, scheduling, queue management, quality assurance, and customer escalations. Experience leading operations managers, supervisors, or team leads and coordinating with workforce management, training, and quality teams to meet service and productivity goals. Experience managing contract delivery against service-level agreements, analyzing contact center metrics, communicating results to clients, and implementing corrective actions. Bachelor's degree (Relevant experience may be substituted for formal education or advanced degree) 10+ years of prior relevant experience Of the 10+ years of experience, a minimum of 2 years of experience required directly leading and managing teams What Would Be Nice to Have : Experience in outsourced contact center operations supporting financial services or mortgage loan servicing, with familiarity with customer relationship management systems, contact center platforms, and workforce management tools.

Job Description: Our Opportunity We are hiring a Software Engineer II to join the Fulfillment Execution Application team in Minneapolis, MN. At Chewy, it is our mission to be the most trusted and convenient destination for pet parents (and partners) everywhere. You will help develop services and applications to enable, support, and optimize software development processes and practices for the fulfillment execution teams. In this role, you will develop new features that enhance existing systems for our fulfillment team members. Further, you will have the opportunity to collaborate across engineering and operations teams to deliver solutions that empower team members to fulfill Chewy customer orders. What You'll Do: Apply algorithms, data structures, and design patterns to build scalable and performant code Design resilient systems for team member-facing web application from scratch Partner with stakeholders and cross functional technology teams on how to best implement desired customer features and enhancements Collaborate with Team Members to drive automated solutions to standardize methodologies, workflows, reusable modules, and tooling Contribute to team’s standard processes for logging, testing, instrumentation, metrics, monitors, alerts, practices, and processes Conduct design and code reviews and raise the bar on engineering rigor and standard Participate in setting guidelines for evolving development practices, as well as mentoring junior engineers Participate in on-call rotations, troubleshooting production issues and improving operational excellence What You'll Need: Bachelor's degree or higher in Computer Science, Computer Information Systems, or similar; or equivalent experience 3+ years of professional software development experience Experience with cloud native technology stacks such as AWS Lambdas, S3, SQS/SNS Experience as a fullstack developer working in Java , React , Typescript or other related languages Ability to think independently and break down product requirements into well-defined tasks Ability to collaborate with stakeholders to ensure development efforts align with stakeholder needs Experience in monitoring production environment, troubleshooting, triaging and addressing production issues Experience with CI/CD pipelines in Jenkins or similar tool Experience with designing, building and deploying scalable, highly available systems Bonus: Hands-on experience with AI tools and proven ability to apply AI to drive productivity.

A Brief Overview The Senior Financial Analyst plays a key role in driving strategic financial planning, analysis, and decision-making across the organization. This position is responsible for preparing detailed financial reports, forecasts, and models to support budgeting, expense management, and performance evaluation. The analyst collaborates with cross-functional teams to provide insights that enhance operational efficiency and profitability. What you will do Develop and maintain financial models to support forecast, budgeting, and strategic planning Develop, implement, and maintain business intelligence reports and dashboards tailored to financial metrics and KPIs Collaborate across departments and functions to deliver insightful analysis and commentary comparing actual performance against plan and forecast, focusing on key business drivers Prepare quarterly and annual financial reports Conduct data analysis to identify trends, anomalies, and opportunities for finance process improvement Understand key systems, how they are used, and attributes to ensure data integrity and accuracy Performs other duties as assigned. Qualifications Education and Experience Required: Bachelor’s Degree in Arts/Sciences (BA/BS) Bachelor’s Degree in Accounting, Finance or equivalent related work experience (CPA preferred) 5+ Years experience with finance and/or accounting Advanced proficiency in excel and other systems that support financial modeling (i.e. PowerBI, Sigma, Oracle, Onestream) Strong understanding of financial principles, accounting practices, and financial statement analysis Preferred: Master’s degree in Arts/Sciences (MA/MS) Insurance/reinsurance industry experience Investment accounting experience CPA, MBA, or CFA Skills and Abilities Required: Excellent analytical, problem-solving and communication skills Strong analytical and critical thinking skills to solve complex financial challenges Advanced technical skills in handling large datasets, with expertise in Excel, DAX, Power Query, and data visualization Advanced skills in managing multiple tasks and projects simultaneously, including the ability to delegate key areas of responsibility. Advanced persuasion skills when working with internal and external partners to resolve issues/problems. Advanced oral and written communication and facilitation skills, demonstrating the ability to convey business terminology that is meaningful and well received by internal and external contacts. Advanced ability to analyze and improve business processes Advanced ability to liaise with individuals across a wide variety of operational, functional, and technical disciplines.

About this role: Wells Fargo is seeking an Associate Executive Specialist to provide administrative support to executives by coordinating activities, preparing business materials, and supporting administrative deliverables. You will organize information and follow-up activities, handle confidential information, and work with others to support leader priorities and team commitments. To learn more about the career areas and lines of business at wellsfargojobs.com . In this role, you will: Support executives with calendar management, meeting coordination, travel arrangements, calls, correspondence, expenses, and related administrative activities. Prepare presentations, reports, meeting materials, and communications by gathering and organizing information from multiple sources. Coordinate administrative deliverables and follow-up activities that support leader priorities and team commitments. Handle confidential information in accordance with applicable policies, procedures, and compliance requirements. Work within manager guidance while developing knowledge of the supported function and its priorities. Collaborate with team members, peers, and internal or external contacts to resolve routine administrative issues and support business goals. Required Qualifications: 6+ months of executive administration experience, or equivalent demonstrated through one or a combination of the following: work experience, training, military experience, education. Desired Qualifications: Experience supporting calendars, meetings, records, correspondence, supplies, or other routine administrative activities. Skill in preparing reports, correspondence, meeting materials, or similar business documents. Experience following established policies, procedures, risk requirements, or controls. Ability to organize and interpret information to support business activities and decisions. Experience coordinating projects or activities and working with stakeholders to support business needs.

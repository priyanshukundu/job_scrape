The Opportunity As a dedicated Treasury Fixed Income Trader , you’ll be a member of the team that monitors and communicates market conditions, conducts trade analyses, and executes fixed income trading activities within established risk, policy, and regulatory guidelines that are appropriate for a depository institution. You’ll help develop recommendations that inform portfolio allocation and investment decision-marking but also be fully engaged in the control environment of USAA’s trading function. Your working knowledge of fixed income markets, liquidity portfolio management, and balance sheet management will be utilized to evaluate market opportunities and contribute to portfolio strategy. Collaboration with senior traders and other stakeholders on portfolio objectives and investment decision-making will be ongoing. This role is remote eligible in the continental U.S. with occasional business travel. However, individuals residing within a 60-mile radius of a USAA office will be expected to work on-site 4 days per week. What you'll do: Monitor fixed income markets, economic developments, and security-level activity to identify trends for investment decision-making. Evaluate securities, analyze market conditions, and help formulate recommendations regarding portfolio allocation, security selection, and trading opportunities for investment decision-making. Produce regular reporting and PowerPoint slide analysis of market conditions, economic data releases, and portfolio performance metrics for stakeholders. Conduct pre-trade analyses, assesses market opportunities and risks, and develop recommendations for portfolio purchases, sales, and positioning decisions. Execute trades within established trading limits, policies, and risk parameters and coordinate with brokers and internal stakeholders to facilitate timely execution, settlement, and reporting. Maintain portfolio management systems, trade records, and analytical tools used to monitor portfolio performance and risk. Monitor portfolio positioning, including cash flow projections, duration, liquidity metrics, and investment guideline compliance, and analyze findings to inform trading decisions and pre-trade investment discussions. Prepares and presents analyses, reports, and presentation materials for management committees and leadership updates. Partner with Treasury, Finance, Risk, and Investment teams to support portfolio objectives and investment strategies. Support regulatory, audit, compliance, and policy-related requests through documentation and reporting activities. Identify and escalate operational, market, or compliance concerns to appropriate stakeholders. Ensure risks associated with business activities are effectively identified, measured, monitored, and controlled in accordance with risk and compliance policies and procedures. What you have: Bachelor's degree in Business, Accounting, Finance, Economics, or a related field; OR 4 years of relevant education and/or experience. 2-4 years of experience in trading fixed income securities. Proficient knowledge of fixed income securities, including U.S. Treasuries, Agency mortgage-backed securities (MBS), Agency commercial mortgage-backed securities (ACMBS), non-agency residential mortgage-backed securities (RMBS), and corporate bonds. Working knowledge of bank balance sheet management, liquidity management, and asset allocation principles. Understanding of fixed income market structure, security pricing, valuation concepts, and trading practices. Knowledge of economic indicators and interest rate dynamics and their impact on investment portfolios. Knowledge of accounting classifications, capital considerations, liquidity metrics, and risk management concepts applicable to fixed income portfolios. Experience performing financial analysis and interpreting market data to support decision-making. Ability to communicate market observations and analytical findings to internal stakeholders. Working knowledge of trade lifecycle processes, including execution, settlement, custody, accounting, and reporting. Demonstrated ability to collaborate across teams and contribute to projects involving multiple business partners. Proficiency with Microsoft Office applications, particularly Excel and PowerPoint. What sets you apart: US military experience gained through military service or gained as a military spouse / domestic partner. Progress toward an advanced degree and/or CFA designation.

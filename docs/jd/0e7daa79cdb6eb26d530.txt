We are more than 121,000 professionals working in multidisciplinary teams with profiles as diverse as financiers, legal experts, data scientists, developers, engineers and designers. About the job: Job Summary We are seeking a highly motivated and detail-oriented Repo Trading Associate to join our Fixed Income trading team. This role will involve supporting the trading desk in all aspects of Rates, Credit and EM repo trading, ensuring efficient execution and risk management. Job Responsibilities Execute repo trades as directed by senior traders, adhering to all internal and external regulations. Monitor market conditions, news, and economic data relevant to fixed income and repo markets. Manage and reconcile trading positions, ensuring accuracy and timely settlement. Prepare and analyze reports on trading activity, P&L, and market trends. Maintain relationships with counterparties and brokers. Assist in the development and implementation of new trading strategies and products. Ensure compliance with all regulatory requirements and internal policies. Collaborate with sales, operations, and risk management teams to ensure seamless execution and support. Identify and escalate any potential risks or issues to senior management. Stay abreast of industry best practices and technological advancements in fixed income trading. Job Qualifications Bachelor's degree in Finance, Economics, Mathematics, or a related quantitative field. 4+ years of experience in fixed income trading, with exposure to repo markets. Strong understanding of financial markets, instruments, and trading principles. Proficiency in Microsoft Excel and other relevant trading platforms (e.g., Bloomberg, Refinitiv). Excellent analytical, problem-solving, and decision-making skills. Strong communication and interpersonal skills, with the ability to work effectively in a fast-paced environment. High level of accuracy and attention to detail. Ability to work under pressure and manage multiple tasks simultaneously. Fluency in English is required; proficiency in Spanish is a plus. Knowledge of relevant regulatory frameworks (e.g., MiFID II, EMIR) is advantageous.

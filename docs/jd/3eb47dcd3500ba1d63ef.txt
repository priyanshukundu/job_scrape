Associate Director, Market Research, Endocrinology
Vertex Pharmaceuticals
Location: Boston, MA
Link: https://vrtx.wd501.myworkdayjobs.com/Vertex_Careers/job/Boston-MA/Associate-Director--Market-Research--Endocrinology_REQ-30706-1

Job Description General Summary: The Associate Director, Market Research, will lead the development and execution of comprehensive market research plans supporting the Endocrinology Business. This role will provide strategic insights for both in-line commercial products and pipeline programs across multiple stages of development and commercialization. Working closely with Marketing, Market Access, Sales, Medical Affairs, Commercial Analytics, Regulatory, Corporate Affairs, Legal, and other cross-functional partners, the Director will translate complex market information into objective, data-driven, and actionable insights. These insights will inform commercial strategy, brand planning, product development, launch readiness, lifecycle management, and business decision-making. This individual will serve as a strategic thought partner to commercial and cross-functional leadership, ensuring that customer, patient, market, and competitive insights are integrated into key strategic and tactical decisions. Key Duties and Responsibilities: Develop comprehensive global market research strategies and frameworks to support the Endocrinology Business, including in-line products, launch assets, and pipeline programs. Lead the design and execution of primary and secondary market research in partnership with Commercial, Marketing, Market Access, Sales, Medical Affairs, Commercial Analytics, Regulatory, Corporate Affairs, Legal, and other relevant functions. Partner with cross-functional stakeholders to identify and prioritize critical business questions across therapeutic areas, customer segments, product-development stages, launch planning, and lifecycle management. Establish clear research objectives, methodologies, timelines, and deliverables that align with business priorities. Design and conduct quantitative and qualitative research across relevant stakeholder groups, including physicians, patients, caregivers, allied healthcare professionals, pharmacists, payers, and patient advocacy organizations. Integrate primary research, secondary research, competitive intelligence, market data, and internal analytics to develop a comprehensive understanding of the market. Ensure that research findings and strategic implications are objective, methodologically sound, supported by data, and directly connected to the underlying business questions. Translate complex research findings into clear, actionable recommendations for commercial and cross-functional stakeholders. Develop and deliver compelling presentations to senior leadership, global product teams, brand teams, and cross-functional partners to influence strategic and tactical decision-making. Support commercial strategy development, market-shaping initiatives, brand planning, indication expansion, launch readiness, and lifecycle-management decisions. Identify evolving market dynamics, customer needs, patient experiences, competitive developments, and other emerging trends that may affect the Endocrinology Business. Partner with Sales and Commercial Analytics leaders to develop, track, and monitor key performance indicators for commercial strategies and tactics. Assess the effectiveness of commercial initiatives and recommend adjustments based on market research, customer feedback, and performance data. Identify and select qualified market research suppliers and manage vendor relationships, project scope, budgets, timelines, and deliverables. Ensure research suppliers perform according to agreed-upon quality, financial, ethical, and compliance expectations. Manage market research investments in a cost-effective manner and in accordance with applicable company policies and industry standards. Ensure all market research activities comply with financial, legal, regulatory, privacy, and ethical requirements. Build organizational understanding of market research capabilities and promote the effective use of insights in decision-making. Perform other responsibilities as assigned. Knowledge and Skills: Demonstrated experience supporting both commercial products and pipeline assets across different stages of development and commercialization. Extensive experience designing and conducting quantitative and qualitative market research. Experience conducting research across multiple customer and stakeholder groups, including physicians, patients, caregivers, allied healthcare professionals, pharmacists, payers, and advocacy organizations. Experience using standard and non-standard pharmaceutical data sources to generate actionable commercial insights. Strong understanding of pharmaceutical market assessment, situational analysis, brand planning, launch preparation, and lifecycle management. Experience managing market research agencies, external suppliers, project budgets, timelines, and deliverables. Ability to analyze complex business issues, synthesize information from multiple sources, and convert findings into practical strategic recommendations. Strong presentation and communication skills, with the ability to communicate complex findings clearly and persuasively to senior leaders and cross-functional stakeholders. Demonstrated ability to influence decisions and build alignment in a matrixed, cross-functional, and global environment. Strong judgment and demonstrated commitment to research integrity, compliance, and ethical decision-making. Experience supporting endocrinology or specialty-care products. Experience supporting global market research initiatives. Education and Experience: Bachelor’s degree in business, marketing, life sciences, healthcare, statistics, behavioral science, or a related discipline. At least 8 years of relevant experience in market research, marketing sciences, commercial insights, business analytics, or a related discipline within the pharmaceutical or biotechnology industry.

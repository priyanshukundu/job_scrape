Overview This position provides complex technical support to data processing systems through enhancement of related software and processes. Develops software and procedures that improve processing capabilities. Analyzes, codes, tests, and implements in coordination with management, associates, and end users. Serves as production system support by resolving issues and ensuring ongoing functionality. May oversee the work of less experienced analysts or assist in special projects as needed. This role requires experience developing, administering, and supporting Hyland OnBase solutions, including workflow automation, integrations, document management, and enterprise content management (ECM) platforms. The associate will partner with business and technical teams to design, develop, implement, and support OnBase-based solutions across the enterprise. Responsibilities & Qualifications Responsibility: System Enhancement - Performs a variety of actions that evaluate and improve department systems, processes, and applications. Utilizes programming specifications to construct modules, develop testing methods, and design materials. Provides new feature time estimates for system changes and assists in implementing modifications. Executes test plans to validate application functions as designed. Designs, develops, and supports Hyland OnBase solutions including Workflow, WorkView, Document Import Processor (DIP), Unity Forms, Application Enabler, and custom integrations. Analysis - Collects data related to user requests and determines scope, time estimates, and system impacts. Inspects business specifications, programming specifications, coding, test plans, documentation, and implementation plans for accuracy. Evaluates business requirements and translates them into scalable OnBase solutions and automated business processes. Business Support - Provides technical support to production systems by addressing reported issues, anticipating maintenance requirements, and ensuring functionality for end user needs. Drafts documentation to be used for system, end user, and operational purposes, including technical specifications and application designs. Supports OnBase production environments and troubleshooting activities including workflow processing, document ingestion, integration services, and performance optimization. Technical Expertise - Responsible for complex involvement in the software development life cycle including the creation, enhancement, implementation, and evaluation of software. Serves as a technical resource for management, associates, and business units. Provides subject matter expertise on Hyland OnBase architecture, Unity API development, REST API integrations, WorkView applications, and enterprise content management best practices. On-call Support – Primarily for change management implementation and deployment.

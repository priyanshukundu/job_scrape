Use Your Power for Purpose At Pfizer, our purpose is to deliver breakthroughs that change patients’ lives. The Manufacturing Sciences and Technology (MSAT) in Andover in-silico Modeling team is seeking a Senior Associate Scientist to support the development and application of computational modeling, scientific software, and data-driven tools for biopharmaceutical manufacturing. The successful candidate will combine strong computational problem-solving skills with an interest in applying engineering and scientific principles to bioprocessing. The role will contribute to multiphase computational fluid dynamics (CFD), bioreactor hydrodynamic characterization, scientific application development, CAD/geometry development, and the creation and management of digital modeling assets. This position is at the intersection of process science, numerical modeling, software development, and visualization, and the ideal candidate is someone who can translate complex computational capabilities into practical tools that scientists and engineers can use. What You Will Achieve In this role, you will: Develop, execute, and analyze CFD models and simulations, including multiphase models, to support hydrodynamic characterization of bioreactors and other bioprocess equipment. Support development of computational workflows for evaluating engineering quantities relevant to bioprocess characterization and scale-up. Create and maintain CAD geometries, meshes, model configurations, and other digital assets needed for in-silico modeling. Develop scientific software and user-facing applications using Python and related technologies to make modeling and data-analysis capabilities accessible to a broader scientific audience. Design reusable, maintainable computational workflows using sound software-engineering practices, including modular and object-oriented programming approaches. Support development of interactive engineering and scientific applications using technologies such as Python/Dash, Microsoft Power Platform, and visualization frameworks. Build visualization approaches that allow scientists and engineers to efficiently explore simulation, equipment, and experimental data. Collaborate with MSAT scientists, process engineers, modelers, software developers, and manufacturing stakeholders to translate technical questions into computational approaches. Document models, assumptions, methods, code, and results in a manner that supports reproducibility, knowledge sharing, and continued development. Present modeling approaches and findings to technical stakeholders and communicate complex computational concepts clearly to both specialist and non-specialist audiences. Evaluate emerging computational methods and software technologies and contribute to continuous improvement of the team's in-silico modeling capabilities. Here Is What You Need (Minimum Requirements) Applicant must have a bachelor's degree with at least 2 years of experience; OR a master's degree with 0+ years of experience; OR an associate's degree with 6 years of experience; OR a high school diploma (or equivalent) and 8 years of relevant experience in Chemical Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Bioengineering, Computational Science, Applied Mathematics, Computer Science, Architecture/Design Technology, or another quantitatively intensive discipline, with relevant computational experience. Demonstrated programming and software development experience with Python, Dash, Microsoft PowerApps/Power Platform, JavaScript, or comparable technologies. Experience developing computational models, simulations, engineering applications, scientific software, or automation tools. Experience working with 3D geometry and/or CAD-based computational workflows. Experience with computational fluid dynamics (CFD), especially multiphase flow or mixing applications in software such as MStar CFD. Experience modeling stirred vessels, bioreactors, fluid systems, or other process equipment. Familiarity with numerical modeling concepts such as meshing, boundary conditions, model configuration, convergence, sensitivity analysis, and model interpretation. Experience with object-oriented programming, software architecture, version-controlled development, or API-based development. Experience automating workflows within engineering, CAD, simulation, or scientific software environments. Strong analytical and quantitative problem-solving skills. Ability to work effectively across scientific, engineering, and software-development disciplines. Strong written and verbal communication skills, including the ability to clearly document and present technical work. Bonus Points If You Have (Preferred Requirements) Demonstrated ability to learn new scientific domains and apply computational methods to complex, real-world engineering problems. Experience in biopharmaceutical manufacturing, MSAT, process development, bioreactor engineering, or scale-up.

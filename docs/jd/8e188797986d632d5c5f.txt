Business Analyst, Legal Technology About the Role As a member of the Service Delivery team within the Global Legal, Compliance & Regulatory organization, you will support AIG's global corporate governance and entity management systems. This role serves as a key partner to Corporate Secretary, Legal, and business stakeholders, ensuring the accuracy and integrity of corporate records, supporting governance processes, and managing entity lifecycle events across a complex global organization. The role also acts as the primary administrator for AIG's entity management platform, leveraging technology and data to enable efficient governance, reporting, and compliance activities. Key Responsibilities: Act as primary administrator for AIG's entity management platform, ensuring data integrity, quality, and system functionality. Manage production, quality assurance, and delivery of governance and entity management reporting. Analyze entity data, identify inconsistencies, and implement sustainable solutions to improve data quality. Partner with technology teams and business stakeholders to identify and implement process improvements and system enhancements. Lead user acceptance testing, platform upgrades, maintenance releases, and reporting enhancements. Maintain technical and process documentation and provide support for user inquiries and system-related issues. Support board management technology platforms in partnership with technology teams and governance stakeholders. Qualifications: Required Prior experience as a system administrator or platform owner with a customer focused mindset Strong organizational, analytical, and problem-solving skills. Ability to interact professionally with senior leaders and executive stakeholders. Strong verbal and written communication skills. Bachelor's degree or equivalent experience. Preferred Experience supporting corporate governance, corporate secretary, or entity management functions within a legal department, law firm, financial services organization, or other regulated environment. Experience managing corporate records, entity lifecycle events, and governance documentation. Experience working within a global, highly regulated corporate environment. Understanding of data analytics Experience serving as an administrator for an entity management platform such as Diligent Entities, GEMS, Blueprint OneWorld, or similar systems.

Job Description As an Material Damage - Associate Manager with National General, you’ll lead and support a team of claims professionals responsible for delivering accurate, timely, and customer-focused claim outcomes. You’ll provide day-to-day leadership, coaching, and guidance while helping the team navigate claim complexity, balance workloads, and consistently execute against business expectations. In this role, you’ll partner closely with claims professionals and business leaders to support operational performance, strengthen technical capabilities, and promote consistent claim handling practices. You’ll monitor team results, identify opportunities for improvement, provide coaching and development, and help remove barriers that may impact the customer or employee experience. This will be an ON-SITE position, located in our Winston Salem, NC office. Key Responsibilities • Ensures adherence to empathetic and responsive customer service in all transactions • Manages processes to ensure delivery of compassionate service that is fast, fair and easy • Proactively identifies and manages risk while eliminating barriers to help achieve business results • Develops and maintains an environment of ethics and integrity that embraces all attributes of our shared vision • Develops, maintains and analyzes operational and statistical data used by market claim office (MCO) and CSA staff • Eliminates barriers to achieving business results • Manages and resolves escalated customer communications, concerns, conflicts or issues • Ensures and enables staff to maintain a connection with the customer by understanding and meeting their needs • Consistently meets band level behaviors, production, quality and/or customer service goals • Effectively coaches and provides development/career guidance for direct reports Experience • 5 or more years of experience (Preferred) Supervisory Responsibilities • This job has supervisory duties.

We are recruiting for a Risk & Controls Analyst – Financial Crime Operations to join our COO team. This is a great opportunity for someone with strong experience in risk, controls, governance, assurance or audit who is confident supporting a robust control environment across a complex financial services business. The role sits within Financial Crime Compliance Operations, but we are equally interested in those who bring strong transferable risk and controls experience and are keen to develop their knowledge of financial crime. With global scope and a strong focus on supporting our private markets business, FCC Ops helps Aviva Investors navigate financial crime risk with confidence. From onboarding investors and counterparties to overseeing AML, KYC and sanctions controls with third parties, and driving operational improvements, every project is an opportunity to protect the business while enabling investment activity and growth. A bit about the role: In this role, you will support a robust and well-evidenced risk and control environment across Financial Crime Compliance Operations. You will complete control design walkthroughs and operating-effectiveness testing, coordinate risk and control assessments, and track risks, issues and actions through to evidenced closure. You will also prepare governance materials and monthly management information, maintain controlled documentation and records, complete data reconciliations, and support data-retention activities, Records of Processing and Data Protection Impact Assessments. Working with Controls Assurance, Risk and Governance, second-line Financial Crime, Data Protection and audit teams, you will identify control weaknesses, recommend proportionate improvements and help embed effective risk, control and privacy considerations into business-as-usual and change activity. Skills and experience we're looking for: Practical experience in risk, controls, governance, assurance or audit within financial services, ideally investment or asset management. Hands-on experience of controls testing or assurance, including assessing control design and operating effectiveness and producing clear, well-evidenced documentation. Experience completing risk and control assessments , identifying control weaknesses and managing risks, issues and actions through to evidenced closure. Experience using risk, control, issue or action-management systems and maintaining accurate records to support effective oversight and governance. Strong data, documentation and communication skills, including reconciliations, accurate management information and clear governance reporting. Excel skills are required and Power BI experience is desirable. An understanding of financial crime risks, including anti-money laundering and sanctions, alongside knowledge of data protection, Records of Processing, Data Protection Impact Assessments and retention requirements, would be valuable but is not essential.

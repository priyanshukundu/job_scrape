JPMorganChase's Corporate Location Management program unites people, community, and workplace to build world-class corporate campuses that operate as single, connected communities. Joining this team means gaining broad visibility across lines of business and functions while directly shaping the employee experience at scale. As a Corporate Location Management Associate at JPMorganChase within the Corporate Location Management team, you will manage initiatives across the People, Community, and Workplace program pillars. You will serve as a central point of contact for all location matters, supporting the Location Manager and Location Leader by coordinating with firmwide and local partners to ensure smooth Corporate Center operations. Your work will have a direct impact on campus culture and the day-to-day employee experience, while giving you meaningful exposure to senior leadership and cross-functional collaboration across the firm. Job responsibilities Manage executive presentations and coordinate senior leader site visits, ensuring a seamless and professionally executed experience. Support end-to-end location management by driving alignment across on-site business functions and acting as the primary point of contact for location matters and issue resolution. Drive employee engagement and elevate campus culture by planning and facilitating site-wide events and initiatives, serving as the voice of the employee. Develop and deliver communications in partnership with local communications teams to keep the campus community informed, aligned, and connected. Lead the rollout of site-specific and firmwide initiatives, coordinating across stakeholders to ensure consistent and effective execution. Coordinate infrastructure improvement projects and workplace enhancements in partnership with the Chief Administrative Office and HR Employee Experience teams. Partner with firmwide Analyst and Intern programs to provide on-site leadership support and contribute to a strong early-career experience. Collaborate with Business Resource Groups, volunteer leadership groups, and other employee networks to facilitate and promote engagement and community-building opportunities.

What you’ll do We’re looking for a Staff Software Engineer to operate across Acquisition Engineering. You’ll set technical direction, raise the bar for system design and execution, and multiply the effectiveness of engineers and engineering managers across the group. Where you’ll work This role will be based in San Francisco .

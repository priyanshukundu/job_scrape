The Opportunity Provides contributions in the design, coding, testing, support and debugging of new software or enhancements to existing software. The Work Itself Contribute to software engineering efforts to deliver well-defined products that generate immediate customer value and deliver high-quality, broadly applicable software contributions. Participate in agile ceremonies to scope, plan, and commit to a backlog for delivery. Participate in DevOps principals practiced by the engineering organization. Collaborates with other Engineers, Technical Leads, Architects, QA, Performance Testers, Scrum Masters, and Product Owners. Uses technical knowledge, creativity and company practices and priorities to obtain solutions to complex problems. Interfaces with senior management to provide and obtain information and to build consensus regarding project direction. Escalate issues to senior management early, before they create significant impact to a project/release. Review and approve all mid-level design documentation. Ensure that detail design documentation is competed, reviewed, and approved, as required. Conduct or participate in code review sessions to ensure quality and performance. Participate and contribute to innovation workshops and hackathons Support the system integration and user acceptance testing resources. Provide timely defect analysis, remediation, and reporting. Review security testing reports/results and remediate potential vulnerabilities in a timely manner. Serves as a mentor to lower level developers. Interacts directly with business clients. The Qualifications We’re Looking For 1+ years of applicable experience and a bachelor’s degree in technical discipline, or an equivalent combination of experience and education. Knowledge of all phases of software development including design, coding, testing, debugging, implementation, and support Experience writing and reviewing software design specifications in an object-oriented development environment 1+ years on experience with Robocorp and Python programming, which is commonly used with Robocorp. Object oriented programming with Microsoft .Net (VB and C#) or VBA, Java Scripting, Python Familiarity with web service interaction (JSON/XML) Strong use of Microsoft Office productivity tools including, Excel, Word, Access, PowerPoint Strong Problem-Solving and Analytical Skills Effective communication skills for technical and non-technical audiences Strong written and verbal communications skills Understanding and ability to work under varying project delivery methodologies (Agile, Waterfall) Proficient understanding of code versioning tools such as GIT. Experience translating product requirements into design specifications. Debugging and problem-solving skills. Ensure quality software is delivered with minimal defects and no go live critical issues. Knowledgeable of unit and automation testing tools.

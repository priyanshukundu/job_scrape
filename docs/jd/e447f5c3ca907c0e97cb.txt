About this role Company: BlackRock Financial Management, Inc. Job Title: Associate Location: 100 Bellevue Parkway, Wilmington, DE 19809 Job Duties: Validating, maintaining, and enhancing the accuracy, completeness, and timeliness of financial instrument reference data used across investment, risk, and operational workflows. Partners with global stakeholders and leverages data analysis and automation to improve operational efficiency and reduce data quality risk. Perform daily and recurring quality control (QC) reviews on security level reference data to ensure accuracy and consistency across internal data systems and downstream consumers. Monitor, oversee, and take action on US time zone–specific critical QC checks and automation jobs to ensure timely issue detection, resolution, and operational continuity. Investigate data discrepancies, exceptions, and breaks identified through automated and manual QC reports, and determine appropriate remediation actions in accordance with established data governance standards. Analyze trends in data quality issues to identify root causes, recurring patterns, and opportunities for process improvement or automation. Develop and maintain automation solutions using Python, SQL, and related data tools to streamline QC checks, reduce manual effort, and improve operational scalability. Execute data extraction, transformation, and validation tasks using structured query language (SQL) and internal data platforms to support QC workflows and ad hoc investigations. Partner with internal technology, data engineering, and product teams to support enhancements to QC logic, resolve data issues, and ensure alignment between business requirements and technical implementations. Coordinate with global DIG team members to ensure consistent application of QC standards and timely resolution of data issues across regions. Prepare documentation and written explanations of data issues, QC logic, and remediation outcomes for internal stakeholders, audit reviews, and operational transparency. Support audit and compliance inquiries by providing data evidence, analysis, and explanations related to DIG quality control processes. Participate in continuous improvement initiatives aimed at enhancing data quality frameworks, reporting metrics, and automation coverage within the DIG QC function. Qualifications: Bachelor's degree in Business Analytics, Finance, Data Analytics or a related field and 24 months of experience. Experience with Python and SQL including pandas, NumPy, pyodbc, paramiko, SQL Server, data validation, and ETL scripting; Data Analysis and Risk Management tools and techniques including trend analysis, exception analysis, team metrics analysis, risk identification, and mitigation; Financial services experience including reference data, securities data, fund data, market data and investment operations; Aha! and Azure DevOps (ADO) experience including requirements tracking, backlog management, and user stories; Client Data Support including ad-hoc issue investigation, client queries, SLA-driven support, and client QC demo and training; Tableau and Power BI; Data Quality Control and Validation including reconciliations, rule-based QC, and exception management; Automation and Process Improvement including workflow optimization, and manual-to-automated transitions; Data Governance and Controls including data ownership, asset class subject matter expert, and controls documentation; Operational Risk Awareness including identifying control gaps, escalation, impact assessment, and preventive controls; and Bloomberg Terminal.

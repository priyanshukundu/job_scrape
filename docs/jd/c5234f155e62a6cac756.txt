Senior Staff Audio Machine Learning Engineer
Boston Dynamics
Location: Waltham, MA
Link: https://bostondynamics.wd1.myworkdayjobs.com/Boston_Dynamics/job/Waltham-MA/Senior-Staff-Audio-Machine-Learning-Engineer_R3349

Role Overview Boston Dynamics is looking for a Senior Staff Audio Machine Learning Engineer to join the Spot Sensing team. In this role, your core mission will be driving the evolution of our Acoustic Change Detection (ACD) capability—taking beamformed and raw audio and turning it into actionable machinery prognostics. While acoustic intelligence is your primary domain , mechanical inspection on Spot is multimodal. Beyond mastering the audio pipeline, you will contribute to the integration of acoustic features with complementary modalities, such as image, video, and thermal, fusing multimodal representations with LLMs/VLMs to empower our end users with prescriptive maintenance actions, not indecipherable data. You will leverage the world’s largest fleet-gathered industrial sensor dataset, utilizing Spot equipped with the Sorama L642 acoustic camera and SpotCAM2 . Key Responsibilities Acoustic ML & DSP Ownership (Core Focus): Design, refine, and scale digital signal processing and machine learning pipelines for our Acoustic Change Detection (ACD) system. Anomaly Detection & Failure Classification: Lead the improvement and modernization of our acoustic detection stack—transitioning from unsupervised models (e.g., BGMM) to state-of-the-art anomaly architectures, and architecting our end-to-end supervised multimodal pipelines for automated fault diagnosis. Multimodal Integration & Prescriptive Maintenance: Partner with the broader Spot Apps organization to integrate acoustic insights with visual, thermal, and VLM/LLM data streams into unified machine health indicators, delivering both clear mechanical diagnoses and recommended corrective actions to plant operators. Industrial Deployment & Verification: Benchmark, optimize, ship, monitor, and continuously improve our solution for our industrial customers. Qualifications & Requirements Experience & Domain Expertise Experience: 7+ years of deep practical expertise in audio feature extraction, audio fingerprinting (e.g., Shazam, DMCA enforcement) and segmentation (e.g., DCASE), machine learning applied to audio data (beamformed and raw), preferably in industrial environments or another domain with similar problems, for the purposes of anomaly detection. Technical Skills & Hardware Languages & Core Tooling: Advanced proficiency in Python and C++, with mastery of numerical and scientific stacks (e.g., NumPy, SciPy, scikit-learn, PyTorch). Acoustic Processing: Expertise in time domain and frequency domain data analysis. Array Processing: Expertise in multi-channel array processing, including beamforming (Delay-and-Sum, MVDR, null-steering), Direction of Arrival (DoA) estimation, and synthetic aperture techniques. Machine Learning & Modeling Paradigms: Expertise in unsupervised and supervised classification architectures for anomaly detection in real-world conditions. Bonus Points Hands-on experience in cross-modal fusion (combining audio representations with computer vision, thermal imaging, or multimodal foundation models/VLMs). Background in rotating machinery health monitoring (bearings, gearboxes, pumps) and familiarity with mechanical vibration/acoustic failure standards. Familiarity with TypeScript/modern web services for integrating your work into Orbit, our fleet management software.

Join Risk Management and Compliance and help keep JPMorganChase strong, resilient, and customer-centered. As an Analytics Associate, Card Risk Management in Chase Card Services, Risk Management and Compliance (Collections Risk Criteria Team), you will develop and execute customer outreach strategies across channels while building short-term and long-term analytics to measure performance, manage risk, and guide strategy decisions. You will design test-and-learn programs, define eligibility and targeting frameworks, monitor operational and customer impact, and deliver leadership-ready insights and recommendations. You will partner closely with Operations, Legal, Compliance, Product, Analytics, Technology, and Credit Strategy to ensure end-to-end execution within the firm’s risk appetite and regulatory expectations. Job Responsibilities Develop multi-channel outreach strategies (call, text/SMS, email, letters) aligned to business objectives, customer experience, and risk appetite. Analyze short-term outreach performance to diagnose drivers, identify issues/opportunities, and recommend rapid iterations to improve outcomes. Build long-term measurement frameworks (KPIs, definitions, attribution, governance) that enable durable performance monitoring and roadmap decisions. Design test-and-learn experiments (segmentation, channel mix, cadence, messaging, timing) including hypotheses, success metrics, and evaluation plans. Execute experimentation measurement (test/control design, eligibility rules, operational feasibility checks) and synthesize results into actions. Optimize engagement, cures, recoveries, and cost-to-collect while balancing customer impact, operational constraints, and compliance requirements. Define eligibility and targeting criteria to ensure the right customers receive the right treatment at the right time, incorporating risk controls. Monitor KPIs such as contact rate, right-party contact, response rate, promise-to-pay, cure/roll rates, recovery dollars, complaints, opt-outs, and capacity impacts. Deliver recurring reporting and insights for senior management, highlighting trends, risks, tradeoffs, and recommended actions. Conduct risk assessments for regulatory, operational, customer harm, and reputational risks; implement mitigations and document controls. Collaborate cross-functionally to align strategy, execution, and governance across stakeholders and adapt to policy/regulatory changes.

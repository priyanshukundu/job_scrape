About the Role Remitly Business is building a two-sided platform that helps businesses and individuals in emerging markets move money across borders. On the send side, we let businesses pay vendors, suppliers, and contractors abroad. On the receive side, we let businesses and sellers get paid in USD, hold balances in stable currency, and withdraw across Remitly's 170+ country disbursement network. As a Staff Software Engineer on Remitly Business, you will set the technical direction for the product engineering that powers this marketplace. You'll lead the architecture of our Go services, APIs, and durable money-movement workflows on Temporal — and, as a well-rounded product engineer, you'll drive the frontend architecture decisions that shape our React/TypeScript applications, even though backend is your home base. This is hands-on technical leadership, not an architecture-only role: you'll code, lead large initiatives from design through launch, raise the bar through design reviews and mentorship, and use AI coding agents as a genuine force multiplier — for yourself and, by setting standards, for the whole team. You will work in a hybrid capacity from our Seattle office. You Will Own the overall technology of the team: lead large, multi-quarter initiatives — planning and sequencing work across engineers to deliver faster, and driving them from design through launch. Design systems that address both current and future needs: money-movement flows at scale on Temporal — long-running, idempotent, observable workflows with correct ledgering, reconciliation, and compliance gating. Drive architecture decisions across the full product surface — service boundaries, API and GraphQL contracts, data models, and the frontend architecture choices (rendering, state, client-facing API shape) they imply — partnering with our fullstack engineers on the React/TypeScript side. Lead team design reviews, ensuring proposed solutions avoid unnecessary complexity and align with Remitly's long-term architecture plans; frame build-vs-buy decisions and assess new technologies for feasibility. Make AI-assisted, spec-driven development (Claude Code, Codex, and similar agents) a core part of how you and the team work — designing team-level standards for AI-assisted development, identifying where AI delivers durable gains, and advising management on how AI adoption changes capacity and estimation. Uphold and contribute to the evolution of our Foundational Excellence standards for security, reliability, and observability; mitigate long-term risks caused by complexity before they become incidents. Actively mentor senior and mid-level engineers; drive efforts to expand the team's knowledge and skills; represent Remitly's values and technical bar in interviews. Help shape short- and long-term roadmaps with Product, Design, Analytics, Compliance, Infrastructure, and Operations — communicating trade-offs concisely and adapting to technical and non-technical audiences.

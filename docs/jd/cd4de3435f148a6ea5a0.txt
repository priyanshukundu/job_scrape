Overview Some risks don’t come with a rulebook, and that’s where you come in. As a Bespoke Risk Analyst at Susquehanna Predictions, you’ll price one-of-a-kind events across everything from legislation and regulation to event cancellations and commodity-linked risks. The ideal candidate has a strong underwriting, statistical, or actuarial foundation with a passion for prediction markets. What matters most is curiosity, sound probabilistic judgment and a desire to solve problems without a playbook available. risks Set the right odds when asked to hedge or take on a bespoke exposure, leveraging data and models to make accurate predictions, and update your probabilities as situations evolve Work with external experts in politics, law, or industry to diligence situations and pressure-test your views Partner with quantitative traders and strategists to turn views into trading and risk-management decisions Build pricing frameworks and analysis in Excel, Python, or other relevant tools What we're looking for 3+ years of experience in underwriting, particularly in specialty insurance, or in a related role such as corporate exotics, structured products, or event-driven risk at a bank or hedge fund A strong foundation in probability, statistics, and actuarial or risk-pricing methods, and the prediction markets and financial markets (traditional markets experience is welcome but not required) Clear communication skills, with the ability to explain your reasoning to traders and colleagues

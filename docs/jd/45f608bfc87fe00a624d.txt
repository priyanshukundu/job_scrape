MFS Investment Management

We put people first and demonstrate care and compassion for our community and each other. Because what we do matters – to us as valued professionals and to the millions of people and institutions who rely on us to help them build more secure and prosperous futures. THE ROLE Apply sound judgment and discretion to address business challenges for the Cash Processing and Collateral Management Platform and the Equity SMA Platform by analyzing processes and environments and leveraging technology across business units. Lead the evolution of business processes that reimagines how MFS ingests and processes cash data, strengthens cash and collateral controls, and improves transparency across key cash views (e.g., trade-date, settlement-date, tradable, and encumbered/collateral cash) and position-level activity. In addition, evaluate processes and technology to deliver a more scalable future target end state for the highly customized and growing Retail SMA business. Working in partnership with stakeholders across Operations, Investments, Compliance, and Technology, you will translate business needs into clear outcomes, actionable priorities, and well-defined requirements. Further, help maintain and support operational risk reduction portal, cash management, collateral management and product master systems. Serve as a leader in an Agile environment for small to midsized projects, the candidate collaborates with product owners, developers and quality assurance teams to deliver solutions within tight deadlines. WHAT YOU WILL DO Performs analysis of business activities to determine business requirements, current and future state processes and areas of risk mitigation. Works within their Agile Team to understand and define needs above and beyond requirements provided by the business. Converts business requirements into functional specifications. Works closely with the project team members, business users, developers, QA and other technology professionals in an Agile environment to ensure project deliverables are completed on time and within budget. Working with the Product Owner, acts as the liaison between business stakeholders and the technology organization to ensure proper implementation of requirements within the expected timetable. Communicates any material modifications of business requirements to business stakeholders. Formulates and defines systems scope and objectives based on both user needs and an understanding of applicable business systems and industry requirements. Works to ensure a positive end-product for business stakeholders by overseeing the implementation of new software. Creates and maintains proper training materials and business documentation. Provides application and production support as needed to the departments and systems they support. Collaborate with the QA team in developing high-level test plans. Reviews test cases developed by the QA team. Works with automated testing team as needed for performance / load testing and automated regression testing. Follows the MFS Software Development Lifecycle (SDLC), ensuring all steps of the process have been followed prior to releasing any code. Provides input into the strategic planning of the department's initiatives and resources. Mentors and advises less experienced Business Systems Analysts and other team members. WHAT WE ARE LOOKING FOR Bachelor’s degree in computer science or related technical field, or equivalent experience. Minimum 6-8+ years of experience in Financial Services supporting Operations, Trading or Quantitative systems. Strong technical skills including a solid understanding of underlying relevant technical architecture and business processes. Proven ability to efficiently utilize resources and communicate with others, both verbally and in writing. Project management skills, proven analytical skills and a demonstrated ability to make independent decisions. An in-depth understanding of Agile framework and systems development lifecycle. Ability to perform detailed analysis on systems at all phases of the project lifecycle. Ability to consider the business implications of the application of technology to the current and future state business environment. Proven ability to develop creative, innovative solutions to meet business needs. Leverage AI capabilities to enhance delivery without sacrificing subject matter expertise. PREFERRED SKILLS, QUALIFICATIONS & EXPERIENCE: Domain expertise in cash processing and collateral management, including trade-date and settlement-date cash, margining (initial and variation margin), collateral eligibility, haircuts, and optimization across cleared and bilateral exposures Knowledge of global settlement, payments, and reconciliation processes (e.g., T+1), including fails management, netting, and cash/collateral reconciliations across custodians, IBOR/ABOR, fund accounting, and counterparties Experience with securities financing and collateral mobility (e.g., repo, securities lending, rehypothecation) and the movement of collateral across custodians, prime brokers, and clearinghouses Experience with SMA business models and operational workflows, including end to end implementation of Portfolio Manager strategy changes for both Model Delivery and SMA Manager traded accounts, onboarding, reconciliations, rotations, technology solutions, and interaction with sponsor platforms Experience working with platforms such as Charles River, Eagle and Acadia Margin Manager are a plus. Project work experience with globally distributed teams is preferred. Applicants must be currently authorized to work in the United States on a full-time basis.

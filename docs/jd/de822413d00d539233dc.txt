Senior Quantitative Risk Developer Associate Department: Enterprise Risk Management / Quantitative Risk Management (QRM) Position Summary The Senior Quantitative Risk Developer is responsible for designing, implementing, and optimizing production-grade pricing, margin, and counterparty exposure models within the Clearing House quantitative risk analytics framework. This role bridges quantitative research and enterprise technology, translating advanced mathematical specifications into high-performance, cross-platform C++ libraries (Linux and Windows). The developer will build and maintain core risk engines covering Value-at-Risk (VaR), Liquidity Risk, Initial/Variation Margin coverage, Stress Testing, and regulatory capital models across all major asset classes cleared by CME Group (Interest Rates, FX, Credit/CDS, Commodities, and Equities). The ideal candidate brings deep hands-on expertise in production C++ development, derivative pricing/risk analytics, and architectural integration with downstream clearing platforms, alongside a proven track record of mentoring junior quantitative talent and collaborating with global cross-functional teams. Principal Accountabilities Quantitative Model Implementation & Architecture Architect, implement, and optimize cross-platform C++ numerical algorithms and analytics within the CME production risk library, translating quantitative research models and mathematical white papers into scalable, high-throughput code. Develop robust portfolio risk analytics, including sensitivity calculations (Greeks), historical and parametric VaR, stress scenario generators, counterparty exposure metrics, liquidity add-ons, and margin coverage frameworks. Implement and calibrate pricing models across diverse product lines, including Interest Rate Swaps (IRS), FX options, Credit Default Swaps (CDS), Swaptions, or Listed Futures & Options. Quality Engineering, Testing & Model Validation Formulate exhaustive unit, regression, and functional test frameworks; partner with Market Risk and Model Risk Governance teams to validate numerical accuracy, algorithmic stability, and benchmark compliance against prototyping code. Partner with QA and Clearing Risk Operations to ensure data integrity, end-to-end numerical accuracy, and seamless integration of analytics into broader enterprise data pipelines and high-throughput clearing architectures. Production Delivery & Systems Integration Partner with Enterprise Technology and Risk IT to transition risk libraries into production environments across Linux/Unix and Windows infrastructures. Define interfaces, APIs, schemas, and runtime performance benchmarks; actively resolve computational bottlenecks, latency issues, and memory profiles. Deliver rigorous technical documentation, system architecture designs, and release notes conforming to regulatory and enterprise model governance standards. Qualifications & Competencies Education & Experience Master’s degree or Ph.D. in Financial Engineering, Quantitative Finance, Computer Science, Applied Mathematics, Physics, or a related quantitative discipline. 4+ years of professional quantitative software development experience within an exchange, clearing house, investment bank, hedge fund, or institutional financial services firm. Demonstrated track record of contributing to production-grade quantitative libraries, risk engines, or derivative pricing platforms. Technical Proficiencies C++ Expertise: Advanced modern C++ (C++17/C++20 preferred) with an emphasis on Object-Oriented/Generic Programming, STL, template metaprogramming, memory management, and multi-threading/concurrency. Operating Systems & Tooling: Strong development and debugging proficiency in Linux/Unix and Windows environments; expert user of Git version control, CMake, and enterprise IDEs (e.g., Visual Studio, CLion). Scripting & Interoperability: Working knowledge of Python for prototyping, test data synthesis, and numerical validation; familiarity with Java, C#, or modern managed runtimes is a plus. Documentation & Data Systems: Experience writing technical documentation in LaTeX, Markdown, or Confluence; working knowledge of relational and timeseries databases (SQL). Quantitative & Market Knowledge Deep mathematical foundation in numerical analysis, optimization methods, Monte Carlo simulations, finite difference methods, and numerical root-finding/calibration algorithms. Strong domain knowledge of counterparty credit risk, multi-asset margin methodologies (e.g., SPAN, historical simulation VaR), clearing house capital requirements, liquidity risk frameworks, and stress testing. Practical familiarity with derivative trade structures, term structures of interest rates, volatility surfaces, and curve bootstrapping methodologies. Professional & Interpersonal Skills Clear written and verbal communication skills; ability to distill complex algorithmic and risk concepts for business partners, model validation groups, and enterprise IT teams.

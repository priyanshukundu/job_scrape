NVIDIA is searching for a creative, highly motivated and independent engineer with expertise in system software with security background to join the SOC System Software organization. This position offers the opportunity to have real impact in a dynamic, technology-focused company impacting SOC based product lines ranging from autonomous vehicles, robotics, consumer graphics, and edge AI. What You’ll Be Doing: Lead all HDCP (High-bandwidth Digital Content Protection) activities across SoC-based projects, leading the feature roadmap, protocol implementation, compliance certification, and delivery across chips, platforms, and products. Develop and maintain secure-engine firmware on a RISC-V based secure processor. Deliver end to end HDCP solution across multiple software stacks spanning Linux, Android for automotive, and consumer platforms. Drive firmware production signing readiness, rollback-prevention strategies, and key-provisioning workflows. Serve as the security primary contact for the team, leading all aspects of threat modeling, static analysis, vulnerability tracking and remediation, and security compliance across all projects. Collaborate with multi-functional teams spanning HW design, boot firmware, power management, validation, and GPU display to align on architecture and delivery. What we need to see: BS or MS in Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, or equivalent experience. 8+ years of experience in embedded systems, firmware, or system software development. Strong RISC-V or embedded processor firmware development experience with privilege-level isolation and secure boot. Deep understanding of cryptography and security protocols, specifically HDCP 1.x/2.x authentication, key management, and repeater topology. Strong familiarity of how HDCP integrates with display protocols, e.g. HDMI, DisplayPort. Consistent record leading cross-team technical initiatives from architecture through certification and delivery. Ways to stand out from the crowd: Hands-on experience with HDCP compliance certification. Experience with SoC-level system integration: firewalls, fuse controllers, privilege isolation, and secure carveouts. Experience productizing DRM protocols (Widevine, PlayReady). Familiarity with automotive security standards (ISO 21434, UN R155).

Job Description Motorola Solutions is looking for candidates with data-driven development experience, including a solid understanding of software development and deployment. In this role, candidates will build/maintain data migration and conversion tools, test scripts, and deployment timelines with the project team. Candidates need the ability to collaborate, prototype, design, and implement data conversion solutions. The successful candidate will be able to perform system integration of data migration solutions for our customers and also have the ability to work with customers to fulfill the business objectives and end-user expectations. This technical person will be a bridge between the customer's business needs and our technical ability to deliver data migration solutions. ​Soft-skills and customer interaction is required. Preferred Qualifications: Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field. Technical/software development experience. Experience in software development; experience (C#, XML, Python and .NET technologies). Able to troubleshoot and debug issues. Support and triage issues with customer integration teams under minimum supervision. Solid networking understanding. Ability to create Project's overall quality strategy and modify as-needed basis. Ability to evaluate software quality through load testing, stress testing and techniques like unit testing and exploratory testing. Validate/deliver data migration solution with the customer based on contract deliverables Strong communication skills and the ability to communicate complex technical strategies with customers.

Job Description The future of money is being built right now. At FIS, we help the world’s businesses, banks and communities move, manage and grow money with confidence. Join a team that values fresh thinking, works together to solve complex challenges, and creates technology that powers everyday life. Role Location US Anywhere About the Role As a Consultant II , you will lead key workstreams for corporate treasury management implementations, with a strong focus on FIS Quantum. You will work with treasury and technology teams to turn business needs into clear system designs and test plans. You will guide clients through setup, testing, training and launch. Key Responsibilities Lead client workshops for treasury system projects. Gather needs for cash, liquidity, payments, bank connectivity, debt, investments and risk. Configure and support FIS Quantum or similar treasury management systems based on approved business and technical requirements. Create clear system requirements, process flows, interface needs and project documents for technical teams. Plan and run system, integration, readiness and user acceptance tests with client data. Track issues and coordinate resolution across project teams. Lead project workstreams, estimate effort, train clients and guide less experienced consultants when needed. Required Qualifications Experience implementing corporate treasury management systems, including requirements gathering, solution design, configuration, testing and client deployment. Strong knowledge of corporate treasury processes such as cash and liquidity management, payments, bank account management, bank connectivity, debt, investments or financial risk. Skill in turning business needs into system details, process designs, data requirements and test cases. Skill in writing SQL queries and views to review data, solve issues and support system integrations. Bachelor’s degree in computer science, information systems or a related field, or an equivalent combination of education, training and work experience. Preferred Qualifications Hands-on implementation or advanced user experience with FIS Quantum or similar enterprise treasury management system in a consulting, implementation or corporate treasury environment. Knowledge of implementation methods, system integrations, data conversion and financial technology. The impact you’ll make As a Consultant II , you will help corporate treasury teams improve visibility, control and efficiency across critical financial processes. Your work will connect treasury needs with the right technology, lower project risk and prepare clients to use the system with confidence. You will also strengthen delivery quality by sharing product, implementation and industry knowledge across the project team. What We Offer You Bring your ideas. Build your future.

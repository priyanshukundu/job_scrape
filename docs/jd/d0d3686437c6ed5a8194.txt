About this role Reporting to the Head of Enterprise FP&A, you’ll be a part of a two-person revenue pod that is responsible for ARR forecasting, variance analysis and strategic finance projects. In this role, you’ll support the ARR and revenue forecast and own key strategic finance workflows such as: Deal structuring and pricing decisions Pricing, packaging and monetization Development of margin models Defining, instrumentation and reporting of key metrics including ARR, NRR, capacity, attainment, CAC payback, LTV / CAC and productivity Deep-dive analysis of business performance About your skills ARR & Revenue Forecasting: Experience building and maintaining ARR and revenue forecasts that incorporate pipeline, capacity, attainment, and renewal data, and explaining variances to actuals with clear, actionable insight. SaaS Metrics & Unit Economics: Deep fluency in SaaS metrics such as ARR, NRR, CAC payback, and LTV/CAC, including defining, instrumenting, and reporting them to drive decisions on growth and efficiency. Pricing & Deal Strategy: Ability to evaluate deal structures, pricing, and packaging using margin and monetization models, and to make clear recommendations on pricing approvals and tradeoffs. Strategic Business Partnering & Influence: Ability to act as a trusted finance partner to cross-functional leaders, assess ROI on new initiatives, and present findings and recommendations that shape leadership decisions. What you’ll be doing Support build and maintenance of ARR forecasting models, incorporating capacity, attainment, pipeline data and renewal data Generate monthly and quarterly SaaS metrics reports covering unit economics, NRR, renewal rates, and attainment against targets Partner with cross-functional leaders on strategic finance initiatives and provide analytical support for pricing strategy and approvals Conduct variance analysis on actual versus forecast results and present findings with recommendations to leadership Qualifications 5+ years of revenue experience in SaaS Experience with Excel & strong business modeling and analytical skills.

Technical Support Associate Analyst - Cigna Healthcare

Help people stay productive by solving technology issues with care, speed, and sound judgment. In this role, you will support employees across office, remote, and virtual desktop settings. You will own issues from diagnosis through resolution while building your skills in endpoint management, Microsoft technologies, security, and enterprise IT operations. Responsibilities • Provide responsive Tier 1 and escalated desktop support through the help desk queue, phone, email, and remote-support tools, keeping employees informed through resolution. • Diagnose and resolve issues with Windows 10/11 devices, Microsoft 365, Outlook, Teams, VPN, virtual desktops, printers, and peripherals so employees can work with minimal disruption. • Escalate complex infrastructure, network, application, or security issues with clear troubleshooting notes that help partner teams act quickly. • Image, configure, deploy, replace, and recover desktops, laptops, and loaner devices while meeting configuration, encryption, security, and software standards. • Support device enrollment, compliance, and BitLocker recovery with Microsoft Intune or similar tools, and manage equipment returns, data transfers, inventory, and lifecycle replacements. • Support remote access through OpenVPN, Omnissa Horizon, or similar platforms, including authentication, latency, profile, and device-change issues. • Prepare equipment and access for new hires, partnering with managers, HR, networking, and IT teams to create a smooth first-day experience. • Create practical checklists, troubleshooting guides, and knowledge articles that improve consistency and help the team solve common issues faster. • Set up and test conference-room technology and provide hands-on, walk-up support for employees in the office. • Contribute to endpoint, security, and support-process improvements with engineering, networking, and security partners. Required Qualifications • At least 2 years of experience in help desk, desktop support, or technical customer service. • Working knowledge of Windows 10/11, Microsoft 365, Outlook, and Teams. • Experience imaging, deploying, and troubleshooting desktops, laptops, printers, and other peripherals. • Understanding of VPNs, virtual desktops, and basic network troubleshooting. • Experience using a ticketing system such as SysAid, ServiceNow, or a similar platform. • Clear communication, strong organization, and a customer-focused approach to managing competing priorities and owning issues through resolution. • Ability to explain technical information in simple terms and work effectively with employees at all levels. Preferred Qualifications • Experience with Microsoft Intune, BitLocker, Active Directory, or Microsoft Entra ID. • Experience with Omnissa Horizon, OpenVPN, or similar remote-access tools. • Familiarity with Microsoft Configuration Manager (SCCM), ManageEngine Endpoint Central, Windows Autopilot, or a similar deployment platform. • Microsoft 365 Certified: Endpoint Administrator Associate, CompTIA A+, Network+, Security+, or a similar certification. • Experience supporting endpoint or security modernization projects in an enterprise environment.

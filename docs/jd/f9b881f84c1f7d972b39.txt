Job title: Accelerator Software Engineer Location: Lyon (Hybrid - 2 days remote, 3 days on-site) About the job We are looking for a Software Engineer (fullstack) to join the Manufacturing & Supply (M&S) Accelerator teams . Inside a small autonomous multi-functional agile team, you will help launch new medicines faster to cover unmet needs and deliver them affordably across the globe. This role is ideal for someone who wants a high level of ownership to not only bring software every day to production but also wants to contribute to building a whole product and to solve actual problems. Main responsibilities Scope & impact Designs, delivers, and runs software solutions that accelerate bringing medicines to the patients. Ensures the scalability, maintainability, and reliability of the systems developed. Contributes to the technical vision within a project scope while aligning with the broader engineering strategy. Prioritization & execution Translates requirements into high-quality, performant code. Collaborates with peers, product managers, and data engineers to ensure timely delivery. Owns technical decisions and ensures effective execution of assigned work. Values unowned or less visible work that enables the team to move faster. Technical excellence Develops strong technical expertise in domains relevant to M&S innovation. Upholds high standards in software engineering, testing, and documentation. Advocates for best practices and contributes to team-wide technical standards. Builds and maintains modern and cloud-native applications. Collaboration & contribution Works closely with teammates and cross-functional partners to solve problems. Supports team initiatives by contributing to shared codebases, tools, and processes. Engages in code reviews, mentoring opportunities, and knowledge sharing. Communicates clearly and proactively with both technical and non-technical stakeholders. What we offer you: A fixed salary over 12 months, supplemented by a short-term incentive, as well as a collective variable compensation based on Sanofi Group results.

Associate Scientist, Histology
Regeneron
Location: TARRYTOWN
Link: https://regeneron.wd1.myworkdayjobs.com/Careers/job/TARRYTOWN/Associate-Scientist--Histology_R51044

Build our future together: At Regeneron, we use science and innovation to develop life-changing medicines for people with serious diseases. We are seeking a Histotechnologist to join our Translational Medicine Department's Clinical Histology Core, supporting Immune Oncology clinical trial studies. In this role, you will use cutting-edge immunohistochemistry and in situ hybridization technologies to help our clinical colleagues understand how our immunotherapies treat and potentially cure patients across a wide range of cancers, working in a cross-functional environment with close interaction with Research and Translational Medicine colleagues. This position offers the opportunity to contribute to a fast-growing, science-driven organization making a meaningful difference to patients worldwide. When & where: Location: Tarrytown, NY Work model/flexibility: Onsite Discover your role: Prepare serial sections from biopsy samples, where section quality directly influences downstream staining and analysis Cut control tissue, such as tonsil, for IHC and in situ hybridization studies Perform H&E staining on automated instruments Operate automated staining platforms, maintaining instruments and preparing bulk solutions Receive clinical samples from outside collaborators, recording sample details and processing tissue Scan slides on an automated slide scanner, QC images, and transfer files to the server Work independently on the planning and execution of staining runs Bring a willingness to learn new techniques and expand your role within the group This role requires: BS with 2+ years of relevant experience Proven track record working with human FFPE tissue Experience accurately and reliably cutting serial sections, including on limited amounts of tissue Experience sectioning biopsy samples Strong organizational, computer, and time management skills, with the ability to work as part of a team and meet tight deadlines Does this sound like you?

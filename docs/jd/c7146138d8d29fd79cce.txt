We are hiring a Software Engineer for our Trust & Platform Security team. This team is building the next generation of cyber security monitoring for Snowflake customers where discovery, detection and remediation occurs in the same system. In the agentic era, threats and vulnerabilities occur at machine speed and scale and we are building a platform to detect and respond to these threats in real time. This team is also responsible for building the next generation of network and data enforcement engines: context aware ingress, data exfiltration protection and unified data movement policies for Snowflake's cloud security infrastructure. You will work at the intersection of security policy and system enforcement, building features that help keep perimeter controls robust, scalable, and integrated with context-aware access frameworks.




AS A SOFTWARE ENGINEER, TRUST & PLATFORM SECURITY AT SNOWFLAKE, YOU WILL:

 - Build and ship components to detect security vulnerabilities and threats, shadow AI and other agentic threats.

 - Develop data loss and ransomware detection mechanisms and ensure signals yield low false positive rates.

 - Write and contribute to design documents for product features, with attention to reliability, latency, and auditability

 - Work with senior engineers and Product Management to turn requirements into well-scoped tasks and deliver them independently

 - Write well-tested, secure code and take part in code reviews

 - Own features through implementation, testing, and production rollout with guidance from senior teammates




OUR IDEAL SOFTWARE ENGINEER, TRUST & PLATFORM SECURITY WILL HAVE:

 - Solid coding skills in SQL, Java, Python and exposure to building distributed or backend systems and willingness for full stack exposure

 - Familiarity with cloud platforms such as AWS, GCP, or Azure, and their security or networking controls

 - Eagerness to learn, take feedback, and grow ownership over larger parts of the system

 - Clear communication and comfort collaborating with cross-functional partners

 - BS/MS in Computer Science, Engineering, or equivalent experience

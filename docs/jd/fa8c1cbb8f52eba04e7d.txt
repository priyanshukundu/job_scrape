Strong client relationships start with a clear understanding of what matters most. Your work will support coordinated priorities, clear accountability and measurable commercial outcomes. Analyze client performance, track revenue trends and KPIs, and synthesize client activity, market developments and relationship dynamics into actionable insights. Partner with the Client Engagement team on management market visits, client meetings, conferences and other strategic client opportunities. Drive accountability for agreed client priorities by clarifying action ownership and facilitating cross-functional follow-through. Partner with CRM, data and PnA teams to maintain a holistic view of client relationships and keep client information and reporting accurate and current. Use AI-enabled tools, advanced analytics and emerging technology to enhance client intelligence and account planning and streamline workflows.

Summary: Chatham's Private Equity & Infrastructure (PEI) team is seeking an Analyst to support private equity sponsors, infrastructure investors, and their portfolio companies in navigating interest rate and foreign currency (FX) risk. The team advises clients on hedging strategies, derivative transactions, and capital markets considerations that support their financing, investment, and financial risk management objectives. In this role, you will partner with experienced advisors to evaluate financial risks, develop hedging recommendations, and support the structuring, negotiation, and execution of derivative transactions. You will gain exposure to private equity and infrastructure investment strategies, corporate and project financing structures, and capital markets while building relationships with clients, bank counterparties, and colleagues across Chatham. Through hands-on experience, mentorship, and Chatham's apprenticeship-style approach, you will develop technical expertise and progressively take ownership of client advisory and transaction execution responsibilities. In this role you will: Partner with experienced advisors to develop and execute interest rate and FX hedging strategies for private equity sponsors, infrastructure investors, and portfolio companies. Analyze market conditions, interest rate trends, and foreign currency exposures to support risk management recommendations aligned with clients' financing, investment, and business objectives. Prepare derivative pricing analyses and generate market indications using Chatham's proprietary models, technology, and market intelligence. Support the structuring, negotiation, and execution of derivative transactions with bank counterparties, including evaluating pricing, terms, and transaction alternatives. Review credit agreements, financing documentation, and capital structures to identify hedging requirements, financial risks, and implications for transaction structuring. Prepare financial analyses, presentations, and recommendations for client discussions with CFOs, treasury professionals, private equity investment teams, and other stakeholders. Coordinate the preparation, review, and negotiation of derivative transaction documentation in collaboration with internal specialists, legal advisors, and bank counterparties. Collaborate with colleagues across Chatham's advisory, transaction operations, and other service teams to deliver coordinated solutions throughout the hedging transaction lifecycle. Participate in client meetings and transaction discussions, developing the knowledge and communication skills needed to take increasing ownership of client relationships and advisory engagements. Develop an understanding of clients' broader financing, capital markets, and financial risk management needs to help identify additional opportunities where Chatham's expertise can provide value. Contribute to team knowledge sharing, training, and continuous improvement of advisory and transaction execution processes. Your impact: Help private equity sponsors, infrastructure investors, and portfolio companies evaluate and manage interest rate and foreign currency exposures associated with their financing, investment, and operational strategies. Support clients in making informed hedging decisions through thoughtful market analysis, derivative pricing, and evaluation of transaction structures.

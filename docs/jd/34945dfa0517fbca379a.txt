Belvedere Trading is currently recruiting for a Risk Analyst to join our Risk team. Our Risk Analysts are embedded with our trading desks as dedicated business partners, each aligned to an asset class such as equities, indexes, commodities, energies, metals, or interest rates. In this role, you will own day-to-day market risk monitoring and position surveillance for your desks, enforce the Firm’s risk controls and limits, and help traders make risk-aware decisions, all with the goal of safeguarding the Firm’s capital, returns, and reputation. The ideal candidate will have 3+ years of experience analyzing market and liquidity risk, a proactive and decisive approach, and a strong understanding of the derivatives markets in at least one of the following asset classes: equities, indexes, commodities, energies, or rates. This is an ideal opportunity for someone who wants to work side by side with tradersand have a direct impact on risk outcomes across the Firm.

Travel Rule engineering operates the TRUST network, a critical piece of Travel Rule compliance across the industry. TRUST defines the protocols members use to securely exchange Travel Rule data with one another, and the team builds innovative solutions with impact across the entire industry. We are hiring a Senior Software Engineer to own substantial work on the network as more institutions join. You will take complex projects from unclear requirements through design, implementation, rollout, and production support, and you will find simple solutions to problems that look messy at first. Your APIs and tooling will be used by exchanges and custodians beyond Coinbase, so what you ship shapes how the industry meets its Travel Rule obligations. What you'll do: Lead complex projects from start to finish: clarify requirements, write the design, ship the implementation, and support it in production. Partner with product, legal, and compliance to keep pace with a rapidly changing regulatory landscape. Partner with engineers across the industry on SDKs, APIs, and documentation that make onboarding onto TRUST fast and predictable. Design for real network conditions: unavailable counterparties, timeouts, retries, duplicates, and malformed data. Take the lead on questions from TRUST members and run the office hours that unblock their integrations. Improve how the team operates by reviewing code and designs, mentoring engineers, and replacing manual support work with tooling. Required Skills and Experience: Solid understanding of distributed systems, including idempotency, retries, ordering, and partial failure. Proven track record of leading complex projects from 0 to 1 with clear ownership and measurable results. Strong backend engineering skills in at least one language, with APIs, databases, automated testing, and cloud infrastructure. Clear communication, including design documents and direct work with partner teams and external engineers. Utilizes generative AI responsibly, maintaining human oversight to deliver business-ready outputs and drive measurable improvements in workflow efficiency, cost, and quality.

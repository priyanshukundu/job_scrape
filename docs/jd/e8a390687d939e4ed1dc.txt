Job Description Summary The Executive Director, Commercial Risk & Contract Management plays a pivotal role in the strategic direction and success of the Wind business globally. This role requires a dynamic individual who can provide effective governance of a complex business which will deliver between up to $10B of annual order intake across New Units, Services and Aftermarket. Recognized as a subject matter expert, you will oversee and ensure compliance to GE Vernova risk processes, bringing assertive leadership, ensuring that risk is thoroughly evaluated and that commercial deals enable successful and profitable execution. This role requires capable leadership, exceptional commercial acumen and the ability to lead teams spanning the North America, Europe and Asia-Pacific regions. The role demands high energy given the need to work proactively to identify risks, ensure the effectiveness of processes and strive for continuous improvement. Le Directeur Exécutif – Gestion des Risques Commerciaux et Contractuels joue un rôle stratégique majeur dans la performance et le développement des activités mondiales de GE Vernova Wind. Dans un environnement international complexe, représentant jusqu’à 10 milliards de dollars de prises de commandes annuelles, couvrant les équipements neufs, les services et les activités après-vente, le titulaire du poste est garant de la maîtrise des risques commerciaux et contractuels ainsi que de la robustesse des processus de gouvernance. Véritable expert de référence, il veille à la bonne application des politiques et procédures de gestion des risques de GE Vernova. Il exerce un leadership affirmé afin de garantir une évaluation rigoureuse des risques et de sécuriser la conclusion de contrats permettant une exécution performante et rentable. Cette fonction exige une solide vision stratégique, un sens aigu des enjeux commerciaux et une capacité démontrée à fédérer et à diriger des équipes réparties en Amérique du Nord, en Europe et en Asie-Pacifique. Le poste requiert également une forte capacité d'initiative et d'anticipation, afin d'identifier les risques émergents, de renforcer l'efficacité des dispositifs existants et d'inscrire la fonction dans une dynamique d'amélioration continue. Job Description Essential Behaviors The Executive Director, Commercial Risk & Contract Management will: Create the agenda – proactively identifying and mitigating key risks to ensure the business remains ahead of emerging threats. Make the complex simple – analyzing issues and making recommendations. Bring a point of view – add value where it matters through commercial judgement. Drive organizational change – digitizing and standardizing processes and mobilizing people to achieve measurable results. Raise the quality of global processes and governance. Exercise high-level judgment and courage to challenge business assumptions in executive forums, acting as a trusted advisor to senior leadership on complex, cross-functional risk decisions . Roles and Responsibilities Provide leadership vision and deliver on priorities for the global Commercial Risk team. [~6 FTE]. Play a key role in the global Commercial Operations Leadership team, working closely with Regional Commercial Directors to proactively address issues and develop strategies to deliver on business objectives and targets. Act as a strategic commercial partner to the business, identifying high-impact risk trends before they manifest into deal issues, and proactively influence commercial strategies to maximize margin and minimize risk exposure. Act as domain and subject matter export for Contracting, Commercial and Technical Risk in the GE Vernova Wind business, a complex business spanning New Units, Services and Aftermarket. Provide contracting and risk underwriting advice across a range of Product and Service offerings (New Units, Services, Re-Powering, Aftermarket) as well as execution scopes (Equipment, Logistics, Installation) and Contracting types (Turbine Supply Agreements, Full Service Agreements, Transactional). Develop, maintain, and execute a robust risk review and delegation of authority process (R – table) globally to ensure consistency and transparency to risk. Mature and transform risk infrastructure, process and tools to deliver year-over-year productivity and enhanced quality of the global risk function. Work across the business segment cross-functionally to ensure all stakeholders are aligned to risk and underwriting, whilst driving process improvements. Work with the Global Projects and Services organisations in the implementation of an end-to-end process of lessons learned in which key issues are identified, screened, subject to detailed problem-solving and then addressed with appropriate countermeasures. Validate risk and reward balance, considering factors such as terms and conditions, technical risks, fulfilment capacity, margin and security of payment. Provide leadership and guidance to the Insurance Risk Manager to mitigate risk exposure by identifying risk profiles from insurance obligations and/or contractual injury/damage exposure in contracts for the supply of equipment and contracts for the provision of maintenance services. Establish a robust framework for deal and risk review, lead risk review meetings, and contribute to approval escalation meetings. Establish connections across the GE Vernova business and implement proactive assessments of risk anticipating and in response to changes in the business environment whilst learning and applying best practices. Review and approve risk modules. Maintain an overview of global deal approval status to ensure deals remain on track. Maintain a robust process for New Country Entry, ensuring that deals are reviewed comprehensively, and we are ready to execute considering Legal and Tax risks, Product compliance, Grid compliance, Project and Service delivery and other factors. T rack, aggregate and perform analytics of global deal risk and provide insights on areas for improvement. Develop tools towards leaner negotiations on terms and conditions, while maintaining the desired risk profile. Lead initiatives within the sales/commercial organization to drive process rigor and simplification within the overall risk management process. Work with a clear focus in supporting the Commercial team to achieve its business plan. Faire évoluer et transformer les dispositifs, processus et outils de gestion des risques afin d'améliorer, année après année, la productivité et la qualité de la fonction mondiale de gestion des risques. Développer les collaborations au sein des différentes activités de GE Vernova et mettre en œuvre des évaluations proactives des risques, en anticipant les évolutions de l'environnement économique et commercial ou en y répondant, tout en identifiant et en appliquant les meilleures pratiques. Examiner et approuver les modules d'évaluation des risques. Assurer une visibilité globale sur le statut des approbations commerciales afin de garantir le bon avancement des transactions. Piloter des initiatives au sein de l'organisation commerciale afin de renforcer la rigueur des processus et de simplifier le dispositif global de gestion des risques, avec pour objectif de soutenir les équipes commerciales dans la réalisation de leur plan d'activité.

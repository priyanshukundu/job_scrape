Inspects, measures, and tests products at various stages of production process and compiles and evaluates results compared to approved standards. Engages in professional written & verbal communication with lab customers regarding inspection data and judgements. Performs required data recording and documentation of measurement results produced through skilled application of the duties listed below. Position Summary Test and inspects products at various stages of production process and compiles and evaluates statistical data to determine and maintain quality and reliability of products by performing the following duties. Key Roles & Responsibilities To perform this job successfully, an individual must have the ability to be able to perform each essential duty satisfactorily. The requirements listed below are representative of the knowledge, skill, and/or ability required. Reasonable accommodations may be made to enable individuals with disabilities to perform the essential functions. Standard Duties: Maintain all quality standards. Perform and sustain 5S activities required for maintaining a World Class Metrology Lab Understand and adhere to current health, safety and environmental policies Understand and adhere to current quality and control policies. Perform all lab processes such as daily dimensional verifications. G, D & T and blue print reading; ASME Y 14.5 Operate technical inspection equipment such as CMM, M&M Gear Analyzer, Vision Machine, CMM, Contour Tracer, Surface Analyzer, Roundness Tester, Optical Contour. Use hand held gages (i.e. micrometers, calipers, etc.) Train others to use to use metrology equipment Perform all Lab processes such as PPAP, dimensional verifications, new product launch, receiving inspection and non-conforming material investigations. Safety, Health and Physical Requirements: May require lifting in excess of 40 pounds during a scheduled shift May require pushing or pulling of manual pallet jacks or hand carts containing full baskets of parts While performing the duties of this job, the employee is regularly exposed to moving mechanical parts and hazardous chemicals. All employees are responsible for knowing, understanding and complying with the safety policies and procedures. Key Competencies Safety Initiative and high sense of urgency Judgement/Decision Making Teamwork/Interpersonal Skills Internal/external Customer Service Orientation Education & Experience Education: High school diploma/GED required. Associate or Bachelor’s degree preferred. Experience: Able to verify dimensions using basic metrology equipment. Ability to operate all types of metrology equipment. G, D & T and blue print interpretation; ASME Y 14.5. Above average verbal/written communication and human relation skills. Familiar with quality standards such as TS16949. Machining experience or background preferred. Computer Skills: To perform this job successfully, an individual should have basic computer knowledge.

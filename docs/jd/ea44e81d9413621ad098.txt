We're hiring a Senior or Specialist Software Engineer for its new Developer Experience team in Atlanta. The role builds AI-assisted workflows, defect-triage As a Senior Software Engineer, Developer Experience reporting to the Sr. Director, Software Engineering, you'll play a critical role in shaping how our teams build, ship, and improve software at scale. As a founding member of our new Developer Experience team, you'll help define the technical direction, build AI-assisted workflows, and deliver tools and platforms that make engineers across the organization more effective. You'll thrive in this position if you're curious, hands-on, and collaborative, have a global mindset, and bring a passion for engineering craft and innovation to a fast-paced, high-impact environment. Key Responsibilities Build AI-assisted workflows that improve how we develop and deliver software, connecting to organization-wide AI initiatives. Develop agents for defect triage and code quality validation, keeping people in control of key decisions. Find new ways to give AI agents the technical and business context they need — such as how applications fit together and who owns them. Run code cleanup campaigns to strengthen operations, measuring outcomes and iterating continuously. Extend agentic capabilities over time across business functions, including client service, legal, and operations. Required Qualifications Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field, or equivalent practical experience. 3–8+ years of software engineering experience, ideally building tools or platforms for other engineers. Proven ability to design and deliver solutions end to end, working independently and with sound judgment in ambiguous situations. Clear communicator with experience in collaborative, matrixed environments. Proficiency in tools such as AI coding assistants, Git, CI/CD pipelines, and Agile practices. Preferred Qualifications Hands-on experience building with generative or agentic AI. Experience in capital markets, fintech, or a regulated industry. Experience mentoring engineers or leading technical initiatives without formal authority. This position will be located in Atlanta, GA and offers the opportunity for a hybrid work environment at least 3 days a week in-office, subject to change, providing flexibility and accessibility for qualified candidates.

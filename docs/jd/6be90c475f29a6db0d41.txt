Senior Software Engineer, Build Systems (Bazel)
Boston Dynamics
Location: Waltham Office (POST)
Link: https://bostondynamics.wd1.myworkdayjobs.com/Boston_Dynamics/job/Waltham-Office-POST/Senior-Software-Engineer--Build-Systems--Bazel-_R3387

At Boston Dynamics, our mission is to deliver the world’s most capable robots. Behind every robot is a lot of software, and on the Central Software (CSW) team, we build the “software factory” that gets it from a developer’s laptop to robots, desktops, and the cloud. We’re looking for a Senior Software Engineer to take the next big leap in how fast and reliably many hundreds of engineers can build and test. Our monorepo spans C++, Python, Go, Rust, TypeScript, and Java/Kotlin, and targets embedded hardware, simulation, machine learning, and cloud services. That means build and test problems you won’t find at a typical web company: cross-compiling for robot hardware, large binary artifacts, and testing that has to bridge simulation and real machines. You’ll make an impact by: Owning the rollout of remote build execution across our monorepo, from proof of concept to production use by every developer. Teaming up with CSW leadership and peers to turn priorities into well-scoped, well-executed, and well-coordinated work. Improving build and test performance by profiling builds, raising cache hit rates, and eliminating non-determinism. Root-causing and fixing build breaks, flaky tests, and speed regressions. Modernizing our build, from migrating to current Bazel rulesets to using the dependency graph to generate SBOMs. Seeing everything through, from implementation and testing to deployment, documentation, and ongoing operations. Becoming one of the go-to Bazel experts on the team, staying current with the Bazel ecosystem and sharing what you learn through docs, code review, and helping teammates. Working in our agile process and communicating progress regularly. What we’re looking for: 5+ years as a software engineer working in large, cross-platform codebases. 3+ years of hands-on Bazel experience in a large codebase, including writing Starlark rules and debugging cache misses. A solid grasp of Bazel internals: configurations, toolchain resolution, and the action graph. Experience with remote execution and caching (e.g., EngFlow, BuildBuddy, Buildbarn, Buildfarm, NativeLink), or a track record that shows you could ramp up quickly. Proficiency in at least one general-purpose language such as Python, Go, Java, or C++. A talent for diagnosing and fixing hard bugs. A bachelor’s degree in a technical field (e.g., Engineering, Computer Science) or equivalent experience. Bonus points: Experience with embedded or robotics toolchains. Contributions to Bazel or open source rulesets. Experience structuring and completing large build migrations or upgrades. Experience with software supply chain tooling (SBOMs, provenance, dependency scanning). Experience operating remote execution infrastructure on Kubernetes. Familiarity with CI systems such as Jenkins, BuildKite, or GitLab. Help scale and optimize the software infrastructure driving Boston Dynamics' next generation of robotics!

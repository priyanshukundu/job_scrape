Associate Scientist - Product Biochemistry
Regeneron
Location: TARRYTOWN
Link: https://regeneron.wd1.myworkdayjobs.com/Careers/job/TARRYTOWN/Associate-Scientist---Product-Biochemistry_R52775

At Regeneron, we use science and innovation to develop life-changing medicines for people with serious diseases. We are seeking a highly motivated Associate Scientist to join our Product Biochemistry department, which delivers comprehensive biochemical and biophysical characterization of therapeutic candidates and products from pre-clinical development through commercialization. In this role, you will support one or more of the department's functional areas, such as early-stage developability assessment, critical reagent support, ligand and toxicology support, or late-stage and post-commercialization product characterization, contributing to analytical method development, product characterization, and technology innovation efforts that deepen our understanding of novel protein therapeutics. When & where: Location: Tarrytown, NY What you’ll do: Prepare samples and perform biochemical and biophysical characterization experiments (e.g., liquid chromatography, light scattering, thermal stability, binding, and immunoassay techniques) to elucidate quality attributes of therapeutic candidates and products across the development lifecycle Support purification, enrichment, and characterization of product variants, critical reagents, and other materials as program needs require Provide input on experimental design and contribute to analytical method development, optimization, and the evaluation of new technologies to address program needs. Take on additional responsibilities in advanced method/technology development to support efforts to characterize higher order (secondary, tertiary, quaternary) protein structure, equilibrium protein-ligand complexes, and/or thermodynamic and hydrodynamic interactions of therapeutic protein drug candidates. Analyze data and document experiments, results, and findings in an electronic laboratory notebook; draft protocols, and present work at group and departmental meetings Draft technical documents to support regulatory filings and contribute to drafting peer-reviewed publications, as needed Collaborate with cross-functional partners across Research, Development, and Industrial Operations to advance program objectives Maintain instruments, troubleshoot instrumental/experimental issues, and order/maintain supplies Let’s find out if we’re a fit: Bachelor's degree in Biochemistry, Chemistry, Chemical Engineering, Biophysics, Biology, or a related field with minimum of 0-3 years of relevant experience. Good laboratory practice skills such as preparing samples, buffers, documentation, and maintaining cleanliness Experience with Microsoft Office suite (Excel, Word, and PowerPoint) for analysis and presentation of data Hands-on experience with one or more protein characterization techniques, such as chromatography (HPLC/UPLC/FPLC), protein purification, capillary electrophoresis/imaged capillary isoelectric focusing (CE/icIEF), differential scanning calorimetry/fluorimetry (DSC/DSF), spectroscopy (UV-Vis, fluorescence, FTIR, Raman, and/or circular dichroism), static/dynamic light scattering (SLS/DLS/MALS), binding affinity/kinetics, Analytical Ultracentrifugation (AUC), and/or immunoassays Familiarity with Excel/MATLAB/JMP/Empower or other coding/scripting tools to facilitate analysis of complex analytical datasets Awareness of biopharmaceutical development stages, from pre-clinical candidate assessment through commercial products Thrive today.

U.S. Bank is seeking a Software Engineer who will contribute toward the success of our technology initiatives in our digital transformation journey. This position will be responsible for the analysis, design, testing, development and maintenance of best in class software experiences. The candidate is a self-motivated individual who can collaborate with a team and across the organization. The candidate takes responsibility of the software artifacts produced adhering to U.S. Bank standards in order to ensure minimal impact to the customer experience. The candidate will be adept with the agile software development lifecycle and DevOps principles. Essential Responsibilities: Design, develop, and maintain end-to-end data pipelines and data products. Build batch and real-time solutions that support analytics, reporting, and AI use cases. Translate business and product requirements into scalable engineering solutions. Engineer for Quality and Scale Create production-grade solutions that are secure, testable, and stable. Apply engineering best practices for coding, testing, version control, deployment, and documentation. Troubleshoot complex data, platform, and performance issues across distributed systems. Operationalize Data and AI Implement DataOps practices, including monitoring, alerting, service-level objectives, and data quality controls. Build reliable data pipelines that support machine learning and AI model integration. Automate repeatable engineering and operational processes to improve delivery efficiency. Collaborate Across Disciplines Partner with product, architecture, data science, analysts, and other engineers and leads to deliver integrated solutions. Participate in technical design reviews, code reviews, and engineering planning. Communicate technical tradeoffs, dependencies, risks, and delivery progress clearly. Accelerate with AI Leverage AI-assisted development tools to boost team productivity Encourage modern engineering workflows and automation Basic Qualifications - Bachelor’s degree, or equivalent work experience - Three to five years of relevant experience Preferred Skills/Experience Strong experience building data pipelines, including batch and streaming workloads. Proficiency with Python, PySpark, and SQL. Experience delivering production-grade data systems at scale. Knowledge of data modeling, integration patterns, and data quality practices. Hands-on experience with Databricks and/or Snowflake. Familiarity with Kafka, Spark, and change data capture (CDC) frameworks. Experience working with APIs, files, events, and other enterprise data integration patterns. Experience with Azure and/or AWS. Understanding of scalability, reliability, security, performance, and cost considerations in cloud data platforms. Experience with CI/CD, source control, automated testing, infrastructure configuration, and deployment practices. Exposure to machine learning and AI data pipelines, feature preparation, and model integration. Experience with AI-assisted development tools, such as Copilot, is a plus. Reliable, high-performing data pipelines and data products operating in production. Faster and more predictable delivery through automation and sound engineering practices. Reusable technical patterns that improve consistency and reduce delivery friction.

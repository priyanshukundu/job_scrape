Who we are looking for Capital Management, a division of Global Treasury, plays a critical role in managing State Street's capital position and ensuring the firm and its legal entities maintain appropriate capital levels under both business-as-usual and stressed conditions. The team is responsible for capital planning and forecasting, capital adequacy assessment, stress testing, capital strategy and optimization, recovery and resolution planning, regulatory analysis, and related governance. We are looking for an Assistant Vice President (AVP), Capital Management who will take ownership of key capital management activities and work closely with senior stakeholders across Treasury, Finance, Risk, the business lines, and other corporate functions. The successful candidate will combine strong financial and analytical skills with an understanding of bank capital and regulatory requirements and be able to translate complex analysis into clear conclusions and recommendations for senior management. Why this role is important to us Capital Management sits at the intersection of strategy, financial performance, risk and regulation. The team helps ensure State Street maintains sufficient capital to execute its strategy, withstand stressed environments and meet regulatory and internal capital requirements. The AVP will have exposure to senior management and will contribute directly to decisions involving capital adequacy, capital allocation, stress testing and capital actions. What you will be responsible for As an AVP in Capital Management, you will: Lead analytical efforts to address complex market, capital, and regulatory challenges, including emerging issues and ad hoc requests from senior management. Conduct quantitative analysis and develop management-ready presentations to support key decisions by Global Treasury leadership and senior governance committees across the Bank. Drive real-time monitoring, management, and sensitivity analysis of State Street’s capital position, assessing the impact of changing market conditions, interest rates, balance sheet trends, and business activity. Collaborate across Global Treasury to support enterprise-wide balance sheet, capital, and liquidity optimization, identifying opportunities to improve financial resource efficiency. Partner across multiple risk disciplines and business lines to ensure capital management strategies appropriately reflect the Bank’s risk profile and the strategic priorities of its core businesses. Support the monthly capital adequacy assessment process and broader capital planning and forecasting activities, including analysis of capital ratios, RWA, earnings, and key capital drivers. Support the annual development of the Capital Plan and Capital Policy and Guidelines, including quantitative analysis, qualitative assessment, senior management materials, and governance presentations. Promote strong risk management and governance by challenging assumptions, identifying and escalating emerging risks, and executing required controls to support effective oversight and regulatory compliance. What we value These skills will help you succeed in this role: Ability to effectively leverage AI tools to enhance analysis, automate processes and improve efficiency Strong understanding of bank financial statements, capital ratios and the drivers of regulatory capital. Strong analytical ability and comfort working with large or complex financial datasets. Ability to understand the relationship between earnings, RWA, balance-sheet movements, stress scenarios and capital generation. Strong attention to detail while maintaining the ability to see the broader commercial and regulatory implications of the analysis. Ability to challenge assumptions and analytical outcomes constructively, rather than simply produce reporting. Excellent written and verbal communication skills, particularly the ability to explain complex topics clearly to senior stakeholders. Strong PowerPoint and Excel capabilities and the ability to produce senior-management-ready analysis with limited supervision. Ability to operate effectively across Treasury, Finance, Risk and business teams in a global organization. Ability to prioritize competing demands and deliver high-quality work under tight deadlines. Curiosity and an interest in understanding how changes in markets, regulation and business strategy affect the firm's capital position. Education & Preferred Qualifications Bachelor’s degree, with a concentration in Finance, Accounting, Economics or a related field preferred Approximately 3–5 years of relevant financial services experience, with experience in Global Treasury or Finance strongly preferred Exposure to capital planning, stress testing, ICAAP/CCAR, recovery and resolution planning, or regulatory capital frameworks is highly beneficial Salary Range: $80,000 - $140,000 Annual The range quoted above applies to the role in the primary location specified.

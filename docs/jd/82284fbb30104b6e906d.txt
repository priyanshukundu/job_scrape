In this role, you will: Investigate account and supplier risk issues by reviewing evidence, identifying patterns, and determining appropriate actions within established processes. Analyze account activity, supplier information, and operational data to identify potential risk, fraud, abuse, or policy violations. Apply sound judgment and documented risk-management procedures to resolve cases accurately, consistently, and within required timelines. Document investigation findings, decisions, and supporting rationale clearly for operational follow-up, auditability, and continuous process improvement. Collaborate with cross-functional partners to escalate complex or high-impact cases, communicate risk insights, and support effective resolution. Safely integrate and operate AI/ML-enabled solutions that improve investigation outcomes, while demonstrating familiarity with AI-driven systems, tools, or workflows and applying AI/ML concepts to real world products. Minimum Qualifications : Required education and years of experience were not provided in the approved role-profile information. Experience investigating account or supplier risk cases using structured processes, evidence review, and sound judgment. Ability to assess risk indicators, analyze relevant data, identify inconsistencies or patterns, and make well-supported case decisions. Experience managing ownership of individual investigations or case queues, including accurate documentation, timely resolution, and appropriate escalation. Proficiency with investigation workflows, risk-management tools, operational systems, and clear written communication. Preferred Qualifications : Experience handling complex, high-volume, or higher-impact account and supplier risk investigations while maintaining quality and consistency. Ability to identify recurring risk patterns and use data-driven insights to improve investigation processes, controls, or outcomes. Experience partnering across operations, compliance, fraud, trust and safety, supplier, or related functions to resolve risk issues. Familiarity with AI-assisted investigation workflows, risk detection tools, or machine-learning concepts applied to real-world products, with the ability to evaluate outputs responsibly. Demonstrated operational excellence through strong prioritization, thorough case documentation, audit readiness, and continuous process improvement.

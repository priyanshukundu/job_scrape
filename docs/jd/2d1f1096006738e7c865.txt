Revolution Medicines is a global, commercial-state oncology company dedicated to discovering, developing and delivering innovative medicines for patients with RAS-addicted cancers. Leveraging its differentiated RAS(ON) tri-complex inhibitor platform, the company is advancing a broad, integrated portfolio of oral RAS(ON) inhibitors designed to directly target the active, cancer-driving state of RAS. Founded on rigorous scientific inquiry and a willingness to challenge long-held assumptions, Revolution Medicines is committed to changing the trajectory of disease for patients with RAS-addicted cancers worldwide. Our people are united by a shared way of working: follow the science, challenge assumptions, act with urgency and hold ourselves to a high standard of rigor—all in service of patients. The Opportunity: Reporting to the Director, Global Competitive Intelligence, the Senior Manager / Associate Director, Competitive Intelligence the Competitive Intelligence role requires strategic thinking and expertise to provide critical contributions to brand strategy, launch and life-cycle planning, forecasting, and product and portfolio decisions. As a key member of the Global Competitive Intelligence team, this role is instrumental in delivering the disease area insights that shape brand strategy, launch planning, forecasting, and clinical development decisions for our assets in development. The ideal candidate will play a crucial role in monitoring and evaluating the competitive landscape, conducting comprehensive threat assessments, and collaborating with key stakeholders to inform strategic planning. This position would ideally be based at our headquarters in Redwood City, CA., however open to remote candidates who are willing to travel to our CA office on a regular basis. Responsibilities Include Integrates disease area findings and cross-functional perspectives into a company-wide view of the external environment and translates findings into implications and recommendations for the portfolio. Contributes to competitive coverage across conferences, publications, data releases, earnings and investor events, and commercial activities and messaging, based on agreed cross-functional priorities. Integrate with cross functional and regional expertise and shares timely implications for development and commercialization. Builds and maintains the disease area competitive landscape across early and metastatic disease setting and inclusive of in and out-of-class competition. Benchmarks competitor clinical data, business models, and capabilities. Collaborates with stakeholders across the company (including Commercial, Medical Affairs, and R&D) to integrate internal and external information, analytics, and data. Aligns stakeholders on research and competitive intelligence objectives and outputs. Distinguishes evidence from assumptions and communicates data quality, uncertainty, and limitations. Maintains an in-depth understanding of Revolution Medicines’ diverse product offerings, competitive positioning, and the broader market dynamics shaping the industry's future. Runs scenario planning and leads competitive simulations ahead of key competitor readouts and our own launch milestones. Supplies competitive assumptions for internal forecasts, including expected competitor entry timing and biomarker defined patient segments. Contributes competitive input to target product profiles, clinical trial design discussions, and lifecycle plans for the disease area. Manages day to day work with CI vendors, congress coverage agencies, and secondary data sources), keeping deliverables on scope, schedule, and budget. Proactively facilitates the identification and prioritization of intelligence efforts critical to strategic planning and execution in partnership with key stakeholders; provides direction for the design and refinement of intelligence plans. Conducts all intelligence gathering activities in compliance with legal, ethical, and SCIP standards, and with company policies on information handling. Required Skills, Experience and Education: Bachelor's degree in life sciences, business, or a related field. Minimum of 8-11 years of experience in the same fields, including at least 3 years supporting oncology programs. Working knowledge of solid tumor oncology drug development and commercialization, with the ability to read and interpret clinical trial data, statistical endpoints, and regulatory documents. Hands on experience covering major oncology congresses and producing congress summaries for brand and medical teams. Proficiency with clinicaltrials.gov, PubMed, and at least two commercial CI databases (e.g., Citeline, Evaluate, AlphaSense, GlobalData). Strong written and verbal communication. Track record of being a collaborative team player, and adaptive to meet the needs of a growing business. Excellent organizational skills and an ability to prioritize effectively to deliver results within reasonably established timelines. Ability to represent Revolution Medicines well: strong interpersonal skills including verbal and written communication. Entrepreneurial mindset, with a tolerance for ambiguity, and a passion for helping cancer patients. Preferred Skills: Advanced degree (PhD, PharmD, MD, MS, or MBA). Previous experience within any analytics & insights function or similar roles at a biotech / pharmaceutical company.

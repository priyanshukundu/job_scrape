Job Summary The Regulatory Reporting Analyst Senior is responsible for data collection, analysis, and meaningful presentation of data to support Regulatory reporting requirements and compliance filing activities. Job Description What You'll Do: Collect and evaluate data from multiple sources to develop reports and filings for submission to the Public Utility Commission of Texas. Leverage skills in business information tools to provide reports in the format required to meet Regulatory reporting needs. Participate in the preparation and monitoring of monthly/quarterly/annual Regulatory reports. Perform special studies, analysis, investigations, and assignments as may be required. Provide assistance to other AEP Texas teams with data requests. Provide assistance in regulatory proceedings including information for data requests, special studies for Commissions, etc. Participate in meetings with Commission Staff related to compliance and reporting filings. Present data and participate in discussions regarding regulatory compliance activities. Provide assistance and data collection support for Internal and external audits. Develop and maintain documentation of compliance filings and reporting requirements. Collect and evaluate data from multiple sources to develop reports as required to support the company’s compliance and reporting filings. Demonstrate familiarity with commission rules and requirements and company filings. What We're Looking For: Education: Bachelor's degree in Business, Engineering, Accounting or related field. Experience: 5+ years experience applying quantitative, business analytics, and computational skills. Other Must Haves: Expertise in working with large data sets. Expertise with SQL queries, database concepts and practices. Expertise with Excel. Experience working in a collaborative, team-oriented environment. Strong analytical and complex problem solving and critical thinking skills. Excellent oral and written communication skills. Where You'll Work: On-Site in the AEP Texas Government Office in Austin, TX office. What You'll Get: Base salary Annual bonus 401K match AEP Pension Comprehensive benefits package that aims to support and enhance the overall well-being of our employees.

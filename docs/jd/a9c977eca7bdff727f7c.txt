Industry/Sector Banking and Capital Markets Specialism Data, Analytics & AI Management Level Senior Manager Job Description & Summary The Opportunity As a Financial Risk Management Senior Manager you will lead client-facing data and analytics work that helps financial institutions strengthen risk, modeling, and technology capabilities, with a focus on capital, liquidity, credit risk, and asset-liability management. As a Senior Manager you will bring strategic thinking, influence, and sound judgment to guide complex work, coach teams, and communicate clear recommendations across varied stakeholders within our Advisory practice. In this role at PwC, you will help shape risk technology and analytics solutions, translate data into actionable insights, and support work that enhances regulatory readiness and informed decision-making. Responsibilities - Leading financial risk management initiatives for banking and capital markets clients, using data, analytics, and technology to support regulatory and risk priorities - Developing risk technology solutions, reporting capabilities, and automated workflows that improve risk identification, measurement, and monitoring - Advising clients on capital, liquidity, credit risk, and asset-liability management through quantitative analysis and data-driven insight - Directing complex project workstreams and coordinating delivery across analytics, modeling, and technology specialists - Translating business needs into data platform requirements, dashboard design, and reporting enhancements - Applying financial risk modeling methods, scenario analysis, and trend review to support informed decision-making - Validating data quality, model inputs, and reporting outputs to support reliable risk and regulatory analysis - Coaching team members through ambiguous work and adapting approaches to shifting client priorities and regulatory expectations What You Must Have - At least a Bachelor's degree - At least 6 years of experience What Sets You Apart - Preference for at least one of the following fields of study: Accounting, Analytics/Data Science, Biology/Biomedical Sciences, Business Administration/Management, Computer Science/Information Systems, Economics, Engineering, Finance, Financial Mathematics/Quantitative Finance, Health Administration/Public Health, Mathematics/Statistics, Operations/Supply Chain, Project/Technology Management, Risk Management/Insurance, Science - Demonstrating senior-level financial risk management within banking environments - Applying data analytics and quantitative modeling to risk decisions - Translating complex findings into client-ready, strategic narratives -

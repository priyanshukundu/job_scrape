Reporting to Sales Desk Management, the Investment Advisory Sales Specialist is a proactive sales partner responsible for accelerating Advisory growth across assigned markets. This role is designed for a highly motivated, commercially minded professional who can identify opportunities, influence outcomes, and consistently drive activity that converts into measurable results. Advisory Sales Specialists will have the following responsibilities: · Originate and advance advisory opportunities by proactively identifying, qualifying, and progressing client and prospect situations across advisory solutions inclusive of: Firm Discretionary and Third-Party Portfolio Solutions, Separately Managed Accounts, Direct Indexing, Mutual Funds, and Money Markets. · Execute targeted FA engagement to uncover needs, generate leads, and drive solution adoption—using a disciplined meeting cadence and follow-up process. · Partner with key market stakeholders to influence net flows by developing market-specific growth plans, prioritizing high-potential opportunities, and coordinating coverage to move opportunities from interest to implementation. · Deliver consultative, needs-based recommendations by translating market views, product positioning, and portfolio construction concepts into actionable ideas for Financial Advisors and their clients. · Build and manage a pipeline of opportunities by tracking outreach, next steps, and outcomes; maintaining momentum through structured follow-ups; and escalating high-impact opportunities appropriately. · Support inbound Financial Advisor engagement with a sales mindset—triaging requests efficiently while identifying commercial opportunities · Leverage CRM to drive commercial outcomes by documenting activity, capturing opportunity details, measuring conversion, and using insights to refine targeting and improve sales effectiveness. · Develop deep product and platform expertise to confidently position Advisory solutions, handle objections, and support implementation steps that convert recommendations into funded assets. · Contribute to team excellence and scalability by sharing best practices, supporting training of new team members, and continuously improving sales processes and playbooks.

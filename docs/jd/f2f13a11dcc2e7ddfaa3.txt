Lead Software Engineer - Low-Power Verification
Cadence Design Systems
Location: SAN JOSE

At Cadence, we hire and develop leaders and innovators who want to make an impact on the world of technology. Join our team and help build the next generation of low-power verification software for the Palladium and Protium emulation and prototyping platforms. As a Lead Software Engineer on the Palladium and Protium UPF R&D team you will be responsible for improving UPF compilation performance and correctness, adding support for new IEEE 1801 LRM revisions, and rebuilding UPF documentation around end-to-end workflows for multi-billion-gate low-power designs. What you will do Contribute to the UPF compilation flow -- improve compilation performance, reduce instrumented capacity, and add self-checks across Parallel Partition Compiler (PPC) and Modular Compiler (MC) flows, including Dielet UPF support. Rebuild UPF documentation around end-to-end compilation workflows, backed by valid SystemVerilog and UPF examples, and keep code and docs in sync through example-driven checks. Work with R&D, PE and AE teams to deploy UPF solutions across key flows (AVIP, UVMA, MC, and Dielets -- each with UPF in 2- and 4-state). Help consolidate and unify UPF compilation across Palladium and Protium. What we are looking for BS in CS, EE, or a related field with 4+ years of experience; MS with 2+ years; PhD with 1+ year; or equivalent practical experience. Working knowledge of IEEE 1801 UPF and RTL languages (Verilog, SystemVerilog, VHDL). Ability to read, review, and reason about C++ code and object-oriented designs. Scripting experience in Perl, Tcl/Tk, and/or Python. Fluency with AI-assisted coding tools (Copilot, Claude, Cursor, or equivalent), with critical evaluation of AI output.

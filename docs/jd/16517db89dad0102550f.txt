ABOUT EARNIN As one of the first pioneers of earned wage access, our passion at EarnIn is building products that deliver real-time financial flexibility for people living paycheck to paycheck. Our community members access their earnings as they earn them, with options to spend, save, and grow their money without mandatory fees, interest rates, or credit checks. We’re fortunate to have an incredibly experienced leadership team, combined with world-class funding partners like A16Z, Matrix Partners, DST, Ribbit Capital, and a very healthy core business with a tremendous runway. We’re growing fast and excited to bring on world-class talent to help shape the next chapter of our growth journey. EarnIn is becoming an AI-native company, embedding AI agents into how we build, analyze, and operate across every function. Teams use AI Agents to move from ideas to execution faster. By letting agents handle repetitive tasks, our people can focus on judgment, creativity, and hard problems. We pair this experimentation with the strict review and security guardrails our regulated financial environment requires, always keeping a focus on privacy and the people who rely on our products. As a company, we are becoming AI-native, and we encourage you to use AI tools throughout much of your interview process. If you use AI during the live interview, just say so; a quick, natural mention of how it helped and why you are using it is all we need. What we're evaluating is your judgment, reasoning, and ability to own and defend your work, not whether AI was involved. Some rounds, particularly those focused on your career history and past experiences, will not allow the use of AI, and we'll let you know of those in advance. When in doubt, ask your recruiter or interviewer directly. POSITION SUMMARY We seek an experienced, passionate, and resourceful Senior Staff Software Engineer to join our product engineering team. WHAT YOU’LL DO Own architecture that spans business units: define domain boundaries, decide where cross-cutting concerns belong, and keep product logic, policy, and funds flow in the right layers. Lead design and architecture reviews across BUs, and record decisions in clear, durable architecture decision records. Drive decomposition and unification roadmaps for shared systems, including how user state is split across domains, and sequence the work so teams can adopt it incrementally. Prevent accumulated complexity by making deprecation, experiment cleanup, and clear ownership part of the design, not an afterthought. Partner with Platform, Money Platform, and BU engineering leads so standards are practical, adopted, and not duplicated. Use AI tools daily for design exploration, code analysis, and review, and build the agents, skills, and documentation that make our architecture legible to both engineers and AI coding agents. Set the bar for responsible AI-assisted development: guardrails, review practices, and patterns that keep AI-generated code consistent with our architecture. Mentor Staff and Senior engineers through design reviews, code, and architecture guidance, and help raise the technical bar across teams. Contribute to hiring and onboarding, and represent engineering in technical discussions with product, risk, and leadership. WHAT WE’RE LOOKING FOR Bachelor’s, Master’s, or PhD in computer science, computer engineering, or a related technical discipline, or equivalent industry experience. 10+ years of backend or full-stack software development, with a track record of designing and evolving large distributed systems. Demonstrated experience defining domain boundaries and service contracts across multiple teams, with practical knowledge of domain-driven design, event-driven systems, and clean architecture. Hands-on, daily use of AI coding and design tools, with concrete examples of how they changed the way you work and the results you achieved. Experience leading cross-team technical initiatives and influencing without direct authority, including resolving disagreements between teams. Excellent written and verbal communication, including the ability to turn complex trade-offs into clear decisions and documentation. Experience in fintech, payments, or other regulated, high-reliability environments is a plus.

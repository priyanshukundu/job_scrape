Reporting to the Sr. Director, Portfolio Access Optimization, the role of Director, Portfolio Access Optimization will be responsible for assessing the strategic, financial, and operational direct and indirect impacts of government programs and policy changes through to driving solutions to maximize opportunities, minimize risks, and ensure compliance with program and policy requirements – particularly those related to the Inflation Reduction Act (IRA). This position requires enterprise-level thinking and influence, strong strategic judgment, and the ability to lead through ambiguity. The director will serve as a subject matter expert on Medicare drug price negotiations and be a thought partner on market access direct and indirect implications of program and policy issues to proactively identify insights, manage risks and frame opportunities for the current portfolio and pipeline. This role is accountable for ViiV’s strategic and tactical preparations for price negotiations – leading cross-functional sub-teams internally (Finance, Medical, Legal, External Affairs, Government Relations), managing external vendor strategic projects, and facilitating overall readiness for comprehensive data packages, successful negotiations and implementation readiness. This role is based at ViiV Healthcare’s U.S. headquarters in Durham, NC and follows a hybrid working model, requiring a minimum of 2 days per week onsite. Candidates who do not currently live within commuting distance of Durham will be required to relocate to North Carolina. This role is eligible for a domestic relocation package.

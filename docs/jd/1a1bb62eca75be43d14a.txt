About the team The Senior Specialist, Software Engineering, Full Stack designs, builds, and operates end-to-end web applications and services that power Zillow’s customer and internal experiences. This role combines deep front-end and back-end expertise to deliver scalable, resilient solutions, mentor other engineers, and drive technical decisions for moderately complex initiatives within a product or platform area. About the role - Architect, develop, and maintain full stack web applications and services, spanning modern front-end frameworks and scalable back-end APIs and data stores. - Lead design and implementation of complex features from concept through deployment, ensuring performance, reliability, security, and maintainability. - Collaborate closely with product, design, data, and adjacent engineering teams to refine requirements, break down work, and deliver high-impact solutions iteratively. - Establish and advocate for engineering best practices in code quality, testing, observability, CI/CD, and incident response, and model these standards in day-to-day work. - Mentor and provide technical guidance to other engineers through code reviews, design discussions, and pairing, helping elevate overall team capability. - Monitor, troubleshoot, and optimize production systems, using data and diagnostics to improve system health, user experience, and operational efficiency. Who you are - Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, or a related technical field. - 8+ years of professional software engineering experience, including significant work across both front-end and back-end web application development. - Proven experience designing, building, and operating scalable, reliable services and user-facing features in a modern cloud environment. - Demonstrated experience collaborating in cross-functional, agile teams and influencing technical direction for complex projects. - Or an equivalent combination of education and experience.

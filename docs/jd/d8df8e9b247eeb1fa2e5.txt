Job Title: Manager, Financial Planning & Analysis Job Description: The Role: We are seeking a highly skilled Finance Manager to join our Corporate Financial Planning and Analysis (FP&A) team. The ideal candidate will serve as the lead finance business partner to our Chief Information Officer, Chief Financial Officer, General Counsel and SVP Human Resources and their leadership teams. As part of the Corporate FP&A team, we will work together to collaborate with business, finance and accounting teams across the organization to help analyze and communicate key drivers of the company’s financial results, as well as provide inputs to executive management packages and scorecards. This is a highly visible role interacting with Executive-level business and finance partners. What You’ll Do: Partner with the Chief Information Officer, Chief Financial Officer, General Counsel and SVP Human Resources to develop Annual Operating Plans and in-year forecast updates that achieve function and enterprise priorities. Prepare and present monthly, quarterly and annual financial reports with variance analyses and actionable insights. Increase the sophistication of Corporate Functions (HR, Finance, IT, etc) budgeting process. Improve the efficiency and outputs of internal processes and assist in implementing “best-in-class " company-wide standards for management reporting, planning, and forecasting. Increase the visibility and transparency of functional spending to senior leaders. Support decision-making through scenario modelling for programs, staffing and technology investments. Develop and demonstrate a high skill level in the use of financial systems with an emphasis on driving efficiencies and automation in reporting. Assist in the preparation of Senior Executive-level financial review packages. Bring a strong work ethic and positive attitude to your responsibilities every day. Develop and lead one direct report. What We Seek: Bachelor’s Degree Finance, Accounting, Economics or a business-related field 5 years of experience demonstrating financial analysis roles. Experience supporting Corporate FP&A or Business Unit FP&A is highly desirable. Proficient in fundamentals of Corporate Finance and modeling of business scenarios. Proficient knowledge of dashboards, business intelligence (BI) technologies, and data visualization. Advanced Microsoft Excel and PowerPoint skills. Experience demonstrating strong system skills; preferred experience with financial software (SAP BPC and/or OneStream a plus). Outstanding Candidates Will Have: Self-motivated positive team player with strong ability to interact with others, work under pressure and adhere to deadlines. Strong analytical and critical thinking skills with the ability to propose positive business solutions to problems. Strong organizational skills with the ability to prioritize multiple tasks in a fast-paced environment with focused attention to detail. Excellent written and verbal communications skills with ability to work cross-functionally at a high level of customer service. Comfortable working with high volumes of data and the ability to identify and summarize key messages. Must be adaptable, able to multi-task and take on additional responsibilities as necessary.

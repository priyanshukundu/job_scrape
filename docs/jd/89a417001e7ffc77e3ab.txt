About This Role The Physical Safety Sr. Associate II serves as the site lead for physical and contractor safety programs. This role provides technical expertise, program ownership, and hands-on support to reduce workplace risk; strengthen safety culture; and enable safe operations, expansions, new modalities, and capital projects. The position partners across Operations, Facilities, Engineering, and Warehousing to translate regulatory and company requirements into practical, sustainable controls. What You'll Do: Physical Safety Program Leadership Own and continuously improve site programs for machine guarding, lockout/tagout, electrical safety, powered industrial trucks, walking-working surfaces, material handling, fall protection, line breaking, and other physical hazards. Interpret applicable OSHA, fire and life safety, consensus-standard, and company requirements; maintain program documentation; support inspections and audits; and track regulatory and internal commitments. Contractor Safety & Project Support Lead contractor prequalification, onboarding, risk review, field oversight, performance monitoring, and post-work evaluation; partner with project teams to ensure contractor controls are incorporated into planning and execution. Provide EHS support for new equipment, production areas, site expansions, shutdowns, maintenance activities, and high-risk work from design through startup. Risk Assessment & Incident Learning Facilitate job hazard assessments, pre-startup reviews, management-of-change safety input, incident learning, and field risk assessments. Verify that corrective actions are appropriate, timely, and effective. Lead and participate in investigations and emergency response activities as assigned, applying objective fact-finding and effective corrective-action principles. Safety Culture & Area Support Coordinate culture initiatives, safety champion activities, employee engagement, and recognition programs. Support VPP management system readiness and sustainment. Serve as the primary EHS partner for assigned functions, including Engineering, Facilities, Label and Pack, and Warehouse operations. Training, Communication & Performance Improvement Develop and deliver role-based training, procedures, tools, and safety communications for employees, contractors, and leaders. Analyze leading and lagging indicators, identify trends, conduct field observations, and recommend risk-based priorities to leadership. Required Skills: Bachelor’s degree in occupational safety, engineering, industrial technology, environmental health, industrial hygiene, or a related field, with relevant professional experience in EHS, manufacturing, engineering, construction safety, or operations; an equivalent combination of education and experience may be considered. Minimum 3-5 years of relevant experience. Demonstrated knowledge of physical safety and contractor safety principles in a regulated manufacturing, pharmaceutical, biotechnology, chemical, or similarly complex environment. Experience conducting risk assessments, supporting incident investigations, developing procedures, and implementing corrective and preventive actions. Working knowledge of applicable OSHA requirements and relevant standards such as NFPA, ANSI, and other recognized industry practices. Ability to independently manage multiple priorities, influence without direct authority, and communicate effectively with employees, contractors, technical teams, and senior leaders. Preferred Skills: Professional certification such as CSP, ASP, CHST, or equivalent. Experience supporting capital projects, equipment startup, warehouse operations, contractor management, or VPP-aligned programs. Experience applying proactive safety-culture tools. Proficiency with EHS management systems, data analysis, dashboards, and digital action-tracking tools. Work Environment and Expectations: This is a site-based role requiring regular presence in manufacturing, warehouse, utility, construction, and office environments. The role may require use of designated personal protective equipment, response to urgent operational needs, and occasional support outside normal business hours. Physical Requirements: Ability to stand, walk, and move throughout manufacturing, warehouse, utility, construction, and office areas for extended periods. Ability to climb stairs and ladders and access elevated or confined work areas, such as mezzanines, platforms, and rooftops, as needed. Ability to bend, stoop, kneel, crouch, and reach to inspect equipment, guarding, and work areas. Ability to wear required PPE, which may include safety glasses, hard hat, safety shoes, hearing protection, gloves, and respiratory protection, as applicable. Visual, hearing, and speech abilities sufficient to identify hazards, conduct inspections, and communicate in emergency situations. Ability to perform computer-based work, including keyboarding and extended screen time, for documentation, data analysis, and reporting.

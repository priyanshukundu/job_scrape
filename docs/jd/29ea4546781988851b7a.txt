Senior Data Scientist
William Blair
Location: Chicago, Illinois, United States

Solutions for Today’s Challenges. Vision for Tomorrow’s Opportunities. Join William Blair, the Premier Global Partnership. As a Senior Data Scientist at William Blair, you’ll turn complex data and business needs into analytics, machine-learning, and AI solutions that support informed decision-making. You’ll partner with business and technology teams to define data requirements, develop and validate models, and deliver scalable solutions using Python, SQL, and modern data technologies. You’ll communicate findings clearly, uphold the firm’s data-governance and security standards, and help improve analytical processes, automation, and data quality. Your work will transform data into actionable insights that support the firm’s evolving business strategies. Responsibilities include but may not be limited to: Define data requirements, compile and validate data sets, and design analyses that support business strategies and decision-making. Partner with business stakeholders to identify opportunities for data science, advanced analytics, machine learning and AI. Plan, coordinate and execute data science projects from initial concept through implementation and ongoing support. Participate in data requirements and solution-design discussions with business and technology partners. Translate business questions into analytical approaches, models, metrics and measurable outcomes. Analyze structured and unstructured data to identify trends, relationships, anomalies and business opportunities. Apply appropriate statistical, machine-learning, AI and data-visualization techniques. Develop analytical models and solutions using Python, SQL and related technologies. Communicate findings, methodologies, assumptions and limitations clearly to technical and non-technical audiences. Guide data validation and model-evaluation processes to ensure results are accurate, reliable and explainable. Ensure data and analytical solutions align with firm data-governance, security and compliance standards. Collaborate with technology teams and vendors to implement, operationalize and support analytical solutions. Identify opportunities to improve data quality, analytical processes, automation and efficiency. Develop and maintain documentation for data science projects, including data sources, requirements, methodologies and specifications. Provide guidance and knowledge sharing to less experienced team members. Monitor industry trends and evaluate emerging data science, machine-learning and AI approaches and technologies. Ensure all activities comply with applicable rules, regulations, policies and procedures. Additional responsibilities as requested. Qualifications: Master's Degree required; concentration in data science, electronic engineering, or a related field 4 – 6 years of experience in data science required; financial services industry experience preferred Strong proficiency in Python and SQL. Experience with statistical analysis, machine-learning techniques and data visualization. Familiarity with generative AI, large language models or other applied AI technologies. Understanding of database architecture, data modeling and cloud-based data technologies. Ability to translate business needs into practical analytical solutions. Strong project management, prioritization and problem-solving skills. Strong data-analysis skills with attention to detail and quality. Knowledge of data science industry trends, methods and best practices.

Director – Enterprise Operations Risk
M&T Bank
Location: Buffalo, NY
Link: https://mtb.wd5.myworkdayjobs.com/MTB/job/Buffalo-NY/Director---Enterprise-Operations-Risk_R92218

Overview Leads a team of primarily managers and senior risk professionals within the Enterprise Operations side of Technology & Enterprise Operations (TEO) First Line Risk. Leads and influences long-term strategic direction for the teams providing first-line risk oversight of the Bank’s commercial, customer and business-line operations, including strategic workforce planning. The teams currently in scope cover commercial operations, customer contact and customer advocacy; wealth, trust and enterprise services; and consumer lending and servicing including asset management. Each team also carries risk oversight of the application and data environments its operations depend on. Team alignment is expected to evolve as the Bank’s operating model and footprint automation change, and the role is accountable for keeping coverage complete and ownership clear as it does. The role leads the shift in our environments from primarily assessing what people do to governing what systems decide, across processes increasingly run by people, bots and AI agents, while preserving the operational risk discipline that remains essential to the Bank. Primary Responsibilities Strategic Risk Leadership Lead risk strategy for commercial, customer and business-line operations, including setting strategic priorities, defining and communicating rationalized risk appetite metrics and thresholds with operations and line-of-business leaders, and developing and implementing advanced risk mitigation strategies aligned with organizational imperatives. Maintain clear, single-point accountability for every capability within scope, ensuring complete coverage, defined ownership and timely escalation, and lead the realignment of capabilities across teams as the operating model evolves. Champion strategic initiatives within the team of oversight, across TEO First Line Risk and Enterprise Operations, to meet the strategic and long-term needs of the organization. Influence, drive and lead opportunities to improve risk management activities that increase operational efficiency and overall outcomes, ensuring a proactive stance against potential risks. Business-Line Operations Risk Oversight Oversee first-line risk across business-line operations, which currently include: Commercial: Commercial loan documentation, post-closing, collateral management and servicing; dealer commercial and agent banking; international trade finance; regulatory reporting; fraud investigation and analytics; and dispute processing. Customer: Customer advocacy and complaint processing, service and sales contact centers, and the customer-outcome view across the processes in scope. Wealth, Trust & Enterprise Services: Client onboarding, trust and investment management operations, document custody, transfer agent, equity trading, tax and IRA processing, records and legal document management, and enterprise operations enablement. Consumer Lending & Servicing: Consumer loan and mortgage documentation, post-closing and servicing; credit card servicing; credit bureau and investor reporting; SCRA servicing; collections and default management; legal and support services; and homeowner assistance. Oversee end-to-end process risk across these operations, including transaction integrity, customer impact, regulatory obligations, process mapping and RCSA, loss and near-miss events, and root cause analysis. Customer Outcomes & Conduct Embed a customer-outcome lens across the teams in scope, using complaint, dispute and contact center insight to identify emerging risk, customer harm and UDAAP concerns, and to prioritize remediation. Promote fairness and customer-outcome testing alongside process testing, particularly where automation or AI influences customer-facing decisions. Application Risk (Light Technology) Provide first-line risk oversight of the application environments that support the business lines in scope, such as commercial, wealth, consumer and corporate function platforms, through technology risk specialists embedded within each team. Oversee application-level risks and controls, including user entitlements and access reviews, application change impacts on operational processes, interfaces and data feeds, application resilience and recovery, vendor-hosted platforms, and end-user computing tools. Partner with the Cybersecurity & AI Risk and Technology Infrastructure Risk teams, which retain oversight of infrastructure and cybersecurity risk, so that technology risks affecting operations are owned and escalated without gaps or duplication. Automation & AI in Operations Advise on the governance of automation and AI use cases within the business lines in scope, including RPA, intelligent document processing, AI agents and generative AI, applying enterprise AI governance standards and risk-tiering use cases by degree of autonomy and customer impact, as adopted by the Bank. Advise on controls for hybrid human, bot and agent processes, including human-in-the-loop thresholds, agent permissions, guardrails, kill switches, audit trails and exception handling, alongside traditional maker-checker, reconciliation and segregation-of-duties controls. Controls, Monitoring & Resilience Direct the evolution from periodic, sample-based control testing to continuous monitoring with automated evidence, partnering with Controls Testing & Assurance. Establish and monitor key risk indicators that pair operational measures (error rates, aged breaks, SLA breaches, complaint trends) with AI-era measures (model drift, override rates, bot failures, unreviewed outputs). Drive operational resilience for the operations in scope: map processes, applications and automations to important business services, assess vendor and concentration risk, and ensure tested manual fallback for critical processes. Understand and adhere to the Company’s risk and regulatory standards, policies and controls in accordance with the Company’s Risk Appetite. Design, implement, maintain and enhance internal controls to mitigate risk on an ongoing basis, and identify risk-related issues needing escalation to management. Reporting, Regulatory Engagement & Governance Shape strategic direction for reporting and documentation by influencing effective reporting outcomes, guiding the team on how to execute against standardized frameworks, and query-based analysis to strengthen risk insight. Guide the strategic approach to regulatory responses for the business lines in scope, proactively engaging with regulatory bodies and leading the organization to develop and implement advanced strategies for long-term regulatory compliance. Cultivate strategic partnerships with internal stakeholders to ensure compliance with policies, and build and maintain relationships with external stakeholders, to support the resiliency of Enterprise Operations and the overall Bank. Maintain M&T internal control standards, including timely implementation of internal and external audit points together with any issues raised by external regulators as applicable. Ensure that risk is embedded in the design of operational process changes, new platforms and automation use cases, engaging at intake and solution design rather than after change or just before go-live. Team Leadership, Talent & Capability Building Build and lead a multi-team organization, including establishing leadership for newly formed teams and filling specialist seats in line with strategic workforce planning. Recruit and develop for the team competencies outlined below, balancing operational risk depth with customer, data, application technology and automation skills so each team is self-sufficient within its scope. Monitor the people-side risks of automation in operations, including skills erosion and oversight fatigue. Influence training and education at senior leader level and drive the execution of advanced training programs that equip stakeholders to manage operational, customer, application and automation risk. Strategically direct financial and business operations with long-term plans in alignment with business objectives and fiscal responsibility. Exercise usual authority of a manager concerning staffing, performance appraisals, promotions, salary recommendations, performance management and terminations. Promote an environment that supports belonging and reflects the M&T Bank brand. Complete other related duties as assigned.Scope of Responsibilities Interacts and collaborates primarily with executive and senior level people leaders across Enterprise Operations, commercial, wealth and consumer business lines, and Technology, and occasionally the Executive Leadership Team. Work is accomplished with periodic direction; strategizes department imperatives based on business unit and Bank imperatives. Exercises judgment in all aspects of the role and exerts significant latitude in determining the strategy of the team. Able to take calculated risks. Typically oversees and leads a team of 15–45 total FTE across multiple teams, with primarily managers and senior managers as direct reports, supported by mid- to senior-level risk professionals and technology risk specialists. Serves as lead representative with regulators for the business lines in scope. Oversees multiple Enterprise Operations risk teams within TEO First Line Risk, with scope expected to evolve as the operating model matures.

Role Profile LSEG (London Stock Exchange Group) is a leading global financial markets infrastructure and data provider. We enable financial markets around the world through trusted data, technology and market infrastructure. Data Collection Development Americas develops and supports real-time systems that collect, process and distribute financial market data from stock exchanges and other data providers across the United States, Canada and Latin America. The Business Analyst works at the intersection of financial markets, data and technology. The role involves developing expertise in assigned market data feeds, analyzing stock exchange specifications and notifications, gathering and documenting business and technical requirements, supporting testing, and helping deliver market changes into LSEG's production systems. Responsibilities Develop knowledge of assigned stock exchanges, market data feeds, asset classes and market structure. Review and interpret stock exchange specifications, technical documentation, market notices and vendor communications. Monitor stock exchange and contributor notifications and assess potential impacts to LSEG products, services and customers. Gather, analyze and document business, functional, technical and data requirements. Translate stock exchange specifications and market behavior into clear requirements for Software Engineers and other technical teams. Analyze messages, fields, data types, identifiers, mappings, code sets and market rules. Create and maintain product specifications, data mappings, test cases and technical documentation. Validate market data against requirements, stock exchange specifications and expected market behavior. Investigate data discrepancies and support root-cause analysis, regression testing and production readiness. Work with data using tools such as Excel and SQL, with opportunities to use Python or similar scripting languages for analysis, validation and lightweight automation. Use approved Generative AI and AI-assisted tools to support specification analysis, requirements drafting, data exploration, test-case development and knowledge discovery. Critically review and validate AI-generated outputs and use AI responsibly in accordance with LSEG policies. Work collaboratively with Business Analysts, Product Delivery Managers, Software Engineers, Quality Assurance, Product and Operations teams. Manage multiple assignments and priorities in a fast-paced, real-time market data environment. Skills & Requirements Strong analytical, problem-solving and critical-thinking skills. High attention to detail when working with data, specifications and testing. Strong written and verbal communication skills. Ability to understand and organize complex technical information. Foundational financial markets literacy, including an understanding or interest in equities, fixed income, derivatives and other asset classes. Understanding or interest in developing knowledge of trades, quotes, bid/ask prices, order books/market depth, reference data and trading sessions. Strong data literacy and familiarity with structured datasets and data analysis. Proficiency with Excel or similar analytical tools. Ability and willingness to develop skills in SQL, Python, APIs, data visualization and AI-assisted analysis. Ability to learn new financial, market data and technical concepts quickly. Ability to work effectively within cross-functional and Agile delivery teams. Desired Skills Degree or equivalent experience in Business, Finance, Economics, Computer Science, Information Systems, Data Analytics or a related discipline. Interest in global financial markets, financial technology and real-time market data. Exposure to SQL, Python, Power BI, APIs, JSON/XML or other data-analysis technologies. Familiarity with Generative AI tools such as Microsoft Copilot and an understanding of responsible AI use. Experience with data-intensive, real-time or production systems is beneficial. Exposure to Agile software development and tools such as Jira, Azure DevOps, Confluence or SharePoint is beneficial. The successful candidate will progressively become a market data and market logic subject-matter expert for assigned feeds while developing strong business analysis, technical, financial markets, data and AI capabilities.

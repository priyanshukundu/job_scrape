TransUnion's Job Applicant Privacy Notice Personal Information We Collect Your Privacy Choices Team Overview The Specialized Risk Group develops data-driven products and solutions that help customers address complex challenges across investigations, identity, fraud, communications, insurance, collections, and related markets. The team brings together expertise across Data Science, Product, Technology, Data Engineering, Solutions, and Operations to transform public records and other complex data assets into scalable analytical and AI-enabled capabilities. This role reports to a Senior Director of Data Science & Analytics. This is a hybrid position and involves regular performance of job responsibilities virtually as well as in-person at an assigned TU office location for a minimum of two days a week. Role Overview and Core Responsibilities Translate complex and sometimes ambiguous business needs into well-defined analytical problems, solution designs, delivery plans, and measurable success criteria. Lead complex data science and AI initiatives from opportunity assessment and proof of concept through validation, implementation, production monitoring, and ongoing enhancement, with minimal day-to-day guidance. Develop differentiated and scalable scores, attributes, predictive models, and AI-enabled decisioning capabilities that advance the Specialized Risk product roadmap and deliver measurable value for TransUnion and its customers. Design reusable analytical features, frameworks, and components that can support multiple customers, products, and business use cases. Apply sound analytical judgment when using public records and other Specialized Risk Group data assets, accounting for data quality, completeness, recency, coverage, permitted use, and potential limitations. Incorporate appropriate model and AI controls, including validation, explainability, bias and stability assessments, documentation, guardrails, human oversight, monitoring, and escalation procedures. Partner with Product, Solutions, Technology, Data Engineering, Sales, Operations, Legal, Compliance, and other stakeholders to define requirements, assess feasibility, resolve delivery dependencies, and transition solutions from experimentation into production. Communicate technical approaches, model performance, AI capabilities, limitations, risks, and anticipated business value through clear presentations, documentation, and discussions tailored to technical and nontechnical audiences. Review analytical work and provide technical guidance to data scientists and analysts, promoting knowledge sharing, consistent quality, and the development of less-experienced colleagues. Required Knowledge and Experiences A master’s or doctoral degree in statistics, applied mathematics, financial mathematics, computer science, engineering, operations research, or another highly quantitative field; or a bachelor’s degree in a quantitative discipline with at least 6 years of relevant professional experience. 5+ years of professional analytical experience directly supporting internal or external clients. Demonstrated ability to apply statistical modeling, predictive analytics, supervised and unsupervised ML (Machine Learning), anomaly detection, pattern recognition, segmentation, or optimization techniques to complex business problems. Experience leading complex analytical projects and managing the full model lifecycle, including problem definition, data exploration, feature engineering, model development, validation, implementation, performance monitoring, and enhancement. Strong business acumen, analytical judgment, project management, and communication skills, with the ability to manage multiple priorities and translate complex technical concepts into clear, actionable recommendations for internal stakeholders and customers. This role may require travel approximately 10% to 20% of the time. Required Technical Skills Advanced proficiency in Python or R for statistical modeling, machine learning, and analytical solution development. Strong proficiency in SQL (Structured Query Language) for querying, transforming, and analyzing large and complex datasets. Experience using distributed data-processing platforms or technologies such as Apache Spark or Hive to develop scalable analytical solutions. Experience applying model evaluation, explainability, stability testing, monitoring, documentation, guardrails, and human-in-the-loop review to support reliable and responsible production use. We’re also looking for the preferred skills below. Whether you are proficient or could use some brushing up, we’re happy to support your career development and growth in: Experience applying emerging AI (Artificial Intelligence) techniques, including generative AI, LLMs (Large Language Models), NLP (Natural Language Processing), RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation), or agentic AI, to practical customer and business use cases. Familiarity with public records, identity, fraud, credit bureau, or other large-scale consumer and business data, including an understanding of data coverage, recency, limitations, and appropriate use. Experience with graph analytics, entity resolution, relationship analytics, network analysis, or link analysis to identify unusual behaviors, hidden relationships, emerging patterns, or elevated-risk entities. Experience combining structured, semi-structured, and unstructured data from multiple sources to create model features, entity-level insights, and AI-enabled solutions. Experience with cloud platforms such as Google Cloud Platform or Amazon Web Services and operationalizing analytical or AI solutions through APIs, batch or real-time scoring, containerization, production deployment, and ongoing monitoring. Experience in financial services, insurance, fraud, digital marketing, or another industry served by TransUnion is strongly valued.

About this role: Wells Fargo is seeking a Senior Software Engineer within the Commercial & Corporate Investment Banking Technology (CCIBT) organization. The role resides in the Enterprise Counterparty Risk Technology (ECRT) team, which develops and supports technology solutions that enable the identification, measurement, aggregation, and reporting of counterparty credit risk across the enterprise. ECRT partners closely with Corporate Risk, lines of business, and Enterprise Counterparty Credit Risk teams to deliver strategic technology solutions that help ensure risks are effectively managed within the firm's established risk appetite. This position will work closely with the Enterprise Counterparty Credit Risk Management organization and requires a strong understanding of capital markets and investment banking. The ideal candidate will possess experience or familiarity with counterparty credit risk, capital markets products, and relevant regulatory frameworks, as well as knowledge of clearing operations, prime brokerage, and broader investment banking business processes. In addition to strong technical expertise, the role requires the ability to collaborate with business stakeholders to deliver scalable, reliable, and risk-focused technology solutions.

The Role Accountable for objective proof that the infrastructure is ready for production. Owns validation, performance baselines, defect closure, and the acceptance evidence that supports customer sign-off and go/no-go decisions. Key responsibilities · Create a requirements-to-test traceability matrix and staged entry/exit gates for site readiness, physical installation, power-on, fabric, storage, platform, burn-in, performance, resiliency, and handover. · Develop version-controlled provisioning, health-check, diagnostic, stress, regression, and evidence-collection automation. · Validate GPU or accelerator health, PCIe, GPU interconnect (for example NVLink/NVSwitch), NIC/HCA/DPU, InfiniBand or Ethernet/RoCE fabrics, storage throughput, Kubernetes, operators, scheduling, and telemetry. · Execute failure and recovery tests including node loss, link degradation, service restart, workload rescheduling, rebuild, replacement, and return to service. · Establish workload-relevant baselines for collective communications, network, storage, inference, training, and platform operations without assuming ownership of every customer model benchmark. · Triage failures, isolate defects, coordinate corrective actions, retest, and maintain auditable defect and acceptance records. · Produce formal acceptance packages containing configuration baselines, test results, exceptions, waivers, evidence, sign-offs, and residual risks. · Use deployment findings and production incidents to extend automated coverage and prevent recurrence. Who You Are Required Experience · 5+ years in infrastructure validation, automation, performance engineering, commissioning, or quality engineering. · Strong Linux scripting and automation with Python, Ansible, APIs, Git, and CI/CD. · Experience defining acceptance criteria, analyzing diagnostics, and managing defects to closure. Preferred Skills · NVIDIA DCGM diagnostics and telemetry, CVT, MLXlink, fabric validation, GPU burn-in, NCCL tests. · Validation of DGX/HGX, InfiniBand, Spectrum-X, BlueField, Kubernetes operators, NVAIE, or Run:ai. · Experience producing customer acceptance packages for NVIDIA DGX/HGX or NVIDIA-Certified AI infrastructure. · NVIDIA or HPC benchmarking credentials and test-engineering credentials (for example ISTQB or equivalent).

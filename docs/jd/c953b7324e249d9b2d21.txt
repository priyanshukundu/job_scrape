Senior Manager of Operational Risk Management Oversight
Citizens Financial Group
Location: Johnston, RI, United States
Link: https://hcgn.fa.us2.oraclecloud.com/hcmUI/CandidateExperience/en/sites/CX_1/job/50491

The Senior Manager of Operational Risk Management Oversight is a senior-level individual contributor position that does not have direct reports, but carries significant responsibility for risk expertise, independent judgment, influence and credible challenge across Consumer Banking. The role evaluates whether material risks and controls have been appropriately identified, assessed, documented, monitored and managed and determines whether the First Line's risk and control assessments appropriately reflect the underlying risk exposure. The Senior Manager oversees Consumer Specialty Operations and unsecured lending products. The position requires a high level of expertise in risk and control assessment and the ability to independently evaluate and challenge the First Line's identification, assessment and management of risks and controls. This position works closely with business and product leaders, First and Second Line Risk teams, Legal, Compliance, Internal Audit and other stakeholders to promote a strong risk and control environment. Success requires the ability to build trusted relationships, influence without direct authority and maintain the independence and judgment necessary to provide thoughtful, credible and effective challenge. Primary responsibilities include Provide independent operational risk oversight and credible challenge across Consumer Banking operational and product areas, assessing whether First Line risk identification, assessment and management appropriately reflect the underlying risk exposure. Evaluate inherent risk, control design and effectiveness, assessment results and residual risk to determine whether risks are appropriately understood, controlled and managed within established risk appetite. Apply significant risk-management expertise and judgment to identify gaps or weaknesses in risk assessments, controls, mitigation strategies and remediation activities and determine when additional action or escalation is warranted. Partner with Consumer Operations, product teams and Risk partners to maintain a comprehensive understanding of business activities, products, processes, regulatory requirements, emerging risks and changes to the control environment. Provide subject-matter expertise and independent challenge related to RCSAs and other components of the operational risk management framework, promoting consistent and effective application of risk-management standards. Identify and evaluate existing, emerging and upstream risks, including risks arising from business, product, process, technology, regulatory and strategic change. Provide oversight and challenge of risk and control issues, including issue identification, risk assessment, mitigation, remediation, reporting and escalation. Review policies, procedures and new or changing business initiatives to identify operational risk implications and assess alignment with established risk frameworks and risk appetite. Research and analyze risk trends, regulatory developments and industry practices to identify potential implications for Consumer Banking and inform risk-management priorities. Support effective regulatory and audit examination outcomes, partnering with Risk, Compliance, Legal and Internal Audit on complex risk and control matters. Build and maintain effective relationships across the First, Second and Third Lines of Defense and applicable regulatory partners, using influence and constructive challenge to drive appropriate risk-management outcomes. Strengthen risk-management capabilities and practices by sharing expertise, providing guidance and identifying opportunities to improve controls, framework implementation and Second Line oversight.

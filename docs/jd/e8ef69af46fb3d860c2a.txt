About this role: Wells Fargo is seeking an Equipment Operator for light banking equipment used for scanning and processing. You'll learn about payment processing, and the financial services industry. Lockbox is a high-volume, fast-paced, deadline driven production environment working behind the scenes with little, if any, customer contact. In this role, you will: Capture and sort mail and documents using low and high-speed scanners and mail sorting equipment Prepare and organize work for subsequent processing Problem solve as a result of machine error messages or jams Perform routine maintenance and simple repairs Escalate equipment issues and work with equipment vendors to address mechanical or software issues or updates Required Qualifications: 6+ months of Equipment Operations experience, or equivalent demonstrated through one or a combination of the following: work experience, training, military experience, education Desired Qualifications: Lockbox experience Ability to troubleshoot equipment and perform basic maintenance and repairs Ability to work effectively under direct supervision and follow specific guidelines Ability to work in a fast-paced deadline driven environment Attention to detail and accuracy skills Meet established production and quality standards Once trained, ability to take initiative and work independently with minimal supervision in a fast-paced structured environment Location : 2975 Regent Blvd, IRVING, TX 75063 Schedule : Friday-Monday 12:30pm-11:00pm (or until work is complete) Equipment Operations: Covers jobs that operate electronic or mechanical company equipment to process their daily work. Responsibilities include capturing, sorting and/or distributing items or documents using company machinery, preparing and organizing work for subsequent processing through machines, and performing general maintenance and repairs.

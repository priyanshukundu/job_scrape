About the team We are seeking a Senior Treasury Specialist to oversee capital adequacy, liquidity stress testing, financial risk management, and contingency funding planning for Bridge National Trust Bank (BNTB)'s Treasury and Reserve Operations. Issuing digital assets that are fully backed by reserves raises new questions for a regulated bank: how much capital and liquidity it needs, how it holds up under stress, and how it shows management, the Board, and regulators that it is safe and sound. What you'll do In this role, you will support Stripe’s proposed national trust bank, Bridge National Trust Bank (BNTB), currently in formation and subject to full regulatory approval by the OCC. You will build and run the bank's capital, liquidity, and financial risk management framework and the governance around it. That includes the analysis, monitoring, policies, and reporting that let the bank grow safely as regulatory requirements for stablecoin issuers continue to evolve. You will partner closely with the BNTB Treasury and Reserve Operations team, the broader Bridge and Stripe treasury team, risk, compliance, finance, accounting, regulatory reporting, product, data science, and engineering. In the near term, you will help design the frameworks and put the first cycles into practice. As the bank scales, you will turn them into repeatable, automated processes. Responsibilities Own capital planning and the ongoing assessment of the bank's capital adequacy Own and report on capital, liquidity, and financial risk metrics against risk appetite and limits; escalate breaches and emerging trends Design and run liquidity and capital stress testing, and turn the results into recommendations for funding and capital strategy Own contingency funding planning, including annual testing and tracking remediation to closure Maintain treasury risk policies and procedures, and prepare management and Board reporting Provide risk oversight of reserve and investment activity (counterparty, concentration, liquidity and interest rate risk); partner with product, data science, and engineering to automate monitoring and reporting Coordinate with the broader Stripe treasury team on capital management initiatives including consolidated capital planning, infrastructure and methodology development and governance frameworks Who you are You are a builder - we are building a new OCC chartered bank and we are looking for folks who thrive in a fast-paced, growing environment. This means one day you may be working on a stress testing methodology and the next day speaking with engineers about a critical liquidity concept to be incorporated into systems. You will thrive here if that energizes you, if you take ownership of whatever is needed, and if you bring the same rigor to a stress test assumption as you do to a Board memo. You combine the discipline of a risk manager with the instincts of a builder, and you can explain capital and liquidity concepts clearly to treasury, product, and engineering partners.

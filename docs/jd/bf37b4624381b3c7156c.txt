Responsibilities Lead the modeling and decision science that powers personalized, coordinated engagement across acquisition, service, and retention journeys. This role is part of the Digital Growth Engine, a multidisciplinary organization focused on building a stronger learning advantage through trusted data, disciplined experimentation, intelligent decisioning, and scalable technology. Develop propensity, churn, lifetime value, next-best-action, recommendation, prioritization, and other decision models that support precision engagement. Combine behavioral, transactional, marketing, digital, and contextual signals into scalable features and decisioning approaches. Partner with experimentation teams to validate model-driven interventions and distinguish predictive accuracy from incremental business impact. Design measurement approaches for model lift, calibration, treatment effectiveness, fairness, stability, and downstream customer outcomes. Work with engineering and product partners to productionize models and decision services in Databricks and connected activation platforms. Guide identity, feature, and data requirements while accounting for privacy, consent, governance, and channel constraints. Mentor technical talent and establish reusable standards for precision engagement modeling and decision science. Qualifications Bachelor's degree in data science, statistics, computer science, economics, engineering, mathematics, or a related quantitative field; advanced degree preferred. 8 plus years experience applying statistical modeling, machine learning, experimentation, or decision science to complex business problems. Advanced proficiency in Python and SQL, with experience working in modern cloud data and ML environments such as Databricks or comparable platforms. Demonstrated ability to translate ambiguous business questions into analytical approaches, production-ready models, and measurable outcomes. Strong communication skills with the ability to explain technical concepts, assumptions, limitations, and recommendations to technical and executive audiences. Preferred Experience Experience working in a large, regulated enterprise and navigating privacy, security, legal, risk, and governance requirements. Experience with digital customer journeys, insurance or financial services, growth analytics, personalization, experimentation, or marketing technology. Demonstrated success working in cross-functional product or pod-based operating models and moving work from insight to implementation. A bias toward measurable outcomes, reusable capabilities, clear documentation, and continuous learning.

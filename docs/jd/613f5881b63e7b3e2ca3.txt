Job Description The JPMorgan Quantitative Trading & Research (QTR) Sales team builds products which directly support the 2000+ member Sales organization. In doing so, we leverage JP Morgan’s vast portfolio of data and analytics and partner closely with Sales, Trading, Trading QTR, Digital Markets, Technology, and other groups. Job Summary: As an Associate for the Quantitative Trading & Research team, you will primarily focused on supporting Credit & Public Finance Sales. This global sales team distributes corporate & municipal bonds, loans, CLOs, CDS, credit indices, and other corporate-credit related products. You will turn complex data into client-relevant signals, content and trade ideas that support both inbound and outbound client workflows, and own each project end to end: from problem framing and data preparation through methodology, evaluation, production implementation and monitoring. Most but not all coding will be in Athena, a python-based JPM framework.

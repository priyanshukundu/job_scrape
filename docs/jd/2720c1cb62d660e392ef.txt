Job Description The Associate Director, Global Manufacturing Communications is a unique opportunity to join our Company as a key member of the Global Corporate Affairs and Manufacturing organizations. This role supports the development and implementation of multi-channel internal and external communications across site, product, supply, crisis, and enterprise priorities. The position requires a communications professional who can connect manufacturing operations, supply chain management, and reputation considerations to clear stakeholder messaging, site-level communications strategies, and execution plans for our manufacturing site in West Point, PA, representing approximately 7,500 employees. The successful candidate will report to our Manufacturing Division Site Communications Director, U.S. and operate in a dynamic, fast-paced environment. This hybrid role requires onsite presence two to three days per week, with travel to other U.S. locations as needed. Primary Responsibilities Integrated Communications Develop and execute integrated communications and editorial strategies that support divisional, site, and regional priorities. Connect site performance to the broader enterprise narrative for both wired and non-wired employees. Communicate how manufacturing performance, supply continuity, product milestones, and operational readiness impact patients, customers, employees, and company reputation. Measure audience reach, engagement, and effectiveness of communication efforts. Site Storytelling and Transformation Identify, develop, and support compelling storylines that highlight site achievements and business accomplishments. Support organizational transformation initiatives through effective change management communications. Develop workforce engagement opportunities that strengthen awareness and understanding of site priorities. Leadership Counsel and Preparation Advise site leadership on strategic communications approaches. Develop supporting communications plans, programs, and materials. Prepare leaders for: Employee town halls Speaking engagements Local announcements Community interactions Government and industry events Media engagement activities. Collaborate with policy and media relations teams when appropriate. Campaigns, Social Media, and AI Integration Lead multi-channel communication campaigns across employee communication platforms. Develop content that reaches both wired and non-wired employee populations. Integrate approved AI technologies into content creation, workflow management, and insight generation while ensuring appropriate oversight, confidentiality, accuracy, and approvals. Content and Review Management Manage development and review of communication materials, including Briefing documents, Talking points, Site newsletters, Digital signage content and Core response documents. Build strong relationships with stakeholders to facilitate timely reviews and approvals. Risk and Crisis Communications Help reduce organizational risk by educating employees on policies and escalating potential reputational concerns. Partner with divisional communications teams to assess risks and prioritize actions. Anticipate emerging issues and develop scenario-based response plans. Develop key messages and leader talking points to support rapid response to: Quality events Safety incidents Supply interruptions Business continuity events Activism-related issues Media inquiries other reputation-sensitive situations. Required Qualifications, Experience, and Skills Bachelor’s degree in communications, Journalism, Life Sciences, or a related discipline. Minimum of eight (8) years of communications experience. Proven experience developing and executing integrated internal and external communications strategies. Experience supporting: Employee engagement Executive communications Media preparedness Product communications Supply communications Change management initiatives Stakeholder issues management. Experience supporting site-level organizational changes. Experience supporting site launches or major operational milestones is highly desirable. Strong understanding of the biopharmaceutical industry and manufacturing environment. Demonstrated business acumen and understanding of how product strategy, supply reliability, quality standards, regulatory requirements, and stakeholder perceptions influence communications planning. Strong executive presence with the ability to influence and advise senior leaders. Experience providing strategic communications counsel and supporting crisis communications activities. Exceptional written and verbal communication skills, including the ability to translate scientific, technical, and business information into compelling communications. Proficiency with social media and digital communication channels. Familiarity with approved AI tools and emerging communications practices. Excellent project management skills and strong attention to detail. Experience managing communications reviews under tight timelines. Experience working with public relations agencies and managing project budgets. Proven ability to manage competing priorities and multiple stakeholder groups effectively. Demonstrated issues-management experience, including identification of emerging issues, risk assessment, and coordination of aligned responses. Exceptional interpersonal skills with the ability to build trusted relationships across multiple sites and stakeholder groups. Ability to operate independently while thriving in a matrixed, high-visibility environment. Preferred Experience and Skills Experience supporting our Research & Development Division, manufacturing organizations, or complex operational communications. Experience directly advising site leadership teams. Experience working within a global pharmaceutical, healthcare, or biotechnology organization. Understanding of how manufacturing operates across a global enterprise network. Experience developing communications programs for wired and non-wired employee populations. Experience supporting labor-relations communications. Proven media relations experience, including building relationships with local, industry, and trade media. Experience managing communications budgets and agency partnerships independently.

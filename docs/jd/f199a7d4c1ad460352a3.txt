About the Team & Role The WEX Authorization Service team is transforming the future of fleet payments through modern, scalable technology that powers our industry-leading fuel card platform. Within our Over-the-Road Experiences organization, we build full-stack applications and distributed services that help fleet managers and carrier administrators manage drivers, fleets, cards, and payment capabilities for critical operating expenses such as fuel and tires. We are looking for a highly experienced, hands-on Staff Software Development Engineer who combines strong backend engineering expertise with full-stack development capabilities and a passion for solving complex technical challenges. In this role, you will help shape the architecture and delivery of scalable, cloud-native applications using Java, AWS, event-driven technologies, and modern web frameworks. You will operate at both the implementation and architectural levels, spending meaningful time writing code while guiding technical decisions, mentoring engineers, and driving engineering excellence across the team. You will also have opportunities to utilize Ai tools and agents to build exciting customer experiences and new agentic capabilities that improve how we build and deliver software. This is an opportunity to influence the technical direction of business-critical products, solve problems at scale, and deliver meaningful experiences for WEX customers. Please note that we are unable to support any immigration sponsorship or transfer ( Now or in the future).

Job Description This role is responsible for leading the use of data and advanced analytics to drive decisions within Auto Claims. This includes: the development, execution, and management of new machine learning predictive modeling algorithms, the coding/development of tools that use machine learning/predictive modeling to make business decisions, searching for and integrating new data (structure and unstructured, internal and external) that improves our modeling and machine learning results (and ultimately our decisions), and discovery of solutions to business problems that can be solved through the use of machine learning/predictive modeling. This role will also manage projects of medium complexity. The role will begin to provide some mentoring and development support to help develop junior data scientists. Key Responsibilities Uses best practices to develop complex statistical, machine learning techniques to build models that address business needs and to improve the accuracy of our data and data-driven decisions. Reviews, evaluates and communicates appropriateness of modeling techniques and results to team, leadership and stakeholders to ensure these are well understood and incorporated into business processes. Collaborates with the team and works on data and complex business problems in order to improve the effectiveness of business decisions and business results through designing, building, and partnering to implement machines. learning/predictive models. Understands the business' problems to identify the optimal modeling and/or analytic approach. Innovates by researching, evaluating, and applying emerging modeling techniques and technologies to improve business outcomes. Utilizes effective project planning techniques to break down moderately complex and occasionally complex machine learning/predictive modeling and/or development projects into tasks and ensure deadlines are kept. Preferred Qualifications Master’s degree or equivalent experience. 3 or more years of experience as a Data Scientist. Supervisory Responsibilities This job does not have supervisory duties. Travel Ability to travel up to 10% to regional office for team meetings.

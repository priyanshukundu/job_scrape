This Software Development Engineer II role is part of the Core AI Experiences team which sits within our Technology division. The team develops AI-powered experiences that help make travel more intuitive and seamless for travelers. In this role, you will design, develop, and maintain high-quality software solutions that empower travel growth and enhance user experiences. In this role, you will: Design, develop, test, deploy, and maintain reliable software across services and technical domains, applying sound engineering practices throughout the development lifecycle. Build agentic software capabilities and AI-driven workflows that improve product outcomes, using responsible approaches to AI/ML-enabled product development. Translate product and technical requirements into well-structured solutions, including low-level system design, API design, data modeling, and implementation plans. Own features and assigned services from design through delivery and ongoing support, troubleshooting issues and improving reliability, performance, observability, and maintainability. Collaborate with engineering and partner teams to deliver integrated solutions, contribute to technical decisions, and adapt across multiple domains and technology stacks. Safely integrate and operate AI/ML-enabled solutions by applying appropriate testing, monitoring, safeguards, and feedback mechanisms in production workflows. Minimum Qualifications: Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science or a related technical field; or equivalent related professional experience. 2+ years of Software Development experience. Experience owning features or assigned services from design through delivery and ongoing support. Experience with one or more programming languages (ex. Java or Kotlin) Experience with programming fundamentals, automated testing, debugging, version control, low-level system design, API design, data modeling, and service integrations. Familiarity with AI-driven systems, tools, or workflows and applying AI/ML concepts to real-world products. Preferred Qualifications: Experience building agentic AI applications or agents using orchestration frameworks or comparable agent-development patterns, with the ability to apply these approaches to practical product use cases. Experience with prompt engineering, understanding LLM behavior, and making practical model-selection tradeoffs across providers and model types based on product requirements. Experience designing or evaluating agentic architecture patterns, including multi-agent or skill-based approaches, and selecting an appropriate pattern for a given customer or product problem. Hands-on experience integrating AI/ML-enabled solutions into production, such as retrieval-augmented generation, intelligent agents, recommendation systems, or decision systems, with emphasis on safety, guardrails, monitoring, and continuous improvement. Experience applying modern engineering practices to AI-centric services, including prompt and policy management, evaluation and observability, agent orchestration, and governance controls to support reliable, scalable deployment.

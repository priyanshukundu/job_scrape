Job Summary The Fusion Center Analyst Associate supports entry-level emergency management operations through all-hazards monitoring, Fusion Center support, incident information intake, situational awareness updates, notification support, and program record maintenance across the AEP footprint. This role assists with collecting, documenting, and routing incident and threat information from approved monitoring sources, internal partners, and external information-sharing channels. The Associate helps maintain common operating picture updates, incident logs, notification records, contact information, readiness materials, and after-action documentation under direct supervision. The role builds foundational knowledge of emergency management, ECC operations, enterprise response coordination, recovery support, and Incident Command System principles while supporting exercises and real-world events. Job Description What You’ll Do: Aids with Fusion Center monitoring, all-hazards information review, common operating picture updates, and shift documentation under guidance. Assists with receiving, documenting, and relaying notifications of Incident Management Team activations as directed. Supports ECC playbook-driven tasks, including information intake, contact validation, documentation updates, notification support, readiness material maintenance, and escalation routing, and basic playbook maintenance activities under direct supervision; flags incomplete, outdated, or unclear information for review. Supports Security Operations, Cyber Incident Response, Enterprise Resilience, and shared-service teams by gathering and organizing incident information. Helps maintain emergency mass notification records, critical event management information, contact lists, and program documentation. Executes assigned support tasks during corporate emergencies, exercises, readiness activities, and standard ECC operations. Assists in identifying documentation gaps, response-operation shortfalls, and follow-up items from exercises or real-world events. Supports development of situational awareness reports, threat briefings, monitoring summaries, and coordination materials. Helps gather training inputs and readiness materials aligned to ECC operations, threat analysis, and coordination standards. Helps monitor approved all-hazards information sources and flags potential impacts, incomplete information, or escalation needs for review by more experienced team members. What We're Looking For: Education requirements are listed below: Bachelor's degree in Emergency Management, Criminal Justice, Homeland Security, Intelligence Studies, Business, Communications, Information Technology, or related field, or equivalent education, certifications, and relevant experience. Work Experience requirement listed below: Experience: 0-1 year in threat intelligence, emergency management, security operations, enterprise resilience, incident coordination, law enforcement, military intelligence, or related disciplines. Preferred Requirements: Basic understanding of ICS, NIMS, FEMA National Response Framework, threat monitoring, documentation management, emergency notification tools, and corporate emergency response concepts. Certification preferred, not required.

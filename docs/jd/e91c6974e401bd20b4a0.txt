Positioned in the first line of defense, this role leverages advanced technical expertise in Information Technology (IT), Cybersecurity, and Information Risk Management to strengthen risk and compliance programs within a technology-focused business operation. This role will partner with business units, Compliance, Risk Management, Audit, and Regulators to identify, assess, and mitigate risks related to the adoption of AI capabilities across the company. Responsible for supporting business and technology leader adherence to the established risk framework and ongoing supervision of business controls and the new enhanced AI risk management frameworks developed by NIST and Cyber Risk Institute for the financial sector and the new OCC Super Intelligence supervisory guidance. The implementation of AI capabilities requires an enhancement to our existing risk and control environment across programs to include risk and control self-assessments, identification and evaluation of control effectiveness, identifying control failures, facilitating risk and compliance remediation, internal and external audits and regulatory exams, and monitoring of the first line of defense to minimize risk exposures and strengthen the overall control environment leveraging industry frameworks. This role will partner across business and technology to manage risk assessment data and uses critical thinking to identify key data-driven insights tied to first line of defense, specifically focusing on the identification of trends and patterns related to IT, Cyber, and Data risks. What you'll do: Directs and oversees AI specific Risk Assessment and Management to include but not limited to: Conducting regular risk assessments of IT systems, applications, infrastructure, and data, identifying vulnerabilities and recommending appropriate mitigation strategies. This includes evaluating risks related to cybersecurity, data management, system availability, architectural health and application failures. Oversight of AI Risk Management procedures/process, accurate regulatory reporting and filing, document governance, risk control self-assessments, procedure governance, control design, new product controls, Function or Line of Business Third Party Governance, or quality governance. Applies a holistic understanding of risk and regulatory compliance to include business strategies and solutions leveraging a deep understanding of information technology, information security, and information management. Responsible for first line of defense data analysis, report preparation and trend analysis, utilizing business intelligence tools. Designs solutions for unanswered business questions and anticipates future business needs. Directs, organizes and oversees action plans designed to enhance risk management practices in alignment with industry frameworks (e.g., ITIL, NIST, TOGAF, COBIT, and CRI). Influences and develops innovative solutions to mitigate risk and prevent risk exposures which result in significant business impacts. Manages the most complex projects involving cross-functional areas within the first line of defense. Defines and outlines new approaches to problem resolution and leads project team from concept through implementation. Advises senior management on the status of their control environment related to risk identification and control weaknesses. Identifies critical areas to monitor and escalates issues and findings to appropriate stakeholders. Serves as a primary resource to cross functional team members and advises on risk mitigation opportunities. Stays informed of changes to the regulatory environment to ensure proper compliance with processes and requirements are followed. Ensures risks associated with business activities are effectively identified, measured, monitored, and controlled in accordance with risk and compliance policies and procedures.

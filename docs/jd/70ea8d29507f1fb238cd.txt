We are seeking a Senior Software Engineer to join the NVIDIA Infrastructure Control Plane team. NVIDIA Infrastructure Control Plane is the glue and front door to DSX OS, bringing together infrastructure capabilities from across teams into a platform that humans and AI agents can use. Designed to support the world’s largest AI datacenters, it presents exciting problems in scale, reliability, and integration. Our work spans everything from low-level network integration to building APIs for humans and agents. You’ll work alongside experienced engineers to develop services in Go, integrate diverse infrastructure technologies, and help make sophisticated systems accessible and reliable. Our stack is composed of many technologies bringing together best-in-class solutions across NVIDIA and OSS. Including but not limited to Kubernetes, Bare metal provisioning, VM provisioning, KubeVirt, NVIDIA Infra Controller, What you’ll be doing: Design and develop backend services in Go that power DSX OS. Build APIs and workflows that enable humans and AI agents to provision and handle compute, networking, and infrastructure resources. Work with Kubernetes and KubeVirt to automate container and virtual machine lifecycle management. Solve distributed systems and scaling challenges for some of the world’s largest AI datacenters Collaborate across teams to integrate infrastructure services and technologies, debugging issues from low-level networking through application APIs. Develop and integrate AI-powered and agentic workflows, with attention to correctness, reliability, and testing. Contribute to design discussions, code reviews, automated testing, observability, and documentation as well as engineering practices. Help develop and grow the OSS community around DSX OS. What we need to see: BS degree in Engineering or Computer Science, or equivalent experience. 8+ years of experience. Advanced Go and Kubernetes experience. Experience developing microservices and distributed systems. Experience delivering software services and APIs used by hundreds or thousands of engineers. Background building and deploying fault tolerant, high availability systems. Background with virtualization and hardware provisioning Familiarity with at least one cloud provider and ideally multiple providers. Familiarity with continuous delivery and integration of software including tools, strategies, and systems.

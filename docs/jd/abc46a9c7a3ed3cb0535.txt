We are seeking an experienced actuary to join our Variable Annuity Valuation team. This position may be filled at either the Associate Actuary or Actuary level, based on the selected candidate’s experience, actuarial credentials and qualifications.
 
In this role, you will play an important part in Protective’s financial reporting process, performing Statutory, GAAP and Tax valuation for variable annuity products and analyzing key drivers of financial results. You will also have opportunities to enhance actuarial models, improve and automate valuation and reporting processes, support new product implementation, and respond to evolving regulatory requirements. You will work closely with Finance and other partners across the organization to analyze results and communicate actuarial insights.
  Key Responsibilities Perform quarterly Statutory, Tax and GAAP valuations and earnings variance analysis for variable annuity products using AXIS. Analyze financial results, identify key earnings drivers and clearly communicate findings to actuarial, Finance and other stakeholders. Prepare monthly, quarterly and annual reports for internal and external stakeholders. Maintain and enhance valuation and business planning models. Evaluate model changes and enhancements, quantify their financial impacts and communicate results to key stakeholders. Prepare and review SOX controls and support internal audit, external audit and actuarial governance requirements. Develop automation and analytical tools to improve the efficiency and effectiveness of reporting and financial analysis. Support new product implementation, regulatory developments and other strategic valuation initiatives. Partner across Actuarial, Finance and other functions to improve valuation, projection and reporting processes. Support ad hoc analysis and management reporting, as needed.  Core Competencies (Skills, knowledge and abilities) Strong knowledge of actuarial valuation, financial reporting and modeling concepts. Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to independently investigate and explain financial results. Ability to understand actuarial models and clearly explain model results and their financial statement impacts. Strong spreadsheet and technical skills, with an interest in automation and continuous process improvement. Ability to manage recurring financial reporting deadlines while maintaining accuracy and attention to detail. Strong written and verbal communication skills, including the ability to communicate technical concepts to both actuarial and non-actuarial stakeholders. Ability to work independently while collaborating effectively within a team and across functions.  Required Qualifications Bachelor’s degree in actuarial science, mathematics, statistics, finance or a related quantitative field. ASA ( Associate of the Society of Actuaries) certification. Minimum of 3 years actuarial experience in life insurance or annuity valuation, financial reporting, modeling or a closely related area. Demonstrated analytical and problem-solving skills, including the ability to independently investigate and explain financial results. Strong spreadsheet and technical skills. Strong written and verbal communication skills.  Preferred Qualifications FSA ( Fellow of the Society of Actuaries) designation. Variable annuity valuation or financial reporting experience, particularly with products featuring guaranteed living or death benefits. Valuation or modeling experience using AXIS. Experience with SQL, VBA or comparable programming and data analysis tools. Experience with SOX controls, actuarial governance, internal or external audit support, and financial result attribution. Experience improving or automating actuarial valuation, projection or reporting processes. #LI-AP1

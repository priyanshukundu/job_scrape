Start your Voyage – Apply Now Get to Know the Opportunity: This role is part of the Voya Investment Management Entry Level Analyst Program, beginning in July 2027. The program is designed to launch and develop the careers of entry-level professionals through hands-on experience, on-the-job development, and structured training. As a Jr. Client Advisor in Insurance Solations you will work closely with a specialized team of insurance asset management sales professionals. You will support asset management sales and advisory efforts for U.S. and non-U.S. insurance companies while collaborating across the full range of business partners, including investment, client service, marketing, consultant coverage, request for proposal, and operational teams. The successful candidate will bring curiosity, initiative, and a strong drive to learn, contribute, and execute. This role is based in our New York office and follows an Office Centric approach requiring on average 3-4 days onsite each week. The Contributions You’ll Make: Support senior members of the Insurance Solutions team with the marketing and distribution of fixed income solutions, alternatives, and equities. Contribute to efforts focused on retaining existing business and developing new sales opportunities. Partner with the Sales team to expand existing client relationships through additional investment strategies. Conduct client, industry, competitor, and financial market research to support sales and advisory activities. Help identify and cultivate prospective clients and new business opportunities. Collaborate with investment, client service, marketing, consultant coverage, request for proposal, and operational teams to advance business growth objectives. Prepare clear, accurate, and timely materials that support client discussions, presentations, and business-development activities. Minimum Knowledge & Experience: Applicants should be graduating with an undergraduate degree in December 2026 or spring or summer 2027. Pursuing a bachelor's degree with a demonstrated interest in financial markets, economics, business, entrepreneurship, or a related area. High school diploma or GED. Examples of leadership, accountability, and ownership of the outcome of a goal, project, or initiative. Ability to work effectively as part of a team and quickly integrate into an organization. Ability to learn complex concepts related to investments, investment products, laws, regulations, or other specialized subject matter. Personal interest in finance and the motivation to learn through independent study. Excellent interpersonal, collaboration, and teamwork skills. Strong project management, organization, planning, and presentation skills. Strong analytical skills and a demonstrated ability to solve problems creatively. Ability to work effectively in a deadline-driven environment without compromising the accuracy or quality of work. Openness to new ideas and a commitment to acquiring new skills. Superior written and verbal communication skills. Proficiency with Microsoft Office products. Ability to pass the Series 7 and Series 63 licensing examinations when required. Preferred Knowledge & Experience: Academic coursework, internships, student-organization involvement, or other experience related to financial markets, investments, economics, business, or entrepreneurship. Interest in pursuing additional industry accreditations, licenses, or professional certifications. Interest in insurance asset management, institutional sales, client relationship management, or investment solutions. Desire to build knowledge of fixed income, alternative investments, equities, and the insurance industry. Financial and insurance asset management educational support will be provided where necessary.

What Information Security and Risk contributes to Cardinal Health Information Technology oversees the effective development, delivery, and operation of computing and information services. This function anticipates, plans, and delivers Information Technology solutions and strategies that enable operations and drive business value. Information Security and Risk develops, implements, and enforces security controls to protect the organization's technology assets from intentional or inadvertent modification, disclosure or destruction. This job family develops system back-up and disaster recovery plans. Information Technology also conducts incident response, threat management, vulnerability scanning, virus management and intrusion detection and completes risk assessments. The Manager, Cyber Risk, is responsible for executing and continuously enhancing the cybersecurity risk management program to ensure risks are effectively identified, assessed, prioritized, and managed in alignment with organizational objectives. Reporting to the Director, Cyber Risk Services, this role plays a key part in operationalizing risk frameworks, maintaining risk visibility, and enabling risk-informed decision-making across the enterprise. This role leads cybersecurity risk capabilities including risk framework and taxonomy implementation, risk register management, risk assessments, and risk response & remediation oversight. It also supports integration with enterprise risk management (ERM) processes and governance forums, ensuring that cybersecurity risks are consistently managed and communicated across business and technology functions. Qualifications 8+ years of experience in cybersecurity, risk management, or information security roles preferred. Strong understanding of cybersecurity risk management frameworks, methodologies, and governance processes. Experience conducting risk assessments, FAIR risk methodology, managing risk registers, and supporting remediation activities. Familiarity with GRC tools and platforms (e.g., Archer or ServiceNow) supporting risk and compliance management. Strong understanding of cybersecurity frameworks (e.g., NIST CSF, ISO 27001) and regulatory requirements. Strong analytical, problem-solving, and communication skills Experience in a leadership or program management role within cybersecurity risk or GRC functions. Experience in highly regulated industries (e.g., healthcare, financial services, or government). Advanced degree (MBA, MS in Cybersecurity, Information Systems, or related field). Professional certifications such as CISSP, CRISC, CISM, or CISA. Experience with ERM frameworks and integration. Responsibilities Cyber Risk Strategy & Governance Support execution of the cyber risk strategy, aligned with ERM frameworks and business objectives. Partner with Cyber Risk Services leadership to implement risk governance processes, methodologies, and operating models, and advise on consistent practices across cybersecurity and business teams. Risk Framework & Taxonomy Implementation Implement and maintain standardized cyber risk frameworks, taxonomies, and methodologies aligned with ERM practices. Ensure consistent categorization, scoring, and prioritization of cyber risks, refining methodologies as threats, regulations, and business priorities evolve. Risk Register Management Maintain a centralized cyber risk register covering systems, applications, and business units, with risks documented, prioritized, and assigned to accountable owners. Review and update entries regularly, partnering with stakeholders to ensure complete risk identification and accurate remediation tracking. Risk Quantification & Assessments Conduct cyber risk assessments including quantitative risk analysis using structured methodologies to identify threats, vulnerabilities, and control gaps and evaluate risk likelihood, impact, and severity. Work with cybersecurity, IT, and business teams to gather inputs and validate outcomes, providing actionable recommendations to reduce risk and improve security posture. Risk Response & Remediation Oversight Support risk mitigation and remediation strategies with stakeholders, prioritizing by risk severity and business impact. Track remediation against defined SLAs and risk tolerance thresholds, monitor effectiveness, and escalate risks as needed Enterprise Risk Management (ERM) Alignment Align cyber risks, controls, and reporting with enterprise risk frameworks and taxonomies. Support integration of cyber risks into enterprise risk reporting and governance, providing input on risk scenarios, trends, and mitigation progress. Vulnerability & Control Remediation Monitoring Monitor remediation performance and SLA compliance for vulnerabilities and control deficiencies, escalating overdue or non-compliant actions. Work with vulnerability management and security operations teams to drive timely remediation, providing risk-based insights to prioritize efforts. Governance Risk & Compliance (GRC) Tooling & Process Enablement Support configuration and management of GRC platforms (e.g., Archer) for risk tracking, reporting, and workflow automation. Define use cases and requirements, integrate GRC tools with risk, compliance, and operational workflows, and drive adoption and optimization across cybersecurity and business teams. Cross-Functional Collaboration & Governance Partner with cybersecurity, IT, compliance, audit, and business teams to embed risk practices into operations, aligning risk insights with Cyber Detection & Response, Exposure Management, and Application Security teams. Engage governance forums and committees to support risk decisions and escalation, and provide documentation and evidence for audit and regulatory activities. Metrics, Continuous Improvement & Maturity Develop and maintain KPIs and KRIs, identify opportunities to enhance risk management processes, tools, and frameworks, and support risk maturity models and roadmaps to improve program effectiveness. Stay current on evolving threats, regulations, and best practices to drive continuous improvement of enterprise risk management capabilities.

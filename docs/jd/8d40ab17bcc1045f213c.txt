HOW YOU'LL MAKE IMPACT

Infrastructure AI is building the systems that will change how infrastructure work gets done at Airwallex. You'll design and operate goal-oriented AI agents that automate SRE, DevOps, and DBA workflows across our global infrastructure.

 - Build goal-oriented infrastructure AI agents on Airwallex’s internal agent platform to automate SRE, DevOps, and DBA workflows, including incident investigation and remediation, infrastructure provisioning, deployment orchestration, database operations, and capacity management.

 - Design reliable agent architectures with planning, reasoning, tool use, error recovery, safety guardrails, auditability, and human-in-the-loop escalation for systems that interact with production infrastructure.

 - Integrate agents with Kubernetes, Terraform, cloud APIs across AWS, GCP, and Aliyun, monitoring and observability platforms, databases, and CI/CD pipelines through well-designed tool interfaces.

 - Define and improve evaluation frameworks for agent effectiveness, including task completion, accuracy, time to resolution, and escalation rates.

 - Partner with SRE, DevOps, and platform engineering teams to identify high-value workflows for automation and translate operational pain points into effective agent capabilities.

 - Help shape Airwallex’s technical vision for AI-driven infrastructure operations by bringing forward new ideas in agent architecture, reliability, safety, and evaluation.



You'll work from our Seattle office on Mondays, Wednesdays, and Thursdays.




WHAT WE'RE LOOKING FOR

 - 5+ years of professional software engineering experience, with strong proficiency in a backend language such as Python, Go, Java, or Kotlin.

 - Strong system design skills, with the ability to architect reliable, safe, and scalable systems that interact with production infrastructure.

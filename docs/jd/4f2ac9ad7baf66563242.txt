What You'll Contribute This individual contributor role plays a key function on the marketing analytics team, working collaboratively with stakeholders to understand their needs and provide meaningful Power BI dashboards while analyzing emerging trend within the data to quickly identify drivers of volume performance, marketing activities, and translate findings into actionable insights to drive meaningful business results. The candidate will be responsible for supporting various marketing channels through performance analysis, reporting and dashboard development, stakeholder presentations, forecasting, data mining, and various ad hoc analyses utilizing a variety of data sources and technology systems. The candidate will possess expertise in Power BI, including DAX, as well as have function knowledge of SQL and SAS. They will leverage data from enterprise platforms and internal data sources to build meaningful dashboards which help identify opportunities, influence strategy, and drive measurable business outcomes. The candidate will develop and maintain production dashboards and processes to enable timely analysis, measurement of results, and delivery of actionable insights to stakeholders. The position requires the ability to identify, study, and solve complex business problems using analytical and technical expertise while effectively communicating results to audiences at all levels of the organization. The candidate is a motivated self-starter with a passion for uncovering insights and solutions from complex data visibility needs and diverse datasets. They will become a subject matter expert in the company's large enterprise data warehouse and Power BI reporting solutions. They will utilize strong technical skills, business acumen, and innovation to drive projects to completion while ensuring the accuracy and integrity of reporting and insights. The candidate is a strategic thinker who can work autonomously and effectively in a dynamic and evolving environment. What You'll Do Creates, enhances, analyzes, and maintains backend datasets, lookup tables, and scheduled jobs all used in the production of reports and dashboards used by end users. Develops dashboards and measurement frameworks to support strategic decision-making. Manages business processes and provides relevant insights to stakeholders. Provides actionable recommendations based on data patterns and performance trends. Partners with stakeholders to identify key business questions and opportunities for data visualization and analysis. Serves as a subject matter expert for all things Power BI. Provides guidance on analytics implementation and reporting best practices. Collaborates with Marketing, Product, Technology, dba, and other business teams to drive BI innovations. Partners on BI experimentation strategies and AI integration within reporting and analytics tools for reporting or process enhancements. Applies creativity and analytical rigor to investigate business problems and identify root causes. Helps respond to strategic inquiries from stakeholders across the enterprise. Engages with peer analytics teams to identify new opportunities, methodologies, and data applications. Evaluates existing reporting, data collection, and analytics processes to identify enhancement opportunities. Streamlines and automates reporting and analysis workflows where possible. Create and maintain data documentation, data dictionaries, and standard operating procedures as needed. Supports improvements in data quality, governance, and measurement accuracy.

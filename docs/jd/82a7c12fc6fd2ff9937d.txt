If Braze sounds like a place where you can thrive, we can’t wait to meet you. WHAT YOU'LL DO Braze’s product is the control center for thousands of the world’s most sophisticated marketing teams. The Frontend Infrastructure & Experience (FIX) team builds the platform foundations that every product engineer relies on and owns key parts of the end-user experience. The team makes it easy for engineers across Braze to build polished, accessible, and consistent UIs. FIX owns our design system and component library, internationalization stack, code generation and scaffolding tooling, and the navigation and routing layer of the product. The team also owns key customer-facing experiences including our data tables and our rich text, drag-and-drop, and code editors. As a Senior Engineer on FIX, you will build high-impact frontend infrastructure and product experiences used across Braze. You will lead important projects from design through rollout, raise the quality bar through technical judgment and code review, and help other engineers move faster by making the product easier to build, test, and maintain. In this role you will: Design and build foundational frontend systems, components, tools, and patterns used by engineers across Braze Lead large projects end-to-end, including technical design, implementation, testing, rollout, and operational follow-through Improve the developer experience of working in a large TypeScript and React codebase through better abstractions, tooling, documentation, and examples Build customer-facing product experiences that are polished, accessible, performant, and consistent with Braze’s product standards Partner with product engineers, designers, and infrastructure teams to solve cross-cutting frontend problems Mentor other engineers through code review, pairing, technical guidance, and clear communication Recent and ongoing work on

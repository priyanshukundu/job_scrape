Job Description: The Team Join Fidelity’s Models and Analytics team within the Collateral and Market Risk Management team to help protect the firm from potential losses from the margin lending and trading activities of both institutional and retail customers of Fidelity Investments. In addition, the team is expanding responsibilities into modeling internal treasury risk for Fidelity’s collateral obligations. This individual will join a team responsible for model ownership of rules that determine lending appetite and providing continuous analysis of the risks inherent in each client’s portfolio and investment strategy. Portfolios are analyzed and evaluated in real-time through extensive simulation and stress analysis designed to estimate market related risks. The team develops and maintains risk models, methodologies and processes to ensure the risk framework remains robust and effective as market structures, strategies and products evolve. The Model’s and Analytics team is a specialized team within Collateral and Market Risk with additional emphasis on financial modeling, leveraging AI tools, strong technical acumen, and independent critical thinking for project-oriented work that brings fundamental change to supplement daily operations.

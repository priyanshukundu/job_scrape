Key Responsibilities Salesforce Development & Configuration Develop and maintain Salesforce Health Cloud solutions using Apex, Flows, OmniStudio, and Lightning Components. Configure Salesforce functionality to support business requirements and enhance user experiences. Build and support custom workflows, automations, and platform-based solutions. Assist with integrations between Salesforce and enterprise applications using APIs and integration platforms. Participate in the design, testing, deployment, and support of new features and enhancements. Follow established engineering standards, coding best practices, and security requirements. Collaboration & Agile Delivery Partner with product owners, business analysts, and stakeholders to understand business needs and translate requirements into technical solutions. Collaborate across Sales, Operations, Marketing, and IT teams to deliver high-quality applications. Participate in Agile ceremonies including sprint planning, daily standups, backlog refinement, and retrospectives. Contribute to peer code reviews, knowledge sharing, and continuous improvement initiatives. Communicate project status, technical challenges, and risks effectively. Testing & Support Develop and execute unit tests and support integration and user acceptance testing. Troubleshoot application issues and production defects. Support application deployments and post-release validation activities. Monitor system performance and identify enhancement opportunities. Participate in root cause analysis and implementation of corrective actions. Support CI/CD processes and software development lifecycle (SDLC) standards. Documentation Maintain user stories, technical tasks, and development activities in Azure DevOps (ADO). Create and update technical, configuration, and support documentation. Document application changes, deployment activities, and troubleshooting procedures. Support knowledge transfer and operational readiness documentation. At Humana, you'll have the opportunity to build technology that directly supports our mission of helping people achieve their best health. Experience with Salesforce Sales Cloud; Health Cloud experience is a plus. Experience working with APIs, integrations, or connected enterprise applications. Understanding of Agile methodologies and software development lifecycle (SDLC) practices.

Job Description Every day at FIS, innovators, problem solvers and changemakers work together to create technology that advances the world of finance. We embrace new ideas, challenge what's possible and empower our teams to make a lasting impact. If you're ready to help shape the future of fintech, unlock your next chapter with FIS. About the role As a Software Engineer Lead, you will provide technical leadership while designing and delivering reliable, secure and scalable software solutions. You will guide complex engineering work from design through production, partner with product and business teams, and help engineers make sound technical decisions. You will also strengthen development practices through thoughtful architecture, code quality, testing and continuous improvement. Your key responsibilities Lead the design, development, testing and delivery of complex software solutions. Translate business needs into clear technical designs and practical implementation plans. Guide technical decisions related to architecture, performance, security, reliability and maintainability. Review code, resolve complex technical issues and improve engineering standards across the team. Mentor engineers and collaborate with product, delivery and business partners throughout the software development lifecycle. What you bring Required qualifications Strong experience with FIS Systematics core banking technology. Strong experience designing, developing and supporting production software. Experience leading complex technical work and guiding engineers through design and delivery. Strong knowledge of software architecture, development practices, source control, automated testing and code review. Ability to troubleshoot complex systems and recommend practical, sustainable solutions. Clear communication skills and experience working with technical and non-technical partners. 5-10 years of relevant work experience Preferred qualifications Experience with cloud-based services, distributed systems or microservices. Experience with continuous integration, continuous delivery and DevOps practices. Experience improving software performance, security, scalability or operational reliability. The impact you’ll make As a Software Engineer Lead, you will help the team turn complex needs into dependable technology solutions. Your leadership will improve how software is designed, built and supported while helping other engineers grow. Through strong technical direction and collaboration, you will contribute to solutions that support the future of financial technology. What we offer you Bring your ideas. Build your future. At FIS, you'll find the support, resources and opportunities to develop your career while helping shape the future of financial technology.

Duties: Responsible for discovering, synthesizing, and analyzing information from a variety of internal and external sources, including tools, processes, documentation, and stakeholders. Act as a partner to both the business and the technology teams. Identify scope gaps and missing requirements by acquiring an in depth understanding of current state (i.e., legacy system) and future state. Identify product team dependencies and plan for resolution. Work cross-functionally to analyze and define key stakeholder business needs. Analyze current and future-state business processes to identify usability gaps and workflow optimization opportunities, and develop process flows, data mapping specifications, and functional requirements using Figma, SQL, and Confluence. Perform requirements analysis, story decomposition, system integration analysis, and data flow design. Translate business requirements into technical specifications by creating epics, user stories, acceptance criteria, UML diagrams, and requirements traceability artifacts to support Agile software development using Azure DevOps or GitHub and Artifactory, Jira and Confluence. Regularly engage in agile activities including sprint planning and backlog grooming. Act as an advisor to the Product owner regarding next steps and approvals. Build high quality user stories and acceptance criteria; work with scrum team to refine user stories. Provide requirement and design support to scrum team. Support development of user interface requirements and design by collaborating with UI/UX tools and teams. Perform data analysis and validation to support application development, user acceptance testing, and business rule verification by developing complex queries, data mappings, dashboard development, and reporting solutions using SQL, Splunk, and Power BI. Support or develop test plans and test cases; participate in test results verification. Assist in building cross-team collaboration and define acceptance criteria for integrated components. Adhere to analysis best practices for epics and user stories. Assist in developing and maintaining policies, standards, guidelines and documentation to ensure that a consistent framework is applied across the enterprise. Leverage Jira or Azure DevOps, and Confluence, to create epics, user stories, and UML diagrams to support Agile sprint planning and backlog grooming. Design and develop process flows, data mapping documents, and ER diagrams, and write complex SQL queries using Oracle or SSRS to support data analysis, gap analysis, and UAT. Define user interface requirements by creating wireframes, screen-level specifications, activity diagrams, and user journey flows using Figma, Visio or Draw.io, and Splunk. Apply online at www.theocc.com . No calls. EOE. About Us The Options Clearing Corporation (OCC) is the world's largest equity derivatives clearing organization. Founded in 1973, OCC is dedicated to promoting stability and market integrity by delivering clearing and settlement services for options, futures and securities lending transactions. As a Systemically Important Financial Market Utility (SIFMU), OCC operates under the jurisdiction of the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC), the U.S. Commodity Futures Trading Commission (CFTC), and the Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System. OCC has more than 100 clearing members and provides central counterparty (CCP) clearing and settlement services to 23 exchanges and trading platforms.

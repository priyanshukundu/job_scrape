Job Description: The Liquidity & Account Services Product Manager supports the day-to-day management, enhancement and delivery of corporate account and liquidity products across TD Securities Canada and the US. The account services portfolio includes account propositions and structures, account opening and lifecycle servicing, account master and core-ledger capabilities, statements and advices, account and transaction reporting, pricing, digital channels and API connectivity. The liquidity portfolio includes virtual accounts, cash concentration, sweeps, pooling and investment services. This is a hands-on individual contributor role. Working with product colleagues and cross-functional partners, the role gathers requirements, supports delivery of agreed roadmap priorities, maintains product documentation, monitors product and service performance, and helps resolve client and operational issues. The role contributes analysis and recommendations to support product decisions. Key Responsibilities Account Services Product Management • Support delivery of the agreed account services roadmap by gathering client and business requirements, documenting product needs and tracking assigned initiatives. • Maintain the account product catalogue, including eligible client segments, currencies, legal entities, booking locations, account types, features, service levels and channel access. • Support enhancements to corporate operating accounts, call/deposit accounts and specialized structures such as escrow, client money/FBO and financial institution accounts, within approved product scope and legal requirements. • Gather and analyze client research, sales feedback, competitor information, service data and regulatory requirements to identify product gaps and contribute to business cases and prioritization discussions. Account Lifecycle Support • Document and improve client and colleague journeys for account opening, documentation, activation, maintenance, restriction, suspension and closure, in line with established product rules, approval processes and service expectations. • Work with Client Onboarding, KYC/AML, Financial Crime Risk, Tax, Legal, Compliance and Operations to clarify documentation and due diligence requirements and support process improvements while maintaining required controls and evidence. • Gather and document product requirements for account master data, mandates and authorities, signatories, account naming, interest and fee parameters, statements, notices, entitlements and downstream reporting. • Support exception management and complex account requests by documenting issues, coordinating input from relevant teams and routing decisions through established escalation and approval processes. • Maintain assigned product documentation, operating procedures, product inventories, terms and conditions, disclosures and client-facing materials, coordinating review and approval with the relevant teams. Account Servicing, Reporting & Digital Experience • Support enhancements to statements, interest and fee advices, balance and transaction reporting, account confirmations, audit requests and service communications. • Document requirements and support testing for account capabilities across client portals, host-to-host channels and APIs, including account visibility, self-service maintenance, alerts, document access and near-real-time reporting. • Work with Implementation, Client Service and Operations to identify manual touchpoints and recurring inquiries, support process improvements and improve first-time-right delivery. • Track and analyze service and experience metrics such as onboarding cycle time, activation success, digital adoption, statement/reporting quality, inquiry volumes, complaint themes, defects and time to resolution; highlight issues and follow up on agreed actions. Liquidity Solutions Product Management • Support the development, testing and enhancement of virtual accounts, sweeps, cash concentration, notional pooling, investment solutions and digital investment / self-service portal capabilities. • Support consistent onboarding, pricing, reporting, channels, servicing and controls across account and liquidity capabilities; identify gaps and coordinate follow-up with the relevant teams. • Gather input from Treasury, Finance, Risk and Legal on product economics, balance-sheet treatment, interest methodologies, liquidity implications and jurisdictional constraints; document requirements and follow up on open questions. Commercialization, Pricing & Performance Support • Prepare and maintain product reporting on revenue, balances, margin, fees, cost-to-serve, investment spend and profitability; analyze trends and highlight variances for review. • Support pricing analysis and implementation of approved account and liquidity pricing, including standard price points, waivers, interest arrangements and periodic price realization reviews; route exceptions through established approval processes. • Provide product information and analysis to Sales, Coverage and Solutions teams for client pitches, requests for proposal, deal discussions and launch preparation. • Prepare and update training materials, playbooks, sales tools and client communications; support product launches and adoption activities and track results against agreed objectives. Product Delivery & Technology Collaboration • Manage assigned product work items from requirements gathering and solution review through approvals, testing, implementation, launch support and post-implementation follow-up. • Translate agreed product needs into epics, requirements and acceptance criteria; maintain assigned backlog items, clarify questions and flag dependencies or prioritization decisions to the responsible product owner. • Coordinate assigned delivery activities with Technology, Digital, Operations, Implementation, Client Service, Finance, Legal, Risk, Compliance and regional product teams; track actions, dependencies and blockers. • Support operational readiness, data quality checks, migration planning, training and support preparation before launch; participate in testing, track defects and follow up on remediation after implementation. • Track third-party and platform dependencies where applicable, document service or delivery issues and coordinate follow-up with the responsible teams. Risk, Controls & Governance Support • Support product governance and risk assessments by gathering information, maintaining records and coordinating reviews with the relevant product and control teams. • Apply established legal, regulatory, financial crime, tax, conduct, privacy, records-management and operational-resilience requirements in assigned product activities; seek guidance and escalate questions where needed. • Prepare documentation and track actions for new product approvals, periodic product reviews, control testing, audit and regulatory requests, and issue remediation. • Monitor reports and service issues relating to account misuse, dormant or inactive accounts, negative balances, unauthorized activity, fee and interest errors and statement/reporting incidents; escalate concerns through established channels and track corrective actions. • Maintain assigned product inventories, control documentation, management information and governance action logs across legal entities and jurisdictions. Collaboration & Communication • Provide clear updates on assigned initiatives, product performance, risks and open issues; present analysis and recommendations to support decisions by the responsible product owners. • Share product knowledge with colleagues, learn from experienced product managers and use consistent practices for requirements, documentation, testing and issue tracking. • Participate in client discussions and internal product training as needed, providing product information and following up on questions and actions. Qualifications • Relevant experience in transaction banking, cash management, treasury services, product management or a related area such as operations, implementation or client service. • Familiarity with corporate account services or relevant account processes, such as onboarding, documentation, account master data, servicing, statements and reporting, pricing and billing, digital channels or controls; willingness to build knowledge across the portfolio. • Familiarity with liquidity solutions such as virtual accounts, sweeps, cash concentration, pooling or short-term investment services is an asset. • Ability to gather and document requirements, organize work items, support testing and implementation, and follow through on assigned deliverables. Experience supporting technology-enabled product or process changes is an asset. • Analytical skills and comfort working with product, balance, revenue, pricing and service data; ability to prepare accurate reports and contribute to business-case analysis. • Ability to work collaboratively with Technology, Operations, Sales, Client Service, Implementation and control partners, and to seek specialist input when needed. • Awareness of the importance of product governance, regulatory requirements, operational controls and issue escalation in a regulated financial institution; willingness to learn the applicable processes. • Clear written and verbal communication, attention to detail, sound organization and problem-solving skills, and a proactive approach to learning and teamwork.
